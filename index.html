Exodus Wars

Galactic Codex & Tactical Information Database

Welcome to the Exodus Wars information database. This site is dedicated to documenting canon sources of information for the series of stories known as "The Exodus Wars." While this front page, and the Authors section is devoted to real life, we hope that the articles themselves will be immersive and entertaining to any reader.

The Exodus Wars were first conceived in 1998, by James Davis and Scott Sanburn. It started as a small site on a cheap web account dedicated to hosting pictures of LEGOTM models. As time went on, we began writing stories to go with the models.

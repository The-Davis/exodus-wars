<noinclude>This is an unfinished navbox under the name <nowiki>"Template:Pelagrim Crisis Navbox"</nowiki>.  It will need further revision to properly document the death of [[Andreas Tischler]] and the destabilizing effect it has on the Mesarthrim and the Pelagrim Sector.

Events I've listed;
*Assault on Iviris - The Assault where Andreas was killed, in the [[Honis]] System.
*Death of Andreas Tischler - self explanatory.
*Homemakers Field Vigil - Vigil held for Tischler.
*Stone Bridge Riots - Riots in response to Tischler's death, they blame the Ixoarchadia.
*Elevation of Eric Francis - Eric Francis becomes Uber General.
*Alliance of Planets Intervention - Titanian Assistance in investigation, additionally vessels sent.
*Intergalactic Incident of Admiral Merchant - Titanian Admiral attacks and detains Ixoarchada vessel leaving Mesarthrim Space.
*Prefecture Incident - Prefecture mistake Titanian "wargames" and patrols for aggression, short battle ensues.

*Declaration of Autonomy - Auellal Declare Independence.
*Auellal Attacks Mesar - Auellal attack Mesar.
*Hinks Arrested - Hinks Arrested.
*Ixoarchada Inquiry Summit - Ixoarchada work to ascertain truth to death of Tischler.
*Hinks Escapes with help from men loyal to him.
*Alliance of Planets Reparations - Alliance of Planets make apology to Ixoarchadia for battle, and payments to Ixoarchadia.  UMC is unable to pay as it is collapsing.

A '''battleship''' is a large, heavily-armored warship with a main battery consisting of the largest caliber of guns. They are larger, better-armed and better-armored than cruisers.

'''Command Ships''', or Flagships, are in charge of a fleet. Usually they are commanded by a flag officer.

'''Dreadnoughts''' are enormous ship classes that can dish out more firepower than most other designs. Unlike which focus only on firepower at a slight expense of speed, Dreadnoughts focus on both armor and firepower, almost completely sacrificing speed and maneuverability.

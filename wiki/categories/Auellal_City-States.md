The '''Auellal League''' only controls the world of [[Caledon]], which was still early in its colonial stages when the Auellal broke away from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. Many cities were established, and were partially isolated from each other during the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

The level of autonomy each city developed to survive the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]' attempt to retake [[Caledon]] made the city-state a logical administrative system for the [[Auellal League]] government. Each city and surrounding region is controlled exclusively by the city's government, though each city-state owes allegiance to the [[Auellal League]] as a whole. Many city-states are controlled by one or two Auellal [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clans]], though others have a majority population of non-aligned families.

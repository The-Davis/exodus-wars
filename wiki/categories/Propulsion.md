'''Propulsion''' systems cover a wide variety of systems. Space propulsion used to rely on chemical rockets, but now uses pure-energy devices like the [[Gravity Cavitation Drive]].

Ground propulsion can include treaded systems, wheeled vehicles, leg actuators, and anti-gravity lift systems

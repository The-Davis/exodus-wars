The '''Legion Corps''' is a component of the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Military of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture|military]]. Officially, a member of the [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Legion Corps]] is a soldier, though they are often referred to by their nicknames, such as "Legionaries", "Carpenters", and "Riggers".

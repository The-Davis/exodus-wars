'''Rikaz o Fii Cai iv Huerna''', literally translated as "The Kingdom of the Cai Family over all of Huerna", is the interstellar government of the [[Lorithos]]. It was formed after the death of [[Dirken Huern]] marked the end of the [[Huern Drastar]] and the rise of the [[Cai Drastar]].  The government was not officially named until the Lorithos found and settled on their new homeworld, [[Huerna]].

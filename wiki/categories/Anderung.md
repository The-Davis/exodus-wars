[[Anderung]] are cold, calculating creatures. They enslave their enemies, or they destroy them. Anderung serve the [[Geckans]] due to biological controls built into their psyche.

Individuals in the [[Anderung]] [[:Category:Races|race]] can be aloof and detached, or cruel and cold. They are the terrifying result of pure logic and utter disdain for life other than their own.

'''Plants''' are lifeforms including familiar organisms such as trees, flowers, herbs, bushes, grasses, vines, ferns, and mosses. Green plants obtain most of their energy from light via photosynthesis.

'''Fungi''' heterotrophic organisms characterized by a chitinous cell wall. Fungi reproduce sexually or asexually via spores. Yeasts, molds, and mushrooms are examples of fungi. In many alien environments, fungi play as important a role in the biosphere as plants to in human environments.

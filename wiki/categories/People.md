All stories need their protagonists and antagonists. Most science fiction will deliver dynamic characters, but tells tales in terms of black and white. With the Exodus Wars, we strive for much more blurred lines. Real people are in shades of gray. Heroes can be flawed, and villains can be redeemed.

Only major characters are listed under the main "[[People]]" category. The rest can be located in various subcategories.

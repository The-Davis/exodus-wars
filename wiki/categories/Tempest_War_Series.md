The '''Tempest War Series''' covers books and stories from the conflict known as the [[Tempest War]].

Betrayal is too strong a word for the war between the United Centusi States and the 
Prefecture. The violent Mesarthrim had too many scars from the war with the Empire to be healed by mere treaties and words. When William Camp gave the order to attack the Prefecture, it was like a madness infected the Mesarthrim people.

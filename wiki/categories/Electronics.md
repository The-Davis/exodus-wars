Electronics includes a wide variety of sub-categories. It generally involves the study of the flow of charge through various materials and devices.

All applications of electronics involve the transmission of power and possibly information. Although considered to be a theoretical branch of physics, the design and construction of electronic circuits to solve practical problems is an essential technique in the fields of electronics engineering and computer engineering.

While advanced communications have little to do with traditional electronics, they are considered part of the same roots.

'''Language''' is a system of spoken, written, or signed words and concepts used by intelligent species for general-purpose communication.

An '''official language''' is the language selected by a government for use in printing any government publications, used in any government broadcasts, and is the language the government conducts its business in. Most official languages are not actually mandated by the government: it is not a crime to speak something else.

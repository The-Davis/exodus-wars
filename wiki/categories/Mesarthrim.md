The [[Mesarthrim]] are a noble race with many virtues. The majority are fundamentalist Christians. They do have an unfortunate tendency to be prone to violence and even self destruction if they have no enemy to fight.

While no race is uniform and precisely the same in every regard, the [[Mesarthrim]] living today have a rabid hatred of the [[Anderung]], and a certain level of disdain for the [[Mesarnak]] still living today.

Many [[Mesarthrim]] are known for taking blood oaths and swearing eternal revenge on their enemies. There's a saying about the [[Mesarthrim]]: "If you make a friend of a [[Mesarthrim]], you have a friend for life. If you make an enemy of a [[Mesarthrim]], that will be about five minutes."

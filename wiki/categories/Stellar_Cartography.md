'''Stellar Cartography''' is the study and practice of making maps of star systems. Star maps are displayed both in 2D and 3D. Stellar Cartography combines both art and science involving the use of symbols to represent certain astronomical phenomena, as well as the ability to visualize the galaxy in an abstract and scaled-down form.

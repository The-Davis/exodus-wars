'''Hypermatter''' is composed of exotic particles the same way that normal matter is composed of particles. Hypermatter is only found in [[hyperspace]]. It is extremely volatile, rapidly decaying into dangerous and unstable particles which are fatal to most forms of life.

Hypermatter, if it could be used in our universe, would be much more powerful than an [[:Category:Antimatter|antimatter]] reaction. So far, all experiments with it have not yielded anything useful.

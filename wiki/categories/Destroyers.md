'''Destroyers''' are medium sized [[:Category:Warships|warships]] that focus on speed and firepower. They are designed to escort larger vessels in a fleet, and destroy large heavy targets.

'''Subspace Prowlers''' are a sub-class of destroyer, mostly used by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].  They use stealth and firepower instead of speed and firepower.

A '''medical condition''' is an abnormal state in an organism that impairs bodily functions. They often cause discomfort, dysfunction, distress, or death to the person afflicted.

Medical conditions include diseases, injuries, disabilities, disorders, syndromes, infections, isolated symptoms, and deviant behaviors.

While many diseases are biological processes with observable alterations of organ function or structure, others primarily involve alterations of behavior.

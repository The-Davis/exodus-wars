Volucris War Series: '''Revelations of the Volucris'''

The [[Tempest War]] ends, leaving the major powers of humanity broken and bitter. The Volucris War is unleashed in all its fury, revealing at long last the mysterious intellect behind the Swarms and its equally enigmatic goals. As great ships are uncovered across the galaxy and begin to come to life, it is apparent that the Volucris mean to do more than merely destroy.

The war begins in [[2329]]. The call for help from [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] goes unheeded: the Prefecture and Ikronin are fully engaged in a war that seems hopeless. The Meroniri have taken an entirely callous view of all non-Meroniri. Only the intervention of the [[Council of Independent Nations]] keeps Lorithan from being wiped out in a week.

A '''computer'' is a machine for manipulating data according to a list of instructions.

The ability to store and execute programs makes computers extremely versatile, and makes them an excellent aid to biological life in making calculations and organizing data.

The most modern computers have the ability to run [[AI]] programs, making them technically intelligent life forms in their own right.

A '''nation''' is any administrative body that controls any sizeable area of space. Typically, a nation controls at least one star-system, but most have several star systems under their flag.

Nations also refer to independent geographical regions on a single planet. By [[First Contact]], most worlds have united under a single government. Their early pre-unification nations are listed in sub-categories of their own.

In the eons of the story of the Exodus Wars universe, many nations have risen and fallen. Some, like [[Pax Anthronoris]] are lost in the mists of time, with only rumors and devastated artifacts left to show their passing. Others, like the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] have endured in more or less the same form for thousands of years.

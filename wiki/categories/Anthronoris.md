The [[Anthronoris]] are the ancient forerunners of the human [[:Category:Races|races]]. In the ancient days so far gone that even the [[Meroniri]] consider them legends, the [[Anthronoris]] carved out [[Pax Anthronoris]] from the fires of interstellar war. They destroyed countless intelligent species in the [[Orion Arm]], and were only stopped from spreading by the even more aggressive [[Ngrligru]].

Ancient members of the [[Anthronoris]] [[:Category:Races|race]] were arrogant, brutal, and confident in their infallibility. The few survivors left today are part of the [[Remnant]]. Some have learned from the mistakes of the past, others are very much the same as before. The [[Anthronoris]] remain effectively immortal.

'''Military Leaders''' are responsible for the training of militaries, the defense of their [[:Category:Nations|nation]], and the destruction of their enemies. Many military leaders listed here are now dead, but contributed to tactics as we know them today. Others are less significant, but are listed for the sake of thoroughness.

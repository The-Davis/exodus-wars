The military of the Mesarthrim Federation is its smallest size since the early days of the Resistance.  However, the Mesarthrim Federation's military is also the strongest the Mesarthrim military has ever been in pure firepower and training.  The Mesarthrim Federation adopted the Auellal governments policy of not having mandatory military service.
 
The [[Hohe Gouverneur]] is the Commander-in-Chief of the Mesarthrim Federation military, which counts the defence of national territory, the protection of Mesarthrim interests abroad, and the maintenance of galactic stability as some of its primary objectives.

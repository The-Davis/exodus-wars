In ancient days, the [[Lorithos]] were a proud race of scholars, poets, warriors, and architects. Their history is marked with some of the greatest accomplishments of humankind, but also of the single most devastating series of wars in history: The [[Burning Wars]].

After the [[Burning Wars]], the [[Lorithos]] were a broken people, ground into the dust of their world. Modern [[Lorithos]] have a wide range of personalities. Many despair at the pain of their conditions, and wallow in the misery of the horrid mutagenic changes in their bodies. Others are full of hope for the future, realizing that they are now recovering and that their culture and [[:Category:Races|race]] are not doomed.

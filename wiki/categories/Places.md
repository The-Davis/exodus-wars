Every story needs a stage. The tales of the Exodus Wars range from epic space battles raging across the cosmos, to the dark recesses of futuristic cities. The Exodus War universe focuses on the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, particularly the region of space known as the [[Orion Arm]].

If it can be mapped with any reasonable accuracy, it's considered a place on the stellar map. Galaxies, spinning away for eternity, are the biggest landmarks. Even relatively tiny space stations are charted if they are of significant importance.

*'''[[:Category:Galaxies|Galaxies]]''' are the largest objects charted by stellar cartographers. So far, no one in the [[Human]] or [[Ngrligru]] [[:Category:Races|races]] has managed to leave their home galaxy, the [[Milky Way]].

*'''[[:Category:Spiral Arms|Spiral Arms]]''' are the second largest category in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy. This category only applies to spiral galaxies, but there has not been an opportunity to explore elsewhere.

*'''[[:Category:Sectors|Sectors]]''' are several dozen light years in length and width, and are usually named after important landmarks within the sector.

*'''[[:Category:Star Systems|Star Systems]]''' are different from mere stars. Stars alone may not be inhabitted, but star systems are. They have planets or asteroids orbiting them that are inhabited by intelligent lifeforms.

*'''[[:Category:Planets|Planets]]''' are rocky or gaseous bodies which orbit stars. Many are inhabitted, either through their natural atmospheres, or through artificial domes or other systems for supporting life.

*'''[[:Category:Moons|Moons]]''' are rocky bodies which orbit planets. Some are inhabitted, usually through the use of aritificial domes and life support systems.

A '''corporation''' is the technical term used for most publicly traded businesses. While the "corporate sense" can still refer to any collective, the term is most commonly used to refer to a privately-owned business that produces consumer goods or services.

There are many powerful corporations throughout the galaxy, and [[:Category:Economics|economics]] is often the driving force for change in politics and society. Without these important forces at work, the galaxy would be stagnant.

'''Bombers''' are military aircraft designed to attack ground targets, primarily by dropping bombs. Strategic bombers are primarily designed for long-range strike missions with bombs against strategic targets such as supply bases, bridges, factories, shipyards, and cities, in order to damage an enemy's war effort. Tactical bombers are smaller aircraft that operate at shorter range, typically along with troops on the ground.

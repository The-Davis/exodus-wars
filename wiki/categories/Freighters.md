'''Freighters''' are any sort of starship that carries cargo, goods, and materials from one starport to another. Thousands of cargo carriers ply the spacelanes every day, handling most interstellar trade. Freighters are usually specially designed for the task, often being equipped with enormous airlocks, cranes, conveyors, and other mechanisms to load and unload.

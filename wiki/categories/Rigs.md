'''Rigs''' are walking vehicles controlled by a pilot. Rigs are usually bipedal.

Most Rigs are war machines, armored fighting vehicles with legs instead of treads or wheels. They provide an excellent weapons platform, and some improvements in mobility over [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]], though more traditional [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]] have better firepower and much better defenses.

Some Rigs are used for civilian purposes such as heavy construction work.

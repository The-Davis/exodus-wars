A starliner is a passenger ship used for pleasure voyages or for transporting people between star systems. Usually aboard starliners, the voyage itself and the ship's amenities are considered an essential part of the experience.

Starliners operate on a mostly set course, using different spacelanes than cargo. Some return with their passengers to their originating port, others ferry passengers from one point to another.

'''Antimatter''' is composed of antiparticles the same way that normal matter is composed of particles. Mixing matter and antimatter leads to the annihilation of both in a massive burst of high-energy photons, and other systems for generating energy. Mixing matter and antimatter is exponentially more powerful than [[:Category:Nuclear Fusion|nuclear fusion]].

Antimatter is only found in massive, high-energy nebulas and star clusters, where many [[:Category:Nations|nations]] harvest it as fuel.

'''Tanks''' are armored combat vehicles designed to operate in direct-fire areas of combat. They usually refer to vehicles designed to engage enemies head-on, using direct fire from a large-caliber gun and supporting fire from machine guns or missiles. Tanks are usually treaded, but some "hovertanks" are equipped with hovercraft technology.

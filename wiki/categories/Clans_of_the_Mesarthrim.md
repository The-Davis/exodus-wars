The [[Mesarthrim]] were organized into an active resistance, unique culture, and rudimentary government. Each of the Clans was led by their respective [[Clan Lord]], the hereditary ruler of the Clan.

The Clans were entirely [[Germany (Terra)|German]] in origin, a crucial mistake which did a fair share to alienate the [[British Empire (Terra)|English]], [[France (Terra)|French]], and [[Spain (Terra)|Spanish]] people and contributed to the eventual fall of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

'''National Leaders''' are responsible for the growth and direction of their nation. Many characters here are now dead, but were instrumental in developing the history of their [[:Category:Nations|nation]] or [[:Category:Races|race]]. Others are only minor players in the galaxy, and are not listed in the main category.

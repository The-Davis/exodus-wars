'''Aircraft Carriers''' are [[:Category:Warships|warships]] designed to deploy and recover aircraft, acting as a deep space airbase. Aircraft carriers allow a naval force to send air power over great distances without having to depend on local bases for staging aircraft operations.

'''Escort Carriers''' are small aircraft carriers designed to escort freighter convoys. Escort Carriers usually only have one squadron of [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] at their disposal.

'''Light Carriers''' are larger than escort carriers, but smaller than fleet carriers. They usually have three or four squadrons of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] at their disposal.

'''Attack Carriers''' are medium sized carriers, capable of launching at least six squadrons of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]]. Unlike most types of carriers, which largely serve as mobile air bases, attack carriers are well armed, and capable of taking on enemy warships on an even footing.

'''Fleet Carriers''' are enormous carrier designs, capable of deploying upwards of a dozen squadrons of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]]. They are the mainstay of most naval air deployments.

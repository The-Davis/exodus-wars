[[Ngrligru]] tend to be aggressive, even feral at times. Most of the time, they do not get along well with non-[[Ngrligru]], though there are some exceptions to their xenophobia.

Among their own, [[Ngrligru]] appear harsh, eating the weak and the old. This is not out of malice or violence, it is a side effect of the "[[Njaa]]", a biological weapon unleashed on them by the [[Anthronoris]] during the [[Sundering]].

The [[Kabila Kimburu]] is a rare and notable exception to [[Ngrligru]] society at large.

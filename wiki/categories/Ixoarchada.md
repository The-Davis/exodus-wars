The [[Ixoarchada]] are one of the most advanced [[:Category:Races]] in the galaxy. Most owe their allegiance to the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], a group of allied republics in the far counter-turn regions of the [[Orion Arm]].

[[Ixoarchada]] people are organized into a caste system, which they use to determine the life path of each of their people. Most people fit the personality of their caste: warriors are fearless and brave, priests are wise and serene, workers are cheerful and long-suffering, and leaders are selfless and sacrificing. Sometimes the caste does not exactly fit the person born into it, but years of breeding and genetic engineering make most [[Ixoarchada]] predisposed to accept and enjoy their role in life.

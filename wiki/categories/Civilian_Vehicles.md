'''Civilian Vehicles''' are mostly wheeled or hovercraft designs, not equipped with armor or weapons. They are usually used for recreation, personal transport, and cargo transport on the ground. Some civilian vehicles may use nuclear power sources, but most use fuel cells, batteries, or hydrogen combustion engines.

The [[Meroniri]] are a subtle and complicated race of artisans and experts. Their long lives allow them to make plans that take decades to come to fruition.

Angering a [[Meroniri]] is surprisingly easy. Even the lowest levels of society are mired in layers upon layers of schemes and machinations, and they do not take kindly to outsiders interfering in their lives. Many [[Terrans]] and [[Ikronin]] have been surprised by members of the [[Meroniri]] [[Tomesa]] suddenly challenging them to a duel (if they consider the outside of equal social rank) or simply beating them senseless (if they do not).

Other [[Meroniri]] are less prone to such behavior, but are no less subtle in their dealings. The famous phrase is well applied: "Trifle not in the affairs of the [[Meroniri]], for they are subtle and quick to anger."

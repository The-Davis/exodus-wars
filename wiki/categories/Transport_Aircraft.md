'''Transport Aircraft''' are designed and used for the carriage of goods, rather than passengers. Military transport aircraft can and do carry troops, though comfort is not their goal. Most transport aircraft have a wide fuselage, wings mounted high up to allow the cargo to sit near the ground, a landing system designed to use unprepared locations, and a high-mounted tail to allow cargo to be easily loaded and unloaded from the fuselage.

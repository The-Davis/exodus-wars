[[Mtomigru]] tend to be thoughtful, quiet, and cautious. Most of the time, they do not get along well with people outside the [[Mtomigru]] or [[Ngrligru]] [[:Category:Races|races]], mostly out of fear. There are some exceptions to their xenophobia.

Among their own, [[Mtomigru]] are seen as peacekeepers and advisors, only affected by some of the symptoms of the "[[Njaa]]", the biological weapon that turned their [[Ngrligru]] brethren into nearly mindless beasts during the [[Sundering]].

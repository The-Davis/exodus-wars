In the period immediately following the [[Volucris War]], [[Siovar Cai]] insisted that as many accounts of the war be documented, and as much history preserved as possible.  Even as the last ships fled the [[Mirdek System]], journals, diaries, and official logs were consolidated together.  In [[2336]], these were published together in the ''Voices from Lorithan'' collection.

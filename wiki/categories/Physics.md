Physics is the branch of science concerned with discovering and characterizing universal laws that govern such things as matter, energy, space, and time. Discoveries in physics resonate throughout the natural sciences, and physics has been described as the "fundamental science" because other fields investigate systems whose properties depend on the laws of physics.

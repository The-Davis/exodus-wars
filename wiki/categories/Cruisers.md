A '''cruiser''' is a type of warship. They are capable of operating in roles largely independent from the fleet. Depending on the technology base, some types of cruisers may carry fighters, and most carry small craft for landing troops or boarding operations.

The standard cruiser designation is usually based on mass. Light, medium and heavy cruisers try to balance firepower, armor, and speed. These are typically [[Terran]] technology base designations.

War Cruisers are the name for [[Meroniri]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]]. [[Meroniri]] war cruisers typically have the gun load of a [[:Category:Battleships|battleship]], and can carry as many aircraft as are needed. War Cruisers can perform any warship role.

Battlecruisers typically occupy the classification between heavy cruisers and battleships. Generally, battlecruisers are similar in layout and armament to battleships but they trade off either armour or firepower for greater speed. A battlecruiser can outrun a battleship, but is either more fragile, or less powerful.

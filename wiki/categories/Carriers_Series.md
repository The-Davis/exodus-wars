The '''Carriers Series''' covers the episodic-style stories covering the exploits and careers of a [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] from several of the major [[:Category:Nations|nations]] involved in the [[Second Exodus War]] and other conflicts.

The ships currently planned for their own series are:
*[[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']] - Serving in the [[Onyx Empire]], and later in the [[Prefecture]] as the FDS ''Judicator''.
*[[UEAS Vyskov|UEAS ''Vyskov'']] - Serving in the [[United Earth Alliance]], and later in the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] as the CNS ''Vyskov''.
*[[UMCS Wolfgang|UMCS ''Wolfgang'']] - Serving in the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and later in the [[United Centusi States]] as the CSS ''Wolfgang''.

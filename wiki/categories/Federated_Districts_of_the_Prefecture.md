The [[Prefecture]] was once a rebellion, and is now a young government, sprouted from the hopes and dreams of [[Anthony Hadrian]], the former Grand Admiral of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. The Prefecture embodies the spirit of the original colonial [[Torimur]], protected by soldiers who truly love freedom and dignity over power and control.

This category covers pages with data on the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

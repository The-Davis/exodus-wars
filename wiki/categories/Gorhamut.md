[[Gorhamut]] are large and powerful, thinking things through carefully before they act. They have created many species through extensive gene-tweaking.

Individual [[Gorhamut]] tend to be calm, reserved, and difficult to persuade to take any action quickly. They are rarely provoked into doing something they would not already choose to do. [[Gorhamut]] had never cared for killing, and their experiments with genetic engineering were largely to eliminate the need for their own armed force.

The [[Remnant]] of the [[Anthronoris]] is the only surviving group of the ancient [[Anthronoris Empire]]. Many are technically immortal, and have lived for thousands of years.

The [[Remnant]] government has several vying ideals. Some wish to restore [[Pax Anthronoris]] to its ancient glory. Others wish to spend their remaining days in hiding. Others wish to rejoin the community of humanity, and act as a guide and guardian to the younger [[:Category:Races|races]] of today.

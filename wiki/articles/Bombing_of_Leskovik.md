The '''Bombing of Leskovik''' was the surprise attack which started the [[Tempest War]].  Elements of the [[United Centusi States]]' Kriegsmarine attacked major population centers on [[Leskovik]] with enough nuclear weapons to flatten every building.  Some [[Prefecture]] ships in the system witnessed the incident and reported it to authorities on [[Tavou]].

Despite eyewitness accounts, forensic evidence, and detailed records of the orders behind the attack, [[William Camp]]'s government never officially took credit for or denied the attack.  Following word of the bombing, [[Anthony Hadrian]] declared war on the United Centusi States, and the Prefecture legislation ratified his decision unanimously.

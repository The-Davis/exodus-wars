The term "Terrandom" refers to all worlds colonized originally and/or exclusively by [[Terrans]].

Examples of Terrandom include [[Earth]], [[Ah Kin]], the entirety of the [[Sol Sector]], and similar worlds.

Non-Terrandom would include the homeworlds of other [[:Category:Races|races]] and their colonies.

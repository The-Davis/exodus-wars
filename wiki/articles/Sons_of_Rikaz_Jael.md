"Heroes of Rikaz Jael"
by Austin Bailey

=====A Poem Recited in Lorithos Schools=====

On through the Gates of Hell they strode,<br>
Into the jaws of Death, so cold;<br>
Now stories told,<br>
Of brave and bold,<br>
Whom gods, not wives or mothers hold;<br>
Died, they, this day, both young and old;<br>
These stalwart sons of Aeton.<br>
Five hundred, all of Aeton's sons,<br>
Their blood 'cross hallow'd field now runs;<br>
Roar'd out their guns,<br>
These hero'd ones,<br>
They'll see not now nor moons nor suns;<br>
For feats of glory now are done,<br>
These noble sons of Aeton.<br>
Who now will pass this martyr's tale,<br>
Of how these sons of Aeton fell;<br>
They died so well<br> 
At Rikaz Jael<br>
On ground contested tooth and nail;<br>
There at the very Gates of Hell,<br>
These fearless sons of Aeton.

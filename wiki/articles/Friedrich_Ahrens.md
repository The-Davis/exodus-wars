Friedrich Ahrens von Stadt was a noble son in the Stadt Clan. Ahrens has also received the Star of Gallantry, the highest award that can be awarded. Ahrens naval career began as an enlisted man. After serving three years, Ahrens took a leave of absence to attend officer training school. Ahrens would work his way up in rank to Joint Chief under [[Arnold Stadt]] shortly serving [[Eric Francis]], then [[William Camp]]. During this time Ahrens and Camp became best friends, and in [[2327]] Ahrens was appointed the Fleet Admiral in charge of the Krieg Flotte, and carrying out the major campaigns of the [[Tempest War]].

Ahrens was killed in [[2335]] when he was tricked into accepting a surrender, which led him directly into an [[Omega Bomb|Omega]] detonation. Ahrens was the last remaining Admiral of the Centusi Kriegsmarine.

==Service Record==
===Anderung War===
During the Anderung War, Ahrens started his career as a Damage Repair Crewman, Second Class. After some time Ahrens completed his basic triage and first response training, working as a Disaster Crew Medic. Ahrens applied several times for officers school, and was eventually granted his request. His first command was the [[Howell class Destroyer]] [[Black Son|Schwarze Sonne]]. Ahrens would serve as Kapitain until 2295 when the Schwarze Sonne was recommissioned as a [[Starmaster class Destroyer]] to a younger Kapitain. Ahrens was promoted to a Staff Commodore under the hero Admiral [[Bradley von Graith]].

===Second Exodus War===
By the time the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] joined the [[Second Exodus War]], Ahrens was Senior Post Commodore directly under von Graith. When von Graith left for [[Terra]] in [[2322]], Ahrens was promoted to Rear-Admiral in command of the Home Guard, Erste Flotte. During this time Ahrens met in secret with Hinks and Camp, making plans to assassinate Andreas Tischler. During this process, Ahrens became faux friends with Eric Francis, Director of Intelligence Services. He used his friendship to misdirect suspicion from himself and Camp, and set Hinks up to be the scapegoat. Ahrens also began secretly misdirecting supplies and material to a deep space asteroid that Camp had found - which would later become the first base for the Centusi States, and eventual prison camp during the Tempest War.

===Mesarthrim Civil War===
Following the death of Andreas Tischler, Eric Francis became Clan Lord and Uber General. Eric Francis and Arnold Stadt both selected Ahrens to serve as the new Naval Joint Chief. Ahrens remained in the relative safety of [[Stone Bridge]], rather then serve directly in the front lines with his navy. In the final hours of the [[Siege of Stone Bridge]], as Auellal Pathfinders made their way into the capital building, Ahrens was summoned to the Silver Chambers, where he served as one of three witnesses to the United Mesarthrim Clans capitulation to the United Centusi State - thus rendering the Auellal's attack on Stone Bridge illegitimate. When von Graith heard Ahrens was there, he contacted Ahrens and criticized him for his part in the Centusi betrayal. The rebuke from his former mentor was hard on Ahrens and he became an abusive alcoholic. Camp and Ahrens became closer friends, and Camp helped Ahrens turn his feelings into revenge against the Prefecture and Auellal.

===Tempest War===
Ahrens was given command of the Centusi Warfleet - the largest, most powerful, fleet the Mesarthrim had ever assembled. With the mighty Warfleet Ahrens became the most successful Admiral in the Tempest War. Ahrens Warfleet successfully captured and subdued two Prefecture Districts - for these actions William Camp awarded Ahrens the Star of Gallantry. As the war wore on, however, more and more of his ships were either held up in repairs, or transferred to other fleets to help bolster them from their losses. During the Second Battle of Centus, Ahrens was responsible for the death of Prefecture Admiral Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall. During the course of the battle, Ahrens and a small number of his ships escaped the system with a small number of Myt'gaul and Myt'herra intent on starting a guerrilla war elsewhere. Their escape took them to Novar, where they secretly set up a resistance. Eventually, Ahrens got tired of the guerrilla war, and took his ships back into space, where he engaged and destroyed the [[Syr Darya]] and her escorts. Knowing the Allies would eventually send help, Ahrens laid an intricate trap - which succeeded, until he was lured into a counter trap and was swallowed up into an omega detonation.

==Legacy==
Following his death, Ahrens was initially recognized as a superior enemy commander by the Mesarthrim Federation, and a commemorative exhibit was planned for him in the now expanding [[Exodus War Museum]]. However, during the course of the exhibit, his complicity in the plot to assassinate Andreas Tischler was uncovered, as well as his theft of Clan material and supplies during the Second Exodus War. The exhibit was immediately dismissed, and his name was added to the the Traitor Totem in Homemakers Field. Additionally, his family line was disinherited from all property within Mesarthrim Space.

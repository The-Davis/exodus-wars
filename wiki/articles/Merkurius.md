===Overview===

Jaytex Mechanized Unlimited held a competition among engineers in the JMU Fighter Production Program to build a superior long-range fighter that embodied the best of each of the current class of fighters.  The Final two teams were permitted to build twelve prototypes of their design and these ships would compete against one another.  The two fighters were the Tornfalk and the Merkurius.  Despite superior speed and agility, design flaws in the flight control system of the Merkurius fighter led to the Tornfalk team winning the competition before the trials were completed.

The X-13 is a close air-support fighter.  It is best-suited for attacking infantry, vehicles, and VTOLs.  Its dogfighting and bomber capabilities, combined with its VTOL lift system, make it an extremely useful atmospheric unit.

===Capabilities===

The Merkurius was an attempt to take advantage of a prototype JMU Zero Point Field Drive.  Manipulation of the ZPF, on a macro level, is key to the superior performance of the Merkurius and enables it to accelerate and maneuver to a far greater degree than any previous JMU produced fighter.

JMU scientist and engineers succeeded in the process of harnessing microscopic superstring fragments to provide almost limitless power to their ZPF drives.  Another key implication of the ZPF fields and superstrings, in addition to that of an enormous power source, is the discovery that the inertia of an object is modified by that objects electromagnetic interaction with the ZPF.

By manipulating and “bending” these superstrings, the Merkurius is able to create distortions in space/time that can be used to push and pull the ship in any given direction.  It is by this manipulation of superstrings that allows the fighter to accelerate and outmaneuver most known fighters.  An important byproduct of this means of propulsion is that the effect of inertia is also negated, allowing the ship to accelerate, stop and maneuver at velocities that would crush a person using most conventional forms of propulsion.  This is especially beneficial when operating in an atmosphere.  The Merkurius has a maximum linear thrust of 1 km/s2.  Tactical combat speeds were envisioned to be between 5,000 to 50,000 KPH in dogfights, with a standard cruising speed of 5,000 KPH.

The armament of the Merkurius includes four fixed-forward Heavy Lansvapen Cannons, two fast-tracking Lansvapen Cannons and eight long-range Blixt Image Recognition missiles.

The tracking and targeting systems of the Merkurius are the latest in technology from JMU.  The Image Recognition System requires special missiles, equipped with targeting computers that are linked directly to the computer system of the Merkurius.  The targeting system records the silhouette, electronic and visual signatures of its target, tracking it until it hits the target or burns out.  The twin fast-tracking Lansvapen Cannons of the Merkurius operate on the same IR targeting system as the missiles, allowing for the computer to designate hostile craft and fire at will at any ship within weapons range or upon any ship on a attack or intercept vector to the Merkurius.

The Blixt IR, like all Image Recognition missiles, is a fire-and-forget missile.  Once the targeting computer identifies a target, the pilot can launch his missiles and turn his attention to other targets.  Blixt missiles are equipped with warp sustainer units enabling them the ability to reach a speed of 3 x c.  Because of the speed at which the Merkurius travels as well as the speed of its ordinance, its long-range sensors are the most advanced JMU could place in the vessel.

The X-13 carries four Lansvapen Cannons.  These models are very versatile, and are the mainstay energy weapon of the Flyer Corps.  It also carriers six Hellion LRMs--missiles packed with both magnesium and phosphorus fuses in the warhead, along with a highly flammable jellied oil compound.  The magnesium triggers ignite on contact, heating up enough to ignite the clinging, incendiary jelly. The phosphorus burns at higher temperatures, and also produces a blinding light capable of causing temporary disorientation or blindness to unshielded eyes.

The X-13 possess several layers of Proton-Bond armor, enabling the fighter to take survive multiple hits from any direction in the event of shield failure.  Technicians included an Anti-Missile System to aid X-13 pilots in evading missiles.

The X-13 is equipped with an autopilot system, so the pilot can relax or sleep during long voyages. The autopilot can be programmed to fly to a single destination, or fly a complex pattern at multiple speeds and destinations.  The onboard computer will alert the pilot when the fighter is near its destination, and also alerts pilots of nearby objects, particularly hostile ones.

The X-13 has full VTOL capabilities, and the speed of a fighter.  Though they're built mostly for atmospheric operations, they are quite capable space fighters.

The Riekur Mk.II combat computer is the X-13's brain.  It tracks and identifies enemy targets, using its database of over two-hundred images stored in memory.  The computer can track up to twenty-five targets at once.  The sensors have a 360 kilometer range.

All fighters are equipped with a survival kit.  The small reinforced box has a medium-sized flashlight, two hand flares, one rocket flare, infrared binoculars, a small mirror, a knife, a five day supply of dehydrated and concentrated food, a homing beacon, a satellite uplink, and basic first aid items.  All pilots carry a standard sidearm, and can opt to carry a single heavy infantry weapon behind the seat.

===Deployment===

The Merkurius Class Fighter is currently undergoing extensive testing and at this time no timetable has been set as to when or if it will be placed into active service.  The speed and maneuverability of the vessels coupled with limitations in the control system proved too much for pilots to overcome, resulting in numerous deaths and loss of prototypes.  Discussions on how to make the Merkurius flight worthy have been made ranging from placing a governing system on the fighter preventing it from reaching maximum speeds and also limiting the agility to replacing the ZPF Drive with another drive altogether.  Twelve vessels were originally produced for testing, at this time a production run has not been started for the Merkurius Class fighter, while ongoing testing of the four remaining prototypes continues.  

X-13s are used to take out infantry, surface installations, tanks, Mechs, or Buggers.  Typically, they are launched from an orbiting carrier, or from a surface base.  X-13s are easily rearmed and refueled.  Because they require no runway, the supply dumps can be close to the front lines.

Pilots like to ambush troops by hiding in valleys or heavily wooded areas, then popping up and firing their weapons.  Though they are heavily armored, X-13 pilots can expect to be targeted and shot down more than any other Icronian aircraft.

===Overview===

The Dragonfly was designed at the end of the Anderung Wars, sometime in late [[2307]], to counter newer [[Anderung]] designs being produced by the ever-more-desperate Anderung.  Based on a shrunken-down Patton frame, the Dragonfly is the fastest and most maneuverable Mesarthrim fighter to-date.

[[Image:Me46Dragonfly1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Dragonfly is designed for speed and agility, and its weaponry suffers from this.  Its on-board armament consists of only two Type-C Blazers, the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type.  Six external hardpoints increase its short-term effectiveness, but once the missiles are expended, its effectiveness is greatly reduced. 

The Dragonfly is outfitted with a custom-designed fighter-grade reactor and engine array, both shrunken-down 50% of a standard model.  Although slower, the low mass and small size of the Dragonfly make it blindingly fast, and startlingly maneuverable.  All this speed comes at a cost, however, as the small fighter's shields can only operate at 25% capacity while at full acceleration, and its weapons cannot function at all.  It takes a very skilled pilot to know just how to handle the Dragonfly in a combat situation. 

[[Image:Me46Dragonfly2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Dragonfly fighters helped finally overwhelm the Protomorph forces in the Mesarthrim Cluster during the last years of the war.  Their speed and agility allowed them to swarm Anderung craft, shattering fighter screens before larger fighter craft could arrive.

During the Second Exodus War, Dragonflies scrapped with the quickest Imperial fighters available, and constantly gained the upper-hand.  During the Mesarthrim Civil War, the majority of the Dragonfly fighters were retained by the UCS, and production facilities were secured.  Today, Dragonfly fighters operate as first-strike fighters and interceptors, reaching enemies and engaging in dogfights before larger, slower fighters can arrive to deliver heavy firepower.

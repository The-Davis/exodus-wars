''Nathaniel Hinks'' is a brother in the [[Stadt Clan]], and a son of [[Willim]].  Nathaniel's gruff exterior, and domineering personality has become his most recognizable trait.  Nathaniel was the first [[Mesarthrim]] Commandant to leave the [[Pelagrim Sector]], leading the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] delegation to meet the [[Alliance of Planets]].

== Early Life ==

Nathaniel was born in [[Nachtstadt]] in the far north of [[Mesar]], one of the safe zones. Nathaniel never had to live with the daily air raids conducted by the [[Anderung]].

At the age of six, Nathaniel left [[Nachtstadt]] for [[Brünshelm]] with his older brother. [[Brünshelm]] was a booming city, growing exponentially as the safe zone was pushed farther towards the contested and [[Anderung]]-held territories.

In [[2275]], at the age of seven, Nathaniel was accepted into the [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Einer|MBHE]], and began training as an officer to serve in the new [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine]].

In [[2285]] Nathaniel graduated early from [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Einer|MBHE]]. He was commissioned as a [[Kapitän Leutnant]] on the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|UMCS Dualität]] in charge of Forward Blazer One.

==The ''UMCS Dualität''==
After receiving command of Forward Blazer One, Hinks received numerous accolades from his superiors for his calm demeanour under fire, and his ability to keep his weapons in the action for an entire fire fight.  After serving as the Gun Captain for two years, Hinks was promoted to [[Korvetten Kapitän]] in charge of the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität's]] Missile Bed.  Hinks held the position with distinction for a year, surpassing his commanders expectations in several heated [[Anderung]] engagements by having the proper warheads loaded when needed by watching the battle unfold and predicting the necessary weapons.

At the age of 20, Hinks was promoted to [[Fregatten Kapitän]], the youngest [[Fregatten Kapitän]] ever.  Hinks was asked to join [[Konter-Commandant]] [[Hilbert Bonn von Loewe|Hilbert Bonn's]] Strategic Staff on the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]].  Hinks served on the Strategic Staff for the next two years until the [[Battle of White Star Crossing|Kampf der Weißen Sternüberfahrt]] in [[2290]].

===Kampf der Weißen Sternüberfahrt===
[[Image:Dulität.jpg|right|200px]]
It was during the [[Battle of White Star Crossing]] that Hinks was giving his chance to command.  During the epic battle, the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|''Anderung's Bane'']] got separated from the rest of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] and faced the onslaught of several [[Anderung]] war cruisers.  The [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]] was beaten to a bloody pulp, losing both all three of its forward [[blazer]] cannon turrets, and suffering extensive decompression.  The [[Anderung]] proceeded to board the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]], abducting crew as they went.  Taking command of a group of [[Myt'herra]], Hinks and the remainder of the Strategic Staff retook the command deck, and made their way back to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]].  As the [[Anderung]] war cruisers started to give chase the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]] rolled showing its ventral missile bed.  Hinks ordered all remaining launchers fired at extreme close range.  The savage barrage caused extensive damage to both vessels.  What was left of the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]] turned towards the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] and broke off its engagement.  Hinks was recognised for his command of the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]] and received the Victory Pin for the battle.

===Kampfgruppe===
After the [[Battle of White Star Crossing]], Hinks was assigned as Chief-of-Strategic Operations of [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|''Anderung's Bane'']], commanding from the war room on the [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Anderungs_Verderben_.28Anderung.E2.80.99s_Bane.29|Dualität]].  It was here that Hinks designed the concept of Kampfgruppe warfare, and officially put forth a request to form the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]].  His plans were accepted with praise and accolades for what the new Kampfgruppe could achieve against the reeling [[Anderung]] Navy.  Hinks did not take command of the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]], but instead joined the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine War Staff]].

==Kriegsmarine War Staff==
In [[2291]], Hinks officially was given Der Adler, ''The Eagle'', and became [[Obersturmbann Kapitän]].  The youngest ever to serve, at the age of twenty-three.  As was customary, Hinks was given a command of his own, and he chose a [[Starknight]] and named it the [[UMCS Blutsöldner]].  It was officially registered as a member of the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]], and therefore was painted in the Kampfgruppe's colours black and red.  The [[UMCS Blutsöldner|Blutsöldner]] was an older [[Starknight]], however, Hinks began a massive retrofit, convincing [[Mesarbauer]] to equip his ship with various prototypes.  Hinks served on the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine War Staff]] for the next fourteen years, pushing the Kampfgruppe policies, and successfully created five additional Kampfgruppes'.  Additionally, during his service he was responsible for the [[Antvirp Plan]] which liberated the continent of [[Mesar|Arakkia]].

In [[2305]], Hinks watched in helpless horror from the bridge of the [[UMCS Blutsöldner|Blutsöldner]] as his former fleet, the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] was massacred and sent running after the [[Kreigsmarine Fiasco]].  Hinks regrouped with the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]].  Hinks formed a [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|War Staff]] in exile and marshalled all the remaining [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] together under his command.  After almost nine months of slow repair and rearmament, the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group|Crimson Tide Battle Group's]] commander [[Bradley von Graith|Bradley Graith]] insisted the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]] return to [[Mesar]] and eradicate the [[Anderung]] forces that had driven the entirety of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] from the system.  Initially, Hinks wanted to send the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] with them, but he knew the importance of [[Mark Edge|Mark Edge's]] Das Reinigen (the genetic weapon, known as the Cleanser to the Kreigsmarine), and couldn't let all the sacrifice and death have been for naught.  So, in [[2306]], Hinks approved the plan to send the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]] back to [[Mesar]].  Hinks and the [[UMCS Blutsöldner]] joined their Kampfgruppe in returning to [[Mesar]].  In the aftermath, Hinks recognised the [[Mesarnak]] [[Bradley von Graith|Bradley Graith]] as the hero of the battle.  However, internally, Hinks was rewarded for defending [[Mark Edge|Mark Edge's]] Das Reinigen.  In [[2308]] Hinks was assigned to the [[Mesarthrim High Command|High Command]].

==High Command==
In [[2308]] Hinks was transferred by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine War Staff]] to the [[Mesarthrim High Command|High Command]].  Hinks was the first attache attached to the [[Mesarthrim High Command|High Command]].  His commission corresponded with [[Uber-General]] [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler's]] victory over the [[Anderung]] at [[Stone Bridge]].

Hinks used his position on the [[Mesarthrim High Command|High Command]] to keep the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] well supplied despite the increasing lack of enemies to fight.

Hinks and the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]] were responsible for eradicating the last [[Anderung Loyalist]] deep space station in [[2318]].  After this battle, Hinks began preparations to retire from active duty in [[2323]] at the age of fifty-five.

===The Second Exodus War===
On January 13th, [[2322]], Hinks was informed that the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] element [[United_Mesarthrim_Clans_First_Fleet#Mesar_Vorhut_.28Mesar_Vanguard.29|Mesar Vorhut]] had been approached and fired upon by an unknown vessel.  Riots had broken out on the UMCS Drache-Mörder, the UMCS Wachsamer Engel, and the UMCS Krieger-König after the vessel had claimed to have come from the fabled home world, [[Terra|Earth]].

Hinks ordered an immediate combing of the debris for computer cores and data modules.  Two days later, a [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps|Explorer Corps]] ship informed the Mesarthim of the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[Alliance of Planets]].  In order to avoid further religious rioting and outbreaks, Hinks took the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]] with the [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps|Explorer Corps]] ship and [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas Tischler's]] [[UMCS Herr des Krieges]].

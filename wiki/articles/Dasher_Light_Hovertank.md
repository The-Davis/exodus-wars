*Powerplant: BzF-880 Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 225 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens
*Armament: 1 Large Neutron Beam Cannon
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

The Dasher is one of the most popular light hovercraft in the Terran sector.  It is employed as a patrol craft by numerous police forces, security contractors, and militia forces thoughout former UEA and Terran-colonized systems.

[[Image:DasherLightHovertank1.jpg]]

The Dasher has proven itself as one of the most commercially successful models in the Blazton Industries catalog. 

===Capabilities===

For military purposes, the Dasher is usually outfitted with a single Large Neutron Beam Cannon.  While the cannon is most often used at much lower intensities than a standard Large cannon, the ability to fire a very powerful Large Neutron Beam blast does give the Dasher the option to disable even much larger craft. 

When lethal, anti-armor grade weaponry is unnecessary, alternate weaponry can be substituted.  Many police forces outfit a stun-cannon or light autocannon firing less-lethal rounds. 

[[Image:DasherLightHovertank2.jpg]]

Dashers are fast, maneuverable hovercraft.  They have traditionally used the Blazton type hover lift system, but more recent variants are now outfitted with the new Kalidasan type lift systems.  Their defensive systems are Blazton standards. 

===Deployment===

In the Blazton Security Forces, Dashers operate as standard patrol and recon craft.  They are far more common than Hydra-type hovercraft, such as the Stinger.  Outside of the BSF, a host of nations and private firms have adopted the Dasher, and it is today considered the market standard for light hovercraft.  At the height of its popularity, the Empire purchased many thousands for planetary police forces, and many of those same Dashers remain in service today.

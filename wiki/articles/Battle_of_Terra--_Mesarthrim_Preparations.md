May 24, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While the Mesarthrim High Command is still debating the exact course of action to take in the Imperial Civil War, Commandant Nathan Hinks requested and received permission to take the Crimson Tide battle group to Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;His navy consisted of forty cruisers of various types, with full fighter complements. The Crimson Tide ground battle group itself was armed with twenty Vulture Siege Tanks, plus thousands of infantry forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nathan Hinks plans to patch relations between the rebels--now calling themselves the Prefecture-- and the United Mesarthrim Clans, in hopes of making Hadrian an ally.

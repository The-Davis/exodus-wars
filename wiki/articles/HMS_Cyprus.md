The HMS ''Cyprus'' was the thirty-second ship in the [[Atlas Class Carrier]] line, given the hull designation CV-32.

The ship was commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2304]], under the command of Commodore [[Jack Hastinger]].

In October of [[2318]], the HMS ''Cyprus'' and its carrier group were dispatched to the [[Epsilon Indi System]], with orders to secure a [[Meroniri]] research outpost for [[Royal Imperial Labs]]. They succeeded in their mission, but were destroyed along with the entire star system when the Imperial Labs technicians activated an experimental exotic matter reactor. The ''Cyprus'' was lost with all hands.

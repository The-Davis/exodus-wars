===Overview===

The smallest of the Volucris flyer varieties is the Sorocabae, known as the Shredder Drone to human pilots.  This is the only Soldier breed that does not require metamorphosis from a nymph.  It grows straight from an egg of its own.

[[Image:Sorocabae.jpg]]

The Shredder has five hearts, located in the base of each of its five spines.  The hearts work at hyper-metabolic rates, producing plasma which the Shredder passes out the rear for propulsion.  Upon reaching a target, the Shredder's four face-claws latch on, and the five spines burrow into the target.  The hearts in the spine bases all create a quadruple-strength charge of plasma, and explode like a plasma bomb.  Attacking is always fatal to a Shredder.

The high speed and small size of Shredders make it easy for them to scoot around fighter screens, and flocks of them descend on human fleets to destroy them.

The Shredder's carapace is very light, engineered to withstand space debris and the like.  It can't take much punishment from human fighters before it is destroyed.

Shredder Drones will be seen in every space battle.  They are also used in ground warfare, as a few of them are just as effective as an aerial bombardment.

The Shredders are only used on heavy warships or heavy ground targets.  They lack the ranged weaponry to deal with small fighters.

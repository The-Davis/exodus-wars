=== Government ===

Main Article: [[Government of the Mesarthrim Federation|Constitution of Mesarthrim Federation]]

The Mesarthrim Federation is comprised of seven Providence's.  The Providence's support a central federal government whose duties include defending the Federation, issuing credit, negotiating trades, and collecting federal taxes.
Each Providence maintains a local municipality.  Each Providence has a Providential Chamber whose duty is to determine laws and regulations under the Constitution.

The Constitution was drafted by Bethany Tischler in January of [[2333]].  The Constitution was signed on February 1st. [[2333]].  On February 12th, [[2333]], the council reconvened and amended the Constitution with the first four amendments.

In order to secure validity, Arnold Stadt used the Convention for a Unified State (TFVMS) of 2324 as his legal precedent.  TFVMS declared that a council would be formed to determine how to solve the problems of the Mesarthrim Civil War.  After the council was unable to solve the problem, TFVMS II was formed to create a replacement government.  TFVMS II regulations listed the members of the council as Arnold Stadt, William Camp, Bethany Tischler, Mark Edge, and Steven Edge.  It also held that four of five members were required to ratify a new government and constitution.

The United Mesarthrim Clans resolution: The Rights of Mesarthrim Autonomy stated that whichever government was formed by TFVMS II would become the successor to the United Mesarthrim Clans.  Alliance of Planets Government of National Unity ordained and ratified this motion in 2324, stating that whichever government the Rights of Mesarthrim Autonomy recognised, the Alliance of Planets would recognise - which initially proved to be the United Centusi States.

In 2325, the Council of Independent Nations formed the National Sovereignty Council to determine the validity of planetary governments (used mainly for reviewing membership applications).  In 2333, The Council of Independent Nations National Sovereignty Council recognised the claims of Arnold Stadt in regards to TFVMS II, and declared the Mesarthrim Federation a legitimate government.

=== Foreign Relations ===

Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Mesarthrim Federation|Foreign Relations]]

The Mesarthrim Federation adopted many of the old Mesarthrim relations and treaties, including;
*Being a member of the [[Tripartite Alliance]];
*Sharing responsibility of the [[Prometheus Stardock]] with the nation of the [[Taviridis Somarchada]];
*Maintain and adhere to the [[Prometheus Protocols]];
*Maintenance and advancement of the [[Cinaed Accords]].
*Free Trade Treaty with the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], until [[2345]];
*Continuing Goodwill and Aide Missions to [[Sol System Authority]].

The Mesarthrim Federation is currently in unresolved talks with the [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi Family]] regarding the [[United Centusi States]] impressive 15 Trillion Sirio debt to the Family.

The Mesarthrim Federation has also filed a law suit with the Alliance of Planets against [[Rahn Industries]] for the remaining Avengers the [[United Centusi States]] bought from them, but were never delivered prior to the start of the [[Tempest War]].

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of the Mesarthrim Federation|Military]]

The United Centusi Armed Forces are divided into three branches:
*[[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] - Mesarthrim Federation Navy
*[[Mesarthrim Federation Heer|Heer]] - Mesarthrim Federation Army
*[[Mesarthrim Federation Spezialtruppen| Spezialtruppen]] - Mesarthrim Federation Special Forces

After the Tempest War the Mesarthrim drastically reduced the size of their standing Kriegsmarine and Heer to its smallest size in Mesarthrim history.  The change was prompted after the end of the Tempest War when the Mesarthrim Federation scrapped most of their smaller, older, vessels and replaced them with larger - more technologically advanced vessels.

Additionally, individual planet garrisons were drastically reduced, and special operations fast reaction forces were put in place to respond to active threats rather then to simply stand static guard.

Main Article: [[Territory of the Mesarthrim Federation|Territory]]

The Mesarthrim Federations' territory was bought with the blood of thousands of [[Mesarthrim]] service men.  Their numerous battles and sacrifices over the course of the [[Tempest War]] forced the [[Prefecture]] and the [[Auellal League]] to accept Mesarthrim control of the entire [[Pelagrim Sector]].
The Mesarthrim controlled planets include;
*[[Mesar]], the 5th planet of the [[Centus System]].  Capital of the Mesarthrim Federation.
**[[Assistance]], orbital habitation station built by the [[United Earth Alliance]].
**[[Emerging Horizon]], former star fortress, acting as refugee station.
*[[Iurin]], the 4th planet of the [[Cinaed System]].
*[[Caledon]], the 6th planet of the [[Cinaed System]].
*[[Comur]], the 6th planet of the [[Tirshan System]].
*[[Lusar]], the 3rd planet of the [[Cirus System]].
*[[Novar]], the 3rd planet of the [[Honis System]].
*[[Tavou]], the 4th planet of the [[Mimosa System]].
*[[Adelson]], the 3rd planet of the [[Omicron Carinae System]].
*[[Kufestin]], the 4th planet of the [[Omicron Carinae System]].

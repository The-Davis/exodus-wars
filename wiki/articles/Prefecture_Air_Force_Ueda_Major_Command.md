Prefecture Air Force
 
Ueda Air Command

Short of the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy Fourth Fleet of the Line|Fourth Fleet]], few fighter groups are as well-trained or skilled as the Ueda Air Command. [[Ada Bailey]] was a highly-ranked Fourth Fleet officer until the [[Battle of Ueda]]. [[Jan Brooks]] decided that the capital world and capital District needed the best defenses available. Her second-in-command was the only choice.

[[Ada Bailey|Bailey]] is a top-ace, with dozens of kills.  She fought in the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Imperial Civil War]], and the [[Volucris Incursion]].  She began her training on [[Alpha Centauri]] during the cloning project, where she became certified in the Raven when she was only 13 years old.

Commanding Officer: Lieutenant General [[Ada Bailey]].

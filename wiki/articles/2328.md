== Events of 2328 ==

The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] begins to show some serious outward signs of internal political change when nearly a third of its veteran military brass resigns, succumbs to sudden disease, or dies in sudden accidents on border patrol inspections.

===January===
Many Meroniri military veterans began to take notice of the changes to the Terinasi when the order for [[Rosh Arufin]]'s capture came down. Many began to question orders. Hundreds of veteran captains resigned in protest, and many ranking admirals were given dishonorable discharges. In less than a month nearly a third of the [[Meroniri]] officers were gone; many from sudden death by "disease or other unknown causes", and many from unprecedented "accidents" on deep space patrol ships.

Sindh became a hellish post for [[Mesarthrim]] in the early months of 2326. The winter forced the Myt'kampfen forces in the Azad continent to halt their advances with their mechanized weaponry, but the Prefecture's Guard units were able to remain mobile on foot.
*January 18 - Heavy Water
*January 27 - Great Elephants!

===February===
The struggle on Sindh slogs on, as the Mesarthrim Myt'kampfen on Azad begin a push into the capital city of Karachi. It takes a full month of crawling through the snow and mud to move from Gwadar to Karachi, but by the end of February the first Myt'kampfen elements meet the Legion of the Serpent and Guard units on the bridges of the Egris River.
*February 6 - The Karachi Death March
*February 17 - Karachi Outriders
*February 24 - Battle of the Scythian Span

===March===
The Mesarthrim strength in the Adha continent of Sindh suffers greatly once the Myt'herra were effectively wiped out by a daring Marine offensive. With their hunters wiped out, the Marines and Guard units operate a mobile war in the Nipur River region, prompting the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine to operate armored river boats to carry Myt'kampfen units up and down the river in search of mobile camps. The tactic only meets moderate success until the arrival of a fresh Myt'herra unit, 1,000 strong, replaces the Myt'kampfen in this aquatic hunter-killer mission.

The Siege of Karachi makes little progress in March. The Legion of the Fox mobilizes out of Sialkot, sending armored infantry down the Egris River in skiffs while their mechanized units march south down the river banks. The Myt'kampfen are forced to defend their north flank while attempting to take the heavily fortified Scythian Span.
*March 4 - Major Setbacks
*March 7 - Hammer and Anvil
*March 13 - River Assault
*March 16 - Jungle Counterstrike


===April===
The commanders of the Mesarthrim Heer forces on the ground meet with the Kriegsmarine forces in space, hoping to effectively end Prefecture air defenses and take the skies for themselves. The Prefecture Air Force bases are well hidden throughout the planet, making an effective bombardment plan impossible, especially with Prefecture Fleet elements making frequent raids on the Kriegsmarine forces. The admiral in command advises that he will begin to lose a battle of attrition if he continues to sustain damages: spare parts for aircraft and warships are dangerously low.

[[Rosh Arufin]]'s ship is on the run, but not truly abandoned. The [[Meroniri]] Suthenar has been in contact, and gives [[Rosh Arufin|Arufin]] a vital mission to carry out: find enough evidence to get [[Naimen Ladosen|Ladosen]] removed from office.
*April 5 - Derelict
*April 17 - Strategic Concerns
*April 19 - Word from Home
*April 24 - Supply Run

===May===
The Mesarthrim manage to bring in much needed spare parts, along with another thousand Myt'herra, which are sent in teams of forty to the surface with four Myt'gaul to each team. Their assignment: hunt down and tag every Prefecture Air Force base for an orbital bombardment. The Siege of Karachi is stagnant. The Legion of the Serpent has managed to keep the Myt'kampfen out, but the Myt'kampfen have fended off every attempted counterattack by the Legion of the Fox. With Myt'kampfen supply lines secured, they manage to force the Legion of the Fox to withdraw back to Sialkot for supplies of their own.
*May 1 - The Supplies Must go Through
*May 6 - Airstrip Hunt
*May 12 - Stalemate at Scythian Span

===June===
During a full Fleet assault on the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine, a third Prefecture Legion, the Legion of the Dragon, is landed in the west of Azad and hidden in the mountains, where they prepare supply bases and build new air bases and SAM sites. General Travis Carpenter sends secret orders to his commanders on the ground, giving them new orders to defend the western mountains at all costs.
*June 5 - All Out Assault
*June 9 - Orders from the General

===July===
The Prefecture First Fleet, under the command of Robert Marshall and powered by the super intelligence of a Prism AI in every warship, arrives to begin a mass evacuation of several factories from the Telra Aji mountains. For two days, elements from the Prefecture and Mesarthrim fleets harass each other, neither able to inflict serious damages. Marshall's maneuvers are an incredibly complicated pattern, constantly tweaked and modified by Prism and her "offspring." In one final devastating move, they manage to lure 10% of the Mesarthrim fleet into a trap, ramming them with fire ships and sending them to their deaths in the oceans of Sindh.

With the long local winter finally over, the Mesarthrim move their deadly war machine into action on the ground. Their plans to simultaneously destroy the Prefecture Air Force bases have been thwarted by their inability to hold space superiority, but neither side has air superiority. Karachi falls in a deadly two week urban battle after Vulture Siege Tanks break through the kill zones on the Scythian Span. The Legion of the Serpent suffers serious losses, losing 5% of its fighting men to death, and a further 15% to capture or injury. The remaining troops pull out to the west, but are forced to abandon a lot of their heavier weapons.
*July 4 - Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase I
*July 6 - Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase II
*July 8 - Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase III
*July 10 - The Thaw
*July 12 - Operation Kriegstadt: Öffnung
*July 20 - Operation Kriegstadt: Mittlere
*July 24 - Operation Kriegstadt: Ende Spiel
*July 30 - Evacuation Under Cover

===August===
The Legion of the Fox moves down the river, this time to face a well-entrenched but weary Myt'kampfen force in Karachi. The Myt'kampfen pursuing the Legion of the Serpent are cut off, but cause serious casualties to the Legion of the Fox in the process.

The factories have been safely evacuated, and the Prefecture Fleets temporarily pull out of Sindh space to regroup. With space superiority firmly in hand, the Mesarthrim fleets launch a combined orbital bombardment, simultaneously striking the air bases previously tagged by the Myt'gaul.

*August 3 - Stopping the Rout
*August 5 - Regrouping the Fleet
*August 7 - Fires of Heaven

===September===
With air superiority firmly in hand, the Mesarthrim suddenly find themselves back in Blitzkrieg mode, driving the Prefecture Legions west towards the Telra Aji mountains. Unknown to the Mesarthrim, the Prefecture has removed its strategic interest in the planet, and is preparing a massive evacuation of its troops.
*September 20 - Blitzkrieg Renewed

===October===
The Myt'herra in Adha suddenly find themselves free of Marine attacks. Marine and Guard elements have simply disappeared overnight, giving the region its first taste of peace in months. The cost in lives is high, with hundreds of dead Marines and thousands of dead Myt'herra.

In Azad, the Myt'kampfen prepare for a major assault, ready to crush the two Prefecture Legions against the sheer cliff walls of the Telra Aji. Three nights of artillery bombardment and air assaults severely weaken the Legions, and they dig in to meet their end head-on.
*October 9 - Silent Jungle
*October 31 - Cornered!

===November===
The Mesarthrim plans for a crushing victory are dashed when the entire Prefecture Galactic Navy, all six fleets, jumps into Sindh. In hours, they have driven off the Mesarthrim fleet, seized air superiority, and evacuated the battered remains of the Legion of the Serpent. The Legion of the Fox has been reduced to a mere 10% of its muster in the vicious fighting on the ground.

Knowing the Mesarthrim have control of Sindh's Ansible and will soon alert Mesar of the Prefecture Fleet's insane move, Robert Marshall withdraws from Sindh, making use of the new antimatter capacitors to return to Ueda.
*November 1 - Overwhelming Force

===December===
The Mesarthrim on Sindh are bloodied, weary, and weakened by the savage year long campaign, but in high spirits and ready to push on to Tejat. Many are angered and mystified when the command comes down to establish a garrison on Sindh, then return to Mesar for the next campaign, this time into the Ueda District.

*December 2 - The Weary Survivors of Sindh

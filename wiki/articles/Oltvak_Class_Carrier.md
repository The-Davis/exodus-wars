===Overview===

Oltvaks are named after a now-extinct giant sea creature.

[[Image:OltvakClassCarrier1.jpg]]

The exact reason the Lorithos ASTF named their carrier after the sea beast is unknown.  It was extinct long before the Burning War brought on the extinction of every sea creature.

===Capabilities===

Combat isn't the primary role of the Oltvak, but it is well armed.  Its main weapon is an array of eight Heavy Blaster cannons, backed up by twenty-five Standard Blaster cannon turrets.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons.  They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

Oltvak Carriers are armed with sixteen Skrike missile launchers and two Sur-Skrike torpedo tubes.  Both can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.

[[Image:OltvakClassCarrier2.jpg]]

Transporting fighters is the job of the Oltvak, and it does it very well.  It carries nine fighter squadrons and three bomber squadrons.  The carrier also has a docking collar for a Gatrir freighter, allowing the transport to carry down additional tanks, infantry and aircraft from the carrier, as well as a dozen shuttles or transports.

The Oltvak is fast, maneuverable, and well-defended.  It's much more effective at defending itself with speed than most other civilizations' carriers.

===Deployment===

With its high capacity--about the same as a Terran Medium Carrier--and high speed--about the same as a Terran Heavy Frigate--it's easy to see why the Oltvak is popular.

[[Image:OltvakClassCarrier3.jpg]]

The Lorithos ASTF fleets field one Oltvak Carrier for every Riiktar Cruiser.  This ensures that every large-scale ASTF campaign will have heavy fighter support.  The Lorithos ASTF currently fields sixteen Oltvak carriers.

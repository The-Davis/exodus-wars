''James Cain'', also known as Jim "Four Hands" Cain or the "Four Hands of the Bastion" is the remarkable weapons officer of the Galactic Navy's Sixth Fleet flagship.  He is a targetting savant, quite capable of tracking three times as many enemies as most human beings.

The Air/Space Intercept Missile designation 05, dubbed the "Hound", was and is the premiere dogfighting missile of the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Federated Districts Air Force]]. It operated on a highly-advanced package of sensor systems, and could be set to pick targets of opportunity and fire without pilot input. While this method was not preferred by more experienced pilots, the system was very reliable. Newer pilots often found their missiles already firing--and successfully striking--enemy targets before the pilots even realized the threat was there.

The Hound operates within an effective range of seventy kilometers within atmosphere.

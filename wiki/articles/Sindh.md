'''Sindh''' is the third planet in the [[Gamma Algedi System]].

== History ==

During the 21st century "Diaspora" colonization era, the Indo-Pakistani community of Terra pooled their resources to start two colonies. One, Tejat, was officially sponsored by the Earth government, while the other, Sindh, was used to start a more independent community.

Sindh worked without the support and resources of Earth as a whole, instead only relying on their families bringing more and more materials as they arrived. Many early years were hard, but by the 22nd century, Sindh was a thriving colony with an unusually cooperative Indian and Pakistani population.

One of the major staples of life on Sindh is the elephant. The original colonists brought a small heard of juvenile Elephas maximus, along with embryos and genetic material from Loxodonta Cyclotis and Mammuthus Primigenius. Over the years, these varieties were spliced into two distinct breeds: Elephas Azad, a wooly elephant thriving in the colder Azad Continent, and Elephas Cyclotis, a species thriving in the steamy jungles of the bulk of the planet. Elephants are used for labor and food in the less developed areas of Sindh.

== Geography ==

Sindh has four continents with distinct populations. Azad, the largest continent situated in the north and west hemispheres, covering approximately 10% of the planet's surface and is the home of a third of the planet's population. It houses the capital city of the world, [[Karachi]], which is situated along a mountain range near the southern shore of Azad. Karachi is an incredible sight to a first-time visitor to Sindh--it is on an island nearly ten kilometers long and four kilometers wide, with the raging Egris River on either side and a plummeting waterfall leading down to the Qasim Sea a thousand meters below. An enormous bridge, the Scythian Span, leads from the northern thoroughfare of the city to the highway network of Azad. The northernmost metropolis city of Azad is Sialkot, the main spaceport of Sindh. It was the site of the first landing of the colonists, and is one of the major commerce sites on Sindh. The Egris River is an important economic landmark of Azad: it is the site of seven hydroelectric dams, all of which are used to generate massive amounts of heavy water for the more important plutonium reactors scattered across the planet.

The western regions of Azad are covered by the enormous Telra Aji mountain range, which is an incredible source of plutonium and other heavy metals. There are eighteen different deep mining sites, which are the source of Sindh's wealth and wellbeing. Cheap nuclear power has been abundant since the earliest years of the colony. The main processing and shipping center, as well as a major site of office buildings, and residential high-rises for the miners is the city of Rawal.

The eastern shores of Azad are much flatter and easier to travel than the horrendous cliffs and fjords of the south. The eastern regions have a prosperous fishing community, with hundreds of small townships and villages centered around the port town of Gwadar. Because Sialkot is the only spaceport on the planet, Gwadar is the hub of trade and shipping between the other continents and Azad.

Adha is in the south and east hemispheres of Sindh, and is much smaller than Azad, covering barely 2.5% of the planet's surface. There is only one metropolis-level city on the continent, the western port of Madhya, which is the trade hub between Adha and the other continents. There are hundreds of cities, towns, and villages scattered across Adha, which serves as a major farming area for the planet. While it can only supply a portion of the planet's needs, which is heavily supplemented by the [[Laojiu District]], the region was once the only source of food for the colony. The tall snowy peaks of the Teltali Mountains feed four major rivers: Tamil, Nipur, Rathi, and Jaratu, which in turn feed an enormous canal system for irrigation. The southern regions of Adha, along the Nipur River delta, are extremely overgrown with jungles, and difficult to travel. There are a few villages along a small highway running between Madhya and the eastern regions of Adha.

Nicobar is less of a continent than an enormous chain of islands in the north and east hemispheres of Sindh. On the island of Godavari is the metropolis of the same name, the only industrial port in the area. A lot of manufacturing on Sindh is done on Godavari.

The cold southern polar region of Thar has no metropolis level cities, but has land far enough north that some set up permanent settlements to hunt and harvest the blubbery Tharish Walrus, which is considered a delicacy on other parts of the planet.

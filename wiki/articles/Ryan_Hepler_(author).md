'''Ryan Hepler''' joined the Exodus Wars efforts during the summer of 2000, launching his first website on 23 July 2000.  He found the Exodus War through a LEGO web ring and had been a fan since its creation.  His early concepts for [[The Remnant]] (originally known as the "Terran Confederation") did not quite fit in, but helped break several conventions in our thinking and seriously challenged the ideas we had in place. Ryan left the group for a time after some creative disputes, but returned to contribute some outstanding expansions in the ancient history of the Exodus Wars universe.

Ryan works both on story development and model design with a special interest in ancient histories.  He is primarily responsible for content associated with the Remnant, although he has contributed models to several other factions including the [[Prefecture Class Destroyer]] (old "Puma"), [[Nautilus Class Subspace Prowler]], [[Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser]], and [[Victory Class Battleship]] (old "Hades") Classes for the [[Prefecture]].  

Ryan originally maintained an avatar within the story under the name of "Grand Admiral Shadow", which was later renamed [[Gaius Shadar]], which is no longer considered Ryan's in-story persona.  

Ryan graduated from the [http://www.psu.edu Pennsylvania State University], University Park campus in May of 2008.  He has a Bachelors of Arts in History and a minor in Military Studies.  

Ryan used the tag [[User:GADM Shadow‎|GADM Shadow]] and now works under the tag [[User:RHepler‎|RHepler]] when contributing to this site.

===Overview===

By the end of 2316, the United Earth Alliance had fallen, and only Titanian colony stood fast in the face of the Imperial juggernaut.  The patriarchs of the Sirius families met, and agreed to jointly increase all of their naval forces.  Such a decision had to be made together, as any family unilaterally building up its fleet could lead to the suspicion or hostility of the others. 

[[Image:TorinoClassFrigate1.jpg]]

The Nakamura created a new class of warship, while the Asanova simply added heavier plate armor, more cannons, and a few extra shield generators to some bulk freighters.  The Berlusconi combined these two paths, taking five existing freighter hulls, stripping down much of their structure, and rebuilding them as warships.  The process took until early 2319, when the five Torinos were launched, and doubled the size of the Berlusconi capital warship fleet. 

===Capabilities===

The militarizing of the Torino class involved the addition of not just more of the same weapons as the standard freighter, but of heavy weaponry as well.  A pair of Ashur Cannon mounted in single-turrets gives the Torino a heavy weapon for use against light warships and gunships. 

A standard Torino has four Advanced Neutron Cannons.  Thanks to the rebuild, Berlusconi shipwrights were able to increase this number to sixteen.  Four additional AA emplacements were also added, giving the militarized Torinos are total of six. 

Almost as an afterthought, Berlusconi shipwrights managed to squeeze eight VOTM Tubes into the lower hull of the Torino.  These missile tubes greatly increase the versatility of the small warship, giving it the capacity to target and fire on nearly any target with a host of various warheads. 

[[Image:TorinoClassFrigate2.jpg]]

The massive utility craft bays in the rear of the civilian Torinos have been converted to fighter bays in the militarized version.  Twenty four fighters can usually be fielded by a Torino, launching rearward on either side of the after twin-turret. 

Much of the changes in the Torino's superstructure came about because of the Torino's role as a modular freighter, made to carry Icronian Standard Shipping Containers (ISS Containers).  These massive module docks had to be sealed off, reinforced, and armored over to prevent them from being larger targets with weak protection.  Additional shielding systems also were mounted throughout the starships. 

===Deployment===

In April 2321, with one ship of the class still undergoing shakedown testing, the five Torinos of the Berlusconi Naval Guard engaged an Imperial vanguard in Sirius space.  The end result highlighted various successes, as well as failures, in the design of the militarized Torinos.

[[Image:TorinoClassFrigate3.jpg]]

Various improvements would be made to the design, until a brief refit period in 2325, which involved installing new control interfaces and Kalidasan weaponry.  Today, the five militarized Torinos are the most common warships in the Berlusconi Naval Guard, and are the ships that most individuals see when cruising through Berlusconi space.

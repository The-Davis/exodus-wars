The Tischler was a [[Mesarbauer]] pet project to replace the [[Me-32 Superiority Fighter - "Sturmfaust"|Sturmfaust]].  The project began in [[2327]], after extensive research was put into the failures of the Sturmfaust against the [[Auellal League|Auellal League's]] [[Sundancer]].  The project poured over thousands of battle reports, repair yard logs, and cannon cams - all of which were being daily supplemented by engagements with [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] as the [[Tempest War]] dragged on.  It took seven years, and three companies, for the Tischler to be accomplished, however, in [[2334]] the first Tischler took flight in service of the [[Mesarthrim Federation]].

==History==
The Tischler, named in honour of [[Andreas Tischler]], was an expensive project undertaken by Mesarbauer to respond to the growing age of the [[Sturmfaust]].  It was noted that the Sturmfaust would need to be all but completely rebuilt from the ground up, so Mesarbauer simply ordered a new fighter designed.  Everything from the basic airframe to the armament was taken back to the drawing board.  As the Tempest War turned dark, Mesarbauer was forced to reassign various members of the design team as more and more of its dwindling resources were needed elsewhere.

in [[2333]], [[Astrolift Company|Astrolift]] hired the old design team and revived the designs for the Tischler - however, Astrolift intended to build it as the Sunray for the [[Auellal Armada]]. Astrolift did the most good for the design, hammering out all the details and even producing several working prototypes.  However, in early [[2334]] [[Arnold Stadt]] formed the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] and the [[Auellal League]] joined the Federation.  Astrolift lost its tax free status, and was required to pay tarriffs and duties to the Federation.  [[Trevor Purden]] was outraged and sold off all of Astrolift's Mesarthrim contracts, including the Tischler/Sunray.  [[Centusi-Luxusbehälter]], luxury civilian starship creator, founded a subsidiary company, [[Centusi-Luxusbehälter#CLB_Sternarbeiten|CLB Sternarbeiten]] and purchased half a dozen contracts from Astrolift, and the bulk of their manufacturing facilities orbiting [[Caledon]].

The fighter was once again named the Tischler, and began immediate production.  CLB Sternarbeiten signed a deal with Admiral [[Oswald Voss]] in [[2335]] making the Tischler the main space superiority fighter for the [[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine]], replacing the old Sturmfaust and Auellal Sundancer.  Following the war, and the [[Treaty of Tavou]], the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]] approached [[Centusi-Luxusbehälter#CLB_Sternarbeiten|CLB Sternarbeiten]] and requested permission to produce the Tischler under the terms of the [[Cinaed Accords]].  The deal was agreed upon, and [[Tavou Imports Trading Group]] was selected as the firm that would build the units for the Marines - resulting in the F-17 Headhunter.

==Development==
As the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] raged on, the dated designs of the Sturmfaust really began to show through.  To combat these problems, the Me-32 was produced.  Replacing the Me-31m, the Me-32 became almost just a band-aid, covering the extensive problems with the airframe, and capabilities of the fighter.  So, in 2327, Mesarbauer acquired every scrap of information it could on the shortcomings of the Sturmfaust, and began to compile an exhaustive list of everything that could be improved about the fighter.  It took four years to determine the proper design, armament, and propulsion for the vessel.  Over the next two years, in conjunction with [[Zweiundzwanzig]] an entire new countermeasure system was devised, and new Pilot-Computer Interfaces were reworked from the ground up by [[Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe]].

When the design team was reassigned, the development did not stop.  [[Mark Edge|Mark Edge's]] [[Mesar Beschränkte Zwischenablage]] Project leader, Clyde Dürmeister, began the [[Homebrew Project]] which began to acquire [[United Centusi States|Centusi State]] technology and equipment to be used by the [[Auellal League]].  Several of the Tischlers' design team, as well as copies of the project log, were smuggled off [[Mesar]] and acquired by Astrolift.  Astrolift was ecstatic with the new fighter design and promptly began to mock up models and simulators.  Through these tests most of the kinks were worked out, and over thirty prototypes were built.  These prototypes were hot items after the war on the bidding market.  Most famously, Prototype #12 was purchased by [[Steven Cue]] in [[2337]] and painted in memory of his late daughter, [[Melissa Cue]].  Prototype 12 is on display at the Cue Residence on [[Caledon]].

After Astrolift sold its rights to the Tischler, and CLB Sternarbeiten acquired them, the Tischler began full scale production.  The model was considered ready for production, and began to be swapped out for in the Kreigsmarine fleet.  The Kreigsmarine would order two variations to the model to serve specific roles; the Km-4D, and the Km-4P.  When the Prefecture Marine Corps ordered the Headhunter they wanted the vessel outfitted with a combination of Mesarthrim [[Blazers]] and Prefecture [[GC-05 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Screamer"|Gauss Cannons]].

==Design==
The most obvious design improvement of the Tischler over the Sturmfaust was the slimming of the body, reducing the overall size of the vessel allowed it to avoid strikes easier.  Additionally, the vessel was rebuilt from the inside out, around the pilot - unlike the Sturmfaust where the cockpit was more or less simply tacked on the front.  This coupled with the emphasis on survivability created a fighter that was a whole new concept for Mesarthrim engineers - not creating a fighter that was simply a large fist, but something that could 'take a lickin and keep on tickin.'

===General Characteristics===
Utilising extensive new technology, the very body of the Tischler seems more like a bastard child then an actual improvement over the Sturmfaust.  However, the new folded Leifstein Folded Composite Armour can offer between three and six times the strength the older equivalent could in the same amount of space.  Additionally, the old Automatic Adaptive shields were improved with Branch Prediction, allowing them to not only tune to the incoming fire, but to also anticipate shift and variations in the bands, meaning more enemy rounds are simply harmless.  Finally, rounding out the defences are the new Base-7 Electronic and Physical Countermeasure system.  The system is intuitive and extensive.  The entire package not only costs extensively, but also required a complete redesign of the Tischlers engine due to placement of the forward ECM pod.

===Performance===
The Tischler was not able to compete in the [[Tempest War]], however, in mop up operations following the war, the Tischler was able to keep pace with smuggling ships and blockade runners.  Additionally, the Tischler has been able to prove its metal in an array of stress tests including pressure, damage, and friction.

===Armament===
In an effort to produce a fighter that would not only carry a pilot into battle, but return him, the designers kept the armament simple - forward mounted cannons, placed close to the centre of fire.

To provide the much needed offensive punch, four medium hardpoints were added to the wings of the vessel.  Additionally, two additional hardpoints were added to the wingtips to give the Tischler some ability to adapt to a wide variety of missions.

===Avionics===
The entire Pilot-Computer Interface for the Tischler was reworked by Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe utilising the newly developed Mesarthrim Self-Aware Entities.  The entities were produced in 2324 by the [[Auellal League]] in conjunction with assistance from the [[Prefecture]] Science Division under the leadership of the [[Cinaed Accords]].  The research took five years, and is still in its utter infancy.  However, the Tischler benefits from the Self-Aware Entity Interface, allowing quicker reaction times and a greater understanding of the pilots surroundings.

==Operational History==
The Tischler has only engaged in combat against rogue elements and smugglers.  However, the Tischler has shown extensive reliability in fleet CAP and SAR roles.

==Operators==
The Tischler is used extensively by the [[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine]] in its Km-4D and Km-4P variations onboard its fleet vessels.  The numerous installations and planets scattered across Mesarthrim Space generally utilise the original version of the Tischler.

The Prefecture Marine Corps contracted the F-17 Headhunter variation to be constructed by [[Tavou Imports Trading Group]].  The Headhunter has become extremely popular with the Marine Corps and is beginning to become a dominate fighter in Marine sorties and missions.

==Variants==
The Tischler spent seven years in development, and by the time it was finished, it still required a few variations to be produced.  The variants of the Tischler are concurrent variations, all being produced at the same time, as opposed to a traditional variant that replaces its predecessor.

''Roshiah Adi Arufin'', or "Rosh", is one of the most famous and well-known members of the [[Meroniri]] race. He is the Asetarun of [[Aseta Salfore]], the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]'s main expeditionary force outside their borders.

=== Early Life ===

Rosh was born only a year before his father [[Auren Arufin|Auren]]'s fateful duel with [[Naimen Ladosen]]. He was raised by his mother, [[Nai Arufin]], but more so by his older brother [[Ferin Arufin|Ferin]].

[[Ferin Arufin|Ferin]] taught Rosh to hate the Ladosens, particularly [[Naimen Ladosen|Naimen]]. The blood feud between the Arufins and the Ladosens was already intense, but [[Ferin Arufin|Ferin]]'s hatred for [[Naimen Ladosen|Naimen]] was so strong it threatened to turn into a full-blown shooting war. Rosh grew up determined to be a fighter without peer, and to bring vengeance against the Ladosens for [[Auren Arufin|Auren]]'s death.

=== Military Training ===

As soon as he was old enough, Rosh apprenticed himself to [[Aseta Salfore]], where he trained in [[Tesashue]] and the [[Pasara]], but also in battlefield tactics and [[:Category:Warships|warship]] command.

=== Salfore's Sting ===

When he was one hundred and thirty years old, Rosh became the commander of his own ship, and soon became well-known throughout [[Aseta Salfore]] and even the whole of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]].

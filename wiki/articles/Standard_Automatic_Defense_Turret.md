The Standard Automatic Defense Turret is an example of a modern [[Terran]] security installations. It was created after the Battle of Mirach, where Racers were first seen.

[[Image:StandardAutomaticDefenseTurret.jpg]]

The turret has two Rotary Autocannons.  The turret can be fired by the simple tracking and identification AI of the turret, it can be fired by remote, or it can be fired manually by a soldier within the turret.  Firing is usually handled by human gunners.

Turrets are deployed at strategic points on every installation of importance.

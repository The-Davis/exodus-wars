'''Njiorin's Well''' is a nebula in the [[Njiorin Sector]], and is rich in antimatter. It is so important to the sector that the entire sector was renamed after it in the [[24th Century]].

The nebula was first discovered by [[Njiorin Osrason]] in the [[22nd Century]].

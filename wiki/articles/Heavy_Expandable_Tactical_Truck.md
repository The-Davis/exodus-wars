*Designation: Heavy Expandable Tactical Truck
*Power Plant: 400L-Series Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 125 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave

===Overview===

The ability to successfully execute massive logistical plans is what helps some generals attain their fifth star and what prevents other commissioned officers from moving up the latter.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck1.jpg]]

When the call went out for a multi-purpose truck able to adapt to almost any condition or cargo, Rahn Industries stepped up to design what became known as the Heavy Expandable Tactical Truck, or the H.E.T.T. for short.

===Capabilities===

Able to hit 100 KPH, the HETT is able to keep up with most of the other vehicles that various governments employ in their arsenal. Couple this with a strong chassis able to carry up to thirty-five tons of equipment and the ability to change out the "sleds" in less than ten minutes, you have a recipe for success.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck2.jpg]]

The HETT comes in a variety of flavors, with every truck interchangeable, having the ability to become whatever it needs to be at a moments notice. Manned by only two people, the driver and the sled operator, the HETT is not labor intense and doesn't require a  team of specially trained soldiers to unload it.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck3.jpg]]

The Standard transport sled offers the easiest way to transport anything from troops to food and medical supplies. Nothing more than a basic pickup truck design, the tailgate folds down for easy loading and unloading of boxed, canned, or other containers.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck4.jpg]]

Water is a fundamental need that almost all species have, without it there would be no life and, consequently, no soldiers to fight wars. Therefore, when it comes to transporting water, or any other liquid for that matter, the two ten thousand gallon tanks are the most heavily armored part of this truck. The ability to thoroughly cleanse these tanks within minutes allows for them to carry dangerous chemicals one minute and clean drinking water the next.


[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck5.jpg]]

Any general knows that in order to win a battle you need to have constant command over your troops and the best way to do this is to be in the thick with them. The C&C sled has all of the resources needed to successfully execute anything from a single operation to a campaign level invasion. The inherent ability to network a number of these vehicles allows for staff to communicate their plans to other easily and quickly.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck6.jpg]]

In any battlefield situation there is always going to be the need to have some sort of ambulance to be able to ferry wounded soldiers off the frontlines and to the waiting field hospitals in the rear. This armored behemoth offers two stretchers and a team of highly trained medics to treat almost any kind of wound, allowing the survival rate of wounded personnel to climb to heights never before seen in modern warfare.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck7.jpg]]

The heart of almost any modern army is ammunition, be it the old gunpowder propelled lead bullets or the high velocity, microsize coilgun rounds of today. Therefore, the ability to successfully transport these much needed supplies to the front lines is crucial to the outcome of the battle. The ammunition transport allows tens of thousands of rounds of everything from field guns to missiles to be transported in an armored box able to withstand ever light anti-armor fire.

[[Image:HeavyExpandableTacticalTruck8.jpg]]

Any army's corps of engineers has the amazing ability to construct virtually anything from nothing, though they are somewhat limited in how to accomplish this by the tools they are given. The crane configuration allows quick and easy assembling of everything from preformed housing to supply depots and other necessary buildings. In a split second, the crane can be used to hoist unlucky vehicles from ditches or used to move supplies to where they are needed most.

===Deployment===

When the HETT was first developed, tested, and finally brought to the consumers, Rahn Industries was one of the first order a fleet of them, being that they trusted their own research and development department. The Rahn Industries Security Force, as well as a number of other offices, have been very impressed by the performance, in almost any situation, of the vehicle.

These reviews, as well as numerous other government and private institution reviews have helped to push the heavy expandable tactical truck to one of the best selling military utility vehicles in Rahn Industries current catalog.

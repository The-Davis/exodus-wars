''Helen Cashaw'' joined the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s Air Defense Force to pay her way through school. She was studying to be a psychologist, and never wanted to be a warrior. 

When the [[Second Exodus War]] began, she proved to be not only an able combat pilot, but one of the best. She scored dozens of kills against the pilots of the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]], until she went up against [[Emil Torsc]]. Torsc killed her in a head-on engagement over the planet [[Mnemosyne]].

=== Early Life ===

Helen was born to [[Michael Cashaw|Michael]] and [[Linda Cashaw]] in [[Titan City]], on [[Theia]]. She had two brothers, [[Jeremiah Cashaw|Jeremiah]] and [[Brady Cashaw|Brady]], both of whom also grew up to serve in the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|military]].

Growing up, Helen was an unremarkable child, known to her friends and teachers as a "quiet girl", who always seemed to be thinking or reading. She was enrolled in the Titan City school district from age 6 to 11, when her poor grades prompted her mother to tutor her privately at home. Helen was eventually re-enrolled into the Titan City school district for her junior and senior High School years, where she blossomed as a student and received multiple athletic and academic awards.

Helen's high school professors noted her intelligence and ability to "read" people quickly. She was very popular in the school counselor's office, where her status as "not one of the adults" aided her in listening to problem students and getting actual results. Helen was actually credited with talking down an armed, suicidal student in [[2309]], and was commended by the mayor of [[Titan City]] and the Titan City police.

=== Formal Education ===

With her natural talent for understanding people and reasoning through crisis situations, Helen wanted to enter the psychiatric health field. Unfortunately, the Cashaws were not wealthy enough to put her through school, and her grades were not high enough to qualify for achievement scholarships.

[[Image:Helen Cashaw full body image.png|300px|right|thumb|Helen Cashaw, rejected by colleges.]]

Helen's brother [[Jeremiah Cashaw|Jeremiah]], a lieutenant serving in the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]], suggested she join the military to get the much-needed financial aid. Helen visited the [[Titan City]] recruitment office, and nearly enlisted before being advised by her mother to try to enroll in one of the military academies.

The [[United Earth Alliance]] operated multiple military academies, and the Air Force Academy on [[Earth]] accepted Helen's application. She entered as a cadet in the class of [[2310]], and graduated in [[2314]] with a degree in psychiatric health and a commission as a Second Lieutenant in the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]], reporting to the Air Force.

===Military Training===

With her basic training and officer school completed in the Air Force Academy, Second Lieutenant Helen Cashaw was sent to pilot candidacy school. It was here that her fellow pilot-candidates gave her the nickname "Little Mouse" for her quiet demeanor and small size. This nickname was appropriate at first, but later in training she showed a more aggressive side.

Helen's military career was only marred by a single black mark. During the last weeks of pilot candidacy school, two male recruits and one female recruit were involved in a brawl, which Helen single-handedly broke up, breaking the nose of one of the male recruits in the process. A formal inquiry was conducted, but Helen was never court martialled and was simply given administrative discipline.

On November 15, [[2314]], Helen Cashaw was given her wings as a certified [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] pilot, and deployed to the [[No.1416 Squadron ADF - "Saint Michael's Sword"|1416th Squadron]] in the [[Sol System]].

===Military Career===

From her deployment in [[2314]] until the [[Onyx Empire]]'s invasion on January 9, [[2315]], Helen had an ordinary military career.

When the [[Onyx Empire]]'s invasion fleet attacked the [[Sol System]] and [[Earth]], Helen was on patrol with a combat-ready [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]], and was one of the few pilots in the system not caught on the ground.

Helen shot down 11 [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] before the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]] declared a general retreat from the [[Sol System]] and she was picked up by the [[UEAS Vyskov|UEAS ''Vyskov'']], which retreated to the [[Lyrae System]].

===Top Ace===

For the first two years of the [[Second Exodus War]], Helen Cashaw was the top ace of the [[United Earth Alliance]], killing over seventy [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] pilots in various battles.

Her incredible killing spree was finally put to an end on July 20, [[2316]], when she engaged [[Emil Torsc]] in single combat over [[Mnemosyne]] in the [[Titan System]]. Despite her outstanding skills, [[Emil Torsc]] was the better pilot, and shot her down. 

Ironically, Cashaw's own psychological insight doomed her. Over the brief course of her final duel with Torsc, she came to understand the inhuman ruthlessness and combat potential of the  man she faced. When the two pilots faced off in a head-to-head pass, Cashaw lost her nerve and broke away, leaving herself vulnerable.

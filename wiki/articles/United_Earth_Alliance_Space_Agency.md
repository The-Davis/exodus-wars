The '''Space Agency''' was the chain of command within the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]] responsible for space defense, exploration, and development of space-based technologies. It was equivalent to the naval forces of most other space-faring militaries.

== Structure ==

The Spacy Agency was made up of two components: the defense fleet (Battle Groups) and the exploration fleet (Exploratory Commands). The Space Agency had a unified chain of command with the rest of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]], reporting to the General-Commander.

=== Battle Groups ===

At the height of its power, the Space Agency had forty Battle Groups. Over the centuries, most of them were mothballed. By the time of the [[Second Exodus War]], the Space Agency had dwindled in size to four Battle Groups, with one in combat-ready status, but lacking a crew. It was re-activated by the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

* The 3rd Space Battle Group, headquartered in the [[Sol System]]. Reactivated in [[2321]] to [[Titan System]].
* The 5th Space Battle Group, headquartered in the [[Holt System]]. Reassigned to [[Titan System]].
* The 13th Space Battle Group, headquartered in the [[Titan System]].
* The 17th Space Battle Group, headquartered in the [[Titan System]].
* The 22nd Space Battle Group, headquartered in the [[Titan System]]. Not combat-ready until [[2320]].

Early Battle Groups were much smaller in size than modern ones. They had no Task Forces, and only three Combat Elements each.

=== Exploratory Commands ===

Exploratory commands were responsible for first flights to new stars, charting safe [[Hyperspace]] routes, surveying [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]], exploring new worlds, and guiding in scientific and colonial expeditions. There were two Exploratory Commands, which were recalled and disbanded during the [[Second Exodus War]] in order to reassign the veteran crews to the 22nd Space Battle Group.

* The Coreward Exploratory Command
* The Hubward Exploratory Command

Exploratory Commands had direct control of Interstellar Exploration Vehicles, which were responsible for multi-year exploration missions. Each starship often cost more than an entire Combat Element, though they were worthless in terms of combat viability.

== Organization ==

The Space Command was given a military and exploratory mission profile, and had its resources split between civil expansion and military defense. This split in budget caused the weakening of the combat assets towards the end of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s history, and was partly responsible for its collapse.

=== Battle Group ===

Battle Groups were the the highest level of combat organization, commanded by a Commodore-Commander. A Battle Group had two Task Forces; one commanded by the Commodore-Commander, the other commanded by a Brigadier-Commander. Each Task Force had a total of six Combat Elements, and twelve Cruisers. Cruisers operated independently of the Combat Elements, often on solo missions.

=== Combat Element ===

A Combat Element was the lowest group level of organization in the Space Agency, though their Cruisers often operated with autonomy outside the support of any other fleet element. Each Combat Element had a Battlecruiser, a Carrier, two Destroyers, four Frigates, and ten Corvettes. Combat Elements noteably outnumbered [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] Carrier Groups, but were usually outmatched in terms of tactics and the sheer number of Carrier Groups available. A Combat Element was led by a Colonel-Commander.

=== Ships ===

The names of commissioned ships of the Space Agency started with "UEAS", designating 'United Earth Alliance Ship'. Additionally, each ship was given a four-number hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and hull number. The names of ships were officially selected by the Assembly, with the President's approval.

The Space Agency's ships were designed by a series of design bureaus, which built hulls of a set designation. Internal systems and weapons were upgraded and updated over the centuries, but the hull designs remained the same. The hulls were given a single number in a series of 1000.

=== Weapon Systems ===

The Space Navy's shipboard weapon systems were focused on two weapon types: beam weapons and missiles.

Their main close-range beam weapon, used for point defense and for navigational safety, was a Graser-based cannon. Their main weapon was a focused Xaser array, which was much longer-ranged and built for shield penetration and high-impact energy delivery.

Missiles were rarely deployed, but included long-range payload delivery systems for large enemy targets.

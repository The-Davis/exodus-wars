==Railways==
Railways on [[Lorithos]] worlds are the main method of ground transportation. Most of the ones on [[Lorithan]] must be protected by enclosed tubes, but they are an efficient and safe method of transportation.

==Starports==
[[Ipsof]] and [[Jagarr]] are equipped with at least one starport, which provides launching and landing facillities for [[Lorithos]] freight ships, shuttles, and fighter craft.

==Orbital Stations==
[[Ipsof]] and [[Jagarr]] both have at least one orbital space station, which act as off-loading and docking facilities for ships that cannot land on planet surfaces, and as a command center for the defense of the colony worlds.

==Kihtor==
The [[Kihtor]] are the master work of the [[Lorithos]]. Four enormous space elevators, bigger and tougher than anything built by any other society, located around the world of [[Lorithan]]. These huge constructs act as homes, strategic planning centers, factories, and as living memorials to the ingenuity of the [[Lorithos]] people.

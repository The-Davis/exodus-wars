'''Strålkransköld Composite Shielding''' is a highly integrated, multi-layer, catch-all shielding system developed by the [[Reigess Suverände]].  Designed to protect a vehicle from overwhelming amounts of fire, its main strength is its ability to disrupt incoming weapons before actually taking damage, thus making it more efficient.  The shield's layers are detailed below.

===Optical Density Screen===

At the outermost level of the shielding, the optical density of the vacuum is modified in a very thin region.  Optical density refers to the degree by which light is slowed by moving through an object (light through a glass lens, for example).  Due to the screen's micro-furrowed shape, it acts like a lens which diffuses incoming beams, thus robbing them of their energy density.  It does not actually take away energy from a beam, rather it disorganizes it and spreads it out somewhat to be more easily dealt with.

Because this screen has such a high optical density, any very high speed particles which enter the screen (i.e. ion cannons, proton cannons, relativistic mass drivers, etc.) dissipate a large portion of their energy in Čerenkov radiation, because they are going faster than the speed of light though the Density Screen.  This is similar to a sonic boom as something approaches the sound barrier, only it produces a mostly harmless burst of light.  Additionally, the Optical Density Screen causes near misses to be harmlessly reflected back into space.  Little to no damage is done in this process. Only direct hits get through this layer very well. 

Perhaps the most beneficial quality is that the Optical Density Screen is maintained even when the shielding system is powered-down, thus providing a large boost to the effectiveness of [[Reigen Järnhud Composite Armor|Järnhud armor's]] effectiveness.  This is extremely useful in the event of an ambush or if catastrophic shield failure were to occur.

===Electromagnetically Pumped Phased Plasma Shield===

This layer is designed to absorb and reflect large quantities of photonic energy from EM weapons.  Plasma-based shields have little or no effect on projectiles.  However, charged particle weapons are significantly disrupted by the plasma shielding, as it is essentially a superconducting system.  This means that the charge from those weapons will be dispersed evenly across the shield perimeter, until it can be neutralized.  It also acts as shielding against things like EMPs, and even helps break up radiation from nearby thermonuclear explosions.

===Enhanced Shield Barrier===

This is the simplest layer, operating similarly to the shielding used by more primitive races, but enhanced to be more efficient, and thus stronger.  The frequency and phase characteristics can be modified to balance ray and particle protection, or to enhance one over the other.  Basically, anything that gets through the first two layers will be significantly weakened and disrupted, and is thus more easily stopped by the primary shields in this layer.

The XH-4 was designed with multiple technologies designed for use by the Royal Imperial Air Force's fighters. The F-9 Valkyrie's magnetoplasmadynamic thruster and stabilizing computers were added for faster and better space ops, and the new composites were incorporated into the airframe. The new technologies added, particularly the guidance computer, allowed the particle field generator to be brought back in. The YH-4 was tested as an attack VTOL, and eventually went live as the AH-4 Lightning. The new technologies were also incorporated into the UH-3A.

== Events of 17971BC ==

The name of the seat of the [[Anthronoris Empire]] is a secret lost to the ages, but it did not fall as easily as the rest of the Anthronoris worlds.  Four titanic [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]] Heavy Combatants survived the [[Sundering]], hidden in the corona-sphere of the capital’s star, and when the [[Ngrligru]] had quieted, they fled deep into the [[Sagittarius Arm]] to one of their hidden [[Void World]]s, which was rendered invisible to sensors.  This Void World was known as Terra Novus, and the remaining Anthronoris literally became known as "The [[Remnant]]".

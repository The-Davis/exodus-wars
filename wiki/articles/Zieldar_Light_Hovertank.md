The Zieldar Light Hovertank is a long-range sniper, capable of destroying sensitive targets from up to one hundred kilometers away.  When in combat, it is almost always escorted by two or more [[Karak Light Hovertank]]s.

In the [[Volucris War]], the Zieldar proved so effective against Volucris targets that every single one was hunted down and destroyed before the [[Ixoarchada]] reinforcements arrived.  The only remaining Zieldars are on [[Jagarr]] and [[Ipsoff]].

== Development ==

[[Dirken Huern]] tasked the Tredan Engineering Guild to follow up on its Karak design with a long-range combatant.  After the [[Volucris Incursion]] in [[2324]], [[Sanor Cai]] studied the Volucris tactics and believed that he could effectively contain and harass a Volucris swarm more effectively with speed and range than with armor and firepower.  The Zieldar was built to fill this role.

Tredan engineers tested the Zieldar against the [[Brehvok Main Battle Tank]], but found that the larger tank could often close the distance and defeat the small sniper tank.  They completed the design to specifications, but when they delivered to the [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]], Tredan engineers warned that the Zieldar would need close-range escorts.  Fortunately, the Karak was already up to the task.

== Combat Use ==

Zieldars are extremely effective long-range snipers.  Their main [[Bukrah]] cannon can be dialed down to pin-point accuracy for assassinations, or dialed all the way up to knock out hardened targets.  Up close, the Zieldar can fight with its single fixed medium Bukrah cannon, though it will be overwhelmed by any serious anti-armor weaponry.

The Zieldar's defenses are similar to a Terran tank's armor.  The Lorithos people never mastered proton-bond armor.  Instead, they protect their ships and vehicles with Tzaiko Composite armor--a tough weave of fibers, slightly stronger than Boron-Nitride.

Zieldars are speedy.  They're designed to move exactly as fast as Karaks, so their escorts can keep up while they deliver their long-range attacks.  Zieldars can turn well, but not as quickly as Karaks.  The clean, no-radiation power of the Zieldar and other Lorithos vehicles comes from the Hydrogen Engine, which generates power from a slow burn of hydrogen gas--converted from super-compressed hydrogen metal.  Refueling a Zieldar is rarely needed, and can be quickly completed at any fuel storage facility.

[[Image:ZieldarLightHovertank2.jpg|200px]]

Zieldars are deployed in bulk to every Lorithos base, on Lorithan and on the colonies.  Each Zieldar is always teamed with a pair of Karaks.  The relationship is practically unbreakable--both units are severely limited in effectiveness if their partner is destroyed.  Zieldars also operate as long range scout/observer craft, and fire-support units during large-scale engagements.

== Volucris War Combat History ==

In the first two days of the Volucris War, the Zieldars fielded by the Lorithos accounted for six million Volucris kills with zero losses. Sanor Cai's strategy of long-range harassment worked superbly, particularly because the Volucris seemed more interested in excavation than in combat.

For weeks, the Zieldars did their deadly work almost completely unmolested. By the second month of the war, the Volucris began to expand their operations and frequently engaged Lorithos forces.  The Zieldar's speed combined with the Karak's effective escort kept their losses low and the Volucris casualty rate high.

By the fourth month of the war, the Volucris losses were estimated to be around sixteen billion. Corpses of the monsters were piled like mountains in the wastelands.  Around that time, Lorithos and allied soldiers began to notice a new Volucris field commander.  This Eviscerator, which the [[Reigens]] dubbed "Motsander", organized brutal counter-strikes against sniper positions.  The Zieldar and Karak teams began to take huge losses.  Within four months, Zieldar survival rate was below 40%, and by the end of the war every Zieldar on the planet was destroyed.  Thanks to the tireless efforts of the Karak teams, Zieldar crew survival rate was somewhere around 57%.  Many Zieldar pilots were reassigned to other roles, though the war was so brutal that most initial survivors were killed or maimed before [[Lorithan]] itself was destroyed in [[2334]].

===History===
Main Article: [[History of Pax Anthronoris|History]]

Pax Anthronoris, the "eternal empire", was the greatest civilization in human [[history]] in terms of territory, military might, technology, and cultural development within their own [[race]]. Among the other [[races]], the [[Anthronoris]] were feared and hated, and rightfully so: they sought to erradicate all non-human life in the name of expanding their territory and securing the unchallenged supremacy of their unending empire.

To this end, they launched [[Sacrum Bellus]], a war to destroy all rivals. It ended in the disaster known as the [[Sundering]], when the [[Ngrligru]] devoured nearly all known life. Pax Anthronoris ended in fire.

== Events of 1955 ==
===January===
*January 22 - Pentagon announces plan to develop ICBMs armed with nuclear weapons.
*January 23 - US President Thomas Dewey gives Pentagon orders not to arm ICBMs with nuclear weapons until effective guidance systems ensure they can be accurately used against only military targets.

===February===
*February 11 - [[The Technology Race]]: Pentagon begins rocket guidance development.


===March===

===April===
*April 5 - Winston Churchill resigns as Prime Minister of the British Empire.
*April 6 - [[Anthony Eden]] becomes Prime Minister of the British Empire.
*April 15 - Ray Kroc opens a McDonald's fast food restaurant; it is the company's ninth since it was founded in 1940. The franchise fails miserably, ending the McDonald's corporation. 
*April 18 - Albert Einstein dies in Princeton, New Jersey.

===May===
*May 14 - The Warsaw Pact is formed by the Communist states of Eastern Europe and the Soviet Union.

===June===
*June 13 - Mir Mine, the first diamond mine of the Soviet Union, is discovered.

===July===
*July 18 - [[The Technology Race]]: The first atomic-generated electrical power is sold commercially.
*July 18 - Beginning of Geneva Summit between United States, Soviet Union, British Empire, and France.
*July 23 - The Geneva Summit ends.
*July 27 - [[The Jihad]]: El Al Airlines Flight 402 from Vienna to Tel Aviv-Yafo via Istanbul shot down over Bulgaria by Muslim operatives.

===August===
*August 20 - [[The Jihad]]: Hundreds killed in Muslim-induced anti-French rioting in Morocco and Algeria.

===September===
*September 15 - Vladimir Nabokov's ''Lolita'' is published in Paris by Olympia Press. Seen as a threat to decency and western civilization, the CIA destroys all copies in the first shipment and sabotages the presses. All copies of the manuscript are destroyed. Nabokov is soon arrested when he is caught molesting a French child, and spends the rest of his life in prison.

===October===
*October 26
**[[Cold War]]: Austria declares permanent neutrality in all future wars.
**Ngo Dinh Diem is elected president in the first free elections in the Republic of Vietnam.

===November===
*November 1 - A time bomb explodes aboard a United Airlines DC-6 killing 44 above Longmont, Colorado. Domestic Communism suspected, but never proven.

===December===
*December 1 - Montgomery, Alabama seamstress, Rosa Parks, refuses to give up her bus seat to a white man. Bus driver James Blake refuses to force Parks to move and is later commended by the Civil Rights movement.
*December 5 - The Montgomery Improvement Association is formed in Montgomery, Alabama by Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Massive publicity movement supported by both black and white locals ends the policy of city buses giving whites seats over blacks.
*December 14 - Bulgaria joins the United Nations.

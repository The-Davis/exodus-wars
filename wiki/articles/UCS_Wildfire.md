When the UCS Kriegsmarine began to rebuild after the Mesarthrim Civil War, they decided on several designs to bolster their fleet. The new short-term naval strategy called for a core of frigates, until larger warships could be amassed, but a few heavy cruisers were purchased immediately. The Avenger class from Rahn Industries was selected as the choice heavy cruiser for the Kriegsmarine. The Wildfire was the first delivered in March of 2324, and all subsequent Mesarthrim Avenger classes were considered part of the Wildfire subclass.

The Wildfire, along with three other Wildfire class cruisers, were delivered to the Kriegsmarine in March, but the other twelve ordered were never delivered. The Tempest War began, and Rahn Industries cancelled the contracts. One of the Wildfire class vessels was stripped down by the Mesarthrim to be reverse engineered.

The Wildfire is extremely well armed. Its heavy weapons payload consists of ten Type-R Heavy Blazers, the heavy anti-warship weapons of the Mesarthrim, as well as a pair of GC-9f Hellguns, a slightly improved model built under license by Fireline Industries on Tincomalee.

[[Image:UCSWildfire2.jpg]]

Backing up the Type-Rs are fifty-two Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannons of the Mesarthrim. The firepower from these Type-Fs alone is enough to repel most any enemy. Additional firepower comes from eight missile launchers, giving it defense against fighters and also the capability for launching larger anti-warship payloads.

Mesarthrim defenses are superior to the defaults used by Rahn Industries, as are its power sources. The Wildfire and its sister ships can move faster than their Avenger counterparts.

[[Image:UCSWildfire3.jpg]]

The Wildfire served throughout the Tempest War with distinction, delivering troops, escorting convoys, and attacking Prefecture shipping. It was crippled by the FDS Triumph early in the war in the failed attack on New Albany, but was quickly repaired and returned to battle.

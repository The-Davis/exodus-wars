The Ixoarchada, literally translated "they of Archada", are one of the oldest human races who travel the stars.  The Ixoarchada have been a space-faring race for more than two thousand years.

===Appearance===

Once, the society of the Ixoarchada was governed through a very strong caste system. While all castes are still basically intact, only a few castes remain very well-defined today. Physical differences between castes are almost non-existent--with the single exception of the [[Rhová Deviri]], the warrior caste.

Physically, the citizen castes--upper, middle, and lower class civilians and the religious orders--are identical.  Their average height is around 2.01 meters--6.6 feet tall. They are significantly stockier than [[Terrans]], and have a slightly higher internal body temperature.

The average Ixoarchada IQ is about 2% higher than the [[Terran]] average.  They are also significantly less aggressive than [[Terrans]]--except for Rhova'Deviri Ixoarchada, of course.

The Rhová Deviri caste is significantly different from the other castes.  Ixoarchada warriors are the largest humans in the known galaxy.  Their average height of 2.13 meters--7 feet tall.  Their muscle growth is much greater than that of a [[Terran]].

Their immune system is enhanced, with greater oxygen capacity in their red blood cells.  Most Rhová Deviri receive several artificial implants and treatments to enhance their battlefield performance.  Due to these genetic and artificial enhancements, the average lifespan of a Rhová Deviri is actually over a decade longer than that of the lesser castes--when combat-related deaths are excluded, that is.

===Lifespan===

Due to modern Ixoarchada medical nanotechnology, the average Ixoarchada lifespan has been extended by several decades, with the average Ixoarchada living to 170 years of age on average, and ages of up to 200 years are not completely unheard of.

The M-28d was a "less lethal" weapon designed by the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] at the request of the [[Omegas]]. The [[Omegas]] often needed to capture well-defended people alive.

The use of flash-bang was widely accepted as a reasonable and effective tactic by civilian law enforcement for centuries, and the [[Omegas]] wanted it for their own use.

Like most grenades, the M-28d is a solid metal sphere blown apart by an explosive charge. Unlike others, it is mostly solid and safe, with a purpose-designed stress point that blows out with a loud report, and a bright flash of light. It is designed to stun, intimidate, and otherwise incapacitate a target rather than kill them.

The M-28d remains in use today by civilian law enforcement and military special forces.

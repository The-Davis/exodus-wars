''Ethan Drake'' (born January 1, [[2300]]) was one of millions of "vats" in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military training programs. He was a genetically-engineered, artificially-gestated soldier, born to be trained to kill.

Drake showed an unusual level of cunning in defensive measures, particularly in repelling boarders and in the defense of starships. He also displayed an innate knack for the operation of naval weapons and the maintenance of starship systems. He was placed in the enlisted ranks of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

Over the course of his career, Drake served with the cold, unimaginative efficiency that was typical of "vats." He was flawlessly competent, to the point where he was elevated to the top enlisted rank in the Royal Imperial Navy, the rank of "Master Chief of His Majesty's Royal Imperial Navy", and made the chief enlisted staff in the upper ranks of the Navy, the direct aide to the [[Grand Admiral]].

Ethan Drake served on Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian]]'s flagship, the [[HMS Jerome Black|HMS ''Jerome Black'']]. When Hadrian openly defied Emperor [[Lucius Black]] and revealed the existence of the [[Prefecture]], Ethan Drake followed his orders from the Emperor to the letter, and attempted to assasinate Hadrian. Hadrian was able to disarm Drake, and stabbed him through the heart with his issued duty knife, instantly killing him.

When word of Drake's loyalty reached the ears of the Emperor, he was posthumously cited with the [[Imperial Medal of Valor]]. Drake had no family other than the citizens of [[Ah Kin]] who fostered him, and their names are lost to history.

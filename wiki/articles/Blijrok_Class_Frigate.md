===Overview===

The first Blijrok was built by the Empress Talire Huern the First, years before the Burning War.

[[Image:BlijrokClassFrigate1.jpg]]

It was hidden, dormant during most of the War, but was found and copied many times over by heiress Kian Huern.

===Capabilities===

The main weapons of Blijrok Frigates are an array of seven Standard Blaster cannon turrets.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons.  They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

In a warship duel, Blijroks can pack quite a punch.  Their torpedo tube and Skrike missile launchers can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.  In the Burning War, the average Blijrok fired more nukes in a day than the Blackis Empire used in a month. 

[[Image:BlijrokClassFrigate2.jpg]]

The Blijrok is the lightest of the fighter carriers in the Lorithos ASTF.  It carries two squadrons of fighters, usually Vadries or Milroks.

With its Garvask Shields and Tzaiko Composite armor, the Blijrok is well-defended against its enemies.  When this is coupled with its great acceleration and maneuverability, it's tough to destroy a Blijrok.

===Deployment===

When Meronir's Republic began helping the Lorithos people rebuild Lorithan, they also built up their defenses with technology the Lorithos could easily rebuild.  The Rikaz Lorithos built two fleets of warships--ninety-four total warships.

[[Image:BlijrokClassFrigate3.jpg]]

Many of them are Blijroks.  The corvette is cheap, effective, and all Lorithos ASTF crews are familiar with them.  The Lorithos ASTF fields sixteen Blijrok frigates.

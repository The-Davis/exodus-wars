The '''Royal Imperial Health Association''' ran the clinics and hospitals in the [[Onyx Empire]], and were responsible for training up doctors, nurses, and other medical staff.

The Health Association was also responsible for reporting any terminal diseases, enfeebled elderly, chronically disabled, or other people required by Imperial Decree to be delivered to the "Blighted Camps." The Health Association was noted for its open protests against [[Lucius Black]]'s policies. Due to its value and importance to the [[Onyx Empire]], the organization could not be eliminated. Many doctors, most notably [[Chris Dayton]] refused to cooperate with the [[Alphas]] in gathering up the "undesireables."

The Health Association was one of the strongest supporters of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], and used its significant resources to help members on the run disappear from the [[Omegas]]' "radar screen".

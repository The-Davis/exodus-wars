Set during the early months of the fourth year of the Tempest War

by Andrew Sears


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A deep smoke swirls through the dark and foreboding room. Its not cannabis smoke, but after a few puffs -- you might just believe it is. The smoke does its job to accent the already overly cliche war room of evil that William Camp had created. The black sinister consoles, with red and yellow lights dominating every surface.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Its a wonder any one of you ever called this man a friend, well, except, he did have us all fooled -- an entire galaxy fooled. Bastard.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This tranquility of hate is suddenly disturbed as two bulky, yes even white eyed, Mesarthrim burst through the black metal doors, shoes clanking on the floor in a surrealistic fashion. This horrific nightmare of what-evil-actually-is has just gotten interesting -- the men; Supreme Admiral Arnold Stadt, and Chief Marshall Nick Wiedenfeld. Two old men from the old days, set in their old ways.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;William Camp, angered by the interruption, snarls, "What can I do for you ''gentlemen''?"
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Oh, it was said like that, like it was in italics. Like it was a curse. Like he had to choke it from his very lips.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stadt has the most evil, most world shattering, most ferocious look on his face it could scare even Cerebus. "You sent my fleet, my fleet into battle without me!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Camp's laugh insults both men deeply and clearly, "Your fleet Stadt? Your fleet? I be- lieve I rule this fancy little government, I believe I am god of this little ceremony, so if you have even the briefest sense of wisdom, you will shut your foolish mouth."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Yeah, you know it, It's enough -- Stadt snaps, and Wiedenfeld backs him up. Their advance on Camp is quick, but the butt of the guards gun is quicker. CRACK! Stadt hits the floor, bleeding from the massive wound that now decorates his face. He struggles for a second to get up, but is stopped by a well placed muzzle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Wiedenfeld stands, almost in shock -- what kind of Mesarthrim would hit a man such as Stadt?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Now as long as you are just laying their Stadt, let me fill you in on a brief piece of in- formation, I am in command here." William holds up his arms as if embracing his war room. "You will obey my commands or our people will be destroyed. And it will be your fault. You see, I have set things in motion that you cannot undo, and if you do not help me -- If you do not help me guarantee success -- then you will die like the rest of our people as the galaxy comes and wipes us from existence. We have the power to win, I mean after all, we are Mesarthrim."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stadt grunts angrily, its a simple response. A sudden laugh echo's through the room. Not a chuckle, not a giggle, no, a maniacal laugh. The kind of thing you would have expected from Camp just now -- but no, it was Wiedenfield.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What's so funny?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Wiedenfeld brings himself quickly under control. "There are things in motion that we cannot undo? Are you a fool William? Do you take us for a fool? You've already lost, I am here stealing your life away." Wiedenfeld turns towards the door.<br>
"Don't even pretend to be a man, your bluffing. You can't even fly your own fighter anymore you hook-handed freak. I sure as hell don't need you to win this war."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stopping. Wiedenfeld refuses to turn to face the man that just mocked him. "You will kill me no doubt. It is about my time anyway. What I have accomplished here is to sow seeds of mistrust among you, making you watch over your shoulder when you need not, and forgetting to when you really should be."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Your riddles mean nothing."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I am a member of the splinter cell operating within your government, your fall is inevitable. Now go, fool, kill everyone, that should keep you safe." With that the honorable, and now late, Nick Wiedenfeld takes his last steps -- steps into history as a single shot echos around the room. It enters and exits directly through his heart.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Such a beautiful shot to kill such a brave man who has served with such dignity.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And, for the moment at least, Camp feels safe.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Bastard''.

''Scott Sanburn'' was one of the few competent and tactically-minded commanders in the [[United Earth Alliance]] at the time of the Earth Invasion. He commanded a brilliant defense of the [[Titan System]] confounding the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] despite impossible odds.

=== Early Career ===

Scott Sanburn was raised in a patriotic family, and growing up on the distant colony of [[Theia]] convinced him of the importance of the military. He enlisted in the [[United Earth Alliance]] [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Defense Force]] on his 18th birthday, and was assigned to the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency|Space Agency]], where his eagerness and intelligence quickly earned him the rank of [[Ranks in the United Earth Alliance Defense Force|Master Corporal]].

Sanburn's superiors soon recognized his leadership abilities and charisma; the young Master Corporal was well-liked by his peers and everyone under his command received top efficiency ratings with every inspection. Sanburn hoped to receive a promotion to the rank of Sergeant and transfer to a [[:Category:Warships|warship]], but much to his surprise he was offered the option to attend officer candidacy school.

In [[2302]], Sanburn began his officer training on [[Earth]], where he quickly excelled. He took the opportunity to study a classical education while he was there, and graduated both from the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s officer candidacy school and from Dartmouth College in [[2307]] with top marks, and the commissioned rank of Lieutenant.

=== The Heritage Crisis ===

After receiving his commission, Lieutenant Sanburn was assigned to the [[UEAS Colin Powell|UEAS ''Colin Powell'']], an [[ADF-5000]] [[:Category:Battleships|battleship]], which was deployed to the [[Titan System]] in response to the [[Heritage Crisis|growing cold war]] in the [[Njiorin Sector]]. For the next five years, Sanburn trained with [[Ikronin]] military elements and drilled against [[Reigen]] military tactics, rising through the ranks to Captain-Commander.

In late [[2310]], Sanburn had the opportunity to prove his value. During the third watch, while both of his superior officers were off shift, the [[UEAS Colin Powell|UEAS ''Colin Powell'']] Sanburn responded to a distress call by [[Ikronin]] and [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] ships. Several pilots had "buzzed" a [[Reigen]] convoy in [[Njiorin's Well]], and had been fired on by [[Reigen]] escorts. In a trademark display of level-headedness and diplomacy, then Lieutenant-Commander Sanburn was able to convince the [[Reigens]] to allow him to recover the damaged [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] craft, and issued an official apollogy on behalf of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

Sanburn was later surprised to find out his commanding officer had been quietly watching the entire incident, and had intentionally left the ship under Sanburn's command when he realized that diplomacy would be more important than firepower.

While the [[Ikronin]] were not happy with Sanburn's sollution, the [[United Earth Alliance]] officially commended Sanburn for his idealism and calm approach to a tense situation.

=== Second Exodus War ===

Two years after the end of the [[Heritage Crisis]], Captain-Commander Sanburn was put in command of the [[Titan System]] defenses, and promoted to the rank of Brigadier-Commander. This unusual jump in rank offended many senior officers, as the [[Titan System]] was a choice posting and Sanburn was still a very young (though undeniably veteran and competent) officer.

At the outbreak of the [[Second Exodus War]], the issue quickly became a moot point, as most senior officers from different regions in the [[United Earth Alliance]] were either killed or captured by the [[Onyx Empire]]. By [[2316]], Scott Sanburn found himself the de-facto [[General-Commander]] of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]], with the full might of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military on his doorstep.

Despite the overwhelming pressure of invasion, the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government on [[Theia]] put great faith in Sanburn and made the promotion official, giving him supreme command in the war against the [[Onyx Empire]].

=== The Sanburn Plan ===

The [[Empire]]'s occupation of [[Earth]] had always been the thorn in the side that galvanized its enemies into action, and Sanburn saw the extreme importance of the world to the [[Terran]] people. As a representative of one of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s colonies, he had the rare wisdom and foresight to realize that bringing back the old government could spark another war when the now-independent colonies refused to recognize [[Earth]]'s sovereignty over them.

Sanburn wrote a lengthy proposal to the [[Alliance of Planets High Council]], recommending that all Member States of the [[Alliance of Planets]] formally cede any sovereign rights to the conquered territory, and that any who were able would begin supplying personnel and material support to rebuild an independent world government for [[Earth]].

Sanburn's wisdom and foresight is credited with preventing a third "Exodus War" that would have been caused by a sudden shift in power over the [[Terran]] cultures as a whole.

=== National Leader ===

After the [[Second Exodus War]], Sanburn encouraged the people of the [[Titan System]] to join the new [[Colonial Commonwealth]]. Today he continues to serve in the [[Titan System]]'s defense force, and helps train the militaries of the other worlds in the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

=== Legacy ===

The people of [[Theia]] have always respected Scott Sanburn as a military leader and national figurehead. His allies on other worlds have honored him in many other ways. The [[Mesarthrim]] named the [[Me-123 Sanburn]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighter plane]] after him. The [[Prefecture]] government commissioned a statue for their military headquarters on [[Ueda]], titled "The Triumphant Three", depicting Scott Sanburn, [[Andreas Tischler]] and [[Anthony Hadrian]].

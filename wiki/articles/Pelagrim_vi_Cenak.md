The star-cluster that first housed the lost and wandering [[Anderung]] society became known among them as "Pelagrim's Fangs." Among [[Terran]] astronomers and astrogators, it was commonly known as "Praesape Cluster."

Today, most of the [[Mesarthrim]] territories are located within the star cluster.

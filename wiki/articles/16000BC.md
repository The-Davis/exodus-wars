== Events of 16000BC ==

At the height of the [[Anthronoris Empire]], humans had a lifespan that was essentially endless, so long as they received regular medical treatments.  The chemical compounds used to synthesize the “immortality serum” were lost when the [[Anthronoris]] lost their holding in the [[Sagittarius Arm]].  The effects slowly wore off, and the [[Meroniri]] began to die.  [[Meronir Tronit]] died at almost 5,000 years old.  The next generation of [[Meroniri]] maintained a lifespan of 300 years, which they retained until modern days.

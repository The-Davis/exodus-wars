'''Patrick O'Neill'''
*Place of Birth: [[Labrea]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Patrick O'Neill'' is a native of [[Labrea]] in the Laojiu District. [[Labrea]] is home to the best armored training camp in the [[Prefecture]] territories. O'Neill trained there for years before the [[Second Exodus War]], and fought many battles for the [[United Earth Alliance]] and [[Ikronin Jurekön]] before finally joining the [[Prefecture]] after the [[Imperial Civil War]].

O'Neill's tactics are similar to [[Ikronin]] warfare. He tends to camouflage units until enemy formations have moved past them, then popping up and flanking from behind. This tactic was perfected in the tundra and snowcaps of [[Kronheim]].

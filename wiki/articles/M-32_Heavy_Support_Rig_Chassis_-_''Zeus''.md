In the Second Exodus War (2315-2322), the Zeus was one of the first heavy Rigs fielded by any faction.  It served with distinction in the Battle of Titanian 12, but was finally decommissioned in the year 2321.
Only some aspects of the original Zeus remain in the Mk.II version.  It has two cannon weapons, and missile launchers, but the mountings and firing arcs are much different.  The new Zeus's cockpit is enclosed, something a lot of dead 2315 Rig pilots probably wish they'd had.

The original Zeus had a chin turret of PPCs, and a head turret of lasers.  The new model has two Rotary Stinger Cannons.  The switch to an ammunition-based weapon does mean that the Zeus Mk.II has to reload frequently, but its modular ammo bins make rearming a simple process as long as an Ammo Truck is nearby.  It also carries two Rotary Thumper Cannons, with an incredible close-range punch.

The Zeus Mk.II is powered by the now-popular Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor.  Without the advances in small reactor technology, the new Zeus would probably be running on the hot and inefficient fission reactor of its predecessor.

Protection of the pilot is a very high priority of the Prefecture.  The original Zeus's operators were vulnerable to pot shots from the flanks, and armor was practically non-existent.  The new Zeus is equipped with Boron-Nitride composite armor, and two types of shield systems to stop weapons fire several meters from the Rig. 
The Zeus Mk.II project began on February 23rd, 2324, and was completed in June of 2324.  Assembly-line production of each Zeus Mk.II takes three days, but because of modern construction rates, the complete Prefecture arsenal of Hollanders was replaced by Zeus Mk.IIs by the end of the year.

Zeus Mk.IIs are used mostly in a support role.  Without arms for grappling, a Zeus Mk.II would be overwhelmed by hundreds of small Volucris or grapple attacks from larger Volucris.  When teamed with a Hercules Mk.II or Hades, the Zeus can perform its duties properly.

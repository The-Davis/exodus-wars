'''Mount McKinley''' is the highest mountain peak in North America at a height of approximately 2700 meters. It was the site of the last stand of the [[Onyx Empire]] on [[Earth]], at the Emperor's Denali Fortress. 

== Location ==

Mount McKinley is located in the central portion of the Alaska Range, which spans much of south central Alaska. It is approximately 209 kilometers north-northwest of Anchorage and 249 kilometers southwest of Fairbanks. The summit is approximately 56 kilometers from the nearest major road, the George Parks Highway.

== Notable features ==

Mount McKinley has a larger bulk and rise than Mount Everest. Even though the summit of Everest is about 2,700 meters higher as measured from sea level, its base sits on the Tibetan Plateau at about 5,200 meters, giving it a real vertical rise of a little more than 3,700 meters. The base of Mount McKinley is roughly a 500-meter plateau, giving it an actual rise of 5,500 meters.

The mountain is also characterized by extremely cold weather. A thermometer left exposed at an elevation of 4,600 meters on Mount McKinley over 19 years recorded a temperature of −73.3 °C at some point during its exposure. There is also a higher risk of altitude illness for climbers than its altitude would otherwise suggest, due to its high latitude. At the equator, a mountain as high as Mount McKinley would have 47% as much oxygen available on its summit as there is at sea level, but because of its latitude, the pressure on the summit of McKinley is even lower

== Layout of the mountain ==

Mount McKinley has two significant summits: the South Summit is the higher one, while the North Summit has an elevation of 5,934 meters and a prominence of approximately 402 meters. The North Summit is sometimes counted as a separate peak and sometimes not; it is rarely climbed, except by those doing routes on the north side of the massif. The north summit marked the center point of the Denali Fortress.

Five large glaciers flow off the slopes of the mountain. The Peters Glacier lies on the northwest side of the massif, while the Muldrow Glacier falls from its northeast slopes. Just to the east of the Muldrow, and abutting the eastern side of the massif, is the Traleika Glacier. The Ruth Glacier lies to the southeast of the mountain, and the Kahiltna Glacier leads up to the southwest side of the mountain.

== Denali Fortress ==

Denali Fortress was one of several retreats for Emperor [[Lucius Black]] constructed between [[2315]] and [[2322]]. It was buried two kilometers beneath the base of the mountain, and centered under the North Summit. The fortress was built to house several escape ships, a thousand Imperial troops, and over fifty thousand proximity mines and automated static defense turrets. The fortress was the Emperor's haven after he abandoned the [[Obsidian Palace]] until he abandoned Earth.

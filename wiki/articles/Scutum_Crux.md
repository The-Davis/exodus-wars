The '''Scutum Crux''' is largely unexplored by human civilizations. It was briefly visited by the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] in [[2324]]. The expedition discovered several star systems inhabitted by the [[Volucris]]. It is believed that the Scutum Crux houses their capital.

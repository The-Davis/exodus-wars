Not since the darkest years of [[Germany (Terra)|Nazi Germany]] has any [[Terran]] [[:Category:Nations|nation]] been as feared or hated as the Onyx Empire. Its policies of murder, warmongering, fearmongering, and terrorism were more thorough and more brutal than any other government's.

Unlike other past totalitarian governments, where such practices were an "open secret" and more a byproduct of the government's rule, the Onyx Empire was proud of its deadly control, and revelled in the bloodshed it unleashed.

===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Onyx Empire|History]]

The Onyx Empire, officially the Sovereign Empire of the Royal House of Black, was built on the charisma and cunning of [[Lucius Black]]. Its history stems from the [[First Exodus War]], but did not officially form until the [[Torimur]] Exiles ended the [[Second Exodus]] and captured the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. Years later, the Onyx Empire expanded its reign across the galaxy in a series of invasions, all part of a conflict known as the [[Second Exodus War]]. The Onyx Empire was defeated and dissolved in [[2324]].

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of the Onyx Empire|Government]]

In its earliest form, the Imperial Government was not in titular control of its territories, and instead exercised de-facto control through a series of agents and conspirators.

When it openly declared itself and seized power in [[2315]], the Imperial Government was formally designed around a dictatorship. [[Lucius Black]] held absolute power, but delegated power to a series of agents and governors who ran his affairs on other worlds.

===Law===
Main Article: [[Law of the Onyx Empire|Law]]

Despite its reputation for ruthlessness and outright cruelty, the Onyx Empire did have the code of Imperial Law, which was designed to maintain peaceful, orderly civilian life. Crimes were well-defined and simple to follow, but personal freedoms were non-existant. Evidence could be collected by agents of the [[Omegas]] without warrant, and anyone could be stopped and searched at any time. Resisting an agent of the [[Omegas]] in any way meant imprisonment or death, usually death.

The ultimate law in the Onyx Empire was the Imperial Decree, which [[Lucius Black]] could issue at any time and for any reason, immediately overriding any other laws on the books.

===Law Enforcement===
Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Onyx Empire|Law Enforcement]]

The [[United Earth Alliance]]'s existing law enforcement was disarmed, but not disbanded. They were given the role of peace officers, traffic enforcers, and most of the other roles they held earlier. Unlike the [[Omegas]], the Imperial Peacekeepers were required to follow traditional rules of due process, but they could immediately call on the [[Omegas]] at any time.

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Empire|Foreign Relations]]

The Onyx Empire existed almost entirely to make war on other nations. The Onyx Empire never formed official alliances or trade agreements with other nations, with the sole exception of the [[Sirius Families]] via the [[Nakamura Family Territory]], but they never became true alliances.

The Onyx Empire did attempt to form an alliance with [[Gaius Shadar]] of the [[Remnant]], in order to gain knowledge of their immortality and their technology. [[Lucius Black]] attempted to uncover their secrets, but never brought them over to his side. In the last years of the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Alphas]] spread official word and rumors that the [[Remnant]] had sided with the Onyx Empire, but this was not the case. The [[Remnant]] fought several battles against the [[Alliance of Planets]] during the [[Second Exodus War]], but they were on errands of their own, not serving the interests of the Onyx Empire.

===Military===
Main Article: [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Military]]

The [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]] was the most widely-publicized and omnipresent face the Onyx Empire presented to outsiders. It was formed with the intended purpose of prosecuting war against its neighbors and taking over their territories.

The bulk of the personnel in the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]] were artificially gestated during the "Perfect Soldier Project" developed and launched on [[Ah Kin]]. Others were [[Torimur]] exiles who fought in the [[First Exodus War]], and many were volunteers from the various worlds conquered by the Onyx Empire during the [[Second Exodus War]].

The [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]] had four branches; the [[Royal Imperial Army]], [[Royal Imperial Navy]], [[Royal Imperial Air Force]], and [[Royal Imperial Marine Corps]]. They strongly believed in a combined arms doctrine, and all four chains of command answered to the [[Grand Admiral of the Onyx Empire|Grand Admiral]], and then to Emperor [[Lucius Black]].

===Intelligence===
Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Onyx Empire|Intelligence]]

The Onyx Empire's intelligence-gathering and counter-intelligence organizations were among the greatest in human space. The [[Omegas]] acted as an extremely effective all-purpose intelligence organization: they gathered intelligence, spread terror, completed counter-intelligence and counter-insurgency missions, and acted as a form of state police for the Empire, answering only to [[Lucius Black]].

The [[Alphas]] had a more benign intelligence role. They were the propaganda machine of the Onyx Empire, spreading pro-Empire propaganda within its borders, and spreading anti-[[United Earth Alliance]] propaganda within the [[Titan System]] and other [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]] before their defeat at the hands of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial military]].

===Transportation===
Main Article: [[Transportation of the Onyx Empire|Transportation]]

Private transportation off-world was strictly regulated by the [[Government of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Government]]. In-system flights were permitted, but all [[hyperspace]]-capable [[:Category:Starships|starships]] were seized by the Onyx Empire.

The [[Royal Imperial Merchant Marine]] was formed to operate all non-military [[:Category:Starships|starships]] under the control of the Empire. The vast merchant fleets operated passenger liners to carry legal civilian traffic, and freighters to carry the goods that made up the backbone of the Onyx Empire's economic power.

===Territory===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Onyx Empire|Territory]]

At the height of its power, the Onyx Empire held control over nineteen [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]], and covered three [[:Category:Sectors|Sectors]]. Its power base was in the [[Sol Sector]], but it had significant holdings in the [[De Mairan Sector]] and the [[Pelagrim Sector]].

At its height, the Onyx Empire controlled thirty-two inhabitted [[:Category:Planets|planets]], and operated its power base from the Imperial Palace in the city of [[Memphis]], on [[Earth]].

===Economy===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Onyx Empire|Economy]]

The economy of the Onyx Empire was centered on the [[Imperial Credit]], an electronic currency which was carefully regulated and controlled by the [[Royal Imperial Bank]]. The Onyx Empire never levied taxes, and only paid its debts through issuing [[Imperial Credit]]s. In order to maintain demand for the [[Imperial Credit|Credit]], the possession of any other currency was outlawed, punishable by death.

Despite their stranglehold on currency and on the production of military hardware, the rest of the economy thrived in the Empire's territories, until the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], when the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] purged all records of ownership and account ballances.

===Demographics===
Main Article: [[Demographics of the Onyx Empire|Demographics]]

The Onyx Empire controlled one of the most diverse populations of [[Terrans]], mostly due to its large territory and the fact that it conquered the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s holdings. No religion was promoted over any other, and no [[race]] was given preference over any other.

===Public Health===
Main Article: [[Royal Imperial Health Association|Public Health]]

Public health oversight was another role held by the [[Alphas]], though their primary mission was to oversee the "Blighted Camps" where the terminally ill, the chronically disabled, and the enfeebled elderly were housed, processed, and terminated.

The [[Royal Imperial Health Association]] was created to treat the "desireable" elements of society permitted access to healthcare by Imperial decree. The treatments and checkups were not free, as part of the [[Royal Imperial Bank]]'s plan to create demand for the [[Imperial Credit]]. The [[Royal Imperial Health Association|Association]] ran clinics, hospitals, and medical schools. The [[Royal Imperial Health Association|Association]] was also responsible for the oversight of doctors' performance and professional standards.

===Culture===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Onyx Empire|Culture]]

The [[Torimur]] culture was promoted and allowed to thrive under the reign of [[Lucius Black]]'s empire. The blending of [[Italy (Terra)|Itallian]] and [[Mongolia (Terra)|Mongolian]] cultures and [[race|racial]] characteristics and cultural values was promoted from the top down by the [[Government of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Government]].

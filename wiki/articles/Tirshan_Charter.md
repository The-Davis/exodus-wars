Following the overwhelming victory of the [[Second Tirshan Uprising]], the years of work that the old TFVMS II council put into creating the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] had finally come to fruition.  The Tirshan Charter was the official formation of the [[Mesarthrim Federation]].  The Tirshan Charter specifically stated that after the conclusion of hostilities, known as the [[Tempest War]], the [[Auellal League]] and [[Comur Resistance]] would become the founding members of the [[Mesarthrim Fedeartion]].  Also, with assurances from the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] would retain any territory acquired from the [[Mesarthrim]].

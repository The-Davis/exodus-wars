'''Travis Carpenter'''
*Born: September 2, [[2296]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Travis Carpenter'' (born January 1, [[2300]]) was part of the artificially gestated children in the Imperial Army program. He was raised as a Rig pilot, and served in the Royal Imperial Army before leading the Prefecture's rebellion on the ground.

==The Phoenix==

Travis Carpenter is the best. No Rig pilot can defeat him, no general can outwit him, no brawler can throw him.

Carpenter was born towards the end of the [[Second Exodus]], and served as a Rig pilot in Emperor Lucius Black's invading army.  In the [[2315]] invasion of Terra, 19-year-old Carpenter earned the rank of Captain, and was given [[Cerberus Company]].

From [[2315]] to [[2322]], Travis Carpenter won battle after battle, and worked his way up to Colonel.  On April 24th, [[2322]], he sided with [[Anthony Hadrian]] in the rebellion that formed the [[Prefecture]].  He was promoted to General on the spot.

General Carpenter's forces never lost a campaign in the [[Battle of Terra]], though they suffered a few setbacks.  Losing a company of Rig pilots to his Imperial brother David caused Travis Carpenter to suffer a nervous breakdown.  His forces floundered while he refused to fight or lead.

When he came out of his stupor, Carpenter was a man reborn.  His fiery new attitude and cock-sure, brash behavior quickly earned him the nickname "Phoenix."  He crushed the army supporting his brother, and chased him clear across the North American continent, eventually cornering both David Carpenter and Lucius Black in the Arctic.

Today, Travis Carpenter has aged, but not matured in attitude.  The fact that he is younger than any other General in the Prefecture Army, and still the best has gone to his head.  He is above reproach in combat, but not pleasant to be around in times of peace.  Unless he respects someone as a superior, he will treat them contemptuously.

One can only hope that General Carpenter will learn to be more charismatic in the future, and win the battles on the home front as well as on the field.

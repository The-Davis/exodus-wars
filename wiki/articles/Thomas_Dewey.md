*Born: March 24, [[1902]]
*Died: March 16, [[1971]]

''Thomas Dewey'' (born March 24, [[1902]]) was the thirty-fourth President of the [[United States (Terra)| United States of America]]. He was victorious in the [[1948]] election, defeating the incumbent [[Harry Truman]]. Dewey's terms as President were marked with great success in developing atomic weapons and energy, stamping out domestic [[Communism]], and spurring on the development of technology. Dewey was succeeded by his Vice President, [[Strom Thurmond]], who's segregationist ideals set him far apart from Dewey's ideas of a truly united [[United States (Terra)|America]].

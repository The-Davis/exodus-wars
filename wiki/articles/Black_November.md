On November 17, [[2318]], Emperor [[Lucius Black]] sent a team of diplomats to [[Theia]] under a flag of truce.  The delegation made an offer which spurred a series of negotiations which became known as "'''Black November'''" among the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]].

The delegation offered a cease-fire agreement which would result in the withdrawal of Imperial forces from the [[Titan System]], and would guarantee no Imperial aggression against any other [[Alliance of Planets]] members.

The Ikronin and the Lorithos, both intimidated by the power of the Empire, brought a vote to the Alliance of Planets' High Council.  Under pressure from its civillian population, the remains of the [[United Earth Alliance]] agreed to the negotiations.  The Alliance delegation was led by [[Naimen Ladosen]] of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]].

Lucius Black was not an easy man to negotiate with.  He had made the offer to distract the Alliance of Planets while he invaded the remaining independent [[Terran]] colonies.  The [[Titan Peace Accords]] were written, but until the disaster of the [[Sirius Incursion]] he was not bargaining in good faith.  The Titan Peace Accords were not signed for nearly two years.

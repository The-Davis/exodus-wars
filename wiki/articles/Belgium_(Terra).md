'''Belgium''' was a country in northwest Europe bordered by the [[Netherlands (Terra)|Netherlands]], [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]], [[Luxembourg (Terra)|Luxembourg]], and [[France (Terra)|France]], with a short coastline on the North Sea. Belgium's last reigning monarch, Philippe I, abdictated the throne on November 1, [[2026]], paving the way for the Belgians to join the [[United Earth Alliance]].

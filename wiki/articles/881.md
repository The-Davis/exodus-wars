== Events of 881AD ==

The population of [[Tolesca]] reaches one million inhabitants.  In this same year, the system-wide population of the [[Bollaria System]] surpasses five billion.  Population pressure in the [[Ularen System]] continues to drive the colonization of these and other worlds.

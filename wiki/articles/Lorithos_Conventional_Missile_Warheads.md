Lorithos ground missiles are rarely used. They are propelled and exploded by clean-chemical compounds. Because of their weak power, Lorithos weapons designers tend to stick with Blasters only.

In many tactical situations in space, good missiles are absolutely necessary, and environmental concerns are less important. While nuclear weapons are still strictly forbidden, the use of small doses of antimatter for explosive tips in space missiles are acceptable.

==Sreiver Missiles==
Sreiver Missiles are the staple ground missile. Like many Terran missiles, Sreivers are cluster-launched, and fed from ammo bins. They have a maximum range of 80 kilometers.

==Skrike Missiles==
Skrike Missiles are the antimatter missiles used in space. Their explosive yield is slightly lower than a tactical nuclear warhead. Fighters can only carry a few--depending on the size of the fighter, but warships can carry dozens, even hundreds.

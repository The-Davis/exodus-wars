'''Chonge Kodwe'''
*Place of Birth: [[Rhea]]
*Allegiance: [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Chonge Kodwe'' holds dual citizenship in the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] and [[Kabila Kimburu]]. Kodwe earned a name for himself during the [[Battle of Terra]], where he aided [[Travis Carpenter]]'s campaign across North America.

Despite his [[Ngrligru]] heritage, Chonge Kodwe is a well-liked and respected commander. Human science has helped treat the [[Njaa]] symptoms of [[Kabila Kimburu]] more effectively than their brethren in the [[Sagittarius Arm]], and they do not suffer from the urge to devour their foes.

==Alien General==

Chonge Kodwe came to [[Terra]] at the height of the [[Imperial Civil War]]. He brought thousands of [[Ngrligru]] and [[Mtomigru]] soldiers with him, and eventually joined the [[Prefecture]] and formed the [[Legion of the Wolf]].

Today, Kodwe and his second-in-command, Brigadier [[Hodar Jana]], enjoy the benefits or citizenship in the [[Prefecture]] and great respect of the people who live there.

Kodwe's Wolf Legion infantry are the best in the [[Prefecture]]. Kodwe and Jana train and drill the Wolf Legion infantry personally. Despite their harsh discipline, career human infantry troops request to serve at least one tour of duty under their command.

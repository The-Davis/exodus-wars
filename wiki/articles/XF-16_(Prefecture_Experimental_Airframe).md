By 2324, the F-4 airframe and design was nearly 30 years old. The Raven was ready to be phased out. The XF-16 airframe was built to overcome the problems of an airframe originally used for a turbojet and a rocket using a Scramjet and a gravity supercavitator. The XF-16 mounted 2 cannons and 4 hard-points. The YF-16 tested against the F-4I, and was able to slightly outperform it. It was cheaper to use purpose-built components than to alter designs to fit on the F-4I's airframe, and the F-16 Piranha was designed to slowly phase out the design.

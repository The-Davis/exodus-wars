The XF-14 prototype was an F-9C airframe equipped with a new gravity cavitation drive system (Supercavitation, uses Interstellar_medium). The drive was extremely powerful, bringing space acceleration rates up exponentially and eliminating the need for solid fuel. The smaller fighters, the F-4H and F-9D were equipped with the drive, but could not power both shields and accelerate. They kept the MPD system as a backup for shields and acceleration at the same time. The F-11C and B-13B were both equipped with the drive and could use their drives and shields at the same time.

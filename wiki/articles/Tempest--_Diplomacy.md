Just after "Tempest: Political Tremors"

by James Davis, Ryan Hepler, Jamal Coleman and Seth Dickinson.

===== Sepulare - 1300 Hours =====

The ''Mäg Ols'' has just completed its final Tesseract for the day, bringing representatives from each of the Member States of the [[Alliance of Planets]] to a meeting of the High Council. The powerful warship eases into a docking station on one of [[Sepulare]]'s many space elevators. Mere minutes later, the ambassadors and dignitaries are all assembled at the headquarters building of the Alliance. The entire process from calling the meeting to the arrival took less than an hour.

===== High Council Chamber - 1305 Hours =====

"This meeting will now come to order," [[Naimen Ladosen]] solemnly intones. "The first order of business is an emergency call for armed action against the [[United Centusi States]], raised by the [[Prefecture]]. As Consul Dayton is still missing, the floor is yielded to his substitute, Admiral [[Jason Bremen]]."

"As I'm sure you are all aware, the forces of the United Centusi States attacked our world, [[Leskovik]]. This was done without provocation or warning. All of the evidence has been provided to you in your briefings." Bremen sighs. "We are not able to continue a war on two fronts. If we focus our attention on the Mesarthrim invaders, we will be unable to assist our allies with any further [[Volucris]] incursions. It is our hope that if you join us, the [[Mesarthrim]] will back off and this war can be ended without further bloodshed."

Lorithos [[Prekohr]] [[Dirken Huern]] stands, and the floor is yielded to him. "Where is Director Camp of the United Centusi States? Why didn't he join us for this meeting? What does he say in answer to these charges?"

"Perhaps he is delayed,” [[Isilahi Kodwe]] innocently suggest, while remaining seated at Kabila Kimburu’s table. “Prime Minister Ladosen?”

“I'm afraid Director Camp rejected our summons when the Mäg Ols arrived at [[Mesar]]," Ladosen replies. "His representative said that pressing matters demanded his attention at home, and that while he sends his regrets, no one else was qualified to handle them.” The Meroniri takes a deep breath, mentally preparing himself for the storm that would come from his next action. “The Mesarthrim transmitted this briefing to [[Rosh Arufin]] before the Mäg Ols came here. General-Commander Beson, please play the briefing."

The Space Police representative--the official Sergeant at Arms for the meeting--punches a sequence into his console, and the briefing appears on the monitors. Ladosen carefully scans the room, examining each representative. He'd only glanced over the briefing when he received it, but he knew the gist of it.

"This is an outrage!” Admiral Bremen jumps from his seat, slamming both fists on the table with a crash. "How dare they suggest that we would nuke our own planet?" Bremen’s calm voice had transformed to a shout in a moment. "Those gutless cowards think we are trying to goad them into a war?! By destroying our world?!"

"With no disrespect Admiral, your people bombed many worlds during the [[Second Exodus War]]. Granted, you had a madman pulling the strings back then, but we must be ready to consider even unlikely scenarios.” Bremen’s sharp gaze could freeze a recruit's boots to the floor, but the Ngrligru keeps her cool, returning his look. "Therefore, consider. Could this have been another plot of [[Lucius Black]]? Your government believes that the Imperial remnant is responsible for Consul Dayton’s abduction. Could these events be related?"

"No, Kuu Kodwe, I'm afraid that it isn't possible." Bremen regains his composure. "While most of Leskovik was obliterated, we were able to pick up enough of a hyperspace drive signature to prove that the ship came from Mesarthrim territory, and had a GRLD system. Our intelligence on the Conclave's capabilities has come from both the Inquisitors and the Lorithos ICD. It's accurate.

"The Space Police and the Lorithos ICD haven't surveyed the remains of Leskovik yet, but the Inquisitors' report is conclusive. The culprit was the United Centusi States." Bremen sits, straightening his uniform with a quick tug to the bottom of the tunic. Academy habits die hard.

"I understand, Admiral Bremen. I don’t want an unnecessary war. The Alliance has yet to deal decisively with the Volucris threat. How do you expect us to confront one of our own members?" 

No one faults the Ngrligru's objections to war. Her Kabila, Kimburu, was driven out of Ngrligru space and nearly destroyed after millennia of conflict.

"The souls of millions of civilians require action. Their murders require justice." Bremen fumes on the inside. Dayton would have a pretty speech ready at a moment's notice. "Further debate is pointless. I propose that we strip the United Centusi States of all rights and privileges of a Member State of the Alliance, and that we provide a unified front against them."

Prime Minister Ladosen nods. "Is there a second?"

Simon Blodgit, the Ikronin representative, raises a hand, knowing that backing the Prefecture in this matter will go a long way towards repaying the debt his people owe to them. "I second the motion."

Ladosen nods. "Any further discussion before we vote on the motion?" This draws an aggravated sigh from Admiral Bremen - which doesn't dissuade the career politicians.

Dirken Huern takes the floor again, looking more than a little irritated. "I feel we're being unnecessarily hasty. Our only proof that the United Centusi States are behind these attacks comes from the Inquisitors - the Prefecture's hired intelligence service. I do not wish to impugn the integrity of the ancient and noble order of Inquisitors, but given the recent recent hardships suffered by the Prefecture itself, they may be too quick to assign blame." He spreads his hands in a gesture of appeal. "Until we are certain that the attack on Leskovik was authorized by the Mesarthrim government - and not simply carried out by a group of fanatics, perhaps bitter veterans of the Exodus War - we should not punish the entire Mesarthrim people. Let us wait for reports from both the Space Police and my own government's Intelligence and Communications Department. I will not agree to act until then."

Admiral Bremen sputters angrily, barely remembering to wait for recognition before speaking. He cuts loose as soon as Naimen Ladosen nods in his direction. "I can't believe that the Lorithos, of all people, would shirk from bringing the perpetrators of a holocaust to justice! If we don't stop Camp and the Mesarthrim, who knows how many more planets might end up like Leskovik? Or like Lorithan, for that matter?"

"We are well aware of the cost in lives that such devastation carries," the Lorithos President snaps back. "We are also aware of the cost of a military confrontation between the Mesarthrim and the rest of the Alliance, especially if it becomes a shooting war. Both the Mesarthrim and the Prefecture have shown ruthlessness and disregard for human life by your continued stockpiling of nuclear arsenals!"

"You're going to thank us for keeping those weapons around when the Volucris come after your planet!" Bremen growls.

Huern's face darkens. "Your Prefecture was born in war, and you still let warrior lead you," he snaps, leaning aggressively towards Bremen. "You're too quick to resort to conflict, and remain unaware of the costs. Your suggestion of using nuclear weapons, even to defend Lorithan, is an insult to us!"

"Gentlemen, you are both out of order!" Ladosen's voice is that of the practiced statesman, completely calm, yet loud enough to be heard over the shouts. The two opponents glare at each other for a moment, then sink back into their seats.

Simon Blodgit raises a hand; Ladosen gratefully recognizes him, hoping to defuse a tense situation. The Ikronin leans forward with the expression of a reasonable businessman. "Philosophical debates over warfare aside," the Ikronin says smoothly, "the real issue is whether presenting a threat of military action against the United Centusi States will undermine our defense against Volucris attack. Perhaps the Meroniri could put pressure on the Mesarthrim, so the rest of our members can keep their attention focused on the bugs? We know the cost of complacency."

"I'm afraid that we cannot provide any military aid or pressure against the Mesarthrim at this time," Ladosen says quietly. "With the exception of the Expeditionary Force, which has already been tasked with observing the Volucris, our military is needed within our own borders."

"At this time?" Blodgit muses. "Enlighten us. When would the vaunted Meroniri military be of assistance to anyone? You refused to aid my government against the Volucris. You sent the incompetent Admiral Richardson to advise the Titanians in their struggle against the Empire. Forgive my ignorance, Mr. President, but I don't believe that the Meroniri have ever done anything."

Naimen Ladosen keeps his expression neutral, refusing to take the bait. "I am not at liberty to divulge the nature of Meroniri military operations."

Simon Blodgit scowls. "Then I'm afraid I have no choice but to make the decision of the Consortium official and public. After the destruction of Oselo, we made a decision that the corporate model we've been operating under will no longer apply to the Consortium."

Dirken Huern's eyes nearly pop out of his head. "You're disbanding the corporations?"

Blodgit laughs. "Don't be absurd. Disbanding? No. Quite the contrary. We are strengthening the corporations by investing in Kronheim's future.

"Over the years, the corporations have become more and more distant from Icronia herself, leaving the planet for bases in space. Having each corporation provide their own defenses to protect Icronia has only led to the corporations providing defenses for themselves and their own interests.

"The Consortium took a frank assessment of our position. We were weak, unable to adequately defend ourselves against the Volucris without the help the Prefecture. And the Alliance? Fah.

"The mighty Meroniri Terinasi Republic sent no ships, no troops, not even medical aid until the danger was over. The Lorithos hid in their stained ivory towers, quavering in fear."

Naimen Ladosen and Dirken Huern fume inwardly, but both sit calmly and listen.

"If the oldest and largest member of the Alliance won't even be bothered with sending aid to help it's first ally, we will take steps to ensure our own security and sovereignty. Already we are rebuilding our destroyed Gates, and moving at least one Gatekeeper Defense Platform to protect each Gate. Central hubs will have multiple platforms and cruisers in constant defense stations."

Even Jason Bremen looks alarmed at this. "Mr. Blodgit, while you and your forces are always welcome in Prefecture space, perhaps such a strong military presence would be considered...overtly aggressive by our less understanding allies?"

Blodgit nods. "I agree, Admiral. Any objections will be completely understood and we will comply. If you do not wish to have an Ikronin military presence within your territories, you will not have a Gate either. We will simply move your Gates into neutral space as close as is legally possible. As always, passage will be granted to all paying customers regardless of governmental alignment."

Ladosen sighs. "Will that be all, Representative Blodgit?"

Blodgit shakes his head. "Hardly, Naimen. We wish to formally announce that the Ikronin Jurekon will be expanding its borders."

The combined shock of Simon Blodgit's rude informality and the revelation of the Ikronin plans are enough to draw sounds of surprise from the representatives.

Blodgit continues. "For centuries, we have merely explored space, typically mining what we needed and moving on. Now we will begin actively colonizing habitable or otherwise strategically useful systems to us. To protect our new territories, we will also be expanding our fleets by well over twenty-fold. As a gesture of openness and continued goodwill towards our fellow Member States...despite our current disagreements, these new fleet numbers and designs will be forwarded to your respective military intelligence departments.

"We have no military secrets, my friends. We never have, and we never will. It's bad for business." He sits, but quickly gets up again before Prime Minister Ladosen takes the floor. "While I have gotten us off the matter at hand, I encourage a vote."

Ladosen takes the floor. "Unless anyone else has anything to say?" A quick survey of the room shows that there is no further input. "Very well. Vote yea or nay. Shall the United Centusi States be expelled from this august body, and be stripped of all rights and privileges as a Member State of the Alliance of Planets?"

The vote is cast. The Ikronin, Titanians and Prefecture all vote yea. The Lorithos and Kimburu vote nay. The Meroniri and Mesarthrim (by reason of their absence) abstain.

"So the vote has been cast and so this council has spoken." Naimen Ladosen gravely intones. "Henceforth the United Centusi States shall take no part in the proceedings of this council, and shall have no voice in the doings of the Alliance. Sergeant-at-arms, remove their flag and chair."

This is done with a murmur of approval from Blodgit and Bremen.

"On the second issue," Ladosen continues. "Vote yea or nay. Shall the Alliance of Planets take an official aggressive stance against the United Centusi States, and actively encourage its Member States to wage war against them?"

This time only the Ikronin Jurekon and Prefecture vote yea. With the sole abstaining vote of the Titanians, the other Members vote nay.

"So the vote has been cast and so this council has spoken." Naimen Ladosen intones. "Until such time as an official treaty can be drafted with the United Centusi States, the Alliance of Planets shall hold an officially neutral stance towards them, and shall offer neither support nor opposition."

Jason Bremen stands, unrecognized by the President. "And there will be no such treaty." He growls. "I can see diplomacy has failed. I'm glad that none of you will try to stop us, but we are now forced to deal with the Mesarthrim in our own way."

Blodgit joins him. "The Ikronin Jurekon stands with the Prefecture."

"As of this moment," Bremen continues. "We are at war with the United Centusi States."

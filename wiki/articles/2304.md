== Events of 2304 ==

===January===
*January 19 - [[Argus Lax]] is chosen as the [[Handelboss]] of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] for the next ten year period.

===February===

*February 24 - The [[Reigess Suverände]] discovers the presence of a non-[[Reigen]] [[antimatter]] well in [[Njiorin's Well]].
*February 25 - The [[Military of the Reigess Suverände|Reigen military]] is dispatched to [[Njiorin's Well]] to investigate the rogue [[antimatter]] well.
*February 28 - The [[Military of the Reigess Suverände|Reigen military]] discovers [[Ikronin]] ships harvesting [[antimatter]] from the unauthorized well. The [[Ikronin]] ships are informed of the sovereignty of the [[Reigess Suverände]] within [[Njiorin's Well]], and told to withdraw. The [[Ikronin]] comply, and the [[Reigens]] give them assistance in removing their equipment without damaging it.

===March===

*March 8 - [[Argus Lax]] receives word that the [[antimatter]] well was removed. He sends word to [[Edvard Raegos]] that the [[Ikronin]] demand fair access to the natural [[antimatter]] sources.

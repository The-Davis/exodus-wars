August 5, 2322

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today, the Prefecture's First Mechanized Division began its assault on the Canaveral Spaceport in the southern Florida district. Initial casualties were high on both sides, and several civilian supply convoys were caught in the middle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle was started when an Imperial recon unit spotted Prefecture Fireflies snooping around the perimeter of the spaceport. Using the recon's target info, an Imperial Catapult blasted two Fireflies to pieces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefecture Ballista artillery Rigs quickly returned fire with their own Arrow IV missiles, and a slugging match between artillery began. For twenty minutes Arrow artillery pieces fired. Finally, the Imperials ceased fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefecture Hades Rigs and Imperial Avatar Rigs met in the field and disengaged after a massive duel. Both sides are locked in stalemate on the ground, and are beginning to send in fighters to gain air superiority.

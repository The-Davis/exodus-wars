Ceres is the smallest identified dwarf planet in the [[Sol System]] and the only one in the [[Ceres Belt]]. It was discovered on January 1, [[1801]], by Giuseppe Piazzi, and is named after the Roman goddess Ceres — the goddess of growing plants, the harvest, and of motherly love.

With a diameter of about 950 kilometers, Ceres is by far the largest and most massive body in the Ceres Belt, and contains approximately a third of the belt's total mass. It is spherical, unlike the irregular shapes of smaller bodies with lower gravity. The surface of Ceres is made of a mixture of water ice and various hydrated minerals like carbonates and clays. Ceres is differentiated into a rocky core and ice mantle. It harbor's an ocean of liquid water underneath its surface, which made it an ideal site for a main trading station for mining and cargo hauling in the Ceres Belt.

Ceres has a small, yet permanent population of traders and miners. Ceres station was the model used to develop the [[Oronus System]] colony.

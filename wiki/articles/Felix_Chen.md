'''Felix Chen'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Felix Chen'' was a member of the [[Royal Imperial Army]], and later a Corporal in the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]]. He led the sabotage of the reactor during the boarding action on the FDS ''Imposis'', and survived the mission. He was later decorated for valor, based on the commendation in the final transmission from Sergeant [[Hap Barlow]].

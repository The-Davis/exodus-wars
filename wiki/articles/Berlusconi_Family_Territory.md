=== History ===

The [[Berlusconi Family]] are the newcomers to the [[Sirius Cluster]], but are the most influential. While the [[Blasius Family]] holds more power and had firmly established significant control over [[Sirius Kuza]], [[Sirius Kirtszi]] and [[Sirius Napoli]]; the Berlusconis have spread their influence throughout the [[Sol Sector]], beyond the scope of the [[Sirius Cluster]].

=== Government ===

The head of the [[Berlusconi Family]] is "[[Il Padre]]", a title currently held by [[Alessandro Berlusconi]]. It is largely due to [[Alessandro Berlusconi|Alessandro]]'s charm and dignity that the Berlusconi are as successful as they are. [[Alessandro Berlusconi|"Papa" Alessandro]] has visited every major world in the [[Orion Arm]], and always brings true benefit to his business partners. [[Alessandro Berlusconi|Alessandro]] runs enormous accounting corporations and law firms on many planets, and offers his world as a tax shelter to most businesses who are interested. The Berlusconis have the fewest obvious criminal ties, which may simply be a sign of how powerful their true ties are.

=== Law ===

Like the rest of the [[Sirius Cluster]], the Berlusconis treat the law as more of a bother than a necessity. However, they recognize that complete and total chaos would be bad for business; not to mention a hazard to life and limb.

To prevent complete anarchy, the Berlusconis recognize and enforce the [[Law of the Sirius Cluster|Sirius Codex]], a simple set of laws to prevent blatant theft and murder from being the rule of life. Under the Codex, it is more important to avoid getting caught than it is to avoid breaking the law.

The [[Berlusconi Territorial Planetary Security Forces|Security Forces]] act as a gendarmerie among the civilian population of [[Sirius Napoli|Napoli]], with a police-oriented constabulary operating out of [[New Florence]].

=== Foreign Relations ===

The [[Berlusconi Family]] has a reputation for almost loveable roguishness and dashing behavior. While members of the upper class view [[Alessandro Berlusconi]] as an incorrigable bandit, he is something of a hearthrob in many social circles. His natural charm and genuine friendliness have won him many allies among nations which traditionally shun both the Berlusconis and the rest of the [[Sirius Cluster]].

=== Territory ===

The [[Berlusconi Family]] controls the [[Sirius Bari System]] as their base of operations and power. The world of [[Sirius Napoli]] (usually simply called "Napoli") is the only inhabitted planet under their control, and some of that control must be shared with the [[Blasius Family]] out of necessity.

On [[Sirius Napoli|Napoli]] itself, the city of [[New Florence]] is the most important economic power base and population center. It is a thriving metropolis, but built with some of the oldest materials and building methods still in use. While the [[Nakamura Family]] looks down on [[New Florence]] as "byzantine", tourists flock to see its "old world charm."

=== Economy ===

Like the other governing authorities within the [[Sirius Cluster]], the Berlusconis adopted the [[Sirio]] as their form of currency.

The economic power of the Berlusconis comes from their financial clout across the [[Orion Arm]], and their banking system being a desireable tax shelter for several international businesses. It is an "open secret" that the Berlusconis offer money laundering, tax evasion, and crooked accounting to anyone who desires to deal with them.

The Berlusconis remain too powerful for even the [[Ikronin]] banking interests to freeze their assets. It is rumored that [[Alessandro Berlusconi|Alessandro]] has a "laundry list" of blackmail that could ruin nearly every financial broker and politician within the [[Orion Arm]].

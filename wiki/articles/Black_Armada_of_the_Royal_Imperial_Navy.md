The '''Black Armada''' was the main campaigner for the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and responsible for much of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military success early in the [[Second Exodus War]]. After the [[Imperial Civil War]], much of the Black Armada became part of the rebellion, and the command was dissolved due to a lack of resources.

= Fleet Listings =

The Black Armada had two fleets, the Third Imperial Fleet and the Fourth Imperial Fleet.

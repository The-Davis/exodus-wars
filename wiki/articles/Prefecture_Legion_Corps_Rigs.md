[[:Category:Rigs|Rigs]] are an example of effective, yet imperfect thinking. The [[Prefecture Legion Corps]] is the only army in the galaxy to field these monstrosities in any significant numbers. In theory, the [[:Category:Rigs|Rig]] is a perfect weapon: a superb heavy weapons platform, a highly mobile fighter, capable of moving over broken terrain without slowing down. Unfortunately, its upright stance gives it a high target profile, and its leg actuators are a highly visible weak spot. Still, the Legions use their [[:Category:Rigs|Rigs]] to great effect.

==Training==
Like all soldiers, [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilots begin their training with six months of Basic Combat Training. Afterwards, soldiers assigned to [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] operations may attend several schools:
*'''Advanced Rig Operations School''' is the standard eight-month [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] certification course. Once completing the course, a [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilot is certified to operate a basic combat [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] under a number of combat conditions and mission profiles. All [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilots receive 15% credit towards completion of Officer Candidacy School, as many of the topics covered are identical.
*'''Urban Warfare School''' is a specialized one-month course covering the use of [[:Category:Rigs|rigs]] in urban warfare environments.
*'''Combat Drop School''' is a specialty school for unusual mission profiles which require "hot drops." Once completing the school, [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilots are tested in air drops under live-fire conditions. Completing this school is mandatory for any [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilot wishing to join Cerebus Force.
*'''Rig Officer Candidacy School''' allows soldiers to earn a commission as Officers if they complete the course. If they complete the course but do not earn a commission, they have the option of enrolling in a Military Academy, where they can earn an automatic commission upon graduation.

==Uniforms==

[[Image:PrefectureLegionCorpsRigBadge.png|45px|left]]

The Legion Corps' Rig pilots wear the [[M-02 Formal Dress Uniform|dress uniform]] with a gear insignia for their right arm, the Legion Corps' emblem on their hat, and the emblem of their specific Legion for their left arm.

Rig pilots wear the [[Prefecture Military Field Combat Uniform|Field Combat Uniform]] with full color Legion, name and rank patches. Most Rig pilots do not wear armor.

==Weapon Systems and Equipment==
Prefecture Legion Corps [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilots have several [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] types and equipment loads available to them depending on the mission profile.
*'''Light Combat Rig''' - All [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] pilots are certified to operate light combat rigs. The [[Prefecture]] has a single light combat rig chassis in use, the [[M-21 Light Combat Rig Chassis - "Hercules"|M-21 "Hercules"]]. This now-venerable design has been overhauled dozens of times since its introduction in the earliest days of the [[Onyx Empire]], and it remains an effective combat platform. [[M-21 Light Combat Rig Chassis - "Hercules"|Hercules]] operators have four direct combat weapons: two [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|light autocannons]] and two [[M-22a Light Neutron Beam Weapon - "Toaster"|light neutron beams]].
*'''Medium Combat Rig''' - The [[Prefecture]] operates the [[M-24 Medium Combat Rig Chassis - "Apollo"|M-24 "Apollo"]] in the medium combat [[:Category:Rigs|rig]] role. The [[M-24 Medium Combat Rig Chassis - "Apollo"|Apollo]] is one of the more modern [[:Category:Rigs|rigs]] in the Legion Corps' arsenal, though the specification dates back to the [[Onyx Empire]]. [[M-24 Medium Combat Rig Chassis - "Apollo"|Apollo]] operators have a total of four weapon mounts at their disposal. The turret mount can accept any two [[M-28 Medium Autocannon - "Thumper"|medium autocannons]], [[M-28a Medium Neutron Beam Weapon - "Scorcher"|medium neutron beams]], or [[M-13 Field Gun|field guns]]. Each arm mount can take any one [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|light autocannon]], [[M-18 Medium Anti-Armor Weapon|MAAW]] (with two shots each), or [[M-09 Light Anti-Armor Weapon|LAAW]] (with four shots each).

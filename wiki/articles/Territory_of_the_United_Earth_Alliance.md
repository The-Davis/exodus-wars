The [[United Earth Alliance]] held sovereign territory over only six [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]], but was the military protector of several non-member "protectorate" worlds.

=== Member Worlds ===

* [[Sol System]] - [[Earth]] (capital), [[Mars]], [[Venus]], [[Luna]].
* [[Alpha Centauri System]] - [[Ah Kin]].
* [[Lyrae System]] - [[Holt]].
* [[Tau Ceti System]] - [[Kalmar]].
* [[Proxima Procyon System]] - [[Bethija]].
* [[Theta Algedi System]] - [[Tejat]].
* [[Titan System]] - [[Theia]], [[Rhea]], [[Themis]].

=== Protectorates ===

* [[Lupei System]] - [[Kalidasa]].

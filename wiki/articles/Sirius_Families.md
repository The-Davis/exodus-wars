The term '''Sirius Families''' refers to the loosely affiliated hereditary [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]], [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]], [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova]] and [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius]] families. The families have sovereign control of the region of space known as the [[Sirius Cluster]].

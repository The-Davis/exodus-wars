The Berkeley Class Reactor was introduced in early 2324 to replace the devastated power grids of Terra.  Most cheap power sources of the 24th century were nuclear fission reactors, which--while cheap--had waste materials that were difficult and sometimes dangerous to dispose of.

The Prefecture Navy had the most experience with nuclear power.  Their ships and interstellar travel systems require incredible amounts of power.  They built the Berkeley to be cheap and reliable, and did well.

The Berkeley is built around four fusion cores.  The array is cooled by a liquid coolant circuit that runs from the cores down to a refrigeration unit two miles beneath the surface.

All four cores are shielded by thick sheets of Boron-Hydrogen composite weaves, similar to starship radiation shielding.  The heat output outside the shielding is still enough to make a person uncomfortable, but there is no radiation outside of the cores.  The excess heat is converted directly into electrical power by thermal transducers.

[[Image:BerkeleyClassReactorComplex.jpg]]

The power output is incredible.  One Berkeley is enough to work as the entire grid for a metropolis.

Maintenance isn't required often, but it is very difficult when it is.  Each core must be shut down in order to work on it.  If the coolant system needs repairs, the entire reactor must be shut down.  Starting the reactor up again takes a forty hours.

After the Navy developed the Berkeley, it sold the designs to everyone with the means to build a working reactor.  The reactors were in widespread-use within a month.  Over a hundred were donated to the Terra restoration project by various private corporations, twenty by Rahn Industries and 25 by AstroLift.  The Prefecture military deploys a Berkeley at every base it uses.

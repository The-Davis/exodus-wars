The UEAS ''Aleksey Tarasov'' was a [[Kirov Class Dreadnaught]] that served in the [[First Exodus War]]. It was commanded by Captain-Commander Zheng Da, and later by Captain-Commander  James Harrison.

The ''Aleksey Tarasov'' was noted in history as the ship that killed political activist and colonial authority [[Jerome Black]], during the [[Bethija]]n annexation campaign of the [[First Exodus War]].

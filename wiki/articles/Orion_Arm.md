The '''Orion Arm''' is the most thoroughly mapped Galactic Arm in the [[Milky Way]], due to it being inhabited by multiple aggressive-expansionist human societies. The bulk of charted space has been explored by the [[Meroniri]] at one time or another, and now the less-advanced human races, particularly the [[Terrans]], are making their presence felt.

Despite the expansionism of all human races, only a few thousand stars and points of interest have been charted and explored. Millions of stars remain unexplored within the Orion Arm.

The Orion Arm is connected to the Galactic Core, and extends outwards for a distance of 30,000LY. The Orion Arm averages 3000LY in width. The coreward edge of the Arm is 2450LY rimward from the Core. The Orion arm is located between the [[Sagittarius Arm]] and [[Perseus Arm]].

==Teeth of Orion==

The [[Teeth of Orion]] is the gateway to the [[Galactic Core]]. The teeth are a series of nearly 14,000 Class O supergiant stars, making navigation into the [[Galactic Core|Core]] a dangerous prospect. The stars are frequently binary and trinary systems, and rarely more than a few hundred light years apart. Their pull on hyperspace makes travel in the lower bands tricky at best, and travel in the higher, faster bands an impossibility.

==Lepodi Sector==

The [[Lepodi Sector]] is considered the "borderlands" of [[Meroniri]] space, and is situated just counter-turn of the [[Teeth of Orion]]. The only inhabited world in the [[Lepodi Sector]] is [[Shia Drasin]], which is so far counter-turn that it's nearly in the next sector. For the most part, the [[Lepodi Sector]] is full of unmanned monitoring stations, automated mining outposts, and exotic matter collectors.

==Unai Sector==

The [[Unai Sector]] is the heart of [[Meroniri]] territory, counter-turn of the [[Lepodi Sector]]. [[Sepulare]], the capital of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], is in the center of this sector. The worlds Minhara, Hamele, Thibana, Caelire, Shaula, Oture, Weshura, and Alderami are found in this sector.

==Masera Sector==

The [[Masera Sector]] is the "gateway" to [[Meroniri]] space, bordering the "wild space" between the Meroniri territories and the homes of the younger races. The worlds Acruso, Meripe, Saete, Herae, Apodisa, Mirfare, Tuseni, and Casoru are located in this sector.

==Pleiades==

The Pleiades is a famous star cluster, with its place in astronomy and myth in nearly every human race. Its incredibly bright stars are full of exotic matter particles, and are currently the claimed territory of the Meroniri, who have massive antimatter wells near each star.

==Njiorin Sector==

The [[Njiorin Sector]] is named for a group of 30 stars known to [[Terrans]] as the Ptolemy Cluster and known to the [[Reigens]] as "Njiorin's Well". The [[Njiorin Sector]] is the furthest turnwise of the younger races, and is home to [[Ikronin]], [[Reigen]], [[Lorithos]], and [[Terran]] territories. The [[Yngard System]] and [[Myrgard System]] are the furthest turnwise, while [[Raegard System]] and [[Mirdek System]] are further counter-turn. The [[Orhak System]], and [[Hamur System]] are located in the rough center of the sector. Njiorin's Well is claimed by the [[Reigess Suverände]], and is a low intensity source of antimatter and exotic particles, but large enough to show a substantial yield.

==Sol Sector==

The [[Sol Sector]] is the heart of the [[Terran]] race's territory. It has been the focus of nearly all recent military and economic history. [[Terra]], [[Mars]] and [[Theia]] are the most populated worlds in the sector, followed by [[Ah Kin]]. The [[Sol System]], [[Alpha Centauri System]], [[Tau Ceti System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], [[Lyrae System]], [[Titan System]], [[Oronus System]] and [[Lupei System]] are also found here, as are the remains of the [[Epsilon Indi System]], where an experimental weapon used during the [[Second Exodus War]] destroyed most of the star itself. The [[Sirius Cluster]] and [[Algedi Cluster]] are also located within the [[Sol Sector]].

==De Mairan Sector==

The [[De Mairan Sector]] is coreward from the [[Sol Sector]], and is where the bulk of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] worlds are located. It is named for [[De Mairan's Nebula]], one of the least useful charted nebulae. The [[Prefecture]] has a few [[antimatter]] wells in the nebula, but they have only yielded enough [[antimatter]] for experimentation, not a stable power source. The [[Hydrae System]], [[Antares System]], [[Beta Arae System]], [[Xi Herculis System]], [[Avior System]], [[Mu Crucis System]], and [[Des System]] can be found in this sector.

==Pelagrim Sector==

The [[Pelagrim Sector]] is named for a cluster of white dwarf stars, known as "[[Pelagrim vi Cenak|Pelagrim's Fangs]]" to the [[Anderung]], and known as the "[[Pelagrim vi Cenak|Praesape Cluster]]" in [[Terran]] astronomy.

The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture| Prefecture]]'s [[Tavou District]] is located here, as are the [[Mesarthrim]] territories. The [[Omicron Carinae System]], [[Mimosa System]], [[Tirshan System]], [[Honis System]], [[Cirus System]], [[Cinaed System]], and [[Centus System]] are located in this sector, as are the remains of the [[Mirach System]].

==Tolana Sector==

The [[Tolana Sector]] is the furthest counter-turn charted sector of the Orion Arm. It is the home of the [[Ixoarchada]]. The inhabited [[Bollaria System]], [[Bregdan System]], [[Cyprana System]], [[Giraxis System]], and [[Ularen System]] can be found here, as well as the [[Bataei System]], [[Chorvat System]], [[Otaruna System]], and [[Plasala System]]. In addition, the [[Tolana Nebula]]--known as the Owl Nebula to the [[Terrans]]--is found here, and is a rich source of antimatter for the [[Ixoarchada]].

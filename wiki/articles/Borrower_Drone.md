===Overview===

The Borrower is an unusual creature.  It isn't a direct threat, by itself or when its hitched a ride on a soldier of some sort or another.  It's hard-wired to run and hide, avoiding conflict.  It is the eyes, ears, and will of the Nobles.

[[Image:BorrowerDrone1.jpg]]

Borrowers have the keenest sense of smell of any Volucris, and are the best at picking out individual scents and pheromones.  With these abilities, they act as the long-range battle communication system of the Volucris swarms, something they sorely lack in space battles.  Only with air to carry scent can they act like a well-oiled machine instead of a mindless mass of bodies.

Understanding scents is not the only ability of a Borrower.  They can secrete odorous mucous, which is exceedingly pungent, and carries for miles.  Each cell of the substance carries all of the information the Borrower wishes to relay, so it can be stored and moved, even by the stupid soldiers.

When a Borrower latches onto a host, it can do one of several things.  On a simple-minded soldier, it can take absolute control.  On a more intelligent host, such as a human, it cannot take control.  The nervous systems of Volucris and humanoids are quite incompatible when it comes to motor skills.  Raw information can be read though--a recording of neurons firing, coupled with specific electrical stimulation, allows the Borrower to record thoughts.  It cannot comprehend them, but it can relay all information to a Noble in command.

[[Image:BorrowerDrone2.jpg]]

Borrowers are everywhere, hiding in the shadows and relaying the commands of the Nobles.  They are possibly the most lethal of Volucris, as they direct and control the swarms at the will of the insidious minds behind it.

== History ==

The first of the [[Sirius Cluster]] settlers was the Nakamura Family of [[Kuza]] in the [[Sirius Aichi System]]. The Nakamura were a wealthy and powerful [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] merchant family on [[Earth]], and were able to fund their own expeditions in the later [[21st Century]]. The head of the Nakamura family is the "Oomi," a position currently held by [[Takashi Nakamura]]. [[Takashi Nakamura|Takashi]]'s legitimate business interests tend to be in the fields of pharmaceuticals and medical supplies, and his less-than-legitimate business interests tend to run along the same lines. [[Takashi Nakamura|Takashi]]'s people smuggle narcotics and other controlled substances into every known world.

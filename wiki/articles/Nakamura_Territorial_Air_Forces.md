In the [[Sirius Cluster]], many things are far from uniform. For transport, dispatch, and shuttle-service, a wide variety of transport and shuttle models are used by all factions. However, each Territory does maintain is own aerospace force for mission-specific operations and general security purposes.

The aerospace forces of the various factions are comprised of quite different craft, but their roles are very similar--keep a tight grip on things in the air, and maintain security over territorial space.

The Nakamura Territory's choices are rather slim in the aerospace field. They sport only one space-capable fighter design, the Shogun strike fighter. On the ground they also have the Naginata, a strike VTOL for troop support.

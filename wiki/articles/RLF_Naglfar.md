Career
*Laid down: March 6th, 2310
*Launched: August 15th, 2312
*Commissioned: RLF Naglfar on November 8th, 2312
*Status: Personal Flagship of the Reigen Crown Prince


When the crown prince of the [[Reigens]] goes to war, his people outfit him with truly special gear. His armor and weapons are unmatched, but his warship is something truly special.

The ship was built in the years leading up to the [[Second Exodus War]], before the [[Reigens]] had made contact with anyone but their Icronian relatives and the rest of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. The ship is built with components handed down through the [[Ikronin]] royal family from the days of the [[Anthronoris]].

The result is the ''Naglfar'', the "ship of the dead" of [[Ikronin]] and Reigen legends. While most of the ship is of [[Reigen]] design, some parts of it were stripped from the hull of a sleeper ship used in their long journey from [[Kronheim]], and the power core is an [[Anthronoris]] antimatter reaction chamber.

The reaction chamber is a relic from the dawn of [[Ikronin]] civilization. It is only marginally better than a standard [[Reigen]] reaction chamber, with a rated 65 gigawatt output, but holds a tradition of service for the [[Reigens]] and the [[Ikronin]] before them.

While the ''Naglfar'' is only marginally better than any other [[Reigen]] heavy cruiser, its the crew is hand-picked, and the ship's computer core is Njorl, an AI grown from the matrix of Prism, given to the [[Reigens]] by the [[Prefecture]] as a gift after they established trade ties and a healthy antimatter market.

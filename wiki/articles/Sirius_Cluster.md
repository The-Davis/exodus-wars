The '''Sirius Cluster''' is a small group of stars within 2LY of each other. There are multiple inhabited planets, which make up the [[Sirius Families]]' holdings.

While it is not technically a sector, the Sirius Cluster is categorized as a "sub-sector" of the [[Sol Sector]].

== Star Systems ==

By the terms of the [[Titan Peace Accords]], the Sirius Cluster in its entirety is recognized as the sovereign domain of the [[Sirius Families]].  The [[Sirius Bari System]], [[Sirius Novgorod System]], and the [[Sirius Aichi System]] are currently inhabited. The families rule the star systems as their own principalities.

There are a total of seven stars in the Sirius Cluster. The remaining four are Sirius B, Sirius E, Sirius F, and Sirius G, none of which are inhabited or useful to the Sirius Families, but are nonetheless claimed as part of the Administration's domain.

===Overview===

It's more than 250 meters long, smarter than the legendary five AIs, and frightfully powerful.  This is the Leviathan, warship of the Volucris Swarm.

[[Image:LeviathinQueen1.jpg]]

The Leviathan is a queen, of sorts.  It produces Shredder Symbiotes like the ones used by the Black Claw.  It also has four huge two plasma-producing glands, five times as powerful as the Blister.  It can slice unshielded warships in half, and bash shields down with ease.

Leviathans also have huge claws.  At close range, it can shred open a warship hull like a tin can.  Close range is where a Leviathan is its deadliest.  Strong as the plasma and Shredder Symbiotes are, they are nothing compared to its uncontested close range combat.  No opposing warship has weapons to counter its claws.

Compared to other Volucris space breeds, the Leviathan is slow.  Compared to any warship built by humans, it's blindingly fast.  Leviathans can close to short range with stunning ease.  Commanders are advised to run, and use high-yield focused weapons at extreme range.

[[Image:LeviathinQueen2.jpg]]

The shell of a Leviathan is harder than Boron-Nitride composite armor.  It's nearly as tough as Proton-Bond armor used by the more advanced humanoid races in the galaxy.  It is highly resistant to radiation, shrapnel, kinetic weapons, and heat.  Past experience leads tacticians to believe that electricity-based weapons will disrupt its communication with the rest of the Swarm, but Leviathans have shown an uncanny independent intelligence.  Even alone, they are very dangerous.  Still, electrifying them does kill the guidance of their Shredder Symbiotes.

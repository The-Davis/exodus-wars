'''Austria''' was a landlocked country in Central Europe. It bordered [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] and [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]] to the north and east, [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]] to the east, the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] and [[Italy (Terra)|Italy]] to the south, and [[Switzerland (Terra)|Switzerland]] and [[Liechtenstein (Terra)| Liechtenstein]] to the west. The capital city was Vienna.

The origins of modern Austria date back to the [[9th Century]], when the countryside of upper and lower Austria became increasingly populated. The name "Ostarrichi" is first documented in an official document from [[996]]AD.

Austria was one of the few countries that includes the concept of everlasting neutrality in their constitution. Austria's insistance on neutrality kept it from joining the [[United Earth Alliance]] until the rest of the world was united.

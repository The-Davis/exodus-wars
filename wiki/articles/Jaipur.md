'''Jaipur''' is the fourth planet in the [[Antares System]].

== History ==

Jaipur was colonized in [[2060]] along with its neighbor, [[Albanus]]. The city of [[Denestin]] was established, but abandoned fifty years later due to the planet having little value.

In [[2319]], the [[Onyx Empire]] captured the [[Antares System]], and re-established the Denestin as a training city for its military.

Jaipur is a very flat world. The highest point is only 150 meters above sea level. The only "sea" is a rather large freshwater lake. When discovered, Jaipur had nothing but lakes and grass. The native grass quickly gave way to the Terran varieties, so it was very easy to grow crops. Jaipur became a farming world.

The great plains of Jaipur made it easy to construct artillery firing ranges. An academy was built, and the Onyx Empire trained 90% of their artillery personnel there. Today, the [[Prefecture]] controls the planet.  The Prefecture Marine Corps are now headquartered on Jaipur.

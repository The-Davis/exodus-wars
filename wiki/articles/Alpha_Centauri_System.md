The '''Alpha Centauri System''' is a trinary star system with six planets, moons, and  millions of asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Alpha Centauri, the planets are [[Ac Yanto]], [[Hulneb]], [[Kumix]], [[Ah Kin]], [[Tabai]], and [[Ahau-Kin]]. Three of the six planets are orbited by moons. All the planets are named after gods and goddesses from Mayan mythology.

==Stellar Navigations==

Alpha Centauri A is the Alpha Centauri System's primary star. Alpha Centauri A is a class G2V yellow dwarf. Alpha Centauri A's heliosphere occasionally combines with Alpha Centauri B's heliosphere to create powerful stellar storms which are serious navigation hazards, but usually space near terrestrial planet orbit is calm with only a particle windspeed of roughly 900,000 kilometers per hour.

Alpha Centauri B orbits Alpha Centauri A at a distance of 35.6AU. Alpha Centauri B is a class K1V orange dwarf. Alpha Centauri B represents the [[hyperspace limit]] of the Alpha Centauri system. Ships cannot jump into higher [[hyperspace]] bands within Alpha Centauri B's orbit, and are limited to the [[Adin Band]].

Proxima Centauri orbits Alpha Centauri A at a distance of 13,000AU. Proxima Centauri is a class M5Ve red dwarf, which is too cold to interact with the planetary systems in Alpha Centauri. Proxima Centauri does present a hyperspace navigation hazard, and in order to jump into the planetary system itself navigators must bypass Proxima Centauri's sphere of influence.

The Alpha Centauri System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Alpha Centauri was the first extra-Solar colony in [[Terrans| Terran]] civilization. The [[Sol System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], and [[Tau Ceti System]] are considered close neighbors of Alpha Centauri.

===Ac Yanto=== 
[[Ac Yanto]] (0.1AU) is the closest planet to Alpha Centauri. It is a tiny sphere, blasted almost completely clear by solar winds. The planet has little to offer, and has never been fully charted or explored.

===Hulneb===
[[Hulneb]] (0.5AU) is close in size to [[Mars]]. It has a thick gas atmosphere that obscures most of the surface. No landing parties have been sent, but satellite fly-bys show nothing of value that cannot be obtained more easily elsewhere.

===Kumix===
[[Kumix]] (0.9AU) is slightly larger than [[Terra]], but has a very thin carbon-dioxide atmosphere and is not currently inhabitable. It may eventually be a target for terraformation. It has one moon, [[Chamahez]].

===Ah Kin===
[[Ah Kin]] (1.25 AU) is slightly smaller than [[Terra]], but is inhabitable and supports a rather comfortable biosphere. It has significant geothermal activity which warms it from inside. The core is believed to be much hotter and more radioactive than Terra's, a theory which is supported by the high concentrations of rich radioactive fuel mined from Ah Kin's crust. Ah Kin has two moons, [[Ah Uuc]] and [[Ah Mun]].

===The Kuha Belt===

The [[Kuha Belt]] (3.2AU) holds most of the asteroids in the Alpha Centauri System, and is mostly made up of small asteroid composed largely of radioactive and metallic minerals. The main asteroid belt occupies the orbit between [[Ah Kin]] and [[Tabai]], between 3 and 4AU from Alpha Centauri A. [[Terrans]] use the belt asteroids to harvest resources valuable for creating composite materials. The belt contains millions of objects over a kilometer in diameter, and are an effectively limitless source of resources.

===Tabai===
[[Tabai]] (7.4 AU) masses 5 times as much as all other planets put together. It is composed largely of hydrogen with many other gas elements and trace particles. [[Tabai]] is many times larger than [[Jupiter]] and has nearly two hundred moons. [[Tabai]] is noted for its magnificent color bands. It is mostly light gray to the naked eye, but has bands of green, blue, and purple.

=== Ahau-Kin ===

[[Ahau-Kin]] (9.2 AU) is unusual among gas giants found throughout the galaxy. It has no moons. [[Ahau-Kin]] is the site of the famous [[Haven Shipyards]], where much of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s war machine was built between [[2300]] and [[2323]]. Ahau-Kin occasionally heats to near plasma temperatures when Alpha Centauri B reaches the close portion of its orbit.

==History==

Whether or not Alpha Centauri was discovered or colonized during the era of the [[Anthronoris]] is completely unknown. Alpha Centauri was discovered by the [[Terrans]] early in their history, and they soon realized it could potentially have a world capable of supporting life.

On December 26, [[2020]], the first [[Terran]] interstellar flight--the [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']]--arrived in the Alpha Centauri System. The first colony was established on March 18, [[2027]].

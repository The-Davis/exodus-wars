The '''Omicron Carinae System''' consists of the star Omicron Carinae, five planets, their moons, and miscellaneous asteroids and debris. 

In order of their distances from Omicron Carinae, the planets are Omicron Carinae I, Omicron Carinae II, [[Adelson]], [[Kufestin]], and Omicron Carinae V.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Omicron Carinae is the Omicron Carinae System's only star. Omicron Carinae is a class G2J yellow dwarf. The Omicron Carinae System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Omicron Carinae is part of the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and is a close neighbor of the [[Mimosa System]].

=== Adelson ===

[[Adelson]] (1.2AU) is the first inhabitable terrestrial planet to be colonized in the Adelson System. It has one moon, known as "Adelson II."

=== Kufestin ===
[[Kufestin]] (1.9AU) is the second inhabitable terrestrial planet to be colonized in the Adelson System. It one moons, known as Shestin.

== History ==

The Omicron Carinae System was discovered by the [[Terrans]] in [[2160]]. [[Adelson]] was colonized first, in [[2160]]. [[Kufestin]] was colonized in [[2165]].

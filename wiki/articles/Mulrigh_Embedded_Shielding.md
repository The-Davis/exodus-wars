'''Mulrigh Embedded Shielding''' is a defensive technology only made possible with the advent of [[Falkran Armor]].  With its invention, Falkran made feasible the ability to actually project a shield within armor itself.  Because Falkran Armor is super-conducting, it can support a very powerful internal field.  The shield functions to both drastically increase the strength of Falkran's inter-nuclear bonds and to also increase the reflectivity of the surface.  Because the field exists inside the armor, generators do not need to project into free space, and can therefore be much more powerful, efficient and compact.  This also allows the shielding system to be highly decentralized, causing it to be massively redundant, and completely omni-directional.

The near absolute reflectivity induced by Embedded Shielding causes Falkran armor to become impenetrable to most EM weapons, and lasers of all wavelengths.  It also greatly reduces its vulnerability to electromagnetic pulse weapons, as the drastic increase in free surface energy allows it to expel even the most powerful magnetic fields, protecting the vehicle or vessel inside.

Because the nucleic bonds are significantly stronger when reinforced with Mulrigh, low velocity projectile weapons are easily deflected, and high-velocity particle weapons have much lower chances of penetrating armor.  Unfortunately, the impacts still take a significant toll by converting energy in the shield into large quantities of heat, which are actively dispersed by the armor's internal features, as well as active coolant systems.

Perhaps the greatest feature of the Falkran/Mulrigh combination is that a powered system has a greatly increased damage threshold, a fixed amount of damage from every impact that is fully deflected, than unpowered Falkran.  Higher Mulrigh field strength translates into a higher damage threshold.  Unfortunately, as the Embedded Shielding is diminished by prolonged bombardment without substantial recharging, the damage threshold drops accordingly. 

The end result of Falkran with Embedded Shielding is that instead of armor acting only as a backup to shielding, giving a net protective strength merely equal to the sum of the two systems, Falkran and Mulrigh operate with great synergy, so that the product is more than merely the sum of the parts.

''Simon Frazier'' (born April 15, [[2265]]) was a starship commander in the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]]. He was assigned command of the 3rd Space Battle Group in [[2311]], and commanded the [[UEAS Gryphon|UEAS ''Gryphon'']] as his flagship.

In [[2315]], during the [[Onyx Empire]]'s invasion of the [[Sol System]], Commodore-Commander Frazier led the defenses in space. He attempted to intercept the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in the outer planets, and again in the [[Ceres Belt]]. The second attempt was a total disaster, resulting in Frazier's death.

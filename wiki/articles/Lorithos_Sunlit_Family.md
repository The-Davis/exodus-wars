A '''Sunlit Family''' is a [[Lorithos]] social term, referring to a male-dominated family.

A typical Sunlit Family is headed by a male Lorithos, who has two wives. By law, only a man born during daylight hours can be the head of a Sunlit Family, and only women born during daylight hours can be married into a Sunlit Family. Men and women born at night must be part of a [[Lorithos Moonlit Family|Moonlit Family]].

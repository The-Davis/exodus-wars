'''Venezuela''' was a country on the northern coast of South America. Venezuela was a former Spanish colony, known widely for its oil industry and its importance in the non-Atomic nations before the rise of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

Venezuelans were eager to reap the benefits of the vast technology introduced by the unified world government, and had their last President sign the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on May 11, [[2014]].

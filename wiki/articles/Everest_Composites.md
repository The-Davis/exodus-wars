*'''Headquarters''': Headquarters in Albanus
*'''Industry''': Composites

'''Everest Composites''' was founded by [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Chemical Laboratory in [[2300]] as a front for producing its war materials for the [[Onyx Empire]].

After the war, Everest Composites' resources were seized by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], and transported to [[Albanus]]. The company was purchased from the Prefecture by [[Rahn Industries]], and it remains a subsidiary of the company.

Everest Composites continues to design and build composite materials. It is involved in construction and in producing military armor. It remains the main supplier of aircraft airframes for [[Rahn Industries]].

== Events of 17936BC ==

The people of [[Sepulare System|Sepulare]] and the other worlds were led at first by the [[Tomesa]], noble houses holding all of the warships and resources necessary for interstellar travel and trade. It was these nobles whose courage held the people of Sepulare and the other 8 worlds together while the rest of [[Pax Anthronoris]] came crashing down.

The problem came when one of the last [[Ngrligru]] raiding parties discovered [[Sepulare System|Sepulare]]. The [[Tomesa]] led warships off to battle, and most of them did not return, though the [[Ngrligru]] raiders were completely destroyed. One of the surviving [[Tomesa]], [[Meronir Tronit]], decided to reorganize the [[Anthronoris]] into a parliamentary republic. In his honor, the nine worlds renamed themselves the [[Meroniri Terinasi]].

The [[Ngrligru]] presence in the [[Orion Arm]] was completely wiped out by this final battle with the people of [[Sepulare System|Sepulare]].

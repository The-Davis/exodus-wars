Originally, the '''gold standard''' was a monetary system in which the standard economic unit of account was a fixed weight of gold. Governments issued "guarantee notes" which would allow the bearer to take that amount of gold at their demand.

The new gold standard was established by the [[Prefecture]]. Instead of printing paper money (which has no inherent value), they issued coins that actually bore a set weight in gold. The 100 [[Peculium]] coin was set at a value of 1 gram of gold, and the remaning denominations were based off of that weight.

The gold standard is not favored by other economies. Most other [[Terran]] [[:Category:Nations]] use [[fiat currency]], while the [[Ikronin]] use a combination of [[fiat currency]] and their own "platinum standard" to back the [[Likvada]]

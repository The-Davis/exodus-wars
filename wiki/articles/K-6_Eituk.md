The Eituk is a heavy strategic bomber, designed mostly for high-altitude bombardment of ground targets.  It is capable of close air support, but is better suited for high and fast operations.

== Development ==

Strangely enough, the Eituk was created to counter the fast and deadly Vadrie during the Burning Wars. Torm Krelbine knew he could not build a faster fighter than the Vadrie, so instead went the other direction and created one of the largest human bombers built to date. Instead of dog-fighting with the Vadries, Eituks were meant to rain countless missiles on them from afar, and then plow through screens of the small craft to strike important targets, such as warships. In this role, they were fairly successful.

== Capabilities ==

The extent to which the Eituk is armed is impressive by the standards of most races. Two massive Heavy Bukrah cannon turrets, nearly the same models as found on the [[Brehvok Main Battle Tank]], form the primary cannon armament, capable of reducing small fighters (and many ground units) to rubble in seconds. Each turret also has a pair of Light Bukrah cannons mounted, to add lighter, faster-firing weapons to a single turret's salvo. The Eituk sports two Medium Bukrah cannon twin-turret. One is mounted above the cockpit for accurately targeting enemy craft at long ranges. The other, controlled by a gunner and engineer in the rear of the bomber, discourages pursuit by enemy fighters. Rounding off the cannon armament are two twin fixed batteries of Light Bukrah cannons, mounted forward, to aid in straffing or anti-aerospace fire.

The cannon armament of the Eituk is impressive by any standards, but more impressive still is the massive ordinance payload carried by a single Eituk. Sixteen Light Universal Hardpoints allow a lone Eituk to unleash torrential amounts of anti-aerospace or air-to-ground missiles designed to strike single targets. Two Heavy Universal Hardpoints usually carry the large Lorithos anti-warship torpedoes, but can be swapped-out for a variety of other heavy ordinance missiles. Lastly, eight Bombardment Ordinance Hardpoints are mounted in the rear of the craft. Designed to level planetary fortifications or troop formations, these eight hardpoints mount downward-facing heavy guided munitions, equipped with a variety of area-effect warheads. A full salvo from these hardpoints can demolish almost any conceivable target.

Controlled by a crew of four, the Eituk is slow and lumbering by most any standards, but its heavy layer of Tzaiko Composite armor and powerful Garvask shield generator keep it extremely well protected. It receives its power from a pair of massive Hydrogen Engines.

== Deployment ==

Following first contact with the Alliance of Planets, the Lorithos resumed production of the Eituk as a long-range patrol/gunship and a heavy bomber. Today, instead of combating Vadries, they are escorted by the small interceptors. Their heavy firepower is unmatched in the ASTF's fighter command.

Most Lorithos Oltvak carriers and other heavy warships carry at least one squadron of Eituks, and thousands are housed in the Hightowers.

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s first fighter squadron, VF-1, commonly known as "Scimitar Squadron", was the premiere space and air combat group of the [[Onyx Empire]], and accounted for more confirmed air-to-air kills during the [[Second Exodus War]] than any four [[Royal Imperial Navy]] fighter squadrons combined.

Scimitar Squadron was the single most effective air combat unit in the entire [[Onyx Empire]], though their closest [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] rivals, [[Marcus Rathbone]]'s [[FS-01 Imperial Air Force Fighter Squadron - "The Wolfhounds"|First Fighter Squadron]] frequently but briefly surpassed their kill record prior to the outbreak of the [[Imperial Civil War]].

=== History ===

In its relatively short 20-year-history, Scimitar Squadron developed one of the most fearsome and well-deserved reputations for efficient and spectacular air combat in known space. It always received the best training and equipment possible, and was transfered to multiple bases, carriers, and stations to take the most advantage of its pilots skills in the hot-spots of the [[Second Exodus War]].

When the [[Onyx Empire]] was created by the proclamation of [[Lucius Black]] in [[2300]], the four branches of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]] were formed. The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s Air Arm was placed under the command of the capable [[Emil Torsc]], though it was well known that he wanted command of the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]].

==== Early Testing ====

In the earliest days of its existence, the Air Arm of the [[Royal Imperial Navy|Navy]] only existed on paper. [[Emil Torsc]] and [[Matthias Carter]] were the first and only pilots in its ranks, and it had no [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]].

Both pilots were "Raven's Wings" test pilots, assigned to evaluate the value of [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] developed by "Project Raven" in space combat. While both [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] and [[Matthias Carter|Carter]] agreed that the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4]] could fight in space, [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] had serious doubts about its viability in long engagements over hostile territory. The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4]] could not land on a [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] quickly enough under combat conditions, and took up too much storage space.

[[Royal Imperial Labs]] developed multiple "X-Craft" between the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4]]'s launch in [[2302]] and the outbreak of the [[Second Exodus War]] in [[2315]]. They finally began developing [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]'s proposed [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|carrier fighter]] with the XF-9 project.

==== Training ====

During the last desperate military build-up leading into the invasion of [[Earth]] in [[2315]], VF-1 was officially formed as the first squadron of the Air Arm of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. All of its pilots were master instructors for the very young cadets who would be thrust into combat in a few short years.

==Sheta Unari==

The '''Sheta Unari''' has been established for nearly thirty thousand years. It has become so complicated that nobody knows the extent of the law anymore. Vast computer systems and teams of legal experts look through the pages of the Sheta Unari every day to find out what applies to different situations.

The laws are archaic in many places, including the requirement of outrageous punishments for seemingly minor infractions. For example, an old law requires all power sources and forms of communications to have zero emissions visible from space, under penalty of the destruction of all property and permanent exile. It is believed that the law dates back to the days of the [[Sundering]], when the [[Ngrligru]] were hunting for the remnants of the [[Anthronoris Empire]].

Additionally, artificial scents are outlawed in public, for no apparent reason.  No one is sure of the reason behind this law, but it has never been repealed and is never enforced.

The danger of the Sheta Unari is the fact that there is no such thing as case law in the [[Meroniri]] legal system. There is no judicial right to throw out laws, nor is there a constitution which the laws must adhere to. Most are not enforced due to apathy of the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] leadership. There is no dedicated law enforcement system to seek out hidden crimes that are not reported by civilian complaints. Without the protections of a constitution or judicial oversight, an overzealous [[Lothenar]] could rule with an iron fist.

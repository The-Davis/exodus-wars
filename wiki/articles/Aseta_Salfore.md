== Structure ==

Officially, a member of the [[Meroniri]] armed forces is an Asetari. Each Aseta is commanded by the Asetarun, a hereditary position handed down over the millennia.

A Salfore is a vicious, stinging creature native to [[Sepulare]]. It is known for its lightning speed and deadly poison.

Aseta Salfore is outfitted with a single fleet of the line, and is armed for expeditionary operations and for first strike action.

== Organization ==

Aseta Salfore is a battle group-based command force. It is the smallest Aseta, and only has a single fleet. Salfore has ten war cruisers and twenty escort cruisers. 

A battle group is a team of a war cruiser and two escort cruisers. These are deployed on regular patrols throughout the [[Orion Arm]]. They have enough firepower to take on an [[Ixoarchada]] force equal to their size, and a [[Terran]] force ten times their size.

An air group is a station, warship, or regional defense organization level for combat and transport aircraft.  Aseta Salfore is not assigned to any planetary defenses or space stations, so their air groups are assigned to their warships.  Escort cruisers have a single air group, usually fighters and interceptors.  War cruisers have six air groups, with enough landing craft to launch their extensive troop complement to the surface of a planet. 

An infantry command is assigned to a war cruiser. Salfore infantry commands are equipped for mobile and fast attack tactics, and for operating with minimal support in foreign territory. Salfore infantry is equipped with the Araros combat system, a combat suit capable of limited flight, and able to be equipped with a wide variety of weaponry and defenses.

== History ==

Aseta Salfore was founded relatively recently in the history of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], some time circa [[8000BC]]. It is the youngest of the Asetas in the Yatisu, and is considered something of a maverick in Meroniri military society. Because of their unorthodox methods, they are tasked with any sovereignty patrols and official actions outside of Meroniri territory.

The current Asetarun is [[Rosh Arufin]], who is widely respected outside of Meroniri society. He has a reputation as a diplomat and a peacemaker, and has even been welcomed aboard [[Ixoarchada]] ships as an envoy...though under heavy escort.

== Uniforms ==

All Yatisu have a single uniform, but wear different colors and emblems to show their school of combat.  The Yatisu Combat Uniform is a technological wonder, equipped with portable shield generators, pressure regulators, and environmental protections far beyond anything lesser civilizations have been able to produce.

The Combat Uniform starts with a skin-tight black undershirt and black pants. The material is a natural disinfectant, which destroys odor-causing bacteria. It also neutralizes body oils and is designed to channel sweat into small reservoirs, where it is purified and injected back into the body.

The next layer is a thin kinetic armor, capable of deflecting most bladed weapons and small arms weapons. The armor, while incredibly tough, is almost weightless.  A soft power-cell is installed within the armor, nestled in the small of the back, where it both powers the uniform’s functions and cushions the lumbar region.  The power cell is hooked up to a light field generator, which can deflect heavier weapons like infantry-level Blazers, rifles, and light explosives. It is also capable of containing an environment for vacuum operations, though the air supply only lasts for minutes at a time. EV suits are preferred, the field is only for emergency decompression.

A long-sleeved uniform shirt goes over the armor, power source and field generator. The shirt is a simple fabric, with only one color. Aseta Salfore wears white uniform shirts, which have a rank patch on the left breast and a school patch on the right. A shoulder rig is worn over the uniform shirt, where a communicator and Arcer energy pistol is stowed.

Loose-fitting cargo-style trousers are worn over the legs.  By default, the pants are the primary color of the school (white in the case of the Salfore), but can activate a 500-color digital camouflage pattern, which changes based on input from optical sensors strategically located throughout the garment.  The pockets usually store extra energy cells for the Arcer pistol and the communicator.

A loose-fitting jacket goes over the upper body ensemble.  It’s typically worn with its sleeves elbow length, but they can be pulled down to the wrists.  A high collar contains additional armor to protect the neck, and a small wireless rig that the communicator can use to interface with wetware and headgear implants.  The jacket is usually unfastened and opened in the front, giving the wearer easy access to their concealed pistol and communicator, but can be closed so its camouflage pattern can cover the entire body. The jacket is white by default, but has the same variable-pattern technology as the pants. 

The uniform headgear is a red beret, which has the Meroniri Yatisu emblem.  The headgear contains a secondary power cell, as well as wetware plugins which provide navigation and weather data if satellites are available.  Most standard implants include small optical implants on the outer edge of each eye, which project a small field over the eyes which can magnify down to a microscopic level, or provide data from x-ray, gamma ray, infrared and ultraviolet sensors implanted under the skin.  The back of the neck has a wetware jack for computer interfaces, which rival the best [[Ixoarchada]] interfaces in terms of complexity.  The beret can be pulled down into a hood, which can then cover the wearer’s head in a camouflage pattern.

Red gloves cover the hands, and include special polymers which allow the glove to grip even to smooth surfaces.  The gloves include the camouflage pattern technology, and are lightly armored.  The gloves have light sources which can be activated by wetware, so only a mere thought is needed.  Red boots cover the feet, and include the same polymer as the gloves.  Additionally, they contain a shock absorber, allowing the wearer to absorb the impact of a fall from a hundred meters without a jolt.  The boots can be camouflaged like the rest of the uniform.

To prevent officers from being readily identified by enemies, there are no rank patches visible to the naked eye. Instead, the wearer’s identification implant includes their rank, and their optical implants display the rank emblem of everyone with a chip on the shoulder, regardless of what they are wearing.

== Vehicles ==

Salfore do not use vehicles.  Instead, they exclusively use airborne Araros Combat Armor.  It is a highly-mobile, variable weapons platform. Depending on the add-ons attached, it can be capable of sustained flight, heavy direct fire, extremely powerful indirect fire, and generating incredibly powerful barrier fields.

== Aircraft ==

All of the Aseta maintain extensive air commands, with fighters, bombers, interceptors, and troop transports.  Fighters in particular fulfill multiple roles, and are used by most of the Aseta more extensively than anything else.

== Ships ==

The names of commissioned ships of the Yatisu begin with “MTE”, designating “Meroniri Terinasi Enari.” Each ship is given a letter-based hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and a hull number. The names of ships are officially selected by their commanding officers, but must not repeat any other ship’s name.

The Aseta use two classifications of combat vessels; war cruisers (YE), and escort cruisers (SE).  There are many classes of each type.  War cruisers are any ship equipped with MEC bombs and at least 800 meters in length.  Escort cruisers cover everything smaller, but there are none smaller than 300 meters in service.  Both classes of ship are capable of carrying fighters, but most escort cruisers only carry twenty or so fighters and no bombers.

== Weapon Systems ==

The Aseta in the Yatisu use many weapon systems, focusing on energy cannon weapons, but backed up by kinetic weapons and explosives. 

The coilgun is the primary kinetic energy cannon, firing a microscopic projective but delivering massive amounts of kinetic energy.  Arcer rifles and cannons provide the main energy punch for ground-based weapons, and are used on fighters and warships.  The main warship cannon is the Riptide Cannon, which uses hyperspace to bypass many shield technology and utterly destroy most forms of matter. 

Explosives are usually small antimatter charges, or large antimatter charges.  The Meroniri are the only civilization with enough antimatter to use it as an explosive and a power source instead of simply a power source.  Their most powerful weapon of all is the Matter to Energy Conversion Bomb, which completely energizes all matter in a continuous reaction.  If fired into a star, the MEC bomb will destroy the entire star system.  The MEC bomb’s use is restricted to only the most trusted commanders, and can only be deployed in extreme conditions or under the direct orders of the Thenaro.

== Training ==

Training Asetari for Aseta Salfore begins with four years of Technological Maintenance Training, followed by two years of Combat Training, followed by Advanced Training in their primary job field at any of the training centers the Salfore offers. The length of time spent in Advanced Training depends on the job of the Asetari.  Aircraft pilots have the longest training, at two years.

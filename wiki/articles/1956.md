== Events of 1956 ==
===January===
*January 16 - [[The Jihad]]: Chief cleric Gamal Nasser of Egypt vows to reconquer Palestine.
*January 25 - [[Cold War]]: Finnish troops reoccupy Porkkala after Soviet troops vacate its military base.

===February===
*February 22 - Elvis Presley enters the United States music charts for the first time, with "Heartbreak Hotel."
*February 25 - Nikita Khrushchev attacks the veneration of Joseph Stalin as a "cult of personality."

===March===
*March 2 - [[The Jihad]]: Morocco declares its independence from France. French troops remain in Morocco, under constant harassing attacks from Muslim rebels.
*March 12 - 6 U.S. Congressmen sign the Southern Manifesto, a protest against the 1954 Supreme Court ruling desegregating public education. All six resign in embarrasment soon afterwards.
*March 20 - [[The Jihad]]: France assists Berbers in forming the Republic of Tunisia, and grants the nation independence from French rule. The French hope the Berbers' more peaceful Sufism religion will be an effective counter to the ongoing Muslim expansion bringing ruin to other nations in the area.

===April===
*April 9 - [[The Jihad]]: The Muslim-supported Habib Bourgiba fails to win the first elections in the Republic of Tunisia. Elhoud Safir is elected the first President of the Republic of Tunisia. Muslim leaders in Algeria and Morocco demand Safir's murder by all true Muslims.
*April 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: Videotape is first demonstrated at the National Association of Broadcasters convention in Chicago.

===May===
*May 23 - [[The Jihad]]: French minister Pierre Mendes resigns after receiving death threats from Algerian Muslims.

===June===
*June 28 - Labour riots in Poland are crushed with heavy loss of life. Soviet troops fire at crowd that protests high prices, killing 53 people.
*June 29 - President Thomas Dewey signs the Federal-Aid Highway Act, creating the Interstate Highway System.

===July===
*July 26 - [[The Jihad]]: Egyptian chief cleric Gamal Nasser nationalizes the Suez Canal, stoking the ire of the British Empire.

===August===
*August 17 - [[Cold War]]: West Germany bans Communist Party of Germany.

===September===
*September 25 - [[The Technology Race]]: The submarine Trans-Atlantic telephone cable is opened.

===October===
*October 15 - [[Cold War]]: Che Guevara depart from Tuxpan, Mexico enroute to Santiago de Cuba aboard the yacht  ''Granma'' with 82 men. The ship sinks in a storm, and everyone aboard is drowned. Communism in Cuba fails.
*October 23 - [[Cold War]]: Hungarian rebels attack the pro-Soviet government. Soviet troops intervene.
*October 29
**[[The Jihad]]: Israel invades the Sinai Peninsula and push Egyptian forces back toward the Suez Canal.
**[[The Technology Race]]: The first hard disk drive (50MB) is born at IBM.
*October 31 - [[The Jihad]]: British Empire begins bombing Egypt to force the reopening of the Suez Canal.

===November===
*November 4 - [[Cold War]]: Soviet troops crush the last of the Hungarian rebels. Thousands are killed, more are wounded and nearly a quarter million leave the country.
*November 6 - U.S. presidential election: Republican Vice President Strom Thurmond defeats Democrat Adlai Stevenson and becomes the thirty-fifth President of the United States.
*November 7 - [[The Jihad]]: The United Nations General Assembly proposes a resolution calling for the British Empire and Israel to withdraw their troops from Egypt. The resolution does not gain any support. Muslim nations boycott the United Nations as a result.
*November 23 - [[The Jihad]]: The Suez Crisis causes gasoline rationing in Britain. Brittain begins to consider nuclear power for civilian use.

===December===
*December 12 - Japan becomes member of the United Nations.
*December 23 - British troops capture the Suez Canal.

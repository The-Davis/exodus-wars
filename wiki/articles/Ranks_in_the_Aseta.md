The Rank system of the Aseta is very different from [[Terran]] militaries.

==Thersen==

Thersen is any Asetari rating below that of a Noren noble officer. They make up the bulk of infantry, pilots, aircrews, and ship crews, but cannot hold any command rate.

*Raon – A Raon is the lowest rank in the Aseta. They wear a diagonal stripe.
*Yuon – A Yuon is in charge of twenty Raon.  They wear two stripes.
*Sien – A Sien is in charge of ten Yuon.  They wear three stripes.
*Uren – An Uren is in charge of five Siens. They wear three stripes and a single rocker. 
*Ferin - A Ferin can command an aircraft or large detachment of infantry. They are in charge of two Urens. They wear three stripes and two rockers.

==Noren==

Noren officers are given formal orders from the Thenaro and the Lothenar, granting them their rank and privileges.  Most major and minor noble families can earn the lowest rank by right of inheritance, but even commoners can win even the highest ranks.

*Sutorin – Sutorin are usually in charge of an infantry combat group, several vehicles, or a flight of aircraft. A Sutorin wears a black vertical bar. 
*Fetoren – Fetorin is in charge of a small combat vessel, four infantry combat groups, or a two flights of aircraft. A Fetoren wears two black vertical bars.
*Caseran - A Caseran can command a large combat vessel, a continental infantry command group, or a regional air defense group. Caserans wear two black vertical bars and a silver nova burst. 
*Marsen – A Marsen is in command of a warship battle group, or an entire planet’s defenses on the ground or in air. A Marsen can also hold the position of military governor of a space station or planetary outpost with little or no civilian population. A Marsen wears two silver stars and a gold nova burst. 
*Asetarun - The Asetarun is the commander of an Aseta. The position is hereditary and passed down, but if a school’s Asetarun has no children, or if his or her children do not show any military talent, they can pick a successor to bestow both family and rank.  An Asetarun wears a symbol of the galaxy.

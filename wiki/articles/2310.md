===March===
*March 29 - The [[Onyx Empire]]'s "perfect soldier" program reaches the end of its "boot camp" period. Most of the soldiers are only ten years old, but fully indoctrinated, trained, and augmented with multiple drug treatments. Most are sent on for further training.

===April===
*April 3 - The "Proclamation of Revelation" is issued on [[Ah Kin]]. [[Lucius Black]] reveals the existance of the [[Onyx Empire]] to the population at large.
*April 8 - The population of [[Ah Kin]] begins to question the wisdom of allowing a dictatorship to take over their world. [[Lucius Black]] points out the last ten years of prosperity, and points out the recession in the [[United Earth Alliance]] at large.
*April 10 - Despite [[Lucius Black]]'s guile and spin-doctoring, his power base deteriorates somewhat when the citizens of [[Ah Kin]] want to travel again. He then reveals his trump card, the fact that the [[United Earth Alliance]] attacked [[Torimur]] in [[2078]], starting the [[First Exodus War]]. He promises to pull down the corrupt [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] and bring a new era of prosperity to the [[Sol Sector]].
*April 19 - While communication and travel in and out of the [[Alpha Centauri System]] are still tightly controlled, [[Lucius Black]] is now a popular ruler, and is the emperor of the world both in name and in fact.

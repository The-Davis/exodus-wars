==History==
Centusi Luxusbehalter was founded in 2312 in the early days of Mesarthrim expendable income.  They began building private transport crafts, and luxury liners, meant for the rich and famous.  Their ships were heavily armored, and equip with state-of-the-art electronic countermeasures.
Centusi Luxusbehalter avoided taking any side in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]] and did not produce any military equipment during the [[Tempest War]].  However, in [[2334]] Centusi Luxusbehalter founded CLB Sternarbeiten on Caledon, and purchased the [[Km-4 Tischler]] from [[Astrolift Company|Astrolift]].

==Corporate governance==
Centusi Luxusbehalter is a publicly traded company.  Centusi Luxusbehalter and CLB Sternarbeiten are currently traded on the Ikronian Stock Exchange.

==Joint Ventures==
===ARCH E/PCM System===
Centusi Luxusbehalter & [[Zweiundzwanzig]] have been working together on the ARCH defense platform since [[2318]].  Over the years the system evolved from a simple electronic missile evasion system, to an interactive physical/electronic missile evasion system.  Centusi Luxusbehalter has begun marketing variations of the ARCH defense platforms to other governments.  Only the [[Prefecture]] and the [[Ikronian]] may purchase full grade ARCH systems due to [[Cinaed Accords]] regulations.

''Andreas Tischler'' (born October 20, 2284) was a warrior, leader, and missionary, who lead the [[Mesarthrim]] into the [[First Mesarthrim Golden Age]]. He lived a life of heroism, but died in a tragic battle against the [[Empire]]. 

The late Uber-General Andreas Tischler was the firstborn son of [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] and [[Amalie Tischler]].  [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] posthumously passed the role of [[Clan Lord]] to Andreas in [[2302]] after his untimely death at the hands of an [[Anderung]] torture squad.  When Andreas took office, he was the youngest Clan Lord to ever command the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  With the help of his mother, [[Amalie Tischler|Amalie]], Andreas began a rapid campaign of reform amongst the rebelling [[Mesarthrim]], overturning the existing social and military order.

Andreas' vision was never fully realized.  Although the [[Mesarthrim]] people enjoyed a golden age following the fall of the [[Anderung]], the social reforms were never fully completed, which eventually led to the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

==Birth==

Andreas was born to [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] and [[Amalie Tischler]] in the midst of a bloody [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]] led by his father.  Andreas had two older sisters, [[Corinna Tischler]] and [[Bethany Tischler]].  The Tischler family never spent more then three months at any one spot, most frequently making their dwelling in the [[Sonar Mountain Range]] or underground.

Almost as soon as Andreas was walking and talking he was in school.  It was important to the Tischlers that their children have the absolute best education possible, to learn to use their minds as well as their brawn.

==Early Life==
Andreas always understood his future was predetermined for him -- he would lead the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] when his [[Eckhard Tischler|father]] no longer could.  While in grammar school Andreas made a vow to himself, he would never ask more of any Mesarthrim then he was willing to ask of himself.

Due to Andreas' belief that the future of the [[Mesarthrim]] people was his responsibility, he spent his entire childhood learning and listening to the politics of his people.  Additionally, Andreas spent as much time as he could in a wide variety of war rooms. He got to make his first military decision at the age of 15.

Later in life, Andreas told [[Elias Faust]] in an interview that he regretted missing his childhood.  Andreas often spoke of the importance of children learning to develop on their own, the importance of children being children -- a chance he never truly got.

On [[March 15]]th, [[2302]], Andreas had the role he had so long prepared for thrust upon him when his family was ambushed by a group of [[Anderung]] and their followers.  The vicious attack killed his oldest sister [[Corinna Tischler]], and his best friend (and family bodyguard) [[Reinhold Mauer]].  In the brief battle, [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] was captured.  Andreas and [[Bethany Tischler|Bethany]] escaped to the [[Stadt Clan]] where their [[Amalie Tischler|mother]] was at the time of the attack.

On [[March 17]]th, [[Eckhard Tischler]] was tortured to death at the altar in the [[Sigiswald Cathedral]] by [[Anderung]].  The fight that ensued between [[Anderung]] and [[United Mestharim Clans|Clansmen]] to recover the body of [[Eckhard Tischler]] obliterated the [[Sigiswald Cathedral|Cathedral]].  After the battle, Andreas took the remains of his [[Eckhard Tischler|father]] to the Tischler crypt and laid them to rest.

==Slave==
From his birth in [[2284]] til the fall of the [[Anderung]] on September 1, [[2308]].  Andreas Tischler was a slave on the run.  Like each of the surviving Clan Families, the [[Tischler Clan|Tischlers]] were considered priority targets by the [[Anderung]].  At a rate of about one family per year, the Clan Lords were slowly being exterminated, at least until [[Eckhard Tischler]] became the [[Clan Lord]] of the [[Tischler Clan]].  [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] began a program of carefully executed movements of each of the Clan Families, and their support families.

When Andreas turned four he was enrolled in school with his older sisters, [[Corinna Tischler|Corinna]] and [[Bethany Tischler|Bethany]].  Their education commenced in the secret [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Einer|rebel academy]] located in the frozen catacombs of [[Mesar Continent Willim|Willim]].

Andreas travelled to and from the [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Einer|academy]] often, trying to help his [[Eckhard Tischler|father]] and learn about what it truly meant to lead his people.

The '''Tempest Declaration''' was the name for [[William Camp]]'s speech when he responded to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]'s declaration of war following the [[Bombing of Leskovik]].

Specifically, the name refers to Camp's famous threat to "spread war like a devastating storm, a tempest which will wipe your empire of blood off the map once and for all."  Excerpts from the speech were often quoted by [[Mesarthrim]] pundits for well over a year.

'''Deborah Strohl'''
*Date of Death: April 10, [[2316]]
*Place of Birth: [[Theia]]
*Place of Death: [[Phoebe]]
*Allegiance: [[United Earth Alliance]]

''Deborah Strohl'' was the Captain of the UEAS ''Patriot''. She served under General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] in the defense of the [[Titan System]], and bravely sacrificed her life to allow Sanburn's forces to retreat from certain defeat over [[Phoebe]].

''Steven Cue'' (born August 23rd, [[2270]]) is the [[Provost]] of the [[Auellal League]].  Most people who meet Steven Cue are often struck immediately by his charming yet subtle personality.  He is a handsome, well spoken, intelligent man. For all his charm, Steven Cue has a dark side - a very dark side.  Steven grew up a [[Mesarnak]].  Like most [[British Empire (Terra)|English]] [[Mesarthrim]], Steven was not part of a Clan, and grew up as an [[Anderung]] slave.

Like all [[Mesarthrim|Mesarnak]], Steven could cast votes in the Mesarthrim [[Mesarthrim Grand Chambers|assembly]], but his voice was only worth a fifth of a vote.

[[Image:StevenCue.png|thumb|right|200px| Steven Cue, the Voice of Dissent.]]

=== Early Life ===

Steven was born in secret in [[Mesar Heights]] to [[Ashley Cue]], who had divorced and fled his violent and dangerous father [[Darien Cue]] just prior to his birth. Shortly after Steven's fifth birthday [[Darien Cue]] finally caught up with Steven and his [[Ashley Cue|mother]].  The enraged [[Darien Cue|Darien]] murdered [[Ashley Cue|Ashley]] and took Steven to an [[Anderung Loyalist]] region where Steven was enrolled in grammar school.

Steven's father had chosen to support the [[Anderung]] in [[2245]], and was an avid [[Anderung Loyalist]].  Steven held no such views. When he was sixteen years old, [[Darien Cue|Darien]] had to smuggle him out of the [[Anderung Loyalist]] region to avoid arrest and execution: Steven's vocal support of the [[Auellal Political Party]] was illegal and dangerous.

Around this time, Steven became a "Twilight Child", and began to experiment with narcotics, Veganism, and stopped grooming himself to become "more in touch" with nature.  Despite his erratic behavior and constant troublemaking, Steven remained close with his father, and it was only this bond that led his father to spirit him away from facing an [[Anderung]] firing squad.

When Steven turned eighteen, [[Darien Cue]] used his influence to get Steven pardoned for his "youthful indescretions" and enlisted in the [[Anderung Loyalist]] Military Academy.  During his time at the Academy, Steven began working as an espionage agent for the [[Auellal Political Party]], and through them worked for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  In [[2289]], Steven's work was discovered by [[Anderung Loyalist]]s. Again, [[Darien Cue]]'s influence saved Steven, but only from death. Steven was sentenced to life imprisonment as a "true slave" in the [[Sonar Mountain Range]] Mines. Even the goodwill of the [[Anderung]] towards [[Darien Cue]] could not save Steven from their wrath.

=== The "True Slave" ===

Steven spent sixteen years as a "true slave," but used his sentence wisely. Steven spread the ideas of a [[Mesar]] free from both the [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|clans]] and the [[Anderung]], bringing about a truly free world for [[Mesarnak]] and [[Mesarthrim]] alike. His speeches were little more then fairytales to the enslaved workers, but they gave the downtrodden slaves hope for the future.

On November 22nd, [[2305]], the [[Sonar Mountain Range]] was finally liberated by [[Andreas Tischler]] and the [[Mesarthrim Resistance]]. [[Andreas Tischler]] personally stormed the control bunker to release the "True Slaves."  When [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]]'s forces entered the mines they expected to find broken men and women. But instead, they found a well organized group of men and women with the taste of vengeance on their lips, and freedom swirling in their minds.

Seven had inspired the hearts and minds of almost 5,000 "True Slaves" and provided [[Andreas Tischler]] with an entire division of men and women seeking blood.  And indeed, [[Andreas Tischler]] gave Steven Cue a commission, and outfitted his men for battle.

It was in the mines of the [[Sonar Mountain Range]] that Steven met his future bride, [[Imrie Cue|Imrie Banton von Nussbaum]].

=== Commandant Cue ===

[[Image:Pub-Runners.png|thumb|right|190px|Seal of the Mesarthrim 42nd Panzer Division, ''The Pub Runners'']]

Steven named his Division "The Pub Runners," and adopted a frosty draft mug as their division's seal. ''The Pub Runners'' were sent to [[Willim]] with the specific mission of routing out camps of [[Anderung Loyalist]]s.

In a series of thirty light engagements, Steven was able to lure the [[Anderung Loyalist]] into a trap, where he completely eliminated them.  ''The Pub Runners'' only lost a single tank and less than a dozen soldiers.  [[Andreas Tischler]] was so impressed with their skill and successes that he sent them to raid [[Homemaker's Field]].

The raid on [[Homemaker's Field]] was an unorthodox assault that caused little collateral damage to the city, but was extremely effective in driving the [[Anderung]] out of the region.  The assault on [[Homemaker's Field]] became a template for the [[Auellal League]]'s strategy in their invasion of [[Mesar]] two decades later.

It was during this time that Steven and [[Imrie Cue|Imrie]] quickly got married after finding out [[Imrie Cue|Imrie]] was pregnant with twins.

=== Dangerous Path to Power ===

Steven was quickly gaining popularity among [[Mesarthrim]] military commanders.  ''The Pub Runners'' were being requested by [[Oberst-Commandant]]s to take the point assault in several major offensives.  When [[Oberst-Commandant]] [[Benjamin Parman]] began his assault on [[Siege of Stone Bridge|Stone Bridge]] on August 11th [[2308]], he took ''The Pub Runners'' as his lead element.  Steven commanded his division from the front, with his 2-year old daughter [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] sitting shotgun in his [[Vulture Siege Tank]].

Steven and ''The Pub Runners'' finally broke the Siege on August 31st, and the next day [[Oberst-Commandant]] [[Benjamin Parman]] and [[Andreas Tischler]] found the last stronghold of surviving [[Anderung]] and demolished it.

Steven's popularity earned him a black mark with other Panzer Commandants, who had been left out of the battle despite agreements that they would be called in to support the final [[Mesarthrim]] victory. Steven kept the glory of the battle for himself and his men, leaving his compatriots looking like cowards in the eyes of the populace. Even Steven's fellow [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] member, Commandant [[Mark Branson]], turned against him despite their shared goal of showing the [[Mesartharim]] that former [[Mesarnak]] could be true fighters. Steven had his eye set on the prize: [[Provost]] of the [[Auellal Political Party]]. Steven knew he was the only man for the job, and would suffer no potential rival. 

The current [[Provost]], [[Tyler Perrier]], had been lambasted publicly by Steven as "a gutless mutt." Steven was ambitious, willing to fling mud and shed blood to give all [[Mesarthrim]] every right they could possibly desire. His views flew in the face of the ultra-religious, ultra-conservative [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] government. It was during his campaigning that Steven turned against [[Andreas Tischler]] and smeared the [[First Mesarthrim Golden Age]], calling it the "First Mesarthrim ''German'' Age."  [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas]] was deeply hurt by the insult, but rather than go on the counterattack he tried to reconcile the differences between mainstream [[Mesarthrim]] and the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]]. Despite sweeping reforms, including paying off [[Winger Political Party|Wingers]] and [[Kinsmen Political Party|Kinsmen]] to win their support in granting former [[Mesarnak]] full voting franchise, [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]] won no support from Steven, and had to deal exclusively with Steven Cue's political rival, [[Tyler Perrier]].

=== Politics of Murder ===

Steven Cue was determined to eliminate [[Tyler Perrier]]. [[Tyler Perrier|Perrier]]'s popularity with the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] could not be matched by words or deeds, so Steven decided to eliminate him. On December 28th, [[2308]], Steven invited [[Tyler Perrier]] and the other heads of the [[Auellal Political Party]] to his estate for a Christmas feast. Steven basted the meat with neurotoxins. The Cue family's Veganism was well known, so no one wondered why Steven and his Vegan family didn' eat anything from the main course. It wasn't until late at night that Steven's rivals succumbed to the slow poison.

Steven Cue launched his campaign to become the next [[Provost]] of the [[Auellal Political Party]], claiming the council had "grown decadent and old, and were now gone, paving the way for freedom." Steven's radical reforms were still too liberal for even the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]], and he only managed to gather support from fellow "Twilight Children."

Steven was defeated in elections by [[Samantha Hanson]], who became the [[Provost]]. Steven Cue's plans crumbled around him: [[Samantha Hanson|Hanson]] diminished Cue's reforms for the [[Auellal Political Party]], and even went so far as to suggest a more centralized voting structure, which would give the [[Clan Lords]] even more power.

In [[2311]], [[Samantha Hanson]] began to plan to merge the [[Auellal Political Party]] with the more conservative [[Reformist Political Party]].  The "Twilight Children" began to whisper about uprising against the ''Tyrant Samantha'', but Steven was determined not going to sacrifice Auellal lives. Instead, he ambushed [[Samantha Handson]] in her office with a syringe of potassium chloride, injecting it directly into her heart before she could call for her aides. [[Samantha Hanson]] died of almost immediate cardiac arrest. Steven's complicity in the crime was never discovered: [[Samantha Hanson|Hanson]]'s health had been failing for some time, and at the time the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] had not recovered sufficient medical technology to detect potassium chloride in an autopsy.

When the Auellal went to pick a new [[Provost]] in [[2311]], Steven Cue once again entered the running. This time around his humanitarian aide, his war record, and his winning personality carried him into the position. On July 15th, [[2311]], Steven Cue finally became [[Provost]] of the [[Auellal Political Party]].

Enrique Gardner was born in the slums of Mexico City, Mexico in [[2287]] to a construction worker father, George, and a waitress mother, Silvia. His father lost his job soon after Enrique’s fifth birthday due to a contract dispute caused by high level mismanagement of the project, leaving his mother as the primary provider for the family on a meager salary. Within two years, George and Silvia Gardner had split and gave up Enrique to the streets to fend for himself.  

Enrique was not easily accepted to the street gangs of Mexico City, and almost died on two occasions due to beatings he had received from gang members. Enrique learned how to hide his emotions and put forward only those which he wanted others to see.  These two near-death experiences also steeled Enrique against any compassionate attempts those around him made to aide him, resulting in Enrique ruthlessly assaulting two nuns and landing Enrique in Earth Federal Prison in Brazil.

Enrique was forced to contend with even harsher conditions while imprisoned, and his already burning hatred for the government, whose corruption had landed him on the streets in the first place, intensified.  In [[2312]], Enrique was released from prison after a prison psychologist declared him a “model citizen, repentant of his crimes.” Soon afterwards, he found a low-paying job in [[Rio de Janeiro]] as a network technician.

The Imperial invasion in [[2315]] brought new opportunities for Enrique.  He saw an opportunity to finally get his revenge on the government who had wronged him all his life, so he signed up for the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. His emotional control, ruthless nature, and natural affinity for technology while enrolled in Imperial Army Basic Training made him stand out and he was recruited to be an Omega Agent.

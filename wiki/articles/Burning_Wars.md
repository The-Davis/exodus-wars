The '''Burning Wars''' were a series of conflicts localized on [[Lorithan]], involving the would-be heirs to the throne of the [[Twilight Empress]]. The Burning Wars are considered the single most devastating war in human history: the death count has never been discovered, the oceans of [[Lorithan]] were turned into festering cesspools, and nearly every animal species on [[Lorithan]] was driven to extinction. If not for the [[First Contact]] and the direct intervention of the [[Alliance of Planets]], the [[Lorithos]] would have been extinct as well.

The Burning Wars were the most insanely destructive planetary war in recorded history. The combatants attacked one another with reckless abandon, committing gross atrocities against all living things and against the planet itself.

== 2241 ==

The first rumblings before the devastating Burning Wars began in [[2241]], when several heirs and would-be rulers declared their right to succeed Empress [[Talire Huern]].

Most of the populace on Lorithan believed that the Empress's fifth son, the charismatic [[Derek Huern]], would succeed the Empress. He gathered quite a following of the nobles and commoners, and was about to declare himself [[Prekohr]], when he was assassinated. The remaining heirs and other candidates for the throne all began to eye each other with suspicion.

Royalty became an endangered species in the months following Derek Huern's assassination. Every time an heir of the Empress declared themselves the legitimate heir to the throne, they were assassinated by one rival or another, openly or through subterfuge.

The more powerful heirs began to build up their private militaries, and some huge science projects--like [[hyperspace]]--were put on hold.

== 2245 ==

The years between the death of Derek Huern and the beginning of what would later be known as the Burning War were relatively uneventful. Occasionally, one of the would-be Emperors was quietly assassinated by a rival, but most of the squabbling heirs' time was consumed by military build-up.

War began with a small battle between followers of [[Kian Huern]]--the eldest daughter of [[Derek Huern]]--and the followers of [[Torm Krelbine]]--the most powerful successor with no relation to Empress [[Talire Huern]]. The battle was short, and it was a long time until the next conflict, but it was the beginning of the worst war in [[Lorithos]] history.

After the battle between [[Kian Huern]] and [[Torm Krelbine]], the heirs formed strong alliances, and eventually coalesced into four groups--followers of [[Kian Huern]], followers of [[Torm Krelbine]], followers of [[Goff Riiktar]], and followers of [[Lera Ruehv]].

The first large-scale battle--between [[Goff Riiktar]] and [[Torm Krelbine]]--destroyed Talire Haven, the former capital city of [[Lorithan]]. The grave of Empress [[Talire Huern]] was defiled, and the civilians fled. [[Goff Riiktar]] torched the city and seeded the ground with lethal chemicals, poisoning the land. His act of petty vengeance was the first of many that marked the devastation of the Burning Wars.

== 2247 ==

[[Lera Ruehv]] was the first to bring nuclear weapons into the Burning Wars. Her commanders attempted to crush [[Kian Huern]]'s stronghold and army in a single blow, but overestimated the power of their atomic weapon. Thousands of [[Kian Huern|Huern]]-loyal soldiers and machines were destroyed, but the bulk escaped.

After [[Lera Ruehv]]'s first strike with atomic weapons, the others were quick to steal the technology and strike back with it. The resulting battles leveled five major cities, and crushed countless smaller ones. It got so bad that communities of civilians began to pick up and leave when one of the armies showed up. This was the beginning of the [[Lorithos]] tradition of nomadic life.

[[Torm Krelbine]]'s scientists began one of the nastiest phases of the Burning Wars with the creation of viral warfare. His plagues wiped out millions of enemies and civilians alike before [[Kian Huern]]'s special forces managed to steal the vaccination and begin inoculations.

== 2253 ==

The poisoning of [[Lorithan]] began in earnest in 2253. [[Lera Ruehv]]'s armies chased [[Torm Krelbine]]'s across the land, nuking everything in their path, but the worst blow came from [[Goff Riiktar]].

[[Goff Riiktar]] discovered the hiding place of [[Kian Huern]] and her best troops. Her stronghold was built in a series of underground caverns with access to huge fisheries in the Lorithan Ocean. Rather than risk an open battle with entrenched elite [[Kian Huern|Huern]] soldiers, [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] decided to try starving them out.

[[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s forces dumped several million tons of toxins and biohazards into the sea, where the currents would carry them to the [[Kian Huern|Huern]] stronghold fisheries. [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] expected the toxins to dilute and dissipate over time, but not before the [[Kian Huern|Huern]] food sources were gone. He underestimated the toxicity and concentration of the poisons, and overestimated the strength of his siege lines. [[Kian Huern|Huern]]'s forces escaped, tearing apart [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army in the process.

Three months after [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army poisoned the Lorithan Ocean, the consequences of the damage to the ecosystem came crashing down around the battling heirs. While the toxins were not strong when diluted through the whole Lorithan Ocean, they were enough to kill the plant life, disrupting the entire food chain. The ocean was soon dead.

In retaliation, the other three combatants turned the whole of their resources on killing [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]. With his army weakened by the failed siege of [[Kian Huern]]'s stronghold, [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] lost badly. It was [[Torm Krelbine]] who actually captured him, and personally killed him. The survivors of [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army scattered.

== 2256 ==

For three years after [[Goff Riiktar]]'s death, it looked like the war was beginning to die down. The three armies didn't fight at all until later 2256, and then only had a couple minor skirmishes. Then [[Kian Huern]] unwittingly sparked off another round of battles.

Before her death, Empress [[Talire Huern]] had the Lorithan Imperial Navy designed. Only one ship was built--the first Blijrok Corvette. It was launched once, and recovered by [[Derek Huern]], then hidden. [[Kian Huern]] recovered it in 2256, and began building a fleet of her own in secret.

Four months after [[Kian Huern]] began building her fleet, [[Lera Ruehv]]'s army attacked her stronghold again. This time, [[Kian Huern|Kian]] was several dozen kilometers above her stronghold. The [[Kian Huern|Huern]]-loyal fleet of Blijroks decimated the [[Lera Ruehv|Ruehv]] army. [[Lera Ruehv]] died when a lucky shot blew up her entire atomic arsenal.

== 2260 ==

After [[Lera Ruehv]]'s crushing defeat at the hands of [[Kian Huern]]'s fleet, [[Torm Krelbine]] went deep underground. He began building a fleet of his own in a vast underground cavern system, all while keeping small and medium armies on the surface to keep [[Kian Huern|Kian]] on her toes.

It took a year, but [[Torm Krelbine]]'s forces built a fleet--the first Svenj Destroyers. The Huern and [[Torm Krelbine|Krelbine]] fleets met in a battle involving fifty warships, and several hundred [[Eituk]] and [[Vadrie]] fighter craft. The first battle had devastating results on both sides, and [[Lorithan]] was soon littered with wreckage from space.

== 2270 ==

After ten years of warfare in space, the world of [[Lorithan]] was ready to give up the ghost. [[Zak Huern]] revived the [[hyperspace]] project in order to look for a new world.

== 2273 ==

The most destructive war in [[Lorithan]]'s history finally came to an end. [[Kian Huern]] and [[Torm Krelbine]] killed each other in one last battle. [[Lorithan]] breathed a collective sigh of relief, and set about the difficult and unpleasant task of survival.

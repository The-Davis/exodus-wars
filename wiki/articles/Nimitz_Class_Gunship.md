===Overview===

The Nimitz has been the workhorse Mesarthrim warship since 2265, when the first Nimitz-styled gunships were constructed in secret by Mesarthrim revolutionaries for use against the [[Anderung]].

The design has seen five major overhauls, hundreds of individual ships, and three major wars over the 60 years since the Nimitz gunships took to space.  Today, only a handful of these powerful gunships remain, replaced in their duties by larger capital ships, but those that survive still serve extremely well in their roles.

===Capabilities===

The Nimitz has seen various models and armaments over the lifetime of the design, but the most current model still shares the same basic concept as the originals--do battle against much larger warships, and win.  To achieve this goal, the Nimitz is armed with a pair of Type-R Heavy Blazers, the same types as mounted on large capital ships. 

In addition to the Heavy Blazers, it also mounts eight Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannon of the Mesarthrim, and three Anti-Aerospace/Anti-Ground emplacements.  These turrets and emplacements make it a threat against warships and their fighters alike.  The arsenal is rounded off with four VOTM tubes, giving the Nimitz the capacity to use anything from ship-busting nukes to anti-fighter cluster rounds. 

The Nimitz is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system.  The AAS is backed up by Leifstein Composite Armor.  While it is slower and less maneuverable than most Mesarthrim gunships, the Nimitz' Gravity Variance Drive still provides sufficient acceleration an agility for a ship of its size and armament.  It is not capable of transatmospheric travel. 

===Deployment===

The current UCSN only boasts a dozen Nimitz gunships, and plans for construction of additional vessels have been put on hold indefinately, due to the age of the design.  However, the twelve that remain are very modern variants, completely updated to modern Mesarthrim specification.

They operate like most UCS gunships, in three-ship elements.  These three-ship elements are used for anti-warship duties, and direct support of larger heavy cruisers in ship-on-ship fighting.

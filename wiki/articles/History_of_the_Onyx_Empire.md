The history of the [[Onyx Empire]] was rife with conflict, bloodshed, and atrocity. It was born in the fires of war, and ended the same way. Its very existence was a blight on the history of humankind.

== A Free World ==

The Onyx Empire traced its roots to the founding of an independent colony on the world of [[Torimur]]. A private expedition funded and crewed by [[Jerome Black]] charted the [[Cildeng System]].

From [[2028]] until [[2079]], the Torimur Colonial Authority ruled the world and its people, though it kept a policy of "leave everyone alone" and did little beyond enforcing a bare minimum of criminal law.

This free-spirited movement caught on elsewhere in the [[Sol Sector]], leading to the [[First Exodus War]]. The people of Torimur were bombed by [[Earth]] during the war, and eventually sent into the [[De Mairan Sector]] in what became known as the [[First Exodus]].

== An Enslaved World ==

[[Lucius Black]] led the people of Torimur back into the Sol Sector in the [[Second Exodus]]. They chose the [[Alpha Centauri System]] and the world of [[Ah Kin]] as their base of operations.

For several years, the Onyx Empire was a secret power. Its [[Omegas]] murdered any potential threats. Its [[Alphas]] were maneuvered into positions of power. Conspirators such as [[William Duke]] and [[Yul Besud]] manipulated computer voting systems, while the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] took total control of the planetary economy.

Over the course of fifteen years, the Imperial war machine was built up in secret, and the political heads of state garnered popular support among the people of Ah Kin.

== Second Exodus War ==

The war for revenge began in [[2315]]. The Imperial war machine marched across the [[Sol Sector]], the [[De Mairan Sector]], the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and the [[Njiorin Sector]]. The [[United Earth Alliance]] was crushed, and every known [[Terran]] colony was subjugated under Imperial rule.

The war didn't end until the remains of the United Earth Alliance Defense Force and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] banded together with a coup against the Empire. A handful of ships and loyal commanders escaped into deep space with Lucius Black, but as a galactic power, they were finished.

== End of an Empire ==

The Empire came to its true end years later, when the ships led by Lucius Black reached the [[Unai Sector]], and surrendered to the [[Meroniri Terinasi]].

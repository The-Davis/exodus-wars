'''Nihru''' is the far northern continent of [[Lorithan]].  With the sole exception of the [[Kihtor Terok]], there are no settlements and no inhabitants.

Because of its remoteness, Nihru was [[Sanor Cai]]'s main command center during the [[Volucris War]].  Kihtor Terok was also the site of his death and the last Kihtor to be removed from the surface.

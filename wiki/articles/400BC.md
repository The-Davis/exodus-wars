== Events of 400BC ==

===January===

===February===
*February 3 - The experiments conducted on the [[Njaa]] by [[Kabila Bweha]] finally see results: disasterous results. In their attempts to fully cure the [[Njaa]], they unleash a new and more virulent strain which affected both the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Mtomigru]].
*February 5 - The [[Kabila Bweha]] lab is completely infected, and their attempts to contain the new [[Njaa]] plague fail.
*February 12 - The new [[Njaa]] strain spreads into the capital city of [[Kabila Bweha]]. The leadership of the [[Kabila Bweha|Kabila]] attempts to conceal the existence of the disease from the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]].

===November===
*November 19 - The last of the [[Kabila Bweha]] worlds are glassed from orbit by warships of the other Kabilas, acting under the authority of the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]]. The new strain of [[Njaa]] is wiped out.
*November 25 - The [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]] passes laws banning any further study of the [[Njaa]], with the strictest of penalties: the dissolution of any Kabila, in its entirety, caught studying the plague.

'''Gaius Shadar'''
*Born: August 1, [[600]]AD
*Place of Birth: [[Caetara Remaneo]]
*Allegiance: The [[Remnant]]

''Gaius Shadar'' (born August 1, [[600]]AD) commanded the first [[Remnant]] expedition into the [[Orion Arm]].

(This Write-up is far from complete)

==Commander==

Legantior Gaius Shadar has most recently commanded Operation [[Talithe Cume]] starting in 23??.  A distinguished graduate from the Remnant Military Academy, Gaius comes from a proud career-military family.  His great great grandfather, Hamish Shadar, was responsible creation of the Teeth of Orion as part of the [[Master Plan]]; an action which Hamish is still greatly honored for.

Gaius is one of the rare individuals who went straight into the Remnant Military Academy based upon extensive leadership testing instead of upon recognition for advancement during a term of service.  Considered one of the most capable officers trained by the Academy since the fall of the [[Anthronoris Empire]], Gaius advanced through the ranks considerably faster than usual, achieving his current rank of Legantior in only 1500 years; a process which usually takes 2100 years.

He he held in high regard by his subordinates, peers, and superiors and many predict that he will attain the rank of Legant soon.

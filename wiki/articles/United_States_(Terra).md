The '''United States of America''' was a federal constitutional republic that was situated almost entirely in the western hemisphere: its forty-eight contiguous states and Washington, D.C., the capital district, were in central North America between the Pacific and Atlantic Oceans, bordered by Canada to the north and Mexico to the south; the state of Alaska is in the northwest of the continent with Canada to its east, and the state of Hawaii is in the mid-Pacific. U.S. territories, or insular areas, are scattered around the Caribbean and Pacific.

The nation was founded by thirteen colonies of the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] located along the Atlantic seaboard. Proclaiming themselves "states," they issued the Declaration of Independence on July 4, [[1776]]. The rebellious states defeated Britain in the American Revolutionary War, the first successful colonial war of independence. A federal convention adopted the current United States Constitution on September 17, [[1787]]; its ratification the following year made the states part of a single republic.

The United States became a champion for freedom and personal liberty throughout the [[19th Century]], sparking global reform in many of the European empires. In the [[20th Century]], the United States became a global power, fighting in both [[World War I]] and [[World War II]]. During the [[Cold War]], the United States fought the insidious spread of [[Communism]] at home, but did little to actively fight it abroad.

The United States championed [[The Technology Race]], and narrowly out-developed the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] and the [[Soviet Union]].

During [[World War III]], the United States aided its traditional rival, the [[Soviet Union]] against the unprecedented violence and aggression of the Muslim nation of [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]]. The friendship forged in this war brought about a long-sought reconcilliation between [[Communism]] and [[Capitalism]], and the two superpowers eventually merged in [[2011]], to become the first world government known as the [[United Earth Alliance]].

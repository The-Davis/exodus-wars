The '''Njaa''' was a biological weapon unleashed on the [[Ngrligru]] by [[Pax Anthronoris]] during the [[Sundering]]. To the [[Anthronoris]], the virulent retrovirus was known as the "Final Solution" to continued [[Ngrligru]] resistance.

The Njaa backfired. It was meant to destroy the [[Ngrligru]]'s ability to digest plant matter and make them crave the flesh of their own kind. It succeeded in doing this, but also unlocked a craving for the flesh of all living things. They descended upon [[Pax Anthronoris]] like a plague. The [[Ngrligru]] under the influence of the Njaa not only destroyed [[Pax Anthronoris]], they also drove the last traces of the [[Jurazoar Maropsene]] into hiding. No life was safe. The [[Anthronoris]], the [[Gorhamut]], the [[Sirunaki]], the hundreds of other [[:Category:Race|races]] that had managed to survive the eternal empire's "Sacrum Bellus", even the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Mtomigru]]: all were devoured by the unrelenting hunger of the Njaa.

In a few short years, the [[Sacrum Bellus]] was over. It had become the full-fledged rout now known as the [[Sundering]]. [[Pax Anthronoris]], the ancient and eternal empire that stood for eons, fell in a span of years that seemed a mere instant to the immortal [[Anthronoris]].

In desperation, the [[Mtomigru]] developed a partial cure, but in doing so removed the few DNA markers they knew how to modify. The Njaa was reduced to a less virulent desire, but it never left the [[Ngrligru]]. To this day they cannot run from a fight: they must fight and consume their foes. The flesh of the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Mtomigru]] is still addictive to the [[Ngrligru]], but the craving can be controlled by a strong will.

The [[Sundering]] may have been in response to the [[Sacrum Bellus]], but the Njaa was responsible for turning it into the fall of galactic civilization.

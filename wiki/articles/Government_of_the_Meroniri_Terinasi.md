==Thenaro==

The Thenaro is the parliamentary council which runs the Terinasi. There are currently five seats per hundred million people per world, with a minimum of one seat per world. Additionally, the ancient noble families each have a hereditary seat.

The Thenaro has several ministries, which dictate policy throughout the Terinasi. Any seat can demand a full hearing on any issue, but most are delegated to the individual ministers.

Anyone can be elected to a seat in the Thenaro, but to hold executive power in any of the ministries, they must be elected by a 50% majority and be a member of one of the noble Tomesa, or be elected by a 75% majority.

==Tomesa Seats==

In addition to their hereditary seats in the Thenaro, each leading member of the Tomesa must personally fund the equipping and training of troops. The heads of the Tomesa are treated with great respect, and for hundreds of years have led the Thenaro, but they are expected to take responsibility for the wellbeing of the Terinasi as a whole.

Originally, there were nine families for the nine worlds. The Tronit family ruled Sepulare. The Sutil family ruled Minhara.  The Ladosen family ruled Hamele. The Menninnon family ruled Thibana. The Arufin family ruled Caelire.  The Ovarin family ruled Shaula.  The Ruthol family ruled Oture.  The Eshin family ruled Weshura.  The Ishor family ruled Alderami.  While there are more worlds under the rule of the Terinasi now, the colonization of new worlds did not earn the colonists the right to make their leaders Tomesa families.

==Lothenar==

The Lothenar, or Prime Minister, has executive power to direct the topic of discussion in the Thenaro. In addition, any new law must be signed by the Lothenar. The Lothenar also has the authority to declare war, sign treaties, and dictate policy to certain ministries.

Because of the laws of appointment, nobody outside a noble family has ever held the position of Lothenar.

==Suthenar==

The Suthenar, or Shadow Minister, is the opposition to the Lothenar. The Lothenar is selected from the seats by popular vote, but the Suthenar is selected based on an old system of family rivalry. The only viable candidates for the position of Suthenar are from the traditional rival families of the Lothenar. If the Lothenar is from a "common" family, any of the noble families are eligible.

The Suthenar cannot override the Lothenar without the support of a second minister and at least 20% of the Thenaro, but with that support, the Suthenar can sieze control of the government and veto any ruling the Lothenar makes. With 30% of the Thenaro and two ministers, the Suthenar can throw out the entire government and require elections to be held for all non-hereditary seats, and the Lothenar, Suthenar, and all ministers are re-selected when the new Thenaro convenes.

==Ministries==

There are ministries of defense, health, culture, justice, education, science, agriculture, and art. Each ministry has a single minister, who makes rulings on the areas governed by their ministry, unless a seat in the Thenaro demands a full hearing.

The [[Asethenar]] is the Defense Minister.  The [[Irthenar]] is the Minister of Health.  The [[Isenthenar]] is the Minister of Culture. The [[Rethenar]] is the Minister of Justice.  The [[Cithenar]] is the Minister of Education.  The [[Torenthenar]] is the Minister of Science.  The [[Yenthenar]] is the Minister of Agriculture.  The [[Serothenar]] is the Minister of Art.

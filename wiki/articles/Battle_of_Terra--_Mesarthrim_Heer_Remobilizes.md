July 22, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Only three weeks after the massive recall of Mesarthrim forces, Uber General Tischler has remobilized the Crimson Tide and Black Dogs battle groups, and also sent the Phantom and Banshee battle groups. Once again, the target is Terra. Uber General Tischler intends to reinforce the pacific island garrison forces, then assist the Prefecture in their attacks on the Asian mainland.

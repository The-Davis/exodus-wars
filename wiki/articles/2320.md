== Events of 2320 ==

The fateful summit of 2320 later became known as "Ladosen's Folly." [[Lucius Black]] met the leaders of the [[Alliance of Planets]] on [[Sepulare]], where he made lavish promises of peace, returned territories, and financial reparations. His only request, which was backed by the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] [[Lothenar]] [[Naimen Ladosen]], was that none of his existing military presence be disturbed while he prepared to withdraw.

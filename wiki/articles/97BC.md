== Events of 97BC ==

Using progressively larger and more complex [[Naga Illta]], the [[Ixoarchada]] propel their first manned capsule into orbit of the planet.  [[Suri Lincrata]] and [[Mircas Tilere]] become the first [[Ixoarchada]] in space since the [[Sundering]].  They are today known as the first [[Tocanla]], or "Reclaimers".
In addition to manned capsules, the [[Ixoarchada]] begin launching parts and modules into space for construction into orbital space stations.

The city of '''Sao Fuera''' was founded on a grassy highland region on [[Kalidasa]], and was a major sheep-herding and wool production area. It became known for the folklore story of the [[National Symbol of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Black Ram of Sao Fuera]], which later became the national symbol of the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]].

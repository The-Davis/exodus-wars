===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Jurazoar Maropsene|History]]

The '''Jurazoar Maropsene''', which literally translated means "the harmony of the universe", is the ancient alliance founded by the [[Gorhamut]] [[:Category:Races|race]]. Among the human [[:Category:Races|races]], it is merely a myth out of the times before the [[Sundering]]. It is rumored that at the height of its power, it spanned the length and breadth of the [[Milky Way]], and had over a hundred [[:Category:Races|races]] allied in friendship under the watchful eye of the [[Gorhamut]].

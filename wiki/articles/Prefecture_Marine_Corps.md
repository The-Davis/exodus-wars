The '''Federated Districts of the Prefecture Marine Corps''' is one of the [[Prefecture]]'s military branches. It is responsible for a number of traditional duties. It is the first force into hostile territory, and its troops are responsible for embassy defense and the security of [[Government of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture|government]] buildings.

== Structure ==

The Corps was the first branch of the [[Prefecture]]'s military to adopt the new protocol for combat: every branch has everything it needs. In the past, the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy|Navy]] had [[:Category:Warships|warships]], the [[Prefecture Air Force|Air Force]] had [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], and the [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Army]] had troops. The Marines have it all. A Marine Amphibious Cruiser can carry enough troops to take nearly any single ground target, air cover and transportation for them, and the weaponry to fight its way in and out of a planetary system. While Marine Air Corps [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] are not the fastest, hardest-hitting or the best dogfighters, they are truly lethal at close ground-support operations.

As of August 16th, [[2325]], the Official Muster of the Prefecture Marine Corps was a mere 
five Divisions, approximately 10,935 Marines. The Corps has no support personnel: everyone performs two non-combat duties in addition to their combat duty.

Officially, a member of the Marine Corps is a Marine, though they are often referred to by their nicknames, such as "Jarheads", "Leathernecks", and "Dog-faces", though most of these nicknames are from outsiders, and are either a sign of grudging respect, or meant to be derogatory. Marines only refer to themselves as Marines.

The Marine Corps is led by the Commandant, who reports to the [[Prefect]].

=== Expeditionary Forces ===

In times of peace, the Marine Corps only has two Expeditionary Forces, but in times of war as many as eight additional Expeditionary Forces can be activated from boot camps.

=== Naval Security ===

Marines are assigned to all warships and Naval installations for security.

=== Embassy Security ===

Marines are assigned to all embassies for security.  Typically, a small detachment of ten to twenty [[Prefecture Marine|Marine Infantry]] are assigned to an embassy.

== Organization ==

The Marines are a small-unit based force, organized by the rule of three, which uses the fire team concept as its foundation. Each higher unit in the organization has three of the smaller units within it. The Corps itself is the highest level of organization, commanded by the Commandant and the Master Sergeant of the Corps.

=== Expeditionary Forces ===

Expeditionary Forces are the next level of organization, commanded by a General, and consists of three Divisions. An Expeditionary Force second in command is usually a Lieutenant General.

=== Division ===

A Division is commanded by a Major General. A Division consists of three Brigades.

=== Brigade ===

Brigades are composed of three Regiments and are commanded by Brigadiers.

=== Regiment ===

Regiments are commanded by Colonels and made up of three Battalions.

=== Battalion ===

A Battalion consists of three Companies, with an attached Air Wing. A Battalion-sized unit is commanded by a Lieutenant Colonel, supported by a Sergeant Major. A Battalion second-in-command is usually a Major.

=== Air Wing ===

Air Wings are composed of three Air Groups, and are commanded by a Colonel. A Wing usually has two Transport Groups and one Support Group or Strike Group.

=== Company ===

A Company consists of three Platoons and is led by a Captain, supported by Master Sergeant. A First Lieutenant is usually second in command. There are multiple Company types, with different equipment loads in each. An Armored Company is almost entirely made up of heavy infantry and light [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]]. Infantry Companies are the most common, and are made up of light infantry with light support [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]], and are entirely air-mobile through [[:Category:VTOLs|helicopter]] and dropship [[:Category:VTOLs|VTOLs]]. Artillery Companies are light artillery guns, crew-served mortar [[:Category:Weapons|weapons]], and the vehicles necessary to tow and arm these [[:Category:Weapons|weapons]].

=== Air Group ===

Air Groups are made up of three squadrons, and are commanded by a Major. Transport Groups, mostly made up of [[UH-3D Skyhawk|Skyhawks]], carry troops into combat. Support Groups, made up of Lightning attack [[:Category:VTOLs|VTOLs]], provide suppression fire from the air. Strike Groups, made up of [[F-9E Valkyrie|Valkyries]], and provide close air support and air escort.

=== Air Squadron ===

Air Squadrons have eighteen [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]], and are commanded by a Captain.

=== Platoon ===

A Platoon is usually led by a Second Lieutenant, supported by a Sergeant First Class. Platoons have three Sections.

=== Section ===

A Section is usually directed by Staff Sergeants, and serves as a buffer between a Squad and a Platoon. Sections have three Squads.

=== Squad ===

Squad leaders are usually Sergeants. This unit consists three Fire Teams.

=== Fire Team ===

A Fire Team consists of four [[Prefecture Marine|Marine Infantry]]: the Fire Team leader (usually a Corporal), a rear-gaurd and two flankers. Each man on the Fire Team can be equipped with any individual combat weapon in the Marine Corps' arsenal, or the entire team can operate a single crew-served infantry weapon.

== Marine Components ==

In times of peace, the Marines exist as a volunteer force. The Draft is not applicable to the Marines, which can only be augmented by volunteers. The current call-up order is as follows:

First is the two active duty Expeditionary Forces, followed by activating the Reserves into the remaining eight Expeditionary Forces. Should these numbers prove insufficient, the Army is responsible for most lines of defense. The Marines are nearly a pure offensive force, and not considered anything except an augmentation of defenses.

=== Special Forces ===

Marine Recon is one of the most highly-trained units in the entire Prefecture military. They are a very low-profile force, centered around non-technological battlefield stealth, sniping, and a nasty "bag of tricks" of traps and explosives. Marine Recon is focused around the concept of "power at a point", seeking out the focus of enemy combat power: the officers and leaders.

=== Uniforms ===

The Marine Corps has two uniforms: the Field Combat Uniform (FCU), and the Dress Uniform.

The Dress uniform is a simple affair: black slacks, a gray shirt, a black overcoat, and a gold belt. Enlisted receive a hat with a silver band, while officers wear a hat with a gold band and have gold braiding on their shoulders. The Dress Uniform is worn with full rank insignia and any badges and medals the soldier has received.

The Field Combat Uniform is an exceptional leap in clothing technology. It has a four-color digital camouflage pattern, the colors of which are changeable by means of a small current that can be run through the suit. Optical sensors observe the surrounding terrain, and change the colors accordingly. All soldiers in the field wear a combat helmet, a jacket with rank insignia, and name patches, which fits over low-profile body armor, and trousers with thigh storage pockets and room for low-profile leg armor. All troops are issued black combat boots, which also serve as the source of the uniform's camouflage power source. The Field Combat Uniform can be worn under powered armor.

=== Vehicles ===

Infantry are the center of Marine combat, but they are usually closely supported by light [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]] and [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]]. The [[M-4 Badger|Badger Light ATV]] is the most commonly used for scouting, light support, and rapid cross-terrain transport. The [[M-21 Goblin|Goblin Medium Infantry Fighting Vehicle]] is designed to fill the same role, but is slower, carries more troops, and has more weaponry.

[[:Category:Tanks|Tanks]] play a major role in the Marine Corps, but does not use the heaviest weapons like the [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Legion Corps]] or the [[Mesarthrim]] [[United Centusi States Heer|Heer]]. The Marines use the [[M-23 Dragon|Dragon Main Battle Tank]], which is designed to cover terrain faster and with more maneuverability than most, but lacking the wide arc of firepower of a tank like a [[M-15 Condor|Condor]] or a Vulture.

=== Aircraft ===

While flight combat is the field of the [[Prefecture Air Force|Air Force]], the Marine Corps maintain a large Air Corps. The [[UH-3D Skyhawk|Skyhawk]] helicopter is the backbone of the Marine Air Corps, allowing Marines to be carried across the battlefield with great speed.

The [[AH-4C Lightning|Lightning]] helicopter is the main support aircraft of the Air Corps. It is used in close combat situations, and can provide excellent fire support under most mission profiles.

The [[F-11D Stuka|Stuka]] heavy assault fighter does not see extensive use, but is the only strike fighter used by the Marines. It is usually armed with heavy air-to-ground weapons, but is occasionaly used for air-to-air combat when the [[Prefecture Air Force|Air Force]] is unavailable.

Following the [[Tempest War]], the Marine Corps began searching for an attack fighter that could protect their drop ships during assault operations.  Finally, the Marine Corps decided on the [[Mesarthrim]] built [[Km-4 Tischler]].  [[Tavou Imports Trading Group]] was selected to modify Tischler airframes for the Marine Corps into the [[Km-4_Tischler#F-17_Headhunter|F-17 Headhunter]].

=== Warships ===

The Hunter Amphibious Assault Cruiser and the Ranger Light Amphibious Cruiser are the weapons that make the Marine Corps unique to the Prefecture military. They are full-fledged [[:Category:Warships|warships]], capable of atmospheric flight and troop insertion. They are designed to fight their way into a target world, launch troops, and stand by to retrieve them if necessary.

=== Training ===

Training Marines for the Corps begins with eight months of Basic Combat Training, followed by Advanced Training in their primary job field at any of the training schools within the Prefecture. The length of time spent in Advanced Training depends on the job of the Marine. Fighter pilots have the longest training outside of the Special Forces, at one year.

Marines wishing to earn a commission as Officers must attend Officer Training School. Academies are only open in times of peace.

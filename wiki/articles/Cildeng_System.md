The '''Cildeng System''' consists of the star Cildeng, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Cildeng, the planets are Cildeng I, Cildeng II, [[Torimur]], Cildeng IV, Cildeng V, Cildeng VI, and Cildeng VII.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Cildeng is the Cildeng System's only star. Cildeng is a class G4V yellow dwarf. The Cildeng System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Cildeng is part of the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Torimur ===
[[Torimur]] (1.04AU) was once inhabited, but is now a desolate radioactive wasteland.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Cildeng System was discovered by [[Jerome Black]], who named every object of value in the planet after himself in some way. Most names were taken from his wife [[Aydan Black|Aydan]]'s central-Asian heritage: the star was named Cildeng, meaning "jet black" in [[Aydan Black|Aydan]]'s native Mongolian. The main colony was named [[Torimur]], meaning "black and shiny."

While the Cildeng System resulted in the rise in wealth and power of the Black family, it was largely meant to be a free world: the first [[Terran]] colony free of the rule of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

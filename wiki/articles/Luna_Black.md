'''Luna Black'''
*Born: December 18, [[2012]]
*Place of Birth: [[Luna]]
*Allegiance: None

''Luna Black'' (born December 18, [[2012]]) was a genetic experiment to mate a human conciousness to a machine processor. She was the [[EI]] known as Luna. Years later, [[Lucius Black]] opened Luna's casing and discovered her inside. He gave her the name "Amelia Black" and raised her as an Omega agent.

==Birth==

The eccentric but brilliant engineer, [[Miles Hlow]], began creating the [[EI]]s in 2006, when he set up the permanent base on [[Luna]], the moon of [[Terra]]. Luna was the youngest of the EIs, and was raised to understand the concepts of long-term strategy, deep space navigation, and colonial sciences. Hlow hoped to expand to the stars using "sleeper" ships, and Luna was meant to be the core of the slow-ship fleet.

==Exile==

The Hlow family fell out of favor with the [[United Earth Alliance]] government, and they moved to the [[Mars]] colony, where [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]] continued his father's work. After the [[First Exodus War]], Hlow saw an opportunity to fulfill the family dream of forging new frontiers, and he volunteered his three EIs to run the exile fleet. Hlow himself accompanied the exiles in what became known as the [[First Exodus]].

==Second Exodus==

Hlow's plans failed to come to fruition, and his intellect was put to work by [[Lucius Black]] in designing cold sleep chambers and [[hyperspace]] drives to return to [[Terrans| Terran]] space in what would become known as the [[Second Exodus]].

During this lengthy return journey, Lucius Black opened Luna's casing and discovered that Luna was actually a human, though a genetically and surgically altered one. During the capture of [[Grolk Kyhra]], Black removed Luna from her chambers and told Hlow that the EI had been destroyed.

==Daughter of an Emperor==

Knowing he would be found out if she kept her real name, Lucius Black renamed Luna "Amelia Black", and raised her as his personal agent. Luna was trained by [[Lavik Nighthawk| Lavik]] and [[Jarek Nighthawk]] in the arts of killing. "Amelia" became the most adept student at espionage, sabotage and assassination.

==Conclave==

When the [[Empire]] collapsed in [[2324]], Lucius Black told Luna to begin going by her real name again. By this time, Luna was a deadly killer, ending lives without mercy or regret. Lucius Black decided to give Luna the opportunity to lead the Omegas.

[[Lavik Nighthawk]] was summoned to the secret headquarters of the [[Conclave]]. Lavik was ordered to hand over the names and contact information of all Omega operatives to Luna.  Then Lucius sent Luna and Lavik Nighthawk to the capital city of [[Sepulare]], with orders to kill each other.  The survivor would be in command of the Omegas--assuming they weren't caught by the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police| Space Police]].

Luna not only passed the test, she humiliated Lavik Nighthawk and the Space Police. She shot Lavik in broad daylight--shooting him through the heart in order to leave his well-known face intact--with dozens of witnesses and ten Space Police officers present.  Despite the immediate effort to find and capture her, Luna evaded detection completely and returned to the Conclave.

==Betrayal==

When Lucius Black surrendured the Conclave to the [[Meroniri]], he betrayed all of his followers. Most were captured by the deadly [[Aseta Joreno]], but Luna Black led a starship to safety, with the help of [[Emil Torsc]].

With nowhere to turn, Luna went to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture| Prefecture]], and requested asylum. While many war criminals in the Omegas were imprisoned, Luna and Torsc were allowed to join the Prefecture.

Today, Luna leads the elite Legion Corps organization known as the Army Hunters.

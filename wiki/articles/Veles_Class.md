The ''Veles''' is the smallest warship employed by the Reliquiae Astrum Classis. Under the Remnant classification system, she is considered a “light combatant” whose primary role is reconnaissance, raiding, and screen duty for the larger medium and heavy combatants.   

== History ==
The “Veles” Class started service with the Reliquiae Astrum Classis in the mid 8th Century BC and has been involved in one way or another in every action the Reliquiae Astrum Classis has taken since then.  

==Weapons==

The ''Veles'' is typical of Remnantine design in its weapons and capabilities.  The first weapon of choice comes in the form of the six Falarica Missile Launchers.  These launchers, which are strategically placed for utilization specifically during a “Shadar Brake,” offer the Veles the capacity to engage while enjoying the protection of the singularity created by the Gravity Wave Drive when attacking in the preferred frontal firing arc.  

Whenever closer ranges dictate an energy engagement, the Veles is will equipped with 7 emitters, the standard Remnantine energy weapon.  This weapons system utilizes a layer in the gravity cone created by the Gravity Wave Drive to allow for instantaneous omni-directional engagement of targets numbering from a single ship to hundreds of smaller fighters, drones or missiles through a variety of firing modes.  

When engaging a single target, the gravity cone morphs itself to allow for the creation of a single beam of energy.  This beam is created from energy delivered to the Gravity Wave Drive’s weapon layer via the seven emitters.  The beam is most powerful whenever the least change is introduced into the gravity cone (i.e. the polar-opposite of the location of the drive’s singularity) but is capable of firing from any area on the gravity cone for very powerful results.  

When engaging several targets, such as fighters, drones, and missiles, the entirety Gravity Wave Drive’s weapon layer stores the emitter’s energy for fractions of a nanosecond and then releases it in either all directions or a specific arc simultaneously.  This effect has often been compared to a miniaturized and controlled version of a star going super nova.  This firing mode’s range is determined by how much energy is introduced into the weapons layer and is very effective at totally clearing the space adjacent to the Remnant vessel and can be done several times a minute at the cost of not being able to fire more powerful beams at singular targets. 

==Defenses==

The Veles utilizes the standard Remnantine defense package, which includes the Aegis Quantum Intelligence Field, the Valadis Shield, Neutronium Armor, and a variety of stealth systems such as the Eris Gravity Jammer, Hecate Cryo-arithmetic Cloak, and a Mimetic Coating.  

The first line of defense, as is the case with all Remnant vessels, is stealth.  The continued survival of the Remnant depends upon being undetectable and all their vessels reflect this.  It would not be an overstatement to claim that the only time a Remnant vessel is detected by another race is whenever it desires to be detected.  But the Remnant is far from requiring only stealth for protection.  The Eris Gravity Jammer works within the Gravity Wave Drive to hide the vessel’s gravity signature while the Cryo-arithmetic Cloak hides its heat and the Mimetic Coating its optical signature.

The shielding systems of the Remnant are the most advanced in the known galaxy.  The Aegis Quantum Intelligence Field actually learns and adapts to enemy fire and is backed up by the Valadis, which is a more conventional form of shielding tied into the Gravity Wave Drive, unlike the Aegis.  

The inclusion of the Valdis Shield was done primarily as a backup to the Aegis in the event that the Aegis needs to be jettisoned, which occurs after several hours of combat whenever the Aegis is in danger of becoming sentient and destroying the Remnant vessel.  This is not to say that the Valdis is not a brutally effective defensive shield in its own right, it just lacks the adaptive properties of the Aegis and its use, in some circumstances, hampers acceleration rates.    

The last line of defense for the Veles comes in the form of Neutronium Armor.  This armor is actually a foam composite which, while very effective against the majority of attacks, is vulnerable to Ngrligru energy weapons and hyper-penetrating warheads     

==Propulsion==

The heart of a Remnant vessel is its propulsion system.  No other race in the known galaxy has been able to develop a means of propulsion with even a fraction of the synergistic effects of the Gravity Wave Drive.  The Gravity Wave drive not only provides propulsion, it also provides the majority of both the stealth and weapons capabilities onboard a Remnant vessel.  Titanically powerful spatial-manipulation generators at the heart of the drive weave a net (or “wedge”) of stressed space around the equipped ship; the patterns, overlaps, and interactions of these stressed-space bands allow for a multitude of functions.

The “wedge” of gravitic distortion that the Gravity Wave Drive produces is referred to as a ‘gravity cone.’  The open end of the cone (which is visible as a field of distorted starlight, several kilometers in diameter at its widest point,) always points in the direction of the ship’s acceleration.  The ship itself is nestled just in front of the cone’s point.  At the ‘point’ of the cone – which would be directly astern if the ship was accelerating straight forward – is a small singularity.      

==Crew Complement==

As with all Remnant vessels, the crew requirements of the Veles are small compared to comparable ships in other races’ navies.  This is accomplished through heavy automation.  The 60 Marines on board are actually backed up by thousands of drones in the event of a boarding or raiding action.

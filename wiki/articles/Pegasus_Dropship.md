The Pegasus Dropship entered service in [[2301]] and is still in service with numerous armies across the galaxy.  The Pegasus became widely known as a tough, reliable, light troop transport and cargo shuttle.  Capable of both atmospheric and space flight, the Pegasus has the flexibility needed by dropships.  The Pegasus also has egress/ingress points on five of its six sides, allowing for hard lock docking in any configuration.

==History==
The Pegasus was designed by [[Centusi Cargo & Carriers]] in 2300 for civilian service.  By 2300, rapid deployment of the Mesarthrim [[Heer]] had been replaced by large moving armies sweeping across the planet.  Initially, the Pegasus proved unpopular amongst civilians, as the vessel consumed enormous amounts of fuel.  So, in a desperate attempt to pull some cash flow back into the company, Centusi Cargo made a bid to the Mesarthrim [[Kreigsmarine]] offering the Pegasus as an assault shuttle.
The Kreigsmarine decided to give the vessel a try.  The Pegasus was up armored, and given a more powerful engine.  In it's first combat engagement the Pegasus proved its worth, thirty squads of [[Myt'herra]] overpowered a [[Anderung]] [[Morningstar]].
Once the Mesarthrim entered the [[Second Exodus War]], Centusi Cargo decided to make the Pegasus available to the Mesarthrims' allies.  The decision led to a massive boom in production.  As the orders grew, Centusi Cargo grew its operations, and refused to contract the construction to other companies.  This led to massive shortages of the ship, and the cost skyrocketed.
Finally, in [[2323]] Centusi Cargo awarded [[Parahs Forak Modar]] exclusive construction rights.  The production boomed, and the Allied market became absolutely flooded with Pegasus'.  The Pegasus began showing up in armies and navies across the sector.

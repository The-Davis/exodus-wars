Set after "A Show of Bad Faith"

by James Davis


=====The Laojiu District=====
     By the night of May 10th the citizens of Härdad are beginning to doubt that the Prefecture will ever be able to repel the invading Mesarthrim. Several bombing runs have gone unmolested by the Prefecture Air Force, and nearly a hundred people have died each day. Icronian and Terran civic leaders hold a town-hall meeting to discuss surrender.
     The majority still refuses to surrender. Surrender is a military choice, but the civilian leaders refuse to even put together a referendum requesting the Prefecture withdraw. A handful of dissenters in favor of surrender are shouted down and leave city hall in disgrace.
     It is their salvation. Since the meeting began, a Mesarthrim agent has been watching. As soon as those in favor of surrender leave, a precision-guided air-strike levels Härdad city hall.

'''Ollivian's Crossing''' is the capital city of the [[Auellal League]].  The central structure of the city is the ''Ollivian Caisteal'', the Castle of Ollivian.

Ollivian's Crossing was built using a vast array of prefabricated structures.  This left the capital with virtually no structures over three stories tall.  Additionally, there is little organization in city planning.

Ollivian's Crossing is the second largest city on [[Caledon]], and the eighteenth largest city in all [[Mesarthrim]] space.

==The Ollivian Caisteal==

The [[Ollivian Caisteal]] was an oppulant structure built from foreign stone.  Its massive structure had fallen into disarray by the time the [[Auellal League]] had arrived on [[Caledon]].  

In [[2322]], at the start of [[Auellal Resistance|the ''Resistance'']], [[Steven Cue]] made several secret trips to [[Caledon]] with Archeologist [[Terrance Thatcher]] and [[Clan General]] [[Marcus Henkel]].  Their goal was to lay the groundwork for the eventual espionage and sabotage efforts against the [[Wingers]] and the [[Kinsmen]] political parties.

An extensive underground command and control structure was built, and one of the most extensive cryptology centers in [[Mesarthrim]] space was built and housed in the castle.

After the death of [[Andreas Tischler]] and the inevitible destruction of the [[Fair Doctrine]], [[Steven Cue]] began to expand the operations in ''Ollivian Caisteal'' to act as both the military nerve center and capital building of the [[Auellal League]].

Today, the structure is a bustling hive of activity as the [[Auellal League]] continues to grow and react to the [[Tempest War]].

==Downtown Ollivian's Crossing==

The downtown portion of Ollivian's Crossing is nestled around the east, south, and western edges of the ''Ollivian Caisteal''.  The northern edge is the southern edge of the [[Griffin Mountains|Gnos Caigeann]].

Downtown Ollivian's Crossing has the highest population density in the city, and has the most tightly packed structures as well.  This close-spaced construction was mainly due to the lack of adequate anti-missile screens and defensive shielding to cover a metropolis-sized city. The population built as much as they could within the safe zone.

As the [[Mestharim Civil War|civil war]] came to a close, the daily raids by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] came to an end and Ollivian's Crossing expanded beyond its defensive perimeter to its current area.

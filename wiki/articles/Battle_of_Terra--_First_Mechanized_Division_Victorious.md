May 27, 2322

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cerberus Company--the elite Prefecture Rig unit led by Captain Travis Carpenter--is known throughout human space for their skill and bravery. Today they saved the First Mechanized Division from destruction.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The First was pinned down by Imperial artillery from the moment they landed in southern Florida. Carpenter's Cerberus Company charged into the surrounding forest, dodging the heavy bombardment. Once within the minimum range of the artillery, the Firefly Rigs of Cerberus Company played cat and mouse with the heavier Shadow Cat and Hunchback Rigs of the Imperial forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Finally, Carpenter took one lance of Fireflies and came out of the forest right next to the Catapult artillery Rigs. His lance opened fire, and they destroyed five of the twelve Catapults before help could arrive. When the Imperial Rigs pulled out of the woods to take out Carpenter, the other two lances of Cerberus company joined him. With twelve Fireflies on the field, Carpenter's team swiftly dispatched the remaining Catapults and escaped into cover again.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial Rigs gave chase, but were ambushed by the now-freed Medium and Heavy Rigs of the First Mechanized Division. The battle ended in fifteen minutes. In all, Carpenter killed three Catapults, and the rest of Cerberus Company killed the others. The Royal Imperial Army lost a full regiment of Rigs, medium and artillery. The First Mechanized Division only lost a single company of medium Rigs.

'''Holt''' is the third planet in the [[Lyrae System]]. It has a population of approximately 3.4 billion people, mostly of French or English descent.

== History ==

Holt is one of the oldest colonies of [[Earth]]. It was first colonized in [[2027]], and was one of the stronger members of the [[United Earth Alliance]] from [[2027]] until [[2315]].

In May of 2315, Holt was invaded by the [[Empire]]. It was taken within the month, but an undercover resistance movement was formed by [[Brennan Connor]], and they were a thorn in the Emperor's side until the planet gained its freedom in [[2323]].

With the [[United Earth Alliance]] destroyed, and the citizens of Holt used to [[Empire|Imperial]] occupation, the most logical move was to join the [[Prefecture]].

Holt under Prefecture control is thriving. Before the war, Holt had no [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates]], but two were built in 2324, one to [[Ward]] and one to [[Ueda]]. Usually a world within a district only gets a gate to the capital of the district, but Holt's military officer academy has earned it a direct link to Ueda for easy access by the military.

The industry of Holt is strong. Reactors and electronics in particular are exported to other markets. The industries are attracted by the military academy, and the families of the trainees give the companies a large pool of able workers.

The natural resources of Holt are pretty slim. Heavier materials for weapons and industrial machinery manufacturing are not common. Most of the asteroids in Holt's belt are rocks, not metal. Holt is pretty arid, so its food production capacity is limited. Holt imports most food from the Laojiu District.

== Geography ==

Holt is dry compared to most worlds its size. It has four continents, which are divided by yawning chasms rather than by seas or oceans. The water of Holt is found mostly in smaller inland "great lakes." The largest continent is Ariège, which is the location of the capital. The smaller continents are Ardèche, Yvelines, and Sarthe.

=== Ariège ===

Ariège is the largest of the four continents, covering 140 million square kilometers. It is home to the largest of the great lakes, Lac Aveyron, which is 8.05 million square kilometers. The capital city of Holt, [[Écarlate Fontaine]], is nestled in a pass of the Montagnes Rusées mountain range, where the Fleuve de Carmin river flows from the upper peaks into Lac Aveyron.

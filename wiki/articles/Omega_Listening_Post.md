The Message Intercept Base was the first Omega installation built.  One was secretly built on Pluto as a listening station, to prepare for the Imperial invasion of Terra.  Initially, the installation was a self-contained base, with aircraft and built-in defenses.

Modern Message Intercept Bases are larger than the 2315 model, and have more sophisticated computer systems.  The modern systems can decrypt intercepted messages at a rate of ten transmission minutes per hour (i.e. an encrypted one-hour full-video message would take six hours to decrypt).  The installations can be networked to tackle super-large decryption jobs.

[[Image:OmegaListeningPost.jpg]]

Normally, the Message Intercept Base is hooked up to a Berkeley reactor, but in the event of an emergency, it has a small self-contained generator.

The Prefecture deploys networks of Message Intercept Bases throughout their territory.  Dozens are placed at border world listening posts, and a large cluster is installed on their capital world of Ueda.

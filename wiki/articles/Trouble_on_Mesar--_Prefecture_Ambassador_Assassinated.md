September 11, 2324

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kim Ali, the Prefecture Ambassador in charge of coordinating the Mesarthrim peace talks, was assassinated. The assassins eluded capture, and the peace talks have ended. Both Mesarthrim factions have withdrawn into their own territory, with nothing resolved.

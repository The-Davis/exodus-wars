The '''Avior System''' consists of the star Avior, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Avior, the planets are Avi I, Avi II, [[Casere]], [[Labrea]], Avi V, Avi VI, and Avi VII.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Avior is the Avior System's only star. Avior is a class G2W yellow dwarf. The Avior System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Avior is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Casere ===

[[Casere]] (0.9AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Avior System.

=== Labrea ===

[[Labrea]] (1.2AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Avior System.

== History ==

The Avior System was colonized in [[2195]].

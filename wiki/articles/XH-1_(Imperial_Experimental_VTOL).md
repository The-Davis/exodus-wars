The XH-1 was the first Imperial hybridized VTOL that could work without the use of rotor blades. It used a variable-thrust Ramjet with afterburners, coupled with a particle shield to act as an invisible wing. While it was equipped with an ion propulsion system for space maneuvering and thrust, it could not carry enough reaction mass for significant space operations.

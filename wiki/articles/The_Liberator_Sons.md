The Liberator Sons was a group of Myt'herra and Myt'kampfen Kaptain's who rebelled against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clan Lords]]. The Liberator Sons became allies of the [[Auellal Political Party]] in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]]. The Liberator Sons refused to evacuate [[Mesar]] with the [[Auellal League|Auellal]], preferring to stay behind as a fifth column fighting against the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]]. By [[2329]] the last Liberator Sons stronghold was captured by Centusi Military Police.

Kaptain [[Peter Wasp]] was captured in their final stand, and flayed in the traditions of the old traitors.

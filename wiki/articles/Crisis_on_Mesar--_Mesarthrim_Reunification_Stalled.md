February 23, 2324
by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====

     Today Uber-General Eric Francis was interviewed by B-Net correspondent David Sevall on the recent withdrawal of Mesarthrim forces from the engagements with the Volucris swarms. 

     David Sevall: "Good evening Uber-General. Thank you for taking time from your schedule. Can you tell us why Mesarthrim forces have been called home?"
     Eric Francis: "Unfortunately, there is an organized rebellion underway, and the rebels have little regard for civilian life. I've needed every soldier and ship at my disposal to keep our union alive.
     "Impossible as it seems, many people in the military still believe Nathan Hinks was telling the truth, and is the rightful Uber-General. Others simply don't trust me or my leadership."
     David Sevall: "How does the threat of the Volucris affect your plans?"
     Eric Francis: "As far as we can tell, they aren't threatening any Mesarthrim territories yet. I'll continue to send forces to assist our allies when I can, but it will only be special forces instead of full troop regiments.
     "Several Commandants took the opportunity to declare themselves independent of my control during the Battle of Mirach, and they escaped into deep space before forces could be mustered to catch them.
     "The best-organized group of rebels are calling themselves the Allied Auellal League. The United Mesarthrim Clans will not recognize them as a legitimate governing organization, but we've decided to release the name in order to receive help from the civilian populace in tracking down our enemies."
     David Sevall: "Uber-General, I understand that some Commandants are operating independently of both the United Mesarthrim Clans and the Allied Auellal League. What is the position of the Mesarthrim High Command? Are they a threat?"
     Eric Francis: "Unfortunately, they are behaving like common pirates and are raiding merchant convoys in Mesarthrim territory. High Command has placed a bounty on their heads, and will repay any merchant for losses incurred by these pirates. Even if I have to reimburse merchants from my own pockets, I intend to see that commerce is not cut off."
     David Sevall: "I have two more questions sir. Will you be withdrawing any of your support to the Terran relief effort, and can you tell us why an Archadian fleet is in the Mesarthrim starsystem of Tincomalee?"
     Eric Francis: "We will continue our financial support of Terra, but unfortunately our medical personnel have to be recalled. The nature of the Archadian presence on Tincomalee is classified, but I can assure you that we are currently friendly towards the United Archadian Republics, and that they are no threat to civilians."

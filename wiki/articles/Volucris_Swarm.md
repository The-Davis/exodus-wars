Mysterious, alien, unknowable. The [[Volucris]] are perhaps the most terrifying of all the species humanity has had contact with, simply because they embody ''alienness'' in a way that even the ravenous [[Ngrligru]] shy away from.

=== History ===

The sum of known Volucris history comes from the limited contact they have had with the human races.  [[First Contact]] was made in a series of raids known as the [[Volucris Incursion]] in [[2324]] and [[2325]].  The swarms worked their way through the [[Pelagrim Sector]], the [[De Mairan Sector]] and the [[Njiorin Sector]].  Then they disappeared.  The swarms were not seen again for eight years, when the [[Volucris War]] began.

The Incursion was a series of raids, where the Volucris sent upwards of a million warriors at a time at an objective and then retreated into deep space. Most lasted no longer than a few hours.  The longest was their first land-fall on [[Mirach]], where they stayed for a few days before moving on.  The Volucris War lasted almost two years.  The Volucris landed on [[Lorithan]] and systematically destroyed everything human before destroying the planet itself.

Many strategists believe it is only a matter of time before there is another Volucris Incursion or another Volucris War.  The motivations of the Volucris remain largely unknown and completely alien.  Several expeditions into the [[Scutum Crux]] have been proposed in an attempt to locate the Volucris homeworld, but the Crux as the Volucris' point of origin is mere speculation.  No expedition has been launched to date.

=== Biological Directive ===

The Volucris do not appear to have a traditional government, though there do appear to be individual Volucris of greater importance than other parts of the Swarm.  Xenobiologists have classified the leadership structure of the Volucris as a "biological directive."

There is talk of a possible entity known as "[[The Otherness]]" which may govern the Swarm, or may *be* the Swarm.  There is insufficient data to be certain. Whatever it may be, The Otherness is an alien mind so frightful and different that even many Volucris, alien as they are, seem to fear its power and influence.

=== Military Operations ===

The Volucris Swarm has engaged in two major military-style operations against humanity, known as the [[Volucris Incursion]] and the [[Volucris War]].  It is unknown if the Volucris themselves see these actions as military in nature, or if they even understand war in the same way that humans do. Their minds are so alien that no human can comprehend their motivations or actions. When they arrive, they kill and lay waste to everything in their path, then leave again.

The swarm travels through space, immune to the perils of vacuum and radiation. Only The Otherness knows their ultimate goal, and the swarm continues on its relentless path.

On the ground, the Swarms are almost unstoppable. They have been slowed down, redirected, and hurt, but never stopped out-right. If a million of them fall, they have billions and trillions more to back them up. From the lowly Nymph to the gargantuan Imperator, these relentless creatures will not stop until they are victorious or annihilated. Cutters, Racers, and Hunter-Racers fly through the ranks of infantry, cutting and burning. Bombardiers, Tyrants and Blisters rain spikes and plasma from vast distances. Slicers take on tanks and heavy war machines head on, while Infiltrators and Borrowers sneak around the battlefield on errands of their own. Burrowers dig massive tunnels under the battlefield, while Trappers lurk in the shadows to tear apart the unwary. Even walled cities are not safe from the enormous Stomper, a breed that can tear down buildings and walls with its monstrous bulk.

Volucris are immune to harm from all forms of EM spectrum radiation, but can be vulnerable to particle radiation. The body of a Volucris absorbs most forms of energy. In space, they cannot be detected on UV or radar frequencies, and but have a strong IR signature. Due to their operating en masse, they are difficult to track individually. Gravimetric sensors can track individual Volucris, but only under deep space conditions: with planetary distortions, detection is impossible.

Unlike space breeds, which run on fission furnaces in their systems, land breeds run on chemical plants, which have an endothermic effect on their carapaces. Volucris are practically invisible in the infrared spectrum on the ground, showing up only as a faint shadow, barely visible. Larger breeds have chemical synthesizing plants within their bodies, but the smaller ones must supply themselves from "Refiner" breeds. Because of the huge amount of energy each Volucris consumes in a fight, they tend to consume the bodies of fallen Volucris to plunder their chemical reserves.

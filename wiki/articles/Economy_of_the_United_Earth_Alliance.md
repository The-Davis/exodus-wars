The [[United Earth Alliance]] had one of the most powerful economies in [[Terran]] history, and later one of the strongest in the [[Sol Sector]] for most of the [[22nd Century|22nd]] and [[23rd Century|23rd centuries]].

Towards the end of the [[23rd Century]], the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]]'s economy began to falter, and was in a full-blown recession by the time the [[Onyx Empire]] invaded in [[2315]]. Economists theorize that if not for the military campaign of the [[Onyx Empire]], the entire [[Sol Sector]] would have been plunged into an economic depression.

===Agriculture===

Agriculture was one of the weak points of the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]]'s economy. During its aggressive-expansionist phase, most "bread basket" colonies attempted to remain autonomous, and resulted in several harsh military campaigns. [[Bethija]] in particular was a major sticking point which agricultural worlds never forgot.

After the disaster and near-genocide on [[Bethija]], the [[United Earth Alliance]] adopted a policy of military protectorates, giving all colonies that did not wish to incorporate full autonomy with military protection in exchange for favorable trade policies. Most farming worlds agreed to the arrangement, but never to a fully favorable extent.

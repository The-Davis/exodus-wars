Dr. ''Reg Brenstein'' is an excellent scientist and military developer.  His specialty is weapons tech.

Brenstein held no loyalty to the [[Lucius Black|Emperor]], so when the [[Imperial Civil War]] began, he simply stepped to the side and waited for a victor to emerge.  When the [[Prefecture]] and the [[Alliance of Planets]] took control of [[Earth]], he offered his services to the Prefecture.

While he's completely trustworthy, Dr. Brenstein is loyal only to himself.  He follows through with his promises, and fulfills his contracts.  As long as the [[Prefecture]] pays him, he'll produce weapons and keep their military secrets safe.

'''Novaya''' is a binary gas giant system, and occupies the third orbit from Mu Crucis..

== History ==

Novaya's twin gas giants were charted and surveyed by the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2195]]. The atmosphere of the planets is made up mostly of argon and hydrogen, which were not in high demand.  There were no inhabitable planets in the Mu Crucis System, so no colonization efforts were made.  Additionally, the unique gravity of the binary planets made large ship navigation hazardous.

In [[2317]], the [[Onyx Empire]] constructed a naval navigation training center on a small space station at the centerpoint of the two planets.  The training center was taken over by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] in [[2324]].  It remains an active starship navigation training center, where new navigators learn to work with unpredictable gravity gradients in combat maneuvers.

The dangerous gravity gradients of the binary planet system makes a natural obstacle course and prime training ground for navy pilots and warship crews. Extensive precautions are taken to ensure that none are killed in accidents.

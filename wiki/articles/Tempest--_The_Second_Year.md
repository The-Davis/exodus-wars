Various unsorted posts from the first year of the Tempest War. All need expansion. 

== Heavy Water - 1/18/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The intelligence gathered by the Myt'gaul and their agents since landing on Sindh has been largely unsuccessful in determining what the Prefecture's main interest in Sindh is. They know that many highly classified shipments went out from Sindh before they began their invasion. The Egris River heavy water plants played heavily into the shipments, but the Mesarthrim only know their importance, not their use.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today, the Myt'gaul intend to destroy the heavy water plants one by one. Mesarthrim reactor technology has no use for the material, but they have no intention of allowing the Prefecture to use their own resources any longer. The Myt'kampfen cannot be diverted from Gwadar, so its up to a covert Myt'gaul mission to get the job done.


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Infiltrating the plant is not easy. A full frontal assault would be a disaster. While not designed as a military stronghold, each plant has a complement of armed guards and a series of machine gun towers. The Myt'gaul opt for a midnight infiltration, sneaking in through the lower drains. They take multiple precautions, but are detected by unusually vigilant workers. Unknown to the Myt'gaul, every worker is also a member of the Sindh Guard, and each is armed at all times.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The discovery leads to a brief gunfight, which ends with one Myt'gaul wounded and five Guard dead. The plant coordinator, an Inquisitor, is notified, and the battle is on.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Myt'gaul commando leader and the Inquisitor match wits in a daring run through the maze-like plant, with deadly firefights, booby traps set by the Myt'gaul, and Inquisitor bomb squads being airlifted in to try to disarm the explosives the Myt'gaul plant at every opportunity.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It is an exhausting ordeal, and even the elite of the elite, the unstoppable Myt'gaul, lose half their number. In the end, they are successful, escaping in ones and twos and remotely detonating their weapons. The plant is destroyed and swept away by the mighty Egris River.

== Great Elephants! - 1/27/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The winter on Sindh is both long and harsh, especially in the northern climates of the Azad continent. Locals usually hole up, traveling only when necessary and enjoying the comforts of home until the first thaw. The Myt'kampfen have taken a page from the local operating book, and have holed up tight.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Elements of local Guard units attack Myt'kampfen installations in Gwadar by night. Their machinery is useless, with an enormous heat signature in the snow and with difficult time operating. Instead, the Guard use an unusual and unique tactic, using the wooly Elephas Azad elephant to carry huge loads of mortars and to tow artillery guns.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The attack fails, but the use of local beasts of labor does not go unnoticed by the Mesarthrim, and they consider conscripting elephants for their own use.

== The Karachi Death March - 2/6/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Attempting to remain in Gwadar for the winter has proven dangerous for the overly-mobile Mesarthrim war machine. Sitting in the huge city has invited sabotage and guerilla attacks. Despite the cold weather and heavy snowfall, the Mesarthrim war machine is on the move again.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The long, trudging march from Gwadar to Karachi is dangerous with the caravan of tanks and heavy weaponry, but the Myt'kampfen know that bringing a fight to the fortified capitol would be suicide without their big guns, but they are unable to move it quickly through the snowy, muddy, uphill slopes along the way. Airlifting is out of the question with Prefecture fighters constantly flying by. Dozens of air raids each day take their toll on both sides.

== Karachi Outriders - 2/17/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As they near Karachi, the Myt'kampfen do not go unchallenged. Scouting elements of the Legion of the Serpent meet the forward light armor of the Myt'kampfen and several skirmishes are fought along the roads. Prefecture infantry set traps and retreat quickly, but cannot hope to stop the tide of Myt'kampfen.

== Battle of the Scythian Span - 2/24/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Scythian Span is a massive superhighway bridge, crossing between the huge island metropolis of Karachi and the mainland, over the deadly Egris River. Normally, this bridge is pulsing with motor traffic, carrying cargo and people all over the continent. Today, the Prefecture have turned it into a choke point that can stop an army.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Span is too important to simply destroy: over a billion Peculiums and thousands of hours went into its construction. Instead, it has been lined with light anti-personnel explosives, razor wire, shaped anti-tank charges, concrete barricades, automated gun turrets, machine gun nests, sniper hideaways, reinforced strong points, and a hundred other dirty tricks and traps.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Against the array of defenses is the might of the Mesarthrim war machine, fully mobile and well supported. The weather is so bad that air support on both sides is tricky, but the Mesarthrim have enough artillery and armor in place that they are unconcerned, and planning a swift victory.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fight begins slowly, with only a small column of light armor probing the outer defenses. A hail of anti-armor weapons fire blasts several to pieces, and the battle is joined in earnest.


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In four hours, the Mesarthrim manage to advance twelve meters. Driving armored vehicles onto the bridge makes them vulnerable to Prefecture fire. Infantry are unable to do enough damage to move forward. The Mesarthrim are forced to eliminate fortifications completely from range with directed fire, then move in on foot to clear the debris. It is slow, bloody work, but it is the only way to keep the bridge useable.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By nightfall, the Mesarthrim have secured fifteen meters of the bridge, and the few Prefecture attempts to retake the real estate have been thwarted.


== Major Setbacks - 3/4/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stuck in a slow advance across the Span, the Mesarthrim forces are struck from the north by the Legion of the Fox. The weather remains too poor to airlift troops, and the horrible terrain keeps the heaviest Prefecture weaponry from being moveable from Sialkot to Karachi. The attack is enough to seriously hamper the progress over the Span, but barely a threat to the Mesarthrim positions.

== Hammer and Anvil - 3/7/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The weather clears for several days, and the Prefecture is in a better position to take advantage of it. Their air forces seize control of the skies long enough to airlift in many heavy weapons, usually under cover of darkness.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By morning, artillery is set up all around the Myt'kampfen positions. Air strikes and ground sorties keep the Mesarthrim distracted until the artillery fires. Every battery fires on the Myt'kampfen forces, inflicting major losses, mostly mechanical, but still significant enough to halt the advance on the Span.

== River Assault - 3/13/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the Myt'herra in Adha wiped out or captured, the Prefecture Marines have run rampant through the Nipur Region. The Kriegsmarine in orbit bring down armored river boats to patrol the Nipur River, and Myt'kampfen to land and raid Marine camps as they are found. The Marines are taken by surprise twice, and two camps are destroyed, one with many deaths and prisoners taken to be sent back to Mesar.

== Jungle Counterstrike - 3/16/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Following the surprise attack on their camps, the Marines find and attack a supply base used by the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine river boats with mortars and anti-armor rockets. The surprise attack wipes out the base, and catches four armored boats moored and with no crew. Only twelve Kriegsmarine sailors and three Myt'kampfen are captured as P.O.W.s


== Strategic Concerns - 4/17/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim Heer, Kriegsmarine, Luftwaffe and Myt'gaul commanders meet to discuss the worsening situation on the surface and in orbit. The surface is particularly bad, with the Marines playing havoc with the occupation of Adha, and the agonizingly slow advances on Karachi being compounded by the increasing frequency of Prefecture air power getting into the sky.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Simply put, the Mesarthrim don't have the air power to match the Prefecture on Sindh, and without Myt'herra to counter the Marines, their operations in the Azad continent are unable to progress at optimum speed--too much is tied up in occupying Adha.

== Word from Home - 4/19/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While his commanders mull over what to do, William Camp sends a coded message to the Kriegsmarine admiral. Help is on the way.

== Supply Run - 4/24/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first of William Camp's promised supplies arrives in a convoy of over thirty Mesarthrim freighters. The vital replacement parts arrive, but are hit hard and fast by the Prefecture's new Seminole warships. A Prefecture Destroyer Group is something the Mesarthrim have not faced in combat before: nearly as fast as a frigate, and capable of shooting down a Battlecruiser if it hits first.

== The Supplies Must Go Through - 5/1/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The second Mesarthrim convoy, another thirty freighters, arrives in Sindh space. This time, the convoy is escorted by a dozen fresh warships and accompanied by three troop transports. The Prefecture Destroyer Group attacks again, only to be fought off. A Prefecture Seminole is destroyed, as are two Mesarthrim freighters, but the vast majority gets through. They bring with them a much needed asset: a new complement of 1000 Myt'herra.

== Airstrip Hunt - 5/6/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;So far, the Prefecture's success in defending Sindh has been from two major factors: harsh terrain, and air superiority when the weather allows. Now hundreds of Myt'gaul and Myt'herra commando teams intend to take away the advantage of air power, and comb the treacherous Telra Aji mountains in search of the Prefecture's numerous airstrips.

== Stalemate at the Scythian Span - 5/12/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fresh supplies air-dropped by the Kriegsmarine have given the Myt'kampfen a much needed break in Karachi. With adequate food, shelter, ammunition and spare parts; they have outlasted the Prefecture's reinforcements from the Legion of the Fox, who withdraw to Sialkot. The Legion of the Serpent, still firmly entrenched in the city and with a stranglehold on fully half of the Scythian Span, lacks the firepower to push out of Karachi to the east, and refuse to surrender the city by withdrawing to the west.

== All Out Assault - 6/5/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A full Prefecture Fleet attacks the Mesarthrim flotilla over Sindh, providing enough of a window for the Legion of the Dragon to drop into the Telra Aji mountains.

== Orders from the General - 6/9/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Carpenter orders the Legion of the Dragon to defend the mountains at all costs. There are vital components being manufactured in those mountains, and the Prefecture must keep the factories from falling into Mesarthrim hands.

== Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase I - 7/4/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Battle between Mesarthrim and Prefecture fleets is always an interesting affair. Both sides have powerful ships with excellent technology at their disposal. The Mesarthrim can travel between worlds with almost no delay between jumps, while Prefecture ships can make jumps that take much less time. Prefecture ships have uncanny accuracy and firepower at extreme distances, while Mesarthrim vessels can tear nearly anything apart at close range.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Until recently, the Prefecture's fleets have relied on smaller, faster frigate class vessels. Against the Mesarthrim, these ships have proven fatal to their crews. The defenses simply cannot withstand the Blazer weapons of the Mesarthrim, and their speed has not been sufficient to outrun the Mesarthrim.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today, the Prefecture has two new challenges for the Mesarthrim to face. Their frigates have gone, replaced by their new destroyers, with improved firepower and defenses, with little degradation in speed. The deadly First Fleet is arrayed against them in battle today, with the legendary Robert Marshall and Prism at the helm, and a highly advanced AI guiding the crews of every warship into battle.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The First Fleet attacks the Mesarthrim in orbit over Sindh, with some success. The Mesarthrim retain enough strategic advantage to avoid significant losses--only a pair of gunships--but do suffer damage to some of their cruisers and are forced to move their orbits.


== Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase II - 7/6/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two days into their attacks on the Mesarthrim fleet, the Prefecture appears to have made a blunder. Their destroyer groups attack in a consistent pattern, accelerating towards the Mesarthrim ship groups, firing enough to force them into less concentrated groups, and flying away again.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It doesn't take long for the Mesarthrim commanders to notice the pattern and begin planning a trap. Unknown to the Kriegsmarine admiral, Prism is behind the pattern, and has plans of her own.

== Breaking the Kriegsmarine, Phase III - 7/8/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine spring their trap, moving large numbers of ships on paths to intercept the Prefecture destroyer group. At first, it looks like they are succeeding--the destroyers are cut off from their usual escape path, and forced to fight through a series of firing lanes that destroys two of the Prefecture vessels and disables a third.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prism's trap is sprung, and she counter-attacks with frigates. The Mesarthrim ignore them at first, thinking them to be the typical under-gunned ships they've dealt with before, and only sending gunships to meet them. Before they realize the danger, the Mesarthrim cruisers are caught in Prism's net. The frigates are unmanned, and are all on collision courses with Mesarthrim warships. The Kriegsmarine admiral realizes his mistake and begins to break away, saving many of his ships in the process. Prism's attack is not the overwhelming success she hopes for, but 10% of the Mesarthrim cruisers are struck by unmanned frigates. Most of those are destroyed, but many are only damaged or disabled.

== The Thaw - 7/10/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sindh is not the most friendly planet in the galaxy, at least in terms of climate. The local winter has been harsh, making travel on foot and by road miserable, and making air travel a nightmare at best--suicide at worst. Now the winter is over, and the Mesarthrim have seized the initiative in the ground war.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Karachi's reprieve is over. Mesarthrim air power takes to the skies en masse, and with their combined arms fully integrated once again, the Mesarthrim move into Karachi at high speed, blasting their way into the city and slaughtering the defenders in brutal house to house fighting.

== Operation Kriegstadt: Öffnung - 7/12/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While taking the Scythian Span was a significant blow to the Prefecture's hold on Karachi, it was far from a death blow. The Mesarthrim general launches "Operation Kriegstadt", a multi-phase plan to take the city relatively intact.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two days of fighting allow the Myt'kampfen to take the first four blocks around the Scythian Span from the Prefecture, and artillery bombing from outside keeps Prefecture heavy war machines from counterattacking.

== Operation Kriegstadt: Mittlere - 7/20/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Myt'kampfen push on into the heart of Karachi, and the Legion of the Serpent falls back. The fighting slows when the Mesarthrim reach City Hall and the Governor's Office, which they hope to take completely intact. Myt'kampfen infantry sent inside are wiped out by Prefecture Army Special Forces, but Myt'gaul elements attached to the Karachi campaign are sent in. In two hour-long operations, the Myt'gaul soundly defeat the Special Forces.

== Operation Kriegstadt: Ende Spiel - 7/24/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Karachi finally falls to the Mesarthrim after months of siege, followed by a brutal campaign of urban warfare. The Egris River gorge has kept the Mesarthrim from fully circling the city, and the Legion of the Serpent manages to retreat to the west after suffering a loss of 20% of its fighting forces to death, capture and serious injury.

== Evacuation Under Cover - 7/30/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim have been mystified as to why the Prefecture has thrown away so many lives defending Sindh, which at first glance is a well-developed world, but largely self-contained and with no exports of military significance.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;What the Mesarthrim don't know is that the extensive Telra Aji mountains are honeycombed with hidden factories and mines, all vital to the Norfolk Project--the Prefecture's Naval Modernization program. In the months of the Mesarthrim invasion, the factories have been dismantled, and the components that could be transported have been loaded up and prepared to be shipped off world. The rest were destroyed to prevent the Mesarthrim from deciphering the new technologies at the Prefecture's disposal.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the Mesarthrim concentrating on the retreating Legion of the Serpent and with fending off the aggressing Prefecture First Fleet, only a few air patrols notice the evacuating transports. Their reports back to their superiors do not go unnoticed, but no resources are available to stop the evacuation. No challenge goes out against the massive airlift.


== Stopping the Rout - 8/3/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The loss of Karachi was a major blow to the Prefecture, and the significant casualties to the Legion of the Serpent have them running hard. Every hour in retreat costs them more in lives, and almost as importantly--machines. The heavy weapons the Prefecture Army relies on cannot keep up the breakneck retreat, and the withdrawal is rapidly turning into a rout.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Help was already on the way before Karachi fell. The Legion of the Fox, based in the city of Sialkot, has been forced to wait for the Egris River's snowmelt flooding to recede enough to allow river boats to bring the Legion of the Fox, along with all of its heavy weapons, to Karachi.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Their approach has not gone unnoticed or unchallenged by the Myt'kampfen. Many Mesarthrim Luftwaffe were launched, causing serious damage to the Legion convoy, but not enough to stop it. The Egris River, while no longer a deadly surge, still runs its course at high speed, moving the Legion's forces past the few land-based attempts to stop it.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Myt'kampfen general is well aware of the failure of his scouts and of the Luftwaffe to stop the oncoming Legion, but is unwilling to immediately abandon his advantage over the retreating Legion of the Serpent. Finally, he has no choice. The Legion of the Fox lands and marches at great speed, attacking the Myt'kampfen from the north as they pursue the Legion of the Serpent. The Prefecture Air Force meets the Mesarthrim Luftwaffe in the skies above, and in the course of the battle, the Myt'kampfen are driven back to Karachi. The cost is high to both the Legion of the Fox and the Legion of the Serpent, who move on to the west towards the Telra Aji mountains.

== Regrouping the Fleet - 8/5/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prism's tactics against the Kriegsmarine were only marginally successful. The Mesarthrim admiral recognized the threat of the unmanned frigates before her trap was fully sprung, and the First Fleet ended up taking more losses than planned. The entire Fleet is forced to leave Sindh to replenish its supplies and make repairs.

== Fires of Heaven - 8/7/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture Air Force has operated from hidden bases in the Telra Aji mountains throughout the campaign, and frustrated the Mesarthrim Luftwaffe to no end. For three months the Myt'herra and Myt'gaul have hunted down these hidden airstrips, and tagged them one by one.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today is the Prefecture's day of reckoning. With the First Fleet no longer harassing the Kriegsmarine, the Mesarthrim admiral is free to set up his fleet in stable orbits and unleashes a combined bombardment, simultaneously striking every Prefecture air base at once. There is no warning, and no escape. Every Prefecture fighter on the ground is destroyed, and only a handful are on patrol at the time. The few survivors are forced to fly on autopilot, without rest or hope of rearming themselves, relying on their nearly unlimited power sources to stay aloft, and their sensors to stay alive.

== Blitzkrieg Renewed - 9/20/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the Prefecture Air Force reduced to a handful of exhausted pilots with almost no ammunition, the Myt'kampfen are winning battle after battle, driving the two Legions kilometers closer to the Telra Aji mountains each day. The close support of the Luftwaffe makes every day a living hell for the Legions. Their courage and their health is nearly spent.

== Silent Jungle - 10/9/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While the Myt'kampfen have been busy clearing the Azad continent and securing Karachi against possible counterattacks, the Myt'herra have had their hands full dealing with Prefecture Marines waging a guerilla-style war in the Adha continent.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In early October, these attacks suddenly cease. Myt'herra combat patrols through the Nipur River region, an area believed to be the hub of Marine activity, find dozens of abandoned camps, but no Marines or Guard units. The peace continues uninterrupted--the Marines have withdrawn in secret.

== Cornered! - 10/31/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The long run from the Myt'kampfen has come to a disastrous end for the Legions of the Serpent and Fox. Both Legions have been forced into a box canyon in the Telra Aji mountains, and are cornered. The Mesarthrim begin a three day artillery bombardment and air strike operation to soften up the Legions for a final, mortal blow.

== Overwhelming Force - 11/1/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Myt'kampfen are ready to crush the cornered Legions, but their imminent victory is foiled by the sudden arrival of all six Prefecture Fleets. The Fleets' overwhelming numerical superiority quickly drives off the Kriegsmarine, and the Navy Air power knocks out the Luftwaffe, allowing for the timely evacuation of 55% of the Legion of the Serpent and 10% of the Legion of the Fox--the only free survivors of the two units.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Kriegsmarine jumps away, thinking to make a quick strike at New Albany, a vital and now presumably undefended Prefecture world, only to find the Prefecture Fleets have all returned to their patrol zones. The Prefecture Navy is no longer crippled by a period of multiple days between hyperspace jumps.

== The Weary Survivors of Sindh - 12/2/2326 - By James Davis ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sindh is now finally secure in the hands of the Mesarthrim. One month after the devastating campaign came to an end, the Mesarthrim can finally view the full horror of the hell that Sindh became for both sides.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For the Prefecture, the cost was beyond measure: two Legions reduced to fragments of their former power, Fleets damaged in vicious space battles, and an entire planet's worth of Air Force fighters destroyed. Their only consolation was the safe removal of the vital components to their Navy's new rapid hyperspace recharging systems.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim have seen their Myt'herra humiliated in battle once, and taken significant losses across the planet. Overall, they fared well, but had a fleet and an army tied up on a planet for over a year with relatively little in the way of strategic gain. They never expected the Prefecture to fight so long or so hard for a planet that seemed almost worthless except for factories with mostly civilian applications.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An uneasy population settles down to rebuild and come to grips with the Mesarthrim occupation.

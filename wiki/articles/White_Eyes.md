"'''White Eyes'''" is a racial slur targeted at the [[Mesarthrim]], referring to the lack of color in their eyes. The term is mostly used by the soldiers of the [[Prefecture]], due to their conflict with the [[Mesarthrim]] in the [[Tempest War]].

The '''Imperial Medal of Valor''' was the highest decoration awarded to members of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military. The citation had to be recommended by an officer of flag rank (such as Colonel) or higher, and required the approval of Emperor [[Lucius Black]].

=== Presentation ===

The medal was always presented to the recipient in person, though due to the war some recipients never had the medal presented to them, and many were captured or killed in action before they could receive their citation.

The citation read as follows:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Let it be known to all present this day that I, Lucius Black, Sovereign Lord of the Royal House of Black and Emperor of all mankind, do this day bestow upon my valorous and loyal subject, (name), the Imperial Medal of Valor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"In the course of executing their duty, my subjects are called to lay down their lives for the good of the Empire. As the testimony of my most loyal and honorable officers shows, today this (man/woman) has shined above all the rest.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"For courage, for fierce determination, and most of all for showing our enemies the wrath of our mighty Empire, (name) has the eternal gratitude of (his/her) Emperor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"As (he/she) has done, so shall you all do.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Long live the Empire!"

The Emperor's speech is traditionally followed by any attendants echoing back "Long live the Emperor!" and the recipient of the Medal of Valor echoing "Long live His Majesty."

=== Recipients ===

Over the course of the [[Second Exodus War]], several hundred [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] soldiers received the Imperial Medal of Valor. Nearly half received it posthumously, and another 10% were in POW camps when [[Lucius Black]] signed their citation.

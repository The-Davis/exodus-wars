Jagarr Raided - 4/6/2324 - by Ryan Wilhelm
Jagarr Orbit
     Jarek Nighthawk sits in the cockpit of his Rig. He is a member of the elite Omega Squadron, the last force in the Conclave to retain the former glory of the Empire. Jarek is preparing for Omega Squadron's first combat operation since the death of his late brother, Lavik.
     The Lorithos military storage world, Jagarr, is the target. That Amelia woman wants something here. It would help if she'd be more specific than 'nab every crate.' Jarek checks his ammunition--Conclave supply lines are notoriously unreliable--then calls the rest of the team. “Fire Team Alpha report.” The other Rig pilots respond, reporting their status. Jarek scowls at the status of the Rigs. Only his Bradshaw is equipped with the modern weapons of their hated Prefecture rivals. He sighs, and goes over the attack plan in his mind one more time while he waits for the dropship to land.

Jagarr ilitary Storage Facility
     On Jagarr, the Lorithos security team is getting its evening briefing from their commander. As usual, nothing was new. In fact, Commander Ocas Mertz delivered a briefing identical to the one he'd delivered for the last three months. Finally, he finishes, and heads for his quarters. As he leaves the command building, his headset buzzes.
     “Commander Mertz? This is Sensor Control. There's an unidentified craft landing near the base. It hasn’t responded to our signals. What are your orders?”
     Commander Mertz freezes in mid-stride. “Activate the infantry 'bots, and alert the ASTF.” he orders, then runs back into the command building.

One Kilometer Away
     The Conclave dropship touches down with a light whoosh of afterburners. The panel-doors swing open, revealing the payload of deadly--if old--Rigs inside. Jarek Nighthawk steps out onto Jagarr's arid surface. "Ugly place, but better than a year in the guts of the Iron Hand." he growls.
     His Rigs deploy, groaning as their ancient metal bodies strain to keep up with Nighthawk's modern weapon. Nighthawk silently curses the Emperor's failure to keep his forces upgraded.
     Nighthawk's communications system activates. “Sir, we're picking up a lot of Lorithos communication activity. They know we're here.”
     “I figured. We weren't exactly trying to be quiet. I forget, do they have Optical communications yet? Eh, forget it--jam every broadcast system. If they have Optics, we'll deal with it.” With the push of a few buttons, Jarek relays the news to the rest of his troops. "Let’s go hunting."
     Nighthawk's Bradshaw runs toward the outer posts of the base. The others follow. Jarek picks up several Lorithos defense turrets on sensors. He draws a careful bead on one target, then obliterates it with a single round of his Thumper Autocannon. The other Omega Rigs quickly eliminate the Lorithos defense grid, and charge into the base.
     "Spread out and look for munitions buildings! Old ones--biohazard."
     One of the Omega pilots responds. "Boss, there's something moving over there--got a sensor lock--hostiles."

Jagarr Military Storage Facility
     Commander Ocas Mertz's Zuura command suit rounds the corner of the warehouse, preceded by a swarm of Krelbines. The Omega agents scatter in the wake of the sudden counterattack--they'd been told to expect no resistance.
     Jarek Nighthawk quickly reacts to the enemy presence. “Engage at range! Down the 'bots, I'll take the command suit."
     A swarm of missiles streaks across the air, bridging the gap between Conclave Rigs and Lorithos robots. The pack of robotic infantry is subdued quickly, after managing to squeeze off only a few rounds of blaster fire.
     Mertz realizes that he's outmatched, but continues to fight anyway. He fires a volley of missiles at Nighthawk's Bradshaw, then backs away while firing his heavy blaster rifle. One shot from Jarek Nighthawk's Thumper cannons, and the Zuura is toppled. Mertz climbs from the burning wreck, wheezing from the smoke, and collapses on the ground.
     After eliminating the Lorithos defenses and troops, the Omega agents split up to perform their assigned duties. Half leave their Rigs to search the base for salvage--particularly Burning War era biological weapons. Other Rigs remain on guard duty, while more carry back salvage to the dropship. Jarek himself begins to plant heavy explosives throughout the base.

One Hour Later
     Jarek Nighthawk and his agents finish their tasks, and board their dropship. Nighthawk hails the Iron Hand, in orbit of the arid world. "We're ready to come home. I found a case of something I think Lady Black will be interested in--pretty complicated, if I read these labels right, but Lorithos was never my language."
The dropship lifts off, and the Lorithos base goes up in a fantastic fireball. "About time, Fire Team Alpha. A pair of Riiktar cruisers just entered the system. We don't want to risk direct space conflict yet."
     All around the planet, many more Fire Teams had completed similar missions--raiding ancient Lorithos Burning War weapons caches, and retreating before the main Jagarr garrison could respond.
     Only one man, Commander Ocas Mertz, survived the assault. He awoke, surrounded by the ruined remains of his command. He stares into the sky, and spits. "He who digs a pit will fall into it. Your weapons will turn in your hands. None can use the forbidden technologies without dying." Before long, a rescue team arrives to carry him to Lorithan to report.

The Iterin Corahl Deseraht is the eyes and ears of the [[Lorithos]] government. The brainchild of [[Mait Onega]] has developed a reputation as the greatest intelligence gathering organization in the galaxy. As the inventors of the Urokon, the [[Lorithos]] have the unique ability to listen in to all but the most secret communications, and by piecing everything together [[Mait Onega|Onega]] and his spooks manage to gather more than most would imagine.

While most in the [[Lorithos]] government do not like to admit it, [[Mait Onega]] has taken his organization along a much more sinister path than merely keeping an eye on everyone and everything. [[Mait Onega|Onega]] has ordered the assassinations of certain people who have subsequently disappeared without a trace. It is rare, and never condoned by the [[Dirken Huern]] or the [[Lorithos]] government, but on occasion the Iterin "takes steps" to ensure the security of [[Lorithan]] and the Colonies.

Iterin Corahl have small observer starships like the [[Adelisk Class Corvette|Adelisk Snooper Corvette]]. The Adelisk prowls the space lanes, looking for news to bring back to the spiders in the intelligence web. Just as sinister and important, but far less impressive are the simple maintenance robots and satellites that run the EPR chain of the Urokon Network. Least noticeable of all are the agents in the field. [[Lorithos]] in general are sickly and downtrodden: the dregs of every society they flee to. Poisoned beyond repair by their world, rejected in every attempt to incorporate themselves into new homes, they make unusually competent information gatherers and messages. All agents are inconspicuous and carry no special equipment. They are trained through excruciatingly difficult mind exercises and hypnotic suggestion to remember every minute detail.

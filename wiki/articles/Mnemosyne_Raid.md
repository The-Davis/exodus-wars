The Mnemosyne Raid was argubly one of the most important strategic operations of [[2322]].  Utilising an effective counter-intelligence scheme, [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] was able to dupe Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] into believing the next major offensive was coming to Mnemosyne.

==Overview==
When [[Andreas Tischler]] finally returned to [[Theia]], he brought with him the complete strength of the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group|Karminrote Gezeiten-Kampfgruppe]] and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]].  The [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] were en route, but had left significantly after the first two.  After Sanburn informed Tischler of the security leak, Tischler immediatly dispatched a fast courier to intercept Erste Flotte, and ordered them to maintain radio silence and to not approach any [[United Earth Alliance|UEA Planets]].

Sanburn and Tischler began circulating the rumour that the entirety of the Mesarthrim Navy had come to Theia, with only token vessels back home protecting against raids or pirates.  Then, they began extensive preparations for the Zweite Flotte and the 3rd Space Battle Group to retake Mnemosyne.  The Crimson Tide Battle Group would escort high capacity drop ships into the system after it had been secured.

Admiral Marshall fell into the trap, he wanted to crush the entire Mesarthrim Navy in one swift stroke.  Marshall moved almost the entire Black Armada to Mnemosyne.  He placed the entirety of the third fleet around Mnemosyne, and portions of the fourth hidden behind moons, meaning to cut off any retreat attempt.

==Alliance Assault==
Early on the 9th, the combined Mesarthrim/Titanian force descended on Mnemosyne.  As they expected, Marshall's fleet stood defiantly protecting the planet.  The initial missile barrage between the two forces was impressively lop-sided.  The much larger Imperial force had complete long-range saturation.  The Titanian ships hung back, unloading their missile payloads, trying to cover the Mesarthrim as best they could as the Mesarthrim charged towards the Prefecture lines.

Marshall was completely floored by the Mesarthrim's tactics, he had not expected a close ranged brawl, as most modern combat was solved at missile range.  However, the Mesarthrim ships lacked extensive missile bays, rather relying on their primary cannons and fighter squadrons for offensive punch.

Marshall called his units that were hiding behind the Alliance fleet into the action, surprising the Titanian vessels.  The opening barrage by the Imperial ships crippled a dozen ships, and destroyed five others.  Titanian commander, Captain William Reeves ordered a general retreat - causing the Titanian forces to abandon the Mesarthrim who were involved in a brutal slugging match.

As the battle wore on, vessel after vessel - both Imperial and Mesarthim succumbed to its wounds, breaking apart, or disintegrating in a ball of expanding gas.

==Aftermath==
In the end, the Mesarthrim were forced to withdraw.  The withdraw, however, was a bit of a quandry to Marshall, the Mesarthrim had taken a significant upper hand in the battle, chasing groups of Imperial ships, additionally their ships were significantly tougher then their Imperial counterparts, taking extensive damage, yet staying in the fight.
Despite this, the Mesarthrim did withdraw - they were ordered to stand in defence for 15 hours, and after that time, they were to withdraw, so the time had come to fall back.  The timing was vital, so that the Mesarthrim could be in place to pounce Cronus when Marshall returned.  Concerned that he had been duped, and that another Mesarthrim fleet might strike Mnemosyne, Marshall reorganised his carrier groups, and left his five least damaged Carrier Groups in orbit of Mnemosyne, and withdrew the remaining carrier groups back to Cronus - where unbeknownst to him, the actual battle was about to take place.

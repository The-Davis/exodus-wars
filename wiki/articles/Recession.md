Economic '''recession''' refers to a consistent drop in a [[:Category:Nations|nation]]'s Gross Domestic Product, or income. Long-term recession can lead to economic depression, or total economic collapse.

Recessions can be caused by sharp changes in prices, either through sudden deflation or sharp inflation. Most [[:Category:Nations|nation]]s have government bodies who either control or monitor their economy in an attempt to predict and deter recessions.

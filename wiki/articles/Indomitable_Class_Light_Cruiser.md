===Overview===

The Avenger Class Cruiser was so successful that many consider its low production numbers and high construction costs a liability to the Prefecture Navy.  Fleets rely too much on the heavy cruisers, and are at a tactical loss when they aren't around.

[[Image:IndomitableClassLightCruiser1.jpg]]

The Indomitable was built to fill the gap of firepower, armor, and numbers.  Rahn Industries worked on the prototype cruiser, and the design shows a lot of promise.

===Capabilities===

The Indomitable is well-armed and armored, but not quite like the warship-crushing Avenger.  Hellguns cannot be included on the vessel--they are too costly for a light cruiser.

The main punch comes from Naval Autocannons.  The Indomitable carries a lot more than the average frigate, but not as many as an Avenger (when its Hellguns are factored in) or a flagship.  The anti-fighter Neutron Cannon load of an Indomitable is numerous and very powerful.

[[Image:IndomitableClassLightCruiser2.jpg]]

The Avenger and Indomitable have identical missile armaments, though an Avenger can carry significantly more ammunition.  With eight missile tubes, an Indomitable still has plenty of options for combat engagements.

Indomitables are well-protected compared to frigates, and fast--though not as fast as frigates.  It can take firepower and give it back without having to withdraw early.  All in all, it is an admirable weapons platform and multi-purpose warship.

===Deployment===

The Indomitable itself was finished on March 2nd, 2324.  The line was released in the 2324-2325 overhaul of the Prefecture Navy.

[[Image:IndomitableClassLightCruiser3.jpg]]

In order to save costs, many Vengeance cruisers were replaced with Indomitables instead of Phalanxes.

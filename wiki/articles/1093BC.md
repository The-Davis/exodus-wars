== Events of 1093BC ==

[[Reigens]] in the [[Renskader]] arrive at a new world and claim it as their own. [[Raegos Direnson]] is crowned [[Kejsare]] of the new [[Reigess Suverände]]. The [[Raegard System]] is named in his honor.

''Njiorin Osrason'' was a [[Reigen]] explorer in the [[22nd Century]]. He is credited with the discovery of [[Njiorin's Well]], which is directly responsible for the prosperity of the [[Reigens]].

He was awarded a title and family name for his contribution, and the nebula was named after him.

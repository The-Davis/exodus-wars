===Overview===

The Curvicosta is barely above the Nymph in terms of direct damage potential, but its speed and reaction times are incredible.  Even advanced AI guidance systems can't follow some of its maneuvers.  This amazing speed has earned it the nickname "Racer."

[[Image:Curvicosta.jpg]]

The Racer's arsenal is three spines.  Unlike those of the Tyrant, these are not ranged.  Two are located in the forward thorax, and are used to skewer small targets in its path.  The third is located on its tail, along with a grappling claw.

This tail and claw are used to grab large targets, bore through the soft armor, and inject corrosive agents into the delicate internal systems.

Along with its blazing running speed, the Racer's reaction time is unmatched.  Even the most advanced guidance AI's can't track some of its reflex movements.  Downing one solo is impossible, as it can sense and dodge incoming weapons fire.  Only area-effect bombardments or two units attacking with crossfire can catch a Racer.

Aside from its lightning speed and reaction ability, the Racer is not very well protected.  Its carapace is relatively brittle, even when compared with weaker breeds like the Cutter.

The Racer's leg motion is so rapid that it raises the internal temperatures of the creature considerably.  It is believed that this internal heat further enhances whatever biochemical reaction that fuels the speedy beast.  Autopsies of Racers have been few and far between, as most are blasted into unrecognizable bits before they stop attacking.  Xenobiologists have deduced from their findings that unlike most insects, the Racer actually has an internal muscle system to move its limbs, instead of forcing extra blood into them to extend and retract.  This would be considered extremely unusual in a Terran or M-Tronian life-form, but xenobiologists have come to realize that unusual is normal with Buggers.

Racers are less common than most small breeds, but hardly a rarity.  They are most often used as the first wave in an assault on an installation or other target with static, automated defenses.  Turret targeting AI cannot track them properly, but it will continue trying to shoot them until ordered to choose new targets by base personnel.

It is very easy to forget something so simple in the heat of battle, and a slightly distracted gunnery officer may let his turrets continue the futile task of shooting at Racers while the more deadly Bombardiers, Tyrants, and Imperators close in and pound the base to rubble.

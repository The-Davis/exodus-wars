The '''Invasion of Earth''' was the series of battles fought between the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2315]]. The invasion marked the official beginning of the [[Second Exodus War]]. The campaign focused specifically on capturing the capital of the [[United Earth Alliance]], [[Earth]].

The invasion ended with the destruction of the official [[United Earth Alliance]] government, though the government survived in a provisional form on [[Theia]] until the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

== Invasion of Sol ==

While Earth and the capital of the United Earth Alliance were the main targets of the Empire's first military campaign, the [[Sol System]] in its entirety was not a soft target that could be safely ignored.  The [[Alliance Defense Force]] had major defense installations around [[Jupiter]], scattered throughout the asteroid belt, and on [[Mars]].

The Empire spared no forces when they launched their invasion.  Their control of the [[Alpha Centauri System]] and its worlds was still a secret, so Lucius Black and Anthony Hadrian kept little in reserve.  The full might of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], [[Royal Imperial Army]], and [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] were launched against the powerful Alliance Defense Force.

=== Contact: Jupiter Station ===

Jupiter Station was the first outpost to face the Imperial Armada.  Jupiter Station was the main system monitoring post, defended by dozens of cruisers and hundreds of fighters.  In the early hours of January 9, [[2315]], twenty fighters from the Royal Imperial Navy's Air Arm met the Alliance's Combat Air Patrol in a brief dogfight.  The engagement was relatively unimportant and brief, but was noteworthy for the fact that Alliance top ace [[Helen Cashaw]] earned her first three kills.

=== Battle of Jupiter Station ===

Six hours after the initial battle, Jupiter Station was on high alert.  At orders from Anthony Hadrian, the Imperial Armada made the Alliance forces wait. Hadrian withheld the attack order until local midnight.  The Alliance forces hailed the Imperial ships multiple times, but no response was given--a directive straight from Lucius Black.

When the attack came, it resulted in a massacre for the Alliance forces.  Jupiter Station itself succumbed to cannon fire in the opening salvo, killing hundreds of Alliance officers and throwing their local command structure into confusion.  The battle only lasted twenty minutes before the Alliance's morale broke.  Forty percent of their forces were destroyed, and half of the survivors fled the Sol System entirely.  The confusion and terror were unprecedented in Alliance history.

=== Passing the Belt ===

By the time the forces at Jupiter Station were broken, [[General-Commander]] [[Ken Hurke]] and his commanders managed to mobilize the rest of the Sol fleet, and prepared to meet the Imperial Armada in the Asteroid Belt.  Hurke remained on Earth itself, preparing for what he believed was an inevitable invasion.

The Alliance's defenses arrayed themselves around the [[Ceres]] transfer station, a central part of the Sol System economy and an important strategic target.  Hurke's commanders believed the Empire would move to secure it before passing the belt.  Their strategy did not take into account the fact that the Empire had no plans to capture the economic structure of the system intact.  The Imperial Armada bypassed the belt altogether and went straight to [[Mars]].  The Alliance ships were caught flat-footed and began a desperate full burn towards Earth.

=== Suppression of Mars ===

Mars was a significant stop on the Imperial invasion route.  Most of the Alliance's military supply depots and ammunition dumps, along with a significant portion of their ground troops' housing and training facilities were located on the surface.  The Onyx Empire managed to immobilize them by bombarding every spaceport on the planet.  They also took out a significant portion of Alliance ground assets from orbit.  The civilian population centers were left relatively untouched, though the city of Par Olympus was hit by several stray round when a nearby ammunition dump exploded.

Aside from quickly destroyed orbital defenses, Mars went undefended by the Alliance.  Fleet assets were still en route to Earth, and the bulk of their air assets were shot down before they could break orbit.

=== Blockade of Earth ===

On January 11, 2315, the Imperial Armada met the remaining Alliance fleet assets in high orbit of Earth.  Anthony Hadrian turned over tactical command of the Armada to [[Robert Marshall]], well known for his strategic skills and tactical genius.  Under Marshall's command, the Alliance fleet was defeated over the course of five days.  The Alliance command ship, the UEAS ''Valdosta'', was destroyed in the mid afternoon hours of January 16, 2315.  The few surviving ships were ordered to retreat to the [[Lyrae System]].

During the battle, many Alliance politicians and industrial leaders attempted to flee the planet. Sixty private ships were shot down, and a further two hundred were captured.  Each captured VIP was brought before Lucius Black, who ordered them imprisoned or killed depending on their status and potential value to the Empire.

== Invasion of Earth ==

After the orbit of Earth was secured, the Imperial Armada spent a full week of apparent inactivity.  Anthony Hadrian, Cecil Au-Yueng, and Marcus Rathbone worked out the details in their plans for a full-scale invasion of the planet.

General Au-Yueng decided on six different landing sites, mostly at sea for the convenience of the Imperial dropships, but close enough to ports to make landing easy on the ground forces. Marcus Rathbone's Air Forces were tasked with seizing air superiority and providing close air support.  Unlike Jupiter Station, Lucius Black wanted Earth captured relatively intact.

=== Initial Landings ===

The United Earth Alliance of the 24th Century was well known for censoring the media.  The first days of the Second Exodus War were completely blacked out from Earth's population.  After their defeat at Jupiter Station, President [[Fernando Meliazio]] believed that revealing the existence of an apparently unstoppable invasion force would cause needless panic.  The heads of the major networks concurred, and one of the largest cover-ups in the history of Earth was executed in flawless secrecy.

The secrecy ended when the Empire made its landings, but from then on the panic of the civilian population was the Empire's problem.

=== Securing a Beach-head ===

Royal Imperial Army divisions landed in the oceans outside San Francisco, New York, Antwerp, Rio de Janeiro, Abu Dhabi, and Belhi. In most cases, they quickly secured their seaports and were able to bring in millions of troops to begin the sweep of the planet.  There was one major exception.

==== Storming of San Francisco ====

The city of San Francisco was close enough to the Earth capital in Denver that Ken Hurke had stationed several divisions there. On January 23, 2315, divisions of the Royal Imperial Army came under heavy fire from Alliance troops firmly entrenched in the city.

The [[Storming of San Francisco]], as it became known, was actually a long, bloody campaign of city fighting.  The Imperial forces were badly outpositioned, but had the advantage of superior numbers, superior firepower, and full air support.

For three weeks, Lucius Black allowed General Cecil Au-Yueng to persist in conventional warfare.  Black wanted to test the effectiveness of his army when it was met on equal terms. The battle threatened to turn against the Empire many times, and several streets were like butcher's shops.  The Alliance forces never gained any kind of offensive momentum, but made the Empire purchase every block of the city with hundreds of lives.

Finally, in mid February of 2315, the last Alliance forces fled the city.  As soon as the Alliance troops were clear of the city proper, Imperial naval forces carpet bombed the area around the city, killing 95% of the Alliance survivors.

=== Pushing Inland ===

While the invasion had stalled at San Francisco, the other five fronts moved forward with lightning speed.  The New York divisions swept the eastern North American continent, and held at the Mississippi River.  The other four fronts met almost no resistance and they swept their continents clean by March.

=== The Fall of Denver ===

On March 25, 2315, the Royal Imperial Army divisions from San Francisco and New York linked up outside Denver, the capital of Earth.  General-Commander Ken Hurke had mustered nearly 130,000 troops to defend the city.

Despite the Alliance's best efforts, Denver fell in a single day.  Lucius Black authorized the full arsenal of the Empire, including nuclear weapons, orbital bombardments, and full air strikes.  The city was razed to the ground.

=== Death of the General-Commander ===

On March 26, 2315, General-Commander Ken Hurke was killed while leading a strategic retreat north towards Ontario, where he had hoped to stage a guerilla war against the Royal Imperial Army. All United Earth Alliance military assets on Earth were neutralized two days after his death.

=== Death of the President ===

President Fernando Meliazio was killed on April 11, 2315, when Royal Imperial Army elements stormed the Presidential bunker. One of the great mysteries of the early Second Exodus War is how the Onyx Empire discovered the bunker's location. A new one was built for each new administration, and it was the most closely-guarded secret of the United Earth Alliance. 

== End of Major Combat ==

The official surrender came on April 12, 2315, but the last shots were not fired until two days later.

== Execution of Officers ==

While not technically combat, every captured Alliance officer was brought to [[Memphis]] on April 14, 2315.  On the orders of Lucius Black, the [[Omegas]] shot every single one of them.  This was one of the first publicized war crimes of the Onyx Empire, and would mark the beginning of a long and dark war.

== Aftermath ==

Lucius Black declared himself the supreme Emperor of Earth and all of [[Terran]] space.  He established his palace and his capital in the city of [[Memphis]].

The bulk of the Imperial Armada and the Royal Imperial Army left Earth to continue the invasion, but several hundred thousand heavily armed troops were left behind to pacify the civilian population.

The Imperial occupation of Earth was a long, dark night in the history of the Terran people.

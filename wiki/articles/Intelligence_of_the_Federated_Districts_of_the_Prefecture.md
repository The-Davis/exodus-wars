National '''Intelligence''' must be acquired, analyzed, and distributed to the appropriate military, diplomatic, government, and law enforcement authorities.

The Prefecture only has military intelligence. They do not monitor civilian communications, and leave criminal deterents to the Federal Department of Law Enforcement. Military intelligence is all gathered and managed by the Central Office of Military Intelligence.

== Intelligence Sources ==

The Central Office of Military Intelligence maintains several sources of intelligence, from overt sources such as military recon data and surveillance, to covert sources such as field agents and foreign operatives.

=== Overt Intelligence ===

Overt Intelligence sources are the primary means the Central Office of Military Intelligence uses for gathering data. The [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] sends escorts with most convoys going to foreign ports, and the escorts conduct active scans of shipping lanes and the surrounding areas while in international space, and use passive sensors to collect data while in sovereign space of other nations. This method of gathering data is considered acceptable in most international circles, so long as they keep their active sensors turned off when in sovereign space.

The Central Office of Military Intelligence also receives all flight recordings, combat camera recordings, and sensor logs from any Prefecture military unit that travels through foreign territory or engages in combat.

=== Covert Intelligence ===

Covert Intelligence was the specialty of the [[Omegas]] under the [[Onyx Empire]], and the Prefecture did not have many sources of it until [[Luna Black]] and [[Emil Torsc]] led their mass defection into Prefecture space. Since then, the Central Office of Military Intelligence and the Army Hunters have begun training covert field agents to infiltrate enemy target areas and to integrate with foreign societies, and to remain entrenched for months or years at a time. Most importantly, the Omegas teach agents to join a foreign society without becoming emotionally attached, as they may be called on at any time to betray and murder their new neighbors.

== Intelligence Analysis ==

No matter how much intelligence is gathered, it is all but useless if it is not analyzed and distributed to the appropriate sources. The Central Office of Military Intelligence maintains an enormous thinktank within its headquarters on [[Canopus]], where all intelligence materials are analyzed and concentrated into briefings for military leaders.

== The Inquisitors ==

The "Inquisition" was a special [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] cell formed in the early years of the [[Second Exodus War]]. [[Anthony Hadrian]] tasked [[Norman Sergius]], a [[Torimur]] conspirator, with rooting out [[Omegas]] attempting to infiltrate the resistance.

The "Inquisitors" are now a small branch of the Central Office of Military Intelligence. They are the best at questioning prisoners to divulge information, using everything from psychology to all-out torture and maiming of prisoners. Much of what the Inquisitors do is a closely-guarded secret.

The Air/Space Intercept Missile designation 01, dubbed the "Javelin", was a computer guided missile weapon which could operate in atmosphere or in space. An operator was required to "lock on" to a target by fixing their sensors on the desired target for three to five seconds, then fire the weapon. The operator had to keep their sensors fixed on the target to send up-to-date coordinates to the Javelin missile, which only had an effective range of five kilometers.

Later models in the ASIM series incorporated internal sensors and could maintain their own locks.

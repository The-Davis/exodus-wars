The Howell Destroyer was the first true warship built by the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]].  Prior to the Howell, the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Resistance]] used upgraded traveller ships or prison ships.  With the [[Mesar Decree]] enacted by their [[Anderung]] overlords, the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Resistance]] had its first real taste of autonomy in government, and promptly began formulating plans to muster an armed force, including a navy.  The Howell was the first ship built for that navy.

== History ==

In [[2185]], after the [[Mesar Decree]], the former threat of [[Anderung]] enforcers on the streets was replaced by the lesser threat of [[Mesarnak]] overseers.  The [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Resistance]] used this shift in power to publicly speak out and organize against the Anderung.  Nearly all the [[Clan Lords]] felt it was imperative to fund and expand the newly-formed Heer (the Mesarthrim Army).

[[Adrian Tischler]] and [[Otto Stadt]] disagreed with the majority consensus, and instead pushed for construction of a navy.  Many of the Clan Lords feared that if the Mesarthrim threatened the Anderung directly in space, then they might return to [[Mesar]].

Adrian disagreed, stating that "Control of Space is just as vital as control of the Planet."  By [[2188]] it was obvious that Adrian Tischler would never get enough support to create an official navy, so Tischler and his friend Otto Stadt started the company [[Kriegshöfe]] and hired on a staff to design and build six destroyers for the Resistance.  Additionally, Kriegshöfe and the new "kreigsmarine" (the Mesarthrim space navy) would be placed in the capable hands of the [[Stadt Clan]].

Kriegshöfe faced a daunting challenge - no Mesarthrim had ever built anything larger than a gunship before, and there were no construction facilities on Mesar that could construct a full-size warship.  It wasn't until late [[2189]] that Kriegshöfe was able to create any kind of schematic.  In [[2190]] Kriegshöfe was finally able to begin construction on the first destroyer, the [[UMCS Fallen King|''Fallen King'']].  While touring the secret facility, Adrian Tischler decided he wanted a line of cruisers as well, and placed an ordered the construction of two cruiser-sized vessels.  This delayed the Howell even further as hundreds of Kriegshöfe employees were transferred to the larger ship project.  By the end of [[2190]] the design staff decided to expand the Howell design, and were able to focus their work on the Howell.

The second hull was laid in [[2191]] and work began on the [[UMCS Blood Throne|''Blood Throne'']].

In mid [[2191]] Adrian Tischler was alerted that there wasn't enough raw material available to build one destroyer, let alone two.  Severely disappointed, Tischler sought assistance from the other Clan Lords.  He was unsuccessful. After three years of no progress, Tischler ordered that both destroyers be scrapped.  The kriegsmarine experiment was a failure.

It was the scrapping of the destroyers that actually saved them.  When the Clan Lords began bidding in the secret auction for the scrap material, [[Bernard Howell]] received wind of the ships.  Howell was a [[Mesarnak]], but also secretly an [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] (aka Twilight Child).  He contacted Adrian Tischler and let him know that the Mesarnak loyal to the Anderung knew about his destroyers, and were planning on seizing the incomplete hulls.

Howell, who was in charge of a Mesarnak repair yard, offered to smuggle enough raw material to complete the ships, but only if the hulls could be smuggled away and saved.  Adrian Tischler took a gamble and decided to trust Howell.  By the end of [[2195]] both hulls were successfully moved to a hidden facility into the [[Sonar Mountain Range]] and construction continued thanks to the new supplies of raw material from Bernard Howell.

In [[2196]], the ''Fallen King'' and the ''Blood Throne'' were completed.  Unfortunately, the ''Blood Throne'' was crippled moments after launch when she struck an outcropping of one of the Sonar Mountains.  The disabled vessel crashed only 84 seconds after her first launch.  The brand new destroyer exploded at the base of [[Mount Cartwood]], killing the entire crew and the entire population of nearby town [[Früston]].  The Mesarnak were able to quickly ascertain the location of the hidden shipyard and ordered an orbital bombardment by an Anderung warship.  The bombardment killed the entire construction force of Kriegshöfe, along with Otto Stadt, who was at the shipyard for the launch.

It took until early [[2197]] before the Resistance was able to find a proper permanent hidden facility for building their line of destroyers and cruisers.  They eventually decided on deep space.  The ''Fallen King'' was charged with the duty of serving as the construction yard for the new destroyers.  From the ship, zero gravity teams would work on a new ''Blood Throne''.  In order to expedite the process Bernard Howell began prefabricating hull sections in his repair yard and shipping them out into deep space via tugs and other ships under his command.  By the end of [[2197]], the ''Blood Throne'' had been rebuilt, and two new hulls were already started; the [[UMCS Fury of Hell|''Fury of Hell'']] and the [[UMCS Dark Agent|''Dark Agent'']] (the latter named in honor of the tireless and secret efforts of Howell and his men).

In [[2198]] tragedy struck the project again.  Kriegshöfe was infiltrated by a Mesarnak spy.  Kriegshöfe's construction location was compromised, and Bernard Howell's name was disclosed to the leaders of the Mesarnak.  Howell was immediately arrested and turned over to the Anderung, but his staff were executed on the spot.  Howell was never seen again, but it was a known custom of the Anderung at the time to scourge traitors until their flesh was flayed from their bones.  Howell likely suffered this gruesome fate.

The Anderung dispatched a single warship to deal with the Kriegshöfe destroyers, but the ships had been warned by Mesarthrim observers.  When the Anderung ship found them they were in high battle alert.  The four destroyers outmaneuvered and defeated the Anderung warship in a thirty minute battle.  The Kriegshöfe workers towed the Anderung hull to a new location, and stripped the vessel for raw material.  From the remains of the vessel they were able to lay the hulls for a fifth destroyer, the [[UMCS Black Son|''Black Son'']] and for a cruiser.

In [[2200]], the destroyer line was officially named the Howell class in honour of Bernard Howell and his sacrifices for the project.  While it is traditional to name a class after the lead ship (in this case, the ''Fallen King''), Resistance leaders felt that re-naming the class after its greatest benefactor was more fitting.

The full complement of six destroyers ordered by Adrian Tischler were not completed until [[2260]] when the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] formed the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] and demanded that Kriegshöfe complete the order.  By [[2261]], the [[UMCS Duality|UMCS ''Duality'']] was completed and joined the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]].

'''Mesar''' is the 5th planet in the [[Centus System]].

==History==

Mesar was first colonized by the [[Anderung]] when they settled the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and was the seat of power for their government, the [[Yurtosh hora ven Serthra]]. After their fall, Mesar served as the home of the [[Mesarthrim]] and [[Mesarnak]] [[:Category:Races|races]] and the seat of government for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

==Geography==
[[Image:Mesar_Map.png]]

Mesar has five continents, covering a wide range of biospheres and climates.  The [[Mesarthrim]] people also have different traditions and cultures based on where they are from.

===Orillian===

*Orillian - "The Forge of a Thousand Kings." Ak'uech d' natha Szithrel Valuken

'''The Forge of a Thousand Kings''' - Orillian is the traditional home of the [[Tischler Clan]] and of the Resistance.  [[Stone Bridge]] and [[Homemakers Field]] were founded here.  Orillian is a damp continent.  It is constantly harassed by typhoons, hurricanes, and an almost constant raining sky.  The muddy swamplands are almost impossible to navigate for tank and rig a like, individual infantry must move across the rolling swamps and hills to tame this continent.  Raised roads are the only safe means of transportation for vehicles to and from in Orillian.  There are very few small villages or towns in Orillian.  If a city is built here, it is built with a purpose, with huge, long anchor posts going deep into Mesar to secure the massive stone structures that are built to create the city.

====The People of Orillian====

The people of Orillian are extremely zealous, both for God and for Mesar.  There is a belief amongst most Orillians' that the constant storms are a very real symbol of God's protection and approval of the Mesarthrim people.  Orillians' often believe they are the wisest of the Mesarthrim, and the ones who were born to hold position above all other Mesarthrim.

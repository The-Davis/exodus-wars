France, or the '''French Republic''', is a country whose metropolitan territory is located in Western Europe and Northern Africa that also comprises various overseas islands and territories located in other continents.

The French Republic is a democracy that is organised as a unitary semi-presidential republic. Its main ideals are expressed in the Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen. It is a developed country with one of the largest economies in the world. France was also a founding member of the United Nations, but was not an initial signitary of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

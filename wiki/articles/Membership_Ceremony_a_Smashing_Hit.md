December 5, 2323

by James Davis and Scott Sanburn

=====Sepulare=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For the first time in many years, the galaxy largely is at peace. There is still a small Imperial fleet out there somewhere, but it is lurking in the shadows of sunless space.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A huge celebration is underway in Retrok, the capital city of Meronir's Republic. They are hosting the huge membership ceremony in honor of the Prefecture and the Titanian Planetary Alliance. The spirit of celebration is heavy in the air. For many people, this is the first time they've had reason to enjoy themselves in nearly a decade.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The massive Alliance of Planets Headquarters, a 3,000-acre facility, has been mostly opened to the public. Some areas are, of course off-limits due to security reasons, but the presence of armed guards does nothing to spoil the mood.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;AP HQ is located about 20km from the city of Retrok, and all of the roads and skyways are jammed with traffic. One could practically climb to the upper atmosphere by using transport ships as stairs. Progress is swift and efficient, but the sheer number of guests and celebrating civilians has pushed Meroniri Air Traffic Control to its limits.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ceremony began in the center of the complex. The courtyard is a sprawling 100-acre grassland with dozens of monuments and a giant fountain in the center. The podium and stage is north of the fountain. A few thousand civilians surround the stage on all sides. The Meroniri officials have provided speakers in strategic location so everyone can hear.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The main ceremonies begin. Various leaders step onto the stage, and the crowd grows silent.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First up is an honor guard for the now-fallen United Earth Alliance. During this ceremony its flag will be retired and the last surviving soldiers and officials will be given an honorable discharge.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The second group is twofold, the Prefecture and the Rikaz Lorithos dignitaries. Next, the Icronian Consortium and Kabila Kimburu joins the procession on the stage. Finally, the United Mesarthrim Clans, and the United Archadian Republics (a non-member ally nation) join the procession.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Meroniri Prime Minister Darel Fusanla salutes the assembled representatives, then addresses the crowd.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Members of the Alliance of Planets; dignitaries, citizens, journalists, and all, welcome. Welcome to the headquarters, the home, of the Alliance of Planets. On behalf of my homeworld, Sepulcre, I am proud to host this historic event. We are gathered here to mark a turning point in human history, the end of the Imperial Occupation War. This war has brought untold horror, and disaster. Now with the threat passed, we can rebuild from the ashes."

(Thunderous applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We have seen one of our most powerful and trusted member states, the United Earth Alliance, brought to complete destruction at the hands of the most lethal enemy to humanity in recorded history. Lucius Black's Empire has brought chaos on a scale unseen in human history before, but in the wake of his defeat, the Alliance is united as never before. Our unity beat the madman's armies into ruin. All the sacrifices--the battles, the pain, and the deaths--they were all worth it. Had we lost the war, humanity would have been finished.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Our thanks alone is not enough. Our thanks must go not just to the soldiers, though they deserve it most of all, but to everyone who wanted to live free and stood up against the oppressor. You have all helped in this conflict."

(Thunderous applause, and cheers.)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"It is with a heavy heart that we must say goodbye to one of our founding member states, the United Earth Alliance. Lucius Black's armies nearly wiped out everyone who swore allegiance to that great nation. We will now conduct a short flag retirement ceremony, and give honorable discharge to the few surviving UEA soldiers. We ask that everyone stand."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The flag is lowered, and all military personnel salute. The national anthem of the United Earth Alliance is played for one final time, and an eerie silence settles over the courtyard.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Scott Sanburn folds the UEA flag, and places it in a sealed container for the Alliance war museum.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The hundred-odd UEA soldiers are all given their final pay for their service, straight from the coffers of Meronir's Republic, and are officially retired from service. The present military personnel salute, but no one claps. It seems strangely wrong to disturb the silence.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The President returns to the podium.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You may be seated.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Over the last few months, after intense negotiations, the Alliance of Planets has granted membership status to two new member states, the Federated Districts of the Prefecture, and the Titanian Planetary Alliance."

(Reserved applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Alliance of Planets has secured the Terran system, thanks to the help of our new Member States. We are still evaluating the situation. The environmental and infrastructure situation is grim. Refugees continue to leave for colony worlds, and we are grateful for the help in money and manpower we have received from private citizens. None of this would have been possible without the assistance of the Titanian government. General-Commander Scott Sanburn will now speak on their behalf."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn approaches the microphone. Years of trauma and suffering are clearly etched into the lines of his face, but he is smiling brightly.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Thank you, Mr. President. During the last decade, humanity has seen some of the worst devastation, loss of life, liberty, and property. I will never forget the day the first bombs hit Titan City, or how we worked hours into the night maintaining the power supplies to our force fields that kept out the devastating Imperial bombardments. It was pure hell, and I'm glad this war is over.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"If not for people like my brave soldiers, or our strong allies, we would not be here today. I want to thank the troops of the Titanian Defense Force who went through hell with me. Good work men."

(Applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The President shakes hands with the General-Commander. Both salute, then the President takes the stand.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Prefecture came from the Empire. I don't need to remind you of the atrocities which the Empire committed. They attacked us without warning, without provocation. They slaughtered our families, leveled our worlds, pillaged and despoiled everything they could lay their hands on. I remind you of these atrocities not so that you will hate the Prefecture, but so that you will remember what it was that they fled from when they sought us as allies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Prefect Anthony Hadrian, once the right-hand man of Emperor Lucius Black, started this rebellion. He hounded the Empire into submission, and helped us chase them from Terra. He leads the Prefecture today. On behalf of the Alliance of Planets, I thank you for your help in the war and welcome you with open arms. Please, speak to your allies."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefect Hadrian approaches the microphone. He is slow, burdened by scars and injuries from his battles. He limps on his right leg.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Thank you Mr. President. And thank you, my allies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"My people have fought a long, hard war against you, people we should have embraced as brothers. We have finally laid aside our foolish hatred and the fanatical cause of a fascist Emperor. The future is bright for our united planets. Our cultures and peoples will mesh into an armor of unity that will be indestructible. We have, and always will emerge triumphant, not as individual nations, but as an unstoppable whole!"

(Applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefect Hadrian salutes to the crowd and rejoins the delegation, where he is greeted with handshakes from General-Commander Sanburn and Uber-General Tischler.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The President again takes the stand.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We will continue to help the Prefecture continue on its path out of fascism. Their economy is weak, but our borders will be open to trade and soon they will walk proudly among the industrial giants of the Alliance. We will now raise the flags of the Titanian and Prefecture Member States."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefect Hadrian and General-Commander Sanburn tie their flags to their respective poles, and raise them into the wind. The applause and cheering is deafening, though it is soon drowned out by an even louder roar. One fighter from each member state flies over the courtyard in a tight V-Formation. The Alliance of Planets Honor Guard fires a twenty-one gun salute. The crowd applauds. A show of military force is an unusual treat at Meroniri-sponsored ceremonies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The noise slowly dies down, and Prime Minister Fusanla speaks again.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We would also like to welcome two special guests. Joining us are representatives from the United Archadian Republics, who has recently signed a friendly non-member treaty with the Alliance of Planets. Also joining us is the CEO of the AstroLift Company, the largest contractor with the Titanian army.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Both parties have made generous contributions to our humanitarian efforts, and both have lent manpower and machinery to our Terran restoration and refugee relief efforts. AstroLift President Trevor Pruden has a few words to say first."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Pruden, a well-dressed man, obviously wealthy and successful, steps up to the podium.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I am truly honored to join you at this historic event. The AstroLift Company shares the vision of a future populated by freethinking citizens of our great civilization.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Our original company mandate was to provide feasible and economical space faring solutions to the consumer, and this as important now as it was before the war. We do more than provide a service - we provide interstellar transport. It is essential that the general consumer have the ability to move about in our vast territories. Without transportation, our economies would stagnate, and the flow of ideas would cease. AstroLift is committed to this mandate more than ever before. We pray for the security and solidarity of our civilizations, and congratulate and welcome our new friends."

(Some applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Thank you, Mr. Pruden. We have seen history here today. Our future is bright. We will continue to be the beacon of freedom in the universe, no matter what lies ahead for us. We will stay on the path we started on so long ago. No matter the cost, the Alliance will stand strong to provide a chance for all citizens of the galaxy to have lives full of life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There is no greater cause, no greater opportunity, or no greater call that one can follow, than to defend the freedom of his fellow man. Once again, for all the brave men and women who gave their lives in this horrible war, and to those who died in the wars past, and to those who may die in the wars we hope will never come, we thank you, from the bottom of our hearts."

(Thunderous applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I want to thank our member state delegations for participating in this ceremony today. There will be a feast for everyone in attendance thanks to the generous sponsorship of the AstroLift Corporation. Please, enjoy yourselves and the freedoms we have won.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Thank you all for coming."

(Applause)

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The President and his staff are departing, as well as the Alliance Member State delegations. They return to the giant Headquarters building surrounding the complex. Tables are being prepared in the courtyard and outside the huge Headquarters building. The crowd is slowly withdrawing for supper.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This has been a monumental day for the Alliance of Planets. The President has introduced new members, bid farewell to an old member, and outlined the future goals of the Alliance. APNET and B-Net will keep you informed of any future updates. APNET/B-Net News, signing off.

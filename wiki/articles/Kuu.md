'''Kuu''' is the title of the tribal chief of an [[:Category:Ngrligru Nations|Ngrligru Kabila]].

The process of choosing a Kuu varies greatly between different Kabilas. Some allow physical challenges. Some hold elections. Some have hereditary families. Typically, a [[Ngrligru]] holds the rank, but sometimes a [[Mtomigru]] manages to garner sufficient support to hold the rank.

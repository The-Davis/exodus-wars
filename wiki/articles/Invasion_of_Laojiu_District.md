The '''Invasion of the Laojiu District''' was one of the greatest strategic [[Mesarthrim]] victories of the [[Tempest War]].  By capturing every major food producing world of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], the [[United Centusi States]] was able to ensure that their opponents were on short rations for the majority of the war.

It was only through support of the [[Ikronin]] merchant fleets, which bought vast food supplies from the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] and sold them to the Prefecture at "reasonable prices" that the Prefecture managed to survive the first three years of the war.  Later several large areas of industrialized Prefecture worlds were re-purposed for agriculture.

===Religion===

The stark reality of [[Kronheim]]--a world locked in an excessively long ice age--has given the Ikronin a grim view of life. Their religions are usually filled with gods indifferent to the plight of their worshippers, and an uncertain reward in the afterlife. This may explain their fixation on the material and the here and now.

===Languages===

The [[Ikronin Jurekön]] still speaks [[Sothark]] as its primary language. This Nordic-like dialect is an odd mix of guttural and "high" speech. Any Ikronin who has traveled will understand [[English]] and speak it quite well. Many pilots, with their roots more in the ancient warrior culture of the Ikronin, are more reluctant to speak English. A native Ikronin who has never left home may or may not speak [[English]], depending on his or her education.

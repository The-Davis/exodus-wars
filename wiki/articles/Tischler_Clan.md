==Clan Lord==

The current [[Clan Lord]] of the Tischler Clan is [[Karina Koch von Tischler|Karina von Tischler]].  [[Karina Koch von Tischler|Karina]] was appointed after the death of [[Eric Francis von Tischler|Eric Francis]] in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

The last actual Tischler to lead the Tischler Clan was [[Andreas Tischler]], after his death, his sister [[Bethany Tischler]] offered to make [[Eric Francis von Tischler|Eric Francis]] [[Clan Lord]] out of respect to him for discovering the betrayal of [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas]].  [[Bethany Tischler]] was briefly the [[Clan Lord]] after the death of [[Eric Francis von Tischler|Eric Francis]], however [[William Camp]] picked a replacement for her, when he exiled her to [[Terra]].

==Schutz de Ton==

Schutz de Ton was the first permanent settlement created by the Tischler Clan, located deep within the Sonar Mountain Range. It was occasionally abandoned for safety reasons, but never for more than a day or two. All members of the Tischler Clan who's remains can be recovered are buried here.

==Geography==

The hereditary Tischler homeland is northern [[Orillian]], specifically the area around [[Stone Bridge]].

==The Name==

The Tischler Clan name is given to all the families adopted by [[Otto Tischler]], the father of the Clan system and the first [[Clan Lord]] of the Tischler Clan.

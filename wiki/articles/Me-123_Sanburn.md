==Overview==
The Sanburn is a design that originated in the last years of the [[Second Exodus War]], as the ground campaign on [[Terra]] reached its peak.  [[Mesarbauer]] designed the fighter for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], based on the venerable Patton fighter's airframe, as a gun-heavy ground-support and attack fighter.  It was named after [[Scott Sanburn]], General-Commander of the remaining Alliance Defense Forces.

 

==Capabilities==
The Sanburn is designed for deliverying maximum damage on its targets.  Its armament is heavy on guns, as well as missiles.  It has a total of ten Medium Ordinance Hardpoints, which allow it to carry anti-fighter or anti-ground missiles in large quantities.  

Additionally, the Sanburn is equipped with six Type-C Blazers, the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type.  With more than any other fighter class, a Sanburn can inflict very heavy damage on targets once it has closed to short and medium range.  

The Sanburn is outfitted with a standard fighter-grade reactor and engine array.  Although it is noticeably more sluggish than the Patton, its heavier weapons and defenses make up for the lack of agility.

==Deployment==

Sanburn fighters began showing up in force at the beginning of 2323, and by the end of the war had chalked up an impressive tally of destroyed Imperial war machines.  They flew more ground-attack sorties on [[Terra]] than any other single fighter class in the war, sometimes operating twenty of every twenty-four hours a days.  After the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], the [[United Centusi States]] managed to acquire the majority of the former [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]' Sanburn fighters, as well as capturing [[Mesarbauer]]'s production facilities in-tact.

==Education==

[[Meroniri]] education places an enormous emphasis on mastery of a field. With their long lifespans, [[Meroniri]] can reasonably expect to have the same expertise as an entire team of specialists in a field.

A young [[Meroniri]] is expected to be fluent in every official language of the human nations of the [[Orion Arm]], and expected to be able to converse in at least one [[Ngrligru]] dialect. [[Meroniri]] children are expected to master advanced mathematics, basic biology concepts, chemistry, physics, and at least one form of artistic creation by the time they reach 30, then spend the next twenty years of their lives focusing on their chosen field. Like everything in [[Meroniri]] life, education is treated like an artform, and every student is expected to have a unique set of skills, advantages and flaws by the time they cease their formal education and begin their focused learning in their field.

==Sport==

The most popular [[Meroniri]] sport is Calinbale, a game of such extreme complexity that very few non-[[Meroniri]] even understand it, let alone enjoy it. A Calinbale field can be any area with a regular geometric shape, with rules changing based on the space available. Goals are scored in an enormous variety of ways, and the equipment used varies by the arena without apparent rhyme or reason. The only consistent pieces of equipment are a small ball about 6 inches in diameter, and black masks which must be worn by every player. True to [[Meroniri]] life, every type of arena includes rules to allow for debate, and most games end up degenerating into long-winded arguments.

The [[Meroniri]] have a keen interest in baseball, which they enjoy due to the enormous number of rules and the traditional arguments known as "rhubarbs." Their more athletic-minded athletes enjoy basketball and soccer. There are ten [[Meroniri]] interstellar baseball teams, two basketball teams, and three soccer teams.

==Literature==
[[Meroniri]] poetry is some of the longest and most complex in human civilization. The [[Meroniri]] do not believe in "the beauty of simplicity," and are prone to ramble on in their poetry. The [[Meroniri]] poet Naolir Resinon is recognized throughout the galaxy for her ballads of unrivalled complexity and beauty, showing that not all [[Meroniri]] poetry rambles on pointlessly.

[[Meroniri]] novels almost always develop fifteen to twenty plot threads, and only resolve two or three by the end. Sequels are almost a requirement of novelists. [[Meroniri]] historical fiction and fantasy is considered some of the most interesting available, and their literature tends to sell well in most markets.

==Art==

The presence of four ocular color receptors--a rare anomaly in most human bloodlines--is common in the [[Meroniri]] eye. Where [[Terrans]] and other humans can perceive several million shades and hues of color, the average [[Meroniri]] can perceive several hundred million. A [[Meroniri]] painting has hues so subtle that only the few outside their bloodline with similar eyes can truly appreciate the work. Even so, [[Meroniri]] paintings are considered masterpieces far beyond what most other cultures have achieved. A painter living on [[Sepulare]] can spend a year carefully preparing all of the pigments and materials needed before even starting a masterpiece.

Every form of art is appreciated by the [[Meroniri]], but besides painting, architecture is cherished and renowned. Instead of the urban sprawl associated with other cultures (particularly [[Terran]]), the [[Meroniri]] build up or dig down. A soaring palatial estate can easily top three kilometers in height, and hundreds of meters in depth. Every window, every pinacle, and every minor detail is hand carved. Even gutters and drains are shaped into statues and carvings. A [[Meroniri]] building is a true work of art, and [[Meroniri]] architects are sought by the wealthy from worlds throughout the [[Orion Arm]].

==Cuisine==

[[Meroniri]] cuisine is almost universally renowned, but is extremely expensive. Most dishes take days of preparation and have hundreds of ingredients, all of which must be carefully balanced and prepared at very precise temperatures.

Vinyards and wineries on [[Sepulare]] are equally famous. The Tula is their fruit of choice; a small, sweet orange-colored berry which grows year-round--though the winter crop is usually considered the prime for their richer flavor.

==Cinema==

Films and similar forms of entertainment rival the "Hollywood-era" of [[Terran]] cinema. Actors, screenwriters and directors are very popular cultural icons among the [[Meroniri]], and film is one of their biggest industries.

==Music==

There hasn't been a piece of [[Meroniri]] music written for less than twelve instruments for hundreds of years. Their music is considered to be some of the most challenging by musicians from all worlds and nations, and some of the most beautiful by music lovers everywhere.

==Holidays==

Holidays are considered a "quaint" concept used by "younger" cultures that still hold onto concepts of set working days and rigid calendars. The [[Meroniri]] do not celebrate any day over any other, despite the often extravagant celebrations they have. Their celebrations are of life itself, and can be held at any time or place on pure consensus of the moment.

==Social Structure==

One of the great criticisms of the [[Meroniri]] is that their social structure is adrift, without an identity of its own. Once considered the greatest society in history, most [[Meroniri]] are now adrift in a sea of cultural annui, devoid of any unique quality to set themselves as [[Meroniri]] apart from the other races.

There are three competing schools of thought. One, represented by the majority in the [[Meroniri]] government, seeks balance and constancy, and is therefore criticized for its ineffectiveness and lack of social identity.

Another, represented by the younger generations of [[Meroniri]]--particularly those in the military--look to the [[Terrans]] as an example of cultural curiosity and outreach, and seek to emulate and expand upon the concepts.

The third, considered a "darker" aspect of [[Meroniri]] society, seeks to return to the roots of the [[Anthronoris Empire]], where the [[Meroniri]] would dominate all life in the universe in all aspects: military, religion, culture--everything.

*Power Plant: Type-200XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 150 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver

===Overview===

The Pursuer was developed in response to the demand for a fast, agile, and almost maintenance free police car that any department could use. This, coupled with the fact that it allows room for two officers and a prisoners, the Pursuer is the most well rounded car out there.

[[Image:PursuerPolicePatrolVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

With the special restraint cage that is built to house a single person, the Pursuer is the first anti-grav police car to be able to carry the prisoner back to the precinct for processing. The restraint system is humane, but also allows for the prisoner to be put to sleep for easier transportation if needed.

The engines that are installed in the Pursuer allow for the vehicle to hit speeds of one hundred and fifty kilometers per hour. Along with the speed, the agility that the craft has allows for some of the best handling in any police car in the current market.

[[Image:PursuerPolicePatrolVehicle2.jpg]]

Although some of the current cars in the market carry fixed weapons, the Pursuer has no need to for any kind weaponry, relying instead on the driver's ability to safely disable a suspect with special maneuvers. However, the car does allow the police to carry some heavier firearms, such as neutron rifles, in the event of a serious crisis.

===Deployment===

Since the Pursuer is relatively new to the market, the number of orders has yet to peek the way that Rahn Industries thought it would. However, R.I. executives are out and about showing off the new car with various demonstrations of its unique abilities and the flexibility that it brings to the department.

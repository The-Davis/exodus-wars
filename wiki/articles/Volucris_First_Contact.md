The [[Volucris Incursion]] began on February 5, [[2324]], in an uncharted star system in the [[Pelagrim Sector]], approximately two light years from the [[Mirach System]].  The Volucris drifted into and interrupted a battle between the fleets of the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. The human fleets retreated, and the Prefecture's forces inadvertently led the Volucris to the [[Mirach System]].

== First Contact ==

The [[Volucris]] [[First Contact]] was made just after the final conflict of the [[Second Exodus War]], a battle between the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]'s Second Fleet of the Line and a sizeable force of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s remaining warships.  The battle was a rout, with Admiral [[Jason Bremen]]'s ships destroying or capturing twenty enemy vessels.  While Prefecture Marines were boarding the surrendering ships, the Volucris struck.

The [[FDS Drake|FDS ''Drake'']] was hit by dozens of explosive Volucris suicide flyers, which at the time were undetectable by the ship's sensors.  The Prefecture thought the Imperial remnant was moving back in for a counter-attack, and began moving out as many ships as possible.  Soon afterwards, a small Volucris transport slipped in through the ''Drake'''s perimeter defenses and nearly a hundred Volucris soldiers boarded the cruiser.

The ''Drake'''s Marine complement was in full combat readiness due to the earlier battle, and were only taken by surprise for a few seconds.  All Volucris soldiers were killed, though about 600 Marines were injured in the fighting.  Most injuries were superficial, though a few required life-saving surgery.

Upon receiving word from the ''Drake'', Admiral Bremen ordered the immediate withdrawal of all Prefecture forces to the nearest inhabitted system.  Bremen planned to make an [[Urokon]] call to Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] to report the hostile First Contact.  All ships made it out intact, though several vessels took severe damage from more Volucris bombings and later had to be drydocked.

== Aftermath ==

In the hasty withdrawal, the Prefecture made a straight jump to the [[Mirach System]], which was invaded by the Volucris shortly afterwards.  It is unknown how the Volucris followed them through [[hyperspace]], but it is speculated that during the boarding of the FDS ''Drake'' some of the more intelligent Volucris were able to plant a temporary homing beacon.

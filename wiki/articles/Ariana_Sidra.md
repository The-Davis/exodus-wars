'''Ariana Sidra'''
*Date of Death: February 5, [[2315]]
*Place of Birth: [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[United Earth Alliance]]

''Ariana Sidra'' was the General in charge of the defense of the North American continent. She was killed when [[Grolk Kyhra]] and his [[Ngrligru]] stormed her command bunker and ate her, along with her command staff.

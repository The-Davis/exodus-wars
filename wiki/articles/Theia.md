'''Theia''' is the sixth planet in the [[Titan System]]. It is the fourth colony of the [[Terrans| Terran]] race. 

== History ==

When the Titan System was discovered by the [[United Earth Alliance]], it was the first proof of non-[[Earth]] based intelligent life for the [[Terran]] race. The surface of Theia and several other worlds were covered in [[Anthronoris]] ruins.

The excited Terrans immigrated to the world in record numbers, settling on [[Titan City]]. The world was expected to be the scientific and archaeological discovery of the century, until the [[Meroniri]] made [[First Contact]] with Earth a few months later.

Despite the archaeological finds being overshadowed by the Terrans joining the interstellar community, the discoveries on Theia continued to expand the understanding of galactic history.

Theia's population continued to expand, becoming a major Terran population center. It became a sovereign state of the United Earth Alliance rather than a mere protectorate, and held its first elections in [[2120]], electing its own [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] and [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]].

By the late [[23rd Century]], the bulk of the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]] was stationed on Theia and other worlds in the Titan System, due to the strained relations with the [[Reigens]] and because the [[Njiorin Sector]] was the frontier for the United Earth Alliance. This distribution of military power made Theia the logical rallying point for the survivors of the Alliance military when the [[Onyx Empire]] began its murderous rampage through other Terran worlds.

The [[Second Exodus War]] saw its turning point in favor of the coalition forces in the Titan System, mostly due to the efforts of [[Scott Sanburn]] and [[Andreas Tischler]], operating out of Theia. Theia also housed the provisional government of the United Earth Alliance from [[2315]] until [[2324]].

After the United Earth Alliance was dissolved in [[2324]], Theia and the other worlds of the [[Titan System]] joined the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

=== Arges ===

So far, the Arges continent, which is in the northern and eastern hemisphere of the planet, is the only one which has seen any colonization effort. [[Titan City]] is the capital of the [[Titan System]] and Theia, and is the site of the first [[Terran]] landing on the planet.  [[Titan City]] and its surroundings are found in the southern peninsula of the continent. [[New Dallas]] is on the northwestern part of the continent, nestled in a gap in the Kaufman Mountain range, near Lake Denton, an enormous freshwater lake.

=== Brontes ===

The Brontes continent has several archaeological digs at any given moment. It has the most ruins from the era of the [[Anthronoris]], and many ruins from afterwards, suggesting that some of Theia’s original inhabitants survived the [[Sundering]], but did not survive for long.

=== Steropes ===

Steropes is located in the southern hemisphere, and is mostly devoid of ruins and appears to have been of little use to the [[Anthronoris]].

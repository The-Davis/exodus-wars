February 16, [[2323]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A climactic struggle between powerful armies is at an end. The Empire has finally been driven off of Terra. For four months, Terra was locked in such savage struggle that no news communication could get out to the galaxy at all. Courier ships were shot down by both sides, and all frequencies of communication were jammed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Life on Terra during those four months was pure hell. The death count--while at a staggering two billion--is still incomplete. The residents of some nuked cities haven't been counted. Even the most optimistic statisticians believe that another billion names will be added to the list before the Alliance Census Bureau submits its report to Alliance of Planets High Council.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Perhaps the worst sights of all are the ghost cities. Phoenix Arizona, Miami Florida, Paris France, Beijing China and Istanbul Turkey were all hit by concentrated microwave attacks from the orbiting Remnant warships. The TDF occupational troops and the civilians were evaporated--only the buildings and weapons were left intact.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bulk of the surviving Imperial forces have escaped. Seeing the mighty Empire toppled, the Remnant has disappeared back to the unknown regions they came from. For now, no one is interested in chasing them. Only the Meroniri can even hope to match the Remnant, and the ancient Meronir's Republic has no interest in embroiling itself in a fresh conflict. Lending one or two ships to counter the Remnant is one thing, hunting them down is another matter entirely. If the Remnant had an army as well as a navy, there is little doubt that the Empire would still control Terra--or Terra would be completely destroyed.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Alliance of Planets High Council has taken control of Terra. The Prefecture was put in charge of clean-up, but the Alliance has decided that Terra will become an independent nation as soon as the war damage has been cleared away.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;B-Net will be releasing details of the conflict as they become known. The communications blackout ensured that some details will never be known.

The Algedi Cluster is a small group of stars within 5LY of each other. Each has an inhabited planet, which makes up the [[Tejat District]]'s worlds. While not technically a sector, the Algedi Cluster is categorized as a "sub-sector" of the [[Sol Sector]].

== Inhabited Star Systems ==

The [[Theta Algedi System]], [[Gamma Algedi System]], and the [[Zeta Algedi System]] are currently inhabited.

== Uninhabited Star Systems ==

There are a total of ten stars in the Algedi Cluster. The remaining five are Alpha Algedi, Beta Algedi, Delta Algedi, Epsilon Algedi, Eta Algedi, Iota Algedi, and Kappa Algedi.

The Krelbine Infantry Robot is a semi-autonomous combatant, with an artificial intelligence capable of identifying human and Volucris target profiles.  Without contrary orders, it will patrol, acquire a target, and track it.  Standing orders can be given to disable or kill a target, or targets can be determined by a higher-authority controller.

== History ==

Torm Krelbine was the greatest warrior of the heirs in the Burning War.  He was fierce, and fought on the front lines with blaster and spear.  The memory of the warlord was strong.  Infantry in the Lorithos [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]] were all issued the same weapons.  When robotic troopers were invented, naming the first after the warlord was a forgone conclusion.

== Capabilities ==

The Krelbine Robotic Trooper's arsenal is a blaster rifle and a reinforced spear.  Alone, a Krelbine is pretty tough, but in a team they're devastating.  Defenses are strong--reinforced Tzaiko Composite armor.

Krelbines are faster than Terran Infantry Power Suits.  They're powered by a small electrical power cell, which can be swapped out by a Zuura command suit in seconds.  Most of the time, Krelbines obey the directions of Lorithos infantry in Zuura suits, but they do have AIs to command them if their commander is killed.

== Deployment ==

Typically, a live Lorithos trooper commands twenty Krelbines.  Their AI is frequently updated.  The original could only point and shoot.  The modern one can pick targets by priority, assess which weapon to use and when, and even make limited tactical decisions based on what condition its counterparts are in.

== Volucris War ==

During the [[Volucris War]], many thousands of Krelbines were lost even in the early part of the war.  [[Sanor Cai]]'s tactics called for high mobility and long range.  The robots, while extremely useful, were neither highly-mobile nor long-ranged. Instead of working with the air support and hovertanks, the robots were used as static defenses to delay the Volucris long enough to evacuate sensitive military equipment or helpless civilians.

After the [[Ixoarchada]] joined the war, a [[Strolza Entity]] was given control over every Lorithos robot.  Their combat effectiveness rose by several orders of magnitude.  The robots became brutal, ruthless killers.  Their parameters were to destroy Volucris at the highest level of efficiency, and they would occasionally cut down civilian targets to get to the enemy. At this point, the war was seen as a "triage" scenario, where only those who could be saved without sacrificing additional lives were given any consideration at all.

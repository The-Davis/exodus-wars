''Miles Hlow'' was an inventor and geneticist in the 21st Century. He invented the [[EI]]s, and funded the first permanent [[Luna]] base as a way to survive [[World War III]]. After the war, he moved to the first [[Mars]] colony, where his son [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]] created the deep space colony ships used in the [[First Exodus]]

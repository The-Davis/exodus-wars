'''Laojiu''' is the fourth planet in the [[Beta Arae System]].

== History ==

Laojiu is the shining gem of the [[Prefecture]]. A paradise world where rich culture meets old-fashioned hard work. Technologically poor, the Laojinese fall back to their roots.

The colony was originally founded by Chinese and Korean emigrants during the slow decline of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The first colony was only six thousand, established in [[2207]] in a seaport landing site on the southeastern shores of the main continent, which later became the capital city, Nju-zanjiang.

The largest continent, Qinghai, saw thirteen separate colonies in the years between the initial landing and the establishment of a colonial government in [[2212]]. The colonial authority ruled with dignity and the respect of the people until the [[Onyx Empire]] established a farming colony on the world in [[2319]].

In [[2323]], the [[Onyx Empire]] was defeated, and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] assumed sovereign control. Open elections ratified Laojiu's membership with the Prefecture.

During the [[Tempest War]], the [[United Centusi States]] briefly held control over Laojiu. In a gesture to appease the interstellar community, [[William Camp]] "liberated" the planet, creating a puppet state known as the "Free Laojiu Republic." When the Prefecture re-took the planet, the puppet government was dissolved and Laojiu regained its position within the Prefecture.

== Geography ==

Laojiu has five continents, covering a wide range of biospheres and climates. The largest continent is Qinghai, approximately 44 million square kilometers. Besides Qinghai, there are four smaller continents. Sichuan (30 million square kilometers), Xinjiang (12 million square kilometers), Guangxi (23 million square kilometers), and Hubei (4 million square kilometers).

=== Qinghai ===

Qinghai is home to the bulk of the population. The capital, Nju-zanjiang is home to 13 million people, and is the site of the main spaceport of the planet. Billions of tons of foodstuffs are shipped out of Laojiu every week, and most of it goes through Nju-zanjiang or its sister city, Guangzhou. There are twelve other metropolis-sized cities in Qinghai: Xiamen, Kao-hsiung, Changsha, Hefei, Baotou, Burgin, Tianjin, Xi'an, Hainan Dao, Wuhan, Chengdu, and Hebei, as well as a population of over 40 million spread throughout smaller towns and farming villages.

=== Sichuan ===

Sichuan is the farming juggernaut of the entire planet, with rice fields covering 80% of the surface of the continent. Only 10 million people live there to work the fields. The farms are largely automated, but the mass of foodstuffs produced daily still require enormous manpower.

=== Xinjiang ===

Xinjiang is a more arid continent, but is still put to use in farming foodstuffs with lower water requirements. Its population of 35 million uses lower levels of technology to keep up the fields, but still has a high level of automation available.

=== Guangxi ===

Guangxi is the equivalent of a tropical and sub-tropical region, and is barely populated with a mere 5 million people spread out over the land mass. Thick jungles and rainforests cover this continent, but nearly 40% of them are carefully cultivated and contain plants with some food or medicinal use.

=== Hubei ===

Only Hubei, the cold southern polar region, has no population or agriculture.

Prefecture '''Marines''' are often lightly equipped for high mobility and ease of maintenance.

Standard Marine Infantry are equipped primarily with the Field Combat Uniform (FCU).  The FCU is an exceptional leap in clothing technology. It has a four-color digital camouflage pattern, the colors of which are changeable by means of a small current that can be run through the suit. Optical sensors observe the surrounding terrain, and change the colors accordingly. The FCU includes a jacket with rank insignia and name patches, which fits over body armor, and trousers with thigh storage pockets and room for low-profile leg armor. All troops are issued combat boots, which also serve as the source of the uniform's camouflage power source.

All Marines in the field wear a combat helmet, which has an integrated Heads-Up Display (HUD).  The HUD is linked to a helmet-mounted sensor net, which can provide information in infrared, ultraviolet, and enhanced night vision.  It can also remotely link to any weapon operated by the Marine.

Typically, Marines carry an [[M-06 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle (SABR)|M-6 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle]], known as the SABR or "Saber".  This weapon is a gauss-based weapons technology, with a selectable output on the firing mechanism.  In the event that stealth is needed, the weapon can be powered down.  It can go from cold to full power in less than a second.  Pulling the trigger powers on and engages the weapon.  The Saber has an integrated scope which has a 20x selectable optical zoom.

Marines also carry two backup weapons, the [[M-04 Combat Knife|M-4 Combat Knife]] and the [[M-05 Pistol|M-5 Pistol]].  Both weapons are very low tech compared to the rest of the Marine's standard combat load.

During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], seven major factions were able to seize power on [[Mesar]] and [[Comur]].  Each faction laid claim to [[Stone Bridge]], and was able to pull together armed militants.

==Uereinigte Mesarthrim Clans==
[[Image:UnitedMesarthrimClansFlag.png|thumb|left|200px|The United Mesarthrim Clans]]<br />
'''Head of State:''' [[Uber-General]] [[Eric Francis]]<br />
The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] were the legitimate rulers of [[Mesar]], and the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]] began as a rebellion against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|UMC]].  Failure to continue vital reforms after the death of [[Andreas Tischler]] was the spark that turned the [[Auellal Political Party]] into the [[Auellal League]], and began the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|war]].

More information available at [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].




==Auellal Logue==
[[Image:AuellalFlag.png|thumb|left|200px|The Auellal League]]<br />
'''Head of State:''' [[Provost]] [[Steven Cue]]<br />
The [[Auellal League]] instigated the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|war]] against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|UMC]] after [[Uber-General]] [[Eric Francis|Francis]] refused to continue to fund several vital reforms that were set into motion by [[Andreas Tischler]] only a month before he died.  The tragic loss cut short any hope the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] had for a free and open [[Silver Chambers]].  Additionally, [[Uber-General]] [[Eric Francis|Francis]] bowed to pressure from other [[Clan Lord|Clan Lords]] and removed any extended representation from the government.  Followed quickly by the [[Silver Chambers]] levying extensive taxes against [[Caledon]].  When [[Steven Cue]] went for the last time to demand equality for the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]], he was beaten within inches of his life by [[William Camp|William Camp's]] personal guards.  After recovering, [[Steven Cue]] returned to [[Caledon]] and prepared for his invasion of [[Mesar]].  The [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] had been stockpiling weapons and traitors following the death of [[Andreas Tischler]], so they were ready.

More information available at [[Auellal League]].

==Overview==

When the Prefecture needed a helicopter that could turn on a dime and was able to speed into and out of battles in moments notice, the Lightning was designed for just that mission. After years of service, the developments in the area of repulsor technology allowed for the doing away of rotors and the creation of the next generation of vertical take off and landing craft.

Following the numerous upheavals in the Blacktron Prefecture's military strategy and tactics, the vehicles that were requested to be produced or upgraded were many, including the Lightning Mk.II. Rahn Industries' designers worked around the clock to produce a better, more dangerous version of the already popular Lightning Mk.II and succeed beyond anyone's wildest dreams.

==Capabilities==

The biggest change when it came to redesigning the Lightning was the fact that short range missile launchers were no longer being used for a variety of reasons. Consequently, when it came to finding a viable replacement, Rahn Industries' designers looked to weapons that had either a greater or equally powerful punch. Within days a suitable replacements, autocannons, were incorporated into the design, using the existing mounts for the SRMs to hitch directly into the vehicle.

Between the hearty punch of the autocannons, the Lightning also packs the ability to kill at long range with a variety of missiles, from Spacehounds and Rattlers to the potent anti-radiation ALARM missiles. The improvements in the communication, targeting, and tracking systems have made the Lightning Mk.III an incredibly dangerous opponent.

To make the craft even more dangerous the design staff incorporated a brand-new power supply, the incredibly powerful X10L-Series reactor, giving the Lightning Mk.III the ability to speed to any hotspot that needs help. Coupled with an amazing stability control system, the craft is almost immune to pilot error and can make turns and maneuvers that would have been classified as suicidal in older models.

==Deployment==
The Prefecture has ordered a fairly large number of replacement units as well as complete upgrades to the rest of the fleet. This means that, along with the Werewolf Mk.III, the third generation Lightning will be one of the most widely used VTOLs ever produced.

Along with the Prefecture, other governments, as well as security firms and other private companies, have expressed an interest in the vehicle. Offering a stripped down version for civilian sale has also bee discussed, even though the speed and weapons capability would drastically be reduced, Lightning could be used as private transportation or recreational vehicle.

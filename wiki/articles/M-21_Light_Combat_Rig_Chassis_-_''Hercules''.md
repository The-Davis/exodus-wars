The original Hercules was a frail machine, the Rig equivalent of a fabric and wood biplane of the 1900s. In the first year of the war, it reigned supreme over the rusted war machines of the over-complacent United Earth Alliance. The Hercules quickly fell into disuse as newer and more powerful weapons were introduced. In 2323, the Prefecture resurrected the design with new technology and structure. Unfortunately, the technology wasn't good enough. In February of 2324, the design received another overhaul--though not as drastic as the redesign, and yet another upgrade in 2325. The armament has changed significantly from the original to the modern Hercules. For example, lasers are no longer included in the design. Instead, two Neutron Cannons are mounted on the flexible torso-mounted turret. The turret can fire in a full 180 degree arc, allowing the Hercules to hit targets behind it without turning. Melee combat is still possible--even preferable in battles with weakling Volucris breeds. The grasping claws that made the original Hercules unique are still present, and have been strengthened. They also house powerful Thumper Cannons. When the Hercules was upgraded in 2324, the ammo bins were expanded to hold 150 rounds of ammo. Originally, the New Albany Mechyards 250XL fission reactor powered the Hercules. The new Type-250XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor has a higher output, increasing the maximum speed slightly--from 86.4 KPHto 110 KPH. The 2325 upgrade further boosted the speed to 140KPH. The entire protective system has been overhauled. The metal plates are replaced with Boron-Nitride Composite armor, and plasma and magnetic shields protect against more damage than the old Hercules could take.
Production of the Hercules was bumped up to the maximum levels after it out-performed the Bushwhacker in every trial during war games. Fortunately, its small size and modular construction made overhauling the Rig easy. The entire Prefecture complement was upgraded by the end of March. The Hercules is the main-line skirmisher of the Legions and the Regulars. It fights at close range to keep the enemy slowed down until heavier Rigs can arrive to dish out more damage. Against small Volucris like Nymphs and Cutters, its a powerhouse of combat capacity. Only larger melee units like the Slicer can match it in hand-to-hand.

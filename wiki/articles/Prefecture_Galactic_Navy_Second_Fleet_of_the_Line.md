The Hammers of the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] is the name for [[Jason Bremen]]'s Second Fleet, led by the flagship FDS ''Triumph''.  The Hammers are the toughest collection of warships and crews around.  They excel in warship-to-warship combat, and are the premier starship navigators.  In any combined-fleet operation, Second Fleet commanders handle the bulk of warship-to-warship action.

===Racial Demographics===

[[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] has a population of almost entirely [[Lorithos]] humans. Some [[Terrans]] and [[Meroniri]] live on [[Lorithos]] worlds, but they are usually non-citizen residents.

===Immigration and Citizenship===

[[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] has had a high emmigration rate since [[First Contact]]. Immigration law is non-existant. Anyone wishing to live on [[Lorithan]] or its colonies are welcome.

===Religion===

The [[Lorithos]] have only had one religion since the [[Dawn Emperor]] united [[Lorithan]] under his rule. The "Twin Gods", Mirdek the Sun King and Ashtir the Moon Queen, represent the dual dominances of [[Lorithos]] culture. During the [[Burning Wars]], religion was almost universally forsaken, but it has enjoyed an enormous surge in popularity in the last three generations, and nearly 65% of all [[Lorithos]] are "orthodox" believers.

Traditionally, practitioners and procelatyzers of other religions were drowned. Since [[First Contact]], this practice has been abolished. Other religions are politely tolerated, but not welcome. The [[Mesarthrim]] set up the only Christian church on [[Lorithan]], but when the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] broke out, the mission was abandoned and no subsequent efforts to convert the [[Lorithos]] have been attempted by anyone.

===Languages===

The [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] not only has not adopted English, but has not even agreed on a single national language.

[[Huorak]] is the main dialect of the [[Lorithos]], and is spoken on all three [[Lorithos]] worlds. The [[Huorak Alphabet]] is the only form of written language the [[Lorithos]] use, and it has a 28-letter cuneiform alphabet.

[[Joresha]] is a second dialect, which uses to [[Huorak Alphabet]] in its written form. Traditionally, members of the [[Lorithos]] military are taught [[Joresha]], a tradition that dates back to the days when the [[Dawn Emperor]] was conquering and conscripting people from all over the world and needed a language that all of his soldiers would understand. [[Joresha]] is used today in most religious ceremonies and observations. [[Joresha]] is spoken on all three [[Lorithos]] worlds.

Because they are new to the galactic community, most Lorithos do not even understand [[English]]. Those that do know it speak it haltingly, often searching for words.

The HMS ''Glorious'' was one of the last ships built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

It was given hull designation CV-43, and commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2318]].

The HMS ''Glorious'' was placed under the command of Captain [[Julia Michaels]], who commanded the ship throughout its career. The ''Glorious'' served with distinction, commanding one of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s more effective task forces.

In the immediate aftermath of the [[Imperial Civil War]], the ''Glorious'' was hunted down by ships loyal to the [[Prefecture]], and was destroyed on March 15, [[2323]]. The furious battle was fought between the ''Glorious'' and its two [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]] escorts against three [[Prefecture]] [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] groups, buying fifteen other [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] sufficient time to escape. The ''Glorious'' was lost with all hands, ending the distinguished career of [[Julia Michaels]].

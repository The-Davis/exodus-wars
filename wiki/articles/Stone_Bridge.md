The name “'''Stone Bridge'''” applies to two [[Mesarthrim]] locations, the Stone Bridge itself and the Stone Bridge city.

== The Stone Bridge ==

The Stone Bridge itself is a massive and ancient construct that spans the [[Gorge of Sorrow]], so named for the millions of [[Mesarnak]] who perished through execution by being flung into its depths by angered [[Anderung]]. The construct is impossibly large and strong by human standards. The visible portion is 130km long and 40km wide, but comparably shallow, with a depth of only a kilometer. The rest of the construct extends into the walls of the gorge for hundreds of kilometers in each direction, getting deeper and deeper. It is believed that the construct is actually an enormous ring buried under the surface of [[Mesar]], though its purpose and history are unknown.

== Stone Bridge City ==

The Stone Bridge city was built over the last few hundred years; first by the [[Anderung]], then by their [[Mesarnak]] slaves, then by the [[Mesarthrim]]. It is a fortified metropolis, and has served as the capital city of [[Mesar]] since the world’s founding by the [[Anderung]] in time immemorial.

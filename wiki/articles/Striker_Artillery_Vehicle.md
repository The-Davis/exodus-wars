*Power Plant: Type-300XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 75 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver

===Overview===

One of the staples of any military is the its ability to quickly and effectively blanket an area with a massive barrage of artillery. The reason that this tactic, even in the age of anti-grave vehicles and mechs, is still important is that it not only has a massive psychological impact on the enemy, but it also keeps them pinned.

[[Image:StrikerArtilleryVehicle1.jpg]]

Therefore, the Striker was built to fill the role of an artillery system that could effectively put down almost constant suppressive fire in the form of rockets.

===Capabilities===

The most important capability that any kind of artillery system has is its extreme range from which it can pound the enemy into submission. The Striker's rockets, specially built for this mission, have a minimum range of approximately two kilometers and a maximum range of approximately one hundred kilometers.

Along with the decent range that the Striker offers, the number of different munitions that a rocket, the ERR-100, can carry is almost mind boggling. The ERR-100 can carry everything from anti-personnel submunitions to full fledge thermonuclear warheads, giving the commanders in the field a variety of options to deal with any given situation.

[[Image:StrikerArtilleryVehicle2.jpg]]

The Striker only requires a crew of two to actually man the vehicle, a driver and a fire control officer who is responsible for the target acquisition and the firing of the weapon. The system can be reloaded within seven minutes with help of a reloading crew of two. This time has been drastically reduced from the original long range rocket systems of the past by almost five minutes.

===Deployment===

As with a number of new systems, the Striker hasn't been out on the market for that long now, but there are already orders pouring into Rahn Industries. Most of the consumers want to upgrade their old, tired  long range rocket systems for the newer, lower maintenance version.

[[Image:StrikerArtilleryVehicle3.jpg]]

As usual, Rahn Industries is offering to buy the older systems for scrap and allowing that to count as credit to new purchases. This allows R.I. to get relatively cheap scrap and helps to recycle the metal that would usually end up in landfills or target ranges.

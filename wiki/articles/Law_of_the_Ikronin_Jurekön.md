[[Ikronin]] laws are simple. The only standing law that cannot be changed is the Articles of Confederation, which each corporation wishing to join the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] must sign.

==Articles of Confederation==
===Article I===
*'''Section 1''': The Corporations of [[Yngard System|Kronheim]] shall be held to the responsibilities framed in these Articles. The Articles may be altered and amended only by a general assembly of the people.
*'''Section 2'''
**''Clause 1'': The Corporations will keep a standing army of no less than 5% of their employees. This army shall be used for the common defense of [[Yngard System|Kronheim]] and its civilians.
**''Clause 2'': Representatives from each Corporation shall be sent to our allies to negotiate treaties on the behalf of [[Yngard System|Kronheim]]. No Corporation will send more representatives than any other Corporation, and each representative shall have one vote.
**''Clause 3'': The Corporations will regulate and mint currency, and agree on a single standard for all the people of [[Yngard System|Kronheim]].
**''Clause 4'': Criminal code enforcement will be enforced by representatives chosen by a general assembly of the people. These representatives will ensure the rights of life, liberty and property for all [[Ikronin]].

===Article II===
*'''Section 1''': The [[Wormhole Gates]] must remain open to all [[Ikronin]]. Each Corporation of [[Yngard System|Kronheim]] shall be responsible for the construction, maintenance and fee collection for travel to and from districts which shall be assigned every five years.

Lasers New laser technology is based on the frequency of the laser's energy rather than the size of the beam. A Large Laser beam is the same size as a Small Laser beam, but the higher frequency of the Large Laser does more damage. Large Lasers emit a blue beam, Medium Lasers emit a green beam, and Small Lasers emit a red beam. There are four types of lasers: Standard, Extended Range (ER), Pulse, and Heavy. Standard lasers are the mainstay weapons of most modern militaries. There is nothing special about them. ER lasers have double the range of normal lasers, but are slightly less accurate. ER lasers also do a little more damage than Standard lasers. Pulse lasers fire in short, rapid bursts rather than a single continuing beam. This makes them much more accurate, and also more powerful. They are equal in range to a Standard laser, and equal in power to an ER laser. Heavy lasers are extremely powerful. Their odd energy signatures make them less accurate than Standard lasers, but they are twice as powerful. Their range is less than Standard and Pulse lasers due to their accuracy problems.

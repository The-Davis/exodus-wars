'''Ayar Station''' is an [[Ixoarchada]] installation in orbit of [[Kalidasa]] in the [[Lupei System]].  It is the only Ixoarchada installation in the [[Njiorin Sector]], and is also their most coreward port-of-call.

After the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]], the Ixoarchada moved quickly to establish a presence in the mid-arm sectors as part of the sovereignty patrols undertaken by Council navy forces.  In 2328, as part of the division of capitals within the Council, Kalidasa was chosen as the third capital, alongside [[Junheim]] and [[Ah Kin]].  It was in far orbit of Kalidasa that the Ixoarchada built Ayar Station.

Approximately 75% of the station's operating budget is paid for by the Taviridis, with the remaining budget provided by other Council members nations.  The chambers of the [[Mutual Defense Council]] are located on Ayar Station, and it is also the headquarters of [[Council Defense Command|CODECOM]] in times of conflict.  

By the terms of the founding charter of Ayar Station, the Taviridis are allowed to maintain a fleet presence in the Lupei System.  Although the station nominally provides material support for ships from all member nations, the majority of its berths and docks are used for Taviridis warships, earning the station its foreboding moniker: the ''Keep''.  

Unofficially, Ayar Station also functions as an Ixoarchada consular station for Council member nations.  Ayar is much closer to most Council worlds than the distant [[Prometheus Stardock]], and so a great deal of diplomatic undertakings are handled there.

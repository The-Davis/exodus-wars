'''Talire Hauren''', the "City of Palaces", was once the capital city of the planet [[Lorithan]] and the [[Rikaz o fii Huern iv Lorithan]]. It was constructed during the height of the reign of [[Talire Huern]], and subsequently destroyed during the [[Burning Wars]]. After the wars, the capital was moved back to [[Hamur Hauren]].

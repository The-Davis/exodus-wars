'''William Duke'''
*Born: September 15, [[2041]]
*Place of Birth: [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''William Duke'' (born September 15, [[2041]]) is one of the oldest survivors from the world of [[Torimur]]. In his early life, he was part of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s Marine Corps. He served as a member of the [[Alphas]], and was sent ahead to [[Ah Kin]], where he integrated himself into their society and eventually became the world's [[Prime Minister of the United Earth Alliance|Prime Minister]]. 

When the [[Omegas]] assassinated [[Jacob Michaels]], Duke became the [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]]. He began enacting changes under [[Lucius Black]]'s orders, and converted [[Ah Kin]] into a covert power base for the [[Onyx Empire]].

William Duke faithfully served the [[Onyx Empire]], even training the [[Royal Imperial Marine Corps]]. After the [[Imperial Civil War]], Duke surrendered to the [[Prefecture]]. He was tried for war crimes, but acquitted when it was discovered that he never aided in any of [[Lucius Black]]'s death programs.

Today, Duke is in charge of training the new [[Prefecture Marine Corps]].

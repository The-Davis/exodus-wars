While the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] had enormous landing craft that could deploy troops on a planet's surface, and the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] had huge cargo [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] that could drop troops into combat situations, the [[Royal Imperial Army]] wanted a [[:Category:VTOLs|VTOL]] that could land large numbers of troops with precision.

The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] developed a large VTOL airframe to move larger numbers of troops. The XH-5 did not work well due to problems with the particle field generator, and the project was eventually abandoned: the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] received its the project's budget to further develop their landing craft.

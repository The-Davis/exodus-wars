The M-28e, like the [[M-28c Napalm Grenade|M-28c]] was designed for urban warfare. Unlike the [[white napalm|napalm]] weapon, which released its contents to burn downwards, the M-28e was designed by the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] to release a highly inflamable vapor and ignite it, creating a miniature firestorm.

Like the [[M-28c Napalm Grenade|M-28c]], the M-28e is not a solid metal sphere blown apart by an explosive charge. It was a composite sphere. The detonator scatters the inflamable vapor in all directions on impact, and detonates a tiny ignition charge seconds later.

Early M-28e models were notorious for blowing up on impact regardless of whether the safety pin had been pulled or not. After a faulty M-28e blew up an entire the ammunition and ordinance of an entire regiment in an accident, Emperor [[Lucius Black]] demanded that the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] refine the design. The modern M-28e remains in use today. It is much safer, but has been known on occasion to release its vapor without the pin being pulled. In these accidents, sparks could ignite the weapon, but no such incidents have been reported since [[2317]].

The '''Lupei System''' consists of the star Nu-II Lupei, the five planets, the moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Nu-II Lupei, the planets are Galeico, Sao Celticos, [[Kalidasa]], Detanos, and Cabral.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Nu-II Lupei is the Lupei System's only star. Nu-II Lupei is a class G4Y yellow dwarf star. The Lupei System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Lupei is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Kalidasa ===

[[Kalidasa]] (0.96AU) is the only inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Lupei System. It has a single moon, Madeira.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Lupei System was colonized in [[2093]]. It became a protectorate of the [[United Earth Alliance]], but was autonomously governed by a colonial authority working under the umbrella of the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]].

In [[2317]], the [[Second Exodus War]] had driven the last remains of the [[United Earth Alliance]] into the [[Titan System]]. When the [[Onyx Empire]] sent a division of the [[Royal Imperial Army]] and a carrier group from the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], the colonial government had no way of defending itself, and was forced to surrender all authority to the [[Government of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Government]].

Towards the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the local [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] managed to take control of [[Kalidasa]], and formed the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]].

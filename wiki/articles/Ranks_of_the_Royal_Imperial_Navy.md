The Rank system of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was a very traditional one, with Enlisted and Commissioned ranks.

== Enlisted Ranks ==

Enlisted is any rating below that of a commissioned officer. The term includes non-commissioned officers. Though it is the lowest of the ranks, it is the most important. The lower-enlisted are the core of the fighting force, while the non-commissioned officers provide direct leadership and training to the sailors.

* Seaman - In the Royal Imperial Navy, Seaman (E1) is used for the lowest enlisted rank, just below Seaman First Class. A Seaman wears a single chevron. Advancement to Seaman First Class is typically given after one year in service, but may get shortened to eight months or less based on merit and achievement.
* Seaman First Class - In the Royal Imperial Navy, Seaman First Class (E2) is the second lowest enlisted rank, just above Seaman and below Petty Officer Third Class. A Seaman First Class wears a chevron with one rocker.
* Petty Officer Third Class - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Petty Officer Third Class(E3) wears two chevrons.
* Petty Officer Second Class - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Petty Officer Second Class (E4) wears three chevrons.
* Petty Officer First Class - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Petty Officer First Class (E5) wears three chevrons and a rocker.
* Chief Petty Officer - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Chief Petty Officer (E6) wears three chevrons and two rockers.
* Senior Chief Petty Officer - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Senior Chief Petty Officer (E7) wears three chevrons and three rockers.
* Master Chief Petty Officer - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Master Chief Petty Officer (E8) wears three chevrons, three rockers, and a star.
* Master Chief Petty Officer of His Majesty's Royal Imperial Navy - In the Royal Imperial Navy the Master Chief Petty Officer of the Navy (E9) is the direct reporter and aide to the Grand Admiral. The Master Chief Petty Officer of the Navy wears three chevrons, three rockers, and the Imperial emblem surrounded by two stars.

== Commissioned Ranks ==

Commissioned officers are given formal orders from the Emperor, granting them their rank and privileges. This "commission" carries a heavy burden of responsibility. An officer is expected to fight, think, and lead better than any enlisted sailor.

* Ensign - In the Royal Imperial Navy an Ensign (O1) is the lowest commissioned rank, one usually given to officers who have just graduated officer's training school or a military academy. An Ensign wears a brass bar.
* Lieutenant Junior Grade - In the Royal Imperial Navy a Lieutenant JG (O2) is the highest rank that can be granted to an officer fresh out of training, and is typically promoted from an Ensign after 18 to 24 months of service, or 2-4 months of combat service. A Lieutenant JG wears a silver bar.
* Lieutenant - A Lieutenant (O3) wears two silver bars.
* Lieutenant Commander - A Lieutenant Commander (O4) wears a gold bar.
* Commander - A Commander (O5) wears two gold bars.
* Captain - A Captain (O6) wear a three gold bars.
* Commodore - A Commodore (O7) wears a silver star.
* Rear Admiral - A Rear Admiral (O8) wears two silver stars.
* Vice Admiral - A Vice Admiral (O9)wears three silver stars.
* Admiral - An Admiral (O10) wears four silver stars.
* Grand Admiral - The Grand Admiral (O11) is the supreme commander of all forces in the Navy, as well as the other branches of the Imperial Military. [[Anthony Hadrian]] was the first Grand Admiral, and he was succeeded by [[Michael Burke]]. The Grand Admiral wears a circle of five stars under an anchor.

The Baihros Estin Fohrvik was first theorized during the latter years of the Burning War. [[Seren Baihros]] was the physicist who headed up the project under the direction of [[Zak Huern]]. Huern's project saved the [[Lorithos]] people, when they managed to build an FTL ship and making first contact with the outside galaxy.

The Estin Fohrvik works with hyperspace, but not in the traditional sense. Most ships generate a Krenschov Effect to physically enter hyperspace, where they remain until they complete their journey. The Baihros Estin maintains two stable portals in hyperspace, or "Fohrvik" as the Lorithos call it, then flings a ship through in a matter of seconds.

The "tunnel" (Estin) opens into and through the [[Sem Band]] of hyperspace. Unlike [[hyperspace]] ships, which carry their generators with them, and unlike [[Wormhole]] Gates, which are two-way, a ship launched through the Estin Fohrvik is on a one-way trip. It must make arrangements from the other side to return.

The Estin Fohrvik can be opened at varying distances, with a limit of 40 light years. Large ships that travel into deep space carry disposable one-shot Estin generators with them, which they drop off, use to open a tunnel, and fly through.

While more efficient methods of travel are available--such as [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole]] Gates and [[Meroniri]] [[Tesseract]] Drives, the Estin Fohrvik is the only method locally available to the [[Lorithos]] people.

'''Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi'''
*Born: October 2, [[1869]]
*Died: August 15, [[1947]]
*Place of Birth: England, [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: India, [[Terra]]

''Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi'' (born October 2, [[1869]]) was a political and spiritual leader of India and the Indian independence movement. He was a proponent of the failed idea of resisting occupation through mass civil disobedience, founded upon total non-violence — which he tried to use to free India from the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]].

Ultimately, the movement failed. Gandhi and his closest friends and leaders in the movement were hanged by British officials.

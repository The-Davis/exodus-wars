The HMS ''Vigilant'' was the lead ship in the [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]] [[Vigilant Class Frigate|class of the same name]]. It was also the first [[:Category:Warships|warship]] of any variety of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and was given the hull designation FG-1.

As a warship, it led a rather unimpressive career, serving in the [[Earth]] invasion and blockade, but being destroyed in the [[Titan System]] campaign in [[2316]].

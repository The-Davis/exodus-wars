In the spring of [[2318]], the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] cells on [[Tejat]] had caused significant problems for [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] operations.  The best known leader of the resistance in the area, [[Talaris Roi]], was believed to be in the city of [[Damanir]].  Roi was famous for her inspirational speeches and songs, and the [[Omegas]] believed that if she were captured or killed, resistance in the [[Sol Sector]] would be undermined.

The Damanir operation was commanded by Omega Brian Alexston.  Two hundred Omegas agents were sent into the city along with heavy weapons squads from the [[Royal Imperial Army]], while Colonel John Lynde's regiment kept the city isolated from the rest of Tejat.  Originally, the plan called for a door-to-door sweep, but after resistance members shot and killed three Omegas agents, Agent Alexston ordered a mass door-to-door sweep and execution of the entire city.

When Colonel Lynde's barricades began to be overrun by fleeing civilians, he detained and questioned several.  Upon learning that Agent Alexston was executing everyone in sight, Colonel Lynde defied orders and moved his entire regiment into the city.  The Royal Imperial Army located and killed Agent Alexston, and Colonel Lynde arrested the remaining Omegas agents and had them shipped back to [[Earth]].

Emperor [[Lucius Black]] became personally involved in the follow-up investigation, and he cleared Colonel Lynde of insurrection charges after determining that Agent Alexston had deviated from the original orders and endangered Imperial assets by engaging in a personal vendetta against the city.

The [[Prefecture]] later used the goodwill generated by the incident to solidify the bond between partisan resistance cells and the rebelling elements of the Royal Imperial Military.  Sadly, Colonel Lynde was killed in the early days of the [[Torimur Civil War]], and never saw the liberation of the city he saved.

This Damanir Incident was unique in that it was the only time in Imperial history where the Omegas were openly defied by the rest of the Onyx Empire at large.

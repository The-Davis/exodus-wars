The Gatrir was built after the Burning War. During the terrible conflict, there was no need to transport tanks and infantry between the stars.

[[Image:GatrirClassFreighter1.jpg]]

When the Rikaz Lorithos became a Member State of the Alliance of Planets, it became necessary to have long-range, high-capacity transports. Commerce was the only hope the Lorithos had, and the Gatrir class freighter helped them achieve it.

Gatrirs carry no fighters, but they do carry infantry, tanks, and the MGTF's STOVL aircraft. The freighter can unload its cargo very quickly, particularly with the help of STOVL transport aircraft. Normally, a Gatrir can drop its troops and vehicles without landing. Loading on Lorithan takes minutes--the Kihtor (Space Elevators) make it easy. On other worlds, it's a little more difficult, because the Gatrir cannot land.

[[Image:GatrirClassFreighter2.jpg]]

Because the Gatrir isn't a combat vessel, it doesn't carry heavy panels of armor or a large number of shield generators. Its Tzaiko armor and Garvask shields are strong enough to ward off cross-fire and debris, but concentrated attacks can punch through.

Gatrirs are almost equally MGTF ships as they are ASTF ships. Many MGTF troopers prefer the decks of their transport to the barren wastelands of Lorithan.

Whenever the MGTF needs to reinforce a Rikaz Lorithos world, the Gatrir is certainly going to be used in bulk. The Lorithos have never built another transport model. They've never seen the need.

Kigao Proton-Bond Armor Kigao armor is made of one of the oddest substances in the known universe. The [[Ngrligru]] scholar caste has understood the concept of bonding sub-atomic particles for centuries, but recently discovered ways to manipulate even finer particles at the quantum level. The properties of active Kigao armor are variable.

It can mimic most human armor systems without error, and can alter its physical state on the fly to deal with different weapon systems. It can even change shape, giving [[Ngrligru]] vessels an almost-organic look. As long as the energy flow is maintained, [[Ngrligru]] warriors can continue to change their armor. The moment energy is lost, it reverts to its default shape and state, which is closest to [[Terran]] composite armor systems.

Shrewd battle commanders will target the energy capacitors used to maintain the armor's capabilities at their earliest opportunity.

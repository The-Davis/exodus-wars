*'''Headquarters''': Headquartered on [[Albanus]]
*'''Industry''': Composites and textiles
*'''Products''': Body Armor

'''Allied Body Armor''', based on [[Albanus]], designs and engineers boron-nitride and other composites into armor and clothing for military and police. They also have a distribution center for private security and private citizens, though their main market is the military.

Allied Body Armor is also famous for its "VIProtector" line of second-chance body armor, designed to be worn under formal wear for ambassadors, politicians, CEOs, and other VIPs.

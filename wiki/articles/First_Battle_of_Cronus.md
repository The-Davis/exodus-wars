The First Battle of Cronus was the first engagement in the prolonged siege of the [[Titan System]], and the first official action of [[Operation Tartarus]]. The three-day battle had surprisingly small losses--it was largely a series of maneuvers jockeying for position around the planet. In the end, it was a strategic and tactical victory for the [[Onyx Empire]].

== Recon Flights ==

On December 3, [[2315]], observatories throughout the [[Titan System]] began to detect [[hyperspace]] jumps in the regions of the outer planets. [[United Earth Alliance]] combat patrols often observed [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|Valkyrie]] flights engaged in reconaissance, though they were too far away to engage in battle.

The flights were seen near [[Phoebe]], [[Tethys]], and [[Cronus]], the three outer-most planets of the Titan System. Given the Titan System's remote location compared to the rest of [[Terrandom]], General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] believed that the inevitable Imperial assault would focus on the creation of a permanent base to use as a staging ground for the invasion of the rest of the System.

With the remains of the 3rd and 5th Space Battle Groups defending [[Theia]] and [[Rhea]], and the 13th Space Battle Group defending the various installations throughout the Titan System, General-Commander Sanburn chose to deploy the 17th Space Battle Group to the outer planets and begin aggressive patrols. On December 5, 2315, Commodore-Commander [[Gordon Dannins]] was given command of the operation, and set his headquarters in orbit of [[Mnemosyne]].

The warships under Dannins' command were deployed to Phoebe, Tethys, and Cronus. Tethys was judged to be the most likely invasion point. With eight moons and a large debris field, it made the planning and placement of static defenses a simple task. Cronus was judged to be the least likely invasion point, and only two [[ADF-5000 Class Cruiser]]s and four [[ADF-4000 Class Destroyer]]s were deployed to defend the planet.

== Diversions ==

Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] was not idle in his preparations. He had assigned the [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy#Fourth Imperial Fleet|Fourth Imperial Fleet]] to the operation, but rather than immediately invade the System, he advised Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] that he wanted to make the Alliance "sweat a bit."

The Fourth Imperial Fleet, under the command of Rear Admiral [[Oswald Voss]], was deployed to a staging point just outside of sensor range of the Titan System. The Fleet began a series of feints into the Titan System, launching entire carrier groups at various targets and immediately withdrawing. The Fourth Imperial Fleet was only at partial strength with four carrier groups (the remaining ships would not be completed until [[2320]]), but the Third Imperial Fleet was en route for the full siege operation.

On December 7, the [[HMS Courageous|HMS ''Courageous'']] and its escorts were dispatched to Tethys, where they staged a dramatic fly-by of the Alliance defenses in the area. While they were well out of range of the main weapons of either side, the Imperial ships took several pot-shots with torpedoes before retreating back into [[hyperspace]]. At the same time, the [[HMS Audacious|HMS ''Audacious'']], [[HMS Magnificent|HMS ''Magnificent'']] and their escorts probed the defenses of Phoebe, while the [[HMS Victorious|HMS ''Victorious'']]'s fighter wings flew recon near Cronus.

For the next four days, the Empire's strategy was to agitate the Alliance commanders into second-guessing their deployment strategy. The four carrier groups of the Fourth Imperial Fleet were deployed to each of the three potential target planets, in varying numbers and for varying times. On December 11, all four carrier groups deployed to Tethys, along with several [[Mercury Class Transport]]s, and waited just long enough for the Alliance ships to get into firing range before retreating into hyperspace. The defenders nerves were on a razor's edge.

== The Battle ==

On December 12, the Battle of Cronus began in earnest. The HMS ''Magnificent'' and its escorts launched a full assault on the Alliance's ships near Cronus, burning down the [[UEAS Disciple|UEAS ''Disciple'']] in the opening salvo. At the same time, the HMS ''Victorious'' jumped to Phoebe, and began saturating the area with as much radio interference as possible, jamming communications for the defenses stationed there.

Commodore-Commander Dannins was convinced that Cronus was a feint, and sent reinforcements to Phoebe. The Empire's forces once again proved to be more of a psychological frustration than an actual danger, and all elements of the Fourth Imperial Fleet withdrew to their staging area.

On December 13 and December 14, the Imperial forces remained quiet. Rear Admiral Voss allowed all of his crews to take an additional two hours of sleep each day, and doubled food allowances. The ships ran dark, waiting for the final step in the battle.

On December 15, all four carrier groups of the Fourth Imperial Fleet jumped back to Cronus, and engaged the defenses in earnest. The battle rose to a fever pitch, and the commanders called Commodore-Commander Dannins, desperate for reinforcements.

The reinforcements never came. The entirety of the [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy#Third Imperial Fleet|Third Imperial Fleet]] had arrived, and jumped into Mnemosyne, Tethys, and Phoebe simultaneously. With twelve carrier groups and the [[HMS Jerome Black|HMS ''Jerome Black'']] to deal with, Dannins ordered a general retreat from Cronus to shore up defenses of the Alliance's vital resources near Mnemosyne.

Retreating proved deadly. The [[UEAS Fall of Moscow|UEAS ''Fall of Moscow'']] and the [[UEAS Suez Canal|UEAS ''Suez Canal'']] both lost shield integrity and were gutted by MAC fire from the destroyers in the Fourth Imperial Fleet. 

As soon as Cronus was secure, the Third Imperial Fleet withdrew from its feinting maneuvers, and reinforced Cronus. Fifteen [[Mercury Class Transport]]s arrived soon afterwards, deploying landing groups from the [[Royal Imperial Army]] and establishing the Empire's new forward base in the Titan System.

== Aftermath ==

Many in the United Earth Alliance's political and command structure criticized Gordon Dannins for withdrawing so early from the battle, but General-Commander Sanburn endorsed and affirmed his command decisions. The Onyx Empire's deployment was extremely effective, and had the defenses of Mnemosyne (a source of vital resources) been sacrificed to reinforce Cronus (a barren wasteland at the time), the Alliance could have found Mnemosyne snatched out from under them. While the Empire had a total strategic and tactical victory, the result could have been a lot worse for the Alliance.

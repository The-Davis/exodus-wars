'''Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Einer''' ''(MBHE)'' is translated as Military Command Headquarters One.  There are three of these facilities located on [[Mesar]], MBHE, [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Zwei|MBHZ]], & [[Militärische Befehl Hauptsitze Drei|MBHD]].  They were built during the resistance, and served as the only permanent military structures during the resistance.

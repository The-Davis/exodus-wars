===Overview===

The Alaska Class Heavy Carrier is the backbone of any fleet's fighter command.  It has the carrying capacity of eight Titan Light Carriers.

[[Image:AlaskaClassCarrier1.jpg]]

The Alaska herself was built by Rahn industries in February of 2324, but the ship of the line model underwent several modifications before the series was approved.  The Blackis Prefecture ordered nine Alaska Class Carriers, and first one, the Utah, launched in May of 2324.

The Alaska Class line is fully equipped with Phoenix Project technology, which made its debut on the First Fleet Flagship Jerome Black.

===Capabilities===

The Alaska Class can fight, but its primary function is a carrier.

The carrier has four main bays, which have a pair of flat-deck runways.  Runways are not necessary to launch fighters, but the wide open space allows dozens of fighters to be launched at once, instead of the limited one or two launches at a time in the popular "hole in the hull" hangar style.  The main bays of the Alaska are for fighters, mostly Piranhas and Stukas.

The carrier also has four secondary bays, two for Bloodhawk bombers, and two for shuttles and transports.  These bays are behind the main bays, and are "hole in the hull" type hangars, so shuttle and bomber launches must be carefully coordinated by the carrier's crew.

The Flight Control computer systems aboard an Alaska are very advanced, and have an AI for each hangar bay to assist in launch coordination.  These AIs, while complex, are nowhere near the power or capacity of Arthur or Prism.  Even with an AI, each bay requires two human operators.

An Alaska can fight, though if attacked by a true warship its much better off relying on its fighter complement or warship escorts.  The main weapon is a Rahn Industries Hellgun anti-warship cannon.  The cannon is very popular in the Blackis fleet, as its fire rate is higher than any other weapon, projectile or energy.

[[Image:AlaskaClassCarrier2.jpg]]

The Hellgun is backed up by twelve Naval Autocannons.  Aside from these cannons and the Hellgun, an Alaska has no further dedicated anti-warship defenses.

The Alaska has a large array of thirty-two fast-tracking Neutron Cannon turrets.  These weapons have become widely used very shortly after their development, as they are just as cheap as lasers and incredibly effective.

Like most Blackis warships, the Alaska class has multiple variable-ordinance tactical missile tubes.  These thirty-two launchers can be armed for anti-aerofighter or anti-warship defense, but have no dedicated function.  An Alaska's commander uses them to his best possible advantage.

The Alaska Class line, like the Jerome Black, uses three shield technologies and advanced composite armor.  With Gravity, Magnetic, and Plasma shields, very little can get through to the vulnerable crew without pounding the ship with hundreds of rounds of ammunition.

The Alaska Class uses huge gravity cavitation drives to move it through space.  These are infinitely more effective than ancient rocket engines or slightly more modern impulse drives, and most warships are being refitted with them.  The Alaska line has the advantage of being built with them.  The thrust to mass ratio allows the Alaska Class to accelerate at 0.28 kilometers per hour per second.

===Troops and Crew===

While the crew of a Blackis warship is required to be armed at all times, it is usually necessary to have heavy weaponry to repel boarders.  To aid in such a conflict, an Alaska has a permanent complement of thirty-five marines and five suits of battle armor.

Despite the large size of an Alaska, it doesn't require a large crew.  Most weapons are handled by targeting AI, with only 25 gunners to assist the computers.  When weapons, engineering, navigation, and fighter maintenance crews are taken into account, the Alaska only needs 100 crew.  It carries 140 in order to give some of the crew time to sleep and eat without worrying about duty or an attack.

It's very fortunate for the crew that the Alaska can be operated by relatively few people, as the ship still must accommodate hundreds of pilots for its fighters.

===Deployment===

Nine Alaska Class Carriers have been ordered from Rahn Industries.  One will be sent to each fleet, except for the First and Fourth Fleets, which will receive two and three respectively.

[[Image:AlaskaClassCarrier3.jpg]]

The original Alaska is currently stationed over Ueda.  Rahn Industries and other companies use it as a test platform for new fighters, bombers, and civilian personal and mass transit vehicles.

One interesting feature of the Alaska Class line is the names of the ships.  Prefecture Fleet Command decided to name them after states of the old North American Alliance of the 21st century.  The first completed was the Utah, and the second was named the Nova Scotia.

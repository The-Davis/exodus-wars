== Events of 2108 ==

The three contacted races--the [[Terrans]], the [[Meroniri]], and the [[Ikronin]]--decided to begin a program of exploration, expansion, and mutual benefit. At first, this was an informal arrangement, but several forward-thinking leaders began to develop [[First Contact]] scenarios.

It was decided that any new races or species discovered would need a friendly, unified face to welcome them into the galaxy as starfarers. With this in mind, the [[Alliance of Planets]] was formed and the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter|Great Charter]] was drafted.

While the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] was wildly successful at exploration, commerce, and scientific achievement, it was never meant to be a means to engage in warfare.

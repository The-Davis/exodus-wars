''Talaris Ruptka Roi'' (born February 24, [[2290]]) was the [[Consul]] in the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] government. She was an important figure in the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] against the [[Onyx Empire]], and was a Senator before her election to the position of Consul.

Roi led the Prefecture through a tumultuous time after the loss of their founding Consul, [[Christopher Dayton]], and the tragic [[Tempest War]].  After the signing of the [[Treaty of Tavou]], Roi conspired to protect [[Anthony Hadrian]] from the treaty-imposed war crimes tribunals, and destroyed evidence that was meant for those trials.  She was impeached from office and publicly flogged in accordance with Prefecture law.

== Birth ==

Talaris Roi is a native of [[Tejat]], born in [[2290]]. Like most of her people, Talaris is almost entirely Indian in descent. While the Indian race and some of their culture emigrated to [[Tejat]], they were predominantly "cultural converts" who followed extremely Westernized traditions.

Talaris was born during the decline of [[United Earth Alliance]] power. While part of a significant upper middle-class family and quite well-off, she had no influence and little interest in the government of [[Tejat]] until after the Imperial invasion in [[2315]].

== Early Life ==

Talaris' father's business assets actually improved significantly under Imperial rule, elevating the Roi family into upper class. Talaris was estranged from her family due to their Imperial ties--many people of [[Tejat]] suffered under Imperial rule: those who submitted profited greatly, but those who refused to submit simply disappeared without a trace. Talaris lost many friends before she discovered the Resistance.

Much of Roi's education during this time was for more aristocratic pursuits, and she mastered several musical instruments.  During the same time, she learned important secrets about her family's business and ties with the [[Onyx Empire]], which later became valuable to the resistance.

== Resistance ==

Talaris Roi joined the resistance in [[2316]], at the age of 26. She worked largely in espionage and subterfuge, rarely taking part in direct combat. Talaris' duties involved spiriting away dissenters before the [[Omegas]]' executioners could arrive, and in finding [[Alphas]]' infiltrators within [[Tejat]] society. The resistance was responsible for the capture of many [[Alphas]], and sending valuable information to other resistance groups on other Imperial worlds.

In [[2318]], the head of the resistance met with Talaris Roi and other members, and shared his vision of a new, free [[Torimur]] government. She did not know it at the time, but the head of the resistance was actually the [[Grand Admiral]] of the [[Empire]], [[Anthony Hadrian]].

Inspired by Hadrian's vision, Talaris Roi became a significant member of the resistance, risking death time and again to sabotage Imperial sources on [[Tejat]]. Despite her best efforts, the [[Empire]] was not driven off of [[Tejat]] until after it was driven off of [[Terra]].

== Prefecture ==

With the founding of the [[Prefecture]], Talaris Roi encouraged the people of [[Tejat]] to sign on, and was eventually elected as a Senator in the District of worlds named after [[Tejat]]. In [[2325]], after the capture of [[Consul]] [[Chris Dayton]], Talaris Roi was appointed interim [[Consul]], a position to which she was later elected.

Roi's tenure as [[Consul]] waas been marked by remarkable changes in diplomatic outreach missions. Ties with the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] were strengthened greatly. The Consortium and the [[Prefecture]] work together on multiple mutual aid projects. Trade relations with the [[Reigens]] and the [[Ixoarchada]] were also opened, causing the [[Prefecture]] economy to expand rapidly.

Talaris Roi's administration during the [[Tempest War]] was often shaky.  She was a superb diplomat, but poorly suited to put forward the aggressive face required to fight the war.  Roi was heavily eclipsed by the office of the [[Prefect]] during the war, and [[Anthony Hadrian]] was often in the forefront of political discussion and news.  Roi was an outspoken supporter of Hadrian's actions, and fought hard for increased funding for the Prefecture military during the war.

After the disastrous loss of [[Laojiu]] and the renewed [[Mesarthrim]] Blitzkrieg in [[2329]], many members of the government called for a surrender, fearing the destruction of their worlds in the manner of [[Leskovik]] and [[Adelson]].  Talaris Roi rallied the more aggressive and borderline representatives in the Senate and Curiate, and managed to defeat the surrender movement.

== Scandal ==

After the war, Talaris Roi was horrified to discover that Anthony Hadrian was being indicted in the [[Magrinoch Trials]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Tavou]].

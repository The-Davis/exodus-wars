'''Nagathu''' is an [[Ixoarchada]] term for a group of warships which is most often translated into [[English]] as "Battle Fleet".  Nagathu, literally "stellar host", are rarely a set number of [[:Category:Warships|warships]], ranging from a few dozen to several hundred, and are assembled on an as-needed basis.  They are the primary elements of any [[Taviridis Somarchada]] naval force, and the commander of a given operation will have complete authority over all ships in the fleet.  

Nagathu are usually named for their system or frontier of origin.  For example, a Nagathu comprised of ships from the [[Ularen System]] would most likely be designated "Nagathu Pithari Ularen".

The '''Grand Admiral''' of the [[Onyx Empire]] was the supreme command of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]], and answered only to [[Lucius Black]].

[[Anthony Hadrian]] held the position from the creation of the [[Onyx Empire]] until he rebelled during the [[Imperial Civil War]].

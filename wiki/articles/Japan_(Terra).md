The '''Empire of Japan''' was an island country in East Asia. Located in the Pacific Ocean, it lies to the east of [[People's Republic of China (Terra)| China]], [[Korea (Terra)| Korea]] and [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Russia]], stretching from the Sea of Okhotsk in the north to the East China Sea in the south. The characters that make up Japan's name means "sun-origin", which is why Japan is sometimes identified as the "Land of the Rising Sun".

Japan was dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] on April 3, [[2011]].

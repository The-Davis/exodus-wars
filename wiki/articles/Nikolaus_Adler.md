''Nikolaus Adler'' is a Myt'kampfen General, having served both the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clans]] and the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] Heer. Adler was sentenced at the [[Magrinoch Trials]] to five years house arrest in [[Kronheim]].

==Service Record==
===Second Exodus War===
Adler commanded the [[Myt'kampfen|Jaguar Panzer]] heavy tank division during [[Operation Imperial Exorcism]]. Adler's Panzer Gruppe routed the [[Royal Imperial Army|Imperial Army]] out of its hill-top positions overlooking 'Death Ridge', allowing Heer artillery to reign down on the prepared Imperial positions. As other elements of the Heer finally took 'Death Ridge', Adler's position came under attack by orbital bombardment. Adler's command car was hit, and he suffered severe injury - removing him from active duty.

===Mesarthrim Civil War===
During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], Adler was approached by his old commander, [[Antol Nik]], and was convinced to support the Centusi States. Adler shipped out for [[Sirius Napoli|Napoli]] where he took command of the [[Northern Mesarthrim Heer|Nordmesarthrim Heer]]. Adler served as a Garrison General, until he was promoted to a Field Marshall, and shipped out for the first major campaign in the [[Tempest War]].

===Tempest War===
Adler served as the head Myt'kampfen General in the [[Invasion of Laojiu District|Laojiu Campaign]]. Early in the campaign, Adler petitioned for the honour of delivering the surrender demands to Härdad, but was superseded by Captain [[Lothar Metzger]] - who ultimately gave his life. Later in the campaign, Adler was responsible for capturing Fathach Méire, and subduing the resistance. Adler would go on to lead his men in securing the [[Avior System]]. Adler's command post is hit severely outside Osarwa on [[Casere]], forcing Adler to relinquish command for almost three months while he heals.

Adler's only combat in the Tempest War is within the Laojiu District. Adler was present at each invasion, and for both counter-attacks, earning him six devices for his service in Laojiu. Additionally, Adler was so successful in his defense of Laojiu, that he was still in control of the district when the war ended.

Adler submitted himself willingly to arrest when he received word of the Centusi States collapse. Adler also arranged for all Myt'kampfen under his command to lay down their arms in exchange for expedited processing as POWs and being returned to their lives.

===Magrinoch Trial===
Adler was the very first defendant of the Magrinoch Trials, and was found guilty on a count of willfully ordering the imprisonment of individuals deemed hostile to the Centusi States. In recognition of his willingness to cooperate with the transition, and peaceful surrender - Adler was charged to only serve five years under house arrest at an estate in Kronheim. Adler was also offered the chance to serve the Federation upon his release to earn a Redemption Star. Adler refused, however, stating his intention to retire.

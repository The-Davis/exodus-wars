===Overview===

When the Rahn Industries Security Force decided that there should be a fixed defensive perimeter around the R.I. facilities, they turned to engineers to give them what they wanted: platforms. With the designing of the Epsilon-class manned defensive platform, the R.I.S.F. knew that there would have to be a counterpart that would be much cheaper and easier to produce.

The Defender-class automated defensive platform was designed as the complement and a cheap substitution to the Epsilon. The primary duty of the Defender platforms is to be the first line of defense against primarily aerospace forces and if need be, capital ships.

===Capabilities===

With the primary duty of defending facilities and planets from aerospace forces, the Defender-class automated defensive platform isn't really meant to take on capital ships. This is shown in the selection of weapons that the small, but resilient platform uses as the first line of defense.

[[Image:PL100Defender.jpg]]

The eight fast-tracking neutron cannons are best used against the primary target of the Defender: aerospace fighters and bombers. The only other weapon that is onboard is the eight variable ordinance tactical missile tubes that allow the Defender to take on larger targets if need be. Using mostly anti-aerospace cluster rounds, the V.O.T.M.T.s can also use anti-capital ship rounds and often carry at least one tube of them.

With a computer brain controlling the platform, for maximum effectiveness the Defender platforms must be used in multiples. This provides maximum coverage and also allows for a great deal of platforms to be knocked out before the entire system collapses.

===Deployment===

With the building of the six major R.I. construction yards, the perimeter of the Rahn Industries domain kept expanding and therefore the need to defend the facilities also kept expanding.

With this, the R.I.S.F. ordered that one hundred of these Defender platforms be strategically placed on the perimeter and throughout the entire domain. This allows for a layered defense and an early warning system against any kind of an attack against Rahn Industries.

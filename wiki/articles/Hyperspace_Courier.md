'''Hyperspace Couriers''' are the modern descendants of "Snail Mail".  They have been used by most races at some point in their histores, and comprised the only method of long-range communication in all [[Terrandom]] until the [[Urokon]] became commonplace after the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

Hyperspace Couriers utilize small, fast ships with their own [[Hyperspace|FTL]] propulsion and large computer cores as their only cargo.  A customer simply uploads a message they wish sent for delivery to a courier company's network, indicates the destination and desired time-frame for delivery, and pays the appropriate fees.  The courier then routes the message to the appropriate, and the ships make their journeys between systems.  Upon arrival, they upload all their messages to the courier's local network, and the messages are sent.

Hyperspace Couriers quickly fell out of use in Terran territories shortly after their adoption of the [[Lorithos]] Urokon.  They are still occasionally used for sensitive information, special occasions of state, and for communicating with systems and colonies outside of the Urokon system.

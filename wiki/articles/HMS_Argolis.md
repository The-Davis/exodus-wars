The HMS ''Argolis'' was the twenty-seventh ship built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

When it was launched, the HMS ''Argolis'' was left in synchronous orbit with the Haven Class stations, occasionally being brought out to train [[Royal Imperial Navy]] crews. It was not activated as a combat vessel until [[2315]], when it was placed under the command of Commodore [[Victor Pryce]]. Commodore Pryce commanded the ''Argolis'' for four years.

In [[2319]], Commodore Pryce was breveted to the rank of Rear Admiral, and given command of four other Carrier Groups. He was put in charge of [[Sirius Incursion|Operation Lawless]], and ordered to take the [[Sirius Cluster]].

In the disastrous battle, the ''Argolis'' was disabled and boarded by [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova]] troops. Rear Admiral Pryce and his men fought for two hours before overwhelming numbers forced them to surrender.

Despite his surrender, the entire crew of the ''Argolis'' was put to death under the orders of [[Nicholai Asanova]]. Rear Admiral Pryce's head was sent back to [[Earth]] as a warning to the Empire.

The '''Argent Haven''' was one of many orbital semi-automated shipyards designed by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] and [[Haven Shipyards]].

The Argent Haven was the birthplace of the [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]] line of [[:Category:Warships|warships]], and its design team was led by Dr. Michael Ranier.

The Argent Haven was located in the [[Alpha Centauri System]], orbiting [[Ahua-Kin]].

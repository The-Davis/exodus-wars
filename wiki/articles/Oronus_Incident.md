The '''Oronus Incident''' occurred on October 11th, [[2326]] when [[:Category:Warships|warships]] from the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], [[Colonial Commonwealth]], and [[Oronus Worker's Coalition]] confronted ships of the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police]] blockade fleet.  The [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Space Police]] fleet was supported by a number of [[Meroniri]] warships from [[Aseta Salfore]]. Ultimately the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] forces withdrew and the blockade of the [[Oronus System]] was lifted.  

== Background ==

Events leading up to the Oronus Incident began with the Meroniri-initiated, Alliance-enforced trade embargo levied first against the Colonial Commonwealth, and then against all members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].  When Council member states ignored the embargo, it turned into a Space Police blockade. Warships from the Commonwealth twice tried to lift the blockade, but were repulsed both times by the Salfore warships from the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] supporting the Space Police blockade ships.

On December 26, [[2325]], the Taviridis Somarchada signed a treaty ratifying membership in the Council of Independent Nations to be effective at the beginning of [[2326]].

On October 7, 2326 a small force of Commonwealth vessels under the command of Captain [[Ángela Navarro]] entered the Oronus System and headed for the [[Evander Point Stardock]].  They were confronted by ships from the Meroniri-backed Space Police blockade fleet in far orbit. After attempting negotiations, a pair of Commonwealth corvettes attempted to run the blockade, but both were turned back after taking fire from Space Police vessels.

The situation remained tense as the Commonwealth force maintained its location and its demands to be allowed to dock, and the Space Police rallied the rest of its ships in the system in anticipation of a possible conflict. Additionally, a number of Meroniri warships took up positions in support of the Space Police fleet. This situation persisted for several hours.

== October 11 ==

Sometime around midnight, [[Oronus Uniform Time]], Space Police picket ships detected a large [[Hyperspace]] signature in far orbit. Meroniri sensors soon detected a dozen Taviridis warships closing quickly.  Ignoring hails from the picket vessels, the warships closed on the Commonwealth ships and took up positions in the fleet.  

With newfound vigor, Captain Navarro again demanded access to Evander Point and commanded all Alliance vessels to leave the system. After some heated rhetoric from both sides, the Commonwealth ships began their approach to the Stardock and a handful of Oronus warships came out to meet them.

As the Space Police vessels maneuvered to intercept the Commonwealth force, the Taviridis warships powered up their primary weapons systems, began active scanning of the Space Police fleet, and matched their movements.  The Meroniri warships responded in kind.

What happened next is mostly speculation, but it is known that the system's [[Urokon]] channels became very active, as did secured communications channels between the lead Taviridis Somarchada and Meroniri Terinasi ships.  After a few tense moments, the entire Space Police fleet withdrew to a far orbit and jumped out of the system with the Meroniri ships.  Most people credit the peaceful end of the standoff to personal negotiations between Meroniri commander [[Rosh Arufin]] and the [[Ixoarchada]] commander [[Silas Vestreia]].

== Conclusion ==

This was the first major show of force by the Council of Independent Nations as part of its new initiative of sovereignty-patrols.  Moreover, it was the first manifestation of the ideological divide between two super-powers in the [[Orion Arm]]--the Meroniri Terinasi and the Taviridis Somarchada.  

Over the next few days, similar patrols were carried out in Colonial Commonwealth, [[Sol System Authority]], and [[Sirius Families|Sirius Family]] space.  By mid-November, all unauthorized Space Police presence had been removed from Council territory.

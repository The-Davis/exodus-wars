''Seras Yozrim'' (born July 7, 2260) is a brilliant architect and engineer. She designed the Kihtor Hightowers.

== Master Architect ==

The Hightowers are the greatest accomplishment of the people of Lorithan. Built in the span of a few short years, the mighty Hightowers represent the mental brilliance of Seras Yozrim.

Yozrim was born in the later years of the Burning Wars, and raised in the earliest years of her life in one of the last refuges. Near the very end of the war, the refuge was breached by one of the battling warships crashing into it.

In the aftermath of the wars, Seras began to brainstorm about a way to build an unbreachable fortress for the survivors. After traveling to Sepulare and seeing the Meroniri space elevators, Yozrim returned and began to design the Hightowers.

Seras Yozrim's genius at design was limited only by the materials she could get her hands on. Seras invented new methods for refining materials, crafting synthetics, and strengthening systems. The robotic armies of the Lorithos are her creation, as are the ships that the Lorithos use. Seras Yozrim even assisted in the development of the Ansible system.

Today, Seras Yozrim is held in high esteem, and she lives in her workshop suite at the top of Kihtor Modar, the central Hightower.

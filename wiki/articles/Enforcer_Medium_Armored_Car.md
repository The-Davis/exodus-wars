*Classification: Medium Armored Car
*Powerplant: NDI Sb-631i Micro-Reactor
*Speed: 130 KPH
*Armor: Prudenski Composite armor
*Shields: Vambrace Shield Generator
*Armament:
**2 x "Gladius" PPBCs
**8 x "Sunburst/Starburst" Pods
**2 x External Hardpoints
*Communications System: Standard Titanian Communications Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Land-SPAR 

===Overview===

The Enforcer is the mainstay combat vehicle of the Commonwealth Army Command.  Well liked among crews for it simplicity, high mobility, and adequate firepower, the Enforcer is an all around excellent unit. 

[[Image:EnforcerMediumArmoredCar1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Enforcer's primary armament is a pair of 'Gladius' Pulsed Particle Beam Cannons mounted in a twin-turret.  Controlled via a gunner behind the driver, aided by a computer, the twin cannons are very effective against even heavy armor, and their energy output is also quite effective against shielding.  

To aid in combat, the Enforcer has eight 'Sunburst/Starburst' Pods, four mounted to either side of the hull.  Sunburst and Starburst are two names for the same system.  Sunburst is used when referring to defensive countermeasures being fired from the launchers.  Starburst is used when grenades or other offensive weaponry are fired from the launchers.  

[[Image:EnforcerMediumArmoredCar2.jpg]]

In addition to its built-in weaponry, the Enforcer is also equipped with a pair of variable-ordinance hardpoints.  A pair of Raytheon Medium Range Surface-to-Surface Missiles are most often carried, and are quite useful in open-field combat.  However, MRSS and other types of missiles are seldom carried in urban environments or in close-range fighting, as having an active missile strapped to the outside of the hull is less than desireable while within cannon range. 

The Enforcer is outfitted with a New Dallas Industries Sb-631i Micro-Reactor.  This reactor pushes the armored car nearly as fast as the smaller Buccaneer, and powers a Vambrace shield generator as well as the main cannons.  The body of the Enforcer is covered with Prudenski Composite armor, and like all Commonwealth Armored Cars, the tires are actually solid material with a Prudenski mesh over the outside. 

===Deployment===

Enforcers are the primary combatants of the Commonwealth Armor Corps.  Their versatility over all terrains, combined with their heavy protection and armament, makes them lethal in the field against other armored targets.

[[Image:EnforcerMediumArmoredCar3.jpg]]

Capable of 360* coverage, their Starburst systems also make them exceptionally useful against groups of infantry or light Volucris.  They are most often deployed in triads, a lead vehicle flanked on either side, that operate as part of the very fluid line-of-battle strategy of the Commonwealth Army Command.

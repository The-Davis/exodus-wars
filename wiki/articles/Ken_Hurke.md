''Ken Hurke'' held the position of General-Commander leading into the [[Second Exodus War]]. He was responsible for drafting the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s response to the [[Heritage Crisis]], and is often blamed for their lack of preparation for the [[Second Exodus War]].

Hurke was nicknamed "Iron-Jaw" by his peers for his flat refusal to compromise on matters he considered to be important.

In the first onslaught of the [[Second Exodus War]], Hurke's command ship, the [[UEAS Valdosta|UEAS ''Valdosta'']], was destroyed by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] while he was still on [[Earth]]. He spent the rest of the war directing the fighting from the ground, and personally directed the defense of [[Denver]].

Hurke was killed while leading a strategic retreat north towards Ontario, where he had hoped to stage a guerilla war against the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. All [[United Earth Alliance]] military assets on [[Earth]] were surrendered two days after his death.

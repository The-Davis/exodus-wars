The limits and reaches of '''subspace''' are something of a mystery to humanity. Unlike [[hyperspace]], which the [[Meroniri]] have completely charted and mastered, subspace was discovered by the [[Ixoarchada]] in the [[23rd Century]], and by the [[Terrans]] in the [[24th Century]].

It is believed that the [[Gorhamut]] mastered subspace long ago. [[Meroniri]] archives have records dating back to the days of the Anthronoris-Gorhamut war, telling of [[Gorhamut]] ships that could appear and disappear at will, and of worlds invisible to the eyes of their enemies.

Unlike [[hyperspace]], which has a faster speed of light than normal space, subspace is a dimension of lower energy levels. No source of light exists in subspace, but some light does leak into subspace.

Under normal circumstances, matter cannot pass into subspace, and any matter within subspace returns to normal space instantly. Only certain types of radiation can freely pass to and from subspace, and bring matter and energy with it.

The simplest and most violent application of subspace is the [[Mesarthrim]] [[Omega Bomb]], which is actually a primitive form of subspace portal. An [[Omega Bomb]] literally tears a hole into subspace, dragging anything in the immediate vicinity inside. The bomb drags all matter and energy to the bottommost depths of subspace--the subspace abyss--a truly timeless realm where all energy ceases to exist, and all matter freezes in a sudden case of total entropy.  

So far, two regions of subspace have been explored, and a third is known, but unsafe for human travel. [[Terran]] "[[Entropy Drives]]" can create a stable [[Omega Radiation]] burst necessary to enter subspace, and safely shield their occupants from the entropic effects of subspace. This allows a ship to travel the "subspace shallows", effectively making them invisible to normal space. By "pinging" short [[Omega Radiation]] bursts, a ship can create brief windows between subspace and normal space, allowing light to bleed into subspace and giving the subspace travellers a hazy view of what's going on "outside." A wise commander can use this data, along with simple matter-based weapons launched outside the [[Entropy Drive]] field, to wage an incredibly effective surprise attack against superior enemy forces in normal space.

[[Ixoarchada]] subspace drives can create a fluctuating [[Omega Radiation]] burst necessary to enter the "subspace depths", where time begins to travel at a slower pace. This slowing of time makes vessels within it harder to track, for those races that even ''can'' monitor subspace, because there is always a lag between where they actually exist and where they seem to exist as monitored from normal space.

The subspace abyss cannot be safely entered by any human technology. Anything dragged into this layer of subspace by an [[Omega Bomb]] or Ixoarchada [[Daihlar Torpedo|Anchor Torpedo]] instantly loses all energy, and all matter reaches a state of total inactivity. It is unknown if time even exists in this layer, as nothing sent to measure it ever returns.  

Detecting anything in subspace is difficult, but possible. [[Omega Radiation]] interacts with normal space, with all layers of subspace, and with all levels of [[hyperspace]]. This causes ships in subspace to have a gravity "shadow" in normal space, which can be detected with the right equipment, but difficult to pinpoint. Destroying a ship in subspace is only possible with an [[Omega Bomb]], or similar [[Omega Radiation]] based weaponry.

The '''Mirach System''' was a small, sparsely inhabitted star system discovered in late [[2323]] by a [[Prefecture]] scout ship searching for hidden [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] fleet assets.

In [[2324]] a colony of 6000 scientists, surveyors and civil engineers was dispatched to turn the only inhanittable planet into a viable colony world.

The [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] retreated to the Mirach System after its fateful engagement with both the Imperial remnant and the [[Volucris Swarm]], sparking the first land battles of the [[Volucris Incursion]].  The Prefecture evacuated all assets to [[Tavou]].  Shortly afterwards, the entire Mirach star system was destroyed by the [[Meroniri Terinasi]].

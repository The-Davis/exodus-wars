The Hangar is a slightly more complicated affair than a tank Garage.  It frequently holds sensitive ammunition--like Orca Artillery Missiles or Apocalypse Nuclear Missiles.  While the greatest of care is taken in arming the fighters and bombers, explosions can happen--rarely.

Still, precautions were taken, and heavy Boron-Nitride Composite armor went into the construction, both inside and out.  The armor is especially thick where hangars meet mechanic crew quarters.

[[Image:StandardizedHangar.jpg]]

Like the tank Garage, the master mechanic in charge of a block of hangars has a tool shack and quarters combinations.  Accommodations for an Aerospace mechanic are slightly better than a vehicle mechanic's, but they're still no five-star.

Simple bases usually have at least six hangars, but the headquarters for an Air Command can have hundreds or even thousands.

The Government of the [[United Earth Alliance]] had an executive branch, a legislative branch, and a judicial branch. 

=== Executive Branch ===

The executive branch was embodied by the office of the [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] and the office of the [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]].

The [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] was a single elected office governing the entire Alliance as the [[:Category:Head of State|head of state]].

The [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] was a conglomeration of elected offices, one per world in the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]], and together representing the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] as a whole with equal power to the [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]].

=== Legislative Branch ===

The legislative branch was embodied by the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]], a tiered democratic council. Anyone could run for the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] so long as they could speak, age or criminal record was not an issue.

Typically, the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assemblies]] of each world only discussed matters affecting their own world. If something affected the [[United Earth Alliance]] as a whole, the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] of [[Earth]] had seniority, but the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assemblies]] of other worlds could send in their votes. Because of the length of time in [[hyperspace]] travel early on, most votes were cast by secure computer over the course of several months.

The head of the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] on each world was the [[Prime Minister of the United Earth Alliance|Prime Minister]]. If there needed to be a face-to-face meeting of multiple [[United Earth Alliance]] officials on [[Earth]], the [[Prime Minister of the United Earth Alliance|Prime Minister]] of each world was sent.

=== Judicial Branch ===

The judicial branch was embodied by the [[United Earth Alliance Judicial Services Bureau|Judicial Services Bureau]], a council selected by lottery. Rather than have any appointed position, citizens could volunteer to serve in any judicial process, and were randomly-selected for any trial on the basis of experience and merit.

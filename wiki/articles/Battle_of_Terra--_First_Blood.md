April 28, 2322

by James Davis


=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One day after Emperor Lucius Black's press release, forces under the command of Emil Torsc--loyal to the Emperor--launched a strike against the rebel aerospace forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighters of the Jerome Black were taken by surprise. The ruthless Imperial fighter pilots were armed with Invader fighters, the best the Empire had before the Gen2 Project. The Ravens of the rebels fought back, but neither side could break a stalemate. Each side lost three fighters before ending the battle and withdrawing.

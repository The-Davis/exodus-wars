The Third Battle of [[Cronus]] was a significant engagement, where an allied fleet of [[Titan System|Titanian]] soldiers and [[Mesarthrim]] soldiers attempted to break the siege of the [[Titan System]].  The Third Battle of Cronus marked the beginning of the end of the [[Onyx Empire]]. This was also the first successful international allied operation of aggression against the [[Onyx Empire]] since the war began.

== Security Leaks ==

Starting in August of [[2321]], the [[United Earth Alliance]] began a massive logistics and strategic initiative, known as "[[Operation Durandal]]." Its purpose was to systematically drive the [[Onyx Empire]] out of the [[Titan System]]. The operation was plagued with intelligence leaks and sabotage from day one. The Empire's terrorism and sabotage efforts were too well entrenched. Operation Durandal's future looked uncertain, until [[First Contact]] with the [[Mesarthrim]] gave the Titan System's defenders some new tactical options.

On January 24th, [[2322]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] government was hastily accepted into the [[Alliance of Planets]], only nine days after making first contact with races outside their star systems. [[Andreas Tischler]] met with General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] on January 25th, and told Sanburn that that he intended to go on the offensive.

Unfortunately, Sanburn's forces were unable to immediately deploy. The war had not been going well for the Alliance. Sanburn's meager defense fleets were perpetually damaged and dry-docked, and the few combat ready vessels were stretched across the entire system.  Andreas left [[Theia]], promising to bring enough warships to hold Theia and take back the rest of the system.

== Exploiting the Omegas ==

While he waited for Tischler's return, Scott Sanburn and the chief of the United Earth Alliance's internal security bureau, [[Joshua Beson]], began a cautious but thorough search for the [[Omegas]] that were leaking their vital strategic information to the Empire. By February 2, Beson managed to identify one of Sanburn's staff, [[Enrique Gardner]], as an Omega Agent.

Rather than eliminate or imprison Gardner, Sanburn suggested allowing him to continue to operate. The Mesarthrim could act as a wild card, throwing off the value of the intelligence leaked to the Empire, possibly causing them to make a fatal blunder.

When Andreas Tischler returned to the Titan System on February 4, he brought the full strength of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] and the [[Crimson Tide Battle Group|Karminrote Gezeiten-Kampfgruppe]]. It was time to put the new plan into motion.

In a full staff meeting, Scott Sanburn and Andreas Tischler decided to launch an assault on [[Mnemosyne]] on February 9th. Sanburn knew full well that Enrique Gardner would soon leak the information to the Empire. After the staff meeting, Tischler invited Scott and Lynn Sanburn to dine with him aboard his flagship. On the way, Scott Sanburn told Tischler of the intelligence leak. Tischler asked what they would do with their strategy compromised. Sanburn simply told Tischler "surprise me."

== The Surprise Attack ==

With Sanburn's blessing to put a new, secret plan in motion, Andreas Tischler conferred with his own commanders, keeping the new battle plan privy only to the Mesarthrim.  The plan began immediately, dispatching a secret courier to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]], they were ordered not to approach any systems until further notice.

Additionally, Tischler began referring to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] as the entirety of the Mesarthrim Kreigsmarine.  This verbage was repeated across Titan News Networks and in all official state documentation.

As news of the Mesarthrims plans to strike [[Mnemosyne]] began to filter in, and the Mesarthrim's Navy, Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] of the Royal Imperial Navy decided that if he could crush the entire Mesarthrim Navy in one stroke, it would make conquest of Centus easy.  So, on February 8th, Marshall moved the entire Black Armada to Mnemosyne, and arranged them across the system to create an effective trap.

On February 9th, As according to plan, the assault on Mnemosyne began.  Both sides took heavy losses, and by the end of the day the Mesarthrim retreated.  Suspecting that the retreat was a feint, Marshall ordered his five least damaged Carrier Groups to defend Mnemosyne, and ordered his remaining Carrier groups to retreat to [[Cronus]] for repairs, rearmament, and refit.

Early on February 10th, Marshall arrived at Cronus.  There wasn't a single undamaged Carrier Group, and the orbital patterns became a mass scramble as ships jockeyed for the repair facilities.  Unbenknownst to Marshall, Tischler, and the entire Erste Flotte were carefully monitoring the system from behind numerous stellar bodies and debris.  The Erste Flotte had spent the last five days silently moving into position.

Mid morning, Marshall ordered six Carrier Groups to rebase to Bethija, where their repair yards could help relieve some of the workload.  After the carrier groups left, Tischler struck.

The combined allied forces of the Erste Flotte, the Crimson Tide Battle Group, the 17th Space Battle Group, and the 4th Air Superiority Group descended on Cronus, missiles locked on and ranges set.  The initial salvo cut the repair yards to shreds, instantly killing over 25,000 repair personal, naval personal, and Imperial armed forces.  It was a grim opening for what would turn into a blood bath.

As the repair yards crumbled and began to plummet to the surface of Cronus, Imperial ships began to make emergency launches, and break orbit.  The Mesarthrim ships were waiting, and a massive slugging match was unleashed as both sides tore at each other.  The opening assault by the allied forces, however, began to waver as more and more Imperial ships came online - there were simply too many ships for the Allies to handle.

Tischler made a quick decision to split his fleet up into smaller elements, and try and drive the Imperial ships apart, where the tougher Mesarthrim vessels could engage them in much more manageable numbers.  As February 10th came to a close, the Allied fleet succeeded in pulling back for the slug match, and blockading Cronus.  Throughout the night, Marshall dispatched couriers to try and contact reinforcements, however each courier he dispatched were gunned down by the 4th Air Superiority Group.

Early in the morning on February 11th, Sanburn summoned Tischler aboard his ship - Gary Hughes was embeded with the fleet.  During the night he had called in the last two operational Titanian Battle Groups, and wanted the Imperial fleet decimated at all costs.  Reluctantly Tischler agreed to a frontal assault against the Imperial Orbital Batteries and Cruiser Lines.  Sanburn was frustrated that after six years of autonomy, he was responsible to the bureaucrats yet again.  Tischler met with his commanders and relinquished his command to Arnold Stadt.  It was on the morning of February 11th that Tischler made his famous ad-libbed speech, 'The Last March of the Mesarthrim.'

The attack began at 0445 standard naval time, the entire Allied fleet moved to engage the defenders.  The initial engagement was brutal.  The entire 5th Space Battle Group succumbed to Admiral Marshalls counter attack.  Their ships broke apart under the withering mac cannon blasts.  On the southern pole of Cronus, Arnold Stadt engaged a pair of Carrier Groups supported by a set of defensive batteries.  The battle was mercifully quick, as once Stadt had crippled the Imperial Carriers, their escorts pulled back to regroup.

Mesarthrim Myt'herra units boarded the defensive batteries, after a brief fire fight, and an even briefer interrogation secession, the batteries were back online, clearing the way for High Capacity Dropships.  By midday the orbit of Cronus was littered with debris and hulks of twisted ships.  Sporadic fighting continued on, however a de facto cease fire seemed to have been declared, as the Imperial forces licked their wounds - regrouped on the northern pole.  The Allied fleet huddled around the southern pole, continuing landing operations through their areas of clear space and clear land.

The [[Operation Imperial Exorcism|ground campaign on Cronus]] entered a brutal stage as the frozen wastelands became large battlefields, where almost any wound was fatal as it exposed the solider to an atmosphere-less rock.  The Mesarthrim Heer were hungry for battle, and quickly overran almost the entire lower hemisphere.  After the first day of fighting, Marshall informed his ground commanders of his intention to withdraw.

Around 2200 hours, Marshall sent a sortie against the Allied positions on the southern pole.  The recon was suddenly hit by a massive Allied counter attack.  The attack continued northward towards Marshalls position.  The battle that ensued saw a heavy engagement between severely damaged vessels.  The battle, however, was a ploy - Marshall was pulling out, and he needed to provide his ships with cover so that they would not be shot down launching from the planets surface.

The plan worked perfectly, and the retreating Imperial Army was able to perform a well executed fighting retreat.  The retreat did, however, cost them dearly in material as most of their heavy equipment had to be abandoned.

On February 12th, Marshall called off any further retreat and ordered all remaining forces on the ground to detonate their sections power-cores, effectively denying the base to the Allies.  Of the eighteen bases still under Imperial control, only seven carried out the suicidal order.

As the morning wore on, Marshall began to get very meticulous about ensuring all Imperial assets in orbit were either towed in, or destroyed.  This caused extensive fighting to continue between the two opposing forces.  However, both sides were afraid to commit to an extensive fight as their fleets were already damaged beyond what would normally have been considered combat worthy.

Around 1600 hours, Omega agents infiltrated sensitive areas of the evacuated command compound, and set off the hidden self-destruct charges, killing thirty Alliance and Mesarthrim personnel. Myt'gaul managed to capture two of the Omega agents, and tortured them for seven hours before killing them. News of the torture and murder reached the ears of the galaxy at large, sparking protests from [[Ikronin]] and [[Lorithos]] activists.

== Aftermath ==

Immediately after Admiral Robert Marshall's escape from the Titan System, Andreas Tischler mustered the survivors of the Zweite Flotte and began recon flights in Imperial territory.  Shortly after the battle, General-Commandant Scott Sanburn re-tooled the United Earth Alliance's resources to rebuilding system infrastructure, now that their military forces did not require constant repairs from unending attacks.

The liberation of the Titan System was an important catalyst in moving [[Anthony Hadrian]] into revealing the Emperor's secret; the truth behind the destruction of [[Torimur]]. More importantly, it marked the beginning of the end of the [[Onyx Empire]]; proving that they could not only be defeated, but routed in open warfare.

The excessive losses taken by the United Earth Alliance caused serious concerns among their people, and kept them from participating in large scale commitments for much of the war.

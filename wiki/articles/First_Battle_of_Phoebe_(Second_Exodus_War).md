== Overview ==

After the humiliating losses at the [[Third Battle of Tethys (Second Exodus War)|Third Battle of Tethys]], Admiral [[Gordon Dannins|Dannins]] and the UEAS ''Haven'' regrouped at [[Phoebe]] with eight Alliance cruisers.

When [[Robert Marshall]] received reports from his scouts of Dannins' precarious position, he began planning a major assault to keep up his forward momentum into the star system.  From April 2, 2316 until April 8, 2316, Imperial scouts conducted dozens of fly-by missions of Firebase Phoebe.  Several scout aircraft were shot down, but were not considered major engagements.

On April 9, 2316, the attack on Firebase Phoebe and Admiral Dannins' ships was launched.

[[Labrea]] - May 24, [[2327]]

=====Mesarthrim TOC, North of the Big Claw, 0330 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Morning, Colonel.  Sleep well?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Colonel [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] nods to Major Rabenstrage. "Well enough."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;His men have died by the thousands in the past twenty-four hours, and no commander ever becomes fully immune to that weight; but [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]], veteran of three bloody wars - the great uprising against the Anderung, the [[Second Exodus War]], and the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] - is accustomed to the burden.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It helps, of course, that his enemies have been dying as well.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We're over the hump, Major," [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] says, leaning over his tactical map. Since yesterday morning, and the difficult assault on the third perimeter, the crimson icons indicating the positions of the [[Laojiu]] [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] have been clumped high on the Big Claw Mountain. "The Guard have been pushed back to their final defensive perimeters."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"At some cost. If we'd had more artillery," Rabenstrage sighs. "If."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''It is a wonder'', [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] thinks. ''I remember the days, during the uprising, when a single continent seemed unimaginably vast - a place that would take years to conquer. And now?  We fight for worlds. Every drop of [[Mesarthrim]] blood spilled here today is well-spent.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rabenstrage watches him carefully. "You think this is a worthy battle, don't you, Fritz? If good Mesarthrim are to die, where better than here?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I do think so." [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] brushes a finger across the tactical map, calling up a sweeping arc of icons. "We just need one more piece of the puzzle, Major, and the Fathach Méire will be ours."

=====Tara'dar Coirp, 0415 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A single Piranha fighter slams down onto the end of the runway, trailing smoke. Halfway down the runway's length, its forward landing gear collapse, and skids in the rest of the way in a shower of sparks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emergency equipment rolls forward to meet the crippled fighter as it slides to a halt. The cockpit cracks open, and the pilot pushes her helmet off wearily. "Reloads!" she shouts, the fatigue in her voice belying her gung-ho words. "I need more Hounds on the rails, the [[white-eyes]] are swarming thick!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The squadron leader's crew chief raises a hand and calls to her. "Lieutenant [[Shuong Mei|Mei]]?  Where's the Major?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lieutenant [[Shuong Mei|Mei]]'s gaze falters. "Last I saw, he and his wingmen were tangling with a Patton. I..." Her face collapses. "He's gone. He just blew up." She gestures ineffectually. "Just...blew up."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Her own crew chief tugs on her arm. "Come on, Lieutenant. Let's get you to the infirmary."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Hell with that! Get me back airborne!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You don't have any forward gear, Lieutenant."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Just rig something!" [[Shuong Mei|Mei]] snarls, reaching for her helmet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The crew chiefs look at each other. Every time the squadron returns from an hour-long sortie, another fighter is missing, or two: and now [[Shuong Mei|Mei]], whether she knows it or not, is the only one left.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm calling it," the head chief sighs. Like [[Shuong Mei|Mei]], he is a [[Labrea]] native, and his loyalty to his people calls stronger than his loyalty to the chain of command. "We're low on ordinance, low on fuel, and out of spares. Everyone who lands, stays down, and I don't care what the hell [[William Parrish|Parrish]] tells me. They've been flying for nearly forty-eight hours. Any longer and they'll be falling asleep in the air."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He shakes his head. "If the Brigadier asks, I'll tell him we cannot provide air cover any longer.  That's all there is to it."

=====Improvised Airfields, North of the Big Claw, 0420 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fleet-based fighters, flying in from orbit, have handled most of the [[Mesarthrim]] air sorties in the past day. Colonel [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]]'s battle plan calls for small numbers of superior [[Mesarthrim]] squadrons to chip away at the [[Prefecture]] air cover while exhausting the enemies' stores of shoulder-launched SAMs. He can't risk his valuable Richthofen and Schleiffen bombers against the swarming enemy fighters and SAM threats...but when the moment comes, he'll commit them. In the meantime, though, [[Mesarthrim]] engineers have constructed improvised airfields, and over the past few days four squadrons of [[Mesarthrim]] fighters - two of Pattons, one of Dragonflies, and one of Richthofen bombers - have been brought down to the ground and held in reserve.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two of the pilots waiting on the ground have already flown in the dogfight swirling above the Big Claw; their damaged planes were redirected to the airfields for emergency repairs. The standby pilots gather around them, eager to hear news of the [[Prefecture]] enemy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have great respect for their Piranhas," one of the pilots explains, gesturing to show the nimble fighters' turn radius.  "Very quick, hard to lock up and harder to stay behind. Their Stukas, on the other hand - they are supposed to be tough, but blazers take them apart easily enough. They might fare well against our gunships, but even a lone Dragonfly should have no trouble with them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Like yours?" a heckler in the audience calls.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The pilot laughs. "It was a SAM that tagged me - they are easy enough to avoid, but they flew thick today! They must have fired thousands up at us! The poor bomber pilots, with their big lumbering beasts, must have taken fifteen or twenty hits apiece."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Suddenly, the PA system rumbles. "''Pilots, to your fighters.''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The eager [[Mesarthrim]] scatter to their planes.

=====Prefecture Fourth Perimeter, Near the Big Claw Peak, 0450 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Well, we've survived the one-day anniversary."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lieutenant [[Feng Zemin]] nods glumly. It's hard for him to maintain the appropriate inspiring demeanor when one of his legs is mostly missing. For the past twenty-four hours his thoughts have kept drifting back to a briefing he once got on prosthetic limbs. He wishes that he'd paid more attention.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another salvo of [[Mesarthrim]] artillery crashes into the mountainside, shaking the bunker slightly. A direct hit will blow them all to hell, but so far they've been lucky.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Sergeant checking his wound dressing won't stop talking; it's a nervous habit of his. "They came on so hard, so fast...and then, noon yesterday, they just stopped. I don't understand it, el-tee; aren't they all about 'lightning war'?  Why give us a break?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''It's because they've already won'', [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] thinks. "I think we bled 'em dry, Sergeant," he says, forcing cheer into his voice. "They must've lost so many men coming up the mountain that they had to call a halt to regroup and rearm."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yeah?" A sallow-faced Private in the corner of the bunker looks up, lips pursed. "Weird thing, Lieutenant. I heard we've lost contact with every one of our positions outside this perimeter. I heard that, overall, we took ''more'' losses than the white-eyes yesterday."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] shrugs. "Could be, Private.  As it is, though, I think we did a damn fine job of hurting them - and we're going to keep that up.  You can bet that Brigadier [[William Parrish|Parrish]] is cooking up some nasty surprises for them, and we'll just do our part."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The man scrubs listlessly at his rifle's magazine well. "Not like we have a choice, is there?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You damn well have a choice, Private!" the Sergeant snarls. "You can die in here sulking when I smash your whining face in, or you can fight like blazes and do [[Labrea]] proud! Now run up to the commo station and check on our resupply request!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Squad transmitters still aren't working," [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] grunts as the Private climbs out of the bunker. "The [[Mesarthrim]] apparently have nothing better to do than jam every piece of electronics on the mountain."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Private falls back into the bunker, panting. "Lieutenant! Orders just came down from the Brigadier! He wants us out of our hardpoints and scattered as far as possible - we've lost our air support and the white-eyes are sending up a whole flock of bombers!"

=====Above the Big Claw, 0455 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Xantippe Two-One, this is Two. How do you feel about heavy metal?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"''Funny thing, Seven, I'm a big fan.''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"''Shall we introduce the locals?''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"''Can't deny 'em the pleasure.''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The last time that 401 Squadron, Assault, Bomber, made a run on the Big Claw, they rain into a hail of SAMs, coming from so many different directions that even the huge Richthofens' automated turrets got confused. None of the colossal warcraft were lost, but a few more seconds on a long, slow attack path might have led to a very different story.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This time is different. The [[Prefecture]] forces are packed high on the mountain, and their fighter cover is gone, withdrawn to Tara'dar Coirp. The skies above the Fathach Méire are [[Mesarthrim]] now. No SAMs rise to meet them; no fighters swoop in to attack. The behemoths sweep past their Initial Points, bomb bay doors unfolding.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"''Rifle! Birds away!''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Each Richthofen unloads its external racks first: ten missiles, two with fuel-air warheads, four armor-piercing anti-installation missiles, and four ADMs to target infantry. One hundred and twenty long-range missiles howl in on the Big Claw. Sherman fighters orbiting the mountain spike their jammers and fire clouds of decoy missiles to saturate the [[Prefecture]] point defenses.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Eight missiles are intercepted by [[Prefecture]] point defenses. The other hundred and twelve impact on target. Nearly five thousand [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] soldiers die in a single instant.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Then the Richthofens begin unloading the ''real'' ordinance.

=====UCS Forward Camp, 0530 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Captain [[Hans von Steuben]] and Captain Graffin of the Myt'herra share a meal. Their companies laugh and joke beneath the looming forms of the parked ELITES battle armor. The Myt'herra are in good spirits despite their losses, and toast the spirits of their fallen comrades. Of all the deaths a good [[Mesarthrim]] could wish for, this is one of the finest.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"It's a wonder that anyone could have survived that," Graffin murmurs, looking upslope. The top of the Big Claw is a burning wasteland, as if God had decided to show his wayward creations exactly what Hell looked like. Even as the two [[Mesarthrim]] watch, an ADM cruise missile unloads a salvo of bomblets, sending another shower of dust and flame skywards. Somewhere, a sniper rifle cracks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Eh," [[Hans von Steuben|von Steuben]] peels an energy bar and tears into it with gusto. "If there's one thing war has taught us, my friend, it is the human being's staggering capability to survive big explosions."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Light flares above the mountain, and suddenly a sonic boom rolls over them. Another series of fireballs bursts across the peak as an orbital bombardment slams into the Big Claw. Graffin laughs and slaps his friend on the shoulder. "My God, [[Hans von Steuben|Hans]], Colonel [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] really hates them! He's pounding the crap out of those poor bastards."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Maybe it's Major Weber whispering in his ear," [[Hans von Steuben|von Steuben]] suggests. "His battalion got torn to pieces by mortars and bombs back at the third perimeter. He's out for blood."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"As am I," Graffin says, taking a swig from his canteen.  "I just prefer to be a little more, erm, light-hearted about it."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Right." [[Hans von Steuben|von Steuben]] grabs up his weapon. "Shall we start getting into position?"

=====Prefecture Tactical Operations Center, 0630 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;No one in the TOC gets to appreciate the sunrise. Officers look towards the roof of the cave anxiously, waiting for a [[Mesarthrim]] penetrator to come crashing through and kill them all.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Brigadier [[William Parrish|Parrish]] reclines, hands behind his head, and thinks. Now that they've lost most of their SAM firing positions, the [[Mesarthrim]] bombardment has wrecked his defenses and inflicted heavy casualties, but like all bombardments against entrenched troops, it can only do so much. ''Sooner or later the [[white-eyes]] will have to come up after us.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Another report from Division Artillery," a Lieutenant shouts over the roar of bombs hitting the mountain above. "They want to know if their fire on the Little Claw is having any effect?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A quick check with a sentry indicates that it definitely isn't.

=====The Little Claw, 0630 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Colonel! I found some breakfast!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"BREAKFAST!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Good." [[Marieke Aloisia|Aloisia]] gestures her driver to sit. "Thanks, Dieter.  It's louder than a [[Blacktalon]] concert up here."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What? I can't hear!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Marieke Aloisia|Aloisia]] shakes her head. The constant pounding of the mortars has been joined by the snarl of Triton air-defense systems, swatting incoming Prefecture howitzer rounds out of the sky. Occasionally, a stray round slips through to pound the mountainside, but so far none have hit the mortar tracks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, they're using the artillery in Tara'dar Coirp?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"They are. Not that it's doing them much good." Grabbing a stimulant tab, [[Marieke Aloisia|Aloisia]] leans out of her foxhole and stares at the holocaust enveloping the Big Claw's peak. "They must be getting desperate."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She shakes her head in awe, thinking of the next stage of [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]]'s plan - a bold gambit, certainly, but one that his battle-hardened troops are well prepared to carry out. If it works, [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]]'s name will live on in [[Mesarthrim]] song for many, many years.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The mortars thunder out another salvo.

=====Big Claw Peak, 0700 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Click.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Click-click.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The soft chirps on encrypted radio channels are the only noise the advancing Myt'herra make. Captain Graffin receives overhead surveillance provided by planes, drones, and orbiting warships, and so he's able to coordinate his advance with the continuing mortar, aerial, and orbital bombardments. The Myt'herra slip between bursting shells and duck beneath cover as ADMs slash overhead.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first [[Prefecture]] sentries appear ahead. Blazers are not quiet weapons. The Myt'herra use their knives; one man in a battleteam distracts the sentry with a sharp, soft call, giving the other the moment's opening he needs to kill the man from behind. The bombardment has left long lines of craters on the slope - plentiful cover for the camouflaged warriors as they head uphill.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It takes them forty minutes to get into position. Myt'herra fireteams crawl into position, dragging kilograms of electronics. Whispered go-codes sound across the radio links. The Myt'herra link their calling equipment into the [[United Centusi States]]' battlenet and begin designating targets.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Captain Graffin and a hand-picked unit continue forward on a special mission. The pointman holds up a fist, calling a halt. "''Hardpoint, zero-two-zero, one kilometer.''"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Acknowledged." Graffin has a [[United Centusi States|UCS]] command set: an enviable little piece of encrypted communications technology. He pings an orbiting warship and retrieves a live camera feed of the area. "We'll swing east and take them in the flank."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;They move out. Mortars fall to the south, farther upslope, throwing up massive clouds of smoke that help conceal their movement. Within minutes, they are in position; every Myt'herra has the endurance of a cross-country runner. The hardpoint is guarded by sentries and a set of Pit Viper automated defense systems.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Captain checks his map. This is the entrance they're looking for.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Flashers up!" A Private throws a blinder grenade, and the Viper turrets begin firing wildly into the air, their sensors overloaded by the thermal burst. The [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] soldiers rush out to check on their malfunctioning equipment, and Graffin's men rise up from the mud and cut their throats; Graffin tosses a burly black Sergeant with a hand-to-hand move and snaps his neck.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The pointman tosses a frag grenade into the hardpoint, clearing out the remaining defenders, and the Myt'herra slip inside.

=====Prefecture Fourth Perimeter, Near the Big Claw Peak, 0745 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I think it's letting up a little!" the Sergeant calls.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lieutenant [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] groans. "Aw, shit. That means they're coming up again."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The platoon's bunker is a crater now; they get out just in time to avoid a penetrator warhead, then scramble back into the wreckage on the theory that the [[Mesarthrim]] wouldn't hit the same spot twice. About half the platoon has been torn to pieces in the ensuing bombardment. [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] can't quite get their screams to stop.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He knows worse is to come. The advantage of the [[Prefecture]] defenses was dispersal, but overnight squads of Myt'herra swept through the woods, destroying outlying emplacements.  Word from the TOC is that the Big Claw is the only remaining [[Prefecture]] strongpoint in the whole Fathach Mere.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] gestures to a nearby Specialist. "Prop me up, I need to take a look around. You two, get ready to move the field gun - "<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Private stands to help [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] up and a blazer bolt takes his head off. His body falls across [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]].<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Shit!" The men manning the field gun open fire, scything a stream of rounds downrange, until the Sergeant calms them down and shuts the gun off. "You'll just give away our position!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Lieutenant stares at the dead Private, then pushes him away. "I think it's too late," he says dully.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"El-tee?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sergeant, tell the men to lay down their weapons," [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] orders. "We're finished."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Sergeant stares at him for a moment. "I'd listen to him," a [[German]]-accented voice says.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Someone grabs for a weapon, and then falls, screaming, as a blazer bolt takes his arm off. Looking around, [[Feng Zemin|Zemin]] sees rocks, pieces of debris, even piles of mud unfolding into the shapes of camouflaged [[Mesarthrim]] warriors. A rifle jabs into his back.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Sergeant throws his rifle to the ground. "All right, men, give it up. Nothing we can do now."

=====Prefecture Tactical Operations Center, 0800 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The tactical map flashes crimson and entire units begin to disappear.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Brigadier [[William Parrish|Parrish]] leans forward, shocked. In an instant, entire platoons vanish, scourged out of existence by impossibly accurate mortar fire or airstrikes.  "How are they doing that?" a Major demands as an entire hardened strongpoint swallows a penetrator and vanishes. "There's no way they can spot our units that precisely!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"''They're inside! They're coming'' - "<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The transmission cuts off in a hash of static. Everyone whirls towards the transmitter; the Lieutenant monitoring it, looking ashen, shouts. "Repeat! We did not copy your last!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I copied," Brigadier [[William Parrish|Parrish]] growls. ''They've slipped through the perimeter''! "Myt'herra are inside the mountain. We need to leave, now. Inform all battalion commanders that we're going to retreat by Case Zulu. Anyone who can't reach their extraction point should surrender."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''My God. This entire mountain was a trap. They pushed us up here, concentrated my forces, slipped Myt'herra in, and blew us to pieces with support fire from their fighters and mortars. We never stood a chance.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[William Parrish|Parrish]] lets out a long, slow sigh.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An explosion sends an overpressure wave washing through the TOC. The sound of blazer fire comes closer and closer.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A squad of [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] soldiers rushes in. "Your Skyhawk's waiting, sir. We've got to move quickly."

=====The Big Claw, 0900 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After the fierce, large-scale fighting of the previous day, the fall of the fourth [[Prefecture]] perimeter is an anticlimax. The [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] units left after the bombardment are targeted by precise mortar fire and airstrikes by the Myt'herra who have infiltrated their positions. The dazed survivors, cut off from either command or retreat, are overwhelmed by a Myt'kampfen charge.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The heaviest fighting comes in the fifth perimeter, within the mountain itself. The close-quarters, tunnel-to-tunnel struggle is fierce and brutal. Captain Graffin's Myt'herra are well-trained in close-quarters-battle, but the Myt'kampfen following them take heavy losses. More than once Graffin finds himself wishing he had a single squad of Myt'gaul to take point.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Big Claw is not the only source of action. All across the Fathach Méire skirmishes and full-out battles rage as the [[United Centusi States]] presses home the attack against [[Prefecture]] forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighting inside the mountain rages on all day as the [[Mesarthrim]] struggle to clear out pockets of [[Prefecture]] resistance. The Myt'herra stop to take prisoners, but the Myt'kampfen, driven to rage by the losses they took the previous day, simply butcher anyone they come across. By midnight the mountain is totally in [[United Centusi States|Centusi]] hands, but the battle for the Fathach Méire rages on. Gone are the battalion-level maneuvers, the precise coordination of air support and infantry advance; now it is a simple slog forward for the [[Mesarthrim]], killing everyone they can find.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By the next day the fighting for the Fathach Méire is over. Final casualty figures reflect the bloody nature of the fighting. The Mesarthrim have lost 27,000 men - 17,000 wounded, ten thousand killed in action, almost all Myt'kampfen who were killed on the first day of fighting or in the tunnel battles later on. Of the 150,000 men the [[Prefecture]] deployed, 50,000 manage to escape the Fathach Méire on foot, by vehicle, or by Skyhawk. 45,000 were killed and 55,000 captured - two thirds of the original [[Prefecture Legion Corps|Guard]] strength in the Fathach Méire.

=====Peak of the Big Claw, 1900 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Colonel [[Maireke Aloisia]] and Colonel [[Fritz Dresdner]] stand side-by-side at the top of the Big Claw, watching the light show in the valley below as Myt'herra skirmish with the retreating [[Prefecture]] troops.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For the first time since the start of the battle, the normally reserved [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] allows his exultation to show. "We did it, [[Maireke Aloisia|Maireke]]. The better part of an entrenched division, routed! [[Labrea]] is ours!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Marieke Aloisia|Aloisia]] rakes a hand through her long hair. "It got my blood going, I'll tell you - there were moments, down there on the Little Claw, where I was sure we'd had it. You fought a fine battle. But we lost a lot of good men, [[Fritz Dresdner|Fritz]]. A lot of good men."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The older man's eyes narrow as he remembers Captain [[Lothar Metzger]] of the Myt'gaul, the lone man who walked into Hardad to deliver the ultimatum of surrender - and paid for his choice with his life. [[Lothar Metzger|Metzger]] was an old, friendly rival, and in a way the two men had even been friends. Now [[Fritz Dresdner|Dresdner]] wishes he'd had a chance to know the man better.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We did," he whispers, watching tracers rip through the woods downslope. "We did."

=====Tara'dar Coirp, Six Days After the Battle of the Big Claw=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Brigadier [[William Parrish|Parrish]] sits in his office and ponders his doom.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Two days''. He had a whole mountain range to position his troops in, heavy fortifications, plenty of air support. He'd anticipated lasting two ''weeks''. Maybe two ''months''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Two days''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And now Tara'dar Coirp is all he has left: cut off by a huge [[Mesarthrim]] force to the south, unable to escape the shadow of the [[Mesarthrim]] presence in the Fathach Méire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Doomed, perhaps.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;But he won't go down without a fight.

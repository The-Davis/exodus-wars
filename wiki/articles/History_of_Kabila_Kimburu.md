The history of [[Kabila Kimburu]] is largely traditional. Much of their hereditary gestahlt was lost and dilluted due to their losses to war against several united Kabilas in the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. Today they number in the millions, a pale remnant of their old numbers.

== Sagittarius Upheaval ==

Some time in the [[23rd Century]], the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]] was divided over a minor territorial issue. The dispute was petty, but there were tribal rivalries that were centuries old. The Kimburuns soon found themselves in an open shooting war against [[Kabila Nyoka]] and [[Kabila Jitu'nge]]. 

Kimburun soldiers fought valiantly, taking hundreds and thousands of Nyokans and Jitu'ngens down with them. All fighting was in vain. Their forces were slaughter by the millions, and then the billions. The rage of the [[Njaa]] escalated exponentially, until Kuu Rijali fled with the last [[Nina'rikebu Class Battleship|Nina'rikebu]] towards the [[Teeth of Orion]], determined to escape the madness of his species or die in the attempt.

== A Cold Welcome ==

The Kimburuns spent many years navigating the treacherous [[hyperspace]] pathways of the Teeth. Finally, in the year [[2271]], they found their way to [[Shia Drasin]]. The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] were less than friendly. They quickly disarmed the ship and all of the Kimburuns, disabled their hyperspace drives, and destroyed their navigation computers to prevent any possibility of other [[Ngrligru]] making the journey through the Teeth of Orion. Rather than accept the refugees on one of their own worlds, they sent them to the [[United Earth Alliance]].

The Alliance was happy to receive the strange aliens. Their political repuation was still somewhat tainted at home by their imperialist policies of the [[21st Century]], and sheltering thousands of refugees kept with the image they were carefully crafting for themselves. The Kimburuns settled in [[Rhea]]'s oceans.

== Rebirth ==

The first spawning season on Rhea was unusually fertile. Millions of young Ngrligru were born; far too many to survive the normal maturation process. The elders made a difficult decision, and sacrificed their entire adult population so the young could absorb their Fahamu Nodes.

The memories and experiences of the adults were dilluted in the process. Much was forgotten, but millions of healthy young Ngrligru replenished those who were lost. Sadly, none of the tribe's [[Mtomigru]] survived the process.

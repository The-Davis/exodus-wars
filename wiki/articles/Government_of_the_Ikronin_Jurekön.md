The [[Ikronin]] government is headed by the [[Handelboss]]

=== Förakrig Corporation ===

Förakrig corporation is one of the older corporations in the [[Ikronin Jurekön]]. It was 
founded before the end of the [[Ikronin]] monarchy in the ancient history of the [[Ikronin]] people. Today it's a very strong contender in tank design.

=== Lax Corporation ===

The Lax corporation, owned and run by [[Argus Lax]], is known for its cutting-edge sensor equipment and computer cores. Among the leading corporations of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], Lax is most interested in exploration and science.

Lax also has extensive entertainment holdings, and is the main producer of films in the [[Orion Arm]], producing everything from historical documentaries to cheap pornographic material.

The Lax Corporation is heavily involved in aerospace design, and manufactures the Banbrytare and Silik Class Scouts.

Recently, Lax formed a mutual partnership with [[Oangaard Gruvfrift]] for surveying and exploring the uncharted systems throughout the [[Orion Arm]]. This partnership has lead to them charting several lucrative asteroid belts and planets.

=== Bestämd Corporation ===

The Bestämd is the oldest and largest megacorp in recorded [[Ikronin]] history. It is currently run by [[Lyna Bestämd]].

Bestämd handles all [[Wormhole Gate|gate]] construction, and is responsible for operation of the major [[Wormhole Gate|gates]]. Private and military gates are constructed by the Bestämd corporation, but are operated by other [[Ikronin]] corporations.

Bestämd is a major marketeer in power generation and drive systems. It had an early lock on computer cores for [[:Category:Starships|starships]] and [[:Category:Space Stations|space stations]] but has recently been losing ground to younger more innovative and aggressive companies such as the Lax corporation and Joramiir Mekaniker Obegränsat.

Despite their lost financial ground, Bestämd still specializes in large scale construction.  The built majority of the Ikronin infrastructure, including most major metropolises, along with most of the permanent orbital traffic around [[Kronheim]]. Bestämd is best known in infrastructure for its mobile markets and agricultural stations.

=== Joramiir Mekaniker Obegränsat ===

Joramiir Mekaniker Obegränsat pioneered and perfected [[Wormhole Gate|gate]] generators for starships, and continue to lead all research in this avenue. Joramiir technicians and researchers are focusing their efforts in creating [[Wormhole Gate|gate]] generators for shuttlers and [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]].

Joramiir's showcase includes their [[Falsniir Class Escort]] and [[Talshiir Class Transport]].

The current owner and company head is [[Símonarson Björn]], who has redirected the company and developed it into a high-tech research firm, which has been credited with many astounding discoveries in the past few decades. The company has always been at the forefront of weapon research but has recently accelerated its programs thanks to the urgency in combating the threat of the [[Volucris]].

Joramiir Mekaniker Obegränsat is divided into three divisions--Science, Engineering, and Security. The Science Division is led by [[Jomar Riekur]] and is in charge of all scientific research. The Engineering Division is led by [[Per-Kristian Phinn]] and is in charge of manufacturing. The Security Division is led by [[Orin Anderson]], an [[Ixoarchada]] mercenary, who commands the ''Far Runner'' and is responsible for the security of all JMU facilities and personnel.

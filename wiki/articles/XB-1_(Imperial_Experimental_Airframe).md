The XB-1 was the first Imperial bomber, based off the F-4 airframe. It was built with much larger fuel tanks for the ion engine to handle the significantly greater mass of the Anti-Warship Torpedoes (AWT). The design was also stripped of its guns, and the heat shielding plates necessary for an atmospheric reentry. The YB-1 was used in attack runs on asteroids with F-4s escorting and F-4s "defending" the target. The YB-1 was put into extremely limited production, but cancelled with only 30 delivered due to fuel problems limiting the range of the bomber too much.

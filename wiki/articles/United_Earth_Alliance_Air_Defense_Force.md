The '''Air Defense Force''' was the air combat, air superiority, combat transport, and bombing arm of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Defense Force]].

== History ==

Development of the Air Defense Force was given mostly to the Royal Air Force of the British Empire during the early years of the United Earth Alliance. While the cultural differences between the old Earth nations were largely lost over the years, the command structure and organization of the Royal Air Force can still be seen in the Air Defense Force.

The aircraft and small spacecraft used by the Air Defense Force were of great value in the many engagements in the United Earth Alliance's history, and instrumental in the desperate defense of the [[Titan System]] during the [[Second Exodus War]].

== Structure ==

At the time of its dissolution in [[2324]], the Air Defense Force had twelve Commands, eight in a defensive posture and four in an offensive posture.

=== Commands ===

Authority is delegated from the General-Commander to the Air Defense Force's Commands. There were Commands for each world in the United Earth Alliance, and several attached to the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency|Space Agency]]'s carriers.

* Earth Command - Re-assigned to Theia in the general retreat.
* Mars Command - Re-assigned to Theia in the general retreat.
* Ah Kin Command - Lost to the [[Onyx Empire]], in secret, circa [[2300]].
* Bethija Command - Re-assigned to Theia in the general retreat.
* Tejat Command - Re-assigned to Theia in the general retreat.
* Holt Command - Re-assigned to Theia in the general retreat.
* Theia Command
* Rhea Command
* 3rd Space Battle Group Carrier Command
* 5th Space Battle Group Carrier Command
* 13th Space Battle Group Carrier Command
* 17th Space Battle Group Carrier Command

=== Group ===

Under the Commands, there were several Groups responsible for different areas of strategy and equipment. Each Command had three Groups.

* Air Superiority Group - Responsible for securing air superiority, destroying enemy bombers, escorting friendly bombers, and similar roles.
* Attack Group - Bombers, ground attack aircraft, and similar attack craft.
* Support Group - Transport aircraft and logistics.

=== Station ===

A Station is a sub-level of organization used to specify a base of operations for one or more Wings. It can signify a single [[:Category:Carriers|Carrier]]'s complement of aircraft, or a single airbase, and other permanent installations.

=== Wings ===

Wings are a group of two or more squadrons. They always operate the same aircraft mission profiles, and often the same type of aircraft.

=== Squadrons ===

A flying squadron is an aircraft unit which carries out the primary tasks of the Air Defense Force. Nearly every squadron is different, but most are commanded by a Captain-Commander and have between 12 and 16 aircraft.

=== Flight ===

A Flight is a sub-division of a Squadron. Squadrons usually have an "A" Flight and a "B" Flight, under the command of junior officers like Lieutenant-Commanders.

== Components ==

The Air Defense Force fielded numerous aircraft, small spacecraft, and some trans-atmospheric craft.

* [[ADF Starfire]] - Vertical Take-Off and Landing Air and Space Superiority Fighter
* [[ADF Firestorm]] - Vertical Take-Off and Landing Air and Space Interceptor
* [[ADF Cyclone]] - Vertical Take-Off and Landing Air and Space Fighter/Attack Fighter Bomber 
* [[ADF Starlifter]] - Air and Space Transport Craft

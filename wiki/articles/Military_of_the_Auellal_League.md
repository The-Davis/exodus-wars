== History of the Auellal Military ==

The Auellal Military was almost entirely financed by outside contributors, including the [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova Family]] and the [[AstroLift Company]].  The support came in many forms, including monetary support, training facilities, and antiquated or damaged vehicles and vessels.  In exchange, the Auellal League offered exclusive trading opportunities once the Auellal League had defeated the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  After the fall of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], the Auellal League was unable to make good on its promises.  So [[Provost]] [[Steven Cue]] offered the one thing he could, exclusive trade with the Auellal League, and the promise of repayment of the debt over time.

== Organization ==

The Auellal armed forces are divided into two branches.  Each branch has sub-branches whose duties are directly related to their capabilities.  At the top of each branch is a individual leader who is responsible only to the Circle.  The [[Fànas triath]] commands the [[Auellal Armada|Armada]], and the [[Ard-auellan Ridir]] commands the [[Auellal Guardians|Guardians]].

== Manpower ==

The total number of military personnel is approximately 110,000 men-at-arms.  The Auellal League's armed forces are made up of ex-[[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and ex-[[United Centusi States]], enlisted men and officers.  With the removal of required military service, enlistment in the Auellal armed forces has tapered off immensely.

* Military manpower
** Military age: 16 years

** Active troops: 110,000
** Military Expenditures Amount: 22.1 Billion [[Likvada]] Annually

== International Stance ==

The [[Auellal League]] is not recognized by any government as a legitimate successor to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], with the exception of the [[Asanova Family Territory]], who have opened trade borders with them.  Additionally, the [[United Centusi States]] claims [[Caledon]] as 'Territory in the state of insurgence.'

== Recent Operations ==

The [[Auellal League]] initiated the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], and were the dominate faction throughout the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]].  After the abrupt end to the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]], the [[Auellal League]] was forced from all their holdings in [[Mesarthrim]] space, with the exception of [[Caledon]].

During the [[Volucris Incursion]] the [[Auellal League]] participated in its first foreign aid mission during the [[Battle of Tavou]], although their assistance was limited in nature, it provided a glimpse of a very different [[Mesarthrim]] for the nations of the [[Orion Arm]].

During the Plague outbreak on [[Terra]], the [[Auellal League]] sent a staff of their top immunologist and geneticist to [[Terra]] to assist.  Also, the [[Auellal League]] sent a small [[Casan Eirmis]] security detail to the ex-[[United Mesarthrim Clans]] MASH Base in Australia and South Africa.

With the outbreak of the [[Tempest War]], the [[Auellal League]] began working in secret with the [[Prefecture]].  Initial support came from the [[Dannsair]] who flew in mixed wings with the [[Federated Districts Air Force|Prefecture Air Force]].  Later in the war, combined [[Auellal League|Auellal]] [[Auellal Armada|Armada]] and [[Prefecture Galactic Navy|Prefecture Navy]] units began working in combined operations.  Their joint efforts culminated in the invasion of [[Mesar]] including a joint invasion of all five [[Fir Turtyle]] divisions, both [[Laoch Saoi]] divisions, the entire [[Casan Eirmis]] force, three [[Seòc Saoi]] divisions, the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]], and several [[Prefecture Legion Corps]].

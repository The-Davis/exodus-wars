The XV-1 was the first Imperial drop-capable combat vehicle. It was a proof-of-concept vehicle, using a fuel-cell electric engine and a standardized dropship hookup which could be matched to any of the dropships employed by the Royal Imperial Navy and Air Force. It was never armed or deployed to the field. Lessons learned and design decisions made were eventually used in the design of "[[:Category:Imperial Military Specification|Military Specification 3]]", the [[M-3 Enforcer]]

'''Falkran Nucleic-Bonding Super-Conducting Armor''' is the standard armor on nearly all [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] military hardware.  Surpassing even the armor systems utilized by the [[Meroniri]], the [[Ixoarchada]] have refined Falkran creation and forging over the centuries.  It is considered one of the masterwork technologies of the Ixoarchada race, and its secrets are jealously guarded, even from their allies.

Falkran armor consists of normal matter put into a hyper-dense state, similar to Neutronium, however the crystalline structure of the bonded nuclei is rather different.  Because the armor is held together by nuclear bonding, rather than electron bonding, it is nearly impossible to ''physically'' break.  It can compress nonlinearly, allowing the force of an impact to be countered over a long time span (the greater the force applied to the armor, the stiffer it becomes in response).  This makes physical attacks against the armor nearly ineffective, and also means that the armor almost never actually takes damage.  It is more prone to catastrophic failure than to being pierced in a single location and thus small and medium energy weapons do no damage against it.

The nuclear bonding allows all electrons in the armor to become "shared."  Because of this, and the fact that it is super-conducting at combat temperatures, electromagnetic weapons are rendered mostly ineffective.  Low and medium energy laser beams of all frequencies are completely reflected, and the energy the armor is forced to absorb from higher energy weapons usually does little damage unless it beaches the armor's critical threshold, at which point a portion of the armor catastrophically fails and becomes a plasma cloud, which usually dissipates the remainder of the weapon blast.

In addition, because it is extremely super-conducting, it renders whatever it is protecting nearly unaffected by even the strongest electromagnetic pulse weapons.  It also greatly diminishes the effectiveness of electrostatic weapons, such as proton or ion cannons, by effectively spreading out the charge over the entire surface, and allowing it to be rapidly drained off.

The armor has densities approaching that of a neutron star, allowing it to stop high-energy particles, such as neutrons from neutron bombs, cannons, etc.  It is also extremely difficult to pierce because the crystalline structure efficiently spreads the force of impact over a large surface area.  

The surface of Falkran armor is chemically inert, incredibly smooth, and thus is utterly unaffected by chemical weapons (acid, napalm, etc).  It also does not react with plasma in the same way as normal matter.  Contact with plasma merely causes the armor's internal temperature to rise.  The armor must remain below a critical temperature to remain super-conducting, but that temperature is very high.  Because of this, even un-powered Falkran armor utilizes some type of thermal dissipation system to prevent it from breaking down and becoming vulnerable to simpler weapons.  Most vehicles and vessels protected by Falkran utilize structures that look similar to large heat-sinks, increasing the surface area of the armor to dissipate heat, as well as specially designed antennas and spires to help bleed-off built-up charges.  These increases in surface area also increase the damage the armored shell can take in general.

Falkran Armor's coefficient of reflection is nonlinearly related to the intensity of what is being reflected, and so low-powered sensor radiation (such as RADAR/LIDAR, etc) is actually not reflected but absorbed, providing a small amount of stealth.

Falkran Armor is very difficult to produce, and is thus very expensive.  It has, however, the benefit that little of the armor is needed for maximum effectiveness, therefore ships, vehicles, and other objects that use it are actually lighter than their conventional counterparts by a significant degree.

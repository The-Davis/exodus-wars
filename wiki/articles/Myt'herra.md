The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] Myt'Herra served as the elite infantry units in the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|UMC Heer]].  The Myt'herra served as special assault, fast-strike, and covert ops soldiers.  During the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]] against the [[Anderung]], the Myt'herra were the backbone of the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]].

Each Legion in the Myt'herra is sworn to a [[Clan Lord]] who serves as the Commander-in-Chief of that Legion, taking orders only from the [[Uber-General]].  The only exception is the Auellal Legion which was formed of [[Mesarnak]] resistance members, and were therefore sworn to no [[Clan Lord]].

==History==
The first Legion was unofficially formed in 2102, approximately fifteen months after the [[Mesarthrim]] were woken by their [[Anderung]] masters.  The first Legion was formed by [[Herra Tischler]], the daughter of [[Otto Tischler]].  [[OttoTischler|Otto]] called the group 'Mein Herra's Tapfere Begleiter' (My Herra's Courageous Companions).  The name stuck, and over time was shortened to just Mein Herra.  In a skirmish outside the newly founded [[Homemaker's Field]] on April 22nd, [[2104]], [[Herra Tischler|Herra]] was killed.  Her soldiers escaped, and upon return to their base camp they adopted the name Herra's Stolz (Herra's Pride).

In [[2105]] [[Otto Tischler]] led a group of [[Mesarthrim]] out of several [[slave camps]] and armed them into a second Legion.  They were put under the command of [[Alfred Dietrich]], a personal friend of [[Otto Tischler|Otto's]].  Now that the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]] had two different 'Legions' it was decided that there would need to be a set of standards, rules, and organisations established.  In a meeting of the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]] leaders, it was decided that soldiers in the Legion would be required to swear loyalty to the Legion's [[Clan Lord]], and requiring a minimum of six years of service from any [[Mesarthrim]] who swore their loyalty.  It would also fall to that [[Clan]] and [[Clan Lord]] to feed, train, and cloth any solider under their command.  This structure helped tend to the needs of the Legion.  It was at this time that the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]] leaders decided the legions would be numbered in order from when they were formed, and would be called 'Herra Legions' in honour of [[Herra Tischler]].

In October of [[2112]], four months after [[Otto Tischler|Otto's]] death, the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]] leaders met and officially formed five Legions out of the rabble that had become the excessively large Herra Legions.  It was at this time that the Herra Legions started a training camp in the polar region of [[Willim]].  The brutal environment was considered an advantage by commanders because it made their soldiers of 'a tougher stock then our jellied oppressors.'

In [[2261]] four more Legions were added to the Herra Legions roster.  Additionally, the Herra Legions officially added the prefix Myt- to their name in honour of their [[Myt'Gaul]] brethren who had turned Ice Crown Bay into a devastating phyrric victory for the [[Anderung]].

In [[2324]] when the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] broke out, the Lordless Auellal Legion was involved in the initial treachery by assaulting and destroying the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|UMC Heer]] command compound, [[The Black Fort]] on [[Schwarz Island]].  The destruction of [[The Black Fort]] cost the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|UMC Heer]] some of its best and most experienced commanders.

Later in [[2324]], three of the Legion [[Clan Lords]] swore fealty to [[Elias Faust]] and the [[United Centusi States]].  This cause major rifts in the Myt'herra, and resulted in the formation of [[the Liberator Sons]].  Over the course of [[2324]], five of the eight Legions would be bought up by [[William Camp]].

In [[2325]] when the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] collapsed, only the Sixth Myt'Herra Legion was still loyal to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|UMC Heer]].  Only 18% of their fighting capacity was left protecting [[Stonebridge]] and the remains of [[Schwarz Island]].  The Sixth Legion was not re-commissioned by the [[United  Centusi States]].

==Structure==
The Myt'herra of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] was split into nine Legions, each Legion was under the employ of a specific [[Clan]], however the Legion was responsible to the defence and service of all of [[Mesar]].  (See Also: Mesarthrim Clan Responsibility (Each Clan was responsible for funding some part of the government, some provided for the Church, others the Myt'Herra, some the Myt'Gaul, others the Navy, some domestic constructions, etc)).  Part of the charge of the [[Clans]] was to provide food, clothing, armour, training, and education for their soldiers.  After [[2200]] when the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]] cast aside the bartering system, and started printing its own money, the [[Clan]] was also responsible for paying their soldiers.

Each legion is lead by their [[Clan Lord]] who in turn is responsible to the [[Uber General]].  The exception to this is the Auellal Legion who does not have a [[Clan Lord]].  The Auellal Legion utilised a system of promotion based on experience and prowess in combat.  Their Auellal General would serve as that Legion's [[Clan Lord]].

The Mesarthrim Myt'Herra are among the most elite infantry units in the United Centusi States Army.  The Myt'Herra operate not only as special assault soldiers and fast-strike forces, but they also serve as the ship-borne naval troopers for the UCS Navy.  Training to join the Myt'Herra begins immediately after Myt'Kampfen training ends, and lasts for an additional year. 

The Myt'Herra wear a fully-sealed, powered-armor suit.  The lightweight Leifstein armor plates offer excellent protection, while maintaining extreme agility and mobility for the wearer.  Enhanced motor controls and motion-assist motors, supposedly based on an [[Ixoarchada]] design, allow not just excellent control of the suit, but also amplified physical strength, already enhanced through genetic engineering, to twice that or an unaugmented Mesarthrim.  The enhanced strength enables Myt'Herra not simply to lift heavier loads and carry larger packs than standard Myt'Kampfen, but also gives them far greater endurance on the battlefield.  The drawback to utilizing the strength enhancing servos is that the battlefield life of a single energy pack is only sufficient for 24 hours of continuous use, and must be swapped out and recharged each day.  The up side to this is that it takes only two hours to fully recharge a battery, and can get power from any standard Mesarthrim vehicle reactor. 

The Myt'Herra's power suit contains an oxygen scrubber and small oxygen supply, giving the Myt'Herra the capacity for upto 12 hours in a non-oxygen environment without replacing the scrubber.  The boots of the suit have special magnetic clamps and gripping surfaces, which allows Myt'Herra to stay firmly planted on the ground in zero-gravity environments, such as on a starships without artificial gravity.  Additionally, the suit has advanced communications gear, and an enhanced field-pack, which holds rations, ammunition magazines, grenades, high explosives, homing beacons, and breaching equipment.  

The standard issue rifle of the Myt'Herra is a Fireline Industries Type 77 Elite Deployment Rifle, T-77E, a custom rifle designed specifically for the Myt'Herra.  The T-77E is a Blazer Rifle capable of firing upto 60 rounds per energy pack.  This rifle can breach infantry armor, and also damage light vehicle armor.  The Myt'Herra utilize this larger, more expensive rifle because of its advantages in range, and a substantial increase in power over the T-55S, utilized by the Myt'Kampfen.  It also has the capacity to fire an unfocused burst, making it lethal and accurate at extremely close ranges.  An enhanced Saidia Optics OP-11 scope is mounted to the rifle and linked to the power suit's internal HUD, allowing accurate targeting at ranges well beyond 2000 meters.  

In addition to the T-77E carried by the Myt'Herra, a Parkt's P-8 combat pistol is also carried, usually on the soldier's field-pack.  This small Blazer pistol fires the same intensity rounds of the T-55S rifle, but has only a third the range of the rifle, and its power pack is only capable of providing energy for ten rounds to be fired.  Despite these limitations, the P-8 is extremely useful for armed entry and CQB operations, where a larger rifle may prove ungainly.  A small under-barrel camera can feed targeting imagery to the soldier's HUD.  

Myt'Herra are the rapid response forces and naval troopers for the UCS Army and Navy.  They undergo extensive training beyond that of the standard Mesarthrim warrior--training which is already very difficult.  The Myt'Herra are among the best trained soldiers in the Terran Sector.

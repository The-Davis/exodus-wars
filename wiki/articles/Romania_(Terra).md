'''Romania''' was a country in Southeastern Europe. Romania bordered [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]] and [[Yugoslavia (Terra)|Yugoslavia]] to the west, the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] to the northeast, and [[Bulgaria (Terra)|Bulgaria]] to the south. Romania has a stretch of sea coast along the Black Sea, and the eastern and southern Carpathian Mountains run through its center.

Romania was one of the earliest signers of the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]], when it joined the world government along with the rest of the [[Warsaw Pact]].

''Sanor Cai'' has played the dual roles of merciful benefactor and military leader.  He was born at the height of the [[Burning Wars]], and served for a short time in the Huern military before deserting and leading his own band of warriors in the wastelands.

== The Merciful Warrior ==

Of all the surviving veteran commanders of the [[Burning Wars]], Sanor Cai is perhaps the most unique. Cai did not serve any of the armies of the heirs to the Huern throne. Instead, he led a small group of soldiers in defense of cities and villages in the path of the catastrophic battles of the [[Burning Wars]].

Cai's forces could never stem the tide of the battle, but they did buy the refugees enough time to flee into the wastelands. Cai acted like a guardian angel, guiding caravans to safe refuges while they lasted.

Eventually, the last refuges fell in the [[Burning Wars]], and Sanor Cai's forces had to disband: their scant support network was gone. Cai wandered the dying wastelands of [[Lorithan]], giving aid where he could. Finally, starving and alone, he stumbled into one of the first [[Meroniri]] relief camps.

Ever since, Sanor Cai has been one of the greatest allies of the Meroniri among the [[Lorithos]].

== The Hishai ==

[[Dirken Huern]] eventually appointed Cai as his [[Hishai]], and the commander in chief of the Lorithos defense forces.  As Hishai, Sanor Cai worked to rebuild the Lorithos military while facing the realities of a dying world and nearly dead people.

Cai's first act was to put nearly his entire budget into robotics research.  As a result, his military had an unsurpassed level of automation and reliance on robots.

Additionally, Sanor Cai banned the use of any sort of weapon of mass destruction in his military--a move that Dirken Huern supported.

== Family Life ==

Sanor Cai married Sarai Diter in 2279.  They had three children, one of whom did not survive more than a year.  Their daughter Nami Cai moved to [[Ipsoff]] where she worked in the field of planetary ecology.  Their son Agen Cai married and had a son, [[Siovar Cai]], who was eventually named the heir to the throne of the Lorithos.

== Volucris War ==

When the [[Volucris War]] began, Sanor Cai was in his 80s, but was still a vital and charismatic commander.  The war took a far greater toll on him than his long years of life, and as Lorithan burned a second time, Cai's life seemed to wither away.  Eleven days before the final evacuation, he died in the early morning hours.

'''Ward''' is the XXth planet in the [[Xi Herculis System]].

==History==

The distance from the star low light output keeps the vegetation level low, and heavy tectonic activity throws up a lot of geothermal energy and heat.

In 2230, colonists decided to move in. They survived, but didn't thrive. With no important resources, Ward had no way to break into the galactic economy.

That changed in 2323, when the Prefecture was formed and began working with the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] to build a [[Wormhole Gate]] network for the new Prefecture territories. The [[Ikronin]] decided that Ward was an ideal world for one of their Gate hubs.

The Gate hub attracted a large infrastructure of supply depots and warehouses, and several biotech corporations working to create plants and animals that can thrive in Ward's low-light, hot and damp environment. This research has given Ward vast jungles and some tree-dwelling herd animals, and lots of useful data to export, but still little in the way of edible plants.

Ward District Capital is the city that houses the government buildings and homes of most Ward District politicians. It also houses the Ward District Home Defense Security Headquarters, and an office of the Prefecture Navy, where visiting admirals can access the local information nets.
Ward's hot, dark, damp climate makes it ideal for growing fungi, and a small industry has grown up around the gourmet varieties. The majority of the original colonists of Ward own the mushroom farms.

The darkest parts of the jungle host the Ward Infantry Boot Camp, the toughest military training camp in the entire Prefecture. The trainees, besides undergoing military training and physical conditioning, must spend at least a week on their own in the jungle.

The future of Ward looks bright. As long as industry continues to grow and the genetic researchers finish creating a viable biosphere, the planet will thrive.

On February 6, [[2318]], the [[Onyx Empire]] invaded the [[Des System]] and occupied [[Des Arc]].  The takeover was led by the [[HMS Secutor|HMS ''Secutor'']], and had no casualties on either side.  All of the [[United Earth Alliance]] forces in the system had withdrawn during the initial invasion three years earlier.

The planet of [[Des Wei]] was cultivated and colonized as a result of Imperial occupation.

The '''Kingdom of Norway''' was a Nordic country occupying the western portion of the Scandinavian Peninsula in Europe. Norway was bordered by [[Sweden (Terra)|Sweden]], [[Finland (Terra)|Finland]], and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

The Kingdom of Norway included the Arctic island territories of Svalbard and Jan Mayen, and Bouvet Island in the South Atlantic Ocean.

King Haakon VIII abdictated the throne by signing the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on November 2, [[2017]].

'''Lina Sveto'''
*Place of Birth: [[Archada]]
*Allegiance: [[Taviridis Somarchada]]

''Lina Sveto'' was the [[Ixoarchada]] ambassador to the [[United Centusi States]] before the beginning of the [[Tempest War]]. She dissolved the Joint Defense Treaty before leaving [[Mesar]] and returning to [[Ixoarchada]] territory.

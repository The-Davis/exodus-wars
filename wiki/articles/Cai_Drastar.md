'''Cai Drastar''' is the traditional [[Huorak]] name of the Cai Dynasty, the line of descent for the position of [[Prekohr]] since [[Siovar Cai]] succeeded [[Dirken Huern]] in [[2350]].

The name [[Rikaz o Fii Cai iv Huerna]] is synonymous with the Cai Drastar. If the Cai line dies out, the government will be renamed with the name of the new [[Prekohr]].  The previous government was the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]].

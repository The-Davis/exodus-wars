*Power Plant: Lorithos Hydrogen Engine
*Speed: 20 KPH
*Armor: Tzaiko Composite armor
*Armament:
**1 Heavy Blaster rifle
**4 Short-Range Sreiver Missile launchers (8 rounds)
*Equipment: Robot Control Circuits
*Manufacturer: Lorithan Defense Department
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard Sensors

===Overview===

When combat robots were first created, operators had to give their undivided attention to directing them.  As AIs evolved and improved, direct input became less and less necessary.  In the early 24th century, operators could actually fight alongside their robotic underlings.

The Zuura is a popular suit for robot operators.  Not only does it supply all the necessary control for robots, it also has an AI built in to move many operations away from the pilot, allowing him to concentrate more on tactics.

===Capabilities===

The Zuura is well-equipped.  Its arsenal is a blaster rifle, Sreiver missiles, and powered fists.  Alone, a Zuura can take on most battle armor and some Rigs single-handedly.  Its defenses--reinforced Tzaiko Tzaiko Composite armor--make it difficult to take down.

[[Image:ZuuraRobotCombatCommandSystem.jpg]]

For combat armor, the Zuura is quite fast.  It's powered by a small Hydrogen Engine, which must be refueled every two days of active duty.

===Deployment===

Typically, a Zuura operator controls twenty Krelbines and forty Ruehvs.  The operator's primary concern is directing his forces.  Combat is secondary.  Normally, Zuuras coordinate closely with tank forces.

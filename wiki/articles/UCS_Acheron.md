===Overview===

The Acheron was originally designed as a massive hauler prototype, begun by Rahn Industries in early 2324.  When the United Centusi States became the RI top-priority in mid-to-late 2324, the prototype was set aside, nothing more than a frame and some internals.  A few weeks later, acquisitions officials from the UCS Navy analyzed the frame, bought it, and towed it to Tincomalee, where it was rebuilt as a battle-carrier and flagship for the UCS fleet.  It was stripped of any standard RI equipment, and completely rebuilt using state-of-the-art Mesarthrim technologies.

[[Image:UCSAcheron1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

Unlike some flagships, the Acheron was not built so much as a heavy gun platform, but more as a specialized battle-carrier/C&C flagship.  Despite this, its weapons payload is still formidable, rivaling the largest flagships of other races.  Its heaviest weaponry comes in the form of four Type-X Heavy Blazers, the same type that were developed for and mounted on the Ares battleship class.  Their output is undeniably powerful, and their range far exceeds any other Blazer type.

Backing up the Type-X Heavy Blazers are a pair of Fireline Hellguns, standard RI Hellguns heavily overhauled by Fireline Industries of Cirus, and a dozen Type-R Heavy Blazers, the tried-and-true heavy cannons mounted on nearly every Mesarthrim warship, large and small.  In addition to these heavier weapons are 145 Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannon of the Mesarthrim, and 28 VOTM tubes, for use against fighters, warships, and stationary targets alike. 

The Acheron's primary power comes from its sizeable aerospace complement.  Carrying fifty-five squadrons of aerospace fighters and fifteen squadrons of bombers, plus numerous shuttles and support craft, in standard configuration, the Acheron is home base to more fighters than the entire naval aerospace corps of some smaller factions. 

The Acheron is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system.  The AAS is backed up by Leifstein Composite Armor, which is roughly 25% stronger than Unitron Tzaiko Composite.  It is also surprisingly quick for a ship its size, as its Gravity Variance Drive allows it to perform complex maneuvers. 

===Deployment===
The Acheron was completed in March of 2325 at the Seiger Naval Shipyards orbiting Tincomalee, and after shake-down testing, became flagship of the UCS Navy 3rd fleet, with a home-base in the Cirus system.  Its impressive armament and formidable aerospace complement make it well suited for either fleet support or solo operations.

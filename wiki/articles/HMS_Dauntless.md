The HMS ''Dauntless'' was the lead ship in the [[:Category:Destroyers|destroyer]] [[Dauntless Class Destroyer|class of the same name]]. It was given the hull designation DG-1.

As a warship, it led an active but undistinguished career, serving in the [[Earth]] invasion, the [[Tejat]] invasion, and the [[Titan System]] campaign. It was destroyed in action against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] in [[2322]].

**ECM Jamming Gear

*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Active Probe 

===Overview===

Upgrading the heavy Werewolf to the new Mk.II was much simpler than the smaller Lightning.  The Werewolf was fast enough.  It only needed its rotors replaced with the efficient and powerful Archadian anti-gravity lift system.

[[Image:Werewolf1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

Like the smaller Lightning Mk.II, the Werewolf Mk.II's weapons payload was not altered in the upgrade.  The primary weapon of the Werewolf is a single chin-mounted Medium Neutron Cannon.  The weapon is mounted on a flexible turret, giving the Werewolf and excellent firing arc.

The Werewolf carries four Medium Range Missile launchers.  Its lighter counterpart--the Lightning--can't fire at the range the Werewolf can handle.  The missiles are backed up by four Medium Ordinance Hard Points.

The Werewolf is quite fast, though not as speedy as the Lightning.  The reactor is a V-85 Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor, which powers the Werewolf up to 250KPH.  This speed, combined with its tough armor, makes the Werewolf much more resilient than earlier gunship models.

===Standard Equipment===

Normally, the Werewolf carries four Hound LRMs.  The weapons are excellent for the Werewolf's role.  With the recent updates to the Hound LRM guidance systems, the weapon is much more effective in atmospheric combat.

Like the Lightning, the Werewolf can carry electronic warfare systems.  The ECM jamming gear and Active Probe are both standard equipment, but the Werewolf can opt to carry ALARM systems.  The reflex weapon is rarely used against Volucris--they have no detectable scanning systems--but against humanoids and Ologrians, the ALARM is a very effective weapon.

===Deployment===

Upgrading to the Mk.II system was easy--Rahn Industries finished testing in November of 2324, three months ahead of schedule.  Because the reactor wasn't upgraded and therefore didn't need to be stress-tested, the company techs were able to push ahead to test flights by October of 2324.  Final shake-downs were finished far ahead of schedule, and a full line of locally-developed replacement parts to the Archadian-built lift system were ready by November.

The targeting system design flaws were corrected in the late versions of the Werewolf Mk.I.  Field techs were able to apply software updates to the ailing targeting computer.  The new pilot/gunner/computer combo was 80% more effective than the originally planned pilot/computer combo, so the double-cockpit design was kept in the Werewolf Mk.II.

The Werewolf is very popular in VTOL assault units.  It can deliver huge amounts of fire, and is much faster than the Skyhawk gunship variant.  It's excellent in softening up landing zones, supporting infantry, and harassing moving troops.

The '''Sirius Bari System''' is a star system with five planets and their moons. 

In order of their distances from Sirius Bari, the planets are [[Uscio]], [[Latium]], [[Nuovo Napoli]], [[Campania]] and [[Molise]].

== Stellar Navigation ==

Sirius Bari is the Sirius Bari System's star. Sirius Bari is a G3V yellow dwarf, slightly larger than Sol.

=== Uscio ===

[[Uscio]] (0.16AU) is a small rocky planet in a tight orbit around Sirius Bari.

=== Latium ===

[[Latium]] (0.75AU) is a barren planet with with a thick sulphurous atmosphere.

=== Nuovo Napoli ===

[[Nuovo Napoli]] (1.1AU) is the main colony world of the system. It has one moon, [[Lombardino]]

=== Campania ===

[[Campania]] (1.9AU) is a medium sized gas giant, with twenty un-named moons.

=== Molise ===

[[Molise]] (4.4AU) is a large gas giant. Molise has fifteen moons.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

[[Nuovo Napoli]] was colonized in [[2034]], largely by [[Italy (Terra)|Italians]].

The Comur Resistance was an uprising that formed under the oppressive rule of the [[United Centusi States]].  The Comur Resistance would eventually become one of the co-founding members of the Mesarthrim Federation.

== History ==
The Comur Resistance officially became organised in [[2327]] under the leadership of Commandant George Hofler von Baum.  [[Frederick Degause]], governor of Comur, supported the rebels, and fed them a steady stream of information from the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] war data banks.  The Resistance based themselves out of [[Tincomalee]] and were mainly concerned with harassing [[United Centusi States Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] offices, and construction yards around that area.  Then, in December, [[William Camp]] was tipped off by a [[Myt'gaul#Monk|Myt'gaul Monk]] that [[Frederick Degause|Degause]] was working with the Resistance.  This lead to the end of what became known as the [[First Tirshan Uprising]] - as Commandant Hofler was killed when forty-seven square blocks were decimated by an orbital strike.  The unknown [[Myt'gaul#Monk|Monk]] took [[Frederick Degause|Degause]] into custody.  The next day, on the steps of the Governors Residence, the [[Myt'gaul#Monk|Monk]] declaired [[Frederick Degause|Degause]] a traitor in the eyes of the [[Mesarthrim Church]] and the [[United Centusi States]].  Cursing his soul to the darkest layers of hell, the [[Myt'gaul#Monk|Monk]] nailed [[Frederick Degause|Degause]] to a crossbeam and crucified him.

It wouldn't be until [[2330]] that the Comur Resistance began to reform.  Approached in secret by the exiled [[Mark Edge]], [[Brian Passley]] agreed to begin to arm the old members of the Resistance.  It would take two years of torment before the Resistance was fully formed, but by September of [[2332]], [[Brian Passley|Passley]] had one and a quarter million men, and fifty [[Vulture Siege Tank|Siege Tanks]].  The Resistance was becoming larger then [[Mark Edge|Edge]] or [[Brian Passley|Passley]] had expected.  So, in January of [[2333]] the Resistance struck several major industrial points, and the [[Myt'kampfen]] barracks in [[Tincomalee]].  The [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] were quick to respond, and elements of the [[United Centusi States First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] were dispatched under the command of their Admiral, [[Anthony Koch]].

[[Steven Cue]] had co-ordinated with [[Bethany Tischler]], and had dispatched several [[Auellal Armada|Armada]] warships filled with [[Auellal Armada#The_Dannsair|Dancers]] to [[Tirshan System|Tirshan]].  The [[Auellal Armada#The_Dannsair|Dancers]] fought the [[United Centusi States Luftwaffe|Luftwaffe]] to a standstill.  By late [[2333]], however, the Resistance was back in hiding as the pressure from the Kreigsmarine warships mounted.  It wouldn't be until [[2334]] when [[Arnold Stadt]] appeared in [[Tirshan System|Tirshan]] carrying false orders - and when he came into point blank range with Admiral [[Anthony Koch|Koch]], [[Arnold Stadt|Stadt]] opened fire.

It would take a little over a month before [[Arnold Stadt|Stadt]] could secure the system.  Once the system was secured, [[Arnold Stadt]], [[Brian Passley]], and [[Steven Cue]] met and officially formed the [[Mesarthrim Federation]].

The Prefecture took over the design line at the height of the Battle of Terra, and captured the new heavy XF-13 airframe. It was ideal for a strategic bomber and torpedo delivery system, as both the Empire and the Prefecture had begun using shield technologies on their warships that the nuclear weapons of the F-9B could not penetrate. The XB-6 was armed with heavy torpedoes, internal bomb bays, and an enormous array of ordinance. The YB-6 prototype fought in the Battle of Canaveral and the Siege of Orlando, and there were B-6 Bloodhawks in use by the final Assault on McKinley.

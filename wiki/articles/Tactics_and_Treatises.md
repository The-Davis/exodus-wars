Many writings on '''Tactics''' have been distributed and studied by the various militaries throughout the galaxy.

'''Treatises''' are essays that focus on various social and scientific issues that face society at large. They tend to have more "fluff" than actual ground-breaking content, but strive to summarize the most important facts of an issue for a layperson.

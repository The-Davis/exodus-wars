The '''Karminrote Gezeiten-Kampfgruppe''', or "Crimson Tide Battle Group" is the premier combat group, or Kampfgruppe, in the [[United Centusi States Kriegsmarine]].  Before that, it served as the lead fleet element of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine]] [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine First Fleet|First Fleet]].

==Formation==
The Crimson Tide Battle Group was the brainchild of [[Nathaniel Hinks von Stadt|Nathaniel Hinks]].  [[Nathaniel Hinks von Stadt|Nathaniel]] was a battle strategist and tactician, and he developed the Kampfgruppe architecture to help coordinate space battles between two large fleets firing across millions of kilometers.  One of the decisions was to create a specific group of warships, utilizing special codes, intensive training, and state-of-the-art equipment, it was decided that one "Kampfgruppe" could decisively turn a battle, or harass an enemy long enough for a stronger force to arrive.

The first Kampfgruppe was assigned seven [[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster Destroyers]] in [[2290]].  In [[2291]], six [[Nimitz Class Gunship|Nimitz Gunships]] were added to the fleet.  The seven Destroyers became known as the Archangels, and the [[Nimitz Class Gunship|Nimitz]] the Firebirds.  Finally in [[2295]], the Crimson Tide Battle Group was at its full strength of twenty-one vessels.

In [[2306]], the Crimson Tide Battle Group came back to [[Mesar]] after the [[2305]] [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kreigsmarine|Kreigsmarine]] fiasco.  Crimson Tide Battle Groups commanding officer, then [[Bradley von Graith|Bradley Graith]], was the only commander in the entire [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kreigsmarine|Kreigsmarine]] to order his men home.  The ensuing slugfest between the Crimson Tide Battle Group and the [[Anderung]] lasted eighteen hours of almost constant battle.  Of the twenty-one ships, twelve were lost -- all were extensively damaged.  But in the end, the Crimson TIde Battle Group stood as the only vessels in orbit of [[Mesar]].  Additionally, there were no surviving [[Anderung]] capital ships left, anywhere in [[Mesarthrim]] space.

==Fate==
In December of [[2330]], the Crimson TIde Battle Group was present in the defense of [[Mesar]], during the course of the battle, the Crimson Tide Battle Group came under fire from [[Atlas Wing]].  The two battle groups turned into each other, and the brutal close quarters cost both fleets almost their entire force.  Only the Archangels made it away.

After the [[Second Prefecture Battle of Mesar|Battle of Mesar]], [[Oberst-Commandant]] [[George Ackerman von Tischler|George Ackerman]] returned to [[Mesar]] under a flag of truce, and surrendered the Archangels to his former commander, [[Bradley von Graith]].

==Composition==
The Crimson Tide Battle Group is further divided into sections, the Archangels, the Firebirds, and the Honorable.  Above them is the [[UCSN Crimson Tide]] herself.

===The Crimson Tide===
[[Image:CTBG_Starlight1.png|thumb|right|250px|The Crimson Tide]]
The Crimson Tide is a [[Starlight class Flagship]].  She was the first of her kind, and a capable machine from inception through to her destruction.  Shortly after [[Fireline Industries]] delivered the [[Starlight class Flagship|Starlight]] they sold general rights and upgrading rights of all their warships to [[Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe]] who then contracted the actual upgrades through [[Mesar Clipboard Limited|Mesar Beschränkte Zwischenablage]].  This was beneficial to the Crimson Tide because she became a weapons platform for [[Mesar Clipboard Limited|MBZ]], one of the leading [[Mesarthrim]] Research and Development firms.  The Crimson Tide stood as the ship that connected the three sub-fleets, and was apart of a sub-fleet herself.  The Crimson Tide always served as the lead vessel in all fleet engagements, and was renowned for being able to take a beating and still continue fighting.

===The Archangels===
The Archangels were the first ships detached to the Kampfgruppe.  They were brand new [[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster Destroyers]].  In [[2315]] the Crimson Tide Battle Group went through an overhaul, and the Archangels were swapped out for [[Starknight class Cruiser]].  In [[2325]], [[UMCS Sariel|Sariel]] and [[UMCS Uriel|Uriel]] were replaced by [[Avenger class Cruiser]]'s for eight months to test the new ships.  They were swapped back due to the enormous crew requirement, and the testing was reducing the efficency of the Battle Group during the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]].

The Archangels were;
* UMCS/UCSN Gabriel
* UMCS/UCSN Michael
* UMCS/UCSN Raguel
* UMCS/UCSN Raphael
* UMCS/UCSN Remiel
* UMCS/UCSN Sariel
* UMCS/UCSN Uriel

No Archangel vessels were ever lost in combat.  The [[UCSN Raguel|UMCS Raguel]] was almost destroyed in the [[Tau Ceti Campaign]] when a defense station orbiting [[Kalmar]] exploded a thousand kilometers off her port bow.  The Commandant was able to save the [[UCSN Raguel|Raguel]], a [[Starknight class Cruiser|Starknight]] was taken from First Fleet and renamed [[UCSN Raguel|Raguel]].  The damaged [[Starknight class Cruisers|Starknight]] was later scuttled.

===The Firebirds===
[[Image:Firebird.png|thumb|right|250px|The Firebirds at [[Mesar]], 2330]]
The Firebirds started out as a group of [[Nimitz class Gunship]]s.  In [[2315]], during the refit they were replaced by [[Werewolf class Gunship|Werewolf class Gunships]].  Then in [[2322]] they were once again replaced by [[Turpitz class Gunship]]s.  The aged [[Turpitz class Gunship|Turpitz]] design was underpowered for [[Nathaniel Hinks|Hinks']] aggressive campaign into the [[Onyx Empire]].  Three Firebirds were destroyed in the first campaign alone.  Their vessels were replaced with [[Nimitz class Gunship|Nimitz]], and stayed [[Nimitz class Gunship|Nimitz]] until their destruction in [[2330]].

===The Honorable===
[[Image:CTBG_Wickliff1.png|thumb|right|195px|The [[UCSN Haswell]], circa 2330]]
The honorable were added to the Kampfgruppe in [[2299]] as a group of seven [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe class Destroyers]].  The honorable are named in honor of early [[Mesarthrim|Mesarthrim heros]].  In the [[2306]] [[Centus Return]], four honorables' were lost, two were scuttled, and one damaged beyond repair.  With the need for quick replacements, the decimated Honorable fleet was issued seven current 'spare' ships.  They ranged from one [[Starknight class Cruiser]] to other [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe class Destroyers]].  Then in [[2307]], the [[Starknight class Cruiser]], [[UCSN Haswell|UMCS Haswell]] was ambushed by an [[Anderung Loyalist]] suicide bomber while covering the Firebirds in [[Anderung]] hunt.  In the [[2315]] refit, all seven vessels were replaced by newly constructed [[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmasters]].

During the [[Onyx Civil War]], the [[UCSN Dodd|UMCS Dodd]] and [[UCSN Tischler|UMCS Tischler]] were destroyed.  Their losses in crew were so heavy that the ships weren't replaced until after the Onyx surrender.  During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], the [[UCSN Wolfgang]] took extensive damage and both the [[UCSN Haswell|UMCS Haswell]] and the [[UCSN Ogden|UMCS Ogden]] were destroyed when the rest of the Honorable tried to get her to safety.  The [[UCSN Haswell|Haswell]] and the [[UCSN Ogden|Ogden]] were quickly replaced by a pair of old [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe class Destroyers]].

* UMCS/UCSN Haswell - '''[[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe]]'''
* UMCS/UCSN Holcomb - '''[[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster]]'''
* UMCS/UCSN Mann - '''[[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster]]'''
* UMCS/UCSN Ogden - '''[[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe]]'''
* UMCS/UCSN Tischler - '''[[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster]]'''

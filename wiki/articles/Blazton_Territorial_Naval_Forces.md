As with all things regarding the [[Sirius Families]], Territorial Naval Forces is more of a collective term for all space vessels operated by the various factions for protection of the three-system [[Sirius Cluster]]. Although officially a single entity, the Sirius Families assigns defense of the three systems their territorial rulers.

At any given time, the number of armed vessels in a single territory may vary due to the presence of various groups, but each family does maintain its own full-time fleet. The fleets ensure family control over a territory, and also serve not so much to keep the peace, but to keep the status quo.

The starships of the Blazton Defense Fleet specifically defend Blazton interests in all of the Sirius Cluster. Most of them are usually deployed in Berlusconi space, since a large portion of Blazton Industries assets are located there. Blazton sites located in other territories rely on the host faction's security forces for protection.

In addition to any warships, the [[Sirius Cluster|Sirius Territories]] play host to trade and merchant ships from across the [[Orion Arm]]. Vast fleets of freighters, transports, haulers, and tankers transfer goods at massive orbital depots, and then set off in the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates|gates]] once again. The Sirius families also maintain their own private trade fleets for transporting "priority goods".

The Blazton Security Fleet is quite small, considering that its responsibilities lie far beyond the company's Sirius holdings. Blazton vessels regularly visit Blazton deep space holdings, and facilities orbiting other worlds. Their powerful Delta missile frigates are quite popular and are frequently seen in modern militaries. The Mesarthrim in particular use the Delta to great extent. The Delta is so popular that it is considered a top-of-the-line warship and most governments do not allow Deltas owned by Blazton in their space. Only Blazton Gunsniper corvettes are allowed at every port of call, and then only one at a time. The most famous Blazton design of all is the Whale, a heavy trans-atmospheric freighter. The Whale is so large that it can transport a city block into space, but is capable of a safe landing at most starport facilities. Whales are a very common sight in every port of call.


The Blazton Security Fleet is a small force of naval warships designed to protect Blazton Industries interests and installations throughout the Sirius Cluster and beyond. These 18 warships ordinarily operate independently, or in pairs at the most. The large capital warships are reserved for defense of key installations, such as Corporate Headquarters in the Berlusconi territory.

The Blazton Security Fleet is under the command of the Naval Security Oversight Committee, which oversees acquisition of new vessels, training of full-time crews, and negotiations with governments over the presence of BSF ships in sovereign territory. They also appoint a commanding officer for all naval defense forces.

Currently, command of the BSF is granted to Commodore Chetana Sharma, formerly of the United Earth Alliance Defense Force. After the fall of the UEA, he escaped to the Sirius district, where even the Empire would not pursue him.

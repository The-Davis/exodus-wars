'''Carla Diez'''
*Place of Birth: [[Glengarr]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Carla Diez'' is an ace VTOL operator. She's an [[Ixoarchada]] (civilian caste), and trained in the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] Defense Militia. Diez immigrated into the [[Prefecture]] for an opportunity to serve in the front-line military.

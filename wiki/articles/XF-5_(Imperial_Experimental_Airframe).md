The next test airframe was the XF-5, which served as a modular weapons test bed. Hardpoints and air-breathing missiles of yesteryear didn't work well on the F-4 in space, so they were not used at first. The XF-5 airframe had many problems, but the advancements made allowed the F-4A Raven to be equipped with four modular weapon mounts.

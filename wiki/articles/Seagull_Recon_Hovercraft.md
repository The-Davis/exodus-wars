*Speed: 365 KPH

===Overview===

In the midst of a massive reconstruction program following the Second Exodus War, the Kalidasa Planetary Authority generated a large number of jobs by designing and constructing its own defense forces.  The Seagull was actually designed prior to the establishment of the KPA, and with revisions, was adopted into the Kalidasa Security Force.

[[Image:SeagullReconHovercraft1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Seagull is not meant for combat, but simply for transport and reconaissance.  It does have a rack to store the weapon of the pilot, but it is otherwise unarmed. 

The Seagull is protected only by a thin layer of Boron-Nitride Composite armor.  The only other protection for the pilot is his own armored suit, as well as his flight harness, which locks him into the Seagull when at top speeds. 

===Deployment===

Seagulls operate all over Kalidasa, and not only in the KSF.  Lighter versions are common recreation craft on the planet.  The militarized version is slightly heavier and slower, but has ranged sensors, military-grade comm. gear, and a bit of armor, as well as advanced flight controls, allowing to perform more advanced maneuvers with less difficulty or danger.  The KSF uses its Seagulls for simple patrol, transport, recon, and other such duties where speed and mobility are key.  Seagulls can even operate as the Kalidasan equivalent of flight-capable infantry or infantry drop-pods, allowing single soldiers to be deployed extremely quickly to specific areas, without the need for a dropship. 

[[Image:SeagullReconHovercraft2.jpg]]

Although classified as simple hovercraft, Seagulls actually operate more like small VTOLs.  The military version has a maximum sustainable altitude of 1000 meters, or can hover only a few centimeters above the ground.

== Events of 17950BC ==

The [[Teeth of Orion]] have been a formidable obstacle to interstellar navigation since [[hyperspace]] was discovered.  The [[Ngrligru]] found their way into the [[Orion Arm]] by following the fleeing [[Anthronoris]], but raiding parties lacked the know-how to chart out return paths to the [[Sagittarius Arm]].  These raiding parties slowly died out as their genetic pool became more and more inbred, but small numbers survived for some time.

The farthest raiding groups perished in the distant [[Ularen System]].  One of the last major successful fleet actions of the [[Anthronoris]] took place over the planet [[Glengarr]], resulting in the Anthronoris warships pursuing the surviving Ngrligru forces coreward, never to return.

Set after "breaking Laojiu's back"

by James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     Admiral Hansen Dietrich of the Mesarthrim is no fool. The Myt'kampfen landing on Labrea are outnumbered 3 to 1, but his plans don't call for immediate direct confrontation. Under direct protest from the Myt'kampfen commanders, he orders fleet air wings to bomb Härdad into submission.
     The first bombing raids start at 0400 hours, local time. The first strikes are surgical in nature, taking out suspected ammunition sites, communications grids, and turning several main causeways into rubble. Only a few hundred Prefecture soldiers die in the strikes, and only 17 civilians working at a Prefecture base die.

     The second strike is more sinister. Mesarthrim bombers blot out the sky, unloading thousands of tons of payload onto the city. Civilians look up in horror, expecting their end to come, but are instead buried in a rain of paper.
     Millions of leaflets scatter across the city, written in English, Icronian, and the local Gaelic and Chinese dialects. "Surrender the city to the Mesarthrim, or be destroyed. Remember Leskovik. Join us, or join them."

'''The Otherness''' is a completely unknown, alien entity.  It controls and directs the [[Volucris Swarm]]. Oddly enough, the name was not given by humans.  The term "Otherness" was discovered during an examination and interrogation of a Volucris worker.


== The Interrogation ==

During the [[Volucris Incursion]], the forces of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] managed to capture over a hundred live Volucris specimens on [[Tavou]].  The [[Intelligence of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Central Office of Military Intelligence]] worked with many ex-[[Royal Imperial Labs]] biologists to decypher some of the Volucris language.  Results were mixed, but somehow they managed to make a connection with a particularly talkative worker.

While it was difficult to understand most of what the worker said, and difficult to make themselves understood by the worker, the interrotgators determined that Volucris workers recognize three biological directives that drive them to obey.  The workers learn from other workers in a sort of oral tradition, and part of those teachings say that one of the directives arose rather recently, some time in the past hundred years.  The worker called this directive "The Otherness."

Shortly after this information was discovered, the worker was able to break free of its restraints, and it killed itself.  Interrogation sessions with other workers were less useful, and interrogation sessions with soldiers were thoroughly useless.

== Possible Explanations ==

It is believed that several worker bodies house a single worker gestahlt consciousness.  Each worker brain seems to be too simple to direct the complex actions that workers have been observed performing.  Some of the things the interrogated worker said supported this belief.

If the Volucris have a gestahlt consciousness, perhaps The Otherness is a newer personality that directs the collective Swarms in a more militant direction.

While the origins of the Volucris are not fully understood, [[Mesarthrim]] lore surrounding the [[Anderung]] suggests that the Anderung and one other race were the result of genetic manufacturing.  Given that the Volucris survive in the vacuum of space and often seem to be more machine than biological, it is possible that the Volucris are yet another result of this mysterious creator race.  If so, The Otherness may be one of these creators controlling its creation from the shadows.

Another possibility is that The Otherness is a parasitic entity which has begun to control some aspects of the Volucris swarms, and is not part of their normal biological makeup.  This would explain the fear and awe the lower Volucris forms seem to have for it, but does not explain the Eviscerators.

== The Eviscerator Connection ==

Unlike worker drones, which speak their own language and communicate with at least four known forms of non-verbal communications, [[Eviscerator]]s have been observed speaking [[English]], [[Huorak]], [[Sothark]], and [[German]].  Eviscerator dialogue is often short and to the point, using an odd speech structure which does not meet any human grammar system, but is understandable.

During the incursion onto Tavou, an Eviscerator confronted [[Ellisaire Berok]] and [[Chonge Kodwe]] in a pitched street to street battle.  Despite the fact that the [[Ixoarchada]]n general was only on Tavou to observe and assess the extent of the Volucris threat, he gave a devastating account for himself in the fight.  After stalking the two warriors through the streets of [[Magrinoch]], the Eviscerator congratulated them on their prowess, but warned that The Otherness was not so easily impressed.  This is the only documented mention of The Otherness by an Eviscerator, but the difference between the Eviscerator's and the worker's attitude is significant.

The Clan Lord is the hereditary ruler of the Clan, and lead statesman for the Clan's territory.

Although not always the case, most of the Clans passed rule down to the first born male child.  Some passed it to the first born child, others it was decided when the child became older.  The Clan system was adopted by [[Otto Tischler]] for the [[Mesarthrim]] people to separate them from the Mesarnak.  If a Mesarnak escaped the [[Anderung]], they were adopted by the family that found and sheltered them.

All 50 of the Clan Families were German in origin. This crucial mistake did a fair share to alienate the English, French, and Spanish people. In many ways this oversight led to the eventual [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

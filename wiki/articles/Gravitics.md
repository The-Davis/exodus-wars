'''Gravitics''' is the study of and manipulation of gravity. Among [[Terrans]], the field was only theoretical until Dr. [[Erika Strout]] first developed gravity manipulation technology in [[2300]]. Very early models were inefficient and failure-prone. By [[2325]], gravity was used for shielding, weapons, and drive systems.

===Overview===

The Usurper is the oldest true capital warship in the UCS fleet.  The original design dates back to 2298, and was originally known as the "Type-9 Escort Carrier".

[[Image:UsurperClassCarrier1.jpg]]

The design was redesignated the Usurper class prior to the Second Exodus War, and was subsequently overhauled.

===Capabilities===

The Usurper is a light escort carrier, but it well armed enough to be a heavy frigate or light cruiser.  Four Type-R Heavy Blazers, the heavy anti-warship weapons of the Mesarthrim, make it a significant threat against warships.

Backing up the Type-Rs are seventeen Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannons of the Mesarthrim.  Additional firepower comes from eight VOTM tubes, giving it defense against fighters and also the capability to launch larger anti-warship payloads. 

[[Image:UsurperClassCarrier2.jpg]]

The Usurper carries six squadrons of fighters in standard configuration. 

The Usurper is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system and Leifstein Composite Armor.  The Usurper is also equipped with a Gravity Variance Drive, and this combined with its small size makes it quite fast.  

===Deployment===

The Usurper was in constant production since it was first created, and production only halted when the StarCorp Fleet Yards were destroyed in the Mesarthrim Civil War.  Since then, no more Usurper escort carriers have been produced.

[[Image:UsurperClassCarrier3.jpg]]

There are currently twelve Usurper carriers in the UCSN fleet.  They serve as the lead ships in strike groups, and many commanders praise them for their survivability, even in extremely harsh conditions.  They operate variably, from solo raiding operations with gunships to working all twelve together to provide aerospace support in fleet operations.

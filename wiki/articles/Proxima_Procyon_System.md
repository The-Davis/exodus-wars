The '''Proxima Procyon System''' is a star system with 6 planets and their moons. 

In order of their distances from Proxima Procyon, the planets are [[Cherkasy]], [[Donetsk]], [[Bethija]], [[Volisk]], [[Rivne]], and [[Lviv]]. Three of the planets are orbited by moons.


==Stellar Navigation==

Proxima Procyon is the Proxima Procyon System's star. Proxima Procyon is a G2V yellow dwarf, almost identical to Sol. Proxima Procyon orbits the binary Procyon A and Procyon B duo at 25,000AU, which keeps planets in Proxima Procyon's orbit a comfortable habitable zone.

Along with light, Proxima Procyon has a relatively weak heliosphere, which does not deflect cosmic rays as well as other stars in its class. This causes local navigation sensor anomalies, and many starship captains have steered around objects that weren't there.

The Proxima Procyon System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. The [[Sol System]], [[Alpha Centauri System]], and [[Tau Ceti System]] are considered close neighbors of Bethija.


=== Cherkasy ===

[[Cherkasy]] (0.05AU) is a tiny planet barely outside Proxima Procyon's stellar atmosphere. It is an irregular ball of rock, and will probably disintigrate or fall into Proxima Procyon in a few thousand years.

=== Donetsk ===

[[Donetsk]] (0.8AU) is a rocky ball rich with minerals used in manufacturing composite materials. It also has a thin nitrogen/oxygen atmosphere--too thin to breathe--and is an ideal candidate for terraforming. There are several permanent domed cities on the surface. It has one moon, [[Odessa]]

=== Bethija ===

[[Bethija]] (1.2AU) is the main colony world of the system. It is a balmy, moist world, with 85% of its surface covered by oceans. It has one moon, [[Kharkiv]]

=== Volisk ===

[[Volisk]] (2.5AU) is a small, [[Mars]]-like world. It is very cold, but has a relatively thick carbon-dioxide atmosphere and some plant life. It has frozen water oceans, but huge resevoirs of liquid water under the surface. Volyn has no moons.

=== Rivne ===

[[Rivne]] (6.1 AU) is a large gas giant, approximately the size of [[Saturn]]. Rivne has an unusual double ring system, covering its equator and its poles. Rivne has 78 moons.

=== Lviv ===

[[Lviv]] (7.3 AU) is an average size gas giant, a little smaller than Rivne. It has a thick methane atmosphere with a lot of slushy water ice in the atmosphere. Lviv has 12 moons, and is home to the [[Antonov Company]]'s extreme environment testing centers.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

[[Bethija]] was colonized in [[2027]], using funds and colonists from the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] region known as the Ukraine. After [[World War III]], the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] joined the [[United Earth Alliance]], and the regional economy in the Ukraine collapsed. With no opportunity on [[Terra]], many opportunists raised the funds needed for a colony project. Nearly 30 million Ukrainians were moved to [[Bethija]] over the years. 

[[Bethija]] was a lawless colony for many years, with socialist and democratic elements vying for control of the colony. Eventually the [[United Earth Alliance]] took over the colony and stabilized the planet. [[Bethija]] became a prosperous world, trading with the [[Alpha Centauri]] colony and establishing a robust interstellar economy.

During the [[Second Exodus War]], [[Bethija]] defected to the [[Empire]] without a fight after hearing how well the people of [[Ah Kin]] were treated. After the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] invaded [[Bethija]] in the late years of the [[Second Exodus War]], they gave the planet independence. [[Bethija]] eventually joined the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

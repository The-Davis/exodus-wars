*Power Plant: Type-250XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 85 KPH
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver

===Overview===

Since almost any species isn't immune to accidents or the effects of aging, the need for a way to transports those who need care has always been present. The Ultramedic is just the latest in a long line of ambulance models that have helped to serve society in the carrying of ill people.

[[Image:UltramedicAmbulance1.jpg]]

Rahn Industries decided to get into the ambulance market when the idea of a cheap, low maintenance, anti-grav transport was proposed by R&D for industrial use. However, when the board was discussing it, the decision to transform the vehicle into an effective means of carrying patients was put into motion.

===Capabilities===

Most of the Ultramedics space is taken up by a state of the art diagnostic system for the human body that includes a number of different monitors and gages. All of this, through miniaturization, has been able to be fitted inside a rather small space and it actually allows for more wiggle room then usually imagined.

[[Image:UltramedicAmbulance2.jpg]]

The Ultramedic has enough room for a driver and two paramedics who can use the onboard storage space to carry almost any kind of equipment they need to save someone's life. This means that between the diagnostic and lifesaving system, most patients are stabilized before they hit the hospital, greatly increasing survival odds.

===Deployment===

The phasing in of the Ultramedic into general use has been slowly but surly building since its introduction to the market and sales seem to be climbing steadily. Most ambulance departments are looking into upgrading their fleets and have seen that the Ultramedic offers the best bang for the buck.

'''Joanna Burns'''
*Place of Birth: [[BG-1 Jerome Black]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Joanna Burns'' is the Executive Officer of the FDS ''Jerome Black'', and is one of the many people born on the long trip home from exile.  Her parents, grandparents and great-grandparents each helped guide the fleet to [[Alpha Centauri]].  She received her post as the tactical officer when the HMS ''Jerome Black'' arrived, and was eventually promoted up to the rank of executive officer.

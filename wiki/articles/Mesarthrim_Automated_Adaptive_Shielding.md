'''Automated Adaptive Shielding''' is the primary, multi-role defense system of the [[Mesarthrim]].  Adaptive Shielding is a highly sophisticated variant of basic [[Terran Gradient Gravity Shielding|Gravity Shields]].  Like other gravity shielding systems, Adaptive Shielding uses steep gravitational gradients to deflect incoming projectiles and energy beams and also to destroy missiles. 

Unlike normal gravity shields, Adaptive Shielding can be tuned for maximum effectiveness by varying the strength and spacing of the gravity gradients.  Although this sounds simple enough, it is a highly complex operation.  Retuning and recalibrating on most Terran warships can take hours.  Thanks to the technology developed by the [[Anderung]] in their own shielding systems, Mesarthrim shielding can re-tune its gradients in seconds, and constantly does so as it monitors real-time battle information.  

Rapid retuning allows the shield's tactical computers to select the best defense against certain weapon types.  Against plasma weapons, it is best to diffuse and spread out the attack with several shallow gradients; against faster particle beams, deflection with a single abrupt gradient is the best strategy; against missiles, several sharp gravitational gradients are required to rip the missile apart.  All of these require different configurations of the shield's gravity gradients.  

Additional benefits of using the Anderung systems as a base include the ability to manipulate more gradients, control their configurations to a much finer degree, and most importantly, the ability for the shield projectors themselves to dissipate the energy of incoming weapons' fire much more effectively.

In combat, Automated Adaptive Shielding is constantly adapting and retuning against incoming weapons fire.  The benefits of retuning can yield an increase in protection capabilities of up to a 50% against a single weapon type.  However, such increased performance can be nullified by using a wide variety of weaponry.

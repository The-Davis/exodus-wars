== Events of 671AD ==

Non-stop work on the [[Nechanos Tolesca]] finally yielded results in the later part of the year when the machine comes to life for the first time since the [[Sundering]].  However, without a proper receiving station, the machine actually does nothing.  [[Ixoarchada]] scientists set to work on building another identical device in the [[Ularen System]], designed as a perfect copy of the original.

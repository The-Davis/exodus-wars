'''Young-Watson Armory''' is the main contractors for the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s military. They offer a wide range of Graser, Xaser, and Maser-based weapons, in particular the main weaponry for warships. 

== Product Line ==

Young-Watson's product line focuses mostly on automated and manually-controlled turret weapons for warships, vehicles, and aircraft, but they have a few designs fielded by infantry.

=== Grasers ===

The Graser is the largest and most powerful weapon developed by Young-Watson. It is a weapon designed around ''Gamma Ray Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation'', causing a highly focused beam of Gamma Rays. The result is a deadly weapon at long range, capable of overwhelming shields and armor.

* YG Series: Warship weapons.

=== Xasers ===

The Xaser is a smaller, but complex and deadly weapon. It is an X-Ray Laser weapon, capable of rapid fire and low energy requirements. It is not very effective at long range, and larger ships often have the armor to shrug off its effects. It is usually used for point defense or for fighter armament.

* XR Series: Warship defense weapons.

Set after the fall of Hardad.

Taming the Fathach Méire - 5/22/2325 - By James Davis

=====Summary=====

     Mountain combat has been tricky throughout the ages of warfare. Even modern aircraft, artillery, armor and shield technology does not truly eliminate the advantage of height and ground cover. Today the Mesarthrim intend to force the Prefecture's Laojiu Guard out of the Fathach Méire, the sole mountain range in Labrea's southern continent, Marisgnách.
    North of the mountains lie long, rolling flatlands with thousands of rice patties and small villages of farmers, silos for storage, and landing sites for food convoys. The Mesarthrim ignore these, as they are one of the targets they want to capture intact.
     The southern foothills of the Fathach Méire is home to the base/city of Tara'dar Coirp, where William Parrish commands the planetary defense. The bulk of the remaining Prefecture Air Force elements on Labrea are housed in hidden, reinforced underground hangars, with no more than a dozen fighters to a location. A huge superhighway leads from Tara'dar Coirp to Laidebaile.
     Unlike Härdad, Tara'dar Coirp has too much strategic value as a transportation hub from the farmlands in the north to Laidebaile for the Mesarthrim to simply bomb it into dust. The Prefecture fortifications are low tech, relying on the natural toughness of the mountain range to make them most effective. Powered armor suits like Grendels are used, but almost entirely at Tara'dar Coirp itself. The troops in the mountains have no high power sources, and are almost completely untraceable by anything short of line-of-sight short range scans.
     General Nikolaus Adler has the unenviable job of taking this deathtrap with only 100,000 Myt'kampfen at his disposal. The bulk of the Myt'kampfen, some 65,000, approach from the south, keeping Laidebaile cut off from Tara'dar Coirp, while 35,000 Myt'kampfen reinforced by 5000 Myt'herra under the command of Colonel Fritz Dresdner approach from the north after picking through the remains of Härdad.

     The first waves of the attack are led by Myt'herra on foot, driving up into the northern foothills and slopes of the Fathach Méire. Dozens of skirmishes between the Prefecture and the Mesarthrim are fought, and hundreds of men are lost on both sides.
     By dark, the Mesarthrim have found sixteen Prefecture bunkers, tunnel entrances, machine gun nests and sniper posts, and only managed to destroy or rout nine of them.

=====North of the Fathach Méire Range, 0300 Hours=====
     Colonel Fritz Dresdner pops another battlestimulant and glances back at his maps. The command center around him is quiet. All the preparations have been made; all the pieces are on the board. All that remains is to give the order to advance.
     His eyes trace the contours of the holographic chart one final time. There: the Big Claw, the key to the Prefecture defenses. Ten thousand feet high, and every inch swarming with Prefecture troops. From that position, they can call down airstrikes on armored columns moving up through the mountains...and our fighters can't root their VTOLs out of that terrain.
     "Are you sure you don't want to take another day to mop up the remaining fortifications, sir?" Dresdner's chief of staff, Major Rabenstrage, has been with him since the days of the great Mesarthrim uprising against the HalbÄnderung. He is a conservative tactician, and more than once his advice has saved Dresdner's command.
     Not this time. "Major, the longer we hold back our advance to combat these Prefecture delaying tactics, the more thoroughly entrenched their troops in the mountains will be. We fight this battle as Myt'kampfen: with speed and strength, not caution."
     Dresdner gestures at his map. "There are still seven Prefecture strongpoints along our front or in the rear areas. We'll envelope them and leave them for later. This is storm warfare, my friend, and I do not think these Guard troops are fully prepared for what we have in store for them."
     Of course, Dresdner thinks, Brigadier Parrish's troops more than make up for in ferocity what they lack in...finesse. I never expected to see a Guard unit as bloodthirsty as these Laojiu roughnecks.
     No matter.
     His eyes swing up to the second-tallest peak in the range, a mere handful of kilometers from the Big Claw. "The Little Claw, the locals call it. The Prefecture has a heavy garrison there as well, but if we approach from the west, up this pass, the mountain itself will protect us from fire coming off the Big Claw. We storm the Little Claw and contest their control of the pass between the two peaks."
     Rabenstrage is more than familiar with the battle plan, but he gestures for Dresdner to continue. The older man frowns as his eyes drift back to the Big Claw. "And then we assault their fortifications on the Big Claw. We'll position mortars on the Little Claw and lay down a curtain barrage to cover our infantry's advance, but that fight will not be the province of generals. It will be up to the men on the ground, the lieutenants and sergeants."
     "The advance will be costly."
     "I am well aware," Dresdner says dryly. "We can only hope that the bulk of the cost falls on the Prefecture." 
     Both men's eyes swing around to the theater clock displayed in one corner of the tactical board.
     0305, it reads.
     "Major," Dresdner says, striding around the table.
     "Sir?"
     "Give the order."

=====The Fathach Méire Range, 0320 Hours=====
     Hundreds of armored fighting vehicles roar to life as the order to advance is passed down the UCS chain of command. Vehicle-mounted speakers boom martial music as troops rush to mount up.
     There are four passes through the Fathach Méire range, and the only one large enough to support a significant troop movement passes right between the Big and Little Claw peaks. It is a killzone on a spectacular level, but it must be taken.
     Colonel Dresdner doesn't want his troops falling into that trap. The advance will be carried out by dismounted infantry, sweeping the woods and establishing a perimeter around the Big Claw. The armored thrust will take the eastern-most pass, what the locals call Switchback Road, and hit the Little Claw from the east. 
     The first shots fired in anger come from the Mesarthrim artillery. Railguns elevate and track, then thunder a massive time-on-target barrage. The fire continues even as the first shells splash down, throwing hundreds of rounds into the air. Fighters shriek in from the north, launching cruise missiles, but veer off quickly before they enter the range of Prefecture shoulder-launched missiles.
     The Big Claw seems to explode as fire washes its flanks. The Myt'kampfen, riding north, watch with hooded eyes as massive explosions scour the forest, shredding acres at a time.

=====The Big Claw, 0350 Hours=====
     "INCOMIIIIING!"
     The shouted alarm jerks William Parrish from his sleep. He rolls off his cot and snatches up the radio handset. "Report!"
     "Ah, Lieutenant Mattingly, Charlie Company, Fourth Battalion. We've got heavy enemy artillery and missile strikes coming down all over the mountain, sir. It's getting pretty hot, over."
     "Understood, Lieutenant. You hold your position and tell me what's going on, all right?  You're my eyes and ears, and I'm counting on you."
     "Understood, sir, the fortifications seem to be holding."
     "And remember: you do not, under any circumstance, activate a shield generator. That'll give your position away, and they'll pound you into the ground."
     "Understood. Charlie-Four-Actual clear."
     Parrish has a primary command center back in Tara'dar Coirp, but it's too far from the front and lacks a secure landline for communication, so he has moved his command to the mountains to oversee the defense himself. He pulls on his uniform and pads up to his Tactical Operations Center, buried in a cave complex deep inside the mountain. The tactical maps there are covered in angry red indicators, marking the projected impact points of Mesarthrim artillery.
     "How are our losses?"
     "We've lost contact with one hardpoint - it looks like they took a direct penetrator hit." The Captain watching the displays shakes his head. "The infirmaries are being swarmed with wounded, and - Jesus Christ!"
     The entire Big Claw seems to shake. A cluster of icons marking the position of friendly units flashes dull gray and blinks out: an entire company of infantry, scattered across more than four square kilometers of mountainside, wiped out in an instant.
     "Fuel-air bomb," Parrish says grimly. "Seal the tunnels. Post men at the gates to open them if we need to bring wounded in."
     "God bless the engineers, sir," the Captain whispers. "If we hadn't had these fortifications ready-"
     "Then we'd be hurting sorely," Parrish drawls. "But the fortifications were ready in time. Engineers did their part - now it's our turn."
     He strides past the Captain and glances over the artillery board. "I wish we had some capability for counterfire."
     The Laojiu Guard entrenched on the Big Claw have nothing more than mortars; the howitzers and missile launchers were too large and unwieldy to bring up into the mountains. Mortars have plenty of punch, but their range is comparatively shorter, so there's no way they can attack the Mesarthrim artillery and silence them.
     On the other hand, they have a hell of a lot of mortars, including a battery of 155mm on the peak of the Little Claw.
     "Get me all the recordings of the Canopus engagement," Parrish orders. "Put them on my displays here. I want to watch them one more time."
     Everyone in the TOC stares at him for a moment as he takes a seat, leans back, and props his legs up on the artillery board. Parrish scratches idly at the side of his head, full aware that the example he's setting will help keep his men calm.
     Strangely, he feels perfectly at peace. Outnumbered and outgunned, holed up in a desperate stand against a well-trained and determined foe? I was born for this fight.

=====Tara'dar Coirp, 0410 Hours=====
     "They're moving! The Mesarthrim are coming up the mountain!"
     All across Tara'dar Coirp, off-duty Guard units rush to the nearest displays to watch the grainy footage transmitted back from the Big Claw. When available computers, televisions, and tactical boards run out, soldiers grab binoculars and find high points to watch the mountain. They are not disappointed: a corona of fire seems to wreath the peak as the Mesarthrim bombardment continues.
     The city has plenty of danger of its own to worry about. A strong Mesarthrim force under General Nikolaus Adler is approaching Tara'dar Coirp from the south, cutting it of from Laideballe. But, for the moment, all eyes are on the Big Claw, where the Guard's beloved Colonel and a vanguard 150,000 strong prepare to meet the Myt'kampfen advance.

=====Forest, North of the Big Claw, 0455 Hours=====
     The Myt'herra take the lead in clearing the woods to the north of the Big Claw. They meet their first resistance at the base of the peak. Gauss rounds snap through woods, blowing trees into splinters, as Prefecture machine gun nests open fire. The Myt'herra dive for cover. Snipers on both sides take aim and fire. A Myt'herra lieutenant crouches behind a boulder and beckons his radioman over.
     "DivArty, Echo-Six-Actual, smoke mission by designator, grid four-niner, over!"
     "Acknowledged, Echo-Six. Shot, over."
     A salvo of artillery shells screams down onto the woods, laying down a thick blanket of chemical smoke. With a blood-chilling war cry, the Myt'herra charge, trees bursting around them as they bound upslope. Warrior after warrior falls, their armor pierced by high-caliber rounds. Breachers blow gaping craters in the woods as the Prefecture soldiers in the bunkers fire blindly.
     The Myt'herra reach the base of a cliff. Laojiu Guard riflemen fire down on them, but the machine guns can't depress enough to engage. Snipers engage the Prefecture troops from downslope as the Myt'herra under the cliff deploy ropes and begin to scale upwards.
     Grenades bounce into the machine-gun nests. Prefecture soldiers scramble for cover or try, futilely, to throw the grenades back, but the Myt'herra have cut the fuses short. Fire belches from the firing ports as the Mesarthrim reach the top of the cliff, snapping their rifles up and mercilessly picking off retreating soldiers.
     "TOC, this is Echo-Six-Actual, we've captured the first line of fortifications, over."
     "Acknowledged, Echo-Six. Proceed with advance."

=====Mesarthrim Armored Column, Switchback Pass, East of the Little Claw, 0600 Hours=====
     Colonel Mareike Aloisia hasn't had much time for rest since Canopus. She's been put in command of the column of tanks headed up the east side of the Little Claw, the smaller peak to the east of the Big Claw. The switchback pass is crowded with massive fighting vehicles; to the left is forest, to the right, nothing but a sharp drop four thousand feet to the woods below.
     "Seems as if we always get the tough jobs, eh, Colonel?" her driver jokes, throwing the Vulture into another sharp turn. The tank's treads grind over a fallen tree on the road. "First a city, now a mountain. What I'd do to be fighting on the rice plains back north."
     "Eyes front, Dieter," Aloisia chides.
     "Delta-One-Actual, Delta-Niner."
     Aloisia bends over her command set. "Delta-One-Actual acknowledges."
     "Colonel, they've mined the pass. We've got to bring up engineers."
     The Colonel looks over her maps. The report from the infantry outrunners, a kilometer up the pass, has added a crosshatched red pattern to the map, blocking the last leg of the advance.
     Shit. "Understood, Delta-Niner. Set up a perimeter."
     She changes frequencies. "TOC, this is Colonel Aloisia. We need one of the on-call engineer companies up here ASAP."
     "TOC acknowledges. Dropships are lifting now."
     "INCOMING!" someone shouts. Acting on reflex, Aloisia slams her Vulture's hatch shut and glances at one of the external feeds. Little Claw's peak flares with sudden light - and the tank's sensors scream as mortar rounds begin to drop on her column. Myt'kampfen soldiers riding on the vehicles' flanks scatter into the woods.
     The first barrage is targeted on the lead armor platoons. The platoon commander has time to click on his radio before forty rounds slam into his Vulture and blow it to pieces. More rounds fall, and Mesarthrim shields sputter and fail. Even the ungodly tough Vultures cannot take this kind of punishment for long, but on the tight pass, surrounded by woods, there is nowhere to go. "TOC," Aloisia radios, her voice still cool, "we are under heavy fire from mortars on the peak of Little Claw. I need air support."
     "Acknowledged, stand by."

=====Prefecture TOC, Beneath Big Claw Peak, 0610 Hours=====
     Brigadier Parrish stands, hands clasped behind his back, and watches. The Mesarthrim have breached the first perimeter at the base of the mountain, but there are four more successively heavier perimeters farther up the slope. And the Mesarthrim column coming up the backside of the Little Claw is well and truly suppressed.
     "Things are looking good," he murmurs.
     "TOC," one of the forward observers reports, "we've got air contacts."
     Parrish nods grimly as a mass of new contacts appears on the tactical map. "Get our own fighters and bombers off the ground. Alert our ground-based SAM units that they've got incoming."

=====Over the Fathach Méire, 0625 Hours=====
     "Bandits, multiple bandits at nine o'clock! Prefecture fighters inbound"
     "I see them. Squadron, break and finger four. Stand by to engage."
     Shoulder-launched SAMs burst from the forest and shriek after the Mesarthrim fighters. The disciplined pilots hold their squadrons together, barely, as Prefecture Piranhas and Stukas thunder towards them from the direction of Tara'dar Coirp. Both sides salvo their missiles, but guidance is difficult with so much ground clutter.
     What develops is a massive dogfight at less than a thousand feet. Fighters traveling at barely subsonic speeds dance and die around the Claw peaks. The UCS' advantage in numbers and technology is more than offset by the massive numbers of SAMs fired by Prefecture troops, and the night burns with long streams of blazer and autocannon fire, exploding missiles and dying aircraft.
     In the midst of the chaos, UCS bombers begin their runs on the Little Claw mortar positions. But a squadron of Piranhas punches through the fighter screen and descends on the bombers. The behemoth craft are well-defended, but they can't line up their ordinance while under fire - especially not with flak and SAMs coming at them from both the Big and Little Claws.
     "TOC," the squadron leader radios, "it's too hot out here. We've got to break off."

=====Approaching Second Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 0700 Hours=====
     Pegasus dropships thunder overhead as the Myt'herra continue their relentless charge upslope. Pockets of Prefecture resistance, sniper ambushes and claymore traps, inflict light casualties, but the Myt'herra are skilled in woodcraft and spot most of the attacks in time to counter them. The mountain's steep slope is their greatest obstacle, but these Myt'herra are trained in mountain warfare on the far more difficult peaks of Mesar itself. To them, this is merely a foothill, barely worthy of comment.
     When they reach the second rally point, they find a battalion of Myt'kampfen disembarking from their dropships. Mesarthrim fighters shriek low overhead, hugging the forest. Captain Graffin, commander of the Myt'herra Alpha Company, beckons his men forward into the clearing.
     A Dolch scout car bounces downhill to meet them. Captain Graffin salutes. "Major Weber, sir, we've secured the first defensive perimeter! There are scattered machine-gun nests remaining, but the primary route up is clear."
     "Colonel Dresdner informed me. Excellent work, Captain." Weber glances past Graffin, looking over the armored Myt'herra, many wounded, all showing the marks of heavy combat. "Your men can rest here, Graffin. My battalion can handle the next perimeter."
     "Sir, with all due respect, we are eager to continue!" Graffin barks, remaining at attention.
     Weber nods slowly. "All right, Captain. Take the lead. We'll give you flank security."
     At that moment, a Patton fighter growls overhead, trailing a long streamer of smoke. It banks away to the north, its engine whining unhappily.
     Graffin's radioman runs up and salutes. "Sir, TOC reports that our fighters are taking heavy ground fire. They need us to move upslope and clear things out."
     The Captain nods and turns back to Weber. "I'll leave my wounded here if you'll set up an aid station."
     "Of course, Captain."

=====Mesarthrim Armored Column, Switchback Pass, East of the Little Claw, 0725 Hours=====
     "Where the hell is that air support!?" Colonel Aloisia screams. Her Vulture is gone, abandoned just moments before it was consumed by the mortar barrage, and now she is huddled beneath a boulder, yelling into a Myt'kampfen's radio. "My column has been shot to pieces! We don't have a single operational vehicle left!"
     The Prefecture mortar barrage first picked off the lead elements, then focused on the rear of the column. With mines ahead and wrecked tanks blocking the switchback pass behind, there is nowhere for the column to go. Several times, Aloisia has heard approaching fighters, but the mortar fire from the top of the Little Claw continues unabated.
     "Ah, stand by, Colonel, they're encountering heavy ground fire."
     "We're taking heavy ground fire!" Aloisia switches frequencies, cutting the Tactical Operations Center off. Although Dresdner has seniority to her, he's nominally the same rank - maybe he'll listen. "Colonel Dresdner, this is Delta-One-Actual, I cannot take this mountain without air or artillery support, over!"
     The radio is silent for a moment. "I understand, Colonel, but our division artillery is busy suppressing the defensive perimeters on the Big Claw. We cannot slacken our fire or the infantry will lose their momentum. Is that understood?"
     "Negative, Colonel, the infantry will lose their fucking momentum when they try to storm Big Claw and find that the mortars over here are still operational! I don't care how many aircraft you lose, those mortars have to be silenced!"
     There is another short silence. "Do you have any mortar tracks remaining?"
     "We have no armor remaining!"
     "I'm sending in the Sixth Battalion of the One-Fifty-Fifth. They'll move their mortar platoons' tracks up the switchback pass to a position where they can provide counterfire on the Prefecture positions."
     Another salvo of mortar shells crashes down nearby, the shockwave blasting the radio from the soldier's hands. Aloisia leans in closer. "Colonel, our mortars cannot provide accurate fire up the fucking mountain! The Laojiu Guard are shooting down on us! They can call in fire missions and airstrikes at their leisure!"
     "Do you have a better solution, Colonel? I cannot spare any divisional artillery to support you, and our aircraft are heavily engaged. The 155th's mortar platoon is your only option."
     "Colonel, what about an orbital bombardment!?"
     Dresdner sighs, a hint of dark humor in his voice. "I was afraid you'd say that. Can you hold while a cruiser positions for a strike?"
     "We don't have much choice, do we?" Another salvo of mortars blows apart a cluster of trees to the north. "Tell them to get their ass in gear!"
     Dieter was right, Aloisia thinks, ducking further beneath the boulder. Why is it that it's my command that always gets shot to pieces?

=====Second Defense Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 0740 Hours=====
     In the Laojiu Guard TOC, Brigadier Parrish watches his displays as a fog of crimson approaches the second defense perimeter. "Hold steady, ladies and gentlemen," he whispers. "Here they come."
     The Myt'herra assault on the second perimeter is preceded by a massive artillery strike. ICM cluster rounds pummel the entrenched Prefecture troops, but few are killed. The engineers did a good job setting up the defenses, and no mere bombardment is going to break them.
     The second perimeter is far stronger than the first, overlooking a killing field strewn with concertina wire and mines. This is not a task that a mere 5,000 Myt'herra can handle. But dropships have been flying in nonstop, and troops have moved up from the staging areas by APC. Thirty thousand Myt'kampfen stand ready for the attack, nearly two brigades, with another three battalions held in reserve.
     Maneuver warfare is about surfaces and gaps, and the Mesarthrim are expert at finding the gaps. The first attacks are probes, single battalions moving upslope under heavy fire, searching for weak points. Within half an hour, Colonel Dresdner has an accurate idea of weak points in the Prefecture defenses - at the cost of nearly a thousand men. 
     Then the main assault begins.
     It is catastrophic: not because it is doomed from the start, or even because of any particular tactics on the Prefecture's part. It is simply a full-out attack on an entrenched enemy, uphill and through minefields. There is no way it could not be catastrophic.
     Mortars crash among the advancing Mesarthrim, smashing APCs and laying down a thick blanket of smoke. The Myt'kampfen are well-dispersed, so casualties are relatively light. Engineer battalions lead the way, guarded by Captain Graffin's Myt'herra and a curtain barrage from the divisional howitzers. They work with patient care even as bullets snarl overhead, cutting through concertina wire, slicing paths through minefields (a task made much easier by the artillery support moving ahead of them.)
     By 0850 hours, a path is open through the defenses. The lead engineers can see the looming bulk of the Prefecture fortifications among the trees - when they dare peer out, risking sudden death by sniper.
     The engineer battalion's commanding Major finally founds a working 'radio' - in actuality a compact microwave transmitter. "TOC," he shouts, over the snap-crack of blazer fire. "This is Golf-One-Actual. We've cleared three lanes through the defenses."
     "Acknowledged, Golf-One. Hold position. We're commencing the advance. Be aware, we have received a call for Foxtrot-Alpha-Echo fire from DivArty to suppress the Prefecture defenses."
     It takes the Major a moment to connect the acronym: Fuel Air Explosive, the next best thing to a tactical nuke. He hastily changes channels. "Battalion, this is Golf One Actual, find some cover! Now! Now! Now!"

=====Above Labrea, 0800 Hours=====
     The Phalanx II cruiser Danzerig has spent the past hour thrusting out-system, turning, and lining up on Labrea's position. Weapons crews have loaded the ship's missile tubes with (barely) subnuclear bombardment warheads. These are battlefield-support weapons, their guidance electronics larger than their warheads. Orbital strikes are difficult and dangerous.
     The Danzerig's drives pulse to full power as it begins its run towards the planet.

=====Second Defense Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 0800 Hours=====
     Silently, tirelessly, ruthlessly, the Mesarthrim advance.
     Mortar fire continues to burst among them as they bound up-slope, the Myt'kampfen scrambling from cover to cover, pausing to squeeze off blazer shots.
     But it is the Myt'herra who lead the way, bounding in impossible arcs, their power armor granting them inhuman grace and speed.
     The Laojiu Guard soldiers on the second perimeter learn to fear them. "Where the hell is that fire coming from?" a Guard private shouts, ducking away from his firing port as another blazer bolt scars the concrete outside."I can't get a target!"
     "Myt'herra!" the nearest sergeant shouts, voice raw.This platoon is charged with holding a machine-gun nest in a thin depression that seems to have become the focus of the Mesarthrim assault."Two kilometer effective range on those blazers!"
     "We need more mortar fire!" Lieutenant Czibor calls, watching the armored figures approaching through the smoke and screaming tracers. "Where's the transmitter?"
     But the radioman is dead, his microwave transmitter equipment ruined by the same blazer bolt that killed him. Nor will their short-range squad transmitters work: Mesarthrim Sherman fighters are overhead, and such weak signals won't punch through the jamming.
     "Keep up the fire!" Czibor shouts, unnecessarily.A man screams and falls back from his firing port, a hole right through his shoulder. Czibor grabs his rifle and looks out. His helmet paints dozens of targets: Myt'herra coming through the cleared lanes in the minefield, dashing from crater to crater.
     The Lieutenant picks a target and opens fire.

=====Mesarthrim TOC, 0820 Hours=====
     Colonel Dresdner scowls at his tactical displays. The assault on the second perimeter is beginning to bog down. Losses have skyrocketed as his lightly armored Myt'kampfen bottleneck in the minefields and are shredded by mortar fire off the Big Claw. Only the Myt'herra manage to continue the advance.
     "We need counterbattery fire on those mortars!" The voice of the distant Captain Graffin is professional but strained."We've got a beachhead but they're interdicting the lanes behind us, and we can't move reinforcements up!"
     "We can't spare any artillery from the curtain barrage," Dresdner snaps. "If we lighten that fire the Prefecture defenses will tear you apart."
     "Then get air support in here! Gunships and armor! It's that or we have to fall back!"
     "I can do one better," Dresdner snaps, wheeling towards his local Air Boss. "Get the ELITES in the air."

=====Prefecture Mortar Positions, Little Claw Peak, 0830 Hours=====
     There are twenty-three 155mm mortar positions set up on Little Claw Peak, guarded by a full brigade of Prefecture infantry in entrenched positions.The mortar crews have been busy for the past several hours, and they've nearly exhausted their ammunition firing both at the Mesarthrim column on the east side of the mountain and the infantry assaulting the Big Claw.But Skyhawks fly in additional ammunition, skimming low over the trees.
     With a commanding field of fire and plenty of infantry support, the mortar crews' position is essentially secure.
     None of them have a chance.
     There are sensor systems to detect airborne threats, but the dogfight still swirling over the twin mountains has them fairly well occupied. By the time the first alarm sounds in Brigadier Parrish's TOC, it's far, far too late.
     "Hotel One-Niner, Hotel One-Niner, vampire, vampire!" Lieutenant Colonel Kearns' radio screams. "Orbital strike on the way!"
     "Aw, shit."
     Kearns looks up in time to see the flare of light as the missiles, still kilometers away, begin dumping submunitions. The brigade's air-defense unit engages, firing countermissiles skyward, but the effort is futile: the salvo is seeded with decoys and moving at a colossal speed.
     "Colonel, it's been an honor."
     The blasts of the arriving missiles kill hundred of Prefecture troops, including Lieutenant Colonel Kearns, but the bulk of the entrenched defenders survive. The mortar positions, however, are totally demolished.

=====Survivors of Mesarthrim Armored Column, Little Claw Peak, 0835 Hours=====

Now, holding a rifle, she accompanies the Myt'kampfen as they make their way through the woods, up the steep slope of the Little Claw, and into the teeth of the Prefecture defenses. This assault was intended to be carried out by armor...but the Vulture siege tanks are gone.
     Scouts move forward to check the Prefecture positions, then return. The fortifications on the Little Claw are more hasty than those on the mountain's larger neighbor, they report: earthworks, primarily, supplemented by some claymores.
     "Advance to contact," Aloisia orders her battalion commanders. "I'm going to set up a field command post here." She saved her command electronics from the Vulture, giving her some limited coordination ability.
     The Myt'kampfen spread into a front two battalions wide, then advance through the woods. The attack is opened by sniper fire and a barrage of grenades. Squad gunners lay down a withering hail of suppressing fire on the entrenched Guard troops.
     Then the Myt'kampfen advance.

=====Second Defense Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 0910 Hours=====
     A flight of Pegasus dropships roar low over the woods, their adaptive shields ready to fend off ground-launched SAMs. The precaution is wise: several ships are hit, but none seriously.
     The dropships pull into a sharp left-hand bank over the second perimeter's killzone. Man-shapes leap from their open troop bays, dropping on tongues of fire. The ELITE armor units come down firing, surrounded by flaring nimbuses of light as their shield generators activate. Rifle fire richochets harmlessly off the armor suits.
     Captain von Steuben, Myt'herra, leads the ELITE company. His armor's HUD is covered in a torrent of information, incomprehensible to the untrained eye. He speaks terse syllables, designating targets for his squads. The descending suits vomit a volley of grenades and rockets, smashing machine-gun nests and scattering armored infantrymen. Only the hardened Prefecture bunkers survive the volley.
     Steuben's suit hits the ground; he crouches to absorb the impact, then straightens and leads his company forward, traveling at an even three-meter lope. They fire at anything their suit sensors highlight as a target, killing dozens of Prefecture infantrymen with direct blazer shots or volleys of grenades.
     "Threats left!"

Laojiu Guard soldiers combine breacher and armor-piercing fire from their rifles to down a few armor suits, and anti-tank SRMs claim more. But there is nothing on the field that can stop the UCS battle armor: they are too fast to be caught in the mortar barrage, too lethal to be effectively targeted by man-portable SRMs, too tough for rifle and machine gun fire.

=====Little Claw Peak, 0930 Hours=====
     "Fall back! Fall back! They're coming in through the second trenchline!"
     The Laojiu Guard are fierce. The Myt'kampfen are fiercer - and better-equipped, and far more experienced. Despite their vast numerical inferiority, despite the loss of their armor, Colonel Aloisia's brigade slices right through the defenses on Little Claw Peak. The fighting is close-ranged and brutal; a moment's hesitation can make the difference between life and death.
     The Mesarthrim own that moment, and it gives them the edge they need.
     For fifteen minutes the Guard troops fall back under the constant Mesarthrim assault, barely maintaining order. Reinforcements arrive, along with air support in the form of Skyhawk gunships. But Colonel Aloisa has SAM traps waiting, and a squadron of interceptors swoops down from the dogfight above to help chase the gunships away.
     The Prefecture troops grow weary, mentally and physically. They don't have the pharmacological cornucopia available to regular Legions, and combat fatigue takes its toll. The Mesarthrim, on the other hand, don't stop coming, not even when whole squads are wiped out by claymore traps or caught in ambushes. Again and again, the ferocious battle cry of the Myt'kampfen rings out over the sounds of combat.
     The Skyhawks return half an hour later, not to deliver additional reinforcements, but to extract the routed survivors of the Guard's Seventh Brigade.

=====Second Defense Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 1020 Hours=====
     With the arrival of the ELITES, only a single pocket of Prefecture hardpoints remains on the second perimeter. The blazer fire comes fast and hard now, and the mortar fire has halved with the destruction of the Little Claw launchers.
     The Prefecture defenders badly need some help - but the battlefield is covered in smoke, and the transmitters on the standard-issue airstrike beacons have the same problem as the squad radios: they can't punch through the Shermans' jamming.
     "I'm going to get over to the next nest, find their OTP transmitter, and call in some air support! Cover me!" Lieutenant Czibor shout, then throws the nest's hatch open. The platoon leans against their firing ports, searching for targets. Blazer bolts sear past.
     The Lieutenant vaults off the top of the nest, hitting the ground and sprinting low. He is halfway across the gap, running behind a spool of concertina wire, when a blazer bolt blows his left leg off just below the knee.
     "Medic!" he screams, gritting his teeth against the pain. The wound is instantly cauterized, but it still hurts like nothing he's felt before - worse than the beating Brigadier Parrish once gave him as a surly Corporal.
     Suddenly, strong hands lift him. He looks back and finds what seems to be half his platoon surrounding him. Riflemen fall to their knees and calmly take aim at the approaching ghosts. An ELITE armor unit looms through the smoke, its blazers cutting right through an unfortunate Specialist.
     "Get back in your positions!" Czibor screams. "You have to hold!"
     "No way we're leaving you out here, sir!" the Sergeant holding him calls. "Let's get you to a radio!"
     The Sergeant carries him all the way to the next nest, his armor running on the double-speed 'berserker' setting, then knocks frantically on the hatch.As soon as it cracks open, he practically dumps Czibor inside.
     "Transmitter!" the Lieutenant screams. "Do you have an OTP set?"
     The inside of this bunker is filled with cloying smoke and shouts. There are dead men scattered everywhere.
     "The officers here are all dead!" A Corporal shoves a transmitter into Czibor's hands. "We've got line-of-sight to the receivers towards the top of the Big Claw, but no one has the authentication codes for the fire control net!"
     "I do," Czibor grunts. "Help me back outside and prop me up."

=====Second Defense Perimeter, Big Claw, 1035 Hours=====

=====Laojiu Guard Third Perimeter, Big Claw Peak, 1205 Hours=====
     Mortars are highly effective artillery pieces, but by their very nature they require time to reach the target. Their indirect flight path renders them vulnerable to detection or even interception.
     Eight Vulture Siege tanks parked on the top of the Little Claw open fire with their heavy railguns, aimed downwards onto the flanks of the Big Claw. The kinetic-energy weapons travel at multiple Mach, so the defenders have no warning at all. The first salvo destroys eight Third Perimeter hardpoints, ripping them open like tin cans, and vomits dust kilometers skyward.
     Captain Graffin and a hand-picked Myt'herra team have discarded their power armor and crawled through acres of minefields to within half a kilometer of the Third Perimeter. Now they call targets for the Vultures.
     Another volley of railgun rounds shatters machine-gun nests and bunkers. Mortars begin to fall, too, as the Little Claw batteries finish mopping up the Prefecture mortars. Then, as if to add insult to injury, a flight of Richthofen bombers rumbles in from the north and unloads a salvo of ADMs. The area-denial missiles cruise around on stubby fins, dropping bomblets on anything that moves. Other missiles blow paths through the defensive minefields.
     The third perimeter garrison doesn't stick around to find out what else the Mesarthrim have in store. They make a quasi-organized retreat to the fourth and heaviest perimeter. This line of fortifications is more than a simple field of hardpoints and machine-gun nests; it has kilometers of depth, dozens of tunnels and hidden bunkers, everything an entrenched infantry unit could want. Here, the Guard will make their strongest stand.

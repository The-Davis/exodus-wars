== Events of 1952 ==

===January===
*January 8 - [[Cold War]]: [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] harbors 8 million refugees from [[Communist]] nations.

===February===
*February 6 - [[The Technology Race]]: In the [[United States (Terra)|United States]], a mechanical heart is used for the first time in a human patient.
*February 26 - [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] Prime Minister [[Winston Churchill]] announces that his nation has an atomic bomb.

===March===
*March 10 - General Fulgencio Batista re-takes power in [[Cuba (Terra)|Cuba]].

===April===
*April 18 - The [[Bolivia (Terra)|Bolivian]] National Revolution introduces sweeping reforms to the nation. The universal vote is introduced, allowing indigenous races and women to vote in all elections. Mines are nationalized, and many agrarian reform laws are passed.
*April 28 - The Treaty of San Francisco goes into effect, formally ending the [[United States (Terra)|United States]]' occupation of [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]].

===May===
*May 1 - [[Cold War]]: [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] threatens to form its own army.
*May 6 - [[The Jihad]]: Farouk of [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]] has himself announced as a descendant of prophet Muhammad. Most Muslims in the region are outraged by his claims.

===June===
*June 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: The keel is laid for the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] nuclear submarine USS ''Nautilus''.

===July===
*July 13 - [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] announces formation of its people's army.
*July 23 - [[The Jihad]]: General Mohammed Naguib leads The Free Officers in the overthrow of King Farouk of [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]].
*July 26 - [[The Jihad]]: The military coup in [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]] successfully ousts King Farouk.

===August===
*August 11 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Arabia (Terra)|Jordan]]ian Muslims overthrow the regime of king Talal, and murder his son Hussein before he can take the throne.

===September===
*September 2 - [[The Technology Race]]: Dr. Walton Lillehei and Dr. John Lewis perform first open-heart surgery at the University of Minnesota.
*September 18 - The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] vetoes [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]]'s application for membership in the [[United Nations]].

===October===
*October 14 - [[United Nations]] begins work in the new [[United Nations]] building in Geneva.

===November===
*November 1 - [[Cold War]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United States]] successfully detonates the first hydrogen bomb at Eniwetok Atoll in the Marshall Islands.
*November 4 - [[United States (Terra)|United States]] presidential election: incumbent Republican [[Thomas Dewey]] defeats Democrat Adlai Stevenson.

===December===
*December 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: First successful surgical separation of conjoined twins in Mount Sinai Hospital, Ohio.

== Events of 2322 ==

===January===
*January 13 - An [[Empire|Imperial]] picket entered the [[Centus System]] in pursuit of an [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps]] vessel. The [[Empire|Imperial]] captain opened fire on the first ship he encountered. The ship's [[:Category:Shields|shields]] were more powerful than anything the [[Empire]] had encountered before, completely absorbing the particle cannons of the [[Empire|Imperial]] picket ship. The unknown vessel proceeded to blow the [[Empire|Imperial]] warship to pieces.

*January 15 - The fleeing [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps|Explorer Corps]] ship was soon rescued by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], who welcomed news of their old home. The [[Mesarthrim]] were an early colony of [[Terra]] that was abducted by the [[Anderung]] prior to reaching their intended destination. [[Mesarthrim]] technology was powerful, and they soon allied with the [[Meroniri]], [[Ikronin]], [[Lorithos]] and the [[United Earth Alliance]] survivors in the [[Titan System]]. The [[Mesarthrim]] subsequently declared war on the [[Empire]].

*January 19 - When he received the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] delegation and [[Andreas Tischler]]'s formal declaration of war, [[Lucius Black]] recognized the danger of an enemy with significantly superior technology, but believed that he could win. He recalled Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] and half of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] to [[Terra]] to prepare for the invasion he believed would soon follow.

*January 24 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] signed the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter]] and were accepted as a Member State.

===February===
*February 8 - While it was not published publicly until later, [[Scott Sanburn]] wrote a letter home to his wife [[Lynn Sanburn|Lynn]] reminiscing on the dark years the two of them had suffered together. The letter was overflowing with patriotic pride for the colonists of the [[Titan System]], and [[Lynn Sanburn]] soon shared it with the [[Titan City]] press. The letter became the first in a series of propaganda campaigns titled "Letters from our Soldiers."
*February 9 - Emperor [[Lucius Black|Black]] had miscalculated yet again. The [[Mesarthrim]] allies of the [[United Earth Alliance]] were not interested in conquest, but in breaking the siege of the [[Titan System]]. A [[Mesarthrim]] fleet, led by Uber-General [[Andreas Tischler]], entered the [[Titan System]] and joined with [[Scott Sanburn]]'s fleets in orbit of [[Rhea]]. Imperial Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] decided that his forces were stretched too thinly, and ordered a full retreat to [[Cronus]]. The ground-defense facilities there were the second-strongest in the galaxy, surpassed only by the [[Lucius Black|Emperor]]'s fortress on [[Terra]].
*February 10 - The combined allied fleets attacked [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s forces on [[Cronus]]. Considering the overwhelming numbers, and being outclassed in technology by both the [[Mesarthrim]] and [[United Earth Alliance]] forces, [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] performed brilliantly. In the course of the two-day battle, [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s forces managed to incur 20% fatalities on the ground forces, and over a 90% rate of minor to severe damage on the space and ground equipment. Marshall's fleet took 60% losses. Only [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s own flagship, the HMS ''Atlas'', was completely unscathed, and that was only due to the superb performance of the crew. [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] was forced to withdraw.
*February 12 - With the loss of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] ships in orbit, the fall of [[Cronus]] was a foregone conclusion. [[Cronus]] was mostly a logistics and intelligence base, and the few thousand [[Royal Imperial Army]] troops on the surface mostly fought a delaying battle to allow the [[Omegas]] to destroy their network of contacts to agents operating on [[Theia]] and other worlds opposed to the [[Empire]]. [[Travis Carpenter]] made a name for himself in this battle, earning his promotion to Colonel.
*February 13 - [[Scott Sanburn]] compiled an extensive report on the remaining [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] troops and equipment and presented it to the [[Titan System]] provisional government. The after-action reports were the first of many sweeping changes to the doctrine of the remaining [[United Earth Alliance|Terran]] government officials.
*February 16 - Uber-General [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]] began to push into [[Empire|Imperial]] territory immediately after chasing [[Robert Marshall]] out of the [[Titan System]]. Rather than risk a deadly battle in the deeply entrenched colony worlds, [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]] began threatening to use a deadly virus. This virus, the [[Apöt]], was developed by [[Anderung]] forces to kill humans.
*February 26 - The city of New Dallas on [[Theia]], long abandoned due to the war effort, was repopulated and opened to immigration. Many war refugees from the [[Empire]] made their homes here, as did many [[Mesarthrim]] seeking to enjoy [[Terran]] life.
*February 27 - The science facilities of Dr. [[Reg Brenstein|Brenstein]], the chief scientist of the [[Empire]], were infected by the [[Apöt Virus]]. Only [[Reg Brenstein|Brenstein]] survived, and then only by injecting himself nearly constantly with a life-saving anti-viral serum he developed. Without assistance, he would soon die.

===March===
*March 3 - Professor [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]]'s science cruiser approached [[Reg Brenstein|Brenstein]]'s facility, and [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]] helped [[Reg Brenstein|Brenstein]] develop a more permanent solution to the [[Apöt Virus]]. Emperor [[Lucius Black]] gave [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]] a full pardon for his life-saving work.
*March 6 - [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4E (Scramjet Atmospheric Propulsion)|F-4E Raven]] variant begins production at all [[:Category:Fighters|fighter aircraft]] factories controlled by the [[Onyx Empire]].

===April===
*April 5 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine]] and [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Heer]] invaded the [[Proxima Procyon System]]. The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] forces there managed to keep the [[Mesarthrim]] forces at bay.
*April 7 - [[Royal Imperial Navy]] Rear Admiral [[Oswald Voss]] manages to destroy many [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] fighters in a surprise attack.
*April 22 - The [[Empire]]'s [[Proxima Procyon System]] Core Fleet breaks under the force of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine]]'s attacks. [[Oswald Voss]] is captured by the [[Mesarthrim]].
*April 23 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer]] defeats all [[Bethija]] garrisons.
*April 24 - The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] headquarters on [[Pluto]] is attacked by [[Volucris]], marking [[First Contact]] between [[Volucris]] and [[Terrans]]. [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]] and some of his assistants were abducted. The [[Volucris]] withdrew before the [[Empire]] could react. No one discovered the cause or motivation of the attack until [[2324]].
*April 28 - Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian]] revealed to the [[Empire]] that [[Lucius Black]] was the one who destroyed the planet [[Torimur]] and betrayed his own troops in the [[First Exodus War]]. To this day, [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] has kept his sources secret, but in the aftermath of receiving this information, nearly half of the forces of the [[Empire]] joined with [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] in a rebellion that became known as the [[Prefecture]].
*April 29 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans High Command]] met to discuss the reports that the [[Empire]] was in the first throes of what would become known as the [[Imperial Civil War]].
*April 30 - [[Lucius Black]] publicly denounced [[Anthony Hadrian]], and ordered his arrest and immediate execution. This command resulted in the first shooting battles of the [[Imperial Civil War]].

===May===
*May 23 - After an initial retreat from [[Terra]], which had troops mostly loyal to [[Lucius Black]], [[Anthony Hadrian]]'s forces returned. Hundreds of [[Royal Imperial Army]] troops loyal to [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] organized themselves into the [[Legion of the Phoenix]]--a name inspired by their leader [[Travis Carpenter]]. The [[Legion of the Phoenix]] began assaults on the southeast regions of North America, the industrial centers of Central Asia, and the heavy metal mining centers of the Middle East.
*May 24 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans High Command]] approved supporting the [[Prefecture]], and formally recognized them as a government. [[Andreas Tischler]] began preparations to invade [[Terra]] with his own forces.
*May 25 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] sent reinforcements to assist the [[Prefecture]], landing in Australia, South America, West Africa, and Northern Russia.
*May 26 - The [[Crimson Tide Battle Group]], under the command of [[Nathan Hinks]], reinforced the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] forces already on [[Terra]].
*May 27 - [[Travis Carpenter]] landed forces in Canaveral, Florida, as the first step in the campaign to wrestle the new world capital of [[Memphis]] from [[Lucius Black]]'s forces.
*May 29 - The Imperial forces defending Australia were completely neutralized by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer]].

===June===
*June 3 - [[Ben Parman]] and [[Arnold Stadt]] did not approve of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] recognizing and allying with the [[Prefecture]]. They attacked the [[Kalidasa System]] against direct orders, destroying several [[Empire|Imperial]] forces, but also killing many members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|Kalidasa Resistance]] and their [[Prefecture]] contacts. [[Empire|Imperial]] Rear Admiral [[Ramesh Jethro]] was able to drive off the [[Mesarthrim]] attack. Both [[Mesarthrim]] commanders were arrested when they returned to [[Mesar]] in disgrace.
*June 7 - The Prefecture forces in the Middle East were wiped out by heavy Imperial attacks, along with nuclear strikes. Hadrian decided not to push his luck by sending in more forces, and instead stepped up operations in North America and Central Asia.
*June 12 - The forced exile to the [[Cinaed System]] of all [[Mesarthrim]] political dissidents was going badly. Many large cities were burned to the ground, and hundreds of people were killed in riots.
*June 13 - The [[Mesarthrim]] moved from its foothold in Australia to assault the Pacific islands. The resistance was light, and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Mesarthrim Heer]] pounded its way through every [[Empire|Imperial]] base and stronghold.
*June 14 - The [[Mesarthrim High Command]] decided to pardon [[Arnold Stadt]] and [[Ben Parman]] rather than lose such valued officers, but reduced their pay by several grades, and imprisoned their families in rather comfortable facilities to ensure the continued loyalty of the Commandants.
*June 25 - Several more cities on [[Mesar]] were burned to the ground, but now the [[Mesarthrim]] rebels were equipped with heavy weaponry provided by [[Omegas]] agents.

===July===
*July 1 - Several thousand [[Mesarthrim]] rebels were killed when they were trapped by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer]] in one of the cities they'd already begun to burn.
*July 10 - [[David Carpenter]], [[Lucius Black]]'s best [[:Category:Rigs|Rig]] pilot and general, was sent to the North American front lines to face off against the [[Prefecture]]. He butchered "Zebra Recon Company," leaving only [[Tyra Doyle]] alive to report back to [[Travis Carpenter]]
*July 19 - Despite the uprisings back home and a loss of some support from the [[Clan Lords]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer]] remobilized on [[Terra]] and soon captured the entirety of the Pacific islands.

===August===
*August 5 - [[Prefecture]] forces renewed their assault in the southeastern region of North America in their march towards [[Memphis]]. For days, they pounded the lines of [[Empire|Imperial]] forces with heavy artillery, and began moving forward. The [[Prefecture]] had air superiority, but they were taking heavy losses in the air and needed [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] reinforcements.
*August 6 - [[Prefecture]] carriers orbiting [[Jupiter]] sent thirty-six [[F-11 Stuka]] heavy [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] to reinforce the [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] on [[Terra]]'s surface. While passing through the [[Sol System]] asteroid belt, the [[F-11 Stuka|Stukas]] were ambushed by a [[Remnant]] [[:Category:Warship|warship]] under the command of [[Gaius Shadar]], and were slaughtered. Only six escaped the belt, and those were finished off by an [[Empire|Imperial]] fighter squadron on the other side.
*August 7 - [[Andreas Tischler]] took personal command of the [[Mesarthrim]] forces on [[Terra]] and began preparations to assault Okinawa.
*August 14 - [[Mesarthrim]] forces began their assault on Okinawa, but were repulsed by heavy [[Empire|Imperial]] defenses. Uber General [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]] called for the assistance of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]].
*August 16 - At the urging of [[Scott Sanburn]], the [[Titan System]] provisional government authorized the remains of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] to mobilize for an invasion of [[Terra]].
*August 19 - The force behind the asteroid belt ambush made their presence known. They were the [[Remnant]], the last pure descendants of the [[Anthronoris]]. [[Gaius Shadar]], their commander, publicly introduced himself to [[Lucius Black]] and offered the services of the [[Remnant]]'s [[:Category:Warships|warship]] in the area for the defense of [[Terra]].
*August 20 - [[Andreas Tischler]] sent a mocking, defiant transmission to [[Gaius Shadar]], attempting to provoke him into getting involved in combat on the ground.
*August 25 - The [[Prefecture]] managed to make an [[Adin Band]] jump into orbit of [[Terra]] with one of their [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]], and began delivering much-needed [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]].
*August 27 - Throughout the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] had done little but pay lip service to the defeat of the [[Empire]] and the restoration of peace. When [[Gaius Shadar]]'s presence on [[Terra]] became widely known, the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] suddenly declared war on the [[Remnant]] and mobilized several ships from [[Aseta Salfore]] to capture [[Gaius Shadar|Shadar]]'s ship.
*August 28 - The first reinforcements from the [[Titan System]], under the command of [[Scott Sanburn]] arrived in Australia to assist the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and [[Prefecture]] forces.

===September===
*September 18 - Canaveral Starport fell to the forces of the [[Prefecture]]. This decisive battle forced the Empire to begin retreating north towards [[Memphis]]. [[Lucius Black]] withdrew to his secret sanctuary in the arctic.
*September 20 - Since the first [[Mesarthrim]] attack on [[Kalidasa System|Kalidasa]], partisan forces led by [[Evaristo Silveira]] began actively resisting [[Empire|Imperial]] rule. They were finally successful when the [[Mesarthrim]] sent in forces to aid them. Kalidasa was secured when many [[Royal Imperial Army]] units revealed their [[Prefecture]] loyalties and surrendered to [[Evaristo Silveira|Silveira]].
*September 21 - [[Travis Carpenter]]'s forces from the [[Legion of the Phoenix]] attacked Orlando, an important crossroads in the main roads from the seaports to the northern part of the peninsula. [[Travis Carpenter|Carpenter]]'s forces captured the city, then repelled an [[Empire|Imperial]] counter-attack
*September 22 - After their success on [[Kalidasa]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] sent their [[Myt'gaul]] special forces to destroy the [[Alpha Centauri Shipyards]]. The mission was only partially successful. Several [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] under construction were destroyed, but the explosives meant for the shipyards themselves were disarmed or removed safely. No [[Myt'gaul]] were killed or injured in the operation.
*September 23 - The [[Mesarthrim]] routed the Imperial forces holding the Japanese islands. The [[Omegas]] launched a secret mission to keep the island and its key intelligence installations out of [[Mesarthrim]] hands. They detonated every nuclear warhead in the silos beneath the surface. The devastation sank several of the islands, killing millions of civilians and soldiers, [[Empire|Imperial]] and [[Mesarthrim]] alike.

=== October ===

* October 4 - [[Scott Sanburn]] joined [[Andreas Tischler]] in the Asia regions, and sent others in his forces to reinforce [[Travis Carpenter]] in the North American region. Within hours, the [[Royal Imperial Army]] attacked the three invading forces on all fronts.
* October 6 - [[Gaius Shadar]] and [[Rosh Arufin]] clashed in a brief battle over [[Mars]] between the [[Remnant]] [[:Category:Warships|warship]] and two [[:Category:Cruisers|war cruisers]] of [[Aseta Salfore]].
* October 13 - After careful planning and preparation, [[Roger Morris]] finally managed to falsify transmitted orders that convinced the [[Empire|Imperial]] garrisons at [[Oronus System|Oronus]] to return to [[Terra]]. A [[Mesarthrim]] liberation force arrived later in the day to discover the slave colony already in the hands of its workers. This was the birth of the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition]].
* October 14 - [[Arnold Stadt]] led a liberation force into the [[Tau Ceti System]]. The [[Resistance against the Empire|Kalmar Resistance]] and [[Prefecture]]-loyal elements of the [[Royal Imperial Army]] were waiting and ready, and struck the [[Empire|Imperial]] garrisons on the ground when [[Arnold Stadt]] sent a demand for unconditional surrender to the [[Empire|Imperial]] governor. [[Kalmar]] was liberated in a day.

=== November ===

* November 6 - The newly-launched [[FDS Avenger|FDS ''Avenger'']] and [[FDS Omaha|FDS ''Omaha'']] intercepted and destroyed the carrier group led by the [[HMS Irtysh|HMS ''Irtysh'']] in the [[Alpha Centauri System]].
* November 18 - A team of the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Prefecture Legion Corps Army Hunters|Army Hunters]] are sent to [[Ah Kin]] to extract Dr. [[Erika Strout]].
* November 19 - The [[FDS Avenger|FDS ''Avenger'']] intercepts and extracts the Army Hunter team and Dr. Strout.

===Overview===

Kondo'jahazi aina Nvodu'pweza are the most commonly seen warships in battles against humanoid warships.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaNvodu'pweza1.jpg]]

As numerous as the Nina'rikebu are, they typically leave the bloody battles to these deadly warships.

===Capabilities===

Nvodu'pweza are exceptional warships.  They carry Mkuki'kali Energy Lances, but only as many as a single Nina'rikebu turret.  The weapons are fixed in a forward position, but the Nvodu'pweza is very maneuverable, more like a fighter than a warship.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaNvodu'pweza2.jpg]]

Nvodu'pweza carry many Bunduki'kubwa Hyperspace Distortion Cannons, and hundreds of Gobori'dogo Relativistic Mass Drivers.  These weapons make it one of the best-armed warships for dealing with smaller targets, particularly fighters.  It also carries nearly a hundred Mshale'liga missile launchers.

===Deployment===

Nvodu'pweza are deployed with most Mia'mbili, but others prefer greater ground troop capacity.  The Nvodu'pweza has no landing craft capacity, so its Ardhi'nyuka are purely for defense.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaNvodu'pweza3.jpg]]

An Ardhi'nyuka serving on a Nvodu'pweza is probably looking for a ticket out, to serve in ground battle.

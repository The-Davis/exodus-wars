==History==

The ''Omaha'' was one of three Avenger class cruisers built and delivered to the [[Prefecture]] while they were still a rebellion. [[Rahn Industries]] was already building the ship when they demonstrated the Avenger class's capabilities to the Prefecture, but were unable to deliver the design to the Prefecture forces in time for the Battle of Terra.

The Omaha and the other Avengers were used to help fill out the Fleets being commisioned by the newly formed Federated Districts of the Prefecture. The Omaha was designated CG-1, and assigned to the First Fleet.

During the crisis on Terra in May of 2324, the Omaha and other First Fleet elements were assigned to escort relief convoys into Terran space, and to help enforce the quarantine of the planet due to the plague.

The Omaha was a participant in the short Battle of Ueda during the Volucris incursion, but the raid was so short that the Omaha did not even fire a shot.

During the peace talks between the United Centusi States and the Auellal League in August of 2324, the Omaha carried Ambassador Kim Ali to Mesar, and remained in orbit throughout the peace talks. Marines from the Omaha were active participants in the manhunt for Ali's assassins, and two Marine fire-teams were killed in a fire-fight with the Omega agents before they were captured.

The Omaha was an active participant in the Battle of Icronia, where it sustained heavy damage and accounted for over a thousand Volucris kills, including a dozen Leviathin warship breeds.

It was during the Battle of Icronia that Douglas, the Omaha's AI, emerged as a result of Prism's linking of the fleet and subsequent violent disconnection. Prism's subroutines remained on the Omaha's C4ISTAR system, and gained independent sentience. At first the new AI thought that it was Prism, but once it discovered the truth it adopted a new name and personality.

During the first months of the Tempest War with the United Centusi States, the Omaha was assigned to raiding Mesarthrim shipping with other First Fleet elements. The Omaha and its escorts destroyed two Mesarthrim freighter convoys en route to Laojiu, but it was later discovered that only ten freighters were carrying important components and supplies.

Currently, the Omaha is serving the First Fleet with distinction.

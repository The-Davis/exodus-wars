The Ziegler class Heavy Cruiser was built specifically for the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] and their Peacekeeping Fleets. Often, the Ziegler is required to operate independently or with a very small number of supporting craft. Duties such as anti-piracy and customs enforcement requires the Ziegler to handle a wide variety of situations.

The vessel has also been designed to assist in planetary support, boosting not only extensive reserves of marines but also fast response drop pods and a high capacity landing ship. For ship-to-ship duties two transport shuttles come pre equipped with universal docking collars and hard lock/soft lock boarding mechanisms.

Due to the extensible nature of these ships, their construction was deemed paramount, and the entire line of nine vessels was completed in three years.
==Design==
The Ziegler was actually designed for peace, and not war. Although she's a powerful ship, and carries an extensive array of weapons - the ships true design is to combat rogue elements that can threaten the peace.

Designed by Mesarbauer engineers following the Second Exodus War the Ziegler was shelved when the Civil War sparked off. Then, following the civil war, the updated design was again shelved as Mesarbauer began building up for war.

It wouldn't be until 2334 when Udet-Knorr AG was formed under the Mesarthrim Federation, that the design would be revived, updated, and carried into full-scale production. Wanting ships that could handle lone combat, Bradley Von Graith pushed hard for the Ziegler and Twilight vessels. These two classes served the young Mesarthrim Federation admirably at the Battle of Novar where their ability to handle a ground invasion and engage in space combat simultaneously was a significant advantage that the ucs could not meet.

The Ziegler is based around three basic ideas; heavy guns, defensive guns, and ground support. Mounting large caliber heavy blazers allows the Ziegler to  brawl, while caring enough lighter blazers to interdict incoming missiles and fighter craft. The vessels also carry four squadrons of fighters and a single bomber squadron.

For ground support the Ziegler boasts six drop pods that are large enough to carry a squadron of tanks, or a company of men. There is also a high capacity drop ship that holds tanks, men, and a squadron dedicated to ground control. If a ship needs to be boarded, the Ziegler has two shuttles that can force their ways onto vessels if the need arise. These shuttle are also equipt with extensive countermeasure and redundant shielding to assist in survivability for the boarding crew.

==Battle History==
The Ziegler has already seen considerable action. The first ships of her class saw action in [[2335]] at the [[Battle of Novar]]. The Ziegler and the Deitrich were the first ships of the young Mesarthrim Federation to respond to the call for support on Novar. Accompanied by several [[Auellal Class Variable Warship|Twilight vessels]], they dropped immediate ground support upon arrival by utilizing their drop pods - a first for a Mesarthrim Heer. During the landings the Kriegsmarine was ambushed by the UCS Warfleet. Although the Ziegler took a pounding, it successfully destroyed a [[Stormforge Class Battleship|Stormforge]] battleship, and the task force crippled a half dozen destroyers.

Following the war the Ziegler made a name for itself policing the newly acquired [[Pelagrim Sector]] from not only pirates, but rogue elements that were either still loyal to Camp, or wanted the Prefecture to retrain control of the sector. The Ziegler's presence helped eradicate these elements and secure a lasting peace in the sector.

==Future Plans==
With the construction of the Ziegler class complete, the plans for the Ziegler have been shelved until one needs to be replaced from battle, or such a time that a refresh of the class is deemed necessary.

The Prefecture have expressed interest in the Ziegler class to act as a warship for their Marine Corps. However, no official purchase or inquiry has been made.

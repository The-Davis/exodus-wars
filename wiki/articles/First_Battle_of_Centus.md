The '''First Battle of Centus''' was a retaliatory strike against the [[United Centusi States]].  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]'s Third Fleet of the Line attempted to decapitate the [[Mesarthrim]] leadership by bombarding the city of [[Stone Bridge]].

Mesarthrim intelligence were able to buy off several Prefecture officers, and the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine had advance warning of the attack.  Two Mesarthrim fleets were waiting, and half the Prefecture Third Fleet was destroyed in the ensuing battle.  The [[FDS Delphinus|FDS ''Delphinus'']] was trapped in subspace, and [[Anthony Hadrian]] was listed as missing in action until he was rescued by an [[Ixoarchada]] subspace vessel.

The failure of the Prefecture's strategy gave the Mesarthrim blitzkrieg an enormous amount of momentum, which the Prefecture did not recover from for several years.

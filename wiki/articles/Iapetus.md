Iapetus was once inhabitable, but its civilization and biosphere were both destroyed, probably during the [[Sundering]]. It is believed that the world was actually the main [[Anthronoris]] holding in the [[Titan System]], and the fact that it was covered by over three hundred metropolis-sized cities supports this theory. The planet’s atmosphere is extremely poisonous now, similar to [[Venus]] in the [[Sol System]]. As such, the Titanians have not been able to mount any sort of archaeological expeditions to the surface.

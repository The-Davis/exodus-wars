Between the two military branches in the [[Auellal League]], the Armada has received the greatest amount of financial and hardware support.  With the new ''LCS'' multi-purpose ships, the Armada is charged with defending the [[Auellal League]] at all cost.  In addition, the Armada is expected to act as the [[Auellal League]]'s arm, reaching out into space to extend the influence of the [[Auellal League]].

==History==

When the [[Auellal League]] emerged victorious from the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], they had few remaining viable [[:Category:Warships|warships]] in their arsenal, and [[Caledon]] had no shipyards to replace their losses.

[[Bradley von Graith]] proposed taking the remains of [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] vessels and the disabled hulks claimed from the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] as war trophies, and putting them together as reclaimed hulls.

[[Oswald Voss]] sought to develop classes of their own and redevelop the Armada into a force to be reckoned with. To that end, working with [[Steven Cue]], they invited the [[Astrolift Company]] to open a tax-free branch on [[Caledon]].  Before long, [[Oswald Voss]] and [[Omar Banks]] of the new [[Astrolift Company|Astrolift Shipyards]] begun designing the first Auellal [[:Category:Warships|warship]] classes.

==Organization==
===Fleet===

Ard-veoir Fogha: [[Anthony Blithe]] ''(Chief Steward of the Offense)''

[[Auellal Armada Planetary Defense Fleet|Planetary Defense Fleet]] is the only major force projection unit of the Armada. It is comprised of an ad hoc collection of vessels.  The most common vessels are the "Mongrel Class" reclaimed hulls from old [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim]] warships.  There are also some old [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] warships that were captured by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] at [[Bethija]]. There are some vessels purchased from the [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova Family]] in service, though they are few in number.

The highlight of the Armada is the new "LCS" multi-purpose warships being built by the [[Astrolift Company]].

===Planetside Installations===

Ard-veoir Dorcha: [[Richard Westington]] ''(Chief Steward of the Night)''

The Armada has a few installations on the surface of [[Caledon]]. They vary in importance and purpose. Military starports are the largest, with [[Auellal Guardian|Guardian]] garrisons, military aircraft landing strips, and launching/receiving facillities for starships capable of atmospheric landings.

Dancer bases are the home of second-line Armada pilot squadrons, where new pilots learn the ropes and older pilots end their careers.

==Ships==

The names of commissioned ships of the Armada start with "XXX", designating 'X X X'. Additionally, each ship is given a letter-based hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and a hull number. The names of ships are officially selected by the Post-Admiral.

The Armada is not as powerful as other traditional navies. Its Mongrel Class vessels (MC) are all unique, cobbled together from various ship hulls and made combat-ready again. They also have a collection of [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Cruisers|Cruisers]] (CG) captured during the [[Second Exodus War]]. They also purchased a [[:Category:Frigates|Heavy Frigate]] (FG), two [[:Category:Corvettes|Gunships]] (SSG), and two [[:Category:Corvettes|Corvettes]] from the [[Asanova Family Territory]].  

The highlight of the Armada is the new multi-purpose warships (LCS) being built by the [[Astrolift Company]]. These ships can change from [[:Category:Cruisers|cruisers]], to [[:Category:Carriers|aircraft carriers]] and a plethora of other configurations with only a few hours of conversion work.

==Aircraft==

Aircraft are an important part of the Armada's fighting capacity. Carrier-based aircraft are able to strike space, air, sea, and land targets far from a carrier while protecting friendly forces from enemy aircraft and ships.

The Dancers use the Sundancer carrier-based aircraft, which is assembled by the [[Astrolift Company]] from Auellal specifications. Each Dancer is given their own Sundancer, which they maintain and fly for most of their lives. A Dancer and their fighter are like one single entity. Aside from the required Auellal League Armada wing markings, the Dancers are allowed to paint and decorate their Sundancers as they like.

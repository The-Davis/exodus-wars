September 18, 2322

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After a month of fighting, the Royal Imperial Army's defense of Canaveral Spaceport has failed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Once again, General Travis Carpenter's Cerberus Company was key in the victory. Instead of their standard Firefly outfits, Cerberus was equipped with heavy Hades Rigs to counter the Imperial Avatars.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial Rig pilot David Carpenter tore through the ranks of the Prefecture forces early in the battle, but spotted Travis Carpenter's red and gray Hades, and gave chase. Travis led his brother on a wild chase away from the battlefield. Without their leader's assistance, the Imperials fared poorly.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Carpenter eventually allowed David to catch up to him. Rather than engage his younger brother in a one-on-one duel, as the younger Carpenter hoped, Travis led him into a trap. Half of Cerberus Company ambushed David Carpenter's Avatar.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;David Carpenter managed to escape the battle, and was last seen heading for the northern provinces of North America. It is believed that he is hooking up with the rest of the Royal Imperial Guard--the Emperor's personal bodyguards.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With a fully operation spaceport captured, hundreds of Prefecture Rig forces and thousands of infantry are landing. With the Mesarthrim in the Pacific, and the Prefecture gaining ground in North America, the Empire can't hope to hold Terra for long.

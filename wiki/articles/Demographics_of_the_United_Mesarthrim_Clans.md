===Languages===

Mesarthrim speak German, French, and Gaelic.  There has been a certain level of bastardization of each language thanks to the [[Anderung]]'s inability to speak the language.  And over time, small parts of the [[Anderung]]'s language, Halrecht, have made their way into the Mesarthrim dialect.

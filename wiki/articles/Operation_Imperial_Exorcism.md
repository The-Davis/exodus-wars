Operation Imperial Exorcism was the code name given to the combined offensive by the [[United Earth Alliance]] and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] to drive the [[Onyx Empire]] out of the [[Titan System]].

==Preparation==
Operation Imperial Exorcism was planned by Brigadier General [[Mark Pulaski]] of the [[United Earth Alliance Army]].  The operation had two major objectives; secure the network of compounds that went deep into the crust of [[Cronus]], and to disable or commandeer Imperial surface weapon installations.  This was under the overall objective of removing the Imperial presence from Cronus in a single, large scale, battle.

Pulaski divided the operation into two theatres.  Hoping to avoid any confusion in the ranks or combat doctrine, Pulaski kept the Mesarthrim and UEAA soldiers separate.  He assigned the Mesarthrim to the surface operation, and the UEAA to the compound network.  [[Andreas Tischler]] agreed with the plan, and selected his trusted General, [[Garrett Koeing]] - [[Clan Lord]] of the Koeing Clan - to lead the Myt'herra across the frozen wasteland of Cronus.  Pulaski himself would lead the UEAA into the compounds.

==Infiltration==
On February 9th, three [[Myt'gaul]] Monks infiltrated Cronus.  Their mission was to prepair the way for the UEAA's assault into the compounds.  Therefore, they rigged several entrance points with remote explosives to allow quicker access.  They also planted remote explosives on numerous defensive structures guarding the entrance to the compounds.

Throughout the day on February 10th the Monks avoided capture, and the next day rejoined the Mesarthrim forces after the initial combat landings had been made.

Following the battle, the three Monks were honoured with the Titan Star, and served as instructors for Titanian Special Forces into [[2323]].

==Initial Landings==
The initial landings came on February 11th, a day later then had been planned.  The massive Imperial fleet in orbit had covered the planet well enough that [[Arnold Stadt]] had not wanted to risk killing the ground forces in their dropships.

The first landings made by the UEAA were assisted by a group of Mesarthrim Monks.  This allowed UEAA troops to easily access the compounds in the south directly, since the explosive decompression was clearing the route of Imperial soldiers.  The UEAA were able to take control of six stations before facing any kind of resistance.  And once they did, the Imperial forces dug in deep.

The Mesarthrim landings were against open frozen tundra - or so they thought.  As soon as the dropships touched down, the Mesarthrim fell under brutal attacks from concealed positions.  Mesarthrim sappers and engineers quickly got to work blowing open vacuum sealed weapon pods, and other weapon emplacements.  The Mesarthrim utilised Mechanized Infantry to move quickly across the plain.  After only ten hours, the Mesarthrim hit 'Death Ridge', a large mountain chain that was heavily fortified.  As they came upon this barrier, they also came under artillery fire from Imperial artillery.

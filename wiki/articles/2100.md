== Events of 2100 ==

Shortly after the [[First Exodus War]], [[Meroniri]] explorers made [[First Contact]] with the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The friendly [[Meroniri Terinasi]] and the [[United Earth Alliance]] got along very well, and the [[Terran]] race was soon a major galactic player.

===January===

===February===

Professor [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]]'s efforts were incredible. At the end of four years, he'd stripped the colony ships down, and built them into a massive warship. The ship was equipped with factories, cryogenic chambers, and enough weaponry to repel a squadron of [[United Earth Alliance]] warships. Unfortunately, the Volstamik-Krenschov drives of the original colony ships were totally ruined. [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]]'s replacement [[hyperspace]] drives would take centuries to propel the [[Torimur]] survivors back towards [[Terra]].

[[Lucius Black]] had patience. He knew he would not age in cryogenic hibernation. The [[Second Exodus]] began.

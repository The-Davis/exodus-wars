The Rank system of the Remnant is a two tier system and unified throughout all three branches of the Remnant Military.  

==Enlisted Ranks==
The enlisted ranks are drawn from the population of the [[Remnant]].  Each citizen receives periodic military training throughout their life, and at one point, they are required to serve a dedicated period of no less than 300 years in the military.  Those who do not wish to make it a career complete their terms of service in the enlisted ranks.  The formal training upon entry into dedicated service is ten years where they learn the basics associated with each of the three branches of the military.  Then, in their final two years of training, specialize depending upon observed natural ability and desire.  

There are three enlisted ranks which I will name later.

==Commissioned Ranks==
Commissioned officers are those Masters who desire to be career military.  Officers are given extensive formal training from the Remnant Military Academy over a period of fifty years.  The enrollment requirement is a demonstrated affinity for leadership from either prior military experience or administered testing.  Each candidate follows a course of study which further develops their skills in each of the specialized three branches of the Remnant military.  This allows for each candidate to become intimately familiar with the duties and skills required by officers throughout the entirety of the military, regardless of their own specialization.  During their last four years of study, the candidates specialize into one of the three branches; the [[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]], the [[Remnant Fighter Corps| Reliquiae Acalanthis Agema]], or the [[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]].  This specialization is determined by both their observed natural abilities and their own personal desires.    

'''Officerior''' - A "lesser officer," the Officerior is the lowest commissioned rank within the Remnant military.  They fill leadership roles on ships of the Fleet, pilot single fighters in the Fighter Corps, and are executive officers in Companies of Marines.  (Needs cooler name)

'''Officer''' - The Officer fulfills senior leadership roles on ships of the Fleet, lead flights of fighters in the Fighter Corps, and command companies of Marines.  

'''Captainior''' - Analogous to a junior ship captain in other militaries, a Captainior typically commands a light combatant or scout in the Fleet, a UNIT of the Fighter Corps, or are the executive officer in regiments of Marines.  (Needs cooler name)

'''Captain''' - Analogous to the rank of a ship captain in other militaries, a Captain typically commands a medium or heavy combatant in the Fleet, a squadron in the Fighter Corps, or regiment in the Marines.  (Needs cooler name)

'''Legantior''' - Analogous to the lower ranks of Admiral or General in other militaries, a Legantior is a "lesser" Legant and generally commands Manipars of the Fleet, wings from the Fighter Corps, or brigades in the Marines.

'''Legant'''  - Analogous to an Admiral or General in another military, a Legant commands Legiars or Scrutias Classii in the Fleet, or a fighter group in the, or a division in the Marines.  

'''Imperiant''' - The supreme commander of the entire Remnant Military.  This person is literal Commander-in-Chief, taking an active role in planning, strategy, and command in battle.  This position is the '''Ministry''' level representative of the Remnant Military.

'''Franklin Delano Roosevelt'''
*Born: January 30, [[1882]]
*Died: April 12, [[1945]]
*Place of Birth: New York, [[United States (Terra)| United States]], [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: Georgia, [[United States (Terra)| United States]], [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[United States (Terra)| United States of America]]

''Franklin Delano Roosevelt'' (born January 30, [[1882]]) was the thirty-second President of the [[United States (Terra)| United States of America]]. He was elected to four terms in office, he served from [[1933]] until his death in [[1945]], and is the only U.S. president to have served more than two terms. Roosevelt was a central figure of the [[20th Century]] during a time of worldwide economic crisis and world war.

''Kian Huern'' was the moonlit daughter of [[Derek Huern]], and the granddaughter of [[Talire Huern]]. She fought in the [[Burning Wars]] against the other three would-be successors of the throne: [[Torm Krelbine]], [[Goff Riiktar]], and [[Lera Ruehv]].

Kian Huern's forces survived until the end, when she died in a final showdown with [[Torm Krelbine]]. Krelbine also died, but left no heirs. While [[Lorithan]] almost destroyed, Kian's son [[Dirken Huern]] was the heir apparent.

Kian is remembered today as the Soresh Prekohr, or Tenacious Princess. While many believe she was responsible for much of the destruction of [[Lorithan]], her claim to the throne is widely recognized as the only truly legitimate one.

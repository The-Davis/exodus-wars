The Starbound class Gunship was designed by [[Fireline Industries]] as a replacement for the [[Nimitz Class Gunship|Nimitz]].  It was the third member of the [[Fireline Industries#Star Series|Star Series]] designs.  The Starbound design was built around the concept of replacing the [[Nimitz Class Gunship|Nimitz]] by capitalizing on its biggest weakness, speed.  The Starbound was designed to be lightly armed, but has significant power reserved for propulsion, and redundant shielding systems.  After the initial prototype was built, seven more were constructed, and set against [[Anderung]] forces to prove their worth to [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] acquisition agents.  Two were destroyed in the combat, and the rest were rescued from destruction by a flight of [[Wickliff class Destroyer|Wickliff's]].  The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] refused to purchase the Starbound contract, and the surviving ships were scrapped in the [[Cinead System]].
In [[2320]], the [[Auellal Political Party]] recovered the wrecks, and turned the [[FIL Sternband|eKs-x1 Sternband]] into a museum ship.  The other five ships were secretly refitted and modernized.  They now serve in the [[Auellal Armada|Armada's]] [[Auellal Armada#Toshiaght_(Vanguard)|Toshiaght]].

==Development==
In [[2263]], [[Fireline Industries]] started production on their [[Fireline Industries#Star Class|Star Class Project]].  The Starbound, was the third vessel designed, and the first vessel meant to be a competitor to a current vessel.

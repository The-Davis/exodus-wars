'''Scott Crosby'''
*Place of Birth: [[BG-1 Jerome Black]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Scott Crosby'', like most of the ''Jerome Black'''s crew, is a native of the ship itself, born and raised during the long voyage from exile to [[Alpha Centauri]].  He has been in his position ever since the fleet met with the colony of [[Alpha Centauri]].

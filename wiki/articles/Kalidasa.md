'''Kalidasa''' is the third planet in the [[Lupei System]]. It was originally colonized in [[2093]], mostly by [[Brazil (Terra)|Brazillian]] interests from [[Earth]].

== History ==

The Kalidasa colony was sponsored by Brazillian interests in the [[United Earth Alliance]] government, several regional Brazillian companies, and by the Catholic Church in an effort to preserve Brazillian culture from a rapidly homogenizing Earth society.

The initial colony landed in [[2093]]. Several cities were founded. [[Nueva Ceará]] was the capital and the site of the main spaceport. [[Santa Guadalupe]] was a key farming city and one of the largest population centers. [[Sao Fuera]] was founded on a grassy highland region, and was a major sheep-herding and wool production area.

Prosperity came to the colony rather quickly, and it was self-sufficient within three years of the first colonial landing. The world became an independent protectorate of the United Earth Alliance. After the [[First Exodus War]] and [[First Contact]], no one in the Alliance was interested in forcing new colonies to accept Alliance rule. Kalidasa remained an independent world until the year [[2317]].

During the [[Onyx Empire]]'s aggressive expansion in the [[Second Exodus War]], Kalidasa was blockaded by Task Force: Judicator, led by Commodore [[Julia Michaels]]' [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']]. Rather than deal with costly partisan fighting and a ground campaign, Michaels authorized the nuclear bombardment of the city of [[Santa Guadalupe]], killing nearly 450,000 civilians. The city remains highly radioactive and is still off-limits to travellers.

Towards the end of the Second Exodus War, [[Evaristo Silveira]] led the local [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] in a series of armed campaigns against the Empire's occupational forces, and was able to seize control of the planet. Since [[2323]], the world has been under the control of the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]].

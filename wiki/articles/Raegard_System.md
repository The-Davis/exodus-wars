The '''Raegard System''' consists of the star Raegard, its six planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Raegard, the planets are Raegard I, [[Junheim]], Raegard III, Raegard IV, Raegard V, and Raegard VI.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Raegard is the Raegard System's only star. Raegard is a class G3B yellow dwarf. The Raegard System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Raegard is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Junheim ===
[[Junheim]] (1.1AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Raegard System. It has one moon, [[Narmast]].

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Raegard System was colonized by the Reigens, formerly part of the [[Ikronin]] race, in [[1093BC]].

'''Ilska Myrd'''
*Place of Birth: [[Kronheim]]
*Allegiance: [[Ikronin Jurekön]]

''Ilska Myrd'' serves in the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] military. She was instrumental in the defense of Oselo, and eventually made the tactical decision to destroy the Oselo Data Center to prevent its capture by the [[Volucris]].

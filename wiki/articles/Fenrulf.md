The Fenrulf is a species native to [[Kronheim]]. It is also found on [[Junheim]], [[Myrdrasil]], and [[Labrea]], though it was artificially introduced to those worlds.

While xenotype classes are sometimes difficult to match exactly, the Fenrulf is considered a mammal of the order Carnivora. The Fenrulf has a wide range of habitats, covering every landmass on [[Kronheim]], the northern regions of Junheim, most of the regions of [[Myrdrasil]], and the polar regions of [[Labrea]].

The majority of Fenrulf sub-species live in the wild, but a significant number are bred and raised in captivity for companionship, work, and food. The [[Ikronin]] use the Fenrulf as a pack animal, the [[Reigens]] breed them as guard animals, and both use certain breeds as a food source. Smaller Fenrulf breeds are popular pets on [[Kronheim]] and Junheim.


== Features and Adaptations ==

The weight and size of a Fenrulf varies wildly. The Great Fenrulf is the largest known example, measuring at an average of 2.1 meters tall and weighing 1,600kg. The Blue Fenrulf is the most common variety, and peaks at 140cm and 375kg. Domestic Fenrulfs meant to be pets are bred for smaller size and are usually around 80cm and 110kg.

Fenrulfs are built for strength and stamina, and are ideal for long sustained travel. They have narrow chests, powerful backs, and muscular legs designed for long, powerful strides. A Fenrulf's average speed at a trotting gait is about 15kph, but when pursuing prey they can gallop at up to 80kph.

Fenrulf paws are wide and powerful, designed to grip rocky surfaces safely and to provide good support on surfaces like snow and mud. There is a fine subcutaneous layer of "green matter" (a fatty substance with protein compounds similar to muscle) that insulates the feet against freezing and absorbs shock when running.

Fenrulfs have two coats and a layer of fat for protection from cold. Their rough outer coat is worn year round, but the sub-coat is shed during the summer and spring if the weather is warm enough. The layer of fat is usually about 3 to 4cm, but in the coldest regions can be as thick as 8cm.

Fur color is usually a bluish-grey, with white and gray thrown into the mix. Fenrulf fur colors can run a wide spectrum from jet black all the way to a sandy color, depending mostly on their surroundings. All Fenrulfs have black noses. Fenrulf eyes are usually green or blue, but brown is not uncommon either.

Fenrulfs have stocky snouts, with their eyes both in the front of their head. Their ears are pointed and relatively long. Fenrulfs have a large razor-sharp horn on their snouts which begins to grow when they are weaned and have cut their first adult teeth, and have pronounced canine teeth that are visible even when their mouths are closed.

== Reproduction ==

Fenrulfs are fertile once per year, towards the end of the cold months. Gestation takes 85-90 days. There are usually between three and five pups per litter.

== Behavior ==

Fenrulfs exhibit strong pack mentalities, and tend to travel in groups in the wild. It is almost impossible to tame a full-grown wild Fenrulf, as they tend to be significantly larger and stronger than a human and will not accept them as dominant. Only pups can be domesticated from wild stock. Most domestic stock are bred to be less aggressive, though the larger breeds trained in guarding and attack have been known to eat a human alive before they could be subdued.

== Domestication ==

On the [[Kronheim]] tundra, a Fenrulf is part of life. Most tundra nomads have a Fenrulf pup given to them at an early age, between 10 and 12 years. Fenrulfs live a long time, often longer than a tundra [[Ikronin]]. Companion Fenrulfs are often put down if their master dies.

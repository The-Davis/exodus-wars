The '''Ngrligru''' (''xenocaro furous''), known as the "Ologrians" by the [[Anthronoris]] and other human [[:Category:Races|races]], are an amphibious species originating somewhere in the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. They were first encountered by [[Pax Anthronoris]] in the [[180th Century BC]], who attempted to wipe them out. The Ngrligru fought back, forming the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]] as a defensive organization. Their response was so vicious that [[Pax Anthronoris]] fell, and the [[Anthronoris]] [[:Category:Races|race]] was split into the numerous human [[:Category:Races|races]] in the violent fall known as the [[Sundering]].

===Appearance===

Ngriligru average 0.9M in height, and 68KG in weight. There is also a sub-race of Ngrligru known as the [[Mtomigru]], or "scholars", which varies greatly in weight and height than the majority of the Ngrligru. Ngrligru are characterised by strong hind legs, a short body, webbed, clawed digits, protruding eyes and a long, sinewy tail. Because their head and spine are directly aligned, Ngrligru walking on their legs appear to be "hunched" over.

Ngrligru are strong swimmers and jumpers, with long hind legs and elongated ankle bones which give them a powerful kick for swimming, and excellent shock absorbers for jumping. They prefer to travel by water, which they slither and kick through at an extraordinary rate. On land, Ngrligru lope along at a quick, bouncing pace, often leaping several times their body length.

Ngrligru have a dorsal fin running along their back, with sharp spines protruding from their spines. There is a thick layer of fatty tissues cushioning the vertebrae from impacts from the doral spines. They also have a powerful caudal fin running along the top and bottom of their tails, which aids in swimming. The Ngrligru also have pectoral and pelvic fins, located on their forearms and hips, which have the same spines as their dorsal fin. They are used for guiding their movements when swimming, and for defense.

====Scale Colors====

Ngrligru are covered in a layer of skin and fatty tissue, which in turn is covered by several types of scales. The chest region is covered in ventral scales, which wrap around the pectoral and abdominal muscles, layering over each other. When lying flat on the ground, it gives the Ngrligru a comfortable surface to lie on. It is tough and armored, difficult to pierce with claws or teeth. Ventral scales are lighter in color than the rest of the body.

The arms, legs, back and head of the Ngrligru are covered in placoid scales, also known as denticles. These rough scales create vortexes in the water as they swim, and make swimming faster and easier. The denticles are often sharp, making it hard for a predator to grab onto a Ngrligru without harming itself. Placoid scales are dark, but can be colored with several patterns.

Ngrligru can be dozens of primary colors, secondary colors, and patterns. Their colors are usually inheritted among families within their Kabila. Green, black, gray, brown, and dark blue are common placoid colors. Yellow, gray, white, and light blue are common ventral colors. Ngrligru also have striped, mottled, spotted, flat or brindled marking schemes with as many as four colors.

====Fin Colors====

In addition to their body fins, Ngrligru have a series of defensive "crest" fins around their heads, particularly near the ears, the crown of the head, and around the neck. Male Ngrligru also have a number of fleshy carbuncles that hang from their chins, known as the "Ndevu."

Males' Ndevu number anywhere from two to ten. Normally, they are only two or three centimeters long, and the same color as their ventral scales. During Ngrligru mating season, the Ndevu lengthen to nearly eight centimeters, and turn bright red. The Ndevu secrete a powerful pheremone which encourages ovulation in Ngrligru females.

Female Ngrligru have larger, brighter crest fins, which curve backwards and are noteably sharper and more defined than male crests. The male crest is curved forwards, and while it is less sharp, the membrane between the crest ribs is much tougher and more resilient than the females.

Ngrligru fins and crests tend to be the same color as their ventral scales, but are approximately two shades brighter. In mating season, both male and female crests turn bright red.

====Eye Color====

Ngrligru eyes protrude from the top of their head, but are protected by a tough, warty ridge around the socket. The outer eyelid is covered in tough scales, allowing an Ngrligru to protect its eyes from damage simply by closing them.

Ngrligru also have a thick, but clear inner eyelid, allowing them to see while swimming underwater. The inner eyelid also acts as protection against the sun: Ngrligru are used to living underwater and direct sunlight can blind them.

Ngrligru eyes are usually brown or gold, with large pupils. They do not have any white membrane in their eyes. Other colors are less common, but blue and purple eyes have been noted before, particularly in the [[Mtomigru]] sub-species.

===Skeletal System===

Ngrligru have a skeleton with some bone and a lot of cartilage. Their skulls, limbs, spines and ribs are all bone. The rest of the body is protected by cartilage, thick in some areas and thinner in others. The stomach in particular is protected by thick layers of cartilage.

===Respiratory System===

Ngrligru are an amphibious species, comfortable on the land and water, but prefering water or at least very wet land. The Ngrligru have three lungs, all of which are lined with a membrane which can absorb oxygen from water or from air.

Ngrligru have several internal pressure bladders which they can fill with air, water, or completely deflate to compensate for pressure. Ngrligru are much less susceptible to changes in pressure than humans, but are more susceptible to explosive decompression. If their pressure bladders are full of air, an Ngrligru will literally explode when subjected to explosive decompression.

Ngrligru can dive hundreds of meters underwater. The deepest recorded Ngrligru swimming depth was a thousand meters underwater. Ngrligru are comfortable at depths of fifty to four hundred meters, and are happiest in shallow, lighted depths.

===Nervous System===

The Ngrligru have a lateral line which runs down their sides where the ventral and placoid scales meet. The lateral line is a highly sensitive nerve cluster, which can sense minute changes in water movement and air movement, giving Ngrligru an uncanny sense of proximity and movement. They rely on their lateral line senses almost as much as they rely on their eyes, and can fight or swim in the dark with little difficulty.

Ngrligru have a unique anatomical structure known as the Fahamu Node. These microscopic nerve clusters are distributed throughout the body, and serve as the memory centers of the Ngrligru. The nodes coordinate a massive gestahlt for the Ngrligru, giving it nearly perfect memory recall.

The nodes cannot be digested. When ingested by another Ngrligru, they float freely in the digestive system until they are absorbed into the bloodstream and eventually reattach to the new Ngrligru's nervous system. Eating another of its kind will literally give an Ngrligru a sense of the victim's memories. A new clutch of Ngrligru will usually eat their parents, giving them a head start on learning.

===Metabolism===

Ngrligru have a voracious appetite. Originally, they were an omnivorous species, living on aquatic plants and animals in the oceans of their homeworld. When the [[Anthronoris]] attempted to wipe them out, causing the war which became known as the [[Sundering]], the Ngrligru fought back with a vengeance. The Anthronoris responded with a deadly biological weapon, which infected the Ngrligru with a virus they called the "Njaa."

The Njaa altered the metabolism of the Ngrligru, destroying the digestive bacteria which allowed them to break down plant matter. It also altered their biochemistry, making their own flesh addictive to them, and giving them an unstoppable craving for meat. The Ngrligru began to feed on one another, but this backfired on the [[Anthronoris]] when the Ngrligru discovered their ability to share memories through feeding on one another.

The Ngrligru hunger could not be sated: they devoured every [[Anthronoris]] settlement they could find. The [[Mtomigru]] were finally able to develop a partial cure, restoring the sanity of the Ngrligru, but to this day they crave the flesh of their own species due to its addictive nature. Their craving for the flesh of others is under control, allowing them to deal peacefully with other species, or subjugate them instead of devouring them.

===Lifespan and Reproduction===

The Ngrligru have a potential lifespan of 200 years, but the average lifespan is less than 60 years. Many are eaten by their fellow Ngrligru. Others are eaten by the vicious predators found on most of their worlds. Thousands die in every clutch at birth, devoured by their siblings before they reach maturity.

Ngrligru have a number of mating seasons per year, depending on the planet they live on. When the water temperature peaks at 40 degrees celcius, the male Ngrligru begin secreting their sexual pheremones. This excites female Ngrligru into ovulating. They lay thousands of eggs per season, usually in clutches of two hundred per day. Unlike human females, Ngrligru female ovaries continuously grow new eggs throughout their lives.

Because their mating seasons are triggered by the water temperature rising to 40 degrees celsius, Ngrligru habitats and settlements tend to be found in areas with cooler water which rises to 40 degrees celsius only once or twice a year. Warmer settlements tend to be quickly overcrowded, and often self-destruct and devour themselves.

Ngrligru eggs are soft until fertilized by the males. The female lays a clutch of eggs in a mud bed in a secluded corner, and the male fertilizes them. Ngrligru males cover the eggs both in sperm and in a substance which covers the eggs in a tough shell. The Ngrligru male then buries the eggs.

Ngrligru young hatch with a sharp spine on their nose, which they use to escape their eggshell. They immediately devour as many of their siblings as they can, then scatter, descending on the nearest Ngrligru settlement and devouring as many adults as they can. Most young and healthy adults leave during hatching season, leaving the sick and elderly to be devoured, passing on valuable experience and information on to the next generation.

===Behavior===

The Ngrligru once had a normal fight or flight response, similar to that of a human. Since the Njaa was unleashed on them, they lost the "flight" response, overwhelmed by hunger. If confronted with a life or death situation, the [[Mtomigru]] cure is retarded by a vicious, ravenous hunger. An Ngrligru will devour its attacker or die trying.

Some Ngrligru can train themselves not to devour a defeated foe, but even those with the greatest self control will still take a bite or two out of their prisoners.

Adult Ngrligru have much greater self control over themselves when dealing with members of their immediate family. They are known to be friendly, even affectionate, with mature brothers, sisters, or surviving parents. If an Ngrligru dies of natural causes, its remains are reserved for its family to eat, passing on its memories to the ones it cared most about.

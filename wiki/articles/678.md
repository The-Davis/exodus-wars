== Events of 678AD ==

The findings of [[Siran Urstana]]’s team are passed along to the engineering teams rebuilding the [[Nechanos Tolesca]].  Many months are spent trying to replicate the structure of the original molecular communications system.  Finally, after months of fine-tuning the two devices and coordinating efforts via [[Hyperspace]] courier, the first message is sent from [[Archada]] in the [[Ularen System]] to [[Tolesca's Moon]] in the [[Plasala System]].  According to legend, the first transmission is simply "Desra ta arcunfio", translated "I hope this works!"  The alleged response is "Aun va!", or "Me too!"

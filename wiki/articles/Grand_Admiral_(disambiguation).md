The title of "Grand Admiral" is used to distinguish a position above an Admiral. It typically means that the officer holding the title is responsible for a great portion of their government's naval forces, if not the naval forces in their entirety.

Known uses:

* [[Ranks of the Royal Imperial Navy|Grand Admiral of the Empire]] - The [[Onyx Empire]] used the rank for their military commander-in-chief, who had control over the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] and had reports from the rest of the Imperial military branches.
* [[Ranks in the Prefecture Galactic Navy|Fleet Admiral of the Prefecture]] - While the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] renamed the title to "Fleet Admiral", the position retained all of its predecessor's naval responsibilities.

The HMS ''Judicator'' was one of the earlier ships built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

It was given hull designation CV-13, and commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2308]]. Its air arm squadrons were initially equipped with [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4 Ravens]], but eventually equipped with the [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|F-9 Valkyrie]], which was far superior in carrier operations.

When it was first launched, the HMS ''Judicator'' was placed under the command of Captain [[Julia Michaels]]. On January 17, [[2318]], the HMS ''Judicator'' was placed under the command of [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]], who retained command of the ship for the rest of its career. At the outbreak of the [[Imperial Civil War]], the HMS ''Judicator'' defected to the [[Prefecture]], and was eventually re-commissioned as the [[FDS Judicator|FDS ''Judicator'']].

=== Commodore Michaels' Command ===

Under the command of Commodore [[Julia Michaels]], the HMS ''Judicator'' earned a reputation for efficiency and smooth operation under pressure.

==== 2317 ====

In January of [[2317]], Commodore Michaels oversaw the conquest of the [[Lupei System]], commanding a task force led by the ''Judicator'' and made up of a total of sixteen [[:Category:Warships|warships]]. The three day campaign had little opposition from the remains of colonial authorities assigned by the [[United Earth Alliance]]. A strategic nuclear bombardment from the task force wiped the city of Santa Guadalupe off the face of [[Kalidasa]], and brought on the hasty unconditional surrender from the colonial authority. The ''Judicator'' remained on station for two months, overseeing the installation of permanent security forces and the now-famous shipyards of [[Kalidasa]].

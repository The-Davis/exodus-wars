The Km-5 Drache is the [[Mesarthrim Federation|Mesarthrim Federations]] version of the [[Cinaed Accords]] [[Cinaed Neoframe Project|TA001-Neoframe]].  At the request of the [[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]], [[Schwarzestein Schiffswerften]] exercised the option to purchase manufacturing rights from the Cinead Accords.  Production of the model began instantly.  [[Prefecture]] analysts observed the Neoframe's progress and exercised their own purchasing rights later that year.

''Zaken Huern'' (born September 18, [[2228]]), affectionately known by his wife as "Zak", was the husband of [[Kian Huern]] and the father of [[Dirken Huern]]. As a "Moonlit" man, he had neither right nor privilege under the laws of the [[Lorithos]], but after the end of the [[Burning Wars]], and the death of [[Kian Huern]], there was no one left. Zak Huern ruled his people as a regent until his son [[Dirken Huern]] was old enough to take the throne.

Zak Huern is considered the savior of the [[Lorithos]] for insisting on the funding and completion of the [[Baihros Estin Fohrvik]], the [[Lorithos]] faster than light system of travel. Without it, it is doubtful that the [[Meroniri]] would have made contact.

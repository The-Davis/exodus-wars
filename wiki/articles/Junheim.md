'''Junheim''' is the third planet in the [[Raegard System]], and home to the [[Reigen]] people, with a population of roughly 14.5 billion. Junheim has five continents.

== Kenvara ==

The continent of Kenvara is bordered by the Charmant Ocean on the west and south, and by the Hygrest Ocean to the east. It is the largest continent on Junheim, covering about 35 million square kilometers of the planet's surface. The world's capital city, Rolsucht, is found nestled in the fjords along the east coast of Kenvara.

'''Venus''' is one of the inhabitted planets in the [[Sol System]].  It was terraformed by the [[United Earth Alliance]] over the course of a hundred years, and had a stable biosphere in the mid-[[23rd Century]].

== Divas Satellite ==

The Divas satellite was a collection of large Kuiper belt objects, moved into a Venus orbit and smashed together until their gravity held them in place.  The massive project was one of the most expensive ones in Terran history, but it resulted in stable tidal forces for Venus.  The name of the moon, Divas, was derived from one of the Roman gods of wealth--a reference to the crippling expense of the project.  The Divas project was so expensive that the United Earth Alliance stopped any further terraformation efforts and focused on colonizing already-inhabitable worlds.

== Imperial Occupation ==

Because Venus was a relatively unimportant world with no military use, it had no defenses and was easily conquered by the [[Onyx Empire]].  It enjoyed a rather hospitable occupation, as most Imperial troops were stationed on Earth.  Venusian citizens were left mostly to themselves, with occasional Imperial inspections to arrest known resistance members and prevent weapons from being shipped off-world.  Other than tight border restrictions, Venus under United Earth Alliance jurisdiction and Venus under Imperial occupation were almost identical.

== Liberation ==

The "liberation" of Venus was one of the lowest priorities of the coalition forces, and when it finally happened it was a rather quiet affair.  The governor of the planet had already drafted and signed a formal letter of surrender and handed it over to the first coalition soldier to land on the planet.  Governor Ruth Cameron was one of the few Imperial governors to not even be tried for war crimes.  For her role in the benevolent occupation, Governor Cameron was simply stripped of all powers and allowed to resume life as a normal citizen.

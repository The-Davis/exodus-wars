The government of the [[Sol System Authority]] is little more than a glorified group of office clerks with guns. The Authority was designed originally so that no member of the [[Alliance of Planets]] would claim [[Earth]] as sovereign territory after the [[Second Exodus War]]. Most of the government exists merely to oversee the rebuilding of the world's infrastructure and economy.

==Charter==

When the last forces of the [[Onyx Empire]] surrendered to [[Anthony Hadrian]] at Mount McKinely, [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] formally turned over [[Earth]] as a whole to the [[Alliance of Planets]] coalition forces led by [[Andreas Tischler]] and [[Scott Sanburn]].

The [[Onyx Empire]]'s occupation of [[Terra]] had always been the thorn in the side that galvanized its enemies into action, and [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] saw the extreme importance of the world to the [[Terran]] people. As a representative of one of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s colonies, he had the rare wisdom and foresight to realize that bringing back the old government could spark another war when the now-independent colonies refused to recognize [[Earth]]'s sovereignty over them.

Sanburn wrote a lengthy proposal to the [[Alliance of Planets High Council]], recommending that all Member States of the [[Alliance of Planets]] formally cede any sovereign rights to the conquered territory, and that any who were able would begin supplying personnel and material support to rebuild an independent world government for [[Earth]].

The [[Sanburn Plan]] eventually became the [[Sol System Charter]], which established a provisional government with a phased plan to bring back executive, legislative and judicial branches of power to general elections over time. Because the world government was little more than a rubber stamp approval on outside actions when it was formed, the new provisional government became known as the "[[Sol System Authority]]."

==Executive Branch==

Planetary Recovery Administration

The Planetary Recovery Administration is in charge of the cleanup of [[Earth]]'s environment and reconstruction of its economy and industry.

Operations of the PRA are handled entirely by the [[Sol System Authority]], without any outside oversight. While all of its resources are tied up on [[Earth]], the PRA's mission includes relief aid to other planets in need of assistance and comfort.

The PRA purchased several dozen mobile hospitals from the Prefecture's Mobile Medical Corps, which it deploys to the newest opened recovery zones. As most of the refugees and survivors have been recovered, the PRA's main role is rebuilding. For that, it has the Ferret, a multi-role excavation and construction vehicle.

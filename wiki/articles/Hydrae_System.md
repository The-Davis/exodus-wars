The '''Hydrae System''' consists of the star R Hydrae, its six planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from R Hydrae, the planets are Melkol, [[Ueda]], [[Estelis]], Shiana, Obera, and Tiante.

== Stellar Navigation ==

R Hydrae is the Hydrae System's only star. R Hydrae is a class G3W yellow dwarf. The Hydrae System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Hydrae is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Melkol ===

Melkol (0.3AU) is a tiny ball of rock in a barely stable orbit around R Hydrae. It was charted in [[2321]] along with the rest of the system, but no survey missions were ever launched. Orbital scans showed no useful resources. 

=== Ueda ===

[[Ueda]] (0.7AU) is a sandy, parched, inhospitable world. It was not considered inhabitable, as it had almost no biosphere and a barely sustainable atmosphere. After ratification of the Prefecture's Constitution, an uncomfortable environment was required for legislative sessions. Ueda was chosen for its proximity to Estelis.

=== Estelis ===

[[Estelis]] (1.1AU) is an unspoiled paradise, and the spiritual successor to [[Torimur]]. By law, only survivors of Torimur and their natural-born descendants may set foot on the surface. Several [[Haven Class Stations]] were placed in orbit by the [[Prefecture]] during the [[Imperial Civil War]], and they remain in orbit today. Estelis has one moon, Sidnum.

=== Shiana ===

Shiana (3.4AU) is a large gas giant, made up mostly of hydrogen, but has an unusual number of of water deposits floating in the atmosphere.

=== Obera ===

Obera (6.7AU) is a large gas giant.

=== Tiante ===

Tiante (7.5AU) is a medium-sized gas giant.

== History ==

The Hydrae System was discovered by Commodore [[Marianne Delray]] in [[2319]], while scouting for new worlds for the [[Onyx Empire]] to exploit. Instead of reporting the value of an inhabitable planet, Commodore Delray falsified her report to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and instead gave the real report to [[Anthony Hadrian]] in person. Shortly afterwards, Commodore Delray's command, the [[HMS Auspicious|HMS ''Auspicious'']], was "destroyed" by enemy ships when Hadrian delivered a second false report to [[Lucius Black]]. The ''Auspicious'' spent the rest of the war in the Hydrae System, installing new [[Haven Class Stations]] and ferrying [[Prefecture]]-loyal personnel into the System.

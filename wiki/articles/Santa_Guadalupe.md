The city of '''Santa Guadalupe''' was a key farming city and one of the largest population centers on [[Kalidasa]].

During the [[Onyx Empire]]'s aggressive expansion in the [[Second Exodus War]], Kalidasa was blockaded by Task Force: Judicator, led by Commodore [[Julia Michaels]]' [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']]. Rather than deal with costly partisan fighting and a ground campaign, Michaels authorized the nuclear bombardment of the city of Santa Guadalupe, killing nearly 450,000 civilians. The city remains highly radioactive and is still off-limits to travellers.

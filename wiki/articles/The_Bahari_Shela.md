===Overview===

The stories about the Bahari Shela are many.  Most are completely false--stories to scare new Conclave recruits.  Others are silly--lies putting down the Ngrligru species, as triumphant soldiers are inclined to do.  One story is true: the Bahari Shela was never a human ship.

In the year 2207, the Ngrligru tribe Kabila Nyoka sent an expedition into deep space in an attempt to recapture some of the planets they raided in the Sundering.  This expedition was led by Grolk Kyhra and the Bahari Shela.  The returning Blackis Empire was intercepted by the Bahari Shela and its two Kifa Class Frigates.  Robert Marshall managed to defeat them--though no one is certain how.

The Bahari Shela was towed to Alpha Centauri and put in storage while the Empire went on its conquests.  Imperial scientists finally got it running again, but only after the fall of the Empire.  The Conclave of Blackis managed to keep the warship from the Blackis Prefecture.

===Capabilities===

The Ngrligru weapons of the Bahari Shela aren't understood well enough to use in battle.  The first (and last) test firing in 2319 destroyed twelve of the Royal Imperial Navy's Vigilis Class frigates, along with the spaceport where the Bahari Shela was docked.  Lucius Black promptly cancelled every repair project, and told them to simply install Blackis technology over the Ngrligru weapons.

For main weapons, the Bahari Shela uses stolen Meroniri Coilguns.  The weapons are quite capable against warships.  Besides the main turrets, the Bahari Shela also carries multiple Neutron Cannons and missile launchers.

The drive system of the Bahari Shela is the only original Ngrligru system still functioning.  Its exact mechanics are unknown, but Conclave scientists have determined that it speeds up light in a bubble around it, and then accelerates to many times normal light speed.  It's roughly twice as fast as an Archadian Ramius-Dawkins drive.  The Bahari Shela had a drive system similar to the Meroniri Tesseract Drive, but that was destroyed in the 2207 battle.

When it was built, the Bahari Shela could carry two Vita'safina craft--similar in size and power to a humanoid frigate.  It also had extensive Rig, infantry and fighter capacity, but the Conclave of Blackis doesn't have the troops and vehicles to use the facilities.  The original frigates were destroyed over a hundred years ago, so their docking ports have been converted for freighters to load up the Bahari Shela with supplies.

===Deployment===

The repaired Bahari Shela was deployed in March of 2324, and Grolk Kyhra took command shortly afterwards.  Most of his crew is Ngrligru, but there are many humans aboard as well.

Grolk Kyhra and the Bahari Shela have been seen in Meroniri territory, but his mission and motives--both Conclave and personal--are unknown.  Meronir's Republic is the only humanoid government that borders to the territories of the Ngrligru Kabilas.

'''Altoeing Foundries''', based on Mars, is a large transport aviation company, responsible for the development of many transport aircraft. It was noted during the [[Second Exodus War]] for its role in assisting the [[Onyx Empire]] in developing the larger airframe used on the [[F-11 Stuka]]. After the war, the company continued to manufacture some key parts for the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], but focused mostly on civilian transport aircraft.

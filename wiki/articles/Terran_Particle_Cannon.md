Particle Weapons Particle Weapons rely on a high amount of energy to accelerate a stream of particles. The stream hits the target causing a wide dispersion of accelerated nuclei. The nuclei tend to cause massive damage to electrical systems and armor. Particle Weapons are extremely effective against shielding, as neither pure energy shields or pure particle shields can stop them. Particle Projection Cannon - The PPC is a very powerful medium-to-long ranged weapon. It does 10T of damage and is the primary weapon of many support Mechs. Extended Range Particle Projection Cannon - The ER PPC is a very powerful long-to-extreme ranged weapon. It does 15T of damage and is the primary weapon of many heavy Mechs. Flamer - While technically an energy weapon, the Flamer behaves most like a particle weapon. It does 2T of damage and is very useful in destroying enemy cities. The Flamer is a short-ranged weapon. Heavy Flamer - While technically an energy weapon, the Heavy Flamer behaves most like a particle weapon. It does 4T of damage and is very useful in destroying enemy cities. The Heavy Flamer is a medium-ranged weapon.

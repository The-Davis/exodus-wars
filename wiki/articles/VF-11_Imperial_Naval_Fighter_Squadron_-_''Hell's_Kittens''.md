The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s eleventh fighter squadron, VF-11, nicknamed "Hell's Kittens", was the main space and air combat group for the [[HMS Cougar|HMS ''Cougar'']], and was noted for its odd nickname for the carrier launch system ("the carrier's having kittens").

Hell's Kittens was under the command of Captain [[Gloria Felix]]. It was decorated with the [[Imperial Battle Star]] in [[2318]].

*Capital: Athens, Greece, [[Terra]]
*Official language: Greek
*Government: Socialist Republic
*Premier: Karolos Benaki (Last)
*Formation: Circa [[3000BC]]
*Dissolved: October 17, [[2015]]
*Population: 13 million (estimated 2015 level)

The '''Socialist Republic of Greece''' was the Greek government that formed after the [[Greek Civil War]], a struggle between the [[Communists]] and the Hellenists. The war ended with the Battle of Grammos-Vitsi, where the Hellenist army was defeated.

The [[Communist]] regime lasted until early [[2015]], when the people of Greece began to protest [[Communist]] rule when they saw the prosperity of the unified world government. The last Premier, Karolos Benaki, capitulated to popular demand and stepped down on October 17, [[2015]]. His last act as Premier was to sign the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]], dissolving the Greek state into the world government.

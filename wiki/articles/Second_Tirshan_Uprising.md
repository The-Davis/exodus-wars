The '''Second Tirshan Uprising''' began in [[2334]], and marked the first major internal threat to the [[United Centusi States]] since the failed [[First Tirshan Uprising]] was violently suppressed in December of [[2327]].  Mass demonstrations were followed by riots and then outright rebellion as the majority of the population of [[Comur]], as well as elements of the [[United Centusi States Heer|Heer]] and [[United Centusi States Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]], led by [[Arnold Stadt]], declared independence from [[William Camp|William Camp's]] government on [[Mesar]] and declared the formation of the [[Mesarthrim Federation]].  

== Background ==

In December 2327, [[United Centusi States Heer|Heer]] forces loyal to [[William Camp|William Camp's]] government on [[Mesar]] arrived in the [[Tirshan System]] and put down a small-scale revolt on [[Comur]].  Although most of the population agreed with the revolt's leaders and their opposition to war with the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]], few openly defied loyalist forces.  The revolt, later to be labeled the [[First Tirshan Uprising]] was put down in a matter of weeks, with most revolutionary partisans killed, and the planet's governor [[Frederick Degause]] arrested and eventually executed for treason.

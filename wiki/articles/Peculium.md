The '''Peculium''' is the [[Prefecture]]'s national currency. It was established in [[2323]] to replace the [[Imperial Credit]] and is based on the [[gold standard]].

==History==

The [[Prefecture]] established the '''Peculium''' standard of currency to replace the [[Imperial Credit]] on June 1st, [[2323]]. While the [[Royal Imperial Bank]]'s sabotage of all electronic databases showing a person's holdings in credits spelled economic ruin for many, the [[Prefecture]] created a formula based on the real value of a person's holdings to determine a fair compensation in Peculiums. Many wealthy upper-class citizens were underpaid, due to most of their assets being in [[Imperial Credits]] rather than real property. Still, the program was somewhat successful, and prevented total economic disaster for the worlds that were once under [[Empire|Imperial]] rule. By [[2324]] the Peculium was a valued commodity and was recognized by banks throughout the [[Orion Arm]].

Unlike the [[Imperial Credit]], which was entirely computer based, the Peculium is based on  metal coinage. The Prefecture Department of the Treasury decided that making a government-run electronic exchange system would not inspire confidence in the new economy in light of what happened with the [[Royal Imperial Bank]]. Instead, they went back to the roots of galactic commerce.

The history of money is a colorful one, but one color dominates: gold. For centuries, the [[Terran]] economy had been a gold-dominated one. During the latter half of the [[20th Century]] and onwards into the [[24th Century]], paper money and electronic transfers were the foundation of currency. This system, known as [[fiat currency]], was used by most governments with the notable exception of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]]'s [[Likvada]].

The Prefecture decided to return to the roots of currency and adopted the [[gold standard]]. While the use of the [[gold standard]] does have the weakness of being unable to insulate a national economy from an international recession, it brings about deflation and prevents hyperinflation of currency.

The new [[gold standard]] set a gram of gold as the standard rate of exchange. The hundred Peculium coin has one gram of gold in it, and the gold content of each additional coin is based on this weight.

==Coins==
*Quarter Peculium - The 1/25P coin has 0.0025g of gold in its core. It is round in shape, and is coated with nickel and colored copper. It is 20mm in diameter. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman god Minerva, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with a raven in flight and the legend "One Quarter Peculium".
*Half Peculium - The 1/2P coin has 0.005g of gold in its core. It is round in shape, and is coated with nickel and colored silver. It is 25mm in diameter. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman god Mercury, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with a flock of five ravens in flight and the legend "Half Peculium".
*One Peculium - The 1P coin has 0.01g of gold in its core. It is round in shape, and is coated with nickel and colored blue. It is 30mm in diameter. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman goddess Fortuna, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with the seal of the [[Prefecture]] and the legend "1P".
*Five Peculium - The 5P coin has 0.05g of gold in its core. It is rectangular in shape, coated with nickel and colored red. It is 40mm high and 80mm wide. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman god Mars, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with the [[Jerome Black (BG-1)|FDS ''Jerome Black'']] in flight, and the legend "5P."
*Ten Peculium - The 10p coin has 0.1g of gold in its core. It is rectangular in shape, coated with nickel and colored green. It is 50mm high and 90mm wide. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman god Diana, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with a phoenix bird clutching a bundle of arrows, and the legend "10P."
*Twenty Peculium - The 20p coin has 0.2g of gold in its core. It is rectangular in shape, coated with nickel and colored yellow. It is 60mm high and 100mm wide. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of the Roman god Jupiter, and the reverse side of the coin is stamped with a running wolf, and the legend "20P."
*Hundred Peculium - The 100p coin is minted from 1g of pure gold. It is minted in the form of an equilateral triangle, 50mm to a side, and is the only Peculium coin to be gold in color. The obverse side of the coin is stamped with the bust of [[Jerome Black]], and the legend "Suffer not the Despair of Poverty" (a phrase [[Jerome Black|Black]] was said to have quoted during the early, hard years of the [[Torimur]] colony). The reverse side of the coin is stamped with a picture of the [[Consulis Sanctum]], and the legend "100P."

All coins are stamped with the legend "[[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]", and the Prefecture's motto, "Freedom before Security, Death before Tyranny."

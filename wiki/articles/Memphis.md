The city of '''Memphis''' rises above the Mississippi River on the fourth Chickasaw Bluff just below the mouth of the Wolf River.

From [[2315]] until [[2323]], the city served as the capital of [[Earth]] and of the [[Onyx Empire]]. The [[Obsidian Palace]] and the [[Onyx Throne]] are still located in the city, largely unspoiled by the [[Second Exodus War]]. After the end of the war, the planetary capital of Earth was moved to [[Rio de Janeiro]].

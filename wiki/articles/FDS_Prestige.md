The FDS ''Prestige'' was the eighth ship in the [[Victory Class Battleship|Victory class]] line of [[:Category:Battleships|battleships]]. It was given the hull designation BG-9.

The ''Prestige'' was placed under the command of Captain Michael Hill, and had a quiet shakedown cruise and patrol assignment for the first two years of its career.  In [[2327]], it served in some early action in the [[Tempest War]].  It was later ordered to set up headquarters over [[Kufestin]] as part of the defenses for the [[Tavou District]].

In [[2330]], the [[Omicron Carinae System]] was invaded by the [[Mesarthrim]].  The ''Prestige'' attempted to prevent the bombing of [[Adelson]], but was destroyed when a trio of Mesartrhim cruisers slipped past its escorts and engaged the ship in close combat.  The ''Prestige'' was out-maneuvered and out-gunned, and found its defenses compromised in a matter of seconds.  Half the crew died in a series of hull breaches.  Captain Hill ordered the ship evacuated, and died setting off the self-destruct system.  Only a quarter of the crew survived their interment in Prisoner of War camps on [[Mesar]].

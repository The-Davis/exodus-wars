The XF-3 program continued using the airframe developed originally for the XF-1, but made several major modifications, such as replacing the previous models' turbojet with a [[ramjet]] engine for atmospheric flight.

Using the [[ramjet]], the XF-3-1 was able to achieve escape velocity and made two successful flights into orbit, but could not carry enough fuel for the ion engine to perform complex maneuvers in space.

The XF-3-2 had a hastily-modified fuel system which the designers at the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] hoped would allow for greated fuel pressure within the airframe. Unfortunately, the second prototype blew up on reentry. The XF-3 program was scrubbed in favor of the upcoming [[XF-4 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-4]] program. The XF-3-1 prototype was stripped down to the airframe and reused in the next program.

== Events of 2332 ==
The Tempest War reaches its bloodiest point when the Prefecture and Ikronin invade Mesar itself. An entire Legion is destroyed in the initial landing. After twelve solid months of brutal battle, the allied forces of the Prefecture, Ikronin, and Auellal League have barely taken a third of Mesar's population centers.

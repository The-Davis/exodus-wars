[[Rahn Industries]]' debut into the warship industry was the massively successful ''Avenger'', which proved to be an excellent battlecruiser design. With the [[Prefecture]] in full-fledged rebellion and open war with the [[Onyx Empire]], Rahn Industries took an enormous financial risk in developing the Avenger, and [[Justin Rahn]] took enormous personal risk when he committed acts of treason against the [[Lucius Black|Emperor]]. The gambit proved to be a financial boon to the company, and a military advantage to the Prefecture; enough ships were completed to give the Prefecture's naval forces an edge they desperately needed.

== Development ==

With most experienced [[Terran]] shipwrights still loyal to the Empire, the Prefecture was in danger of falling behind in the arms race. Thanks to the discretion of Commodore [[Marianne Delray]], the Prefecture had a safe haven to build ships in the [[Hydrae System]], but had no designers with the skill to incorporate the new technologies that the Empire was rapidly incorporating into their naval arsenal.

[[Anthony Hadrian]] began having his most loyal [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] agents searching for potential allies in the industrial sector. [[Talaris Roi]] found an unlikely candidate; a freighter designer operating out of [[Tejat]]. Justin Rahn had a small business, but big dreams.

After being passed over twice for major shipping contracts with the Empire, and civilian contracts nearly dried up, Rahn Industries was on the brink of bankruptcy. With nothing to lose, Justin Rahn leaped at the chance to design a new ship class, despite the chance that he would be killed if the Empire ever found out.

Rahn designed a beautiful ship. The flowing lines of the Avenger starkly contrasted the design philosophies of [[Reg Brenstein]] which were visible in the Empire's ship designs. Construction went at a breakneck pace, and two ships, the [[FDS Avenger|FDS ''Avenger'']] and [[FDS Omaha|FDS ''Omaha'']], were completed by November of [[2322]].

On November 6, 2322, the FDS ''Avenger'' and FDS ''Omaha'' were launched into service, under the command of Admiral [[Jason Bremen]]. Bremen, determined to run the ships through their paces, sought out and ambushed a full carrier group, led by the [[HMS Irtysh|HMS ''Irtysh'']]. A barrage of shots from the battlecruisers' new Hellguns hulled the ''Irtysh'' before it could launch fighters, and a torpedo spread scattered the frigate escorts before they could establish an effective lane of fire. With their superior firepower and defenses, the battlecruisers overwhelmed the ''Irtysh'''s destroyer escorts before the frigates could regroup, then picked off the frigates one by one. The results were undeniable: the technology of the new battlecruiser completely outclassed the Empire's naval arsenal.

After Dr. [[Erika Strout]] defected to the Prefecture, she added the new [[Terran Mass Drivers|Relativistic Mass Driver]] to the Avenger's arsenal.

The Prefecture requested as many battlecruisers as possible, but only one more--the [[FDS Drake|FDS ''Drake'']]--was completed by the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

After the war, when the Federated Districts of the Prefecture became a legitimate government, Rahn Industries received lucrative contracts for thirty battlecruisers. Under new laws passed by the Prefecture, Rahn Industries was permitted to export the design (minus several key technologies) to other [[:Category:Nations|nations]].

== Design ==

The Avenger is built around a standard frame and many standard systems, but some are left up to the purchasing navy to deliver. The Federated Districts of the Prefecture uses a trio of Berkeley fusion reactors, while the [[United Centusi States]] uses a single Cybeck fusion reactor. These specialized parts must be built by their appropriate sub-contractors and delivered to Rahn Industries shipyards to be installed.

One standard component on the Avenger class is a trio of MAC-5 "Hellguns", one of the weapons designed and built in-house by Rahn Industries. The weapon debuted on the FDS ''Avenger'', and shots fired from the powerful cannon system accounted for half of the kills in its performance trial. Each Hellgun has a wide firing arc, one above and one below the ship. The Hellgun outclassed the earlier Magnetic Accelerator Cannons used by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], compacting the design into a turret and significantly increasing exit velocity.

[[Image:Avenger Class Battlecruiser Side View.png|right|200px]] The Avenger has firing controls for a fixed main weapon, fifty-two torpedo launchers, and eighty point-defense weapons. Firing control can be linked to automatic tracking systems, set to firing-lane patterns, locked in one direction, or manually controlled by a member of the crew. The Prefecture uses an RMD-1 relativistic mass driver as its primary weapon, the CIWS-5 for point defense, and a variety of anti-warship torpedos, bombardment ordinance, and anti-fighter missiles in its torpedo tubes.

[[Image:Avenger Class Battlecruiser Front and Bottom View.png|right|200px]]
Rahn Industries offers composite armor plating of its own design, but the Prefecture has Series-10 composites delivered for installation. Because shield technologies are so unique to each navy, Rahn Industries does not include any shields by default, but has shield control systems that can accept most shield systems with little modification.

Propulsion comes from five GCD-1R "Albany" gravity cavitation drives. Rahn Industries control systems have been unable to work well with other systems. If a customer wants a different drive system, they have to install it themselves. By default, the engines are rated at 36KPH per second at 100% safe reactor output. [[Mesarthrim]] reactors have been known to work at higher speeds.

All systems are tied into the C<sup>4</sup>ISTAR, the Computerized Command, Control, Communications, Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition and Reconnaissance. The system is supplied as hardware only, on a series of powerful processors from the Calpaca research centers. Software varies, but the Prefecture has been known to install the mysterious Prism-class AI, which has seen widespread use throughout their military since the [[Battle of Kronheim]]. The nature of the AI is one of the most closely-guarded secrets of the Prefecture military.

The Avenger line is extremely profitable to Rahn Industries, which has billed all of its customers a total of two trillion Peculiums to date for delivered Avenger class cruisers.

Unlike the [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]], which relies heavily on robotics, the Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr still relies on human operators for its [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] and [[:Category:Starships|starships]].  Their ships patrol the space around their star systems, while their aircraft ply the skies.

With its limited territory, [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] has a significant concentration of its forces in one place.  While the full power of any of the other powers in the [[Njiorin Sector]] could overwhelm the Lorithos, most other powers would not be able to concentrate enough military power to attack them unmolested.  Additionally, the Lorithos have excellent relations with the [[Alliance of Planets]] in general and the unassailable [[Meroniri Terinasi]] in particular.

== History ==

The Ailen Suvak was born in the fires of the [[Burning Wars]]. Early on, the would-be heirs took to the skies, and by the end of the war enormous spaceships were battling in orbit. As soon as they took the reins of power, [[Dirken Huern]] and [[Sanor Cai]] took steps to change the way the Ailen Suvak operated.

The Ailen Suvak is much more mobile and streamlined than most modern navies, operating in small units that would barely qualify as task forces in another nation's navy. Rather than a huge organization system, everything is under a single, cohesive chain-of-command. The Ailen Suvak is led by the [[Hishai]].


== Organization ==

The highest level of organization in the Ailen Suvak is a Teska Nok, which has seventy-two warships. The Teska Nok are rarely grouped together in one unit. There are three Teska Nok in the Ailen Suvak, one for each of the [[Lorithos]] worlds. Each Teska Nok has six Kruz Orgas, and also includes [[Kihtor]] or space stations and their fighter complements.

A Kruz Orga is a group of twelve warships and ninety fighter craft.

An individual warship has three divisions in command known as Worsas (watches), which allow the crew and officers to rotate shifts and get a proper amount of sleep in non-combat situations.

The lowest level of organization is a Senal Orga, which is made up of ten fighters.

== Uniforms ==

The Ailen Suvak has three uniforms: a Duty Uniform and a Dress Uniform, which are used by ship crews, and a Pilot Uniform. All uniforms include a shoulder patch with their branch of service and their rank insignia.

The Dress Uniform is an extravagant one: blue slacks with a gold stripe, a tan shirt, a black cape with silver trim, and a silver belt. Enlisted wear a wide-brimmed hat with a blue gem set in the front, while officers wear the same hat with a gold brim, a diamond in the front, and black braiding on their shoulders. The Dress Uniform is worn with full rank insignia and any badges and medals the soldier has received.

The Duty Uniform is designed for comfort and flexibility during duty. The undergarments are designed to absorb moisture and dry quickly, and are treated to prevent irritation and smell in confined quarters. The uniform itself has black trousers with blue trim, black boots, and a tan jacket with blue trim. The only patches allowed on a utillity uniform are the branch of service patch, and rank patches.

The Pilot Uniform is designed to allow the wearer to survive in a vacuum, if necessary, among other things. The first portion is a one-piece black jumpsuit, made of a special fabric that is lightweight, flexible, and can be sealed when the boots, gloves, and helmet are worn. The boots and gloves are gray, and the helmet is gold with black Wing insignia on the sides. The helmet is attached to a two-part life support system. One plugs into the aircraft's life support system. Should the aircraft's systems suffer from catastrophic failure, a secondary life support system attached to the suit kicks in, providing the pilot with breathable air and a comfortable temperature for 6 hours, or simply breathable air for 12 hours. A gray carrying web is attached to the outside of the jumpsuit, which gives the pilot hookups for a sidearm, survival gear, an emergency communications system, and a parachute. 

== Ships ==

The names of commissioned ships of the Ailen Suvak start with "PAS", designating 'Prekohr's Ailen Suvak'. Additionally, each ship is given a letter-based hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and a hull number. The names of ships are officially selected by the [[Hishai]].

The Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr takes the role of assault, defense, and troop transportation. With no separate Navy branch, the Lorithos Ailen Suvak has warships, fighters, bombers and transports at its disposal.

The smallest Ailen Suvak warship is the Gaivik Intelligence Corvette, a "snooper" variety of ship, used both by the ASTF and the ICD. The majority of the Ailen Suvak's warships are Blijrok Frigates and Svenj Light Destroyers. Just as important but fewer in number are the Riiktar Medium Destroyers and the Omarr Heavy Destroyers. Rarest of all are the Broktai Command Ships.

The Ailen Suvak's vessels are not just for slug-fests. The Gatrir Heavy Freighter is a common sight in the space-lanes, bringing trade to and from the [[Lorithos]] worlds. The Oltvak Carrier and its fighters are a vital part of the defense of [[Lorithan]] and the colonies.

The Vadrie Light Interceptor is the most common [[Lorithos]] fighter, followed closely by the Milrok Heavy Fighter. The Eituk Assault Fighter is rare in the colonies, but a common sight in defense of Lorithan itself.

== Weapon Systems ==

One notable difference between the Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr and any other Navy/Air force is the complete lack of nuclear and other weapons of mass destruction. Ailen Suvak warship commanders are reluctant to bombard a world with even conventional weapons. Memories of the [[Burning Wars]] are too fresh in their minds.

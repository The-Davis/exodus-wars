A '''Moonlit Family''' is a [[Lorithos]] social term, referring to a female-dominated family.

A typical Moonlit Family is headed by a female Lorithos, who has two husbands. By law, only a woman born during night hours can be the head of a Moonlit Family, and only men born during night hours can be married into a Moonlit Family. Men and women born during the day must be part of a [[Lorithos Sunlit Family|Sunlit Family]].

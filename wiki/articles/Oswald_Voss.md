''Oswald Erik Voss'' (born March 11, [[2267]]) was a dour veteran of the [[United Earth Alliance]] military, assigned to the [[Mars]] defense forces. He served the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] loyally, but had no battle experience and was passed over for opportunities in the [[Njiorin Sector]] during the [[Heritage Crisis]]. Voss was still stationed in the [[Sol System]] when the [[Onyx Empire]] invaded, and was forced into service in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

Voss' [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] career ended when he was captured by [[Mesarthrim]] [[Myt'gaul]], and he was imprisoned in a [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] prisoner camp on [[Caledon]] until the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]]. During the war, his knowledge of naval tactics caught the eye of [[Steven Cue]], who appointed Voss to an important position in the [[Auellal League]].

===Early Life===

Oswald was born on [[Mars]] to [[Ernest Voss|Ernest]] and [[Alina Voss]]. Oswald led a normal, healthy childhood and was eventually enrolled in the [[Mars]] Technical University, where he studied [[Hyperspace]] Theory and majored in interstellar navigation.

===Military Career===

Just prior to graduation, Oswald found himself in some trouble--and rather serious debts--with several local "organizations". Rather than risk his life or his family, he fled the area and enlisted with the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]]'s Space Agency, where his advanced background in interstellar navigation was put to good use. Before long, he was accepted to officer candidacy school, and was allowed to apply his earned University credits towards a degree.

Oswald Voss graduated officer candidacy school and was granted a commission, but assigned almost exclusively to [[Sol System]] posts. He served a brief tour of duty in the [[Tau Ceti System]], but was denied his requested transfer to the [[Titan System]] to see action during the [[Heritage Crisis]].

===Imperial Invasion===

By [[2314]], Voss was back in the [[Sol System]]. During the [[Onyx Empire]]'s sudden invasion in [[2315]], Voss was able to keep his ship and crew alive despite the overwhelming odds against them, and was able to surrender his ship intact. After serving two years in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s labor camps, he was forced into service in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

With the [[United Earth Alliance]] gone, and after suffering at the hands of the psychological torture experts of the [[Omegas]], Oswald Voss became a loyal Imperial commander, and was given the command of the [[HMS Victorious|HMS ''Victorious'']], eventually rising to the rank of Rear Admiral.

===Two Civil Wars===

Voss was assigned command of the defense of the [[Proxima Procyon System]]. During the later years of the [[Second Exodus War]], Voss fought off an important [[Mesarthrim]] offensive, but later fled into deep space.

While on the run, Voss was located and attacked by ships under the command of [[Jordan Marshall]] and [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]], but he managed to kill [[Jordan Marshall|Marshall]] before escaping again.

When the [[HMS Victorious|HMS ''Victorious'']] was nearly out of supplies, Voss risked returning to inhabitted space, and was located by ships of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. Voss and his crew were captured by [[Myt'gaul]], and placed in a prison camp on [[Caledon]].

During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], Oswald Voss met [[Steven Cue]], who was liberating multiple [[Auellal Political Party]] dissident prisoners on [[Caledon]] and turning the entire planet into a major base of operations. [[Steven Cue|Cue]] was a great leader of men and a cunning ground tactician, but knew little of space combat. Oswald Voss offered [[Steven Cue|Cue]] his services, and led most [[Auellal League]] forces in space during the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

After the war ended and the [[Auellal League]] formalized itself as an official government, Oswald Voss was given the rank of [[Chiarn Caggee]] by [[Steven Cue]].

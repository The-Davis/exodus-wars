The M-6 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle (SABR), or "Saber", is a gauss-based weapons technology, with a selectable output on the firing mechanism.  In the event that stealth is needed, the weapon can be powered down.  It can go from cold to full power in less than a second.  Pulling the trigger powers on and engages the weapon.  The Saber has an integrated scope which has a 20x selectable optical zoom.

For close-range combat, the Saber can fire 12mm "flechette" ammunition. Every 10 meters of flight, the round would break in three pieces. After more than 30 meters, the shrapnel was too small to be effective, but at close range it was devastating.

For mid-range combat, the extremely popular "breecher" round was available. This round featured a secondary chemical propellant and an explosive head with proximity sensors. Upon nearing the point of impact, the chemical propellant ignites, tripling the velocity of the round. It explodes just after penetrating, to an incredible effect. Breechers had been in use for a long time, but were not available on a standard rifle before.

The last round was a super-sonic sabot-stabilized round. It was effective at extreme ranges, and had sensors built in to compensate for wind interference.

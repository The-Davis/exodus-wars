===Overview===

If the ''Jerome Black'' is the symbol of [[Torimur]]'s indomitable spirit and tenacity, the ''Iron Hand'' is the symbol of its lust for power and desire to conquer.  The ''Iron Hand'' is old, hard, and cruel.  Its fierce outline darkened the skies of all the worlds brought under the dominion of the [[Onyx Empire]].

[[Image:HMSIronHand1.jpg]]

Like its brother, the ''Jerome Black'', the ''Iron Hand'' was once a colony ship, one of the vessels that brought the wayward [[Torimur]] rebels home from exile.  The revamp of the ''Iron Hand'' came before the ''Jerome Black'', and was less drastic. The ''Iron Hand'' is much closer in appearance to the old colony vessels.

===Capabilities===

The ''Iron Hand'' doesn't have the flexibility or number of roles as its counterpart, but it can do a lot of things.  It's best at fighting and carrying smaller vessels.  All of the invasion armies of the [[Onyx Empire]] were delivered from the huge bays of the ''Iron Hand''.

The main weapons of the ''Iron Hand'' are the Hydra Head near-relativistic mass drivers.  The weapons are fast-firing, spitting their cruel fire at rates unmatched even by Hellguns.  Individually, none of them packs the direct punch of the "Big Joe" mass drivers its counterpart carries, but each can fire five times for every one shot from a "Big Joe."

[[Image:HMSIronHand2.jpg]]

A single Rahn Industries Hellgun anti-warship cannon is the ''Iron Hand'''s insurance against attack from the bottom.  The cannon has 180 degree articulation on a horizontal plane and 90 degrees on the vertical.  Anything attacking from the bottom must deal with it.  The Hellgun's shells do not have the awesome kinetic power of those of a Big Joe, but the incredible rate of fire (40 rounds per second) more than makes up for it.

The smaller weapons of the ''Iron Hand'' are flawed.  They were built in a rush, as part of the first buildup for invasion in [[2315]].  While the old lasers and autocannons have been replaced with modern weapons, the numbers and positions remain the same.  The ''Iron Hand'' is somewhat vulnerable to fighter attack.

The ''Iron Hand'' is slow, one of the slowest accelerators in modern warfare.  When it still used the primitive rockets of the 21st century, the ''Iron Hand'' cost a fortune to run every day.  With modern gravity cavitation systems, it's no longer expensive, but it's still slow.

The central core of the ''Iron Hand'' is a dead thing, no longer functional.  It was damaged beyond repair in [[2207]] by attacking [[Ngrligru]].  The processors still run, and with human input they can run the vessel, but the ancient spark of intelligence is gone.

Luna was the mind of the ''Iron Hand''.  Like her sibling-machines, Luna was a hybrid biomechanical brain bonded with the vessel.  The brain is an artificially created life form, and this is the damaged portion.  It is a ruined and charred husk, sitting within its mechanical womb.  Out of respect for the dead creature that sheltered them in their long trek home, no one will touch Luna's remains.

===Deployment===

When the [[Onyx Empire]] ended, [[Lucius Black]] took direct command of the ''Iron Hand'' and moved it far from prying eyes.  Under his direction, the vessel received all of its upgrades.  Right now, no one but Conclave agents know where the ''Iron Hand'' is located.

[[Image:HMSIronHand3.jpg]]

Through the reports of Inquisitors questioning captured Omega agents, the [[Alliance of Planets]] has learned that many Conclave soldiers believe the ''Iron Hand'' is haunted.  With a mad emperor in command, and a dead brain-machine hidden in its core, the ''Iron Hand'' does have the feel of a ghost ship.  Only the crew itself scoffs at the idea. Other Conclave crews stay off the ship when they have the option.

*Classification: Light Armored Car
*Powerplant: NDI Sa-909 Micro-Reactor
*Speed:  150 KPH
*Armor:  Prudenski Composite armor 
*Shields:  Vambrace Shield Generator 
*Armament:
**2 'Rapier' PPBCs
**4 'Sunburst/Starburst' Pods
*Communications System:  Standard Titanian Communications Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Land-SPAR 

===Overview===

Larger than the Guppy, the Buccaneer is a dedicated combat craft designed to be fast, all-terrain, and have a significant punch.  Based on the success of the venerable ADF AV-series of tanks, the Buccaneer is a reliable vehicle with a solid pedigree.  

[[Image:BuccaneerArmoredCar1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Buccaneer is armed quite well for a vehicle its size.  The light attack craft is outfitted with a pair of medium 'Rapier' Pulsed Particle Beam Cannons mounted in a twin-turret.  Controlled via a gunner behind the driver, aided by a computer, the twin cannons are effective against medium armored targets, as well as shielded targets, and are also effective against infantry.  

To aid in evasion and stealth, the Buccaneer has four 'Sunburst/Starburst' Pods, two mounted on either side of the cockpit.  As Sunburst pods, these devices can emit a range of specialized countermeasures, from simple smoke/debris cannisters, to hot-burning decoy flares.  As Starburst pods, they can fire a wide variety of short-range grenade rounds.  In this case, they can be used to destroy infantry, Volucris, or other light targets in close proximity.  Starburst is the Commonwealth's version of vehicle-mounted melee weaponry. 

The Buccaneer is outfitted with a New Dallas Industries Sa-909 Micro-Reactor.  This reactor generates enough power to push the Buccaneer as fast as a Guppy, and yet still power a shield generator.  The body of the Buccaneer is covered with Prudenski Composite armor, and the tires are solid material covered with a Prudenski mesh. 

===Deployment===

[[Image:BuccaneerArmoredCar2.jpg]]

Buccaneers are the basic light skirmishers of the Commonwealth Army Command.  They are used for raiding and light support in open-field fighting.  They are also quite effective in urban fighting, where their high agility and excellent handling on city streets combine to make them lethal against larger, slower vehicles, or against smaller, lighter infantry and armor suits.

The national symbol of the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]] is an enormous black-wooled ram, known as the "Black Ram of [[Sao Fuera]]."

In the earliest years of the Kalidasa colony, shepherds had to deal with a vicious reptilian predator known as the "Caçador." The Caçador was common in the Sao Fuera region, where it preyed on sheep, and even humans if it found them alone or in small groups.

The legend of the Black Ram says that one dark night a shepherd was out searching for a stray lamb, when he was attacked by the Caçador. Before it could make a meal of him, it was driven off by an enormous black ram. The ram not only injured the Caçador, it returned to the shepherd and helped him back home.

While many consider the legend to be absurd, it is extremely popular among Kalidasans, to the point that they adopted it as their symbol of [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] during the [[Second Exodus War]] and as their government symbol afterwards.

Biologists note that Caçadors are still common on Kalidasa, and are still in the habit of eating sheep, rams or otherwise.

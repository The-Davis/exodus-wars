The '''Novaya System''' consists of the star Mu Crucis, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Mu Crucis, the planets are Mu Cruci I, Mu Cruci II, [[Novaya]], [[Calpaca]], Mu Cruci V, Mu Cruci VI, Mu Cruci VII, and Mu Cruci VIII.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Mu Crucis is the Mu Crucis System's only star. Mu Crucis is a class G4X yellow dwarf. The Mu Crucis System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Mu Crucis is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Novaya ===

[[Novaya]] (1.01AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Mu Crucis System. It has one moon, Ayado.

=== Calpaca ===

[[Calpaca]] (1.2AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Mu Crucis System. It has one moon, Calseti.

== History ==

The Mu Crucis System was colonized in [[2195]].

'''Phoebe''' is a gas giant planet in the [[Titan System]], with extensive exotic gasses and even a limited supply of antimatter. The planet’s usefulness was not discovered until recently, but it is now used to power newer experimental systems the Titanians are developing for the [[Colonial Commonwealth]]. During the [[Second Exodus War]], the planet was a key site in the earliest space battles, and General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] used it as a staging point in his counterstrike on the Imperial fleet.

''Naimen Tanis Ladosen'' (born August 13, [[2032]]) is the [[Lothenar]] of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. He is a crafty ruler, running both the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] and the [[Alliance of Planets]] with over a hundred plots at once.

===Early Life===

As a [[Meroniri]], Naimen Ladosen has witnessed centuries of history, and seen many acquaintences among the "lesser" [[:Category:Races|races]] grow old and die. He was born in [[2032]], when the [[Terrans]] were barely leaving the [[Sol System]].

===The Duelist===

While growing up in the rank and file of [[Ladosen Tomesa]], Naimen chose dueling as his artistic focus. He was a master of the [[Pasara]] by the time he was sixty, becoming deadly in an art that takes most duelists over a century to achieve.

When he was seventy years old, Naimen challenged [[Auren Arufin]] to a duel to the death. The bitter rivalry between [[Ladosen Tomesa]] and [[Arufin Tomesa]] had already lasted for centuries, but the fight between the two made matters much worse. [[Auren Arufin|Auren]] was the undisputed master of [[Tesashue]], and everyone--even Naimen's own family--believed the senior duelist would kill Naimen in a matter of seconds. [[Auren Arufin|Auren]] instead succumbed in a five minute fight. After defeating his foe, Naimen decapitated him, and threw [[Auren Arufin|Auren]]'s head at the feet of son, [[Ferin Arufin]]. This gross insult was never forgotten, and for centuries rumors that Naimen had poisoned [[Auren Arufin|Auren]] before the fight persisted.

===Thenaro===

When he was one hundred and thirty years old, Naimen was appointed head of the [[Ladosen Tomesa]], and earned their hereditary seat on the [[Thenaro]]. He was a master politician, weaving hundreds of elaborate schemes and plots at any given moment. His blood feud with [[Ferin Arufin]] continued to worsen, and the two were constantly at each other's throats during their long careers in the [[Thenaro]]

===Lothenar===

In [[2286]], Naimen was elected to the position of [[Lothenar]]. Given their constant enmity, [[Ferin Arufin|Ferin]] was immediately chosen for the position of [[Suthenar]].

Naimen was one of the most cunning and ambitious [[Meroniri]] to earn the [[Lothenar]] position since the founding of the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]]. He kept the [[Alliance of Planets]] intact, and began slowly turning the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Space Police]] into his own group of personal enforcers. By the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] was almost universally hated outside of their own sphere of influence.

When [[Lucius Black]] sought sanctuary and political asylum, Naimen had found a kindred spirit. They were equally matched in cunning, but [[Lucius Black|Lucius]]' mind for domination and control far outshined the plans of Naimen. [[Lucius Black]] was soon his closest personal advisor, though only in secret. In public, he was treated as a trophy prisoner, and often paraded around in chains.

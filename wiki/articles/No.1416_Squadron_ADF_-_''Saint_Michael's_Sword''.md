The 1416th Combat Squadron of the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]], known as "Saint Michael's Sword", was a [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] squadron assigned to the [[Sol System]]. It was noted as [[Helen Cashaw]]'s first posting, where she earned the title of combat "ace."

By [[2319]], so many pilots had died or been retired with disabling injuries that the squadron number was retired.

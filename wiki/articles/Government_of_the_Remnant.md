The Remnant is governed by a long line of monarchs assuming the title of '''Emperor''' or '''Empress''' (need cooler names).  This line has remained unbroken from the times of the war with the [[Geckans]] as the last '''Emperor''' of the [[Anthronoris Empire]] was part of the population evacuated to the [[Void World]]s as they escaped the [[Sundering]].  In the almost 200 centuries since that time, there have been a total of five '''Emperors'''.  

The '''Emperor''' steps down at a time of their choosing, well in advance of the onset of what is considered "old age" by the Masters.  Each has taken special care to train their successor in the ideals of Anthronorii rule and this successor is always a child of the sitting '''Emperor'''.  

The '''Emperor''' is aided in governing the Remnant by a counsel of seven '''Lords''' (needs a cooler title).  One commands the Reliquiae Astrum Classis under the title of '''Grand Admiral''', which is responsible for all offensive and defensive actions of the Remnant military.  Another one oversees the '''resource ministry''', which is responsible for the management of all resources available to the Remnant.  Yet another directs the '''production ministry''', which manages all production within the Remnant.  One is responsible for the '''research ministry''', which is in charge of furthering the Remnant's body of knowledge in all things.  One leads the '''ministry of public health''', which is primarily responsible for the maintaining of Anthronoris longevity through administering of the Vitaeternum treatment to all children and the Sin Terminus Treatment to '''Military''' commanders as well as any other concern for public health.   Another heads the '''ministry of learning''', which validates the myriad of degree equivalents which a typical Master will receive in their lifetime.  And the last, and one of the more prestigious postings, is to the '''ministry of histories''', which actively maintains and acquires new histories to be added to the data banks on board the Praetorias.

There are also four '''Dukes''' who represent the four remaining Void Worlds in the Remnant.  During the time of the Anthronoris Empire, this number would be much greater, and each '''Duke''' would be responsible for more than one planet or even system.  

This form of government is changed from that of the days of the Empire.  Trade within the Empire at the time was very important and currency usage widespread.  With the need to remain unseen, trade is all but illegal now and resources are obtained by ships of the Reliquiae Astrum Classis transporting various asteroids to the habitats for exploitation.  Although seeming harsh, the people of the Remnant have had millennia to adjust to this strict form of government.  They understand that necessity has created the current system and have been lucky to have had thoughtful and just leaders for a very long time.  Just as the Masters have always aggressively destroyed exterior threats to their well being, they have likewise been ever vigilant against against internal corruption.

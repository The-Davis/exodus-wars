The Phoenix Project's improvements to missiles are very dramatic. Gen2 missiles were mostly dumb-fire or laser guided. With the introduction of super-small electrical circuits, cheap yet advanced guidance computers could be built for missiles. These computers make course corrections, and have some evasive programming to avoid being shot down by anti-missile systems.

The basic propulsion for missiles was changed from a chemical compound reaction to a micro cavitation drive, similar to the gravity cavitation drive systems used by fighters and warships. The drive speeds the missile towards its targets at near-relative speed.

The warhead on missiles has also changed from the old liquid chemical compound explosives used before. The modern warhead is a Ferro-Aluminum Thermite compound, ignited by a Magnesium trigger. The reaction (2Al(s) + Fe2O3(s) --- Al2O3(l) + 2Fe(l)) is an extremely energetic one. Once heated by the Magnesium trigger, the aluminum is oxidized by the iron. The heat levels range between 2000 and 3000 degrees Celsius, depending on the additional fuel in the target. 

There are four basic types of missiles in use today; Short Range (SRM), Medium Range (MRM), Long Range (LRM), and Artillery.

Every missile launcher can take sub-type rounds of the missile it was built for.

==Short Range Missiles==
SRMs are very fast, very powerful weapons. They are most commonly used on Rigs and vehicles as close dueling weapons, or on defense turrets. Most SRMs have a top range of 10 kilometers. There are a few kinds of sub-types of SRM.
*'''Streak SRMs''' - Streak SRMs are five times as fast as the normal missile. They can reach relative speeds in outer space. In atmosphere, they move so quickly that their outer shell and the air around them catches fire. Streaks also have an optical communication link back to their launcher's targeting computer, allowing much more advanced course correction. 
[[Image:TerranStreakSRM.jpg|200px|center]]

*'''Rattler SRMs''' - Rattlers are some of the cheapest missiles around. Their range is even shorter than other SRMs--it caps out its accurate range at 1.5 kilometers--but the weapon is excellent for fighter dogfights and similar situations. 
[[Image:TerranRattlerSRM.jpg|200px|center]]

*Inferno SRMs - Infernos are Rattler SRMs packed with magnesium fuses in the warhead, along with a more flammable jellied oil compound that requires high heat to begin burning. The magnesium triggers ignite on contact, heating up enough to ignite the clinging, incendiary jelly.
[[Image:TerranInfernoSRM.jpg|200px|center]]

*Splatter SRMs - "Splatter" Anti-Infantry SRMs are nasty little weapons. Once the fighter or VTOL launches them, heavy weights force the nose straight down, then the engines engage and drive the missile into the ground. Contact triggers detonate the weapon immediately before impact, spreading shrapnel everywhere. This splatter effect is similar to the old "Daisy Cutter" weapons used in the 21st century, but is in a much more compact and powerful package. 
[[Image:TerranSplatterSRM.jpg|200px|center]]

*Cobra SRMs - Cobras are designed for air-to-ground assault. They're similar to Rattlers in power, guidance and speed, but its guidance systems are optimized for avoiding anti-missile systems so it has the best possible chance of hitting a static target. 
[[Image:TerranCobraSRM.jpg|200px|center]]

==Medium Range Missiles==
MRMs are built for quantity. They are smaller and less destructive than any other type of missile, but they fire in wider spreads and have some excellent sub-types. The average MRM has a range of 100 kilometers.
*Shrapnel MRMs - The Shrapnel warhead has a built-in proximity sensor, triggering the explosive inside before contact. [[Image:TerranShrapnelMRM.jpg|100px]]
*Hammer MRMs - Hammer MRMs are extremely powerful warheads, capable of causing extreme damage if they hit their target. Unfortunately, their targeting systems are a little unreliable, requiring closer ranges than most MRMs. [[Image:TerranHammerMRM.jpg|100px]]
*Mole MRMs - The mole warhead has a delayed trigger and a chemical afterburner. The moment it hits its target, the afterburner ignites, boring the warhead deeper into the target. The resulting sub-armor damage often does more damage. [[Image:TerranMoleMRM.jpg|100px]]
*Boulder MRMs - Like the Hammer, Boulder MRMs are much more powerful than the average Medium Range Missile, but unlike their cheaper counterparts do not suffer accuracy problems. The weapons are popular for conventional bombing runs, but are harder for quartermasters to acquisition. [[Image:TerranBoulderMRM.jpg|100px]]
*Hawk MRMs - The Hawk is a powerful Medium Range Missile. Its guidance systems and speed are a little below average, but Hawks are strong enough to knock out heavy bombers in a single hit. [[Image:TerranHawkMRM.jpg|100px]]

==Long Range Missiles==
LRMs have the longest atmospheric range of any missile. On Terran-controlled planets, their guidance systems can jack into satellites via microwave transceiver. With satellite guidance, an LRM can travel up to 300 kilometers and hit a target with 80% accuracy.
*Torpedo LRM - Torpedoes are the only missile that also works on underwater targets. They are three times as damaging as standard LRMs, but travel slower and only have a maximum atmospheric range of 150 kilometers. [[Image:TerranTorpedoLRM.jpg|100px]]
*Hound LRM - Hounds are advanced warheads. Their maneuvering and guidance systems are excellent, and they can accelerate nearly as fast as a Streak SRM. On contact, the missile can either detonate normally, or break up into five smaller warheads to spread the damage out over a wider target space (i.e. firing on a swarm of Volucris Drones). The Hound is the most popular fighter-to-fighter dogfight missile.[[Image:TerranHoundLRM.jpg|100px]]
*ALARM LRM - The ALARM is an Automatic Long-Range Anti-Radar Missile. The common term is ALARM Reflex Missile. When the ALARM Reflex Missile detects probing of any sort, it fires off a missile immediately. [[Image:TerranALARMLRM.jpg|100px]]
*Wren LRM - The Wren is a very powerful Long Range Missile. Its guidance systems and speed are about average, but a Wren is strong enough to knock out heavy bombers in one or two shots. This makes it very popular among interceptor pilots. Its size and weight makes it a little unwieldy in dogfights, so the Hound remains king for fighter-to-fighter combat. [[Image:TerranWrenLRM.jpg|100px]]
*Archer LRM - Archers are designed for air-to-ground assault. They're similar to Wrens in power, guidance and speed, but its guidance systems are optimized for avoiding anti-missile systems so it has the best possible chance of hitting a static target. [[Image:TerranArcherLRM.jpg|100px]]

==Artillery Missiles==
Artillery Missiles are the most powerful missile in the Terran arsenal. Their range is limited to about 120 kilometers, but they are a hundred times as damaging as every smaller missile type. Artillery Missiles are much larger than SRMs, MRMs, and LRMs.

===Arrow Artillery===
The Arrow series of Artillery Missile is atmosphere-only, and the most commonly used Artillery Missile system. The warheads do heavy splash damage upon impact.
*Arrow I - This model was fielded from 2010 to 2100
*Arrow II - This model was fielded from 2100 to 2150
*Arrow III - This model was fielded from 2150 to 2275
*Arrow IV - This model was first fielded in 2275 and is currently in use.

===Orca Artillery===
The Orca is the premier space Artillery Missile of the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. It is the primary warship-buster of the Bloodhawk. The weapon isn't a fire-and-forget missile. It requires a straight line of sight, and sometimes must receive course correction information from the bomber or warship that fired it.

[[Image:TerranOrcaArtilleryMissile.jpg|100px|center]]

===Bullshark Artillery===
The Bullshark is a scaled-down version of the Orca, with a slightly less powerful explosive punch. Instead of the very best explosives, it carries more sophisticated targeting systems, and is much faster. The weapon is a fire-and-forget warhead.

[[Image:TerranBullsharkArtilleryMissile.jpg|100px|center]]

===Apocalypse Artillery===
The Apocalypse is the oldest warhead in the Terran arsenal, dating all the way back to the [[United Earth Alliance]]. It has evolved over the centuries to encompass the new guidance and propulsion systems of [[2324]], but the massive nuclear warhead is still the power behind the Apocalypse. The [[Prefecture]] government has tight restrictions on the arming and firing of Apocalypse missiles, but they are still frequently used in space battles far from inhabited planets. Unfortunately, the [[Empire]] had no qualms with using the Apocalypse en masse, and it is the cause of many of the woes plaguing [[Terra]] today.

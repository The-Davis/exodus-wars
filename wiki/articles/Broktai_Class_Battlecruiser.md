===Overview===

When the [[Onyx Empire]] renewed its offensives in 2321, the Lorithos ASTF was taken aback by the size of many of the ships in the new Imperial fleet.  The design of a Command Destroyer class was created and put into production beginning in 2321 and 2322.

[[Image:BroktaiClassBattlecruiser1.jpg]]

This class was dubbed the Broktai class.  Broktai is the name for Lorithan in Joresha, one of the languages spoken by the Lorithos people.

===Capabilities===

The Broktai Command Destroyers are the most powerful class of warship ever produced by the Rikaz Lorithos.  The main weapons of Broktai are an awesome array of nineteen Heavy Blaster cannons, mounted in fixed and turreted emplacements all over the ship.  These nineteen heavy turrets are supported by an additional sixty Standard Blaster cannon turrets, giving it greater firepower than two Riiktars.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons.  They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

Broktai Destroyers carry eighteen Skrike missile launchers, which can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.

[[Image:BroktaiClassBattlecruiser2.jpg]]

As a Command Destroyer, the Broktai are heavily armed, but also carry a substantial aerospace complement.  The heavy destroyer can carry ten fighter squadrons and three bomber squadron.  This gives it a complement of combat craft equal to an Oltvak carrier.

For a ship of its size and power, the Broktai has the quick acceleration and agility characteristic of Lorithos warships.  Despite its size, it still has an acceleration rate that can best a Terran heavy frigate.

===Deployment===

The Broktai were developed in 2321, and the first of the ships came out of the dock yards in mid 2322.  Testing was completed, and they were deployed to the fleets in late 2322.

[[Image:BroktaiClassBattlecruiser3.jpg]]

Only four Broktai were constructed, and two were deployed to each fleet.  As fleet command ships, they are designed to each lead an attack wedge from the fleet in assaults on enemy starships.  They are the most powerful warships in the Lorithos ASTF.

== Events of 2318 ==

===January===
*January 8 - The [[Atlas Class Carrier|Atlas]] class vessel hull designated [[HMS Glorious|CV-43]] is completed and delivered to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].
*January 17 - [[HMS Glorious|CV-43]] is officially commissioned as the [[HMS Glorious|HMS ''Glorious'']]. The [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']] is placed under the command of Captain [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]]. Captain [[Julia Michaels]] is transferred to the [[HMS Glorious|HMS ''Glorious'']].

===February===
*February 6 - [[Des System]] is captured by the [[Onyx Empire]]. [[Des Arc]] is occupied, and the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] begins construction of a new pilot academy on [[Des Wei]].

=== March ===

* March 11 - The [[Damanir Incident]] begins.
* March 12 - The [[Damanir Incident]] ends.

===July===
*July 9 - [[Joshua Duram]] captured by the [[Omegas]] during a failed extraction of political refugees on [[Tejat]].
*July 11 - [[Omegas]] execute [[Joshua Duram]] by firing squad.

===August===
*August 6 - [[Talaris Roi]] writes and sings the first musical rendition of "[[Is Titan Still Free?]]" in a series of [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] broadcasts.

===September===

=== October ===

* October 17 - A carrier group led by the [[HMS Cyprus|HMS ''Cyprus'']] leads an attack on the [[Epsilon Indi System]], driving out an unarmed research station.
* October 19 - [[Royal Imperial Labs]] personnel arrive in the [[Epsilon Indi System]].
* October 21 - Imperial scientists activate an experimental [[Meroniri]] exotic matter power source, destroying the entire [[Epsilon Indi System]].

=== November ===

* November 17 - In what became known as "Black November" among the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], the first of the false negotiations began. [[Lucius Black]] offered a cease-fire agreement with the [[United Earth Alliance]], which the civillian population greatly desired. Due to popular demand, the [[Alliance of Planets]] offered to negotiate a peaceful end to the war.

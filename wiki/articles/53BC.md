== Events of 53BC ==

In only a decade and a half, the population of [[Iridi Archada]] had topped 100,000, as had the population of a handful of other colony cities on the twin moons of [[Glengarr]].  This was due in part to the wave of expansionist fever that had gripped much of the [[Ixoarchada]] race, the sense of rediscovery  evident in many writings and reports from the time, but also due to the ever growing population pressures being felt on [[Glengarr]].

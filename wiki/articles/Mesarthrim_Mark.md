The '''[[Mesarthrim]] Mark''' was the official currency of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and after the Clans were dissolved on March 14, [[2324]], the Mark was the currency of the [[United Centusi States]]. The Mark is divided into 100 Pfennig. A Ten Pfennig coin is called a Groschen.

The [[Mesarthrim]] mint Pfennig coins, Groschen coins, quarter-Mark coins, half-Mark coins, and print one, five, ten, fifteen, and fifty Mark notes. The [[National Bank of the Clans]] never allowed remote transfers of wealth like credit cards and checks. Large transfers had to be done in person, with a [[Blood Seal]] involved.

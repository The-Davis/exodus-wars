The '''Royal Imperial Bank''' was responsible for the regulation of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s currency, the [[Imperial Credit]].

The bank was noted for its devastating economic revenge at the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], when it purged all records and effectively eliminated the life savings of all citizens of territories held by the [[Onyx Empire]].

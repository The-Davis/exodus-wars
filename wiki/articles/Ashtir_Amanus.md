Ashtir Amanus was the wife of the Legiant [[Mirdek Amanus]]. During the [[Sundering]], she formed a plan to safeguard as much of their homeworld as she could.

While Mirdek fought in orbit, Ashtir sought to preserve as many of their people as she could. Knowing the single Praetoria in orbit could do nothing for long, she decided to save the children of her people. Ashtir chose the Dzarning Mountain Range as a refuge, hiding children and food in caves. 

The adult Anthronoris of Lorithan fought long and hard, doing everything they could to draw the attention of the ravening Ngrligru away from their precious treasure hidden away in the mountains. Billions died so millions could live. The savage Ngrligru feasted on the Anthronoris of Lorithan, but never discovered their children.

Knowing his doom was at hand, Mirdek Amanus sacrificed all he could to bring Ashtir aboard, then sent his Praetoria on a final run against the Ngrligru. He brought the flaming wreck of his ship and dozens of Ngrligru vessels crashing down into the depths of the ocean of Lorithan, letting the waves bury Anthronoris and Ngrligru alike. Mirdek and Ashtir died hand in hand, entombed beyond the reach of their monstrous enemies.

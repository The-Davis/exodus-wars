=== August 13, 2325 ===

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In an odd and sudden move, [[Lucius Black]] brings the entirety of his fleet to [[Sepulare]], the homeworld of the [[Meroniri]]. The Imperial remnant is led to believe that he has plans to deal with the Meroniri, and indeed he does, but not what his commanders believe. Lucius Black promptly surrenders to the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], begging for sanctuary from the [[Prefecture]].<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Meroniri accept on the condition that the Empire stand down and be disarmed. Caught like rats in a trap, the [[Omegas]] and Scimitar Squadron fight back. [[Emil Torsc]] leads his Vampires to the ''[[Ushujaa Chonge]]'', escaping the Meroniri gauntlet.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The remaining Imperial warships are disarmed, and most scrapped. Only the HMS ''Iron Hand'', an important symbol due to its status as a colony vessel, remains intact, but stripped of all weapons and defenses.

=== August 14, 2325 === 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Once in custody, Lucius Black is questioned at length, but treated well. Eventually, he is visited by [[Naimen Ladosen]], [[Lothenar]] of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] and President of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. Ladosen and Black talk at great length, in private, before Ladosen leaves and orders Black's guards to treat him well and give him all the comforts he requires.

=== August 25, 2325 ===

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When Lucius Black surrendered to the Meroniri Terinasi, Chris Dayton was recovered and sent home, apparently unharmed. Many officials in the Prefecture government, Dayton himself included, are concerned that the possibility of latent brainwashing may have been implanted in Dayton's mind. For the good of the Prefecture, he resigns and retires to [[Estelis]].<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It takes some time to set up elections, but by August 25, 2325, the Tejat Senator and interim Consul--[[Talaris Roi]]--is selected by the electors as the Consul of the Prefecture, giving up her Senate seat.

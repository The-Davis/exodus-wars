Technical Difficulties - 4/5/2324 - by Ryan Wilhelm

Terra - Houston Restoration Camp, Texas Decontamination Zone
     While the Legion of the Wolf and the Archadian armies struggled to survive in Magrinoch on Tavou, the people in the Texas Decontamination Zone on Terra have been dropping like flies. Professor Josiah MacArthur Hlow still lives, and has been joined by medical geniuses from across the galaxy.
     Hlow kicks the sterile-white double doors, cursing under his breath as he does. The normally calm, meek doctor is furious, his face tinged with red. He is flanked by Doctors Steven Edge and Natanel Gabriel. Edge is a Mesarthrim, and the top bio-weapons researcher of the United Centusi States. Gabriel is a Lorithos, sent by Rikaz Lorithos to lend their vast knowledge on viral warfare countermeasures and viral DNA damage.
     "Spreading?!" Hlow's voice is strained, almost a scream. "How can the damned thing be spreading? We're in lockdown!" Hlow throws his clipboard and papers across the room. It breaks, and papers fly everywhere.
     "You really should get a PDA." suggests Dr. Gabriel. "Paper records were outdated even in your day. Anyway, we have recent reports from Beijing and Ankara, and unconfirmed rumors of contamination in Grenada."
     Hlow bends to pick up his papers, crawling under a desk to reach for one. He bangs his head as he stands, bringing out another uncharacteristic stream of profanity. "Sorry, sorry." he sighs. "I thought we were bringing this under control, but I'm obviously wrong." He checks a supply list, which is on the top of his stack. "Dr. Edge, your government's shipment of Apöt samples should have come in by now. Let's check the spaceport authority and see when they'll be ready for the lab."
     "You're the Professor." Dr. Edge replies. The two head for the door. "Coming Dr. Gabriel?"
     The Lorithos shakes his head. "My people should have finished setting up our accelerated incubation chamber. I have to put it through shakedown tests, then we can start forcing this virus through several generations worth of mutations, so we can start predicting how it will change."
     Hlow nods, and the two men leave Natanel Gabriel to his work.

     Edge and Hlow walk in silence for several minutes, until Dr. Edge speaks up. "You know that I can't guarantee these samples will teach us anything. I honestly don't think your viral plague here is the Apöt. The HalbAnderung designed it to be airborne, but we know this plague is spread through fluid ingestion and physical contact."
     Hlow nods. "I just want to make some comparisons. I've had some of my assistants analyzing this virus. I don't have my old notes on the Apöt virus from Pluto Research Station, but the protein coating seems familiar. But it also resembles Ebola, three strains of flesh-eating viruses, and even smallpox to an extent. We just have to follow every lead."
     "Do you think it could be smallpox?"
     "That would be a stretch. The last major outbreak of smallpox was in 2006, when the Al Fatah organization released it in Paris during the last French stand of World War III. The United States managed to contain it, and we haven't seen a single outbreak since then."
     "I appreciate the history lesson Professor, but--" Dr. Edge stops dead still, a shocked look on his face. Hlow squints at him, then waves his hand in front of the Mesarthrim's eyes. Steven Edge gasps. "Dear God. The silos! The Asian nuclear silos!"
     "What about them?"
     "When the Mesarthrim High Command was still alive and kicking, and preparing for our invasion of Imperial-occupied Terra, I was involved in a certain intelligence briefing." Edge begins to pace up and down the hall. "According to the Lorithos ICD and our own sources, Emperor Lucius Black had a huge stockpile of biological weapons buried all over Asia, Europe, and North America. We thought his plan was to alter his nuclear delivery systems to launch plagues, but he never did. When Japan's silos were sabotaged, the stockpiles must have leaked."
     Hlow rubs his forehead. "We might have a plague on our hands of galactic proportions. I remember when Emperor Black built that stockpile--against my wishes, I might add. There were things in there that would make your blood freeze. Figuratively and literally." He pauses. "If your theory is correct, we are probably already doomed."

     Early the next morning, a bleary-eyed Professor Hlow steps into the laboratory's decontamination unit. The room's sensors detect his entry, and the process begins. The machinery sprays his suit with volatile poisons, killingand cleanses everything. As the light blinks green, he enters a world of lab equipment--a room with air so dangerous that a single puncture in his suit would mean instant death.
     Hlow surveys the crew of scientists, hard at work. Dr. Ryan Hash, the senior researcher from New Harvard University on Ward, is running scans of various DNA structures. An Archadian next to the Ward researcher is compiling data into a list at lightning speed--this is Jeremy Locke, an Archadian "super-programmer," said to be able to log and recall up to a trillion database tables on the fly, without an artificial index.
     Across the lab is a Mesarthrim scientist from the Armed Allied League, the young Dr. David Lewinski. Professor Hlow once said that Lewinski was so young and inexperienced that he couldn't tell viruses from bacteria. Still, the Mesarthrim financial support was appreciated by everyone.
     Lastly, the Lorithos scientist Dr. Gabriel was hard at work, though Hlow didn't know what he was currently working on. He decided to find out. "How's it going, Natanel?"
     "It is like, how do you say...curse Terran language...it is like stabbing yourself in the eye."
     Hlow squints at the Lorithos. "Eh? I don't get it."
     "Ah yes, I remember the word. It is a pain." Gabriel shrugs. "I have some more information on this thing."
     "OK." Hlow hums before continuing. "So, do you have the incubation time mapped out?"
     "Yeh. I got it all checked. On average, it's two hours, but I've seen about one in ten incubate in thirty minutes."
     "Half an hour."Hlow looks suspiciously at the chart on Dr. Gabriel's screen.
     "Yeh. Six completely healthy specimens died within thirty minutes of exposure. I am sorry to say that they were not animal specimens--some people are being exposed, despite our quarantine and decontamination efforts."
     Dr. Steven Edge's voice breaks in over the communications system. "Doctor Gabriel, Professor Hlow. I have confirmed that the virus is indeed a mutation of the Apöt virus. But that's not all." Dr. Edge pauses for dramatic effect. "What we have on our hands is a virus that mutates every generation. But we have one more problem. Professor, please meet me in your office. Privately."

     Dr. Edge is already waiting when Hlow walks in. "Hlow, I have a question for you."
     "Shoot." the Professor replies.
     "Just how did that virus get here?" Dr. Steven Edge demands. "The Mesarthrim never brought it to Terra. They only fired it on Imperial forces once, and only only traces of that were left in your research lab on Pluto."
     Hlow nods. It is a valid point. "I destroyed the last samples before my faked bug abduction. I don't know how the virus got here. Is that all?"
     "No." Edge continues. "I was suiting up to join you in the biohazard lab, when I caught sight of a security bulletin from the local authorities. The United Centusi States were sending Dr. Ulysses Brenner to join our research efforts, but he was shot by a sniper when he was unloading from his shuttle--less than fifteen minutes ago."
     Hlow frowns. "Strange. Very strange--do you think these events are related?"
     "I do. Think about it. Only Mesarthrim have a large supply of the virus. The AAL and the UCS are at each other's throats. A UCS scientist shows up, and he gets shot. So, what does that tell you?
     "It tells me I need to call in some advice. Edge, let's contact the Alliance High Council--discreetly."

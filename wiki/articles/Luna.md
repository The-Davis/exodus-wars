'''Luna''' is [[Terra]]'s only moon, and is the fifth largest moon in the [[Sol System]].

Luna has no natural inhabitable environment, but its "Maria", or "seas", are home to enormous permanent biospheres.

The first [[Terran]] colony was established in a domed city in the Anguis sea, a 150 kilometer basin. The circumference of the basin was later lined with magnetic coils, and the basin was filled with an artificial atmosphere, water, and arable soil. The Anguis basin soon became an inhabitable area, and the success of the first Lunar colony led to the creation of mirror colonies in the other basins.

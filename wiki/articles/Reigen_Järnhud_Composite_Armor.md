'''Järnhud Composite Armor''' is the modern descendant of the armor systems brought by the [[Reigens]] on their original colony starships.  It is widely viewed as the most sophisticated composite armor system in use, and is even comparable to the modern [[Ikronin]] proton-bonded armor, developed long after the Reigens left [[Kronheim]].
Järnhud Armor is composed not of just a single composite material layer, but rather a series of highly-advanced, synergetic layers that work in tandem to create a very effective protection system.  The four layers of the armor are detailed below.

===Ablative Layer===

The outermost layer is designed to deflect lasers and other optical weapons.  Upon impact, this thin layer vaporizes in a tiny area, creating a plasma that deflects the shot or at least dissipates much of its impact energy.

===Primary Alloy===

Formed from an ultra-strong, nano-formed composite/alloy, the primary layer is designed to absorb or deflect most of the energy impact inflicted by most energy weapons.  This layer is similar to [[Lorithos Tzaiko Composite Armor]], but is slightly more robust.  Its primary advantage is that it is easy to repair in the field with nanobots, even during combat situations.

===Nuclear Spin-Locked Collapsed Matter Substrate===

This layer is comprised of a web-like structure which reinforces the armor.  Although it makes up only 10% of the armor's total mass, it absorbs 95% of most physical impacts, and helps to evenly
distribute heat via active and passive heat conduction systems.  It is very difficult to physically destroy, compared to the primary alloy.

===Superconducting Endo-Shield===

The final layer is composed of multiple layers of energized, superconducting materials used to deflect magnetic fields and pulses, such as from EMP and MASER weaponry.  This layer is not physically very strong or tough, but it works well at deflecting electronic weapons and some forms of radiation.  However, it is well protected beneath the other three layers.

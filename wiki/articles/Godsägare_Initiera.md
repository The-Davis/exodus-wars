The Godsägare Initiera (Gentleman's Initiate) was the founding organization of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], decades ago. 

The Initiera has grown from being a simple gentleman's club into a full-fledged political entity, a prime example of the pecularities of the [[Ikronin]] government apparatus.

The Initiera is a meeting place for diplomats, businessmen, CEOs and other politicians; where they make deals and discuss matters of state.

The Godsägare Initiera is commonly believed to be the highest authority in the Jurekön, though this has never been confirmed or denied by the [[Ikronin]]. The inner workings of the Initiera are a mystery to non-[[Ikronin]] and to underprivaledged [[Ikronin]].

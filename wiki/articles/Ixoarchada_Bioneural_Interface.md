The [[Ixoarchada]] have all but perfected the art of blending man with machine, from nano-tech augmented immune systems to purely bionic organs and limbs.  Though wary to use implants excessively, '''Ixoarchada Bioneural Interfaces''' are the predominant way nearly everyone in the [[Tolana Sector]] interacts with computers and other devices.  Simply put, the Bioneural Interface latches onto the user's nervous system, effectively making the computer an extension of their body.  There is a variety of different Bioneural Interfaces; each suited for different tasks.  

===Level 5===

Long ago, the Ixoarchada determined that there had to be a better way to interface with a computer than levers, buttons, or motions.  After several failed attempts at interfacing directly to the brain through induction interconnects, Ixoarchada scientists focused their efforts on creating a neural interface that connected directly into the ulnar and median nerves in the wrist.  This is optimal because a very large percentage of the cerebrum is devoted to the control of hand muscles and nerve input.  In addition, the nerves are close enough to the skin that a noninvasive interconnect can be used.  

Operation of devices with Level 5 Bioneural Interfaces is quite straightforward: the user places their wrist, palm side down onto the interface pads, and allows the optical nano-fibers to connect with the tiny interface chips buried in their skin.  This gives them a set of "virtual hands" to use, complete with tactile feedback.  The implant, of course, blocks the neural commands from reaching the user's muscles.  Some systems allow the user to manipulate three dimensional interface objects; others simply use muscular command combinations to control software, not unlike archaic keyboard button combinations.  

Because there is no actual motion involved, individuals can input verbal information as fast as they can speak it, and can react quicker than older style manual interfaces.  When combined with direct-retinal stimulation implants, allows starship crews to operate much more efficiently.  This interface is also used on almost all heavy weaponry, allowing them to be easily controlled with a minimal amount of expended personal effort, and with much less risk of accidental firing.  

This form of Bioneural Interface is used widely because it is the least invasive, and involves only a trivial implant. It also presents no risk of system shock.  All Ixoarchada, civilian and RhováDeviri, receive a Level 5 Bioneural Interface soon after birth.  The Level 5 Interface has been the standard for several hundred years, and nearly every modern device, computer, access panel, and doorways requires such an Interface to operate.

===Level 4===

After many years of hard work, Ixoarchada scientists were able to create what is now known as the Level 4 Bioneural Interface.  This interface utilizes an external induction system, which is fitted behind the spine.  It does not require an implant, and is very efficient, however the sensory feedback is not very detailed.  Usually Level 5 Bioneural Interfaces are used in conjunction with this configuration, to increase tactile feedback.  

This system does not block neural impulses, and is primarily used in the operation of civilian devices that control artificial exoskeletal articulation, such as construction and quarry machines.  There is very little risk of system shock from use of this device, and even a full-power feedback surge only causes temporary paralysis and minor spasms.  Level 4 Bioneural Interfaces are commonly used by many individuals, military and civilian, involved in the operation of heavy machinery and large vehicles, as they have few side effects.

===Level 3===

The natural extension of the Interface system involves a spinal implant, which has full blocking capability.  The Level 3 Bioneural Interface implant is at the base of the brain stem, and because of this fact, it blocks most spinal reflexes, as these are mostly undesirable; it is not efficient for a powersuit to "flinch" in battle.  It has a much higher sensory resolution, which translates into much better tactile and kinesthetic feedback.  The implant also connects through the optical cortex, allowing visual information to be presented to the user.  

The main problem is that the implant is highly invasive and difficult to remove.  There is also a reasonable risk of system shock under extreme conditions.  Although implants have failsafe optical connections, which cannot suffer from power overloads, such implants can cause extremely strange sensations and impulses during malfunction, which sometimes leads to dizziness, vertigo, and nausea.  These systems are primarily used in RhováDeviri powered armor, such as the [[Ansau Suit]], due to their similarity in structure to humans.  It is also used in some high-speed, non-combat aircraft and spacecraft.

Level 3 Bioneural Interfaces are fairly invasive, and are used almost exclusively by RhováDeviri.

===Level 2===

The Level 2 Bioneural Interface was by far the hardest to develop.  It involves a highly invasive, although not terribly dangerous, implant, which is inserted in the brain.  This implant adapts to the host brain over a training period of about one year, and actually works on a thought/impulse level, merging itself imperceptibly with the user's consciousness.  It excels at interfacing humans with computers, and is used as the human-computer interface component of [[Strolza Entity|Strolza Entities]].  

This brain-level neural interface is impossible to remove from a brain without causing irreparable damage, and malfunctions can lead to neural destabilization and eventually insanity.  The risk of system shock when using this interface is significant, however the triple-redundant safety measures almost never fail.  These systems are delicate and expensive, but highly effective, as they allow the connected computer to read the users thoughts and intentions directly and with lightning speed.  They can also allow two humans to communicate directly with each other, which is especially useful for scientific researchers to quickly share complex ideas.

Level 2 bioneural interfaces are used by high ranking command personnel, tactical officers, and chief engineers within the RhováDeviri, and also by researchers and scientists coordinating projects across planets or even between star systems.

===Level 1===

The Level 1 Bioneural Interface is mostly a Level 2 Interface, which is enhanced to encompass the motor/sensory capabilities of the Level 3 Interface.  It is profoundly imersive, completely surrounding the user with artificial sensory information while providing a mental link to a [[Strolza Entity]], and allows operation of the system at a subconscious level.  It was developed and is used exclusively for the purpose of starfighter piloting by the [[DreináDeviri]].

The level of speed and coordination with which [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] fighters fly would not be achievable without the use of this interface technology.  It carries a significant risk of system shock, and several fighter pilots have died from use of this interface, although its redundancy and security system almost always works as intended.  Level 1 Interfaces cannot be removed after insertion, and are very expensive, and difficult to maintain and repair.  Level 1 Interfaces are also often combined with complete life support systems in case an individual is immersed for extended periods.

The primary drawback of the Level 1 Interface is that the process of complete immersion within the system, and going back and forth between, takes its toll on the conscious mind of the user.  Commanders try to limit the total number of times a pilot activates and deactivates the system, sometimes keeping a single pilot immersed for extended periods, as the ultimate result after decades of use can be a complete severing of the conscious mind from the body.  Due to the extreme risks involved, Ixoarchada DreináDeviri are forbidden from utilizing neural accelerators.

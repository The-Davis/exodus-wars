Prefecture Military personnel are ordered to give no quarter to surrendering troops of any nation currently engaged in a state of war with the Prefecture.  Said troops are to be eliminated with extreme prejudice.

This order was first issued by General [[Travis Carpenter]] on October 16th, [[2322]] in order to keep all active duty troops at the front lines fighting the enemy rather than guarding prisoners of war.  The command was re-issued as a Standing Order by the Prefect on December 22nd, [[2322]].

An entire nation surrendering in order to end a war is not subject to this order. The Prefecture Military does not commit acts of genocide.

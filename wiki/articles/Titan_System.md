The '''Titan System''' consists of the star Titan, twelve planets, their moons, and billions of asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Titan, the planets are [[Oceanus]], [[Coeus]], [[Crius]], [[Hyperion]], [[Iapetus]], [[Theia]], [[Rhea]], [[Themis]], [[Mnemosyne]], [[Phoebe]], [[Tethys]], and [[Cronus]]. The worlds are named after the twelve Titans of Greek mythology.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Titan is the Titan System's only star. Titan is a class G9T yellow sub-giant. Titan has a strong heliosphere with a particle windspeed of roughly 4.5 million kilometers per hour. Starship navigation is dangerous closer to the star itself.

The Titan System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Titan is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

== Orbital Bodies ==

Because of Titan's size and luminosity, planets in the inner system require environmental controls for any habitats.

The middle region of the Titan System is where the inhabitable zone of Titan is located. Two inhabitable terrestrial worlds are located in this area. There are several terrestrial planets in the mid system area of Titan.

The area beyond the inhabitable zone is known as the outer region, and is extremely useful to the inhabitants of the Titan System. Gas giants in the Titan System have many moons, and are quite useful to the colony.

=== Oceanus ===

[[Oceanus]] (0.65AU) is the closest planet to Titan, and is a tiny ball of rock that is too hot for even for most artificial environments.  Orbital scans show that the [[Anthronoris]] once had facilities on the surface, but it is unknown why.  No [[Terran]] surveys have ever been landed on the surface due to the extreme surface conditions.

=== Coeus ===

[[Coeus]] (0.85AU) is a medium sized planet, rich in resources.  The remains of several [[Anthronoris]] mining facilities were found on the surface, but they were completely unusable and had to be demolished before the Titanians could begin using [[Coeus]]’ resources.

=== Crius ===

[[Crius]] (1.2AU) is an irregularly-shaped ball of rock, possibly a large asteroid that slipped past the other worlds in the system.  The [[Anthronoris]] never used it, but the Titanians set up several automated metal mining, refining and manufacturing plants on its surface. It is one of the cornerstones of the Titanian civil economy, which supports their robust lifestyles.

=== Hyperion ===

[[Hyperion]] (3 AU) has little to offer the Titanians, but evidence suggests it was once an important world in the [[Anthronoris]] defenses of the Titan System. [[Hyperion]] once had a breathable atmosphere, but it was probably artificially created and has long since vanished. Seven warship hulks were found crashed on the surface, and scans indicate that at least ten more were buried by the elements over the thousands of years since the [[Sundering]].  Many expeditions have been launched to the hulks, but nothing of worth has been found. A new project is in the works to excavate one of the buried hulks to see if being buried has preserved any of the [[Anthronoris]] technology.

=== Iapetus ===

[[Iapetus]] (4.1 AU) was once inhabitable, but its civilization and biosphere were both destroyed, probably during the [[Sundering]]. It is believed that the world was actually the main Anthronoris holding in the Titan System, and the fact that it was covered by over three hundred metropolis-sized cities supports this theory.  The planet’s atmosphere is extremely poisonous now, similar to [[Venus]] in the [[Sol System]].  As such, the Titanians have not been able to mount any sort of archaeological expeditions to the surface.

=== Theia ===

[[Theia]] (5.2 AU) is the main colony world of the Titan System, and is considered a "Parallel [[Earth]]." It has a single moon, [[Eos]]. [[Theia]] is noted for its important role as the rallying-point for the remnant of the [[United Earth Alliance]] and for the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] during the [[Second Exodus War]].

=== Rhea ===

[[Rhea]] (7.1 AU) was originally planned as a second colony, but there were not enough volunteers at first. When [[Kabila Kimburu]] reached the [[Orion Arm]] and made friendly contact with humanity, they needed a home.  [[Rhea]]’s vast oceans provided excellent feeding and hunting grounds for them, and they built huge underwater cities and habitats.  Later, when [[Earth]] fell to the [[Onyx Empire]], many refugees moved to both [[Theia]] and [[Rhea]] and found new homes.  [[Rhea]] was briefly overrun by the [[Onyx Empire]], but the [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] settlements went unnoticed.

=== Themis ===

[[Themis]] (9.2 AU) has a large civilian population, mostly to support the extensive military installations the Titanians have built over the years.  Oddly enough, [[Themis]] has an extensive network of floating cities, using simple tethers to keep the cities in predictable locations.  This is due to [[Themis]]’ extremely active tectonic system, which shook apart the first Titanian defense base built on the continent.  Despite its problems, [[Themis]] is an important part of the Titanian defense system, with dozens of orbiting installations, drydocks, and shipyards.  General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] used the world as his headquarters for much of the [[Second Exodus War]], though he was briefly ousted by an [[Empire|Imperial]] incursion.

=== Mnemosyne ===

[[Mnemosyne]] (11 AU) is an enormous gas giant, with over sixty moons.  The Titanians have extensive mining facilities, which they use to exploit a wide variety of materials.  Because of the extensive moon systems, the Titanians have managed to be self-sufficient since the colony was founded. There is no sign that the [[Anthronoris]] ever used any of [[Mnemosyne]]’s resources.  [[Mnemosyne]] was the site of several key battles during the [[Empire]]’s thwarted invasion, and the Titanian fleet was able to avoid destruction by using [[Mnemosyne]]’s moons and gravity well to keep the [[Royal Imperial Navy|Imperial Armada]] from overwhelming them.

=== Phoebe ===

[[Phoebe]] (11.6 AU) is another gas giant, with extensive exotic gasses and even a limited supply of antimatter. The planet’s usefulness was not discovered until recently, but it is now used to power newer experimental systems the Titanians are developing for the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].  During the [[Second Exodus War]], the planet was a key site in the earliest space battles, and General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] used it as a staging point in his counterstrike on the [[Empire|Imperial]] fleet.

=== Tethys ===

[[Tethys]] (12.4 AU) is a gas giant, but is significantly smaller than [[Mnemosyne]] or [[Phoebe]]. It has eight moons, but has not been exploited for resources yet.  [[Tethys]] was the site of some of the first blows in the invasion of the Titan System by the [[Onyx Empire]], but is actually almost worthless in terms of tactical importance.  The Titanians have no installations there, and the [[Onyx Empire]] never built any.  Its only strategic value at the time was the Titanians’ hope that they would be victorious and win an important victory for morale.

=== Cronus ===

[[Cronus]] (16.2 AU) is an inhospitable ball of rock and ice.  Its remoteness and lack of any useful resources made it an unlikely site for a military headquarters, but the [[Onyx Empire]] exploited its crags, canyons, mountains and climate to turn it into a fortress.  This [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] foothold in the Titan System held for six years.  Today, [[Cronus]] is the site of a [[Colonial Commonwealth]] military installation, where the joint forces of the four [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] worlds coordinate most of their joint military operations and share military intelligence.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

Titan was the seventh [[Terrans| Terran]] colony, founded in the [[22nd Century]], well after the [[First Exodus War]].  For years, the scientific community suspected that intelligent life existed somewhere in the galaxy.  When they found Titan, they found the first proof of the existence of intelligent life outside the [[Sol System]].

Most inhabited worlds orbit around white or yellow dwarf stars. Titan is unique: a yellow giant with an unusually wide inhabitable band.  It is technically classified as a Class G9T star, and has twelve planets and over a hundred moons orbiting its various gas planets or free-floating in its asteroid belts.

Titan was once part of the [[Anthronoris Empire]], and the first exploration ships from [[Earth]] immediately discovered this fact when they found a wrecked warship hulk several kilometers long and the remains of many ancient cities on the sixth planet in the system.  The millennia of decay were not kind to the [[Anthronoris]] technology, and the eager colonists found nothing of use other than the structures themselves.

The sixth world was dubbed “[[Theia]].” Each of the twelve planets took a name from one of the twelve Titans of Greek mythos.  Theia is a beautiful world, with a biosphere that had nearly swallowed the ancient cities that dot its surface. The first colony from [[Earth]] landed in the only one completely clear of natural encroachment, and dubbed it [[Titan City]].

The city was carefully picked apart by scientists and archaeologists, but nothing useful could be recovered.  The remains of millions of bodies were found throughout the city, most decayed to the point that they were unrecognizable, but some fossilized skeletons were found intact. The discovery of multi-thousand-year-old human remains this far from [[Earth]] was one of the most fantastic discoveries in human history…until a [[Meroniri]] starship showed up in orbit of [[Earth]] itself a few months later.

Despite the decay of [[Theia]]’s ancient wonders, it became one of the most successful [[Terran]] colonies in the [[United Earth Alliance]].  The twelve worlds were all home to incredible resources and wealth, many of which had already been revealed by its deceased inhabitants in the ages long gone.

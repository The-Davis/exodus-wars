'''President''' was the highest elected executive political office in the [[United Earth Alliance]].

==Elections==

The first President of the [[United Earth Alliance]] was selected by a mutual agreement of the President of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] and the Premier of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]]. [[Martina Acklin]], a Swiss national, was chosen as the first President for the purpose of neutrality and fairness.

Afterwards, the President held office for a term of five years, and a limit of a single term. Electronic elections were held on December 31st the year before the term expired, and the winner was sworn into office the following day. The President of the [[United Earth Alliance]] was elected by a simple majority. Anyone could run, and anyone could vote. Election campaigns often had hundreds of candidates, though only a few were considered "serious contenders" at any given time.

The Third Fleet is commanded by Prefect [[Anthony Hadrian]], the supreme Military commander of the [[Prefecture]].  The fleet is led by the flagship FDS ''Delphinus'' (BG-3), one of the finest-maneuvering warships in the galaxy.  The Third Fleet's role is Naval Intelligence.  It controls 90% of the recon probes and scouts.

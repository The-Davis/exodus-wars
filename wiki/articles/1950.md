== Events of 1950 ==

===January===
*January 6 - The [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] recognizes the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]] after the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] make reparations for the HMS ''Amethyst'' incident.
*January 9 - The [[Israel (Terra)|Israeli]] government recognizes the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]].
*January 13 - [[Finland (Terra)|Finland]] forms diplomatic relations to the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]].
*January 23 - [[The Jihad]]: The Knesset passes a resolution that states Jerusalem is the capital of [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]].
*January 24 - [[Cold War]]: Klaus Fuchs, a [[East Germany (Terra)|German]] physicist, surrenders to [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] authorities on charges of being a [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] spy.
*January 31 - President [[Thomas Dewey]] orders the development of the hydrogen bomb in response to the detonation the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]]'s first atomic bomb in [[1949]].

===February===
*February 1 - Chiang Kai-shek is executed by the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] [[Communist]] Party for leading the Nationalist [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] government.
*February 9 - [[Cold War]]: In his speech to the Republican Women's Club, Senator [[Joseph McCarthy]] accuses the Department of State of being filled with 205 [[Communists]].
*February 11 - [[Vietnam War]]: Two Viet Minh battalions attack a [[France (Terra)|French]] base in Indochina.
*February 12
**[[Vietnam War]]: [[Communists]] riot in Paris against [[France (Terra)|French]] occupation of French Indochina and in support of the Viet Minh.
**[[Albert Einstein]] warns that nuclear war could lead to mutual destruction.
*February 14 - The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] and the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]] sign a mutual defense treaty.

===March===
*March 1 - [[Cold War]]: Klaus Fuchs is convicted of spying for the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]]. KGB operatives break him out of prison and smuggle him back to [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Russia]].
*March 8 - [[Cold War]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] announces it has developed its own version of the atomic bomb.
*March 22 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]] demands that the [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] remove all of their troops the in Suez Canal region.

===April===
*April 27 - The [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] formally recognizes [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]].

===May===
*May 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Huntsville Times runs the headline, "''Dr. von Braun Says Rocket Flights Possible to Moon.''"

===June===
*June 25 - [[Korean War]]: [[Korea (Terra)|Korean]] War begins.
*June 28 - [[Korean War]]: [[Korea (Terra)|North Korean]] forces capture Seoul.

===August===
*August 8 - [[Winston Churchill]] supports idea of pan-European army allied with [[Canada (Terra)|Canada]] and the [[United States (Terra)|United States]].

===September===
*September 1 - [[Cold War]]: [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungarian]] major general Laszlo Viragen killed by [[Communists]] as he attempts to defect to [[Austria (Terra)|Austria]].
*September 15 - [[Korean War]]: Allied troops land in Inchon, occupied by [[Korea (Terra)|North Korea]], to begin the Battle of Inchon.
*September 19 - [[Cold War]]: [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] purges [[Communist]] officials.

===October===
*October 7 - [[Cold War]]: The [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] invasion of Tibet begins.
*October 15 - [[Cold War]]: In [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] the [[Communist]] Party retains power in national elections.
*October 20 - [[Cold War]]: [[Australia (Terra)|Australia]] passes the [[Communist]] Party Dissolution Act, later upheld by the High Court.

===November===
*November 4 - [[United Nations]] ends the diplomatic isolation of [[Spain (Terra)|Spain]].
*November 8 - [[Korean War]]: An F-80 piloted by USAF Lieutenant Russell Brown intercepts two [[Korea (Terra)|North Korean]] MiG-15s near the Yalu River and shoots them down.
*November 11 - The Mattachine Society is founded in Los Angeles as the first homosexual-liberation organization. [[CIA]] operatives set fire to the building housing the first meeting, killing everyone inside.
*November 22 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslims in [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]] riot against the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]]'s occupation of the Suez region.
*November 26 - [[Korean War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] troops, operating under the flag of [[Korea (Terra)|North Korea]] launch a massive counterattack against [[Korea (Terra)|South Korean]] and [[United Nations]] forces, ending any thought of a quick end to the conflict.
*November 29 - [[Korean War]]: [[Korea (Terra)|North Korean]] troops force a desperate retreat of [[United Nations]] forces from [[Korea (Terra)|North Korea]].
*November 30 - [[Korean War]]: President [[Thomas Dewey]] threatens to use nuclear weapons in [[Korea (Terra)|Korea]].

===December===
*December 11 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslims riot in [[Singapore (Terra)|Singapore]], leaving 18 dead and 173 injured.

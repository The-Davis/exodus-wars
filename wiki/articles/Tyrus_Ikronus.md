'''Tyrus Ikronus'''
*Place of Death: [[Kronheim]]

''Tyrus Ikronus'' was a relative of the [[Anthronoris]] emperor, who was stranded on what would later become known as [[Kronheim]] after the [[Sundering]]. Tyrus became the leader of the planet, and the father of the [[Ikronin]] people.

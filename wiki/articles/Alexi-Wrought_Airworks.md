'''Alexi-Wrought Airworks''' - Alexi-Wrought Airworks is an aerospace company formed from the merger of Alexi Synthetics and Wrought Aerospace Engineering in [[2267]]. Its primary market was civilian commerical and industrial aircraft and composites for the latter half of the [[23rd Century]].

In the early [[24th Century]], the company was chosen by the [[Onyx Empire]] to act as their primary provider of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] during the military buildup leading up to the [[Second Exodus War]].

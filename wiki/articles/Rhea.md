Rhea was originally planned as a second colony, but there were not enough volunteers at first. When [[Kabila Kimburu]] reached the [[Orion Arm]] and made friendly contact with humanity, they needed a home. Rhea’s vast oceans provided excellent feeding and hunting grounds for them, and they built huge underwater cities and habitats. Later, when [[Earth]] fell to the [[Onyx Empire]], many refugees moved to both [[Theia]] and Rhea and found new homes. Rhea was briefly overrun by the Onyx Empire, but the Kimburu settlements went unnoticed. 

== The Rhea Massacre ==

Rhea was the site of the infamous [[Rhea Massacre]], an Imperial war crime which ended the lives of over 200,000 civillians.

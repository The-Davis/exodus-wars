The [[Ikronin Jurekön]] military is slightly more relaxed than others, but it is still extremely efficient in its chain of command and order structure. Ranks are the same in each branch, but the organization is different.

== Kopman Marinen ==

The Kopman Marinen does not organize into grand fleets or task forces like other navies. Instead, it has multiple "Flottas" of ships, typically fifteen starships in each Flotta. A merchant convoy is usually a single Flotta.

To command a starship in the Kompan Marinen, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Kommendör. To command a Flotta, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Kommodör.

== Flygvapnet ==

The Flygvapnet are the least organized of the military branches. It has the most soldiers of fortune and thrill seekers, but still manages to keep some structural integrity.

The highest level of organization is a Fåglar, which is the go-between for the Corporations to their free-spirit mercenary pilots. Individual squadrons--Skvadrons--are usually tight-knit groups who sign service contracts together.

To command a Fåglar, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Överste. To command a Skvadron, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Kommendör. To fly an aircraft, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Löjtnant.

== Armen ==

The Armen has few levels of organization. Instead of upper-level Armies, each Corporation army is the uppermost level of organization. Below Corporate Army is the Regemente. Each Regemente has five Stridgrupps. Each Stridgrupp has three Kompanis. A Tank Kompani has twenty-four tanks, and an Infantry Kompani has sixty troops.

A Kommodör is usually in charge of a corporate army. To command a Regemente, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Kommendör. To command a Stridgrupp, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Överste. To command a Kompani, an [[Ikronin]] must hold the rank of Kapten.

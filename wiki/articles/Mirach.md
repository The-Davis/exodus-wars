'''Mirach''' was an inhabitable planet in the [[Mirach System]].  It hadn't even been properly named before it and the entire star system were destroyed in [[2324]].

== History ==

The Mirach System was colonized in early [[2324]], and 6000 colonists had settled in two cities.  The star system was still being surveyed and the planet was still being mapped when the [[Volucris Incursion]] began.  The colonists and survey teams were evacuated, and the Volucris took over. It was the first ground battle of the Incursion.

The Meroniri took decisive action, and used a Mass-to-Energy Conversion bomb on the star.  The entire star system was destroyed.  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] filed a formal protest with the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] over the destruction of their sovereign world, but nothing came of it.

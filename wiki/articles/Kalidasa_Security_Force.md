The '''Kalidasa Security Force''' is a small army formed solely for the defense of the planet [[Kalidasa System|Kalidasa]] and its outlying colonial possessions. Like the [[Kalidasa Navy|Kalidasan Navy]] and [[Kalidasa Air and Space Corps|Air and Space Corps]], all units in the Kalidasa Security Force were created and designed in-system.

==Structure==

The Kalidasa Security Force operates as both the army of the [[Kalidasa Planetary Alliance]], as well as the government police force, aiding local law enforcement when the situation calls for it. Although they are well equipped to defend and patrol [[Kalidasa System|Kalidasa]], the [[Kalidasa Security Force]] is not designed for large scale wars or to launch invasions of other systems.

==Vehicles==

Most [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasan]] vehicles are hovercraft. The Seagull, Albatros, and Pelican are all excellent light combat [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]], but not well armored. For heavy combat, the Sentry, Chariot and Cavalier [[:Category:Tanks|hovertanks]] come into play. They can handle a direct fight.

The [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] excel at artillery on the battlefield, and use three purpose-built designs to bring heavy fire into play. The Grizzly is a light portable field gun, and its bigger cousin the Crocodile is a heavier design. For truly devastating firepower, the Mammoth heavy field artillery piece can be brought to bear.

Kalidasans have reserve and security troops for standard infantry operations, but for prolonged combat, they call on their HAZARDS suit: a high-powered battle armor design.

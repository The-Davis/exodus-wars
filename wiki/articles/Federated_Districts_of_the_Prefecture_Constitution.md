We the survivors of [[Torimur]] have written this Constitution in order to establish justice, ensure the life and liberty of our citizens, provide for defense, and to preserve the sovereignty of the [[Prefecture]] and its allies. 

==Article I==
===Section 1===

All legislative powers granted by this Constitution shall be given to a Congress, which shall consist of a Senate and Curiate.

===Section 2===
*Clause 1: The Curiate shall be composed of members chosen every four years by the people, civilians and citizens, of the Prefecture.
*Clause 2: Representatives of the Curiate shall be apportioned among the Districts of the Prefecture, according to the number of persons residing in the District.  The enumeration of Representatives shall be determined every eight years through a census of all persons. The number of Representatives shall not exceed one for every hundred thousand citizens, but each district will have at least one Representative per inhabited star system.
*Clause 3: If a seat in the Curiate goes unfilled, the Governor of said seat's District shall appoint a Representative to fill the vacancy.
*Clause 4: The Curiate has the power to impeach the Consul, and will pass judgment on an impeached Prefect.
*Clause 5: Judgment in cases of impeachment shall not extend further than to removal from office, and disqualification to hold any office or military rank within the Prefecture.
*Clause 7: The Chancellor of the Curiate will be chosen at the beginning of each Congressional session by popular vote of the representatives.

===Section 3===
*Clause 1: The Senate of the Prefecture will be composed of three Senators from each District.  Senators shall be chosen every eight years by the citizens of the Prefecture.  Each Senator will have a single vote within the Senate.
*Clause 2: The Consul of the Prefecture shall be President of the Senate, but will have no vote, unless the Senators are equally divided.
*Clause 3: The Senate will try all impeached Consul, and have the power to impeach the Prefect.  The Consul shall not be convicted without a two-thirds majority vote.
*Clause 4: Judgment in cases of impeachment shall not extend further than to removal from office, and disqualification to hold any office within the Prefecture.

===Section 4===
*Clause 1: All bills intended to become law within the Prefecture must originate in the Hall of Districts, but the Senate may propose or concur with amendments.
*Clause 2: Every bill that passes the Curiate and the Senate must be presented to the Consul and Prefect before becoming law.  The Consul and Prefect both must sign it for the bill to become law.  If one or the other objects, a two-thirds majority of both Congresses will pass the bill.  If both the Consul and Prefect oppose the bill, then it shall not become law without a unanimous vote from both Congresses.

===Section 5===
*Clause 1: The Congress has the power to collect taxes to provide for the defense and infrastructure of the Prefecture, but all taxes shall be uniform throughout the Districts and on all citizens.
*Clause 2: Taxes may not be imposed on land, homes, or property passing to next-of-kin upon death.
*Clause 3: The Congress has the power to coin money and regulate its value.  It may also regulate the value of foreign currency.
*Clause 4: The Congress must use a uniform standard of Weights and Measures meeting the specifications of the Metric System.
*Clause 5: The Congress has the power to provide for the punishment of counterfeiting the securities and currency of the Prefecture, as well as piracy in international space.
*Clause 6: The Congress has the power to establish trade routes in space, and to regulate traffic thereof.  Said routes are subject to toll and tax as seen fit by the Congress.
*Clause 7: The Congress has the power to make all laws necessary for carrying into execution the powers stated in this Constitution.

==Article II==
===Section 1===
*Clause 1: The executive power shall be vested in the Consul of the Prefecture.  He shall hold his office for a term of eight years before being subject to a second election.
*Clause 2: Each District shall appoint electors, equal to the number of Senators and Representatives to which the District is entitled in the Congresses.
*Clause 3: The electors will meet and vote by ballot for the Consul.  The votes must be tallied and signed by each elector, then certified.  The certified tally will be presented to the citizens of the Prefecture by the Chancellor of the Hall.  The person having the greatest number of elector's votes will be the Consul.
*Clause 4: No person who has served voluntary service in the Prefecture Military shall be eligible for the position of Consul, as it is a civilian position.  Draftees are exempt from this clause.
*Clause 5: If the Consul is removed from office by death, resignation, inability to act as Consul, the Chancellor of the Hall shall act as Consul until an election can be held.  Said election must be held within six months of the Consul's removal.
*Clause 6: The Consul must take the following Oath of Office:--"I do solemnly swear that I will faithfully execute the Office of Consul of the Prefecture, and will, preserve, protect and defend the Constitution of the Prefecture."
*Clause 7: No person shall be elected to the office of the Consul more than twice.

==Article III==
===Section 1===

The judicial power of the Prefecture, shall be vested in the Court of the National Justice, and in lesser courts as the congress directs.  The judges, both of the National Justice and inferior courts, will hold their offices for a term of twenty years.

===Section 2===
*Clause 1: The judicial power will be over all cases, in law and equity under this Constitution, the laws of the Prefecture, and treaties made with the Allied Planets.
*Clause 2: The trial of all crimes shall be by Jury; and must be held within the District the crime was committed in.  If the crime was committed in International space, or in null-District space, the trial shall take place in the Court of the National Justice.

==Article IV==
===Section 1===

A standing army will be kept by the Prefecture, and commanded by a Prefect.  The military powers of the Prefecture will be vested in the Prefect.

===Section 2===
*Clause 1: The Prefect has the power to make treaties with other nations, but the treaties must be ratified by the Curiate, Senate, and Consul.
*Clause 2: The Prefect or his officers may draft a citizen into the military when the Prefecture is at war with an enemy.  A draftee will be given full salary of a regular soldier, and given an option to leave the service at least once every two years.
*Clause 3: The military regular personnel are subject to court martial, and are not protected by the rights of Prefecture citizens.  No draftee will be held in court martial, but will instead by tried under the Court of the National Justice.

==Article V==

The Congress, whenever two thirds of both the Curiate and Senate deem it necessary, may propose amendments to this Constitution.  Said amendments must be ratified by the Districts of the Prefecture. 

==Article VI==
===Section 1===

The citizens of the Prefecture recognize the inalienable rights of man, and thus place them in this Constitution to ensure that these rights are upheld for all citizens.

===Section 2===
*Clause 1: Congress shall make no law establishing a religion, or prohibiting the free exercise of religion.
*Clause 2: The right to absolute freedom of speech, freedom of the press, freedom of broadcasting, and also the right to petition the government for redress of grievances shall not be denied by any law.
*Clause 3: The military and other government forces shall not intervene against peaceful assemblies, but may observe carefully to ensure that no law is broken.
*Clause 4: In order that no oppressive government ever usurp the freedom of the people, the right to keep and bear weapons shall not be limited or infringed by law, regulation or policy of government officials.
*Clause 5: Unwarranted search and seizure of private property is unlawful.  Any persons killed while breaking and entering private property, providing acceptable proof is provided, shall not be considered murdered.
*Clause 6: No person shall be forced to testify as a witness in court, and shall be free to go about their business if they so choose.
*Clause 7: No person shall be subjected to trial twice for the same crime.
*Clause 8: All persons have the right to rapid trial and two appeals of the results of their trial. Likewise, they have the right to know beforehand the charges that they are facing, and have the right to legal counsel.
*Clause 9: Excessive bail and fines must not be imposed. Because punishment must be both cruel and unusual to be effective, only cruel and unusual punishments shall be carried out.
*Clause 10: The sentence of death against human criminals shall be carried out through a public hanging, through use of rope and gallows of whatever materials the executioner deems fit.  Alien death sentences shall be carried out according to the ruling of the courts.
*Clause 11: Neither slavery nor involuntary servitude, except as a punishment for crime, shall exist within the Prefecture. 

==Article VII==

The ratification of the Convention of Admirals shall be sufficient to establish this Constitution between Districts.

This Constitution is hereby ratified by the ruling council of the Prefecture military on this day, April 30, in the year Two Thousand Three Hundred and Twenty-Three.

The signatures of [[Antonius Aurelius Hadrian]], [[Robert Marshall]], [[Travis Carpenter]] and [[Marcus Rathbone]] are attached to the original Constitution.

The Fifth Fleet is commanded by Admiral [[Jeff Nolan]].  The fleet is led by the flagship FDS ''Judicator'' (BG-5), built in honor of the famous Vigilant Frigate [[Jeff Nolan]] commanded during the [[Second Exodus War]].  The Fifth Fleet is the current location of the Atlas Fleet--Judicator, Gorgon, Manticore, Atlas, and Sirius--the greatest of the Royal Imperial Navy's battle groups.  Today, the Fifth Fleet is used mostly for border patrol.

'''Leifstein Composite Armor''' is the standard in secondary defense for the [[Mesarthrim]]. In terms of complexity and effectiveness, it is more akin to [[Reigen]] [[Reigen Järnhud Composite Armor|Järnhud Composite]] or [[Ixoarchada]] [[Friklos Armor|Friklos]] than to lesser composites such as [[Lorithos Tzaiko Composite Armor|Tzaiko]] or [[Terran Composite Armor|Terran Composite]].  

Leifstein's outermost layer is an ablative substance derived from [[Anderung]] fusion reactors, which is plated over an actively-cooled superconductor.  The ablative layer absorbs or deflects the initial impact of energy weaponry that pierces shielding, while the superconductor negates the effects of electromagnetic and electrostatic charges.  Behind the superconductor is a gap to absorb the blast of armor-piercing warheads, followed by a thick layer of woven composite plating to defeat projectiles.  Technically, only this last layer is actually "Leifstein Composite", named after the scientist that finally stole the secret of its construction from the Anderung, although the whole of the layers are together the armor ''system''

For additional protection against missiles and projectiles, there are many reactive panels (small shaped charges tied to a proximity sensor) integrated into the outer ablative layer.  When the sensor detects an imminent missile impact, it triggers the charge.  The blast can deflect the incoming weapon, or at least softens the impact.

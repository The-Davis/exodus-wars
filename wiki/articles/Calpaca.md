'''Calpaca''' is a large inhabitted asteroid in the Mu Crucis belt.  It houses an extensive computer research lab headed by [[Benjamin Hlow]]

== History ==

The Calpaca asteroid was charted by [[Onyx Empire]] survey teams when the [[Novaya]] academy was being built.  It was an ideal remote location for [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' new Cryptography Center.  [[Yul Besud]] and Benjamin Hlow set up operations in a compound within the asteroid.

After the fall of the Empire, the Cryptography Center was seized by the Prefecture's [[Intelligence of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Central Office of Military Intelligence]]. Most of the staff was allowed to remain on site, but Yul Besud was determined to be too much of a security risk and was removed.

== Events of 16451BC ==

The troops under the command of [[Meronir Tronit]] were organized into a training and combat group known as [[Aseta Joreno]].  As more troops were brought into the fold one of [[Meronir Tronit|Tronit]]’s lieutenants, [[Sarel Rivarsen]], decided to form her own school, [[Aseta Serada]].

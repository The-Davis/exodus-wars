The city of '''Denver''' is located in the South Platte River Valley on the High Plains just east of the Front Range of the Southern Rocky Mountains.

From the formation of the [[United Earth Alliance]] until [[2315]], Denver served as the capital of [[Earth]] and of the Alliance. The Grand Assembly of the United Earth Alliance was located in the city until [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] troops burned it to the ground during the invasion of Earth in 2315. After the invasion, the new Imperial capital was established in [[Memphis]].

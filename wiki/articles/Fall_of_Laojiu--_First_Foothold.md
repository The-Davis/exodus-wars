Set after "Tempest: The Proclamation"

by Seth Dickinson and Austin Bailey

=====Prefecture Deep Space Detection System, 0110 Hours=====
     This star system, located on the very edge of the Prefecture's Laojiu District, is lifeless, nameless, and worldless. Its only significance lies in its position: straddling the easiest hyperspace route into Laojiu space.
     Although the system's swollen binary stars have no natural satellites, there is a single technological artifact in the system; an early-warning buoy deposited six months earlier by a Prefecture Essex carrier. The buoy's power cells are quiescent, leaching out only enough energy to keep the buoy's passive gravity sensors working.
     Something ticks within the buoy's mechanical brain. Fail-safe systems kick in, examining the sudden change in sensor readings. Automatic algorithms find a fault: a number outside the pre-programmed range of probable inputs.
     A subroutine rechecks the sensor's input. The gravity sensors, capable of detecting the subtle distortions caused by passing vessels in hyperspace, are extremely fragile. Perhaps something is wrong.
     Nothing is wrong. The sensor readings, however, improbable, are correct: over a hundred masses have just flickered past in hyperspace, almost grazing the system's hyperdrive limit.
     The buoy activates its Ansible, shrieking a warning across the stars.

=====Essex Carrier Polaris, Edge of the Laojiu System, 0115 Hours=====
     "What the hell is going on?" The Polaris' captain strides onto the bridge, rubbing sleep from his eyes.
     "We just received a warning from one of the deep-space surveillance systems, sir," the lieutenant manning the COM station reports. "It's detecting a massive hyperspace transit."
     "Confirm that!" the captain demands. "Can we get a vector? Have any other perimeter satellites detected anything?"
     "No, sir." The COM officer frowns at his displays. "It's difficult to believe that so many ships could travel on such a small hyperspace lane, especially when it comes so close to so many stellar-size masses. The precision they'd need to maneuver like that - it must be taxing their hyperdrives to the limit. Maybe it's a software error, sir?"
     The captain has already understood. "I don't think so, Lieutenant. Are the readings good enough to determine the type of drives in use?"
     "Let me-"
     "Sir!" the tactical officer shouts. "Multiple exit flares - forty thousand kilometers! Ships exiting hyperspace all around us!"
     "Identify!" The captain reaches for his shock frame. "Helm, charge the KF drive and prepare to jump!"
     The tac officer's eyes widen as dozens of crimson warnings fill his displays. "Mesarthrim warships, sir! Carriers, cruisers, transports, gunships - it's a whole damn fleet!"
     More alarms sound. "Vampires!" the tactical officer barks. "Twenty - thirty - forty missiles at zero-six-zero!"
     The captain's plots are filling with swarms of hostile icons. "Helm, take us out of here!"
     "I can't get a plot, sir!" the helm officer shouts. "The exit flares - they're confusing our nav systems!"
     The Essex's neutron cannons open fire, targeting the incoming missiles, but it is a losing battle. Mesarthrim shipkiller warheads penetrate the Polaris' shields and rip her fragile hull apart like an eggshell.

=====Laojiu Planetary Defense Center, Nju-zanjiang, 0230 Hours=====
     Even in the full light of day, the Mesarthrim fleet is visible from the surface of Laojiu. A pale aurora flickers as Mesarthrim blazers gut orbiting satellites.
     Standing in the chaos of the planet's military command center, Governor Zhong watches the disintegration of his world's orbital defenses with the resigned acceptance of a true Confucian. We have labored for years to build our world, he thinks. Now the buzzards descend to snatch our handiwork away from us.
     "Is there nothing we can do?" one of his aides asks. "Ships we may call upon?"
     "Our Ansible relays were the first to get knocked out. We couldn’t get a message out before it was hit. Our only hope is that the Icronians managed to get a message out on their inter-gate lines. Otherwise, it’s up to us. Emilia Baker's fighters will fight them if they try to land, but she will not send her squadrons to defend the satellites - not after what happened at Canopus. We must accept their control of our skies."
     Another aide signals to Zhong with an upraised hand, bringing him to a COM display. The bearded face of a Soren Irikrag, the Icronian Commander of the Laojiu district, fills the screen.
     "Governor Zhong, I must be brief. Moments ago Myt’herra began boarding our stations. They’ve landed by the hundreds. Our last sensor readings before they were jammed showed dozens of warships. When we fall, they will come after you.”
     Despite the violence about him, Irikrag is somber and calm—he knows his own fate is sealed. "We shall hold them to the last man. Canopus shall be avenged. Prepare your defenses."
     Zhong bows his head. "Any time you can buy will help us prepare."
     There is a brief silence. Irikrag awaits the inevitable question—a question Zhong asks, though he already knows the answer.
     “Commander…any news over the inter-gate?”
     “Before we shut down the gates, we got through to the Gulrýnnur.” The old Icronian’s voice falters. “There will be no reinforcements. Marshall said there just aren’t enough ships to mount an attack…but you knew this.”
     “Yes Commander. Good luck.”
     “May the gods protect you, Governor.”

=====The Mesarthrim Fleet Orbiting Laojiu, 0800 Hours=====
     Admiral Hansen Dietrich, commander of the Mesarthrim 2nd Fleet, is not a man to isolate himself amidst electronics and maps as his men fight. He is standing on the flight deck of his carrier as the first wave of Pegasus assault transports returns from their missions, waiting for a first-hand report from his Myt'herra.
     The Pegasus' troop bay hisses open, and Dietrich's eyes narrow as the first Myt'herra file out. Their blazers are scorched by discharge, their armor spattered in blood and grime. Many of them are wounded; medics rush into the bay to collect litters.
     Dietrich steps up to the Marine colonel in charge of the assault. "I was told the Icronians would not surrender."
     "The Icronians wouldn't give up the gates." The Colonel brushes a hand across his face wearily. "We had to clear them out, room by room. Even the engineers and accountants fought - it was like they were possessed. By the time we reached the reactors or the Gate controls, they had been thoroughly destroyed.
     "We took prisoners.” The Colonel nods back at the ship. “Five of them.” Out of a crew of several hundred.
     Admiral Dietrich nods. "We tried to negotiate with them, but they wouldn't even accept our signals. Other Myt’herra companies are still engaged on two of the gates - the Icronians have barricaded themselves in.”
     He hesitates. "If your men are prepared to deploy again-"
     "We're Myt'herra, sir." The Colonel snaps a salute, drawing another grim nod from Dietrich.
     "Very well, Colonel. Re-arm and speak to my tactical officers. They'll tell you what to do."

=====The Laojiu District=====
     By the end of April 15th, the Mesarthrim have achieved complete space superiority in the Laojiu district. Forty-five warships set up a blockade over Laojiu, while another twenty-five block off Labrea. Casere, Miyada, and Altona each have 10 warships in orbit. In each case, the Mesarthrim strip the worlds of all orbiting satellites and board every manned facility. None of the Icronian gate crews surrender; just as in Laojiu, thousands are killed as Myt’herra storm the Gates.
     Five Prefecture Essex carriers constituted the entire naval defense of the district. Although three managed to make a short stand over Labrea, it was an act of futility against the overwhelmingly superior Mesarthrim forces. Only one survives the day.
     The governors of Miyada and Altona surrender their worlds less than twenty-four hours after the Mesarthrim issue an ultimatum. In only a day, only Casere, Labrea, and Laojiu remain in Prefecture hands, and the inhabitants of the Laojiu District settle in for a long siege.

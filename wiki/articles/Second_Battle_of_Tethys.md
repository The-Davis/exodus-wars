== Overview ==

Since its installation thirteen days earlier, the fighters of Tethys Anchorage launched multiple missions against the Imperial forces stationed on Cronus.  They caused some loss of life and of war materiels, and were an endless source of consternation to Admiral [[Emil Torsc]] and his Combat Air Patrols.  At his insistence, Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] authorized the destruction of Tethys Anchorage as the next step in [[Operation Tartarus]].

=== Alliance Forces ===

Tethys Anchorage was still defended by the 17th Space Battle Group's 8th Combat Squadron--made up of the UEAS ''Haven'', UEAS ''Bainbridge'', UEAS ''Spite of War'', and UEAS ''Kitty Hawk'' and commanded by Admiral [[Gordon Dannins]].  The station itself had six fighter squadrons on permanent assignment.

=== Imperial Forces ===

Admiral Marshall assigned two carrier groups, one led by the [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']] and Commodore [[Julia Michaels]] and the other led by the [[HMS Secutor|HMS ''Secutor'']] and Commodore Andrew Bartleby, to the mission.  Commodore Michaels was given operational command.

== Order of Battle ==

Commodore Michaels timed her attack to launch just as the majority of the United Earth Alliance's fighter squadrons were returning from an attack on Cronus.  As soon as Tethys Anchorage brought in the majority of its fighters, the HMS ''Secutor'' and its escorts launched a strike at the UEAS ''Haven'' and the UEAS ''Kitty Hawk''.  The ''Kitty Hawk'' suffered serious damage to its engines, but the ''Haven'' and the ''Bainbridge'' pursued the ''Secutor''.

Almost immediately, the HMS ''Judicator'' and its escorts launched their attack on the station itself.  The ''Judicator'''s fighters attacked the launch bays of Tethys Anchorage, while the carrier's escorts attacked the UEAS ''Spite of War''.

Within minutes, most of the Alliance fighters were trapped inside the station.  The launch bays were jammed or destroyed.  A mere ten minutes after the first shot was fired, the orbital engines of Tethys Anchorage were shot to pieces, and the station lost its orbital position.  The installation was doomed.

With her mission accomplished, Commodore Michaels gave the withdrawal order to her forces.

== Aftermath ==

Tethys Anchorage was a total loss.  Within two hours of its orbit destabilizing, the station was pulled into the gas giant.  Forty of its sixty-member crew were not accounted for, and were presumed Killed In Action.  Five Alliance fighter squadrons went down with the station, and half of the squadron that escape the station were destroyed.  Other than the damage to the ''Kitty Hawk'''s engines, none of the ships under Dannins' command suffered any more than superficial damage.

The Imperial forces did not escape unscathed.  Eight fighters were shot down in their attack runs on Tethys Anchorage, and the [[HMS Elgon|HMS ''Elgon'']] was lost with all hands after it maneuvered too close to the UEAS ''Haven'' and was gunned down.

While the loss of Tethys Anchorage was only a minor strategic loss to the United Earth Alliance, it was a much more significant loss of morale.  Their first offensive since the start of the war had proven ineffective.

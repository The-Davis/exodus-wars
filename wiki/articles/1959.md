== Events of 1959 ==
===January===
*January 16 - [[The Technology Race]]: The HIV virus is discovered in the Congo. Fearing its assault on the human immune system, the British SIS, US CIA, and Soviet KGB send in hundreds of operatives to kill every infected human and animal.
*January 8 - Francois Pasqua is inaugurated as the first president of French Fifth Republic.

===February===
*February 6 - [[The Technology Race]]: At Cape Canaveral, Florida, the first successful test firing of a Titan intercontinental ballistic missile is accomplished. The Titan hits a target the size of a small car in the middle of the Pacific Ocean with pinpoint accuracy.
*February 17 - The United States launches the Vanguard II weather satellite.
*February 19 - The British Empire grants the Republic of Cyprus independence as a Commonwealth Nation.

===March===
*March 17 - Tenzin Gyatso, the 14th Dalai Lama, flees Tibet due to Chinese occupation, and travels to the British territory of India.
*March 18 - President Strom Thurmond signs bill allowing for Hawaiian statehood.

===April===
*April 9 - [[The Technology Race]]: NASA announces its selection of seven military pilots to become the first US astronauts: Malcolm Carpenter, Gordon Cooper, John Glenn, "Gus" Grissom, "Wally" Schirra, Alan Shepard, and "Deke" Slayton.

===May===
*May 9 - [[The Technology Race]]: The British Empire announces its selection of seven Royal Air Force pilots to become the first British astronauts: Perry Evans, Terry Carver, Michael Simmons, George Squire, Thomas Stirrup, and David Walker.

===June===
*June 3 - The Republic of Singapore becomes an independent member of the Commonwealth Nations. Lee Kuan Yew is the first Prime Minister.
*June 9 - [[Cold War]]: The USS ''George Washington''  is launched as the first submarine to carry ballistic missiles.

===July===
*July 3 - [[The Technology Race]]: The medical research group studying Minamata disease comes to the conclusion that mercury poisoning is the cause.
*July 7 - [[The Technology Race]]: [[Venus]] occults the star Regulus, allowing astronomers to determine the diameter of [[Venus]] and the structure of [[Venus]]' atmosphere.
*July 24 - [[Cold War]]: At the opening of the American National Exhibition in Moscow, United States Vice President Joseph McCarthy and Soviet Premier Nikita Khrushchev have a "kitchen debate."

===August===
*August 7 - [[The Technology Race]]: NASA launches Explorer VI from Cape Canaveral, Florida.
*August 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: Explorer VI sends the first picture of [[Terra]] from space.
*August 21 - Hawaii is admitted as the 50th U.S. state.

===September===
*September 13 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Soviet Union's Luna II crashes onto the [[Luna]] as the first man-made object on [[Terra]]'s moon.
*September 25 - [[The Jihad]]: Sri Lanka's prime minister is assassinated by Muslim agents from Arabia.

===October===
*October 7 - [[The Technology Race]]: Soviet probe Luna III sends back the first photos of the far side of [[Luna]].
*October 13 - [[The Technology Race]]: United States launches the Explorer VII spacecraft.
*October 31 - The HIV epidemic continues to spread in the Congo. Residents of the region riot, blaming the French for the murders that have been taking place. The covert KGB, CIA, and SIS murder missions continue undetected.

===November===
*November 11 - The neutrino is first detected by Clyde Cowan and Fred Reines.

===December===
*December 1 - [[Cold War]]: The Antarctic Treaty is signed by twelve countries, including the United States, the British Empire, and the Soviet Union. This landmark treaty set aside Antarctica as a scientific preserve and bans military activity on that continent.

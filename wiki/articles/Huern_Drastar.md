'''Huern Drastar''' is the traditional [[Huorak]] name of the Huern Dynasty, the line of descent for the position of [[Prekohr]] since its founding.

The name [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] is synonymous with the Huern Drastar. When the Huern line died out, the government was renamed with the name of the new [[Prekohr]].

The Huern Drastar was succeeded by the [[Cai Drastar]] in [[2350]].  The government was renamed [[Rikaz o Fii Cai iv Huerna]] after the Lorithos settled on their new homeworld in [[2355]].

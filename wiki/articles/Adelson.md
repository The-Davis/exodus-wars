'''Adelson''' is a planet in the [[Omicron Carinae System]]. It was rendered uninhabitable by military action in [[2328]].

== History ==

The [[Terrans]] founded a colony on Adelson in [[2160]]. It was a non-occupied protectorate of the [[United Earth Alliance]] until [[2319]], when it was conquered by the [[Onyx Empire]]. Adelson was liberated by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] in [[2323]], and turned over to the sovereignty of the Prefecture in the [[Treaty of Memphis]].

Despite the official sovereignty of the Prefecture, the United Mesarthrim Clans occupied the planet until the interim government held open elections to determine whether Adelson would remain under Prefecture control or become independent. Sparked by a current of anti-[[Mesarthrim]] racism and fear, the people of Adelson voted to remain in the Prefecture by 86% to 14%. Elections were held on July 6, [[2323]], and Adelson was officially placed into the [[Tavou District]].

Adelson was chosen as the hub of [[Ixoarchada]] commerce with the [[Prefecture]] due to its location.  The key export of Adelson is the advanced anti-gravity VTOL lift system invented by the Ixoarchada. However, Adelson's limited manufacturing capacity makes it difficult to meet demand, and the trade implications of the [[Tempest War]] mean that the VTOLs of the [[Prefecture]] will not be purely anti-gravity based for some time.

Because of the wealth available in the manufacturing businesses of Adelson, it enjoyed a brisk immigration rate from other Prefecture Districts. Employment at the factories and the construction companies busy building new factories was easy to come by. The jobless rate of Adelson was very low from [[2323]] until its demise in [[2328]].

Adelson was one of the few worlds that was a perfect [[Earth]] parallel, requiring no major imports from other worlds. The agricultural industry was nothing like a [[Laojiu District]] world's, but it met the local demand nicely.

== Geography ==

Adelson's capital city was New Detroit. It had a major interstellar starport a few kilometers from the capital, known as the Dianon Intelstellar Spaceport.

== Destruction ==

In the conflict known as the [[Tempest War]], Adelson was marked for invasion by the [[United Centusi States]]. The [[Prefecture]]'s defenses repelled several invasion attempts, resulting in an order from [[William Camp]] to bombard the planet with nuclear weapons.

Prior to the bombardment, the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] indirectly intervened, evacuating their citizens living on the planet. Under the terms of their treaties, the [[Mesarthrim]] were forced to let them through. While they could not evacuate the entire [[Terran]] population, they used a loophole to evacuate all [[Terran]] employees of [[Ixoarchada]] export companies--companies which saw a sudden hiring boom immediately before the evacuation.

Adelson's population centers were contaminated by nuclear fallout, and the Prefecture declared the planet uninhabitable, and evacuated the remaining survivors. Several billion cultures of [[Zander's Strain]] were left behind on Adelson in an active attempt to reclaim it, and Adelson is estimated to regain viability for human inhabitation by the year 2415.

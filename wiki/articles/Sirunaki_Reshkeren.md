==History==

After the [[Sundering]] of humanity, the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]] began a massive campaign of expansion and conquest into the far reaches of the [[Perseus Arm]]. They have found and conquered dozens of diverse alien species, many of which are now extinct. The [[Sirunaki]] were another matter.

The [[Sirunaki]] were extremely powerful when the [[Ngrligru]] made contact, and for over two hundred years they fought against each other. Several entire Kabilas were wiped out in the struggle, and their names are lost to the ages. In the end, the [[Sirunaki]] fell, but were too numerous and too useful to eliminate entirely.

Today, the [[Sirunaki]] people exist as slaves to the [[Ngrligru]], and have finally begun rebuilding their power base. A few [[Sirunaki]] colonies have been established in secrecy, and have opened friendly ties with the [[Remnant]]. The Sirunaki Reshkeren is an active rebellion, which shines like a beacon of hope for the dozens of species still enslaved by the [[Ngrligru]].

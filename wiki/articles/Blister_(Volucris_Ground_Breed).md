The Nigrotaeshaia--or Blister, as its surviving victims call 
it--is one of the larger soldier mutations, and one of the more powerful ones. It's a towering giant compared to most breeds--only the Imperator is larger. With powerful attack and defense capabilities, it's made quite a name for itself on the battlefield.

The Blister only has a single weapon, besides its massive size. It has a huge plasma gland similar to the small-scale one found on the Bombardier. It can fire jets of plasma so hot and so large that it can melt a light Mech down to nothing with a direct hit.

Two large fins on the Blister are used to cool the beast while it isn't firing. Internal temperatures and pressures can reach a point so high that Blisters can simply explode. Sometimes a lucky shot from a humanoid will take off one of the cooling fins, taking the Blister out of the battle.

While it is slow, the Blister is very well armored. It can take a long pounding from heavy Mechs, particularly with ranged weapons. Like its cousins, the Tyrant and Imperator, it is resistant to radiation-based weapons like Neutron Cannons. It takes careful planning or a lot of luck to take down a Blister.

Blisters are relatively rare, but finding a few in a large-scale operation is a certainty. They are the best assault units the Buggers have, especially when used as artillery or Mech-disablers. Because of their low mobility, they tend to be a lot more predictable than the smaller soldier varieties.

The Clansguard was developed for the Mesarthrim Federation by [[Staatliche Werften]], or State Shipyards. State Shipyards was founded in [[2325]] by [[Elias Faust]], to provide work for Mesarthrim following the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]]. The Clansguard was their side project - and in the end, it showed. The Clansguard was arguably the most underpowered and cheaply produced Mesarthrim warship to date. Sixty-three vessels were launched for a mere 28 unique commissions. When the State Shipyards factory was captured at the end of the [[Tempest War]], the six new hulls that were in production were sold for scrap, rather then given commissions in the [[Mesarthrim Federation]]. None of the sixty-three vessels built survived the [[Tempest War]].

The class earned the nickname, "Iron Coffin".

==Design==
The State Shipyards was privy to [[William Camp|Chancellor Camp's]] plans to retake territory lost to the Prefecture in the [[Treaty of Memphis]]. To this end, they produced a Destroyer that was quick and easy to make. This resulted in some very important cutbacks. The Clansguard did not utilize the latest in Leifstein folding, and did not use the new shields developed by Zweiundzwanzig. These two decisions meant that the Clansguard was entering the battle using technologies the [[Onyx Empire]] had learned to adapt to in the [[Second Exodus War]]. Additionally, the design only called for minimal heavy armaments in order to cut down on costs. It was decided that the Clansguard should rely on hunting in packs rather then individual survivability. This design was contrary to Mesarthrim naval philosophy that almost always required Commandants to break away and enter into slugging matches with their opponents.

Early Clansguards had a problem with the arming mechanisms for the scuttle charges, whereas when a ship is about to be destroyed, all critical systems self-destruct, making recovery by opposing forces more difficult. The problem the Clansguard had was that when it armed the scuttling charges on the Omega weaponry, it would actually accidentally detonate it. Six Clansguards took all hands with them when the resulting Omega blasts pulled the escape shuttles in.

The Clansguard did succeed in providing the Centusi States with a new Destroyer. However, in several engagements with Auellal [[Mongrel Vessel|Mongrels]], the older Starmaster and Howell were able to take on and destroy the Clansguard. In one instance an Auellal Mongrel, Lliv'th Coral, engaged and destroyed three Clansguards one after another.

One major design flaw was discovered in [[2330]] when a Clansguard attempted to deploy an Omega Mine, and the release mechanism got caught trying to unlock from the bomb. Unable to cancel the arming sequence, the ship was destroyed when the mine detected the vessels own gravity signature. In a follow up investigation, it was discovered that the clamps designed and built for the Clansguard unlocked vertically opposed to the standard horizontal locks, and therefore the mine would get pinched on release and not float away. An entire collar system had to be designed so that the Clansguard could once again carry the Omega weaponry.

In [[2333]], all Clansguards being built and undergoing repairs were ordered to have immediate armament and armor upgrades. The upgrades came too few too late, and in reality were still to minuscule of a fix against the new Auellal Twilight Vessels.

==Battle History==
During the Tempest War, few Clansguards were given the opportunity to prove their worth. Either their Admirals were too worried to commit the vessel into a major engagement, or the ship was destroyed fighting. Only the first fleet saw significant success using the Clansguard. The First Fleet successfully used Clansguards to lay minefields, and trick Prefecture captains into chasing them into minefields.

In all, the Battle History of the Clansguard was considered a resounding failure, and many analyst still wonder how such a lemon of a design made its way to be the lead destroyer in the Centusi States.

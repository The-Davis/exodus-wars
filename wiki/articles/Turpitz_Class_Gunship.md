===Overview===
   
The Turpitz is the second oldest class in the United Centusi States Navy.  The current Turpitz gunships serving in the UCSN are the third major variants of the original Turpitz class, which was designed and put into production in 2294. 

[[Image:TurpitzClassGunship1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

Where the Nimitz was built to be a brawler, the Turpitz was designed as a fast-attack ship--not as heavily armed, but far faster.  A pair of Type-R Heavy Blazers make up its primary anti-warship firepower. 

Additional firepower comes from four Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannon of the Mesarthrim, and three Anti-Aerospace/Anti-Ground emplacements, giving the Turpitz a substantial anti-aerospace armament. 

[[Image:TurpitzClassGunship2.jpg]]

The Turpitz is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system.  The AAS is backed up by Leifstein Composite Armor.  Its Gravity Variance Drive gives it impressive maneuverability, but it is incapable of transatmospheric travel. 

===Deployment===

The Turpitz class has been in use in various Mesarthrim navies over the past 30 years.  There are few left in the UCSN, only sixteen.  These few Turpitz, however, are Mk III-D variants, the newest (redesigned prior to the Battle of Terra), and serve well as anti-warship and raiding gunships.

[[Image:TurpitzClassGunship3.jpg]]

They usually operate in pairs or in three-ship elements.

'''Labrea''' is the XXth planet in the [[Avior System]].

==History==

Labrea was founded by the [[Empire]] in the early years of its power. It was close to Laojiu, a prime real estate for farming. The Laojiu area was important to Imperial interests, so a regional garrison needed to be founded.

Labrea was an obvious choice. A sparsely-vegetated, nearly flat world with little geological activity. Labrea was meant to be a high-traffic world, with multiple landing sites for troop transports, and numerous barracks and training grounds.

The Royal Imperial Army assigned Colonel William Parrish to the garrison in 2320, and had little to do with the world afterwards. Parrish's recall orders were intercepted by Prefecture loyalists during the [[Imperial Civil War]], and the entire Laojiu regional forces of the Empire were left out of the bloody [[Battle of Terra]].

When the Empire was defeated, Laojiu threw its loyalty in with the Prefecture, and the garrison at Labrea was absorbed into the Legions.

==Geography==

Labrea has xx continents, covering a wide range of biospheres and climates.

Labrea only has two continents. The harsh northern continent, Dubh, is home to a secondary training base. Icronians seeking gainful employment away from home have made a training settlement where they teach Prefecture soldiers survival skills in colder climates. Nearly 400,000 tradesmen, wanderers, trappers, and outdoorsmen call the city of Härdad their home. Aside from Härdad, there are no other settlements, but the mountainous terrain is home to many edible fauna.

The main continent, Marisgnách, is a rolling flatland, with little variation in climate. The southern regions are covered by a mire, completely uninhabited by humans and unused by the Prefecture Army. Almost dead-center in the continent is a very small mountain plain, Fathach Méire, running east to west. Nestled in the southern foothills of Fathach Méire is Tara'dar Coirp, the garrison command post for the entire Laojiu District.

Aside from the vast Army base and spaceport, there is a sizeable town, Laidebaile, of roughly 2 million people on the northern shores of Lake Sovinian. Lake Sovinian marks the beginning of the huge southern swamps which never see the footsteps of Labrea settlers.

The flatlands of Marisgnách lend themselves well to the growing of crops, but only rice will grow properly. Because the land is so flat, with no proper drainage, the whole continent tends to stay muddy year-round, and many areas are prone to flooding for years at a time. A small population of about 1 million is scattered across the rest of Marisgnách, raising crops and farms. Oddly enough, the chief edible meat of the region is alligator, which is a favorite among the locals.

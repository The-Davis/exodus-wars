== Events of 497AD ==

Engineering teams arrive in the [[Bollaria System]], not to help develop the planets, but to repair and refit the thousands of [[terraformation]] satellites orbiting [[Drisonir]] and [[Nielstra]].  Their [[hyperspace]] drives are removed and replaced with modern type FTL drives.

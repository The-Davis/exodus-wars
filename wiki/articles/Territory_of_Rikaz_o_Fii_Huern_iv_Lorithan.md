There are few worlds that [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] has claimed, but they defend the claim strongly and work hard to cultivate their territory.

== Lorithan ==

[[Lorithan]] is the cradle and the possible tomb of the [[Lorithos]]. It sheltered them during the [[Sundering]] and gave rise to [[Hamur Huern]]'s empire, but was nearly destroyed in the [[Burning Wars]]. The [[Lorithos]] hope to restore their world to its ancient beauty. [[Lorithan]] is located in the [[Mirdek System]].  
In [[2334]], [[Lorithan]] was destroyed by the [[Volucris]] at the end of the [[Volucris War]].

== Jagarr ==

[[Jagarr]] is a colony founded in the [[Hamur System]] in [[2300]]. It is an arid desert world, but a clean world which can support life. Millions of Lorithos have flocked to the world in hopes of creating a new home.

== Ipsoff ==

[[Ipsoff]] is a colony world founded in the [[Orhak System]] in [[2308]]. Ipsoff has a series of tiny islands, and no central landmass. Despite the lack of living space, billions of Lorithos have settled on the new world. Vast fleets of fisheries and plankton farmers, floating cities, and orbitting space station arcologies make up for the lack of land.

Introduced in [[2190]] by [[Kriegshöfe]], the Sickle was created during the early stages of the 'effective [[Mesarthrim]] resistance.'  It was the first offensive vessel constructed by the resistance.  The Sickle used stolen [[Anderung]] technology, all it's heavy weapons, its propulsion, even its defensive shielding.  The only component entirely [[Mesarthrim]] built - other then the hull - was the computer core.  The ships were built in deep space without the use of a shipyard.  The Sickle were truly the pride of the early [[Mesarthrim]].  When one took to the sky in battle, a sense of high moral joined with it.

==Development==
The Sickle's forward pod holds ammunition stores, two flight decks, and bunks for [[Myt'herra]].  The rear pod of the Sickle is dedicated to defensive systems.  The Sickle was built to go toe to toe with [[Anderung]] vessels and therefore redundant shielding systems, strong armor, and point-defense systems were installed.  The Sickle was developed to be a brawler, and was instrumental in destroying most of the [[Anderung]] fleet, and forcing the [[Anderung]] to accept a pyrrhic victory at the [[Battle of Ice Crown Bay|Schlacht der Eis-Krone Bucht]].

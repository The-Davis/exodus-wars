'''Evan Kronic'''
*Place of Birth: [[Kronheim]]
*Allegiance: [[Ikronin Jurekön]]

''Evan Kronic'' is a pilot in the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] military. He was instrumental in the defense of Oselo, and devised the strategy that won back air superiority.

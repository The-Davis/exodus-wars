== Events of 5078BC ==

The [[Ikronin]] were knocked far back in progress from the [[Anthronoris Empire]], hiding in the large polar regions of their world.  When the threat was finally over, the technology that sustained their garish lifestyles was gone. They quickly managed to restore themselves to a [[Jet Age]], and were soon making baby steps back into space, though none of the great minds behind the [[Anthronoris]]’ mastery of hyperspace had perished in the [[Sundering]].

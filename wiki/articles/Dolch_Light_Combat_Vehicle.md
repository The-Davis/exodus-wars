*Size Designation:  Light Combat Vehicle
*Powerplant: Mesarthrim SV-3a Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 160 KPH
*Armor: Leifstein Composite Armor
*Shields: Automated Adaptive Shielding
*Armament: 2 x Type-B1 Blazers
*Communications System: Standard Ranged Comm. Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard Sensors Suite

===Overview===

The Dolch is the all-purpose utility vehicle of the UCS Army, as well as the primary ranged scout/patrol vehicle. The sturdy, simple design is perfect for moving squads of infantry quickly over any terrain.

[[Image:DolchLightCombatVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Dagger is meant for patrol and transport, not combat.  However, it is not entirely defenseless.  It is armed with two Type-B1 Blazers in a manned turret in the rear.  The Type-B1 Blazer is the smallest type mounted on vehicles.  Together, the two cannons are capable of taking down infantry, as well as light armored vehicles.  At close range, they are also effective against heavier armor. 

The Dagger is outfitted with an SV-3a reactor--a small reactor, but more than enough to move the Dagger around at top speed.  Although it is small, the reactor also powers a small shield projector, and the frame of the vehicle is covered in Leifstein Composite. 

[[Image:DolchLightCombatVehicle2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Daggers are deployed in every ground theatre that UCS Army forces find themselves.  Its uses are near-limitless, and include recon/patrol, transport, light raiding, and troop-deployment.  Every Mesarthrim soldier has spent time behind the controls of a Dagger, and they are well liked for their durability, simplicity, and reliability.

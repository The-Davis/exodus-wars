The '''Tronit Class Warcruiser''' is the paragon of [[Meroniri]] engineering and artistic design. It is the symbol of Meroniri authority and power throughout the galaxy, and is either welcome or feared in every port of call in the [[Orion Arm]].

The Tronit was designed to be able to take on the [[Ngrligru]]'s heavy Kondo'jahazai warships, most of which are around 22 million cubic meters. The Tronit can easily tackle a single Duma'kapunga in a head to head fight. Its design is focused around the destruction of other larger warships, and it leaves the smaller Vita'safina to its [[Astrala Class Escort Cruiser|Astrala escorts]]. Its offensive firepower is second to none, save perhaps the warships of the [[Remnant]], and the class has been refined over many hundreds of years to the point of near-perfection.

== Where Giants Tread ==

<small><small>("Where Giants Tread" was written by a [[Kalidasa]]n trader after witnessing a Tronit Class War Cruiser and an [[Ixoarchada]] [[Tsirada Class Battleship]] face off near the shipping lanes of the [[Lupei System]])</small></small>

Peering across the empty gulf of space, two titans warily eye one another.  Each one watches, willing...''daring'' the other to make the first move.  Two dragons whose paths should never have crossed, separated by the unfathomable vastness of space, prepare to unleash cataclysm.  These champions of [[Tolana Sector|Tolana]] and [[Unai Sector|Unai]]-—have met after long millennia apart, and they hold the power of the gods!

Caught between such foes, men flee in their ships to safer ports.  Merchants and traders, travelers and the watch, all scatter to the four corners to escape the expected onslaught.  They are wise to flee. In battle, these children of the ancients can unleash such power as was not seen in the War for All Earth, and they stand now ready to bring on Ragnarök.

Treading to the very brink of war, cooler heads at last prevail and pride is sacrificed for peace.  This clash shall not transpire—not today.

Lupei shipping traffic returns to normal as the Tsirada and the Tronit depart the system, returning to their berths...for now.

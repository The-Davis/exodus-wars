Infantry were beginning to make a major comeback in the last battles of the [[Imperial Civil War]], but when the [[Volucris Incursion]] began with the [[Battle of Mirach]], severe shortcomings were discovered. The super-fast [[Volucris]] light units cut through them like butter, and it only took the [[Battle of Mirach]] to convince army commanders to stop deploying infantry against the bugs.

It was obvious that unarmored infantry was difficult to deploy properly in modern warfare. Attempts at properly armoring infantry against bug claws and conventional humanoid weapons was initially met with failure. Even steroid-enhanced humans couldn't carry the heavy full suits necessary for protection. Troops of the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] could carry most suits, but they were the result of centuries of genetic engineering and surgical implants.

[[Image:M16Excalibur.jpg]]

The solution was many-tiered. The weight issue was partially solved when Boron-Nitride Composite armor was created. Not only was it lighter and tougher than metal, it could be woven in a pliable cloth-like material for gloves and other areas that need to be more flexible, but could be solid for the chest-plate and leg-guards. This system could actually be carried, but was a terrible hindrance to movement and speed.

The flexibility and motion problem was solved with a series of pressure-responsive motors. The normal motion of the trooper would add a small power boost to their movement, just enough to speed them up to their normal running speed. They can also switch on a short boost of double power for charging into battle and rapid retreats, but consistent use of double speed rapidly wears out the power supply. This motion system has an important failsafe built in: it will not move out of a trooper's normal range of motion, so something crushing a trooper won't be assisted by the power suit's motors.

The suit's power runs on a very efficient battery, which is limited to a day of normal output plus five double-power boosts. Making a reactor small enough for a power suit is possible, but shielding the trooper from radiation in the limited space and size of a power suit is not. Other forms of power are too heavy or bulky for power suit use, but some are used in full battle armor.

The CNSCSU helmet was designed for all forms of Prefecture armor. It contains the communications, computer, and navigation systems, and has hookups for more advanced systems that are used in full battle armor suits. The CNSCSU has a Heads-Up Display in the visor with screens that can display Identification Friend/Foe information, range to target, trajectories and velocities of targets, and can zoom in or display different spectrums (i.e. infrared).

In vacuum operations, the modern infantry trooper in a power suit doesn't have to put up with the vulnerable and clunky oxygen tanks that the first space troopers had to wear. The power suit sports a detachable air scrubber, which pulls the carbon out of the CO2 the trooper exhales. The filter needs to be replaced twice per day, but it's small and easy to snap in and out of the power suit.

The arsenal of the modern infantryman is extensive, but the Mobile Infantry suit tends to stick with the Selectable Assault Battle Rifle, a highly advanced weapon that can be adjusted to fill different roles and calibers, and has attachments for shotguns, grenade launchers, and other systems.

Infantry using the suit can also use Light Anti-Armor Weapons, Squad Automatic Weapons, Sniper Rifles, mortars, and man-portable crew-served field guns. Each soldier also carries a 10mm sidearm, a combat knife, and a variety of explosives; such as HE grenades, self-burrowing subterranean mines, and even beacons to guide in air strikes and orbital bombardments.

Mass-production of the Mobile Infantry suit is cheap and easy, and both the Prefecture Legion Corps and the Prefecture Marine Corps use it extensively. It is also used by the Terran Embassy's Sol Defense Force, and other governments are looking at buying the system for their own armies.

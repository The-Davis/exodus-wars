The '''First Exodus''' was the exile of the [[Torimur]] colonists from the [[Sol Sector]] to the [[De Mairan Sector]]. It began on Torimur, and ended on [[Eciluge]].

== Planning ==

As a result of the [[Treaty of Volyn]], the [[United Earth Alliance]] was required to assist the survivors of the bombing of Torimur in evacuating their stricken planet, and required to provide them with transport to a new world.

Three Alliance mega-freighters were converted into sleeper ships. The ships, rechristened as the ''Jerome Black'', the ''Aydan Black'', and the ''Torimur's Hope'', were filled with long rows of cryogenic sleeper tubes. The survivors of Torimur boarded the ships, and were sent towards the De Mairan Sector.

== The Exodus ==

The journey was very rapid. The latest in [[hyperspace]] technology was used to speed the ships and their passengers into their remote exile as quickly as possible. When the ships arrived in the remote [[Taygeta Tauri System]], they automatically disabled their hyperspace drives and entered into a safe orbit around Eciluge.

[[Lucius Black]] and those loyal to him immediately began plotting to return to the [[Sol Sector]], but there were many challenges to overcome. Nearly a third of the exiles had succumbed to the radiation poisoning they had received from Torimur. Sorting out the dead from the living in their cryogenic chambers was a grisly task. [[Robert Marshall]] in particular was horrified to find that his wife Maya Marshall had died mere days before their arrival.

It was decided that Eciluge would have to be their home for some time. Unfortunately, while the world was inhabitable, it was only barely inhabitable. Temperatures rarely rose above freezing. ''Torimur's Hope'' was landed on the surface and used as a makeshift city, and its drive systems were cannibalized for the other two ships.

== Planning Revenge ==

It was easy for Lucius Black to convince his compatriots to return with him and seek revenge. [[Anthony Hadrian]] in particular swore to make the United Earth Alliance pay for their sins in blood.

Despite the terms of the Treaty of Volyn, Lucius Black had not fully disarmed. A cache of weapons was hidden aboard the ''Aydan Black'' by his [[Omegas]]. More weapons were developed by [[Reg Brenstein]], and the two ships in orbit were soon under reconstruction as ships of war.

By [[2086]], the ships were ready. The ''Torimur's Hope'' was raised back into orbit, and fitted with the fastest drives the exiles had available. It was sent ahead of the other two. The ''Jerome Black'' and the re-christened ''Iron Hand'', now powerful battleships, followed at a much slower pace. The [[Second Exodus]] had begun.

== First Exodus Memorial ==

While Eciluge is no longer inhabited, it is the site of thousands of graves. After the [[Second Exodus War]], many of the survivors of the two Exoduses returned to Eciluge to pay their respects.

An obelisk of carved onyx was placed in the center of the graveyard. Each survivor donated a single drop of blood, which was sealed in a vial in the center of the obelisk. A plaque was placed on the obelisk. It reads "''We who lie here are the victims of a crime beyond measure. Justice and revenge were purchased with the blood of the innocent. History must remember. Do not commit the sins of our brothers a second time.''"

The graves were originally unmarked, but thanks to an anonymous donation, the survivors were able to place a single stone of onyx over each grave. As part of the plan, the monument is not tended. The ice and permafrost of Eciluge are intended to bury it and preserve it for the ages. By the end of the [[24th Century]], the monument will no longer be visible.

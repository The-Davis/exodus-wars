== Events of 2300 ==

===January===
*January 11 - The [[Kihtor Modar]] is completed on [[Lorithan]] as a industrial center and commercial hub.

===February===

*February 16 - The [[Torimur]] exiles enter the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. The [[Alphas]] and the [[Omegas]] sent ahead of the main fleet receive coded messages to begin their plans.
*February 18 - [[William Duke]] succeeds [[Jacob Michaels]] as [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] of [[Ah Kin]] after [[Jacob Michaels|Michaels]] is assassinated by the [[Omegas]]. [[William Duke|Duke]] is and always has been a member of the [[Alphas]].
*February 21 - The [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] army base on [[Ah Kin]] is wiped out: during annual routine medical checkups, doctors who are members of the [[Omegas]] or sympathetic to the [[Alphas]] inject the officers with sedatives. The enlisted ranks are disposed of with honorable discharges, "accidents", or are poisoned. The armory is sealed for the use of the [[Torimur]] exiles.
*February 22 - The [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]]'s navy assets in the [[Alpha Centauri System]] are wiped out. Nearly 10% of the crew of the [[UEAS Radiance|UEAS ''Radiance'']], an [[ADF-4000 Class Destroyer]], are [[Omegas]]. They pump deadly neurotoxins through the ventilation system. The [[ADF-3000 Class Frigate]]s in the system are soon dealt with in the same manner.
*February 23 - [[William Duke]] bans all interstellar travel out of the [[Alpha Centauri System]].
*February 24 - [[Lucius Black]] moves into the governor's manor on [[Ah Kin]]. This marks the official end of the [[Second Exodus]].
*February 28 - [[William Duke]] enacts "emergency measures", ordering all married couples with stable incomes to take in as many children as possible, for a period not to exceed six years.

===March===

*March 3 - The [[First Imperial Decree of the Onyx Empire|First Imperial Decree]] formally establishes the [[Onyx Empire]], and [[Lucius Black]] claims all known worlds as his sovereign territory.
*March 4 - All branches of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]], the [[Royal Imperial Labs]], and other institutions of the [[Onyx Empire]] are formally created.
*March 6 - [[Yul Besud]] cracks the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s election system, allowing [[Lucius Black]] and [[William Duke]] to manipulate the results of all [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] elections on [[Ah Kin]]. [[Arthur]] is tasked with creating believable results which will consistently put a majority of [[Alphas]] in office.
*March 13 - The [[Kihtor Terok]] is completed is a communications mecca on [[Lorithan]], where the prototype Ansible network is controlled and maintained.

===April===

*April 9 - An [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps]]' ship, the [[UEAS James Cook|UEAS ''James Cook'']], makes [[First Contact]] with the [[Reigens]] during the first non-[[Reigen]] survey of [[Njiorin's Well]]. The crew is surprised when they discover the [[Reigens]] speak fluent [[Sothark]].
*April 13 - The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] send a delegation to [[Njiorin's Well]] to deliver navigation coordinates to [[Sepulare]] and invite the [[Reigess Suverände]] to send a delegation to the [[Alliance of Planets]].
*April 15 - The [[Kejsare]] of the [[Reigess Suverände]], [[Edvard Raegos]], pays a state visit to [[Sepulare]] and the [[Alliance of Planets]].
*April 17 - The [[Alliance of Planets High Council]] invites [[Edvard Raegos]] to one of its meetings. During the meeting, [[Edvard Raegos]] asks the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] delegate, [[Argus Lax]] when he will be able to meet the [[Kejsare]] of [[Kronheim]]. Much to his surprise, [[Edvard Raegos]] learns that [[Kronheim]] no longer has a [[Kejsare]].
*April 19 - [[Argus Lax]] receives a written claim of sovereignty from [[Edvard Raegos]], who claims to be the heir to the throne of [[Kronheim]] by right of descent.
*April 20 - The [[Ikronin Jurekön]] send a full delegation to [[Sepulare]] to meet [[Edvard Raegos]]. The [[Ikronin]] claim that they have been independent for centuries because the line of kings died out. Historical documents are produced showing the legitimate establishment of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] government and the legal abolishment of the [[Kronheim]] monarchy. [[Edvard Raegos]] recognizes their claim of independence and cedes all [[Reigen]] claim of sovereignty.
*April 22 - The [[Alliance of Planets]] invites the [[Reigess Suverände]] to join as a Member State. [[Edvard Raegos]] advises he will not accept immediately, and will give his answer the following year. The [[Kejsare]] is given a copy of the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter|Great Charter]] and escorted home.

===May===

*May 11 - [[Lavik Nighthawk]] is admitted into the [[Royal Imperial Health Association]]'s [[Rio de Andes]] hospital with severe flu-like symptoms, fever, and delirium.
*May 12 - While being treated by [[Chris Dayton]] for delirium and other symptoms, [[Lavik Nighthawk]] reveals the secret behind the destruction of the [[Torimur]] colony. [[Chris Dayton|Dayton]] has no idea what the [[Onyx Empire]] is or what [[Torimur]] was, but vividly remembers the terrified, haunted screams of his patient.
*May 19 - The [[Kihtor Ihjar]] is completed as a military base on [[Lorithan]].

===June===
*June 15 - [[Kael Rejoc]] and a [[Reigen]] delegation visit [[Kronheim]] in the first state visit to their original homeworld. The [[Ikronin Jurekön]] invites the [[Reigess Suverände]] to establish a permanent embassy in [[Vighelir]].

=== July ===
* July 11 - The [[Kihtor Enwir]] is completed as a military base on [[Lorithan]].
* July 23 - The hull of the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] is laid down in orbit of [[Alpha Centauri System|Proxima Centauri]] by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] workers.

===August===
*August 14 - The [[United Earth Alliance]] embassy to the [[Reigess Suverände]] opens in [[Rolsucht]], on [[Junheim]].
*August 15 - The [[Reigess Suverände]] hosts a state visit from sitting [[United Earth Alliance]] President [[Kevin Westington]].

===September===

*September 18 - While celebrating his 72nd birthday with friends and family, [[Zaken Huern]] falls ill and is hospitalized.
*September 23 - The [[Reigess Suverände]] embassy to the [[United Earth Alliance]] opens in [[Denver]], on [[Earth]].

===October===

*October 4 - Medical specialists from the [[United Earth Alliance]] and [[Meroniri Terinasi]] diagnose [[Zaken Huern]]'s illness as a previously unknown strain of cancer.
*October 14 - Members of the [[Earth Congress of Industrial Labor Organization|ECILO]] testify at the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Earth Assembly]] over concerns of the apparent growing recession of the [[Economy of the United Earth Alliance|economy]].

===November===
*November 6 - The [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Earth Assembly]] and the [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier of Earth]], [[Elias Roverson]], form a special economic committee to develop working sollutions for the economic recession.

===December===
*December 9 - [[Zaken Huern]] dies of cancer complications.
*December 11 - [[Dirken Huern]] declares a month of mourning for the passing of his father.
*December 12 - [[Zaken Huern]]'s body lies in state in the ruins of the old palace.
*December 14 - [[Edvard Raegos]] and a [[Reigen]] delegation come to [[Lorithan]] to pay their respects to [[Zaken Huern]].
*December 19 - [[Kevin Westington]] and [[Elias Roverson]] head the [[United Earth Alliance]] delegation to [[Lorithan]] to pay their respects to [[Zaken Huern]].
*December 22 - [[Zaken Huern]]'s body is launched into [[hyperspace]] out of respect for his work in developing [[Lorithos]] faster-than-light travel.
*December 31 - The [[United Earth Alliance]] Presidential election votes are tallied. [[Marian Janos]] receives 39% of the total vote, the majority over the other five serious contenders in the election.

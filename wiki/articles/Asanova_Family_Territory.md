== History ==

A close second to the Nakamura's in the age of their colony is the Asanova Family of [[Kirtszi]] in the [[Sirius Novgorod System]]. The Asanovas were outcasts from new [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] after the Asanova family aided the Muslims in smuggling weapons into Moscow before [[World War III]], and were forced to flee [[Terra]]. Eventually they amassed enough of a fortune to strike out from [[Alpha Centauri System|Alpha Centauri]] for a colony of their own. The head of the Asanova family is the "Velikiy Kniaz," who is currently [[Nicholai Asanova]]. Nicholai is the most aggressive of the three families, maintaining powerful standing armies and fleets. While on the surface, Nicholai's businesses engage in mercenary protection, rumors abound of shakedowns and blatant acts of piracy.

*Power Plant: Standard Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 50 KPH
*Equipment:
**Excavator
**Bulldozer Attachment
**Mining Tools
**Construction Tools
*Armament: 1 Light Autocannon
*Manufacturer: Civil Motors
*Communications System: Standard Optical Transceiver

[[Image:FerretMultipurposeVehicle1.jpg]]

The Ferret is a self-contained excavation, construction, mining and recovery vehicle.  Its crew can tackle almost any job on the ground.  Other vehicles are required for building construction, but the Ferret can handle roads and similar construction jobs by itself.

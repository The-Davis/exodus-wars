===Overview===

The Scipio class was designed in 2287, but no ship of the class was ever built.  This changed in 2305, when a dramatic rise in Centauri militarism created a need for true warships in the Sirius Cluster.  On January 1st, 2315, three Scipio class Frigates were launched alongside their flagship, the Giuseppe Garibaldi.  Days later, the worst fears of the Berlusconi were confirmed when an armada of warships seized Terran space.  

[[Image:ScipioClassFrigate1.jpg]]

The Scipio class has undergone minor refitting since 2315, with the only significant refit occuring in 2325, with the installation of new control interfaces and Kalidasan weaponry. 

===Capabilities===

As with most Sirius warships, Ashur cannons make up the primary anti-warship armament of the Scipio.  A pair of twin-turrets on the lower hull are quite capable of dealing with large warships and punching through heavy armor and shielding. 

The 2325 refit of the Scipio class saw the installation of twenty-two Fast-Tracking Advanced Neutron Cannons for anti-warship duty.  Additionally, these Kalidasan-made cannons and eleven Anti-Aerospace Emplacements provide excellent anti-aerospace protection and point-defense. 

Like the larger Giuseppe Garibaldi, the Scipio class is equipped with an arsenal of thirty VOTM Tubes.  These weapons can fire on any targets that the Scipio may face. 

[[Image:ScipioClassFrigate2.jpg]]

Also like the Giuseppe Garibaldi, aerospace fighters are carried.  Two Uragano squadrons and two Stella Nera squadrons are usually carried, although the proportion is variable.  There are not, however, capabilities for carrying the larger Pugno Morte bombers. 

Scipios are light and fast, but are well protected by the best in Terran defensive technologies, such as Boron-Nitride and Barrier Plasma Shielding, to name a few.. 

===Deployment===

The Scipios have only seen true warship combat once, in April of 2321, and they performed magnificently.  Today, they operate as the primary defense warships of the Berlusconi Territory.

[[Image:ScipioClassFrigate3.jpg]]

Their acceleration is superior to all capital warship classes in the Sirius Territories, except for the Nakamura Katana and Wakizashi classes.  However, it is more heavily armed than either of these classes.  Despite a lack of true warship-to-warship combat, the Scipios are maintained in typical Berlusconi style, as a deterrent to outside intervention.

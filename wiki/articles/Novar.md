'''Novar''' is the third planet in the [[Honis System]].

==History==

When Novar's history was first penned, the [[Anderung]] controlled the planet. The mineral rich planet seemed to have always had an Anderung outpost of some sort. In the year [[2200]], the Anderung gave day-to-day control over to their [[Mesarnak]] [[Overseer Administration of the Mesarthrim Clans|Overseers']]. The Overseers' would hold Novar until [[2295]] when the bloody [[War for Novar]] began, lasting six years - and ending with the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clans]] taking control of the strategic planet. After the Clans collapse, control peacefully shifted to the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]]. The Centusi States began systematically strip mining the planet to supply their war effort. In [[2333]] the [[Auellal League|League]] initiated a blockade of Novar, and began inserting [[Auellal Guardians|Laoch Saoi and Etlee Cabbil]] onto the surface, supporting them with carrier born aircraft. In [[2334]], the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] reinforced the battered League Guardians. Novar would resist, and force the new [[Mesarthrim Federation|Federation]] to commit Naval and Ground assets to finally subdue the planet in [[2335]].

After the Federation took control of Novar, they began an aggressive program of privatizing the natural resources, and relying on taxes from the private corporations, rather then trying to run them all as state assets - as had previously been done. [[Novar Zusammensetzung]] has become the largest corporation on Novar.

==Geography==

Capital city: [[New Hanover]]

Novar has two continents; Gohurut and Halidres. Their most dominating feature is the extensive mountain ranges covering the surface of Novar. These mountain ranges have historically hindered invading forces from quick resolutions to conflict. Most of the surface of Novar is covered in a rich dark-green short grass. Most vegetation on Novar has dense mineral composites within it, and is often very lethal. Almost all food on Novar is imported, with the exception of several large hydroponic installations. The large rock formations the cover Novar make wheeled transportation labor-some, meaning most residents make use of public VTOL transit services, or privately owned VTOLs. Due to the extensive use of aircraft, Novar has the most numerous, and busiest professional flight controllers in the Federation.

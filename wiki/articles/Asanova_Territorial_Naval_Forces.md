As with all things regarding the [[Sirius Territorial Administration]], Territorial Naval Forces is more of a collective term for all space vessels operated by the various factions for protection of the three-system [[Sirius Cluster]]. Although officially a single entity, the [[Sirius Territorial Administration]] assigns defense of the three systems their territorial rulers.

At any given time, the number of armed vessels in a single territory may vary due to the presence of various groups, but each family does maintain its own full-time fleet. The fleets ensure family control over a territory, and also serve not so much to keep the peace, but to keep the status quo.

In addition to any warships, the [[Sirius Cluster|Sirius Territories]] play host to trade and merchant ships from across the [[Orion Arm]]. Vast fleets of freighters, transports, haulers, and tankers transfer goods at massive orbital depots, and then set off in the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates|gates]] once again. The Sirius families also maintain their own private trade fleets for transporting "priority goods".

The Asanova Territory's Black Fleet is a powerful group of vessels with an unusual history. Leading it is the ''Kirov'', a destroyer that has been rebuilt so many times in its hundred-plus year history that its original class can't be recognized. Similarly, the ''Alexandrovna'' is a corvette that has changed hands many times, and traces its service history back to the [[France (Terra)|French]] fleet in the [[First Exodus War]]. The Black Fleet only has two dedicated military vessel classes: the Petrov frigate and the Tupolev gunship. Its other combat class of vessel, the Caspia class, has two variants an armed version that qualifies as a schooner, and an unarmed heavy freighter model. The Asanovas also deploy the Belarus class modular transport, a design similar to the wildly popular [[Rahn Industries]] Bulk Hauler.


The Asanova Black Fleet is one of three separate naval forces maintained in the Sirius District. These navies are kept not only to deter attacks from external foes, but also are the most physical manifestations of the delicate balance of power that exists within the Sirius District. The forces are kept large enough to compete with each other, but kept small enough to deter a weapons build-up.

The Asanova naval force is known as the Black Fleet, a reference to a [[United Earth Alliance]] resolution passed in [[2225]]. The resolution banned unauthorized private naval forces in an attempt to halt a rise in piracy, and such private navies as those in the [[Sirius Cluster]] were labled "Black Fleets". Although the [[United Earth Alliance]] claimed the Sirius Cluster as an autonomous administrative district, it never attempted to enforce this law, and it was later retracted.

Admiral [[Dimitri Lavrov]] is the commander of the Black Fleet, and a chief advisor to the Asanova family. He oversaw the Fleet's modernization following the [[Second Exodus War]], and has pushed its priority to the fore in the face of modern threats.

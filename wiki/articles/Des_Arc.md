'''Des Arc''' is the second planet in the [[Des System]]. It has a sizeable population of roughly 1.2 billion people, mostly of Polynesian decent.

== History ==

Des Arc was discovered in [[2218]] by the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]], but was not part of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s colonization effort. Instead, several [[France (Terra)|French]] corporate concerns sponsored a colony, drawing from the French Polynesian people for willing colonists--a group who had not participated in the [[diaspora]] so far.

The first colonists wanted to use the world's unusual volcanic system to set up a series of mines and factories to ship products around the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The thick seas of near-surface magma made ideal sources of raw materials and refinery heat. A simple bore into the ground made an ideal furnace, and no fuel was required.

At first, the air was not breathable, but a terraformation project from [[2218]] until [[2230]] solved that problem. In [[2240]], the first major industrial complex was built, followed by a spaceport and a few cities in the following years. Most cities were built around the rare heavily-vegetated areas. The spaceport, for example, is built on the outskirts of a ten-acre sequoia forest.

Des Arc's economy was kept strong by the demand for their construction and mining machinery, but they received a large boost in [[2318]] when the [[Onyx Empire]] annexed them after their invasion of [[Terra]]. The [[Onyx Empire]]'s war machine soon had the industrial capacity of Des Arc working overtime. When the [[Imperial Civil War]] ended, Des Arc became a member of the [[Prefecture]].

== Planetary Features ==

Des Arc has a single "super-continent" covering its entire surface. It has a "lake-ocean" covering a large basin over 65,000 kilometers in diameter, but only 400 meters deep.

Most large cities are built on the shores of the basin. The cities of Des Crag and Des Ore are the largest. Des Ore is home to the Sequoia Spaceport, where most of the planet's shipping takes place.

Des Arc's entire crust is thin, with only a kilometer of soil and rock between the surface and a sea of magma. The entire surface is a whole, there are no continental plates or fault lines that seismologists have been able to detect, so earthquakes aren't a problem.

Since the terraformation project was completed in [[2230]], the vegetation of Des Arc has been sparse. Soil quality is poor, though the world's climate is wet enough to support more vegetation. Oddly enough, it never rains on Des Arc and there are no clouds. New water must be flown in from the atmosphere of [[Des Uvo]] every so often.

When it was discovered, Des Arc's atmosphere was thick with sulfurous acid, similar to [[Sol System|Sol]]'s [[Venus]]. It took two years of scouring the air with filtration systems to keep the air from being poisonous to humans, and then ten years of growing oxygen-producing algae to get the air's oxygen levels to a comfortable breathable level.

It takes Des Arc 25 hours, 32 minutes and 16 seconds to rotate around its axis. From Des Arc, the apparent motion of celestial bodies in the sky is to the north. Des Arc orbits Scorpus Des every 359.132 days. Des Arc's single moon, [[Arc Carus]], orbits around Des Arc every 23.4 days.

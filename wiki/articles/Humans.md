'''Human''' was originally the name of the ''homo sapiens'' species, but the term has been expanded to include all intelligent homonids--members of the ''homo'' genus.  There are several known species and sub-species, all of which are capable of intelligent communication and often capable of inter-breeding. All humans are known to be descended from the [[Anthronoris]] race.

== Anthronoris - ''Homo Originis'' ==

[[Anthronoris]] are the precursor to all known members of the ''homo'' genus.  With the exception of the few members of the [[Remnant]], the Anthronoris are extinct.

== Terrans - ''Homo Sapiens'' ==

[[Terrans]] are one of the most common human races.  The name Terran refers to descendants of [[Earth]]ers.  Terrans are dominant in the central [[Orion Arm]].

== Mesarthrim - ''Homo Mesarthes'' ==

[[Mesarthrim]] are a larger, stronger, more aggressive off-shoot of Terrans.  They are native to [[Mesar]].  Mesarthrim are the result of genetic engineering.  Mesarthrim are limited to the [[Pelagrim Sector]] of the Orion Arm.

=== Mesarnak - ''Homo Mesarthes Mesarnak'' ===

[[Mesarnak]] are a sub-species of Mesarthrim.

== Ixoarchada - ''Homo Archades'' ==

The [[Ixoarchada]] are a human species native to [[Glengarr]].  They are dominant in the outer Orion Arm.

== Meroniri - ''Homo Sepulares'' ==

The [[Meroniri]] are a human species native to [[Sepulare]].  They are dominant in the core systems of the Orion Arm.

== Ikronin - ''Homo Kronheimar'' ==

The [[Ikronin]] are a human species native to [[Kronheim]].  They are dominant in the [[Njiorin Sector]].

== Juns - ''Homo Junheimar'' ==

The [[Juns]] are a human species native to [[Junheim]].  Culturally, they have been largely absorbed by the Reigens.  Most pure genetic Juns are still known as Reigens due to their culture.

=== Reigens - ''Homo Junheimar-Kronheimar'' ===

The [[Reigens]] are classified as a sub-species of the Juns, and are the result of an intermingling of Ikronin and Jun bloodlines.

== Lorithos ==

The [[Lorithos]] are a human species native to [[Lorithan]].  Their sphere of influence is limited to three planets in the [[Njiorin Sector]].

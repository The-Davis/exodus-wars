'''Crofton''' is the XXth planet in the [[Xi Herculis System]].

==History==

Crofton is one of the more hospitable worlds in the Ward District. Its pleasant climates and large, un-domed cities alone are enough to attract tourists from Holt, Ward, and especially Bolan. This tourist economy has been the strongest point of Crofton's founding since its founding in 2323.

Crofton was founded by Richard Crofton, a wealthy executive of Blazton Heavy Industries. He and Blazton Industries helped move millions of refugees from war-torn Terra to the unspoiled haven of the world that soon took on the name of its founder.

Crofton is one of the rare worlds found with a complete biosphere of its own. It even had some animal species, though few more advanced than rodents and small birds. Terraformers found Terran plants and animals that would merge seamlessly with the environment, making the world suitable for human inhabitation without destroying the existing life.

By mid-2324, three major cities were thriving, and multiple small farming and mining communities had sprung up in the areas around the cities.

The Crofton system has a huge belt of comets, which pass within view of the planet every two years. Astronomers and archaeologists both agree that no comets come close enough to threaten the world.

This brilliant and safe display of the marvels of space are the major draw for tourists from other hospitable worlds.

Richard Crofton still lives on the planet, and has started the Crofton Institute for Economics, which is well on its way to becoming one of the premier commerce research companies in the galaxy. The Prefecture hires this and other similar companies to do its research on the economic status of its planets, much like the US Department of Commerce did in the 21st century.
The large numbers of civilians has made it necessary to install planetary defenses and station a garrison. The Crofton garrison is the most-desired post in the Ward District. Commanders try to balance out the times in this and other garrisons to keep soldiers rested and at peak performance.

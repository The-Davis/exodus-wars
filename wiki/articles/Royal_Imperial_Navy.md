The '''Royal Imperial Navy''' is a component of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Military of the Onyx Empire|military]]. Officially, all members of the Royal Imperial Navy are sailors, but they are often known by their nicknames, such as "squids."

The might and power of the [[Onyx Empire]] was never more apparant than when an armada from the Royal Imperial Navy entered a new [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] to conquer it.

==Structure==

The Royal Imperial Navy was made up of three components: active-duty fleet (the Armadas), garrison fleet (the Core Fleets), active-duty air (the Armada Air Command), and garrison air (the Core Air Command). The Core Fleets and Core Air Command were active duty sailors, but were not deployed abroad and used older-model ships and weapons. The Royal Imperial Navy was led by the Grand Admiral, who reported directly to the Emperor.

===Armadas and Armada Air Arm===

At its height, the Royal Imperial Navy had two Armadas. The Armadas were led by the best tacticians and warship commanders available. Most of the Admirals were commanders in the Torimur Merchant Fleet during the [[First Exodus War]].

* The [[Iron Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy|Iron Armada]], led by [[Anthony Hadrian]] and headquartered on [[Earth]].
* The [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy|Black Armada]], led by [[Robert Marshall]], and headquartered on [[Ah Kin]].

===Core Fleets and Core Air Arm===

Each [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] in the [[Onyx Empire]] had a small Core Fleet and attached Core Air Arm. The Core Fleets were led by Vice Admirals, who were often up-and-coming go-getters, but were also frequently older officers nearing the end of their careers. Core Fleets were small, made up of a single Carrier Group on permanent assignment.

===Planetside Installations===

The Royal Imperial Navy had multiple installations on planetary surfaces. They were typically commanded by Rear Admirals or Commodores, depending on the size and role of the installations. Military starports were the largest, with Marine complements, military aircraft landing strips, and launching/receiving facillities for starships capable of atmospheric landings. Naval Air Command installations are the home of second-line Navy pilot squadrons, where new pilots learn the ropes and older pilots end their careers. Marine bases were also subject to the scrutiny of the Navy chain-of-command, though a Marine flag rank was typically in direct command.

===Space Stations===

Space Stations were numerous and had multiple roles. There were stations in planetary orbit, stellar orbit, and deep space. Typically, they served as ports of call, cargo transfer points, static defenses, and forward Naval Air Command bases. A Space Station was usually under the command of a Commodore.

=== Naval Air Arm ===

The Naval Air Command is in charge of the fighters, bombers, dropships and shuttles used by the Fleets, installations and all other areas needed. The fighting Navy aviators are under the command of the Armada Air Arms.

Each of the Armadas has an Air Arm. All space stations and planetside installations' air squadrons were under the command of the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]].

Armada Air Arms:
* The Iron Air Arm, attached to the Iron Armada.
* The Black Air Arm, led by [[Emil Torsc]] and attached to the Black Armada.

== Organization ==

The [[Onyx Empire]]'s Royal Imperial Navy was an armada-based force, with operational command of each fleet in an Armada given to its respective commanders. Unit make-up was broken up at Carrier Group level.

=== Armada ===

Armadas were the highest level of organization, commanded by an Admiral. An Armada had two Fleets; one commanded by the Admiral of the Armada, and one commanded by an Admiral. Each Fleet had a total of twelve Carrier Groups, and a total of six Cargo Groups.

=== Carrier Group ===

The Carrier Group was the heart of the Royal Imperial Navy's battle doctrine. Each Carrier Group was made up of one [[Atlas Class Carrier]], three [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]]s, and ten [[Vigilant Class Frigate]]s. The Carrier Group was led by the [[Atlas Class Carrier|Atlas]]'s Captain, who was given the rank of Commodore.

A carrier group's main method of battle was to have the frigates set up a mobile "lane of fire", while the bombers and destroyers tried to drive enemy capital ships into the lane of fire and destroy them. The carrier would monitor the battle as a whole, and the carrier's Captain and battle computer would calculate the best location for the lane of fire at any given time.

Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] and [[Prism]] were, of course, the best at doing this. Fighter wings from the carrier would fly [[Combat Air Patrol|CAP]] for the carrier to deflect incoming missiles and bombers, and would escort their own bombers or try to force enemy fighters into the lane of fire.

=== Cargo Group ===

A Cargo Group, frequently called a "convoy", was responsible for moving large amounts of [[Royal Imperial Army]] troops and war materiel. Each Cargo Group was made up of five [[Mercury Class Transport]]s and twenty [[Pelican Class Freighter]]s.

The main role of the [[Mercury Class Transport|Mercury]] was to transport troops to orbital drop zones, but it was well equipped to defend the convoy from attack. The lead cruiser was typically led by a Captain with the honorary rank of Commodore, but if a Carrier Group was involved the honorary rank was dropped and the convoy defered to the Carrier Group's Commodore.

=== Ships ===

The names of commissioned ships of the Royal Imperial Navy started with "HMS", designating 'His Majesty's Ship'. Additionally, each ship was given a letter-based hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and a hull number. The names of ships were officially selected by the Grand Admiral, with the Emperor's approval.

The Royal Imperial Navy had more equipment than any of the other branches of the Imperial Military. Huge, unique Battleships (BG) were built as Flagships for the Armadas. Aircraft Carriers (CV) defended the Armadas from bomber attack and served as flagships for the Core Fleets. Versatile Cruisers (CG) filled the role of convoy escort and troop transport, while powerful Destroyers (DG) smash through enemy defenses, and agile Frigates (FG) created the deadly "lanes of fire" that made the Royal Imperial Navy the scourge of the early [[Second Exodus War]].

In its other areas of responsibility, the Royal Imperial Navy called on planetary orbit stations (PS), stellar orbit stations (SS), and deep space stations (GS) as resupply depots and observation posts. The Navy also used a wide variety of support ships: freighters (AK), hospital ships (AH), and enormous dropships known as landing ships (LS).

=== Aircraft ===

Aircraft were an important part of the Royal Imperial Navy's fighting capacity. Carrier-based aircraft are able to strike space, air, sea, and land targets far from a carrier while protecting friendly forces from enemy aircraft and ships.

The Naval Air Arm used the Valkyrie, Ocelot, and Gyrfalcon carrier-based aircraft. The Navy also used C-series dropships like the Air Force and Marines did.

The Naval Air Arm had three squadron designations: Fighter Squadrons are given the prefix "VF", bomber squadrons are given the prefix "VB", and cargo squadrons are given the prefix "VC." Squadrons usually have an official emblem or mascot which the squadron quartermaster paints on the wings or tails of the aircraft, and pilots are often allowed to design their own nose art.

=== Weapon Systems ===

The Royal Imperial Navy's shipboard weapon systems were focused on two weapon types: cannons and missiles.

Three cannon types, the Close-In Weapon System (CIWS), Gauss Cannon (GC), and Relativistic Mass Driver (RMD), fill important roles in Naval Combat. The RMD serves as a main weapon, capable of splitting a planet in half if fired correctly. Gauss Cannons serve as a more restrained weapon of choice, when the target needs to be disabled instead of destroyed outright. The CIWS is a point-defense system, designed to shoot down targets within a few dozen kilometers of a warship.

Only one type of Missile System is used for ship to ship combat: the Anti-Warship Torpedo (AWT) is the only one with the propulsion, warhead, and gravity breaching systems necessary to actually pierce a warship's shields. The other types are used for different roles. The Tactical Land Attack Missile (TLAM) is used for attacking surface targets, in anything from a pinpoint cruise strike to an all-out "level all of civilization" bombardment. Anti-Air Missiles (AAM) are used as a longer-range defense system against fighters and bombers.

'''Hungary''', officially in English the '''Socialist Republic of Hungary''', is a landlocked country in the Carpathian Basin of Central Europe, bordered by [[Austria (Terra)| Austria]],  [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)| Czechoslovakia]] and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]]. Its capital is Budapest. Hungary was dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] on April 13, [[2011]].

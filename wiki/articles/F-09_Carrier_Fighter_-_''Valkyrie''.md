The F-9 Carrier-Based Air and Space Superiority Fighter, known by the name "'''Valkyrie'''", was responsible for the [[Onyx Empire]]'s strategy of seizing space superiority in orbit of a planet before inserting ground troops. It was a ubiquitous presence throughout the [[Second Exodus War]], fighting in every engagement from the first invasion of [[Earth]] to the final battles as the Onyx Empire crumbled.

== History ==

The Valkyrie was conceived as a true carrier-based fighter, unlike its predecessor, the Raven.

From the time of its first flight and well into the [[Tempest War]], the Valkyrie earned a reputation for reliability, deadly combat effectiveness, and unmatched versatility.

== Development ==

While the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] of the [[Onyx Empire]] was happy with the [[F-4 Raven|F-4D Raven]], the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] could not use the versatile fighter for fleet support. It simply took too long to launch and recover the fighter from [[Atlas Class Carrier]]s. It wasn't equipped to handle a carrier launch, and all attempts to jury-rig a system failed due to poor performance. The XF-9 project was designed as a carrier-launched airframe, and proved to be highly successful.

Unlike the F-4D, which needed to be ferried slowly from place to place, the XF-9 could launch nearly instantly. [[Emil Torsc]] was consulted at every stage of the design, and was the first pilot to test fly the aircraft. He immediately declared it a winning design, capable of seizing space superiority in nearly any situation.

The XF-9 fighter was equipped with two cannons and tested against the F-4D, where it performed admirably, breaking speed records. The YF-9 prototype fighter was designed into a nuclear payload delivery system, which it did very well, carrying AWT-1 "Apocalypse" torpedoes. The final design, the F-9 Valkyrie, was created and launched in 2310.

== Design ==

The F-9 required none of the caution and secrecy that surrounded the Raven's construction. By 2310, the [[Onyx Empire]]'s control of [[Ah Kin]] was in the open. Construction was able to proceed at full capacity, and fighters were delivered to their assigned positions as soon as they were completed.

A total of 2680 F-9s were built between [[2310]] and [[2315]].

=== General Characteristics ===

The Valkyrie kept its design consistent in every variant; a large intake for the ramjet (and later scramjet) on the underbelly, wide wings with outer-ridge stabilizers, and twin tailfins with horizontal stabilizers, two main guns, and six ordinance hard points.

=== Performance ===

Because of its role in the Naval Air Arm, the Valkyrie had to operate frequently in both space and atmosphere. Pilots loved the wide surface control surfaces in space because of the enormous number of control thrusters, but complained that atmospheric controls could be sluggish at higher airspeeds. It wasn't until the much later F-9B variant that all performance issues were corrected.

=== Armament ===

Every variant of the Valkyrie carried two cannons and six hard points. Most variants used projectile cannons, but prior to the development of reliable gauss-based cannon technology, the fighters were equipped with modular weapon ports to carry beam weapons if needed.

The hard points were designed to carry up to heavy torpedoes, but more often used medium range interceptor missiles. The Onyx Empire often used the Valkyrie as a nuclear payload delivery vehicle, against military and civilian targets.

=== Avionics ===

[[Image:F-9 Valkyrie Front View.png|right|150px]]
The Valkyrie took advantage of the avionics package that was developed in the Raven project. All variants used the later model avionics system, taking advantage of its interface which merged all of the controls into a single package integrated into the pilot's Heads-Up Display. The throttle was installed as a lever on the left-hand side of the cockpit, with rudder control pedals on the floor at the pilot's feet. The control-stick had an integrated weapons-selector switch and trigger, as well as sensor control buttons. The pilot rarely needed anything than these three main controls, but an optional control panel was installed on the right side of the cockpit for more advanced functions. 

== Operational History ==

The F-9 took part in every major battle of the [[Second Exodus War]], much of the [[Volucris Incursion]], and most engagements in the [[Tempest War]].

== Operators ==

[[Image:F-9 Valkyrie Top View.png|right|300px]]
Today the Valkyrie is used almost exclusively by the Prefecture Galactic Navy's Air Arm, though the Prefecture Marine Corps has a few squadrons based off of their Amphibious Assault Cruisers for air support and ground attack missions. Both the Navy and Marines use the Valkyrie's fixed Gauss Cannon armament. The GC-1 was the original weaponry, but the modern GC-12 20mm cannon is more accurate, lighter, and has a faster firing rate. The Valkyrie also features an [[Ixoarchada]] software AI performance-enhancement system. This system isn't necessary for standard operations, but is used for extra evasive maneuvering when the is at full 215 KPH/S accelleration, a speed at which most pilots cannot maneuver.

The Royal Imperial Navy designed the Valkyrie to carry the AWT-1 Apocalypse nuclear torpedo, which it is still capable of doing. It can also carry the FFB-2 Apocalypse nuclear bomb for bombing hardened ground targets. The Empire had only two rules for deploying the Apocalypse weapons during the Second Exodus War: plenty and often. Terra's near-total destruction came from the heavy use of nuclear weapons by the Empire in 2322 and early 2323. The Prefecture still allows its Valkyries to carry the weapons, but has strict regulations for their use. In space, Valkyrie pilots can do as they please, but on a planet they must be a minimum distance from civilians, or receive a direct order from a high-ranking officer.

Prefecture Marine pilots prefer a more destructive ground-attack configuration, and also use an electronic warfare configuration. They equip their Valkyries with ECM Pods, Active Probes, and the AGM-3 ALARM Reflex Missile. The Air-Ground Missile known as the ALARM is an Automatic Long-Range Anti-Radar Missile. The common term is ALARM Reflex Missile. Reflex weaponry has been in use since the 20th Century, but it only became known as "reflex" weaponry in the early 24th Century. When the ALARM Reflex Missile detects probing of any sort, it fires off a missile immediately. Usually an ALARM-equipped Valkyrie flies ahead of the pack and takes out ground defense tracking systems. Other Marine pilots carry Free-Fall Bombs and Air to Ground Missiles.

[[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] Valkyries have less use for nuclear weapons than their now-defunct Royal Imperial Navy counterparts. Instead, they prefer to carry six ASIM-5 Hounds. The Air-Space Intercept Missile carries a powerful explosive warhead, and can often destroy or cripple a fighter.

== Variants ==

From [[2310]] until [[2330]], the Valkyrie was in full production. Each variant was armed with two cannons and six hard points, though many of the technologies changed over the years.

The full production F-9 Valkyrie was built by [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]], and quickly delivered to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s carrier-based squadrons. All 2,690 Valkyries were completed by [[2314]], ahead of their deadline. The F-9s built were designated with serial numbers from F09-0000001 to F09-0002690.<br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/><br/>

=== F-9A (Scramjet Atmospheric Propulsion) ===

The F-9 performed well for the Royal Imperial Navy, up until the Mesarthrim introduced fighters with extremely high atmospheric speeds. The F-9's top speed was roughly Mach 4.5, while Mesarthrim fighters could maintain speeds of up to Mach 20. The Empire incorporated a Scramjet into the new F-9A variant, bringing its airspeed up to Mach 24.

=== F-9B (Aeroelastic Control System) ===

The [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] and [[Prefecture Air Force]] took over advancing the design after the fall of the [[Empire]]. The Empire's F-12 Vampire introduced the highly maneuverable aeroelastic control system, which the Prefecture incorporated into the F-9B variant.

=== F-9C (Shield Systems Installed) ===

Rahn Industries licensed the design from Alexi-Wrought Airworks on Alpha Centauri, and incorporated shield designs into the F-9C, and later gravity cavitation drives F-9D. Both the F-9C and F-9D variants were short lived due to suffering from power shortages when both the shields and drives were operated at the same time. The shortages were fixed on the F-9E by adding a new fusion reactor. Both Alexi-Wrought Airworks and Rahn Industries still manufacture the F-9E, though Rahn Industries built models receive the "r" subvariant designation.

===Architecture===

Gorhamut architecture is astoundingly complicated.  They usually build their homes from solid rock, and decorate everything.  Etchings and carvings are their chief interest, coupled with live plants and animal menageries.

===Social Structure===

Early observations of surviving Gorhamut, coupled with pre-Sundering records, led many to believe that the Gorhamut are completely benign, fighting only when goaded into action.

Modern records debunk this theory.  The Gorhamut/Anthronoris war was actually started by the Gorhamut.  Apparently, if something is perceived as a threat to their species, they will not stop trying to destroy it.  This ideology led to the creation of the [[Anderung]] to fight their first war for them, and the creation of the [[Volucris]], to execute their revenge.

Gorhamut have a passion for nurturing non-intelligent life.  Many, if not most of the worlds inhabited by humans today are the result of Gorhamut terraformation projects early in the galaxy's history.  Professor Hlow--the only human to visit their new, hidden home--has said that it is a veritable paradise.

A Gorhamut is happiest in a wide, open area with thick grass, small forests and many pools of water.  A very wet version of the Serengeti Plains would be an ideal Gorhamut habitat.

The Gorhamut, despite the advanced technology and vast empire they built over the eons, were eventually dominated by the constant adaptability of the Masters and the ravenous appetite for power of the [[Ngrligru]].  Their last great technological inspiration led them to build three hidden shelters, one of Gorhamut, one of Gorhamut knowledge, and one of the Gorhamut's mightiest creation: the Volucris species.

It is believed that after the war with the Anthronoris, the Gorhamut abandoned the Anderung, considering them a failure.

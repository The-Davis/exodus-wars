The Grendel is the strongest battleaArmor suit in the Prefecture military, and among the strongest man-made armor around. While it's slow speed hurts it in open field warfare, its strength in urban and indoor battles make it a necessity to the Prefecture war machine.

Development was a difficult process for the Grendel. Five designs were rejected, and six prototypes were destroyed in testing. The first production run had fatal design flaws--six test operators were killed when vital systems failed in combat testing--and the whole line was scrapped while the bugs were worked out.

After the Battle of Mirach, the Grendel was finally finished on February 25th, 2324. The development teams added Phoenix Project technology at the last possible minute, but the final test models performed excellently.

Because of its large size, the Grendel can carry two BT-6 MAAWs on its shoulders. While it could carry heavier cannon-based weaponry, it doesn't have much room for ammo bins. Each MAAW has two shots.

While it has the power to carry a variety of crew-served weapons by itself, the most useful for the Grendel's role is the MiD-8 heavy SAW. This SAW is too large even for a crew of four to handle, but can lay down heavy cover fire.

Grendel operators are known to also engage in close combat, taking a melee fight to enemies like the Volucris. Its powerful arms can rip apart most enemies, while its hardened hull can deflect the claws and beaks of a Volucris soldier.

The Grendel is the favorite of the Prefecture Legion Corps, though expense keeps them from deploying it to all of their infantry. For the Legions, firepower is more important than mobility, so the Grendel is an obvious choice.

In the Prefecture Marine Corps, mobility is key, and thus the Grendel is a liability. Instead of dumping it altogether, the suit is used in Special Operations teams as a mobile weapons platform.

'''Hishai''' was the hereditary title of the military leader of the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]], and later of the [[Rikaz o Fii Cai iv Huerna]]. Literally translated, it means "Champion", but a more accurate description would be "Champion General".

== Military Duties ==

The Hishai serves as the champion of the [[Prekohr]]. For thousands of years, the Hishai has put down rebellions, pursued bandits, and fought against any who would dare to challenge the [[Prekohr]]'s divine rule.

== Posthumous Honor ==

Very rarely, the Prekohr will post-humously name someone a Hishai for outstanding valor and bravery in battle. There can only be one living Hishai at a time, but sometimes the deeds of a deceased soldier warrant the highest honor a Prekohr can give--recognizing them by naming them champion.

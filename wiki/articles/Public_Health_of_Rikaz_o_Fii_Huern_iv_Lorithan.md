'''Public Health''' in the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] is helped by its advanced medical science system, but it is crippled by the many mutations in [[Lorithos]] populations. The recent advances in medical technology, such as organ regrowth and synthetic blood simply do not work on most [[Lorithos]].

[[Lorithos]] doctors are very skilled in treating burns, radiation poisoning, chemical poisoning, and other injuries caused by war. Their expertise is frequently sought after localized disasters like fires, reactor leaks, and chemical plant accidents. While these are rare, they tend to be disastrous when they do occur.

== Events of 2011 ==
The year 2011 saw the creation of a new unified government that brought the ideals of the west and the east together in peace for the first time. The United States of America, the Commonwealth Nations, the Warsaw Pact Nations, and Japan were all dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The new capitol buildings were built in Denver, Colorado.

===January===
*January 1
**The [[United States (Terra)|United States of America]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
**The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]]. 
*January 2 - [[Canada (Terra)|Canada]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*January 6 - NASA and SSP are merged into the [[United Earth Alliance]] Space Agency.
*January 28 - The [[Orion Spacecraft| Orion I]] Flight is launched from the Gagarin Space Center in Ontario.

===February===
*February 1 - [[Poland (Terra)|Poland]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*February 7 - [[Australia (Terra)| Australia]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*February 9 - [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*February 11 - [[Orion Spacecraft| Orion I]] Flight returns to [[Terra]] safely.
*February 12 - [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*February 19 - [[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

===March===
*March 11 - [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*March 12 - [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*March 15 - The [[Orion Spacecraft| Orion II]] Flight launches to the [[International Space Station]].

===April===
*April 3 - [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*April 7 - [[Bulgaria (Terra)|Bulgaria]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].
*April 13 - [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

===May===

*May 16 - [[Yugoslavia (Terra)|Yugoslavia]] signs the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

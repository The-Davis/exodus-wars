Gloria Felix was born in [[2286]] in Exeter, England. She joined the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s Air Arm in [[2315]], and received command of [[VF-11 Imperial Naval Fighter Squadron - "Hell's Kittens"|VF-11]] in [[2318]], becoming one of the first non-[[Torimur]] and non-"vat" pilots to receive command.

Felix was very proud of her accomplishments and her recognition as one of the first squadron commanders not native to [[Torimur]]. She had a quirky attitude and everyone’s initial impression of her was that she was harmless--which was hardly the case. She held her pilots to very high standards and had a low tolerance for mediocrity. Felix's piloting skills were considerable, and she once managed to survive a simulated dogfight with [[Emil Torsc]] for three minutes before the top ace defeated her.

Felix's pleasant demeanor hid a fiery core. Her quirky attitude belied her skill as a natural pilot.

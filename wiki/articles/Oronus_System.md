The '''Oronus System''' consists of the star Oronus, the three planets, their moons, and the [[Morris Belt]] asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Oronus, the planets are [[Thankur]] and [[Sorek]]. [[Sorek]] is orbitted by many moons, particularly [[Remut]], the site of the Oronus colony.

==Stellar Navigation==

Oronus is the Oronus System's only star. Oronus is a class F3Y orange star. The Oronus System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. The Oronus System is part of the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Thankur ===

[[Thankur]] (6.7AU) is a medium-sized gas giant. It has 15 moons, none of which are inhabitted or named.

=== The Morris Belt ===

The [[Morris Belt]] (8.2AU) is an enormous asteroid belt, and the heart of the wealth of the Oronus System.

=== Sorek ===

[[Sorek]] (9.3AU) is a large gas giant. It has 20 moons, one of which serves as the inhabitted colony of the Oronus System.

==== Remut ====

[[Remut]] is a large moon, technically a planet. It has multiple domes on the surface, all of which are linked by a series of tunnels. The moon also has a powerful planetary shield, built to deflect harmful radiation. It is too cold for terraformation.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Oronus System was colonized in March 7, [[2290]], in an effort led by Robert Morris. It was his plan to establish an industrial colony on [[Remut]].

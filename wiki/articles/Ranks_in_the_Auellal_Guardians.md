The first Guardians were formed by [[Steven Cue]] in secret in [[2312]] on [[Caledon]].  After paying off the local garrison commander, [[Steven Cue|Steven]] gathered up local citizen and political exiles who were sympathetic to the cause.

Using his Shock Troops from ''The Pub Runners'', [[Steven Cue|Steven]] gave each of them parallel officer ranks, and entrusted the [[Auellal Captain|Caiptean]] to promote and train the locals as Guards.

==Guards==

==Rank Structure==
The Auellal operate in larger elements then most other forces, the level of organization, communication, and leadership required by their people is therefore at a paramount.

*A '''Dùn''', or ''fortress'', is a command group, usually including a variety of special units, including the commander, his tactical and logistic staff, intelligence officers, standard bearers, and MASH units.
**There are '''5 Guards''' in a Bagaids Dùn.
**There are '''40 Guards''' in a Drongs Dùn.
**There are '''250 Guards''' in a Oiks Dùn.
**There are '''42 Guards''' in a Caipteans Dùn
*A '''Bannal''', or ''gang'', is made up of six Dara Gard, and led by one Ceud Gard.
*A '''Bagaid''', or ''cluster'', is made up of three Bannal, and lead by a small five man Dùn which is led by a Ceud Sarjant, with supporting Dara SarjantI 
*A '''Drong''', or ''tribe'', is made up of eight Bagaid, and led by a ninth forty man Dùn which is led by a Eabee, with a Riaghlair, at least two supporting Dara Riaghlair, and a staff of Ceud Sarjant and Dara Sarjant.
*A '''Oik''', or ''post'', is made up of six Drong, and led by a ninth Dùn which is led by a Ceud Ridir.  The Ceud Ridir has a Dùn of two hundred-fifty men at his disposal, encompassing an entire MASH, and intelligence group.
*The '''Oik''' includes a '''Caiptean Dùn''' which is led by a Caiptean, and a staff of Dara Caiptean and Ceud Ridir's.  There are forty-two men in a Caiptean Dùn.  His staff is not a field staff usually - therefore there is no reson to maintain a MASH unit with them.

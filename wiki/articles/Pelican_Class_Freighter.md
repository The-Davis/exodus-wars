The Pelican is one of Rahn Industries' flagship products, dating back to the days when it produced nothing but freighters for deep space shipping. Using the idea of a modular loading system, the Pelican medium freighter is able to carry a rather large amount of cargo in easily detachable pods that can be individually swapped in and out. Later models began to use the new Rahn Industries Anti-Gravity Lift System that allows a Pelican to land on any planet surface and unload its cargo.

[[Image:PelicanClassFreighter1.jpg]]

With six modules able to carry seven thousand tons each, and the forward hold able to carry an additional ten thousand tons, the Pelican-class medium hauler can carry significantly more cargo than competing classes of the same size. Cargo tenders and docking installations equipped with Rahn Industries components can load, unload, and reload cargo in minutes rather than hours.

[[Image:PelicanClassFreighter2.jpg]]

With only forty crew members needed to run the entire ship, the living quarters on a Pelican are some of the best on a mass produced freighter. This is because of the massive amount of automation that is used throughout the ship and the modules. Even though there is a well-deck, it isn't big enough to carry anything other than a shuttle or two. The Pelican must look to its escorts for fighter support if it is attacked.

[[Image:PelicanClassFreighter3.jpg]]

Pelicans are usually deployed in convoys of up to twenty and escorted by frigates or escort carriers. Most Pelicans spend the majority of their time in space on the trading lanes, moving their huge cargoes from one world to another. Most Pelican crews are on rotation and are able to see their families for a month before having to go back out into space for a month.

[[Image:PelicanClassFreighter4.jpg]]

Rahn Industries has done something rather clever: their Pelicans are designed to use components from competing models. As part of a marketing strategy, any customer wishing to buy a Pelican can sell their current freighter to Rahn Industries for scrap in return for a discount on a Pelican to replace it. Rahn Industries then turns around a strips the competitor's model bare for parts, and puts the working ones into a new Pelican. This marketing strategy has worked wonders and has helped to bolster Pelican sales into the thousands.

== Events of 8000BC ==

The [[Ikronin]] world held onto much of their civility and dignity, and before long they were once again united under one world government.  The [[Anthronoris]] had an Empire, which the [[Ikronin]] tried to hold in high status.  There were relatives of the Emperor, and one of them, a man named [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]], became the leader of the [[Ikronin]].

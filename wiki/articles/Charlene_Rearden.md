'''Charlene "Charlie" Rearden'''
*Place of Birth: [[Albanus]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Charlene Rearden'' commands the FDS ''Bannock'''s best Piranha squadron. Rearden is a relatively new squadron commander, part of the second graduating class from Wei Air Force Academy.

'''Chile''', officially the '''Republic of Chile''', is a country in South America occupying a long and narrow coastal strip wedged between the Andes mountain range and the Pacific Ocean. It is bordered on the west by the Pacific Ocean, on the north by [[Peru (Terra)| Peru]], on the northeast by Bolivia, on the east by Argentina and on the south by the Drake Passage.

Chile was a late signitary of the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]], and was dissolved into the unified world government on August 9, [[2013]].

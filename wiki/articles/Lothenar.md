'''Lothenar''' is the head of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]'s [[Thenaro]] legislative body. The position also serves as the head of state. The position is traditionally one to oversee debate and political argument, but it has extreme powers under special circumstances.

== Powers and Responsibilities ==

The Lothenar has, for all intents and purposes, unlimited political power under any state of emergency. This tradition never left the lawbooks, even though it was originally intended for [[Meronir Tronit]]'s use in directing the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] in battling for its survival.

Under normal circumstances, the Lothenar runs the day to day business of the [[Thenaro]], and officially approves any of their decisions.

== Abuse Prevention ==

The position of [[Suthenar]] was created as a way to keep the Lothenar in check. While the position has little power on its own, it can quickly gain the support it needs to stop an unpopular Lothenar in his tracks.

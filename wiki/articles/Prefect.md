The '''Prefect''' is the only military executive national-level political office in the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

==Powers and Responsibilities==
===Executive Duties===

The Prefect is responsible for any treaties made with any foreign [[:Category:Nations|nations]].

===Military Duties===

The Prefect acts as the supreme commander of the [[Military of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

===Abuse Prevention===

The Prefect can be relieved of duty by a majority decision from the Prefecture Senate and a super-majority decision from the Prefecture Curiate.

==List of Prefects==

* [[Anthony Hadrian]] - First Prefect - Served from [[2323]] to present.

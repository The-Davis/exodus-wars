===Overview===

The Phaelopendra is a very effective fighter against most armored human targets.  It's heavy claws can punch through Mech armor, and its size makes it difficult for Battle Armor to face it.  Like the small "Cutter," the Phaelopendra's claws are super-reinforced, sharpened, and lined with microscopic barbs.  This has earned the Phaelopendra the name "Slicer."

[[Image:Phaelopendra.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Slicer is highly effective against most anything on the ground.  It's big, has powerful claws, and it can reach a good ten feet in front of itself when attacking, giving it a wicked attack radius.  Even mighty Rigs must be cautious when dealing with a single Slicer, and must flee when meeting them in packs.

The strength of the Slicer is devastating power, strong armor, and decent speed.  Slicers are hard to kill, and usually survive battles.

The Slicer has decent speed, and can manage a gallop of about 50HPH.  It's carapace is mostly energy and heat resistant, so lasers and missiles are mostly useless.  Cannons and PPCs do decent damage at close range.

===Deployment===

Slicers usually appear in battle in numbers of a hundred or less, and serve to punch through lines of tanks and Rigs.  They live long lives if they aren't killed, and can develop their intelligence over time.  The older the Slicer, the smarter and stronger it is.  A Slicer that has lived over a hundred years is among the most deadly fighters around.

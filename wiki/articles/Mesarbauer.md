==History==
Mesarbauer was founded by Geschaftsführer [[Edmond Baumgartner]], Lord of the Baumgartner Clan, Protector of the Clans Economy in [[2132]] using almost the entire treasury of the fledgling clans.  [[Edmond Baumgartner|Edmond]] was convinced that a strong navy would be vital to defeating the [[Anderung]], so he bet heavily in the company.  [[Edmond Baumgartner|Edmond]] established Mesarbauer so that the [[Clan Lord|Tischler]] Clan was the sole owners of Mesarbauer.  However, in [[2265]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], as a whole, became the majority owners of shares in Mesarbauer, making it the first national state construction organisation.  During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], [[Strauss Energie]] bargained for all Mesarbauer stock when they called in their loans they made to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] government.  The Geschaftsführer of [[Strauss Energie]] sold the majority of shares in Mesarbauer to [[William Camp]], in exchange for exclusive power distribution rights on [[Mesar]] under the [[United Centusi States]].  Mesarbauer came under criticism just prior to the [[Tempest War]] from the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] for what they called an illegal interstellar monopoly.  However, once the [[Tempest War]] began, the charges were dropped since the [[United Centusi States]] and [[Ikronin Jurekön]] were now officially at war.  During the [[Tempest War|war]] [[William Camp]] nationalised all [[Mesarthrim]] Corporations.  Mesarbauer absorbed all remaining non-[[Auellal League|Auellal]] [[Fireline Industries]] assets were freed from [[Strauss Energie]].

==Corporate governance==
Mesarbauer is currently a priatley ownerd company.  Mesarbaur is owned by [[William Camp]], Lord of the [[Clan Lords|Tischler]] Clan, and [[Chancellor]] of the [[United Centusi States]].

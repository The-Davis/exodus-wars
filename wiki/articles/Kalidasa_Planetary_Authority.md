The '''Kalidasa Planetary Authority''' has sovereign control over the world of [[Kalidasa]] and by extension control over the [[Lupei System]]. The Authority was formed when the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] movement on Kalidasa was able to sieze control of the planet towards the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

=== History ===

Main Article: [[History of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|History]]

The Kalidasa Planetary Authority took over administration of the [[Lupei System]] after the end of the [[Second Exodus War]]. The Kalidasa Planetary Authority is now busy with the task of reconstruction of the planet and system resources, as well as the normalization of relations with other nations.

[[Prolíder]] [[Evaristo Silveira]] is the head of the [[Kalidasa|Kalidasan]] government. His resistance forces aided the [[Mesarthrim]] in their campaign against the [[Empire]] on [[Kalidasa]], and he was richly rewarded for his efforts. [[Evaristo Silveira|Silveira]] put everything he had into forming the first independant [[Kalidasa|Kalidasan]] government, and won the first free elections as a result.

In [[2325]], the Kalidasa Planetary Authority joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]] in order to better represent itself in the [[Orion Arm]].

=== Government ===

Main article: [[Government of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Government]]

The Kalidasan government works on the quasi-socialist systems of economics made popular by the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] in the post-war galactic economy. Sectors of the economy considered vital to planetary defense or core resources are controlled by the Planetary Authority itself, while services and less vital areas are allowed to be independently or corporately owned.

The powers of the [[Prolíder]] are only slightly limited by the laws of the Planetary Authority, and at any moment he has the authority to nationalize a sector of the economy for the good of the planet.

=== Foreign Relations ===

Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Foreign Relations]]

Many diplomats were surprised at [[Evaristo Silveira]]'s decision to nationalize not only the [[Onyx Empire]]'s orbital shipyards, but also the ships themselves. The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] had claimed the [[:Category:Warships|warships]] under construction, but offered to defend [[Kalidasa]] in exchange. When Silveira refused, the resulting diplomatic meltdown nearly resulted in open warfare.

Despite their often criticized debut into galactic politics, the Planetary Authority is well respected among other [[:Category:Nations|nations]], particularly members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Military]]

The Kalidasa Planetary Authority's armed forces are divided into three branches:
* [[Kalidasa Space Navy]]
* [[Kalidasa Security Force]]
* [[Kalidasa Air and Space Corps]]

Unlike many other small independent nations, the [[Lupei System|Kalidasans]] have the advantage of owning unspoiled pieces of military hardware from the days of [[Empire|Imperial]] occupation. Their shipyards are still intact, as are their weapons factories, which supply unique solutions to their defense needs.

=== Territory ===

Main Article: [[Territory of the Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Territory]]

Aside from the [[Lupei System]]'s main inhabitted planet, [[Kalidasa]], the Planetary Authority controls several independent orbitting space stations, known as ''Haven''-Class construction centers. These highly-sophisticated installations were the secret to the [[Onyx Empire]]'s early successes and vast fleets; capable of assembling major superstructures without the aid of human input, only needing the assistance of its living operators to install the finer components the bulky machinery cannot handle.

The Planetary Authority uses some of its ''Haven''s as cargo centers, but most serve their original purpose: building and refitting [[:Category:Starships|starships]].

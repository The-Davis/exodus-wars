Hyperion has little to offer the Titanians, but evidence suggests it was once an important world in the [[Anthronoris]] defenses of the [[Titan System]]. Hyperion once had a breathable atmosphere, but it was probably artificially created and has long since vanished. Seven warship hulks were found crashed on the surface, and scans indicate that at least ten more were buried by the elements over the thousands of years since the [[Sundering]]. Many expeditions have been launched to the hulks, but nothing of worth has been found. A new project is in the works to excavate one of the buried hulks to see if being buried has preserved any of the Anthronoris technology.

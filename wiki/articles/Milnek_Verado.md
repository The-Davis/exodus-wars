'''Milnek Verado'''
*Place of Birth: [[Veratul]]
*Allegiance: [[Taviridis Somarchada]]

''Milnek Verado'' is a Captain in the [[Ixoarchada]] special forces. He was indited by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] for conspiracy to kill [[Andreas Tischler]], but was later acquited when it was revealed that [[Nathan Hinks]] was a traitor.

===Overview===

The Nigrotaegoliath is the largest soldier breed, and a terrifying sight.  It towers over light and medium Mechs, and is impervious to infantry and tanks.  It is known as the "Imperator" by human soldiers.

[[Image:Nigrotaegoliath.jpg]]

The Imperator is armed with spine-launchers, similar to those of the Tyrant.  It is armed in front, above, and on either side with these weapons.  It apparently has enough stomach capacity to store more than enough ammo before battle.  Unlike the Tyrant, the Imperator has no need of anchoring itself to launch these weapons.

The Imperator is also armed with plasma glands, exactly like those of the Bombardier.  It has two, and can fire them in several arcs in the front.

Finally, the Imperator has "shrapnel spines," which launch from glands on the top of the Volucris.  These tiny spines are smaller than bullets, but travel at near-sonic speeds.  The Imperator uses them to repel airborne attackers. 

The Imperator is even slower than the Tyrant, but very powerful, and capable of taking assault Mechs head-on without flinching.  An Imperator can win battles against superior numbers, which is very unusual for Volucris.  Just one Imperator on a planet is cause for extreme concern, and entire battle plans can be centered around taking one down.

The Imperator's carapace is extremely resistant to weapons fire.  It can simply shrug off missile bombardments.  The chitin is shaped in microscopic bubbles so closely packed that no apparent hole can be seen.  This cell organization gives the chitin extremely effective inertial dampening qualities.  Like all Volucris, the Imperator's cells are resistant to heat and radiation.

Imperators are found in small numbers in large-scale operation.  Volucris Nobles have no qualms with using the Imperator in assaults, but the Imperator requires huge amounts of resources to arm.  Like most Volucris, it has little need to eat except when it's frequently fighting and moving.

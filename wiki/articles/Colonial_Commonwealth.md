The '''Colonial Commonwealth''' is all that remains of the glory of the former [[United Earth Alliance]]. The Commonwealth is the economic and political successor to the Alliance, and is often seen as a simple continuation of the Alliance that was.

=== History ===

Main Article: [[History of the Colonial Commonwealth|History]]

After the [[Second Exodus War]], the peoples of [[Ah Kin]], [[Kalmar]], and [[Bethija]] joined together to better redevelop themselves, and to better defend themselves in the future. The people of the [[Titan System]] joined soon afterwards. The Commonwealth is still busy rebuilding after the destruction from war, and has set about forging alliances with new friends and old enemies.

In [[2325]], the Colonial Commonwealth became one of the founding members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]] in order to better represent itself in the [[Orion Arm]].

=== Government ===

Main Article: [[Government of the Colonial Commonwealth|Government]]

The Commonwealth has no central government. Each of the worlds have their own government, with only a loose coalition meeting on a regular basis. The governors of each world act as representatives in the Commonwealth, which works for mutual defense and economic benefit. There are only two national leaders of the Commonwealth: The [[General-Commander of the Commonwealth|General-Commander]] of the military, a position currently held by [[Scott Sanburn]]; and the [[Secretariat of the Commonwealth|Secretariat]], who is responsible for taking notes at the meetings of the governors and reporting the changes in government of the individual planets, a position currently held by [[Gary Hughes]].

=== Law ===

Main Article: [[Law of the Colonial Commonwealth|Law]]

Most laws of the Colonial Commonwealth have been kept from the old [[United Earth Alliance]] government. Criminal law is relatively simple, while civil law can be more complex.

Like the United Earth Alliance, the Colonial Commonwealth does not allow capital punishment for crimes. Imprisonment is the typical criminal penalty, though most prisons have more of an emphasis on rehabilitation than on incarceration.

=== Law Enforcement ===

Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Colonial Commonwealth|Law Enforcement]]

The Colonial Commonwealth has a series of law enforcement organizations. All law enforcement is performed by civilian law enforcement officers. The military does not enforce laws in the Commonwealth.

The Commonwealth's urban and suburban patrol and law enforcement is handled by the Colonial Police Service, a successor to the United Earth Alliance's Metropolitan Police Service. Rural and remote areas are patrolled by the Colonial Marshal Service. Both services report to their planet's Bureau of Investigation, which ultimately reports to the Commonwealth Police Oversight Committee.

=== Foreign Relations ===

Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Colonial Commonwealth|Foreign Relations]]

The Commonwealth gets along well with all members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. It does not recognize the authority of the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Space Police]], which has led to a lot of tension with the [[Alliance of Planets]].

There is also significant internal political pressure to limit dealings with the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. Many members of the Commonwealth resent that [[Tejat]] and [[Holt]], former Alliance worlds, chose to join with the Prefecture instead of their former compatriots.

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of the Colonial Commonwealth|Military]]

The Military of the Colonial Commonwealth is one of the few truly national organizations in the Commonwealth. Most jobs are handled on planetary-government levels, but the military has defense jurisdiction over all four [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]] in the Commonwealth.

The Colonial Commonwealth's armed forces are divided into two branches:
* [[Colonial Commonwealth Air and Space Command|Colonial Air and Space Command]]
* [[Colonial Commonwealth Army Command|Colonial Army Command]]

The military in its entirety reports to the [[General-Commander of the Commonwealth|General-Commander]]. The first General-Commander was [[Josef Siroski]] of [[Bethija]], but when the [[Titan System|Titanians]] joined the Commonwealth, the position was given to [[Scott Sanburn]].

=== Intelligence ===

Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Colonial Commonwealth|Intelligence]]

The Commonwealth absorbed as much of the old [[Intelligence of the United Earth Alliance|Central Intelligence Agency]] as it could, and organized its own new covert surveillance department. The new Colonial Intelligence Agency (which kept the same acronym and most operational procedures) emphasizes computer surveillance and covert infiltration of foreign computer networks. Colonial "hackers" are very skilled, and often the cause of embarrassment to foreign military officials.

=== Transportation ===

Main Article: [[Transportation of the Colonial Commonwealth|Transportation]]

With four distinct [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]] and their planets, the Commonwealth has to provide a working interstellar transportation infrastructure. Most merchants have access to [[hyperspace]] drives of one form or another, or use the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates]] installed near their [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]]. Each world has their own network of land, air, and sea transport.

While access to hyperspace travel is not difficult to obtain, operating a hyperspace-capable vessel in Commonwealth territory is strictly regulated. Large travel and shipping conglomerates hold a lot of political clout with members of the Commonwealth government, and through their lobbying efforts laws have been passed that subject independent operators to extreme scrutiny. Most private transporters are unable to access anything above the [[Tri Band]] and are encouraged to use alternate methods of transportation.

=== Territory ===

Main Article: [[Territory of the Colonial Commonwealth|Territory]]

The Colonial Commonwealth controls four star systems, each of which is independently governed. The planets in the Commonwealth only share a common military, currency, and system of laws. Currently, the [[Alpha Centauri System]], [[Tau Ceti System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], and [[Titan System]] are part of the Colonial Commonwealth.

=== Economy ===

Main Article: [[Economy of the Colonial Commonwealth|Economy]]

The economy of the Commonwealth is partially socialized. Some aspects are regulated, such as extreme safety issues, and compatibility standards, but others are unregulated and freely operated and traded. The Commonwealth has also nationalized certain vital sectors of industry, such as power generation, most raw materials, and most public utilities and social necessities. Certain basic foodstuffs such as grains are nationalized, while more luxury items are handled by private farms or imported.

=== Demographics ===

Main Article: [[Demographics of the Colonial Commonwealth|Demographics]]

The Commonwealth has a diverse [[Terran]] population, with Russians, South Americans, and North Americans inhabiting most of its worlds. The [[Titan System]] world of [[Rhea]] also has a large [[Ngrligru]] population, but most of them hold citizenship within [[Kabila Kimburu]] rather than the Commonwealth. The Commonwealth has experienced a post-war baby boom, and has seen a population explosion among most demographic areas.

=== Public Health ===

Main Article: [[Public Health of the Colonial Commonwealth|Public Health]]

The field of medicine is partially nationalized and socialized, and partially private. The Commonwealth government runs emergency medical care facilities and innoculation programs, and subsidizes certain areas of the medical field which it considers to be vital to public health. 

Other areas of public health such as regular check-ups, alternative medicines, nutrition, and cosmetic surgeries are neither regulated nor run by the Commonwealth government.

=== Culture ===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Colonial Commonwealth|Culture]]

Culture in the Commonwealth is considered "modernized industrial" in the [[Terran]] sense. It centers around a balance of government involvement and personal responsibility, and emphasizes a lack of judgement in family choices and morality. They spend a lot of their free time in leisure activities such as sports and entertainment, and enjoy the classical arts.

The ''' Reliquiae Astrum Classis''' is the primary means which the Remnant maintains contact with the galaxy at large.  The Reliquiae Astrum Classis is the dominate branch of the Remnant military because it provides all inter-system transportation for both the [[Remnant Fighter Corps| Reliquiae Acalanthis Agema]] and the [[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]] as well as resource retrieval for the Remnant [Void World]]s.  The Fleet, as it is more commonly referred to as, is responsible for offensive action, Void World defense, reconnaissance, intra/inter system transportation, and resource collection operations. 

==History==

The Reliquiae Astrum Classis' history goes back to dates unknown and her battles fought in countless ill-remembered wars from the past.  At one time, the Anthronoris Astrum Classis was the most powerful military organization known throughout the [[Milky Way]], but even it would eventually fail to the long [[Gorhamut]] and [[Ngrligru]] wars, which were fought over several millennia.  The Reliquiae Astrum Classis, as it would eventually be known, picked up the pieces after the [[Sundering]] and the fall of the [[Anthronoris Empire]] and shepherded those few surviving Masters across the galaxy to their new homes in the Habitats in accordance with the [[Master Plan]].

==Structure==

===Fleets===

The Fleets are divided into two types: Legiars and Scrutias.  The Legair Classii are primarily responsible for offensive action and are a made up of a collection of Manipars to allow for maximum flexibility during campaign.  There is currently only one Legair Classis in operation, and its vessels are drawn entirely from new construction.  The Scruntia Classis is primarily responsible for defensive operations for Remnant assets and are made up of all the remaining Anthronoris vessels as well as the vast majority (approximately 3/4ths) of the newer types.  There is Scruntia Classis currently in the Fleet.  They too are organized into Manipars to allow rapid reinforcement of engaged positions taking advantage of the Remnant's advanced hyperspace systems.

==Organization==

The Fleet is divided into two distinct types of fighting units, the Legair and the Scrunta Classii.  The Legair is designed for maximization for offensive roles while the Scrunta is maximized for defense.  Each is comprise of Manipars, and each Manipar is made up of four vessel Cohorts.  The difference between a Legair and a Scrunta is the total number of Manipars as well as the specific breakdown of each Manipar's Cohorts.  

'''Legair''' - Commanded by a Legant
*3 Manipars - Each commanded by a Legantior
Each Legair Manipar contains:
**2 Heavy Combatant Cohorts (8 Total Vessels)
**4 Medium Combatant Cohorts (16 Total Vessels)
**1 Light Combatant Cohort (4 Total Vessels)
**2 Scout Cohorts (8 Total Vessels)

36 Total Vessels per Manipar,
108 Total Vessels per Legair

The Legair is centered around heavier firepower, with the majority of the strength coming from Medium Combatants to provide flexibility.  There are currently no Anthronoris type vessels assigned to the singular Legair in the Fleet.  The Legair's three Manipars rotate assignments between the one posting in the [[Orion Arm]] and two postings in the [[Sagittarius Arm]].

'''Scrunta''' - Commanded by a Legant
*5 Manipars - Each commanded by a Legantior
Each Scrunta Manipar contains:
**1 Heavy Combatant Cohorts (4 Total Vessels)
**2 Medium Combatant Cohorts (8 Total Vessels)
**4 Light Combatant Cohort (16 Total Vessels)
**2 Scout Cohorts (8 Total Vessels)

36 Total Vessels per Manipar,
180 Total Vessels per Scrunta

The Scrunta is centered around a balanced structure of smaller combatants supporting larger ones.  The aforementioned numbers do not include the Anthronoris type vessels assigned to each of the Manipars of the singular Scrunta in the Fleet.  Four of the Manipars of the Scrunta is assigned to one of the four Void Worlds, with the remaining one on ready standby as it oversees resource collection operations.

===Habitat-side Installations===

The Fleet maintains numerous planetary installations in the four Remnant Void Worlds.  The majority of these are located within each of the Void Worlds' Great Rings, although a few are also maintained on planetary surfaces.  These serve a variety of functions including initial training, leave facilities, maintenance shops, support functions, and permanent residences for Fleet personnel.  These facilities also maintain several heavy anti-orbital defensive emplacements.

===Space Stations===

Space Stations are far more numerous and important to the Fleet than planetary installations.  Hundreds occupy the interior volume of the Void World's sphere, from some in planetary orbit to the majority in strategic points within the sphere.  Each of these stations is heavily armed and armored to serve as a second line of defense if hostile forces penetrated the sphere.  Additionally, the Remnant maintains several stations in deep space throughout areas of interest around the galaxy.  These are heavily stealthed and even more heavily defended.  Space stations function as intermediate training facilities, supply depots, maintenance shops, support functions, observatories, listening posts, research facilities, and semi-permanent residences for Fleet personnel.

===Ships===

The commissioned ships of the Reliquiae Astrum Classis fall into four categorizations; scout, light combatant, medium combatant, and heavy combatant.  Each serves a specific set of roles, but generally, Remnant vessels are more versatile than other races ships at performing tasks not normally assigned to them.  These categorizations are both historical and functional, as the Anthronoris Astrum Classis fought in a very formalized formation structure in its day.  Although large formations are rare, these roles still are vital to the Task Force structure of the Legiars and Scrutias.

Remnant ships are named for historical figures and battles as determined by both the '''Military''' and '''Ministry of History'''.

===Weapon Systems===

The Reliquiae Astrum Classis utilizes essentially three types of weapons; the '''Emitter''', the Falarica Missile Launcher, and the '''Torpedo Tube'''.  A student of Remnant Military Doctrine would also recognize that two additional "weapons" are used heavily by the Remnant; those being their amazing electronic warfare capabilities and their fighter craft.  

The '''Emitter''' draws its capabilities from the Gravity Wave Shield, as do most other systems on board a Remnant vessel.  Simply put, depending upon its selected firing mode, the '''Emitter''' can very effectively engage both single targets and entire swarms of smaller targets (i.e. missiles and fighter craft) with ease.  A more detailed technical and operational overview can be found here.  

The Falarica Missile Launcher has the capacity to launch a variety of warheads, although the preferred type is antimatter.  The missile launcher is the first traditional weapon used by the Remnant in an engagement and it is not uncommon for a Remnant commander to conduct the entirety of the battle at missile range because of the advantages the Remnant enjoys in that field.  The missile launcher is the preferred type for long range engagements because it is the only traditional weapons system capable of engaging while using the "Shader Brake";  placing the vessel's drive singularity between itself and the enemy as a shield against incoming fire.  The missiles are able to maneuver around the singularity and strike the hostile target without exposing the Remnant vessel.  Remnant vessels are designed with their missile launchers strategically placed to take advantage of this maneuver.  

The '''Torpedo Tube''' is the last and least used of the traditional Remnant weaponry.  It has the capacity to deliver its larger warhead at much higher velocities, but usually these munitions are unable to be used during Shadar Brakes.  They can be very useful to deliver Autonomous Combat Unit pods to hostile ships to facilitate boarding and disruption action, as well as quickly deploying sensor drone screens, so generally each Remnant vessel employs at least one torpedo tube.

The Remnant also uses two non-traditional weapons systems.  The first being their without-equal electronic warfare capacity.  Rarely is a Remnant ship detectable unless it desires to be so.  Secondly, the Remnant employs its fighters in a non-traditional way.  

Because the '''Emitter''' allows ships ample anti-fighter/missile defense, Remnant fighters are employed in an entirely different way.  The Remnant fighter is more akin to an extension of its vessels energy weapons suite than it is an individual combatant.  Common practice calls for squadrons of fighters to be launched at the enemy and approach to extremely close energy range where they utilize their one weapon with devastating effect.  Squadrons train to be able to systematically and in unison skim a hostile vessel's hull, inflicting pinpoint damage on key systems, knocking them out.  It is very rare for a Remnant fighter to be deployed against opposing fighters, since they are of little threat to Remnant capital ships.  Even Remnant fighters are unhindered by hostile fighter craft due to their utilization of the Valdis Shield.                    

===Special Forces===

The Reliquiae Astrum Classis does not maintain any special forces due to the small size of the Remnant population.  The [[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]] performs any necessary Special Forces function.

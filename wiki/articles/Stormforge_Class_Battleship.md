When [[Andreas Tischler]] left to fight the [[Third Battle of Cronus]] he called a meeting with the owners of Mesarbauer. Tischler had been impressed with the intel the Lorithan had shown him on the Imperial fleet, and gave one simple order; "When I get back, I want a bigger ship." With that, the Stormforge was born.

The Stormforge class was named after the resistance cells scattered around [[Mesar]] during the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]]. Five were named in honour of [[William Camp]] and after the [[Tempest War]] the two survivors are being sold for scrap rather then renaming.

==Design==
The Stormforge was the Mesarthrim's first attempt at designing and building a large ship - and they were on a time limit too. When Tischler saw the final ship, he was impressed but asked, "Why'd they make her so ugly?"
The Stormforge was built as a platform to carry the new heavy blazers into battle. Additionally, redundant shielding and armor were installed to help protect the mighty vessels. Since the vast majority of the Mesarthrim fleet were Destroyers at the time, it was decided that most defensive duties would be handled by them and not the Stormforge. 

The Stormforge was also designed as sixteen separate pieces. This decision was made to allow sixteen shipyards to work on the ship at once. This feature of the design was dropped in 2325 after only seven ships were built.  The final assembly was very difficult, and a new armor had been developed by  [[Mystery Steel]] that used much larger sections of composites and made these predefined seams difficult to connect. One Stormforge, the [[Astrunger]] actually separated along several seams during an engagement with Imperial remnants shorty after the fighting died off. Had her escorts not formed a line, all hands would have been lost.

==Battle History==
The Stormforge saw action at the very end of the Second Exodus War in a few final skirmishes over Earth and in captured territory. Only one Stormforge was lost, the [[Astrunger]] and that was due more to poor construction, then actual battle.

The Stormforge true test of Battle came in the Civil War. A single Stormforge took one a trio of Prefecture Avengers and won, forcing the heavily damaged ships to retreat. Two Stormforges held Caledon under siege for two months, gunning down numerous Auellal counter-attacks. In fact, Caledon wasn't liberated until the Auellal got their hands on a Stormforge of their own.

The Stormforge tripled in numbers during the Civil War, jumping from only eight ships to twenty-four. In William Camps arms build up, another 11 were built, and five more replaced those that had been lost. The Mesarbauer shipyards were producing the Stormforge at an astounding rate.

The Stormforge was also a backbone in the Tempest war. It was a Stormforge that killed Admiral Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall and the Judicator and Vizeadmiral Bradley von Graith and the lorrach Claigionn. A Stormforge also chased down the  Delphinus and trapped her in sub-space. The Stormforge enjoyed an impressive six-to-one kill heavy vessel kill ratio in the Tempest War.

In a darker claim to fame, it was a Stormforge that fired Omega weaponry on Peshat. As well as glassing the surface of Bolan.

Following the collapse of the Centusi States the remaining Stormforges were adopted by the Mesarthrim Federation, but are being phased out in favor of the newer anti-matter powered Aigar battleships.

==Future Plans==
The Stormforge line is being retired. All Stormforge's still in service have been re-designated as support battleships, usually serving as a rear guard. The Mesarthrim Federation has opened a new budget to maintain the longevity of these ships. The Mesarthrim Federation is also in the process of seeking bids to sell off the hulls of six of the Stormforges that have now been mothballed.

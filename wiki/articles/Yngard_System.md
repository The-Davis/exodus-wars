The '''Yngard System''' is the home [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] of the [[Ikronin]]. It is also the capital of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]]. It has multiple planets, including [[Kronheim]].

== Stellar Navigation ==

Yngard is an unusually small yellow dwarf, barely capable of supporting a habitable band. The Yngard System is located in the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Kronheim ===

[[Kronheim]] (2.6AU), the homeworld of the [[Ikronin]], has an irregular orbit, which often takes it dangerously far into the outermost reaches of the habitable band, resulting in several thousand year-long ice ages.

Ngrligru militaries have a very simple method of organizing warriors, similar to most human models.

Twelve Ngrligru infantry units make one "Thenashara" of force. A human equivalent is a platoon of twenty infantry.

Two Nusu'askari or Shujaa'kireaktive make a "Mbili" of force. Two Thenashara of infantry also make a Mbili.

Ten Mbili make an "Ishirini" of force. One warship is also a single Ishirini. The human equivalent is somewhere between a company and a battalion of troops.

Ten Ishirini make a "Mia'mbili" of force. This is the highest form of organization within an Ngrligru tribe. The human equivalent is a regiment. Most Ngrligru Kabila have three to five Mia'mbili, but the larger Kabila have as many as twenty.


The Kabilas do not believe in or use separate branches to divide military power. The whole works together, often in a barely organized horde of raging bodies, but frequently in lethally well-organized groups of elite forces.

On the ground, Ngrligru warriors are known as Saada'jeshi. The Saada'jeshi are more infantry-intense than any other force. Usually, Saada'jeshi only use vehicles as a ride into and out of battle. Once on the field, Saada'jeshi wearing powerful combat suits--known as Ardhi'nyuka--slash their way through their enemies, often stopping to feed on the still screaming fallen.

While Ardhi'nyuka make up the backbone of the Ngrligru Saada'jeshi, they do use vehicles. The powerful and dreadful-looking Kiyowe'afrit skiff is a prime example, used to ferry in warriors and provide support and a way out. Ngrligru warriors are brave, but not morons.

Ngrligru pilots are known as Tukufu'ndege. Their tactics are very poor, due to their near-fanatic emphasis on individual glory and bloodlust. If not for their technological edge, they would lose every battle. Pilots use Shujaa'kir, the Ngrligru fighter craft, which they frequently customize and decorate with blood and gore. An individual Shujaa'kir is a macabre example of the violent mindset of an Ngrligru.

Ngrligru space forces are known as the Seyyedia'mashua. They ride the horribly powerful Manoari--Ngrligru warships--into battle. Most Manoari are superior to all but the ships of the Meroniri and the Remnant. Most Kabilas only have two of the largest ships, and several smaller vessels. There are three recognized classes of Ngrligru warships.

The Nina'rikebu is the largest of the warships. These are the motherships, over a kilometer in length. They are so horrendously expensive to build that no experimentation with the shape and equipment has ever been made. There is only one class of Nina'rikebu.

Next are the Kondo'jahazi, the cruisers. Unlike Nina'rikebu, the cruisers have a few different classes of vessel, and are usually less than a kilometer in length.

The smallest warships are the Vita'safina. These frigates are usually carried into battle by motherships, but are still as large or larger than their humanoid counterparts.

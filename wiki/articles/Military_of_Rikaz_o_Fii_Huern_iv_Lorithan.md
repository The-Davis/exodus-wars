The Lorithos military is an example of a superbly-organized and automated machine.  Due to the cripplingly low numbers of Lorithos able to fight, their military relies on robotics for most front-line combat.


== History of the Lorithos Military ==

The military of the [[Lorithos]] has one of the most self-destructive histories known to the human species. Their traditions date back to [[Hamur Huern]], who founded the [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr|Mozihr Getah]] in the [[Lorithos Bronze Age]], thousands of years ago. The [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr|Ailen Suvak]] was formed during the [[Burning Wars]], and caused more death and pain to their own race than any air force or navy in history. Under the direction of [[Dirken Huern]], the [[Lorithos]] military has begun to regain its honor and pride after feeling like butchers.

The [[Hishai]] is the Commander-in-Chief of the [[Lorithos]] military, which counts the defence of national territory, the protection of [[Lorithos]] interests abroad, and interstellar disaster relief as its primary objectives.

== Organization ==

The head of the armed forces is the [[Hishai]]. He is the literal Commander-in-Chief, taking an active role in planning, strategy, and command in battle. However, the [[Prekohr]] can always overrule the [[Hishai]] if he or she desires.

The Lorithos armed forces are divided into two branches:
* Army ([[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]]), including
** Hovertanks
** Robotic Infantry
** STOVL Craft
* Navy ([[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr]]), including
** Planetary Defense Fleets
** Expeditionary Fleet
** [[Kihtor]]
** Space Stations
** Naval Air

== Manpower ==

The total number of military personnel is approximately 1,500,000. The [[Lorithos]] rely on robotic infantry to fill its numbers on the ground. In space, most of their warships are largely automated. Only their pilots are in great demand.

* Military manpower
** Military age: 20 years
** Available Military Age: 30,000,000
** Fit for Military Service: 5,000,000
** Reaching Military Age Annually: 400,000
* Active troops: 1,500,000
* Military Expenditures Amount: 31.2 Billion [[Shero]]
* Percent of GDP: 1%

== International Stance ==

The [[Lorithos]] military doctrine is based on the concepts of national pride and military adequacy. Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan is a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], but has had little support from the other Member States in adapting to the post-Exodus War environment.

== Recent Operations ==

Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan has a defensive stance due to their lack of advanced hyperspace systems, and their low numbers. Their robotic troops are of paramount importance in their defensive plans.

The [[Second Exodus War]] was the [[Lorithos]] military's first taste of battle since the [[Burning Wars]], and it left a foul taste for hostility in their mouths. Their only engagement with the [[Royal Imperial Army]] of the [[Empire]] was a disaster.

== Events of 476AD ==

Periodic planetary scans in the [[Bollaria System]] indicate that surface and atmosphere of [[Drisonir]] and [[Nielstra]] now support life, and overall [[terraformation]] rate is above 99% complete.  Similar scans of [[Aedapos]] indicate its level of [[terraformation]] at 94%.  

In the [[Ularen System]], this news is greeted with elation and excitement.  The feeling of the rebirth of a great civilization and the destiny of the [[Ixoarchada]] among the stars is palpable in most populations.  Construction of massive colonization vessels begins, industrial satellites, prefabricated factories, and every other necessity the colonists might need are prepared.

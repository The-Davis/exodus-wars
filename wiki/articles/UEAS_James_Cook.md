The UEAS ''James Cook'' was an exploration vessel, commissioned and operated by the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]]. It frequently operated under the banner of the [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps]].

The UEAS ''James Cook'' is credited for initiating [[First Contact]] with the [[Reigens]].

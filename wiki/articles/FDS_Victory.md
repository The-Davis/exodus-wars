The FDS ''Victory'' was the first ship in the [[Victory Class Battleship]] line.  It was placed under the command of Admiral [[Marie Sarnou]]. 


== Deployment ==

Admiral Marie Sarnou took command of the ''Victory'' on its first voyage, on December 4th, 2324.

The Fourth Fleet Flagship is assigned to support major multi-Fleet operations.  It serves as fighter command for all fleets during such operations.  Admiral Sarnou is a proud leader, from a French family with a rich military history.  Ever since the French Army's numerous victories in World War III, the Sarnou family has been filled with famous commanders.

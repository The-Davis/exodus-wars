The Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser was built as a proof-of-concept for Dr. [[Richard Holder]]'s [[Entropy Drive]].  It was adopted by [[Anthony Hadrian]] as his personal flagship, and is both an effective combatant and a subtle command ship.

The design proved to be too expensive to deploy en masse, and the smaller [[Nautilus Class Subspace Prowler]] was developed to take full advantage of the unique features of the ''Delphinus''.

== Development ==

At the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] began developing factories and depots on [[Estelis]] and [[Albanus]].  [[Rahn Industries]]' enormous financial resources were put to use with dozens of projects and military contracts at once.

Among their many contracts was the personal flagship for the [[Prefect]], dubbed the "''Delphinus''".  It was originally conceived as a massively heavy weapons platform, designed to hit hard and fast, then escape combat.

[[Image:Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser Side View.jpg|200px]]

Unfortunately, the incredible power requirements for the ship's eight Relativistic Mass Drivers meant that it was not a particularly fast design.  Instead, the first prototype [[Entropy Drive]] was deployed on the ''Delphinus'' for combat testing.  The use of [[Subspace]] technology would allow the ship to acquire a target, slip in close undetected, enter normal space, attack, and disappear back into the murky depths of subspace.

== Combat History ==

The only ship in the class was the lead ship, the [[FDS Delphinus|FDS ''Delphinus'']].  The Curiate and Senate determined that the cost of the ship was far too high to deploy numerous ships of the same type, and chose the smaller Nautilus Class--available for a third of the cost--for deployment in greater numbers.

[[Image:Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser Top View.jpg|200px]]

The FDS ''Delphinus'' saw action in the [[Tempest War]], but was lost in subspace after a failed attack on [[Mesar]].  With the help of the [[Ixoarchada]], it was recovered after the war and refurbished. It became the flagship of [[Hannah Baruk]] after she became the [[Prefect]] when [[Anthony Hadrian]] was removed from office and disappeared.

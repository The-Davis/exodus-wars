The ''Drake'' was one of three Avenger class cruisers built and delivered to the [[Prefecture]] while they were still a rebellion. [[Rahn Industries]] was already building the ship when they demonstrated the Avenger class's capabilities to the [[Prefecture]], but were unable to deliver the design to the [[Prefecture]] forces in time for the [[Battle of Terra]].

The ''Drake'' and the other Avengers were used to help fill out the Fleets being commisioned by the newly formed [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. The ''Drake'' was designated CG-2, and assigned to the [[Second Fleet of the Line]].

When the Second Fleet was dispatched to destroy the last of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], the ''Drake'' took heavy damage from the [[Remnant]] vessel assisting the [[Empire]]. It was able to repair the damages, and was back in action by the time the [[Volucris]] attacked the same day. The ''Drake'' was boarded, but its Marine complement managed to repel the [[Volucris]].

Following the battle with the [[Volucris]], the ''Drake'' had to spend several weeks in drydock undergoing repairs to its shield systems.

In the first days of the [[Tempest War]], the ''Drake'' and the rest of the [[Second Fleet of the Line]] defended [[Albanus]] from a [[Mesarthrim]] fleet even as [[Mesar]] itself was under attack. The ''Drake'' performed brilliantly, destroying two Mesarthrim gunships and crippling a Delta frigate.

Currently, the ''Drake'' is serving the [[Second Fleet of the Line]] with distinction.

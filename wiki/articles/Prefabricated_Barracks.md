The barracks is a simple building, but it is very important.  It serves as the home for the pilots, infantry, and base staff.

Each barracks houses 28 infantry, pilots, or base staff, and a senior officer in command of the barracks.  The troops reside in the two wings of the building.  Each has a bunk, a trunk, and a dresser for clothing.  The senior officer lives in the rear of the building.  His quarters are slightly larger, but not much more so.

The main entrance of the barracks has a front office, where the base communications uplink is located.  Computers located there allow off-duty soldiers to communicate with family.

Multiple barracks buildings are installed on every base installation to house the crew and soldiers stationed there.

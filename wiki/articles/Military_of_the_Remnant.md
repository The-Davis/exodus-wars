==History of the Remnant Military==

The military of the Remnant is a direct descendant of the military of the [[Anthronoris Empire]] and follows many of the same traditions and organizational structures.  Although the majority of those units used by the three branches of the Remnant military have changed, a select few are still in service from before the [[Sundering]] and the fall of the Anthronoris Empire.  These are the [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]] and Hastati Classes, which serve as command ships in the Reliquiae Astrum Classis.  

The Imperatanis is the Commander-in-Chief of the Remnant military, which counts the defense of remaining Remnantine assets, the securing of an environment favorable to the rebirth of the Anthronoris Empire by whatever means necessary, and the collection and delivery of raw resources to the [[Void World]]s as its primary objectives.

==International Stance==

[[Remnant]] military doctrine is based on the concepts of defense of Remnantitine resources and interests and creating an environment favorable to the rebirth of the Anthronoris Empire by whatever covert or overt means necessary.

==Recent Operations==

The Remnant is currently on a offensive stance against the [[Ngrligru Kabila Duka]], a stance it has held for the last 10,000 years.  Although unable to produce the numbers necessary to engage in open war with the Ngrligru, the Remnant constantly raids Ngrligru shipping and outlaying stations whenever the opportunity presents itself.  

Additionally, the Remnant, through [[Gaius Shadar]]'s limited Operation "[[Talithe Cume]]" (Little one, I say to you, arise), had some interaction with the [[Onyx Empire]] in 2xxx.  Although it was initially seen as a information gathering operation, it quickly escalated through the manipulations of [[Lucius Black]] and was terminated.

The '''Sagittarius Arm''' and the [[Carina Spur]] are connected together. Only the Sagittarius Arm touches the Core. The Sagittarius Arm is the home of two intelligent species: the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Sirunaki]]. The [[Remnant]] of the [[Anthronoris]] also makes their home somewhere in the [[Carina Spur]], though the locations of their hidden sanctuary worlds are unknown to anyone except the most trusted captains and navigators of the Remnant.

==Horns of Sagittarius==
The Horns of Sagittarius is the gateway to the Galactic Core from the Sagittarius Arm. Unlike the Teeth of Orion, it is fairly easy to navigate: two massive star clusters of Class O supergiants are like heralds into the dangerous maelstrom of varying gravitational forces found in the Core.

==Kidaka Sector==
The Kidaka Sector is the territory of the dregs of [[Ngrligru]] society. The sector is named for the Kidaka Kafuyakini--Lagoon Nebula to humans--a source of low-quality exotic plasma which the lesser [[Ngrligru]] tribes use for their weaponry. The Mbwa'mwitu world of Jambo'kifa, the Pua'joka world of Kuf'shu, the Ukiwa'sana world of Na'kiu, the Theluji'nde world of Fasu'ro, and the Jahanu'fasi world of Kera are all located in this sector.

==Kishenzi Sector==
The Kishenzi Sector is named for the Kishenzi Chana, a star cluster that is the source of nearly all [[Ngrligru]] antimatter. The powerful Jitu'nge tribe operates most of the antimatter wells found in the cluster, but must deal with the raids of from lesser tribes. The Jitu'nge maintain a stronghold on the world of Koa'se, where they maintain their strangehold on the power source of the cluster. The Jahanu'fasi worlds of Serofu and Remau, and the Barafu'jaha worlds of Kuato'maru and Erualo are located in this sector.

==Tai Sector==
The Tai Sector is the territory of the Moshi'chui and Kivuli'dubu tribes, and is named for the Tai Kafuyakini, or Eagle Nebula. The Nebula is one of the better sources of weapons-grade plasma, and the Moshi'chui and Kivuli'dubu work well together in fending off the plasma sources from stronger tribes. The Mosi'chui worlds of Ker'sai, Tuo'aru, Kivu'aza, Izuta, Opura, and Eta'yau can be found in this sector. The Kivuli'dubu worlds of Sau'fera, Kea'roa, Yau'ti, Polu, Suraka, and Kasivu can be found in this sector.

==Aheri Sector==
The Aheri Sector is named for the Aheri Kafuyakini, or Omega Nebula. The Nyundo and Moto'mbega tribes constantly fight each other for the resources of the nebula. The Nyundo worlds of Kesha, Gor'vau, Cua'ra, Yoa, Ashedao, Buchar, and Hera'su are located in this sector. The Moto'mbega worlds of Usclito, Iyufe, Crilare, Etodu'krisa, Skioc, and Avu'droca are located in this sector.

==Poli Sector==
The Poli Sector is one of the least valuable areas of [[Ngrligru]] space. The Kito'koho and the Mwezi'paka tribes hold territories here, and are the proverbial "bread basket" of [[Ngrligru]] space, with vast fisheries on their worlds. The Kito'koho worlds of Mefiso, Uwada, Eshibra, Quemefi, Lunipa, Oulo, Yadruso, and Fuyarabe are located in this sector. The Mwezi'paka worlds of Osopu, Obrao, Glaku, Jeliu, Eluka'ukli, Kruten, and Kibro are located in this sector.

==Uveani Sector==
The Uveani Sector is the heart of [[Ngrligru]] space, where the mighty Kabila Jitu'nge has the bulk of its worlds. It is also the location of the enormous Saggitarius Star Cloud, the cluster of antimatter and exotic matter producing stars the galactic arm is named for. The Jitu'nge capital is Gara'su, and is believed to be the ancestral world of the [[Ngrligru]] species. The Jitu'nge worlds of Iupu, Otha'gli, Elodrifa, Epa'tu, Usufro, Lira'temoi, Las'coda, Aiwhi, Ukumiw, and Kedygrak are located in this sector.

Set after "First Foothold"

by James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     In Laojiu and Labrea, the Mesarthrim mercilessly pound strategic points on the surface with orbital bombardment. Only a few squadrons of Prefecture fighters survive the bombardments to fly sorties against vastly superior Mesarthrim fighters.
     Laojiu's few military installations are knocked out, and no significant military presence remains to face the Mesarthrim Myt'herra when they land and seize the civilian spaceports. Laojiu is soon darkened by hundreds of Pegasus dropships bringing in the Mesarthrim in force.
     Labrea remains unmolested, for the time being. It is home to the Prefecture's District garrison, with thousands of well-armed, well-trained troops entrenched in hardened positions. Brigadier William Parrish waits, calculating his chances of victory with his imminent doom throwing its shadow over his already dark counsel.
     Casere is quickly dominated by the might of the Mesarthrim juggernaut. A single Mesarthrim division is all it takes to capture Osarwa, the capitol city. The Governor of Casere, Ju Tao, is arrested for refusing to surrender the planet, and sent back to the rapidly-growing prison camps on Mesar. After the Governor's arrest, the militias on Casere become active, and begin their campaign of resistance.

Expanded version

by James Davis and Seth Dickinson

Nju-zanjiang - Laojiu - Laojiu District - 0550 Hours
     Dawn comes early to Laojiu’s western hemisphere on the fourth day of the Mesarthrim siege. The few citizens of Nju-zanjiang who have managed to put their worries aside long enough to sleep are awakened by the hypersonic shrieks of missiles passing overhead. Moments later, the sky burns with nuclear fire.
     Dozens of warheads, nuclear and conventional, pound every military facility on the planet: communications relays, command centers, militia armories, motor pools, SAM sites, and more. Destruction is total. The Mesarthrim’s ‘clean-burn’ nuclear warheads leave no fallout, but many civilians are caught in the blasts and incinerated. Electromagnetic pulse from aerial detonations fries civilian electronics and leaves the planetary Internet in tatters.
     Governor Zhong sits cross-legged on the lawn in front of his pagoda and watches his planet burn. The command center where he witnessed the arrival of the Mesarthrim, a mere ten miles to the south of his residence, is a burning ruin. There is nothing he can do to help his people: his car and his communications equipment were ruined by the EMP, and no one is answering his hails on the hardened land-line that runs into his basement.
     He watches the skies and waits.

Above Laojiu - 0600 Hours
     “We’ve got incoming! Bandits twelve o’clock high, one hundred miles!”
     “I see them. Squadron: stack, spike, and ready to fire.”
     The tattered remnants of Emilia Baker’s Laojiu Fighter Command are flying without ground-based radar support, without SAM defenses, and without hope. Squadron after squadron of Dragonfly and Patton fighters descend on them, followed by hundreds of Pegasus dropships.
     All across the continent, Prefecture fighters pop out of ground cover and scream upwards at full afterburner, hoping to score hits on a few of the dropships. There can be no retreat: these ragged few are the only fighters that got off the ground ahead of the incoming bombardment. There is nowhere left to land.
     “Wait for the v-deuce bracket. Take the shots as they come.”
     “They’ve got me spiked!”
     “Foxtrot-two inbound! Notching!”
     Swift Dragonflies pounce on the attackers. Missiles fly, and Prefecture fighters explode. The survivors loose their LRMs and pull into punishing high-G turns, heading groundwards. But many of them, Stukas and Bloodhawks, are simply too slow; they are caught and forced into dogfights against suicidal odds.
     Contrails twine and entangle for a few brief minutes. When the light show quiets down, the Laojiu Planetary Air Command has effectively ceased to exist.

Nju-zanjiang - Laojiu - 0620 Hours
     Laojiu is darkened by hundreds of Pegasus dropships, swooping low over the civilian spaceports on every continent. Troop carriers swing low and disgorge squads of grim-faced Myt’herra. They sweep the spaceport buildings while gunships secure the perimeter.
     Within minutes, the spaceports are completely under UCS control. No coherent military force remains to contest their domination.

Labrea - 0700 Hours
     “That’s it, sir. Check complete.”
     Colonel William Parrish bows his head in thanks as an aide finishes his run-down of the surviving Laojiu Guard forces. As on Laojiu, the Mesarthrim launched a heavy bombardment, but Parrish’s men were ready, and casualties were generally light. Wisely, Parrish dispersed his armor and air assets across the continent, and most of them survived.
     “How’s our readiness?” he asks.
     “We have four squadrons of dropships loaded up and ready to deploy; that’s our mobile reserve. Our combat engineers should have finished repairing the fortifications at all our major fighting positions within the hour. All our surviving fighters are on Ready Five, but we don’t have enough airstrips to get them up in anything less than ten minutes.”
     Parrish smiles, projecting confidence. “Well, then, it appears that all the pieces are in place. You look a little haggard, Lieutenant Sun. Why don’t you get some rest?”
     The aide salutes and vanishes, his pace quickening at the thought of some much-needed sleep.
     Alone in his office, Parrish lets down his façade and sighs, admitting his own weariness.
     Why don’t they come? he wonders. What are they waiting for?
     If they do come, what are my chances?
     He’s afraid he knows.
     Parrish sits sleeplessly, keeping his dark counsel, waiting for doom to descend.

Casere - 0710 Hours
     “Sir, you must hurry!”
     Governor Ju Tao grits his teeth in very un-Confucian frustration as he bends over his desk. “The Mesarthrim cannot get these files! These are my Ansible codes, encrypted communiqués, contingency plans - everything!”
     The Prefecture soldier standing at his office door, a member of Tao’s personal bodyguard, raises his rifle. “Stand back, sir.” A burst of Gauss shots tears the computer to pieces.
     Tao takes his hands off his ears. “Thank you, soldier.”
     The two rush out into the hallway, Tao dragging a small valise, containing only the most vital items from his house: a few data chips, his personal PDA, and a few personal memorabilia.
     A gaggle of aides, escorted by another bodyguard, joins them at the stairs up to the roof. “What’s happening in the city?” Tao asks.
     “The white-eyes are all over Osarwa.” The female aide looks distraught; Tao remembers that her family is still in the city. “They’re – well, they’ve brought a whole division into the area. Tanks roll down our streets. That was the last I heard before we lost communications.”
     As they reach the roof, a mere half-dozen meters from the Skyhawk waiting for them, Tao glances east, towards Osarwa. “I hope-“
     His prayer is cut off by a tremendous thunder clap. A blazer bolt sears through the Skyhawk’s cockpit, killing the pilots instantly. The bodyguards tackle Tao, throwing him to the ground, as a pair of UCS fighters thunder overhead.
     Tao raises his head in time to see four Pegasus dropships descending around his house. Myt’herra troopers stand poised in the open bays, rifles raised. A tremendously amplified voice booms over them: “DROP YOUR WEAPONS! STAY PRONE!”
     One of the bodyguards moves the wrong way – perhaps raising a weapon, perhaps trying to push it away. He is shot a dozen times. One of the aides screams as the smell of burning flesh fills the air.
     Tao throws his hands up. “We surrender!” he shouts.
     One of the Pegasi swings up to the roof, and Myt’herra swarm out, followed by a uniformed Mesarthrim officer who glances over the fallen civilians with cool contempt. “Governor Tao?”
     “I am,” Tao snarls, pushing himself to his feet. “Is this the Mesarthrim way – to come for me without even a request to parley? This is your beloved honor?”
     The Mesarthrim’s eyes are unreadable: pale white. “I am Major Bissell. You shouldn’t speak of honor, Governor – not when you were the one trying to flee.” He steps closer as his soldiers begin cuffing the civilians. “But we can do this like civilized men, Governor. Surrender your world to Director Camp and the United Centusi States, and I will see to it that our occupation is as honorable as you could desire.”
     “Never,” Tao hisses. “This is our world, not Camp’s. You will never take it from us by force.”
     “A shame.” Bissell gestures his men forward. “Take them back to the fleet. Governor Tao, you will enjoy the hospitality of Mesar’s prison camps. Perhaps then you’ll come to understand what you could have saved your people.”

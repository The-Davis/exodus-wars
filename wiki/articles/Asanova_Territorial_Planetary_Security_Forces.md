The Sirius factions employ various forces, regular and otherwise, to secure their planets and orbital stations. Aside from the various agents, guards, and "supervisors" in the Sirius Administration, each faction maintains its own version of a standing army for defense from the outside, and to quell dissidents internally.

The Asanovas are known for their attention to high-powered attack weapons. The center of their tactics is the Hammer Mobile Assault Platform, a powerful weapons platform capable of ordinance delivery of any sort. For air defense, the Scorpion half-track is equipped with anti-air weaponry. Infantry play an important role as well. The typical Asanova infantrymen are nicknamed "Yuri" in honor of their Russian leaders. Most troops are moved in Tatar APC or in Bear half-tracks. Some Asanova are trained as sharpshooters, and are given the title "Adder" upon graduating marksman school.

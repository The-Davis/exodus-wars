==History==
The '''International Space Station''' ('''ISS''') was a research facility in space. The station was in orbit of [[Terra]] and could be seen from the surface with the naked eye. Its altitude was 450km.

The International Space Station was a joint project between five space agencies: NASA, the Soviet Space Program, the Commonwealth Space Agency, the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, and the European Space Agency.

After the [[United Earth Alliance]] was formed, the UEA Space Agency took over control of the station, though the name was kept.

The International Space Station was continuously inhabited from November 2, [[2000]], until its retirement on August 1, [[2026]]. The station was moved to a [[Sol]] orbit at 0.95AU and turned into a museum.

==Scientific Research==
One of the main goals of the International Space Station was to provide a place to conduct experiments that require one or more of the unusual conditions present on the station.  The main fields of research include biology, physics, astronomy, and meteorology.

The most famous experiments to date were Yuri Krenschov's [[hyperspace]] experiments, which allowed the [[Terrans]] to expand into neighboring star systems.

==Construction Jobs==
The International Space Station's biggest role after the [[hyperspace]] project was completed was to house the construction of the first [[Interstellar Exploration Vehicle]]. When the [[IEV-1 Pytheas| UEAS ''Pytheas'']] completed its mission, the International Space Station was used to build the third and the fifth [[Interstellar Exploration Vehicle]] before the [[United Earth Alliance]] Space Agency began to move its missions to other facilities.

==Docking Collars==
The International Space Station has docking collars to accomodate the [[Orion Spacecraft]], the [[Interstellar Exploration Vehicle]], and the [[Ares Rocket| Ares]] Heavy Cargo Rocket.

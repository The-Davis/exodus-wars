Joshua Cartör held more awards and distinctions than any other Mesarthrim. Cartör's service was a long and brutal one, always led from his specially modified Siege Tank, the ''War Wagon''. Cartör served under Uber-General Tischler in the Sonar Mountains, and would become his Heer Joint Chief following the retaking of Stone Bridge. Cartör was the last General to lay down his arms in the civil war. After two weeks of fighting in [[William]] with no news from Stone Bridge, his officers convinced him to surrender his exhausted army to the [[Auellal League]]. Cartör refused to accept the Auellal Leagues claim to power, and was held as a prisoner of war until the Centusi States negotiated for his release. Cartör led the Armored [[Myt'herra]] Legions in the Tempest War. He was killed in [[2332]] by a Prefecture tactical nuclear barrage against his forward operating base on [[Sindh]].

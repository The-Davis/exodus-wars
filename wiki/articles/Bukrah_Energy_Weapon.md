The [[Lorithos]] '''Bukrah''', or '''blasters''', are clean plasma weapons. They are roughly equivalent in power and function to a [[Terran]] autocannon. [[Lorithos]] blasters are less sophisticated than the related [[Ngrligru]] [[Mkuki’kali Energy Lance]] and [[Ixoarchada]] [[Jiorna Node|Jiorna]] and [[Krinim Cannon|Krinim]] weaponry.

Blasters are the primary weapons of the Lorithos forces; they are accurate and powerful at short and medium ranges, with an unremarkable rate of fire. Like the Energy Lance, a blaster fires plasma. Unlike the [[Ngrligru]] weapon, Lorithos technology uses magnetic rather than gravity fields to collect and accelerate the discharge.

The blaster is nowhere near as deadly as the Energy Lance, but it’s still powerful. Rather than a beam, it fires a distinctive orb of plasma, which resembles a sputtering miniature sun. Lorithos military forces take pride in the blaster’s efficiency and reliability. It barely ever requires maintenance and can fire thousands of shots on only a small power cell and a canister of gas ammunition.

Blasters are extremely environment-friendly, and don’t leave radiation or other lasting scars on the combat zone. Blasters are not particularly long-ranged, because the plasma orbs lose coherency over time and eventually disintegrate. This is a particular problem in planetary atmospheres. They compensate for this weakness with surprising power; upon impact, a blaster orb releases a tremendous amount of heat over a wide area as well as delivering a kinetic shock and brief, intense magnetic pulse which can scramble electronics. Blasters are thus very powerful against both infantry and armored vehicles: personnel can be killed by heat and kinetic force, and even when a blaster bolt doesn’t penetrate tank armor or shields, it can still fry internal systems with the EMP effect.

Besides the EMP, blasters are not very good at penetrating armor, but they can generally overwhelm defenses with sheer firepower. Because the blaster bolts are charged, magnetic shields can often deflect them. Plasma shields are only mildly effective against blasters. Ground commanders note that blasters have a considerable ‘splash effect;’ they distribute their damage over a wide area, which makes them excellent at killing large groups of lightly armored enemies – especially the lighter Volucris breeds. Unfortunately, many Volucris are resistant to plasma weapons, which means blasters have a hard time against them.

==Components==
The blaster has three main components: the power source, the plasma generation chamber, the acceleration/focusing chamber, and an attached computer system which monitors the firing process and adjusts it for maximum efficiency.

Blasters require ammunition in the form of compressed gas. When the weapon is armed, gas is drawn into the plasma generation chamber and subjected to an intense electrical current supplied either by a battery or a vehicle’s hydrogen engine. Atoms in the gas are stripped of their electrons by the current, creating positively charged ions. Within moments the gas inside the chamber has been converted into plasma – superhot matter on the verge of becoming energy. The positively-charged plasma haze is drawn into the acceleration/focusing chamber by magnetic fields, which then shape it into a globular orb shape.

The resulting projectile is launched by a system very similar to a Terran autocannon – a railgun-like series of magnets which draw the projectile forward with a negative charge, then switch polarity when it passes and thus continue contributing to its acceleration. Because the positively charged atoms of the plasma orb tend to repel each other, the weapon’s range is limited once it leaves the barrel. The longer the barrel is, the more tightly focused the plasma bolt, and the longer range it is. The original, experimental versions of the blaster fired a tightly focused stream of plasma rather than an orb.

Unfortunately, they suffered from a problem that is known in Terran physics as Aifven cavitation. In short, it led to the weapon ripping itself apart when these tightly focused beams were fired.

Eventually, Lorithos scientists discovered a way to dampen the problem, but only if the plasma projectile was symmetrical. The lance-like beam was discarded and the modern orb shape adopted. It spreads damage out over a wider area and has rather low penetration against armor, but it has a wider splash radius, which can often be an advantage.

The '''Convention of Admirals''' was a meeting of military commanders who were part of the [[Prefecture]] rebellion, and key members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|Resistance]].

The convention wrote the [[Constitution of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], and organized the public referendum and elections on former worlds of [[the Empire]] to determine which [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]] would gain independence and which would join the [[Prefecture]].

The HMS ''Thessaly'' was the four hundred and seventy first [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]] of the [[Vigilant Class Frigate|Vigilant class]] in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].  It was commissioned in February of 2318, and spent the next 10 months under the command of Captain Josiah Cleburn on patrol through the [[Sol Sector]].

In December of [[2318]], the ''Thessaly'' was assigned to the Command Operation Force (COPFOR) [[Royal Imperial Navy#Carrier_group|carrier group]] as part of [[Operation Lawless]].  During the [[Sirius Incursion#Battle_of_Sirius_Kuza|Battle of Sirius Kuza]], the ''Thessaly'' was badly damaged when its primary propulsion was knocked out and its orbit began to rapidly decay.  Captain Cleburn ordered the crew to attempt to accelerate the ship and maneuver into a shallow trajectory, hoping to crash-land the ship in the ocean.  However, without sufficient velocity, and lacking atmospheric control, the ''Thessaly's'' course became a free-fall, and Captain Cleburn issued the order to abandoned ship.  He and his bridge crew were on board the ''Thessaly'', still struggling to arrest its fall, when it crashed into the outskirts of [[Ishikawa]], killing some seven thousand inhabitants.  

Ironically, Cleburn was awarded for his actions by both the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura family]].  Emperor [[Lucius Black]] assumed that his actions had been intentional, and he commended the entire crew and posthumously awarded Captain Cleburn the [[Imperial Medal of Valor]].  When the Nakamura confirmed that the crew had attempted to avert the crash and that Cleburn went down with the ship attempting to ditch in the ocean, they pardoned and released the entire crew--the only captured Imperial personnel not imprisoned and/or executed after the battle.

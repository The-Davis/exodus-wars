===Overview===

Vita'safina aina Kifa are the most common Ngrligru light warship.

[[Image:Vita'safinaainaKifa1.jpg]]

Their unique saucer shape is synonymous with Ngrligru invasions--so much so that "flying saucers" were remembered by all humanoid races in their ancient history.

===Capabilities===

Kifa carry simple weapons, by [[Ngrligru]] standards.  Their main weapons are Bunduki'kubwa Hyperspace Distortion Cannons, similar to Meroniri Riptide Cannons.  According to most [[Mtomigru]] historians, the weapons were actually stolen during the Sundering, but never upgraded.  The Meroniri have made great strides, and their Riptide Cannons are superior.

[[Image:Vita'safinaainaKifa2.jpg]]

For anti-fighter defenses, Kifa carry Gobori'dogo Relativistic Mass Drivers.  These cannons are as powerful as most humanoid cruiser main weapons.

===Deployment===

Kifa are usually assigned to a Nina'rikebu to defend it and act as landing craft.

[[Image:Vita'safinaainaKifa3.jpg]]

They can operate in deep space by themselves, but don't carry enough supplies for extended operations.

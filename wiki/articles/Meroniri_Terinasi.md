=== History ===
Main Article: [[History of the Meroniri Terinasi|History]]

The '''Meroniri Terinasi''', or [[Meronir Tronit|Meronir's]] Republic, are a collection of worlds and people of remarkable power and strength. They survived the legendary [[Sundering]] almost completely unscathed, a shining memory of the splendor and majesty of the [[Anthronoris]] at the height of their power.

[[Meroniri]] society has actually grown in opulence and magnificence since the fall of the [[Anthronoris]], thanks mostly to their powerful technology. The [[Meroniri]] treat every aspect of their lives like an artistic pursuit. Art is the most important pursuit to any [[Meroniri]], and the creation of beauty is their ultimate cause.

Certain historical icons, such as [[Meronir Tronit]], have survived the test of time far longer than other figures of their time. The intimate details of [[Meronir Tronit|Tronit]]'s life are commonly known, while even the true names of [[Terran]] figures of the same time are of dubious accuracy.

Protecting the culture of the [[Meroniri]] falls on the [[Meroniri Yatisu|Yatisu]], the [[Meroniri]] military.  The [[Meroniri Yatisu|Yatisu]] traces its origins back to the legions of the [[Anthronoris Empire]], and have many traditions of their own. A lot of the [[Meroniri Yatisu|Yatisu]]’s records are of great historical importance simply for their detailed battle recordings of events that took place thousands of years ago.

=== Government ===
Main article: [[Government of the Meroniri Terinasi|Government]]

The Terinasi is led by a governing body known as the Thenaro, which is a parliamentary council with a single council with many seats, and several Ministries. There are several "[[Tomesa]]", or families, who have a hereditary seat in the Thenaro, while the bulk of the seats are up for grabs in annual elections. The "[[Lothenar]]" is the Prime Minister, who is selected by the majority party, while the "[[Suthenar]]" is the Shadow Minister, who keeps the [[Lothenar]] in check with partial veto power.

=== Law ===
Main Article: [[Law of the Meroniri Terinasi|Law]]

The [[Sheta Unari]]--Common Law--is a monolithic system of laws and codes dating back to the days of the [[Anthronoris]]. It is thousands upon thousands of pages in length, and very difficult to fully decipher.  Every aspect of life is covered in the [[Sheta Unari]], to an almost absurd degree. Even personal hygiene and sleeping habits are covered by the law, much to the distress of outsiders trying to assimilate into [[Meroniri]] culture. Children are raised to these standards from birth, and usually understand them.

=== Law Enforcement ===
Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Meroniri Terinasi|Law Enforcement]]

Law enforcement in [[Meroniri]] society is simple. Anyone accused of committing a crime is simply ordered to appear before the courts. If they do not, the military is sent to bring them. There is no "civilian police" force. Surveillance of [[Meroniri]] homes is total, and crimes rarely go unsolved. The [[Meroniri]] do not recognize the right to privacy.

The courts hear the evidence against the accused, and then a jury of sixteen passes the verdict. There are three sentences: a term of imprisonment, a term of servitude, or permanent exile. The [[Meroniri]] maintain a prison world which they keep permanently in the dark (technology-wise) by destroying any power source built on the surface. This world’s location is top secret, known only to the heads of the [[Aseta]], and the ministers of the government.  The world is believed to be located somewhere in the [[Teeth of Orion]], and is not officially listed as a [[Meroniri]] world.

=== Foreign Relations ===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Meroniri Terinasi|Foreign Relations]]

The [[Meroniri]] are the founding Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], but their international image has become repugnant lately due to their efforts to spearhead the trade embargo against the members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Relations with nearly every nation--ally or no--are cold but polite at best, and confrontational at worst.

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of the Meroniri Terinasi|Military]]

The [[Meroniri Yatisu]] is a powerful armed force, divided into four "[[Aseta]]", or "schools" of specialized training. Each is fully trained for air, ground, space and water operations, but have distinct disciplines and tactics they specialize in. Currently, the schools are [[Aseta Serada]], [[Aseta Joreno]], [[Aseta Fuleno]], and [[Aseta Salfore]].

=== Intelligence ===
Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Meroniri Terinasi|Intelligence]]

[[Meroniri]] intelligence has atrophied over the years. They maintain a vast network of listening posts along the [[Teeth of Orion]], but rely more on diplomatic channels to hear news from other human nations.

The listening posts are some of the most sophisticated devices in the known galaxy. Each is capable of monitoring every known spectrum for non-random patterns, and the computing power to analyze them for known algorithms. There is more brute force computing power in a single listening station than in three centuries of [[Terran]] computer science combined.

=== Transportation ===
Main Article: [[Transportation of the Meroniri Terinasi|Transportation]]

The [[Meroniri]] have dozens of worlds, but do not use ground transportation anymore. Most [[Meroniri]] fly in vehicles powered by gravity-well drives, or walk. Their cities take up very little space, except upwards, so flight makes the most sense when covering the vast unspoiled lands between cities.

=== Territory ===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Meroniri Terinasi|Territory]]

[[Sepulare]] is the capital of the Meroniri Terinasi, but they have many other worlds. [[Minhara]], [[Hamele]], [[Thibana]], [[Caelire]], [[Shaula]], [[Oture]], [[Weshura]], and [[Alderami]] are located in the [[Unai Sector]]. [[Shia Drasin System|Shia Drasin]] is located in the [[Lepodi Sector]]. [[Acruso System|Acruso]], [[Meripe System|Meripe]], [[Saete System|Saete]], [[Herae System|Herae]], [[Apodisa System|Apodisa]], [[Mirfare System|Mirfare]], [[Tuseni System|Tuseni]], and [[Casoru System|Casoru]] are located in the [[Masera Sector]].

=== Economy ===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Meroniri Terinasi|Economy]]

The [[Meroniri]] economy is mostly internalized, and deals in interplanetary trade. [[Meroniri]] rarely buy industrial goods from other nations. They do make use of more luxurious items, such as fine cuisine and similar goods.

The [[Ikronin]] [[Likvada]] is the de facto standard of interstellar commerce, and the [[Meroniri]] have adopted it as their official currency.

=== Demographics ===
Main Article: [[Demographics of the Meroniri Terinasi|Demographics]]

The [[Meroniri]] were originally nine surviving worlds from the [[Anthronoris Empire]] before the [[Sundering]]. The Sepulare Meroniri is the most common sub-race, and while most of the nine worlds have homogenized over the millennia, there are still a handful of sub-races that make up the [[Meroniri]] population.

=== Public Health ===
Main Article: [[Public Health of the Meroniri Terinasi|Public Health]]

Disease is nearly unheard of among [[Meroniri]]. Their immune system is boosted from birth by a rigorous inoculation system, and several immune-boosting genetic enhancements which their society implemented a few thousand years ago.

[[Meroniri]] hospitals are among the most renowned, though they are slightly more reluctant to share their secrets than other societies, and have been surpassed by the [[Prefecture]] as a leader in health and medical exports.

=== Culture ===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Meroniri Terinasi|Culture]]

[[Meroniri]] culture survived and thrived despite the predations of the [[Ngrligru]] throughout the Sundering, and was thus able to build upon a unique ideology.

To the [[Meroniri]], life itself is an art form, and they themselves are the paragons of this art. Every aspect of life is treated like an art, from commerce to politics, from conversation to agriculture, and from warfare to science.

This leads to some odder behavior, at least from the point of view of other races. A [[Meroniri]] may purposefully lose a debate in order to weave a brilliant tapestry of ultimate conquest in the Thenaro. A [[Meroniri]] tactician's mind is completely unpredictable: each is imaginative and a creative genius--unknowable in its complexity, or unshakeable in its simplicity.

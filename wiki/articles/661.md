== Events of 661AD ==

A survey mission on [[Tolesca|Tolesca’s]] moon discovers an apparently artificial edifice in one of the many impact craters on the moon’s surface.  Further inquiry reveals a large, carved chamber sealed by a highly complex mechanical door.  The door ceased functioning millennia earlier, so specialized equipment is requested for further investigation.

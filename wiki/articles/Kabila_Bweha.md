===History===
'''Kabila Bweha''' was filled with mostly human-sympathizers, who wished to live in a peaceful galaxy governed by a united group of races. Bweha and [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] both voiced this opinion, and were eventually attacked by Kabilas [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]] and [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]].

While [[Kabila Kimburu]] escaped into human-controlled space with roughly half of its forces intact, Bweha wasn't so lucky. Their territory was wedged between [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]] and [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]], and the violent Kabilas literally tore the Bweha army to pieces. All ranking leaders of Kabila Bweha were brutally butchered, and the surviving troops and weapons of Bweha's military were divided between the conquering tribe. Nothing is left of Kabila Bweha but memories.

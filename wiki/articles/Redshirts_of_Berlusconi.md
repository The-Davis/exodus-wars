Redshirts are the title of [[Berlusconi Family Territory]] infantry.

In the Berlusconi Family Territory, the only units who come close to the status of professional soldiers are the Berlusconi Redshirts.  The Redshirts, comprised of former Imperial conscripts, mercenaries, various army defectors, and others with military experience, are an elite among the various "soldiers" of the Berlusconi family.  They help to enforce the edicts of the [[Sirius Cluster]]'s official stance of "hostile neutrality", and keep the more hardened elements in the Berlusconi territory in line. 

Redshirts are equipped with a Blazton Industries PA-24 Composite Armor suit.  The protection offered to an individual is superb, and the servo-assisted motion allows a large equipment pack to be worn on the back.  While the packs are very useful, most Redshirts rarely, if ever, carry one.  Few "missions" that Redshirts might be sent on would last any substantial amount of time. 

Redshirts also utilize a Blazton-type integrated helmet, which is armored and tied into the armored suit's CPU, communications, and targeting gear. 

While most agents of the Berlusconi family use a wide variety of weapons, only the Redshirts have what might be called a "general issue" weapon.  Most Redshirts employ a Kodiak Arms K-5 Carbine, a modified version of the Prefecture's M-6 MBR.  The K-5 has the same variable-round capabilities as the larger K-18, but has a shorter barrel, an omni-mount for various hardware, and a Saidia Optics OC-6 sight, the same as used on the Commonwealth's Watson Rifle.  The K-5 is an optimum mix between a long-range MBR, and a short-range CQB Sub-Machine Gun. 

[[Image:RedshirtsofBerlusconi.jpg]]

Like their Sapper counterparts in the Blazton Security Forces, Redshirts carry a Blazton Model 08 Pistol in holster on their side.  

Redshirts operate in the cities, slums, and urban areas of the Berlusconi Territory as elite shock troopers.  In reality, they perform few daily duties.  They are only called on for putting down various underworld factions that may step out of line, or going to the frontiers to enforce the will of the "Boss".

When deployed, Redshirts are usually overly equipped to combat the foes they face.  Their powered armor makes them nearly impervious to most non-military weaponry, the K-5 can slice through any police or civilian-grade body armor, and their tactical computers and HUDs give them a vast superiority in terms of battlefield awareness.

Less disciplined "officers", slightly better than common thugs, handle most day-to-day duties within the Territory.  Only Redshirts see anything akin to true combat.  Their lack of professionalism as soldiers is more than made up for by their tenacity and familiarity with their own urban environments.

The Active Probe is a high-powered radar system designed to cut through any counter-measures, even the most sophisticated of Electronic Countermeasure suites.

The Active Probe is certain to find any target, but it paints an easy target for radar-seeking missiles, making the bearer of the Active Probe very vulnerable. An Active Probe is crucial to retrieving battlefield data before combat.

Recon crews track down deadly weapons like minefields, hidden infantry and tanks, and proximity bombs. Active Probe-equipped recon ships have high fatality rates, but there are always more brave souls ready to fill the ranks.

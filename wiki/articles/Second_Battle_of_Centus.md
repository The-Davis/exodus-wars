The '''Second Battle of Centus''' was the united effort of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], the [[Auellal League]] and the [[Ikronin Jurekön]].  They launched a major invasion on the [[Centus System]].

The Prefecture's Fifth Fleet of the Line jumped the gun, resulting in the loss of the [[FDS Judicator|FDS ''Judicator'']] and the death of Admiral [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]].  After the unified coalition forces attacked, they were able to seize space superiority over [[Mesar]].

The invasion of the surface resulted in the loss of an entire Prefecture Legion within the first ten minutes of battle.  The ground campaign was so bloody that an estimated 500 million Mesarthrim died before it was over.

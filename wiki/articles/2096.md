== Events of 2096 ==

For sixteen years, the [[Torimur]] exiles flew through [[hyperspace]]. This journey was known as the [[First Exodus]]. Finally, the series of jumps ended. The computer systems aboard the colony ships destroyed the Volstamik-Krenschov drives that had propelled them to the outer reaches of the galaxy.

The world the [[United Earth Alliance]] chose to dump the [[Torimur]] forces on was hostile and nearly uninhabitable. [[Lucius Black]] took command again quickly, vowing revenge and victory. He began collaborating with Professor [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]] to make the repairs and modifications necessary to return to [[Terra]].

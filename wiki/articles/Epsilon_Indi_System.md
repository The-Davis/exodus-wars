The '''Epsilon Indi System''' was a star system with seven planets, moons, and millions of asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Epsilon Indi, the planets were Indi 1, Indi 2, [[Zardos]], Indi 3, Indi 4, Indi 5, and Indi 6. Many of the planets are orbited by moons.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Epsilon Indi is the Epsilon Indi System's star. Epsilon Indi was a class K5Ve star. It has been collapsed by an unknown device being developed on [[Zardos]].

The remains of the Epsilon Indi System are located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Zardos was the first inhabited alien world to be visited by [[Terrans| Terran]] civilization. The [[Proxima Procyon System]] and [[Tau Ceti System]] are considered close neighbors of Zardos.

== Orbital Bodies ==

The inner Epsilon Indi System was the region containing [[Zardos]] itself and the other two terrestrial planets. There were three terrestrial planet orbiting Epsilon Indi, none of which had moons.

The middle region of the Zardos System was home to an asteroid belt, the gas giants and their moons. The two outer planets, or Gas Giants, made up the bulk of the mass orbiting Epsilon Indi.

=== Indi 1 ===

Indi 1 (0.4AU) was the closest planet to Epsilon Indi. It was an unremarkable ball of rock with no useful resources and no atmosphere.

=== Indi 2 ===

Indi 2 (0.65AU) was the an unusually large rocky planet with a thin, toxic atmosphere. It was roughly twice the size of an ordinary terrestrial planet, and dense enough that its surface felt like walking in 3Gs.

=== Zardos ===

[[Zardos]] (1.05AU) was an inhabitable terrestrial planet, with a thin but breathable atmosphere. It was home to a [[Meroniri]] listening post, and was the base of operations for their counter-turn [[First Contact]] teams prior to the formation of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. Later, the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] sponsored a research center on its surface, which was destroyed by an experimental weapon during the [[Second Exodus War]]. The planet had one moon, Zardos B.

=== Asteroid Belt ===

The Zardos belt was unique for the presence of solid state exotic matter, which has never been found in any other system to date. It was the primary focus of the research center, and is believed to be the cause of the destruction of the star system.

=== Indi 3 ===

Indi 3 (5.4 AU) is a relatively small gas giant, only 60 times the diameter of an average terrestrial planet. It was extremely dense, with a gravitational pull close to that of the local star, leading many to believe that it was also a source of exotic matter. Indi 3 was never explored before it was destroyed with the rest of the system. Indi 3 had no moons.

=== Indi 4 ===

Indi 4 (7.03 AU) was an enormous gas giant, with an equatorial radius of over 120,000KM. Indi 4 was comprised mostly of lightweight gasses, making it less massive than it appeared to be. Indi 4 had 78 moons.

=== Indi 5 ===

Indi 5 (7.6 AU) is an average gas giant, with a hydrogen atmosphere and 45 moons. It was not charted or surveyed prior to the destruction of the system.

=== Indi 6 ===

Indi 6 (8.02 AU) was a terrestrial planet in terms of composition, and covered mostly by methane and water ice. It was the site of a test lab for the more dangerous experiments in the Zardos System, but was destroyed along with the rest of the star system.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The [[Meroniri]] came to [[Zardos]] looking for remains of the [[Anthronoris]] civilization. They discovered some artifacts on the surface, but nothing of significant value. After making contact with the [[Ikronin]], the [[Meroniri]] hoped to discover other civilized relatives among the stars.

The [[Aseta Salfore]] was based on [[Zardos]] and began exploring and charting many star systems in the area, even discovering the [[Sol System]] during the height of [[World War III]]. Rather than interfere, they waited for the [[Terrans]] to discover [[hyperspace]] and develop successful colonies before making contact.

Later, the Epsilon INdi System was made the site of a multi-national research laboratory, focused on studying the system's rich supply of solid state exotic matter. Tragically, during the height of the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Onyx Empire]] found out about the system and attacked. In October of [[2318]], the [[HMS Cyprus|HMS ''Cyprus'']] led a carrier group to the system, driving the unarmed lab off the surface. The [[Onyx Empire]]'s science team activated an experimental power source, which destroyed Epsilon Indi and every planet in the system. The [[HMS Cyprus|HMS ''Cyprus'']] and its carrier group were wiped out, along with all Imperial personnel in the system.

The resulting particle cloud is so dangerous that even Meroniri Field Generators cannot protect the ships from its effects, and the Zardos System is a hyperspace navigation hazard that must be bypassed by most ships heading within 2LY of it.

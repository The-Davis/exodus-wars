===Overview===

The Worker is a marvelous mesh of primal instinct and developed intelligence.  It can perform a vast array of tasks, from mining to complex medical repairs to its fallen brethren.

[[Image:BasicWorker.jpg]]

They are highly intelligent, and live as a sub-culture within the Volucris species.  They communicate with a combination of antennae signals, claw motions, pheromones, and a chittering vocal language.  The bulk of their communication is through scent.

Workers that are not actively working have been observed in activities that can only be described as games or performances.  Prefecture xenobiologists are very interested in capturing a group of Workers alive to observe their behavior.

The Worker is mostly harmless if met on the battlefield.  It is entirely non-aggressive, and is only concerned with administering medical help to the Soldier varieties locked in combat.  If attacked, the Worker will defend itself with its four claws.  ADF soldiers are not advised to engage a Worker in combat, as Soldiers will defend it more fiercely than they defend themselves.

The Worker is fast enough to get its work done, and no faster.  They are very nimble and flexible though, allowing them into tight spaces.

The Worker's carapace is light, and can withstand things like avalanches from mining accidents, but isn't very useful against a sustained attack.

Wherever Volucris are found, Workers are found in great numbers.  They are the backbone of the Volucris swarm, as the Soldiers cannot fight without the raw materials the Workers produce, and cannot be healed properly without the Worker's medical skills.

'''Jean Sarnou'''
*Place of Birth: [[Holt]]
*Allegiance: [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Jean Sarnou'' is a [[Senator]] in the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] government. He was once a commander for the [[United Earth Alliance]] military, and led the [[Holt]] resistance against the [[Empire]] during the [[Second Exodus War]].

==United Earth Alliance==

During his service with the [[United Earth Alliance]], Sarnou commanded the UEAS ''Bonaparte''. He fought against the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in the [[Invasion of Earth]].

==Holt Resistance==

During the [[Second Exodus War]], Jean Sarnou led the resistance against Imperial occupation. His greatest achievement was the sabotage and total destruction of two [[Royal Imperial Navy]] cruisers in resupply orbit.

==Senator==

When the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] was formed, Sarnou opposed the idea of [[Holt]] joining the [[Prefecture]], but when the people voted to join, he decided to work within the new government. Sarnou was elected Senator for the newly formed [[Ward District]].

The Sirius factions employ various forces, regular and otherwise, to secure their planets and orbital stations. Aside from the various agents, guards, and "supervisors" in the Sirius Administration, each faction maintains its own version of a standing army for defense from the outside, and to quell dissidents internally.

A unique situation is that the Blazton Security Forces have exclusive jurisdiction over areas under Blazton Industries supervision. Their worker's cities, planetary installations, and space-faring vessels are strictly under the guard of Blazton Security personnel. Thusly, Blazton Industries maintains a Security Force nearly comparable to the standing armies of any one of the Sirius territories.

The Blazton Security Forces operate several designs on the ground. The Dasher is the most common, a light hovercraft used to patrol Blazton installations and parking lots. The Targe is also common, and sees significant use both by Blazton and in civillian markets as a heavy ground transport. Blazton does use two dedicated military models, the Stinger heavy hovertank and the Skywatcher missile system. Blazton "Trailblazers" are their typical security outside of installations. Blazton also keeps a small cadre of elite heavy infantry, only a few dozen, known as "Sappers."

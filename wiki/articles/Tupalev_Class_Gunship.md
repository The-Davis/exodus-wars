===Overview===

Designed over fifty years ago, the Tupolev Gunship is today the primary light warship of the Asanova Territory.

[[Image:TupalevClassGunship1.jpg]]

This small warship patrols Asanova space, and provides a compact, cheap, and efficient, if not spacious, means for projecting and enforcing Asanovan authority in the territory. 

===Capabilities===

Unlike some gunships, the Tupolev is not meant for warship combat, although it is not completely unequipped for such.  It is armed with the same number of Advanced Neutron Cannons as a Caspia Armed Freighter, but in a much smaller package.  While these cannons are design for use against capital warships, the Tupolev employs them as long-range anti-aerospace weapons.  They can also destroy transports and freighters in just a few volleys.  Five anti-aerospace emplacements provide medium and close-range firepower against fighters, gunships, and transports attempting to resist Asanovan authority. 

[[Image:TupalevClassGunship2.jpg]]

The Tupolev is protected by Boron-Nitride Composite armor, a new development from the Prefecture that is now available on the open market.  This armor is many times more effective than the aged plate armor originally mounted on the Tupolevs.  Terran-standard shielding also adds to the defenses of the Tupolev, making it nearly impervious to the cannons and non-breaching missiles of pirate and smuggling craft. 

===Deployment===

The Asanova Black Fleet has five Tupolev Gunships.  These gunships operate as patrol craft, guarding tariff collection stations, domestic shipping lanes, and industrial facilities.  The Tupolevs also act as escorts for VIPs and convoys of extreme importance.

[[Image:TupalevClassGunship3.jpg]]

Internally, they are small and cramped, with little in the way of on-board amenities, so they are usually only deployed for one or two week deployments before they are docked and the crew is swapped out.

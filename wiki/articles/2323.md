== Events of 2323 ==

=== January ===

* January 6 - The remains of the carrier group led by the [[HMS Arbiter|HMS ''Arbiter'']] (CV-11) was ambushed by a flight of three [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] [[Starmaster Class Destroyer]]s in the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. The [[HMS Fearless|HMS ''Fearless'']] was ordered to cover the retreat of the rest of the carrier group. Despite being outnumbered and outgunned, the [[HMS Fearless|''Fearless'']] managed to outmaneuver its opponents and inflict critical damage to one of the ships, and held back the other two long enough for the carrier group to escape. In the aftermath, the [[HMS Fearless|HMS ''Fearless'']] was scuttled by its captain.

=== February ===

* February 12 - Captured [[Omegas]] agents had revealed the location of [[Lucius Black]]'s hidden stronghold: [[Mount McKinley]]. [[Prefecture]], [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and [[United Earth Alliance]] forces soon converged on and breached the defenses. During this pitched battle, [[Jack Ivonrud]] and [[Emil Torsc]] fought their legendary duel to the death. [[Lucius Black]] fled to his orbiting fleet, and abandoned the [[Sol System]]. His enigmatic [[Remnant]] allies left with him.
* February 16 - The last elements of the [[Royal Imperial Army]], [[Royal Imperial Air Force]], and [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in the [[Sol System]] surrendered to [[Anthony Hadrian]].

=== March ===

* March 15 - Eighteen [[:Category:Warships|warships]] still loyal to the [[Onyx Empire]] were located in the [[Theta Algedi System]] attempting to recover food, parts, and other supplies from space stations formerly held by the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial military]]. Three [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] groups from the [[Prefecture]] engaged the ships, but were thwarted by the [[HMS Glorious|HMS ''Glorious'']] and its escorts. The [[HMS Glorious|''Glorious'']] and its escorts went down with all hands, but the remaining fifteen ships escaped with millions of [[Imperial Credits|credits]] in supplies.

=== April ===

* April 15 - [[Lucius Black]]'s final farewell to [[Terra]] unleashed every nuclear weapon on the planet, opened every viral storage unit, and every toxic chemical weapon system. This "Scorched Earth" attack wiped out nealy every major metropolis. The coalition forces were only spared by the fact that most of them were in space or still in the battlefield.
* April 20 - The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] recalled [[Aseta Salfore]], and began sending assistance to aid in rescuing civilians from the charred cities destroyed in [[Lucius Black]]'s "Scorched Earth" attack.
* April 25 - Using his authority as the former [[Grand Admiral]] of the [[Onyx Empire]], [[Anthony Hadrian]] officially surrendered all forces on [[Earth]] to the coalition forces led by [[Scott Sanburn]] and [[Andreas Tischler]]. The surrender was not unconditional, and the terms of the [[Treaty of Memphis]] allowed the rebellion known as the [[Prefecture]] to keep any territories of the [[Onyx Empire]] which chose to remain with them in free elections.
* April 30 - The "Convention of Admirals" met on [[Ueda]] to discuss a free government to succeed the [[Empire]]. Under the terms of the [[Treaty of Memphis]], their first decision was to ratify the terms and allow all territories to choose whether they would accept their rule or not. The [[Prefecture]] rebels officially became the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], making them the first new constitutional republic formed in more than a hundred years.

=== May ===

* May 1 - After the initial panic from [[Lucius Black]]'s final attack on [[Terra]], most regions were stabilized. [[Rio de Janeiro]] was established as the main starport and refugee camp, and civilians began to flee [[Terra]] in every ship they could find.
* May 2 - [[Scott Sanburn]] drafts and presents his proposal which later becomes known as the "[[Sanburn Plan]]."
* May 3 - As part of their "Outreach Program", the [[Prefecture]] declassifies and distributes many technologies fielded by the [[Onyx Empire]] during the [[Second Exodus War]].
* May 4 - The [[Prefecture]] held referendums and elections on every former [[Empire|Imperial]] world. All civilians were able to cast their votes for the first time since the rise of the Empire, possibly the darkest period in their history. First they chose if they would be independent or accept the rule of the [[Prefecture]], and if they accepted [[Prefecture]] rule they cast their votes for Senators and Curiators. These first signs of freedom led to the first significant economic gains in the galactic economy since the beginning of the [[Second Exodus War]].
* May 16 - After rejecting the [[Prefecture]], the [[Alpha Centauri System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]] and [[Tau Ceti System]] united into the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].
* May 19 - After rejecting the [[Prefecture]], the [[Lupei System]] forms the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]].
* May 30 - The [[Alliance of Planets High Council]] listened to the [[Sanburn Plan]], and ultimately accepted [[Scott Sanburn]]'s proposal. The [[United Earth Alliance]] would be formally dissolved, and a new independent government would slowly build up [[Earth]] to an independent democratic republic.

=== June ===

* June 1 - The [[Alliance of Planets]] formed the Terran Planetary Recovery Administration as one of the first components of what would become the [[Terran Embassy]], and charged it with restoring [[Terra]] to working order.
* June 4 - [[Rahn Industries]] is granted a "carte blanche" license to develop the world of [[Albanus]] for manufacturing under the jurisdiction of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

=== July ===

* July 6 - [[Adelson]] votes to become part of the [[Prefecture]], and [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] occupation forces withdraw.

=== August ===

=== September ===

* September 19 - After seeing the end of the hostilities of the [[Second Exodus War]], the leaders of [[Taviridis Somarchada]] finally decided to end their isolationism and made [[First Contact]] with the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].
* September 24 - An [[Ixoarchada]] delegation was invited to [[Sepulare]], where they were offered membership within the [[Alliance of Planets]]. The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] politely declined, but signed trade agreements and non-aggression pacts with all Member States of the [[Alliance of Planets]], and took the first steps towards interstellar trade.

=== October ===

=== November ===

* November 26 - The Terran Planetary Recovery Administration released an enormous report on the status of the decimated world.

=== December ===

* December 2 - The [[Sol System]] was secure from any further incursions of [[Lucius Black]]'s forces. [[Lucius Black|Black]]'s fleet was in hiding, as was the [[Remnant]].
* December 23 - The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] hosted a massive celebration on [[Sepulare]]. In a grandiose ceremony of epic proportions, the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] was officially welcomed as a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], and the [[United Earth Alliance]] was permanently dissolved. At the recommendation of [[Scott Sanburn]], the [[Titan System]] provisional government joined the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] and adopted their system of government and currency.

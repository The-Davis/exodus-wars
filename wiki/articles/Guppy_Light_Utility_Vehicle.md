*Classification: Light Utility Vehicle
*Powerplant: NDI Sa-884 Micro-Reactor
*Speed: 150 KPH
*Armor: Prudenski Composite armor 
Shields: Vambrace Shield Generator 
*Armament: 2 x 'Stiletto' PPBCs
*Communications System:  Standard Titanian Communications Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Land-SPAR 

===Overview===

Designed for general transport, patrol, and utility duty, the General Use Patrol Type-E, GUP-E, is a very popular vehicle among Commonwealth Army Command soldiers.  Known affectionately as the "Guppy", this light, all-terrain vehicle is excellent for quickly transporting small groups of infantry around the battlefield and elsewhere.

[[Image:GuppyLightUtilityVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

Designed primarily for patrol and transport, the Guppy is armed only defensively.  A pair of light 'Stiletto' Pulsed Particle Beam Cannons are mounted on turrets in the rear of the vehicle.  These turrets allow the two rear passengers to lay down suppressing fire against hostiles.  These two energy weapons are extremely lethal against infantry, and can also ward off light armor. 

The Guppy is outfitted with a New Dallas Industries Sa-884 Micro-Reactor.  This reactor generates just enough energy for the Guppy to reach top speed, fire its weapons, and even power a small shield projector.  The body of the Guppy is also covered with Prudenski Composite armor, and the tires are solid material covered with a Prudenski mesh. 

===Deployment===

[[Image:GuppyLightUtilityVehicle2.jpg]]

Guppies are used for nearly every conceivable role within the Commonwealth Ministry of Defense.  Transport, patrol, scouting, raiding, and troop-deployment are just a few of the uses.  Some versions of the Guppy have even found their way into the hands of local law enforcement.  There are also a few modified versions operating as ambulances in both the military and civilian sectors.

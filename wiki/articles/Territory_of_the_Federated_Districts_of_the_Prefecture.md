The Prefecture has dozens of worlds, and a wide range of diverse cultures on their worlds. Some star systems have two inhabited planets, while others are alone.

==Laojiu District==

The District of Laojiu is one of the core districts of the Prefecture, and has fewer military units and garrisons. Industry on the Laojiu worlds is devoted to farming and other production of foodstuffs. The people and politicians are laid-back, let-things-be type people. They have fine principles and core values, and are a great asset to the Prefecture as a whole.

During the hostilities known as the [[Tempest War]], [[Mesarthrim]] forces from the [[United Centusi States]] drove all [[Prefecture]] forces out of the District, and stomped out all civilian resistance. The [[Mesarthrim]] formed a new civilian government for the region, which became known as the Free Laojiu Republic. Most Laoji people were secretly opposed to the government, including the puppet head-of-state himself. Ten-Gou Zin secretly skimmed off the top of food shipments right under the nose of his Myt'gaul handlers, who did not understand the complex accounting legers. When the [[United Centusi States]] were defeated at the end of the war, Ten-Gou Zin was tried and acquitted of treason, then later elected to the Senate.

The Labrea Garrison is a polar-opposite of the laid-back locals of Laojiu. The soldiers who protect the Laojiu District are some of the most gung-ho, rough-and-tumble soldiers in the Prefecture Military. Their tank commanders are often rotated to other district garrisons and to military academies to teach tank tactics and operations.

==Tavou District==

Tavou is a tight-knit line of four worlds, all of them are along the border of the Volucris-occupied space. Because of the odd nature of the Volucris attack patterns, Tavou is in no immediate danger of overrun, but the Tavou worlds are given great amounts of resources to keep the border secured. The Tavou Guard are the among the best-equipped units in the Prefecture military, surpassed only by Travis Carpenter's Legion of the Phoenix.

During the Tempest War, the District was nearly torn apart by the Mesarthrim and the Volucris. The United Centusi States invaded the District in the hopes of undermining the heart and soul of the Prefecture's armies: most Prefecture soldiers settled their families on Tavou. Leskovik was destroyed in the first strike of the war, and things went downhil from there. Kufestin was taken over by the Mesarthrim for months, and Adelson fell to the Volucris and was destroyed.

The Tavou Guard are trained and led by Brigadier [[Kenneth Russell]], the second-finest officer in the entire [[Prefecture Legion Corps]]. He graduated second in the Empire's now-legendary officer's training school, where the likes of [[Travis Carpenter]] were trained in tactics.

The Tavou Guard are arguably the best-suited unit to fight bugs--or [[Volucris]], as Brigadier Russell insists on calling them by their proper name. Since the Second Contact Battle on February 5th, 2324, the Volucris have constantly raided Tavou and the other three worlds with small forces. Even when they aren't dealing with a Volucris raid, Brigadier Russell runs the on-duty troops through drills and war games. No one is better suited to command border armies than Brigadier Kenneth Russell.

==Tejat District==

Tejat is the furthest turnwise District in the [[Prefecture]], and thus borders several foreign nations. The [[Meroniri]], [[Lorithos]], [[Reigens]] and [[Ikronin]] all border at least part of the District, and thus the Tejat worlds see many exotic visitors and an influx of unusual goods. As a hub of foreign travel, the Tejat District world of Canopus is a perfect home for the Inquisitors. The Inquisitors are a vital piece of Prefecture military intelligence, so all measures are taken to keep Canopus safe from enemy infiltration or attack.

The Tejat District is mostly inhabited by refugees from Indian and Pakistani regions of Terra. They are well-disciplined and well-organized people, and produce some of the finest products in the Prefecture economy. Front-line commanders are always clamoring for more Tejat Guard to be rotated to the front, as their strict training from birth molds them into the best infantry and battle armor operators in the army.

Because of its military importance, Tejat worlds were targetted twice by the Mesarthrim during the Tempest War. Canopus's intelligence facilities were crippled by an early attack, and the world of Sindh was taken in a year-long campaign of bloody fighting. Sindh was not retaken in the course of the war. The occupational Myt'kampfen eventually surrendured peacefully.

The current [[Consul]], [[Talaris Roi]], is a native of [[Tejat]]. Her strong love for freedom and social ties made her a natural agent of the Tejat Resistance against the [[Empire]]. When the [[Prefecture ]]took control, she easily won a seat in the Senate. Roi works very hard on leading the [[Prefecture]], and her elegant social ways have won friends for the Prefecture among the [[Reigen]] and [[Ikronin]] people.

==Ueda District==

The capital world of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] is [[Ueda]], and the District of Ueda has some of the most important planets in the [[Prefecture]]. [[Estelis]] is the primary manufacturing world, [[Albanus]] is a huge world of scientific research, [[Novaya]] and [[Jaipur]] hold huge military academies, and [[Calpaca]] holds a massive computer complex housing the giant Arthur EI system. If the [[Prefecture]] was invaded, the District of Ueda would be the safest place to live.

Ueda itself holds some modern architectural marvels. Most of the old [[Terran]] "Wonders of the World" were rebuilt here, based on the models found in Professor [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow|Hlow]]'s library. The museums of human history are enormous, taking up a good 800 acres of land. They are the brainchild of [[Benjamin Miles Hlow]] and [[Chris Dayton]], who are both determined to ensure the survival of human culture, even in the face of catastrophes like Terra's near-annihilation.

==Ward District==

Ward is a district largely colonized by people of [[France (Terra)|French]] and [[British Empire (Terra)|English]] descent. Senator [[Jean Sarnou]] and his associates are very cautious politicians, preferring to avoid certain issues. When the issue of liberties and freedom come up, they throw caution to the wind and become fierce debaters. Ward is definitely in good hands politically.

Several planets in the Ward District are home to excellent academies. Ward itself is home to a major military training camp, [[Holt]] trains infantry officers, and Wei is home to one of the best pilot academies. [[Bolan]] is home to three physics labs, and Crofton has a major institute of economics.

The garrisons are commanded primarily by two very capable warriors. Lieutenant General [[Waylan Brown]] is a veteran pilot, and very capable of keeping Ward space clear. His record is perfect, and he has never lost a fighter. He's a very tough commander, and lives on Bolan full-time, the miserable hell-hole that most soldiers stay on as short as possible.

[[Brennan Connor]] is a fiery infantry commander, and a native of the Ward district. He served with the [[United Earth Alliance]] army until the [[Empire]] destroyed it, then fought for the Holt Resistance until the fall of the [[Empire]]. The [[Prefecture]] recognized his leadership skills and offered him a command.

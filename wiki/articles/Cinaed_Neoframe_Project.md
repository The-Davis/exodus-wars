The Cinaed Accords were a technological and cultural exchange program between the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].  One of several projects was the TA001-"Neoframe".

The Neoframe Project was designed to share airframe, propulsion, power production, and navigation technologies, while allowing each government to equip armaments and defenses suitable to their own tactics and logistics support.

The design shared the sparrow-tailed engine profile characteristic of Mesarthrim designs, while it built on a more conventional looking forward frame typical of Terran designs.

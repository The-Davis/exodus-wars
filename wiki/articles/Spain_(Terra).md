'''Spain''' was a parliamentary monarchy located in southern Europe. It was once among the largest [[Terran]] empires in history, but its power crumbled in the [[19th Century]]. Spain was largely unimportant in international politics, and King Philip VI finally abdictated the throne in [[2023]] in favor of the unified world government. Spain became part of the [[United Earth Alliance]] on August 2, [[2023]].

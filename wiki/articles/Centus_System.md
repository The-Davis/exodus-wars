The '''Centus System''' consists of the star Centus, the XX planets, the moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Centus, the planets are XX, XX, [[Mesar]], XX, XX. XX planets are orbited by moons. The planets are named from words in the [[Anderung]] language.

==Stellar Navigation==

Centus is the Centus System's only star. Centus is a class G3O yellow dwarf. Centus has a strong heliosphere with a particle windspeed of roughly 3.5 million kilometers per hour. Starship navigation is affected by Centus's heliosphere, but it is often a hazard for lower-quality ships.

The Centus System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Centus is part of the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and is a close neighbor of the [[Cinaed System]] and the [[Tirshan System]].

==Orbital Bodies==
The inner Centus System is contains the terrestrial planets. Only [[Mesar]] is inhabitable.

===Mesar===
[[Mesar]] (1.6AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Centus System. It has one moon, XX.

==Discovery and Exploration==

The Centus System was the base of operations for the [[Anderung]]. [[Terrans]] from [[France (Terra)|France]], [[Spain (Terra)|Spain]], [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]], [[British Empire (Terra)|England, Wales, Scotland and Ireland]] were abducted by the [[Anderung]] on their way to various colonies, and became known as the "[[Mesarnak]]", slaves of the [[Anderung]]. Eventually they rose up and conquered their alien masters, and became known as the [[Mesarthrim]].

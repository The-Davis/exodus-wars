===Overview===

The Trapper is a nasty creature, first seen in the Battle of Tavou.  They were first thought to be the Volucris equivalent of a land-mine--on Tavou they hid in camouflaged burrows and grabbed soldiers at random--but it is now known that they are Volucris special operations shock troopers.

[[Image:Avicularia.jpg]]

Weapons on the Trapper are few in number, but they are very combat-effective and have multiple non-combat purposes.  With its claws, the Trapper can burrow several meters per minute, and with its spinnerets it can quickly scale great sheer heights with ease.

Trappers are at home anywhere.  Their eight legs allow them to walk on the floor and ceiling of humanoid buildings at the same time--giving them extreme stability.  They are equally flexible in tunnels, in the open, and in low or no-gravity environments.

Most of the time, Trappers are used in close-environments of human bases and warships.  They are very effective there--especially on warships where they can disable the gravity and have a tremendous maneuverability advantage.  Trappers live for several decades, and they can become quite intelligent--for a Volucris soldier.

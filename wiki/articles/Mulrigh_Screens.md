As effective as [[Mulrigh Embedded Shielding]] is, its capacity for specialized defense, in the form of '''Mulrigh Screens''' is what makes it a truly magnificent system.  Its embedded nature allows specialized defensive screens to be projected outside of the armor.  Different combinations of these fields can be used to tailor the defenses of a ship or vehicle to a specific foe. Many of these screens are actually modern applications of archaic systems used before [[Falkran Armor]] was created.  Most of these systems are simple and have been miniaturized so that the field generators are no longer the hulking, inefficient behemoths that they once were. 

Although there are many screens that a pilot and captain may activate at any given time, the most commonly available fields are listed here.  Unless otherwise stated, fields may be projected simultaneously, given enough power.  The largest warships may maintain a number of screens at the same time, giving them maximum defense against all enemies, whereas a smaller vessel, tank, or fighter may only maintain a single screen geared against a specific foe, thus saving power for other vehicle systems.

===Corona Screen===

Starting as a lab curiosity, [[Ixoarchada]] scientists found that it was possible to manipulate turbulence in the plasma of a star's atmosphere to form a protective barrier, allowing ships to stay extremely close to the star.  It is designed to hide the vessel from sensors and to allow ships to escape from combat into the broiling hell of a stellar corona.  However, the power requirements for such screens are high, and only capital warships are capable of maintaining such screens for a prolonged amount of time.  Due to the power requirements, and the importance of the field when it is needed, no other screens may be active at the same time as a Corona Screen.

===Sensors Countermeasures Screen===

Because there is no high-energy shield barrier to attract the attention of sensors, and interfere with outgoing signals, the Sensors Countermeasures Screen can intelligently interfere with enemy scanners, making long-range identification and location of Ixoarchada vehicles difficult.  Using multi-phased signals to defeat passive sensors, and spoofed feedback data to fool active sensors, the SCM screen can cause missiles and torpedoes to lose lock.  It also significantly increases the margin of error for medium and long-range weapons directed at it.  The signals put out by the SCM screen can also help a warship's passive sensors, reducing the need for active sensors that may give away position. 

===Gravitic Screen===

The Gravitic Screen is a meshed series of barriers designed to absorb gravitational waves.  Useful against gravitational weapons, such as those employed by the [[Anderung]], [[Ngrligru]], or even the [[Meroniri]] and [[Remnant]], it is also used to allow Ixoarchada ships to take shortcuts through more hazardous regions of hyperspace and to jump into star systems much closer to their star than otherwise would be possible.  

As an aside, theoretical evidence points to the ability of a Gravitic Screen to reduce the effective radius of an [[Omega Bomb]] by 10%.  This has never been tested, however.

===Particle Screen===

The Particle Screen is a series of concentric spheres of intense magnetic fields used to deflect incoming charged particles.  Such a screen works best against lower velocity beams or low-mass beams, like electron beam weaponry, such as that employed by the Anderung in the past.  Against more advanced weaponry, Particle Screens are seldom employed.

===Projectile Screen===

Designed to protect early space-faring ships from space debris, the Projectile Screen is a thin barrier of modified vacuum, presenting a physical barrier, which stops or slows slower moving projectiles.  This is very useful when used with the SCM screen because it can cause premature detonation in missiles.  

Such screens are also often maintained on fast moving anti-grav craft in space and on planets, protecting them from debris, shrapnel, and various forms of flak.  

===Ray Screen===

A Ray Screen creates a barrier in space by modifying the optical density of the vacuum.  This causes near misses of lasers and other EM weaponry to be refracted harmlessly away, thus reducing the effective cross-section of the ship.  A primitive system, this screen only affects photonic weapons (lasers, the EM component of nuclear explosions, etc), but otherwise is ineffective against more advanced weaponry.  As a result, such fields are not often activated.

===Stealth Screen===

Designed specifically to mask low-level emissions of a ship, the Stealth Screen dampens energy output from a vessel and blends in with the radiological background of its current region.  Though hardly impossible to detect, it can hide vessels at long range, or in high-radiation environments, such as nebula.  This screen cannot be used in conjunction with any other screen, and the primary Embedded Shielding system must be powered down to standby.

===Subspace Screen===

Only installed on the newest or most important warships, the Subspace Screen has two uses.  Its powerful [[Subspace]] barrier field reinforces the barrier between Subspace and normal space, causing any nearby vessel or weapon submerged in Subspace to lose field cohesion and feed back into normal space.  This prevents subspace torpedoes from scoring direct hits (destroying them if they do not detonate prior to impact), and further can cause nearby vessels in Subspace to expend more energy to stay "submerged".  

Further theoretical work indicates that a Subspace Screen maybe be able to reduce the effective radius of an Omega Bomb.  While utterly useless against a direct hit from an Omega Bomb, a sufficiently powerful Subspace Screen should be able reduce the effective radius of the weapon by up to 40%.  This, like the counter-Omega effects of the Gravitic Screen, are untested.

The technology for such a screen has existed for some time, but was never though necessary.  However, interaction with a vessel of the [[Prefecture]] in Subspace alarmed Taviridis naval designers, and the Screen was made more widely available.

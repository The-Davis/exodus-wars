The '''Titan Peace Accords''' were the result of the "Black November" proposal first put forward by the [[Alliance of Planets]] in [[2318]] in an attempt to end the [[Second Exodus War]]. The treaty was signed by [[Lucius Black]], but was ultimately worthless, as he violated it shortly afterwards. Many critics believe that the treaty would have resulted in the demise of the [[Titan System]] if not for the timely intervention of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

=== "Black November" ===

The term "Black November" was commonly used by members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] and patriots within the [[Titan System]] to refer to November 17, [[2318]], when then-Premier [[Larry McNichols]] bowed to pressure from certain members of his government and offered to negotiate a peace treaty with the [[Onyx Empire]].

Much to the surprise of McNichols' critics, Lucius Black agreed to meet on neutral territory. Because they had not been involved in the Second Exodus War so far, both sides called on the [[Sirius Families]] to host and mediate the hearings.

=== The Treaty Process ===

The drafting of the treaty took nearly a year, during which time combat did not stop. The diplomatic corps of both sides were nearly driven mad by the legalese and minute changes demanded by Emperor Black. Elections were held in the Titan System, and McNichols' successor, [[Barbara Dannins]], nearly withdrew from the peace talks. Finally, everything was ready, and all parties involved met on [[Sirius Kuza]] on October 24, [[2319]].

=== The Cease Fire ===

While the Onyx Empire made no concessions in the first signing, they agreed to cease hostilities. There were no engagements between the Onyx Empire and the [[United Earth Alliance]] until [[2321]].

=== The Peace Accords ===

The final summit for the Titan Peace Accords was held in [[2320]], when the terms were finalized and all involved parties ratified the treaty.

In the treaty, Emperor Black agreed to permanently withdraw from the [[Titan System]], and pay reparations for the damage to civilian targets in the system. The surviving United Earth Alliance representatives ceded all claim to [[Earth]] and the other conquered territories.

Essentially, this "peace treaty" was nothing more than a conditional surrender. After five years of constant war, the survivors were desparate for an end to the conflict, even if it meant leaving their former countrymen under occupation.

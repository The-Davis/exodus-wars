===Overview===

Omarr, the first Hishai of [[Hamur Huern]] during the unification of [[Lorithan]], was the greatest general of the people of Lorithan.  Although precise records of his exploits have been lost thanks to the Burning Wars, his name and reputation has survived by memory alone.

[[Image:OmarrClassHeavyDestroyer1.jpg]]

When the design for a Heavy Destroyer was created after the Lorithos ASTF's startling defeat in the Titanian system, Omarr was the name chosen.

===Capabilities===

The main weapons of Omarr Heavy Destroyers are an array of eleven Heavy Blaster cannons.  Eight are mounted in twin-turrets, two are fixed-forward, and one is mounted in a nose-turret on the Omarr's front.  These eleven heavy turrets are supported by an additional fourty-one Standard Blaster cannon turrets.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons.  They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

Omarr Destroyers carry sixteen Skrike missile launchers and four Sur-Skrike torpedo tubes, which can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.

[[Image:OmarrClassHeavyDestroyer2.jpg]]

While it is very well protected, the Omarr is faster than most humanoid frigates.  It is at least as powerful as a Terran heavy cruiser, and substantially faster.

===Deployment===

Omarrs were designed and built following the disaster in the Titanian system.  The Lorithos ASTF analyzed the data from the battle and used it to create the Omarr Class Heavy Destroyer.

[[Image:OmarrClassHeavyDestroyer3.jpg]]

Few of these mighty warships have been built to-date, and they are as-yet untested against enemy warships.  Like all Unitronian warships, they are actually quite simple and very reliable.  The Lorithos ASTF fields only eight Omarr heavy destroyers.

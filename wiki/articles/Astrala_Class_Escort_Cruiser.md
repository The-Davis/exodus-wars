The '''Astrala Class Escort Cruiser''' is seen everywhere in [[Meroniri]] space. It engages in frequent patrols of their worlds and interests throughout the [[Orion Arm]], and often makes visits to friendly ports of call in other human sectors.

The Astrala is built to engage smaller ships while its "big brother", the [[Tronit Class Warcruiser]] handles the largest targets.

'''Gary Hughes'''
*Place of Birth: [[Theia]]
*Allegiance: [[Colonial Commonwealth]]

''Gary Hughes'' is a Councilman in the [[Titan System]] government. He was the target of an assassination attempt on March 30, [[2321]], but it was thwarted by [[Scott Sanburn]]'s defense forces.

*Classification: Heavy Armored Personnel Carrier
*Powerplant: NDI Sr-i98 Micro-Reactor
*Speed: 100 KPH
*Armor: Prudenski Composite armor 
*Shields: Vambrace Shield Generator 
*Armament: 2 'Rapier' PPBCs
*Communications System:  Standard Titanian Communications Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Land-SPAR 

===Overview===

The Chevalier is a rather large craft--one of the largest in the Commonwealth Army Command.  Aside from a driver and gunner, the Chevalier can carry eight Army Regulars or Guard's Riflemen in and out of battle safely in the confines of a heavily armored shell. 

[[Image:ChevalierArmoredPersonnelCarrier1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Chevalier sports armor and shields comparable to the massive Archer Heavy Armored Car.  However, it's weaponry is significantly lessened.  As an APC, it is not designed to do battle with other heavy armor.  However, it is well armed none-the-less.  A pair of 'Rapier' Pulsed Particle Beam Cannons can protect the Chevalier from smaller attack craft, and keep it clear from aerial threats.

To make up for its poor maneuverability relative to most Commonwealth ground craft, the Chevalier sports eight 'Sunburst/Starburst' Pods--a pair mounted at each corner of the vehicle.  These launchers can keep smaller targets from moving to close range, and can cover the Chevalier as it moves in and out of battle.

The Chevalier is outfitted with a New Dallas Industries Sr-i98 Micro-Reactor.  This is a very powerful reactor, similar to the Archer's, but whereas much of the Archer's power is reserved for its Claymore cannon, the bulk of the Chevalier's energy goes to powering its large Vambrace shield generator.  Backing up the shielding system is a formidable layer of Prudenski Composite armor.

[[Image:ChevalierArmoredPersonnelCarrier2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Chevaliers are heavy Armored Personnel Carriers that operate in groups of three, carrying two squads of twelve infantry between them.  Usually escorted by Buccaneers or Enforcers, they can charge into heavy enemy fire to deploy their infantry, and then lay down fire to support their troopers.  They operate behind heavier combatants in open field combat, and are excellent at transporting large numbers of infantry rapidly through an urban environment.

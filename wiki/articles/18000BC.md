== Events of 18000BC ==

The Ngrligru Kabila Duka banded together to attack the Anthronoris Empire.  Hundreds of colonies were annihilated in the initial strikes.  The Sagittarius Arm was largely dominated by the Anthronoris, but was quickly conquered by the Ngrligru.  The Anthronoris were driven into the core, and back towards the Orion Arm.  The Anthronoris lost thousands of warships in the struggle, but enough survived to defend several worlds long enough for the invading Ngrligru to lose their enraged drive.

While their civilization was destroyed, the worlds of Lorithan, Icronia, Terra and Glengarr survived.  They were cut off from the tiny remnant of the Anthronoris, and degenerated into various states of decay, often into a Stone Age or worse.

Two segments of the Anthronoris Empire survived relatively intact, but only just barely. While the populations of hundreds of outlying worlds were literally devoured by the Ngrligru, nine worlds survived through military might and stubborn resistance against the Ngrligru menace. Another group fled the destruction in huge starships, traveling far from the reach of the Ngrligru and carrying the last Emperor with them.

The nine worlds were the seed of what would eventually become the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. For almost a year after the Anthronoris Empire's demise, the Ngrligru attacked the remaining core worlds, but they eventually stopped. The hunger of the Ngrligru was sated by the lives of majority of the civilians and soldiers of the Anthronoris Empire. The invasion ended.

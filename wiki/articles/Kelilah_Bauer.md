Kelilah Bauer von Eberhart spent her first nine years of service as a [[Overseer Administration of the Mesarthrim Clans|Overseer Commandant]]. With their collapse, Kelilah was arrested and worked for the next ten years on receiving her Redemption Star so that she could serve once again. In [[2319]] Kelilah was supposed to be commissioned as an officer in the Clans Kriegsmarine. She, however, took her Star and instead fell in with a band of smugglers. Kelilah smuggled supplies for [[Friedrich Ahrens]] from the Clans, and when the Centusi Kriegsmarine was being formed, Ahrens repaid her with a commission as Fleet Admiral of the [[United Centusi States Support Fleet|Stütze Flotte]]. During her time as Admiral of the Stütze Flotte, she was judged a war criminal for ordering her crews to '..airlock, shoot, or starve...' any Prisoners of War brought on board.

Kelilah was captured in [[2333]] by [[Berlusconi Territorial Planetary Security Forces|Berlusconi Red Shirts]] with her command staff. She was never heard from again, however, members of her command staff reported that she applied for asylum with the Berlusconi. The Berlusconi never have stated if they granted it or not.

Following her disappearance, [[Steven Cue]] stated he believed she received a new Berlusconi identity and was serving in their Navy. One day, Cue issued a request to officially grant her the Foreign Service Badge for her "Service to the Foreign Sovereign Nation of the Berlusconi Family.." In a surprising show of support of Cue's position, Arnold Stadt personally approved the request - and an envoy presented the badge to [[Alessandro Berlusconi|Papa Berlusconi]], asking him to ensure Kelilah received it. It is unknown if she ever did.

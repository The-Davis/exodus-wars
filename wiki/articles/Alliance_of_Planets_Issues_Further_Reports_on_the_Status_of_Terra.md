November 26, 2323

by James Davis, Scott Sanburn, and Andrew Sears

=====Sepulare=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After nearly five months of assessing and working on the collateral war damage of the Terran system, the Alliance of Planets has issued a comprehensive report of damage to the Terran solar system, and that of Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The overall damage is massive, a calculated $872,000,000,000. The most damage was to Terra itself. The Alliance Environmental Council has concluded that Terra is virtually uninhabitable, except for a few regions, most notably the South American region.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The majority of the remaining survivors – those that were not taken by the Empire as slaves or conscripted soldiers – are being evacuated to various locations throughout the Alliance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The war was devastating. I’d have to say it was the worst conflict Terra has ever seen," commented Admiral Jassinsky, head of the TDF Medical Division. "We will have to change Terra’s planetary rating from Inhabitable to Habitat-Required until the environmental damage can be repaired."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Several firms and various governments have committed to providing various environmental repair and sustainability packages in order to help Terra, notably the AstroLift Company.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;According to the official report, most cities in the Americas, Europe, Asia, and the Pacific Island belts are in total ruins. Japan in particular has suffered massive tectonic trauma, causing a large portion of the island chain to sink into the ocean.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Power, water, communication, and sanitation systems are either completely destroyed or barely running.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The [[United Earth Alliance]] was wiped out when the Empire invaded eight years ago. With the Empire gone, the bureaucratic and governing vacuum is tremendous. Crime is rampant, and even the mighty Space Police of the Alliance of Planets is hard-pressed to keep civil order in the inhabitable regions.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;All efforts are being made to evacuate as many people as possible from the areas suffering from the worst devastation. Starships in orbit continue to scan the terrain nonstop, trying to locate survivors. The undertaking is nearly impossible. As high-tech as Alliance technology is, the radiation on the planet is a scanner-operator’s nightmare. Automated Imperial jammers and weapons grids are still operational; adding hazards to low-flying rescue ships. The post-war casualties are nearing a thousand lost to weapons grids, which Alliance bombers failed to spot and eliminate due to the radiation.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;These hazards do nothing to stop the brave rescue workers of the Alliance. Every hour another rescue plane flies into the Rio de Janeiro airport, the only metropolis-sized city unscathed by the nuclear holocaust that took place a few short months ago. Supply and refugee convoys are flooding into the city nonstop, and transport starships bound for refugee colonies on other Alliance planets are filled to capacity.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Alliance Member States, particularly the Titanians and Mesarthrim are rising to meet the needs. Their army engineers have worked around the clock, building landing ports to accommodate the refugee starships, and shelters to accommodate those who do not want to leave Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"These people have seen hell,” said a Mesarthrim engineer. “The last few days of the war were the worst. The Empire blew up its own nuclear silos, unleashing fatal levels of radiation worldwide, just so the Alliance wouldn't get its hands on their arsenal. Those pigs shot civilians who were just trying to escape.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Alliance Medical Director Admiral Dr. Jassinsky had more to say. "We are dealing with the worst situation the Alliance has ever encountered. My staff is dealing with diseases and epidemics of viruses we haven’t even seen before. We’re either dealing with radiation-induced mutations, or an outbreak of weapons-grade germs engineered by the Empire.”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Alliance Terran Disaster Recovery Task Force submitted a final report to the Alliance High Command on the 19th. The report documented several items:
*72.7% infrastructure was destroyed.
*78.3% agricultural land was destroyed.
*96.9% Airport / Spaceport facilities were razed.
*68.8% Utility Properties were destroyed
*100.0% Policing Forces destroyed
*2.8 Billion People Killed (Estimated)
*Overall Cost Estimate: 872 Trillion [[Likvada]]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The 2,965-page report has listed everything from building loss to estimated time of repairing various items.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"This is very hard to take in," commented TDF General-Commander Scott Sanburn. "Even though I lived and fought here through the last few months, I didn’t realize how much damage we caused. What a horrible war."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When asked about future of the old United Earth Alliance and what Titanian Defense Forces had planned, Sanburn had little information. "I don't know for sure. The Alliance High Council and the Alliance President are meeting to discuss our options. We will announce more definite plans soon. Right now we are concentrating on rescuing and evacuating as many people as possible. Maybe then we can start talking about the old Terran government and rebuilding this world.”

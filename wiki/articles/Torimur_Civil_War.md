The '''Torimur Civil War''' was fought between forces loyal to [[Lucius Black]] and [[Anthony Hadrian]]. It was mostly fought during the [[Second Exodus War]]'s Battle of Earth, but had theaters of combat on other [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] worlds.  The term referred exclusively to combat between the Onyx Empire's loyalists, and the rebelling [[Prefecture]].  Combat that took place with other factions during this time period was known as the Second Exodus War.

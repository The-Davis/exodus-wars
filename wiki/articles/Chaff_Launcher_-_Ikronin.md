'''[[Ikronin]] Chaff''' countermeasures consist of a cloud of millions of tiny metal strips and particles. This mixture interferes with sensors and missiles. The chaff fills one 500 foot square with these strips and particles.

Incoming smart munitions find it hard to navigate the detritus field and are likely to detonate prematurely. Energy beam weapons find a portion of their energies deflected by the particles within the field.

Sensors also find much of their beam energies redirected, greatly reducing their effectiveness.

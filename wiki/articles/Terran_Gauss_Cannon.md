The '''Gauss Cannon''' is a medium energy form of the Mass Driver, capable of launching projectiles at high velocities.

Gauss Rifles While Autocannons are extremely powerful weapons, there is a limit to what can be accomplished with chemical explosions for propulsion. The Gauss Rifle is a new look on projectile weaponry. Instead of using explosive ammunition, it uses a powerful particle accelerator to hurl shells of nickel-ferrous alloy at its target. Gauss Rifles are very common among Terran units, as the quartermasters prefer the ease-of-maintenance of the non-explosive ammo.

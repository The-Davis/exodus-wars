'''Tahlili Ayala'''
*Date of Death: March 15, [[2324]]
*Place of Birth: [[Rhea]]
*Place of Death: [[Tavou]]
*Allegiance: [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Tahlili Ayala'' was a Colonel in the [[Legion of the Wolf]], and was one of the [[Ngrligru]] from [[Kabila Kimburu]]. She was killed by the [[Volucris]] during the [[Battle of Tavou]].

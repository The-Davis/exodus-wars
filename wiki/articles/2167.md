== Events of 2167 ==

===January===

===February===
*February 14 - [[Reigen]] explorer [[Njiorin Osrason]] discovers the vast [[antimatter]] supplies in the nebula later dubbed [[Njiorin's Well]].
*February 19 - [[Reigess Suverände]] claims the entirety of [[Njiorin's Well]] as a national resource, to be exploited and shared equally by all [[Reigens]] and [[Juns]].
*February 27 - [[Njiorin Osrason]] is awarded a family name and title in recognition for his discovery. All of his descendants keep the surname "Njiorin" in his honor.

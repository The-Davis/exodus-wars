Laojiu is a very low profile for defense.  While it is an important food source, it isn't tactically feasible to attack it. Laojiu contributes so much to the galactic food supply that any attacker would inevitably hurt itself by hurting the supply lines.

Emilia Baker runs a relaxed command. Patrols and maneuvers are conducted on a regular basis, but if Samuel Arias or Jan Brooks were to observe, they'd find the quality control atrocious.  Still, the Liaden Air Command is in decent shape, and capable of driving away the odd pirate that drifts in.

Commanding Officer: Lieutenant General Emilia Baker

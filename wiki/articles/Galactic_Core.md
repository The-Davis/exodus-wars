The '''Galactic Core''' of the [[Milky Way]] galaxy is a huge sphere of super-dense stars and radioactive gas, all orbitting a massive black hole.

The core holds an important place in [[Meroniri]] mythology. They believe the black hole is the source of entropy in the universe, and through entropy powers the rebirth and creation of energy in the form of [[hyperspace]]. There is some scientific merit to this belief, though the mysticism behind it is largely ignored by other [[:Category:Races|races]].

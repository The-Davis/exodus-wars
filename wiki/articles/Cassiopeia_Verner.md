'''Cassiopeia "Case" Verner'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]

''Cassiopeia Verner'' is a member of [[Charlene Rearden]]'s squadron. Prior to joining the [[Prefecture]], she was one of the [[Empire]]'s artificially gestated soldier-children.

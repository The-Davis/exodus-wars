November 8, 2324

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The First Joint Defense Fleet (FJDF), a defense project started by the now defunct United Mesarthrim Clans and by the Taviridis Somarchada, is nearing completion. The United Centusi States--heir apparent to the government of the late Uber-General Andreas Tischler--has taken over the FJDF responsibilities of their predecessors.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The FJDF only awaits completion of the Prometheus, a super-cruiser several kilometers in length. The incredibly powerful warship and its escorts will serve as a primary line of defense against the common foes of the Mesarthrim and Archadian people.

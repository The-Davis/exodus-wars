== Events of 17940BC ==

The nine worlds that remained from the mighty [[Anthronoris Empire]]--[[Sepulare System|Sepulare]], [[Minhara System|Minhara]], [[Hamele System|Hamele]], [[Thibana System|Thibana]], [[Caelire System|Caelire]], [[Shaula System|Shaula]], [[Oture System|Oture]], [[Weshura System|Weshura]], and [[Alderami System|Alderami]]--lost little in the way of their technological capacity, but they lost the fire that made them the greatest [[Anthronoris Empire|Empire]] in history. The majority of the survivors turned inwards, to developing a self-contained utopia among the surviving world. Some had the foresight to preserve what was left of the [[Anthronoris]], and began to record everything they still knew into a library.

Whether or not this was a wise move is debatable. Today, we have the knowledge contained in these libraries, but with the greatest scientific minds of each world devoted to preserving the old, nothing was done to start fresh until all of the scientific momentum of the [[Anthronoris]] was lost. The library still exists, deep beneath the surface of [[Sepulare System|Sepulare]]. The surviving [[Anthronoris]] instead devoted themselves to culture and to art, and never fully recovered the drive to expand and change.

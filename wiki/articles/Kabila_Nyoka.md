===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Nyoka|History]]

''Kabila Nyoka'' was by far the strongest of the [[Ngrligru]] Kabila until the year [[2207]], when Kabilas [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] and [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]] surpassed them. Today the Nyoka are rabid enemies of the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Robert Marshall]], whom they blame for their fall from grace.

When the two renegade Kabilas, Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] and [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] declared a wish to join with the humans as friends, the other Kabilas literally fought each other for the right to exterminate the upstarts. Nyoka and [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] won that right, and waged war with [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] and [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]].

Nyoka and [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] managed to wipe out all of [[Kabila Bweha]], and Nyoka managed to absorb and entire Mia'mbili of [[Kabila Bweha|Bwehan]] soldiers and weapons. They also destroyed many troops that would have been taken by [[Kabila Jitu'nge]], creating a rift of enmity between themselves and their powerful rivals.

[[Kabila Kimburu]] fared better, escaping into human-controlled space.

In [[2207]], Nyoka's Thelathini na nane Mia'mbili was attacked by [[Empire|Imperialists]] returning to the [[Sol Sector]] for their revenge. The Nyoka Mia'mbili lost everything except the ''Bahari Shela'' to [[Kabila Damu'kizuka]] saboteurs. While completely outnumbered, the ''Bahari Shela'' fought back with devastating results. Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] finally managed to capture [[Grolk Kyhra]] and the Mia'mbili's Nina'rikebu intact.

Since the loss of the Thelathini na nane Mia'mbili, Kabila Nyoka was hurt badly in several raids conducted by Kabilas [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] and [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]]. [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] managed to capture one of Nyoka's key factory worlds, as well as a pair of their valuable warships.

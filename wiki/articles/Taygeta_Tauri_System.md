The '''Taygeta Tauri System''' consists of the star Taygeta Tauri, the planet [[Eciluge]], and a few comets.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Taygeta Tauri is the system's only star. Taygeta Tauri is a class G1L yellow sub-dwarf. The Taygeta Tauri System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Taygeta Tauri is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Eciluge ===

[[Eciluge]] (2.6AU) is a barely inhabitable ball of ice. It has no moons. It was inhabitted for a few short years by the refugees from [[Torimur]], between the [[First Exodus]] and the [[Second Exodus]].

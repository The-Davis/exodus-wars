==January==

The Prefecture's Norfolk Project ships began to make serious advancements in January, with their first subspace lurkers and deep-space probes detecting and observing Kriegsmarine fleet movements as they prepared a massive invasion force. In the later days of the month, Inquisitors began to redeploy field agents to discover the Mesarthrim plans.
*1/03/2327 - Black Revelations

==February==

The Invasion of the Ueda District begins with an all-out attack of Albanus. Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine elements are unable to directly assault a fortress world like New Albany, and must launch well-organized surgical strike groups. Robert Marshall and Marie Sarnou direct the defense of Albanus.

==March==

The invasion of Albanus continues, with two landing attempts and a major assault on several space docks. Prefecture Naval Air Command leads an almost flawless defense, but one space dock is lost in the course of battle, causing major damage to the Prefecture's repair capacity.

==April==

It becomes abundantly clear that while the Prefecture's defense of Albanus will hold, the Mesarthrim can continue to build up forces and supply the Kriegsmarine almost indefinitely. The Prefecture's only hope to keep their manufacturing base is to cut the Mesarthrim supply lines.
*4/26/2327 - Glimmer of Opportunity

==May==
With the Prefecture's attack carriers and subspace lurkers wreaking havoc on their shipping, the Mesarthrim campaign against Albanus falters badly. The Mesarthrim launch a massive naval attack, hoping to regain the edge. Only a well-timed recall of the attack carriers saves the Albanus defenses from disaster.

==July==

Mesarthrim advances into the Ueda District have almost completely failed. Their forward momentum was completely arrested by their failure to take New Albany, and their smaller invasion forces into the other Ueda worlds were met with stiff resistance from the Icronians. With few options remaining, the Mesarthrim launch a grudge attack against the heavy population centers of Jaipur before withdrawing from the sector, forcing the Prefecture to focus inwards in rescue operations.

The Tempest War's brutality finally reaches a point where the civilian governments of the Prefecture and the Consortium fear that the total destruction of their civilizations is a very real possibility. They launch the Myrdrasil Project, a joint colonization effort, to set up hidden worlds well away from the combat zones. It is hoped that they will provide a remnant if the Mesarthrim win, and an asset for the future if the Mesarthrim are conquered.

==September==

With the Mesarthrim blitzkrieg finally stopped, the outcome of the war hangs in the balance. Neither the Prefecture nor the Mesarthrim have the resources for another sustained assault, but the Mesarthrim stranglehold on food production will soon tip the balance in their favor again. Anthony Hadrian launches a bold campaign to retake Laojiu, starting with the deployment of the Hunters.

==October==
The Myrdrasil Project has been an outstanding success. Already four colonies are being built on new worlds, and the Consortium's plans for peaceful expansion are finally being realized.

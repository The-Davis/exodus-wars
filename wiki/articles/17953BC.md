== Events of 17953BC ==

Terra Novus became a haven to the surviving ships not aligned with [[Sepulare]] and its allies.  Eventually, the [[Remnant]] gathered a small fleet of warships, and rescued several million people from the devastated worlds around the [[Sagittarius Arm]].  The Remnant’s fleet began scouting out additional safe havens.  A long scouting mission discovered that Aboukir, a former light industry world, had survived the Sundering intact.  The entire Remnant fleet gathered to evacuate the world to safety.

They were mistaken: the [[Ngrligru]] had not overlooked Aboukir, they had preserved it to harvest slaves.  The [[Ngrligru]] attacked during the evacuation, wiping out two thirds of the fleet, including the [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]] Heavy Combatant Auxilium. Over two million Remnant died in the conflagration.  The Remnant fleet evacuated as many survivors from Aboukir as they could and fled.

As vulnerable as they were, the Remnant knew they could never openly rebuild their Empire.  The Emperor was deposed for his violent policies, and a young niece was named Empress in hopes that she would lead the Remnant in a strategy that would survive.

The Remnant left Terra Novus, and fled to the far reaches of the Sagittarius Arm.  The Remnant sought out one of the four remaining [[Void World]]s, [[Caetara Remaneo]] — or “All that Remains.”

The pride of the Anthronoris was their [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]] Heavy Combatants.  The Auxilium was lost in the disaster at Aboukir.  The Cohonesto, Minerva and Adoreus survived, but the trip to Caetara Remaneo was hard on them.  The Cohonesto was crashed onto the surface to be used for spare parts. The Minerva was parked in stellar orbit, in hopes that it could be repaired.  The Adoreus remained in service, and became the command ship of the [[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]].

The Remnant began rebuilding on Caetara Remaneo as well as the three remaining Void Worlds, [[Nomus Orba]], X, and Y.

''Thomas deGraff'' (born March 19, [[2252]]) is an unsung hero and villain of the [[Second Exodus War]]. He was directly responsible for the development, testing, and production of many of the aircraft used by both the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] during the conflict.

=== Early Life ===

Thomas deGraff was born in Rotterdam, in the Holland district of [[Earth]]. He received a basic education in his native region, but moved to the French district to study mathematics at the University of Lille. He graduated with honors in [[2274]], receiving separate degrees in Mathematics and Physics.

After graduation, deGraff moved to the American district, where he studied Aerospace Engineering and graduate-level Physics at Yale University. He received both degrees in [[2277]], and soon received a job offer from [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]].

As part of his job, deGraff had to move to [[Ah Kin]] to work in the company's main development laboratory. While working there, deGraff continued his education at the University of Rio de Andes, where he eventually completed his disertations on Physics and Aerospace Engineering in [[2286]].

=== Project Raven ===

When the [[Onyx Empire]] took over the planetary government of [[Ah Kin]] in [[2300]], Dr. Thomas deGraff was just as much in the dark as everyone else. When the office of the [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] approached Alexi-Wrought looking for a director for a special project, deGraff volunteered. He had no idea he was about to become a prisoner in all but name.

Dr. deGraff was made the head of [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Aerospace Laboratory, but was confined to the main campus; a walled compound a few kilometers outside of Rio de Andes. He learned much of his new masters' technological goals, but little of their intentions. Despite balking at his confinement, he had no qualms with working on their aerospace projects.

Under Dr. deGraff's direction, the laboratories developed multiple airframes, which became increasingly advanced and complex. At first, deGraff only had access to derivative technologies, but was allowed to branch out more and more into the experimental and theoretical.

When the first [[F-4 Raven]] came off the production lines, Dr. deGraff rode in the trainee seat for its maiden voyage, while the aircraft was piloted by [[Sarah Grissom]].

=== Imperial Civil War ===

During the war, Dr. deGraff managed to keep his head down, and avoided much of the violence that plagued most of the [[Sol Sector]].

When [[Ah Kin]] was liberated, and subsequently voted to leave the [[Prefecture]] in favor of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], Dr. deGraff returned to his former position at Alexi-Wrought Airworks. Before departing, he plundered the Aerospace Laboratory of many of its technological secrets (most of them his own inventions), and was able to successfully patent his life's work. Rather than pay licensing fees or suffer the political problems of willfully violating foreign patents, the Prefecture bought out his technology outright.

With his newfound fortunes, Dr. deGraff purchased majority shares in Alexi-Wrought, thus gaining control of many lucrative military contracts. He is now largely retired, nearing age 80.

The Piranha was designed in the last weeks of the the [[Imperial Civil War]].  It was designed specifically to counter the Vampire, but when the war ended the plans were shelved.
When the [[Volucris]] were encountered on February 5, [[2324]], the Piranha was built to meet the threat.  Two Piranha squadrons fought in the [[Battle of Mirach]], and performed well against the [[Volucris]] Shivas, except for flaws in their armor systems.  Sky Marshal [[Jan Brooks]] ordered an upgrade of the technology and for production to be kicked into overdrive.

In [[2325]], the [[Prefecture]] Congress ordered an overhaul of the entire military, and the [[Prefecture Air Force]] was the recipient of some major advances.  Many of them were incorporated into the Piranha.
The prototype Piranhas that met the Volucris on Mirach were quite advanced compared to their Stuka and Sparrow Hawk counterparts, but still nothing compared to the results of the Phoenix Project.

Trade with the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] resulted in fighter-sized gravity cavitation drives, which when added to an array of impulse engines created the most maneuverable fighter in space.  When a new fusion reactor was added to the mix, the Piranha's drive system was perfect.  The Muon fuel supply of the reactor will last a thousand years.  Piranhas can fly forever.

The drive system was capable of unlimited potential thrust, provided that enough power could be supplied.  With just the fusion reactor, the Piranha could accelerate at hundreds of kilometers per hour per second.  In 2325, massive capacitors were added to the mix, giving the Piranha a top rating of 790 kilometers per second per second.  It could sustain this sort of thrust for only twenty minutes before the capacitors were drained.  The capacitors can recharge from the fusion reactor, but it takes three hours.  The Piranha carries a small matter/antimatter charge which can give the capacitors an extra five minutes in one second, but it's a one-shot deal and antimatter is scarce in the Prefecture's armories.
Prototypes of the Piranha featured Magnetic shielding purchased from the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], but the Phoenix Project produced shields with similar properties that could be built with [[Terran]] technology.  A Plasma barrier was added to the protective mix, making the Piranha effectively immune to outdated laser weapons.
Originally, the Piranha was armed with Lasers and PPCs.  The Piranha was intended to be an ammunition-free fighter.  The new Piranha is armed with two Large Neutron Cannons, which are effectively ammo-free, but also carries two Hephaestus Cannons.  These heavy cannons are superb for ground strafing, but are usually for anti-bomber attacks.
For heavier weapons, the Piranha carries four Hard Points, two Medium and two Heavy.  Small missiles, gun-pods, and special equipment can be carried on these Hard Points.
The only true failure of the original Piranha was its armor.  The Nitinol alloy created by Fryga Metalworks performed exactly as it was supposed to, but metal armor was simply no longer useful against advanced weaponry.  Bugger plasma and acids simply melted it off the frames.  The only reason the Piranha project succeeded was because the fighter performed so well that it rarely got hit at all.  The new Boron-Nitride Composite Armor is thousands of times more effective, though hot plasma and corrosive acids will eventually eat through.  Nitinol alloy is doing well in the private sector, where its self-repairing properties make it popular for non-combat vehicles and machines.
While the gravity cavitation drive and impulse engine array gives the Piranha excellent maneuverability, it is difficult--practically impossible--for an un-augmented human pilot to bring out the full potential and operate the Piranha at peak efficiency.  [[Taviridis Somarchada]] sold the [[Prefecture]] the AI software necessary to perform the most advanced feats of combat maneuverability.  With the software augmenting the pilot's abilities, the Piranha is among the best fighters in the galaxy, even rivaling the cyborg-piloted [[Ixoarchada]] models.

Normally, the Piranha carries two Hound dogfighter LRMs and two Wren anti-bomber LRMs.  The Hound first became popular when the Piranha was built.  Back then it was known as the Spacehound.  It is still an unmatched missile for fighter-to-fighter combat

Wrens are much more efficient at anti-bomber combat than other weapon.  Unfortunately, they are almost completely worthless against quick Bugger flyers, which can simply dodge at the last second.  When expecting combat with Volucris, Piranha pilots usually carry a second pair of Hounds.
The Piranha's Electronic Warfare variant carries two equipment pods.  One carries ECM gear, to cloak the presence of the fighter and jam homing missiles.  The other pod carries an Active Probe system, which unlike most sensors actively "pings" to locate enemies.  Most gravity sensors simply look for gravity fields.
It also carries two ALARMs, Automatic Long-Range Anti-Radar Missiles.  The common term is ALARM Reflex Missile.  When the ALARM Reflex Missile detects probing of any sort, it fires off a missile immediately.  Usually an ALARM-equipped Piranha is uncommon.  Pilots prefer to fight.
The Valkyrie Mk.II and Bloodhawk are best suited for ground attack and supporting ground forces, but Piranhas can handle the job too.  The ordinance carried for ground attack is changed to some pretty heavy weapons.

For attacking fortified installations, the Piranha carries two Cobra SRMs, which are designed to avoid anti-missile systems and home in on stress points.  It also carries Shrapnel MRMs for taking out clusters of infantry.
Prototype Piranhas were tested against the Volucris in the Mirach Campaign, which took place in February of 2324.  The redesign and redeployment of the Piranha took two months, and full-powered Piranhas hit the front-line fighter squadrons in April of the same year.
Modern production systems are reminiscent of the war production of the USA during World War II.  Factories can churn out Piranhas at a rate of ten per day.  Assuming that production continues at a good rate, the Piranha will completely replace the Jackal and Sparrow Hawk as a space superiority fighter.

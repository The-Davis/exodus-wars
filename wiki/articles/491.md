== Events of 491AD ==

Fifteen years of preparation culminates in the arrival of the first colony ships to the [[Bollaria System]].  The ships arrive and set down in their predetermined landing zones, cleared years in advance by automated factories.

The worlds of [[Drisonir]] and [[Nielstra]] are born.  Each colony ship’s landing is the founding of most of the major [[Iridi]] on these planets today.

Army Regulars make up about a third of the infantry forces within the Commonwealth Army Command.  These heavy soldiers are more geared towards direct combat than their lighter Riflemen counterparts.  

The standard Army Regular is outfitted with a heavy Prudenski Composite powered-armor suit.  The use of assisted motion servos on the heavy armor enables an Army Regular to operate unhindered despite the suit. 

Army Regulars carry a large amount of gear in within their armor suit.  Additional ammunition, grenades, and high-explosives are carried.  During longer campaigns, the armored suits can be equipped with larger back-packs for carrying rations and campaign gear.  

[[Image:ColonialArmyInfantry.jpg]]

The helmet of an Army Regular is an armored extension of the suit containing built-in communications gear.  The helmet has an opaque blast-screen protecting most of the face (a lighter armored layer can extend up from the suit if needed), and inside the blast-screen is a head-up display.  The HUD can display a host of tactical information gained from the few sensors on the suit, display direct visual feed from the regular's rifle, and even allow the suit operator extensive night-vision capabilities.  A sealed helmet can also be equipped, allowing the Army Regular to operate in environments with non-breathable gas, as well as in vacuum. 

Army Regulars use the same standard-issue Commonwealth Watson Rifle as the Guard's Riflemen.  The enhanced HUD in a Regular's helmet can display more information from the Saidia Optics OC-6 sight. 

Army Regulars are employed as the primary combat soldiers in the Commonwealth Army Command.  Most lighter duties are left to the Guard's Riflemen, while Army Regulars focus on support of mechanized units in the field, front-line combat, and heavy urban/CQB assault and defense roles.

The Rank system of the [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr]] is a simple one, with Wehrmon and Kihtan ranks.

==Wehrmon==
Wehrmon is any rating below that of a Kihtan officer. The term includes non-Kihtan officers. Though it is the lowest of the ranks, it is important. The Wehrmon are the core of ship crews and maintenance teams that keep the [[Lorithos]] war machines running.

*Seghra - The Lorithos rank of Seghra (W1) is held by all unintelligent robotic units, for the purpose of organization only. All Seghra classified robots lack the capacity to give orders.
*Somur - Somur is a rank that can be held by either a human or an intelligent robot in the Lorithos forces. A human Somur is usually a non-combat position, and is typically assigned to robot or starship maintenance. Somur do not have a rank emblem.
*Rehg Maht - A Rehg Maht is a human rank, and typically is responsible for subsystems in starships, gun turrets, and similar jobs. A Rehg maht wears a black crescent moon.
*Maht - A Maht is a Senal Orga level supervisor, in charge of fighter maintenance for the group. A Maht wears two black crescent moons.
*Suraf - A Suraf is a Worsas level supervisor. A Suraf wears three black crescent moons.
*Ara Suraf - An Ara Suraf is a Kruz Orga level supervisor. An Ara Suraf wears four black crescent moons.
*Breg Suraf - A Breg Suraf is a Kihtor level supervisor. A Breg Suraf wears four black crescent moons and a silver sun.
*Zuura Suraf - A Zuura Suraf is a Teska Nok level supervisor. A Zuura Suraf wears four black crescent moons and two silver suns.
*Ersa Suraf iv Suvak - The Ersa Suraf iv Suvak (usually just known as the Ersa Suraf) is the direct aide to the [[Hishai]]. There is only one Ersa Suraf in the [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr|Ailen Suvak]], and his main duty is to act as the [[Hishai]]'s advisor in understanding the Wehrmon ranks. The Ersa Suraf wears four black crescent moons and three silver suns.

==Kihtan==
Kihtan officers are given formal orders from the Prekohr and the Hishai, granting them their rank and privaledges. This carries a great deal of honor and responsibility. Any Kihtan is expected to fight, think, and lead.

*Urta So - An Urta So is usually a low rank, in charge of a specific area of a starship, or a navigator/gunner on a fighter. An Urta So wears a gold sun with rays.
*Urta De - An officer holding the rank of Urta De is a pilot of a fighter or bomber. An Urta De wears a gold sun with rays and a silver moon.
*Krel Mag - A Krel Mag is second in command of a Senal Orga. A Krel Mag wears a gold sun with rays and two silver moons.
*Jigor - A Jigor commands a Senal Orga, or is the third in command of a starship. A Jigor wears a gold sun with three silver moons.
*Urta Kasoff - An Urta Kasoff is second in command of a starship. An Urta Kasoff wears a gold sun with three silver moons and a black crescent moon.
*Kasoff - A Kasoff is in command of a starship. A Kasoff wears a gold sun with three moons and two black crescent moons.
*Ortun - An Ortun commands a Kruz Orga. An Ortun wears two gold suns.
*Urta Resun - An Urta Resun coordinates the air defenses of a [[Kihtor]]. An Urta Resun wears two gold suns and a black moon.
*Resun - A Resun commands a Teska Nok. Resuns report directly to the Hishai. A Resun wears two gold suns and two black moons.
*Hishai - The [[Hishai]] is the supreme commander of all [[Lorithos]] forces. The [[Hishai]] wears three gold suns and the [[Lorithos]] emblem.

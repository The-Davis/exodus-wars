The "Burning World," or Igneus Orba, represents one of the greatest engineering achievements of the [[Anthronoris Empire]].  Constructed to be used as a final refuge during the [[Sundering]], the Igneus Orba is in essence a collection of three planet-sized black bodies coated with a lattice of an advanced metamaterial.  The basic idea behind the Igneus Orba is to create a constellation of hollowed planets in orbit of a star, with the orbit being entirely within the star's corona.  This makes them undetectable to outsiders and inaccessible without particularly strong particle shielding.  Three planets are placed in precise orbits so that their gravitation effect on the star cancel each other out, thus making it very unlikely that an outside observer will notice their presence via gravitational effects on the star.  Their use of metamaterials and black body nature preclude any other form of detection.   

Whereas the period for each of the three planets is absurdly short, taking only hours to complete one orbit, the inhabitants take no notice as their frame of reference is internally focused and artificial instead of external.  This allows for the observance of a 100,000 time-unit standard day as it has been defined by the Remnant and the Anthronoris Empire for millennia before it.  

Access to the interior of the planets is provided by enormous louvers, which allow entire star ships to pass through the outer layer of the planet to the hollow core.  The area immediately outside the louvers, which is tidally locked to facing the core of the star, is protected by immensely powerful shielding creating a bubble for ships to shelter for shorter stays not requiring access to the planet's core.  

The core of each planet is home to a tiny artificial star and sun shield.  The sun shield is a semi hemispherical construction which orbits the microstar to create the effects of day and night to the inhabitants of the planet.  The inner surface of the sun shield, which always faces the microstar, is constructed with another lattice of advanced metamaterial to both prevent refection of the microstar's radiation back upon the day side and collect solar energy.  The outer surface of the sun shield, which always faces the night side of the planet, is the hub of all construction, manufacturing, and star ship activity for the planet.  Great shipyards, titanic garrisons, and numerous factories are built into the structure, and the outer surface is always abuzz with activity.  

Resource collection for the planet is achieved by siphoning gases and heavy metals from the star which the planets orbit.  Great care is taken to not exceed the normal rate of mass decay expected for the star.

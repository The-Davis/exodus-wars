The '''Master Plan''' was drafted by [[Pax Anthronoris]] during the [[Sacrum Bellus]] to eliminate all alien life, and particularly covered dealing with the stubborn resistance of the [[Ngrligru]]. It was planned in three phases, with the ultimate goal of eradicating the perceived alien threat to humanity and securing more territory for the [[Anthronoris]].

==The First Plan==

The original plan is believed to have been developed by [[Argus Shadar]] and [[Hayden Lupus]], though the only known surviving records from the era come from the archives of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. After the results of the first phase were observed, the final result was heavily modified.

===The First Phase===

The [[Anthronoris]] had nearly been halted in their prosecution of the [[Sacrum Bellus]] by the determined resistance of the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]]. The fierce [[Ngrligru]] warriors defended themselves and the other [[races]] alligned against [[Pax Anthronoris]].

The [[Anthronoris]] conquered the science of biology eons before launching the [[Sacrum Bellus]]. With the development of the "[[Sin Terminus]]", they knew how to literally conquer death itself. Using their limitless knowledge, the [[Anthronoris]] began creating a biological weapon for use against the [[Ngrligru]]. This weapon, the "[[Njaa|Final Solution]]", was intended to destroy the [[Ngrligru]]'s ability to digest plant matter and make them crave the flesh of their own kind. With an insatiable hunger for their own kind, the [[Ngrligru]] would only be a threat to themselves.

===The Second Phase===

The second phase of the Master Plan called for the securing of the [[Orion Arm]]; an area which was already secured, scoured of alien life in the last several centuries of the [[Sacrum Bellus]]. The [[Ngrligru]] had yet to fight their way into the [[Orion Arm]], but the [[Anthronoris]] were unsure if they could keep it that way.

[[Pax Anthronoris]] would deploy a large fraction of its Legiars to the [[Orion Arm]]. The initial plan was to cut off [[Ngrligru]] access to the [[Orion Arm]] by holding key points near the [[Galactic Core]], in the region known today as the [[Teeth of Orion]]. Once these points were under [[Anthronoris]] control, the Legiars in the [[Sagittarius Arm]] would systematically move towards the [[Teeth of Orion|Teeth]], driving the few remaining [[Ngrligru]] ahead of them and into the waiting defenses established in the [[Teeth of Orion|Teeth]].  

In typical [[Anthronoris]] fashion, the objective of the sweeping Legiars was to ensure that no [[Ngrligru]] would remain behind the [[Anthronoris]] march to the [[Galactic Core|Core]], and so each [[Ngrligru]] planet encountered was to be bombarded into molten slag. These tactics would guarantee an [[Anthronoris]] victory: so long as future habitability was not a factor, a lone ship could neutralize the target. The [[Ngrligru]] were thought to be reluctant to respond in kind against [[Anthronoris]] worlds because they would be too occupied with devouring their own kind.  

===The Third Phase===

With the [[Orion Arm]] secured and the [[Sagittarius Arm]] swept clean of [[Ngrligru]] resistance, the [[Sacrum Bellus]] campaign would continue on its way, wiping out the [[Mtomigru]], [[Gorhamut]], and [[Sirunaki]].

==Unexpected Mutation==

The mutation of the "[[Njaa|Final Solution]]" was unexpected. It changed into a plague now known to the [[Ngrligru]] and [[Mtomigru]] as the [[Njaa]], and threw the [[Ngrligru]] into a ravenous frenzy. They turned on their enemies and allies alike, unable to restrain themselves in the face of insatitable hunger. The [[Mtomigru]], [[Gorhamut]], and [[Sirunaki]] were unprepared for such a drastic change and were hit hard by the [[Ngrligru]] onslaught. The lesser [[races]] of the [[Gorhamut]]'s alliance were utterly consumed. Even [[Anthronoris]] worlds began falling at unbelievable rates. The [[Sundering]] had begun. An opportunity was seen in this disaster.

==The New Plan==

With the galaxy engulfed in a war so fierce that it eclipsed the [[Sacrum Bellus]], the [[Anthronoris]] saw the [[Njaa]] as an opportunity. Although the bulk of [[Pax Anthronoris]] would be lost, the [[Anthronoris]] realized that the [[Milky Way]] could be cleansed of all competition in the process. The Master Plan was modified.

The new Master Plan called for the total sacrifice of all [[Sagittarius Arm]] holdings in favor of devoting their remaining resources to secure the [[Orion Arm]]. With a concerted effort, the [[Orion Arm]] could be made safe from the [[Ngrligru]] threat, or at least would be entirely cleansed of life so as to not attract Ngrligru attention. Hidden cells of [[Anthronoris]] could be seeded throughout undeveloped worlds in the [[Orion Arm]] to rebuild [[Pax Anthronoris]] after the [[Ngrligru]] consumed themselves.  

It was thought that with enough success in cleansing the coreward systems, the [[Ngrligru]] might not even notice entire [[Anthronoris]] [[:Category: Star Systems|systems]] of [[Anthronoris]] surviving intact in the rimward areas. If this held true, these [[:Category: Star Systems|systems]] would be tasked with securing the planned [[Sagittarius Arm]] [[Habitat]]s.     

===The First Phase===

[[Pax Anthronoris]] began deploying a large fraction of its remaining Legiars to the [[Orion Arm]] as initially planned.

As the Legiars neared the [[Teeth of Orion]], several Legiars under Legant [[Argus Shadar]] met stiff resistance from an [[Ngrligru]] fleet. Hopelessly outnumbered, [[Argus Shadar|Shadar]] realized that the [[Galactic Core|Core]] choke points could still be secured using a tactic he had developed millenia before.

If he was doomed to lose his [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]]s, then he was going to get the most out of their destruction. Forcing the battle in an usually dense cluster of stars, he deployed his Praetorias contrary to usual doctrine. The catastrophic energies unleashed in their destruction would be on such a titanic scale that [[hyperspace]] in the area would be scarred forever.

With the massive levels of energy at their disposal the [[Anthronoris]] had no need to skim the [[Galactic Core]] when travelling between spiral arms.

Even in defeat, the first phase would succeed in blocking travel between the [[Orion Arm]] and the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. The [[Teeth of Orion]] were created, forged in the deaths of hundreds of thousands of [[Ngrligru]] and [[Anthronoris]], and thousands of warships.

===The Second Phase===    

In the depths of the [[Sagittarius Arm]], the "chaser" Legiars were not faring so well. They were moving coreward towards the [[Orion Arm]] as planned, but were taking horrendous losses to the [[Ngrligru]]. Along the way, they discovered several [[Anthronoris]] holdouts and evacuated as many as they could. When they were briefed on the revised Master Plan, they were reassigned to distracting the [[Ngrligru]] while the emperor's [[Habitat]]s in the [[Sagittarius Arm]] were completed.

===The Third Phase===

With the [[Void World]]s complete and the [[Orion Arm]] secured, the [[Anthronoris]] enacted the Third Phase. Those who were selected for movement to the [[Void World]]s were given an additional genetic modification, one which slowed the reproduction rate to one which the [[Void World]]s could sustain indefinitely without need for additional territory. Those remaining outside of the [[Void World]]s fought on to maintain the illusion that the [[Void World]]s did not exist--in fact, most [[Anthronoris]] had no idea they existed. The refugees within the [[Void World]]s watched as their "eternal empire", [[Pax Anthronoris]], was consumed by the [[Ngrligru]].       

In the [[Orion Arm]], several [[Ngrligru]] warbands still roamed the stars. They were hunted down at great cost to the Legiars in the region. Then something unexpected happened.  [[Meronir Tronit]], a noble with no claim of the throne of [[Pax Anthronoris]], decided to forge his own empire. As one of the few that knew the secret of the [[Void World]]s, he cut off all contact with other [[Anthronoris]] survivors, dooming them to destruction or primitive survival.  

He kept nine worlds under his protection. He told the people of [[Sepulare]] and the other worlds that [[Pax Anthronoris]] was gone forever, and that their future was in their own hands.

[[Meronir Tronit]] erased all references to the Master Plan and the [[Void World]]s, and replaced it with stories and legends. The fundamental institutions of [[Pax Anthronoris]] were systematically removed, replaced with the ideals of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. The [[Void World]]s were now a forgotten memory, their occupants abandoned by the Legiars that were meant to rescue them.

===The Fourth Phase===

Realizing that help was no longer coming from the [[Orion Arm]], the occupants of the [[Void World]]s began to think of themselves as the last [[Remnant]] of [[Pax Anthronoris]]. The [[Ngrligru]] had not consumed themselves as hoped, so conventional action would have to be taken.

The [[Remnant]] organized themselves and began developing technologies which would allow them to safely leave the [[Void World]]s.  Within a few centuries, the [[Remnant]] began sending out "seeder missions", planting deep-cover families on the surviving worlds, knowing that the [[Remnant]] would need to rearm and reunite the sundered [[races]] of humanity for any hope of victory.

Great observation networks were set up to monitor the development of the survivors.  When necessary, concepts would be clandestinely reintroduced by the various deep-cover families.  The [[Remnant]] would secretly work to rebuild each [[race]] while raiding [[Ngrligru]] targets of opportunity. Periodically, expeditions would be sent out to test the resolve of the survivors. When the time was right, they will raise [[Pax Anthronoris]] from the ashes of history, rebuild the eternal empire and destroy the [[Ngrligru]] once and for all.

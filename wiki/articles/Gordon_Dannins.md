'''Gordon Dannins'''
*Date of Death: June 22, [[2316]]
*Place of Birth: [[Theia]]
*Place of Death: [[Cronus]]
*Allegiance: [[United Earth Alliance]]

''Gordon Dannins'' was an Admiral in the [[United Earth Alliance]] defense forces, under the command of General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]]. Dannins was dispatched with a sizeable cruiser force to try to cripple the Imperial invasion force on [[Cronus]].

Dannins was somewhat successful, but while he was withdrawing a lucky shot caused catastrophic decompression to the bridge of his ship, the UEAS ''Haven'', killing him and his command crew. The rest of the crew was able to limp home to [[Theia]].

The Sirunaki are an interesting alien race, much more "alien" than the [[Ngrligru]] or [[Gorhamut]] given that they do not easily fit a definition of class or even phylum by human standards.

===Appearance===

Sirunaki average 2.2M in height, and 90KG in weight. Sirunaki are thin, even gaunt in their build. Sirunaki have a humanoid endoskeleton, which is light and thin, but strong. They also have a partial exoskeleton, which varies in thickness over various parts of their bodies. This exoskeleton is shed every few months or so. Sirunaki muscles are wiry and tough, and are clustered around areas with no exoskeleton which gives them room to move more easily.

The chest region of a Sirunaki is very bony, fully enveloped both by a thin portion of its exoskeleton and by barely subdermal ribs and bony plates. The chest is almost rigid, barely allowing enough expansion to breathe.

The limbs of Sirunaki are long and lanky, but their exoskeletal plates make it more comfortable to keep their arms slightly bent than straight out, and their legs bowed. Despite their extreme height, Sirunaki tend to be hunched over.

Sirunaki hair is thick, almost bony, and very sharp at the tips. It grows slowly over the years. Young Sirunaki are born with hair less than a centimeter long, and soft. As they age, it hardens and grows. By the time a Sirunaki is fully mature, their hair sticks straight out from the back of their head, eventually reaching lengths of 25cm or more, at which point it stops growing due to the weight on their heads. Sirunaki can trim their hair if they want, but it is so hard and thick that it is difficult.

===Respiratory System===

The Sirunaki have long, thin lungs which stretch from their neck to the base of their abdomen. The lungs are a unique tissue substance which is common to most forms of life on the Sirunaki homeworld, but has not been seen so far anywhere else. Sirunaki, like most other organisms native to their homeworld, are facultative anaerobic lifeforms: they can use a variety of atmospheric gasses as the final electron receptor for chemical energy generation. Sirunaki can breathe oxygen, nitrogen and nitrates, carbon dioxide, manganese, and iron. While several of these electron receptors are solid matter in temperatures Sirunaki can survive in, they can inhale powdered substances and survive without breathing in at all for extended periods of time. Inhaling water will drown a Sirunaki like it will drown a human, but artificial resuscitation must be done within seconds or the water will do permanent damage to their lungs.

Sirunaki inhale through their nose. Their trachea is only connected to their nose, and not to their mouth.

===Metabolic System===

Sirunaki have a row of sharp teeth for tearing meat, plant matter, and rock. Their teeth are firmly embedded in their jaws, which are so strong they can lift twice their body weight with their mouths alone. Solid food is sent to their first somach, which stores it, then rocks are used to help grind it down. The Sirunaki food stomach does not have acid or similar enzymes to break down food.

Sirunaki have two proboscis-like appendages which they use to drink water, which is passed straight to their second stomach. The second stomach takes food ground up in the first, and begins to mix water and stomach enzymes to break down the minerals for use in the body. Afterwards, the intestines take over in a process similar to human digestion.

Likewise, their waste disposal systems are extremely adaptive: Sirunaki can dispose of any toxin through exhaling, solid waste, urine, saliva, or by forming a necrotic cyst on their body which they simply break off and discard. Sirunaki have six different liver-like detoxifying organs in their bodies: it is practically impossible to poison a Sirunaki.

===Senses===

Sirunaki are very photosensitive. They do not like bright light, and do not do well in the light. Direct sunlight retards their ability to detoxify, breaks down the cells of their exoskeletons, and quickly overheats them. Sirunaki do not sweat, and can only lose excess body heat through radiating heat in a cool area, or by rapidly flushing water out of their bodies through necrotic cysts, which is very painful and will leave a Sirunaki scarred until they molt.

Sirunaki can see very well in the infrared spectrum, and can hear in what a human would consider an ultrasonic frequency. They also have a refined sense of smell and are better at scent distinction than most canine species.

===Lifespan===

Sirunaki usually live 70 to 80 years. Sirunaki females usually lay a clutch of two or three eggs every one or two years. A Sirunaki child hatches fully developed, except for their exoskeleton which will not harden until they reach 6 or 7 years old.

===Reproduction===

Sirunaki males and females are almost equals in strenth, agility, and propensity to fight or flee. Their egg-laying gestation is largely credited for this: their eggs do not require care while gestating. Sirunaki females do not lactate to feed their young. Instead, both males and females gather food for their young.

The behavioral similarities, combined with the fact that Sirunaki reproductive organs are internal for both sexes, makes it almost impossible for a human observer to tell if a Sirunaki is male or female, unless the Sirunaki is a gravid female preparing to lay eggs. [[Ngrligru]] can tell the difference by smell, and of course Sirunaki do not have difficulty telling each other apart.

Unlike [[Ngrligru]] and [[Gorhamut]], which have mating seasons and show little interest in each other otherwise, Sirunaki are very affectionate to their mates. Like humans, Sirunaki mate both for procreation and recreation.

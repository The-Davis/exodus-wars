''Matthias "One-Wing" Carter'' (born September 13, [[2059]]) is one of the pilots made famous by the [[Second Exodus War]]. Like [[Emil Torsc]], who he served under in [[Scimitar Squadron]], Carter has a reputation for cruelty. Unlike [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]], Carter actually deserves this reputation.

=== The First Exodus War ===

Matthias Carter was a [[:Category:Starships|starship]] navigator during the [[First Exodus War]], and saw little of the fighting other than piloting his ship from place to place. He had a natural talent for navigating in space, and could make starships practically dance on command. Despite his talent, he was packed up and shipped off with the rest of the [[Torimur]] exiles.

=== Project Raven ===

When the [[Torimur]] exiles returned to the [[Sol Sector]] and took covert control of the [[Alpha Centauri System]], Carter was hand-picked to be one of the "Raven's Wings" test pilots in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s "Project Raven."

During the project's test of the YF-4 prototype of the [[F-4 Raven]], Carter suffered an ammunition explosion during atmospheric reentry. His prototype, F04-Y03, lost a wing in the explosion and nearly entered a fatal tailspin. Carter was able to correct the flight path and bring the prototype in for an incredibly non-explosive crash landing. The feat earned him the nickname "One-Wing", which stuck with him throughout his career.

=== Second Exodus War ===

After leaving the Raven's Wings and becoming a flight instructor, Carter was assigned to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s Air Arm, under the command of [[Emil Torsc]]. Carter was given the Navy rank of Captain.

As a member of [[Scimitar Squadron]], Carter was often in the front lines, and racked up an impressive 89 fighter kills.

=== The Imperial Civil War ===

Carter's loyalties were to [[Torimur]]'s ideals of independence; not to [[Lucius Black]]'s imperialism. Despite his cruel and often sadistic tendencies, Carter was the model of effeciency as a commander and a pilot. He joined the [[Prefecture]] in the struggle, and was given his own squadron in the Prefecture Air Force.

[[Image:Matthias Carter - Looks can be deceiving.png|thumb|right|300px|Matthias Carter relaxes: looks can be deceiving.]]

He was busted down a few ranks after committing several acts of insubordination and even a few minor war crimes. He was discharged from the Air Force and stripped of his rank and pension, but allowed to continue flying with the rank of Lieutenant in the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]'s Air Arm. Since his disciplinary hearing and sentencing, the now Lieutenant Commander Carter has become a model officer, if a little gruff and very egotistical. Carter serves as [[Charlene Rearden]]'s second in command.

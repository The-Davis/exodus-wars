'''Fii Huern''' was the ruling family of the [[Lorithos]] from the dawn of its history until the [[24th Century]].  In the 24th Centuary, the last Huern [[Prekohr]], [[Dirken Huern]], was succeeded by [[Siovar Cai]], leading [[Fii Cai]] to rise to dominance.

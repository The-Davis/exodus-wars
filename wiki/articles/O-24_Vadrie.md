The O-24 Vadrie was developed as the primary air and space fighter of Huern-loyalist forces during the Burning War era. Today, they are the premier patrol and interdiction/interception fighters in the Lorithos ASTF. Lorithos call fighters "Oran." The Vadrie was the first such design capable of space flight. All previous Oran are lost to the ages.

The Vadrie survived the Burning War and has remained a staple light fighter in the Lorithos ASTF arsenal. Its fixed armament consists of two Light Blaster Cannons. These relatively low-powered cannons are mainly useful against ground craft and other light fighters. Six missile hardpoints provide heavy firepower against heavy fighters, bombers, or gunships.

The Vadrie utilizes a Tzaiko Composite armor layer, and also has a thin Garvask shield which protects it from energy weapons and offers limited protection against cannons and missiles.

Lorithos fighters like the Vadrie are misfits, in a way. Unlike most modern fighters, their engines push from the rear, instead of accelerating every part of the ship at once. The drive system is quite powerful, and has no emission. It receives its power from the Hydrogen Engine. Vadries must refuel, but only rarely--usually once per week, even when flying every day.

Like all Lorithos fighters and bombers, the Vadrie also has excellent atmospheric maneuvering. Its sleek, swing-wing design and squat profile give it excellent atmospheric properties. After the Burning War ended, Vadries were still available in great quantities. They were the kings of space combat until the Milrok was built, and most pilots knew how to fly them likes aces. The Vadrie was an obvious choice to keep as an interceptor.

The ASTF keeps Vadries on all of its carriers, and in the Hightowers. The interceptor is ill-suited for ground support, but can keep sky and space clear for Eituks and transports.

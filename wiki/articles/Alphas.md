The '''Alphas''' were a propaganda and information dissemination organization that worked for the [[Onyx Empire]]. Officially, they answered to no one but [[Lucius Black]]. 

The Alphas were barely known to anyone outside of the upper echelons of the power base of the [[Onyx Empire]]. The Alphas were responsible for infiltrating and replacing multiple tiers of society within the [[United Earth Alliance]] during the build-up before the [[Second Exodus War]].

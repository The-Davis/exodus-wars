The '''Kalmar System''' is a star system with three planets, moons, and thousands of asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Tau Ceti, the planets are [[Kalmar]], [[Kelar]], and [[Sulmar]]. Only [[Kalmar]] is orbited by a moon.

==Stellar Navigation==

Tau Ceti is the Tau Ceti System's only star. Tau Ceti is a class G8V yellow dwarf. Tau Ceti's heliosphere is usually calm with only a particle windspeed of roughly 1.2 million kilometers per hour. Tau Ceti is a "metal poor" star, which is likely the cause of the Kalmar System having so few planets.

The Tau Ceti System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. The [[Sol System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], and [[Alpha Centauri System]] are considered close neighbors of Kalmar.

===Kalmar===
[[Kalmar]] (0.85AU) is the closest planet to Tau Ceti. It is slightly larger than [[Terra]], and has low average surface temperature, with polar caps covering 15% of the of the surface. It has one moon, [[Sulman]]

===Kelar===
[[Kelar]] (6.05 AU) is roughly 20 times the size of [[Kalmar]]. It has a thin hydrogen atmosphere, and a weak gravitational field. It appears brown and orange to the eye, with yellow streaks.

===Sulmar===
[[Sulmar]] (8.0 AU) is twice the size of [[Kelar]] and is much more dense. It has a thick hydrogen and helium atmosphere. [[Sulmar]] is mostly orange to the eye.

==History==

Tau Ceti was explored early in the [[21st Century]], and was the second star visited by the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The [[IEV-2 Leif Erikson|UEAS ''Leif Erikson'']] first charted the system on March 19, [[2023]]. The first colony was established on [[Kalmar]] on June 21, [[2027]], establishing the city of [[Lethminster]].

In 2316, the planet was conquered by the [[Onyx Empire]]. The world was liberated by the Titanians in [[2323]], and eventually joined the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

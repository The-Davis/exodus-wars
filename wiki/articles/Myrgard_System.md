The '''Myrgard System''' is the site of the joint [[Terran]]/[[Ikronin]] colonization project, known as the "Myrdrasil Project".

== Stellar Navigation ==

Myrgard was chosen because it was currently uncharted and off the trade routes. It was hoped that in the event of a catastrophic loss in the [[Tempest War]], which was in full swing at the time, there would be a remnant of both cultures involved in the project.

=== Myrdrasil ===

[[Myrdrasil]] (1.4AU) is the inhabitted world in the system, colonized by the [[Ikronin]] and the [[Prefecture]].

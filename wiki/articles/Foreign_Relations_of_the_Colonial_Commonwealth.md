==International Organizations==

===The Alliance of Planets===

The [[Colonial Commonwealth]] is diametricly opposed to the [[Alliance of Planets]] since the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police]] blockaded their worlds for no apparent reason.

===Council of Independent Nations===

The [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] is a founding member of the [[Council of Independent Nations| Council]]. It maintains trading ties with all other members.

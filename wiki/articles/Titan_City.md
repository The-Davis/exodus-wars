'''Titan City''' is the capital city of [[Theia]], and also of the [[Titan System]]. It served as the regional government headquarters during the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s tenure as sovereign over the system, and remained the capital city when the [[Titan System]] elected to join the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

Titan City is also known as "the unshakeable city" and "Beacon City" for its role in the [[Second Exodus War]], and its importance to the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] against the [[Onyx Empire]].

The BC-1 was derived from an early version of the [[M-23 Light Beam Weapon - "Toaster"|M-23]]. By stripping the [[M-23 Light Beam Weapon - "Toaster"|M-23]] of its turret mounts, flash suppressor, operator's heat shield and similar trappings needed for use in ground warfare, [[Royal Imperial Labs]] technicians began to develop a laser weapon appropriate for use by the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] and [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

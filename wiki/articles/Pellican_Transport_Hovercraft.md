*Speed:  200 KPH
*Armor:  Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields:  Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament: 1 Machine Gun (500 rounds)
*Communications System:  Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Standard 

===Overview===

In the midst of a massive reconstruction program following the Second Exodus War, the Kalidasa Planetary Authority generated a large number of jobs by designing and constructing its own defense forces.  One of the most numerous designs produced was the Pelican Transport Hovercraft.  This design proved to be one of the most reliable and utilitarian designs produced, and has since been adopted, in alternate configurations, by a number of other Kalidasan organizations, including police, fire response, and cargo transport companies. 

[[Image:PellicanTransportHovercraft1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Pelican is not design for combat.  It is equipped with an automated Heavy Machine Gun Turret, but this is mostly for defense.  The Pelican's main role is to move quickly and deploy its cargo where needed.  It ordinarily carries either eight fully equipped KSF Security Troopers, or a pair of Grizzly Field Guns.  Its large cargo bed can accomodate a whole host of other cargos as well. 

With a top-end speed of 200 kilometers per hour, the Pelican relies on its speed more than anything for defense.  It is covered with Boron-Nitride Armor, and has a light shielding system, but is my no means a true combat craft. 

===Deployment===

[[Image:PellicanTransportHovercraft2.jpg]]

Pelicans serve in many roles in the Kalidasa Security Force.  They transport cargo, personnel, and weaponry wherever needed, act as first-response vehicles in disaster relief, and are used additionally as police vans, fire fighting trucks, and cargo transport vehicles.  Their main combat-related role in the KSF is the deployment of KSF troopers into combat.  While they would not usually be fielded in open combat, they are excellent in cities and urban environments, where they can deploy their infantry without exposing themselves to enemy fire.

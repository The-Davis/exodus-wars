The '''Kalidasa Air and Space Corps''' is responsible for all [[:Category:VTOLs|VTOL]], [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]], and [[:Category:Bombers|bomber]] support in the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasan]] military. Using custom-designed fighter-craft, the KASC patrols the system in space, as well as providing aerial patrol and surveillance, as well as [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] support in the event of combat within the system.

==Structure==

The Air and Space Corps is divided into VTOL craft (Air Corps) and [[:Category:Aircraft|Aerospace craft]] (Space Corps). These two groups work together to help secure Kalidasa in the skies or in the stars.

The elegance and power of the Kalidasan aerospace designs is unmatched in most of human space.

==Aircraft==

The [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] call their combat [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] "Lutador." The [[L-3 Aurelius]] is the backbone of their forces, an exquisite patrol fighter with enough firepower and maneuverability to tangle with anything in known space. At longer ranges, the [[L-14 Octavius]] is used, as it can patrol the entire star-system without refueling or rearming. In the event of combat, the [[L-6 Constantine]] heavy strike fighter can be called out.

While not as impressive as their customized aerospace craft, the Kalidasans maintain an excellent [[:Category:VTOLs|VTOL]] corps, with the Cyclone light patrol VTOL performing regular air patrols, and the Tempest coming in to help in the event of heavy combat.

==Traditional==

The term "traditional law" refers to the [[Lorithos]] tort system, which is based on a series of complicated traditions and practices dating back to the days of the [[Dawn Emperor]]. Most contracts and sales of property must be approved by the Prekohr or his local agents, and their say is final. The Prekohr can make a final say in any matter.

A contract that the Prekohr approved was traditionaly considered binding under pain of death. Now that the [[Lorithos]] do not approve of the death penalty, broken contracts usually have a form of enforced servitude as compensation.

==Criminal Law==

[[Lorithos]] criminal law is considered very simple, and somewhat lenient by interstellar standards. A Scale of Light serves as judge and jury in any case, but the only sentence is exile, imprisonment, or forced servitude. On [[Lorithan]], exile is essentially a death sentence, as no one can survive in the wastelands, but officials give sentenced prisoners the supplies and protective gear necessary to reach the nearest outlying settlement.

==Religious Law==

Religious law is the only law the Prekohr can be held to. This type of law is no longer enforced under most circumstances. It is observed and respected by most [[Lorithos]], and it does still officially govern marriage law.

==The Eight-Fold Cornerstone==

When the [[Dawn Emperor]] began his reign over the whole of Lorithos, he laid down the foundation of his empire in eight laws which became known as the eight-fold cornerstone. The number eight became so significant to the [[Lorithos]] that they adopted a base-8 number system.

"Mirdek and Ashtir called by name me, [[Hamur Huern]], the [[Mircan Prekohr]], who feared the Twin Gods, to bring about the rule of righteousness in all the lands."

The First is this, that all men of our world shall consider themselves tenants of the Twin Gods. Shining Mirdek and Gentle Ashtir have blessed [[Lorithan]] with arable land and pasture. Mirdek blesses our crops, and Ashtir gives us rest. While all may dwell in the lands of [[Lorithan]], they must treat the land as if it were sacred, and do not allow it to spoil. No bridge, nor canal, nor tower built upon this land shall be allowed to decay and falter. The work of your hands must be done with pride, for all is done in the names of Mirdek and Ashtir.

The Second is this, that the temples of Mirdek and Ashtir shall never be emptied. At all times a Priest of Mirdek and a Priestess of Ashtir shall offer up praises to the Twin Gods. No priest or priestess shall ever work as laborers, nor shall they partake in the base pleasures of the world. All who live in our world shall tithe to the temples so that the servants of the Twin Gods shall never be wanting.

The Third is this, that property shall solely belong to the one that makes it, until that one disposes of it by sale, lease, barter, gift, dedication, deposit, loan, or pledge. In any disposition of property, there must be a contract, a deed of ownership, and witnesses. A buyer is responsible for the validity of a deed. If one buys goods that are stolen, and the true owner finds him out, the buyer must show his bill of sale and return the items. If the buyer does not show a sale in good faith, he shall be judged a thief, and drowned.

The Fourth is this, that any who owns a house or other dwelling place that wishes to lease it out may do so, but they shall be responsible for all that goes on within that house. A lease cannot be signed unless the home is in good repair, but once it is signed the tenant must strive to keep the home in good repair. At will, a landlord may turn out a tenant, but must make fair compensation if due notice of a grievance is not given.

The Fifth is this, that any who works for another for wages must be fairly compensated for all labors rendered, and must take a share in the fruits of his labors. If a master dismisses a laborer, it must not be without just cause. As a laborer has obligation to a master, so also does a master have obligation to his laborer.

The Sixth is this, that trade and debt must be conducted in honorable fashion. Any free person may offer himself as bond in exchange of a loan for a term of eight years. If at the end of the eight years, the bondsman has not repaid his debt, all of his property must be sold to make good the debt. If even then the debt is not repaid, the bondsman himself shall be forfeit to the debtor, and he shall labor for him until the debt is repaid. Trade likewise shall be the same: if a merchant takes goods on debt, he shall be required to repay all debt on the sale within eight days of the transaction, or his business shall be forfeit to the debtor.

The Seventh is this, that families shall live in peace and honor. A man born under the sun is master of his house, and a woman born under the moon is master of her house. Those born under the sun and those born under the moon shall never intermarry. A man born under the sun may take two wives, and a woman born under the moon may take two husbands. For a marriage to be legitimate, a suitor must approach the master of a house and ask permission to marry. The ceremony must be held at the temple of the Twin Gods, and blessed by the parents of both parties, and the other spouse if the suitor is already married once.

The Eighth is this, that those who participate in immoral or criminal acts shall be accused by the Light of Justice, and tried by the Scales of Light. Once accused, the defendant shall be in flux until proven either guilty or innocent. If the courts cannot come to an acceptable conclusion, the [[Prekohr]] shall rule on the guilt or innocense of the defendant. Any crime of bodily harm to another shall be dealt with by inflicting equal harm to the convicted. A crime of theft, murder, rape, heresy, or bearing false witness shall be dealt with by drowning the convicted.

'''Canada''' was a country occupying most of northern North America, extending from the Atlantic Ocean in the east to the Pacific Ocean in the west and northward into the Arctic Ocean. Canada shared land borders with the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] to the south and northwest.

Canada was a member of the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth of Nations]] until the Canadian parliament approved the signing of the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on January 2, [[2011]].

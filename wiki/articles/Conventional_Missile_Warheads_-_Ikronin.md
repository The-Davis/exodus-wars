[[Ikronin]] missiles are based on the same propulsion system for their cannons, but instead of the launcher providing the energy, the missile itself carries the propulsion and power source.

The warhead on [[Ikronin]] missiles are micro-nukes. The blast yield can be programmed for anything from knocking out a single building to taking out a kilometer-wide area.

There are only two basic types of [[Ikronin]] missiles; cluster missiles and single missiles. Cluster missiles are similar to the bin-fed xRM missiles used by most other civilizations, and single missiles are usually fixed to exterior hard points.

==Klunga Missiles==
Klunga Missiles are very fast, very powerful weapons. They are usually used on hovertanks, but Flyer Corps Aerospace fighters can also use them. Klunga missiles have a maximum range of 100 kilometers.

==Engang Missiles==
Engang Missiles are specialty missiles like the Hounds and ALARMs of the Prefecture. Their maneuvering and guidance systems are much better than the average Klunga missile, and their range is well over 250 kilometers, depending on weather conditions. Engang missiles can carry much more powerful warheads, even antimatter or mass-energy conversion bombs.

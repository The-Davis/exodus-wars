== Events of 2324 ==

===January===
*January 3 - The Terran Planetary Recovery Administration released another report on the status of [[Terra]] in a session of the [[Alliance of Planets High Council]].
*January 8 - [[Andreas Tischler]] hoped to expand the [[Mesarthrim]] [[:Category:Races|race]]'s sphere of influence through diplomacy and trade. The [[Ixoarchada]] leader [[Drimont Mandara]] invited [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]] to a joint technology summit on [[Glengarr]].
*January 9 - [[Andreas Tischler]] and [[Nathan Hinks]] were welcomed by the [[Ixoarchada]] government when they landed on [[Glengarr]]. The [[Ixoarchada]] and [[Mesarthrim]] Technology Summit Continues.
*January 12 - [[Ixoarchada]] and [[Mesarthrim]] Technology Summit Concludes
*January 15 - [[Mesarthrim]] Delegates Head Home From Joint Technology Summit
*January 16 - Rumors of [[Andreas Tischler]]'s Death
*January 20 - [[Uber-General]] [[Andreas Tischler]] Confirmed Dead
*January 21 - Riots on [[Mesar]]: Anti-[[Ixoarchada]] Groups Cry for Blood
*January 22 - [[Taviridis Somarchada]] Response
*January 26 - Investigation Findings Released
*January 27 - Verbal Banter Between Leaders
*January 28 - Alliance of Planets Responds
*January 29 - Culprit Discovered, Truth Found
*January 31 - Treachery Abounds, Hinks Escapes

The fledgling relationship between the [[Ixoarchada]] and the [[Alliance of Planets]] was nearly destroyed when a combined [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and [[Taviridis Somarchada]] operation was botched, resulting in the death of Uber General [[Andreas Tischler|Tischler]]. Despite the tragedy, the culprits were found and peace was restored.

===February===
*February 1 - [[Auellal League]] declares autonomy from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and claims the [[Cinaed System]] as its sovereign territory. All political prisoners on [[Caledon]] are released.
*February 3 - [[Mirach System]] colony project launches with refugees from [[Terra]]. Two cities are built on [[Mirach]], dubbed simply "Colony One" and "Colony Two." Prefabricated structures allow several hundred thousand refugees to be housed safely.
*February 5 - The last remnant of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was found by the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], and its location was revealed to the Alliance of Planets as a gesture of goodwill. The subsequent attack on the [[Empire|Imperial]] fleet truly ended the [[Second Exodus War]], but marked the beginning of the [[Volucris Incursion]].
*February 6 - Fleeing the [[Volucris Swarm]], the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy Second Fleet of the Line|Prefecture Second Fleet]] inadvertently led them to [[Mirach]], where the first ground battle of the [[Volucris Incursion]] took place.
*February 12 - The first wave of [[Volucris]] was defeated by coalition forces with ease, but the second wave was so damaging that the [[Prefecture]] government began making plans to evacuate the two refugee colonies.
*February 18 - The evacuation of [[Mirach]] began under the watchful eye of coalition forces.
*February 19 - Cornered and outnumbered, General [[Travis Carpenter]] calls in a carpet bombardment of [[Mirach]] from the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy Fourth Fleet of the Line|Prefecture Fourth Fleet]], violating the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter]].
*February 23 - The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] publicly admits the existence of the [[Auellal League]] rebellion and declares a state of emergency at home.

===March===

The Terran recovery effort was severely stalled when there was an outbreak of a particularly virulent plague in the North American regions. The entire planet was quarantined to prevent the spread of the virus.

Galactic stability was further threatened by two major events. The Mesarthrim government, reeling at the loss of their beloved leader, crumbled completely. The Volucris pushed their invasion further, by attacking the Prefecture world of Tavou.

===April===

The remains of the Empire, reorganized into the cult-like "Conclave", resurfaced in April. The elite Omega Squadron terrorist unit was revealed to be behind the Terran plague, and attempted to use it to drive a wedge between the Prefecture and the former United Mesarthrim Clans.

===September===
Relative peace was brought to the Mesarthrim sector when the Alliance of Planets Space Police quelled the minor rebellious factions. Only the United Centusi States and the Allied Auellal League remained. Unfortunately, peace talks between these two factions broke down when the presiding ambassador was assassinated--presumably by the Conclave.

*September 1: Space Police Quell Rebellion
*September 11: Prefecture Ambassador Assassinated

===Overview===

The United Earth Alliance was blind to the danger that awaited it for over a decade, but the powers-that-be in the Sirius Cluster could not ignore such threats.  In response to increasing Centauri militarism, the Giuseppe Garibaldi was laid down in 2308, with construction proceeding at a slow pace over the next six years.  It was completed and launched, amid much fanfare, on the first of January, 2315.  Just over a week later, the Imperial Armada invaded the Terran system, and defeated its meager space forces in a matter of hours.  

For the Berlusconi, the Giuseppe Garibaldi was built as a flag vessel for the Naval Guard.  The most advanced warship in the Sirius Territories, the Giuseppe Garibaldi is not only a powerful warship, but also a very advanced command and control vessel, with an extensive sensors array and EW/ECM suite.  It is the key projection of Berlusconi power in the Sirius Territories, and a symbol of the influence of the Berlusconi Territory abroad.  Its power and weaponry is meant as a deterrent against those that would meddle in the affairs of the Sirius Territories.

===Capabilities===

Despite being an advanced sensors warfare platform, the Giuseppe Garibaldi also carries an impressive array of conventional weaponry.  As with most heavy destroyers, cruisers, and flagships, the primary heavy weapons of the Giuseppe Garibaldi are a pair of "Estoc" Relativistic Mass Drivers.  These two cannons, mounted in a pair of massive turrets on either side of the rear of the ship, give the Giuseppe Garibaldi a powerful punch at extreme range, and a distinct tactical advantage over those ships lacking such weapons.

In addition to the pair of Estoc RMDs, six Ashur Cannons are mounted in a pair of turrets on the ship.  A twin-turret is fitted on the belly of the Giuseppe Garibaldi, and a powerful quad-turret is mounted on the foredeck. 

[[Image:NGVGuiseppeGaribaldi2.jpg]]

The Garibaldi is equipped with twenty Kalidasan-made Fast-Tracking Advanced Neutron Cannons, for additional anti-warship (as well as long-range anti-aerospace) duty.  Ten Anti-Aerospace Emplacements provide excellent cover against aerospace craft, as well as excellent point-defense against missiles. 

The most versatile weapons on the Giuseppe Garibaldi are its complement of thirty-two VOTM Tubes.  If it were not for the C&C functions of the Giuseppe Garibaldi, it might more aptly be designated a Missile Destroyer. 

In addition to its own weapons, the Giuseppe Garibaldi carries five squadrons of fighters--usually two squadrons of Uraganos and three squadrons of Stellas Nera.  Additionally, the Giuseppe Garibaldi carries a single squadron of bombers, the hulking Pugnos Morte, for anti-warship and anti-gunship duty.  Various other shuttles, dropships, and transports can also be carried. 

The Giuseppe Garibaldi is outfitted with the best defenses available in the Terran sector.  Boron-Nitride armor, and standard Terran shield array make it well defended against other ships of Terran make.  Beyond these defenses, however, is a vast array of advanced sensors, electronics warfare systems, and electronic countermeasures.  The Giuseppe Garibaldi's advanced sensor array may be the most advanced in all of Terran space.  Where the Berlusconi got these systems is still the subject of much debate. 

===Deployment===

Political wrangling and back-door negotiations kepts the Empire from expanding into the Sirius Cluster during the Exodus War--with one exception.  It was on this occasion in April of 2321 that the Giuseppe Garibaldi first fired its guns in anger, as the Sirius Territories united to destroy an Imperial picket fleet.  It was this show of force that kept Black's ambitions regarding the Sirius Cluster in check. 

[[Image:NGVGuiseppeGaribaldi3.jpg]]

Since 2321, the Giuseppe Garibaldi has not seen any serious combat.  Minor skirmishes amongst the Sirius families hardly counts, and the brief rise in (unauthorized) piracy following the Exodus War has subsided.  The Giuseppe Garibaldi now mainly acts as a symbolic warship, and is used by Alessandro Berlusconi as a personal flagship on important visits.  However, it is still maintained at 100% capability.  Like many of the advanced weapons in the Berlusconi arsenal, its power is in its deterrent capability--the power to prevent a war instead of winning it.

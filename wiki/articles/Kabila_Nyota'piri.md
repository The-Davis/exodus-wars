===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Nyota'piri|History]]

'''Kabila Nyota'piri''' had a brief fling at the top of [[Ngrligru]] society. After the death of [[Ona Raha]], [[Kabila Wingu'fira]] lost its claim to the head of society. Nyota'piri stepped in, bringing with them their shamanistic teachings.

Shamanism was long out of use when [[Ona Raha]] brought science and enlightenment to the [[Ngrligru]], so many warriors were fascinated by the different teachings. Unfortunately for Nyota'piri, the popularity of shamanism died again when the uneducated warriors realized that it had no real power.

Today, Nyota'piri is extremely unpopular in the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]]. They only maintain their high-rank status from their military might. They are indifferent to the idea of war with humanity, listening only to the spirit voices they claim they can hear. If their "guiding spirits" ever tell them its time for war, there is no doubt that Kabila Nyota'piri will be the most ravenous and dangerous enemy of the humanoid races.

===Culture===
Main Article: [[Culture of Kabila Nyota'piri|Culture]]

Kivuli - The Kivuli family leads Kabila Nyota'piri. They are a deeply spiritual family, following shamanistic traditions which they claim predate Ona Raha and his teachings. Despite their religious fervor, they do not hesitate to put the advanced technology of their race to its deadly use.

The first resistance movement against the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] was formed on March 19th, [[2329]] by [[Antol Nik]], [[Joshua Cartör von Fuerst]], and [[Nicholas von Weidenfelt]]. By the time [[Arnold Stadt]] joined the Splinter Cells in [[2332]], two of them would be dead.

==History==
William Camp's takeover of the Centusi States was far from clean, or universally accepted. Antol, Cartör, and Weidenfelt combined forces and began forming splinter cells across Mesathrim space. Each commander continued to control there forces, maintaining their access to high quality military equipment and intelligence.

===Antol Nik===
Antol took the Black Dogs Myt'herra Armored Cohort to the Centusi States early in the Civil War to avoid "sacrificing his men in the Auellal's war". [[Elias Faust]] recognized this and let Antol into the Centusi States inner circle. Antol learned more then he wanted, and began formulating his insurrection. The Black Dogs were sent into combat early in the [[Tempest War]], making Antol's subterfuge difficult. In [[2334]] Antol and the Black Dogs abandoned Bolan, becoming the First Armored Cohort of the Mesarthrim Federation.

===Joshua Cartör von Fuerst===
Cartör was the last General to lay down his arms in the civil war. After two weeks of fighting in William with no news from Stone Bridge, his officers convinced him to surrender his exhausted army to the Auellal League. Cartör refused to accept the Auellal Leagues claim to power, and was held as a prisoner of war until the Centusi States negotiated for his release. Cartör became friends with fellow commander, and helped form the Splinter Cells. Cartör was killed in 2332 by a Prefecture tactical nuclear barrage against his forward operating base on Sindh.

===Nicholas von Weidenfelt===
Weidenfelt served in the Mesarthrim resistance against the Anderung. During the war, he was shot down, and recovered by the Overseers who took his hand and welded a hook in its place - ending his career as a combat pilot. Weidenfelt served as Air Marshall for both the Clans and the Centusi. In [[2330]] Weidenfelt taunted Camp with knowledge of the Splinter Cell to protect Stadt - who still at that point had not joined the Splinter Cells - from being executed by Camp. Weidenfelt's sacrifice was a major blow for the Splinter Cell early on.

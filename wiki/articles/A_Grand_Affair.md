Set late in the first year of the [[Tempest War]], before the [[Reigens]] and [[Ixoarchada]] join the [[Council of Independent Nations]], which is a closely-guarded secret at this point to avoid [[Meroniri]] interference.

by James Davis

=====The Invitation=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Consul Talaris Roi and Prefect Anthony Hadrian sit in a conference room outside the Consul's Sanctuary. Hadrian glowers at Roi, his elbows resting on the table.  "Don't get me wrong, Talaris," he begins.  "I know exactly how much we need antimatter.  My Seminole Class Destroyers and the Marine Corps cannot respond to [[Mesarthrim]] threats without it.  But I really don't think this is necessary."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I see no alternative, Anthony.  The Senators are all on their way, and we will brief them.  It will be up to them to decide." Talaris Roi gently pats Anthony Hadrian's calloused hand with her own, her dark brown skin a striking contrast to his.  "I know you prefer a military solution, but there isn't one.  Let diplomacy handle it this time."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A knock on the door announces the arrival of the Senate Commerce Committee.  Senators Cephas Ugall, Colin Archibald, and Jean Sarnou enter and take their seats.  In the Senate itself, or while meeting with the Curiate, these men would be very stiff and formal.  Dealing with Talaris Roi, a former Senator herself before she was formally elected Consul in August, they are very relaxed, even jovial.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, Talaris, tell us about this new trade agreement." Cephas Ugall leans back in his chair, folding his arms across his chest.  The Senator is a small man of English decent, graying at the temples as he approaches his later middle age, who keeps his mustache and goatee dyed and oiled.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"My dear friends," she smiles.  "Since Prefect Hadrian completed the Norfolk Project, our forces have been able to respond to Mesarthrim incursions in the Tejat District.  Unfortunately, we won't be able to keep that up for very long.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The strength of the most powerful modern technology is antimatter.  We were recently able to retrieve a small amount of antimatter, but our only source has dried up.  Without antimatter and the other forms of exotic matter necessary to refine it into a useable form, we will lose what little tactical advantage we've gained."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, we find new source, or we buy it," Jean Sarnou suggests.  "I fail to see the problem."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The problem, unfortunately, is that antimatter is rare.  It is highly sought after by the more advanced civilizations of the galaxy. Much akin to crude oil reserves of the 21st Century, antimatter is the fuel of choice and marks the barrier between high technology and merely "good" technology.  In essence, the Meroniri and Ixoarchada are "first world" countries, while we are "third world" with only nuclear fusion available to us."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I see..." says Colin Archibald.  "There are many Ixoarchada in my District, but since the war with the Mesarthrim began, they've stopped selling us anything with significant military value."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I thought the Ixoarchada were neutral, but friendly to us?" Ugall frowns.  "Why wouldn't they sell us technology like they used to?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Because they don't give a damn what happens beyond their borders." Hadrian scowls.  "The Ixoarchada say this isn't their war, and they won't support either side.  They seem perfectly content to park over Comur with a fleet, keeping us out of Tirshan entirely."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, we buy it from the Meroniri," suggests Archibald.  "They seem to have more than enough."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You are correct," says Roi. "For centuries, the Meroniri have had the only consistent source of exotic matter, and have been able to make the legitimate claim that they are the only ones with any use for it: the trade of power generation technology is forbidden in the Alliance of Planets Grand Charter. Until we recently developed antimatter reactor technology, no one else had a need for antimatter.  Unfortunately, as great as our need for antimatter is, the Meroniri are refusing to sell their own sources.  They claim a greater need for it themselves and that the sale would violate the spirit of the Charter if not the letter."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Anthony Hadrian chuckles.  "I had my doubts about joining the Alliance because of the trade limitations, but the prospect of allies seemed to outweigh the loss of trade.  How ironic that no one but the merchant Ikronin are willing to help us."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Of course!" Archibald exclaims.  "The Ikronin will sell to anyone, and consider us very close friends.  They'll sell us antimatter."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Roi shakes her head sadly.  "The Ikronin, while fast friends with us and the greatest merchants in the galaxy, have no antimatter to sell. It is simply too hard for them to procure.  They can't sell what they don't have.  But there is another source.  The only source we know of outside of Ixoarchada or Meroniri space is the Reigess Sovereignty--a small, barely known, rarely involved world."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I've never heard of them," says Ugall.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have," retorts Sarnou.  "Only briefly mentioned when I was meeting with Ikronin delegates regarding trade in my District.  The Reigens are...related...to the Ikronin.  Oui?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes Jean," says Roi. "The Reigens are actually closely related to the Ikronin. The Reigens were originally a slow-ship era colony of Kronheim that was forgotten back in the days when Ikronin royalty was deposed.  They discovered star travel on their own, and actually opened trade relations with my people on Tejat before the Empire defeated us.  They've continued to expand their ties with outside worlds, and recently reestablished ties with the Ikronin--an unfortunate move for us.  Relations between the Ikronin and Reigens, while not hostile, are terse."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"How so?" queries Hadrian.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Reigens are a monarchy," explains Roi.  "The Reigen king has a legitimate, if irrelevant claim to the now defunct Ikronin throne.  These strained relations between the Ikronin and Reigens are...difficult to explain.  They don't hate each other, but the Reigens look at the Ikronin as a low-brow pack of merchants, while the Ikronin consider the Reigens an unworthy "elite" group.  They'll work and trade together, but will never be friends."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"See, this is exactly why I'm not too keen on what you're proposing, Talaris," Hadrian scowls.  "The Ikronin are my best shot at winning this war with the Mesarthrim.  We can re-establish the Gates and use those to move our troops around.  It won't be pretty, but they're a powerful ally and not worth upsetting over some antimatter."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I disagree, and so does your entire staff," Roi counters.  "And while you have the authority to make military treaties, trade agreements are the jurisdiction of the office of the Tribune.  I have already consulted Simon Blodgit, and he assures me that a trade agreement with the Reigens will not harm our current alliance with the Ikronin.  I have signed a trade agreement with King Raegos that will give us useable antimatter in enough quantities to supply all of our Fleets for five years.  Two matters remain.  The Senate must ratify it..."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Which will happen," Ugall smiles.  "What else?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We have a party."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The three Senators squint across the table at each other.  Jean Sarnou speaks first.  "A party."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"A ball, in fact.  The King insisted on hosting our dignitaries, along with distinguished guests from other governments."  Roi smiles.  "I expect it will be magnificent.  I haven't been able to participate in an event like this in years.  Life in the Senate, and the Resistance before that didn't allow for such luxury."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, who is invited?" asks Archibald.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Everyone."

=====Welcome to Junheim=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Reigen homeworld, Junheim, is a highly advanced world.  Unlike their Ikronin brethren, they did not suffer a civil war or an ice age.  While it took them many more centuries than the Ikronin to develop faster than light travel, they thoroughly exploited their own star system, colonizing and mining everywhere but Junheim itself, which they kept pristine for living, farming and herding.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The star system is protected by the Bejistät Rihlim Raumsarga, "His Majesty's Royal Navy."  Ever since [[Raegos Direnson]], a younger son of an Ikronin king, led a slow-ship expedition to this world, the royalty has led the population kindly and well.  The Raegos family is highly respected and loved by the Reigen people.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today the skies of Reigess are packed with ships from every known world in human space.  The FDS ''Jerome Black'' is merely one of many ships invited to the "dansa engelska", grand ball, being held in honor of the new deal between the Reigess Sovereignty and the Federated Districts of the Prefecture.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On the surface, the dignitaries are gathering at the Raegos Palats Tillstånd, home of the Raegos family.  It is a sprawling and elegant compound of buildings, parks, and fountains.  The Reigens have constructed a massive runway and hangar attached to the palace for use by the royal family, which has been cleared out for the use of the dignitary landing parties.  Landing first is the Prefecture delegation, escorted by a full squadron of Stukas. Their Skyhawk is accommodated in the hangar itself, while the Stukas are parked out in the yard and tended to by Reigen mechanics.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Anthony Hadrian, resplendent in his black and gold dress uniform, escorts Talaris Roi, equally dazzling in a gold silk bandhini saree with black trim. They lead the delegation of Prefecture dignitaries: Senators from each District, Admiral Marie Sarnou to represent the Galactic Navy, General Patrick O'Neill to represent the Legion Corps, Lieutenant General Anjela Kaleem to represent the Air Force, Brigadier William Duke to represent the Marine Corps, and Norman Byar from the Inquisitors.  Officially, the Prefecture retinue is thirty-six: twelve delegates and two armed bodyguards.  Unofficially, there are a lot more.  Many Inquisitors have come in ahead of the foreign delegations, and slipped unnoticed into various locations throughout the palace.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture delegation is met by a Reigen woman at the exit to the hangar.  "Honored guests, welcome to Reigess," she greets them with a smile, bowing low in front of them.  "I am Rakel Lefdottr, and have been appointed your guide and interpreter.  Please, follow me."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bankettsal is a massive room, over two thousand square meters, with a ceiling supported by soaring archways and covered in carvings along the arches.  An enormous mural covers the ceiling, depicting major events in Reigess history: the landing of Raegos, the founding of the first city, the unification with the Juns, the discovery of antimatter, and many other great discoveries and disasters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The entrance is an arched doorway, with its carved wood doors flung open, and guarded on either side by Reigen Likniir soldiers, dressed in dazzling arrays of white and purple, dressed in tabards bearing the Raegos family crest and armed with simple sidearms.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The far wall from the doorway is a raised dais, with thrones for the Raegos family.  The "Bejistät König"--king--is Lorrodi Raegos, and impressive man in his later fifties.  His consort is the "Drottning"--a title for the wife of the king--is Viveka Slanj nee Raegos, a small and frail lady in her early forties.  Their son, the "Fenig"--crown prince--is Jiliur Beskolyda nav Raegos, tall and fair, towering over the average Reigen or Ikronin, barely into his twenties.  The royal family is dressed in elegant clothing, simple in cut but dazzling in design, and Lorrodi wears a platinum circlet set with purple sapphires.  They all rise as heralds begin ushering in and announcing the guests.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture delegation, guided by Rakel, is at the very end of the line.  "As the guests of honor, you will be announced last.  We have many customs, most of which are out of date and rarely used, but for an event like this Bejistät König Raegos insisted.  No shortcuts.  Each delegation is introduced in their own language, with full titles.  They first meet the Bejistät König and the Drottning, then they are personally escorted to their seat by the Fenig.  Everyone in attendance today is held in high regard by the Bejistät König."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Anthony Hadrian sighs.  "This is not my area of expertise, Talaris," he whispers to Roi.  "I have no idea what to do."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You don't need to worry about a thing, Prefect," Rakel cuts in.  "We use ceremony to show our respect to you.  We don't expect you to follow any custom you aren't familiar with."

=====Announcing the Guests=====  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"In the name of Bejistät König Lorrodi Raegos, and the most honored Raegos family, I bid you all welcome to Reigess," a Reigen man in the livery of the Raegos family, bearing an LCD panel with floating text on it, walks into the center of the room between the delegates and the Raegos family.  "I am Arek Leifson, Förebud--herald--of the Raegos family.  The people of Reigess hope that all of you will come to know us as friends.  We favor none, and desire the kinship of all."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Leifson begins reading from his LCD.  "From the Oronus Worker's Coalition, we welcome Director Roger Morris.  Many of our honored guests do not know this honorable man.  Oronus is a small mining operation that has kept its independence from the major conglomerates.  Director Morris was one of the first to come to us with the offer of trade, and has brought great profit to both his workers and the Reigen people.  Welcome, Director, in the name of Lorrodi Raegos."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Roger Morris is a well-dressed but unimposing man, with only three bodyguards accompanying him.  He approaches the throne with no fanfare, but Lorrodi Raegos rises to meet him, warmly greeting Morris with a handshake and a kind word, unheard by the audience.  Morris smiles in response, bows, and takes his seat at the banquet table.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Leifson continues.  "When we first discovered the maze of hyperspace, the Sirius Territories were one of the first organizations to officially recognize us.  For decades, ties with the territorial families of the Sirius Cluster have been a sign of importance and legitimacy.  Many of you are familiar with the Sirius Territories, having dealt with one of the families or with this man: Gregor Blazton the third, the CEO of Blazton Incorporated.  I believe we also have the honor of meeting his wife, Katrina Blazton."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Gregor Blazton is a man rapidly approaching middle age, the strain of running his massive holding company showing in his care-worn eyes and graying temples.  He wears a green sash with the gear sigils of his company across his chest, highlighting a grey silk suit which fits him well.  Katrina is no exceptional beauty: her high cheekbones, dark hair and fair skin show her Russian heritage from her Asanova relatives.  The couple are escorted by six armed bodyguards in the security uniforms of Blazton Incorporated.  They approach the throne, where Gregor and Katrina both greet the Raegos family, then take their seats.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have no doubt that some of you do not know our next guests," says Leifson.  "The Sirius Cluster was colonized by three families of great dignity and strength, stemming from diverse populations of old Terra.  I welcome the "Velikiy Kniaz", Nicholai Asanova.  I welcome the "Ōomi", Takashi Nakamura.  I welcome Padre Alessandro Berlusconi.  These families, with their ties throughout human space, have helped us make great strides in joining this great galactic society."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The three heads of the families have come alone, and are each dressed in impressive, almost outlandish regalia, and unarmed.  Nicholai Asanova approaches the throne first, flourishing a cape behind him with an outlandish bow.  Takashi Nakamura bows stiffly to each member of the Raegos, his proper and traditional manners icy compared to the showmanship of Asanova.  Last of all Alessandro Berlusconi approaches the Raegos family, wrapping Lorrodi in a huge bear hug and kissing him on each cheek.  The three patriarchs each take their seats, casting each other sidelong glances.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Kalidasa was greatly victimized by the [[Onyx Empire]], despite the fact that they were never part of the late United Earth Alliance.  The people of Brazil colonized this pristine world on their own, without aid or advice from the Americans that eventually came to dominate Terra and its primary colonies.  They were a peaceful people, and the Empire turned them into a manufacturer for its weapons of war.  We have not had the pleasure of dealing with this great nation--the Kalidasa Planetary Authority--but the Reigen people look forward to a long and prosperous trade relationship with Presidente Evaristo Silveira."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Kalidasan retinue is actually over a dozen, all dressed identically.  Evaristo Silveira is well protected by very low profile security.  Despite that Silveira and Lorrodi have never met, they greet each other warmly: the length of time for knowing one another seems to have no influence on how the Reigens treat their guests: all are friends, and all are welcome.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We had many years of history to catch up on when we discovered hyperspace travel.  We must give special condolences to the Colonial Commonwealth for all the pain and hardship they have been through.  Their worlds were taken multiple times, by the Onyx Empire in the early years of the war, by the United Mesarthrim Clans near the end of the war, and by rebel factions within the Mesarthrim before the meltdown of their own government.  These three worlds have banded together for the hope of the remnant of the United Earth Alliance: Alpha Centauri, who took in over five hundred million Argentinean refugees from Terra; Kalmar, founded by Canadian interests; and Bethija, the Ukrainian colony.  Governor Nicodemo Guadalupe Piazzaro of Alpha Centauri, Governor Yosyp Golovnya of Bethija, and Governor Jacques Marcileau of Kalmar--we welcome you all."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As the Commonwealth delegation is approaching the throne, Hadrian nudges Roi.  "Don't trust Piazzaro," he whispers.  "When Marshall and I were first getting the Resistance organized, he was part of a South American cell that got leaked to the Omegas early on, and was the only survivor.  He definitely was responsible, but I never caught up with him before he moved into Alpha Centauri politics."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Leifson pauses in his introductions of the Commonwealth, then continues, setting aside special consideration for the next guests. "The heroes of the great war cannot be listed without mentioning the [[Titan System]], now part of the great Commonwealth.  They fought the Onyx Empire, for a long time without the help of anyone else: a vastly superior force had them completely surrounded, but they fought them off and defeated them.  We welcome the hero of Titan, General-Commander Scott Sanburn.  He was the military architect of what is arguably the greatest defense in modern military history."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sanburn approaches the throne.  "My lord, my lady," he bows, and speaks briefly but at a tone audible to the other guests.  "I am greatly honored to meet new races.  It is what the Alliance of Planets was founded for.  I hope to see continued cooperation between the Alliance and the Reigess Sovereignty."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Indeed," Lorrodi smiles.  "I am eager to meet the legendary Meroniri.  I have much to discuss with their Prime Minister."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Commonwealth delegation has been seated, and Leifson continues.  Before he even begins speaking, everyone in the room has risen to their feet to welcome the lone dignitary from the Terran Embassy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"He is already famous even among our people," says Leifson.  "The courageous diplomat, daring explorer, and exemplary mind is always welcome here and everywhere: Josiah MacArthur Hlow.  Thousands of recent advancements in medicine, shield technology, and drive systems owe their results to the mind of this man.  Welcome indeed."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hlow approaches the throne, and bows humbly before the Raegos family.  He is a familiar sight to the royalty, and Jiliur nav Raegos in particular is glad to see him, pumping his hand like an old friend.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hlow takes his seat, and Leifson continues.  "The cause of peace, even at the cost of your own prosperity, has long been abandoned by many.  The Reigens wish to give special honor to Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan, the Rikaz Lorithos.  They have suffered much at their own hands, and none can truly appreciate the horrors of war as much as they can.  We welcome their Prekohr, Dirken Huern, the spokesman for peace.  We welcome their Hishai, Sanor Cai, the guardian of peace."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dirken Huern behaves oddly, when compared with the other delegations.  He gets down on his knees, and bows prone before the Raegos family.  He rises slowly back to his knees before speaking.  "Honored family, until now I have never been privaleged to visit this great world. Since we Lorithos first had contact with the Reigens, I have been eager to meet a fellow sovereign government.  Until recently, we lived under the rule of the Empress Talire Huern, my ancestor.  We delight at the opportunity to see a system in place that allows for the peaceful succession from one ruler to another."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Viveka Slanj nee Raegos beams at him.  "Then you must come back in a few years.  My son will receive the throne on his thirtieth birthday.  We do not pass the throne upon death.  My husband's father still lives in retirement on an estate south of here."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dirken Huern rises, and the two Lorithos take their seats.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We were saddened when most of our Ikronin brethren sent their regrets," Leifson continues.  "Still, we are pleased to welcome the Jaytex corporation leader, Simon Blodgit."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Blodgit smiles somewhat sadly.  "Majesties, I am afraid the bulk of the Jurekön trace their lineage back to the merchant families that overthrew the Ikronin royalty.  We hope you understand that no insult is meant by their absence.  We have traditions much like your own, and the others must observe them.  I am more pragmatic.  I hope to find new ways to benefit both our peoples through new trade deals."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Raegos family's greeting to Blodgit is polite, but somewhat chilled compared to the other delegations.  Blodgit takes his seat, and Leifson continues.  "This is almost entirely a gathering of the human species, but we wish to give special welcome to the representatives of the [[Ngrligru]] race: the Kabila Kimburu.  Kuu [[Isilahi Kodwe]], we welcome you and your kind."  The [[Ngrligru]] woman is embraced by Viveka and personally escorted to her seat.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before Leifson can continue the introductions, another Reigen approaches and whispers to him briefly before leaving.  "There seems to be some mistake here.  The Meroniri were supposed to be here, but they have not arrived yet.  Instead, we will continue with the [[Taviridis Somarchada]].  Much like our own people, the Ixoarchada have only recently made themselves known to the galaxy at large.  It has been our pleasure to host them on many occasions before, and now to welcome them to this great event."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Ixoarchada delegation is stunning.  The entire council approaches the thrones, flanked by towering RhováDeviri warriors in armor and livery of their caste, armed with nothing but their impressive size and imposing splendor.  The RhováDeviri chant softly as they approach, a strange, soft, deep sound that tugs at the hearts and minds of all who listen.  The entire delegation bows, and with the exception of the RhováDeviri, who are too large to be properly seated, take their places at the banquet tables.  The RhováDeviri excuse themselves and return to the quarters provided for them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Leifson continues.  "The next delegation, Bejistät König Lorrodi Raegos wishes to announce and welcome personally."  Leifson bows towards the king, who rises from his throne.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"When I learned of the Mesarthrim, I was greatly impressed," begins Lorrodi.  "They are a people after my own heart, honorable, loyal, brave.  I was greatly troubled and grieved to know that they became sundered into two peoples, and it would greatly please me to see the two of you united again.  Do not mistake me, I do not mean for either to gain victory over the other: you are all brothers, and like brothers I would wish that you solve your differences in truth, not in blood.  Tonight, consider yourselves as one at my table.  Let Centusi and Auellal once again be Mesarthrim, if only for this night."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;William Camp and Steven Cue, the leaders of the Mesarthrim factions, stare at one another balefully, as if daring the other to make the first move.  Their honor holds, for now.  Each mumbles something inaudible to the gathering at large, and takes their seats.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lorrodi continues as his wife and son rise to stand next to him.  "Now we come to the reason for this gathering.  They are last, and in the place of honor, though we consider you all friends and hope to consider you all allies.  We welcome the Federated Districts of the Prefecture.  Antonius Aurelius Hadrian, I bid you welcome."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefect steps forward, escorting Talaris Roi. "I accept your welcome, great king," he says, bowing.  "And accept your friendship gladly.  I bring a token of our friendship," he continues, as he hands a small optical disc to Lorrodi.  "This is an AI seed program: install it within a sufficiently powerful trinary computer system, and it will grow into a friend and ally loyal to the death.  Our Fleets now have these wondrous creatures driving them.  Prism, their mother, is the heart of the great ship ''Jerome Black'' that orbits above us."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lorrodi smiles, and hands a small glowing object to Hadrian.  "And this, my friend, is the object of your desire.  Antimatter...a single atom of anti-hydrogen, safely sealed away as a mere token of our friendship.  As we speak, the first shipments are arriving on Albanis to power your Fleets."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Viveka steps in.  "For you, Talaris Roi, I have a special gift.  This ring has been handed down from mother to daughter in my family for generations."  She hands Roi a small platinum ring set with a sapphire.  "Its twin is on my finger.  My sister died childless, and it was returned to me.  I offer it to you, that our peoples may know friendship and harmony throughout the ages."  Tears cloud the eyes of both women as they embrace.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Thank you," Talaris Roi can barely manage a whisper.  "I have also brought a gift, though it seems nothing in comparison.  I offer you this," she says, handing Viveka a leather-bound tome.  "It is a declaration of principles, all of which are shared between us...it seems silly, but I felt it was good to show how much we have in common, when these ceremonies show how different we are."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I don't think it silly at all," Jiliur nav Raegos steps in with a smile.  "Come, let us join the feast."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The royal family as a whole leads the Prefecture delegates to the head table, but stop short when five powered armor suits enter the room, striding past startled Likniir.  "I'm sorry I'm late," a new voice booms across the hall, sounding harsh and brazen in contrast to the others gathered.  "Matters of greater importance, I'm afraid." Darel Fusala, the Lothenar of the Meroniri Terinasi, enters the hall, preceded, flanked, and trailed by armored Meroniri and a lone councilor.  He takes his seat in silence, without introducing himself--though introductions are hardly necessary.  It is impossible that anyone would not recognize the leader of the mightiest race in the galaxy.  He nods slightly at the Raegos family, barely paying them any notice at all--a gross insult.  His councilor, a man completely cloaked in white robes, takes his seat next to him.  The armored Meroniri file out of the room.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Important matters, I'm sure," Lorrodi smiles, overlooking the affront.  He sits at the head of the table, with Anthony Hadrian on his right.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Do you know who that is with Fusala?" asks Hadrian.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No...should I?" Lorrodi responds, squinting across the massive table at the man in question.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's Lucius Black!" snarls Hadrian.  "That's the Emperor!"

=====A Mix of Food and Politics=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Much farther down the banquet table, the guests are not privy to the conversations between the Prefecture delegation and the royal family, and are free to discuss matters of their own.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Gentlemen, shall we?" asks Yosyp Golovnya.  The other Commonwealth governors nod their assent, and all slowly move about their area of the table.  Yosyp Golovnya sits with Evaristo Silveira of Kalidasa, while Jacques Marcileau speaks with Dirken Huern.  Both governors chat briefly with their respective companions before uttering an identical sentence.  "I'm going to make you an offer you can't afford to refuse."   

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Takashi Nakamura excuses himself from his own seat, claiming the need to stretch his legs.  While the first course is being served, he sits down with Lord Kael Rejoc, a Reigen nobleman of considerable wealth and power.  "Lord Rejoc, may I join you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rejoc smiles.  "Takashi Nakamura, indeed you may.  Though I thought you would prefer the company of the rest of the Sirius delegation."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nakamura sighs before signaling to a Reigen servant.  "Sake, if you please," he nods to the young Reigen woman, who curtsies and fills his cup with a warm liquid.  "It was most kind of your king to bring import so many of our favorites.  Anyway, no, I do not prefer the company of my...compatriots."  He shakes his head sadly.  "Berlusconi is a buffoon, and Asanova is as dull as a boulder."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I see, I see," Rejoc chuckles.  "Well, you are most welcome to join me.  What is on your mind, Mr. Nakamura?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Lord Rejoc," Nakamura oozes.  "I understand we have a mutual interest in...pharmaceuticals." 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Alessandro Berlusconi does not partake directly in the first course in the banquet.  Instead, he spends half of the first course walking around the table, chatting with the servants and helping them with their duties.  Many of the dining dignitaries hear snatches of "So you were born in the northern mountains?  Fascinating!  I remember as a boy I would..." and "No, no, of course!  I understand entirely..." and similar blurbs from the chatty Padre.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Near the end of the first course, the Padre finds a seat with Roger Morris.  "So, Mr. Morris.  Tell me, what is it like living on Oronus?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Padre," Morris nods.  "You've been to Oronus.  I'm sure your memory isn't that stale."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ah, yes.  I've been there.  But never lived there."  Berlusconi shifts his considerable bulk into a more comfortable position.  "Roger, you're usually much happier to see me.  What is troubling you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Meroniri," At the mention of the powerful and increasingly dangerous Terinasi, Morris and Berlusconi share a conspiratorial nod.  "They cut off the Commonwealth.  They cut off the Ixoarchada.  The larger worlds don't know it yet, but the Space Police are not under command of just the Alliance of Planets anymore.  They're starting to enforce the wishes of the Meroniri...they've started dictating where and when the Coalition can sell our material."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"How odd," Berlusconi muses.  "And your primary markets would be the Prefecture and the Ikronin, correct?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Originally, yes, says Morris.  "But for some reason the Meroniri do not allow us access to the Gates.  We can only sell to whoever can come to us.  Right now, that's the Meroniri themselves, and the Mesarthrim.  Because we have no other markets available, they give us a pittance for the rare materials we mine--our sweat and blood."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Indeed?" Berlusconi asks.  "Well, I have a proposal for you, Roger.  A way out of this little predicament of yours that should get you much profit.  In return, someday I will ask you for a favor."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Viveka Raegos and Talaris Roi chat merrily throughout the first course, but Anthony Hadrian does not touch a bite or speak.  He glowers across the room at Lucius Black, ignoring the friendly chatter around him.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Anthony?" Hadrian starts at a gentle touch on his arm.  "Shouldn't you eat something?" Talaris Roi smiles slightly, but worry darkens her eyes.  "What's wrong?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Lucius Black," Hadrian replies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Is that so?" Lorrodi Raegos asks.  "This man, this Lucius Black, he is everything that is wrong with you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"He is everything that is wrong with everything," Hadrian replies.  "There is no man, no thing, more foul and evil than Lucius Black."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Really?" Lorrodi strokes his chin.  "You would lay the burden of every ill in the universe at the feet of this one man.  This Emperor--and a deposed Emperor at that.  In fact, I believe you were the one who deposed him.  It seems that the harm he could cause is past."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"As long as he draws breath, he is a threat," Hadrian hisses.  "He was never a danger to anyone directly.  His tongue is poison, and he turns the ears of all to his own will.  It is only a matter of time until his Empire rises again."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"For all our sakes, I hope you are wrong," Lorrodi smiles.  "Ah, here comes the second course.  Let's enjoy it, and forget this evil man for now."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As the second course is being served, Hadrian slowly begins to draw his firearm.  Lorrodi watches him out of the corner of his eye, doing nothing until Hadrian's weapon is nearly visible above the table top.  Lorrodi grabs Hadrian's wrist with a vice-like grip and stops him with a cold glare.  "There has been no death in this house in twelve generations.  You are the guest of honor, and my friend, but rest assured, if there is one death here tonight, there will be a second tomorrow."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hadrian's eyes lock with Lorrodi's for one grief-stricken moment, but he relaxes and re-holsters his weapon, then takes up his fork instead.  "Supper then."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dinner proceeds, ranging from simple yet elegant dishes to meals that would make the heartiest and pickiest gourmand break down in tears and beg to sup at a Reigen table until the end of their days.  Drink is imported from all over human space, and pours freely.  The meal finally ends after twelve courses, and the guests leave the table to walk about the palace and mingle.  Now the political wrangling begins in earnest.

=====Mingling and Dealing=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Scott Sanburn and Dirken Huern walk through the crowd, nodding to random people when they make eye contact.  "I must admit, General-Commander, this plan of yours is not the first interesting one I've heard tonight," Huern comments.  "Jacques Marcileau wanted to buy our hyperspace tunnel technology in bulk.  A great boon to the Lorithos indeed.  But your idea is a boon to everyone.  What was it you called it again?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"An Interstellar Red Cross.  The United Earth Alliance used to harbor a similar organization known as the International Red Cross," Sanburn explains.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Total neutrality for a highly advanced medical and humanitarian organization may be exactly what we need," Huern muses.  "It won't bring peace, but it will help the people in need around the galaxy.  You have my support, and we'll work out a place to serve as a headquarters."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I was hoping that Lorithan..." Sanburn begins.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No, not Lorithan," Huern interrupts.  "You aren't a political man, General-Commander, and I respect that, but if you haven't figured it out yet you need to realize this soon: the Alliance does not have the respect it once had.  If you want this to work, you have to find somewhere that isn't controlled by the Alliance."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Not Lorithan then," Sanburn frowns.  "I have heard the rumors, but if they're true, a place that isn't controlled by the Alliance essentially means..."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Practically nowhere," Huern finishes the dark sentence for him.  "You didn't hear this from me, Sanburn," Huern looks nervously over his shoulder, then drops his voice to a low whisper.  "I would seriously consider replacing Joshua Beson in your command staff.  He spent too much time in the Space Police."  Huern suddenly raises his voice again to a level too loud for a private conversation.  "Yes, General-Commander, the person you should talk to is Lady Kikylia Stirngrat!  She has estates all over Reigess, and the Stirngrat family has had an active hand in Reigen charities for years.  We owe them a lot on Lorithos, as a matter of fact."  Huern whispers once more.  "Speak with me privately when this is over.  My people owe the Meroniri too much.  I'm not even sure if Sanor Cai wouldn't turn me over to Fusala."   

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lorrodi Raegos has begun walking around the crowd, and is trailed by Simon Blodgit and Steven Cue.  The Provost of the Auellal looks from Blodgit to Raegos with quick darting glances, taking care not to be caught staring.  He wrings his hands, then stops in mid-stride for half a moment before speaking.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Majesty," Cue begins.  "I must offer my apologies for my behavior when we were introduced.  I was most surprised that you greeted William Camp and myself together, and as equals."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Think nothing of it Steven," Lorrodi turns and smiles, then reaches out and puts a hand on Cue's shoulder.  "I consider everyone a friend tonight, and everyone is welcome under my roof.  I know your people are all used to politics and second guesses, but I deal with both my hands in the open."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That isn't what I meant," Cue is suddenly aware that he is breathing normally again, the bated breath relieved by Lorrodi's friendly answer.  "Mesarthrim are not the same as Terrans.  We were very recently split from them, not even three hundred years ago, but we have changed.  In some ways we have changed for the worse.  William Camp represents a part of the Mesarthrim that I wish was dead and buried.  He cannot survive without conflict...without conquest.  He may very well win against the Prefecture.  If he does, he will crush my people.  There can be no reconciliation between the Centusi and the Auellal.  Once we are gone, he will find a new target, and then another, until there is nothing left to conquer.  Then their hatred and aggression will turn inwards, and they will plunge themselves into a new dark age."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Strong words," Simon Blodgit cuts in.  "Strong words that I happen to agree with.  In some small way, the Auellal and the Centusi factions of the Mesarthrim represent what our two people could have become, if we had remained in contact."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lorrodi frowns, but nods.  "I believe you may be right," he sighs.  "The Reigens and the Ikronin were an extremely warlike society before the Ikronin ice age and the Reigen departure.  We are all a kin, in a way.  Look back into the dawn of mankind, and we all stem from common stock.  And we have never truly put down our aggressive ways.  The Mesarthrim under Camp represent the darker part of us all.  But still, I would hope to see this matter resolved peacefully."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Blodgit sighs and nods.  Cue shakes his head grimly.  "A noble wish, but a vain hope."   

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Prime Minister!" William Camp sidles up to the Meroniri leader, oozing polite obeisance.  "How good to see you again!  I can't begin to tell you how much I regret missing the last Alliance High Council meetings I was...allowed...to visit.  It is unfortunate that the United Centusi States are no longer welcome in the Alliance."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ah, William," Darel Fusala frowns and shakes hands with the Mesarthrim Director.  "Believe me, you aren't the only one regretting the ruling of the High Council."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I seem to recall reading the final tally of the vote that expelled us," Camp taps his chin thoughtfully, smirking.  "You abstained."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"An oversight," Fusala sighs.  "I should have used our veto power.  If you were still a Member State I could have stopped this destructive war...I'm glad to see the Laojiu worlds independent now.  Even my own people rely on Laojiu for food.  Stability trumps morality in any case.  We need to survive.  By the way, just between us..." Fusala looks around conspiratorially.  "Forming the "Free" Laojiu Republic was pure brilliance.  Congratulations."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fusala walks off, leaving Camp to ponder his words.   

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, the prize of the royal litter," a cold voice interrupts Jiliur nav Raegos and Anjela Kaleem from their polite conversation.  Lucius Black has pulled back his white hood, showing the collar and chain that still binds his head, hands, and ankles together.  Two glowing diodes are attached to his temples, flashing green and blue.  "If you can spare a moment of time?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Jiliur stiffens at the insult, and politely excuses himself from the Prefecture Air Force general.  She nods and leaves.  Jiliur turns, and straightens, towering over the deposed despot.  "What do you want, Lucius?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, the crown prince knows my name.  What other tricks did they teach you?  Can you balance a treat on your nose?"  Black's sneer darkens further.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Was there some point to this, or did you just come to my house to insult me?"  Jiliur sighs, taking a pull from his drink and rubbing his temples.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes, there was," Lucius drops his hateful gaze, instead looking puzzled, stroking his chin as the chains clink.  "Tell me something.  You have lived your life sheltered from real experience, and will have this entire world thrust upon your shoulders with little but a classic education to prepare you for it.  Tell me, prince-ling: what do you want?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I don't think that's any of your business.  Why should you care how the Reigen people are governed?  By all accounts, you were a tyrant in your day, and executed millions of people." Jiliur glares at Lucius.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ever hear the saying "the history books are written by the victors?"  Anthony Hadrian and his...constituents...won their little rebellion," Lucius sighs.  "If I did anything wrong, it was trusting a man with a heart for treason.  The fact of the matter remains: I built my empire with my own hands.  You lived a sheltered life and earned nothing."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You should watch your tongue while in my father's house," Jiliur growls.  "Your continued health is at his sufferance."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Please," Lucius Black rolls his eyes.  "What could you possibly do to worsen my condition?" He holds up his hands, pulling at the shackles.  "The Meroniri keep me chained like a wild animal.  They stuck these...things...in my head," he points at the diodes in his temples.  "If I think about escape, I get headaches.  If I try to escape, I pass out.  Your threats mean nothing to me, prince-ling.  But take this advice: your family has ruled this world because they have the love of their people, not because of competence.  Things have changed: now your world is one among hundreds.  You will ascend to the throne soon, despite your total lack of qualification.  Learn, or the galaxy will devour it, as it has many before you."  Lucius Black stalks off, leaving an angry but thoughtful Jiliur behind him.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Talaris Roi and Anthony Hadrian have stepped outside the dining hall, enjoying a quiet moment together on the balcony.  Raegard has set, and the two relax in the cool breeze.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Something's bothering you Anthony," Roi leans against the balcony rail, allowing her hand to touch Hadrian's.  "And that bother's me.  You nearly blew it back there with Lucius Black.  You lost your cool.  I've known you all these years, and I've never seen you lose it like that."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You know an ideal, Talaris." Hadrian pulls away, and paces across the balcony with his hands behind his back.  "You know the leader of the resistance.  You know the Admiral.  You know the Prefect.  You don't know Anthony Hadrian."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's not an explanation," Roi frowns, tapping her chin with her finger.  "And you don't give either of us enough credit.  You were the reason I was able to get Tejat to join the Prefecture as a whole instead of being a single cell of the resistance.  You the person, not the ideal.  We pulled it off together, along with Robert, and Jean, and...and...Chris."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Things are different now.  Back then, it was a matter of survival and revenge.  It was all I cared about.  I didn't give a damn if this dream won out or not.  Chris and Jean were the schemers who made it work, and Robert won us the troops we needed to do it.  I wanted Black dead.  That's all I wanted."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Things have changed," Roi admits.  "But you have as well.  You can't indulge in revenge any more than I can indulge in..." she trails off and turns away from Hadrian.  "Jordan's gone, and Robert isn't the way he used to be.  Chris has changed, he can't be trusted.  You and Jean are the only ones I can rely on anymore.  You're the only one I..." she stops talking, letting the words die unspoken.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I see," Hadrian stops pacing, and actually looks Roi in her eyes, which are wet with barely held back tears.  "How old do you think I am, Talaris?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"How old do you think I am?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You were born in 2060 or so, but you were in cold sleep for more than 200 years, so I suppose that would make you..."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"All of 262 years old," Hadrian cuts her off.  "They don't tell you the dark part of cold sleep.  You're not asleep.  You're frozen, helpless, hopeless, lost.  Imagine spending that entire time, knowing your worst enemy is next to you, unable to do anything about it but wait in impotent silence.  I have spent more than three lifetimes in servitude to Lucius Black, and I knew what he did.  I'll fight the Mesarthrim for you, but this is the end.  I've ended too many lives, seen too much suffering."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I understand," Roi slowly approaches Hadrian, then hugs him tightly.  "You're one of the few friends I have left, Anthony.  I need you to keep it together.  This isn't about our dreams anymore.  This is about our people now.  Can I count on you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hadrian looks down at the small frame of his friend, and gently pats her back.  "Yes.  You can count on me."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The two stare out at the Reigen stars for nearly half an hour before they are joined by Viveka Raegos.  Viveka smiles at them both, sharing an empathetic moment.  Servants bring chairs, and all three sit and share stories from years long gone.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Not all of the party is for the rich, classy and political.  Many of the less sophisticated guests have gathered to one side of the room, where a towering, eight foot tall Ixoarchada RhováDeviri and a four foot tall [[Ngrligru]] are arm wrestling.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ha!" Paelops Kautin, the Ixoarchada, slams Golb Kodwe's arm, winning the match.  "A bet's a bet, my friendly fish-man!" Kautin booms.  "Triple shots, down the hatch!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Golb Kodwe smiles, revealing row upon row of razor sharp fangs.  "Indeed!  But I believe I'll have more than that!" he crows, and grabs a triple shot glass in each hand, downing the potent liquor from each in one swig.  "You're big, Kautin, but I'll bet a song you can't hold your drink as well as I can!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The assembled RhováDeviri, [[Ngrligru]], Prefecture Marines, and Reigen Likniir all hoot with laughter at the challenge.  "Drink!  Drink!  Drink!"  The chant is started by Kautin's brother RhováDeviri, but is quickly taken up by all the assembled fighters, drawing annoyed stares from a few of the higher class revelers on the fringes of the main crowd.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Very well!" Kautin smiles, slapping the [[Ngrligru]] across the back.  "I propose a contest!  Food -and- drink!  Surely our hosts can help us settle this matter?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before long, two huge trays of prime steak, and two enormous kegs of quality Reigen ale are in front of the two.  They dive in with gusto, goaded on by cheering and catcalls from their compatriots.  In the end, it's a draw...of sorts.  Kautin has consumed two entire sides of beef, but passed out from drinking.  Kodwe has drained an entire keg, but refuses to touch another bite.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"A tie!  A tie!"  The rowdy crowd hoists the two gastronomic champions above their heads, and drain the remaining keg among themselves.  A few of the more sober members notice some sharp looks from the higher-ups, and nod towards the exit.  An impromptu band is struck up, and the crowd filters out, singing a cacaphonous mix of drinking songs in several languages at once.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So, explain to me Professor," Roger Morris swirls his drink and takes a swig, stretching out in a comfortable chair.  "How can you say that the restriction of technological trade is beneficial to local economies?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Professor Hlow, Roger Morris, Gregor Blazton, and Lorrodi Raegos are all seated in front of a huge fireplace in a small lounge adjacent to the ball-room.  Hlow and Lorrodi are playing a game of chess, and Hlow is engaged in lively debate with their other companions.  Lorrodi simply plays, only listening to the conversation.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Simple," Hlow responds.  "Truly advanced societies like the Meroniri enjoy a level of advancement so far beyond most others that if certain technologies were available for sale, they would bankrupt themselves.  Consider the reactor trade from Tejat to the rest of the Prefecture.  If the Meroniri were to suddenly come in, with cheap and readily available energy, that entire economy would cease to exist.  The Prefecture lacks the infrastructure to support a parasite class: "welfare" does not exist and never will.  Oh, by the way, check in three, mate in six." Hlow moves a knight, leaving Lorrodi to ponder his next move.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"But the invisible hand of the market has always compensated for advancing technology before," argues Gregor Blazton.  When fuel cells and nuclear power replaced crude oil, the oil conglomerates simply used their financial clout to invest capital in the new markets."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Clever," Hlow remarks to Lorrodi's move, and promptly takes the Reigen king's bishop with a rook before returning to the debate.  "When we're talking about a contained economy, you're right.  A planetary economy is just that, but we're talking multiple contained economies with links to outside markets.  How could nuclear energy suppliers obtain components a thousand years beyond their own technology?  Certain things simply cannot be sold from one civilization to another.  It dilutes the value of each civilization.  A lesser civilization used great power without understanding it, and the greater civilization now has a competitor with power it doesn't know how to use."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Interesting," Blazton murmurs.  "What do you think?" he asks Lorrodi.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I think the Professor is about to lose his queen," Lorrodi smiles.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The four continue their quiet conversation, enjoying the peaceful intellectual exercises and ignoring the political wrangling going on outside.

=====Secrets, Despair, and Hope===== 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It is now past local midnight, and some of the party retires for the night.  The Meroniri delegation leaves, watched by the independents like a large predator is watched by nervous animals.  Padre Berlusconi raises his glass to Darel Fusala and the white-robed Lucius Black as they pass, but no one says a word.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;All night long, the representatives of the independents have been waiting for this moment.  One by one, they approach Lorrodi Raegos: Roger Morris, Gregor Blazton, Nicholai Asanova, Takashi Nakamura, Alessandro Berlusconi, Evaristo Silveira, Nicodemo Piazzaro, Yosyp Golovnya, and Jacques Marcileau.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Majesty, we have a matter of grave importance to discuss," Asanova begins.  "It concerns the Meroniri, and their Alliance of Planets."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We want to know if you intend to join this Alliance," prompts Morris.  "And if you do, there are things you must know about them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"And we have an alternative proposal," offers Golovnya.  "One that I think will be more beneficial to you and your people than anything the Meroniri will promise you...and I assure you, any promises they make will not be kept."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Jiliur Raegos each walk alone in the garden paths, shadowed by Likniir.  He broods in silence, scowling at the walls around him and the shadows of the soldiers around him.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You seem troubled, young prince," Mircas Rasune approaches him.  The towering Ixoarchada dwarfs Jiliur, who is tall by Reigen standards.  "Share your concerns, and it will lighten the burden of your heart."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You carry yourself like a warrior, but speak like an oracle," Jiliur muses.  "Why?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"For us, the roles are not so far apart."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Very well," Jiliur sighs.  "I met Lucius Black, and he said some things that were already troubling me since I learned about your worlds and your wars.  I'm not sure I can lead our people, and I don't want to live a life of luxury."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You mean you don't want to live a life of peace," Rasune deep voice thunders softly.  "You believe that fighting a war will bring you the experiences you believe you want.  I am ancient, even by RhováDeviri standards.  I have seen three separate centuries and have fought many battles.  War is not what you should seek, nor adventure."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Explain?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The RhováDeviri are born into a life destined for war.  We gladly step between harm's way and the Taviridis, but I would trade all the glory and power for a life of peace.  Peace, once won, should not be cast aside for adventure.  Think about that before you seek the stars.  If you leave here, you will only find pain and death."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rasune leaves the Reigen prince alone.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The night is nearing an end, and the guests still present have gathered in the banquet hall for a final toast.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lorrodi Raegos raises his glass.  "To the future.  To peace.  To the long life and good health of you all."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The assembled guests raise their glasses and echo his toast.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Good night to you all.  You are always welcome to return."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The party breaks, and Lorrodi and Viveka Raegos retire to their room.

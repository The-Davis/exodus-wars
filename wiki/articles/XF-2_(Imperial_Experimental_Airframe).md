The XF-2 program continued using the [[XF-1 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-1]] airframe, but served as an [[Ion Propulsion|ion engine]] test bed. While highly successful in terms of space use, it could not achieve escape velocity at all, because the ion engine would not function in atmosphere.

After two flights, one launched from space to test the [[Ion Propulsion|ion engine]] and one from a runway to test the use of the [[Ion Propulsion|ion engine]] in boosting the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] to escape velocity, the XF-2 program was terminated and the staff were reassigned to the [[XF-3 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-3]] program.

''Michael Burke'' (born August 16, [[2064]]) was one of [[Lucius Black]]'s inner circle of conspirators in the years leading up to the [[First Exodus War]]. As such, he was in a position to evacuate his family from the surface of [[Torimur]] prior to its destruction.

The death of his homeworld was not something that Burke approved of, but it was a tactic that he understood. Like Lucius Black, Michael Burke believed that [[Earth]] could be forced to surrender if it was forced to engage a trained military in open warfare.

When the [[Torimur]] fleet won space superiority in the [[Sol System]], Burke had a major disagreement of strategy with Black. Black favored a personal approach: sending in ground troops to seize the capital, while Burke favored remaining in space and holding the planet hostage with threats of bombardment.

After Black's invasion failed, Burke believed that the war could not be won. During the next engagement with the Earth forces, he surrendered. Even after the exile and the return, Lucius Black never forgot or forgave Michael Burke for his perceived cowardice. It cost him the command of the new [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

Despite falling out of favor with the Emperor, Michael Burke was still an experienced and loyal commander. Even though he knew the horrible secret of [[Torimur]]'s destruction, he still believed in the Empire and believed in [[Lucius Black]]. He was given a commission in the Royal Imperial Navy with the rank of Vice Admiral, and given command of the [[HMS Menoetius|HMS ''Menoetius'']].

Vice Admiral Burke served in the Iron Armada, under Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian]]. Unlike [[Robert Marshall]]'s Black Armada, which actively sought out and subjugated the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s forces, the Iron Armada conquered independent colonies and acted as a garrison force. Burke saw little action in most of the [[Second Exodus War]], until the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] joined the war and pushed the [[Onyx Empire]] back into its core territories.

During the [[Mesarthrim]]-led counter offensive, Vice Admiral Burke was able to make a name for himself, winning several battles and turning several routs into pyrrhic victories for his enemies. He was issued a promotion to full Admiral by [[Lucius Black]], and assigned command of four carrier groups that hunted down and destroyed several [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and [[United Earth Alliance]] troop and supply convoys.

Admiral Burke's meteoric redemption was not over. When Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian]] openly rebelled against [[Lucius Black]], Admiral Burke rushed his task force back to the [[Sol System]], narrowly beating the return of Admiral [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s forces. Burke led an attack on the [[HMS Iron Hand|HMS ''Iron Hand'']], and managed to land a strike force on the ship. It was not completely successful, but tipped the balance of power on the crew to the Imperial loyalists. Grand Admiral Hadrian was forced to flee, but by then the [[HMS Jerome Black|HMS ''Jerome Black'']] had returned to the [[Sol System]]. The ships under Admiral Robert Marshall's command had already dealt with their internal loyalty disputes, and the rebellion was firmly in control. Hadrian was safely spirited away.

For his bravery and loyalty, Lucius Black promoted Michael Burke to the rank of Grand Admiral, succeeding the rebellious Anthony Hadrian. Burke's command was efficient and talented, and was largely responsible for making the war continue as long as it did. Without his skills, the Empire would undoubtably have fallen quickly after losing so many of its forces to rebellion and to enemy combat.

In the final battle of the [[Imperial Civil War]], on February 5, [[2324]], Michael Burke was killed in action. Because most of the loyal [[Onyx Empire]] personnel were killed or fled into deep space, there were few first-hand accounts of Burke's death. After [[Emil Torsc]] defected to the [[Prefecture]], he gave a full report to his new superiors, and revealed the details of Michael Burke's death.

The HMS ''Iron Hand'' was boarded by the Volucris during the battle, and in the brutal deck-to-deck fighting, Michael Burke was mortally wounded. His final command was to retreat from battle. Lucius Black, believing that his Grand Admiral was falling back on his "cowardly ways", withheld life-saving medical aid. Michael Burke died of his injuries.

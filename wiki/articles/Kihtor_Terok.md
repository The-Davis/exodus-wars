'''Kihtor Terok''' was the second of the [[Kihtor]], and was completed on March 13, [[2300]]. It is anchored in the northern continent, Nehru, on the world of [[Lorithan]]. It is a communications and information networking hub. Kihtor Terok served as the strategic command center of the coalition forces during the [[Volucris War]].

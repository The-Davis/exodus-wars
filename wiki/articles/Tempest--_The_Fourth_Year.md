With the United Centusi States progress into Prefecture territory halted, the Prefecture and Ikronin turn their attention towards recapturing the Laojiu District, the single most vital source of galactic food production. When Laojiu is recaptured in late November, Director William Camp orders the withdrawal of Mesarthrim forces to reconsider strategy.

==January==

The Prefecture campaign on Laojiu continues. It is a well-organized small-unit resistance, spearheaded by the Hunters. It is a highly-effective top-down campaign, with officers targeted for death or capture. The Mesarthrim retaliate by slaughtering a village of Laojian people.

==February==

Back home on Mesar, several top commanders begin to protest William Camp's brutal tactics, and one of his Commandants, Nicholas Weidenfeld, is executed by Camp's guard after he threatens William Camp.

The Laojiu Campaign against the Mesarthrim continues to succeed. The success of the Prefecture's Fourth Fleet in defense is put to the test on the offensive, and long range fighter and bomber sorties are used with great success to cripple the defenses the Mesarthrim have put in place over the planets.

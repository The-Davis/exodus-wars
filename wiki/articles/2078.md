== Events of 2078 ==

=== January ===

* January 18 - The tax issue from thirty years previous began to flare up again between [[Torimur]] and [[Earth]]. Several members of the Assembly; Jackson Merrill, Danielle Hughes, and Max Thurough began legislation to force the annexation of both Torimur and the [[Sirius Cluster]].
* January 31 - [[Lucius Black]] appears before the Assembly. He delivers a fiery speech, decrying the imperialism of the Alliance and defying them to try to take Torimur.

=== February ===

* February 4 - [[Juliska Blasius]] appears before the Assembly. Rather than delivering the fire and brimstone style of speech given several days earlier by Lucius Black, she simply points out each of the Assembly members sponsoring the new annexation legislation, and tells them "in ten days, you will be dead." Blasius returns to the [[Sirius Cluster]].
* February 14 - The infamous "Night of Knives" began at 2200 hours on February 14. Jackson Merrill, Danielle Hughes, and Max Thurough were each murdered in their beds.
* February 21 - While attempting to escape [[Earth]], [[Andor Blasius]]--the brother of [[Juliska Blasius]]--is captured. While he refuses to speak, his presence implicates the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]] 

=== March ===

* March 6 - Despite the deaths of the annexation bill's sponsors, the Assembly moves forward with the legislation and authorizes the annexation of the [[Sirius Cluster]]. Additionally, Alliance forces are authorized to use deadly force against any members of the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]].
* March 18 - The United Earth Alliance's 2nd Space Battle Group is deployed to the [[Sirius Cluster]].
* March 27 - The 2nd Space Battle Group arrives in the [[Sirius Novgorod System]] and blockades [[Kirtszi]], marking the beginning of the [[First Exodus War]].

=== April ===

* April 8 - [[Lucius Black]] brings [[Torimur]] into the [[First Exodus War]] with a formal declaration of war against the [[United Earth Alliance]]. His fleet is small, but full of veteran navigators and pilots who know [[hyperspace]] routes better than their Alliance military counterparts.

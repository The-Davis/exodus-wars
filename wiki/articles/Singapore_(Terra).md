'''Singapore''' was a [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]] nation, sharing the language, currency, laws and customs of the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]].

During the colony's direct control by the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]], it was a hotspot of the Muslim rioting and violence that marked the earliest years of the [[The Jihad|Jihad]] before the formation of [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]]. Singapore was able to keep out Muslim influence and remained a mostly Buddhist and Christian culture.

Singapore signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] in [[2011]] along with the rest of the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]].

by Seth Dickinson

=====High Orbit Debris Ring - Holt - Ward District - 0830 Hours=====
     “Nice run, Bravo-Six,” Range Control comms. “Two solid missile hits on Target Alfa; gun accuracy naught-seven-six. Turn to 187 mark 21 and form up on Bravo Leader.”
     “Roger, Control, one-eight-seven mark two-one,” Flight Lieutenant Toshi Parman replies, pulling her Piranha into a sharp turn and nosing up out of the target range. She, along with Bravo Squadron and most of the Fourth Fleet’s fighters are engaged in live-fire exercises against derelicts.
     As she rolls back into formation with the rest of her squadron, Parman glances up through her canopy at Holt VII. The gas giant planet looms above the Fourth Fleet; striated bands of clouds circling its equator give it the appearance of a gigantic yellow eye. 
     “Excellent shooting, Bravo-Six,” her squadron leader comms. 
     “Yeah, it’s tough putting a smart missile into a derelict,” one of her wing mates remarks sarcastically.
     Parman blushes; she’s never really fit in with her gung-ho military comrades, preferring to remain quiet.
     “Charlie Squadron, move to your initial point and prep for your run,” Range Control orders. “Armament check upon reaching-”
     “All fighters, knock it off, knock it off!” The sudden shout comes from one of the range observers, parked in a shuttle. Knock it off is the codeword for an emergency halt to the exercise. “Unidentified ships on the range!”
     Parman rolls her ship onto its back so her canopy is aimed down into the debris field. She sees a sharp flash of light amidst the tumbling debris, followed by several more. She recognizes the actinic starbursts, and her SPAR system confirms her suspicion:
     Optical hyperspace signature detected. Multiple ships emerging from hyperspace. Range 1,000,000 kilometers. ID follows.
     “A million kilometers?” someone exclaims. “They’re right on top of us!”
     Parman is still staring at her SPAR screen, waiting for the ship IDs. The SPAR system drinks in incoming light, matches the images against computerized files, and comes to a conclusion.
     2 MES-CV-TERRA: Terra Medium Carrier
     4 MES-FF-DELTA: Delta Frigate
     2 MES-CVE-DAVIS: Davis Escort Carrier
     2 MES-CL-INDOMITABLE: Indomitable Light Cruiser
     15 MES-CT-SEARS: Sears Gunship
     15 MES-CT-NIMITZ: Nimitz Gunship
     The Mesarthrim are here! she realizes, and tightens her grip on her fighter’s controls.

=====Flagship Victory, 0830 Hours=====
     “Sitrep! Get me numbers and types of hostiles. Tactical, I want a fighter attack pattern!” Admiral Marie Sarnou struts onto the bridge of the Victory, her cool demeanor hinting that she’s been waiting for the Mesarthrim to show up. The admiral is alert and in control. She fastens herself into her command chair and glances at the repeater display on its arm.
     United Centusi States ships are popping out of hyperspace within half a million kilometers – practically shouting distance in a space borne engagement. 
     “They’re not in battle formation, ma’am,” one of her tac officers notes. “I don’t think they were expecting to emerge right on top of us.” 
     Sarnou nods, fighting a savage grin off her face. The surprise has been reversed, it seems. She doesn’t even need to think about her next decision – not after Leskovik. 
     “All ships, weapons free,” she tells her com officer. “Tactical, Air Boss, fighters will be using Attack Plan Able. Let’s hit them while they’re still organizing. Comm, tell me when Sky Marshal Brooks gets her fighter in the air. We’ll turn aerospace command over to her.”

=====Piranha Bravo-Six, 0835 Hours=====
     “Attack pattern Able,” the fleet Air Boss sends. Parman knows that plan by heart - Bravo Squadron, and the fleet’s other Piranhas, will attack first, scattering defenders and breaking up any Mesarthrim fighter screen so that bombers can slip through and attack the capital ships. “Switch to assigned squadron frequencies for vector assignments.”
     Flight controllers on the Victory and other carriers hastily work out flight plans, assigning each squadron a vector to avoid collisions while allowing them room to maneuver.
     Hundreds of Piranha and Stuka fighters wing-over and thrust towards the Mesarthrim fleet, forming into a neat attack wedge. Bombers orbit behind them, waiting for an opening in the enemy defenses.
     Mesarthrim gunships scramble to form a screen over their capital ship charges; fighters swarm out of the carriers like bees from a disturbed hive and form up by squadron, trying to organize for a counter-charge. They’re too slow. The Sears and Nimitz gunships are only beginning to set up their fire lanes when the first squadrons of Prefecture Piranhas reach them.

     Lieutenant Parman grins like a maniac as her Piranha jolts and bounces as blazer fire explodes around it; the low rumble of the drive system on full throttle thrums away in the background, hurling the Piranha across hundreds of kilometers every second. The combination of sensations is exhilarating.
     All around her hundreds of other fighters, their plasma shields glowing like comets, plummet towards the Mesarthrim force. Blazer bolts streak around them, long yellow-white lances of energy; but the Mesarthrim aren’t in formation and the fire is sporadic and ineffective.
     The Prefecture ships’ Archadian-based AI maneuvering systems send them whirling out of the way most of the time. Somewhere in the distance, a Piranha takes a direct hit from a blazer and blows apart in a spectacular fireball. Parman swallows, fighting away nerves.
     Crimson highlights flash across her HUD as the nearest Mesarthrim gunship comes into range.

=====Flagship Victory, 0835 Hours=====
     The bridge of the Victory is dominated by a gigantic tactical holotank. Admiral Sarnou watches the wedge of Prefecture fighters drop towards the Mesarthrim fleet. Her opponents are still disorganized; fighters, gunships, frigates, and carriers are spread out and intermixed over nearly two thousand kilometers. 
     Still, she can see a gunship screen starting to emerge, and many of the frigates seem to be swinging into firing position.
     Her fears are confirmed as red threat indicators blossom amongst the Mesarthrim fleet. “Missile sensors in target-acquisition mode!” one of her tactical officers calls. “They’re locking onto our fighters.” 
     Admiral Sarnou knows that the Delta frigates have formidable missile payloads. “They’re aiming to break up our charge. Detail some of our Piranhas to shoot down incoming missiles. They won’t get them all, but it’s the best we can do.”
     “Admiral,” one of her officers calls, “Sky Marshal Brooks’ squadron has just launched.”
     Admiral Sarnou nods. “Turn over command of the fighter attack to her, then let’s begin maneuvering to support our ships. Helm, plot me a course closer to the UCS fleet. I want all ships ready for a full missile salvo on my mark.”

=====Piranha Bravo-Six=====
     “All right, Bravo Squadron,” Bravo Lead comms. “We’re entering attack range. Break by elements and engage enemy fighters as they-”
     Warning: hostile missile lock, Parman’s flight computer blares. Evading.
     Seemingly hundreds of crimson warning symbols dance across her HUD. An oblong shape interrupts the flow of starlight a thousand kilometers ahead. Her SPAR system tags it as a Delta frigate, a dedicated anti-fighter ship. It rolls lazily, presenting one broadside towards Bravo Squadron
     One hundred and fifty anti-fighter cluster missiles spring from the Delta’s tubes. Their grav-cav drives engage, and they leap towards their targets at hundreds of Gs.
     “Break!” Bravo Lead calls. “Evasive maneuvers – damn – damn -”
     Bravo Squadron flies into an inferno. Not all of the missiles are targeted on them, but there are enough to blot out surrounding space with fire. Only last-minute evasive and the aid of the AI flight control system save them from total annihilation. 
     A direct hit blows Bravo Lead’s Piranha to shreds; she ejects, as do three other pilots whose ships crumple and disintegrate. Two more aren’t so lucky; the fireballs of their exploding fighters devour them. 
     Nine Piranhas emerge from the fireball. Sweating, Parman glances at her threat indicators and sees that the Delta has turned its attention to other threats. But they aren’t out of trouble yet. “Fighters inbound,” she calls.
     Breaking out of their orbit around a nearby carrier, six Mesarthrim Pattons, small and sleek, descend on them, firing blazers and missiles. Bravo Five takes a missile hit to his shield generators; the Pattons blow his fighter to pieces with concentrated fire as they scream past.
     “Bravo, form on me,” Parman calls. She swings her fighter onto the Pattons’ trail and toggles on her drive capacitors. “Lock and fire!”
     Her targeting systems struggle against Mesarthrim ECM as the Pattons reach five thousand kilometers and double back for another pass. The Prefecture ships greet them with a volley of missiles; the Mesarthrim reply with their own warheads.
     The exchange is lopsided. The Pattons are smaller, swifter and tougher than the Piranhas. Parman grits her teeth in frustration as her target jinks aside contemptuously, a maneuver even the best Prefecture fighters can’t match, and dodges her missile. Bravo-Four takes a hit and drops out of formation, her drive sputtering.
     The two squadrons make a head-to-head pass, spraying cannon-fire in the brief fraction of a moment before they pass each other again. Parman manages to hit a Patton, but its adaptive shields absorb her fire. One of her squad mates is luckier, chewing through a UCS fighter’s shields and pock marking its armor. The Mesarthrim return the favor by annihilating Bravo-Four.
     “They’re too tough,” Bravo-Seven calls. “I can’t break their shields even when I do get a lock.”
     “Concentrate your fire,” Parman shouts. “Double-team them. We’ve got to make up for their superior ships with better teamwork. Split by pairs after the next pass and start dogfighting.”
     The Piranhas swing through a wrenching turn in time to meet the Pattons again; this time, the Prefecture formation splits up, each element focusing their fire on an individual Patton. The UCS fighter that was damaged earlier comes apart under neutron-cannon barrage, spraying debris across the star field. The combat splits up into a wide-ranging dogfight. Parman and Bravo-Seven chase after a Patton with a particularly good pilot, which thrusts away from them, trying for a bit of maneuver room. Parman works her triggers but manages nothing except glancing hits, harmless to Mesarthrim shields. Suddenly the Patton cuts its throttles, dropping its acceleration to zero. Before she can react, Parman has screamed past it and it is on her tail.

=====Stuka ‘Sigil of Vengeance’, 0835 Hours=====
     Jan Brooks is slammed back into her seat as one of the Victory’s launch catapults hurls her fighter into the void. She glances at her cockpit displays, locates the other ships of her squadron, and finds them already dropping into formation.
     “Mike Squadron, this is Victory flight control, now transmitting an attack vector. Good luck, Sky Marshall.”
     A right line paints itself across one of her tactical displays, stabbing away from the gas giant and into the heart of the Mesarthrim fleet. She orients her fighter along the vector and jams her throttles forward. She and her squadron-mates have trained together for so long that they no longer even need to communicate by voice; they follow effortlessly. 
     “Luck,” she replies, ever confident, “won’t be necessary.”
     A quick glance over her tactical displays is reassuring. Her fighters are all over the Mesarthrim, pouring missiles into the enemy gunships and swatting UCS fighters from space. The Prefecture fighters outnumber their UCS opponents nearly five to one. The hostile Delta frigates have fired a few missile volleys, but because the Mesarthrim fleet isn’t in formation, they have no good firing lanes. Still, they’ve inflicted casualties – the missile salvos have claimed fifteen Prefecture fighters, a respectable number given that the Prefecture has nearly five-to-one air superiority.
     Brooks nudges her fighter onto a new course, aiming for a clot of gunships at the near end of the Mesarthrim fleet. We can’t let them concentrate their forces or organize. Within half a minute they are inside firing range.
     “Fighters incoming,” one of her wingmen warns.
     Brooks looks up and sees three red delta-shapes on her HUD, indicating enemy fighters closing into attack range – a few thousand kilometers away. A slow red light strobes on her threat-warning panel as they probe against her craft’s ECM, trying to attain a lock.
     “Elements Two and Three, head them off. All other fighters, make an attack pass on those gunships.” Our Stukas are perfect for anti-gunship work; we’ll have enough firepower to break through even UCS adaptive shields.
     Six of Mike’s fighters peel off and swing away towards the inbound UCS fighters. Brooks and a three-ship wedge of her Stukas continue on course towards the gunships, firing missiles. The gunships turn to meet them; blazer fire streaks past as three Nimitz take aim at her squadron. Brooks throws her fighter up on one wing, yanks the controls back as far as they’ll go, and climbs out of the fire lane, followed by one wingman. The other two break away in the opposite direction.
      She swerves her nose around – forcing her fighter to move one way while aiming another – and spikes the lead Nimitz’s shields with neutron bolts from a hundred kilometers. Her wingmen follow suit, coordinating their fire for maximum effect. 
     “Making a run,” Brooks informs her wingmen. She pushes her throttles wide open, converting her momentum from a sideways drift into a forward rush towards the gunship. Her rangefinder clicks over to less than fifty kilometers; she screams past the gunship a milliseconds later, firing everything.
     The Stuka rattles as its Hephaestus cannon vomit projectiles. The gunship shudders and rolls away under the impact; she switches to neutron cannons and turns to make another pass. Her rangefinder steadies at 250 kilometers, then drops rapidly. She screams between the two other gunships in the formation, jinking out of the way of their blazer fire.
     At a hundred klicks, the wounded gunship zeroes in on her with blazer fire; she risks a quick spray of neutron cannon bolts, then pulls up hard and accelerates away on the Z-axis of her previous course. One of the other Nimitz bounces a blazer bolt off her shields, nearly throwing her ship into a spin.
     Two of her wingmen come in on the wounded gunship in a scissors attack. Their cannon fire glances harmlessly off the gunship’s adaptive shields, but they draw its fire. The last member of her attack wedge comes up from below and unloads a Boulder missile into the gunship, catching its adaptive shields while they’re still tuned to deflect neutron bolts. The Nimitz’s belly armor buckles; Brooks slashes beneath the gunship, pumping Hephaestus rounds into the gash. Bleeding atmosphere, the Nimitz lurches through a sharp turn and accelerates away. 
     The three Nimitz close into tight formation and begin withdrawing towards a nearby carrier, putting the safety of a few hundred kilometers between themselves and Mike Squadron. Momentarily free of distractions, Brooks turns to her tactical display and begins issuing orders.

=====Flagship Victory 0840 Hours=====
     “Admiral, we’re in position,” the gunnery officer reports. 
     Marie Sarnou nods. “Warn Marshall Brooks that we’re about to fire.”
     Moments later, the entire Fourth Fleet salvoes its missile tubes, pouring nearly a thousand warheads into space. Admiral Sarnou doesn’t risk any more – if her missile flight is too dense, she might hit her own fighters.
     The missiles fire grav-cav drives, accelerating at multiple Gs for a few moments, then cut their drives and coast towards the Mesarthrim carriers; the Prefecture fighters part to let them pass. The enemy gunships are too distracted to intercept the missiles, and Sarnou nods hopefully as the carriers begin redirecting their blazer fire to point-defense. 
     Explosions rip through the Mesarthrim ranks as missiles are hit by blazer fire or deflect harmlessly off adaptive shields. A few lucky shots get through a Delta’s shields, putting craters in its hull and disabling a handful of missile tubes. 
     “I can confirm at least fifty hits,” gunnery reports; the rest of the missiles were shot down before impact, deflected or absorbed by shields, or drawn away by electronic warfare. Most of the impacts aren’t damaging; space is big and the Mesarthrim capships are well-armored. “No kills or disabled, but we broke their capital ship formations up. With the escape vectors they're on, and our fighters' positions, the Mesarthrim won't be able to regroup any time soon."
     Sarnou nods again. “Good enough.”
     “They’re returning fire!” the sensor officer warns. “At least four hundred missiles inbound!”
     Sarnou shrugs. “Let them try. They don’t have an effective formation or clear fire lines. I don’t think we’ll even be bruised.”

=====Piranha Bravo-Six=====
     “Damn it!” Flight Lieutenant Parman snarls. “Bravo-Seven, where are you?”
     The Patton on her tail fires again. Blazers penetrate her Piranha’s shields, spattering on her armor and awakening damage alarms. She throws her fighter into a frantic dive, jinking and rolling in an attempt to shake her pursuer. 
     Hostile missile lock, her computer cautions. Parman curses in reply, then sees a red-highlighted shape swooping in from above – a Sears gunship. It adds its firepower to the fury concentrated on her. A blazer bolt pierces her weakened shields and strikes the Piranha in its left wing.
     Sweating, Parman swings her fighter out of the path of the diving gunship and toggles on her drive capacitors, temporarily boosting her speed; she jettisons the missiles on her crippled left-wing hardpoints to gain a bit more acceleration. 
     She claws for distance, pursued by a pair of Mesarthrim fighters and the gunship. “Bravo-Seven, there are multiple bandits on my tail. I need cover,” she calls. “Any Bravo elements, Bravo-Six is in trouble.”
     She gets a brief lock on another Mesarthrim ship, nearly two hundred kilometers away, and fires off an LRM; she doesn’t have time to follow it before her pursuers jump her again. She swings to port, burns away for a few moments – chased by a stream of blazer fire - then drags her nose back around and charges. 
     Her attack is well-timed. Bravo-Seven – thank God he showed up – fires a pair of missiles at the gunship just as Parman looses her last SRM at one of the fighters. The Mesarthrim attack wedge breaks apart as each ship undertakes frantic evasive maneuvers. Her SRM strikes the nearest UCS fighter; Parman locks her cannon on it and blows a wing off as she screams past. It spins away, crippled. Finally, a solid hit – those bastards are tough! 
     “Thanks for the save, Bravo-Seven. I’m Winchester and badly damaged. I’ll sucker that fighter in and you nail him,” Parman orders.
     Eager for revenge, the other Mesarthrim pilot whips his fighter through a sharp turn and comes screaming in towards her. Parman throttles back and feigns heavy damage, then shoves her throttles forward as her foe enters cannon range. The Mesarthrim fighter chases after her, vying with her ship’s ECM for a missile lock; Bravo-Seven drops onto its tail and savages it with neutron cannon bolts from 150 klicks.
     The UCS ships’ adaptive shields sparkle under the assault, but the pilot twists his ship aside, and its spectacular maneuverability saves it from death – for a few moments. Parman swings her nose around and lines her cannon up. The two Prefecture pilot’s combined fire manages to take the Patton out. It blows apart in a spectacular fireball. 
     “Bait and switch,” Seven says, sounding satisfied. “Gets ‘em - ”
     The Sears gunship swings in from below and hammers Bravo-Seven with a blazer salvo. Unlike the Prefecture pilots, the Mesarthrim don’t need solid hits to do serious damage. Parman hears a terrified shout on the comm as Seven’s Piranha disintegrates, but doesn’t have time to check for an ejection pod. The gunship is after her now, and she’s back where she started.

=====Flagship Victory=====
     “Keep up the pressure, Marshal Brooks,” Admiral Sarnou calls. “We’ve almost broken through.” Missiles from the UCS fleet have rocked the Prefecture ships, but inflicted only minor damage. Neither fleet has tried to close with the other, for good reason. 
     A million kilometer’s separation gives both sides some time to react to incoming fire, but the sheer volume of missiles has effectively pinned the UCS capital ships in place; if they break formation, they’ll start taking hits. The Prefecture capships are content to sit back and support their fighters. Thus, the vast majority of today’s fighting is being carried out by fighters, bombers, and Mesarthrim gunships.
     The Fourth Fleet’s experienced pilots clearly have the upper hand; they’ve taken casualties, but not nearly as many as the Mesarthrim have. Sarnou watches as one of Bravo Squadron’s pilots – tagged Bravo-Six - courageously draws a gunship and a pair of fighters away from the carrier they’d been guarding, leaving a hole in the Mesarthrim screen.
     Sarnou considers the situation for another moment, reminding herself to have that pilot commended. The time is right, she decides, and reopens her comm with Sky Marshal Brooks.
     “Marshal Brooks, the Mesarthrim fighter screen seems to have fallen apart. I recommend we send in the bombers before they have a chance to reform.”
     Jan Brooks pauses a moment before replying – Sarnou notes her Stuka is just finishing up its fourth dogfight. “As good a time as any, I suppose. Have flight control vector the bombers in through that weak spot, and assign Golf and Romeo squadrons to escort the bombers in. I want your ships in position to retrieve and rearm the bombers when they’ve finished their attack runs.”
     “Admiral,” one of her officers calls. “The Wyoming and the Nebraska report that they’ve finished arming our reserve fighters and are ready to launch.”
     Sarnou gestures at the tac-map. "Wyoming, Nebraska, move to incoming coordinates with Aeolus, Gage, Mahopac, Amphion, Norris, and Ross. Empty fighter bays. Let’s show these bastards who they’re messing with."

=====Stuka ‘Sigil of Vengeance’=====
     “Fighters, three o’clock high!” one of Mike Squadron’s pilots calls. “They’re attacking the bombers!” Jan Brooks scans her HUD and sees a formation of crimson-highlighted delta shapes swooping in on her bombers. “Let’s go help out Golf Squadron,” she orders.
     The Mesarthrim ships tear through the Piranhas escorting the bombers, destroying two, but heavy fire from the bombers themselves gives them temporary pause. Tuning their shields to hold off neutron cannon bolts, the Mesarthrim fighters swoop in from all directions, focusing on one bomber at a time.
     One Bloodhawk has fallen by the time Mike squadron arrives. The escort Piranhas rally around Sky Marshal Brooks as she leads her attack wedge in.
     She scores her first kill from nearly five hundred kilometers, putting her last missile into a Patton just as three Bloodhawks hammers it with neutron cannons. “Break by elements and engage,” she orders. 
     Her Stuka covers the half a thousand kilometers in seconds, giving her time to rake another fighter with neutron cannon bolts as it crosses her path. A Mesarthrim ship, eager for a prestigious kill, drops onto her tail, but her wingmen chase it away. 
     “Gunships low,” a pilot from Golf squadron calls. A wedge of Nimitz are climbing into the bomber group from below, searing surrounding space with blazer fire. 
     “Romeo squadron, get on those gunships,” she orders, nudging her ship out of the way of a diving Mesarthrim Patton. “When you’re out of munitions, disengage and finish off those fighters.”
     She swings onto the Patton’s tail, but it pulls hard to port and streaks away, back towards the bombers. One of Golf’s Piranhas dives to engage it; Brooks breaks off and looks for other targets.
     Threat-warning indicators spring into life across her HUD – a huge number of missiles are trying for a lock on her. “Victory, what the hell is that?” she snarls, and gets her answer as she sees a pair of Delta frigates edging into the path of the bombers. 
     Contrails explode from the flanks of the Deltas as they fire, and one and hundred and fifty missiles swarm out into the fray. At least five are locked onto her fighter; she swings around, drops her crosshairs across the lead one as it breaks two thousand kilometers, and fries it.
     "Mike, form up on me,” she shouts, throttling up and charging towards the incoming missile flight. The missiles’ engines puff as they adjust their course; they’ll impact in mere seconds. She waits a heartbeat, then slams her throttles as far forward as they’ll go and triggers her engine capacitors. The sudden velocity change catches the missile’s electronic brains off guard; she shoots between two of them, one detonates on her magnetic screen, and the last is blown apart by her ship’s AMS system.
     Her squadron comes through intact. Golf and Romeo aren’t nearly so lucky. Deltas are dedicated anti-fighter frigates. Romeo Squadron, pinned between their gunship opponents and the missile salvo, vanishes in a tremendous hail of fireballs; only five fighters remain intact. Golf fares slightly better, with seven survivors emerging from the cloud of hellfire. Brooks is heartened by the sight of escape pods drifting through the debris – most of the pilots managed to escape their doomed ships. 
     Brooks lines up on the nose of the nearest Delta. Range: Five thousand kilometers, her computer informs her. She waits for the range to drop, spikes the Delta’s shields with neutron cannons, then rolls away to avoid a storm of retaliatory blazer and autocannon fire from the frigate. She knows her armament can barely harm the frigate, but if she can distract it from the bombers, they may be able to deal a fatal blow. 
     “Mike, cover those bombers,” she orders. Tension thrums through her veins – another salvo might force the Bloodhawks to break off.
     The Deltas fire again, spraying missiles into the bombers from seven thousand kilometers. “Chase those warheads down!” Brooks snarls.
     The Stukas aren’t exactly suited to missile-interception duty, but they manage to bag a fair number of missiles with precision fire. Most of those that remain are spoofed by ECM or shot down by the bombers. Several Bloodhawks are crippled or destroyed nonetheless. Mesarthrim fighters break off their dogfighting with the Piranhas and descend on the wounded, hoping for a few tactically vital kills.
     Mike squadron swoops in and hammers them; Brooks double-taps a Patton with Hephaestus fire as she screams past, rattling it. It veers away, its astounding maneuverability carrying it to safety before her wing mates can finish it off. 
     The dogfight quickly swings in the Prefecture’s favor; it also prevents the Deltas from firing more cluster missiles, for fear of hitting their own fighters. The Mesarthrim fight bravely, but they’re no match for the elite pilots of Mike. Brooks curses the fact that Mesarthrim rarely retreat and never give up; it makes the battle far bloodier than it should be.
     “Mike, this is Victory flight control. Able Squadron has just concluded an engagement and is vectoring in on your position. ETA thirty seconds.”
     Brooks nods – reinforcements aren’t necessary, but they’ll certainly save lives.
     The arrival of additional Prefecture fighters brings the battle to a close; the remaining Mesarthrim Pattons and Nimitz are mopped up quickly.
     With the nearby fighters gone, the Bloodhawks are clear to begin their runs. Brooks swings her Stuka’s nose around towards the bombers just in time to see the lead flight launch their Orca torpedoes. The breacher-equipped warheads blaze brightly for a few moments as they build up speed, and then fall dark, coasting.
     The two Delta frigates abandon their efforts to line up another missile salvo and begin targeting the torpedoes. The lead Orca flight is blown apart by counter-missiles; blazer fire catches another pair of torpedoes, but within moments they begin scoring hits. Most of the Orcas blow up harmlessly on the frigates’ adaptive shields, but finally the lead Delta takes a glancing hit as a torpedo detonates meters from her hull. The small warship’s entire frame shudders, and she rolls to port, bleeding atmosphere from a rent in her thin armor. Brooks assesses the damage with a practiced eye as she and Mike Squadron begin strafing the damaged frigate. Its port-side missile broadside has been disabled, and its armor breached; internal explosions and loss of atmosphere must have done even more damage.
     “Put another torpedo into her, then focus your fire on the other frigate,” she orders. 
     Mike Squadron draws the wounded frigate’s fire until another Orca hits it amidships. The direct hit splits the frigate nearly in half, and it pours wounded atmosphere and debris into space.

=====Piranha Bravo-Six=====
     Lieutenant Parman has just about resigned herself to her fate. Her engine capacitors are drained, her cannon have been damaged, and she is boxed between two fighters and a gunship. Blazer bolts streak around her, casting harsh light into her cockpit.
     Suddenly, an autocannon salvo rakes the gunship, tearing it to shreds. Neutron-cannon spikes and missiles send the fighters scurrying for cover.
     Parman banks to port and sees the carrier Nebraska cruising into the edge of the battle zone, turrets reaching out to swat at Mesarthrim fighters. Relief washes through her. “Thanks for the save, Nebraska,” she says. “Request emergency landing vector for damaged Piranha Bravo-Six.”
     Minutes later, she settles her Piranha to a rough landing inside the Nebraska.

=====Flagship Victory=====
     “We’re almost there,” Admiral Sarnou comments. 
     The Prefecture fighter attack has left the UCS screen in tatters, and Bloodhawk bombers slip through to strike at the capital ships. Orca torpedoes slam into warships all across the Mesarthrim fleet; most are shot down or deflected by shields, but enough get through to inflict heavy damage. Once the Bloodhawks have exhausted their munitions, they swing back to their carriers to rearm.
     Piranhas and Stukas strafe the capital ships, following up on bomber attacks and mercilessly finishing off damaged gunships. The Prefecture doesn’t take prisoners.
     A Terra carrier, damaged earlier in the battle, has the misfortune of suffering a direct Orca hit to an auxiliary reactor; fire vomits from her stern before failsafes kick in and save her from destruction. Cheers sound around the Victory’s bridge.
     By this point the missile barrage from the UCS Deltas has dwindled to near nothing. Admiral Sarnou orders her capital ships to close in and deliver the coup de grace.
     “Energy spike!” her sensors officer calls. “The UCS ships are recovering fighters and powering up their GRLD drives!”
     “Full acceleration,” Sarnou barks. “Let’s hit them with everything we’ve got!”
     A final, furious torpedo attack, accompanied by a few long-range mass driver rounds from the Fleet, destroys three more Mesarthrim gunships before they can jump out. A constellation of new stars blaze for a few moments as the UCS ships enter hyperspace, then all is quiet.
     The Victory bridge crew cheers again. Admiral Sarnou isn’t quite as jubilant. That was just a raiding force, one that had the misfortune of arriving while we were ready for them. We’ll have to hold the Fleet on alert, and send ships out to cover the other worlds in the Holt District. We didn’t even manage to kill one of their capital ships. Should I have been more aggressive? No – losses were high enough as it is. Even outnumbering them five-to-one they put up a stiff fight.
     The butcher’s bill is short. She’s lost thirty-five fighters, and twice as many are damaged. The UCS suffered heavier losses – their fighters were nearly wiped out, and half of their gunships were annihilated. 
     “Admiral,” her communications officer calls. “I’m getting reports that the other Fleets are engaging Mesarthrim raiding forces.”
     “All right, people, back to your stations,” Sarnou shouts, quieting the celebrations. “We may have won the battle, but the war’s not over yet.”

'''Viveka Slanj nee Raegos'''
*Place of Birth: [[Junheim]]
*Allegiance: [[Reigess Suverände]]

''Viveka Slanj nee Raegos'' was the second daughter in the Slanj family, and won the favor of Lorrodi Raegos in his youth. The two were married before Lorrodi assumed the throne of the [[Reigess Suverände]]. Viveka is the Drottning, or queen, of the [[Reigens]].

May 23, [[2322]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After a month of stalemate while both sides prepared for battle, Anthony Hadrian's war with Lucius Black stepped up a notch. The former Grand Admiral launched a full offensive force to take the planet from the Empire. Hadrian committed his entire army--the First Legion--to the invasion of Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hundreds of Whale Class Dropships landed in key locations on the planet. The First Mechanized Division was assigned to capturing the spaceport facilities in the southeastern portion of the former United States, the Second and Third Mechanized Divisions were sent after industrial centers in Asia, and the Fourth and Fifth Mechanized Regiments were sent into the middle-eastern countries to capture the nuclear power plants.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;So far, only the First Mechanized Division has met resistance. As soon as their dropships hit the dirt, they came under fire from the Royal Imperial Army's Catapult artillery Rigs, firing from several kilometers away.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The rebel Rigs scoured the area, cleaning out the artillery spotters that had been guiding the missiles of the Catapults. The First Mechanized Division is still pinned down and fighting hard in the everglades of Florida, but the other regiments are pushing towards their goals.

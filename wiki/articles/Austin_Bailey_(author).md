'''Austin Bailey''' found the Exodus War website shortly after it went online in 1998, following the story for several years before contacting James Davis with an offer of models and story ideas.  Since the development of the [[Ixoarchada]] in early 2001, Austin has been a contributor of stories, ideas, and models, as well as a general editor for the various incarnations of the Exodus War website.  

Most importantly, Austin considers himself the "resident fan" amongst the authors and tries to view things from the angle of the readers.  He currently resides in Washington, D.C.

Austin uses the tag "[[User:ABailey|ABailey]]" when contributing to this site.

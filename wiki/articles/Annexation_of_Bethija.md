== Overview ==

After conquering [[Earth]], [[Holt]], and [[Tejat]] in rapid-fire succession, the [[Onyx Empire]] began planning for its most difficult invasion: the [[Titan System]]. The system was a fortress, and by now all surviving [[United Earth Alliance]] forces were flocking to the banner of General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]].  One of the best choices for staging an invasion was [[Bethija]].  It had large spaceports for transporting materiel, and was close enough to the Titan System to make a round trip in a little over two days at maximum speed. When [[Robert Marshall]] and [[Cecil Au-Yueng]] put forward their proposal to invade Bethija before invading the Titan System, Emperor [[Lucius Black]] immediately approved.

When the first ships of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s invasion fleet arrived in the [[Proxima Procyon System]] on October 2, [[2315]], they found absolutely no defenses waiting for them.  Instead, they were hailed by representatives of [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] Leonid Shevchenko, offering to immediately surrender the planet to Imperial authority.

== Bethijan Debate ==

Elements of the United Earth Alliance's 13th Space Battle Group had been assigned to the defense of Bethija, but after word came of the rapid loss of Earth, Holt, and Tejat, the Bethijan people wanted no part of the bloodbath.  Premier Schevchenko put forward legislation to dissolve ties with the United Earth Alliance and to invite Imperial rule.

Many people balked at Schevchenko's plans, but he gained enough support in the planetary Assembly to pass the proposal into law.  The 13th Space Battle Group was ordered to withdraw, and by September 26, 2315, Bethija was completely undefended.

== Transition to Imperial Rule ==

When the Royal Imperial Navy found no resistance and an open invitation to take over the planet, they sent word back to Earth.  Lucius Black was initially suspicious, but ordered the invasion to operate under a strict "self defense only" rules of engagement until he ordered otherwise.  Imperial occupation was accepted without resistance, and by early November, Black was convinced the Bethijans were acting in good faith.

To reward Premier Schevchenko for his wise behavior, Lucius Black allowed him to become Imperial Governor--though largely in name only.  Leonid Schevchenko found that his governorship was at the mercy and whim of his "special advisor", Sanja Khuree.

Schevchenko and Khuree ruled Bethija in the name of the Onyx Empire largely without incident until Bethija was captured by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] towards the end of the war.

The '''Reigenjord''' is one of the oldest [[Ikronin]] and [[Reigen]] artifacts, dating back to the days of the [[Anthronoris]].  It has been maintained, repaired, and had its parts replaced over the thousands of years since its construction, but still shows a semblance of its original design and combat abilities.

It is traditionally worn by members of the Reigen royalty in battle.  Its last use was by [[Jiliur Raegos]] in the [[Volucris War]].  After his death, [[Lorrodi Raegos]] ordered that the Reigenjord be enshrined with Jiliur's body.

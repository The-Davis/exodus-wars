The Gauss Cannon is a medium energy form of the Mass Driver, capable of launching projectiles at high velocities.

The "Shrieker" was the second Gauss Cannon design developed by the [[Royal Imperial Labs]]. It was designated GC-02, and was developed to be used as a main weapon for the [[Royal Imperial Air Force|Air Force]]'s [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]].

The GC-2's smaller cousin, the [[GC-01 Light Gauss Cannon - "Piper"|"Piper"]], suffered from several design flaws that resulted in its discontinued use by the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] after the initial test runs of their [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#YF-4 (Pre-Production Armed Prototype)|YF-4]] prototype [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]]. When the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4D (Computer-Aided Avionics Package|F-4D]] variant of their "Raven" fighter was developed to combat the stiff resistance in the [[Titan System]], [[Royal Imperial Air Force|Air Force]] pilots complained that their weapons could not punch through the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]]'s [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]]' shields quickly enough.

The GC-2 was developed to return to the paired-weapons load tested in the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#YF-4 (Pre-Production Armed Prototype)|YF-4]]. Future [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4]] variants would mount a GC-2 along with a [[:Category:Beam Weapons|beam weapon]] of one variety of another.

Engineers developed the GC-2 with slightly larger nickel-ferrous projectiles than those used by the [[GC-01 Light Gauss Cannon - "Piper"|GC-1]], and a significantly more powerful magnetic accelerator. The net result was a 68% increase in kinetic energy delivered to a target at optimum range.

The "Shrieker" derived its name from the high-pitched whine typical of most gauss cannons, which comes from the capacitor used to charge the magnetic accelerator. Unlike its smaller [[GC-01 Light Gauss Cannon - "Piper"|"Piper"]] cousin, the "Shrieker" required a cooldown period between bursts, and could not be fired in a continuous stream. Its bursts sounded like a sudden shriek each time it was fired.

The Shrieker was one of the first stable Gauss Cannon variants, and saw consistent use by the [[Onyx Empire]] for years, and even limited use by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

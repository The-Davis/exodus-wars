*Population: 75 million (estimated 2046 level)

The '''Kingdom of Thailand''' was an East Asian kingdom. It was extremely reluctant to join the [[United Earth Alliance]]. When King Bhumibol Adulyadej died on the throne in [[2016]], it was expected that the Thai monarchy would end. Instead, the crown prince Maha Vajiralongkorn took the throne, and violently put down several rebellions attempting to unify Thailand with the rest of the world. He was assassinated in [[2046]], and his son signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] to avoid the same fate.

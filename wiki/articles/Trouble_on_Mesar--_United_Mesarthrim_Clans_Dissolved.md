United Mesarthrim Clans Dissolved - 3/14/2324 - by Andrew Sears

     In a shocking move, the Mesarthrim High Command has disbanded. The United Mesarthrim Clans government is now defunct. Uber-General Eric Francis dissolved the council shortly before this morning's press conference.
     Eric Francis: "Friends, this is a decision which we did not come to lightly. Dissolving the High Council is the end of a great tradition of military might and honor.
     "With the end of the United Mesarthrim Clans, I will be changing my allegiance to one of the new factions. William Camp, friend and trusted advisor of our late beloved leader, has formed the United Centusi States. All forces still loyal to the former High Command will become part of the UCS, and we hereby formally claim the star and worlds of Centus as our legal territory."

     UCS officials demanded the withdrawal of all renegade factions from the Centus starsystem. Currently, only the Allied Auellal League--Mesarthrim warriors from the world of Picket's Grave--have complied. This is rather unexpected, given that the AAL was the best-organized and most belligerent of the enemies of the United Mesarthrim Clans. AAL leader Steven Cue had this to say:
    Steven Cue: "All League troops will be off of Mesar within the hour, and my warships will be heading for home before the day is out. Furthermore, the League wishes to show our support to the people of Terra. We will be donating one billion Likvada to the Terran Recovery Project, and we will be sending medical professionals to aid in curing the plague. We hope that this will show the sincerity of our desire to become a Member State of the Alliance of Planets, and to remain independent of the UCS."

     Up until now, B-Net has been unable to obtain details on any of the renegade Mesarthrim factions, with the single exception of the Allied Auellal League. It is feared that minor rogue factions will soon rush in to fill the power vacuum created on Mesar with the withdrawal of the Allied Auellal League.
     There are still many other questions that we hope to have answered. Is the AAL really as bad a threat to peace as former Uber-General Eric Francis says? Are the United Centusi States the legitimate replacement to the Mesarthrim High Command? B-Net will keep you informed.

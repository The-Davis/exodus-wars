August 16, [[2322]]

by Scott Sanburn

=====Theia=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Scott Sanburn of the Titanian Defense Force contacted B-Net representative Michelle Jarvens with important news on the Battle of Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn: "After some debate, the Titanian Defense Force has decided to commit to helping the Mesarthrim and the Prefecture. We'll be sending in mostly ammunition and supplies for their troops, but we will operate all fortifications and send some assault units to assist in the attacks."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Michelle Jarvens: "General-Commander, is this a wise course of action? Is the Empire really contained on Terra?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn: "Somewhat. The Empire's forces outside of the Sol System are Core Fleets. They're defensive units only, not trained or equipped to attack a fortress like the Titan System. We can afford to send the support our allies need."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Michelle Jarvens: "Thank you, General."

'''Benjamin Miles Hlow'''
*Date of Birth: [[2302]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: None

''Benjamin Miles Hlow'' (born 2302) does not follow the path of genetics or medicine like his father and grandfather before him, but takes great interest in the development of computer and [[AI]] technology.

==AI Technician==

While he lacks the genius of his father in mechanics, quantum physics and medicine, Benjamin Hlow is still a brilliant technician, mathematician and computer programmer.

Among Benjamin's accomplishments are the [[Calpaca]] computer center in the [[De Mairan Sector]], maintenance of Arthur the EI, and many modern programming accomplishments.

Benjamin was originally an [[Empire|Imperial]] during the [[Imperial Civil War]], but quickly abandoned them after the [[Battle of Terra]] began. He became disinterested in politics entirely, and secluded himself in his home on [[Calpaca]].

Benjamin and his father soon reconciled after the war's end, and often collaborate together on using computer science and medicine to improve lives around the galaxy.

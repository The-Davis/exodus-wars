The '''Capricorn Haven''' was one of many orbital semi-automated shipyards designed by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] and [[Haven Shipyards]].

Capricorn Haven was the birthplace of both the [[Vigilant Class Frigate]] and the [[Atlas Class Carrier]], and was the main design center and operations headquarters for [[Haven Shipyards]] throughout the company's lifetime. The design team stationed there was led by [[Reg Brenstein]].

The "Letters from Sasha Brrvil" were a series of journal entries, reportedly written by a 10-year-old [[Lorithos]] girl during the [[Volucris War]].  Their authenticity is unknown, but they helped generate a groundswell of support for the Lorithos refugees.

===== The Last Sunset =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa came home from work with a sad look on his face. I could tell he was worried, but when he saw me, he only smiled and asked how my day was.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He walked past me into the house, and went to Mama's room, shutting the door behind him. They talked in hushed tones, and when I opened the door, they were silent and looked at me. Mama was crying. They both looked at me, and I was told to shut the door and go to my room.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A bit later, Papa came in and asked if I wanted to go into the City for dinner, and maybe go to the Ikronin Market. I was so happy, since we did not often go because Papa often worked late, or had to go to meetings.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We went to dinner and I tried some type of fish. Papa said it was from Ipsof, and that it was special--I didn't like it. Mama was silent the whole time.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After dinner, I had lots of fun at the Ikronin Market. There is always something wonderful to see at the Market--some new animal, or machine, or flyer. There were no machines or flyers this time, but there was a good selection of shows to see at the holoplex, so we went to one.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I fell asleep in the show, and woke up as we arrived home late in the evening. The sun was just going down, and I peeked out at it for a few seconds as Papa carried me in the house. I fell fast asleep.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Long Dark started that night.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I miss seeing the sun.

===== The Long Dark =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We got to Heiress Point several days ago. Papa brought us here the morning after we went to the Ikronin Market. At first I was excited, getting to go to the City again. But the sky stayed dark, and the City seemed dead.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When we arrived, Papa took met with his boss, Mr. Dkeel, who gave us a mag-rail pass and told us to head to Heiress Point. Heiress Point is the main mag-rail station and spaceport on the planet. It's a few hour's ride away, even on the mag-rail.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa says Heiress Point is built over the Old Palace. He said the Old Palace was destroyed even before he was born.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We left for Heiress Point later that day. There were many people at the mag-rail station, but there were not enough seats on the trains for everyone. We had a pass, so we were allowed on.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I saw a few people I knew there, like Jirkl, a friend from school, and Opiz Likte, one of my teachers. They did not get on the train with us.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When we arrived here at Heiress Point, there were even more people than in the City. We did not have a pass to get beyond Heiress Point, so we were told to get off the train and get one. Papa ran to get us tickets, but he didn't come back for a few hours. When he got back, he said that we couldn't leave for a day.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It was still dark outside, and had been all day.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mr. Dkeel arrived a bit later, and we went to another building outside of the spaceport. Papa's company had an office in the building, so we spent the night there.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I woke up to the Thunder.

===== The Thunder =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;My eyes shot open to a terrible boom. It was like the ground itself shook and a giant had knocked himself into the wall of the building. Half an hour later, there was another boom. This continued all day long, with a another giant boom every half hour. They were not all as loud or as close, but we felt them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa and Mr. Dkeel went to go find some food at the markets, and Mama tried to comfort me. All I could do was cry.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When Papa returned, he said the markets were empty, and there was no food to be found at the stores. But the building we were in was deserted, so Papa and Mr. Dkeel had searched the top floors and gotten into the cafeteria, so we had something to eat.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa said that they had gone to the mag-rail station and were told that the station was not running, and only space flights were being made for the day because there were so many people trying to leave. I didn't understand why. The people at the station were supposed to call Mr. Dkeel's office when the rails started running again.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I wanted to go outside later, but Papa said I couldn't, since there were so many people. We just settled in to wait for the call.<br>
And still the Thunder continued.

===== The Army =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I had seen the police in the City before. I had seen the desert patrols around our small town. I even knew Reilir's dad, who was the local Justice in our town.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;But I had never seen the Army. I had never seen the fighting robots marching, or the floating tanks in the streets. They scared me.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When Papa saw them in outside the next day, he went to try to talk to them. They told us we needed to stay inside where it was safe and to not come outside until someone comes to get us to take us to the mag-rail station. I still didn't see why we had to leave, and I didn't understand why we had to stay inside.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It was still dark and I wanted to play. There were no games on any of the machines at the office, and nothing to play with. I was reading through letters on one of the office's computers, and then the networks went down, and I couldn't bring up anything anymore. Mr. Dkeel tried to call the maintenance company, but none of the lines would work.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It was later that night that Papa and Mr. Dkeel went looking around the building, and Mr. Dkeel came back to tell us they had found a vidscreen on the fifth floor. Mr. Dkeel told Mama to come with him and Mama told me to stay in the office.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I didn't want to be by myself, so I followed them upstairs. They walked into the room with the vidscreen and I saw the door shut. I went up to the door to see if I could hear anything through it, but it was too thick. I pushed the door open a bit to peek in at the vidscreen.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa jumped up when I screamed. The pictures on the screen were terrifying. I had never seen the Monsters before.<br>

===== The Monsters =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I ran out of the room and back to the office, all the way down the stairs. I could tell Papa was behind me, but I didn't want to stop.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There were pictures on the vidscreen of monsters. Not the kind you see at the holoplex. These were real. They were running through the streets in the City, or maybe it was somewhere else. Some were giants, bigger than buildings, and some were smaller.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And there were people there too. The monsters were killing them--eating them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I ran to the couch and started crying. Papa came in and held me.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I woke up in the middle of the night. There were still tears in my. There was a lot of noise coming from the street, so I went to the window to see what was going on. That was the first time I saw them.<br>

===== The Foreigners =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mama was already at the window when I got up to go see what all the noise was. She said Papa and Mr. Dkeel had already gone down stairs to see what was happening.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;From the window, all I could see was bright lights. Papa and Mr. Dkeel had turned off all the lights in the building a few days earlier so that the building would seem empty. With it dark inside and out, the light was blinding.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Once my eyes adjusted, I started to make out some of the shapes in the road. At first I thought it was the army, but these were not the fighting robots I had seen days earlier, or the same floating tanks that had crowded the street earlier in the week--the ones that we say pass quite often. The looked different, and the men in the machines looked different.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;That's when Mr. Dkeel burst into the door.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"They're here! They're here!" he was yelling excitedly.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mama and I jumped when he burst in. I didn't know what he was talking about.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Who's here, Gerroed?" Mama asked.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Everyone! The Reigens! The Ixoarchada! The Commonweath and the Kalidasans! The Council has sent us an army!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At this, Mr. Dkeel ran back out of the room. I didn't know who he was talking about, but Mama got very excited and told me to get dressed to go down in the street. I threw my clothes on and we ran down to the street.<br>

===== The Parade =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By the time we got downstairs, there were already a lot of people in the street, all standing around watching the long line of men and machines go by, marching on their way to Heiress Point.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When Papa saw us he ran to us. He was very excited.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I want you to see this Sasha. These men are going to save us!" he said.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He picked me up and put me on his shoulders and then went back to the side of the street. This was the first time I saw the foreigners. Some of them were like the men I had seen in the market, "sons of Terra" Papa called them. The men marched by, with their helmets on and their visors up. Some looked happy to see all of us, but most had hard looks on their faces, and some looked scared.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I had learned about Terra in school. It was the homeworld of the [[United Earth Alliance]], who came to Lorithan to help us out of the Burning. But I had seen Terrans before. Many of the aid workers at the community hospital are Terran. We call them voseiska*.<br>
<small><small>(*This is a play on the Huorak words for healthy, seiska, and doctor, voseik.)</small></small><br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa pointed to a giant black and blue machine on two legs. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's from the Commonwealth, Sasha! They were part of the Alliance!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After a row of the black and blue machines marched by, more machines came, but these were not the Terrans.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The men that strode by were like Aprizkiv*. Some were giants themselves, towering over the rest of us as they walked by. They wore giant suits of armor, and were larger than a fighting robot.<br>
<small><small>(*Roughly translated as ‘angels' or ‘gods' of some sort. A generalized, non-specific noun, although treated as a proper noun.)</small></small><br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Ixoarchada came! They're actually here!" Papa yelled to Mama.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He pointed to the men in their large suits of armor as they marched along. I could barely see their faces, though. There was metal and machinery all over them. They all stared ahead and never looked at us. There were also iron giants! They looked like men, but were as tall as a building. They walked along, holding equipment and smaller machines. I asked Papa if there were men inside, but he said he didn't know.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Then I saw a tall, white and purple machine, with small machines on each side. The men themselves seemed to float in the air, with nothing actually touching the ground.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On top of the big machine was a man in a long purple cape. He looked like the men I see at the Ikronin Market, but his hair had gold in it, not just pure white. It was like a ray of sunlight in the sky.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A few people in the crowd began to cheer, and Papa pointed to the man.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Do you know who that is, Sasha? Do you?" he asked.<br>
I shook my head no.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's the Reigen Prince! That's Crown Prince Jiliur! He's come to help us!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When Papa said this, I understood. I had heard about the Reigens once or twice in school. They have a King and a Queen like Lorithan's Prekohr used to be. I think the teacher said it was wrong to have a King, but nobody seemed to mind a Prince being here.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After the line of men finished, Mama turned to Mr. Dkeel and Papa.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Is that all? Is that everyone? I saw nobody from the Alliance. Where are the Meroniri and the Ikronin? What about Titan? Didn't we help them?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Papa shook his head and tried to calm Mama. "I'm sure they have landed somewhere else. The Prefecture is probably here somewhere as well. I bet they came here like they came to Kronheim! I bet the whole Alliance has come! Maybe even the Mesarthrim!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We were all very excited, and we went back in the building. Before long, the patrols started, and we saw all the men that had marched by earlier begin to move about the streets. Papa and Mama tried to explain to me that people from the whole galaxy had come to save us from the monsters, and that soon we could go back home. I was very happy when I fell asleep that night.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;That's the last smile I can remember.

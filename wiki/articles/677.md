== Events of 677AD ==

At the beginning of the year, a group of scientists headed by [[Siran Urstana]] finally makes a breakthrough in the understanding of the [[Nechanos Tolesca]].  Instead of analyzing the technical logs for the device, they happen upon more general scientific information in one of the computers from the [[Fauril Konisin]].  By the end of the year, they finally grasp the paired-molecule concept that was the basis of the vast communications network operated by the [[Anthronoris]].

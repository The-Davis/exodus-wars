==Overview==

The M-29 60mm Mortar is a smooth bore, muzzle-loading, high-angle-of-fire weapon used for close-in support of ground troops. It can be broken down and carried by a crew of three. It breaks into three components, the cannon itself, the bipod, and the baseplate. There is also a sight attachment which can be carried in the baseplate.

The mortar is gravity-fired: one of the operators drops a shell into the muzzle, and the gunner fires the weapon.

==Development==

Developed by arms designers during the last leg of the [[Second Exodus]].

==Ordinance==

The '''High Explosive (HE)''' mortar shell can deliver a powerful explosive rated at 400 kilojoules for any target at "ground zero." The shell's triggering fuse can be set for a proximity burst activated by distance from a heat source, or EM field. The fuse can also detonate on impact, shortly before impact, or a few seconds after impact.

The '''[[White Phosphorus]] (WP)''' mortar shell can be loaded with various loads of phosphorus, designed to deliver smoke screens, or delivery incendiary bursts.

The '''Illumination (ILLUM)''' mortar shell delivers a slow-burning, high-illumination candle, which deploys on a parachute at the apogee of its flight. The operators use this when their forces have numerical superiority, and the stealth of darkness would work against them.

The '''Infrared Illumination (IR)''' mortar shell delivers a covert heat source which falls to the ground and provides heat-based illumination for night vision technology. It is used when the operators are supporting forces who need to use stealth during night operations, and visible light illumination would work against them.

==Operators==

The [[Royal Imperial Army]] was the first to deploy the mortar to their troops.

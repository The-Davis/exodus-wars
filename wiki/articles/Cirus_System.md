The '''Cirus System''' consists of the star Cirus, the XX planets, the moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Cirus, the planets are XX, XX, [[Lusar]], XX, XX. XX planets are orbited by moons. The planets are named from words in the [[Anderung]] language.

==Stellar Navigation==

Cirus is the Cirus System's only star. Cirus is a class G3O yellow dwarf. Cirus has a strong heliosphere with a particle windspeed of roughly 3.5 million kilometers per hour. Starship navigation is affected by Cirus's heliosphere, but it is often a hazard for lower-quality ships.

The Cirus System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Cirus is part of the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and is a close neighbor of the [[Cinaed System]] and the [[Tirshan System]].

==Orbital Bodies==
The inner Cirus System is contains the terrestrial planets. Only [[Lusar]] is inhabitable.

===Lusar===

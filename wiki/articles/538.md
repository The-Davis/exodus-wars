== Events of 538AD ==

The fleet of [[terraformation]] satellites leaves the [[Bollaria System]] and arrives in the [[Plasala System]].  They are met there by additional satellites from the [[Ularen System]].  They move into orbit of [[Tolesca]] and begin their centuries-long work.  

[[Ixoarchada]] scholars and engineers hope that the increased number of satellites, fully three times more than used on [[Veratul]] or [[Drisonir]], as well as new terraformation technologies and techniques, will reduce terraformation time to only three centuries.

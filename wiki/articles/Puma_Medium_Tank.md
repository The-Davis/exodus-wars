*Armament:
**1 Large Extended-Range Neutron Cannon
**1 Small Autocannon (1500 Rounds)
**2 Rattler Multi-Purpose Missiles

===Overview:===

After the Condor was finished and the production models rolled out, Rahn Industries design teams began to look around for other products to improve. Since the Alpha Blue project systematically upgraded all the tanks, the designers decided to follow that path again. With another round of testing and surveys complete, R.I. R&D decided to vastly improve the Puma Mk.II tank.

[[Image:PumaMediumTank1.jpg]]


===Capability===

As with all upgrades, the Puma Mk.III has all the newest technology that was at the designers disposal when the plans were drawn up. Since the original Puma was consider the Condors little brother, designers decided to give the Puma Mk.III a number of its big brothers characteristics.

The first similarity that is seen is the addition of the same cannon that the Condor Mk.III uses as its main gun. This gives the Puma Mk.III the same range and firepower as its bigger brother, but in a smaller package. Along with the main gun, the new tank also supports the Rattler multi-purpose missile system, allowing for increased firepower for larger targets. To round out the weapons complement is a small autocannon with 1,500 rounds, located in front of the commanders hatch on the turret.

Along with the weapons similarities, the Puma Mk.III also boasts the four tread system, allowing for greater survivability and better control in combat. This, combined with an increased amount of armor, allows the Puma Mk.III to take much more of a beating then its original model. 

[[Image:PumaMediumTank2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Since the Puma Mk.III offers almost the same firepower and survivability as the Condor Mk.III for a lower price, the demand for the medium tank is skyrocketing. Even though the tank isn't able to take as much of a beating as its big brother, the fact that it can now support it even better is helping to further sales.

The FDS Visage was destroyed during the [[Second Battle of Centus]] when it took extensive fire from Raumstraff Island.  The Visage broke apart, killing the entire crew, however, several large fragments of its super-structure and powerplant collided with Raumstraff Island, causing extensive damage - however not removing the installation from the fight.

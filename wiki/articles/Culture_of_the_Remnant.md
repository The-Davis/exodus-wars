To understand the culture of [[the Remnant]], one must understand the history that has brought them to the current day.  

The [[Anthronoris]], also known as the "Masters," originally evolved over countless millennia much like their offspring, the various human races, have; slowly with spurts of spurts of violence and peace.  This all changed abruptly with the discovery of the Sin Terminus (needs a cooler name) Treatment.  This "without end" was a genetic modification which allowed the Anthronoris the prospect of a seemingly unending lifespan.  Their usual lifespan of several hundred years expanded to immortality while maintaining a fairly robust reproductive cycle.  This drastic change irrevocably changed the fate of the entire galaxy.  

Now, where only a few world were needed to contain the entirety of the Anthronoris people, hundreds would be required.  The cycle of life and death had been so skewed that the original six systems had become overcrowded in a matter of four generations.  The Anthronoris began a program of research and expansion.  An aggressive nature when it came to conflict and expansion because the central cultural trait.  Discovery flourished as experiments previously interrupted by the deaths of the researchers knew no such hindrance.  The Anthronoris quickly became advanced beyond any other known race in the galaxy.  As their empire grew, countless lesser races had to be exterminated as part of the [[Sacrum Bellus]] to free up valuable real estate.  The exact number is lost to history.  

Then the [[Ngrligru]], seeing the atrocities the Anthronoris were committing against race after race, took a stand against the Pax Anthronoris.  Their will to fight surprised the Anthronoris, and losses quickly mounted.  The "Final Solution" or [[Njaa]] was developed and deployed by the Pax Anthronoris.  The result of this form of bio warfare irrevocably changed galactic history.  The resultant [[Sundering]] wiped out nearly all of the remaining Anthronoris.  What was left fled and became the [[Remnant]].  

The Remnant retains its Anthronoris origins and its people prize both aggression and discovery.  The Vitaeternum, a lesser "life eternal" treatment is still administered to Masters youth, which increases their lifespans by thousands of years.  It was decided as part of the [[Master Plan]] that a modification would be made to the Remnant genome that would slow reproduction levels until such time that the [[Ngrligru]] no longer proved a threat to a reborn Anthronoris Empire.  For this reason, each child is a treasured gift to its parents, relatives and the society at large.  

The Remnant also, since the days of the Empire, espouses the ideal that the end always justifies the means.  This can be seen time and again in their extermination of competing races early on through their tactics used during the Sundering.  They are a very vengeful people who would rather destroy an entire planet than let it fall into the hands of their enemies.  

They view the younger races in a different light because of those races' special relationship with the Remnant.  The Remnant often refers to the younger human races as their '''"children"''' (needs a cool Remnantine name), including even the [[Meroniri]] despite their advanced nature.  But the Remnant does not see itself as an elderly ancestor whose time has past but rather as a vibrant enforcer who is not hesitant to use the rod whenever the '''children''' falter.  The Remnant very much wishes to reunite the children and rebuild its empire of old and covertly seeks ways of attaining this.    

Masters pay great respect to those of greater age because of the sheer volume of experience obtained through a long life.  With this is a great respect for histories because of the unique opportunity to often be able to talk with those who had witnessed great events of the past.  Family connections, especially to those families associated with great deeds in the past, also hold considerable sway in the Masters society.

Masters likewise greatly honor those who have willingly answered the call to arms in defense of the Remnant.  Personnel of the Reliquiae Astrum Classis are always held in the highest regard because every Remnant citizen knows that if these warriors fail, their very existence is assured to cease at the hands of the [[Ngrligru]] tribes.        

The concept of time for a typical Master is very foreign to a member of any other race.  Because of their longevity, units such as years and decades are not sufficient for tracking significant differences in time.  A type of hours, minutes, and days are used in the planning of military operations as well as for a few other activities, but for the most part, a Master measures the span of his or her life through a remembrance of great past events that they have borne witness to, or notable deeds they have performed.

The Mesarthrim Federation '''Kriegsmarine''' is the chief offensive and defensive force for the [[Mesarthrim Federation]].  Unlike other space navies, the Kriegsmarine does not station fleets in orbit of its planets.  Instead, a single fleet is harbored at a deep space anchorage.  The Kriegsmarine is also responsible for customs patrols and enforcement.

== History ==

The Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine was an ad hoc creation of [[Arnold Stadt]] in [[2334]].

By 2334, the [[Tempest War]] had become an ugly and desperate war for the [[United Centusi States]].  On January 2nd, [[William Camp]] sent Arnold Stadt and the [[United Centusi States Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] into [[Prefecture]] space with orders to recover all remaining military assets from [[Mesarthrim]]-held planets, then to conduct massive nuclear bombardments in the [[Beta Arae System]] and the [[Avior System]] in a territory-denial scheme.

Stadt did not follow his orders.  He had secretly been in contact with [[Mark Edge]], [[Steven Edge]], and [[Bethany Tischler]].  The three set up a clandestine meeting with the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], where it was decided that if Arnold Stadt could guarantee the downfall of Camp's government, the Taviridis Somarchada would not honor their treaty to intervene on behalf of Camp, and would recognize a new Mesarthrim government.

In May of 2334, Stadt took the Zweite Flotte to [[Comur]].  The planet was liberated and the new Mesarthrim Federation was born.  After the [[Second Tirshan Uprising]], Mark Edge and Bethany Tischler officially welcomed the [[Auellal League]] into the Mesarthrim Federation.  The Kriegsmarine blockaded [[Mesar]] and Stadt named [[Bradley von Graith]] as his Chief of Staff.  The entire [[Auellal Armada]] joined the Mesarthrim Federation's Kriegsmarine.

On August 15th, the Centusi States' Kriegsmarine launched its last major attack.  The coalition fleet took severe losses, including von Graith's [[Iorrach Claigionn|''Iorrach Claigionn'']].  The Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine did not secure the Mesarthrim colonies until [[2335]], when it finally scoured the [[Pelagrim Sector]] of all remnants of the United Centusi States.

In 2335 Arnold Stadt resigned his commission and took his role as ''Hohe Gouverneur'' of the Mesarthrim Federation.  [[Oswald Voss]] replaced Arnold Stadt and began an aggressive program of disbanding and selling off unused Mesarthrim warships.  Voss completely reworked the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine, replacing the numerous fleet of small vessels with a smaller core of larger and more powerful warships.

== Fleets of the Line/Peacekeeping Fleets ==

The Mesarthrim Federation only maintains a single Fleet of the Line, whose duties are to both project Mesarthrim influence as well as defend the sovereignty of the Mesarthrim and her Allies.  There are three Peacekeeping fleets which are kept on station at one of three anchorages.  These fleets duties are to protect any Mesarthrim planet or installation that may fall under attack by hostile forces, as well as to actively combat piracy of any form in Mesarthrim Space.

The Fleet of the Line is known as the [[Arbiter of the Mesarthrim|Schiedsrichter der Mesarthrim]], ''or Arbiter of the Mesarthrim'' (usually referred to as simply the Arbiter Fleet).  The fleet consists of 45 vessels, and is under the command of Konteradmiral Sandra Tlass.  The flagship is the [[Charon Class Battleship|MFS Münzensammler]].

The Peackeeping Fleets each contain fifteen vessels and are;

The [[First Peacekeepers|Erste Friedenswächter]], commanded by Flottillenadmiral George Heindmann von Stadt.  It is anchored at Raumstraff Island, and its flagship is the MFS Stormforge.

The [[Second Peacekeepers|Zweite Friedenswächter]], commanded by Flottillenadmiral Susan Gilsun von Urner.  It is anchored at Krier Depot, and its flagship is the MFS Stonebridge.

The [[Third Peacekeepers|Dritte Friedenswächter]], commanded by Flottillenadmiral Kevan Higel von Meister.  It is anchored at Comur Ein, and its flagship is the MFS Gefallen König.

== Planetside Installations ==

The Kriegsmarine maintains several planetside installations.  The largest compound in in [[Mesar#Willim|Brünshelm]] where the Kriegsmarine Headquarters is, and the newly built [[Stadt Academy]].  There is also a Kriegsmarine facility in [[Stonebridge]] to work directly with the government.  Extensive command and control facilities also exist on [[Comur]] managing the massive shipyards and production facilities.

== Space Stations ==
The Kriegsmarine maintains fifteen different orbital stations, and several thousand smaller (or unmanned) early warning and supply stations scattered across Mesarthrim Space.

The Stations maintained by the Kriegsmarine are;
* Hoffmann Repair Yards - the largest, most extensive repair yards in Mesarthrim Space.  Hoffmann is made up of interconnecting Repair Yards, cobbled together over the years.  Located in orbit around [[Mesar]].  Hoffmann Yards was extensively damaged during the [[Tempest War]].  One notable repair yard is [[Bernard Howell|Bernard Howell's]] old repair yards, still in use.
* Comur Zukunft-Arbeiten - Former [[Anderung]] shipyards and fabrication facilities.  Appropriately named the Comur Future Works - these facilities are laden with [[Anderung]] technology and are as much a science project as an active construction yard.  Most of the facilities systems and sub-systems can be understood and used by Mesarthrim engineers, however, there are still entire portions of the station that are not.
* Ankerplatz ''(Anchorage)'' - A deep space weapon storage facility, including any [[Omega Bomb|Omega]] weaponry.  This facilities location was compromised by the [[Auellal]] during the [[Tempest War]].  Officially the [[Prefecture]] have requested it closed down and the munitions detonated at a safe location, however Ankerplatz still remains an active Kriegsmarine facility.
* Raumstraff Island - Historical anchorage for the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine.  The original anchorage was constructed in [[2202]] to serve the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|resistance's]] tiny kriegsmarine.  Raumstraff was heavily damaged in the [[Tempest War]] when it crippled the Prefecture [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]] [[HMS Visage|FDS Visage]], and the vessel crashed into the facility.  Only tradition kept the Mesarthrim Federation from scrapping Raumstraff.
* Krier Depot - Orbiting Tavou, Krier is an old Prefecture [[Haven Class Station]] that was in orbit and was seized by the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]].  It now serves as an anchorage for the [[Second Peacekeepers|Zweite Friedenswächter]].
* Comur Ein - Newly built supply harbour for supporting the [[Third Peacekeepers|Dritte Friedenswächter]].  The base serves as a supply point for all Mesarthrim vessels in the area.

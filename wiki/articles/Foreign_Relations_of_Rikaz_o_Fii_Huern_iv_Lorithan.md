== International Organizations ==

=== The Alliance of Planets ===

[[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] is a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], and joined in [[2291]]. The [[Lorithos]] have long been considered the "conscience" of the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]], and has stood for limitations on dangerous technologies, and has always voted for peaceful sollutions to diplomatic problems.

=== Council of Independent Nations ===

Officially, [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] follows the trade embargo against the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. It was passed against the protests of [[Dirken Huern]], but the [[Lorithos]] faithfully abide by the rules of [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]]. Unofficially, through the [[Iterin Corahl Deseraht]], [[Dirken Huern]] has dealings with the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]], though no one has been able to prove it.

== Individual Governments ==

=== Taviridis Somarchada ===

The [[Lorithos]] currently have little direct dealings with the [[Ixoarchada]], as their worlds are too far from [[Lorithos]] space for their [[hyperspace]] technology to reach. The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] did assist the [[Lorithos]] in embedding the [[Ansible]] system in [[subspace]], but due to the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] trade embargo, no [[Ixoarchada]] merchants have visited [[Lorithan]] or the colonies since then.

=== Auellal League ===

The [[Lorithos]] are officially neutral in the [[Tempest War]], and thus do not deal with the [[Auellal League|Auellal]].

=== Colonial Commonwealth ===

The [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] is covered by the trade embargo, and the [[Lorithos]] do not have any official dealings with them. While they do not trade together, the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] and the [[Lorithos]] are on good terms, as [[Dirken Huern]]'s outspoken protests against the embargo are well known.

=== The Onyx Empire ===

The [[Onyx Empire]] is now defunct, but engaged several operations against the [[Lorithos]] when it was still a power in the galaxy.

=== Ikronin Jurekön ===

The [[Lorithos]] have fewer dealings with the [[Ikronin]] that they do with the Meroniri and the [[Terrans]], due to their poor economy making their worlds a poor market for the [[Ikronin]]. Their skill in engineering and metalurgy is of interest to the [[Ikronin]], so some trade exists.

=== Kabila Kimburu ===

[[Kabila Kimburu]] is one of the biggest customers of [[Lorithos]] food exports from [[Ipsof]]. The seafood of [[Ipsoff]] is considered a delicacy most everywhere, but to the [[Ngrligru]] it is addictive. Due to the addictive effects of the foodstuffs, trade is regulated by the [[Alliance of Planets]].

=== Kalidasa Planetary Authority ===

[[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasa]]'s bid for independence was strongly supported by the [[Lorithos]] [[Iterin Corahl Deseraht]], and they worked with [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasan]] resistance cells and [[Mesarthrim]] commandos to drive out [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] occupation forces. Since then, the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] have not acknowledged the [[Lorithos]] support, and ignored the [[Lorithos]] prior to the trade embargo. Since the embargo, the [[Lorithos]] and [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] have not had any contact, official or otherwise.

=== Meroniri Terinasi ===

The [[Lorithos]] practically worship the [[Meroniri]] for their help in restoring the [[Lorithos]] as a people and as a society. This influence is unprecedented in [[Lorithos]] history, as the [[Prekohr]]'s disagreement with the [[Meroniri]] has resulted in a drop in his own popularity rather than in the popularity of the [[Meroniri]]. The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] owns several missions and installatios on [[Lorithan]] itself, but has little to do with the colonies.

=== Kabila iku Gol'nda ===

The [[Lorithos]] have had no contact with the [[Ngrligru]] living in the [[Sagittarius Arm]].

=== Oronus Worker's Coalition ===

[[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] was hit hardest by the trade embargo and the intial meddling by the [[Meroniri]], and have held a grudge against most of the [[Alliance of Planets]] as a result. Despite the [[Prekohr]]'s protests against the embargo, the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] are not willing to reopen diplomatic ties with the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] itself, and only deals with the [[Prefecture]] and the [[Ikronin]] as individual governments.

=== Reigess Suverände ===

The [[Lorithos]] and the [[Reigens]] are relatively close neighbors, and have good relations, despite the [[Lorithos]] abiding by the trade embargo. The [[Lorithos]] and [[Reigens]] have discretely signed mutual defense treaties, and the crown prince of the [[Reigens]], [[Jiliur Raegos]], has been known to visit [[Lorithan]] at times.

=== The Remnant ===

The [[Lorithos]] have had no contact with the [[Remnant]].

=== The Federated Districts of the Prefecture ===

The [[Prefecture]] often contends with the [[Lorithos]] in [[Alliance of Planets]] meetings, but has little to do with them otherwise. Their official stance is neutral.

=== Sirius Families ===

The [[Iterin Corahl Deseraht]]'s secret dealings with the [[Council of Independent Nations]] is usually through the [[Berlusconi Family|Berlusconis]] of [[Sirius Cluster|Sirius]], but the relationship is strictly under the table.

=== Sirunaki Reshkeren ===

The [[Lorithos]] have had no contact with the [[Sirunaki]].

=== Sol System Authority ===

The [[Lorithos]] used to receive significant material support from [[Earth]] in the days of the [[United Earth Alliance]], but since the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], they have not been able to repay [[Earth]] in kind. They have sent their scientists and doctors who specialize in the care of war victims, and have been a great help in terms of knowledge and manpower. The [[Sol System Authority]] and the [[Lorithos]] are allies, despite the fact that the Sol System Authority joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]] after the embargo was put in place.

=== United Centusi States ===

The [[Lorithos]] did not condone the [[Prefecture]]'s war against the [[United Centusi States]], but does not support them in their war either. Their official stance is neutral.

=== Volucris Swarms ===

The [[Lorithos]] have tried to understand the [[Volucris Swarm]] as a people since they first appeared, but every attempt at contacting them has resulted in disaster. The [[Lorithos]] hope to eventually make peaceful contact with the [[Volucris]] and firmly establish that they are intelligent beings with rights.

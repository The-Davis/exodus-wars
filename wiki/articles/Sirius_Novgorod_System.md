The '''Sirius Novograd System''' is a star system with six planets and their moons. 

In order of their distances from Sirius Novogrod, the planets are Novogrod I, Novogrod II, Novogrod III, [[Kirtszi]], Novogrod V, and Novogrod VI.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Sirius Novogrod is the Sirius Novogrod System's star. Sirius Novogrod is an F3V orange dwarf, slightly larger than Sol.

=== Kirtszi ===

[[Kirtszi]] (1.3AU) is the main colony world of the system. It has one moon, [[Salkutsk]]

== Discovery and Exploration ==

[[Kirtszi]] was colonized in [[2030]], largely by [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungarians]] and [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviets]].

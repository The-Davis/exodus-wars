The '''Saeger Industrial Group''' is a heavy weapons manufacturer, originally based on [[Ah Kin]], which later expanded to [[Albanus]] to continue selling to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

The Saeger Group was mostly responsible for heavy weapons manufacturing.

The Karak light hovertank is an effective close-range skirmisher and combatant.  In modern combat, it is almost always teamed up with the [[Zieldar Light Hovertank]].  Its close-range arsenal protects the longer-range sniper in battle.

Despite its aggressive role, the Karak was originally built for a much more merciful mission.

== Development ==

After the [[Burning Wars]], the Tredan Engineering Guild approached [[Zak Huern]] with two military contract projects. While the armies of the heirs were broken and scattered, there were still billions of refugees and bandits in the wastelands.  The Tredan guild had a vision for an armed courier to spread the word of the new [[Prekohr]], [[Dirken Huern]], and to bring supplies to the weary survivors of the Wars. Zak Huern approved the project, and gave what little resources he could towards meeting that goal.

The Karaks were sent out into the wastelands as fast as they could be built.  Hundreds were sent to scout out safe shelters, locate survivors, and lead them to what few havens still existed.

== Modern Use ==

The Karak first saw use as a combatant in the [[Volucris War]].  It was designated an escort for the stand-off weapons used by the Zieldar hovertank.  The symbiotic combat team works with perfection.  Karaks are close-range scrappers.  Their arsenal is inaccurate at long-range, but up close they can pound even heavy enemies to scrap.

== Capabilities ==

The Karak carries eight [[Bukrah]] cannons.  Two medium cannons on a turret give the Karak a complete firing arc, while four turreted light blasters aid in attacking enemies in multiple directions.  Two fixed light blasters give the Karak its front-arc punch.

Karaks are fast.  They were originally built as couriers and armed scouts, but now they exactly match the speed of the Zieldar, so they can defend them in their long-range attacks.  Karaks have a superb turning radius.  The clean, no-radiation power of the Karak and other Lorithos vehicles comes from the Hydrogen Engine, which generates power from a slow burn of hydrogen gas--converted from super-compressed hydrogen metal.  Refueling a Karak is rarely needed, and can be quickly completed at any fuel storage facility.

== Deployment ==

Karaks are deployed en masse to every Lorithos [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]] base, both on [[Lorithan]] and on the colonies.  Two Karaks are always teamed with a Zieldar.  The relationship is practically unbreakable--both units are severely limited in effectiveness if their partner is destroyed.  Karaks can also operate alone as scouts and raiders, utilizing their speed an maneuverability to the fullest extent.  Karaks are also still used extensively as couriers when modern communications fail.

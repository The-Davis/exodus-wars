More Dead in Texas - 3/13/2324 - by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefecture medics have been scouring the land around Houston for any information on the nature of the plague. So far, Mobile Medical Corps scientists have been unable to identify the method the virus or bacteria uses to move from one victim to the next.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The plague is not limited to humans. Emaciated animals--skin and bones only--have been found everywhere. The current death count in the Houston is forty-six.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Terran recovery effort director, Professor Hlow, has recommended a quarantine of everyone who has been to Terra in the last three months. He is currently working with the best medics in Rio de Janeiro to find a cure.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hlow: "If not for some differences, I'd swear this was the Apöt virus. It kills a lot less quickly, and the old cure isn't working.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The origin of this plague is probably from an Imperial bio-weapons stockpile, or a common disease mutated by the radioactive fallout. We'll stop it, regardless of the cause."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Help is on the way. Because of the plague's uncanny resemblance to the Apöt virus, the only expert on the virus, Dr. Steven Edge.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dr. Edge has been in the protective custody of the United Archadian Republics. With the recent break-down of the United Mesarthrim Clans government, Steven Edge has been on the run from some rather unsavory rogues from the various factions from the broken Mesarthrim Clans.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Edge is scheduled to arrive in four days, courtesy of an Alexander Class warship from the First Joint Defense Fleet.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In other news, Uber-General Eric Francis of the beleaguered and broken Mesarthrim government has an important announcement, which will be aired live tomorrow.

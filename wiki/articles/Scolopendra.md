===Overview===

The Scolopendra is a very primitive type of soldier, yet hideously effective against soldiers armored in anything short of full power armor.  It is usually called the "Cutter" by human soldiers, and for a good reason.  It's legs are super-reinforced with chitinous armor, and are sharpened to wicked edges.  Along the legs are microscopic barbs. When a Cutter hits a "soft" soldier, the leg pierces the soldier's skin, then pulls out large gibbets of flesh as the Cutter moves to its next victim.  Death is slow and painful.

[[Image:Scolopendra.jpg]]

The Cutter is highly effective against unarmored infantry, but little use against heavily-armored units.  It's claws and leg blades are a lot more effective than those of the Nymph, and its boosted metabolism and adrenal systems allows it  to attack more quickly.

The strength of the Cutter is numbers, like the Nymph, and enhanced armor.  They're a lot tougher than Nymphs, and frequently survive battles.

The Cutter is fast, skirting about the battlefield at 60KPH.  It's carapace is highly laser and ammo-resistant, but its adrenal systems shut down under the extreme application of heat and electricity.

Cutters are very common on a battlefield, as their metamorphosis from nymph only lasts a day.  It is easy to kill for a Mech or even a soldier with a suit of light battle armor.  Cutters are usually deployed to kill civilians.

'''Jorge Santos'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Conclave]]

''Jorge Santos'' was a member of the [[Royal Imperial Army]], and later served the [[Conclave]]. He was a member of the strike team that captured [[Chris Dayton]]. After the surrender of the [[Conclave]], he was sentenced to a life sentence on a [[Meroniri]] prison world.

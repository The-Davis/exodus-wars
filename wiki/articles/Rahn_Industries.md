'''Rahn Industries''' was founded in [[2300]] by [[Justin Rahn]]. The company mostly catered to small independent space industries, such as the now bankrupt Armortech Unlimited. Rahn Industries built small-scale transports and bulk haulers, and made excellent profits in the freighter market until 2322. When the winds of war began to blow, Rahn Industries prepared to face it with strength and ingenuity.

During the [[Second Exodus War]], Rahn Industries stayed out of the war machine market in order to avoid forced "contracts" with the [[Empire]]. When the war "ended" with the transition to the [[Imperial Civil War]], [[Justin Rahn]] decided to have his best naval designers work on combat vessels.

The first of these ventures resulted in the Triumph flagship, built for the Prefecture. Building of the Triumph was a decisive gamble--if the Empire won, the company would have been crushed--but the fears of investors were unfounded. The Prefecture was eventually victorious and now took control where the Empire left off.

As the dust settled and the fires were put out, Rahn Industries supplied the new Prefecture government with military and civilian essentials. The company won a number of exclusive and high-profit contracts from the Prefecture military. The contracts enabled Rahn Industries to expand its business to encompass a number of other high-profit markets in other civilizations.

From the huge merchant markets of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] to the devastated homeworld of [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]], Rahn Industries' future is bright and with [[Justin Rahn]] at the helm, the company is sure to do well.

== Divisions ==

Rahn Industries operates some of the most modern and extensive facilities for design, manufacturing, and even as living quarters for the workers employed by the company. They offer extensive designs to anyone who wants to buy, with the exception of anyone actively engaged in hostile action against the Prefecture, where Rahn Industries is based

As with any big company, Rahn Industries has extensive and complex construction facilities throughout the Prefecture's territory. Most of the profits of the company go into expanding their already extensive operation--you have to spend money to make money. Every building from the mammoth headquarters space station to the vast factories of New Albany is always bustling with activity.

The first home of the Rahn Industries facilities was on Earth, when the now-defunct United Earth Alliance ruled the planet and the colonies. The company owned some small orbital shipyards and an office complex headquarters on the surface. Rahn Industries was able to build its bulk haulers and conduct its business in relative safety.

After fourteen years of conducting business in the Sol star system, the company moved its entire operation to deep space to lighten its tax burden. Ironically, the United Earth Alliance was wiped out and Terra was invaded by the Empire a mere year later. Thoughts that perhaps Justin Rahn had prior warning of the Imperial invasion were debunked through a thorough investigation by the Alliance Space Police.

After nine years in deep space, riding out the war, Justin Rahn decided to plan for another move. After careful consideration, the CEO announced that he would be moving his headquarters and entire operation to the Ueda District and the planet New Albany. This new home has provided the company with all the growth potential and raw materials that it needs to continue work well into the future.

The company's current headquarters is a massive space station that houses the business minds of Rahn Industries. It is the ultimate show of wealth and power of the company.

The Rahn Industries headquarters station is one of the most sophisticated and complex stations ever built. It started off as something of a pipe dream, but the success of Rahn Industries allowed them to update and remodel the station several times during construction, including the latest massive upgrade.

The station itself houses a crew of approximately five thousand and a security force of six hundred. The living quarters are relatively spacious and comfortable. Most of the space on the station is offices, sleeping quarters, cafeterias, and recreation rooms--including ten swimming pools.

The four main arms of the station house the design studios of the four branches that Rahn Industries specializes in--space, air, ground, and VTOL systems. The rest of the station arm space is taken up by maintenance, communication, and storage areas.

The top three floors of the central tower are devoted to Justin Rahn as personal living quarters and entertainment space. The rest of the tower is devoted to administrative and executive offices and suites, allowing the top brass of the company to deal with the commerce that is essential to the company's survival.

The original Rahn Industries construction yards are nothing like their modern facilities. The originals were barely better than metal straps welded together to house the small transports the company built early in its history. Today, the Rahn Industries construction yards can hold mighty cruisers.

The orbital construction yard was first conceived of after Rahn Industries began it's move from Earth to [[Albanus]]. Justin Rahn wanted docks with the capacity for anything the company wanted to build, even additional construction yards.

After the first construction yard was built, the other five followed quickly, and were finished in two years. The last was opened for business in September of 2324. Today, the night skies of Albanus are bright with the huge artificial satellites.

Each construction yard has eight docks, allowing for work to progress on multiple ships at once. The largest ship that the company currently offers--the Avenger Class Heavy Cruiser--only takes up half of one dock. Larger flagships and similar unique ships must be built in sections in different docks.

At the heart of the construction yard is what most workers call "The Tower," which serves as the brain of the station. The tower houses a docking apparatus, where large capital ships can re-supply and give its crews some shore leave. The Tower is where construction yard workers sleep, eat, and live during their two-month on, one-month off shifts.

Ground-based production facilities are relatively new to Rahn Industries. At its inception, the company only built space equipment, so a ground factory wasn't needed for several years. After the move to New Albany, the company was offered a number of profitable contracts by the Prefecture. Justin Rahn decided to expand the scope of the business in order to accept them. Once the decision was made, construction began on the surface of New Albany. The new factory complexes were built in the already busy industrial heart of New Albany, and the planet's economy grew 30% with the new jobs from the factories.

The factory complexes can produce everything--tanks, hover craft, and VTOLS. The surface factories employ over one hundred thousand people. On average, each factory complex employs seventeen hundred people, including all support, security, and executive personnel. The working conditions and industrial safety standards are the best in the galaxy--matched only by the best [[Ikronin]] corporations. Accidents are almost unheard of, and no Rahn Industries employee has ever died on the job.

=== Warship Development Center ===

Situated over the Prefecture's world of Albanus, the Warship Development Center functions as Rahn Industries think tank and prototype center for the largest contracts the company receives. Even though the primary designing goes on in one of the arms of Rahn Industries headquarters station, situated over the economic powerhouse of New Albany, the refining and actual prototype models are built here.

Essentially nothing more than a construction yard dock, the Warship Development Center appears to be a quite utilitarian place, but this is far from the truth. With some of the best food in the corporate world and with more than one cafeteria, Rahn Industries makes sure to keep its workers and designers full and happy. Also, when not working the eight hour shifts, employs are treated to some of the best onboard accommodations next to the main headquarters station.

This atmosphere helps to ensure that minds are sharp as tacks and have the nutrition needed to help facilitate the dreaming and designing of some of the best warships in the galaxy. Protected by the RISF and the Prefecture military, the Warship Development Center is one of the best defended places in the Prefecture and in Rahn Industries' sphere of influence.

=== Security ===

With extensive manufacturing and storage facilities operating in deep space, the Rahn Industries Security Force operates as defenders of those facilities. While considered a valuable military contractor, the Prefecture does not allow the RISF to operate anything but security agents on the ground within Prefecture territories. The RISF's heavy weapons are exclusively for deep space.

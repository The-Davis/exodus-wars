*Designation:  Interceptor
*Powerplant: aaH21 Fusion Reactor
*Armor:  Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields:  Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament:
**4 Large Neutron Beam Cannons
**4 Medium Ordinance Hardpoints

===Overview===

The Yak-163 SuperCobra is the Asanova territory's answer to the need for a heavy fighter.  Old, simple, and with less than stunning looks, the Yak-163 is designed with the typical Asanovan inclination towards heavy firepower and armor over speed and agility.

[[Image:Yak163SuperCobra1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The SuperCobra sacrifices as much power as it can in pursuit of its devasting firepower.  Four Large Neutron Beam Cannons, the same armament as can be found on a Prefecture Stuka, give the Yak-163 enough firepower to knock other fighters out of the sky, do damage to bombers and gunships, and destroy most targets on the ground.  A military-grade armament of this power also gives a single SuperCobra enough firepower to handle most smuggling transports and pirate craft that make the mistake of drifting into Asanova space--without permission, that is.

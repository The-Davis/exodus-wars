The [[Terran]] conflict known as the '''Greek Civil War''' was a brief conflict in the aftermath of [[World War II]]. The Helenist movement sought to restore the monarchy, but the [[Communists]] sought to establish a Socialist government. The [[Communists]] were ultimately victorious, and the [[Greece (Terra)|Socialist Republic of Greece]] endured until the [[Greece (Terra)|Greeks]] signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

The UEAS ''Stalingrad'' was a [[Kirov Class Dreadnaught]] that served in the [[First Exodus War]]. It was commanded by the General-Commander of the [[United Earth Alliance]], Pavel Egorov. After his son's death with the destruction of the [[UEAS Hood|UEAS ''Hood'']] in [[2078]], Pavel led the ''Stalingrad'' in an attack against [[Torimur]]. He launched a neutron bomb attack on the surface, killing thousands.

The [[United Earth Alliance]] was the first united government of [[Earth]]. It expanded the influence of the [[Terran]] [[:Category:Races|race]] out into the stars, colonizing dozens of worlds and uniting many worlds under its banner.

== Foundation ==

The [[World War III|third world war]] of Earth was devastating on many of the superpowers of the era. The clashing ideals of Communism and western Capitalism were finally reconciled after nearly a century of cultural warfare and the non-shooting competition known as the [[Cold War]].

A [[United Earth Alliance Charter|charter]] for a new world government was written. Each signature would effectively dissolve the government of a [[:Category:Terran Nations|nation]] and cede its authority to the new [[Government of the United Earth Alliance]].

In the year [[2011]], the largest superpowers of Earth signed the charter, marking the birth of the United Earth Alliance.

== Expansion ==

From [[2027]] until the early [[2050]]s, the United Earth Alliance sponsored many government colony projects on worlds like [[Ah Kin]], [[Kalmar]] and [[Holt]]; and authorized independent colonial projects like [[Torimur]], [[Bethija]] and the [[Sirius Cluster]]. Earth saw nearly 5% of its population depart for new worlds over the course of thirty years.

== Colonial Imperialism ==

For many years, the independent systems and the United Earth Alliance got along--more or less. Certain radical elements of the Assembly wanted to control the colonies, and the colonies did not want to be controlled. [[Jerome Black]]'s Torimur colony was a particular thorn for the Alliance, refusing to recognize any Alliance authority outside of Earth itself, and regularly sponsoring smuggling missions past Alliance customs even onto worlds that were officially members of the Alliance.

The world of Bethija became the first flash-point in the conflict that eventually resulted in the [[First Exodus War]]. In [[2063]], the parliament of Bethija was dissolved, and many members of the former parliament petitioned the United Earth Alliance to annex the world and bring stability.

Many people on Bethija objected to the annexation, resulting in a conflict that spanned several months. The world was eventually pacified, despite the aid of Jerome Black, who died in the conflict.

== First Exodus War ==

As the United Earth Alliance tightened its control over worlds in the [[Sol Sector]], two worlds proved to be a consistent source of resistance and political embarassment. [[Torimur]] and [[Kirtszi]] flatly refused to submit to Alliance laws and regulations.

Several members of the Alliance Assembly attempted to pass legislation to annex both worlds through military force. [[Juliska Blasius]], the Tanár of the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]], and her brother [[Andor Blasius]] assassinated three key Assembly members, hoping to end the legislation. Instead, it galvanized the more radical elements of the Assembly into railroading the annexation legislation into law. The Alliance invaded the [[Sirius Novgorod System]], starting the [[First Exodus War]].

The war went well for the Alliance. They won every early engagement, until Torimur forces broke their forces in the [[Sirius Novgorod System]]. In retaliation, a furious United Earth Alliance commander attacked [[Torimur]] with neutron bombs, slaughtering thousands.

After the unwarranted use of weapons of mass destruction against Torimur, many of the independent protectorates threatened to join the seperatist forces. The United Earth Alliance promptly withdrew its forces and offered to meet with [[Lucius Black]] and [[Juliska Blasius]] in [[Volyn]], the capital city of [[Bethija]]. Blasius was in hiding on Kirtszi, but Black agreed to meet. The [[Treaty of Volyn]] was drawn up and signed.

Under the terms, the parties responsible for the destruction of Torimur were brought up on charges of war crimes and were sentenced to life in prison. Many radical elements of the United Earth Alliance government were disgraced by the scandal, and lost their positions. In exchange, the people of Torimur agreed to find a new home in the [[De Mairan Sector]], allowing the United Earth Alliance to save face.

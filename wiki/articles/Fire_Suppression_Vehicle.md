*Power Plant: Type-325XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 75 KPH
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver

===Overview===

Even in the days of reinforced concrete buildings and fire suppression systems, there occasionally arise a day when firemen and their fire trucks are needed. The Suppressor offers the most reliable and effective mobile fire suppression system ever developed for civilian use.

[[Image:FireSuppressionVehicle1.jpg]]

Rahn Industries learned a number of different lessons from the original designs of wheeled systems and decided to opt for the anti-grav version.

===Capabilities===

One of the biggest gripes that was heard throughout the professional and volunteer departments was the lack of nozzles to combat the fires. Therefore, Rahn Industries R&D added a second nozzle and reconfigured where they were placed, making it so that two firemen can fight two separate fires at the same time on either side of the truck.

The ability to carry crew was also another major hang-up with the original wheeled versions. However, that has been taken care of and the four person crew can sit comfortably in the roomy cab with all of their equipment. The Suppressor also sports larger storage space to allow for the transportation of other life saving equipment, from repelling lines to plasma cutters.

[[Image:FireSuppressionVehicle2.jpg]]

One of the other biggest improvements over the other designs was the expansion of the tank, who's original capacity was about three hundred gallons of foam. Now, the tank of able to hold about four hundred and fifty gallons and has improved pumping mechanisms and the ability to accept a number of different fire suppressants.

===Deployment===

The Suppressor fire engine has been out about the same amount of time as its cousin, the Savior rescue vehicle. Most of the departments that bought the Savior system have also bought a number of Suppressor systems. This has lead to the protection of millions of people and homes by some of the best equipment on the market today.

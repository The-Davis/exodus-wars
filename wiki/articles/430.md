== Events of 430AD ==

Years of debate end when the [[Parcna Cathairn]] declares the [[Arcada Forenara]].  Henceforward, all future foundings of colonies on [[Grana Prisa]] (prime worlds) will constitute their own sovereign [[Iridi]], not extensions of preexisting, off-world [[Iridi]].  However, any and all orbital stations, deep-space colonies, and installations on [[Grana Provina]] (frontier worlds) do constitute sovereign territory of their founding [[Iridi]].  

All [[428]]-foundings are declared independent, sovereign [[Iridi]].

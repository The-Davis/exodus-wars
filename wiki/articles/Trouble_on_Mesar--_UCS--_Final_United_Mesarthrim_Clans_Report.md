UCS: Final United Mesarthrim Clans Report - 3/15/2324 - by Andrew Sears

     As its first order of business, one of the contenders for succession of the Centus government--the United Centusi States--released all of their information on the last actions of the Mesarthrim High Command, and the Mesarthrim civil war.
     The United Mesarthrim Clans left behind a large debt: over one billion Meroniri credits to Terra, and six-hundred million Meroniri credits to other various Alliance of Planets Member States. The war that ended with the dissolution of the United Mesarthrim Clans also left behind many, many Mesarthrim corpses.
     William Camp: "Paying off the United Mesarthrim Clans' debts will be impossible for quite some time. Even after our economy recovers, we still have to deal with our rival, the Allied Auellal League. The League is keeping the breach between Mesarthrim open--we cannot end our war until they disarm and submit. This war hasn't ended. It's just paused."
     The Allied Auellal League quickly responded, with this statement from their leader, Steven Cue.
     Cue: "The League will be glad to pick up the debts of our former government. We accept the burden of responsibility--we started the war, but we have the right to rise up against an oppressor.
     "If the paying of the debts can keep the peace of the Mesarthrim alive, then any amount of money is a low price to pay. However, the League demands its right to autonomy. We will not re-assimilate into the United Centusi States."

     Though the Mesarthrim civil war was short, it was very high in loss of life. B-Net has the final figures available, from both the UCS and the AAL. Over a billion--1,034,781,811, to be exact--have died. This is more than 40% of the Mesarthrim population. Some non-Mesarthrim from the failed Alliance of Planets peacekeeper force were also killed, and they were numbered among the final death count.

The HMS ''Atlas'' was the lead ship in the [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] [[Atlas Class Carrier|class of the same name]]. It was also the first [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and was therefore given the hull designation CV-1.

The ship was commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2302]]. Its air arm squadrons were initially equipped with [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4 Ravens]], but eventually equipped with the [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|F-9 Valkyrie]], which was far superior in carrier operations.

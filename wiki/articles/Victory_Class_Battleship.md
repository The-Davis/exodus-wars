As the aftershocks of the [[Second Exodus War]] slowly died away, the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] found itself in an unusual position for a young government.  With the [[Onyx Empire]] defeated, and the [[Sanburn Plan]] keeping the [[United Earth Alliance]] from re-acquiring its territories, there was a significant power vacuum, and the Prefecture had the resources and positioning to fill the void.

Part of the plan called for a rebuilt military.  Many ships from the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] were still intact, but were battle-worn and beginning to show their age.  The new [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] wanted a symbol of strength that would show the galaxy that they had the means to safeguard their worlds.

While the [[Avenger Class Cruiser]] was an effective ubiquitous presence in interstellar space, the Victory Class Battleship was conceived as a rare, but potent show of force.  The design team was encouraged to “think big”, and the ship was meant to be posted at every major port of call in Prefecture territory.


== Design ==

[[Haven Shipyards]] was contracted for the design, but with [[Rahn Industries]] successfully lobbying for so many defense contracts, two construction contracts were awarded to the industrial giant.

A total of ten battleships were ordered, with half to be delivered by [[2324]] and the rest to be delivered by [[2325]].  Construction of each took just over a year, even with several [[Haven Class Shipyards]] working in conjunction on an individual ship.

== Battle History ==

[[Image:Victory Class Battleship Side View.jpg|200px]]

The first ship in the class, the [[FDS Victory|FDS ''Victory'']], was delivered to the Prefecture Galactic Navy, and tested in combat against the [[Volucris]] during their incursion into the [[Mimosa System]].  It performed admirably, but the class’s real trial by fire did not come until the [[Tempest War]].

The Victory Class performed rather well against [[Mesarthrim]] vessels, which tended to be smaller and often had shorter range on their main weapons.  The Victory’s armaments were sufficient to break through Mesarthrim defensive technologies, but the class suffered maneuverability issues and one ship, the [[FDS Prestige|FDS ''Prestige'']], was lost with half its crew when a trio of Mesarthrim destroyers managed to get in close and bring its defenses down in a matter of seconds.

Most Victory Class Battleships are used as long range weapons platforms, or against similarly armed ships with similar maneuverability profiles.  In fleet actions, wise commanders deploy screens of cruisers, destroyers, frigates, and fighters to shield the Victory battleships long enough to do their job

[[Image:Victory Class Battleship Top View.jpg|200px]]

Most importantly, the Victory Class Battleship carries a massive command and control center, and has all of the hardware needed for an [[Engineered Intelligence]].  The Prefecture hopes to cultivate a volunteer program to give its entire fleet these amazing creatures, though for now [[Prism]] is able to run several fleets’ worth of ships at once by slaving additional hardware to her own systems.

== Future Plans ==

The Victory Class Battleship is considered a success, and has an appreciable kill ratio for ships of its size.  The Prefecture’s Senate and Curiate have approved the budget to replace all the ships lost in the [[Tempest War]], and budgeted for two additional ships in the class to be completed in [[2340]].

The Second Exodus War was a devastating interstellar conflict, ranging across dozens of worlds and involving almost every nation in the [[Orion Arm]]. It lasted from [[2315]] to [[2323]].

Many worlds were silently slaughtered. The [[Omegas]] of the [[Onyx Empire]] killed millions of people over the years.

Others were devastated by direct conflict. [[Earth]] was considered the final battlefield of the war, and many areas of the planet were completely destroyed, some even sinking beneath the ocean.

== Precursor ==

While armed conflict did not actually begin until [[2315]], the groundwork for the Second Exodus War began in [[2300]], with the formation of the [[Onyx Empire]]. The infiltration and isolation of the [[Alpha Centauri System]] combined with a subtle takeover of the local government gave the new regime a stable base of operations and a valuable source of manpower.

Over the next 15 years, [[Lucius Black]] consolidated his military, cultivated his popularity among the civilian population, and began a multi-pronged campaign of propaganda and assassination of any dissident voices.

== 2315 ==

The true war began in 2315, with the invasion of the [[Sol System]]. After only three months, major combat was at an end, though the Onyx Empire fought against partisan resistance forces for the better part of the year.

The Empire's war machine seemed unstoppable. They marched through the [[Lyrae System]] in less than a month, and had secured the [[Theta Algedi System]] by August. The [[Proxima Procyon System]] capitulated to Imperial rule without violence.

By the end of the year, the Empire had besieged the [[Titan System]], which had nearly impenetrable defenses and an iron-willed commander in charge of strategy: [[Scott Sanburn]].

== 2316 ==

The siege of the Titan System went on in [[2316]] with no end in sight. Both the Empire and the [[United Earth Alliance]] gained and lost ground, nearly fighting to a stalemate.

Later in the year, Scott Sanburn managed to bring together a coalition against the Empire, with forces from the [[Lorithos]] and the [[Ikronin]] bolstering his own in an attempt to re-take Earth. The planned counterstrike would not take place until the following year.

== 2317 ==

At the beginning of [[2317]], the coalition's counterstrike was launched, and failed. Their losses were so great that all support for the United Earth Alliance dried up.

The Empire began expanding its reach into independent worlds in the [[Sol Sector]], starting with the [[Lupei System]].

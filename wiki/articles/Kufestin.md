'''Kufestin''' is the fourth planet in the [[Omicron Carinae System]].

== History ==

[[Terran]] colonists on [[Adelson]] expanded their hold in the [[Omicron Carinae System]] to Kufestin in [[2165]]. It enjoyed the same independent protectorate status as [[Adelson]] until it was conquered in [[2319]] by the [[Onyx Empire]]. It was liberated in [[2323]], and joined the [[Prefecture]] in open elections the same year.

After the serious [[Volucris]] attacks on Tavou in [[2324]], the capital offices of the [[Tavou District]] were moved to Kufestin. Kufestin suffered no [[Volucris]] attacks throughout the [[Volucris Incursion]], and became a safe haven for the District legislature and governor's residence.

Kufestin is a relatively cold world for humans, with a global average temperature of 285K (12C, 53F) and an unusually thick forest. The terrain is flat, and there are no large bodies of water, except at the poles, where there are large frozen glaciers.

Because its climate is similar to [[Kronheim]], it is a favorite vacation spot of the [[Ikronin]] people. The Tavou District is a coveted assignment for [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gate]] workers. Compared to [[Kronheim]], Kufestin is warm, but not hot and uncomfortable for [[Ikronin]].

Besides tourism and normal commerce to serve the Tavou District capital, Kufestin is relatively untouched by industry. It does have a healthy export of saplings bound for [[Earth]], but when Earth is eventually reforested, the market will dry up.

Kufestin is actually boring for most residents of the Tavou District. The District tends to attract people who enjoy conflict and strife, so Kufestin is a major let-down. Immigration to Kufestin is very low, but most people born there tend to stay.

After the bombardment of [[Adelson]] in [[2328]], Kufestin experienced a population surge of refugees, and eventually stabilized to its present population levels. It was targetted by the [[Mesarthrim]] for a similar fate, but elements of the [[Prefecture]]'s Third Fleet successfully repelled the attempted bombardment.

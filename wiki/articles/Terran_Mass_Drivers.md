A '''Mass Driver''' is an extremely high-velocity, long to extreme range projectile weapon, used mostly by [[Terran]] militaries. Early mass drivers were classified as Magnetic Accelerator Cannons, while later mass drivers were classified as Relativistic Mass Drivers. Both variants see continued use in most Terran militaries.

== Magnetic Accelerator Cannon ==

The '''Magnetic Accelerator Cannon''' was first developed by a sub-laboratory of the [[Royal Imperial Labs|Imperial Physics Laboratory]], under "Project: Terminator." The weapon's main quality was extreme range and accuracy. It could be fired with pinpoint accuracy at distances up to 15 million kilometers, and in high speed combat situations could be fired with high accuracy at distances up to 10 million kilometers.

=== MAC-1 "Sniper" ===

The first model of Magnetic Accelerator Cannon, the MAC-1, was completed in time for the launch of the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']]. The MAC-1 had a twin accelerator coil system that ran much of the length of the ship, a full 300 meters. Production of the MAC-1 was handled by the [[Saeger Industrial Group]], using specifications delivered by Dr. [[Erika Strout]].

=== MAC-2 "Bulldog" ===

The second model of the Magnetic Accelerator Cannon, the MAC-2, was developed for the [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]]. The MAC-2 used a single accelerator coil system that was only 80 meters long. The shorter length of the accelerator meant a loss in range, but the weapon was designed for Destroyers, which fought at closer ranges. The MAC-2 was combat rated at 4 million kilometers.

=== MAC-3 "Longshot" ===

The MAC-3, developed for the [[Vigilant Class Frigate]]s built between [[2318]] through [[2322]], was a significant upgrade to the MAC-1. It had equal physical characteristics to the MAC-1, but significantly reduced energy requirements and slightly improved fire control systems.

=== MAC-4 "Grappler" ===

The MAC-4, developed for the [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]]s built between [[2318]] through [[2322]], was a significant upgrade to the MAC-2. It had equal physical characteristics to the MAC-2, but significantly increased velocity and heat management hardware.

=== MAC-5 "Hellgun" ===

The development of the [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser]] by [[Rahn Industries]] resulted in the penultimate Magnetic Accelerator Cannon, the "Hellgun." The Hellgun sacrificed accuracy for extremely improved rate of fire. Advanced accelerator coils allowed Rahn Industries to reduce the weapon down significantly, so it could even be mounted on a turret if necessary.

== Relativistic Mass Driver ==

The '''Relativistic Mass Driver''' was introduced with the development of gravitics technology. It is a very high energy Mass Driver, capable of launching projectiles at extremely high velocities, to the point that they are measured as percentages of light speed.

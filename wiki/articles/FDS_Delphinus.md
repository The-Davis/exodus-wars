The only ship in the [[Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser|Delphinus class]] was the lead ship, the [[FDS Delphinus|FDS ''Delphinus'']].  The Curiate and Senate determined that the cost of the ship was far too high to deploy numerous ships of the same type, and chose the smaller Nautilus Class--available for a third of the cost--for deployment in greater numbers.

The FDS ''Delphinus'' saw action in the [[Tempest War]], but was lost in subspace after a failed attack on [[Mesar]].  With the help of the [[Ixoarchada]], it was recovered after the war and refurbished. It became the flagship of [[Hannah Baruk]] after she became the [[Prefect]] when [[Anthony Hadrian]] was removed from office and disappeared.

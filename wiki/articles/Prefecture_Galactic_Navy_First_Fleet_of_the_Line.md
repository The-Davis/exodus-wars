The Vanguard of the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] is [[Robert Marshall]]'s First Fleet, led by the flagship ''Jerome Black''.  Its role, besides combat, is the coordination and command of all of the other fleets.  Every ship commander outranks a captain of a ship from another fleet, so coordination in multi-fleet action always comes from the First Fleet.

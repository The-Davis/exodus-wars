September 22, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim enjoyed yet another success in a long line of operations. Today the Black Dogs and Banshee battle groups took Japan after several days of fighting.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim losses were heavy. Very few troops weren't wounded in the fighting. Even Uber General Tischler was injured when an Imperial Arrow IV artillery missile blew up a few meters away.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim armies are preparing to continue their series of invasions, in order to buy more time for the TDF reinforcements to arrive.

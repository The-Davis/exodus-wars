The '''Volucris''' are a swarm of life-forms that move between the stars and are capable of faster-than-light travel.  They are a living species in that they breed, age, eat, and die, but the Volucris are closer to machines than any other species in the galaxy. Their bodies are hard, run by internal furnaces that rival a nuclear reactor. They can naturally manipulate microwave frequencies and [[Wormholes]] as easily as any advanced machine built by humans. Many have weapons built into their bodies just as deadly as any plasma cannon or rifle.

Despite their stark differences from humanity and their strange ways, there is a logic and a motive behind their actions. They are not wholly violent and aggressive, but their motivations are often difficult to decipher.

== Psycho-Xenopological Profile of the Volucris ==

=== Overview ===

Unlike [[Humans]] and [[Ngrligru]], the Volucris seem to be a collective of vastly different sub-species working together under a single mind and motivation.  It is easier to think of the Volucris species as a single entity, and individual bodies as cells of that entity. Their appearance is something like hive insects, but the similarities are superficial.

=== Social Structure ===

The Volucris social structure is a mystery.  Humans have managed limited communication with captured workers and with a single [[Eviscerator]], but have gleaned little useful information.  The workers seem to fear and be in awe of something called [[The Otherness]], which is the presumed leader of the Volucris--if it is an entity at all.  There seems to be some sort of caste system in place to organize workers and soldiers, but the exact structure and its rigidity are mere speculation.

=== Language ===

The Volucris have been observed speaking some sort of verbal language, but have also been seen making complex gestures in repeated patterns which appear to be a form of non-verbal communication.  It is believed that advanced pheromone control has given them other forms of non-verbal communication, but all experiments with captured Volucris have only yielded empirical evidence--no solid results.

=== Architecture ===

The Volucris have several distinct styles.  On the ground, they live in tunnels, or in huge hive structures that are built on top of permanent tunnel entrances.  Tunnels are lined with waxy secretions, which appear to be a natural concrete.

There are no recognizable patterns or decorations to Volucris tunnels in the visible spectrum, but sensors indicate unusual and sometimes beautiful variations in the ultraviolet spectrum.  It is believed that this is indicative of a civilization more advanced than mere ravenous beasts.

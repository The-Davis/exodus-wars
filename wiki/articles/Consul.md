'''Consul''' is the highest elected executive political office in the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

Under the constitution, the Consul is elected for a four-year term. The Consul makes treaties, presides over the Senate, and advises the Prefect in military matters.

==Powers and Responsibilities==
===Executive Duties===
The Consul is invested with the civilian executive power of the state and heads the civilian government of the [[Prefecture]]. Any law requires their approval without a supermajority of the legislative branch.

===Civil Duties===
For the most part, Consular power is limited to the civil sphere. The [[Prefect]] has the bulk of the powers and duties in the military sphere. Most internal machinery of the [[Prefecture]] is under the Consul's supervision and jurisdiction, particularly civilian law enforcement. The Consul has the right of summoning and arrest, which all sworn civilian law enforcement share in.

The Consul also presides over the Senate, and determines the agenda for the day, but cannot vote in any Senate votes except to break a tie.

===Military Duties===
While most military duties are the role of the [[Prefect]], the Consul does negotiate and write treaties. The [[Prefect]] must approve them, but the [[Prefect]] cannot request or write them.

===Abuse Prevention===
Abuse of Consular power was prevented with the impeachment system. If a Consul abuses his or her power, the Senate and Curiate have the power to impeach the Consul and remove them from office. Additionally, the [[Prefect]] and Legislation together can check any order of the Consul.

Additionally, the Consul's term in office is only four years, and they are limited to a single term.

The military of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] is possibly the single most advanced military in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy. It is capable of destroying entire star systems, and jumping its fleets from one side of the galaxy to the other in minutes. Only the forces of the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] and the [[Remnant]] can stand toe to toe with them with any hope of survival. Noteably, the [[Ngrligru]] can face the Meroniri if they have superior numbers on their side.

== History of the Terinasi Military ==

The [[Meroniri]] Yatisu is a powerful armed force, divided into four "Aseta", or "schools" of specialized training. Each is fully trained for air, ground, space and water operations, but has distinct disciplines and tactics they specialize in.  The oldest school is [[Aseta Joreno]], and was founded by [[Meronir Tronit]] out of the remains of the [[Anthronoris Empire]]’s military.  Despite overwhelming odds, the [[Aseta Joreno|Joreno]] were able to defend the original nine worlds against the last days of the Sundering, though they sacrificed many [[Anthronoris]] worlds in order to do so.

Over the centuries, the remaining schools were founded. The most recent foundation was the [[Aseta Salfore]], founded only a few hundred years ago. The [[Aseta Salfore|Salfore]] is the only Aseta to get involved in non-[[Meroniri]] affairs, and is known to the galaxy at large as the Meroniri Expeditionary Force. 

The [[Meroniri]] military is the most technologically advanced in the known galaxy. Even the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Remnant]] are only close seconds in terms of the power the [[Meroniri]] can bring to bear against their foes.  The Aseta emphasize reliance on technology, and have many automated systems which reduce the need for vast armies

== Organization ==

The head of the armed forces is the Asethenar, the Minister of Defense.  The Asethenar is an administrative Commander-in-Chief, letting the heads of the Aseta deal with the actual strategy for defending the Terinasi or waging war against their enemies.  The Sheta Unari is rather unusual, putting the military at the disposal of a number of different authorities. The individual Aseta can actually go to war on their own behalf without the authorization of a higher governing body.

== International Stance ==

[[Meroniri]] military doctrine can basically be summed up as a “friendly threat.” The [[Meroniri]] rarely begin fights, but frequently send [[Aseta Salfore]] out as a show of power. Recently, they were used to enforce a blockade against several [[Council of Independent Nations]] worlds. Only the threat of [[Ixoarchada]] retaliation prevented open warfare, and then only barely. This drastic shift from their centuries of peaceful noninvolvement has stemmed mostly from the election of [[Naimen Ladosen]] as [[Lothenar]].

== Recent Operations ==

The [[Meroniri]] recently suffered major losses in the [[Teeth of Orion]] against a small [[Ngrligru]] incursion. Several listening stations and their defenses were wiped out. The [[Meroniri]] responded by hunting down and destroying the invaders, then using their Tesseract system to jump into an [[Ngrligru]] planetary system and irradiated an entire continent. It is unknown how the [[Ngrligru]] will respond. 

[[Aseta Salfore]] forces have engaged ships from the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] in combat, but used surgical strikes to disable the ships without killing anyone.  When faced with the might of the [[Ixoarchada]] military, the [[Aseta Salfore|Salfore]] blockades backed down. Rumors say that [[Naimen Ladosen]] had ordered [[Aseta Salfore]] to engage all opposition, and only the countermanding orders of [[Rosh Arufin]] prevented an all-out shooting war.

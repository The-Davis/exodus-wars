February 12, [[2323]]

by Seth Dickinson

=====Mount McKinley - Alaska - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The two fighters spiral upwards through the crystalline air of Alaska, their drives leaving long wakes of seared air, like heat-haze over a summer desert. The swept-winged, graceful form of the Prefecture Raven doggedly pursues the sleek-delta Imperial Vampire through twisting evasive curves and wild Immelmans.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The other aerospace pilots in the area watch in fascination, distracted from their own duels--for these are the champions of "the war to end all wars", the knights who have led their enemies to their graves, the fearsome phantoms who have inspired a thousand tales of glory.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Vampire whirls and spins with dizzying speed and coordination, her engine trailing brilliance over the dark mountain below. The Raven claws after it with dogged persistence, her engines blazing hellish fury. Both fighters struggle against gravity, seeking to escape the web like embrace of Earth, soaring towards the sky, jockeying, side-slipping, plummeting, and climbing in a blazing dance of death at speeds beyond reckoning.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The sleek aircraft race towards the blackness of space, the Raven still clinging to the Vampire’s trail. Both fighters trace wispy mazes of contrails that are twisted, shredded, and rewoven by the Raven’s cannon-fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The two fighters are like meteors, surging upwards in their orbit, weaving around each other in a timeless dance – a truly epic battle, a private war that stretches back into the mists of time and onwards evermore into the future. The beauty and elegance of the dogfight is breathtaking – as is the savage screaming of the engines and the weapons, hot fire like Zeus’ thunderbolts, cutting the very winds apart.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;They are the greatest pilots humanity has ever produced, artists who weave together the strands of war and death into a seamless tapestry of beauty and victory, but one of them is doomed to fall.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As the two dueling titans reach the apex of their flight, a lone ray of sunlight – like an arrow of Apollo – illuminates the darting fighters and sears their image into memory forever.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;---the Vampire, whirling like a dervish as she traces a long arc across the sky, her wings spread wide and weaving contrails like burning swords, her flaps exploding outwards like the edges of a ceremonial fan, thrusters blazing like fireworks---<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;---the Raven, balanced on pillars of fire, stubbornly blazing cannon-fire after its dancing rival, moving faster than a bullet and with the stubborn determination of a bulldog, lancing towards the heavens like Icarus reborn---<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;---and then the sunbeam passes, the legend ends, and the victor soars triumphant into an eternity of tales.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kyra Olson watches with her heart in her throat as Jack Ivonrud’s Raven and Emil Torsc’s Vampire vanish through a thick bank of clouds, the Prefecture ace closely tailing the Imperial, Torsc. The flames of their engines fade fast, a twisting mass of phoenix fire that scatters slowly on the winds, until there is nothing left of their passage but the echoes of their flight.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She knows she will never see anything like this again. Ivonrud and Torsc are both legends- one a paladin, a shining exemplar that everyone in the Prefecture fighter corps aspires to be, the other a dreaded shade who lurks in every pilot’s nightmares.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kyra thought she was going to die when she saw Torsc’s Vampire closing on her minutes ago, but then Ivonrud’s Raven came soaring out of the heavens like the Archangel Michael and saved her from the Reaper’s grasp.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And now Ivonrud, master pilot, scourge of the Imperial forces and beacon of hope among the Prefecture air wings is dueling Torsc to the death in the cloud-studded skies of Terra, far above the hell-scorched mountain fortress of the Emperor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She can’t do anything to help Ivonrud – she will only get in his way. All she can do is wait…and pray. <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kyra watches the clouds above with the greatest feeling of trepidation she’s ever experienced. Her guts seize up and her clenched nails dig through her gloves and into the skin of her hand, drawing blood. She stares into the roiling clouds above as her fighter arcs gently upwards. All she wants is an answer. Has Ivonrud – gentle, admired, and heroic – finally proven his supremacy? Or has the legendary rivalry between Ivonrud and Torsc come to a far darker conclusion?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A fighter emerges from the clouds, whirling through a victory loop, trailing smoke and moving fast, like a bat out of hell. Kyra squints at it, trying to get a visual identification, knowing that this is the winner of the epic duel. She gets her answer as Emil Torsc ends her life in one blazing moment of flame and loss.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Jack Ivonrud is dead!”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The call spreads across Imperial communications like wildfire, touching off a new blaze of hope in the hearts of everyone who hears it. The Imperial fighters that duel over Mount McKinley boil with renewed energy as the outnumbered Imperials redouble their efforts.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On the slopes of the mountain below, Imperial infantry and armor crews let loose a ragged cheer and fight on with increased vigor, blunting the allied advance. Mesarthrim troops fall in battle, refusing to retreat as a cyclone of Imperial fury hammers into their flank. The Prefecture falls back under the pressure, and the ground battle dissolves into havoc.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emil Torsc cannot restrain a wide grin as he sees his squadrons charge back into the fray, their drives sparking far below, like a swarm of fireflies on the Arctic ice. He can see renewed hope in their darting mass, haloed by the geysers of gunfire and detonations from the ground battle below.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He spots a Prefecture fighter – another Raven – climbing towards him from below. After the duel with Ivonrud, this foe seems pathetically slow– a waddling bovine inviting his attentions. He squeezes his triggers, and that simple motion shatters the enemy fighter into a blazing mass of ruined metal that falls towards the tundra below.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“For the Emperor,” he mutters, but not without a subtle hint of irony. He repeats his words on the all-ships frequency, this time loud and sincere. The voices of his fellow pilots take up the call, like the chant of priests in some dark ritual, dedicating their latest sacrifice.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“For the Emperor! Jack Ivonrud is dead!”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Jack Ivonrud is falling. He plummets towards the atmosphere, his every nerve afire with pain and rage. Adrenalin courses through his blood; his pupils are thrown wide-open like the gaping windows of an abandoned house, drinking in every last scrap of light like a greedy beggar.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The shattered ruins of his Raven surround him, a jagged mass of metal and flame. The main body of the fighter is gone, but his escape pod detached successfully as cannon fire stitched towards his cockpit.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Is this how my life ends''? he wonders, his thoughts fogged by the searing pain jagging into his brain. ''Not in fire – but cold''?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Air, bereft of any trace of heat, screams through the riddled sieve that was his cockpit. He cannot tell if he is screaming as well--the sound is too loud--but he wants to scream, the pain is so intense and biting. His helmet is locked to his ejection seat--it has been since bail-out, so he cannot look down--but at his waistline there is a cold deeper even than space, and a cleaving agony like nothing he’s ever felt or even imagined. Below the pain there is...nothing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The cold is everywhere around him, save in his--that is the fire of pain. He throws himself into the fire of his mind's eye, and in the forge of agony, burns away the shame and anguish of knowing he has lost--the certainty that, like the damned angels of the primeval rebellion, he will never fly again.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Death comes.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emil Torsc looks upon the sleek shape of the Emperor’s escape ship and experiences a pang of fear. “My lord,” he says, forcing himself to sound confident and proud. "We may not have triumphed here today, but I killed Jack Ivonrud in your name."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Congratulations, Emil,” Lucius Black sneers. "So you’ve ended a petty rivalry, while we have lost a world. Were our need for pilots not so desperate, I would teach you a lesson."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Stop your ravings, old man'', Torsc thinks. He remains silent and nudges his Vampire into closer formation with the shuttle. After a long silence, Black speaks again, this time his voice thrumming with some of the charisma and power of old.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We have lost Terra before. This time, in our departure, we left it in ruins. The Alliance of Planets and their rebel allies will become complacent soon enough, while we gather our strength in the shadows and prepare to strike again."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Emperor's voice sends a chill down Torsc’s spine. There is an edge of cold steel in it – but also an undertone of bubbling madness. This second defeat has cost the Emperor much of his sanity.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“The Exodus War may be finished, Torsc,” Lucius Black snaps. “But by no means am I.”
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Torsc’s proximity sensors squeal. As he and the Emperor’s ship pass the orbit of Luna, fleeing fallen Terra and the Empire’s loss, a Prefecture fighter arcs out to meet them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"For Ivonrud!" An enraged pilot screams.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Torsc remains silent, but he banks his Vampire away from the Emperor’s ship and steels himself to kill in Lucius Black’s name once again.<br>

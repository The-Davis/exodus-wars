'''Rikaz Lorithos Iskerso''' ''Adelisk'' is the first ship of the Intelligence and Communication Department, designed and ordered by Mait Onega. Mait Onega's ICD are the eyes and ears of the Rikaz Lorithos, and The Adelisk and other ships of its class are the senses of the ICD. Without it, the brains Intelligence community wouldn't know what to expect or how to handle threats.

Adelisk Surveillance Ships are unarmed. They rely on their small size, speed, and excellent armor and shields for survival. The bulk of the Hydrogen Engine's output is devoted to powering the sensor and detection systems, as well as artificial stealth and masking systems.

The Adelisk is equipped with a number of advanced sensors systems, from a Nullifier Core, which can hide the small ship from even the most advanced sensors, to detection systems that can find even ECM-equipped fighters, small stealth vehicles, and other such hard-to-detect starships.

[[Image:Adelisk Class Corvette Side View.jpg|150px]]

For wider range detection, the Adelisk carries a single large sensor array, designed to detect the subtle maneuvers of fighters and small transport craft.

Adelisk Surveillance Ships are widely used by the Lorithos ICD in military and civilian roles, although the Adelisk has been replaced by the Gaivik in many military roles. In military, they provide intelligence information for MGTF and ASTF units. In civilian roles, the surveillance ship carries Urokon maintenance crews, as well as intelligence operatives.

[[Image:Adelisk Class Corvette Top View.jpg|150px]]

Because of its importance in the success of the military and the strength of the galactic economy, the Adelisk Surveillance Ships has enjoyed an already two-decade long career, and is certain to enjoy a several more decades of service.

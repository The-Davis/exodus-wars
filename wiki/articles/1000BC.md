== Events of 1000BC ==

In the earliest records of their recorded history, the [[Ikronin]] people were united under the rule of a single king. The monarchy lasted for many generations, but stifled technological development under a strict system of bureaucratic rules and regulations. It wasn't until the last king died with no heir that the [[Ikronin]] truly began to thrive as a star faring culture. Their society was reborn, embracing capitalism in its purest form.

A number of private companies soon seized control of most of the lands in [[Kronheim]], and began branching outwards into dozens of space stations and space habitats.  Rather than waste resources with warfare or other forms of competition, the companies united into the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] for cooperation and prosperity.

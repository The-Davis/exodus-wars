'''Tzaiko Composite Armor''' was invented after the [[Burning Wars]]. [[Lorithos]] armor changed little during the [[Burning Wars]], despite the advances in deadly weaponry.

It was similar to the metal ablative armor used by the [[Terran]] factions in the [[First Exodus War]]. Modern armor was developed after the [[Burning Wars]], during the initial recovery effort while negotiations with the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] and [[Meroniri Terinasi]] were still shaky.

The armor is composite, made of crystalline Tzaikate, a compound of Carbon found only in the post-War mines of [[Lorithan]]. The strength and impact absorption capabilities are thirty percent stronger than [[Terran]] Boron-Nitride Composite armor.

Tzaiko is fabricated into long strands a single molecule in width. Billions of these strands are woven into thick sheets of armor. Unlike [[Terran]] armor, a secondary anti-radiation plate is unnecessary. Tzaiko blocks most forms of radiation, and [[Lorithos]] people are immune to many types of radiation.

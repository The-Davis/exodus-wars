===Overview===

[[Image:Violacipodis1.jpg]]

The Black Claw is a latecomer to the Bugger war.  It is similar in offensive power to its smaller cousin, the Shiva, but it is significantly faster and more intelligent.

The Black Claw's wings each contain two plasma-producing glands similar to those of the Bombardier, which it can fire with deadly accuracy at human fighters.  The destructive power is very high, similar to a Terran autocannon.

Like the Shiva, the Black Claw carries two Shredder Symbiotes.  Unlike the Shiva, the Black Claw can actually produce its own drones.  It requires large quantities of food and three hours of rest to do so, but it saves the offspring of Battle Queens for Shivas.  Black Claw drones are more like Shredder Drones than the Symbiotes of the Shiva.  They can make course corrections, so they are much more like fire-and-forget missiles.

The Black Claw's plasma propulsion is blindingly fast and maneuverable.  It is nearly impossible to engage in a dogfight, as only the most maneuverable fighters can keep up.

[[Image:Violacipodis2.jpg]]

The Black Claw's carapace is very resilient, and extra resistant against radiation.  Terran Neutron Cannons are rather ineffective.  Pilots are advised to use traditional cannons or missiles.

Black Claws are the elite fighters of the air and space forces of the Bugger Swarm.

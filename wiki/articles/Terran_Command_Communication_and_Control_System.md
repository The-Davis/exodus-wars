Gen2 Command, Communication and Coordination Controller (C3) The C3 System is nearly forgotten amidst the new weapons and other technologies born of the Gen2 Project, but it is one of the most vital systems on the battlefield. Using advanced communications systems, all weapons from a mighty starship down to a lowly Navy Trooper are interconnected. Each C3 is controlled by a C3 Controller. The C3 Controller device acts as a middleman between multiple C3 systems, sending the correct targets to the correct weapons. Imagine if a C3 sending coordinates to a landing Aerospace fighter accidentally sent them to a nuclear missile launcher! When the Empire is attacking an enemy, the C3 Controller is mounted on specially designed mobile command unit. When we are defending, or are on a prolonged siege/campaign, it is installed on a permanent base.

Gen2 Command, Communication and Coordination System (C3) The C3 System is nearly forgotten amidst the new weapons and other technologies born of the Gen2 Project, but it is one of the most vital systems on the battlefield. Using advanced communications systems, all weapons from a mighty starship down to a lowly Navy Trooper are interconnected. Each C3 is controlled by a C3 Controller. C3 was initially developed to allow support units such as Catapult Mechs and Earthquake tanks to bombard enemy targets without ever seeing the target. Using the C3 system, a fast scout Mech or hover vehicle could feed its sensor info to a long-range bombardment Mech or tank, allowing the slower heavier combatant to get a clear shot without worrying about the target's defenders.

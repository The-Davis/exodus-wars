The F-4 Raven was the mainstay fighter of the [[Onyx Empire]], and later of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. It had a long and distinguished career, earning it a fearsome reputation among the pilots of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

Ten main variants were developed and flew with the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] and [[Federated Districts Air Force]]. Even after the final version, the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4I (Fusion Reactor Installed)|F-4I]], was retired from front-line service, it still sees active use by the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Federated Districts Air National Guard|Air National Guard]], and with the [[Sirius Families]].

===History===

The Raven had a long development history. It was first designed to be an air and space fighter by [[Royal Imperial Labs]].

The "Raven Project" was first launched in [[2300]] as part of [[Lucius Black]]'s master plan to build up the [[Onyx Empire]]'s air power. The [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] was commissioned, and many pilots earned their reputations as the "Raven's Wings" in the project's test pilot Corps. [[Marcus Rathbone]] performed so admirably as a pilot that he narrowly edged out [[Emil Torsc]] for command of the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]].

The Raven Project had three failures, the [[XF-1 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-1]], [[XF-2 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-2]], and [[XF-3 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-3]] before finally reaching success with the [[XF-4 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-4]]. This final airframe was chosen to be the "Raven", and the fighter prototype--the YF-4 Raven--made its debut on January 7, [[2302]].

There were four YF-4 prototypes built, and all were armed with different weapon profiles to be tested for effectiveness in a series of trials.

===Development===

The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] was developing hundreds of different programs simultaneously. The [[Royal Imperial Army]] and [[Royal Imperial Marine Corps]] shared the same nomenclature in weapons design; the [[:Category:Imperial Military Specification|Imperial Military Specification]]. The [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] and [[Royal Imperial Navy]] adopted their own standardized nomenclature for weaponry, and gave their [[:Category:Starships|starships]] and [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] simple letter and number designations.

The weapons programs designed for the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] and [[Royal Imperial Navy]] required designs that could be operated in many different hostile environments, and survive harsh and sudden temperature and pressure ranges.

The [[Royal Imperial Army]]'s [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|M-22]] had an ideal damage profile and firing system for use as a primary weapon for the YF-4, and it was tested on the first prototype. In the atmosphere of [[Ah Kin]], the weapon performed admirably. Test pilot [[Matthias Carter]] tested against multiple target profiles, from strafing runs to dogfights against drone targets.

[[Image:F04Raven2.jpg|left|150px]]
Problems came up when [[Matthias Carter]]'s YF-4 was tested in a dogfight against a drone remotely piloted by [[Emil Torsc]]. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]'s drone only had "painting" lasers which would allow observers to record hits scored against [[Matthias Carter|Carter]]'s YF-4, but [[Matthias Carter|Carter]] had live ammunition. [[Matthias Carter|Carter]] flew best in space, and soon had [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]'s less-capable drone outmatched in orbit. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] opted to lose [[Matthias Carter|Carter]] in [[Ah Kin]]'s atmosphere. During reentry, the ammunition in the YF-4's [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|M-22]] suddenly exploded. The caseless ammunition could not take the sudden changes in heat and pressure while being fired during atmospheric reentry. The fact that [[Matthias Carter|Carter]] corrected the diving corkscrew and survived the ditched landing which completely destroyed his YF-4 is a testament to his skill as a pilot. The [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|M-22]] was abandoned as a potential main weapon.

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was developing an ammunition-based weapon design for their [[Close-In Weapon System]] which did not rely on explosive propellants. The [[GC-01 Light Gauss Cannon - "Piper"|GC-1]] nearly matched the [[M-22 Light Autocannon - "Stinger"|M-22]] in damage and rate of fire. Unfortunately, the weapon suffered from reliability issues and frequently had to be repaired or replaced in the field.

As an alternative, [[Royal Imperial Labs]] offered the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]], affectionately dubbed the "Toaster." It did less damage and had a slower rate of fire, but was much more reliable. The mounting and fire control systems between the two could easily be swapped, and the ammo bins for the [[GC-01 Light Gauss Cannon - "Piper"|GC-1]] were designed to be replaced with additional capacitors for the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]]. The YF-4 program was completed, and the first F-4 Ravens were ready for duty by the end of [[2302]].

===Design===

The original F-4 Raven was built undercover. The [[Onyx Empire]] existed in secrecy: [[Lucius Black]] still feared discovery by the [[United Earth Alliance]], and despite the [[Omegas]]' stranglehold on transportation and communications on and off of [[Ah Kin]], the first F-4s were painted with [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] markings. To keep up with the charade, the F-4s seen by [[Ah Kin]]'s civilian population were unarmed, and were touted as a new surveillance and topography-charting atmospheric flyer.

Three hundred F-4s were built between [[2302]] and [[2303]], and were built with two seats and used to train flight instructors for the [[Onyx Empire]]'s academies.

====General Characteristics====

Every version of the Raven was characterized by a sleek, streamlined body, wide swept wings, cannards, and a single tail-fin. In the trainer and early two-seater models, pilots complained of the cramped cockpit arrangements. Later models which only had a single pilot and compact digital controls were praised for their comfort and ease of use.

====Performance====

The Raven was always just behind the technology "grade curve" when compared to its opponents' best [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]]. The [[United Earth Alliance]] designs outclassed the earlier Raven variants, and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] designs outclassed the later Raven variants. It was only towards the end of the Raven's career that it finally began to meet and surpass competing models, but it was destined to be replaced by the newer [[F-16 Superiority Fighter - "Piranha"|F-16]].

Until the introduction of gravitics-based propulsion, the Raven suffered in space combat maneuverability. While its thrusters could pull off nearly any maneuver, pilots often passed out from the G-forces. The [[Onyx Empire]] was never able to duplicate inertial dampening technology used by the [[United Earth Alliance]].

====Armament====

Every Raven designed and equipped for combat has always had at least two fixed cannon weapons. Earlier variants relied on laser-based beam cannons, while later variants were equipped with increasingly-advanced varieties of gauss cannon designs.

====Avionics====

Early incarnations of the Raven had difficulty with their control systems. The original F-4 variant's avionics were designed to detect if the Raven was in space or in atmosphere, and switch control responses based on the results. This system was prone to failure due mostly to the inexperience of the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] staff in dealing with radiation shielding of computer components.

Later variants vastly improved on the avionics system, developing an intuitive interface which merged all of the controls into a single package integrated into the pilot's Heads-Up Display. The throttle was installed as a lever on the left-hand side of the cockpit, with rudder control pedals on the floor at the pilot's feet. The control-stick had an integrated weapons-selector switch and trigger, as well as sensor control buttons. The pilot rarely needed anything than these three main controls, but an optional control panel was installed on the right side of the cockpit for more advanced functions.

===Operational History===

The first Ravens to see combat were F-4C variants used in the invasion of [[Terra|Earth]] in the first battle of the [[Second Exodus War]]. Before the year was out, the F-4C variant was replaced by the F-4D variant, which became the [[Onyx Empire]]'s workhouse for most of the [[Second Exodus War]]. The [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] had almost exclusive use of the Raven. The T-4 variant was used as a trainer by all branches of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Military of the Onyx Empire|military]], but only the [[Royal Imperial Air Force|Air Force]] used it in combat.

After the [[Second Exodus War]], the Raven's schematics were given away by the [[Prefecture]] as spoils of war to anyone who asked. The [[Prefecture]] kept the design for their own use, and immediately produced a new variant that surpassed the best available at the time. Despite the fact that the shared design was slightly inferior, the [[Sirius Families]] adopted the design as their main [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]], and continued developing the line for their own use.

===Operators===

The Raven's use by the [[Onyx Empire]] was highlighted by [[Emil Torsc]]'s early victories against the [[United Earth Alliance]], particularly his victory against [[Helen Cashaw]] while piloting an [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4D (Computer-Aided Avionics Package)|F-4D]] variant.

After [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] switched to the [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|F-9 Valkyrie]] and later the [[F-12 Superiority Fighter - "Vampire"|F-12 Vampire]], the Raven was kept famous by [[Jack Ivonrud]] and [[Marcus Rathbone]], who never willingly flew any other [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]].

Since its retirement from front-line combat in the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Federated Districts Air Force]], the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4I (Fusion Reactor Installed)|F-4I]] variant has continued to see combat in the air battles of the [[Tempest War]] against the [[United Centusi States]]' Luftwaffe. The [[Sirius Families]] have also continued to develop their own variant, which has so significantly improved on the older variants' performance that it is affectionately known as the "Super Raven."

===Variants===

The F-4 was developed extensively over its lengthy career, resulting in a total of ten main variants. Typically, a main variant was developed using a combination of the most popular field modifications, and the addition of any major technologies developed in the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] aerospace laboratories.

The YF-4 was the testing variant flown through a series of trials by the "Raven's Wings" test pilots. It was equipped with a "Series-1 Fission Reactor" developed by [[Royal Imperial Labs]], and armed with a variety of different weapons for testing in various mission profiles.

The YF-4 had a relatively low maximum airspeed, which was later corrected in the production version of the fighter. Additionally, the airframe occasionally suffered from turbulence and overheating problems during reentry. With the exception of [[Matthias Carter]]'s explosive crash-landing, the YF-4 never crashed due to heat. [[Matthias Carter|Carter]]'s crash was blamed on ammunition problems rather than the airframe itself.

Only four YF-4 prototypes were built and tested before the production model F-4 was completed and launched. They were designated with serial numbers F04-Y01, F04-Y02, F04-Y03, and F04-Y04. The prototype destroyed in [[Matthias Carter]]'s crash was designated F04-Y03. The other three prototypes were scrapped for parts at the end of the testing program.

The full production F-4 was built in-house by [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' aerospace laboratory. This early version was not long-lived due to the advances in weapons technology developed by the XF-5 project and the subsequent development of the F-4A variant.

All original F-4s were built with two seats, and were used as trainer aircraft by the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] to train flight instructors for [[Lucius Black]]'s planned military projects.

Because the existence of the [[Onyx Empire]] and its military build-up were still a closely-guarded secret, the original F-4 was unarmed and was painted in the colors of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. All completed F-4 airframes had mounting points and fire control systems for the standard weaponry tested on the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#YF-4 (Pre-Production Armed Prototype)|YF-4]], but did not have the weapons mounted by default to preserve the illusion that the Raven was merely a surveillance [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] with a purely benign purpose.

Only three hundred original F-4s were built between [[2302]] and early [[2303]] when the F-4A variant was ready. The F-4s built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0000001 to F04-0000300. By the time the F-4C was developed, maintenance crews were instructed to simply scrap and replace any existing F-4s. No examples of the original F-4 are known to exist.

After completing the F-4, [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' aerospace laboratory was assigned to developing the [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] airframe. The [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] was designed to incorporate modernized modular weapon systems into the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] arsenal.

The [[Onyx Empire]] still had the ability to manufacture weaponry the [[Torimur]] rebels had used during the [[First Exodus War]], and had some examples of modern weaponry used by the [[United Earth Alliance]]. The original air-breathing missiles from their [[First Exodus War]] arsenal and early hydrazine rockets simply did not meet the standards of merged air and space operations. [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] commanders did not want to deal with the logistics nightmare of changing weapons loads based on two operational areas of both air and space.

To deal with this problem, the Air/Space Intercept Missile (ASIM) series of weapons was developed as a primary standoff weapon. The [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] was developed to mount these, along with the fire control systems and targetting systems needed to operate them. While multiple variants of the ASIM series were developed, the [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] and later the F-4A variant were only designed to take advantage of the [[ASIM-01 Short Range Guided Missile - "Javelin"|ASIM-1 "Javelin"]].

The [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] itself proved inferior to the F-4, so the F-4A variant was developed to take advantage of the [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]]'s new technologies.

After a significant amount of time had passed without discovery by the [[United Earth Alliance]], and the civilian population of [[Ah Kin]]'s docile acceptance of quasi-dictatorship rule, [[Lucius Black]] no longer feared discovery quite as much as he had before. The F-4A variant was armed, using a pair of [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]] lasers for ease of maintenance and simplicity of use. Additionally, the latest Ramjet and Ion Engine models--the Series-2 designs--developed by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] were incorporated into the F-4A, significantly increasing the F-4A's performance and efficiency.

Like the original F-4, the F-4A was built as a two-seater, this time for training new recruits in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s academies. Now-famous pilots like [[Jack Ivonrud]] and [[Jan Brooks]], earned their wings operating F-4A trainers.

There were just over a thousand units of the F-4A variant built by [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' aerospace laboratory before the variant was cancelled in favor of the F-4B. The F-4As built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0000301 to F04-0001321. The F-4A was the last variant built in-house by [[Royal Imperial Labs]]. All subsequent Raven variants were constructed by military contractors.

By the year [[2305]], the [[Onyx Empire]]'s presence on [[Ah Kin]] and its control of the government was an "open secret", after a fashion. While the government and military of the [[United Earth Alliance]] remained oblivious to the growing threat in the [[Alpha Centauri System]], the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura family]] had discovered the [[Onyx Empire]] and cut a favorable deal with them.

The [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] assistance allowed the [[Onyx Empire]] to begin openly building up a military presence, and the mass-production of the new F-4B variant was part of that process.

The [[XF-6 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-6]] was a second attempt at improving on the F-4 and fixing the flaws with the [[XF-5 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-5]] model. The airframe was developed to take advantage of new composite materials based around the Boron-Nitride compound developed by the [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' chemical laboratory. The new composite was lighter, stronger, and cheaper than the earlier carbon composites and alluminum used in earlier F-4 variants. The [[XF-6 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-6]] itself was cancelled, but the F-4B variant had an airframe built around the Boron-Nitride composite.

Rather than bog down the [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' aerospace laboratory with building the thousands of airframes needed by the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]], [[Lucius Black]] allowed [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]] the access to the designs necessary to become a military contractor.

[[Alexi-Wrought Airworks|Alexi-Wrought]] produced 2500 F-4Bs between [[2305]] and [[2307]], and incorporated several improvements in engine technology and performance. The F-4B was the first dedicated single-seat [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] design used by the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]]. The airframe developed for the F-4B remained in use for the rest of the Raven's career, and all existing versions were upgraded to later variants rather than being scrapped. The F-4Bs built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0001322 to F04-0003821.

In [[2306]], the conscript training program in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s academies began in earnest. Many young recruits were chosen to be pilots, and needed a trainer aircraft. The F-4 and F-4A still existed, but were not numerous enough for the huge numbers of recruits sent through the academy program.

The T-4 was designed with a purpose-built training control system, and could be operated from two sets of controls, both the trainer and the trainee, or taken over by remote by an instructor on the ground.

Unlike the earlier F-4 and F-4A two-seaters, which were technically true [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], the T-4 had no weaponry at all, and was intended to train recruits and junior pilots the subtleties of altering altitude, escape velocity, and atmospheric reentry in a combat [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]]. The airframe was subtly altered with safety in mind: automatic systems could detect potentially fatal maneuvers and correct them. Tailspins and similar crash situations which could not be escaped by ejecting could be salvaged. In the event of total failure, the airframe actually sported full-plane parachutes, and ejected the engine from the frame to arrest any propulsion or chance of an explosion.

The T-4s built were constructed by various sub-contractors and were designated with serial numbers unique to their companies. Many T-4s were sold to private training firms after the [[Second Exodus War]], and remain the single most popular air and space trainer in human space.

In early [[2307]], [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Propulsion Laboratory completed development on the Magneto-Plasmadynamic Thruster. The prototype was tested on the [[XF-7 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-7]], but proved too unstable for use as a main drive as intended. Test pilots could not control the airframe, and three [[XF-7 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-7]]s were lost to crashes as a result of instability. In the end, it was decided that the MP Drive was useable as an afterburner, but not as a main drive.

When [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]] began its [[2307]] production of the Raven, they began incorporating several newer sub-components. [[Tolwin Power Incorporated]] further developed [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Series-3 Fission reactor, and introduced the AC-5 compact fission reactor. [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]] also began building ramjets and ion drives in-house.

[[Alexi-Wrought Airworks|Alexi-Wrought]] produced conversion kits for all existing F-4Bs, and built another 800 F-4Cs each year until the beginning of the [[Second Exodus War]] in [[2315]]. The F-4Cs built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0003822 to F04-0010222. The [[XF-8 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-8]] promised additional changes to further increase the performance of the Raven series, but the F-4C remained the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]]'s mainstay [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] into the first years of the [[Second Exodus War]].

The [[XF-8 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-8]] was developed immediately before the invasion of [[Terra|Earth]], but the focus was on developing an effective carrier-launched fighter for the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. The [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|F-9 Valkyrie]] was developed with the [[XF-8 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-8]]'s new computer guidance system, which stabilized the problems with the Magneto-Plasmadynamic Thruster.

As the brutal conquest of the [[United Earth Alliance]] continued, the enhancements were incorporated into the F-4D variant. Many of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s pilots reported difficulty in destroying [[United Earth Alliance]] fighters, due to the resilience of their shields against the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]] laser, so the engineering teams responsible for developing the F-4D opted to replace one of the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]]s with a new, heavier Gauss Cannon design, the [[GC-02 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Shrieker"|GC-2 "Shrieker"]].

The F-4D was the true workhorse of the [[Onyx Empire]]. There were more F-4Ds built than any other variant, or of any other [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]]. The [[Onyx Empire]]'s demand for the Raven was so high that they added [[Reimao Airworks]], [[Consolidated Spaceworks]], and the [[Antonov Company]] as contractors after [[2315]]. Each company built production lines enabling them to build 1200 Ravens per year. Along with [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]]' production levels, a total of 4600 F-4Ds were constructed each year from [[2316]] to [[2322]]. The F-4Ds built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0010223 to F04-0037823.

When the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] entered the [[Second Exodus War]] in [[2322]], they brought the significant air power of their Luftwaffe into use against the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]]. The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven" #F-4D (Computer-Aided Avionics Package)|F-4D]] was seriously outclassed by the [[Me-32 Superiority Fighter - "Sturmfaust"|Me-32]] fighter fielded by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim]] Luftwaffe. In the first several engagements against the [[Onyx Empire]], the [[Me-32 Superiority Fighter - "Sturmfaust"|Me-32]] enjoyed a 4 to 1 kill ratio over the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven" #F-4D (Computer-Aided Avionics Package)|F-4D]].

Combat analysis showed that the Luftwaffe pilots' most significant advantages were in defenses and speed. The [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] had fought against enemies equipped with shield systems since day one, but the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]' level of technology was well beyond the weapons fielded by the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Luftwaffe pilots simply sneered at the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]], which was completely ineffective against their shields. Only the [[GC-02 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Shrieker"|GC-2]] and missile weapons were any use against [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim]] pilots' shields.

Even if an [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] pilot flying a [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4D (Computer-Aided Avionics Package)|F-4D]] brought its weapons to bear, the [[Me-32 Superiority Fighter - "Sturmfaust"|Me-32]] was far faster and could simply fly out of range, particularly in atmosphere where it enjoyed maximum airspeeds of Mach 16.7.

Since [[2320]], [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' propulsion laboratory had been developing a new engine for atmospheric flight. The Scramjet was extensively tested on the [[XF-10 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-10]], and finally approved for use in the new F-4E variant on March 6, [[2322]]. The F-4E enjoyed a massive boost in speed, bringing its maximum airspeed up to Mach 18, giving it a slight edge over the [[Me-32 Superiority Fighter - "Sturmfaust"|Me-32]] in atmospheric pursuits.

Additionally, the [[BC-01 Light Beam Cannon - "Toaster"|BC-1]] was removed from the F-4E and replaced with a second [[GC-02 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Shrieker"|GC-2]]. The new [[ASIM-02 Medium Range Guided Missile - "Wren"|ASIM-2]] missile also replaced the error-prone [[ASIM-01 Short Range Guided Missile - "Javelin"|ASIM-1]].

All manufacturers assigned to building the F-4 were shipped as many Scramjets as could be produced and ordered to ramp up production of the new F-4E variant. Due to shortages in engine components, production dropped from a total of 4600 per year to 4250 in [[2322]]. When the [[Imperial Civil War]] reached a fever pitch in [[2323]], production on all [[Terra|Earth]]-based factories ceased, and the liberation of [[Kalidasa]] ended all production from factories based there. Production in [[2323]] was a mere 600 [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], all of which were captured by the [[Prefecture]]. The F-4Es built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0037824 to F04-0042674.

While the [[Onyx Empire]]'s invasion of its neighbors lost its forward momentum in [[2322]], [[Royal Imperial Labs]] continued to develop and test new technologies well into the [[Imperial Civil War]]. One of these technologies was the aeroelastic control surface, introduced on the [[XF-12 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-12]], and later deployed on the [[F-12 Superiority Fighter - "Vampire"|F-12 Vampire]] superiority fighter.

By the time the technology was mature enough to be incorporated into a Raven variant, the [[Onyx Empire]] had fallen, and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] had captured nearly all laboratories organized and controlled by [[Royal Imperial Labs]].

As a gesture of goodwill and sharing in the spoils of war, the [[Prefecture]] declassified and distributed the design specifications of the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4E (Scramjet Atmospheric Propulsion)|F-4E]] variant to all [[:Category:Nations|nations]] in the [[Orion Arm]]. This "Outreach Program" was completed on May 3, [[2323]], and included multiple technologies besides the Raven.

The [[Prefecture]] began working with [[Rahn Industries]] to incorporate aeroelastic control surfaces into their new F-4F variant. The variant also added a balance of short and medium range missiles, replacing two [[ASIM-02 Medium Range Guided Missile - "Wren"|ASIM-2]]s with new [[ASIM-03 Short Range Guided Missile - "Cobra"|ASIM-3]]s, which were designed for closer-range engagements.

Production began in June of [[2323]], but only 600 F-4F variants were completed in [[Rahn Industries]]' [[Albanus]] factories before the variant was replaced by the more advanced [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4G (Shield Systems Installed)|F-4G]] variant. The F-4Fs built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0042675 to F04-0043275.

Soon after completing the first month's production run of the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4F (Aeroelastic Control Surfaces)|F-4F]] variant, the laboratories formally operated by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] revealed new developments from the [[Onyx Empire]]'s last experimental fighter design, the [[XF-13 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-13]]. The [[XF-13 (Imperial Experimental Airframe)|XF-13]] was simply an [[F-11 Heavy Fighter - "Stuka"|F-11]] airframe stripped of all weapons and equipped with a prototype shield generator. The [[Federated Districts Air Force]] soon cancelled all outstanding orders for new [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"#F-4F (Aeroelastic Control Surfaces)|F-4F]]s, and sent [[Rahn Industries]] the design requirements for the new F-4G variant.

[[Justin Rahn]]'s first order of business was to use components developed and built in-house for as many systems on the F-4G as possible as a cost-cutting measure. The components from the [[Antonov Company]], from [[Tolwin Power Incorporated]] and from [[Alexi-Wrought Airworks]] were all replaced by subsidiary companies wholly-owned by [[Rahn Industries]].

The new reactor, developed by [[Rahn Industries]]' energy sub-company [[Rankine Generators]], had to significantly boost total power production to meet the needs of the shield system. New engine components were developed and built in-house by [[Dwight Propulsion Laboratories]], for a slight overall increase in performance at the cost of higher energy needs.

Production of the F-4G began in July of [[2323]], and 1200 were built before the development of the F-4H variant in December of [[2323]]. The F-4Gs built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0043276 to F04-0044476.

In early [[2324]], the [[Prefecture]] managed to develop its first gravity control systems, finally entering the field of study known as "gravitics." This unlocked several advanced technologies for them: gravity shielding, artificial gravity, limited anti-gravity, and most importantly the gravity cavitation drive.

The [[Prefecture]]'s [[XF-14 (Prefecture Experimental Airframe)|XF-14]] airframe was their first [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] equiped with a gravity cavitation drive system. Using gravitics-based propulsion had some strong advantages. Expendable fuel was not required to propel the craft equipped with the drive. Inertial issues were also greatly reduced: every part of the craft and all of its contents were accelerated at the same rate and in the same direction at the same time.

Unfortunately, gravitics-based propulsion had its disadvantages as well. While the need for solid fuel was eliminated, the energy drain on a reactor was greatly increased. When [[Rahn Industries]] began work on the new F-4H variant, they had to use the Rankine AC-11r fission reactor, which took up all of the available space freed up by eliminating the Magneto-Plasmadynamic Thruster's fuel tanks, and still lacked sufficient power for both the gravitics drive system and the shield generators at the same time. Attempting to install a fission reactor with the necessary 50mW output to power shields and accelerate at the same time resulted in an airframe that wasn't viable for atmospheric combat.

Finally, [[Rahn Industries]] offered to deliver several new weapon systems at once, and roll all profits from the F-4H variant into funding the development of an improved compact fusion reactor. While the [[Federated Districts Air Force]] had begun field-level conversions of some F-4G variants, the new weapon systems would greatly benefit from new fire control hardware and software.

The new [[GC-05 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Screamer"|GC-5]], dubbed the "Screamer", was ready to replace the older [[GC-02 Medium Gauss Cannon - "Shrieker"|GC-2]]. The newer design reduced power consumption per burst, and increased the effective range and rate of fire of the weapon. The F-4H also incorporated the new [[ASIM-04 Long Range Guided Missile - "Hawk"|ASIM-4]] missile design.

The F-4H variant was produced from April of [[2324]] until November of [[2324]], with a mere 100 completed each month. Earlier variants were all converted in a massive upgrade program. The F-4Hs built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0044477 to F04-0045277.

In December of [[2324]], [[Rankine Generators]] completed its first prototype compact fusion reactor, and delivered it to the [[Prefecture]] for testing on the [[XF-15 (Prefecture Experimental Airframe)|XF-15]] program. The program met with instant success, and the [[F-16 Superiority Fighter - "Piranha"|F-16]] program was soon launched to take full advantage of the developments in technology over the last 22 years.

The [[Federated Districts Air Force]] wanted to replace the aging Raven, but knew they needed to have a fully-modernized [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] available until the completion of the [[F-16 Superiority Fighter - "Piranha"|F-16]] program.

Aside from the addition of the new fusion reactor, the F-4I variant only added the new [[ASIM-05 Medium Range Guided Missile - "Hound"|ASIM-5]] missile to the Raven's armament. The [[ASIM-05 Medium Range Guided Missile - "Hound"|ASIM-5]], quickly nicknamed the "Hound" became one of the most famous, reliable and deadly weapons in the [[Prefecture]]'s arsenal.

The production of new F-4Is was very low. Nearly all of the orders were simply for the new fusion reactors to convert existing F-4H variants to F-4Is. Only 250 new F-4Is were built before the launch of the [[F-16 Superiority Fighter - "Piranha"|F-16]]. The F-4Is built were designated with serial numbers from F04-0045278 to F04-0045528.

The F-4S is the variant developed and fielded by the [[Sirius Families]]. It is affectionately dubbed the "Super Raven" for its vast performance enhancements.

The "Super Raven" was built to bring the [[Sirius Cluster]]'s forces more in line with higher levels of technology emerging in the [[Sol Sector]]. It represents a good middle point between the F-4I's performance levels and those of the [[F-16 Superiority Fighter - "Piranha"|F-16]].

Besides giving the air forces of the [[Sirius Families]] a powerful and effective [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]], the Super Raven also represented an advanced saleable product which could bring much-needed revenue into the [[Sirius Cluster]] and give them some semblance of respectability as a military power.

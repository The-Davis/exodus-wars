The Rank system of the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] is a very traditional one, with Enlisted and Commissioned ranks.

== Enlisted Ranks ==

Enlisted is any rating below that of a commissioned or warrant officer. The term includes non-commissioned officers. Though it is the lowest of the ranks, it is the most important. The lower-enlisted are the core of the fighting force, while the non-commissioned officers provide direct leadership and training to the soldiers.

* Airman - An Airman wears a single chevron.
* Airman First Class - An Airman First Class wears two chevrons.
* Senior Airman - A Senior Airman wears three chevrons.
* Staff Sergeant - A Staff Sergeant wears four chevrons.
* Technical Sergeant - A Technical Sergeant wears five chevrons.
* Master Sergeant - A Master Sergeant wears five chevrons and a rocker.
* Senior Sergeant - A Senior Sergeant wears five chevrons and two rockers.
* Chief Sergeant - A Chief Sergeant wears five chevrons and three rockers.
* Chief Sergeant of the Air Force - The Chief Sergeant of the Air Force is the direct reporter and aide to the General of the Air Force. The Chief Sergeant of the Air Force wears five chevrons, three rockers, and the Imperial emblem surrounded by two stars.

== Commissioned Ranks ==

Commissioned officers are given formal orders from the Emperor, granting them their rank and privileges. This "commission" carries a heavy burden of responsibility. An officer is expected to fight, think, and lead better than any enlisted soldier.

* Second Lieutenant - A Second Lieutenant wears a brass bar.
* First Lieutenant - A First Lieutenant wears a silver bar.
*Captain - A Captain wears two silver bars.
* Major - A Major wears a brass oak leaf.
* Lieutenant Colonel - A Lieutenant Colonel wears a silver oak leaf.
* Colonel - A Colonel wears a silver wolf's head.
* Wing Commander - A Wing Commander wears a silver star.
* Major General - A Major General wears two silver stars.
* Lieutenant General - A Lieutenant General wears three silver stars.
* General - A General wears four silver stars.
* General of the Air Force - The General of the Air Force was the supreme commander of all forces in the Royal Imperial Air Force. [[Marcus Rathbone]] was the first General of the Air Force. When he joined the [[Prefecture]] in the [[Imperial Civil War]], the whole of the Royal Imperial Air Force was absorbed by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s air arm, and placed under the command of [[Emil Torsc]]. The position was never filled again.

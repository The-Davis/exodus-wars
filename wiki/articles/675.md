== Events of 675AD ==

A perfect copy of the [[Nechanos Tolesca]] is completed on [[Archada]].  It is powered up, but despite constant attempts for more than six months, no message is ever successfully sent to the [[Plasala System]].  [[Ixoarchada]] scientists go back to the drawing board and reanalyze every facet of the [[Nechanos Tolesca]].

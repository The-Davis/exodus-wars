===Racial Demographics===
The Prefecture has mostly Caucasian, African, Indian-Asian, and Asian racial populations, mostly coming from Terran American, Chinese, Indian, French and Irish population centers. American-descended Caucasians and Indians share the highest population density at 35% each, with the Chinese following at a close second with 15%.

===Immigration and Citizenship===
The Prefecture recognizes non-military persons as having two possible statuses, civilian and citizen. No candidate for immigration is allowed to become a citizen immediately, but if certain standards are met, immigrants may become civilians. An immigrant wishing to become a civilian must be able to speak Terran Standard English fluently.

Only healthy persons are welcome to enter the territories of the Prefecture and make their homes there, so all persons seeking status as a civilian must be free of diseases and be immunized against all common contagious diseases.

The Prefecture frowns on societal burdens, so everyone seeking status as a civilian must have a sponsor already living within the Prefecture, or own property at or above a value of 200,000 Peculiums. They also requires that all immigrants have no criminal record within the ally nations of the Prefecture.

Immigrants failing the language, immunization or sponsor requirement are given a one month visa of entry to attempt to complete the requirements. Immigrants who fail to complete the requirements but attempt to remain after their visas expire are tracked down and subject to execution.

Citizens have much more power than civilians, and thus have much greater responsibility. The Prefecture recognizes that the requirements for citizenship must be strict, in order to keep those who would abuse power away from it. Because those who cannot defend themselves are a danger to everyone, anyone wishing to become a citizen must own a firearm and be able to shoot accurately--accuracy being defined as 70% hits on a still target and 55% hits on a moving target.

Citizen candidates must be four-year civilians of the Prefecture, and have committed no crimes during their stay as civilians. Candidates for citizenship must either serve for two years in the military or pay a non-military citizen fee of 100,000 Peculiums.

Citizenship is not a permanent status, and it can be forcibly revoked by the judicial system. Anyone convicted of a crime requiring more than five lashes is stripped of citizenship for three years and must then reapply. For violent crimes, citizenship is a moot point, as they are executed.

Citizenship can be willingly forfeited with no penalty. If a citizen wishes to take up residence in an ally nation and become a citizen of that nation, he or she may do so, and return as a civilian at a later date if they so choose. They must, however, go through to process of becoming a citizen again.

===Religion===

The Prefecture's constitution forbids the government from mandating a religion, but religion is quite common anyway. Christianity is the most common, with 75% of religious people identifying themselves as Christian. Budhism is second. Some violent religions, Islam in particular, are strictly banned, and anyone found practicing them is subject to arrest and flogging.

===Languages===

English is the official language of the Prefecture and all residents are required to be able to speak it fluently. Government documents are only provided in English, and services to non-English speakers is strictly prohibitted by law.

hile no other language is official, many people are bi-lingual. Chinese, French, and Gaelic are the most common non-English languages.

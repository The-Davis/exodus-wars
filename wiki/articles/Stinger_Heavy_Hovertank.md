*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

The Stinger is the heaviest module for the Hydra platform, and requires extensive modifications to the Hydra platform, involving overhauls of the cockpit, hover-lift system, and rear fuselage.  The result is a heavy hovertank that combines high speeds and heavy firepower into a single package. 

[[Image:StingerHeavyHovertank1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Stinger is the most heavily armed of the Hydra craft modules.  In addition to the standard pair of Medium cannons, the Stinger also mounts two Large Neutron Beam Cannons in a turret in the rear.  In addition to the heavy cannons, it also mounts a pair of SRM launchers, giving it a very heavy ranged punch. 

Like all Hydra craft, the Stinger is equipped with a Kalidasan hover lift system, and is protected by Boron-Nitride Composite armor, a Plasma Barrier, and Magnetic Screens. 

===Deployment===

[[Image:StingerHeavyHovertank2.jpg]]

Designed as fast strike units, Stinger hovertanks operate within the Blazton Security Forces as the primary combat craft in a potential conflict.  Outside of the BSF, Stingers have been sold to a number of corporate security firms and civilian defense forces.  Orders are expected to pick up in a number of post-colonial settlements and independent systems.

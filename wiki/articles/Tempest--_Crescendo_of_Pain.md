Set some time in the first year of the Tempest War

by Andrew Sears

     Space.
     Silence.
     Foreboding and unequivocal complete silence.
     Of all the territories of space, this is the one your mother warned you to stay out of, the Mesarthrim space.
     They don't really ask questions...
     They don't really like questions...
     No, in fact, they usually skip the question and go straight to the answer -- a master alarm on your control board warning you of a missile lock.
     It seems however, fighter corps have this mentality that is... odd, at best. They don't really hate each other, at least amongst the human races. It's more of a mutual respect. If the opposing pilot can eject, let him. And you don't necessarily have to pound the hell out of an opposing fighter, just get him out of action.
     But on the dawn of this battle, there is no respect -- only cold-to-the-bone hatred.
     Auellal and Centusi fighters race at each other more vicious for blood then the Einherjar will be at the Battle of Ragnarok.
     Their inner bloodlust and hate is sealed in blood as the space between them is filled in earnest with all manners of utensils of death.
     It's music of death and destruction would fill an orchestra if only the dead lack air didn't squeeze out any sound.
     No matter, sound is unnecessary.
     No mercy.
     No remorse.
     No time to think and ponder.
     If you can hit a cockpit, all the better.
     These men and women racing towards each other could have been friends, lovers, brothers, sisters -- but it is all changed now. Changed by a war of an ideal against a lie. Both sides see the latter as the former.
     It was only seconds, only a heartbeat, the entire conflict summed up in less time then it takes to pray for forgiveness for the massacre you just completed.
     In a spiraling decay of airless space dozens of souls float away -- maimed, severed, strapped, but most of all... dead.
     I pray the leaders of these nations do not think too highly of themselves for what has happened here today.
     Their self-righteous command did not share their sons fate today, for they hope to dictate the fate of the suns.
     And most important of all, the cost?
     Complete.

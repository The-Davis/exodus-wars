===Overview===

The rebirth of the Marine Corps meant that infantry would have to be able to move around the battlefield at high speed.  The All Terrain Vehicle design was re-examined by the Corps, and chosen as the chief method for rapid transport and assault.

[[Image:M32Badger1.jpg]]

After considering the effectiveness of the Mesarthrim Dolch Light Assault Vehicle, Rahn Industries' Badger Sport Utility Vehicle was chosen for military modification.  The Badger ATV was the result. 

===Capabilities===

The Badger's design was centered around the Rifle Team, and moving that team at the fastest possible speed.  With a capacity for three Marines and their weapons, the Badger can carry any type of team, and with a top speed of 645KPH on road, it puts most vehicles to shame.

Off-road, the Badger must sacrifice speed for maneuverability, but its powerful field guns keep down the threat from infantry targets.  The Badger uses the same hybrid repulsor/antigravity cushioning technology to keep jolts and bumps from throwing its crew from the vehicle.  Its suspension system can survive a fall from 50 meters without stress, and its braking system can slow the vehicle from top speed to a full stop in under three seconds.

===Deployment===

[[Image:M32Badger2.jpg]]

While the Army considered using the Badger, they instead focused their resources on upgrading higher-capacity APCs like the Armadillo.  The Marine Corps uses the Badger in almost all engagements, and all Marines are trained to drive the Badger.  Final deployment to the Corps was completed by November of 2325.

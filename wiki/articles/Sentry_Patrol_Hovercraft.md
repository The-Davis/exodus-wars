*Speed: 220 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament:
**2 Machine Guns (1000 rounds)
**4 SRM Launchers (40 rounds)
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

In the midst of a massive reconstruction program following the Second Exodus War, the Kalidasa Planetary Authority generated a large number of jobs by designing and constructing its own defense forces, with every part of the design and construction being done in-system.  The Sentry Light Hovertank was designed to be an all-purpose patrol and scout craft.

[[Image:SentryPatrolHovercraft1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Sentry is designed as a general patrol and scout vehicle, and is thus only lightly armed.  It's primary weapons are a pair of Machine Guns mounted in a twin-turret above the tank.  These weapons are very useful against infantry and other light units, but are quite useless against any sort of heavily armored target. 

To make it a threat to vehicles and other armored targets, the Sentry has been outfitted with four Short-Range Missile launchers, just like those on the large Chariot.  They are useful against vehicles, as well as infantry. 

With a high top-end speed and excellent maneuverability, more akin to a Unitronian hovertank than a Terran type, and a light layer of Boron-Nitride as well as a Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens, the Sentry is well protected for its small size.  It relies mostly on its speed for protection, but its armor and shields can protect it from most weapons fire, at least for a short period of time.  

===Deployment===

[[Image:SentryPatrolHovercraft2.jpg]]

Sentries were designed to be light patrol and scout vehicles.  Whether as police or military units, Sentries can operate alone or in groups to quickly move from one point to another in search of targets.  Police particularly like them because they are cheaper than Chariots, while offering sufficient firepower to deal with anything that most criminal elements would field.  In the event of a hostile invasion, most heavy combat would be handled by larger Cavalier Heavy Hovertanks, and Sentries would be used for infantry support and recon.  They are well built for any sort of anti-infantry roles, especially in urban situations.

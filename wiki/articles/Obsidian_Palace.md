The '''Obsidian Palace''' is located in [[Memphis]], Tennessee, in the North American region of [[Earth]]. The palace's foundation was laid in May of [[2315]], and construction was completed in [[2316]]. Over six thousand forced laborers and four hundred skilled craftsmen were assigned to the project.

== Design ==

The main palace building covered 8,000 square meters, and was made of Imperial Obsidian, a building material made from grinding obsidian and other materials into a useable structure. Large portions of the structure were developed from pre-fabricated molds, which were then filled with Imperial Obsidian.

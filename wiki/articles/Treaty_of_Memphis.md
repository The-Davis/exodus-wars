With the end of major hostilities on [[Earth]], many believed that it was time to formally put an end to the [[Onyx Empire]] and to the war. With the planet relatively secure, peace talks began in [[Memphis]], in the former Imperial capital.

== Negotiations ==

Despite the obvious war crimes and atrocities committed by the Onyx Empire, representatives of the [[United Earth Alliance]] insisted on proper legal proceedings and parley between legitimate governments. The [[Alliance of Planets]] was asked to mediate, and [[Dirken Huern]] was appointed head of the oversight committee. Under escort from the [[Meroniri]] [[Aseta Salfore]], Huern arrived in the Sol System on April 20.

The Meroniri commander, [[Rosh Arufin]] kindly offered to transport in representatives from all concerned parties. Representatives from [[Sindh]], [[Tejat]], [[Holt]], [[Laojiu]], [[Ah Kin]], [[Bethija]], [[Kalmar]], [[Theia]], and [[Mesar]] were all brought to the negotiating table.

[[Kim Ali]] of Sindh and [[Talaris Roi]] of Tejat were appointed as representatives for the rest of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]]. [[Christopher Dayton]] represented the formalized rebellion of the [[Prefecture]]. [[Anthony Hadrian]], acting in his capacity as the [[Grand Admiral]] of the Onyx Empire, initially represented the regime, but was later forced to give up his position to [[Cecil Au-Yueng]] due to a perceived conflict of interests.

[[Andreas Tischler]] represented the [[Mesarthrim]] Clan Lords, and [[Barbara Dannins]] represented the [[United Earth Alliance]].  [[Alessandro Berlusconi]], [[Nicholai Asanova]], [[Takashi Nakamura]], and [[Blasius Gregöri János|Gregor Blasius]] were present as representativess of the [[Sirius Cluster]].  

=== Formal Surrender ===

Cecil Au-Yueng's first order of business representing the Onyx Empire was to immediately and unconditionally surrender all Imperial forces on the planet Earth and in the [[Sol System]] to the coalition, and to surrender all Imperial forces under his command elsewhere in the galaxy to the [[Prefecture]].

In turn, [[Christopher Dayton]] offered the surrender of the Prefecture's forces, but only if certain conditions were met. He told the committee "we offer peace to our neighbors, but not at the point of a gun. We must be allowed to join the community of nations as equals, not as slaves."

=== The United Earth Alliance Proposal ===

Barbara Dannins proposed a return to status quo, offering the Prefecture sovereign rights over any territories colonized by the Onyx Empire, offering independence to any systems that were independent at the start of hostilities, and requesting the return of all sovereign territory of the United Earth Alliance. Dannins explained that "the Alliance stood for peace and prosperity for nearly three hundred years. It was only ended through treachery and violence." Nicholai Asanova took particular offense to such a claim.

This proposal met with sharp retort from the resistance. Many in the resistance felt a strong sense of allegiance and obligation to Anthony Hadrian, whom they saw as their "savior" from the tyranny of the Empire. Talaris Roi argued that "the Prefecture has won our worlds by right of conquest, and won our hearts through acts of courage."  

When Andreas Tischler suggested that Mesarthrim riot troops could aid in pacifying any unwilling civilian parties, the table erupted into a shouting match. Dirken Huern quickly adjurned the meeting for the day.

=== The Sanburn Plan ===

When the talks resumed, [[Scott Sanburn]] requested the opportunity to speak on behalf of the United Earth Alliance. Sanburn knew that the Onyx Empire's occupation of Earth had always been the thorn in the side that galvanized its enemies into action, and Sanburn saw the extreme importance of the world to the [[Terran]] people. As a representative of one of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s colonies, he had the rare wisdom and foresight to realize that bringing back the old government could spark another war when the now-independent colonies refused to recognize [[Earth]]'s sovereignty over them. 

Sanburn wrote and presented a lengthy proposal to all parties gathered at the treaty talks, recommending that all Member States of the [[Alliance of Planets]] formally cede any sovereign rights to the conquered territory, and that any who were able would begin supplying personnel and material support to rebuild an independent world government for Earth. He told the representatives "our galaxy is rich in resources and living space. It is time to stop fighting over our common heritage, and start sharing it like brothers and sisters." He went on for some time, but was interrupted by applause. The Sanburn Plan was adopted for determining the fate of Earth.

Sanburn's wisdom and foresight is credited with preventing a third "Exodus War" that would have been caused by a sudden shift in power over the [[Terran]] cultures as a whole.

=== A New Constitution, A New Order ===

While the fate of Earth had been decided, many worlds were still hanging in the balance. Kim Ali and Christopher Dayton wrote up a proposal for allowing each world to make their own decision.

Dayton requested the treaty recognize the [[Hydrae System]] as the sovereign territory of the Prefecture. Most representatives did not even know the system existed, so they quickly agreed to the request.

Ali suggested that all worlds currently occupied by the Onyx Empire would be occupied by peacekeeping forces from the United Earth Alliance and the United Mesarthrim Clans. Soon afterwards, each world would be allowed to vote for their fate: independence, join or re-join the United Earth Alliance, join the United Mesarthrim Clans, or join the new Prefecture government. The Alliance of Planets would oversee and ensure free elections on each world. 

There was some objection to the treaty's terms. Barbara Dannins noted that it had no stipulations for the repayment of war grievances, or to create a court for war crimes. In answer, the Prefecture agreed to take on 50% of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s war debts, to be paid over a period of 50 years. It was also agreed that any two nations could bring charges of war crimes against former Imperial personnel, and that trials would be held on Earth, starting in 2324. This final condition won over most of the hold-outs. The Treaty of Memphis was ready.

=== Siriun Clause ===

The United Earth Alliance officially claimed the Sirius Cluster as its own territory before the war.  Many officials were convinced that they had gained their independence through treachery, as  it was forced upon the United Earth Alliance in the Titan Peace Accords, and they sought to include the Sirius Cluster in any agreements concerning former Alliance territories.  The idea of Alliance "peacekeeping" forces in the Sirius Cluster was unacceptable to the patriarchs of the Sirius Families.

In the course of negotiations, debate between Nicholai Asanova and Barbara Dannins became increasingly heated. At one point, Nicholai stood before storming from the room, and pointed towards Anthony Hadrian.  He told Dannins that if she wanted to know of the treachery leading to Siriun independence, that the Grand Admiral was the man to ask.

Ultimately, the "Siriun Clause" was inserted into the Treaty of Memphis.  It singled out the Sirius Cluster as exempt from provisions concerning former United Earth Alliance territories, and defined the Sirius Cluster as a sovereign entity recognized by all signatories of the Treaty. Shortly after the treaty was signed, details of the [[Sirius Incursion]] were "leaked" to press agencies by anonymous sources.  

== Signing the Treaty ==

On April 25, 2323, the treaty was drawn up. All committee members signed the treaty, marking the official end of the Second Exodus War.

== Criticism ==

Afterwards, there were some critics of the treaty. They pointed out that the members of the resistance were essentially self-appointed representatives of their worlds, and were acting in collusion with the Prefecture.

Additionally, the creation of the Prefecture as a government drew much criticism from the [[Mesarthrim]] worlds. They believed that many of the war crimes trials were not carried out in good faith, and that by allowing former Imperial officers to take part in the Prefecture's government, the Terrans were essentially trading one empire for another.

[[William Camp]] cited the Treaty of Memphis as a grievous slight against the [[Mesarthrim]], none of the planets that were liberated by the [[Mesarthrim]] were included in the [[Mesarthrim]] sovereignty.  In addition, the "upstart Prefecture" used its "sudden economy" to hamper the [[Mesarthrim]] Import/Export exchange.  This became one of the most important causes of the [[Tempest War]].

Despite the misgivings and eventual destabilization of the [[Pelagrim Sector]] and [[De Mairan Sector]], most historians consider the Treaty of Memphis to be a fair compromise, which did much to stabilize the [[Sol Sector]] for several years.

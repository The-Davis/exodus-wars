===Architecture===

[[Sirunaki]] dwell in subterranean habitats, which they keep mostly dark. They cook their meals near their surface tunnels, so the light doesn't filter down to their more comfortable sleeping and working areas. Most [[Sirunaki]] cave systems are only 10-20% natural, the rest carved and shaped by hand.

[[Sirunaki]] build necropolises above ground, nothing else. Since the dawn of their society, they have not buried their dead. Underground is for the living, the surface is for the dying and the dead. Ancient [[Sirunaki]] were placed on flat rocks on the surface during the night. Modern [[Sirunaki]] are laid to rest in vast tombs, which are built from stone and rock mined from their homes underground.

===Social Structure===

[[Sirunaki]] society ranges from small villages of two or three family cave complexes to vast underground complexes with thousands of families and millions of people. [[Sirunaki]] families are geriatocracies, with the family elders ruling the family.

===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Wingu'fira|History]]

'''Kabila Wingu'fira''' is famous in [[Ngrligru]] ancient history. They were the first Kabila to give refuge to [[Ona Raha]], the scribe and scholar who brought enlightenment to [[Ngrligru]] society.

Since ancient times, they have stayed constant and powerful, but never all-powerful like [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] or [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]]. Wingu'fira are devoted more towards their heritage and history than their future.

Wingu'fira did not play an active role in the destruction of the two renegade Kabilas, but they did give support to the vote that started the war. By traditional [[Ngrligru]] values, only [[Ngrligru]] life is worth anything--and even then it's only considered very fancy cuisine by most [[Ngrligru]], the only civilized cannibal society in the universe

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of Kabila Wingu'fira|Foreign Relations]]

Today, Wingu'fira remains a very influential member of the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]]. They are strong advocates of war with everyone who isn't [[Ngrligru]], for whatever reason they happen to be able to think of. Despite their heritage, they are just as violent and unpredictable as most [[Ngrligru]]--their motivation is just different.

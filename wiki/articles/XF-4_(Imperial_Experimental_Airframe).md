The XF-4 was the final prototype design with the first airframe. It was outfitted with a fission reactor which it used instead of fuel combustion for the Ramjet. Converting the entire fuel tank system to the ion engine gave it a significantly wider operational radius. The design was approved, and it was outfitted with twin cannons and became the YF-4. The YF-4 performed well against existing air fighters, and several production test models were produced. Finally, it became the F-4 Raven which was the first Imperial air and space fighter.

The '''Volucris Incursion''' was a series of attacks by the [[Volucris]] species against several human factions. It began in [[2324]] with the attack and destruction of the [[Mirach System]], and ended after a raid on [[Kronheim]].

Human strategists eventually discovered that the [[Volucris]] were targetting [[Wormhole Gates]] for travel coordinates, and back-tracked to the central hub on [[Kronheim]]. Their ultimate goal was never discovered, but with the data they stole from the central hub the Volucris obtained the locations of every human world with a gate.

A possible reason for gathering this information was to locate sites for actual invasion.  Eight years later, the Volucris invaded [[Lorithan]] in the conflict that became known as the [[Volucris War]].  Their ultimate goals are still being researched and debated.

== Background ==

Until recently, the exact cause or reason for the incursion remained an unknown factor.  However, after the conclusion of the Volucris War, information began to surface which shed light on the origins of the Volucris in the [[Orion Arm]], and their mass migration toward the mid-arm regions.

While it is well documented that [[Anderung|Anderung's]] presence in the [[Pelagrim Sector]] was as a result of being driven from the [[Tolana Sector]] due to the millennium-long war between the [[Yurtosh hora ven Serthra]] and the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], what was unknown at the time of the Incursion was that the Volucris, too, had existed in the Tolana Sector since the fall of the Gorhamut civilization.  Whereas the Anderung's civilization sprung up in the coreward quarter of the sector, the Volucris had survived further rimward.

As the last of the Anderung were fleeing to the Pelagrim sector, the Ixoarchada encountered the Volucris, and conflict again broke out.  Hardened by a thousand years of war, the Ixoarchada had little desire to share their newly won territory and ultimately drove the Volucris from their territory after almost three centuries.

As the Volucris lost their worlds, they began a fighting withdrawal towards the core.  Initially, the Ixoarchada suspected that the Volucris might be looking for the Anderung, but the vast hive instead traveled between uninhabited systems, stopping in each to examine core planets, then moving on.  As this continued, the Ixoarchada began to suspect that the Volucris were instead looking for something long-since vanished, from the age of the [[Gorhamut]]--perhaps the Gorhamut themselves.  Investigations into these worlds, as well as conquered Volucris worlds, revealed much insight into possible motives and destinations.

Among the Taviridis there were many who argued against destroying the Volucris, instead hoping to follow them into the mid-arm and discover their ultimate destination.  This eventually led the Ixoarchada to make [[First Contact]] with the Mesarthrim, drive the Volucris into the Pelagrim Sector, and ultimately to intervene on [[Lorithan]].  Some have even argued that the event of [[2324#February|February 5, 2324]] were intentionally orchestrated by the Ixoarchada in order to warn the [[Terran]] nations of the impending incursion.

== Course of the Incursion ==

=== 2324 ===

The Volucris Incursion began on February 5, [[2324]], in an uncharted star system in the [[Pelagrim Sector]], approximately two light years from the [[Mirach System]].  The Volucris drifted into and interrupted a battle between the fleets of the [[Onyx Empire]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], who had been given the location of the Onyx fleet by the Taviridis Somarchada. The human fleets retreated, and the Prefecture's forces inadvertently led the Volucris to the [[Mirach System]].  After a hurried evacuation of the planet, the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] intervened and destroyed the entire star system. Many people thought this was the end of the Volucris threat.

A month later, the Volucris Swarm arrived in the [[Mimosa System]] and landed a significant force on the surface of [[Tavou]].  The Swarm engaged the [[Prefecture Legion Corps]] en masse, and General [[Travis Carpenter]] and General [[Chonge Kodwe]] developed many of the strategies used to combat the Volucris.  The [[Ixoarchada]] sent [[Ellisaire Berok]] and [[Palontir Taukin]] as military observers, but did not directly intervene in the fighting.  The [[Auellal League]] also sent their Pathfinders to aid the Prefecture, in hopes of gaining experience and intelligence on the new alien threat.  After only five days, the Volucris withdrew, leaving behind several million of their own dead.

On April 10, 2324, the Volucris launched their last raid in Prefecture space, this time moving from their earlier strikes in the Pelagrim Sector to the Prefecture's capital world of [[Ueda]] in the [[De Mairan Sector]].  The raid only lasted a few hours, and the Volucris were not seen again for ten months.

=== 2325 ===

In late March of [[2325]], the Volucris Swarm invaded the [[Yngard System]].  They raided several convoys and harassed several combat vessels.  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] sent significant material and military aid the the [[Ikronin]], and reinforced [[Kronheim]] well in advance of any actual Volucris landings.  The Prefecture suffered the first major starship loss when the [[FDS Imposis|FDS ''Imposis'']] was infiltrated and destroyed by Volucris.

On April 2, 2325, the Volucris landed on Kronheim en masse. The battle lasted only a single day, but left thousands of Ikronin and Terrans dead, and uncounted millions of Volucris destroyed.  The city of [[Oselo]] was burned to the ground and razed by the Volucris, and the rubble bore the brunt of their attacks.  After raiding several key installations in Oselo, the Volucris disappeared and did not return until they attacked [[Lorithan]] and began the [[Volucris War]], eight years later.

== Aftermath ==

After the fighting ended on [[Kronheim]], the Federated Districts of the Prefecture and the [[Ikronin Jurekon]] became close allies.  Relations between the two nations had been cool and tempered with distrust, but the Prefecture's seemingly selfless support of the Ikronin solidified relations between the two and eventually helped form the basis of the [[Tripartite Alliance]] many years later.

== Impact of the Incursion ==

The Volucris Incursion marked the end of the Volucris presence in the Tolana Sector, and the beginning of complete Ixoarchada hegemony in the outer-arm.  The appearance of the Volucris in the Pelagrim and other sectors showed the mid-arm human nations that there was yet another alien species on the horizon, and unlike the [[Ngrligru]], with whom they could negotiate and even ally, there was nothing to do but weather the storm of the Volucris.  Many strategies were developed for fighting and surviving a Volucris raid, but no one could produce any ideas on actually ''defeating'' them.  A full-fledged war was unthinkable, until [[Volucris War|one began]] eight years later.  

It is believed that during the eight years of between the end of the Incursion and the beginning of the War, the Volucris continued to explore uninhabited systems, continually looking for further clues as to their final destination.  Although it has never been made public, it is also widely believed that the Ixoarchada continued to monitor the swarms, and occasionally harass them to keep them on the move.

The Battle of Ward was the first naval battle for the [[Mesarthrim Federation]], and the last battle that [[Arnold Stadt]] commanded. The Battle of Ward marked the first victory in a series of victories securing the [[Xi Herculis System]]. Although both fleets we equal in fire power, Stadt was able to pull off a majorly lopsided victory.

==Prelude to Battle==
Only five days prior to the Battle of Ward, a mutiny was staged against Admiral [[Hansen Dietrich]], resulting in his death and the deaths or numerous commissioned officers. The new "Admiral" of the fleet, had given himself a field promotion from Second Commandant. The mutiny and execution of officers was unknown to Admiral Arnold Stadt, so he came prepared to fight fast and brutal.

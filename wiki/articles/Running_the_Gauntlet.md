by [[James Davis (author)|James Davis]]

May 25, [[2327]]

By the end of the 25th of May, the Prefecture in the Fathach Méire are completely routed. Fully two thirds of their men are dead or taken prisoner, and the remaining third is in a running battle from the peak to the southern foothills and the relative safety of Tara'dar Coirp.

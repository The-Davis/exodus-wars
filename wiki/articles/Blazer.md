The '''Blazer''' is the primary weapon of choice of the [[Mesarthrim]] civilizations. It's a plasma-based energy weapon, noted for its loud report, bright flash, and powerful concussive punch at close range. The Blazer's only weakness is poor performance at long range, where it loses out to [[Lansvapen]] weapons or [[Terran Mass Drivers|Mass Driver]] weapons.

Blazers are murderously effective short-range, high-damage energy weapons, excellent at penetrating armor and shields.  They fire bolts of plasma at extremely high velocities.  Blazers are based on [[Anderung]] technology, and have a few peculiarities that set them apart from other human plasma weapons.    

Technically, Blazers are brutally simple. The basic components of a Blazer are a power source, a pre-firing chamber, and an acceleration coil.  Gas is drawn into the pre-firing chamber, and an intense electrical current ionizes it, transforming the gas into energetic plasma.  The cloud of plasma is funneled down the acceleration coil, which shapes it into a bolt.   

Blazers have a few technical oddities.  Although the plasma bolt is very dense, intended for maximum penetration, it's not well-focused; plasma bleeds off in all directions as the bolt travels.  Blazers are designed this way, to maximize damage at the cost of range; an unfocused plasma bolt deals more collateral damage than a focused bolt.  

Some blazers have the capability to fire an 'unfocused shot', in which the blazer's focusing circuitry is deactivated.  The result is a short-ranged fan of plasma, something like a flamethrower.  It's ineffective against most armor and shields, but devastating against lightly armored targets like infantry and smaller [[Volucris]] breeds.        

Plasma, magnetic, and gravity shields are all effective against Blazer bolts at range, but at close range Blazers penetrate them with ease.  Proton-bonded armors, and [[Falkran Armor|Falkran]] in particular, are both particularly resistant to blazer fire. 

Blazer bolts travel so quickly that they're rarely visible as more than a violet flash

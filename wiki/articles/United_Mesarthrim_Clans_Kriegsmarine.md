The '''Kriegsmarine''' was the naval power of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  They were charged with the defense of all [[Mesarthrim]] space, and with being the first strike offensive force of the [[Mesarthrim]].  The Kriegsmarine was also charged with carrying the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Heer]] and [[United Mesarthrim Clans Luftwaffe|Luftwaffe]] into battle.
In [[2275]], Space Installations were also added to the jurisdiction of the Kriegsmarine.

==History==
The Kriegsmarine was the most expensive venture of the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]].  Not only did it require huge shipyards to be hidden from the [[Anderung]], but also costed many lives stealing and acquiring the resources the ships needed.  The [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] or ''First Fleet'', was finally assembled in [[2244]], sixteen years before the foundation of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  The fleet consisted of a [[Sickle class Crusier]] and nine [[Avänion class Corvette|Avänion class Corvettes]].  The fleet was entirely purchased and paid for by [[Kriegshöfe]].  The expense almost bankrupted the company, but proved vital to the war effort.

As the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] marched towards victory, the security and production concerns gave way to labor and recruitment shortages, these shortages came in [[2270]] right during the formation of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] or ''Second Fleet''.  In [[2321]] when the [[Onyx Empire|Onyx]] picket was destroyed, recruitment skyrocketed as many young Mesarthrim hungered to see new places and worlds.

When the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] attacked [[Mesar]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] and [[United Mesarthrim Clans Third Fleet|Dritte Flotte]] were both stationed at [[Terra]].  Only the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] stood in defense of [[Mesar]].  The problem was that the fourth element of [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet#The Users|Die Benutzer]] were [[Mesarnak]].  The [[Mesarnak]] betrayed the remainder of [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]], and cleared a path for [[Steven Cue]] and his invasion army to land on [[Mesar]].  The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet#The Users|Benutzer]] became the core of the [[Auellal Armada]].

==Structure==
The Kriegsmarine uses fleets as 'bodies' for smaller elements within the fleets.  Each element has its own element commander, and can be directed off to cover a certain objective or installation.  Often times elements are detached from the fleet on assignment.  The most common example of this is the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet#The Preachers|Prediger]] element of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] was dispatched to [[Titan System|Titan]] in [[2321]] after [[first contact]] and stayed there until the fall of the [[Onyx Empire]] in [[2324]].

===Fleets===
The [[United Mesarthrim Clans First Fleet|Erste Flotte]] was formed in [[2244]] by [[Anthony Kilhund von Stadt]].  At it's peak it boasted 65 vessels in 6 atypical elements, led by the [[UMCS Black Parade|UMCS Schwarz Parade]].

The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Zweite Flotte]] was formed in [[2270]] by [[Hansel Worterburg von Baum]].  At it's peak it boasted 40 vessels in 4 atypical elements, led by the [[UCMS Pride of our Fathers|UMCS Stolz unserer Väter]].

The [[United Mesarthrim Clans Third Fleet|Dritte Flotte]] was formed in [[2322]] by [[Arnold Stadt]].  At it's peak it boasted 48 vessels in 8 typical elements, led by the [[UCMS Anderung's Dirge|UMCS Anderung Trauergesang]].

==Command Structure==
Due to the slow evolution of the Kriegsmarine, and the relative chaotic organization that predated it, the Kriegsmarine adopted a very ridged command structure to avoid a rabble or gang from rising up within their ranks.  The central kriegsmarine authority oversees all of the Kriegsmarine assets, and has historically been the solemn duty of the [[Stadt Clan]].

===Kriegsmarine Law===
The Kriegsmarine keeps a very separate judicial system from the rest of the [[Mesarthrim]].  Kriegsmarine tribunals almost always end in capital punishment being carried out against any felony.  Misdemeanors are punished using a variety of torturous or painful lessons.  The lightest of offenses usually get flogged.

===Kriegsmarine Officers===
Officers are held personally responsible for their subordinates actions.  If a subordinate is caught stealing, his commanding officer is given part of the punishment.  A superior officer never punishes a man who is not directly under him.  This order was enacted to give the Officers a very direct responsibility for their men.

==Traditions==
The Kriegsmarine have numerous traditions, including some to the service, and some to the State Church.  Religious ceremony is very important to the Kriegsmarine, and all officers and enlisted men are required to attend an all hands chapel service.  This service may be pre-empted, however, if the vessel is operating in an enemy zone, or under a direct threat.  Another tradition amongst enlisted men in the Kriegsmarine, is when it comes time for them to marry, they will return to their first posting, and their ceremony will be held in that vessels chapel.

As for traditions of the service, each officer and enlisted man in the service have the names of their vessels they have served on tattooed on their right shoulder blade, and each of their fleets tattooed on their left.  During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]], Kriegsmarine officers would cut the flesh from [[Auellal League|Auellal]] or other rebels backs when they were captured, removing the memory of their service from their flesh.

Another common tradition in the service is for commanding officers to carry a personal flag aboard their vessels.  These flags are often adorned with the accolades they have received, and are usually hand crafted by the officer themselves.  These flags hang above the entry into the CIC on their ship.  Planet side high ranking officers tend to have the flag hang in their offices or on the pole at their posting, below the [[Mesarthrim State Church|Church's]] and [[United Mesarthrim Clans|UMC's]] flags.  Again, in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]], [[Myt'gaul]] agents would infiltrate  [[Auellal League|Auellal]] or other rebel vessels and burn their commanders flag.

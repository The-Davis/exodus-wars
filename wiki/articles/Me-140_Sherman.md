===Overview===

The Sherman is a relatively new design for the [[United Centusi States]]' Luftwaffe.  Created during the final years of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], it was designed to be an electronics warfare and Wild-Weasel type aircraft used in planetary assaults to detect and take out sensors grids, SAM sites, and other threats to landing crafts.

[[Image:Me140Sherman1.jpg]]

The Sherman, while visibly similar to the Anox fighter-bomber, is different in nearly every aspect of its design.

===Capabilities===

The Sherman is designed to detect enemy sensors stations and blind them until they can be destroyed.  It usually sends other fighters to intercept enemy positions, but it can destroy them itself.  For this, the Sherman is equipped with three Type-C Blazers, the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type.  In addition to these three cannons, the Sherman has four medium hardpoints on its wings, which usually carry Auto-Response Anti-Sensors missiles.  These ARAS missiles automatically fire upon sensors grids as soon as they scan the Sherman. 

[[Image:Me140Sherman2.jpg]]

The Sherman is outfitted with a standard fighter-grade reactor, and an impressive engine array, allowing it to carry heavy payloads of weaponry.  Its drive system has been significantly upgraded over the older Anox' drive system, and the fact that it carries a lighter payload gives it substantially increased maneuverability, although it is still no fighter craft.

===Deployment===

Sherman fighters most often operate as Wild-Weasel Electronic Warfare fighters, tracking down surface defense stations and sensors grids and destroying them.  A flight of Shermans is also common among most large aerospace forces on attack missions, as their Electronic Warfare equipment can hide the fighters from sensors, or at least render them ineffective at range.  They are additionally used as long-range scouts and AWACS/C&C aerospace craft.

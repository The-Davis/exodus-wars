The Terran Regulars use a single powered battle armor suit, the Hector, for their heavy ground operations.  It's used for heavy assault and garrison duty, but is not very mobile or fast.  Because of the highly mobile nature of their infantry, the Hector is rarely used for anything but the largest assault missions.  Due to the Terran Embassy's non-aggression treaties and small territory, these rarely happen.

Because of the relatively sedentary duty, Hector operators are switched out with regular infantry every week.  Basically, both Divisions trade places.

Unknown date in 2326.

by James Davis and Andrew Sears

=====Leskovik Outer System Patrol - 2410 Hours=====
     The Fidelity, Leskovik's primary Essex patrol carrier, is well into the third watch. The ship is about 100 astronomical units from Leskovik, and has just dropped off another fighter screen. In a few minutes, it will make a short hop on the Kovalski-Fahr drive to pick up fighters closer to the planet.
     One small indicator begins to blink, alerting the lone officer on duty. Ensign Paul Dickson looks up from the book he'd been reading, then walks over to examine the sensors. Two buttons later, and a series of numbers flashes on the screen.
     Ensign Dickson opens an intercom channel to the skipper's quarters. "Commander Matthews, we've picked up an FTL footprint. The computers think there's another vessel on its way here, or already in the area."
     After a short delay, Ensign Dickson repeats the message. The commander finally responds. "Give me possible courses. Where could they be, who are they? Is it one of ours?"
     An NCO calls Dickson's attention back to the monitors. "Correction sir, there is a ship in the area. There was a huge gravity spike just a moment ago. We're trying to locate the ship. And no sir, it's not ours. Everything but the military takes the Gates, and the military always sends a calling card ahead."
     "Analysis on the spike?" the commander asks. "Could it be pirates?"
     Dickson checks the indicators on the monitor, then responds. "No sir, not pirates. It's too fast for FTL. It's too fast even for KF."
     "Then who?"
     "From the speed and the only possible vectors, it can only be Mesarthrim or Archadian."

=====UCS Attack Vessel - 2415 Hours=====
     The Aeris Class Destroyer Dreary Mist tears free of hyperspace. Its GRLD systems shut down, and the vessel's weapons begin to power up. The Captain of the vessel smirks at the sight of the Prefecture patrol ship, oblivious to the lurking destroyer.
     A moment later, the destroyer fires a single volley, smashing through the debris shields and rupturing the Fidelity's hull. With its gravity and plasma shields down, the carrier has no chance. The breached reactor bleeds off into space, taking the doomed crew with it.

=====Tavou Central - 2450 Hours=====
     Colin Archibald swipes his ID card through the reader at the door. The red LED blinks once, signaling an error. "Damn!" Archibald swipes the card again. Finally, on the third try, the green LED blinks, and the door swings open to admit him into the Tavou District's central command center. "Sorry I'm late. Get maintenance to look at the security door. Now, tell me what happened."
     Colonel Kenneth Russell, commander of the Tavou Regulars and veteran of the Battle of Mirach, salutes Archibald. "Thirty-five minutes ago, the patrol carrier Fidelity was lost while patrolling Leskovik."
     "Lost? As in destroyed? Stranded? AWOL?" Archibald queries. "And aren't patrol carriers under Navy jurisdiction?"
     "Status of the Fidelity is unknown. It missed a rendezvous to pick up an Air Command patrol. That's how we learned about it. The HDS branch office on Leskovik has dispatched a Mercy cruiser to run search and rescue in the area. Marshal Arias will be reporting the findings in--"
     "Right now." Regional Marshal Samuel Arias walks into the command center, still in full flight gear. "Just landed. We found what was left of the Fidelity."
     "So what happened?" asks Russell.
     "Bugs?" Colin asks, reaching for a mug of coffee.
     "I'm not certain, but no." Arias grimaces. "Definitely not bugs. There's no trace of plasma fire or acidic breakdown." With a quick set of keystrokes, he sends his report from a small PDA to the command center's presentation system. As the screen activates, Arias highlights several features. "This is the bulk of the hull. There's one main breach, where the KF drive array was blasted off completely. There are several minor breaches throughout the structure, and the bridge was completely blown out. We found twenty bodies, but ten are still unaccounted for. Preliminary autopsies show death from exposure to vacuum, further supporting our theory. The Fidelity was destroyed by hostile forces. They fired one shot, using an energy weapon powerful and accurate enough to hit just the reactor. Bugs would cause a lot more secondary damage. Ballistic weapons wouldn't destroy the reactor, and explosives would kill the crew before spacing, and cause burn damage to the tissue."
     "An unprovoked attack then. Who did it?" Archibald demands. "Is this isolated, or the forerunner to an invasion?"
     "I'm not sure. A flotilla from the Fourth Fleet has been dispatched to investigate and try to identify the source of the damage." Arias replies.
     Before anyone can continue the conversation, the Ansible system activates. "Tavou Command! This is HDS, Leskovik. ID confirmation on emergency sub-frequency. We are under attack! Little time left, coded burst transmission incoming!" The Ansible cuts off.
     "Did we get the burst?" Archibald demands.
     "Got it." Russell confirms. "Oh God, no...Defense Condition One. Bring Tavou Command up to full capacity. Arias, scramble all patrols. Get everything into defensive positions. Mr. Archibald, this is now a military affair, but I still need your help."
     Arias salutes and dashes out the door as dozens of technicians and Marines enter at breakneck speed. The command center roars to life with the sound of activity and communications.
     "What do you need, Colonel?" Archibald asks. "Who's doing this?"
     Russell hands him a data disk. "Get this to Ueda. Fast. Don't let it get sent over Ansible again, and don't read it until you're well away. Just go. I'll have a shuttle waiting for you on the roof."
     "WHO'S ATTACKING?!" Archibald shouts.
     "The Mesarthrim. The UCS. Now go!"

Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan has a nationalized economy on [[Lorithan]]. While companies exist, they are little more than names for administrative divisions on the [[Kihtor]]. On [[Jagarr]], most industry is run by the military. On [[Ipsof]], the economy is regulated, but is mostly a free market.

==Sectors of the Economy==

===Industry===
The [[Lorithos]] are the most successful robotics engineers in the galaxy. Automation is their specialty, and their AI software--while lacking the self-awareness of true AIs--is quite useful. They are also the best engineers and metalurgists around.

===Energy===
The [[Lorithos]] are self-sufficient in terms of energy, using [[Hydrogen Engines]] powered by hydrogen gas harvested from the gas giants of their star systems. These systems, while clean and efficient, are not wanted outside of [[Lorithos]] space and are thus not exported.

===Agriculture===
The [[Lorithos]] have an enormous aquaculture economy on [[Ipsof]], where they harvest sea creatures and grow most of the foodstuffs consumed by the people of [[Lorithan]]. [[Jagarr]] also has a thriving agriculture idustry, but one that has very limited exports.

===Tourism===
Tourism is strong on [[Ipsof]]. [[Ngrligru]] enjoy the mild oceans, which are similar to their worlds back in [[Kabila Duka]] space. [[Kabila Kimburu]] [[Ngrligru]] frequently visit, and some even live there.

===Arms Industry===
The [[Lorithos]] produce most of their own weapons, but do not export to any other nations.

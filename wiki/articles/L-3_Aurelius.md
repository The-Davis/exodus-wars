The "Lutador" is the name for Kalidasan fighters.

In the midst of a massive reconstruction program following the Second Exodus War, the Kalidasa Planetary Authority generated a large number of jobs by designing and constructing its own defense forces, with every part of the design and construction being done in-system.  The Aurelius Patrol Fighter was designed to be the mainstay craft of the Kalidasa Air and Space Corps.  
The Aurelius is the mainstay patrol fighter of the KASC.  It is neither heavily armed, nor heavily armored.  However, it does carry a respectible armament for such a small fighter.  Its energy weaponry consists of four Neutron Beam Cannons, two Medium and two Small.  These make it capable of scrapping with other fighters in the air, or attacking targets on the ground. 

In addition to its cannons, the Aurelius is has eight external ordinance hardpoints with which it can carry various missiles, bombs, and other gear to suit any specific missions it might conduct. 

The Aurelius is designed in typical Kalidasan fashion, with more emphasis on speed and agility than on armor and shields.  Still, it is outfitted with Boron-Nitride armor as well as a Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens. 

Aurelius Patrol Fighters fill most every role in the KASC.  They operate as aerial patrol craft, space reconaissance fighters, and general shuttle-craft as well (slight modifications allow two people to fit in the cockpit with relative ease.)  They are small, quick, and relatively inexpensive (as true aerospace craft go).  All in all, they are solid designs deployed en masse throughout the Kalidasa Air and Space Corps.

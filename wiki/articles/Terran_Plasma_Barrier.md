Barrier Plasma Shielding The Barrier Plasma Shield is a cloud of magnetically confined low-temperature plasma. It is placed around a warship as a defense against energy weapons. Plasma shields have many free-floating electrons that cancel out energy fields, causing photons to bounce off the surface of the shield. This is very effective against all forms of energy weaponry, but is useless against projectile weaponry. The unfortunate side-effect of plasma shields is that it effectively cancels out long-range sensors. Scout ships must be deployed for long-range battlefield communication. The Fighter Barrier works on aerospace fighters. It can project out to fifty meters or more. It must be cycled off to use long-range scanners. Warship Barrier - The Warship Barrier has a range of six hundred meters.

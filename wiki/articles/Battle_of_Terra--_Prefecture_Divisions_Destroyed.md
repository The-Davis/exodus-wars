June 7, [[2322]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture--the forces loyal to Admiral Hadrian--suffered a devastating blow today. Their Fourth and Fifth Mechanized Divisions, operating in the middle-eastern regions of Terra, came under heavy fire from Imperial Behemoth units.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Fourth Division--made up mostly of light Rigs--had little chance of surviving a close-range battle with Imperial Assault Rigs, so they began a strategic retreat. The Fifth had several lances of their own Assault Rigs, and engaged the Imperial forces in a heated battle that lasted ten minutes.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When the smoke cleared, the entire Prefecture Fifth Mechanized Division was wiped out. The Imperial Assault Rigs were too slow to catch up with the fast light Rigs from the Fourth Mechanized Division, and abandoned the chase.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Fourth prepared to escape into outer space, and called in the Prefecture Fleet's dropships. While the Fourth was helpless and vulnerable in the loading process, Imperial forces from Scimitar Bomber Squadron launched nuclear warheads and vaporized the entire Division and several dozen dropships. The Gobi desert was turned to glass in a twenty kilometer radius.

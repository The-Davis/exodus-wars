== Racial Demographics ==

Since settling on [[Junheim]], the [[Reigens]] began to change in appearance from their [[Ikronin]] relatives.

== Immigration and Citizenship ==

Anyone may immigrate into Reigen society, but they must swear fealty to the Kejsare, and admit that they are subject to his laws.  Immigrants must also acknowledge that they are only caretakers of the Kejsare's property, and nothing they own is their own.

== Religion ==

The Reigens believe in a pantheon made up of the same gods as the [[Ikronin]], but their beliefs completely differ after that one similarity.

They still believe in Father Aden, Mother Marin, Brave Raab, Hallowed Siran, and Dark Foren, but do not believe them to be the selfish or heartless deities that their [[Ikronin]] cousins do.  Oddly enough, the Reigens do not believe that the gods created the universe.  One of the central dogmas of Reigen religion is that the origin of the universe is unknowable.

Father Aden is seen as the source of law and order in the world, though he apparently draws his authority from some higher power, possibly a natural law beyond definition.

Mother Marin is the source of intelligent life and of human fertility.

Brave Raab is the guardian and warrior of the Reigens.

Hallowed Siran is the goddess of dreams and of imagination.

Dark Foren is the god of death.  Foren is seen as a kindly, but sad soul.

== Languages ==

Reigens speak Sothark.  Some know English as a trade language.

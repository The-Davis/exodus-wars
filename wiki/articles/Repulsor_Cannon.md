The [[Ikronin]] projectile weapon is much different from its counterparts. Most [[Alliance of Planets]] Member States use magnetic launch systems, but the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] uses repulsor power similar to the anti-gravity lift systems used on their hovertanks.

Ammunition for the [[Ikronin]] cannon weapons is mostly composite, usually carbon crystals. Most anything can be used as ammo, as long as it's the right shape and size. At high velocities, the composition of the ammunition doesn't matter much.

An ammunition round's caliber is rated by hundreds of micrometers in diameter: Small Autocannon rounds are 200 micrometers, Light Autocannon rounds are 500 micrometers, Medium Autocannon rounds are 1 millimeter, and Heavy Autocannon rounds are 2 millimeters.

All ammo rounds have a length double their diameter.

==Storbjudn Cannons==
Storbjudn Cannons are the basic hovertank weapon on most [[Ikronin]] [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]]. They have two-millimeter ammo rounds, fire five rounds per second, and have a barrel exit velocity of 11.6 kilometers per second (Mach 35).

==Spungöra Cannons==
Spungöra Cannons are lighter vehicle weapons with rotary barrels, used as backup on several [[Ikronin]] [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]]. They have one-millimeter ammo rounds, fire ten rounds per second, and have a barrel exit velocity of 8.3 kilometers per second (Mach 25).

==Jagare Cannons==
Jagare Cannons are special weapons designed to destroy Rigs at long range. Their ballistic systems are specifically built to disable the leg joints, effectively crippling a Rig. They have three-millimeter ammo rounds, fire two rounds per second, and have a barrel exit velocity of 9.9 kilometers per second (Mach 30).

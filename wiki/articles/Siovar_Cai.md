''Siovar Cai'' (born August 19, [[2299]]), is the heir to the [[Prekohr]] of the [[Lorithos]]. After [[Dirken Huern]]'s wife died childless, Huern vowed not to remarry, and named Siovar as his heir.  This was unprecedented in thousands of years of Lorithos history.  Since the dawn of their civilization, [[Fii Huern]] had led their people. Siovar had to struggle to overcome the uncertainty and distrust of his people, but eventually became a popular symbol of hope to the Lorithos.

== Young Life ==

Siovar Cai was the son of Agen Cai and his wife Namar.  Siovar was born on [[Kihtor Modar]], but his family soon moved to [[Ipsoff]] to take advantage of the cleaner environment.  Siovar lived on a floating city for most of his life.

Siovar was educated mostly by foreign tutors via the [[Urokon]].  He learned from [[Meroniri]] and [[Terran]] instructors, particularly in the fields of politics and history.  Siovar didn't know it, but he was being groomed for leadership.  [[Dirken Huern]] and Siovar's grandfather [[Sanor Cai]] had marked him to be the heir to the throne in the likely event that Huern failed to sire an heir.

== Heir ==

Unfortunately, and as expected, Dirken Huern's wife Arisha died in [[2324]].  The royal couple was childless.  After a period of mourning, Dirken Huern announced that he would not remarry, and he named Siovar his heir.

Siovar returned to [[Lorithan]], where he began receiving training directly from Dirken Huern in the day to day operations of government.  Dirken Huern survived the evacuation of Lorithan, but passed away shortly after the government settled on Ipsoff.

== Prekohr ==

As the new [[Prekohr]] of the Lorithos, Siovar Cai focused on finding new worlds for the Lorithos to settle on.

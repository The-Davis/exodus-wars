The Erste Flotte ''(or First Fleet)'' was the main [[Mesarthrim]] 'Attack Fleet' in both the [[Anderung War]] and the [[Second Exodus War]].  During the [[Onyx Civil War]], the First Fleet became the 'Guardian of Terra' taking part in operations against Imperial strongholds throughout the system.  Eventually, the fleet would return to Mesar where her allegiance was sold to the [[United Centusi States]].

==Formation==
The First Fleet was an initiative sponsored by [[Adrian Tischler]] and [[Otto Stadt]]. The two men started [[Kriegshöfe]] with the plan to build a navy for the rebellion. The construction of the fleet took from [[2188]] until [[2220]]. Kriegshöfe completed the navy in [[2220]] with 10 Destroyers and 9 Cruisers. In [[2221]] [[Mesar Luftfahrt]] came to Kriegshöfe with designs for a fleet carrier and a proposal for a fleet battleship. Stadt and Kriegshöfe agreed, and production began on the two ships. By [[2229]] the two ships were completed.

The fleet operated as a semi-organized band from [[2230]] until [[2260]] when the fleet was vital in the [[Battle of Ice Crown Bay]]. Had the fleet not arrived, the battle would have been an amazing victory for the Anderung - and not a devastating pyrrhic victory. After the Battle, the rag-tag fleet was officially recognized as the First Fleet, with plans being made to construct a Second Fleet.

==Fate==
In May of [[2324]], the First Fleet was ordered home from [[Terra]] to help control the growing [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Rebellion]].  However, when they reached [[Mesar]], their hailing port had fallen under attack by the traitorous elements of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Second Fleet|Second Fleet]].  The fleet, low on provisions, and short on orders, suffered extensive damage and withdrew. They were then confronted by [[William Camp]] and [[Elias Faust]] who were leaving for [[Ipsoff]] to work out a solution to the declining cultural crisis and [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]].  Groß-Commandant Wilfred Stafgard, and the entire fleet received a 'bonus' and extended leave to be the guests of the [[Berlusconi Family Territory]] for the near future.  On May 28th, [[2324]], the First Fleet left the orbit of [[Mesar]] for its final time as a [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] fleet.

The First Fleet was the single largest defection in the history on the [[Mesarthrim]] people.  Later, in [[2329]], Groß-Commandant Wilfred Stafgard was executed by [[William Camp]] for his treachery.

==Composition==
The First Fleet is further divided into sections, The Mesar Vanguard, The Anderung's Bane, and The Tin Cans. Leading the elements are the fleet ships; Knight of Night and the Eternal Night.

===Fleet Vessels===
The fleet vessels serve as the central element that holds the elements together. The Fleet Vessels will attach themselves to which ever element the Groß-Commandant feels needs them for that mission.

The Fleet Vessels were;
* Knight of the Night - Clan Lord class Battleship
* Eternal Night - Usurper class Carrier

===Mesar Vorhut ''(Mesar Vanguard)''===
The Mesar Vanguard was created out of the first [[Sickle class Cruiser]]s built. In [[2260]] the group became the Vanguard of the Mesarthrim First Fleet. This element became the backbone of the First Fleet, serving as the primary element in offensive operations.

[[Image:2190Sickle.png|right|200px]]
The Mesar Vanguard's were;
* Marching Band
* Lookout
* Warrior King
* Dragon Slayer
* Wise Senator
* Vigilant Angel
* City on the Hill
* Timekeeper
* Mesar’s Pride

===Anderungs Verderben ''(Anderung’s Bane)''===
The Anderung's Bane was a series of [[Howell class Destroyer|Howell destroyers]].  They hold the distinction of being the first warships built by the Mesarthrim Resistance. The Anderung's Bane served with distinction their entire career.

[[Image:2188Howell.png|right|200px]]
The Anderung's Bane were the;
* [[Fallen King]]
* Blood Throne
* Fury of Hell
* Dark Agent
* Black Son
* Duality

===Die Zinndosen ''(The Tin Cans)''===
The Tin Cans was a group of [[Howell class Destroyer|Howell destroyers]] that have special outfitting to serve as long range recon ships. The four ships usually operate together to gather intel, rather then operating as single ship elements.

[[Image:2188Howell.png|right|200px]]
The Tin Cans were the;
* Heavy Screen
* Dark Storm
* Constant Vigilance
* Loud Murmur

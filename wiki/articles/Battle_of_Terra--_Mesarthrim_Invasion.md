May 26, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Three days after Beijing fell to the Prefecture, Crimson Tide battle group reached the Sol starsystem and set up orbit around Luna--Terra's moon. Ten Mesarthrim dropships made rapid and dangerous plummets into the atmosphere of Terra, avoiding Imperial aerospace defenses and splashing down in the Pacific ocean.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One of the ten dropships took a bad bounce off the atmosphere and broke apart, scattering men and machines across the stratosphere to die painful deaths, but the crews of the other nine managed to bring their own cargos down safely.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim soldiers left their landing craft and beached in Tasmania, a small island just south of Australia. They were met by only a token force of infantry and light Rigs of the Royal Imperial Army, but those were dispatched without difficulty.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Crimson Tide battle group set up a temporary supply dump and towed their dropships to Tasmania's shore. From there, the forces went north, using landing barges to cross to the Australian mainland. They swept across the bush lands to Sydney, where they were met in force by Imperial troops--a company of Behemoths and a lance Hunchbacks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Light Armored Vehicles and Vulture tanks of the Mesarthrim rushed to meet the mighty giants of the Empire, exchanging huge volleys of firepower. The Behemoths took out a company of LAVs, but then the Vulture Siege Tanks opened fire with their arsenal.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The LAVs scattered, but the slow Behemoths and Hunchbacks took the full brunt of the salvo. The Hunchbacks were down in an instant, completely pulverized by the Vulture's attack. Two Behemoths went down, but the remaining ten kept coming.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Behemoths opened fire, concentrating on one of the Vultures. The salvo pierced the armor and detonated the ammunition inside. The Behemoths were quickly put down by the remaining Vultures.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial infantry kept up the defense of Sydney as long as they could, but within an hour the battle was over and the Cresting Wave flag of the United Mesarthrim Clans was flying over Sydney's capitol building. After raising the flag, Commandant Hinks had this to say:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Nathan Hinks: "This planet belongs to the civilians of Terra. I have freed Sydney from the yoke of Imperial oppression. I will protect Sydney and not stop until we've destroyed the Empire."

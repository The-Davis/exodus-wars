===Overview===

The Whale Class Hauler was developed in 2304, and used extensively throughout the territory of the now-fallen United Earth Alliance.  It's popularity netted Blazton Heavy Industries billions of UEA Dollars in sales.

During the Second Exodus War, Whale Class Haulers were used by all sides for transport of troops and mechanized units to various theaters of conflict.  The Whale was capable of landing on a planet with atmosphere, but only after a landing zone was secured.

[[Image:WhaleClassHauler1.jpg]]

In 2323, the Blackis Prefecture requested competing designs for a bulk hauler that could fight in a jam.  Blazton Heavy Industries and Rahn Industries both competed for the contract, but Blazton's overhauled Whale design beat out the Typhoon of Rahn Industries.

===Capabilities===

The overhauled Whale was built to be able to take off and land with much more ease than the original.  The hauler still requires a secured landing zone, but unlike the original Whale, it can land in rough terrain if a full-fledged spaceport is unavailable.

The take-off and landing time of the new Whale is only ten minutes.  Blazton Heavy Industries invented an anti-gravity lift system to launch the Whale into space.  Blazton plans on marketing the lift system to mass-transit manufacturers.

Four gigantic leg-like landing gears support the Whale when it's landed.  The original 2304 Whale required a runway twelve miles long to take off and land, or an old-style rocket pad to take off.  With the anti-gravity system and new landing gear, it can take off and land vertically without the huge energy consumption.

[[Image:WhaleClassHauler2.jpg]]

Depending on the type of cargo its carrying, the Whale can unload in anywhere from ten minutes to four hours.  It's easiest to unload troops, and hardest to unload fully assembled Rigs.  Each Whale is equipped with four robotic lifters, and and a power-assistance suit for each member of the crew.

Whales can carry most everything, though for special cargos they need a day or two to convert the cargo bays.  For example, to move liquids or gasses, pressurized tanks and pipe systems need to be installed.  For troops, cold-sleep chambers need to be installed, or simply berths for shorter journeys.

For protection against boarders, a Whale has berths for five soldiers.  The Blackis Prefecture assigns Home Defense Security Officers instead of marines, as most Whales are owned by the non-military branches of the Prefecture.

The Whale has magnetic and plasma shields, but not gravity shields.  Gravity shields are a purely military technology, and have not been publicized by the Phoenix Project.  Still, these shields and the composite armor can keep the Whale safe against most raiders.  Only a concentrated assault can breach the defenses, and four Neutron Cannon turrets discourage assault.

Because it is a civilian class vessel, the Whale has no long-range FTL system.  Kovalski-Fahr drives are restricted to military use.  For long-range trips, Whales use the Icronian Wormhole Gate system, a humongous network of artificial, stable wormholes created and maintained by the Icronian Consortium.

===Deployment===

The Whale Class Hauler is used throughout human civilization.  The Prefecture uses it for military and non-military cargo hauling.  Most private corporations own several Whales for their hauling jobs.

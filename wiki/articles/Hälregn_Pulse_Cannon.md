The [[Reigen]] '''Hälregn Aggregated Fusion Pulse Cannon''' is principally composed of a multi-stage, contained [[:Category:Nuclear Fusion|Fusion]] or [[:Category:Antimatter|Antimatter]] reactor which uses magnetic and gravitic fields to focus and collimate the exhaust into a beam of outgoing weapons fire.  It fires energetic plasma, neutrons, and high-energy photons at a target.  Although effective against all targets, the beam is particularly useful against adaptive shielding and defense systems.  No matter how well shields are tuned, adaptive shields cannot completely optimize themselves for the weapons fire, as it is too diverse.

A single reactor can power multiple Hälregn Cannons, and the intensity of each can be modulated based on how much output is available from each reactor.  Larger ships will utilize more reactors, with each housed within the hull of a warship.  This has the added bonus that most of the weapon is located inside the ship, with only the collimating chamber protruding from the hull.  The chambers are "barrels", which more closely resemble gimble-mounted, vectored-thrust exhaust nozzles, and can quickly change their bearing.

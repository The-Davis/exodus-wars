April 7, 2325

by James Davis

=====Chambers of the People of the Pen - Mesar - 1245 Hours=====
     The People of the Pen are one of the few civilian branches of Mesarthrim governments. When the United Mesarthrim Clans High Command was dissolved in favor of the United Centusi States, the powers of the Director’s office were balanced by the democratically-elected “People of the Pen.” Right now, they are hearing debate between two people. 
     Governor Charles Degause is the leader of the Tincomalee colony, a Mesarthrim world that is co-defended by the United Archadian Republic. Degause favors a diplomatic end to the war before it can escalate any further. 
     Director William Camp is the executive head of the United Centusi States, and his motivations and goals are his alone. For months now, the Director has become more reclusive and irritable. 
     “Citizens, friends, we must weigh a heavy issue before us here today,” Degause begins. “Yesterday, the Third Fleet of the Prefecture attacked our homeworld. Our valiant warriors defended our skies and routed the forces of Prefect Hadrian. Based on all reports, we even managed to kill the vaunted leader of the Prefecture. 
     “While we continue to investigate their accusations that our own aggression started this war, we must prepare for peace. While I have no doubt in the superiority of our forces, if we prosecute this war to our best abilities, our victory will be a hollow one. If the Prefecture unleashes the full might of its war machine, our victory will be a pyrrhic one. 
     “War is an instrument entirely wasteful toward righting wrongs. It multiplies loss, and yields a total disruption of society. It will bring the ruination of our economy as our industry is given totally over to the making of weapons of war. 
     “We must undertake a single task, a great task; refuse to kill, injure or cause suffering to our fellow man, and order our lives by the rule of love even if the Prefecture is bound by hate. You all know that evil can only be overcome by good. I’m not asking you to simply know it. You must live it.” 
     There is stunned silence throughout the assembly of the People of the Pen. Charles Degause sighs. He knows he has already lost before William Camp even begins to speak. 
     “Charles, Charles, Charles,” Camp sighs. “Beware lest in your anxiety to avoid war you obtain a master, to quote Demosthenes. 
     “You speak of the rule of love. I agree. I am to love my neighbor as myself. I do. I love in a sensible way, in the midst of a violent galaxy, at a tumultuous point in history. This is why I do not pursue the ruinous path of ''pacifism'' that you espouse!” 
     —there is a sharp intake of breath at the sound of the darkest word in Mesarthrim vocabulary— 
     “If I were to do this evil thing, I would be deserting the people who need our help. I would be shirking my duty! I would be without honor. 
     “Yes, war is an ugly thing, but not the most wretched. It is when moral and patriotic feeling has decayed to the point that nothing is worth war, that we see the most wretched thing. The person who has nothing worth fighting for, nothing that he cares for more than his own safety, is a miserable creature. You have no chance of being free unless better men make you free and keep you free with eternal vigilance. 
     “Through war, we will secure freedom and safety for our people. I must be granted the powers necessary to prosecute this war to its fullest extent.” 
     Unlike the congresses and leaderships of other nations, the People of the Pen do not applaud William Camp. They converse in hushed tones, and quickly come to a decision. Each enters his vote. 
     “For the duration of the war, Director Camp is granted supreme executive power, to bring destruction to the enemies of the United Centusi States, without mercy and without regret. So shall we uphold the honor of the Mesarthrim.”

     Hours later, Charles Degause still wanders alone in the wings of the assembly hall, silently lamenting the fate of his people.

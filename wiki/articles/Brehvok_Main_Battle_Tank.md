Brehvoks are the oldest of the Lorithos hovertanks still in use, but the most fearsome weapon in the arsenal of the [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]]. The Brehvok line began its service before the end of the [[Burning Wars]]. The squat profile, heavy arsenal and tough armor serves superbly against most enemies.

== Development ==

During the Burning Wars, the Sai-Ven Engineering Guild was absorbed by the advancing armies of [[Kian Huern]].  The chief engineers had significant ties to [[Torm Krelbine]].  Outraged at their audacity, Kian Huern had them executed, and told the remaining guild-smiths that they would share their masters' fates unless they built her a weapon to show their loyalty.

Serok Ven, son of the slain guildmaster, designed the Brehvok and built the first prototype in three days.  Even at the height of the war, Lorithos engineering was amazingly refined and driven with a passion that has never been rivalled by any race.

At the time, the Brehvok was armed with three heavy cannon turrets that operated on a black-powder derivative.  Later models began using the [[Bukrah]] cannon system.

== Modern Systems ==

[[Image:BrehvokMainBattleTank2.jpg|200px]]
Today, like all Lorithos hovertanks, the Brehvok is armed primarily with Bukrah cannons. The main power comes from a trio of heavy Bukrah turrets, the highest yield of all Lorithos ground cannons, and a pair of medium Bukrah fixed in the forward arc.

Brehvoks carry much thicker woven plates of Tzaiko Composite armor than their lighter counterparts. This slows the tank significantly--Karaks and Zieldars outpace them easily, so Brehvok crews are trained to work on their own. Like all Lorithos vehicles, the Brehvok is powered by the Hydrogen Engine, which generates power from a slow burn of hydrogen gas--converted from super-compressed hydrogen metal.

In the [[Burning Wars]], the Brehvok was one of the few conventional weapons--at first. In the last decade of the War, it was converted to a biological delivery system, spreading plagues and pestilence everywhere.

Brehvoks are deployed as tough core fighters. The Karaks and Zieldars harass, but the Brehvok destroys. It can take on Rigs, tanks, infantry and bugs alike.

The '''Krinim Cannon''' is the standard heavy weaponry on [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] warships.  An advanced form of High-Energy Linear Particle Accelerator, it is a significant improvement to the age-old idea of simply accelerating particles to high velocities to be used as projectiles.  The Krinim operates similarly to the smaller [[Jiorna Node]]s, but at much higher energy levels, and incorporates some techniques used by the [[Anderung]] in their own particle weapons.  

The drawback of this higher energy process is that Krinim Cannons cannot operate on smaller scales, and simply cease to function at lower power levels.  Because of this, Krinim Cannons are only mounted in large turrets on heavy warships, or in fixed positions on smaller warships, and are used strictly in anti-warship roles.

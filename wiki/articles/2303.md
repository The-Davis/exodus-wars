== Events of 2303 ==

===January===

*January 8 - [[Castor Mancini]], a member of the [[Ah Kin]] [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]] operations, manages to recover uncensored communications from the [[Sirius Cluster]]. The local [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]] operations now know the truth: all of the information [[William Duke]] has given the people of [[Ah Kin]] is a lie.
*January 10 - [[Chizuko Miyazawa]], a member of the [[Ah Kin]] [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] operations, manages to get a [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]] to share the information recovered by [[Castor Mancini|Mancini]].
*January 13 - Don [[Leon Ferri]], head of the [[Ah Kin]] [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]] operations, decides to turn all local resources towards blowing the cover of the [[Onyx Empire]].
*January 15 - [[Jarek Nighthawk]] works out a deal with the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] on [[Ah Kin]]. In exchange for starting a mafia war with the [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi]], the [[Onyx Empire]] will create a safe house for the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] drug labs.

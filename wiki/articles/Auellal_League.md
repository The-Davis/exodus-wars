===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Auellal League|History]]<hr>
The '''Auellal League''' was founded out of severe ideaological differences between certain [[Mesarthrim]] factions. The [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] wanted many more rights, most importantly the removal of the [[Germany (Terra)|German]] [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clan]] System. Only the charisma, statesmanship, and slow reforms of [[Andreas Tischler]] kept the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] from falling apart. When he died in [[2324]], the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] gave [[Eric Francis]] one chance to uphold the reforms, when he refused, the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] struck from their hidden bases on [[Caledon]]; and officially formed the Auellal League.  At the end of the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], they were forced to retreat from [[Mesar]] and permanently settle on [[Caledon]].

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of the Auellal League|Government]]<hr>
The Auellal League adopted a parliamentary system to govern their small world.  The intention was to create a foundation in which each of the [[:Category:Auellal City-States|Auellal City-States]] could receive a fair chance of promoting and enacting their individual piece of legislation. Most of all, a major importance was placed on keeping heritage and tradition away from politics.  The actual [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|Charter of Autonomy]] was written by [[Anthony Howe]], a lawyer from [[Holt]], who emigrated to [[Mesar]] during the [[Imperial Civil War]].

===Law===
Main Article: [[Law of the Auellal League|Law]]<hr>
When the Auellal League was formed the first, and most primary, concern was to remove the umbrella of the Church and [[Clan Lord]] from the legal system.

When [[Anthony Howe]] wrote The [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|Charter of Autonomy]], he intentionally left out all references to [[Clan Lords]] and religious institutions.  And, as stated in the [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|charter]], anything not mentioned in the [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|charter]] became the responsibility of the individual [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]].  No individual [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]] may pass a law that violates the [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|Charter of Autonomy]].

===Law Enforcement===
Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Auellal League|Law Enforcement]]<hr>
The Auellal League does not maintain a national Law Enforcement branch.  Each [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]] is required by the [[Auellal Charter of Autonomy|Charter of Autonomy]] to maintain its own judicial, law enforcement, and incarceration services.  The Auellal League does, however, maintain a national [[Auellal Law Enforcement Academy|training facility]], the [[Auellal Law Enforcement Academy|Gnos arm sgoil]], located in the [[Griffin Mountains|Gnos Caigeann]].

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Auellal League|Foreign Relations]]<hr>
The Auellal League is currently in a state of War with the [[United Centusi States]].  Additionally, the Auellal League is assisting the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] in their ongoing military activities against the [[United Centusi States]].  Most other governments currently do not recognize the Auellal League as a legitimate and autonomous [[Mesarthrim]] state.

===Military===
Main Article: [[Military of the Auellal League|Military]]<hr>
The Auellal League's Military is divided into two divisions:
*[[Auellal Armada| The Armada]]
*[[Auellal Guardians| The Guardians]]

The Auellal Military was almost entirely financed by outside contributors, including the [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova Family]], the [[Reigess Suverände]], and the [[AstroLift Company]].  The support came in many forms, including monetary support, training facilities, and antiquated or damaged vehicles and vessels.  In exchange, the Auellal League offered exclusive trading opportunities once the Auellal League had defeated the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  After the fall of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], the Auellal League was unable to make good on its promises.  So [[Steven Cue]] offered the only thing he could, favored trade status with the Auellal League, and the promise of repayment of the debt over time.

===Intelligence===
Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Auellal League|Intelligence]]<hr>
'''The [[Auellal Cryptology and Intelligence Center|Ealadh a' cuilidh]]''' was created out of the remains of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] cryptology centers.  Most of the Intelligence utilized by the Auellal League is gathered by the [[Auellal Casan Eirmis|Casan Eirmis]].  Additionally information is usually passed on by informants, or bought from other intelligence agencies.

The main purpose of the [[Auellal Cryptology and Intelligence Center|Ealadh a' cuilidh]] is cryptology, a duty in which they excel.  By using the [[Anderung]] [[Halrecht]] dialect and complicated cyphers, the [[Auellal Cryptology and Intelligence Center|Ealadh a' cuilidh]] has developed an almost perfect form of cryptology.

===Transportation===
Main Article: [[Transportation of the Auellal League|Transportation]]<hr>
Transportation within each [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]] is governed and maintained by the applicable laws within each [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]].  Spanning between [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-States]] are high speed mag train lines, and traffic lines.  The two [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-States]] connected by the lines are responsible for equal governing, enforcement, and maintenance of the lines.

The central authority on transportation has standards that must be kept for each and every line.  If the line does not meet these safety, maintenance, and quality standards, the violating [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-States]] will be held responsible until the standard is met.

Air transportation is regulated by the [[Auellal Armada|Armada]] and their air controllers.  Each [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]] maintains an starport, however traffic control is controlled centrally by the [[Auellal Armada|Armada]].  Commercial commodity and passenger lines are free to move about Auellal airspace, however are subject to the rules, regulations, and laws of the [[Auellal Armada|Armada]].

===Territory===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Auellal League|Territory]]<hr>
The Auellal have only managed to secure their single homeworld, [[Caledon]], located in the [[Cinaed System]].

===Economy===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Auellal League|Economy]]<hr>
The Auellal League have a very weak economy.  The entire government is still being propped up by third-party support.  There are no exports from [[Caledon]].  The internal economy is currently self-sustained with ample food sources and raw material sources.  With the war with the [[United Centusi States]] continuing, the Auellal League has had to import more and more raw materials. With no economic power to back up a national mint, the Auellal League uses the [[Ikronin]] [[Likvada]] as their standard currency.
 
===Demographics===
Main Article: [[Demographics of the Auellal League|Demographics]]<hr>
The Auellal League has a significant [[British Empire (Terra)|English, Scottish, Irish, and Welsh]] population.  There are also remnants of the [[France (Terra)|French]] and [[Spain (Terra)|Spanish]] cultures present.  There is also a significant number of [[Germany (Terra)|German]] [[Mesarthrim]] who chose to side against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. 

===Public Health===
Main Article: [[Public Health of the Auellal League|Public Health]]<hr>
Health care and general public health is excellent in the Auellal League.  

While the [[Auellal Guardians|Guardians]] were on [[Mesar]], fighting in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], [[Provost]] [[Steven Cue]] negotiated with the [[Interstellar Red Cross]] to provide medical aide to his soldiers.

After the war, the [[Interstellar Red Cross]] continued to assist the Auellal on [[Caledon]].  After several months, the [[Interstellar Red Cross]] constructed numerous hospitals all across [[Caledon]].  Day to day healthcare is now provided both by International and local Doctors operating out of the [[Interstellar Red Cross]]'s hospitals.

===Culture===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Auellal League|Culture]]<hr>
The culture of the Auellal League has many similarities to that of the [[United Centusi States]].  However, it is the contrast that drove them apart.  Ideas like true freedom of religion, representation, and the removal of absolute religious authority are some of the hallmarks of the Auellal League.

Most [[Mesarthrim]] on [[Caledon]] enjoy fine dining, and sports.  The most popular sports are Rugby and Ice Hockey.  There is also a large percentage of the population that actively engage in live theater and crafting.  The most common sentiment on [[Caledon]] is one of a euphoria, generally attributed to the feeling that there is finally a place where [[Mesarthrim]] can come to be completely free.

== Events of 529AD ==

The [[terraformation]] of [[Aedapose]] is completed, and colony ships leave the [[Ularen System]] bound for their new home.  Much of the preparation work is accomplished by the satellites remaining from the colony preparation of [[Drisonir]] and [[Nielstra]].

===Languages===

The spoken [[Gorhamut]] dialect is known as "[[Suhyka]]", and is a strange language.  No other species has ever been able to speak or understand it, not even their genetic creations.  Apparently, many of the sounds are out of the hearing range of humans, [[Volucris]], [[Anderung]], and even [[Ngrligru]].

To deal with their creations, the [[Gorhamut]] created the [[Anderung]] dialect, Halrecht.  This guttural, throaty language is easy to learn, but difficult to master.  [[Gorhamut]] speak very broken [[Ngrligru]] and [[Lorentien]], due to their lack of movable lips.  Any sound that requires lips, they mangle.

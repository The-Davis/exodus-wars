The '''Ceres Belt''' is the region of the [[Sol System]] located roughly between the orbits of the planets [[Mars]] and [[Jupiter]]. It is occupied by numerous irregularly shaped bodies called asteroids or minor planets. The asteroid belt is named for [[Ceres]], the largest dwarf planet in the region.

The '''Tempest War''' was an interstellar conflict fought predominatly between the [[United Centusi States]] and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].  The [[Auellal League]] was already at war with the Centusi States when the Tempest War began.  In [[2328]], the Auellal League and the Prefecture officially signed a strategic alliance.  In [[2330]] the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] declared war on the Centusi States after several [[Wormhole Gates]] had been destroyed.  The [[Comur Resistance]] first began in [[2329]], but was suppressed.  In [[2331]], the [[Comur Resistance]] reformed, changing their tactics to be more subversive.  In [[2334]], [[Arnold Stadt]] brought the remains of his [[United Centusi States Second Fleet|fleet]] to [[Comur]] and ignited a full fledged revolution.

== Background ==

In [[2323]] the [[Treaty of Memphis]] was signed, ending the [[Second Exodus War]].  In the treaty, the Prefecture gained three-quarters of the territory formerly controlled by the [[Onyx Empire]] as well as numerous new colonies.  Additionally, the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] were asked to relinquish territory they had secured during the war.  With the loss in territory, many Mesarthrim claimed foul play on the part of the Prefecture and the [[Alliance of Planets]].

Additionally, the Prefecture's economy received almost instantaneous stimulation and a significant boost, while the Mesarthrim economy was equally stifled.  [[Andreas Tischler]] had worked out a solution with the [[Auellal Political Party]], but he was killed before he was able to enact any reforms.  In [[2324]] the Auellal League attacked the Clans, demanding immediate reforms.  The [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] was mercifully quick, but left over 1.25 billion Mesarthrim dead.

In the aftermath of the Mesarthrim Civil War, the [[United Centusi States]] came to power under the leadership of [[William Camp]].  Camp pushed the Mesarthim to adopt a system of colonialism and mercantilism to rescue the destroyed Mesarthrim economy.  The Centusi States decided to reclaim the territory they lost to the Prefecture, and in April of [[2327]] they bombed [[Leskovik]] and started the Tempest War.


== 2327 ==

The Tempest War began in [[2327]] when elements of the United Centusi States Kriegsmarine obliterated all of [[Leskovik]]'s population centers, wiping out 90% of human life on the planet.

Shortly after the attack the Prefecture declared war on the United Centusi States, a move which the United Centusi States reciprocated.  William Camp claimed that the Prefecture was igniting aggression against an entire people while its de facto leadership was a group of "rogue admirals".

The Prefecture tried to end the war quickly by striking at [[Mesar]].  The assault failed, costing the Prefecture half of its Third Fleet of the Line.  Aside from the first strike by each side, the Tempest War's beginning was relatively uneventful.  The year ended with the [[First Tirshan Uprising]].


== 2328 ==

On January 1st, [[2328]], the war began in earnest.  The Auellal offered aid to the Prefecture and the two governments signed the [[Cinaed Peace Treaty]].

The United Centusi States cut their way into Prefecture territory in an aggressive blitzkrieg--committing two fleets to capturing the entire [[Laojiu District]].  The United Centusi States also launched several retaliatory strikes against the Auellal by capturing [[Iurin]] and bombing [[Caledon]] with nuclear weapons.  The blitzkrieg was a major success: in every engagement the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine routed the Prefecture vessels.

Later in 2328, a Mesarthrim task force was dispatched to [[Holt]] with orders to disable its industrial capibilities.  The fleet engaged the superior Prefecture defenses and routed them, then launched nuclear strikes against every major industrial center on the planet.  By the end of the Holt campaign, the United Centusi States had managed to neutralize the last of the Prefecture forces in the Laojiu District.

As 2328 began to draw to a close, William Camp saw a chance to secure a beachhead in the [[Tejat District]], and ordered the Kriegsmarine's Second Fleet to the [[Gamma Algedi System]].  The invasion was in trouble from the outset.  The invasion force was under-powered, and poorly supplied.  Several combat landings ended in fiascos and cost the Mesarthrim time and resources, and more importantly momentum.

== 2329 ==

By early [[2329]], the Prefecture had been able to rebuild some of Holt's infrastructure, and started to bring some of its industry back online. Infuriated with the ineffectiveness of his earlier strike against the planet, William Camp ordered that Holt be invaded and held against the Prefecture.  The United Centusi States forces were able to quickly roll over the Prefecture forces stationed there.  The Prefecture's strategic command were not expecting the Mesarthrim to capture a planet they had previously nuked, and had only left enough soldiers on the surface to assist in disaster relief and crowd control.  The troops on the ground only had light weapons and transport vehicles.  The force of nearly 60,000 Prefecture soldiers were allowed to surrender, and they were promptly shipped to a deep space prison camp. In one of the greatest tragedies of the war, the prison ship--a [[Mercury Class Transport]]--miscalculated its hyperspace jump and everyone aboard was killed.

In mid-2329, the Prefecture launched its most ruthless campaign in the war: [[Operation White-Eye]].  The [[Prefecture Legion Corps Army Hunters|Army Hunters]] were tasked with an apparently impossible mission: infiltrate Mesar and assassinate, sabotage, and terrorize any and all targets of opportunity.  [[Luna Black]] had Prefecture doctors cut the eyes out of Mesarthrim P.O.W.s and surgically implant them in her Army Hunters.  The Hunters were inserted into Mesarthrim space, usually by [[Nautilus Class Subspace Prowler]]s.  Operation White-Eye launched 2000 missions between 2329 and the end of the war.  Only a fraction of them were successful, but the effects on the Mesarthrim populace were noticeable.

Despite the tragic losses on Holt, the Prefecture and Auellal counter-attacked the United Centusi States' holdings on [[Pesht]] and [[Sindh]], and were able to rout the Mesarthrim fleet in orbit of Sindh.  The Prefecture landed a full Legion on Sindh.  The Auellal did not fare as well at Pesht, and the United Centusi States was able to withdraw a significant portion of its forces unmolested.  Even William Camp knew that Pesht and Sindh were lost, and he wanted to cut his losses and regain momentum in the sector.

As the occupying forces began to withdraw, the citizens of Pesht began to attack the fleeing Mesarthrim infantry.  During the firefight, an order was passed down the chain of command to strike the planet with Omega weapons.  The ordered was carried out, and the entire planet was destroyed, leaving only minute fragments.  The order was one of the worst break-downs in communication in the war, and one of the most disastrous.  Thousands of Mesarthrim troops were still on the ground when it happened. The bulk of the Auellal forces were lost, and the entire civilian population was killed.

The majority of the Mesarthrim forces survived the destruction of Pesht and were ordered to the [[Theta Algedi System]] to secure [[Tejat]] itself. The assault was suspiciously quick.  The Prefecture only offered token resistance.  Many important civilians had already been evacuated, and thousands more were armed with man-portable weapons for a guerilla resistance.  The United Centusi States was unaware of the ruse, and began establishing a major base on top of a proverbial hornet's nest.

== 2330 ==

After the successful capture of Tejat, the United Centusi States' fleet was reinforced and dispatched to secure the rest of the [[Tejat District]].  The [[Zeta Algedi System]] fell easily--most of the Prefecture forces that had been stationed there were still on [[Sindh]] in the neighboring [[Gamma Algedi System]].

When the Prefecture and Auellal attempted to re-take the Gamma Algedi System, they found an enormous debris field and a graveyard of derelict warship hulls.  The coalition feared that the United Centusi States had been annihilated by [[Volucris]] or some other unknown force. When the allied fleet probed the debris for any clues, they were ambushed by the Kriegsmarine hidden within--the field was a ruse.  The coalition fleet fell under a withering barrage of Blazer fire and was completely destroyed.  This fateful attack ended the life of [[Jason Bremen]], a veteran Prefecture commander.  Emboldened by their success in Gamma Algedi, the United Centusi States reinforced Sindh, but were unable to defeat the entrenched Prefecture Legion.

In the [[Omicron Carinae System]], [[Adelson]] was captured by the United Centusi States Second Fleet--[[Arnold Stadt]]'s command.  The Kriegsmarine Second Fleet managed to rout the Prefecture's vastly superior Fifth Fleet of the Line, led by [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]]. After the battle, [[Anthony Hadrian]] marked Stadt as the most dangerous Mesarthrim in the galaxy and offered an extensive bounty for proof of his death.

After Stadt's victory in Omicron Carinae, William Camp ordered the capture of the rest of the [[Ward District]].  The Kriegsmarine's Third Fleet was sent to the [[Xi Herculis System]] with orders to secure it.  The Third Fleet was woefully outgunned by the Prefecture and Auellal forces stationed there, but Prefecture strategic command ordered that the system be abandoned rather than risk a costly confrontation.  The Kriegsmarine's Fourth Fleet was dispatched to the [[Des System]], where they met a small but determined Prefecture fleet detachment that was evacuating all military assets from [[Des Wei]].  The evacuation mission failed, but managed to inflict serious losses on the Kriegsmarine before being captured.

As 2330 came to a close, the first of the Auellal League's re-tooled [[Haven Class Shipyard]]s completed a run of their Twilight Class Variable Configuration warships.  [[Oswald Voss]] took a small command of the new ships into battle in the [[Theta Algedi System]] against elements of the Kriegsmarine First Fleet.  The battle was an impressive skirmish.  While neither side took any actual losses, the United Centusi States took heavy damage and had to withdraw to [[Canopus]] for repairs.

The Auellal only intended to harass the Centusi forces at Theta Algedi and were not carrying any assault troops, but Oswald Voss could not deny the important strategic advantage of uncontested space superiority.  He sent an emergency [[Urokon]] transmission to the Prefecture and requested a Marine Expeditionary Force be sent to invade [[Tejat]].  The Marines didn't arrive until early [[2331]], but the armed citizens of Tejat started a successful uprising against the United Centusi States garrison, supported by Dancers and Twilight vessels in orbit.


== 2331 ==

In the opening months of [[2331]] the Tempest War looked even more grim.  Stadt took his second fleet to [[Des Arc]], where the defending Auellal fleet refused to surrender even after taking heavy losses and was annihilated.  The battle for the Des System marked the destruction of the last Auellal "Mongrel" vessels in service, and also marked the death of Provost [[Steven Cue]]'s daughter, [[Melissa Cue]].  With the destruction of their fleet, the Auellal's Armada was reduced to a total of eight vessels, the Twilight ships.

By late March the uprising on Tejat, bolstered by the arrival of the Prefecture Marines, forced the surrender of over 80,000 wounded and starving Myt'Kampfen.  Myt'Kampfen leaders ignored orders to detonate nuclear weapons across the planet and surrendered control of the dangerous weapons peacefully.  William Camp called them traitors and cowards, and sent a lone gunship to nuke his own men in the Prisoner of War camps.  The Auellal managed to intercept and destroy the gunship before it could accomplish its mission.

In early April, William Camp reinforced his troops in the [[Lyrae System]] in preparation for an invasion of the [[Udea District]].  Auellal spies intercepted the plans and sent word to the Prefecture.  A major offensive was launched to destroy the Centusi invasion fleet.  The Prefecture's First, Fourth, and Sixth Fleet of the Line were sent along with the few remaining Auellal Armada vessels and attacked the Kriegsmarine's Second, Third, and Support Fleets.  The battle was the largest and longest single sustained naval engagement of the war.  Over forty hours passed between the firing of the first shot to the retreat order.  A total of seventy ships were lost, and many more were damaged.  The Prefecture and Auellal failed to secure the Lyrae System and liberate [[Holt]], but caused so many Centusi losses that the invasion of the Ueda District was cancelled.

During the space combat in the Lyrae System, the Prefecture Marine Corps attempted to use its new combat dropships to land on Holt, accompanied by Auellal Pathfinders.  The mission was intended to spearhead the liberation campaign.  The Prefecture ships performed superbly, flawlessly landing their soldiers and providing excellent cover fire.  The Marines cut through the Mesarthrim forces thanks to close air support and a total lack of Mesarthrim space forces--until the coalition withdrew from the Lyrae System.  The Marines and Pathfinders offered to surrender, but the United Centusi States' troops fired on them.  When the Marines realized they would not be allowed to surrender, they fought to the last man.  Many civilian videos of were taken of Mesarthrim butchering wounded soldiers trying to surrender, but none made it past the Mesarthrim censor nets until after the war.  This was a fatal move for William Camp.  After hearing about the murder of surrendering troops, Arnold Stadt began to undermine Centusi command.

By May, it looked like the Prefecture was about to completely collapse.  Two out of their five Districts were completely controlled by the Mesarthrim, and two more were partially occupied.  Only the [[Ueda District]] was completely untouched, and only its constant production of weapons of war was keeping the Prefecture military from disintigrating under the ruthless assault.  With the [[Laojiu District]] lost to them, the civilian and military population were on short rations.  Oddly enough, the Mesarthrim managed to goad the Ikronin into joining the war when they started taking over Wormhole Gates in the systems they had captured.

In July the United Centusi States attacked the [[Mimosa System]], home to [[Tavou]], a world that many thought was unassailable.  The Mesarthrim were met by an [[Ikronin]] force. The Ikronin had dozens of Gatekeeper stations and hundreds of combat vessels, all freshly supplied and unmarred by the war.  The Kriegsmarine elements decided to break off the attack.  The United Centusi States attempted several other excursions into the [[Tavou District]], but each time the Ikronin were there waiting for them.  Unknown to William Camp and the other Centusi leaders, Arnold Stadt was feeding the Ikronin information. The Ikronin and their vast fleets of ships stimied every Mesarthrim incursion for the rest of the year, often without firing a shot, allowing the Prefecture and Auellal to begin to recover from the shock and horror of their outrageous losses.

== 2332 ==

In [[2332]], the United Centusi States struck a devastating blow to the Auellal League.  William Camp had been ignoring [[Caledon]], thinking that it was still defended by the Armada.  He did not realize that the Auellal had committed all of their forces in aiding the Prefecture, and had been relying on Caledon's relative worthlessness for security.  The planet was defended by a single Twilight vessel equipped as a Cruiser.  The vessel was disabled and captured by a Myt'Gaul attack team.  William Camp considered invading the planet, but in the end ordered its bombardment from orbit.

The Centusi victory over Caledon was overshadowed by a massive attack carried out by a combined Prefecture/Auellal fleet.  The Prefecture had spent half of the last year repairing and refitting enough ships to rebuild its crippled fleets.  The Auellal had also ramped up production in its Haven Class Shipyards, resulting in more Twilight ships being built.  The coalition attacked and liberated the [[Zeta Algedi System]], and destroyed a quarter of the Kriegsmarine Third Fleet in the process.

After successfully liberating the Zeta Algedi System, the combined armada turned to the [[Xi Herculis System]].  The Kriegsmarine First Fleet had been ordered by [[Urokon]] transmission to destroy every city and industrial center in the system, and to withdraw afterwards.  The coalition fleet managed to drive off the Kriegsmarine First Fleet before it could evacuate the Mesarthrim ground forces on [[Ward]] and [[Crofton]], but [[Bolan]] had already been evacuated and nuked.  Over the course of the several months, Prefecture Marines, Auellal Pathfinders and two Prefecture Legions took back Ward and Crofton inch by bloody inch.

In October, the Prefecture was able to reinforce Sindh, and finally liberated the planet from Mesarthrim occupation.

== 2333 ==

After launching several diversionary fly-bys of captured systems in [[2333]], the Prefecture finally converged on the [[Xi Herculis System]], along with Auellal and Ikronin allies.  Over the course of several months, both sides engaged in brutal planet-to-planet fighting similar to the [[Titan System]] campaign in the [[Second Exodus War]].  The worlds of the Xi Herculis System suffered extensive damage from weapons of mass destruction used by both sides.  By the end of the year, the system was liberated and in Prefecture hands.

== 2334 ==

In early [[2334]], elements of the Kriegsmarine's Fourth Fleet were withdrawing from the Xi Herculis System when they were captured by Ikronin forces.  Arnold Stadt secretly arranged for the several vessels to be returned to him at a secret rendezvous--ships with crews loyal to him.  Shortly afterwards, Stadt incited the [[Second Tirshan Uprising]], which lasted well into July.  The uprising tied up the Kriegsmarine First Fleet and compromised most of the Kriegsmarine Second Fleet, which was mostly loyal to Stadt.  Arnold Stadt, the Edge Brothers, and Bethany Tischler struck their signatures from the United Centusi States Constitution.  They officially dissolved the government and declared that the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] was the legitimate successor to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

In June the Prefecture's Fifth Fleet of the Line attacked Mesar.  Auellal and Ikronin forces were sent to reinforce the Prefecture, but did not arrive before the death of Admiral [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]] and the loss of the [[FDS Judicator|FDS ''Judicator'']].  The Kriegsmarine Warfleet avoided destruction in the fighting, continuing the war on their own.  Over a hundred [[Mercury Class Transport|Mercury Class Transports]] carrying five Prefecture Legions, as well as three full Prefecture Marine Expeditionary Forces began their landings.  The Prefecture invasion of Mesar was as bloody as could be expected: an entire Legion was destroyed within hours of landing. The second wave brought two more Prefecture Legions, the bulk of the Auellal ground forces, and six Ikronin divisions.

By August, the ground campaign on Mesar was turning into a bloodbath to rival the Mesarthrim Civil War.  Auellal forces on [[Novar]] withdrew to support the Mesar campaign, leaving Novar in the hands of the United Centusi States. After the fall of Homemakers Field, [[Steven Cue]] made an impassioned plea to all Mesarthrim to join the Mesarthrim Federation. His plea was a resounding success and a large number of Mesarthrim left the Centusi States.

In December, the Legion of the Phoenix and the Second Marine Expeditionary Force neared [[Stone Bridge]]. William Camp and his government abandoned the city leaving six Myt'Gaul Monks and Jün the First Acolyte behind to destroy the city. Arnold Stadt took the opportunity to lead a team of Laoch Saoi into the city. In the fierce battle for the capital building, Arnold Stadt was hit twice and medics were rushed in to save his life. In the end, the Monks and the First Acolyte were killed, taking thirteen Laoch Saoi with them. A week later, Laoch Saoi Ceud Ridir Aiden Paige, who had commanded the assault that killed the First Acolyte, was found crucified upside-down out side his barracks with all his skin flayed from his body - in the style of the Anderung.

With the capital of [[Mesar]] in the hands of the [[Tripartite Alliance]], the battle continued on [[Mesar#Arakkia|Arakkia]] and the Sonar Mountain Range.

On December 31st, the last day of the galactic year, one hundred and forty-four mid to high ranking officers from across the Tripartite Alliance were found crucified upside-down out side their barracks and command compounds with their skin flayed from their bodies.
Of the 144 killed, the targets were evenly distributed across the allies, each losing 48 officers. Appalled by these attacks, the Ikronin government removed their forces from the surface of Mesar. Nobody knows the identities of those responsible, but the Myt'Gaul were blamed in media outlets across the galaxy.

== 2335 ==
In January of [[2335]] Steven Cue sponsored a massive ceremony in [[Stone Bridge]], welcoming all Mesarthim to come to the [[Mesarthrim Federation]]. This effort proved successful, as many Mesarthim abandoned the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] and pledged their allegiance to the Mesarthrim Federation. The event cost the Centusi States so much support that several the fighting on the surface of [[Mesar]] was reduced to cave hunting in [[Mesar#Arakkia|Arakkia]] and fighting on and under the frozen plains of [[Mesar#Willim|Willim]].

The fighting also continued on [[Novar]], the [[Prefecture]] sent several Legions to support in the fighting. Over the course of the month, the Mesarthim overran several drop positions and the War Fleet in orbit forced the reinforcing fleet to withdraw. The battles began to heat up on Novar. In one of the retreats the [[United Centusi States Warfleet|War fleet]] forced the Prefecture navy to pull back and the Legion was order to lay down arms. After the soldiers of the legion surrendered, they were loaded onto a transport, and rather then be sent to a deepspace penal colony, they were sent directly into Novar's sun, Honis.

January ended with another 144 officers being crucified upside-down, flayed. This time is was 72 Prefecture officers, and 72 Mesarthrim.

February started on a low note when the Prefecture learned the fate of their lost Legion. Anthony Hadrian wanted to send a group of Centusi prisoners into the Centusi star. Stadt confronted Hadrian and convinced him such a move would jeopardize the Tripartite Alliance, and could cause an armed conflict between the Prefecture and the Mesarthrim Federation.

Mid February a combined Mesarthrim/Prefecture force captured [[Mesar#Willim|Willim]], the victory saw the capture of [[Elias Faust]], [[William Camp|Camp's]] right hand man. [[Chonge Kodwe]] learned from Faust of the capture and death of William Camp in [[2334]]. Stadt quietly admitted to Hadrian and Kodwe that Casan Eirmis forces captured Camp, he was interrogated and died during interrogation. His death had been kept secret, as his "inactivity" was bringing Mesarthrim converts over daily.

Finally, on February 23rd, Commandant Kleiss surrendered the forces still alive on [[Mesar#Arakkia|Arakkia]]. There was an initial hope that the Centusi forces across the galaxy would lay down their arms. However, neither [[Novar]], nor the [[Territory_of_the_Federated_Districts_of_the_Prefecture#Laojiu_District|Laojiu District]] laid down their arms. The Prefecture dispatched several legions to retake the Laojiu District.

On February 27th, the Prefecture moved on [[Gefangenenlager Acht]]. When the guards realized they were under attack they opened all the prison compartments to space, killing all the prisoners of war. The crew then swallowed explosives. The crew waited until they were onboard the Prefecture ships before they detonated the bombs. The destruction crippled one ship, and destroyed another. This situation was duplicated at five other deep space prisons, resulting in the deaths of most of the Prefecture and Mesarthrim Prisoners of War being held by the Centusi.

February ended with another 144 officers being crucified upside-down, flayed - 72 Prefecture officers, and 72 Mesarthrim officers.

The Prefecture was enraged at the continued terrorism they were falling under, Hadrian approached Stadt inquiring what it would take to end the war, and stop the Myt'Gaul attacks against their officers. The next day Stadt, Cue, and Bethany Tischler went to Novar, and called for a cease fire with Clan Lord Ada Brandt, leader of the Heer on Novar. Ada Brandt accepted the cease fire, as her forces had been terribly bloodied. The four sat down to negotiate the surrender of Novar. In the end, Ada agreed to have her forces lay down their arms. As the word passed through the ranks, there was some brief suicidal assaults were made by Centusi soldiers who either did not want to face the trials or did not want to simply give up.

As mid March came, Mesarthrim space settled, and the Prefecture and Mesarthrim governments came together began making arrangements for a lasting peace. There was a lot of hostilities initially as Centusi soldiers came home and had to face their former enemies.

The [[Treaty of Tavou]] was signed and the nations involved in the war began the long, slow healing process.

== Aftermath ==

When William Camp disappeared, it was because he was killed by Arnold Stadt.  Stadt's new [[Mesarthrim Federation]] seized control of the United Centusi States and forced a cease fire with the Prefecture, Auellal, and Ikronin.

The resulting cease fire was later formalized in the [[Treaty of Tavou]], which returned control of the systems in the [[Algedi Cluster]] and [[De Mairan Sector]] to the Prefecture.  The United Centusi States was rendered illegitimate by the remains of the TFVMS II council, and the Mesarthrim Federation took its place as the legitimate Mesarthrim government.  Additionally, the Mesarthrim Federation was given control of the entire [[Pelagrim Sector]].

Finally, the [[Magrinoch Trials]] were held, covering atrocities caused during both the [[Second Exodus War]] and the Tempest War.  The scandal which ended [[Talaris Roi|Talaris Roi's]] political career and resulted in [[Anthony Hadrian|Anthony Hadrian's]] disappearance tarnished the trials.  Twenty-seven officers on both sides were tried for executing Prisoners of War.  Of those twenty-seven, eighteen were sentenced to prison, and two were sentenced to death.

The Auellal League joined the Mesarthrim Federation.  In order to secure the peace, the Prefecture and Ikronin invited the Mesarthrim Federation to take the Auellal League's place in the [[Tripartite Alliance]] with them.  There was a brief fallout during the [[Magrinoch Trials]] when the Mesarthrim pushed to have Talaris Roi tried as an accomplice after the fact for Anthony Hadrian's war crimes, but after her impeachment the matter was dropped.  The [[Mesarthrim Federation]] renewed not only their commitment to the [[Tripartite Alliance]], but also to the [[Cinaed Accords]], allowing technology and information to freely flow between the three nations.

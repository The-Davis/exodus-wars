===Education===

Education of children is not formalized until they are twelve years old. Parents are expected to teach their children basic morality, civic responsibility, reading, writing, and mathematics by the time they reach this age. "Juvenile delinquency" is punished five times as harshly as adult offenders comitting the same crimes (with the obvious exception of death penalty crimes). Juveniles have no rights to privacy and are subject to being stopped and searched by local authorities, unless they are with their parents or guardians.

Once they reach twelve years of age, children may continue their education at home, enroll in a private school, enroll in a military academy, or attend a government science institution. The first two years of their formal education is to give them a broad scope of life, before becoming involved in a specialty.

By the time they reach adulthood at eighteen years, they are expected to be able to make a living for themselves, and have the opportunity to become full citizens of the [[Prefecture]], either by paying the fees, or by serving the [[Prefecture]] military.

Higher education, with the exception of medical schools and military specialty schools, is a strictly private affair. Colleges and trade schools offer training in a number of highly specialized fields. Most schools in the [[Prefecture]] do not believe in the classic "well-rounded education" philosophy. Approximately 70% of graduates are specialists.

===Sport===

Most people play or take an interest in sports. People in the [[Prefecture]] tend to be more violent and aggressive than earlier Terrans, and their taste in sports shows it. Extreme fighting competitions, martial arts, boxing, and similar forms are the most popular sports. Martial arts are no longer a one-on-one event, with teams of four competing in most Prefecture leagues.

Classic sports such as baseball and football are still quite popular, and teams in the Prefecture take part in league play with other Nations.

===Literature===

While literacy is practically required to live in the [[Prefecture]], and is required for citizenship, classic literature is only enjoyed by approximately 35% of the population. Reading and writing are a lost art for the most part, and literature enthusiasts usually purchase foreign books for their reading.

===Art===

There are no current popular artists in the [[Prefecture]]. Classic art such as painting and sculpture is rarely purchased or considered valuable within the Prefecture. The closest thing they have is architecture, which tends to follow a classic Roman style.

===Cuisine===

Food varies wildly from world to world in the [[Prefecture]]. [[Tavou]] is known for spicy, meat-filled dishes, while [[Laojiu]] is known for its incredibly balanced flavors and delicacies. Most residents of the [[Prefecture]] prefer to order delivery foods or eat at restaurants.

===Cinema===

Movies are very popular in the [[Prefecture]], but are hardly the cultural centerpiece of yesteryear. Actors are considered "blue collar" workers, and are only worth a few hundred thousand [[Peculiums]] per year.

===Music===

Music in the [[Prefecture]] usually centers around a snare drum beat, with a saxaphone or piano lead. Vocals are typically a solo or duet. The current popular genre is "jazz rock", and is the main type being written. Earlier genres still sell well.

===Holidays===

The [[Prefecture]] celebrates VT Day, which commemorates their winning the [[Imperial Civil War]] on [[Terra]], and Founding Day, which celebrates the ratification of the Constitution. Local populations celebrate religious holidays, and [[Rahn Industries]] (the largest employer in the Prefecture) has a twelve paid holidays, which are also observed by most other employers.

===Social Structure===

[[Prefecture]] culture shuns diversity and differences in its midst, but welcomes outsiders who are clearly defined as outsiders. A resident of the [[Prefecture]] who refuses to speak English and does not conform to societal norms would be ridiculed and shunned, but a foreign national who is merely visiting tends to receive respect and friendship.

Families in the [[Prefecture]] tend to be patriarchal "nuclear" families, with a married couple raising three or more children. The father is the major provider of income, while the mother is in charge of the house and rearing the children. While there are exceptions, they tend to be quiet minorities or complete outcasts.

The [[Prefecture]] rejected the ideals of "tollerance" and "equality" embraced by the [[United Earth Alliance]] in its heyday. The society in general believes that individuality is one thing, but difference is dangerous and alien and should only be welcomed at arm's length.

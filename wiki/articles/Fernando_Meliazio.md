''Fernando Idrios Meliazio'' was born in the Sinaloa region of Mexico, on [[Earth]]. He was the eldest son of an affluent political power broker, and eventually used his family influence to win the positition of [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. He had the misfortune to be in office during the [[Onyx Empire]]'s invasion of [[Earth]] in [[2315]], and was killed when [[Royal Imperial Army]] elements stormed the Presidential bunker. One of the great mysteries of the early [[Second Exodus War]] is how the [[Onyx Empire]] discovered the bunker's location. A new one was built for each new administration, and it was the most closely-guarded secret of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

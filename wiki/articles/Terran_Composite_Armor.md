Of all the technology changes from the Phoenix Project, armor is one of the most appreciated. Everything from infantry flack jackets to star-traveling warships uses armor.

Pre-Phoenix armor was old, dating back to 2080 when warships used titanium and similar alloys for armor. In the 20th and 21st century, metal worked well against weaponry that barely broke the sound barrier, but with modern weapons nearing relativistic velocity, metal doesn't help a bit.

Modern armor is composite, made of crystalline Boron-Nitride (BN) and solid Hydrogen-Boron, with small amounts of other materials mixed in. The strength and impact absorption capabilities are stronger than the old metal armors by a thousand-fold. The crystalline Boron-Nitride is fabricated into long strands a single molecule in width. Billions of these strands are woven into a sheet of armor. This primary sheet has incredible tensile strength. When the armor is struck by a projectile at high velocities, the projectile bounces off, and the inertial force is transferred to the armor and equally distributed throughout the mass of the armored object. The damage dealt by the equally-distributed inertial energy depends on the size of the target. 

Armored infantry will take much more damage than a massive warship. Boron-Nitride is chemically inert, making it non-reactive to acid. Behind the primary armor sheet, Hydrogen-Boron molecules bonded with non-valence electrons form a secondary sheet. Non-valence electron bonding is a thousand times denser than valence electron bonding, making the armor much more effective at stopping neutron weaponry. The Hydrogen-Boron sheet also acts as a first-defense insulator against ionic attacks. Finally, there is a thin sheet of armor with EMP-blocking reflectors. This third armor sheet is the thinnest, and is rarely struck by weapon attacks at all. Without it though, energy that is otherwise harmless could scramble delicate electronics and other components.

Specs on Boron-Nitride:
*Molecular Weight: 24.83
*Density: 22.7 g/cm3
*Dialectric Strength: 100 volts/mil
*Electrical Resistivity: 1.7x1013 ohm-cm
*Thermal Conductivity: 0.08 cal / (cm-sec-K)

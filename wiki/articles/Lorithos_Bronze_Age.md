The '''Lorithos Bronze Age''' was the dawn of [[Lorithos]] civilization.  After the [[Sundering]], the survivors managed to rebuild to metal smelting and domestication rather quickly compared to [[Earth]].

[[Hamur Huern]]'s dynasty rose during this age, and lasted into modern times.

The '''Kabila iku Gol'nde''' is the main tribal council government of the [[Ngrligru]] [[:Category:Races|species]].

==Aims==

A mysterious yet brutal race, the [[Ngrligru]] have long prided themselves with conquest. For thousands of years, the [[Ngrligru]] have killed, maimed, and conquered numerous planets.

[[Ngrligru]] are divided into multiple Kabila, each led by a "Kuu" who represents the tribe in the Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri, the [[Ngrligru]] tribal council.

==Membership==

All Kabilas have a seat on the tribal council of the Kabila Duka. The Kabilas are numerous, and vary in size from star-spanning empires like that of [[Kabila Jitu'nge]], to tiny single-world holdings like [[Kabila Mbwa'mwitu]].

[[Kabila Damu'kizuka]] is the second most-powerful Kabila, and worked together with [[Kabila Jitu'nge]] to overthrow [[Kabila Nyoka]] many years ago. Other powerful Kabilas, like [[Kabila Wingu'fira|Wingu'fira]], [[Kabila Nyota'piri|Nyota'piri]], and [[Kabila Almazi'pa|Almazi'pa]] have massive holdings in [[Ngrligru]] space, with worlds and resources rivalling entire human nations.

Some Kabilas only have a handful of worlds and no interest in galaxy-spanning power like their larger neighbors, though they frequently war among other similar-sized Kabilas for a better place in the pecking order. Strongest of these middle-weights is [[Kabila Kito'koho|Kito'koho]], followed closely by the [[Kabila Mwezi'paka|Mwezi'paka]]. Kabilas [[Kabila Nyundo|Nyundo]], [[Kabila Moto'mbega|Moto'mbega]], [[Kabila Kivuli'dubu|Kivuli'dubu]] and [[Kabila Moshi'chui|Moshi'chui]] also have respectable holdings of at least half a dozen worlds each.

The smallest Kabilas barely have more than a world or two to their names. The [[Kabila Jahanu'fasi|Jahanu'fasi]] have three, and are constantly fighting off the [[Kabila Barafu'jaha|Barafu'jaha]], who have two. The [[Kabila Theluji'nde|Theluji'nde]], [[Kabila Ukiwa'sana|Ukiwa'sana]] and [[Kabila Pua'joka|Pua'joka]] all have single worlds, and a few deep space colonies. Only lowly [[Kabila Mbwa'mwitu|Mbwa'mwitu]] is lower, with a single barely-inhabitable world to call their own.

===The Disgraced===

The [[Ngrligru]] of the Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri no longer speak of the last two Kabilas. [[Kabila Kimburu]] no longer dwells in the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. It was nearly destroyed in a war with the other tribes, and fled to the [[Orion Arm]] where it is now a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. [[Kabila Bweha]], Kimburu's sole ally, was completely wiped out in the same war.

==Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri==

The Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri is the name of the [[Ngrligru]] tribal council that meets whenever the tribes as a whole feel there is some threat, need, or pressing issue to discuss. Most tribes govern themselves without the influence of others, but certain issues are always discussed. [[First Contact]] and enslavement of a new race, for example, requires a meeting of the Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri.

Each tribe sends their [[Kuu]] to represent them at the meeting.

July 15, 2324

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With their entire military updated, the Prefecture has donated a massive sum of money to the two major Mesarthrim governments. The United Centusi States and the [[Auellal League]] have both received billions of Peculiums in defense hardware.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rahn Industries and Blazton Heavy Industries have been commissioned to create dozens of warships for both Mesarthrim governments. Several warship hulls designated for sale for the Prefecture were re-designated for the use of the United Centusi States.

'''Kenneth Russell'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

The Tavou Guard is trained and led by Brigadier ''Kenneth Russell'', the second-finest officer in the entire [[Prefecture Legion Corps]]. He was one of the best in the old [[Royal Imperial Army]], barely beaten out in early battles by the now legendary General [[Travis Carpenter]]. No one is better suited to command border armies than Kenneth Russell.

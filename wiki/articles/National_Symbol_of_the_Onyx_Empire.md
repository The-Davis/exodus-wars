The wolf was chosen as the symbol of the [[Onyx Empire]] by [[Lucius Black]]. He often said "I will be as a wolf among their sheep", when talking about his plans to overthrow the [[United Earth Alliance]].

[[Lucius Black]] chose the wolf's paw as the symbol to cover the [[Onyx Empire]]'s flag. He also briefly considered keeping wolves as pets in the Imperial Palace in [[Memphis]], but abandoned the idea because he felt it would be irritating to deal with the mess and he was not particularly fond of animals.

When he formed the [[Alphas]] and the [[Omegas]], [[Lucius Black]] gave them the wolf's head as their symbol and badge of office.

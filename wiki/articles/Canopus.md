'''Canopus''' is the third planet in the [[Zeta Algedi System]]. It shares a binary orbit with its sister planet, [[Anilam]].

== History ==

Canopus is an oddity among [[Prefecture]] worlds. Most are abandoned [[Anthronoris]] colonies pre-dating the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], repopulated by the burgeoning Prefecture populace. Others are former colonies of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Canopus is neither. It was once inhabited by an unknown alien species, which disappeared millenia before its discovery by the United Earth Alliance in [[2270]].

Today, Canopus is home to the Central Office of Military Intelligence, the intelligence agency of the [[Prefecture]].

The newer colonists on Canopus are encouraged and welcomed by the Prefecture. Cities of colonists have given rise to industry and farms. Only vehicles are in short order on Canopus, and are usually imported from [[Albanus]] or other large industrial centers.

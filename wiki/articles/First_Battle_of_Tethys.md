== Overview ==

Following the initial Imperial victories in the [[Titan System]], General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] made plans to disrupt the Imperial base on [[Cronus]].  Cronus shared a relatively close orbit with [[Tethys]], the 11th planet in the Titan System. Their orbits were sufficiently close for Alliance fighters and bombers to make regular attacks on Imperial fortifications of Cronus without the assistance or support of Alliance capital ships.

The Tethys Anchorage installation was established as a fighter base for the [[United Earth Alliance]].  It was dispatched to Tethys on January 4, [[2316]], shortly after [[Gordon Dannins]]' command swept the planet free of Imperial pickets.

=== Imperial Forces ===

The Onyx Empire did not have a permanent presence in orbit of Tethys, but had dispatched the carrier group under the command of the [[HMS Cougar|HMS ''Cougar'']] in order to watch for United Earth Alliance ship movements. The carrier itself was under the command of Commodore [[Santeri Steinns]], newly assigned to the ship and nervous to not repeat the disaster overseen by his predecessor the year before.

=== Alliance Forces ===

General-Commander Sanburn dispatched four ships--UEAS ''Haven'', UEAS ''Bainbridge'', UEAS ''Spite of War'', and UEAS ''Kitty Hawk''--the 8th Combat Squadron of the 17th Battle Group to sweep Tethys prior to the installation of Tethys Anchorage.  The commander of the 17th Battle Group, Admiral [[Gordon Dannins]], personally commanded the mission.

== Order of Battle ==

The UEAS <i>Kitty Hawk</i>'s crew reported contact with the HMS ''Cougar'' at 1300 hours, and the Alliance cruiser exchanged fire with several of the carrier's escorts before its allies came to the ''Kitty Hawk'''s aid.

The battle was very brief, and neither side suffered any losses.  Commodore Steinns withdrew almost immediately upon seeing the full scope of the Alliance forces.  Steinns had not been under orders to hold the planet, and was under strict instructions to report any United Earth Alliance ship movements.

== Aftermath ==

Tethys Anchorage was placed in a stable orbit by 1700 hours, and was fully operational by 2230 hours. The 8th Combat Squadron remained on station as defense, and six fighter squadrons were moved in to begin harassment operations against Cronus.

From January 4 until January 17, the United Earth Alliance managed to launch twelve bomber raids on Cronus, but none caused lasting damage or was longer than a few minutes.

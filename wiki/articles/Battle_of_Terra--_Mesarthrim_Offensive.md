August 7, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler's forces had the difficult and dangerous trip to Terra yesterday, arriving without incident--despite the ambush of Stukas the previous day. His four battle groups landed in New Zealand.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And hour after landing, the Crimson Tide, Black Dogs, Phantom and Banshee battle groups began a multi-pronged assault of the islands of the Pacific. Before the day was out, Uber General Tischler had taken every western Pacific island south of Okinawa. He believes that Japan will fall soon.

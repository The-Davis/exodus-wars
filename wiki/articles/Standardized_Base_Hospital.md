The hospital is essential in keeping soldiers in top fighting condition.  Aside from surgery and injuries, it also serves as a place for routine checkups and screening.

[[Image:StandardizedBaseHospital1.jpg]]

The hospital can house 28 patients in its two ICU wings.  Most patients are released the day they come in, even from massive injuries and blood loss, but complex procedures like viral scrubbing and limb regeneration takes a lot more time.

[[Image:StandardizedBaseHospital2.jpg]]

The rear of the building is for emergency surgery.  Up to six patients can be taken at once.  The equipment is complex enough for all but the most difficult procedures.  Only advanced facilities on Ueda and civilian medical science centers are more complex.

[[Image:StandardizedBaseHospital3.jpg]]

Base hospitals are installed on every garrison with troops.  Usually one is enough for a few thousand soldiers, but in some cases another hospital building will be installed.

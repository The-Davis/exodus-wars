==Year One==

Tariffs of Cruelty - 10/20/2325 - By James Davis

     Meronir's Republic has been a valuable customer of many Archadian goods since they made contact. Cybernetic and computer interface systems, refined exotic matter, and other extremely sophisticated technology are all used in Meroniri everyday life.
     The Alliance of Planets' trade laws forbid the sale of any weapons, drive systems, and power generation methods. The Meroniri believe in allowing each civilization to develop these fundamental technologies at their own pace. They themselves are exempt: as the paragon of scientific development, there is nothing they cannot make for themselves if they choose to do so.
     Without warning, the Meroniri sever ties with the United Archadian Republic. There is no mention of grievance or hostility, simply a refusal to purchase Archadian goods or reply to Archadian diplomatic envoys. Archadian freighters are politely, but firmly turned back from Meroniri ports, and from the ports of Meroniri protectorates.
     Diplomats and trade councils are met with smiling but uncompromising Meroniri delegates, and sent home empty-handed.
     Delegates from the Colonial Commonwealth, independent planets formerly a part of the United Earth Alliance and enjoying the military and police protection of the Meroniri, protest loudly at the sudden halt of Archadian goods coming to their worlds. While the "fundamental" technologies were always banned, the Commonwealth possesses sufficient power generation of their own to power Archadian anti-gravity systems and shield technologies. The protests of the Commonwealth delegation go unheard and ignored.


What Plague? - 10/22/2325 - By James Davis

     Two days after lodging official protests of the ban on Archadian goods, the Colonial Commonwealth finds itself on the receiving end of a ban of its own. The worlds of Kalmar and Bethija are both placed under "protective quarantine", enforced by the Space Police, when Meroniri health officials warn of a spread of the Conclave- engineered "black plague" unleashed by Jarek Nighthawk a year previously.
     The Commonwealth's capitol, Alpha Centauri, is not subject to the ban, but even officials from the Commonwealth are kept away from their own world, some at the point of a gun. A few medical teams from Alpha Centauri are allowed to land on Kalmar and Bethija, but when they try to leave later, they are turned back by Space Police cruisers. One ship refuses to turn back and is fired upon until it is disabled, an action which turns into tragedy when a Space Police Officer misses his target and breeches the hull, killing several people aboard. Most of the medics are returned to the surface for treatment for vacuum and burn injuries, but survive.


No Apology - 11/7/2325 - By James Davis

     With neither explanation nor apology, the Meroniri lift the ban on travel to Kalmar and Bethija, withdrawing the Space Police blockade.
     Delegations from Alpha Centauri are met by the Meroniri Minister of Health, Caspa Altani, who simply dismisses all concerns and claims of the Colonial Commonwealth.


I Can Serve No Longer - 11/9/2325 - By James Davis

     Two days after the controversial quarantine of Kalmar and Bethija, one of the veterans of Meronir's Republic and one of the most public government figures--Minister of Defense Derri Meninnon--resigns from public office. Typically when an official of Meninnon's caliber resigns, he is seen off with speeches, parties and great fanfare.
     Meninnon's retirement is quiet, muted, almost sinister in its silence. He is not seen again in public, and only a brief video recording of his announcement is ever seen.


Busted! - 12/19/2325 - By James Davis

    The Alliance of Planets Space Police is charged with regulating border and commerce laws. According to the Grand Charter of the Alliance, the Space Police's jurisdiction is all of Alliance and international space, where they stop smuggling of contraband, check the stream of human traffic from one government to another for wanted criminals, and deal with international piracy--a rarity these days.
    Contraband varies from government to government. Only power generation, weapons, and drive technologies are expressly forbidden by the Charter itself, but certain items are banned in every government. Most narcotics and mind-altering agents are universally banned. Some things are banned only in one or two governments, and considered a strange ban by others. The United Centusi States, for example, forbids the sale of non-Christian religious material.

    Outsiders do not respect or observe the Alliance of Planets bans. The Nakamura family of Sirius owns enormous pharmaceutical conglomerates which do business throughout the galaxy, all of which are considered legitimate businesses. The demand for contraband narcotics is very high, and their shadier interests meet the demand. Rather than risk a link between their pharmaceuticals and their illegal drug labs, the Nakamura set up shadow labs on rogue freighters, space stations, and other unscrupulous locations.

     Today, the Space Police located and destroyed a drug lab in far orbit of Alpha Centauri in Colonial Commonwealth space. Dozens of Commonwealth and Sirius citizens, criminals by Alliance standards, are arrested under Alliance law. The Commonwealth and the Nakamura fume silently, but their protests are silenced when they realize that the Space Police have acted outside their jurisdiction with the guns of Meroniri warships backing them up.


The Council of Independent Nations - 12/26/2325 - By James Davis

     When great events and grand conspiracies are at hand, one expects to see cloaks, shadows, and daggers. The sight of a corporate boardroom does not conjure the thoughts of dark deeds.
     The boardroom is the lair of Roger Morris, a cunning businessman who has managed to carve a niche for himself in galactic business the face of the overwhelming monopolies held by the Icronian Consortium. The Oronus Coalition, his brainchild, has risen from the ashes of a failed Earth mining colony to become one of the premium heavy material suppliers in the galaxy. Since its inception, Oronus has had a steady customer in the form of the Prefecture--owner of a powerful war machine that grows daily, feeding on the supplies Oronus and others have to offer.
     All that changed when, for shady reasons known only to themselves, the Space Police decided to declare the sales of Oronus goods a violation of the Alliance Grand Charter--despite the fact that the Oronus Coalition never signed the Charter and never joined the Alliance. Goods prepared to go to the Prefecture were seized with only a pittance in reparations, and instead sold to the Mesarthrim at market prices. The only answer to Morris' angry complaints was the addition of a Meroniri warship to the Space Police blockade.

     Now this has changed. Since the meeting on Reigess, the independent factions have formed a plan to end the superiority of the Alliance. A new treaty, the Articles of Independence, has been signed to form the Council of Independent Nations.
     The Oronus Coalition, the Reigess Sovereignty, the Colonial Commonwealth, and the Kalidasa Planetary Authority all signed on early, but the coup today was getting real muscle to back them up. The United Archadian Republics only recently joined the galactic community, but have already earned themselves a fearsome reputation for having powerful technology and incredibly effective armed forces. Many believe they could go toe to toe with the Meroniri and win in a fair fight. Today, the United Archadian Republics signed on as a member of the Council of Independent Nations, shocking the Alliance of Planets and the galactic community at large.

     The first order of business was outlawing all Space Police from exercising powers of law enforcement in Council member space without a warrant signed by the Council of Independent Nations. This writ was first ignored, but later complied with when a coalition of warships led by the Archadians surrounded the Space Police blockade of Oronus and politely told them to leave.



Archadian Membership - 12/26/2325 - By James Davis

     "Hold the presses!", the shout would have been only a few centuries ago. Today it was a scramble to update live news feeds. In a political move that shocked the entirety of Human Space, the United Archadian Republics signed on a member of the newly-formed Council of Independent Nations. Alliance officials, until this point still courting the powerful Republics for membership, have expressed surprise over the news. At the time, it was still hoped that the powerful newcomer to the galactic stage could help strengthen the shaken Alliance, still recovering from the shock of a war between its own Member States.

     The responses of some member states have been negative. When contacted about this development, the office of Dirken Huern expressed "great disappointment" that the negotiations with the Archadians had failed to bring about Alliance Membership, as the Archadian Republics would have "been a substantial addition to the stability and influence of the Alliance of Planets."

     More critical were the comments made by Darel Fusanla's office, which stated that the Meroniri saw this action as "an attempt to escalate the progression towards a bipolar environment amongst Human governments. It comes in clear defiance of the principles of peace and equality that the Alliance was founded upon. They have rejected the Alliance, and thus rejected everything we have worked to hard to create."

     However, not all have taken such a view. "We all choose our own path towards our goals, and different peoples find different ways. The people of those Republics have made their choice," said Talaris Roi, Interim Consul of the Prefecture.

     Outside of the Alliance, most place the cause of the move upon the Alliance itself, and its emphatic attempts to enforce its will on non-Alliance members, either through indirect regulation, or through direct policing and enforcement. Upon hearing of the news, Governor Yosyp Golovnya of Bethija pointed out that, "It is now apparent that even great powers have begun to feel the heavy hand of the Alliance. We welcome our friends, the Archadians, to the Council."

     Presidente Evaristo Silveira of the Kalidasan Planetary Authority noted that the membership of the Archadians has "given us the means by which we can finally enforce our own sovereignty and independence from the Alliance Space Police. It will also open up trade opportunities that were no available to us when the Icronians and Meroniri were the ones dictating our trade policies."

     A brief press release by the Kribasis Mitara, the parliamentary body of the Republics, cited "economic, social, and idealistic" differences between the Archadians and the Alliance policies, and expressed "great [happiness] regarding the new alliance with the other independent nations of the galaxy." No members of the Kribasis Mitara were available for comment.

     No matter how one views the news, it is the biggest shock to the political arena since the Centusi States were cast out of the Alliance and the war with the Prefecture began. The full effects of this decision will not be known for some time, but political scientists, economists, and diplomats are already beginning to analyze the possible outcomes. Some see a happier Human sector, where the lesser nations band together to create their own large economic free trade zone and political bloc, which could possibly coexist alongside the Alliance of Planets. Others are taking a much more grim view, saying that this could possibly pull other nations in the Terran Sector away from the Alliance of Planets.

==Year Two==

No Compromise - 2/4/2326 - By James Davis

     Darel Fusanla meets with Dirken Huern in private, and spends several hours discussing the possibility of a treaty between the Council of Independent Nations and the Alliance of Planets. Fusanla scoffs at the notion repeatedly, shooting down every possibility and perk of the plans, and in the end flatly refuses to support any action regarding the Council.
     Despite the lack of support from Fusanla, Dirken Huern calls a meeting of the Alliance High Council. The Icronians and Prefecture main representatives are unavailable, and so they offer nothing to the debate. Reigen representatives are called to testify on behalf of the Independents.
     The meeting goes badly, and ends after Darel Fusanla goes into a shouting tirade against the Reigens, and storms out of the council chambers.


No Deal - 5/19/2326 - By James Davis

     Relations between the Council of Independent Nations and the Alliance of Planets has worsened to a point where they are nearly at war with one another. The Lorithos attempt to open talks for open trade with the Council worlds, a vote which the Meroniri block. Any ship from a Council world entering an Alliance world is subject to search and seizure, as well as crippling tariff levels.


This Outrage Shall Not Stand - 9/15/2326 - By James Davis

     Tensions between the Alliance of Planets and the Council of Independent Nations have soared. Cargos of goods from Alliance space have been seized by Council worlds, and vice versa. Merchant crews from either side have been detained on frivolous charges at ports. Military leaders speak of police action or all-out war.
     In the midst of the chaos, the Alliance High Council has been bickering endlessly. Dirken Huern of the Lorithos has been pressuring the High Council to normalize relations with the Independent Nation worlds, and Darel Fusanla of the Meroniri has been frustrating his attempts at every turn.
     Now Fusanla has brought a new vote to the table. Claiming that interaction between the Alliance and Council is leading to war, he proposes a ban on trade between them, and puts it to a vote. With the Icronians and Prefecture boycotting the High Council meetings for their refusal to help fight the war against the Mesarthrim, only the Lorithos, Titanians, Kimburuns and Meroniri are present to vote. The Kimburuns and Titanians both surprise Dirken Huern by abstaining.
     Unknown to Huern, Fusanla has pressured both of the tiny Member States into abstaining. While the Lorithos vote against the ban, and the Meroniri vote for it, the Meroniri force the issue by using their "founding member" status to cast a tiebreaker vote. The outraged Dirken Huern storms out of the council chambers, vowing to never return so long as Fusanla behaves in such a tyrannical fashion. Fusanla, outraged at the insult, refuses to allow Huern access to a Meroniri vessel for the trip home. Huern must instead wait months for a Lorithos vessel to make the arduously long hyperspace journey, and finds himself effectively under house arrest until they arrive.



==Year three:==

Meroniri stability was seriously called into question when five of their ships of the line disappeared without a trace in a single day. Probes into the disappearance are hampered by government red tape, so Rox Augustin, one of their veteran combat captains, begins investigations of his own.
*1/17/2327 - Creep the Paths That Giants Walked

With the Alliance of Planets' stability in question, and environmental aid to Lorithan beginning to falter, the Lorithos launched one of their most ambitious projects since the creation of the Hightowers--a concentrated effort to find a suitable colony for a Diaspora Colony--total planetary evacuation.

The Alliance of Planets' relations with outside factions completely fails when the Meroniri opt to completely sever ties with all members of the Council of Independent Nations. Embassies are shut down and diplomats are forced out of Meroniri space.

In retaliation for the outrageous Meroniri actions, Dirken Huern opts to abandon the Alliance of Planets, and is nearly murdered as a result. Only the watchful agents of Mait Onega keep him from being assassinated by the frantic civilian population.

Rosh Arufin's expeditionary probe begins to make some disturbing discoveries into the lost starships on the border. He decides to take his ship into the perilous Teeth of Orion, an area full of dangers most of his young crew know nothing about.

Rosh Arufin's mission into the Teeth of Orion has uncovered the truth: the Ngrligru are not behind the recent destruction of Meroniri patrol vessels. In fact, the Ngrligru are suffering from serious losses of their own. Arufin meets with an Ngrligru Kuu, and learns of the Fleet of the Dead.
*8/19/2327 - Ghostly Echoes of Ancient Powers

The Lorithos' deep range exploration vessel has made many new discoveries, including several suitable worlds to call home. Dirken Huern's popularity, once so low that assassination attempts were made daily, now soars to new heights. Preparations are made to move the population to New Lorithan, when the Tehniat makes an incredible discovery.
*9/19/2327 - The Stellar Catapult

Rosh Arufin returns to Sepulare with the truth behind the disappearances, only to be met by cold reality: Darel Fusanla already knew. The secret behind the shift in Meroniri society comes to light with terrifying alacrity as sweeping changes are made to the Terinasi. Augustin finds himself on the run from his own government.


==Year Four==

The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] begins to show some serious outward signs of internal political change when nearly a third of its veteran military brass resigns, succumbs to sudden disease, or dies in sudden accidents on border patrol inspections.

Many Meroniri military veterans began to take notice of the changes to the Terinasi when the order for Rox Augustin's capture came down. Many began to question orders. Hundreds of veteran captains resigned in protest, and many ranking admirals were given dishonorable discharges. In less than a month nearly a third of the Meroniri officers were gone; many from sudden death by "disease or other unknown causes", and many from unprecedented "accidents" on deep space patrol ships.


Rosh Arufin's ship is on the run, but not truly abandoned. The Meroniri Shadow Minister has been in contact, and gives Arufin a vital mission to carry out: find enough evidence to get Fusala removed from office.

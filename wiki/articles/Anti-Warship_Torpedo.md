'''Anti-Warship Torpedos''' are weapons typically fitted on [[:Category:Warships|warships]], but also used on [[:Category:Bombers|bombers]] and even space stations or static weapon platforms.  They carry heavy payloads, and are designed to slip past ships' [[Close-In Weapon System]]s and shields in order to deliver their destructive power.

Forward-firing torpedos can be specially equipped as Kinetic Penetrators, or "Breechers."  They use the momentum of the firing ship to gain extra speed and destructive power, and are one of the few torpedo or missile technologies capable of dependably breeching gravity shield systems.

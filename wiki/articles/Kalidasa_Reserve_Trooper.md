While the core of the KSF is the Security Trooper, there is also a large number of Reserve Troopers in support positions in the KSF.  These soldiers are not the front-line fighters, but instead carry out most of the support, policing, and civilian-related duties.  They are volunteer soldiers that serve shorter tours of duty than Security Troopers, and rarely see combat. 

As second line troops, Reserve Troopers are not outfitted with powered armor.  They instead wear the standard Kalidasan military jacket, to keep warm in the cool environments on Kalidasa, and a body armor vest under that, but no heavy armor. 

Reserve Troopers are issued a smaller utility pack than standard Security Troopers.  It holds standard communications equipment, but has fewer long-term combat equipment, such as high explosives, entrenchment tools, and orbital homing beacons.  

[[Image:KalidasaReserveTrooper.jpg]]

The KSF Reserve Trooper's standard issue weapon is the Kalidasan-made Infantry Rifle, Model 2325, or IR-25--the same rifle as the standard Security Trooper.  However, except in special circumstances where Reserve Troopers would be required to perform in combat roles, they rarely carry specialized rounds, such as breechers and super-sonics. 

The KSF employs Reserve Troopers throughout their planetary garrisons, usually as heavier supplements to local police forces.  They are a common site on Kalidasa.  Most security checkpoints, such as at spaceports, major commerical centers, and other important locations, are handled by Reserve troopers.  Civilians see them often, but rarely mind the additional security.  While they are well trained for their roles as police forces, they are poor combat troops and thus would not usually be employed in such situations except in some urban defense operations.

'''Charles de Gaulle'''
*Born: November 22, [[1890]]
*Died: June 4, [[1958]]
*Place of Birth: [[France (Terra)|France]], [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: Algeria, [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: French Republic

''Charles de Gaulle'' (born November 22, [[1890]]) was a [[France (Terra)|French]] military leader and statesman.

Prior to [[World War II]], he was an armored warfare tactician and an advocate of the concentrated use of armoured and aviation forces. During [[World War II]], he reached the rank of brigadier-general and then became the leader of the Free [[France (Terra)|French]] government-in-exile. Between [[1944]] and [[1946]], following the liberation of France from German occupation, he was head of the [[France (Terra)|French]] Provisional Government.

de Gaulle was reinstated as the head of the [[France (Terra)|French]] Provisional Government after the Fourth Republic failed. de Gaulle and one his son, [[Phillipe de Gaulle]] wrote the Constitution of the Fifth Republic.

Charles de Gaulle was assassinated by Muslim separatists during a state visit to the war-torn Algeria province on June 4, [[1958]]. de Gaulle's death was the end of talks of ending the Algeria war, and fired the French into eventually crushing the Algerian Muslim population and driving them out of the province.

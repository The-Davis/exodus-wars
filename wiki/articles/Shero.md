The '''Shero''' is the national currency of the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]]. It was established in ancient history by the [[Dawn Emperor]] as a national currency.

== History ==

The [[Dawn Emperor]] established the Shero standard of currency at the dawn of the united [[Lorithos]] government, thousands of years ago. The Shero is minted from electrum, a gold and silver alloy. The Shero was minted in one, five, and twenty Shero denominations. Since the death of [[Talire Huern]], no new Shero have been minted, though [[Dirken Huern]] has expressed interest in reviving the currency. Since [[First Contact]], the [[Lorithos]] have adopted the [[Ikronin]] [[Likvada]] for most transactions.

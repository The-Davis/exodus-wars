== Events of 2326 ==

Almost immediately afterward the United Centusi States will be formed, on January 1, 2326.

===January===
*January 1 - Official formation of the [[United Centusi States]] as a legitimate government. "Trilateral Packet" sent to all [[:Category:Nations|nations]] as the first step in diplomatic ties.

===February===
Political tensions in the galaxy have eased slightly after the formation of the [[Council of Independent Nations]], but now as the [[Rikaz Lorithos]] attempt to normalize relations between the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] and the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]], they are met with trouble from the Meroniri, who boycott an attempt at creating a friendly non-aligned status for the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]].
*February 4 - No Compromise

===March===

===April===

===May===
A second attempt at normalizing relations between the [[Alliance of Planets]] and [[Council of Independent Nations]] is brought to the [[Alliance of Planets High Council|Alliance High Council]]. Instead of offering a friendly non-aligned status, [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] attempts to establish a neutral trade agreement with the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]], but once again are vetoed by the [[Meroniri]].
*May 19 - No Deal

===June===

===July===

===August===

===September===
The [[Alliance of Planets High Council|Alliance High Council]] takes a new vote, this time led by the [[Meroniri]], to ban trade between the [[Alliance of Planets]] and the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Outraged, the [[Rikaz Lorithos]] muster the votes to strike down the ban, then walk out of the [[Alliance of Planets High Council|High Council]] chambers, promising to boycott all future meetings until relations with the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] are normalized.
*September 15 - This Outrage Shall Not Stand

===October===
*October 7 - Beginning of the standoff between [[Colonial Commonwealth]] and [[Meroniri Terinasi]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] in the [[Oronus System]], part of the series of events later known as the [[Oronus Incident]].
*October 11 - [[Taviridis Somarchada]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] drive off the blockade of the [[Oronus System]]. This marks the end of the [[Oronus Incident]].

===November===
*November 16 - [[Council of Independent Nations]] sovereignty-patrols drive off all blockades of their worlds.

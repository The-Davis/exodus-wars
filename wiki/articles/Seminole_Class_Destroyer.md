=== Overview ===

The Prefecture Galactic Navy learned quickly from the disastrous lessons of the war with the Mesarthrim and their tangles with the Volucris.  Light frigates simply didn't have the defenses to fend off Volucris suicide attacks.

Cruisers, while well-defended, did not enough firepower to punch through Mesarthrim defenses.  No Prefecture warships could make two consecutive jumps with their Kovalski-Fahr drives.  The Admirals began the Norfolk Project to deal with these gaping flaws in their fleet.

=== Capabilities ===

The Seminole is the first result of the Norfolk Project.  It's barely larger than a frigate, but easily as well-defended as a cruiser.  It's updated armament puts the pre-Norfolk versions of the mighty Avenger to shame.

Once reserved exclusively for flagships, the extraordinarily-powerful Relativistic Mass Driver has been scaled down in size, while losing none of its functionality or power.  Two are mounted in the front of the vessel, giving the Seminole a devastating punch.

Hellguns were once extremely expensive, and couldn't be used on anything smaller than a medium cruiser.  The Norfolk Project spared no expense in the research and development process, and managed to bring the cost, size and mass of a Hellgun to less than that of a Naval Autocannon, rendering the less powerful weapons completely obsolete.  The Seminole carries six of these deadly weapons, giving it the equivalent firepower of three pre-refit Avengers.

The ubiquitous Neutron Cannon is still used for point defense on the Seminole, and the weapon did not receive any significant upgrades.  It's targeting and tracking capacity is another matter.  Several computer cores were salvaged from the Battle of Icronia, each containing a working Prism gestalt AI.  While the principles that allow these new AI programs to work are still not entirely understood, the Seminole features a computer core designed to give an AI a lot more leeway in controlling the ship.  These new Osceola-series AIs perform exceedingly well, and can group together with efficiency that puts the Prism gestalts at the Battle of Kronheim to shame.

Aside from its point defenses, the Seminole has nothing remarkable in its defensive systems.  The usual array of gradient gravity shields, magnetic deflector screens, and plasma shields are all in effect.  Interior bulkheads and compartments have been designed to make hull breeches much more difficult, and less damaging to the ship as a whole.  Very small areas can be completely sealed off to prevent air loss or to isolate hazardous materials.

Special considerations had to be given to hazardous materials.  While the advancements in weaponry it produced were useful, the main focus of the Norfolk Project was power generation and storage.  Kovalski-Fahr drives couldn't hold enough power to make more than one jump in a forty-hour period, which stalled fleet deployments and response times.  Something had to be done.

The answer was antimatter.  While it was the toy of Meroniri engineers and scientists for generations, Terrans could never find enough antimatter to experiment with, let alone use as a power source.  Since its inception, the Alliance of Planets had strict trade laws forbidding the sale of weapons and power sources across borders.  An answer came when the Prefecture began trade with the Reigess Sovereignty.

The Reigens--an off-shoot of the Ikronin--were well-known in galactic circles as traders and explorers for centuries.  Their tiny size kept them from qualifying for Alliance membership.  Their presence in the galaxy was miniscule, until the discovery of antimatter sources within their territory.

The Prefecture was quick to react to the news, and bartered an extremely lucrative deal with the Reigens for copious amounts of antimatter.  The trade agreements were conducted rather quietly, and earned some contempt from the Meroniris, who did not like their monopoly on antimatter toppled.  Nonetheless, the Norfolk Project had all the antimatter it could use, and the Seminole was the first warship to be equipped with an antimatter reactor.

The technology for antimatter containment and control is still primitive, keeping the Prefecture from using it to power an entire ship.  Instead, it is used to instantly charge up a Kovalski-Fahr drive after a single jump.  After one charge, the antimatter reactor control protocols require it to be powered down, vented into space, and diagnosed before bringing it up again.  This process takes twenty minutes, but engineers believe that in a few short years they will be able to run the antimatter reactor non-stop, allowing Prefecture ships to make unlimited jumps--perhaps even to power a Tesseract.

=== Deployment ===

The FDS ''Seminole Wind'', the FDS ''Narcoosee'', and the FDS ''Kewetanee'' were the first ships in the Seminole Class to finish their shakedown tests, and they did it in a trial by fire during the Mesarthrim siege of Albanus.  The three ships returned from their shakedown cruise to meet the Mesarthrim head-on, and performed flawlessly.

The Prefecture's new shipyard system has moved away from Albanus.  The Galactic Navy now prefers to avoid keeping its eggs in one basket, and has shipyards scattered around Prefecture space in deep-space locations.  It takes longer for each individual shipyard to build a warship, but the sheer number being built at the same time helps.  The Galactic Navy expects to replace its entire frigate complement by mid-2326.

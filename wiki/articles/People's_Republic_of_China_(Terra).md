The '''People's Republic of China''', commonly known as '''China''', is the largest country in East Asia. With over 1.6 billion people, it has one of the largest populations of any country in the world.

The [[Communist]] Party of China has led the People's Republic of China under a single-party system since the state's establishment in [[1949]]. The defeated the Nationalists during the [[Chinese Civil War]], cutting off their retreat to Taiwan and wiping them out.

Due to its vast population, its rapidly growing economy, its large research and development investments and military spending, its status as a declared nuclear weapons state, and other capabilities, the People's Republic of China is considered one of the superpowers of [[Terra]].

China was a member of the [[Warsaw Pact]] and was one of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]]'s strongest allies in the [[Cold War]].

China was dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] on March 11, [[2011]].

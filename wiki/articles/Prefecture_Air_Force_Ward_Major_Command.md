Prefecture Air Force
 
Ward Air Command

The Ward Air Command is garrisoned on Bolan, a truly hellish world of hard radiation and fiery temperatures.  The aerospace pilots hate it, and frequently petition to have the headquarters moved.  They are overruled every time by Waylan Brown.

Lieutenant General Waylan Brown is hard as nails.  He believes in constantly training in the worst possible space conditions, and Bolan qualifies.  He's never lost a fighter of his own.

Commanding Officer: Lieutenant General Waylan Brown

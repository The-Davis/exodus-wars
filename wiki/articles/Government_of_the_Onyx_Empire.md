The Government of the [[Onyx Empire]] was a totalitarian dictatorship. At any time, the Emperor could issue a decree, making any contradicting law null and void.

[[Lucius Black]] recognized the need for delegating, and created the position of Planetary Governor for each of the worlds, with the authority to issue governatorial decrees. The Governors' decisions were frequently checked by the Emperor, and if he disagreed with their decision, a Governor was often immediately replaced.

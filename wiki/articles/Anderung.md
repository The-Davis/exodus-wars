The '''Anderung''' (''xenopetra morphous''), originally known as the "Serthra" by their [[Gorhamut]] masters, were the first artificially-created lifeform designed by the [[Gorhamut]]. They were "uplifted" from a small, rather harmless silicon-based life form on the [[Gorhamut]] homeworld.

The Anderung were once noble protectors of their masters and their allies, but after the sudden and violent change in the [[Ngrligru]] caused by the [[Njaa]], the Anderung suffered from an almost universal case of Post Traumatic Stress Disorder. Since then, they were a broken race, wandering the galaxy in search of their masters.

===Appearance===

The typical resting Anderung is an amorphous creature, about 250kg in weight and roughly spherical in shape, about two meters in diameter. The Anderung have a gelatinous tissue which serves both as skin and muscle, which can be shaped, hardened and softened at will.

When moving, hunting, eating, or fighting, the Anderung will change shape to suit their needs. Their internal organs must bet protected, but only take up a rough spherical area about 25 centimeters in diameter. Anderung will form and use any number of limbs as needed. They can have thick, heavy limbs for powerful blows and fighting, or thousands of tiny tendrils for fine motor skills.

====Facial Features====

Anderung have no need for a face. Their entire bodies are covered with olfractory receptors and audio-sensitive nerve clusters, allowing them to smell and hear with their whole bodies. The Anderung eat by enveloping and absorbing prey directly into their bodies.

Eyes are another matter. Anderung have an internal mesh of light-sensitive cells, which they can focus on one or more areas to form their equivalent of eyes. In a non-social environment, Anderung typically move these cells to the end of psuedopods and give themselves stereoscopic vision.

When dealing with the [[Gorhamut]], Anderung would adopt a shape similar to their masters': four limbs, a tail, a long neck and a head with a mouth and eyes. Their faces are usually rather bland, but it makes it easier for them to deal with non-Anderung by adopting a relatively simple form. When they were the masters of the [[Mesarnak]], they adopted a relatively human-looking form.

===Respiratory System===

Anderung require oxygen as a catalyst in their metabolism. They absorb it directly through their surface into their internal organs.

===Metabolism===

Anderung eat meat, but dissolve it down into simpler components before absorbing it into their digestive system.

===Lifespan and Reproduction===

Anderung reproduce by "budding" asexually. They release a "spore" with a packet of their main silicon-based outer tissue, which grows into the nervous system and internal organs of a new Anderung.

===Behavior===

Anderung tend to be moody and prone to violence towards any non-Anderung and non-[[Gorhamut]]. This is a new behavioral trait, the result of the [[Sundering]]. In the past they were much more peaceful in most of their dealings.

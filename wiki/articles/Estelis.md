'''Estelis''' is the third planet in the [[Hydrae System]], a nearly unspoiled paradise of a world.

== History ==

Estelis is the "spiritual heir" to the legacy of [[Torimur]]. After its discovery in [[2319]], [[Anthony Hadrian]] recognized its potential strength as a symbol for his secretive rebellion against the [[Onyx Empire]]. The "vats" were easy to manipulate: nearly all of them obeyed their immediate superiors without question. It was the original colonists of Torimur--who held positions of authority within the Imperial military--that Hadrian had to win over in order to achieve success.

From its discovery until [[2322]], Hadrian kept the world secret, safeguarded only by the [[HMS Auspicious|HMS ''Auspicious'']], and Commodore [[Marianne Delray]], a closely-trusted friend. During the open declaration of the rebellion and the [[Prefecture]] in 2322, Hadrian promised all original inhabitants of Torimur and their natural-born descendants enormous holdings on Estelis.

The inhabitable regions of the planet were divided into permanent, non-transferable deeds, and given to the original colonists of Torimur and their families. Later, [[Prefecture]] law made it illegal to own land on Estelis by anyone other than the colonists and their descendants. It was even made technically illegal to land on the planet aside from land-owners, but the law in that regard was not widely enforced.

==History==

The ''Jerome Black'' is the oldest and strongest warship in the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]. It is named for the brave founder of [[Torimur]], the father of [[Lucius Black]]. Unlike his megalomaniacal son, [[Jerome Black]] was a hero to his people and content to rule his own colony of [[Torimur]] in peace.

The ''Jerome Black'' has undergone multiple overhauls, and once had everything but its central computer core replaced back in [[2310]]. The original ''Jerome Black'' served as the colony ship that brought the wayward [[Torimur]] army home from exile. Today, the colony vessel has been revamped into a mighty flagship.

[[Image:BG1JeromeBlack1.jpg]]

The modern version of the ''Jerome Black'' has components contributed from the private sector and the Prefecture Science arm. Many new technologies made their active-duty debut on the ''Jerome Black''.

The capabilities of the ''Jerome Black'' are multitudinous. It serves as a warship, a troop transport, and a flagship all at once. It excels at everything it does, but it's best at naval combat.

==Weapons==

The ''Jerome Black'' sports some of the finest weaponry available. Almost every weapon on board is brand new as of March, [[2324]]. Some were even prototypes.

The main arsenal of the ''Jerome Black'' is a a quartet of huge RMD-6 "Reapers." The huge barrel belies the tiny projectile (barely a millimeter in circumference) that the cannon actually fires. At the exit velocities (near-relativistic) the Reapers fire, a large shell would tear the entire ship to pieces from the kickback alone. Even a tiny projectile is enough to transfer lethal amounts of kinetic energy. These weapons have fore and aft firing arcs with a 90 degree vertical arc. They do not protect the bottom of the ship.

Seventeen twin-linked [[Rahn Industries]] GC-9 Hellgun turrets are the ''Jerome Black'''s secondary anti-warship weaponry. A Hellgun's shells do not have the awesome kinetic power of those of a Reaper, but the incredible rate of fire (40 rounds per second) more than makes up for it.

[[Image:BG1JeromeBlack2.jpg]]

A total of twelve AWT-20 "Orca" Torpedo launchers are fixed in the prow and on the port and starboard sides of the ship, including four in a rear-firing configuration. These huge, short-range missiles are excellent for taking out a ship once the shields are down. The Jerome Black can fire hundreds before it needs to restock.

The rest of the Jerome Black's anti-warship arsenal is comprised of sixteen GC-2 cannons. Like the Reapers and the Hellgun, these use tiny shells, but their shots travel at incredible speed.

For anti-air defense, the Jerome Black employs 90 CIWS-15 neutron cannons. These nasty little weapons are lethal, and unlike lasers, they are very difficult to shield against.

The Jerome Black also employs a complement of AAM-10 and TLAM-12 missile launchers, for anti-fighter and ground-bombardment missions.

==Defenses==

For the first time, a Terran warship had modern shield technology. The Phoenix Technology Project created three shield technologies, all of which were deployed first on the Jerome Black.

Gradient Gravity Shielding is the most effective, yet most fragile shield technology in use. It can stop oncoming warheads and bullets cold, smashing them to bits on a steep gravity incline. Gravity manipulation is a brand new technology still in its infancy, but its finer points are rapidly being fleshed out. The Jerome Black's gravity shields have 55 couplings, which serve to keep the shield generators running longer. Each coupling can sustain an average of twenty shots from a naval autocannon before failing.

Two technologies go into the Deflector Screens. Magnetic Screens are less powerful than gravity shields, but they can take many more shots before failing. These screens are the outermost shield on the Jerome Black, and take some of the missile and cannon hits before the more delicate gravity shields stop the bulk of the oncoming weapons fire. Plasma Barriers are the only shield capable of stopping pure energy weapons. These shields are very close to the hull of the Jerome Black.

The Boron-Nitride Composite Armor of the Jerome Black is another first. For centuries, Terran warships have used hopelessly outdated metal plates for armor. This explains the staggering losses of warships during the [[Imperial Civil War]], and why the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] has been reduced to less than a hundred vessels. The Jerome Black's armor is made up of millions of microscopically-fine threads of Boron-Nitride, the only known crystalline substance stronger than diamond.

==Propulsion==

The Jerome Black uses gravity cavitation drives to move it through space. These require no fuel at all, but even these advanced drives can't accelerate the ship faster than 20 KPH/S. These drives require no emission systems, merely generators. The huge generators were placed at the rear of the ship purely out of habit and for visual appeal. Unlike rockets and impulse engines, gravity cavitation drives accelerate every part of the ship at once, so the crew and internals don't suffer from inertial forces.

==Crew Complement==

The Jerome Black doesn't just fight, it carries forces into battle. It has a permanent Marine complement, and has the capacity to carry twenty times its Marine complement comfortably.

The general crew quarters of the Jerome Black are quite spacious. Despite the crew and Marine complement, unless the ship is on a campaign, it usually doesn't have to cram in more than 2200 or so people in its kilometer-long length.

[[Image:BG1JeromeBlack3.jpg]]

The entertainment and eating accommodations are among the best in the fleet. The infantry recreation room in particular is amazing.

==Heritage==

The central core of the Jerome Black is old, but still extremely powerful. Some of its processors have been upgraded over the centuries, but the main systems are identical to its original design.

Prism is the mind of the Jerome Black. She is a hybrid biomechanical brain bonded to the core of the ship. The brain is an artificially created life form, designed by Miles Hlow, the grandfather of the famous Josiah MacArthur Hlow. Only a handful of these machine-creatures have been built, and only two of those have survived since their creation in the mid 21st century.

Prism has the capacity to think and to learn, but is bound to obey the commands of the ship's commander.

==Commander==

Robert Marshall took command of the Jerome Black on January 22nd, 2324. This ship was the fiftieth revision of the two-hundred-year-old vessel, and the second hull.

The [[First Fleet of the Line]] Flagship is assigned to do whatever is necessary for the safety of the sovereignty of the [[Prefecture]]. Marshall's flawless past command record promises that the ''Jerome Black'''s future will be bright.

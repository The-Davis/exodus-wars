Set after "Tempest: Attack on Canopus"

by James Davis

=====Ueda Airspace, 1000 Hours=====
     The Skyhawk streaks over the skies of arid Ueda, flanked on either side by two Piranhas. Wary-eyed Marines sit in silence, watching the Admiral seated on the passenger bench next to them.
     “There’s the airstrip,” the port-side gunner comments. “We’ll be landing in a second. Better strap in.”
     The dropship’s intercom buzzes. “Everyone set back there?” the pilot asks. “We just got clearance, and the escort is in position.”
     “Yup,” the gunner replies. “Just take us in whenever you’re ready.”
     The Skyhawk slows down to a hover, and touches down gently on the Prefecture Senate’s landing pad. In seconds, it is surrounded by heavily-armed Marines, who form an escort for the VIP.
     Security changes since the brazen abduction of the Consul have been very thorough. Even the leaders are checked hundreds of times by security programs before they are allowed to land.
     The Senate is its usual self: hot, noisy, and disorganized. By law, the Senate is required to meet in a place totally exposed to the elements, and to only draft new laws in the heat of the day. Any law that fails to pass before the day is out is shredded and work starts over the next day. So far, it has kept every law blissfully simple. In some Districts, it’s possible to pass the bar exam by pulling an all-nighter on your first day of legal classes.
     Marines pour into the room, clearing a path for their VIP.
     “It’s good to be back,” Hadrian sighs. “Ladies and gentlemen, shall we begin?”
     Interim-Consul Talaris Roi smiles with relief. “I’m glad to see that you escaped from Centus unscathed.”
     “Thank you ma’am,” Hadrian replies. “The Admirals and Generals will be joining us by Ansible. The Curiate should arrive soon, and then we’ll begin the broadcast.”

     A few minutes later, Hadrian addresses the assembled Congress and military leaders. The meeting is being broadcasted by the Ansible over the entire Prefecture.
     “On April 2, 2325, the Federated Districts of the Prefecture were abruptly and intentionally attacked by the forces of the United Centusi States. The Prefecture was at peace with the Mesarthrim, and at the request of their leaders we have been trying to help bring stability to their region of space.
     “Despite the mounting evidence against them, the Mesarthrim leaders saw fit to deliver messages containing malicious lies to our friends in the Alliance of Planets. Their actions have sown the seeds of doubt among our allies, and there will be no intervention by the Alliance.
     “The distance of Leskovik from Mesar makes it obvious that the attack was deliberately planned many days ago. In the midst of their plans of treachery, the Mesarthrim deliberately sought to deceive the Prefecture with falsehoods and messages of hope for continued peace.
     “The attack on Leskovik has caused severe damage to our industry. Very many Prefecture lives have been lost. Many of our ships were destroyed while they were attempting to retaliate.
     “On April 6th, the Mesarthrim government also launched an attack against our forces defending Tavou, Holt, and New Albany. Last night, Mesarthrim forces attacked Canopus. This morning, Mesarthrim forces invaded the Laojiu District.
     “Mesar has undertaken a blitzkrieg extending throughout our territory. The facts speak for themselves. You all must understand the implications to the very life and safety of our infant nation. 
     “As Prefect, I have taken all necessary measures to defend our planets. I personally led a counterattack against their homeworld, which managed to deal significant damage to many of their warships. 
     “We must always remember the barbaric character of our enemies. No matter how long it takes to defeat this treacherous foe, the Prefecture people in their righteous might will win through to absolute victory. 
     “I believe that I spoke truly for us all when I declared war against the United Centusi States. We will not only defend ourselves to the death, but will make very certain that these wretched foes shall never endanger us again. 
     “Hostilities exist. There is no denying that our people, our worlds and our livelihoods are in grave danger. Therefore, I must recall all of the Legions; bringing our armed forces back to their full strength. I have every confidence that the Congress will ratify my decisions, for each of them represents the unyielding determination of our people. This wanton and reprehensible attack will not go unanswered. We will gain the inevitable triumph - so help us God.”
     The Senate chamber explodes with applause. It takes only minutes to tally the votes. There is no dissent. The Legions have been reformed, and there will be war.

'''Ulysses Brenner'''
*Date of Death: April 5, [[2324]]
*Place of Birth: [[Mesar]]
*Place of Death: [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: None

''Ulysses Brenner'' was a [[Mesarthrim]] medical research specialist assigned to help [[Josiah Hlow]] during the [[Terran Plague Outbreak]]. He was assassinated by Omega Agents on April 5, [[2324]] before he could begin assisting the Terran relief effort.

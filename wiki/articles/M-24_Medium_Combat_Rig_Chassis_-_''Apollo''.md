The Royal Imperial Army's Apollo model was a rare sight--only four were ever built, but it had an almost mystical aura about it that made Rig pilots fall in love with it. The design never made it to full production due to the expensive and difficult-to-produce shield systems.

During the recovery effort on Terra, a half-wrecked prototype Apollo was recovered on the trashed slopes of Mount McKinley, where Lucius Black's last Terran stronghold had been located. Most of the systems were beyond repair, but the Prefecture Research and Development teams on Estel-Mundis were eventually able to rebuild it.

Exact replication of the Apollo was impossible--there was too much Remnant technology involved in the creation--but eventually a satisfactory armament and equipment system was chosen.

For main weapons, the Apollo Mk.II has the turret-mounted cannons that were the signature of the original Rig. Prefecture projectile weapons managed to advance the old Thumper Cannon design that the original Apollo was equipped with, but the science teams simply shrugged and gave up when they saw the odd weapon the Remnant had armed the prototype with. "Better to stick with the basics than get ourselves blown up by something we don't understand," said R&D Chief Robert Brimston.

Melee combat wasn't possible with the original, but the prototype had arms. They weren't operational or completely intact when the prototype was found, and the lack of control systems in the cockpit led R&D teams to believe that the Empire never got them working. Dr. Brimston decided to correct this oversight, and gave the arms the machinery necessary to fight in melee combat.

The odd turret was the limit of the prototype Apollo's armament, but Prefecture R&D decided to take it further. Engineers added Neutron Cannons to each arm.

Prefecture scientists were completely baffled by the prototype Apollo's power core. It looked like a simple fission reactor, so they attempted to start it up. Fortunately, Dr. Brimston had the foresight to test it in an isolated location--a rogue asteroid several light years from any inhabited starsystem. The power core, the asteroid, and all observation equipment on the asteroid were completely annihilated, with no physical debris left to show that anything had ever been there. The scientists wisely switched to an easier to obtain Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor. They also added jump jets, something neither the original Apollo nor the prototype had.

The prototype had no trace of the shield system that made the original Apollo famous. With none of the original systems intact, Prefecture scientists had no way to figure out what the Royal Imperial Army and the Remnant had done, so they used stock Rig protective systems--Boron-Nitride Composite armor with plasma and magnetic shield systems.

The wrecked Apollo prototype was found in April of 2323, and the recreation of the model was finally finished in January of 2324. The test Apollo Mk.II was actually built from the ground-up. No Rig pilot or test pilot was willing to go near the old wreck after hearing what had happened to the power core. Production of front-line units was completed by April of 2324, exactly one year after the wreck was found.

The Apollo Mk.II is the successor of the Bushwhacker. Originally, the role of front-line brawler was intended for the Hercules Mk.II, but the light Rig turned out to be better suited for the harassment and skirmishing role of its 2315 namesake. The new Apollo filled the role perfectly. With its well-rounded armament, it can handle most Volucris breeds and most humanoid threats.

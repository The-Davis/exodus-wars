'''Ju Tao'''
*Date of Death: June 29, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Casere]]
*Place of Death: [[Casere]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Ju Tao'' was the governor of [[Casere]] before the [[Mesarthrim]] invasion on April 19, [[2325]]. For his refusal to surrender the colony, he was shipped back to [[Mesar]] as a P.O.W. 

When the people of [[Casere]] began to revolt against the [[Mesarthrim]] occupation, Tao was sent back and tried by a military tribunal, then hanged for inciting rebellion, despite his actual innocence of being involved in the resistance.

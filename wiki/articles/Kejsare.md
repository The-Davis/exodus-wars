The '''Kejsare''' is the king of the [[Reigess Suverände]]. It is a hereditary position, held only by members of the Raegos family, in direct line of descent from [[Raegos Direnson]].

The position is inherited when the heir (who holds the title of "Fenig") reaches the age of 35. If the Kejsare dies before the Fenig reaches the age of 35, the Fenig immediately becomes the Kejsare, but holds no power unless they are 20 years old or older. A regent, typically from the Rejoc family, is appointed to govern on behalf of the Kejsare until he is an adult.

The wife of the Kejsare holds the title of Drottning. She has the power to select the regent if needed, but otherwise holds no real power. Traditionally and historically, the Drottning has been the closest and most trusted advisor of the Kejsare, and most have almost 50% de facto rule in the decision-making process.

Historical Kejsares:
*[[Raegos Direnson]] - The first Kejsare
*[[Edvard Raegos]] - Retired in [[2312]]
*[[Lorrodi Raegos]] - Reigned from [[2312]] until retiring.

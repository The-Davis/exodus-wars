The '''Mtomigru''' (''xenocaro furous acutous''), or "scholars", are the larger, wiser, less aggressive subspecies of the [[Ngrligru]]. They developed later in the natural history of the [[Ngrligru]]. The first known Mtomigru was [[Ona Raha]], who brought civilization to the savage Kabilas scattered through the oceans of the [[Ngrligru]] homeworld.

===Appearance===

Mtomigru are larger than normal [[Ngrligru]]. They average 1.2M in height, and 80KG in weight.  Their muscles are much more developed than their smaller brethren, and their brains are significantly different.

Mtomigru are characterised by strong hind legs, a short body, webbed, clawed digits, protruding eyes and a long, sinewy tail. Because their head and spine are directly aligned, Mtomigru walking on their legs appear to be "hunched" over.

Mtomigru are strong swimmers, but much less maneuverable on land than [[Ngrligru]]. Their hind legs are shorter and less developed, but well muscled for swimming. Their tails are stronger, making them better swimmers than [[Ngrligru]]. Mtomigru rarely leave the water, but on land they can walk at a comfortable pace. They are poor runners and cannot make the powerful leaps the [[Ngrligru]] are known for.

Mtomigru have a dorsal fin running along their back, with sharp spines protruding from their spines. There is a thick layer of fatty tissues cushioning the vertebrae from impacts from the doral spines. They also have a powerful caudal fin running along the top and bottom of their tails, which aids in swimming. The Mtomigru also have pectoral and pelvic fins, located on their forearms and hips, which have the same spines as their dorsal fin. They are used for guiding their movements when swimming, and for defense.

====Scale Colors====

Mtomigru are covered in a layer of skin and fatty tissue, which in turn is covered by several types of scales. The chest region is covered in ventral scales, which wrap around the pectoral and abdominal muscles, layering over each other. When lying flat on the ground, it gives the Mtomigru a comfortable surface to lie on. Mtomigru ventral scales are softer than [[Ngrligru]] ventral scales, and usually darker in color than the rest of its body.

The arms, legs, back and head of the Mtomigru are covered in placoid scales, also known as denticles. These rough scales create vortexes in the water as they swim, and make swimming faster and easier. Unlike [[Ngrligru]], a Mtomigru's denticles are never sharp, but are rough. Mtomigru placoid scales are lighter than its ventral scales, and can be colored with several patterns.

Mtomigru can be dozens of primary colors, secondary colors, and patterns. Their colors are usually inheritted among families within their Kabila. White, light gray, red and light blue are common placoid colors. Black, dark gray, brown, and dark blue are common ventral colors. Mtomigru also have striped, mottled, spotted, flat or brindled marking schemes with as many as four colors.

====Fin Colors====

In addition to their body fins, Mtomigru have a series of defensive "crest" fins around their heads, particularly near the ears, the crown of the head, and around the neck. Male Mtomigru also have a number of fleshy carbuncles that hang from their chins, known as the "Ndevu."

Males' Ndevu number anywhere from two to ten. Normally, they are only two or three centimeters long, and the same color as their ventral scales. During Mtomigru mating season, the Ndevu lengthen to nearly eight centimeters, and turn bright green. The Ndevu secrete a powerful pheremone which encourages ovulation in Mtomigru females.

Female Mtomigru have larger, darker crest fins, which curve backwards and are noteably sharper and more defined than male crests. The male crest is curved forwards, and while it is less sharp, the membrane between the crest ribs is much tougher and more resilient than the females.

Mtomigru fins and crests tend to be the same color as their placoid scales, but are approximately two shades brighter. In mating season, both male and female crests turn bright green.

====Eye Color====

Mtomigru eyes protrude from the top of their head, but are protected by a tough, warty ridge around the socket. The outer eyelid is covered in tough scales, allowing a Mtomigru to protect its eyes from damage simply by closing them.

Mtomigru also have a thick, but clear inner eyelid, allowing them to see while swimming underwater. Unlike [[Ngrligru]], Mtomigru only live in shallower water with sunlight, and are not sensitive to light when they are above water.

Mtomigrue eyes are usually blue or purple, with large pupils. They do not have any white membrane in their eyes.

===Skeletal System===

Mtomigru have a skeleton with some bone and a lot of cartilage. Their skulls, limbs, and spines are all bone. The rest of the body is protected by cartilage, thick in some areas and thinner in others. The chest and stomach in particular are protected by thick layers of cartilage.

===Respiratory System===

Mtomigru are an amphibious species, comfortable on the land and water, but prefering water or at least very wet land. The Mtomigru have three lungs, all of which are lined with a membrane which can absorb oxygen from water or from air.

Mtomigru have several internal pressure bladders which they can fill with air, water, or completely deflate to compensate for pressure. Unlike [[Ngrligru]], Mtomigru pressure bladders are atrophied. They cannot dive as deep underwater as their relatives. They are less susceptible to explosive decompression than [[Ngrligru]], but will still die quickly.

Mtomigru can dive only a hundred meters or so underwater. They rarely settle below the lighted regions of the oceans, and almost never venture below sixty meters.

===Nervous System===

The Mtomigru have a lateral line which runs down their sides where the ventral and placoid scales meet. The lateral line is a sensitive nerve cluster, which can sense changes in water movement, giving Mtomigru a strong sense of proximity and movement. They can use their lateral line senses, but not nearly as much as they rely on their eyes. Mtomigru are not nearly as adept at navigating or fighting in the dark as [[Ngrligru]], but can swim comfortably in the dark.

Like [[Ngrligru]], Mtomigru have the Fahamu Node. These microscopic nerve clusters are distributed throughout the body, and serve as the memory centers of the Mtomigru. The nodes coordinate a massive gestahlt for the Mtomigru, giving it nearly perfect memory recall. Unlike the [[Ngrligru]], the Mtomigru actually secrete Fahamu Nodes towards the end of the mating season, feeding them to their hatchlings.

The nodes cannot be digested. When ingested by [[Ngrligru]] or another Mtomigru, they float freely in the digestive system until they are absorbed into the bloodstream and eventually reattach to eater's nervous system. Eating another of its kind will literally give a Mtomigru a sense of the victim's memories. A new clutch of Mtomigru lacks the ravening hunger of a clutch of [[Ngrligru]], and are fed new memories by their parents rather than eating the parents themselves.

===Metabolism===

Mtomigru, unlike [[Ngrligru]], have a normal appetite. They relied on a different bacterial strain to digest plant matter, and were unaffected by the "[[Njaa]]" unleashed by the [[Anthronoris]]. The Mtomigru live on aquatic plants and animals in the oceans of their worlds.

During the [[Sundering]], Mtomigru scientists discovered a way to alter their own bacteria to be compatible with the [[Ngrligru]] to a certain extent. It restored the sanity of the [[Ngrligru]], but could not end their craving for the flesh of their own species.



===Lifespan and Reproduction===

The Mtomigru have a potential lifespan of 500 years, and the average lifespan is around 200 to 250 years. The Mtomigru were not fully immune to the [[Njaa]]--like the [[Ngrligru]], their flesh was altered and is addictive to the [[Ngrligru]]. Many Mtomigru die when they ae eaten by their [[Ngrligru]] brethren. Other Mtomigru are eaten by the vicious predators found on most of their worlds, though they live in safer environments than many [[Ngrligru]].

Mtomigru usually only have one mating season per year, depending on the planet they live on. When the water temperature reaches its lowest point at about 20 degrees celcius, the male Mtomigru begin secreting their sexual pheremones. This excites female Mtomigru into ovulating. Female Mtomigru only lay a few dozen eggs per season, usually in clutches of five to ten. Like [[Ngrligru]] females, Mtomigru female ovaries continuously grow new eggs throughout their lives.

Because their mating seasons are triggered by the water temperature dropping to 20 degrees celsius, Mtomigru sometimes have to migrate to colder climates to raise young. Their main habitats, shared with the [[Ngrligru]], are usually in warm tropical regions. This allows the Mtomigru to raise their young in relative safety, returning when they can defend themselves. Most Mtomigru survive their infancy and young childhood, but many are eaten by [[Ngrligru]] during early adulthood if they do not learn to defend themselves.

Mtomigru eggs are soft until fertilized by the males. The female lays a clutch of eggs in a mud bed in a secluded corner, and the male fertilizes them. Mtomigru males cover the eggs both in sperm and in a substance which covers the eggs in a tough shell. The Mtomigru male then buries the eggs. Both parents watch over the eggs until they hatch, and then raise their young together. Unlike [[Ngrligru]], Mtomigru tend to have permanent mated pairs which are bonded for life.

Mtomigru young hatch with a sharp spine on their nose, which they use to escape their eggshell. Unlike the [[Ngrligru]], they do not attack their siblings and do not crave meat from the moment they hatch. They are soon brought up to speed by eating the memories fed to them by their parents, and they live on a mostly herbacious diet until their teeth are sharp enough to cut meat and their claws are strong enough to hunt. Once able to defend themselves, the Mtomigru family returns to their settlement.

===Behavior===

The Mtomigru have a normal fight or flight response, similar to that of a human. The [[Njaa]] did not fully effect the Mtomigru, and as such they have a much more sensible threat assesment. It is because of this that Mtomigru are always the military advisors and often the strategists in modern Mtomigru and [[Ngrligru]] society.

Mtomigru are known to be very affectionate among their immediate families. Because they do not desire to eat one another, they enjoy one another's company immensely. They are usually able to cooperate with Mtomigru from other Kabilas with little difficulty. To a human observer, Mtomigru within an immediate family all appear to be lovers or sexual partners: they are very tactile and like to constantly touch one another, often nuzzling or hugging their family members. However, this is not the case. Mtomigru and [[Ngrligru]] take no sexual gratification from physical contact. In fact, they do not experience physical attraction at all in a way a human would understand.

The Mtomigru behavior with one another is said to be identical to the way the [[Ngrligru]] were before the [[Njaa]] was unleashed on them.

As with all things regarding the [[Sirius Families]], Territorial Naval Forces is more of a collective term for all space vessels operated by the various factions for protection of the three-system [[Sirius Cluster]]. Although officially a single entity, the [[Sirius Families]] assigns defense of the three systems their territorial rulers.

At any given time, the number of armed vessels in a single territory may vary due to the presence of various groups, but each family does maintain its own full-time fleet. The fleets ensure family control over a territory, and also serve not so much to keep the peace, but to keep the status quo.

In addition to any warships, the [[Sirius Cluster|Sirius Territories]] play host to trade and merchant ships from across the [[Orion Arm]]. Vast fleets of freighters, transports, haulers, and tankers transfer goods at massive orbital depots, and then set off in the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates|gates]] once again. The Sirius families also maintain their own private trade fleets for transporting "priority goods".

The Nakamura Navy is less well developed than their competitors and allies in the Sirius Cluster. Their Odashi class destroyer is not as ellegant as the Berlusconi flagship, and not as powerful as the Asanova flagship, but is inexpensive enough that the Nakamuras have two destroyers instead of one. Also unlike the other families, the Nakamuras have three dedicated military vessel classes: the Katana frigate, the Wakizashi corvette, and the Shuriken gunship. The Nakamura freighers are dedicated hauler classes: the Ushi a heavy hauler and the Uma a light hauler.

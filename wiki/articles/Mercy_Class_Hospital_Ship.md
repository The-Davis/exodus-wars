===Overview===

In 2323, the Home Defense Security force was given the job of creating a mobile medical team that could help the deathly ill refugees of Terra.  This job led to the creation of the Mobile Medical Corps, which in turn led to the development of the Mercy Class Medical Cruiser.

[[Image:MercyClassHospitalShip1.jpg]]

The Mercy is the equivalent of an entire city of hospitals in space.  Every imaginable specialist field is represented in some way by the medical personnel, from cardiologists to proctologists.  The only thing a Mercy's medical teams can't do is raise the dead, and they jokingly claim that they'll be able to do that with enough research.

===Capabilities===

The bulk of the Mercy's interior is devoted to berths for recovering personnel, and the crew and troops aboard the ship.

The sickbeds are an advanced technological affair.  Most beds are simple classic padded mattresses and sheets, which more mobile patients enjoy.  These are equipped with robotic arms that can perform simple functions and monitor the patient.  For the more critical patient, a body-sized tube of gel encases them while they recover, to avoid friction and bedsores.

The surgical tools available aboard a Mercy are extremely advanced.  Only the most advanced planetary hospital--the Schweitzer Institute of Medicine on Fronde, in the Fukuroi District--is more advanced than the Mercy's surgery center.  Surgeons can literally dismantle a brain piecemeal, keep it alive, repair the damage, and put it back together.  Naturally, such a drastic surgery has severe and long-lasting side-effects, but even the most terrible surgical side-effects go away after a year or so, and even being invalid is better than death.

[[Image:MercyClassHospitalShip2.jpg]]

Lost organs have traditionally been difficult to replace.  In the past, donator organs must be harvested from recently deceased cadavers.  In the past, in the early 22nd century, a means of growing organs was developed by the people of Holt.  Unfortunately, this technology was lost in the Second Exodus War, as most of Holt's industry was leveled in the Blackis invasion.

Today, new organs can be created if a small sample of the original or a compatible organ can be found.  Cells from the organ are treated with enzymes which cause cell mitosis--the cells to grow and divide.  Unfortunately, simple mitosis isn't enough.  The cells must be shaped around a weave of composite fibers, which must be removed after the new organ is properly shaped.  This is a very delicate process.

The completed organ has never been rejected by the immune system once its placed in the patient, but these "meta-organs" are rarely perfect.  The average meta-organ is about 90% as effective as the original.  Complete breakdown is unheard of, but sometimes performance is as low as 40% of the original.  In such situations, the organ is replaced again, though repeated open-body surgery can lead to system trauma and infection.  Most surgeons are reluctant to replace an organ more than twice if it isn't necessary for survival.

The MASH teams are all housed in the Narwhal, which rides on the underbelly of the Mercy Cruiser.  All of their maintenance is performed there, to save space for medical equipment on the Mercy.  The Mercy does carry a complement of shuttles, but they are serviced at planetary spaceports or aboard other vessels.

The Mercy's shields and armor are not exceptionally strong, but it rarely sees attackers.  No humanoid species will attack a medical cruiser, and it hides in deep space when Ologrians or Volucris are involved.  The Mercy will only rush to the front lines on its FTL drives when its Narwhal or a Nightingale is bringing up a load of critically injured personnel.

===Deployment===

The Mercy is in widespread use everywhere.  The Mobile Medical Corps isn't restricted by borders, as its merciful works are welcome everywhere.

Usually, two Mercy's are deployed to follow every Prefecture Fleet.

[[Image:MercyClassHospitalShip3.jpg]]

The Mercy itself is assigned to Terra, where it oversees most of the medical relief programs for the radiation-sickness and plague stricken planet.

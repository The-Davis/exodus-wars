The '''Mesarthrim Rebellion''' was an underground organization which eventually developed into the [[Clan Lords]] system and united under the banner of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. It was responsible for the destruction of the [[Anderung]] government.

The rebellion was made up of members of the [[Mesarnak]] [[:Category:Races|race]], but they adopted the name [[Mesarthrim]] for themselves after the rebellion became a highly organized movement.

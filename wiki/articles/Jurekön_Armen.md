The [[Ikronin]] Armen relies more on hovercraft than other weapons. The hovercraft design is well-suited for the tundra of [[Kronheim]], and larger hovercraft are quite capable of carrying heavy weapons. Wheeled and tracked vehicles are also used, but usually only for utility purposes.

Rigs are not used by the Ground Defense Force, though large battle armor models are used. Some are close to Rigs in size, and carry several weapons.

Most of the time, the Ikronin Ground Defense Force is only used on [[Kronheim]] itself. In fact, most of the soldiers are part-time, only required to train a few weeks out of each month, and only required to serve in combat during a declared war.

The [[Ikronin]] hovertanks have gained legendary status after their performance in the [[Battle of Kronheim]] against the Volucris became common knowledge. The backbone of the Armen is the Gamätande, a heavy hovertank with powerful weaponry. The Gamätande is assisted by the smaller and faster Eldsvåda, a medium harasser and skirmisher. Strongest of all is the lumbering Mesk Bränning, a deadly destroyer which can level buildings and topple Rigs in a single shot.

[[Ikronin]] infantry usually use common powered armor, similar to the type universally used by Prefecture soldiers. These highly trained mobile infantry are transported by Karriäranse and Piantro APCs into battle, and supported by Makkel heavy battle armor.

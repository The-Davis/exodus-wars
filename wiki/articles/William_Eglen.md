'''William Eglen'''
*Date of Birth: November 8, [[2290]]
*Place of Birth: [[Bethija]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''William Eglen'', the FDS ''Delphinus''' communications officer, is a native of [[Bethija]], and joined the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] during the negotiations of the [[Titanian Peace Accords]].  He believed that the Navy would become a way to protect [[Bethija]] without fighting in an invasion.

When the [[Empire]] broke the Peace Accords, Eglen nearly deserted, but managed to keep a position in the [[Bethija Core Fleet]], where he wouldn't have to be involved in any invasions.  As soon as the rebellion against the [[Empire]] became public, Eglen joined in the efforts.  He found his way into the Prefecture, and eventually earned a position within the reformed Prefecture Galactic Navy, aboard the [[BG-3 Delphinus]].

''Hamur Huern'''s rule marks the beginning of [[Lorithos]] history. He was the "Mircan Prekohr", or "Dawn Emperor", who brought civilization to the [[Lorithos]] after the [[Sundering]] nearly destroyed them.

Hamur Huern is one of the greatest heroes in [[Lorithos]] legends. Many compare [[Sanor Cai]]'s bravery and kindness with the ancient emperor, thought they compare the emperor's wisdom with that of [[Dirken Huern]].

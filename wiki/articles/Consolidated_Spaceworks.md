'''Consolidated Spaceworks''' is a leading interstellar aerospace manufacturer and advanced technology company formed in [[2009]] by the merger of Lockheed Martin with Mikoyan-Gurevich and BAE Systems. It was headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland until the [[Mars]] colony was opened for private development in [[2025]]. Consolidated Spaceworks moved its headquarters to the colony in order to promote civilian space flight.

Consolidated Spaceworks is the [[Sol System]]'s largest air and space developer. Throughout its history, 90% of Consolidated Spaceworks' revenues came from the [[United Earth Alliance]].

The city of '''San Francisco''' is located at the tip of the San Francisco Peninsula, with the Pacific Ocean to the west, San Francisco Bay to the east, and the Golden Gate to the north.

In [[2315]], the city was the site of a key battle in the [[Second Exodus War]]. It was noted as one of the few seaports to stand against the [[Onyx Empire]] in the early part of the invasion.  The battle became known as the [[Storming of San Francisco]].

As a result of the Onyx Empire's scorched earth tactics during their retreat from Earth, San Francisco was burned to the ground and completely depopulated in [[2323]].

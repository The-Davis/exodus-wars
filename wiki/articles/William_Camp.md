''William Camp'' is the leader of the [[United Centusi States]], and was a member of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] military before the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]].

== The Mesarthrim Civil War ==

William Camp was a member of the investigative council that was formed by [[Bethany Tischler]] to determine if the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim government]] was salvageable in the face of the numerous splinter factions in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]].

William Camp worked tirelessly with Dr. [[Mark Edge]], [[Bethany Tischler]], [[Arnold Stadt]], and [[Elias Faust]] to rebuild the late [[Andreas Tischler]]'s government. After three months of debate, fury, and failure, William Camp locked himself in a hotel room on [[Ipsoff]]. After only three days he emerged with a constitution and handbook that outlined what he felt would be a proper [[Mesarthrim]] government. His constitution was simplistic and short-sighted, but it sparked a mindset of fresh hope for the council, and they began working on a new form of government for the [[Mesarthrim]], rather than trying to reform the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clans government]]. The group decided to create a very simple constitution, but attached a cross-reference handbook and encyclopedia to explain their concepts in the constitution.

Very little of the final [[United Centusi States]] constitution was written by William. After submitting his initial draft of the constitution, he spent most of his time trying to win the loyalty of the surviving [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] Commandants. William made hundreds of perilous journeys between [[Mesar]] and [[Ipsoff]]. If he couldn't win them with words, he bought them with coin.

== The Golden Snake ==

Soon, William became known across Mesar as the "Golden Snake." Commandants looking for a way out of a losing war approached him in private, and sold their loyalty and their brigades for a cheap price. This finally attracted the interest of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] High Command, and Uber-General [[Eric Francis]] issued an execution warrant for William Camp. In late [[2323]], an assassination attempt was almost successful, and left William missing two fingers on his left hand. Rather then having new fingers grafted onto his hand, William opted to use their loss as a political weapon and secured true loyalty among the dispirited Commandants, and among civilian groups.

== The Peak of the Civil War ==

As the [[Auellal League]] began to press towards [[Stone Bridge]], William began to push the members of the Trilateral Administration to form a working government so he could mobilize his military to claim the territory of the former [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. Eager to end the war, the Trilateral Administration agreed and appointed William Camp as Vice Chancellor so he could have the power to command the war. [[Antol Nik]] was appointed Chancellor.

When the [[United Centusi States]]' dropships landed on [[Mesar]], [[Antol Nik|Antol]] slowly and carefully debated on which faction to declare war against. His timidness enraged William, who launched a series of quick and deceptive black ops missions which forced every faction except the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clans]] into declaring war against the [[United Centusi States]]. Thanks to some timely hospitality from the [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi family]], the [[United Centusi States Heer]] was completely re-armed and rested. Nearly every engagement was a complete success.

== Schism in the Centusi States ==

Almost a month after the [[United Centusi States]] joined the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], a group of POWs revealed William Camp’s unauthorized black ops that provoked their faction into war. Although [[Antol Nik]] was acting Chancellor, he still commanded his Panzer division and at the news of his betrayal, he attacked William’s headquarters. Antol’s elite “Black Dogs” killed off almost the entire command staff of the [[United Centusi States Heer]].

William escaped [[Antol Nik|Antol]]’s attack because he was away meeting with Großadmiral Cranston in orbit, discussing the defection of the entire [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim]] Second Kriegsmarine to the [[United Centusi States]].  After the attack, the Trilateral Administration sent word to William that [[Antol Nik]] was now considered an enemy of all [[Mesarthrim]], and that William should now assume the role of acting Chancellor, and that [[Elias Faust]] would be dispatched to take over his job as Vice Chancellor.

== Legitimate Successor ==

By the time [[Elias Faust]] arrived, William had met several times in secret with Uber-General [[Eric Francis]]. They agreed that before the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] could capture [[Stone Bridge]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] would officially recognize the [[United Centusi States]] as its legitimate successor. This would give the [[United Centusi States]] complete legitimacy, both in the galactic arena and to the [[Mesarthrim]] whose faith was shaken by the terrors of [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]].

On the eve of the deal, a surprise [[Auellal League|Auellal]] attack on downtown [[Stone Bridge]] killed Uber-General [[Eric Francis]], and Council Members [[Claus Winkel]] and [[Corina Weber]]. Not only were these three highest remaining members of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] dead, William Camp was critically wounded, ultimately losing his hearing in his left ear and his left leg below the knee. Despite the trauma, he had the prize: a signed and witnessed document officially recognizing the [[United Centusi States]].

William used the neutral status between the [[United Centusi States]] and the [[Auellal League]] to force the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] to accept a cease-fire. [[Steven Cue]] agreed to the deal and sent the cease-fire order in the nick of time: [[Auellal League|Auellal]] troops were a few hundred feet from the Grand Chambers in [[Stone Bridge]]. It was the narrowest escape of William's life.

With the [[United Centusi States]] now the legal successor of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and in firm control of [[Mesar]], the Trilateral Administration created the Trilateral packet, which included the Constitution, Handbook, Encyclopedia, Reference, and the official recognition of the [[United Centusi States]] by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. The packet was sent to every [[:Category:Nations|nation]] in the known [[Milky Way|galaxy]], and posted in every news outlet they could find.

William’s shrewd bravery and sacrifice ended the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], and sent the [[Auellal League]] back to [[Caledon]], without the prize of [[Stone Bridge]].

== Chancellor ==

After William was outfitted with a new leg, the Trilateral Administration officially appointed him Chancellor of the [[United Centusi States]], and then dissolved itself. Almost immediately William initiated an aggressive rebuilding program of the [[Mesarthrim]] military forces, and was able to convince many allies, particularly the [[Prefecture]], into assisting in the rebuild.

While they had the reins of power, William Camp and [[Elias Faust]] were not fully in control of the [[United Centusi States]]. The [[Tischler Clan]] was still a major power broker among the [[:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clans]], and without their support William Camp's power base would crumble. William arranged with [[Mait Onega]] of the [[Lorithos]] to have [[Bethany Tischler]] poisoned: a slow, weakening poison that appeared to be a wasting disease. When she became too weak to run the affairs of the [[Tischler Clan]] any longer, William Camp convinced her to give him power of attorney, and he became the de facto [[Clan Lord]] of the [[Tischler Clan]].

The only two [[Mesarthrim]] who knew William Camp's plots were the scientists; doctors [[Mark Edge|Mark]] and [[Steven Edge]]. Before they could reveal what they knew, [[Elias Faust]] had them arrested under charges of conspiring with the [[Auellal League]] to undermine the [[United Centusi States]]. Without trial, they were stripped of all rights, titles and property and exiled to [[Terra]]. This last coup, left William Camp and Elias Faust with nearly unquestioned power over the [[United Centusi States]].

The only possible opponent to William Camp's control was [[Arnold Stadt]]. The [[Stadt Clan]] was decimated in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], and [[Arnold Stadt|Arnold]] was the only surviving member of the immediate family. William deployed [[Arnold Stadt]]'s command into space near the [[Cinaed System]], with orders to monitor the [[Auellal League]] and engage any hostiles. In his absence, William assumed the mantle of [[Clan Lord]] for the [[Stadt Clan]], giving him unheard-of political power: the title of two [[Clan Lord]]s.

Eventually, even the State Church was brought under William Camp's control when he bought the votes to appoint his brother-in-law to the office of State Reverend. With this extra grasp, William was able to shut down any dissent among the [[People of the Pen]] that might disagree with his political agenda.

After consolidating his power William Camp made his goals very distinctively clear: the nationalization of all Mesarthrim commodities and industries. William wanted to shed the chains of the inferior imports of commodities and raw material from other nations. William made a brash drive towards a more strictly mercantile economy by levying stringent tariffs against all non-[[Mesarthrim]] goods.

== The Tempest War ==

William Camp's ambitions began to bankrupt the [[United Centusi States]]. The [[Mesarthrim]] people lacked many raw materials and basic commodities. In his power-mad mind, this left William with only one option; to expand the [[Mesarthrim]] empire until they had every resource they needed. Camp turned his eyes towards the [[Prefecture]], one of the most powerful economies in the galaxy.

William ordered his most dangerous black ops mission yet; meant to goad the [[Prefecture]] into being the "aggressor" in the war, and opening the field for the [[Mesarthrim]] to capture vital planets within the [[Prefecture]].

The surprise was complete, and the mission was a success. The next day the [[Prefecture]] declared war on the [[United Centusi States]], and the bulk of the [[Alliance of Planets]] refused to support the [[Prefecture]] due to a sever lack of evidence.

== Death ==

William Camp was named as a defendant in the [[Magrinoch Trials]], but he had disappeared prior to the end of the war.  The details surrounding his death are unknown, but after significant pressure was placed on the newly formed [[Mesarthrim Federation]] by the [[Ixoarchada]], [[Arnold Stadt]] grudgingly produced the deceased defendant's head as proof that the [[Mesarthrim]] had already dealt with Camp.  The rest of Camp's remains were suspected to be somewhere within [[Tischler Clan]] territory.

The [[Volucris]] Worker is an intelligent, curious, and often friendly breed of Volucris. While they originate from a [[Volucris Mother]], they continue to grow and develop on their own in such a way that would be considered reproduction by humans.
[[Image:VolucrisWorker.png|200px|thumb|right| An example of a Volucris Worker.]]

==Appearance==

Workers average 1M in height, 1.5M in length, and 140KG in weight. Workers are thickly built, with corded muscles, tough chitin, and sharp claws. They have an internal skeleton, but also have a thick, scaly hide in mobile areas and diamond-hard chitinous carapaces around less mobile vulnerable areas.

Volucris Workers have eighteen airholes along their torso that runs straight to their lungs. They breathe through their chests, but do not require oxygen. Their metabolisms are based of a different chemical reaction that humans have not deciphered yet. Even in a vacuum, all [[Volucris]] survive without apparent discomfort.

Workers have powerful hind legs that can propel them along at great speeds. Their front legs are just as powerful, but they possess hands capable of fine motor skills and manipulating small objects. On their backs Workers have two huge clawed limbs, which are primarily used for digging and heavy lifting. A Worker can carry up to 30 times its body weight. Workers have tough, short tails which they use for balance while running and as a counterweight while lifting.

==Metabolic System==

Workers are mobile chemical plants. Their bodies run on a biological chemical factory. Human science has not fully analyzed all of the enzymes present yet, but Workers appear to both run on and transport this material to other [[Volucris]] breeds.

==Senses==

Workers have six eyes. Two see in the visible light spectrum, two see in the ultraviolet spectrum, and two see in the infrared spectrum. They also have an organ between their eyes that is sensitive to radio frequencies, but human science has not deciphered precisely what it is for. Workers have an enormous olfractory lobe in their brains, which can detect and decipher billions of different scents.

==The Worker Gestalt==

While Workers have an individual consciousness, they have multiple bodies. When a Worker is born to a [[Volucris Mother]], it only has one body, but early in its life cycle it "reproduces" asexually, and then continues to do so on a regular basis. Each of these bodies shares the same mind gestalt with the rest of itself.

==Lifespan==

It is estimated based on cellular regeneration rates in captured Worker specimens that an "individual" body can live up to 30 years. This is only a rough guess. The gestalt itself can live indefinitely, continuing to produce new bodies.

==Social Structure==

All Workers obey "The Will" of their Hive without question. Most Workers enjoy talking to themselves, but are just as friendly to other Workers. They tend not to like Warriors.

===Overview===

In 2323, the Home Defense Security force was given the job of creating a mobile medical team that could help the deathly ill refugees of Terra.  This job led to the creation of the Mobile Medical Corps, which in turn led to the development of the Narwhal Class Dropship.

[[Image:NarwhalClassLandingShip1.jpg]]

The MASH team is made up of five vehicles.  The Narwhal is a large vehicle for transporting the rest of the MASH team to and from a planet.

===Capabilities===

The Narwhal was designed by Blazton Industries with the same basic systems as the Whale, but its innards were custom built to carry four MASH teams.

The take-off and landing time of the Narwhal is a mere four minutes, much less than the Whale.  It's entire belly is designed to support the weight, so no landing gear need to be deployed.

The Narwhal only needs to dock with a Mercy Class Medical Frigate, simplifying the design process tremendously.  The combination of the already built Whale's design specs and the exact dimensions of the Narwhal made it possible to design and build five in two months.

[[Image:NarwhalClassLandingShip2.jpg]]

The Narwhal has magnetic and plasma shields, but not gravity shields.  Gravity shields are a purely military technology, and even though the MASH teams are technically military-funded, they are still a civilian-run force.  Only Ologrians and Volucris will attack a Narwhal or other medical unit, so defenses aren't quite so important.

The Narwhal has neither short nor long-range FTL systems.  It relies entirely on the Mercy Class Medical Cruiser for inter-system transport.

===Deployment===

The Narwhal is used to deploy four full MASH teams alongside regiments during a planetary assault.  It can also be used to evacuate large numbers of wounded in the event that the armies on the ground are overrun.

[[Image:NarwhalClassLandingShip3.jpg]]

A single Narwhal is deployed to every Mercy Cruiser.

The Milrok was the first post-Burning War aerospace craft built by the Lorithos. Designed to bridge the gap between the small, agile Vadrie and the massive Eituk, the Milrok is the chief space superiority fighter in the Lorithos Air and Space Tactical Force.

In terms of armament, the Milrok completely outclasses the smaller Vadrie. A pair of Turret-Mounted Medium Blaster cannons provides heavy firepower capable of tracking agile targets, and four Fixed Light Blaster cannons back up this turret with a lethal volume of fire. Six hardpoints, as on the Vadrie, ensure that heavier ordinance can be carried for use against larger targets.

Tzaiko Composite armor and a powerful Garvask shield are applied to the Milrok to a far greater degree than its smaller cousin. Because of this, the Milrok is known as a very rugged and survivable fighter, able to take a great deal of punishment and return pilots home safely.

The Milrok, like all Lorithos fighters, has a zero-emission pusher-type drive powered by a Hydrogen Engine.

Milroks were developed after the Burning Wars, and thus never saw combat during those dark days. The only full-scale combat Milroks have seen were in various battles in the Titanian system during the recent Exodus War. Although the Lorithos expeditionary forces took great losses during the course of the conflict, this was due mostly to bungling by Meroniri commanders. In one-on-one engagements and fighter-only combat, many Imperial pilots met their match (and their end) flying against Lorithos Milroks.

The ASTF keeps Milroks on all of its carriers, and in the Hightowers, as they are the primary fighters of the ASTF. The powerful fighters are used for space-superiority roles, as well as limited ground-support operations.

[[Ngrligru]] '''Mkuki'kali''' Energy Lances are incredibly powerful energy weapons. They have three gravity-generating coils to contain plasma. An additional coil is full of liquid hydrogen (LH2), which is supercharged with massive amounts of energy, causing ionization of the LH2.

The ionized LH2 flings electrons and protons everywhere, creating a swirling vortex of particles that will obey the gravity coils. The heavy protons are near cloud's center, and the electrons orbit the outside of the cloud. The cloud is pushed past the first coil and into the space between it and the second coil.

These coils are coupled together, so their gravity field bulges outwards in the space between the coils, creating a "bag" to hold the cloud. The cloud comes out of this pair of coils spinning very fast, a few billion times per second.

When ions are in the middle of the bulging "bag" between the gravity coils, the gravity field keeps the ions circling unhindered. If they stray towards the source, the ions are constricted, forcing a faster orbit--the ion is reflected back towards the other gravity coil without any energy used at all--it swaps its forward momentum into and back out of rotational energy, giving it 100% efficiency.

[[Ngrligru]] have completely BYPASSED Newtonian force exchange. This means that the weapon can hold any amount of plasma without having to increase the gravity field strength. All that is needed is "enough" power, and they can keep adding plasma to the chamber until the ions reach saturation and start bumping each other out of the gravity fields.

Of course, there is a third gravity coil--the aft array. It's used to modulate the plasma flow leaving the weapon barrel. There's a little quirk in plasma physics called the Alfvén speed. It works in reverse of the sound barrier in 21st century jet craft--without a fix, jets could not go faster than the speed of sound, because the cavitating exhaust would suck the engine backwards.

Likewise, the plasma stream of the Mkuki'kali will cavitate and tear the weapon clean off the ship. The last coil--the decoupling array--eliminates this cavitation, allowing the plasma to exit at proper velocities. There is still some recoil, because of the thrust of the sub-Alfvén plasma, but carefully modulated turbulence from the other two gravity coils keeps this interference at a minimum.

The result: a stream of plasma at extraordinarily high specific impulse. On average, the stream travels at 4,828,030 kilometers per hour, and has a temperature of 1,000,000 degrees Kelvin (1,754,000 degrees Fahrenheit). The plasma in the stream is still spinning. The stream is typically 5 centimeters in diameter, so the outer electrons are spinning at 46,671,000 kilometers per hour. At these speeds and temperatures, the hydrogen is actually beginning to undergo nuclear fusion.

On impact with the target, the beam sheds hard neutron radiation, like a thousand [[Neutron Cannons]] going off at once. When the [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' scientists experimented with the Mkuki'kali Energy Lances, they had problems with charge exchange.

When unionized hydrogen makes it into the plasma chamber, it gets hit by a high temperature proton, causing the unionized atom to give away its electron. A hot, uncharged atom isn't a problem for [[Ngrligru]] machinery--they use gravity to contain the reaction. [[Empire|Imperial]] scientists were using magnetic fields to contain the reaction. The uncharged hot atom was able to escape, and deposit its energy on all nearby structures. In other words, it killed the rest of the containment field, vaporizing the entire lab, then shed hard radiation throughout the entire area, resulting in the eventual destruction of everything aboard the space station.

There were no subsequent experiments on [[Ngrligru]] Mkuki'kali Energy Lances.

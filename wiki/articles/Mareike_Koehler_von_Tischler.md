''Mareike Aloisia Koehler von Tischler'' (born July 18, [[2295]]) is one of the more famous [[Mesarthrim]] field commanders to emerge from the bloody [[Second Exodus War]]. She earned the nickname "Bloody Mary" from her peers, and was famous for her foul mouth and irate temper in combat. During one particularly violent struggle, Mareike's tank was caught in a trap by [[Royal Imperial Army]] troops. Mareike escaped the tank alive, then led her crew in a daring strike to locate the [[Onyx Empire]]'s local commander. She ended up butchering him alive, spattering herself and the walls in his blood and earning her nickname.

===Early Life===

Mareike Koehler was one of the few [[Mesarthrim]] born free during the centuries of slavery under the [[Anderung]]. By the time of her birth, the city of [[Brünshelm]] was a completely free safe zone, with no [[Anderung]] anywhere within striking distance.

The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] completely swept [[Mesar]] of all [[Anderung]] presence by the time Mareike was 13 years old. Like most young [[Mesarthrim]], Mareike idolized the warriors, particularly the [[Myt'gaul]] and the fearless [[Andreas Tischler]].

===Working Life===

Mareike was something of an oddity among the devout and traditional [[Mesarthrim]]. While women were allowed to do any job they pleased, it was still a common belief supported by the [[Mesarthrim State Church]] that a woman's role was to be a good wife at home.

Mareike was a devout follower of the [[Mesarthrim State Church|Church]], but refused to marry and received special dispensation from her local Reverend Father to pursue a career. Mareike was a "Machtenkopf", or "gear-head", and loved to tinker with machinery. She was apprenticed to a mechanic shop, and eventually became a master mechanic and bought out the shop.

===Military Life===

After [[First Contact]] with the [[Onyx Empire]] in [[2322]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] remobilized for war, and the call went out for a fresh generation of warriors. The then 27-year-old Mareike was determined to drive one of the legendary [[Vulture Siege Tanks]].

Most of the [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clans]] would not accept a female tank driver, but the [[Tischler Clan]] agreed to accept Mareike into their [[Myt'kampfen]]. She served on a Dolch crew during most of the early [[Second Exodus War]].

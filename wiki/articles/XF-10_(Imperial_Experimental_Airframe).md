The F-4D and F-9 performed well up until the Mesarthrim introduced fighters with extremely high atmospheric speeds. The F-4D's top speed was around Mach 3, and the F-9's top speed was roughly Mach 4.5. Mesarthrim fighters could maintain speeds of up to Mach 20. The Empire began developing the XF-10 airframe as a testbed forScramjet drives (Scramjet), which they incorporated into the F-4E and F-9A, bringing their airspeeds up to Mach 18 and Mach 24 respectively.

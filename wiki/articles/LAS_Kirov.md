===Overview===

The exact origins of the ship now called Kirov are unknown.  What is known is that it is of Terran make, and has been in the possession of the Asanova family for at least 150 years.  Most historians agree that the Kirov was originally one of two ships: a Humpback class heavy hauler, or a Trinidad class deep space mining platform.  Both classes of ships were industrial vessels in production during the mid 22nd century.  However, the Kirov as it exists today is the product of over a century and a half of refits, overhauls, rebuilds, and upgrades. 

[[Image:LASKirov1.jpg]]

The modern Kirov serves as the flag vessel of the Asanova Territory's Black Fleet.  It is an awkward, hulking vessel that qualifies as neither the fastest, most nimble, nor most beautiful of starships.  However, it is quite powerful, and is the key projection of Asanovan family's power. 

===Capabilities===

As the sole heavy destroyer in the Black Fleet, the Kirov is armed with significant anti-warship weaponry.  A pair of "Scythe" Relativistic Mass-Drivers, built by Blazton Industries, and two Blazton Ashur Cannon twin-turrets make up the heavy armament of the Kirov.  The Ashur turrets can track vessels quickly, and the twin Scythe cannons are useful for bombarding enemies at extreme range. 

The Kirov is equipped with eighteen Fast-Tracking Advanced Neutron Cannons.  The ANCs, developed during the Kalidasan Reconstruction, are smaller and more efficient than those developed during the Phoenix Project, and they also have a faster recharge rate.  These cannons give the Kirov additional anti-warship firepower, and also, along with a dozen Anti-Aerospace Emplacements, give the Kirov extremely heavy anti-aerospace protection.  The larger number of AA emplacements is due to the role of the Asanovan warships--their usual enemies are small smuggling vessels or pirate fighters.  Brigands rarely have capital warships, so most Asanovan warships have a significant anti-aerospace armament. 

The Kirov is the only Asanova warship armed with Variable-Ordinance Tactical Missile Tubes.  Asanovan naval tactics involve closing to energy range quickly, so they are often considered unneeded.  However, the Kirov has sixteen tubes, and can use them for additional firepower against warships at all ranges, as well as for destroying aerospace craft at distances beyond cannon range.  

[[Image:LASKirov2.jpg]]

The Kirov is one of two Asanova warships that carries an onboard fighter complement.  A total of thirteen fighter squadrons give the Kirov a significant aerospace force.  

The Kirov is protected like a true naval flagship.  A thick layer of Boron-Nitride Composite Armor, in addition to an impressive mixture of Gradient Gravity Shields, Magnetic Deflector Screens, and a Barrier Plasma Shield make the Kirov a very well protected craft, typical of Asanovan design.  Its heavy defenses are made possible by sacrifices in the drive systems, which accelerate the destroyer at a rate similar to the Prefecture's Avenger class vessels, which have nearly twice the mass of the Kirov. 

===Deployment===

The Kirov rarely finds itself in combat with anything in the Asanova system.  Its normal role is to serve as an aerospace command post for Asanovan patrols.  In reality, its purpose is two-fold.

[[Image:LASKirov3.jpg]]

One is to deter potential attacks by hostile forces from outside the system, specifically the other families in the Sirius District, and the other is to remind those in the Asanova Territory where the true power lies.  Old as it is, the Kirov has the firepower to lay waste to any vessel in Asanova space, and would do so if the orders were given.

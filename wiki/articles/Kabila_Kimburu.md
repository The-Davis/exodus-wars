'''Kabila Kimburu''' is the only [[Ngrligru]] [[:Category:Nations|nation]] in the [[Orion Arm]].

=== History ===

Main Article: [[History of Kabila Kimburu|History]]

Kabila Kimburu was a powerful tribe among the [[Ngrligru]] in the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. During a particularly violent tribal war, the Kimburun were driven out of their holdings, and in desperation fled through the [[Teeth of Orion]]. In the year [[2271]], a single [[Nina'rikebu Class Battleship|Nina'rikebu]] entered the [[Lepodi Sector]], and begged for sanctuary from the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. The Meroniri escorted the Kimburun to the [[Titan System]], where the [[United Earth Alliance]] gave them the oceans and shorelands of [[Rhea]].

=== Government ===

[[Isilahi Kodwe]] is the "[[Kuu]]" of Kimburu. For thousands of years, the Rijali family led Kimburu, but they died out during the tribe's cannibalistic rebirth. [[Chonge Kodwe]]'s exploits in the [[Second Exodus War]], and later as a general of the [[Prefecture]]'s Legion of the Wolf won his family great renown back home, allowing his older sister Isilahi to win the reins of power.

=== Law ===

One of the founding principles of the new Kimburun government, a mandate that was forced upon them by the [[Meroniri]] was that they would never take up arms on their own behalf, nor would they ever have a standing military. All weapons technology was confiscated. While this law grates against the violent-natured [[Ngrligru]], they have been allowed to join foreign militaries.

The [[Colonial Commonwealth]] has some special forces units made up of Kimburuns, but they are not allowed to mix with human units.

The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] is much more open to the Kimburuns. [[Chonge Kodwe]] brought thousands of troops to their aid during the [[Imperial Civil War]], and earned a commission as a General in the [[Prefecture Legion Corps]]. Ngrligru are allowed to integrate with human troops somewhat, but are mostly segregated into their own infantry companies and brigades.

=== Foreign Relations ===

From their arrival in the [[Orion Arm]] until the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the Kimburun were a protectorate of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Their protectorate status now falls to the jurisdiction of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

Because they are forbidden from maintaining a military of their own, the bloodlust of the [[Ngrligru]] is difficult to sate. Many bands of Kimburun Ngrligru mercenaries work throughout the Orion Arm, and several thousand are citizens of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] and permanent members of its military.

=== Territory ===

The Kimburun control only part of the world of [[Rhea]]. They have sovereign control over the oceans and the shores, but their jurisdiction ends six kilometers inland.

=== Culture ===

The tribal traditions of the Kimburuns are barely remembered, but the highest families are still greatly respected among their culture. The Rijali were once the royal family, but died out during the rebirth of the tribe. Now the Kodwe family is the greatest and most revered among their tribe, mostly due to the work of [[Chonge Kodwe]] in foreign territory.

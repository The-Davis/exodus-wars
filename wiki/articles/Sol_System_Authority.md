===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Sol System Authority|History]]

The Sol System Authority is the governing force on [[Earth]], [[Luna]], [[Mars]], and [[Venus]]. With the planet essentially destroyed in the great [[Battle of Terra]] at the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], and the bulk of the survivors of [[Earth]] fleeing to the stars, the governing council is largely a ceremonial position.

[[Terrans]] are a tough lot, and their planet is more resilient than others. For some time as the dust settled, many thought that [[Earth]] would become another dead world like [[Lorithan]], but it has pulled through remarkably well and is well on the way to recovery. Fixing the planet's environment, economy and infrastructure is still an ongoing project, but the people of [[Earth]] have gained a lot of headway, and help is never far in the form of funding and material support from the [[Meroniri]], the [[Lorithos]], and the [[Ixoarchada]].

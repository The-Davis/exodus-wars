===History===
Main Article: [[History of the United Centusi States|History]]<hr>
After the fall of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] during the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] in [[2324]], the United Centusi States arose as the dominant power in the [[Mesarthrim]] cluster. Encompassing the [[Mesarthrim]] systems of [[Centus System|Centus]], [[Cirus System|Cirus]], and [[Tirshan System|Tirshan]], the United Centusi States rose up and brought order again to the [[Mesarthrim]] people. The warrior traditions of the [[Mesarthrim]] have been combined with democracy to form a stronger, more unified [[Mesarthrim]] nation.

The United Centusi States has also taken over the role of the former [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] in galactic affairs. The United Centusi States replaced the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] on the [[Alliance of Planets High Council]], and has succeeded the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] on the [[Prometheus Council]].

The legacy of the death of the late Uber-General [[Andreas Tischler]], the betrayal of many of the former [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] High Command, and the destruction wrought in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] have cast a terrible shadow on the future of the [[Mesarthrim]] people--a future no longer so bright as it seemed at the height of the Clans.

January 3, [[2324]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nearly a year into the aftermath of the Imperial occupation, Terra is finally showing the first signs of recovery.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Alliance of Planets has released a follow-up to their initial report.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;North America is still mostly uninhabitable. The Canadian wilderness housed the Emperor in the final hours of the occupation, and some of the heaviest fighting took place there. The entirety of both American/Canadian coastlines are still bathed in lethal levels of radiation. Refugee camps have been set up in the central plains, but a disturbingly-low number of people are coming in. It is estimated that 85% of the population of the North American population was killed in the fighting.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;South America is rated at 60% recovery. Refugee camps have evolved into full-fledged cities. The introduction of the Berkeley Reactor by the Prefecture Navy has made cheap and abundant power available for advanced construction machinery. The cities of Rio de Janeiro and Buenos Aires have both been restored to full capacity, but it will be a long time before they are productive industrial centers again.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Asia has suffered the greatest amount of tectonic damage. It housed multiple Imperial nuclear silos, all of which were detonated as the Imperial Army pulled out. Two thirds of the landmass of the Japanese islands sank into the Pacific Ocean. Over a million bodies have been recovered, but millions more have been lost at sea forever. The radiation poisoning of the region is confined underground, but every major city has been flattened by the weeks of earthquakes. Recovery is difficult, as the land is torn up and nearly impossible to build on. Erecting permanent structures is taking longer than expected.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Australian islands have been built up as the military base of operations. All Alliance of Planets member states have soldiers there, coordinating the recovery efforts. The civilian casualties were lower here than in any other populated landmass. Recovery is at 100%.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The African continent has recovered quickly. Plans are underway to terraform the ancient wastelands of the north into livable environments. Recovery is at 75%.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Europe has suffered more than any other continent. It has taken tectonic damage, and is literally soaked with lethal radiation. At this time, only heavily-shielded scouts are allowed into European land. Many refugees have been rescued, but many have died of radiation poisoning.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;To sum up, three continents have been largely recovered. With only a year to work with so far, this is an incredible accomplishment.

Set during the seventh and final year of the Tempest War

By James Davis

=====Stone Bridge=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Jones! Brinkley! 'Nades on the door!" Sergeant Rick Cuthbert waves his first Assault Team forward. The rest of the Squad One and the platoon hang back from the looming edifice in front of them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The two Marines charge the entrance. Private Frank Jones shoulder-slams the door, smashing it down. PFC James Brinkley tosses two grenades inside. They dive to either side of the entrance, wincing at the blast and the following screams.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There's at least four hundred people in this hotel!" Staff Sergeant Andrei Devoe leads to rest of the platoon to the entrance. "Most, if not all are armed. Check your targets if you want, but I advise against it. We got a job to do, so move! Move! Move!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Thirty Marines charge into the building, followed by Devoe. The first sight that greets them are the bodies. Half a dozen [[Mesarthrim]] lie, twisted and bleeding from shrapnel wounds.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sarge!" Brinkley calls. "There's a kid here. He's alive."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Damn," Sergeant Cuthbert growls. "There were women too. But look, there're rifles everywhere. They were all armed."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Shoot the wounded, and lets move." Devoe snaps. "This building is twelve stories tall, and probably filled with [[Mesarthrim]] and booby traps."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A soft, pinging sound is the first warning. "GRENADE!" Private Jones warns, leaping on the bouncing ball of death. The Marines scatter in the wide lobby, looking for cover. With a deafening roar, the High Explosive grenade incinerates Jones, barely muffled by his powered armor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Smith!" Sergeant Cuthbert yells. "LIGHT 'EM UP!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Corporal Nate Smith carries Squad One's MiD-7 SAW, and quickly puts it to good use. The weapon barks once, twice, then picks up momentum and sprays the landing above them with too many rounds to count. A scream marks the death of the Mesarthrim grenadier.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sergeant Cuthbert, you and Squad One with me. We're taking the second floor," orders Staff Sergeant Devoe. "Sergeant Fowler, you and Squad Two take the third floor. Sergeant Rogers, Squad Three to fourth floor. Keep in contact, watch for booby traps."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Squads Two and Three head up two separate flights of stairs. Occasional bursts of gunfire mark their progress.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Smith, you have point," orders Sergeant Cuthbert.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The big Corporal, and his lethal MiD-7 take the lead. The second floor is the landing above the lobby, where the Mesarthrim grenadier met his demise. "Nothing here Sarge," he reports. "We cleared this earlier."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Good," says Devoe. "Squad Two, report."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Third floor is clear," Sergeant Fowler's voice comes clearly over the microwave transceiver. "Privates Morris and Vaughn KIA. PFC Strong wounded, but mobile. Sent him to the ground floor. Twelve [[Mesarthrim]] down. Sir, there were five kids here."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Armed?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir, one of them shot Vaughn through the head when he tried to convince her to surrender peacefully."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Carry on then," Devoe scowls. "Squad Three, report."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Fourth floor is still contested!" Sergeant Rogers yells. "I count at least twenty-seven [[Mesarthrim]] here, mostly grown men. They're fighting hard, but they aren't armored."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighting continues, room by room. The [[Mesarthrim]] force the Prefecture Marines to pay for every inch with blood. One by one, the platoon troops fall, wounded or killed. At last, only the final floor remains.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Staff Sergeant Devoe surveys the carnage before him. Corporal Smith stands at his side, loading a fresh ammo drum into his MiD-7.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What a mess." Sergeant Cuthbert sighs. "One room to go. Sir, I have to ask. Are they fighting this hard because they're born warriors, or because they know we'll kill them anyway?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You know we don't kill unarmed civilians, Sergeant." Devoe replies. "But once they've taken up arms, there's no turning back." Devoe studies the last door, quietly thinking. "Sergeant, I'm taking Squads Two and Three back to ground level. We're getting our wounded and dead out of here. Just hose the room and come home." Devoe heads downstairs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Right," Cuthbert acknowledges. "Brinkley, open it up."<br>
PFC Brinkley tries the knob, and finds it unlocked. Sergeant Cuthbert steps into the room, seeing no threat. Smith and Brinkley follow.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ah, geez. Sarge, it's just a kid." Brinkley finds a small boy, cowering under a bed in the corner.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Armed?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Doesn't look like it." Brinkley grins. "Hey, come on out fella."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Gehen sie zur Hölle." The boy spits.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What'd he say?" Sergeant Cuthbert approaches the bed, leaning over to get a look at the small Mesarthrim.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sarge! Look out!" Corporal Smith enters the room, a scowl on his face. "Remember Vaughn!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As if on cue, the boy pulls a small remote from his pocket, and triggers it. An explosion rips through the floor, and Sergeant Cuthbert falls. The powerful, shaped charge blows out the threshold of the room, creating a gaping hole all the way to the foundation, sending the Sergeant plummeting down twelve stories to his death.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Brinkley narrowly escapes death by leaping onto the bed. Smith is knocked back out of the room, and he staggers to his feet, cursing.<br?
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The boy jumps from under the bed, and tries to edge his way out of the room. He's stopped by Brinkley's armored hand. "Devoe was right." he snarls, drawing his sidearm. "No turning back."

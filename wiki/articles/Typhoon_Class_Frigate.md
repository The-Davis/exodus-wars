===Overview===

The Typhoon Class prototype was first built in March of 2323, shortly after the Emperor's forces fled Terra.  Rahn Industries built it to fill the need of an armed heavy transport that could bring in the much-needed relief supplies for Terran refugees while fighting off bandits or Imperial stragglers.

[[Image:TyphoonClassFrigate1.jpg]]

The Typhoon Transport was pitted against Blazton Heavy Industries Whale Class Hauler, and the freight contract was awarded to Blazton after their hauler outperformed the Typhoon in landing and unloading times.  Justin Rahn isn't a man to give up a design contract easily, so he concentrated on the area the Typhoon had outperformed Blazton's Whale: combat.

===Capabilities===

The redesigned Typhoon was built to be a combat Frigate.  It is better-defended than the Stormer or Intrepid class frigates, and better armed.

The weaponry of a Typhoon is built mostly around the Neutron Cannon.  It has nineteen of these turrets, fifteen of which can protect the frigate's underbelly.  Typhoons are large enough to look like good targets for aerospace fighters, so fleet admirals use them to lure the fighters away from slower ships.  The Neutron Cannon turrets are used to take out fighters with excellent efficiency.

Typhoons do have a single Naval Autocannon available, but it is used mostly in planetary bombardment.  Typhoons are rarely used to attack warships.  That duty is saved for the smaller and faster Intrepids, much larger cruisers, or aerospace bombers.

[[Image:TyphoonClassFrigate2.jpg]]

Typhoons have ten missile launchers, but they don't have Apocalypse or Orca artillery missiles.  The Typhoon commander has an array of cluster missiles or smaller, non-artillery missiles for his launchers.

The Typhoon Class uses the three Phoenix Project shield technologies and advanced composite armor.  Its protection is equal to an Aztec Medium Carrier.  The Typhoon's gravity cavitation drives can accelerate it at up to 0.65 KPH/S.

===Deployment===

The Typhoon Frigate was well received by the Prefecture Fleet admirals, and was soon used throughout the fleets, particularly Jason Bremen's Second Fleet.

[[Image:TyphoonClassFrigate3.jpg]]

The original Typhoon prototype still exists.  The Typhoon itself is used as a weapons test platform, as its over-large hull can support large weapons.  The Jerome Black's huge mass drivers were tested using the Typhoon as a test bed.

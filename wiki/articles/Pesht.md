'''Pesht''' is the fourth planet in the [[Gamma Algedi System]].

== History ==

Like its neighbor [[Sindh]], Pesht was colonized by Pakistani and Indian nationals during [[2036]].

== Geography ==

Pesht has two continents, Shari and Balkh. The Balkh flatlands are a wide, grassy prairi, which is full of good sources of irrigation, making it ideal grain farming land.

The capital of Pesht is the city of [[Taluqan]].

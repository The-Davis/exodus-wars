The F-4S "Super Raven" was developed from the blueprints released by the [[Prefecture]] in [[2323]].

===History===

The Super Raven was developed for use by the main [[Sirius Families]]'s air forces.

===Development===

[[Korshov Airworks]] was chosen to develop the new variant

===Design===

The Super Raven was intended to improve on the original in every way possible.

====General Characteristics====

The Super Raven is characterized by a sleek, streamlined body, wide swept wings, cannards, and a single tail-fin. The Super Raven only has a single pilot and compact digital controls were praised for their comfort and ease of use.

====Performance====

The Super Raven marked a significant improvement on the Raven, but was not quite as good as the Piranha.

====Armament====

Every Raven designed and equipped for combat has always had at least two fixed cannon weapons. Earlier variants relied on laser-based beam cannons, while later variants were equipped with increasingly-advanced varieties of gauss cannon designs.

====Avionics====

The Super Raven kept the Raven's advanced avionics system, developing an intuitive interface which merged all of the controls into a single package integrated into the pilot's Heads-Up Display. The throttle was installed as a lever on the left-hand side of the cockpit, with rudder control pedals on the floor at the pilot's feet. The control-stick had an integrated weapons-selector switch and trigger, as well as sensor control buttons. The pilot rarely needed anything than these three main controls, but an optional control panel was installed on the right side of the cockpit for more advanced functions.

===Operational History===

After the [[Second Exodus War]], the Raven's schematics were given away by the [[Prefecture]] as spoils of war to anyone who asked. The [[Prefecture]] kept the design for their own use, and immediately produced a new variant that surpassed the best available at the time. Despite the fact that the shared design was slightly inferior, the [[Sirius Families]] adopted the design as their main [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]], and continued developing the line for their own use.

===Operators===

Air forces of the [[Sirius Families]] use the Super Raven.

===Variants===

There have been no variants of the Super Raven

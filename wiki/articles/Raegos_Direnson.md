'''Raegos Direnson'''
*Date of Birth: July 7, [[1516BC]]
*Date of Death: December 5, [[997BC]]
*Place of Birth: [[Kronheim]]
*Place of Death: [[Junheim]]

''Raegos Direnson'' (bord July 7, [[1516BC]], known later simply as ''Raegos'', was the second-born prince of the [[Ikronin]] kingdom during the beginning of the current Ice Age. Raegos led a slow-ship colony away from the planet, hoping to find a safe new world for his people. They eventually founded the [[Reigess Suverände]].

==Prophecy of Greatness==

When Raegos Direnson was born, the Skalds sang at his cradle of greatness. The Kejsare of the Ikronin at the time, Diren, believed in the truth of the Skalds' words, and was determined that Raegos would lead the Ikronin to a new era.

In [[1516BC]], when Raegos was born, Kronheim was locked in the grip of the Long Winter. Diren knew his people would not be able to change Kronheim's climate the way their Anthronoris ancestors did, but he had hope for a new colony in deep space. Early in his reign, Diren ordered the creation of the Naglfar, a cold-sleep ship designed to carry Raegos and thousands of Ikronin through space to a new world. Along with the Naglfar, dozens of smaller ships--a fleet known as the Renskader--were built to house hundreds of other Ikronin for the same journey.

Diren planned to raise Raegos to lead. Before his son could even walk, Diren gave him two Fenrulf pups, Stirka and Sötni (Sothark for Strength and Cunning). Raegos and his Fenrulfs were trained to fight, track, and work together as a team. Diren gave Raegos over to the tutelage of Njorl the Wise, a renowned Skald who told Raegos of the legends of the nobility and power of the Anthronoris. Raegos never forgot the tales, and privately vowed that he would find a new way to lead the Ikronin: one of valor and splendor rather than the selfish greed of the Ikronin that began with Tyrus Ikronus.

==The Journey That Made a Man== 

In 1502BC, when Raegos was 14 years old, Diren gave him the Korfangr, a battle rifle designed by the Kejsare of the Ikronin from five generations in the past. Diren told Raegos to live off the land for two years, and return.

Raegos, Stirka and Sötni went out into the wilds of Kronheim. His loyal Fenrulf companions were a great asset, often catching his food or guiding him to shelter in the middle of the storms that were growing far too common as the Long Winter worsened.

One day, six months into his journey, Raegos came upon an Ikronin man, slashed by wild beasts and freezing to death on the tundra. This man was Rejoc Arekson, one of the hunters of a small town called Oselo. Raegos wrapped Rejoc in his own cloak, and Rejoc revived enough to tell Raegos that he had been hunting with companions, but wild Fenrulfs had gotten the better of him. Rather than lend aid, Rejoc's companions stripped him of his possessions and left him for dead.

Raegos was made of nobler stuff than other Ikronin. He pulled Rejoc onto Stirka's back, and sent Sötni out to find food. Raegos and Stirka marched for three hours, taking turns carrying the wounded Rejoc. They found a sheltered valley where a canopy of evergreens kept out the snow, and before long Raegos had a roaring fire and Sötni had returned with a fresh kill.

For three days, Raegos and his Fenrulfs nursed the dying Rejoc back to health. When Rejoc recovered his strength and his wits, he was astonished that a boy Raegos' age would sacrifice so much time and risk so much to save a man he had just met, and to ask nothing in return. Rejoc pledged his life and service to Raegos, and swore that he and his family would serve Raegos forever on.

Together, Raegos and Rejoc returned to Oselo, only to find Rejoc had been twice betrayed. Rejoc left behind a wife and small child. He returned to Oselo expecting to find their joy at his return, but found instead that his companions had lied about his death, and one of them, Slanj Tyrsen, was already planning to force Rejoc's wife Elyse to marry him.

In a blind fury, Rejoc attacked Slanj in the middle of Oselo, beating him again and again. Slanj was bleeding copiously before Raegos caught up to Rejoc. In his rage, Rejoc did not hear the calm, commanding words of Raegos telling him to stop. Stirka and Sötni heard. Rejoc was ready to kill Slanj with his bare hands when he felt powerful teeth in each arm. The Fenrulfs made certain he heard the words of their master.

Raegos reminded Rejoc of his oath, and told him to stand before him. Rejoc, while raised to be selfish like most Ikronin, was an honorable man and true to his word. He released Slanj, and hearkened to the words of Raegos. Slanj tried to slink away, but a sharp word from Raegos had Stirka and Sötni growling in his face.

Then Raegos spoke: "My brother, this man has indeed wronged you, but his life is not yours to take. Will his shed blood undo the wrongs that he has done?"

Rejoc knew in his heart that Raegos spoke the truth, but his anger still burned hotly within him. "I cannot explain it, my lord, but I must strike true. My family has been violated and my trust has been betrayed."

Then Raegos said; "Like your life before, the life of this man is now mine, for I am the Fenig of the Ikronin. Strike true if you must, but now you must strike me. I have taken his guilt upon myself."

Rejoc protested, crying out "Lord! I cannot do this thing! I swore myself to your service!"

But Raegos was wrathful with Rejoc, and shouted; "Moments before you were ready to kill in the name of vengeance! I have named this man unto myself, as you named yourself and your family unto me! If you would have the blood between you redeemed, then strike, or be known a coward for the rest of your days!"

And Rejoc struck Raegos, a blow hard enough to fell a mighty tree. But Raegos stood unmoved, unwavering in his resolve, and spoke: "Now is your anger sated? If not, strike again."

But Rejoc's fury was quenched, and he knelt, weeping at the feet of Raegos, begging his forgiveness for the foolishness of his wrath.

Raegos raised him back to his feet, saying: "All is forgiven. Nevermore strike a man in anger, only strike in the name of justice." Then he turned to Slanj, and said "You have wronged your brother and fellow Ikronin. I have paid the price in your stead. What will you do now?"

Even Slanj, treacherous and merciless in his deeds before, was moved to tears at the mercy of Raegos. Like Rejoc before him, Slanj knelt at the feet of Raegos, and swore fealty to him.

==The Reigess== 

And so Raegos left Oselo, but he was no longer alone. Besides Stirka and Sötni, now Slanj and Rejoc travelled with him, though Rejoc's wife and daughter remained behind in Oselo. Together, Raegos and his companions travelled the length and breadth of Kronheim, and did many great deeds of mercy and valor. By the time a year had passed, Raegos had more than a hundred companions sworn to him, and the whole of Kronheim was telling tales of the boy prince who took no reward for any of his deeds. The name "Reigess" began to spread throughout the lands: the Reigess Companions was the name Rejoc suggested for their group, and their deeds were renowned above all others.

==The end of one journey, the start of another==

In 1500BC, Raegos' journey was at an end. He left a boy, but came back a young man, strong and hard from his journey. Stirka and Sötni returned with him, loyal and brave as ever. Raegos' companions were sent back to their families, but told to return to meet the Kejsare, Diren, and account for their deeds.

Diren was secretly pleased with the mercy his son showed, but they had violated the Rules of Trade and Equivalency established long ago by Yngvi the Aged. The rulers of the Jurekön demanded that Diren publicly denounce the altruism of the Reigess Companions, and with a heavy heart, Diren complied with their wishes.

It was time to give Raegos the rest of his heritage. In secret, Diren gave his son the Reigenjord, a powered armor suit that once belonged to Yngvi the Aged, which dated back to the days of the Anthronoris Empire. He confided his wishes for Raegos and the Ikronin, and Raegos readily agreed to lead the dangerous mission and the Renskader into unknown parts.

When the Reigess Companions came to meet the Kejsare, Raegos asked if they would come with him, and to a man they volunteered. The Naglfar was prepared, and soon volunteers who wanted to be a part of Raegos' greatness had come from all corners of [[Kronheim]]. The families of the Reigess Companions could not be denied, but so many had heard of the bravery and wisdom of Raegos that many had to be left on [[Kronheim]].

Raegos and Diren embraced one last time, and the Naglfar slipped away from [[Kronheim]], surrounded by the ships of the Renskader. Before sealing themselves in the cold sleep, Raegos spoke to the entire fleet: "I know not where we go, or what we shall find when we get there. Kronheim is lost to us, but we are the bold who will forge on anew. Today, we deny the name Ikronin. It is a name that honors the cruelty of Tyrus Ikronus, who took the secrets of long life to himself and watched with pleasure as his friends died one by one. This armor, Reigenjord, was worn by Yngvi, my ancestor, when he slew the betrayer Tyrus. Today, we take for ourselves a new name. Nevermore shall we be Ikronin. Henceforth, we shall be Reigen!"


==A New World== 

It was over four hundred years before the Renskader found a safe home, but when it did, Raegos and his followers awakened to find a brave new world. The Reigen people prospered under the rule of Raegos Direnson, and his loyal companions, the Reigess, ruled wisely and justly alongside him for the rest of his days. And to this day, the sons of Raegos rule the [[Reigens]], always remembering the laws of mercy and kindness their patriarch brought to his people.

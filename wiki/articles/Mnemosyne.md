'''Mnemosyne''' is a gas giant planet in the [[Titan System]]. It is an enormous gas giant, with over sixty moons. The Titanians have extensive mining facilities, which they use to exploit a wide variety of materials. Because of the extensive moon systems, the Titanians have managed to be self-sufficient since the colony was founded. There is no sign that the [[Anthronoris]] ever used any of Mnemosyne’s resources. Mnemosyne was the site of several key battles during the [[Onyx Empire]]’s thwarted invasion, and the Titanian fleet was able to avoid destruction by using Mnemosyne’s moons and gravity well to keep the Imperial Armada from overwhelming them.

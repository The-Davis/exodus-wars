The HMS ''Elgon'' was the ninteenth ship in the [[Vigilant Class Frigate|Vigilant]] class of frigates. It was given the hull designation FG-19.

The ''Elgon'' served as an escort ship for the [[HMS Secutor|HMS ''Secutor'']].  It was destroyed in the [[Second Battle of Tethys]] by the UEAS ''Haven'' on January 17, [[2316]].

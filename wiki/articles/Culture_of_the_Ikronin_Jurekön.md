[[Ikronin]] are not a relatively homogenous culture like most nations are. [[Kronheim]]'s seemingly permanent ice age, combined with an uneven wealth system and a star-faring technology level has led to classes so different that they are considered three distinct sub-races of [[Ikronin]]: the Tundra [[Ikronin]], who live on the bare tundra; the Equatorial [[Ikronin]], who live in the temperate zone of the planet; and the Arcology [[Ikronin]], who live in orbital space habitats.

===Architecture===

Ikronin architecture is simple, even dull.  They focus on blues and whites, which blend in well with their surroundings.  They prefer practical to beautiful.  Decor is always spartan, unless it involves expensive entertainment items or gadgets. Ikronin are very fond of home appliances.

===Social Structure===

Most of the time, Ikronin lead one of two common lifestyles.  They either tend to be loners, hunting and working on the tundra; or corporate employees, working in the slightly balmier equatorial regions or in space.

Ikronin are not particularly warlike, like [[Mesarthrim]] and certain [[Terrans]], but they aren't pacifists either.  They tend to keep to themselves, defending their homes and business interests--allies or resources--viciously.

Inhabited Ikronin worlds are practically nonexistent.  They colonize only briefly, stripping the resources they need, cleaning up the mess they make, and leaving.  Only [[Kronheim]] itself is a permanent home.  Because of this reluctance to expand aggressively, the Ikronin population is relatively small compared to the other humanoid races.

'''Tavou Imports Trading Group''' - Based on Tavou. Imports and markets [[Ixoarchada]] technology. It is a wholly-privatized and nationalized company: each of the republics within [[Taviridis Somarchada]] owns an equal share in the company. They carefully choose which technologies they market outside of their territory, balancing national security with fiscal policy.

The XH-3 was the first VTOL-dropship designed to carry troops to and from the surface at high speeds, and operate on the surface. Its ramjets had an unlimited power supply from its reactor, and it had enough reaction mass for two hours of sustained thrust in space from its ion propulsion system. The YH-3 was tested extensively, but the particle field generators failed in combat testing, costing several crews their lives. Instead, traditional wings were installed for atmospheric operations, which could be retracted in space to allow it to fit on carrier decks and cruiser welldecks. The design was finally finished, and designated the UH-3 Skyhawk.

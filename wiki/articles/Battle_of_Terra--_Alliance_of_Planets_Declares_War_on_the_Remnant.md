August 27, 2322

by Scott Sanburn

=====Sepulare=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For the first time since the Apöt virus crisis, the Alliance of Planets High Council released a statement to B-Net news.

***From the Offices of Prime Minister Darel Fusala***<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the past few months, there have been several developments in the war against the Empire. Normally it has been the policy of the Alliance of Planets to remain uninvolved in civil wars that of non-Member States. However, the Empire is hostile towards all of the Alliance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Because of this, the Alliance of Planets will give monetary aid to the Prefecture. We originally intended to limit our aid to funds and supplies, but with the arrival of the Remnant, the High Council has unanimously voted to send military force to Terra.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At this time we formally declare war on the Remnant for allying with the hostile Empire. We defy their demands for immediate surrender, and we will bring the fight to them.

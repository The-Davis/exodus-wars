The Mesarthrim Civil War was a highly localized attempted rebellion against the ruling Clan Lords by the clan-less Auellal political party following the death of Andreas Tischler. Despite success on the battlefield, the Auellal were outmanevoured politically by a group of rogue Clan Lords who won the right of succession for their new government, the Centusi States.

The main battlefield was localized around the metropolises; Stone Bridge, Black Island, Homemakers Field, and Brünshelm. During the course of the Civil War, the Clan Lords' military was constantly tied up in beauracy as individual commandants formed rogue elements in an attempt to secure power for themselves and their families.

The political maneourvering of the Centusi States secured the loyalty of these elements, denying the Clan Lords' much of their military strength. In the end, the Centusi States were able to use their political leverage, and expanding military might to force the Clan Lords' to accept their plans of succession - denying the Auellal all the progress they had fought for on the battlefield.

==Territory==
The territory contested in the Civil War was mainly military in nature. The Auellal League was predominately interested in forcing the Clans to uphold the reforms promised to them by Andreas Tischler, not destroying the government. To this end, their targeted attacks were meant to cripple the Clans military and domestic command structure until such a time that Uber General Francis would be willing to negotiate favorable terms to the clan-less Auellal.

In the end, rather then negotiate with the Auellal, Uber General Francis negotiated with William Camp, leader of the conservative Mesarthrim Rights political party. Their cooperation saw the formation of the Centusi States, and the defection of large numbers of Clan military assets. 
===Stone Bridge===
Ultimately, Stone Bridge was the key to success, and was the most bitterly contested. The Auellal were able to secure vast sections of the city, but were mere minutes shy of capturing the capital building. Auellal Pathfinders entered the Grand Chambers, right after TFVMS II was officially signed. Realizing they lost the battle of legitimacy, the Auellal evacuated Mesar before the new Chancellor could order his new navy back to Mesar, and cut off their supply line. The loss at Stone Bridge ended the Auellal's aspiration for equality in the Mesarthrim government, and led them to establish their own Mesrnak government.
===Homemakes Field===
Homemakers Field was a major strategic victory for the Auellal agains the Clan Lords allies - the Saints of Luther. The Auellal took control of the city after only a week of sustained fighting. The Clan Lords were unable to reinforce their garrison, and the Department of Internal Security fell into the Auellal's hands almost completely intact. The Auellal also secured the Intelligence Bureau - but the Monks of Luther were able to torch most of the campus first.
When the Auellal evacuated Mesar they took the entire security repository with them, giving their Ealadh a' cuilidh an incredible edge over the Mesrthrim in the Tempest War. 
===Black Island===
Home to the Heer, Myt'Herra, and the Luftwaffer, Black Island was an important target for the Auellal from the outset. By the end of the conflict the Auellal, and their allies the Liberator Sons, had successfully laid siege to the island, but never completely broken it's back. Over a thousand military officers died on the island from starvation and illness.
===Brünshelm===
Home of the Krigsmarine command post, the Auellal made the city a major priority. Also, due to strong anti-Mesarnak opinions, it was the hardest city to hold. Auellal occupation was bloody, and only served to drive a deeper wedge between the two groups. The Clan Lords Kriegsmrine was essentially absent in the Civil War. This was due in part to the heavy losses they suffered in the Second Exodus War, but also in the fact that William Camp was actively paying commandants to not respond to their command post.

==Legacy==
The Mesarthrim Civil war saw the end on the Clan Lords, and the rise of a more police state Mesarthim government. The Auellal political party also disbanded, forming it's own government, the Auellal League. Chancellor Camp used his absolute power to start the Tempest War - which would ultimately lead to the formation of the Mesrthrim Federation, and the Second major Mesrthrim Golden Age.

==Conspiracy==
There have been many questions raised over the Auellal withdraw from Mesar, and why they simply left, rather then capturing the TFVMS II delegation, and claiming control. A delegation that included Stadt, who was a member of TFVMS I & II, and would eventually found the Federation and use Cue's model of government as a base. The biggest question asked is why did Stadt and Cue let the Centusi states be formed, leading to a war costing millions of lives? 
Speculation ranges from Stadt wanting one last chance at glory, to Stadt and Cue playing Camp as the war mongering scapegoat in an attempt o gain more territory for the Mesarthrim - which in the end actually happened.

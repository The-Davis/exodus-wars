== Overview ==

The four months after the [[Second Battle of Tethys]] were busy, but not with military engagements.  Multiple refugee convoys from other Alliance worlds began arriving in the [[Titan System]].  Many were captured, but many more made it to the safety of [[Theia]] and [[Rhea]].

The Alliance provisional government ordered General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] to make protecting refugee ships as high a priority as possible. Alliance cruisers scoured the star system for incoming [[hyperspace]] signatures, and moved to intercept as quickly as possible. 

[[Royal Imperial Navy]] assets began to recognize the Alliance tactic, and started making frequent in-system jumps in hopes of diverting Alliance assets away from refugees and into potential traps.  After several near scrapes, General-Commander Sanburn tried to get the Alliance provisional government to allow his commanders to pick and choose their patrols more carefully.  He was turned down.

On April 1, [[2316]], one such interception ended in disaster for the Alliance. A major refugee convoy arrived in the system relatively near [[Tethys]].  Under their standing orders, four ships under the command of Admiral [[Gordon Dannins]] intercepted the hyperspace signature.  They found a convoy of six refugee ships, but were soon attacked by a carrier group led by Imperial Commodore [[Julia Michaels]].

=== Alliance Forces ===

The ships under Admiral Dannins command were from the 8th Combat Squadron, which had been operating in the outer rim planets without relief for months.  The UEAS ''Haven'', UEAS ''Bainbridge'', UEAS ''Spite of War'' and the UEAS ''Kitty Hawk''.

=== Imperial Forces ===

Commodore Michaels had a clear numerical advantage over Admiral Dannins' forces.  Her ship, the [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']], had a full carrier group escorting her.  She also had a full fighter complement at her disposal and her crews were well rested.

== Order of Battle ==

The Imperial carrier group's frigates closed to cannon range and fired a salvo at the convoy, but immediately withdrew when the Titanian cruisers counter-attacked. The UEAS ''Bainbridge'' and UEAS ''Spite of War'' moved to intercept the HMS ''Judicator'' while the UEAS ''Haven'' and UEAS ''Kitty Hawk'' attacked the Imperial frigates.

The ''Judicator'' fled at top speed, putting several million kilometers between itself and the convoy.  The ''Bainbridge'' and ''Spite of War'' kept up their pursuit, scoring several particle beam hits and managing to do significant damage to the carrier.  The Alliance cruisers were taken completely off guard when the Imperial carrier group's three destroyers attacked.  Commodore Michaels had held them in reserve, and her retreat had been a feint to draw the cruisers into an ambush.

The UEAS ''Bainbridge'' was struck by multiple Magnetic Accelerator Cannon shots and missiles.  The cruiser's shields failed, and the ship was gutted from one end to the other.  All hands were lost.  The ''Spite of War'' realized the danger and was able to escape close range under cover of the wreck of the ''Bainbridge''.  The ''Spite'' moved to regroup with the ''Haven'' and the ''Kitty Hawk'', but was overwhelmed by the Imperial destroyers' combined fire. The UEAS ''Spite of War'' suffered the same fate as the ''Bainbridge''.

Admiral Dannins realized that the odds were completely against him, but he refused to abandon the refugee convoy. The civillian ships required five minutes to power up their drives for the short-range hyperspace jump to Theia.  Dannins and his surviving ships gave it to them.

The crew of the UEAS ''Kitty Hawk'' was later commended for bravery above and beyond the call of duty.  The ship kept both the convoy and Admiral Dannins' ''Haven'' clear of missiles, swatting many of them with its particle cannons, and taking several other hits.  While the ''Kitty Hawk'' sacrificed itself on defense, Admiral Dannins went on the offensive, destroying three Imperial frigates and one of the destroyers.

Ultimately, the convoy escaped.  The ''Kitty Hawk'' took too much damage to escape.  Admiral Dannins ordered the crew to abandon ship and surrender.  The ship's commander, Captain Eric Stelan, surrendered to Commodore Michaels at 1100 hours Naval Time.

== Aftermath ==

While it suffered some damage, the UEAS ''Haven'' made it safely to the Alliance of Planets' forward garrison at [[Phoebe]].  Admiral Dannins immediately sent his report to General-Commander Sanburn.

The crew of the UEAS ''Kitty Hawk'' were taken as P.O.Ws and sent to Imperial prison camps. The ''Kitty Hawk'' itself was so badly damaged that Imperial engineers couldn't salvage anything from it.  Commodore Michaels towed it to [[Cronus]] so newer ship crews could use the hulk for target practice.

Immediately after he received the report of the loss of the ''Kitty Hawk'' and the ''Spite of War'', General-Commander Sanburn met in private with Premier [[Lawrence McNichols]], the head of the provisional government. The meeting was not recorded and no notes were taken, but after Sanburn left, he had full authority to run the military as he saw fit.  There would be no more futile attempts to intercept and escort refugee convoys.  They would make it to safety, or they would not.

Premier McNichols was so shaken by what transpired that his aides initially thought he had been assaulted, but he assured them that this was not the case.  The Premier never revealed what happened before his death at the hands of Imperial assassins, but he did tell his aides "I've never seen a man possess such cold fury. He didn't shout or raise his voice, and never made a single threat, but I've never been so afraid."

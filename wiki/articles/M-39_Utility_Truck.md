===Overview===

The utility truck is a death-trap on wheels, but it serves a vital role on the battlefield.  Without utility trucks, artillery pieces and ammo-reliant tanks and Rigs would be doomed to swift defeat.

[[Image:M35UtilityTruck1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The utility truck can carry Arrow IV ammo.  These heavy missiles are well designed, and don't explode without their triggers being armed.

The rest of the cargo is made up of ammo for SRMs, MRMs, LRMs, and cannons.  Unfortunately, these weapons are not as complicated, and will explode if shot with incendiary or energy weapons.  An ammo truck driver's best bet is to floor the gas pedal and pray if he's spotted by an enemy.

The cabin of the ammo truck is very well armored, so the return trip from reloading is usually safe.

===Deployment===
Utility trucks are deployed when supply lines are relatively secure, and when ammo-reliant Rigs such as the Ballista are being used on the front lines.  In emergency situations, the ammo truck will also be sent out when supply lines aren't so secure.

[[Image:M35UtilityTruck2.jpg]]

In very rare cases, ammo trucks have been sent as bombs on wheels.

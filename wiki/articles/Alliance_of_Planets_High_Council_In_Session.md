Alliance of Planets High Council In Session - 3/28/2324 - by James Davis

     In the aftermath of the Battle of Tavou, the Alliance of Planets High Council was called into session. They have just finished their meeting, and Prime Minister Darel Fusanla is ready to release the findings of the Council to the general public.
     Fusanla begins. "We've learned a lot in the last few days about the new threat to our peoples. What we thought was a mindless horde has shown signs of intelligence. We know they can change their tactics, we know that they can speak, and we know that they can use weapons.
     "For everything we know, there are more puzzles waiting for us to solve. What do they want? How do they gain their information on us? Where did they come from?"

     The president shuffles some papers before continuing. "The involvement of my home government, Meronir's Republic, has been lacking in this and other wars. We did not decide to become fully involved in the war against Lucius Black until it was over. Our internal bureaucracies kept us from launching our weapons into battle earlier.
     "I am proud to announce that the Republic has formed a new group of warships--the Expeditionary Fleet--to meet and deal with any threats to Meronir's Republic or other Member States of the Alliance of Planets. Effective immediately, the Mäg Ols and its escorts are relieved of their duties in the Armadas, and are to deploy to Tavou to investigate and track down the Volucris swarms."

     "The Terran plague is running its course, but is showing signs of slowing. It has been recognized, and will soon be dealt with in full, but details cannot be released yet. Professor Hlow will personally deliver his report as soon as he can lift the quarantine of Terra--we believe it will be within a standard Terran week.
     "The Alliance is strong. We will not falter in our goals of peace and prosperity for all peoples. Good night." The president bows to the assembled reporters, then retreats into the High Council chambers.

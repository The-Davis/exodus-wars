*Speed: 310 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament: 1 Machine Gun (500 rounds) - Optional
*Communications System:  Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Standard 

===Overview===

Perhaps the most numerous craft to come out of the massive reconstruction program by the Kalidasa Planetary Authority is the Albatros Light Transport.  This design is a light, fast, utilitarian craft capable of carrying three passengers, safely, at high speeds.  It has become the mainstay utiliarian craft of not just the Kalidasan Security Forces, but also local police and medical teams. 

[[Image:AlbatrosLightTransportVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Albatros is designed as a utility craft that can quickly and safely transport a small number of personnel or cargo from place to place.  In the event that it is used in a more hazardous area, an automated Light Machine Gun Turret can be fitted under the nose.  The turret can also be manually controlled by one of the passengers in the back via a HUD jack into a standard infantry trooper's helmet.

[[Image:AlbatrosLightTransportVehicle2.jpg]]

With a top-end speed of over 300 kilometers per hour, the Albatros is designed for speed over armor for its own defense.  It is covered with Boron-Nitride Armor, and has a light shielding system, but is my no means a combat craft.  That being said, it is quite a simple craft, and can take a substantial amount of abuse before there is any damage that would prevent its operation. 

===Deployment===

[[Image:AlbatrosLightTransportVehicle3.jpg]]

Albatroses are the general purpose transports for the Kalidasa Security Force.  They transport cargo, personnel, and light weaponry wherever needed, act as first-response vehicles in disaster relief, and are used additionally as police vans, medical transports, and courier vehicles.  Their main combat-related role in the KSF is the moving of KSF troopers into and out of combat zones.  They would never be deployed in open field combat, but they can operate as city patrol vehicles because of their ability to cover large amounts of city very quickly.

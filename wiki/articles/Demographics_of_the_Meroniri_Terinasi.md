==Religion==

[[Meroniri]] religion is very unregulated and almost casual. Most [[Meroniri]] believe in pantheism, that every molecule of the universe has a spark of the divine within it, and the more complex the organism, the greater the spark. They believe that on occasion, an "avatar" of the universe will manifest within a sentient being to bring about a great change, and based on the personal character of that avatar, the change will be for a greater good or a greater evil.

To the [[Meroniri]], there are four divine forces in the universe: creation, entropy, chaos, and order. None of these forces is inherently good or evil. Creation is the flow of life, matter and energy into the universe. Entropy is the demise of life, matter and energy. Chaos is the inherent uncertainty of existence as a whole, while order represents the natural tendency of intelligent beings (ergo, greater sparks of the divine) to change existence to fit their needs.

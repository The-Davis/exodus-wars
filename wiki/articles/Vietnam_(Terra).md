Vietnam is the easternmost nation on the Indochina Peninsula. It is bordered by [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|China]] to the north. On the country's east coast lies the South China Sea. The Republic of Vietnam was formed when the [[France (Terra)|French]] government defeated the Viet Minh in the [[Vietnam War]]. The country was dissolved on July 12, [[2018]] and assimilated into the [[United Earth Alliance]].

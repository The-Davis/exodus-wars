The M-8c was a nasty variation of the standard M-8. It was first suggested by [[Royal Imperial Army]] soldiers fighting their way through bunkers on [[Terra]]. They wanted incendiary charges to send down in front of them to burn out the [[United Earth Alliance]] defenders. The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] tested and eventually approved a grenade design with a [[white phosphorous]] filling.

Unlike most grenades, the M-8c was not a solid metal sphere blown apart by an explosive charge. Instead, it was a composite sphere, and the detonator released a chemical which disintigrated the composite. The phosphorous then ignited and spread over its target.

The M-8c remains in use today, and is popular in urban warfare and bunker clearing.

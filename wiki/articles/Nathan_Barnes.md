'''Nathan Barnes'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Nathan Barnes'' is a pilot in the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]'s Air Command. He serves in the [[First Fleet of the Line]]. He was also an ace in the [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] before the [[Imperial Civil War]].

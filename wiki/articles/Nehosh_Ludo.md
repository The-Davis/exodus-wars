''Nehosh Ludo'' was a wastelander, born to unknown parents in unknown parts of the barren and hellish landscape of [[Lorithan]].  He became part of the elite [[Shadan Nahru]] special forces unit.

== Volucris War ==

If not for his participation in the [[Volucris War]] Nehosh Ludo's name would be almost completely unrecognized.  When [[Jiliur Raegos]] began leading multiple sorties into the Lorithos wastelands to look for survivors of the [[Volucris]] predations.  Nehosh joined them as a guide, and concealed his background in the Lorithos special forces.

During battles with the Volucris, Nehosh would slip away unnoticed and attack from the rear using sniper fire or rigged explosives.  The smarter Volucris were too wary to be caught by his tactics, but Nehosh was too canny to mistake a smart Volucris breed for a stupid one.

Jiliur's skald, Seil Helden nav Riimar, noticed Nehosh's contributions and made note of it in his later epic, but respected the wastelander's privacy and did not reveal his brave deeds to Jiliur or his men.

Nehosh Ludo openly fought for the first and the last time in the battle of Rikaz Jael, where he was killed by the overwhelming numbers of Volucris.  Other than his mention in the epic, he received no eulogy or recognition for his bravery.  It is unknown if he had any surviving family.

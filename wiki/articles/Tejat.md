'''Tejat''' is the third planet in the [[Theta Algedi System]]. It was colonized in [[2195]], largely by [[Terran]] immigrants from the region of India, on [[Earth]].

== History ==

During the expansionist period of [[Earth]] in the [[22nd Century]], the [[Theta Algedi System]] was charted by the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Survey flights and geological expeditions in [[2180]] determined that the third planet, named "Tejat" after chief geologist Dr. Avinash Tejat, was an ideal location for a self-sufficient colony.

The first flights from [[Earth]] arrived in [[2195]], and the city of Kerala was established as the capital. The planet was formally made a member of the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2200]], and elected its first [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] in [[2201]].

Tejat was an economic and social mecca in the [[United Earth Alliance]] for well over a century, serving as host to [[Lorithos]] and [[Ikronin]] visitors due to its proximity to the [[Njiorin Sector]]. During the [[Heritage Crisis]], the cold war between the [[Ikronin]] and [[Reigens]] in the early [[24th Century]], a lot of this traffic subsided.

When [[Earth]] was invaded by the [[Onyx Empire]] in [[2315]], Tejat suddenly saw itself receiving thousands of frightened refugees and displaced political and military leaders. The population watched in fascinated horror as [[Holt]] fell as well, then prepared for their own invasion.

On August 7, [[2315]], Tejat was invaded by the [[Onyx Empire]]. Despite a valiant effort by the handfull of [[United Earth Alliance]] warships available to them and their military assets on the ground, the planet was doomed. The Premier of Tejat, [[Chetram Singh]], surrendered to the [[Onyx Empire]] on August 29, [[2315]].

Despite their defeat, Tejat was the site of many [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] cells throughout the rest of the [[Second Exodus War]]. [[Talaris Roi]] became a well-known leader in the resistance, and became famous throughout the [[Sol Sector]] for her inspirational speeches and songs. She was so hated by the [[Onyx Empire]] that at one point the [[Omegas]] began a door-to-door sweep and execution of the entire city of [[Damanir]], on a tip that she was currently hiding there. Only the direct intervention of the [[Royal Imperial Army]] kept the [[Omegas]] from inciting an open rebellion. This [[Damanir Incident|incident]] was the only time where the [[Omegas]] were openly defied by the rest of the Empire at large.

After the end of the [[Second Exodus War]] in [[2323]], Tejat elected to remain under the jurisdiction of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. This was largely due to the campaigning by [[Talaris Roi]], who was an outspoken admirer of [[Anthony Hadrian]]'s leadership.

== Geography ==

Tejat has four continents, covering a wide range of biospheres and climates. The largest is Orissa, covering almost a quarter of the planet's surface. Tripura and Mizoram are relatively equal in size, and together are nearly as large as Orissa. Jharkhand is the smallest continent, but is noted for its comfortable biosphere and as the location of the capital city, Kerala.

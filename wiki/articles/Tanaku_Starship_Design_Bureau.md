The Tanaku Starship Design Bureau was one of three design boards run by the [[United Earth Alliance]], along with the [[Graham Starship Design Bureau]] and the [[Langenacht Starship Design Bureau]].

Tanaku was noted for developing the [[ADF-5000 Class Cruiser]].

== Structure ==

Officially, a member of the [[Meroniri]] armed forces is an Asetari.

Joreno is a mythological figure within [[Meroniri]] culture.  He or she was an avatar of entropy, believed to have been born at the height of the [[Sundering]]. According to the myth, Joreno absorbed the entropic force of the deaths of billions of sentients during the cataclysm, and ascended as an avatar of the universe.  Joreno supposedly ushers the souls of the dead to their fate, whatever it may be.  [[Meronir Tronit]] founded Aseta Joreno from the remains of the [[Anthronoris]] military. Legend today says that [[Meronir Tronit|Meronir]] actually knew Joreno in person. 

Each Aseta is commanded by the Asetarun, a hereditary position handed down over the millennia. [[Meronir Tronit]]’s line remained unbroken for thousands of years, but eventually his line died heirless in 7,000BC.  A new Asetarun was adopted into the Tronit family, and the line continues on. 

Aseta Joreno is outfitted with twenty fleets of the line, which are armed for offensive war strategy.  They are also responsible for the defense of [[Sepulare]], and have fifty stations spread throughout the territory of the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]].

== Organization ==

Aseta Joreno is a fleet-based command force, with command of each fleet given to its respective Marsens.  In the event of all-out war, Aseta Joreno commands the remaining Aseta.  Fleets of the line are each made up of ten war cruisers and twenty escort cruisers.  A Marsen commands a war cruiser as flagship of the fleet. 

A battle group is a team of a war cruiser and two escort cruisers.  These are deployed on regular patrols away from the fleet, and have enough firepower to take on an Ngrligru force three times their size, and a Terran force ten times their size.

An air group is a station, warship, or regional defense organization level for combat and transport aircraft.  Escort cruisers have a single air group, usually fighters and interceptors.  War cruisers have six air groups, with enough landing craft to launch their extensive troop complement to the surface of a planet. 

An infantry command is assigned to a war cruiser. Joreno infantry is equipped with the Araros combat system, a combat suit capable of limited flight, and able to be equipped with a wide variety of weaponry and defenses.

== Special Forces ==

Aseta Joreno has the only special forces, known as the Joreno Yarin, named for horrid creatures in Meroniri myth which could kill the soul of a human and leave their empty shell behind.  The Yarin are based on a single war cruiser, which can deploy anywhere in the known galaxy at a moment’s notice.  While extremely well-equipped, the Joreno Yarin are actually poorly trained compared to the special forces of other human nations.  To make up for the flaws, they frequently train in low-tech environments to avoid atrophying their basic combat skills. 

== History ==

Aseta Joreno was founded before the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]].  As the oldest school in the Yatisu, the Joreno enjoy the respect of the remaining schools, though they have intense rivalries with the [[Aseta Serada|Serada]] and the [[Aseta Salfore|Salfore]].  The Joreno have the coveted duty of protecting [[Sepulare]] itself. 

The current Asetarun is [[Therion Tronit]], who has been known to leave [[Meroniri]] territory to train with other militaries. He is not welcome in most circles, but has been able to pay off many distinguished military professionals into training him in private.

== Uniforms ==

All Yatisu have a single uniform, but wear different colors and emblems to show their school of combat.  The Yatisu Combat Uniform is a technological wonder, equipped with portable shield generators, pressure regulators, and environmental protections far beyond anything lesser civilizations have been able to produce.

The Combat Uniform starts with a skin-tight black undershirt and black pants. The material is a natural disinfectant, which destroys odor-causing bacteria. It also neutralizes body oils and is designed to channel sweat into small reservoirs, where it is purified and injected back into the body.

The next layer is a thin kinetic armor, capable of deflecting most bladed weapons and small arms weapons. The armor, while incredibly tough, is almost weightless.  A soft power-cell is installed within the armor, nestled in the small of the back, where it both powers the uniform’s functions and cushions the lumbar region.  The power cell is hooked up to a light field generator, which can deflect heavier weapons like infantry-level Blazers, rifles, and light explosives. It is also capable of containing an environment for vacuum operations, though the air supply only lasts for minutes at a time. EV suits are preferred, the field is only for emergency decompression.

A long-sleeved uniform shirt goes over the armor, power source and field generator. The shirt is a simple fabric, with only one color. Aseta Joreno wears red uniform shirts, which have a rank patch on the left breast and a school patch on the right.  A shoulder rig is worn over the uniform shirt, where a communicator and Arcer energy pistol is stowed.

Loose-fitting cargo-style trousers are worn over the legs.  By default, the pants are the primary color of the school (black in the case of the Joreno), but can activate a 500-color digital camouflage pattern, which changes based on input from optical sensors strategically located throughout the garment.  The pockets usually store extra energy cells for the Arcer pistol and the communicator.

A loose-fitting jacket goes over the upper body ensemble.  It’s typically worn with its sleeves elbow length, but they can be pulled down to the wrists.  A high collar contains additional armor to protect the neck, and a small wireless rig that the communicator can use to interface with wetware and headgear implants.  The jacket is usually unfastened and opened in the front, giving the wearer easy access to their concealed pistol and communicator, but can be closed so its camouflage pattern can cover the entire body. The jacket is black by default, but has the same variable-pattern technology as the pants. 

The uniform headgear is a black beret, which has the Meroniri Yatisu emblem.  The headgear contains a secondary power cell, as well as wetware plugins which provide navigation and weather data if satellites are available.  Most standard implants include small optical implants on the outer edge of each eye, which project a small field over the eyes which can magnify down to a microscopic level, or provide data from x-ray, gamma ray, infrared and ultraviolet sensors implanted under the skin.  The back of the neck has a wetware jack for computer interfaces, which rival the best [[Ixoarchada]] interfaces in terms of complexity.  The beret can be pulled down into a hood, which can then cover the wearer’s head in a camouflage pattern.

Black gloves cover the hands, and include special polymers which allow the glove to grip even to smooth surfaces.  The gloves include the camouflage pattern technology, and are lightly armored.  The gloves have light sources which can be activated by wetware, so only a mere thought is needed.  Black boots cover the feet, and include the same polymer as the gloves.  Additionally, they contain a shock absorber, allowing the wearer to absorb the impact of a fall from a hundred meters without a jolt.  The boots can be camouflaged like the rest of the uniform.

To prevent officers from being readily identified by enemies, there are no rank patches visible to the naked eye. Instead, the wearer’s identification implant includes their rank, and their optical implants display the rank emblem of everyone with a chip on the shoulder, regardless of what they are wearing.

== Vehicles ==

Joreno use vehicles to some degree.  The Araros Combat Armor fills more roles than any vehicle in most militaries.  It is a highly-mobile, variable weapons platform.  Depending on the add-ons attached, it can be capable of sustained flight, heavy direct fire, extremely powerful indirect fire, and generating incredibly powerful barrier fields.

== Aircraft ==

All of the Aseta maintain extensive air commands, with fighters, bombers, interceptors, and troop transports.  Fighters in particular fulfill multiple roles, and are used by most of the Aseta more extensively than anything else.

== Ships ==

The names of commissioned ships of the Yatisu begin with “MTE”, designating “Meroniri Terinasi Enari.” Each ship is given a letter-based hull classification symbol to indicate the vessel's type and a hull number. The names of ships are officially selected by their commanding officers, but must not repeat any other ship’s name.

The Aseta use two classifications of combat vessels; war cruisers (YE), and escort cruisers (SE).  There are many classes of each type.  War cruisers are any ship equipped with MEC bombs and at least 800 meters in length.  Escort cruisers cover everything smaller, but there are none smaller than 300 meters in service.  Both classes of ship are capable of carrying fighters, but most escort cruisers only carry twenty or so fighters and no bombers.

== Weapon Systems ==

The Aseta in the Yatisu use many weapon systems, focusing on energy cannon weapons, but backed up by kinetic weapons and explosives. 

The coilgun is the primary kinetic energy cannon, firing a microscopic projective but delivering massive amounts of kinetic energy.  Arcer rifles and cannons provide the main energy punch for ground-based weapons, and are used on fighters and warships.  The main warship cannon is the Riptide Cannon, which uses hyperspace to bypass many shield technology and utterly destroy most forms of matter. 

Explosives are usually small antimatter charges, or large antimatter charges.  The Meroniri are the only civilization with enough antimatter to use it as an explosive and a power source instead of simply a power source.  Their most powerful weapon of all is the Matter to Energy Conversion Bomb, which completely energizes all matter in a continuous reaction.  If fired into a star, the MEC bomb will destroy the entire star system.  The MEC bomb’s use is restricted to only the most trusted commanders, and can only be deployed in extreme conditions or under the direct orders of the Thenaro.

== Training ==

Training Asetari for Aseta Joreno begins with four years of Technological Maintenance Training, followed by two years of Combat Training, followed by Advanced Training in their primary job field at any of the training centers the Joreno offers. The length of time spent in Advanced Training depends on the job of the Asetari.  Aircraft pilots have the longest training, at two years.

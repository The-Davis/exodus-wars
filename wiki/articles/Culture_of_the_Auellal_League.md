==Education==

Children in the [[Auellal League]] begin school at the age of four, and continue through five years of basic grammar school. From there, they receive eight years of specialized studies in a variety of fields, ending their last two years in electives of their choosing. Then students move onto three year institutions where their elective is fully realized, and they are trained to join the work force. Students must demonstrate competence of required material before moving between the three grades of schooling.

All [[Auellal League|Auellal]] Education is subsidized by the central government, with almost a sixth of the entire nation's budget going to education and education related expenses; including labs, schools, salaries, libraries, museums, and interactive training environments. The [[Auellal League]] enjoys a exceptionally high level of academic achievement.

Individual City-States may offer private schooling, but there is usually a supplemental cost to families who use the City-State school system.

== Events of 1949 ==
===January===
*January 20 - Elected [[United States (Terra)|U.S.]] President [[Thomas Dewey]] begins his first term.
*January 25 - [[Cold War]]: Forces from the [[Communist]] Party of [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|China]] and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] enter Peking.

===February===
*February 8 - [[Cold War]]: Cardinal Mindszenty of [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]] is executed for treason against the [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungarian]] [[Communist]] government.

===March===
*March 2 - [[The Technology Race]]: The B-29D Superfortress ''Lucky Lady II'' under Captain James Gallagher lands in Fort Worth, Texas after completing the first non-stop around-the-world airplane flight.
*March 25 - [[Cold War]]: The extensive deportation campaign was conducted in [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania]]. The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] authorities deported more than 92,000 people from Baltics to remote areas of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

===April===
*April 4 - [[Cold War]]: The North Atlantic Treaty is signed in Washington, DC, creating the NATO defense alliance. 
*April 20 - [[Cold War]]: Royal Navy frigate HMS ''Amethyst'' goes up the Yangtze River to evacuate [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]] refugees escaping the advance of the Mao's [[Communist]] forces. Under heavy fire it runs aground off Rose Island.
*April 26 - [[Cold War]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] troops kill over 300 [[Communist]] [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] while rescuing the HMS ''Amethyst''. The ship succeeds in its mission of rescuing [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]] refugees.
*April 23 - [[Cold War]] [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] [[Communist]] troops take Nanking.

===May===
*May 11 - [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]] is admitted to the [[United Nations]] as its 59th member.
*May 12 - [[Cold War]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] lifts its Blockade of Berlin. 
*May 22
**[[Cold War]]: After two months in Bethesda Naval Hospital, James Forrestal is murdered by KGB operatives.
**[[The Technology Race]]: [[EDSAC]], the first stored-program computer, begins operation at Cambridge University.

===June===

===July===
*July 20 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]] and [[Arabia (Terra)|Syria]] sign a truce to end their nineteen-month war.
*July 27 - [[The Technology Race]]: Initial flight of the de Havilland Comet, the first jet-powered airliner.
*July 31 - The HMS ''Amethyst'' completes its mission up the Yangtze River and successfully rejoins the fleet at Woosung the next day. With most [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]] citizens evactuated, the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] withdraws its forces from [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|China]].

===August===
*August 29 - [[Cold War]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] tests its first atomic bomb.

===September===
*September 13 - [[Soviet Union]] vetoes [[United Nations]] membership of [[Ceylon (Terra)|Ceylon]], [[Finland (Terra)|Finland]], [[Iceland (Terra)|Iceland]], [[Italy (Terra)|Italy]] and [[Portugal (Terra)|Portugal]].
*September 24 - [[Cold War]]: Laszlo Rajk, ex-foreign minister of [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]], is executed by the [[Communist]] Party as a [[CIA]] informant.

===October===
*October 1 - [[Cold War]]: Birth of the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]]. [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] immediately recognizes their legitimacy and sends additional troops to aid the ousting of the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] nationalists.
*October 16 - [[Greek Civil War]]: Civil war ends in [[Greece (Terra)|Greece]]. [[Communists]] victorious.
*October 17 - [[Cold War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] [[Communist]] troops take Canton.
*October 27 - [[Cold War]]: [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] [[Communist]] troops fail to take Kinmen in the Battle of Kuningtou. With Soviet aid, [[Communist]] [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] advance towards Taiwan unhindered.

===November===

===December===
*December 8 - [[Cold War]]: Nationalist [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] evacuation to Taiwan fails when the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] Navy blockades the island. [[Communist]] [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] capture all remaining Nationalist forces on the mainland.

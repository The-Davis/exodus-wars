Energy Weapons The Phoenix Project completely revamped the most basic weapon used by [[Empire|Imperial]] forces: the laser. Lasers are outdated. Most shields can ignore them completely, and now many forms of ablative armor can shrug them off. Several new weapons have been developed to replace them.

The Neutron Beam Cannon uses a cyclotron to accelerate deuteron (Hydrogen-2 isotope) particles. These rapid-moving particle streams are split by magnetic fields into two separate beams, one of protons and one of neutrons. These beams are projected onto the target. Most shield technologies deflect the protons, but the neutrons hit the target, causing impact damage and creating isotopes of the matter in the target, in most cases causing the material to become intensely radioactive. There are three firing modes.

In Flood mode, the Neutron Beam Cannon fires the beams in a wide spread, but at low speeds. So many neutrons impact the target, that no matter how dense the armor is, some neutrons get through to kill crew and damage internal systems. Flood mode does little to damage the structure and armor of the target, and only hurts the internal workings. Flood mode is slow enough that it is possible to dodge if the target's navigation computers pick it up and avoid it. Due to the heavy splash damage, flood mode is not used on inhabited planets.

In Intense Fast mode, the Neutron Beam Cannon fires the beams in a tight spread at super-fast speeds. The neutrons impact the target with such incredible force that it creates gamma rays and other types of radiation within the target. It fries the target inside, and blasts massive holes in the hull. If the neutrons hit the fusion mass of the reactor in the target, the resulting reaction is catastrophic.

In Intense Slow mode, the Neutron Beam Cannon fires the beams in a tight spread at speeds about the same as a bullet out of a gun. The neutron beam hits the armor and creates isotopes of the matter in the armor, irradiating the armor. The structure of the target deteriorates after multiple hits. This form is closest in function to the old laser weapons, as it does structural damage only. There are four types of Neutron Beam Cannons, which are used on a range of war machines, from tanks to warships. Small Neutron Beam Cannon - The Small Neutron Beam Cannon is the successor to the Small Laser. It can only fire in Intense Slow Mode. Medium Neutron Beam Cannon - The Medium Neutron Beam Cannon is the successor to the Medium Laser. It can fire in Intense Slow Mode and Intense Fast Mode. Large Neutron Beam Cannon - The Large Neutron Beam Cannon is the successor to the Large Laser. It can fire in all three modes. Naval Neutron Beam Cannon - The Naval Neutron Beam Cannon is the successor to the Naval Laser. Unlike the other types of Neutron Beam Cannons, it doesn't bother with cyclotron-accelerated Hydrogen-2. It simply fires pure beams of neutrons with a gravity-powered launcher. This version of the cannon can only fire in Intense Fast Mode.

Particle Projection Cannon (PPC) - The Phoenix Project version of the PPC has the heat output and energy requirements of the standard Gen2 PPC, but the extended range and boosted power of the Gen2 ER-PPC. The principle remains the same: electrified particles are propelled by magnets towards a target. The resulting shock is very damaging to most systems, particularly organics like unarmored infantry or Volucris.

Cyclor - The Cyclor weapon is an extremely efficient weapon of destruction. Three particle acceleration magnets are used to contain a full-fledged nuclear explosion. The nuclear blast is then directed at an enemy target. The Cyclor has double the range and power of a Naval Neutron Beam Cannon. Unfortunately, the energy requirements are beyond the scope of most ground vehicle reactors. The extraordinary cost of the Cyclor also makes it difficult to field many of them. A Cyclor's cost is comparable to that of an entire 21st century Naval fleet with supporting aircraft.

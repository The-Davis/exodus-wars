The '''Sirius Incursion''' refers to the failed invasion of the [[Sirius Cluster]] by the [[Onyx Empire]] in January of [[2319]].  Officially classified as [[Operation Lawless]], it was the opening salvo in the Empire's spring offensives.  However, what was supposed to be a relative cakewalk turned into a complete rout of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], the first major loss suffered by Imperial forces.  More importantly, it diverted substantial fleet and army assets from the [[Titan System]], which ultimately led to the collapse of [[Operation Tartarus]] and the negotiation of the [[Titan Peace Accords]].

== Background ==

By the end of [[2318]], the Onyx Empire had consolidated control of Terran-colonized space.  Lucius Black's imperial holdings encompassed nearly the entirety of the [[De Mairan Sector|De Mairan]] and [[Sol Sector|Sol]] Sectors, and extended into the [[Njiorin Sector|Njiorin]] and [[Pelagrim Sector|Pelagrim]] Sectors at the extremes.  In all of Terran space, there were but two exceptions to the Emperor's rule--the distant Titan system and the Sirius Cluster in the heart of the Sol Sector itself.  The last bastion of the deposed [[United Earth Alliance]], and home to the remnants of the [[Military of the United Earth Alliance|Alliance Defense Force]], Titan had been under siege since December of [[2315]].  

The Sirius Cluster, on the other hand, was another matter.  Although the [[United Earth Alliance]] claimed authority over the Sirius Cluster, they long ago ceased attempting to enforce their rule.  Due to this, the Sirius Cluster continued to enjoy ''de facto'' autonomy as the Empire subjugated the rest of [[Terrandom]].  To [[Lucius Black]], this arrangement was unacceptable in the long-term.

== Operation Lawless ==

The primary goal of Operation Lawless was to swiftly take control of the Sirius Cluster and replace the patriarchs of the [[Sirius families]] with appointed Imperial Governors, cementing Emperor Black's hold over the Sol Sector and capturing the significant production facilities maintained by the families.  

This operation was viewed as vital and urgent, but not necessarily high-risk.  The most up-to-date Imperial intelligence, gleaned from captured [[United Earth Alliance Central Intelligence Agency|CIA]] reports, indicated that the Sirius Families had very disorganized defense forces.  Most records indicated that the [[Black Fleets]] operated by the Sirius families were made up of antiquated Alliance Defense Force vessels and armed freighters, most of which were now mothballed, and their general level of defense technology was thought to be rather poor.  Thus, it was decided that a significant show of force would be sufficient to compel a surrender.

Analyzing available intelligence, Admiral [[Michael Burke]] devised the initial invasion plans, and Grand Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] allocated five full [[Royal Imperial Navy#carrier group|carrier groups]] to the operation.  Additionally, a flotilla of ten [[Mercury Class Transport|Mercury Class Transports]] with a full compliment of ten [[Royal Imperial Army]] battalions was committed for the expected occupation.  Commanding the forces was [[Victor Pryce|Commodore Victor Pryce]], who was brevetted to Rear Admiral for the Operation.

The ships and soldiers were mustered, and deployed from the [[Sol System]] to [[Tejat]] in the [[Algedi Cluster]] on the 30th of December, [[2318]].  The plan called for the five carrier groups to split up.  Three of the carrier groups and six Mercury Transports were to deploy to the [[Sirius Aichi System]], the most populous and industrially developed of the three inhabited systems in the cluster, with a single carrier group and a pair of Mercury Transports sent to the [[Sirius Bari System|Sirius Bari]] and [[Sirius Novgorod System|Sirius Novgorod]] systems.  

To ensure a prompt surrender, the three fleets in the Sirius Aichi System, capital of both the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] and [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius]] families, were to demand an immediate surrender of the system and the two families, and simultaneously offer substantial "financial incentives" to the families for their cooperation in convincing the other two families to likewise surrender.  The Nakamura in particular were expected to be a great asset in this regard, as they had already aided the Empire in the past.  

After space-superiority was established, the Mercury Transports were to disgorge their Army battalions to help convince the families of the futility of resisting.  The soldiers were to stay as a permanent garrison under the direct control of the Imperial Governors, who were each hand-picked by Lucius Black.  The appointed Imperial Governors were [[Terrance Brookings]], [[Henri Monteblanc]], and [[Carmen Solana]].  In the Sirius Cluster they are often referred to as the [[Charlatan Princes]].

Expecting little opposition, the carrier groups were tasked with ensuring the systems were free of threats and overseeing the Army landings.  Once the Imperial Governors had been installed, the carrier groups were to rendezvous in Sirius Aichi, resupply, and depart the Sirius Cluster for the Titan System by January 14th in order to take part in Operation Tartarus, leaving only a single frigate patrolling each system until a proper garrison force arrived from Earth.  The Mercury Transports were to return to Earth, take-on new battalions, and likewise deploy to the Titan System.  The timetable for the entire operation was between five and fifteen days, with the expected duration being only a single week.

== Siriun Intelligence ==

Although the Sirius families had long suspected that the status quo could not be maintained, they were first alerted to an impending operation when they began coordinating their intelligence operations.  Despite the extreme animosity between the Nakamura and Berlusconi in the Sol Sector, reason of mutual survival prompted them to forgive, for the moment.  

Although they could not actively track troop deployments, the substantial business connections of the families allowed them to follow the trails of weaponry, ship parts, troop transport ships, and also monitor which shipyards were most active.  As more and more men and ships began to arrive in the Sol Sector, Berlusconi operatives infiltrated a rebel cell on Earth.  This particular cell contained a number of Imperial Army civilian contractors, and these contacts indicated a large operation being planned.  The entire invasion forces was covertly monitored in orbit by "civilian" vessels in the employ of the Asanova family.  The time-table was discovered when [[Blasius állam-Nehézipar]], building military hardware under contract for the Empire, intentionally delayed a delivery to Earth, citing manufacturing problems.  Blasius representatives were told the parts must arrive no later than December 23rd.

The final piece of the puzzle was discovered indirectly on the 26th of December.  The Berlusconi-linked rebel cell obtained details about "Operation Tartarus", which called for a force of five carrier groups arriving with "remaining strength" from the Sol Sector.  This and other information indicated that a large force of warships, half of which would come from some operation in the Sol Sector, were to launch a massive operation in the Titan system on the 15th of January.  The Sirius patriarchs met and [[Alessandro Berlusconi]] presented the information from the cell.  As the Sirius Cluster was the only non-Imperial-held territory left in the Sol Sector, they all agreed that an attack was imminent and began mobilizing everything at their disposal.  

== Siriun Preparations ==

Over the next few days, ships began to arrive in the Sirius Cluster.  In addition to the personal fleets of the Sirius families, dozens of armed merchantmen were pressed into service, as well as every warship of the Blasius állam-Nehézipar corporate fleet, and every available mercenary was hired.  Long hidden away in bunkers or far-orbit shipyards, tanks, fighters, and warships began maneuvering to prepared defensive positions.  The Sirius families were not aggressively militant, but they were not fools either.  

Altogether, the families were able to muster over 30 capital warships, another 73 gunships, and 15 militarized freighters that had been prepared over the previous months.  Although still outnumbered in terms of capital warships and gross tonnage, the Siriun fleets had the added advantages of deception and surprise.  The plan was to marshal all of the warships in the Sirius Aichi System in the hopes that the bulk of the Imperial forces would try to take [[Sirius Kuza]] first, and thus dominate two families in one blow.  This plan, put forward by [[Takashi Nakamura]], was highly unpopular with the Berlusconi and Asanova commanders, but they ultimately agreed.  Overall command of the naval forces was given to Commodore [[Chetana Sharma]], commander of the Blasius family's naval forces.  Other Siriun commanders included Admiral [[Dimitri Lavrov]] and Marshal [[Cristobol Bartolomeo]].  Also present was Chief Gunnery Officer [[Hannah Baruk]], future Admiral of the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]].

By the 31st of December, everything was in place.  For the sake of keeping up appearances, merchant shipping and even some of the families' private couriers were allowed to operate normally, going in and out of the Sirius Cluster on standard trade routes.  Only the [[Ikronin]] were alerted ahead of time, warned at the highest levels of diplomacy late on the 31st, and they shut down their [[Wormholes|Avmaska-Stig]] for "scheduled maintenance" only hours later.

== Invasion ==

On January 2nd, at 0100 [[Naval Standard Time|Hours Naval]], the five carrier groups and their accompanying Mercury Transports arrived in their assigned systems.  As planned by the Imperial Admiralty, and hoped-for by the Siriun forces, three carrier groups arrived in the Sirius Aichi system.  

Each carrier group that arrived in the Sirius Bari and Sirius Novgorod Systems encountered exactly what they expected to encounter--nothing.  As the warships arrived in the system, civilian traffic scattered in all directions, and vehement diplomatic protests were made to the flagships' captains from the family heads themselves.  The warships took up their positions in orbit of [[Sirius Napoli]] and [[Sirius Kirtszi]] and they demanded immediate surrender of the worlds.  Even before replies were heard, the Mercury Transports made course for low orbit to launch the Royal Imperial Army invasion forces.

For the ships arriving in Sirius Aichi, things were a bit different.  Much of the merchant traffic scattered, but some freighters kept about their business, maneuvering for starports or space stations, or preparing to leave system.  The three carrier groups, combined as the Command Operation Force (COPFOR), encountered only the ''[[STS Alexandrovna]]'' and a pair of seemingly-antiquated [[Petrov Class Frigate|Petrov Class Frigates]].  Commanding the ''Alexandrovna'', [[Dimitri Lavrov]] demanded the ships turn back, as they were in violation of Sirius Household space.  Ignoring the small, obsolete warships, COPFOR warships spread out thinly around the planet in order to cut off any escape vectors.  

Governor-Apparent Carmen Solana personally demanded the surrender of [[Blasius Gregöri János|Gregor Blasius]] and Takashi Nakamura, ordering them to have their forces on the planet stand down and accept the Imperial occupation.  Agreeing to confer with not only one another, but their fellow patriarchs, Blasius and Nakamura bought substantial time "negotiating".  As the Sirius Cluster was linked via [[Urokon]], and need not rely on hyperspace courier, the ruse was believable.

== Battle of Sirius Kuza ==

As the Siriun heads "conferred", their forces were already taking their positions.  By not offering battle in the outer system, the Sirius families took a great risk, giving Imperial Army forces landing opportunity and Imperial Navy forces the ability to launch preemptive strikes on the planets.  However, in taking up positions in high and mid-orbit, the Imperial carrier groups had committed themselves to an invasion, and were ill-deployed for warship combat.  

As the Mercury Transports moved into deployment positions, a convoy of seemingly innocuous freighters  moved towards the planet.  Paying little heed, Rear Admiral [[Victor Pryce]] ordered a pair of his frigates to intercept them, adding to the prize purse of the fleet.  The frigates were already in weapons range before they could distinguish the heavy weaponry replacing cargo pods on the militarized [[Torino Class Frigate|Torino]] and [[Caspia Class Armed Merchantman|Caspia]] freighters.  The opening shots of the battle were fired against the frigates and their fighter escorts, crippling the unsuspecting Imperial forces in moments.

The trap was now sprung, and to Admiral Pryce's horror, a fleet of warships, hidden among civilian traffic and in the shadow of Sirius Kuza's [[Kuza Ios|moon]], coalesced into formation above the planet.  Outside of the planet's gravity, the Siriun fleet held a vastly superior fighting position, and as the STS ''Alexandrovna'' pulled into formation, Dimitry Lavrov demanded surrender of the Imperial fleet with his famous quote, "You will all leave here in tramp freighters--Alive or dead, I care not."

Despite the fact that his fleet was ill-deployed for a naval engagement, Admiral Pryce responded with weapons fire and the battle commenced in earnest.  Having never lost a major fleet engagement, Pryce and his captains were confident in victory.  However, the battle quickly went from a lopsided engagement to an utter route, as the well-planned Siriun attack went off almost exactly as planned.  Planet-based fighters and bombers arrived to attack Imperial carriers, and as Imperial ships maneuvered to escape orbit, gunships and bombers forced them into the firing lanes setup by planetary defense stations that had remained silent during the invasion.  

The fighting was fierce on both sides, with many ships destroyed, but after only five hours, the battle was all but lost for the Imperials.  Multiple frigates had struck their colors, and the [[HMS Argolis]] was crippled and boarded.  Only one of the Mercury Transports managed to disgorge any substantial portion of its Imperial Army battalions, but no dropships ever made it to the surface.  

The most severe damage dealt by the Imperial forces was when [[Captain Josiah Cleburn]] of the [[HMS Thessaly]] guided the ship down to the planet's surface in a near free-fall, hoping to land it in the ocean.  Instead, the ship crashed in the outskirts of the city of [[Ishikawa]], damaging several square kilometers of the city, and killing nearly seven thousand inhabitants.  By 1500 Naval, the entire invasion force was destroyed or crippled, and both Victor Pryce and Carmen Solana were captured.

== Rimworlds Massacre ==

In the Sirius Bari and Sirius Novgorod systems, the Imperial operations went off smoothly.  Their forces away in Sirius Aichi, the systems quickly "capitulated", offering surrender and allowing the Imperial Army forces to land.  Their missions accomplished, the Imperial carrier groups departed the systems for the Sirius Aichi rendezvous on January 4th.  

Commodore Sharma ordered a number of crippled Imperial warships towed to far system orbit, leaving their distress beacons active, and hid Siriun naval forces near the twin outer-rim worlds of [[Sirius Niigata]] and [[Sirius Kagawa]].  As CG Bari arrived in the system, they responded to the distress beacons.  Despite operating at high alert, the Imperial ships were ambushed by a force of ships from Sirius Niigata.  When CG Novgorod arrived in the system, they responded to calls for reinforcements, and moved to support CG Bari.  

As the carrier groups moved into formation with one another, the rest of the Siriun naval forces moved to engage in battle from Sirius Kagawa, and defense stations on both planets launched hundreds of anti-warship missiles.  Once again, the combination of surprise and superior fleet deployment paid off for the determined Siriun naval forces.  With incoming weapons fire from so many vectors, the Imperial fleet found it impossible to setup effective ranged defense formations.  

Commodore [[James Arnette]] ordered his ships to close on the enemy forces, hoping to reduce the effectiveness of the planetary batteries.  However, this only decreased the effectiveness of the Imperial forces' own [[Terran Mass Drivers|MAC Cannons]] while increasing the effectiveness of the shorter-ranged Siriun [[Ashur Cannon|Ashur Cannons]].  He was also unprepared for heavy bombers, and flights of Berlusconi [[Pugno Mortale]] bombers, heretofore unknown to Imperial forces, wreaked havoc on the Imperial frigates.

Caught in the gravitational center between the two outer-rim worlds, retreat by [[Hyperspace]] was impossible, and the Imperial carrier groups were routed.  Against the wishes of Commodore Arnette, Terrance Brookings offered to surrender to the Siriun forces.  This was followed by Commodore Arnette's order for a general retreat when several Imperial vessels struck their colors and were fired upon regardless.  As per their orders, the Siriun forces would take no prisoners.  

Commodore Arnette's own [[HMS Athens]] went down fighting, action for which Arnette was posthumously awarded the Imperial Medal of Valor.  Only the Vigilant Class Frigate [[HMS Ventis]] managed to escape by [[Porpoise Jump|porpoising]] to deep space, where the ship drifted while attempting self repairs.  It took nearly two weeks for the reactors and [[Volstamik-Krenschov Drive]] to be repaired sufficiently for the ship to jump to the Algedi Cluster.

== Sirius Napoli and Kirtszi Retaken ==

With the Imperial fleet presence destroyed, the Siriun forces under Commodore Sharma began the methodical extermination of Imperial Army forces in the Sirius Bari and Sirius Novgorod Systems.  With no way to contact the Empire, the Royal Imperial Army forces were isolated.  

When news of the naval victory came to Sirius Napoli, Alessandro Berlusconi finally ordered his hidden forces into the open.  Many Imperial checkpoints were overrun, and the garrison was surrounded and besieged.  Hoping to quickly end the conflict, Alessandro Berlusconi made a one-time offer of amnesty to any Imperial Army soldiers who surrendered, promising death for those who did not.  The offer expired on January 8th, and over two-thirds of the Imperial forces surrendered.  By the 12th, those who did not were dead after a massive assault on the [[Nuova Firenze#Old_City|Old City]] by the elite of il Padre's [[Redshirts of Berlusconi|Redshirts]].  Those that surrendered were imprisoned until repatriation in [[2320]].  

On Sirius Kirtszi, [[Nicholai Asanova]] had a personal score to settle with any who would dare invade his world.  The [[Alexeigrad]] metropolitan area and its surrounding territory was occupied by two Royal Imperial Army battalions, totaling over 60,000 men.  However, within the city and in secret locations nearby, Nicholai had mustered a force of over two million fighting men and machines.  On January 6th, Nicholai ordered the assault to commence, and his forces attacked the Imperial garrison from within the city and from without.  Despite a dogged defense, the onslaught was unstoppable, and all Imperial forces were "purged" by January 15th.

== Consequences ==

When no news arrived by [[Hyperspace Courier|courier]], and the carrier groups failed to arrive in the Titan System by the 15th of January, Imperial commanders began to worry.  On the 16th, Lucius Black summoned Nakamuran ambassador [[Kirogi Kagami]] to an audience in his very throne room to inquire about the goings-on in the Sirius Cluster.  An hour of round-about questioning failed to reveal any particulars.  Finally, Emperor Black declared that he had personally sent an exploration fleet into the Sirius Cluster.  Ambassador Kagami calmly replied that he, "had not heard of any such ships, but would keep an eye out...personally."  Siriun traders and transports continued about their business as usual.

Despite the views of other patriarchs for a reserved response to the situation, Nicholai Asanova could not let such actions to go unpunished.  Asanovan soldiers had lead the attack on HMS Argolis and captured Rear Admiral Pryce and Governor-Apparent Solana, and when Nicholai's forces retook Alexeigrad, Henri Monteblanc was also captured alive.  Soon after his capture, Monteblanc was hanged in [[Alexei's Square]] before the victorious Asanovan troops.  

Rear Admiral Pryce and Governor-Apparent Solana, however, suffered much more barbaric fates.  Upon their delivery to Nicholai's court, he had them each select a messenger to deliver messages to Emperor Black.  Pryce chose himself, and Solana chose [[Alfred Godfrey]], his aide-de-camp.  On the 18th of January, Alfred Godfrey arrived on Earth and was summoned to the [[Obsidian Palace]].  It was there that he delivered Nicholai Asanova's "diplomatic response"--the heads of Victor Pryce and Carmen Solana.  

In a fit of rage, Emperor Black had Alfred Godfrey publicly scourged, and posthumously stripped Victor Pryce of all medals and commendations.  Further, he struck from the official records all commendations and awards given to the Governors-Apparent, and had their estates and assets seized.  He even demanded a new invasion force be put together.

However, Admirals Hadrian and Burke eventually calmed him and talked him out of it.  There simply were not enough ships for another invasion without pulling forces from the Titan System.  They knew that a slacking of pressure could cost them all of their gains in the system, and could allow the Alliance Defense Forces go on the offensive.  They also felt that if news of the debacle got out, it would be greatly demoralizing for Imperial forces, shattering the image of the "Impervious Imperium", and could incite conquered systems to rebel.  In the end, the Empire sued for peace.

== Conclusion ==

By the end of January, the Imperial campaign in the Titan System had ground to a halt due to the loss of five carrier groups in Operation Lawless, and Operation Tartarus ultimately collapsed.  Not only had the Royal Imperial Navy suffered its first major, strategic loss of the war, but it had been caught completely unprepared and was utterly routed.  Emperor Lucius Black himself had suffered humiliation when he received the diplomatic correspondence from the Nakamuran and Asanovan ambassadors.  

The Sirius Incursion was the only major loss the Empire suffered until the entrance of the [[Mesarthrim]] into the [[Second Exodus War]].  The terms agreed between the Sirius families and the Empire were that all reports of the battles would be suppressed in exchange for the Emperor's promise to never again attempt annexation of the Sirius Cluster, as well as official Imperial recognition of the Sirius Cluster's independence.  Imperial soldiers and sailors held in detention were repatriated by 2320, but the Siriun families kept all captured vessels and military hardware as war prizes.

In March of 2319, the Onyx Empire officially recognized the "independent principalities in perpetual sovereignty over the Sirius Cluster". 

After the collapse of Operation Tartarus, the Sirius families offered to mediate the [[Titan Peace Accords]], in which they gained recognition by the United Earth Alliance and the Alliance of Planets, in exchange for their official neutrality.

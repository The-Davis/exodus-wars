The '''Socialist Republic of Yugoslavia''' was the Yugoslav state that existed from the end of [[World War II]] until it was formally dissolved in [[2011]] with the signing of the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]]. It was a [[Communist]] state that was formed from the remains of the pre-war Kingdom of Yugoslavia. The country was proclaimed in [[1943]] and named Democratic Federal Yugoslavia. In [[1946]], it became the Socialist Republic of Yugoslavia.

Under the leadership of Premier Josip Tito, Yugoslavia pursued a policy of neutrality during the [[Cold War]]. Yugoslavia continued its policy until it joined the emerging unified world government in [[2011]].

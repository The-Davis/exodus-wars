=== Overview ===

The Bastion is a newcomer to the Prefecture Navy.  Designs began in 2322, based on the battle for supremacy in space over Terra.  In the long campaigns in the Terran cities, the army that could call down orbital bombardments usually won.

Options in the Battle of Terra were limited.  It was either nuclear or conventional missile strikes.  Most cannons didn't have the range or accuracy for anything short of leveling everything for many kilometers around the target zone.  The Bastion changed this.

=== Capabilities ===

The "Talisman" Relativistic Mass Drivers aren't built for warship combat.  They're capable of it--a single salvo can crack an unshielded warship like an egg--but the targeting systems and motors for aiming the cannons aren't designed to handle targets moving at even the slow speeds of warships.  Instead, it can make micro-fine adjustments.  A Talisman can be used for wide-area bombardment, or take out a lone soldier--though his immediate surroundings would be destroyed.

While the Talismans can be used at extreme range, the Bastion must resort to its secondary weapons--Hellguns, Naval Autocannons, and Neutron Cannons.  Every possible approach vector is covered by multiple weapons.

Missile launchers back up both the Talismans and the secondary weapons.  They can be aimed at rapid-moving targets, or used in carpet bombardments.  Missile bombardments, especially nuclear and anti-matter, are strongly discouraged by treaties and general military practice, but Admiral Baruk likes to have her options open.

Fighter screens for the Bastion have to come from the Sixth Fleet's carriers.  The Bastion only carries twenty-four Bloodhawks.  The Bloodhawk crews of the Bastion specialize in ground support, instead of the standard ground support/warship assault training that most pilots receive.

Because of its mission profile, the Bastion frequently has to remain motionless over a planet, providing support fire for ground forces.  It has to be able to take the worst punishments, including entire swarms of Bugger suicide drones.  Because of this, it is extremely well-protected.  It carries more shield generators than any other three Prefecture vessels combined.  It also has Archadian proton-bond armor for its bridge and the housings of the Talisman Mass Drivers.

=== Deployment ===

Admiral Hannah Baruk took command of the Bastion on its maiden voyage, on March 2nd, 2324.  The vessel didn't see action until after the Battle of Tavou--it was sent to help with the swarms there, where it performed a successful bombardment of the Bugger landing zone, rooting out the entrenched invaders.

The Sixth Fleet Flagship is assigned to support all major Prefecture Army campaigns.  Hana Baruk has little experience in the military navies, but her skill in independent naval combat and her history as a pirate hunter earned her the post.

The '''Heritage Crisis''' was a cold war between the [[Ikronin]] and the [[Reigens]] for control of the antimatter in the [[Njiorin Sector]]. The crisis began in [[2304]] when the [[Ikronin]] began building illegal antimatter wells in [[Njiorin's Well]]. The [[Reigens]] already owned multiple wells in the star cluster, and claimed it as a whole. The [[Ikronin]] begin building wells, in defiance of [[Reigen]] sovereign rights.

[[Argus Lax]] was a major antagonist, but [[Lorrodi Raegos]] finally solved the problem peacefully in [[2312]].

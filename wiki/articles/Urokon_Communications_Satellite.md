There are two major contenders for the title of "Backbone of Galactic Commerce."  One is the [[Ikronin]] Wormhole Gate system, which connects worlds for cheap and reliable transportation.  The other is the Lorithos [[Urokon]] Network, a communications grid that allows for truly instantaneous communication from anywhere to anywhere, regardless of the distance.

[[Image:UrokonCommunicationsSatellite.jpg]]

The mechanics of the network itself are long and complicated, but the satellite itself is relatively simple.  Thousands of these satellites maintain the EPR particle chains which make the network operate.

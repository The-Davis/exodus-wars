'''Joseph McCarthy'''
*Born: November 14, [[1908]]
*Died: May 2, [[1957]]
*Place of Birth: Wisconsin, [[United States (Terra)| United States]], [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: Maryland, [[United States (Terra)| United States]], [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[United States (Terra)| United States of America]]

''Joseph McCarthy'' (born November 14, [[1908]]) was a prominent member of the [[United States (Terra)| United States]] Senate. He was one of [[Thomas Dewey]]'s prominent supporters, and was largely responsible for leading the public charge against domestic [[Communism]] in the [[United States (Terra)| United States]].

McCarthy was considered a national hero when over one thousand [[Communists]] and their sympathizers were convicted in the [[United States (Terra)| United States]] State Department and Army. McCarthy died an early death in [[1957]] from acute hepatitis, often thought to be the work of [[KGB]] poison.

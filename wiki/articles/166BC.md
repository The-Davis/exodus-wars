== Events of 200BC ==

The various [[Iridi]] of the [[Parcna Somglengarr]] reach an agreement on a new capital for their race, a universal city-state representing all [[Ixoarchada]].  [[Cathairn]], a small [[Iridi]] in the central mountains of [[Iremastha]], becomes the new capital of the [[Parcna Somglengarr]], and the precusor to the current [[Taviridis Somarchada]] is birthed in the new city and its councils.

The '''Zeta Algedi System''' consists of the star Zeta Algedi, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids.

In order of their distances from Zeta Algedi, the planets are Za I, Za II, [[Anilam]], [[Canopus]], Za V, Za VI, Za VII, and Za VIII.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Zeta Algedi is the Zeta Algedi System's only star. Zeta Algedi is a class G5L Yellow Giant. The Zeta Algedi System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Zeta Algedi is part of the [[Algedi Cluster]] in the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Anilam ===

[[Anilam]] (6.7AU) is one of two binary inhabitable terrestrial planets in the Zeta Algedi System. It has no moons, but orbits around its sister world, Canopus.

=== Canopus ===

[[Canopus]] (6.7AU) is one of two binary inhabitable terrestrial planets in the Zeta Algedi System. It has no moons, but orbits around its sister world, Anilam.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Zeta Algedi System was colonized in [[2195]].

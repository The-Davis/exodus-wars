The XB-4 was a brand new large airframe, designed to incorporate the MPD, an internal bomb bay, and external racks for AWTs and free-fall bombs. Initial tests met with some of the most catastrophic failures in Imperial aerospace history, with two XB-4s exploding in unmanned test flights, and one blowing up on the ground and killing an entire maintenance crew. It was determined that hydrazine, the fuel of choice when the MPD was used as a booster for the F-4C, was too volatile in large quantities for sustained use. The XF-8 and XB-4 projects were both competing for design time and for resources, and the XB-4 project was scrubbed. Instead, changes were incorporated into the B-2A and B-3A.

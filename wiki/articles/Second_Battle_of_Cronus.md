The Second Battle of Cronus was an operation planned by [[Scott Sanburn]] to strike at the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s campaign headquarters.  Admiral [[Gordon Dannins]] volunteered to lead the daring mission, taking the 2nd Guard Fleet with him.  [[Dannins]] and his men made a historic account of themselves - to the last man.  In the end, the entire 2nd Guard was eliminated, and thousands were taken captive.  [[Dannins]] was killed early in the battle when his [[:Category:Warships|warship]] took a direct hit to it's bridge.

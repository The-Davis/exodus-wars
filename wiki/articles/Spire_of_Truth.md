The beautiful Spire of Truth was built at the direction of the Prefecture Department of Inquisition.

The Spire is the central nervous system of the Spy Network.  Most of the levels of the tower are used for Spy Network offices which receive information from agents in various starsystems, and can send orders with OTP encryption systems--completely unbreakable code used by most militaries.

[[Image:SpireofTruth.jpg]]

The entry level is the only room in the Spire devoted to security.  The offices on that level have screens and speakers for the Spire's network of cameras and microphones.  Security officers can be deployed to any room in the Spire in two minutes tops.

Immediately above the security offices is the Interrogation level.  Most questioning is handled in hidden locations spread throughout the galaxy, but some high-profile prisoners are interrogated in the Spire.

The top level of the Spire of Truth is the office of the Grand Inquisitor.  Contrary to popular belief, the Grand Inquisitor does not actually live in his office. He usually leaves for six hours at a time, but his home's location is highly classified, to the point where even his own spies cannot find it.

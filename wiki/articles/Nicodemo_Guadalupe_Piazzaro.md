''Nicodemo Guadalupe Piazzaro'' (born September 18, [[2280]]), or "Nic Piazzaro" as he prefers to be called, is the current planetary head of state on [[Ah Kin]] in the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. Despite serious doubts of his early life and rumors that he was on the run from a shadowy past, Nic Piazzaro was able to win over many people in the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], and won in open elections to become the governor of [[Ah Kin]].

== Early Life ==

Nic Piazzaro was born into a middle class family in the city of Córdoba, a large metropolis in the Argentinan region of [[Earth]]. He spent his early life in scholarly pursuits, eventually enrolling in the Universidad Nacional de Córdoba (National University of Córdoba) in [[2302]], studying architecture to follow in his father's footsteps. Nic graduated with full honors in [[2306]], then continued his studies for another two years in the University of Paris, where he graduated with a Master's Degree in [[2308]]. By [[2310]], Nic Piazzaro had returned to Córdoba and taken over his father's business. The Piazzaro family was already a well-known name throughout the South American regions, but Nic took the company global, putting his fluent command of the [[English]], [[French]] and [[Chinese]] languages to good use.

== The Imperial Invasion ==

By [[2315]], Nic Piazzaro was a very successful and happy man. He was married to his high school sweetheart, [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro]], and they were living in great comfort in Buenos Aires. Their first son, [[Emilio Piazzaro]], was already 14 years old and keen to follow in Nic's footsteps.

Then the [[Onyx Empire]] invaded [[Earth]]. The initial invasion was brutally short, but the first months of the occupation thrust the Piazzaro family into Hell itself. The [[Onyx Empire]] froze all of the assets of Nic Piazzaro's company, "pending investigation" as to whether he ever built or designed anything used by the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]]. Their home was seized for the use of the [[Empire]], and the Piazzaro family was forced out onto the streets. The family would have starved, but a former business rival of Nic Piazzaro, [[Juan Delgado-Romas]], showed his true colors and welcomed the Piazzaro family into his home. [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]] survived the predations of the [[Onyx Empire]] by cooperating with the [[Alphas]] in spreading positive propaganda about the [[Empire]], and had been rewarded with the leadership of the Argentinan branch of the [[Royal Imperial Bank]].

Before long, Nic Piazzaro was back on his feet, working as a close second for the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] under his former rival. Despite their former competition in the days before the [[Empire]], [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] and Nic Piazzaro became life-long friends.

== The Resistance ==

While working as a low-level clerk for the [[Royal Imperial Bank]], Nic's son [[Emilio Piazzaro|Emilio]] discovered the true nature of the [[Imperial Identification Implant]] and heard rumors about the death camps run by the [[Omegas]]. Despite his young age, [[Emilio Piazzaro|Emilio]] joined the [[Resistance against the Empire|Resistance]] in early [[2317]]. Nic Piazzaro knew about his son's decision, but would not betray his family.

On August 11, [[2317]], [[Emilio Piazzaro|Emilio]] was shot by a group of [[Omegas]] while trying to smuggle fake [[Imperial Identification Implants|implants]] to a hidden cell of elderly refugees. [[Emilio Piazzaro|Emilio]] escaped and ran home, but died on his parents' doorstep. The [[Omegas]] watched, but did nothing immediately.

When he found his son's body and heard the story of his death, Nic Piazzaro swore revenge against the [[Onyx Empire]]. The local members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] put him in contact with [[Anthony Hadrian]], who was known only as the "[[Prefect]]" to the members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], who met him in disguise and asked for Nic's help in organizing a high-level cell of [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] within the [[Royal Imperial Bank]].

The [[Omegas]] were unable to learn the identity of the "[[Prefect]]" as they had hoped, but they learned of Nic Piazzaro's involvement with the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]].

== Betrayal ==

For three months, Nic Piazzaro ran an efficient and effective cell of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], saving the lives of many "undesireables" by manufacturing fake [[Imperial Identification Implants|implants]] and "cooking the books" to get them the funds they needed to survive. The [[Omegas]] did nothing until November 23, [[2317]], when the kidnapped Nic's wife [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]], threatening to kill her if Nic did not cooperate with their counter-insurgency operations.

Nic's heart was heavy with the burden, but knew that he could not lose the rest of his family. Rather than be a poison at the heart of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], he staged a highly-publicized raid against a small and unimportant cell. On December 3, [[2317]], the [[Omegas]] struck, but only four members of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] were killed by his betrayal, and his cover as an [[Empire|Imperial]] agent was blown.

Despite his cooperation, the [[Omegas]] would not release [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]], prefering to use her as leverage for future cooperation.

== On the Run ==

With [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] still in prison, [[Emilio Piazzaro|Emilio]] dead, and his ties with the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] severed, Nic Piazzaro was a broken man. On December 15, [[2317]], he attempted suicide by hanging himself, but was thwarted when [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] found him and cut him down. Nic was barely hanging on to life, but [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]] was a true friend, and secretly took him to his own private physician.

When he could speak, Nic confided the truth of his past to [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]], hoping his friend would stay true to the [[Onyx Empire]] and turn him over to the Imperial Police to have him killed. Despite [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]]'s cooperation with the [[Empire]] and his position as head of the Argentinan branch of the [[Royal Imperial Bank]], he was a true friend and a brave man. [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]] promised Nic that he would be reunited with [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] by the end of the year.

On December 28, [[2317]], [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] bribed several members of the Imperial Police to look the other way while he conducted some "personal business" in Buenos Aires' red light district. In reality, this dark locale was home of the [[Omegas]]' prison for women associated with the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]]: many were forced to work in brothels as "breeders" for the [[Onyx Empire]]'s workforce and war machine. Fortunately, [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] was considered too important for such base punishment and was spared a horrible fate. Masked and cloaked, Nic and [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]] and a half dozen men who worked for both of them in the old construction days stormed the prison, freeing [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] and dozens of prisoners.

Once reunited with [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]], Nic had no plans and did not care what happened to the former prisoners they had freed. [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]] had been more farsighted and merciful. The men he chose to help them were all unmarried, and known for their compassion and honor. The former prisoners were escorted into the safe zones in the Andes Mountains, and many ended up marrying their rescuers. It was hardly a fairy-tale ending, but the nightmare of the brothels was over and most of them spent the rest of the [[Second Exodus War|war]] in relative safety and happiness.

While they were no longer prisoners, Nic and [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] were now on the run both from the [[Onyx Empire]] and from the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]]. Nic's betrayal was well-known, but the rescue of the prisoners was done in secret. He didn't know it at the time, but [[Anthony Hadrian]] was already gunning for him.

Once again, [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] proved the quality of his character, and smuggled Nic and [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro]] onto a tramp freighter, which soon had them safely delivered to [[Theia]] in the [[Titan System]].

== Sanctuary ==

Nic and [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio Piazzaro]] settled on [[Theia]], but Nic was not satisfied. The guilt of his actions was finally catching up with him, and the two of them were only beginning to be able to properly grieve over the death of their son.

The people of the [[Titan System]] welcomed them. Many were refugees from [[Earth]], and others were from the various colonies of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro|Mio]] fit in well with the local population, but Nic began to sink into depression.

If not for the "[[Voice of the Resistance]]", Nic may have tried suicide again. He had managed to find a job working in one of the many makeshift war material factories built by the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]]'s provisional government. During a hard night working the third shift, he heard the "Voice of the Resistance" transmissions for the first time. The transmission spoke of [[Earth]] and the colonies, and the hardship under the rule of the [[Empire]], but there were messages of hope and encouragement for the people fighting to keep [[Theia]] and the [[Titan System]] free. To his surprise, he thought he recognized one of the voices in the transmission as [[Juan Delgado-Romas|Juan]]'s. It gave a simple message in Spanish before another person began to tell their story, but the words stuck in Nic's ears: "No pare, mi amigo. Recuerde nuestra lucha."

This pinpoint of hope lit a fire in Nic's conscience. He was determined to give something back to the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] and redeem himself.

== Redemption ==

In [[2319]], the [[Onyx Empire]] was negotiating the details of the [[Titan Peace Accords]] with the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government. The [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] was firmly against the treaty, as were many members of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]].

The "[[Voice of the Resistance]]" broadcasts began to be more frequent and urgent, and Nic Piazzaro found his opportunity to win back his own self-respect and lay the guilt of his earlier actions to rest. Nic joined the "[[Voice of the Resistance]]" broadcast ship, which travelled to [[Empire]]-occupied worlds under the guise of a freighter, and recorded the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] transmissions to be rebroadcast near other worlds. At the time, the [[Ansible]] network being built by the [[Lorithos]] was still a pipe dream.

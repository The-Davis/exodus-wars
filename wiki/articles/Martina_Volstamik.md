'''Martina Volstamik'''
*Place of Birth: [[Terra]]

''Martina Volstamik'' was a power generation and regulation engineer, and invented a nuclear fusion reactor that would fit within a small car. Her research was used in the creation of the Volstamik-Krenschov Jump Drive in collaboration with [[Yuri Krenschov]].

''Talire Huern'' was the "Ashen Prekohr", or "Twilight Empress" of the [[Lorithos]]. She represented the direct line descent of rulership since [[Hamur Huern]].

Talire outlived two of her children, leaving the line of succession unsure. When the most obvious choice, [[Derek Huern]], was assassinated, it sparked the terrible [[Burning Wars]] that ravaged [[Lorithan]] for decades.

Talire is remembered as a graceful leader, but her rule marks the end of an era.

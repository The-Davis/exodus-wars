*Career
**Laid down: September 12, 2322
**Launched: May 23, 2323
**Commissioned: FDS Imposis on May 28, 2323.
*Fate: Destroyed in the Battle of Icronia on April 2, 2324

The ''Imposis'' was one of three Avenger class cruisers built and delivered to the Prefecture while they were still a rebellion. Rahn Industries was already building the ship when they demonstrated the Avenger class's capabilities to the Prefecture, but were unable to deliver the design to the Prefecture forces in time for the Battle of Terra.

The Imposis and the other Avengers were used to help fill out the Fleets being commisioned by the newly formed Federated Districts of the Prefecture. The Imposis was designated CG-3, and assigned to the First Fleet.

The Imposis was assigned a veteran crew and given the task of challenging new cruisers and ships during their shakedown missions. The Imposis spent months showing junior crews the tricks of operating an Avenger, and ensuring the new ships being delivered could maneuver and operate at their promised specifications.

The Battle of Kronheim marked the end of the Imposis. The Volucris had a biological weapon that was previously unknown, and microscopic Volucris spawn were smuggled in by way of food supplies. The entire crew was eaten alive from the inside out. Marines from the First Fleet were sent in to shut down the shields, and bombers destroyed the cruiser to prevent its capture by the Volucris.

The name Imposis has not been struck from Fleet listings, and it is intended to be reassigned to a cruiser in the future.

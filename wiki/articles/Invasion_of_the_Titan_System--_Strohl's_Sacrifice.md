=====Titanian 11 - Titanian System - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the wake of the April Fools Debacle and the loss of the Bainbridge, Kitty Hawk, and Warspite, Scott Sanburn realized that Titanian 11 could no longer be held. The three cruisers had made up nearly an eighth of his available heavy capital ships; he simply no longer had the firepower to stand up to Marshall’s fleet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sanburn ordered a general retreat to the orbit of Titanian 9, abandoning Titanian 10 and 11 to the Imperials. The Imperial Atlas Wing immediately began moving their ships forward, securing the outer planets and establishing supply depots, defense stations, and repair yards in orbit of Titanian 10.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One of the most dramatic episodes of the war occurred during the TDF retreat to Titanian 9. The ADF-5000 cruiser Patriot, which had been damaged during the January 17th battle over Titanian 11, was still stranded in dock near Titanian 10 when the order to retreat came. Supplies required to bring her engines back on-line had been held up by the Imperial blockade.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Captain Debora Strohl, knowing her ship was unable to keep up with the rest of the Fleet, volunteered to stay behind at Titanian 10 and hold off the Imperial advance. Her request was denied, but she defied orders and prepared to meet the full wrath of the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Captain Strohl turned the Patriot’s weapons on the abandoned TDF facilities, destroying fuel, supplies, weapons caches, and orbital docks to prevent them from falling into Imperial hands. She loaded most of her crew into the Patriot’s fighters and shuttles, and launched them towards Titanian 6, hoping another friendly ship could pick them up.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On April 10th, only a day after the TDF retreat, Atlas Wing arrived at Titanian 10. The Patriot made a good accounting of herself; Strohl and her skeleton crew of volunteers held off the Imperials for nearly an hour.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At 1400 hours the Imperials managed to board the Patriot. Strohl triggered the self-destruct sequence, and went down with her ship.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Strohl’s sacrifice frustrated Robert Marshall. He’d counted on using the supplies and facilities that the TDF had abandoned on Titanian 10 as a staging base for a rapid offensive. Denied these vital resources, he called off his plans to immediately press an attack against the inner Titanian system.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall and Atlas Wing spent all of May building bases around Titanian 10 and preparing to move farther in-system; reinforcements poured in from the rest of the Empire. Marshall didn’t let the TDF rest, though. He stepped up his siege-and-harassment campaign.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On a daily basis, Vigilant cruisers and Artemis carriers probed the defenses of the inner planets, launching flights of bombers to attack the TDF fleet in dock. Marshall had developed a god complex early in the war; he wasn’t afraid to spend lives as long as he was hurting his foes even more.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Although the raids were expensive, the constant Imperial harassment kept the TDF crews tired, their ships damaged, and the civilian population worried. Meanwhile, well-rested and fully supplied Imperial ships continued to join up with Marshall’s fleet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial victory in Titanian seemed inevitable.

The '''Sirius Aichia System''' is a star system with five planets and their moons. 

In order of their distances from Sirius Aichi, the planets are Aichi I, Aichi II, [[Kuza]], Aichi IV and Aichi V.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Sirius Aichi is the Sirius Aichi System's star. Sirius Aichi is a G2V yellow dwarf, almost identical to Sol.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

[[Kuza]] was colonized in [[2038]], largely by [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]].

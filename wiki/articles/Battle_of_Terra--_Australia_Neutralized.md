May 29, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears 

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Empire was quick to respond to the Mesarthrim invasion force in Australia. The Royal Imperial Army sent three regiments of Rigs and infantry to take back Sydney. The Mesarthrim Crimson Tide battle group headed out of the city to meet the Imperial forces, and were immediately met with an artillery barrage.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Crimson Tide spread out and took cover, but the Catapults already had them ranged and locked. A lance of Mesarthrim LAVs was destroyed before Commandant Hinks could recall them into the relative safety of the city.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim Vulture tanks pushed forward, blasting the legs out from under one Catapult and sending the others into a panicked retreat. The artillery missiles of the Catapults couldn't pierce the Vultures at long range, and they couldn't fire at short range without killing themselves.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Hinks let the other three Catapults run for a while, then called down a strike from the air. The Catapults were blown to pieces by Conqueror bombers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before the Empire's forces could move in to attack, several TDF dropships landed in the city. A regiment of Grasshopper Rigs had been delivered by General-Commander Sanburn to assist Commandant Hinks. Faced with superior enemy numbers, the Imperials retreated, heading for Asia.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The combined Crimson Tide and TDF regiments gave chase. By the end of the day, every Imperial soldier on Australia was killed, captured, or driven to the Asian mainland. After the battle, the Crimson Tide battle group left a garrison of troops in Sydney, and prepared to move on the islands of southeast Asia. The Sydney Garrison now awaits the arrival of an emissary from the Prefecture.

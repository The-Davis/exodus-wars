==International Organizations==

===The Alliance of Planets===

The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] founded the [[Alliance of Planets]], and the [[Meroniri]] representative is the President of the [[Alliance of Planets High Council|Alliance High Council]]. Many of the Member States now see the [[Meroniri]] as a "bully state" of sorts, running the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] as an extension of their government rather than a group of diplomatic allies.

===Council of Independent Nations===

The [[Meroniri]] spearheaded the trade embargo against the [[Council of Independent Nations]], and refuses to have any dealings with them. This aggressive embargo is illegal by many older standards of international law, and is worrisome to many uninvolved parties. Some fear that the [[Meroniri]] will eventually destabilize the relative peace that has been won since the days of the Exodus Wars.


==Individual Governments==

===Taviridis Somarchada===

When the [[Ixoarchada]] first introduced themselves to the galactic community, the [[Meroniri]] met them on friendly terms. It soon became clear that the [[Ixoarchada]] presented a threat to the [[Meroniri]] supremacy in military and technological advantages.  Relations cooled, and then soured as the [[Ixoarchada]] began to thwart the [[Meroniri]] machinations against the worlds that would eventually form the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Since the formation of the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]], the [[Meroniri]] have not had friendly dealings with the [[Ixoarchada]].

===Auellal League===

When the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] was torn apart by [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]], the [[Meroniri]] sent ships from [[Aseta Salfore]] to stabilize space shipping lanes in the region, but did not get involved in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|war]] itself. After the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|war]], the [[Meroniri]] recognized the [[United Centusi States]] as the legal successor to the old [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clans government]], and never recognized the legitimacy of the [[Auellal League|Auellal government]], and thus have not dealt with them.

===Colonial Commonwealth===

The worlds that now make up the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] were traditionally allies of the [[Meroniri]], back in the days of the [[United Earth Alliance]].  The [[Meroniri]] enforced a blockade of the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] worlds, on the direct orders of [[Naimen Ladosen]]. It is unknown why he ordered it, but relations with the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] worlds have been hostile ever since. When the [[Titan System]] joined the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]], many [[Meroniri]] considered it a personal betrayal, and some have suggested annexing the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] into the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]].

===The Conclave===

As the successor to the [[Empire]], the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] was officially opposed to the [[Conclave]], but never actively hunted them or tried to destroy them. Today, the [[Conclave]] is defunct, but before it was dissolved the survivors of the [[Conclave]] surrendered to the [[Meroniri]]. Their exact fate since then is unknown, but the chained [[Lucius Black]] has been seen in public in the company of the [[Lothenar]], [[Naimen Ladosen]].

===Ikronin Jurekön===

The [[Meroniri]] expanded rimwards after they no longer feared the return of the [[Ngrligru]], and eventually made first contact with the [[Ikronin]]. The [[Ikronin]] were the first friends of the [[Meroniri]], and together they planned the founding of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. For years, the [[Ikronin]] were content to control commerce and trade, and had no interest in politics. Today, relations with the [[Ikronin]] have cooled somewhat because some members of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] oppose the [[Meroniri]] policy of controlling lesser civilizations, but the [[Ikronin]] as a whole are still friendly.

===Kabila Kimburu===

The [[Meroniri]] made first contact with the [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburuns]] as they were leaving the [[Teeth of Orion]], and welcomed them into human society and the [[Alliance of Planets]]. Since then, the [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburuns]] and the [[Meroniri]] have been on very friendly terms. A significant portion of the [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] population lives within the territory of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], but the [[Meroniri]] overlook this to maintain access to [[Ngrligru]] intelligence information.

===Kalidasa Planetary Authority===

When [[Kalidasa]] began to establish itself and seized control of the [[Empire|Imperial]] shipyards, the [[Meroniri]] kept the [[Prefecture]] from forcefully taking the [[Empire|Imperial]] [[:Category:Warship|warship]] hulls from the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]]. It was expected that the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] and the [[Meroniri]] would be on good terms, but when [[Naimen Ladosen]] began his trade embargos, the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] were swept up into the mix and soon joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Since then, the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] have been hostile towards the [[Meroniri]].

===The Federated Districts of the Prefecture===

The [[Meroniri]] is officially an ally of the [[Prefecture]], but relations have been extremely strained between each other since [[Naimen Ladosen]]'s blockade on [[Oronus]]. Aside from [[Alliance of Planets]] meetings, the [[Meroniri]] and the [[Prefecture]] have little to do with each other.

===Kabila iku Gol'nde===

While the [[Teeth of Orion]] prevent a major incursion, the [[Ngrligru]] are the only true threat to [[Meroniri]] power. Since the disaster of the [[Sundering]], the [[Meroniri]] have been wary of the [[Ngrligru]] returning to the [[Orion Arm]].

===Oronus Worker's Coalition===

The people of [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] had nothing to do with the [[Meroniri]] since their founding.  The materials the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] mined and sold were too low grade to be valuable to [[Meroniri]] industry, and their culture was a limited self-contained one with little to offer the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]]. Recently, [[Oronus]] was blockaded by the [[Meroniri]], and since then the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition]] has been extremely hostile towards the [[Meroniri]]. The blockade led to the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Coalition]]’s membership in the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

===Reigess Suverände===

While the [[Meroniri]] have had contact with the [[Reigens]], they consider them a very backwards society, and have as little to do with them as possible. The [[Reigens]] are a source of irritation to the [[Meroniri]], as they would prefer to keep the galaxy's supplies of [[antimatter]] under their own control. Still, the [[Reigens]] and [[Meroniri]] maintain diplomatic ties, mostly from the determined friendliness of the [[Reigen]] king. The [[Reigens]] have nothing to offer the [[Meroniri]] in trade, and the [[Meroniri]] jealously guard their secrets.

===The Remnant===

The [[Meroniri]] had some contact with the [[Remnant]] during the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], but only fought against one of their ships and crews. The [[Remnant]] has not had any contact with the [[Meroniri]] since then. The [[Meroniri]] consider the [[Remnant]]’s technology and tactics a threat, due to knowing nothing about them. Battle recordings from the brief contact during the [[Second Exodus War]] are constantly studied by the Aseta to look for a suitable defense.

===Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan===

The [[Meroniri]] are practically worshipped by the [[Lorithos]] for the work they've done in restoring [[Lorithan]]. While many [[Meroniri]] look down on the [[Lorithos]] for their poor health, short life-spans, and poor education, they tend to see them as misguided children and help them as much as they can.

===Sirius Territorial Administration===

The [[Meroniri]] have no love for the [[Sirius Families]], and look down on the entire [[Sirius Cluster]] as a collection of lawless scum. Meroniri ships do not visit the Cluster, and do not allow any ships from Sirius Families to do business at their ports.

===Sirunaki Reshkera===

The [[Meroniri]] have had no contact with the [[Sirunaki]] and do not know that they exist.

===Terran Embassy===

The [[Meroniri]] have provided some support for the rebuilding of [[Terra]], but have very little real political interest in the world. Most of the interest is from private donors and former government officials who served in the early days of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. The [[Meroniri]] were officially allies of the [[Terran Embassy]] until it joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Now they enforce the trade embargo against the [[Terran Embassy|Embassy]], as they do against the rest of the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] [[:Category:Planets|worlds]].

===United Centusi States===

The [[Meroniri]] opposed the idea of bringing the [[Alliance of Planets]] into the [[Tempest War]], and it is rumored that [[Naimen Ladosen]] privately supports the [[United Centusi States]] in the conflict. Officially, the [[Meroniri]] are neutral towards the [[United Centusi States]], but it is believed that a lot more goes on behind the scenes.

===Volucris Swarms===

The [[Meroniri]] have engaged the [[Volucris]] a few times in battle, and have always been victorious due to their advanced weaponry and fighting the [[Volucris]] only in areas advantageous to the [[Meroniri]]. There has been no diplomatic contact between the [[Meroniri]] and the [[Volucris]], and it is unknown if there ever will be. The [[Meroniri]] consider the [[Volucris]] a force of nature to be prepared for and recovered after rather than a hostile force.

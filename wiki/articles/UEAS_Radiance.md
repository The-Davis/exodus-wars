The UEAS ''Radiance'' was an [[ADF-4000 Class Destroyer]] assigned to the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. It was part of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]], but was infiltrated and taken over by the [[Omegas]].

From [[2300]] until [[2315]], the ''Radiance'' was used to dupe the [[United Earth Alliance]] into believing all was well on [[Ah Kin]]. The regular annual inspectors were given tours of the ''Radiance'', and kept away from sensitive areas being devoted to the buildup of the [[Onyx Empire]].

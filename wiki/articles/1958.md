== Events of 1958 ==
===January===
*January 4 - [[The Technology Race]]: Sputnik I burns up in the atmosphere.
*January 31
**[[The Technology Race]]: Explorer II is launched into orbit.
**[[The Technology Race]]: James Van Allen discovers the Van Allen radiation belt.

===February===
*February 1 - [[The Jihad]]: Egypt, Syria and Saudi Arabia are united into the new nation of Arabia.
*February 5 - [[The Jihad]]: Gamel Nasser is named Imam of Arabia.
*February 11 - [[Cold War]]: Chen Yi succeeds Zhou Enlai as the People's Republic of China's Minister of Foreign affairs.
*February 14 - [[The Jihad]]: Iraqi King Faisal II denounces the formation of the theocracy of Arabia.

===March===
*March 17 - [[The Technology Race]]: The United States launches the Vanguard I satellite.
*March 26 - [[The Technology Race]]: The United States launches the Explorer III satellite.
*March 27 - [[Cold War]]: Nikita Khrushchev becomes Premier of the Soviet Union.

===April===
*April 4 - [[The Technology Race]]: The British Empire launches the Berkshire I, the first British satellite.

===May===
*May 15 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Soviet Union launches Sputnik III.
*May 23 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Explorer I satellite ceases transmission and deorbits.
*May 30 - The bodies of unidentified soldiers killed in action during [[World War II]] and the [[Korean War]] are buried at the Tomb of the Unknowns in Arlington National Cemetery.

===June===
*June 1 - Charles De Gaulle is brought out of retirement to lead France by decree for six months.
*June 4 - [[The Jihad]]: Charles De Gaulle visits Algeria. The French leader is assassinated by Muslim rebels.

===July===
*July 7 - President Strom Thurmond signs the Alaska Statehood Act into United States law.
*July 14 - [[The Jihad]]: King Faisal II of Iraq is murdered and Abdul Qassim assumes power in the name of Arabia. Iraq plans to become a territory of Arabia.
*July 15 - [[The Jihad]]: Pro-Western government of Lebanon requests aid of the United States and British Empire to quell a Muslim uprising. Help arrives too late, and 5,000 United States Marines arrive to find Beirut occupied by Muslim soldiers from Arabia. Lebanon becomes a territory of Arabia.
*July 17 - [[The Jihad]]: British troops arrive in Jordan to prevent Iraqi invasion.
*July 26 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Explorer IV satellite is launched.
*July 29 - [[The Technology Race]]: The U.S. Congress formally creates NASA.

===August===
*August 3 - The nuclear powered submarine USS ''Nautilus'' becomes the first vessel to cross the North Pole under water.

===September===
*September 12 - [[The Technology Race]]: Jack Kilby invents first integrated circuit.
*September 28 - In France, a majority of 79% says yes to the constitution of the Fifth Republic.
*September 30 - [[Cold War]]: The Soviet Union performs nuclear tests at Novaya Zemlya.

===October===
*October 1
**[[The Jihad]]: Morocco cedes authority to [[Arabia]] and becomes a territory of the larger nation.
**[[The Technology Race]]: NASA starts operations, headquartered in Houston, Texas.
*October 2 - [[The Jihad]]: French Guinea rejects the French Fifth Republic Constitution. Arabia assumes control over the region. French military lacks the resources to retaliate.
*October 11 - [[The Technology Race]]: NASA launches the first spacecraft under its direction, the Pioneer I.

===November===
*November 25 - Sudan gains autonomy as a self-governing nation, but retains economic ties with France in what became known as the French Community.
*November 28 - Chad, the Congo, and Gabon become autonomous republics within the French Community. Tunisia joins the French Community.
*November 30 - Gaullists win parliamentary elections in France.

===December===
*December 14 - The ''3rd Soviet Antarctic Expedition'' becomes the first ever to reach the Southern Pole of Inaccessibility.
*December 21 - With Charles de Gaulle dead, Francois Pasqua is elected president of France.

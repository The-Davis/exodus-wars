=====Unknown Place, Unknown Time=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worker is alone, so alone.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hello Human. Human is here to hurt Worker again? Worker thinks Human is cruel to hurt Worker.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human says Human that is, is not Human that was. Worker does not understand. Human says “I am Inquisitor, not guard,” but Worker does not understand. What is “I?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human says that Human’s job is to question, not to hurt. Worker understands that. Worker’s job is to work.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human asks, “What do the Volucris want with Lorithan?” Worker does not know these things. Worker does not know what Volucris are. Worker does not know what Lorithan is. What are these things, and why does Human want to know?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;What are people? What is city? These things confuse Worker. Human speaks as if Human were not also Human.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Explain? Human does not know? Is Human really not also Human? Worker obeys what Worker can, Worker will try to explain. Worker is also Worker. Everything Worker sees, Worker also sees. Everything Worker hears, Worker also hears. If Worker feels comfort and rest, Worker who is still working also feels better. Worker is also Worker. Does Human understand?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;What is “collective?” What is “hive mind?” Worker does not know these words.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human still does not understand. Worker does not know what other Worker knows. Worker knows what Worker knows. Worker cannot know what other Worker knows, because other Worker is not also Worker.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worker cannot explain better. If it is other Worker, it is not Worker. Worker does not know why. Worker does not know how.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Yes, Worker knows what Fighters are. Fighters are many. Fighters protect Worker, and Worker feeds Fighters. Worker obeys what Worker can. Worker does what Worker is told.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;No, Fighters do not tell Worker what to do. Fighters do not tell Worker anything. Worker thinks Fighters do not understand anything. Worker tries to speak to Fighters, but Fighters do not listen. Fighters do not answer. Worker thinks Fighters are not like Worker. Fighters do not know pain. Worker knows pain. Fighters do not know fear. Worker is afraid of Human.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Why should Worker not be afraid of Human? Human who is not also Human took Worker from Mother. Human who is not also Human caused Worker pain. Now Worker is alone, so alone.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human still does not understand? Worker thinks Human does not understand many things. Worker will try to explain.  Human asks “if Worker is also Worker, how can Worker be alone?” Worker is not certain.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worker sees with Worker’s eyes. Worker also sees with Worker’s hands. Worker also sees with Worker’s nose. Worker also sees the Will. When Worker sees the Will, Worker is also Worker. When Human who is not also Human took Worker, Worker stopped seeing the Will. Now Worker is lost. Worker cannot find Worker who is also Worker. Worker is slipping away.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Does Human know what sadness is? Fighters do not know what sadness is, but Worker knows. Worker thinks Human knows too. Worker remembers another place and another time. Worker was digging for Fighters. Mother wanted something from place where Humans live, and sent Fighters through Worker’s tunnels to get it. Worker came up from the ground, and small Human was holding dead Human. The sound small Human was making is like the feeling in Worker’s heart. Small Human made sound like fear, then giant Human was there. Small Human made sound like joy. Small Human called giant Human “Kida.” Worker does not know what this means, but worker thinks that small Human and giant Human are like Human, but are not also Human.<br>
Giant Human hurt Worker. Worker did not understand why. Worker did not want to hurt small Human. Worker did not want to hurt giant Human. Worker wanted to run away, but Worker could not run away. Worker…died. Maybe Worker then was Worker who is also Worker, and not Worker. Is this what Human means by “individual?” Worker does not know, but if Worker dies and Worker is also Worker, Worker does not end. If Fighters die, then Fighters end.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Now Worker does not know if Worker is still also Worker. Worker cannot see the Will. Worker is afraid if Worker dies, Worker will end. Worker is afraid of Human. Worker does not want Human to hurt Worker, so Worker obeys what Worker can.<br>
Human says Human will not hurt Worker, but Worker is still afraid. Worker knows fear, but Worker also knows hope. Worker thinks that maybe it does not matter if Worker cannot see the Will. Worker thinks that maybe if Worker dies, Worker will be Worker who is also Worker, and Worker will not end.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worker thinks it is worth the chance. If Worker must be alone, Worker does not want to live. Will Human let Worker die?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Human says “You are a Volucris, and only a specimen. You cannot ask for anything.” Now Human leaves, and Human who is not Human will come back. Other Human is guard. Guard Human likes to hurt Worker, but will not let Worker die. Human is so cruel to let guard Human hurt Worker for his fun. Goodbye, Human. Worker hopes that Worker will die before Worker sees Human again.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worker is alone, so alone.

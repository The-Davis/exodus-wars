Neutrons have been used as projectiles for centuries due to their ease of acceleration and their stability compared to charged particles.  '''Jiorna Nodes''' take the neutron cannon one step further.  Instead of merely hurling bundles of neutrons at relativistic speeds towards a target, the Jiorna Nodes locks the phase of the entire group of neutrons together, causing them to behave as one massive particle.  This greatly reduces the cross-section of the impact, allowing for much greater armor penetration.  Such phase-locked neutron beams are also unaffected by planetary atmospheres, and smaller versions can be used on ground-based vehicles.  The initial Phase-Locked Plasma Converter, from which Jiorna Node technology evolved, was actually taken from [[Anderung]] vessels captured or destroyed during the many early battles between them and the [[Ixoarchada]].

Jiorna Nodes get their name from the fact that they do not utilize traditional barrels or linear accelerator projectors, but rather fire from emitter nodes.  The emitter nodes are omni-directional firing prisms contained in a focusing dome.  These operate as the final focusing element of Jiorna weaponry, and are the only part of the weapon that protrudes from the hull of a warship.  This allows each node a maximum field of fire from a given position, and the ability to change targets and firing angles nearly instantaneously, as the focusing dome rotates and elevates quickly.  This layout also protects the cannon itself from attack.  

Jiorna Nodes have adjustable outputs, and can be used in either a rapid-fire mode for hitting agile targets, or in a slower, larger firing mode to deliver crippling shots at a much lower frequency to take out more heavily defended targets.  On smaller vehicles and fighters where space is at a premium, more traditional cannon configurations are often mounted, reducing the firing angles of the Jiorna Nodes to a forward-conical shape relative to the cannon.  This is counterbalanced by either the extreme maneuverability of the vehicle or an independent turret mechanism.

by James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     The general populace of Härdad is unimpressed by the Mesarthrim demands, at first. For one full day, the Mesarthrim fly over the city, not dropping any bombs, or any leaflets. Some fly slowly at high altitude, some fly so low to the ground that pedestrians can look up and see the white-eyed faces of the pilots, if only for a split second as the bomber goes screaming by.

     It's late-afternoon when the Mesarthrim give their first show of force, supporting their promise. Three large targets; a shopping center, an office building, and a mass-transit hub are all hit with heavy explosives. The damage is huge office buildings, packed with civilians. When the dust settles, over 2,000 civilians and 800 Prefecture soldiers are dead.

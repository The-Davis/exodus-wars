'''Mihel Rivarsen'''
*Place of Birth: [[Sepulare]]
*Allegiance: [[Meroniri Terinasi]]

''Mihel Rivarsen'' is the leader of the [[Aseta Serada]]. During the [[Second Exodus War]], Rivarsen was sent to the [[Titan System]] as a military advisor. His advice was pathetic, leading to several defeats.

Rivarsen went home in disgrace, realizing just how outdated [[Meroniri]] tactics were. Since then, he has begun training [[Aseta Serada]] in new tactics, hoping to bridge the gap.

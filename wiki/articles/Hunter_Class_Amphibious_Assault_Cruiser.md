===Overview===

Marines assigned to attack an enemy world deploy from a base via an Amphibious Cruiser. These ships are designed for sustained flight in an atmosphere, and can land in water if needed.  The Hunter Amphibious Assault Cruiser carries three Marine Divisions, known as a Marine Expeditionary Force, along with their 27 attached Marine Air Wings. It is fully capable of delivering all forms of Marine weapons to the battlefield, and is armed well enough to punch a hole in planetary defenses instead of simply slipping through.

[[Image:HunterClassAmphibiousAssaultCruiser1.jpg]]

Both of these are part of the Marine Corps' role of securing a safe landing zone for a Legion. Marines cannot take planets, but they can take spaceports.

===Capabilities===

Amphibious Cruisers are not weapons by themselves.  They deploy with at least some Navy elements, which must engage an enemy long enough for the Amphibious Cruiser to slip past or break through planetary defenses.

The Hunter is the largest and most powerful Amphibious Cruiser.  The first, Marine One, was converted from an Avenger Class Heavy Cruiser due to short notice of the redeployment and reorganization of the Marines.  Unfortunately, the original Avenger Class is not capable of sustained atmospheric flight.

As of 2325, the Prefecture only had limited anti-gravity capability.  Early purchases from the Archadians had been successfully reverse-engineered for VTOL propulsion, but nothing even remotely powerful enough to allow a warship of the magnitude of an Avenger to remain aloft in an atmosphere.

Due to the Archadian/Mesarthrim mutual defense treaty, it would have been a political nightmare to buy components from the Archadians.  Instead of anti-gravity, the Prefecture opted to purchase Repulsor technology from the [[Ikronin]].

Repulsor technology has a significant advantage in the shapes and properties of the fields it generates.  It can project a nearly frictionless surface for Icronian hovertanks to float on, or instantly increase the resistance of the surface a hundred-fold, braking the hovertank 
speed.

[[Image:HunterClassAmphibiousAssaultCruiser2.jpg]]

The disadvantage of the Repulsor is in power requirements.  Repulsors were not meant to lift warships into orbit.  They were meant to allow Ikronin tanks to float effortlessly, and to give Ikronin fighters a smooth landing without wheeled landing gear.  Hunters cannot sustain more than a 5000 meter altitude or reach escape velocity without massive power output--which is provided by the antimatter reactor.

Like the Seminole Class Destroyer of the Navy, the Hunter was one of the first results of the Norfolk Project to reach the line.  It is meant to be capable of numerous jumps per day, and with the output from an antimatter reactor nearly instantly-recharging its Kovalski-Fahr capacitors.  The antimatter reactor is also required to lift the Hunter out of an atmosphere and into escape velocity.

===Carrying Capacity===

The Hunter carries an entire Marine Expeditionary Force.  This is a very effective offensive force, capable of engaging defenses many times larger than itself, assuming it continues to receive support.  If a Legion does not reinforce an Expeditionary Force, it is doomed to be overwhelmed by superior numbers and eventual exhaustion of the Marines.

A typical Expeditionary Force has three Divisions, and 27 Marine Air Wings.  All Marines are trained Infantry, and many are trained in powered armor, vehicle, artillery, sniper, or anti-armor operations.  A Hunter can deploy any number of Infantry, but each Division only has enough vehicles for a third of its Marines.  The rest are equipped with powered armor or other Infantry equipment loads.

===Crew Accommodations===
The bulk of the empty space of a Hunter is devoted to vehicles, VTOLs, fighters, dropships, and infantry weapons.  The tiny crew of 100 Navy personnel have little to do with the Marines on board, and generally keep to themselves.  Hunters are large enough to offer all crew and Marines comfortable, but spartan. When at a Marine base, Navy personnel usually stay planet-side, where accommodations tend to be more enjoyable.

===Prism-Spawn===
Since the Battle of Kronheim, hundreds of stable Artificial Intelligences have been created, with the knowledge of how to recreate themselves.  Fanatically loyal to humanity in general and the Prefecture in particular, these "Prism-Spawn" have spread across the Blackis Galactic Navy's Fleets, and are installed standard on every computer capable of supporting one.

===Deployment===

The first Hunter Class Amphibious Assault Cruiser, Marine One, was deployed in July 5th, 2325.  The next four were not built until late 2326, when the Marine Corps actually had enough numbers to warrant the formation of additional Marine Expeditionary Forces.  For much of the Tempest War, Marines were instead deployed aboard the smaller Ranger Class Light Amphibious Cruiser.

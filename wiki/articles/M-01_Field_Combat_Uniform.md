Military Specification 1 (M-1) was the first creation of the [[Empire]]'s military. It was a simple combat uniform, designed for the soldiers of the [[Royal Imperial Army]].

The M-1 specification continued to be developd over the years, until it developed into the [[Military of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture Military]]'s '''Field Combat Uniform''', an exceptional leap in clothing technology. It has a four-color digital camouflage pattern, the colors of which are changeable by means of a small current that can be run through the suit. Optical sensors observe the surrounding terrain, and change the colors accordingly. All soldiers in the field wear a patrol cap, a jacket with Legion insignia, rank insignia, and name patches, which fits over low-profile body armor, and trousers with thigh storage pockets and room for low-profile leg armor. All troops are issued black combat boots, which also serve as the source of the uniform's camouflage power source. The Field Combat Uniform can be worn under powered armor.

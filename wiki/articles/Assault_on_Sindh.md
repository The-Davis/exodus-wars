The '''Sindh Campaign''' was one of the worst setbacks to the [[United Centusi States]].  While the initial assault was a complete rout against the regular Prefecture forces, the civilian militia and guerrilla forces left behind by the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]] became a severe thorn in their sides.

On several occasions, Mesarthrim commanders wanted to abandon the less-than-useful world in favor of diverting forces to more valuable targets, but [[William Camp]] ordered that all opposition be crushed in order to make an example of the planet.

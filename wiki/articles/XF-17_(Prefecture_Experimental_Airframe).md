The XF-17 was an airframe designed for extremely close support of troops. It was designed to take brutally sudden changes in acceleration, G-forces and negative-gee, and turning rates. The XF-17 was equipped with two cannons and six ordinance hard points. The YF-17 was highly successful as a strike fighter, and as an atmospheric dog-fighter. It could turn on a dime, dive like a rock, and was equipped with a highly advanced antigravity system to take it back up from an otherwise fatal dive. It suffered from poor acceleration rates in space, and poor high speed in the atmosphere. It was designated the F-17 (haven't thought of a name yet, was the Phantom) and sent to the Marine Corps.

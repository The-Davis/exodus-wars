== Events of 1402 ==

In 1402, a third star with planets was discovered 4.7 light years from Bollaria. Several probes were sent to the planets. The sixth planet, which was dubbed "Tel Devon," had acceptable temperatures and gravity for terraformation. The satellite factories were sent in.

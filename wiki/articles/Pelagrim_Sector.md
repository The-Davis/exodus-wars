The '''Pelagrim Sector''' is largely inhabitted by the [[Mesarthrim]], and was once the site of the [[Anderung]] power base. The Pelagrim Sector is named for a cluster of white dwarf stars, known as "[[Pelagrim vi Cenak|Pelagrim's Fangs]]" to the [[Anderung]], and known as the "[[Pelagrim vi Cenak|Praesape Cluster]]" in [[Terran]] astronomy.

The Pelagrim sector was discovered by [[Yaron Pelagrim]], one of the [[Ven Yaron]] of the [[Anderung]]. The [[Anderung]] settled in the sector, and formed the coalition known as the [[Yurtosh hora ven Serthra]].

=== Border Systems ===

The [[Omicron Carinae System]] and [[Mimosa System]] are located more corewards, near the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Pelagrim's Fangs ===

The [[Tirshan System]], [[Honis System]], [[Cirus System]], [[Cinaed System]], and [[Centus System]] are located in the cluster of stars known as "[[Pelagrim vi Cenak|Pelagrim's Fangs]]".

=== Uninhabited Star Systems ===

The remains of the [[Mirach System]] are still located in the Pelagrim Sector, but there is nothing of any value in the system and it is never visited. The [[Zeta Carinae System]] is also of note, if only for being the historical site of both [[First Contact]] with the [[Volucris]], and the last open battle of the [[Imperial Civil War]].

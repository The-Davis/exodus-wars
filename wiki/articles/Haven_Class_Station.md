The Haven Class Station was a drydock and harborage platform for starship construction, repair, resupply, and re-arming. They were designed by [[Haven Shipyards]], and were so efficient that a single station had the facilities to manufacture an entire [[Atlas Class Carrier]] from processed materials in eleven months' time. If the parts were fabricated elsewhere and assembled on the station, the time could be cut to four months.

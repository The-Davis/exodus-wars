The HMS ''Cougar'' was one of the earlier ships built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

Its fighter squadrons were noteable for their odd nickname for the carrier's rack launch system. The fighter squadrons were collectively nicknamed "the Kittens."

When it was launched, the HMS ''Cougar'' was placed at the head of Carrier Group 6, and placed under the command of Commodore [[Dennis Charron]]. Commodore Charron oversaw the ten years of command leading up to the [[Second Exodus War]], a tour of duty that largely consisted of training exercises in deep space with the rest of the fleet. The first engagement with the forces of the [[United Earth Alliance]] ended Commodore Charron's career and his life, and nearly ended with the destruction of the HMS ''Cougar''. In the aftermath, the ''Cougar'' was entrusted to the command of Commodore [[Santeri Steinns]], until the ship and its entire crew disappeared from history in the first battle with the [[Volucris]] in [[2324]].

=== Commodore Charron's Command ===

Under the command of Commodore Charron, the HMS ''Cougar'' was an unremarkable ship. It served with the rest of the fleets, participating in training exercises and getting the "vats" (genetically-engineered, artificially-gestated soldiers) ready for combat.

==== 2315 ====

The blockade of [[Earth]] and the [[Sol System]] was one of the largest military operations of the [[Second Exodus War]], with both of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s Armadas participating in full strength. During the blockade, the HMS ''Cougar'' and its escorts captured several fleeing Alliance ships. Rather than destroy them, Commodore Charron ordered his crew to bring the transports into the ''Cougar'''s main bay so its Marine complement could sweep the transports for Alliance officers. Instead of the vulnerable officers they expected, the Marines of the ''Cougar'' found themselves facing over a hundred well-armed soldiers of the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s army. The Imperial Marines were overwhelmed in the ensuing firefight, and the bridge of the ''Cougar'' was compromised. Commodore Charron was killed, and the soldiers of the Alliance managed to cripple the carrier before they were gunned down. The HMS ''Cougar'' spent nearly a year in drydock back in the [[Alpha Centauri System]] before it was pronounced combat-ready and turned over to Commodore Steinns.

=== Commodore Steinns' Command ===

Commodore Santeri Steinns turned the HMS ''Cougar'' into a renowned raider and intelligence liaison. It often penetrated deep into the [[Titan System]] to drop off coded dispatches for Omega operatives, or to pick up encrypted messages.

==== 2324 ====

In the aftermath of the [[Second Exodus War]], the HMS ''Cougar'' fled to the outskirts of the [[Pelagrim Sector]] with the bulk of the surviving [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. In the battle that marked the official end of the Second Exodus War and the beginning of the [[Volucris Incursion]], the ''Cougar'' engaged the [[Volucris]] in combat. It is unknown if it survived the battle, but the crew has not been seen in civilized space since then.

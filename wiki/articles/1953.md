== Events of 1953 ==
===January===
    
*January 7 - [[Cold War]]: President [[Thomas Dewey]] announces the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] has developed a hydrogen bomb.
*January 20 - President [[Thomas Dewey]] is sworn in for his second term in office.

===February===
*February 11 - [[Cold War]]: The Soviet Union breaks diplomatic relations with Israel.
*February 28 - [[The Technology Race]]: James Watson and Francis Crick announce that they have discovered the structure of the DNA molecule.

===March===
*March 1 - Joseph Stalin suffers a stroke and collapses after an all-night dinner with Soviet Union officials.
*March 5 - Joseph Stalin dies after 26 years of ruling the Soviet Union.
*March 6 - Georgi Malenkov succeeds Josef Stalin as Premier.
*March 14 - Nikita Khruschev selected general secretary of the Soviet [[Communist]] Party.

===April===
*April 25 - [[The Technology Race]]: Francis Crick and James Watson publish their description of the double helix structure of DNA.

===May===
*May 9 – [[The Jihad]]: France agrees to the provisional independence of Cambodia under the leadership of Norodom Sihanouk.

===June===
*June 8 - Austria and Soviet Union form diplomatic relations.
*June 16 - Soviet Union and Yugoslavia form diplomatic relations.
*June 18 - [[The Jihad]]: Monarchy of Egypt overthrown by a Muslim theocracy.

===July===
*July 26 - [[Cold War]]: Fidel Castro and his brother killed in a disastrous assault on the Moncada Barracks. Cuban Revolution falters.

===August===
*August 8 - [[Cold War]]:Soviet Premier Georgi Malenkov announces that the Soviet Union has a hydrogen bomb.
*August 18 - The Kinsey Report, considered a threat to American ideals, is censored by the CIA.
*August 19 - [[The Jihad]]: The CIA attempts to overthrow the government of Mohammed Mossadegh in Iran. Operation Ajax fails. Shah Mohammad Reza Pahlavi killed by Mossadegh.
*August 20 - [[The Jihad]]: French government ousts the sultan of Morocco and exiles him to Corsica.

===September===
*September 5 - [[United Nations]] does not accept the Soviet Union's suggestion to accept the People's Republic of China as a member.
*September 7 - Nikita Khrushchev becomes head of the Soviet Central Committee.

===October===
*October 6 - [[The Technology Race]]: The UNIVAC 1103 is the first commercial computer to use random access memory.
*October 30 - [[Cold War]]: US President Thomas Dewey formally approves the top secret document National Security Council Paper No. 162/2, which states that the United States' arsenal of nuclear weapons must be maintained to counter the Communist threat. Nuclear expansion is not approved for fear of total annihilation, and only a sufficient arsenal to destroy tactical targets is approved.

===November===
*November 9 - Cambodia becomes independent from France.
*November 29 - [[Vietnam War]]: French paratroopers take Dien Bien Phu.

===December===
*December 2 - British Empire severs diplomatic ties with Iran.
*December 8 - [[The Technology Race]]: [[United States (Terra)|United States]] President [[Thomas Dewey]] delivers his "Atomic Future" address to the United Nations General Assembly in Geneva, promising the development of new technology for treating radiation sickness and for cleaning up nuclear waste.
*December 10 - Albert Schweitzer was given the 1952 Nobel Peace Prize.

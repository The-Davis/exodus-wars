== Events of 2325 ==

The [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] will start in [[2324]] with the death of [[Andreas Tischler]], but won't end until late [[2325]] with the dissolution of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

===February===

===March===
The Volucris came out of their lull with a vengeance, attacking the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] with tremendous force. Their hit and run raids were only the beginning. April saw the temporary end of the Volucris Incursion when the Battle of Kronheim concluded.

*March 18 - [[Ikronin]] Convoy Attacked
*March 27 - Prefecture Forces Mobilized
*March 28 - Battle of Kronheim: Aces High

=== April ===

* April 2 - [[Kronheim]] saw the true fury of the [[Volucris]] unleashed. Never before had they attacked with such deadly precision, singularity of purpose, or cunning ability. The full might of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] was thrown up against them, and would have been crushed if not for the intervention and aid of the [[Prefecture]].  The main battles of the [[Volucris Invasion of Kronheim]] were fought on this date.

===August===
Unknown to most, a different sort of war is brewing. Always quiet and enigmatic, the Meroniri entertain a most interesting guest on their homeworld.

*August 13 - Sepulare, Sanctuary!
*August 14 - Who can Fathom the Heart of the Abyss?

===September===

*September 16 - The Red Cross

===October===
All is not well in the [[Alliance of Planets]]. The Meroniri Terinasi, the most stabilizing force in the galaxy, suddenly and arbitrarily alters its trade agreements with many independent factions. Harsh embargos are put in place on [[Ixoarchada]] goods, and travel from [[Meroniri]] space to the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] is banned for "health reasons."

*October 20 - Tariffs of Cruelty
*October 22 - What Plague?

===November===
The [[Meroniri]] quarantine of Commonwealth space is lifted with no explanation or apology. One veteran of [[Meroniri Terinasi|Meroniri government]] affairs and one of the few openly public figures in their government, the Minister of Defense Derri Meninnon, suddenly offers up his resignation and disappears from the public eye.

*November 7 - No Apology
*November 9 - I Can Serve No Longer

===December===
Long ignored and nearly forgotten, the [[Reigess Suverände]] begins to flex some newfound economic muscle. Lucrative trade agreements with the [[Prefecture]] allow the [[Reigens]] to put themselves on the proverbial starmap.

Increasingly upset with the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]]'s stance on the use of certain substances, the [[Sirius Families]] opens talks with the [[Reigens]] and the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], resulting in a new organization to counter the all-reaching authority of the [[Alliance of Planets]]. The [[Council of Independent Nations]] enters into the galactic arena of commerce and military power.

*December 17 - A Grand Affair
*December 19 - Busted!
*December 26 - The Council of Independent Nations
*December 26 - Ixoarchada Membership

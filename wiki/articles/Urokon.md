The Ansible was first conceived by a Lorithos theoretical physicist, Vor Krasin, in the years of the Huern Dynasty. It could not be built until late 2300, long after Krasin's death. The project was headed and funded by the Vor Krasin communications interest, a Lorithos company founded by the physicist's descendants, and assisted by a team of scientists from every Member State in the Alliance of Planets. The transceiver allowed for instantaneous communication over any distance.

The Ansible uses an application of particle spin-correlation theory, first documented on Terra in 1935 and known as the Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) theory. Vor Krasin independently theorized it in 1994. This effect is such that chained particles are so intertwined that when one changes polarity, every particle in the chain changes in the exact opposite way instantaneously.

Vor Krasin took the theory farther than Einstein. He believed that by manipulating magnetic fields, the particle chains could be spun in a controllable fashion. Krasin managed to create a particle chain and manipulate it with a magnetic field, but it was microscopic and hardly useful at the time.

The Alliance of Planets science team created a machine which constantly observes an EPR particle chain. This was the receiver. They also created a fine-tuned magnetic manipulation machine for transmission. The basic Ansible transceiver was completed when they devised a transmission-reception binary code, so that the transceiver can interpret binary changes in polarity to video and sound.

The early Ansible was good for the controlled environment of a laboratory, but EPR chains had difficulty surviving in regular atmosphere. Hyperspace, with its multiple predictable bands, offered an excellent place to install the particle chains. The Lorithos used their Bailey Hyperspace Tunnel to access a single stable band with transmitters and receivers within hyperspace. Every time a message needed to come through, the Tunnel opened up into normal space and connected to a normal Optical Transceiver or Microwave Transceiver.

The Ansible system was installed on every inhabited planet in the Alliance within one year of the deployment of the gravity strings and EPR particle chains. The transceivers are hooked up to public networks, allowing everyone who can afford the moderate access fees the ability to transmit instantaneously to anywhere in the inhabited galaxy. It has been a huge boon for galactic commerce and military communication. The Ansible paid for itself in access fees alone within a month of activation.

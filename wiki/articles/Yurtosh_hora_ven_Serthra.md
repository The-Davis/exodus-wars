===History===
Main Article: [[History of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|History]]

The Yurtosh hora ven Serthra was a coalition of [[Anderung]] warlords known as the [[Ven Yaron]]. After the chaos of the [[Sundering]], they fled the [[Sagittarius Arm]], and somehow managed to make their way to the [[Orion Arm]] and the [[Pelagrim Sector]]. They settled on a world they dubbed [[Mesar]].

For centuries, the [[Anderung]] searched for the [[Gorhamut]], their beloved masters and creators. During their long travels, they instead found the [[Ixoarchada]], a [[human]] [[:Category:Races|race]] dwelling in the [[Tolana Sector]]. The [[Anderung]] made no distinction between the [[Ixoarchada]] and the hated [[Anthronoris]], and fought dozens of wars with them until they were defeated and driven back to their holdings in the [[Pelagrim Sector]].

In the [[21st Century]], the [[Anderung]] found easier prey. The [[Terrans]] in the [[Sol Sector]] were only just beginning to learn the use of [[hyperspace]]. The [[Ven Yaron]] made a decision: if they could not find their masters, they would become masters themselves. The [[Anderung]] captured many [[Terran]] colonists, and developed them into the slave-race known as the [[Mesarnak]]. This eventually led to their downfall when [[Otto Tischler]] led the rebellion known as the [[Mesarthrim]], which later became the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and completely wiped out the Yurtosh hora ven Serthra.

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Government]]

The [[Ven Yaron]] rule their subjects in a dictatorship, but it is similar in fashion to a militaristic tribal system. Most [[Ven Yaron]] have trusted advisors which they consult before making major decisions. While an [[Anderung]] is expected to obey the words of the [[Ven Yaron]], it is neither uncommon nor unexpected for a [[Ven Yaron]] to listen and consider if an [[Anderung]] objects to their commands.

===Law===
Main Article: [[Law of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Law]]

[[Yaron Pelagrim]] was the traditional founding [[Ven Yaron]] of the Yurtosh hora ven Serthra. He governed the [[Anderung]] loyal to him in a relatively benevolent fashion, and constantly led expeditions away from home in the hope of finding the [[Gorhamut]].

The [[Anderung]] were governed by a few "Durosh", or "directives", which were simple and easy to understand. When they created and enslaved the [[Mesarnak]], the law system became much more complicated, and many Vordurosh, or "absolute directives" were created which applied only to the [[Mesarnak]].

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Foreign Relations]]

The Yurtosh hora ven Serthra's main objective for centuries was finding and reuniting themselves with the [[Gorhamut]]. Most [[Anderung]] remembered and loved their masters. They never found them.

They did find the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], with whom they fought relentlessly. The [[Anderung]] were finally pushed back into their own territory, but soon found the [[United Earth Alliance]], and captured their citizens and changed them into the [[Mesarnak]]. The last foreign government they met was the result of this slavery: the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], the doom of the [[Anderung]].

===Military===
Main Article: [[Military of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Military]]

The [[Anderung]] were originally created as guardians of the [[Gorhamut]]. They were meant to complement their allies, the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Volucris]], in battle. When the [[Njaa]] changed the [[Ngrligru]] into voracious monsters, the psyche of the [[Anderung]] was altered. Their computer interface gave the [[Anderung]] an artificially "gestahlt ego", and the [[Anderung]] as a whole suffered from their own equivalent of Post Traumatic Stress Disorder.

While bred to protect, they now have changed this into a violent will to dominate and conquer, coupled with the hopeless loneliness and loss of their masters.

===Territory===
Main Article: [[Territory of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Territory]]

At the height of their expansion and power, the [[Anderung]] held multiple [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]] in both the [[Pelagrim Sector]] and the [[Tolana Sector]].  Prior to their defeat by the [[Ixoarchada]], their primary Tolana Sector holdings included the [[Cyprana System|Erian System]], [[Irthun System]], and the [[Yish-Val System]].  When they were finally and completely defeated by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] in [[2308]], they only had four: the [[Tirshan System]], the [[Honis System]], the [[Cirus System]], and the [[Centus System]].

===Economy===
Main Article: [[Economy of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Economy]]

The [[Anderung]] were "uplifted" from a foraging species, and had no instincts for raising food on their own. Fortunately for their survival, they learned most of the skills needed and were able to create a stable economy of trade. Their currency, the [[Qhreg]], was more of a system of favors rather than an actual monetary currency. Towards the end of their civilization, slaves, technology, and food were their main trade goods among themselves.

===Demographics===
Main Article: [[Demographics of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Demographics]]

The Yurtosh hora ven Serthra was not an exclusively [[Anderung]] organization. The [[Mesarnak]] actually outnumbered their taskmasters towards the end of the [[Anderung]] dominion in [[2308]], and not all of them were unwilling slaves.

===Culture===
Main Article: [[Culture of Yurtosh hora ven Serthra|Culture]]

The [[Anderung]] culture suffered from an unusual malady. They had an incurable sense of loss from the separation from their masters, and an almost neurotic need to destroy or dominate all life they encountered.

Prior to their downfall, the [[Anderung]] did manage to create many great wonders in their culture, some of which are still upheld by the [[Mesarthrim]] and the [[Mesarnak]] today.

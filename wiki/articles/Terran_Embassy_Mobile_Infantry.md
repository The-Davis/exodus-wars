The Terran Regulars use the M-16 Excalibur powered infantry armor for their troops.  Their arsenal is quite powerful, but not as extensive as the Prefecture Army's.  The automatic rifle is the standard-issue weapon.  They also use the SRM Delivery System and the portable field gun.  They also have access to smaller ordinance like shrapnel grenades and beacons to guide in air strikes.

[[Image:TerranEmbassyMobileInfantry.jpg]]

Switching to power armor didn't mean retiring the drop pod.  With thick panels and internal padding, the drop pod can protect a soldier inside much better than his power armor.  Retro-rockets fire at the end of descent to soften falls from higher heights.

Drop pods are very resilient.  Knocking one out of the sky is much more difficult than shooting down old-fashioned paratroopers, and the carrying capacity is much higher.  Infantry drop pods are deployed for clearing out landing zones for infantry aboard Beluga dropships.  The drop pods unit can hold any of the infantry weapons load-outs.

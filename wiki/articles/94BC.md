== Events of 94BC ==

An automated probe called ''[[Lima]]'' is assembled in orbit and launched from [[Glengarr]] to [[Archada]], sending back a host of information on the moon's surface and enviroment.  A month later, the probe ''[[Consas]]'' is sent to [[Dellada]] on a similar mission.  However, the probe is lost due to a malfunction in the controls on descent and is destroyed on impact.  ''[[Losis]]'' is launched from orbit two months later and lands successfully on Dellada, finishing its mission successfully.

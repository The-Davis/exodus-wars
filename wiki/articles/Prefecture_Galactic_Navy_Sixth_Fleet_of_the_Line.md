The Sixth Fleet is commanded by Admiral [[Hannah Baruk]].  The fleet is led by the flagship FDS ''Bastion'' (BG-6), one of the heaviest bombardment warships ever built.  The Sixth Fleet is well-suited to most roles, but is best used for ground support and invasions.

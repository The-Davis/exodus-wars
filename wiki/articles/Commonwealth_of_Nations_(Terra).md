The '''Commonwealth of Nations''' was an association of former colonies of the British Empire, which shared the laws, form of government, currency and language of the Empire itself. Unlike colonies, the Commonwealth of Nations had their own militaries, and parliamentary governments separate from the British Empire.

The members of the Commonwealth of Nations were dissolved in [[2011]] when the individual countries signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

== Events of 483AD ==

The first precursor to colonization, massive satellite factories, arrive in the [[Bollaria System]] and begin preparing landing zones for future colony landings.  

Many of the terraformation satellites begin to go offline.  Rather than use these satellites as building material, as happened on [[Archada]], [[Dellada]], and [[Veratul]], colony planners already have their eyes on other worlds in other systems.

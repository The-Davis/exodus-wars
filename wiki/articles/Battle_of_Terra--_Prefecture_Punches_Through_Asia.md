May 25, [[2322]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Third and Fourth Mechanized Divisions--loyal to Anthony Hadrian--have captured the city of Beijing, formerly the capital of China. Weapons facilities surrounding the city were looted. Regiments from the Third Division went north from Beijing, clearing Empire's infantry forces out of small settlements.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Fourth Mechanized Division left two regiments of medium Rigs to guard the captured city, then headed south, intent on capturing the massive Rig production facility in Hong Kong. Now, two days after leaving Beijing, the Fourth Mechanized Division is pinned down by a division of Behemoth assault Rigs loyal to the Emperor. The Third Mechanized Division is rushing to assist the Fourth, but they are being harassed by light Rigs and infantry as they go.

The XB-4 was a brand new large airframe, designed to incorporate the MPD, an internal bomb bay, and external racks for AWTs and free-fall bombs. Initial tests met with some of the most catastrophic failures in Imperial aerospace history, with two XB-4s exploding in unmanned test flights, and one blowing up on the ground and killing an entire maintenance crew. It was determined that hydrazine, the fuel of choice when the MPD was used as a booster for the F-4C, was too volatile in large quantities for sustained use. The XF-8 and XB-4 projects were both competing for design time and for resources, and the XB-4 project was scrubbed. Instead, changes were incorporated into the B-2A and B-3A.

With the XF-8's successes, and the F-9 Coyote rolling off the assembly line, the Imperial engineers were free to return to the drawing board on the new XB-5. A significant deadline, T-Day, the invasion of Titanian, loomed overhead. The Grand Admiral and the Emperor demanded a new medium bomber be ready to support the fleet against the significant Titanian Defense Force, and to support the Army against their considerable embedded defenses. Using xenon as a fuel instead of hydrazine helped with the stability issues, as did the incorporation of the computer systems designed for the XF-8. The YB-5 was built to accommodate a crew of two instead of just one. The first 40 B-5 Warhawks rolled off the assembly line in time for the first invasion into the Titanian system.

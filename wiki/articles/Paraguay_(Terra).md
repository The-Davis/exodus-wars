'''Paraguay''', officially the '''Republic of Paraguay''', is a landlocked country in South America. It lies on both banks of the Paraguay River, bordering [[Argentina (Terra)|Argentina]] to the south and southwest, [[Brazil (Terra)|Brazil]] to the east and northeast, and [[Bolivia (Terra)|Bolivia]] to the northwest, and is located in the very heart of South America. The name "Paraguay" is derived from the Guaraní word pararaguay meaning "from a great river". The "great river" is the Parana River, which produces the greatest amount of hydroelectric power in the world.

Paraguay was dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] world government on March 18, [[2013]].

The '''Mesarnak''' (''homo mesarthes'') are descended from [[Terrans]] who emigrated from their homeworld in the [[21st Century]] in cold sleep. Before they arrived on their intended destination, [[Kalmar]], their vessel was intercepted by an alien species known as the [[Anderung]]. The sleeping colonists were all abducted and their vessel was hurled into [[Kalmar]]'s star. They were taken to the planet [[Mesar]] and awoke into a life of enslavement. They suffered almost three centuries of enslavement, warfare, and atrocities which molded them into a culture of war and death. The colonist were named "Mesarnak" by their overlords. The derogative name meant "slaves of Mesar".

Before awaking the colonists on [[Mesar]], the [[Anderung]] studied and adapted the Mesarnak genome to suit their needs, this genetic engineering created a "super slave" for the [[Anderung]].

===Appearance===

The Mesarnak were taken from several European [[Terran]] stocks, noteably from the [[British Empire (Terra)|British isles]], [[France (Terra)|France]], [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]], and [[Spain (Terra)|Spain]]. All Mesarnak come from Caucasian stock of one sort or another, but the more "Aryan" racial appearances tend to be found in the [[Mesarthrim]] rather than the Mesarnak.

====Skin Tone====

All Mesarnak have white skin, and tend not to tan easily. Some are even pale beyond what would be considered normal among their [[Terran]] forebears.

====Hair Color====

Mesarnak tend to dark brown, light brown, or reddish hair. Most blonde Mesarnak are from [[Germany (Terra)|German]] descent, and adopt the [[Mesarthrim]] name.

====Eye Color====

All Mesarnak have genetically-enhanced eyes. The Mesarnak eye has an additional lens its surface.  By squinting hard, Mesarnak can force the lens forward, effectively "zooming" their focus forward by a factor of four to eight times. Mesarnak see farther and in greater detail with the naked eye than their ancestors could. The iris and pupil can also dilate or constrict to a much greater degree than most humans, alloing them to see in the dark to a greater degree or look directly into a light source without suffering damage or discomfort.

These genetic advantages caused several cosmetic side effects. The added control of the eye was caused by the genetic addition of fifteen additional ocular muscles in each eye socket, requiring increased blood supply. The blood flows from a major arterial and veinal group which is visible in stark contrast on their faces, giving the area around their eyes a "spiderweb" appearance of darker skin.

Additionally, Mesarnak eyes are colorless--[[white eyes]]. The eye actually loses its color over time, when a baby Mesarnak is born, their eyes appear colored like most other human eyes, but as they grow older, the separating lenses begin to cloud over and become entirely white. When a Mesarnak child hits puberty, their eyes will periodically bleed as the eye finishes growing, and reaches its final size. If the eye does not properly grow, it will need to be surgically bled or they will begin to experience ruptures in their ocular blood veins, which can permanently scar or blind them.

===Circulatory System===

While tweaking the human genome, the main goal of the [[Anderung]] was to enhance the strength and endurance of ''homo sapiens'' into a powerful and efficient slave. Ideally, the end result--''homo mesarthes''--would be able to do twice the work in the same amount of time.

To achieve enhanced endurance, the [[Anderung]] altered the Mesarnak heart. The genome was altered to add two additional chambers. The heart's increased blood capacity could not be supported by the existing pulmonary arteries. The lungs' blood systems were doubled, then a second aorta was added to allow additional bloodflow to pump throughout the body. This redesigned circulatory system allowed oxygenated blood to effectively and efficiently flow throughout the body.

===Metabolism===

The average Mesarnak eats more in a single meal than a normal human eats in an entire day.  Despite an almost ravenous appetite, it is difficult to find a fat Mesarnak. Their naturally high metabolism burns energy so quickly that fat does not form easily, and most nutritional substances that humans do not digest well, such as sugars, are burned or eliminated rather than being stored. As a control mechanism, the [[Anderung]] wanted Mesarnak to rely on daily calorie intake. Once separated from a regular food source, Mesarnak quickly starve to death. A benefit of their altered metabolism is extraordinarily efficient protein processing. Mesarnak can build more muscle mass from less strenuous activity than most humans.

===Lifespan and Reproduction===

The [[Mesarthrim]] and the Mesarnak have similar lifespans. They are genetically the same species, ''homo mesarthes''. Culturally, there are often differences in their life cycles, but typically only five to ten years.

Mesarnak reach puberty by eleven to twelve years, and full adulthood by twenty years. Mesarnak females are fertile from the time they reach puberty until they are about sixty years old. Mesarnak males can sire children from about two or three years into puberty until they are about seventy-five years old.

The [[Anderung]] wanted a numerous slave population, so they artificially boosted the ''homo mesarthes'' genome to have an increased sex drive and fertility rate. While Mesarnak embryos still gestate for nine months, the mothers recover within days and are fertile again almost immediately afterwards. During ovulation, the female Mesarnak's hypothalamic system releases ultra-heightened levels of gonadotropin, estrogen and progesterone. Mesarnak women become agitated and extremely aggressive when fertile. Mesarnak males have a constantly heightened level of testosterone, four times that of a typical ''homo sapiens''. This causes unusualy heightened aggression levels in Mesarnak men at all times, a side-effect the [[Anderung]] did not expect, which may explain the downfall of their control over their slaves in the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]].

Essentially, without realizing it, the [[Anderung]] designed a species built to fight and to breed, gave them the strength and endurance to carry out their bloodlust with brutal efficiency, and gave them the fertility rates to outbreed their slave masters.

===Behavior===

The behavior of the [[Mesarthrim]] and the Mesarnak are the logical result of their design. By most human standards, left unchecked, a Mesarnak is a common barbarian, thinking of nothing but reproducing and fighting.

Throughout their history, the instincts of the [[Mesarthrim]] and the Mesarnak have been held in check by some form of outside control. Before the rise of the [[Mesarthrim]], the Mesarnak were completely controlled by the [[Anderung]] slave masters. The [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] introduced the state church and the rigid [[:Clans of the Mesarthrim|clan]] structure.

Since the dawn of the [[:Clans of the Mesarthrim|clan]] structure, Mesarthrim society adopted a rigid code of honor, mostly upheld by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] government. This is simply another example of external control, not a natural behavior of their makeup.

Without strict control, it is impossible to say if the Mesarnak will uphold the ideals of honor introduced by [[Otto Tischler]], or if they will degenerate into a barbarian menace.

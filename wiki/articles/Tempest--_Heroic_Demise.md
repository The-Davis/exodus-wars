This takes place during the month of December in the fifth year of the Tempest War

by Austin Bailey

The blackness of the far-regions of darkest space is instantly illuminated as warships descend from hyperspace, reverting to a more familiar plane of existence. The Prefecture Fifth Fleet has just emerged from a jump out of Auellel League space and is preparing to make a run for Mesar. Along with the Prefecture 5th is a host of troop transports, support ships, and landing craft. Two Prefecture Legions and the bulk of the Auellal Army, along with the entire Auellal Navy, a force of nearly as many warships as the Prefecture 5th, are preparing to run the gauntlet and take the space station Emerging Horizon, and then land on the planet and commence the assault on the major United Centusi States military strongholds. 

''This will be no walk in the park,'' Prefecture Admiral Nolan thinks. Despite the UCS’ defeat at the hands of the Prefecture in the Battle for Albanus, the UCS still outnumbers the Prefecture fleet by 5-to-1 odds. However, the Prefecture’s Admiral Nolan has the advantage of surprise. The UCS fleet is entirely unready for an attack only two days after their gamble for New Albany, a gamble that failed and cost them a large number of ships. Additionally, the Centus system is no longer entirely foreign terrain with the entrance of the AAL into the war on the side of the Prefecture.

Nolan types in some orders on his command chair, and the fleet proceeds according to the plan. The AAL Navy will remain on the outer fringes of the system with all of the troop ships, while the BP forces proceed with an attack meant mainly as a diversion. Once the UCS fleet is significantly distracted with the 5th Fleet, the AAL Navy and combined troop ships will make a short FTL jump closer to the planet, and make a run for the surface. The AAL Navy will be playing escort for the troop ships, and if all goes according to plan, they should land on the surface without suffering any losses. 
The primary purpose of this entire endeavor is to land troops on Emerging Horizon and Mesar. The first ground target is the Andreas Tischler Interstellar Spaceport. Once this is completed, all fighters of the fleets will be sent planet-side to provide support and will be based at the spaceport, while the BP and AAL ships jump out of the system.
“All ships advance to waypoints Alpha.” This simple command sets the entire plan in motion. The die is cast, and now the fates will have their say.

====Space Station Emerging Horizon - Mesar, High Orbit – 0630 hours====
“Sir, we’ve got incoming!” A young ensigns’ call heralds the arrival of the Prefecture fleet. 
“On screen!” the watch captain orders. 
Quickly a large sensor reading and accompanying information screens fill the main viewing port of the Emerging Horizon’s bridge. “What do we have here Mr. Baker?” the captain asks the young ensign manning the sensors station. 
“Unknown at this range sir, and approaching in the shadow of the planet so we cannot get accurate optical lock. We just picked up FTL signatures, and suddenly they were here.”
Reviewing the sensors screen, the watch captain scans quickly through them to try and seek out some sort of useful information. “It’s short range FTL. There’s no major hyperspace trail. It could be anybody. Sound general quarters!” 
A hail of alarms and sirens go off throughout the station. Mesarthrim rush to their stations to man defensive platforms, while pilots get to their fighters. 
“Admiral Stadt!” A young officer bursts into the dark room, and awakens Stadt suddenly. “Sir! We’ve got incoming of unknown strength and origin!” 
“If it’s those damned AAL again then I’m really going to be mad. Get basic optical scanners online, standard wave scanners…you know the drill,” is Stadt’s simple reply. 
“Sir, they are approaching in the planet’s shadow, and have just recently come out of FTL. They’ll be in range in only 15 minutes.” 
“Fifteen minutes!?! Why wasn’t I alerted earlier? Why didn’t we detect them earlier?” 
The young officer’s silence tells Stadt that he does not know, and Stadt quickly surmises that they must have entered the system on the far side of another planet, most likely the furthest planet in the system. It would take someone familiar with the Proximae Centauri system to make such a jump and know where to enter, so he quickly decides it must be an AAL raid. 
“That doesn’t make sense,” Stadt muses. “The AAL wouldn’t make an attack this close to Mesar.”
“Admiral on deck!” Stadt’s entrance onto the Emerging Horizon’s command deck is announced as he quickly rushes in to see what is happening. “Give me a report Captain Meyers.” 
The watch captain quickly links Stadt’s command chair with his own, and soon Stadt can see all available information. 
“What is the status of the fleet Captain?” Stadt inquires. We’ve got 50% of our forces docked in orbital stations, 40% already mobilized, and 10% in dry-dock undergoing repairs. The ships in dry-dock should be underway within the half hour.” 
“That’s not fast enough Captain…make it within the next 20 minutes!” Stadt barks.

Flagship Judicator – 0635 hours
“Attention! Attention! Approaching craft or crafts! Identify yourself. You have entered restricted United Centusi States space and are on a course for Mesar. Please change your bearing to the coordinates being sent to you and await inspection. If you continue on your present course, you will be fired upon.” This warning from the UCS forces officially decrees that there is no going back anymore. 
“This is our hour men! Now is our time! For Leskovik!” 
Nolan’s rallying cry sends out a cheer throughout the Judicator and the Prefecture fleet. At this, the entire fleet unleashes a full volley of missile fire. At this range, targeting is not 100% accurate, but with a fleet’s worth of missiles, 100% accuracy is not needed, and getting in the first strike in this conflict can mean the difference between victory and defeat.

Space Station Emerging Horizon - 0635 hours
“Sir! We’ve got missiles inbound bearing 95 dash 354!” 
Ensign Baker routes the incoming trajectory information to the command station’s monitors. “Get all shields online now! I want weapons systems at 100%, and get me some fighter cover damn it! What’s the status on the fleet?” 
Stadt’s commands are follow through swiftly. “Sir, the fleet is moving around from the far side of the planet and should be on an intercept path momentarily. They have launched fleet fighters, as well as planetary fighters, and we’ve emptied our fighter bays. Contact in 10 minutes.” 
“Good Mr. Baker!” 
Stadt looks at his command screen and notices some blue dots not moving. “Mr. Baker, why aren’t those ships in sector 8 moving to intercept?” 
“Those are rear guard units incase we are attacked from the far side of the planet,” Baker responds. 
“You mean if they jump out of Centus itself? Please! Get those ships here now! Incase they haven’t noticed, we are under attack!” 
As Stadt finishes his sentence, a hail of missiles strikes the station and the surrounding fleet. No major damage is inflicted, although fighter losses are reported. 
“Sir! Computers have finished analyzing the missiles and explosions. They are Prefecture missiles, conventional type.” 
“Then our adversaries here are the Prefecture, no doubt, Mr. Baker. Sound the attack. Route the coordinates and plans from my station to the commanders of the fleet. Captain Meyers, you have the bridge. I am heading to the Tunguska to lead the fleet.” 
At this, Admiral Stadt leaves the bridge and heads for the docking bay, where the massive Tunguska super-carrier is docked and waiting for its commander.
As he is walking out, Director William Camp bursts onto the bridge. “Stadt! What the bloody hell is going on here? Give me reports!” 
“The Prefecture has come to play, sir, and I shall teach them a lesson.”

Flagship Judicator – 0640 hours
“Sir, we report minimal damage on missile strikes, few direct hits, minimal enemy losses. Seems only a few dozen fighters and a pair of gun ships we lost.” 
“Damn! That volley should have done far more damage,” Nolan thinks. “Prepare all tubes again Mr. Donovan! Captain Pritchard!” 
Nolan calls over his intercom to the commander of the fleet’s lead Avenger. “Aye Captain?” is Pritchard’s response. “Get our Avengers between us and their fleet. Head for the far side of the station, see how many of them you can get to follow you. We’ll lead the bulk of the frigates in support of you as we circle around the other side of the station.” 
Nolan knows that if he can get his biggest hitters to make a push, it will require that the UCS sends its largest cruiser to intercept them, hopefully opening up a window through which the troop transport ships can make it down to the planet. 
That space station Emerging Horizon itself is a shimmering beauty in the light of Centus, an almost pure-white sun. It’s smooth, flowing outline, a product of Archadian engineering and architecture, stands out against the blackness of space, as does its bristling arsenal and myriad of support vessels. Past that outer wall, deep inside, it holds nearly 10,000 personnel, soldiers, and pilots. Nolan thinks once again, “This really won’t be a walk in the park.”

Space Station Emerging Horizon - 0645 hours
Ensign Baker is monitoring his data screen, and the precious moment he has been awaiting finally comes. “All naval ships finally mobilized sir. We’re keeping the damaged ones near the back of the fleet…All Prefecture ships in range, sir. Awaiting orders from the fleet.” 
Over the intercom comes a reply. “All ships, this is the Tunguska. Fire at will!” 
At Stadt’s command, the entire fleet lets loose its fury. “You heard the Admiral Mr. Baker, give them a volley from the main guns” Captain Meyers orders. 
The volley from the Mesarthrim fleet is overwhelming, but not devastating. The power of the Mesarthrim weaponry lies in its close-range punch, far great than any Prefecture neutron cannon, but at range the Mesarthrim weaponry quickly loses much of its power. Still, the volley is powerful to say the least, and the shields of the Intrepid frigate Napa, the fleet’s lead ship, buckle under the volley. It is crippled by the blast, and a general evacuation order is given, followed moments later by dozens of life-pods blasting into space.
In the area between the fleets, the fighters of the Prefecture and Mesarthrim navies grapple for space supremacy. The Mesarthrim interceptors are much faster than the Prefecture’s heavy Stukas and Bloodhawks, but the larger Prefecture craft can take more punishment. 
“God bless the High Tribune!” one Prefecture pilot thinks, as the Archadian software on his Valkyrie takes over for a fraction of a second to dodge an incoming fusion-pulse round. “I’d be dead if he hadn’t gotten buddy-buddy with those Archadians!” 
Even without a complete fighter grade APACHE and level 1 interface, the basic software reacts quickly to incoming threats, making it harder to hit the small fighter-bomber. 
This fleeting thought and action, taking less than a second, are soon forgotten as the pilot gets back to the task at hand. The Valkyries and Bloodhawks are making a run on the Emerging Horizon, hoping to take out its weapons stations so that it cannot support the fleet. As the fighter close to within range of the station, the Emerging Horizon’s anti-fighter turrets light up in a blaze of fury. 
By this point, the Prefecture’s Avengers have circled around the Mesarthrim fleet and are in a position to fire on the station, while the Judicator and its frigate escorts circle further out with the fleet’s carriers, directing the battle and pulling the bulk of the Mesarthrim forces away from the planet.

Flagship Judicator – 0700 hours
“We’ve taken another hit Admiral!” a young bridge officer reports. 
The small sub-capitals of the UCS Navy have closed with the Prefecture fleet, and now the fighting is taking place at frighteningly close range. The space around the fleet is glowing white with bursts of plasma, neutron beams, and missile contrails. “The Haddock is down sir, and we’ve lost the Union as well.” The hail of fire from the Mesarthrim fleet is taking a toll on the smaller ships of the 5th.
“Damn! Take us to coordinates 47 mark 95!” Nolan orders, moving his fleet up and away from the Mesarthrim, hoping to pull them even further away from the planet. “We’ve got to get an open window for the ground forces. Pull the Avengers off the station and have them meet us at waypoint Foxtrot…pull the fighters back too.”
The entire Prefecture fleet begins pulling away from the station. Despite the massive amount of firepower being unleashed by both sides, most shields are holding up. The 5th has lost nearly a half dozen of its frigates, and the rest of the ships are being beat pretty hard. On the other side of the fray, the UCS fleet’s flagships, Tunguska and Ares are taking the bulk of the damage, as the Prefecture fleet is concentrating on these two warships instead of trying to take out the hundreds of smaller craft comprising the Mesarthrim fleet. 
The Emerging Horizon itself is the constant victim of hundreds of missiles from fighters and warships, but its massive shielding system is holding up, for the most part. 
“We’ve got a ship coming up our starboard, Admiral.” 
“Identify!” Nolan orders. His screen lights up. “Ship: Trial By Fire. Flagship, UCS 3rd fleet. Sub-capital, FJDF design. Threat-level: High.” 
“Blast!” Nolan thinks. “Send fighters to intercept, as well as Mataco, Graham, and Lamar.” 
The Admiral has cause for worry. The Trial By Fire was a gift to the UCS fleet from the UAR, and was the first operational First Joint Defense Fleet warship. Its Prometheus technologies and Archadian computer systems make it quite an adversary, even for a warships a mere 200 meters long. 

Flagship Trial By Fire – 0705
“Torpedo tubes 1 and 2 fired! Reloading. Turrets 1 and 3 disabled sir.” 
Helmsman Rogers is fresh from the academy, but has already been assigned to the Trial By Fire. “Thank you helmsman. Take us over the bow of the Judicator, invert, and fire on her with our HELPAC.” Admiral Cranston is a veteran of many battles, and has fought Protomorphs, Imperials, and the AAL. “When we break over their port, circle back around over their bridge and see if you can’t get some fire on their main gun.”

Flagship Judicator – 0710
“Our main gun is taking fire sir, and it’s ready to overheat.” 
“Shut it down!” Nolan commands. 
“It’s now or never,” Nolan thinks. “With our main gun out, we can’t put any more heavy fire on that station from this range. We are far enough out, and we are just about surrounded by the Mesarthrim fleet.” As he’s thinking, another Prefecture frigate’s yellow blip on the command screen goes out. 
“Alright!” Nolan suddenly yells. “Comm! Get me Stewart and O’Neill!” 
Less than a second later, the leaders of the Legion of the Dragon and Legion of the Tiger, respectively, answer on the communications system. “What you need Admiral?” 
“We’ve opened the doors as wide as they’re going to be. You need to get in now before they shut.” 
“Aye Admiral!” O’Neill responds. 
“Godspeed men!” is Nolan’s reply.
Moments later, the blackness of space on the far side of the planet is suddenly lit up with ships exiting short-range FTL. A lone UCS warship is on this side of the planet, taking a break from the battle to repair damaged systems. The ships of the AAL Navy quickly blast the UCS ship before it can even get a report out.

Flagship Tunguska – 0720
“Admiral Stadt! We’re getting reports from sensors planet-side. A large force of ships has entered planetary orbit!” 
“What? Where?” Stadt questions. “Sector 8.” 
“Damn it all. Nolan is tricky, isn’t he. Get the Ares and her escorts over there, hit them before they can get into the atmosphere.”
The Ares leaves combat, taking with it its powerful guns, which have been hammering the Prefecture 5th. This allows the 5th to regroup, and enter a defensive formation more useful in stopping the smaller sub-capitals that make up the bulk of the UCS fleet. 
The Ares closes on the far side of the planet, but it is too late. The massive Prefecture troop ships and the League’s landing barges have already made it planet side, and the only ships waiting for the Ares are those of the AAL Navy. They pounce on the Ares, which has only a handful of its escorts, as the rest are coming around the other side of the planet. For a moment, the AAL has the upper hand, and the League’s ships do significant damage to the Ares and her escorts
However, this advantage is soon lost as the remainder of the Ares’ escorts come around the other end of the planet, effectively surrounding the AAL ships. Realizing they cannot win this fight, the AAL ships go over the north-end of the planet, and proceed towards the Prefecture fleet, blasting UCS vessels from behind.

Eagle class Hauler Albany – 0725
Over a loudspeaker being broadcast to all of the hundreds of Eagle, Provider, and Beluga class dropships, General Hal Stewart is addressing his troops. “Most every landing zone we hit will be hot. These Mesarthrim are warriors, the whole lot of them. There will be no civilians, and no quarter will be given, not that they’d surrender anyway. You have your targets; take care of them. Godspeed to all of you.”
As Prefecture and League dropships land all over Mesar, planetary defenses everywhere light up the night sky. Most all the allied forces are landing on the night-side of the planet where it will be harder to track them in Mesar’s moonless night sky. Over the chatter on the communication lines, Stewart hears the scream and crackle of an Eagle drop-ship as it explodes from anti-air fire on the ground. In a brief moment of irony, the massive dropship crashes to the ground, crushing the defense emplacements that destroyed it.
Stewart’s own dropship hits the ground, and as soon as the doors open, neutron blasts and plasma bursts light up the night sky. The Legion of the Dragon is landing in the center of the largest continent on Mesar, about 400 miles due-south of Stone Bridge. The Legion of the Tiger lands some 300 miles to the northwest of the massive Mesarthrim capital of Stone Bridge. The AAL’s Army, some 300,000 strong, lands about 320 miles northeast of Stone Bridge, and must break through a large Mesarthrim fortress stronghold to get to the Capital. 
The plan is to approach the Capital from the northeast, northwest, and south, and capture it within a few days. After that, they are to branch out and take control of the entire continent, at which point, more troops and naval forces will hopefully be diverted from Icronia to support them. 

Flagship Judicator – 0750
“We’ve taken major damage sir. Sensors report 25% casualties, and another 15% close to losing combat-effective status. AAL Commander Balic reports he’s lost a fifth of his warships, and the rest are heavily damaged. They’ve meshed in with our forces, but we’ve got to get out of here soon, or the Ares and its escorts will have completely flanked us and we’ll be cut off from our current course. The forces have landed on the ground, and O’Neill’s Legion has already taken the airfield and spaceport at New Salem.” 
“Good,” Nolan replies. “Prepare to send all our fighters planet-side to support the troops, and we’re getting the hell out of here before we get blasted to bits.
As he says this, the Judicator shudders from a weapons blast. The Ares’ main turrets have knocked a large chunk out of the back of the Judicator. “Damage control, report!” Nolan snaps. 
“Engines are out captain, and the KF’s completely gone.” 
Another concussion shudders the ship, as a blast from the Tunguska’s main cannon hits the Judicators cannon as it was preparing to fire, destroying the weapon. The Judicator is now dead in the water, with only defensive turrets and missile tubes online, and shields that are failing fast. 
“Captain, what do we do?” Nolan stares at the face of the bridge officer, and for a moment, he sees the face of Jordan Marshall looking at him over the display screen, telling him it’s all over. 
“No. I’ll not go down like Jordan. This isn’t an inescapable trap. I’ve just got to figure a way out,” Nolan thinks. 
His mind is racing. With a crippled ship, and a crew too large to evacuate entirely before the Mesarthrim are upon him, he prepares to give the word for the rest of the fleet to run for the rim and make for AAL space. He orders his crew start arming themselves to repel boarders. 
“An Admiral of the Prefecture Navy would make quite a war-prize and bargaining chip in this conflict,” Nolan thinks.
Just as he is preparing to order the retreat, the sky behind him is lit up brighter than day as long-range hyperspace drives tear back into normal space. For a second, a spark of hope lights Nolan’s face, but this hope flickers and fades as the sensors get a positive ID. It is the Prometheus, in all her glory, escorted by the rest of the First Joint Defense Fleet. 
“Damn…I made a gamble that the Archadians would stay out of this…looks like they called my bluff.” 

Space Station Emerging Horizon – 0800
“Sir, we’ve got ships inbound, on course to intercept the Prefecture fleet. We’ve got a positive ID. It’s the 1st JDF,” the young ensign Baker announces as his sensors screen lights up with information. 
“Excellent!” Director Camp responds. “Then our teams in Tincomalee have succeeded in seizing the fleet! Hail the Prometheus, send our battle plan, and my congratulations to Generals Wiedenfeld and Frank for succeeding in the operation with their Myt’herra. Captain Meyers, prepare to give the order for full attack to all Mesarthrim space forces, and get me Admiral Stadt.”
The comm. officer carries out the order. However, when the Prometheus is hailed, it is neither Campaign-General Nick Wiedenfeld, commander of the Myt’herra, nor Rear-General Erica Frank, commander of the Myt’gaul, who answers him from the bridge of the Prometheus. Instead, Admiral Julius Descartes, former general of Tincomalee’s Army, and current second-in-command of the 1st JDF, greets Stadt from the other end of the comm. line. 
“Ah, Director Camp, what a pleasure.” Descartes greets Camp almost jovially. 
“Descartes! What are you doing here?” Camp asks, stunned to see Descartes. 
“I am here in response to a call for help from the  Prefecture. We are here to intervene on behalf of the Prefecture fleet, and have already contacted them about support and relief for their most damage warships.” 
“But the Archadians are non-hostile towards the UCS! Surely the Prometheus Council will not allow this!” Camp questions. 
“But the Council has already granted permission. When UCS special forces attempted to board and capture FJDF vessels, they declared war against the Prometheus Council, and broke the Sears-Ramius Pact in an attempt to undermine Archadian authority over the FJDF. Consider this a formal declaration of war against the United Centusi States by the Prometheus Council, the Independent Nation of Tincomalee, and the United Archadian Republic.” 
Descartes’ calm demeanor is infuriating to Camp, but soon he regains his composure, always the superb diplomat. “See here Admiral Descartes…I don’t know what so-called ‘special forces’ units you are referring to, but no Mesarthrim units have been in the Tincomalee system sin…” 
Descartes cuts him off, suddenly losing his calm disposition. “Director Camp! Do you take myself of the Prometheus Council for a fool? Aboard this very ship is General Wiedenfeld, who has already told us what we needed. We would have General Frank here as well, but she felt death would be more honorable than becoming a prisoner-of-war. Perhaps you may want to charge your sidearm, lest you too become a war-trophy and…” 
The comm. channel is cut off from Camp’s end.
As Director Camp stands on the bridge, stunned, Admiral Stadt’s voice comes over the line. “Camp, what the hell is going on up there? What is the 1st JDF doing here…what the hell did you do?” 
Director Camp starts to explain the attempt to seize the 1st JDF, but is cut off by Stadt before he can finish. “Sir! With all do respect, we have no qualms with the Archadians, and here you go stirring up more trouble than we can handle. And what’s this about Tincomalee declaring independence?” 
While Camp tries to explain the situation, something in Stadt gives way; something snaps. Thinking about Tincomalee, the planet on which he grew up, Stadt realizes the folly of the entire endeavor, and realizes Camp for what he is—another Lucius Black, hell-bent on taking control by manipulating people’s fears and desires for revenge. All his talk of the Alliance’s betrayal and the enduring evil of the Prefecture was simply a ploy to assert his control over the fractured Mesarthrim peoples with the promise of a mighty nation once again. 
Stadt cuts Camp short. “I’m sorry Director Camp. It has been my life’s honor defending Mesar from all threats that have surfaced for the past 40 years, but in this final battle, I must finally say no. It is the twilight of Mesar and the UCS. I hereby resign my command post.”
As he finishes this statement, he contacts Admiral Descartes, announcing his intention to surrender his ship to the Archadian Republic, knowing full well that they would treat him as a surrendering dignitary. The Prefecture would take no prisoners and execute him on the spot. Upon his request, coordinates are sent to Stadt, and seconds later the Tunguska makes a GRLD jump out of the system into Archadian controlled territory, leaving her fighters and the rest of the UCS fleet behind.

Flagship Carthia – 0815
Admiral Palontir Taukin takes a last quick glance at his command screen, and then gives the order. “All ships of the fleet! Disengage hyperspace drives on my mark. Mark!” 
Moments later, on far side of Mesar, Ramius-Dawkins drives tear through the fabric of the universe and drop into normal space as the Archadian 4th fleet, led by the Carthia, pulls itself free from hyperspace. “It seems the situation is just as Admiral Descartes described it. All fighter squadrons prepare for launch. Frigate groups, take up defensive patterns, and cruisers more to primary positions.”
Only seconds later, fighters from the Carthia and Archadian 4th scream towards the Mesarthrim fleet. Simultaneously, the Prometheus empties her fighter bays, as does the rest of the 1st JDF, and sends them on support patterns for the beleaguered Prefecture fleet.

Flagship Trial By Fire – 0810
“What in the hell is going on?” Admiral Cranston asks Director Camp over the comm. channel. 
“Admiral Stadt is a traitor to the Mesarthrim race, and has defected to the Prefecture,” Camp replies. “Admiral Cranston, you now have command of the space forces.” 
Cranston is an excellent commander, but he is no Stadt. He knows that with the presence of a fresh Archadian battle fleet and the 1st JDF, the tide of the space battle is now turning now. With the 1st JDF reinforcing the Prefecture 5th and AAL fleet, and the Archadian 4th coming up his rear, his fleet is now boxed in with the Emerging Horizon to his left. The Ares and her escorts are cut-off from the rest of the fleet on his right. Thousands of fresh fighters enter the fray, with thousands streaming from the Carthia and Prometheus alone. The Archadian fighters, with their APACHE augmented pilots, scream through the Mesarthrim fighter forces, most of which having been in combat for over two hours. 
The performance of the UAR 4th Fleet’s fighters is only surpassed by the performance of the 1st JDF’s fighters, which not only have the advantage of hybrid Archadian-Mesarthrim technology, but also have some of the best of the best Archadian and Mesarthrim fighter pilots. These fresh fighters pour out of their bays and pounce on the UCS fleet. Reinvigorated, the Prefecture fighters storm out of their defensive formations and make runs on Mesarthrim warships.
However, the battle is far from over. The UCS fleet is still clustered together, and on its home turf. It cannot retreat, and so with their backs to the wall the Mesarthrim warships fight desperately to turn back the overwhelming tide of new combatants. Cranston splits the fleet, ordering the Chimera to lead half of the fleet back towards the Archadian 4th, and takes the Trial By Fire, flagship Ares, and the rest of the fleet towards the Prefecture 5th, hoping to get close enough to obscure the firing angles of the Prometheus and her escorts at his own ships.

Flagships Judicator – 0830
“Engines are back online sir, and running at 72%, but KF is still entirely offline, and the main cannon won’t fire again until we can get to a dry-dock. Admiral Descartes has sent coordinates for us to move to, which will allow us to concentrate fire on the Mesarthrim fleet, but keep most of their firing angles blocked by the deployment of the FJDF.” 
Nolan sees the wisdom of the maneuver. “Then make it so. Move us there, send word to the fighters of our new position. Keep fire concentrated on that Ares, maybe we can take her out.” 

Flagship Carthia – 0845
“Send the bombers after that flagship, divert fighters to match their fighter screens, and send the rest of the fighters after their sub-caps. Move the Delstichs up front, but give me a wall of frigates between their sub-caps and us. All ships in sector 3, come up under their belly and strike at those Deltas and Phalanx.” 
Admiral Taukin’s orders are relayed to the fleet, and soon the Archadian fleet branches out to meet the oncoming tide of UCS warships.
The fleets are now meeting and dishing out destruction at extremely close range. The fresh Archadian 4th is bashing the already damaged Mesarthrim fleet with warship salvos, as well as massive amounts of fighter ordinance. The UAR 4th Fleet is a fighter-driven force, and carries more fighters than any other Archadian fleet. The majority of the UCS fighters went with the other half of the fleet, and now the ships led by the Chimera are at an extreme disadvantage. 
Still, the battle is vicious, and several Archadian frigates are disabled or lost to the rush of enemy warships. However, the superior position of the Archadian fleet at the beginning of the engagement, and the support of thousands of fighters has spelled certain doom for the Chimera and its escorts. 
To confound matters for the UCS forces, the majority of the warships that came with the Chimera were Blazton Delta frigates. While excellent at long range due to their heavy missile load, at close range they fair poorly, and their size makes them vulnerable to heavy weaponry. All 5 of the fleet’s remaining Terra carriers are with the Chimera, but without their fighters for protection, all but two are soon struck down.

Flagship Trial By Fire – 0855
“Curse those damn First Defense ships! Curse Camp for opening up that can of worms. All ships, form up on…” 
A sudden blast rocks the Trial By Fire, cutting off Admiral Cranston mid-sentence. “Damage report!” 
“We just took a direct hit from the Prometheus’ photonic cannons. Shields are entirely down. The armor will hold for some times, but not forever…what do we do?” 
Cranston considers his options. If he keeps fighting, his ship will soon be destroyed. The Prefecture takes no prisoners. At this, Cranston makes his decision. “Signal the Prometheus. Announce the surrender of the Trial By Fire, and that we’ll turn the ship back over to the Prometheus Council.” 
“Surrender sir?” 
“That’s right helmsman. I don’t like it anymore than you, but dying in a crippled warship is neither heroic nor valiant. Signal Admiral Descartes. Damn…we almost had those Prefecture sons of bitches.”

United Centusi States Space-0855
On this side of the conflict, the Prefecture fleet is faring much better now that they have extensive fighter support and fire support from the 1st JDF. The massive Prometheus leads the charge, her massive relativistic cannons pounding into the lead UCS warships, and photonic cannons eating away at the shields of the larger vessels. 
Soon, the large vessels of the fleets pair off to take each other down. The remaining Avenger Mk IIs of the UCS fleet are now the targets of attack by the heavy cruisers of the Prefecture 4th and 1st JDF. It is a slugging contest between these massive heavy cruisers, each dishing out astounding amounts of firepower, and consuming any smaller ships that might stray between them. 
Then the Ares comes to its end as it evades the Prefecture frigate Ramsey, which had lost steering control and was headed on a crash course for the Ares. As the Ares levels off its climb, it comes directly in the path of a broadside from the Prometheus. Already heavily damaged, several salvos from photonic cannon turrets, torpedo tubes, and three pairs of Class 8 EMDs cause the shielding to buckle and large chunks of the Ares are sent flying into space. 
The Ares manages only a single salvo from each of its main guns, which slam into the Prometheus’ forward section. The force of the impacts is dispersed all over the massive ship. The Ares’ Captain gives the order to abandon ship as another blast tears through the front of the Ares, and life-pods spew forth from the ship as its reactor goes critical. Moments later, the entire ship is incinerated, consuming also a nearby Mesarthrim Sears class gunship and heavily damaging the Prefecture frigates Fife and Sage. 
As the mighty ship is destroyed, the UCS fleet falls into disarray. Not retreating, they continue to battle on, but with their flagships gone, there is a lack of organization and no clear battle plan. Camp tries to rally the forces from the Emerging Horizon, but the amount of comm. chatter now on the lines makes it difficult to communicate. Were the UCS forces to regroup now, they could put up a stiff defense, but the chaos that ensues is critical. Here and there, a pair of gunships make a run on a single target, but other than that, it has degenerated into paired-off battles between a single UCS ship and its enemy. 
The Mesarthrim fleet, built for quick raids and lightening strikes, is now in a drawn-out slugging contest, and its weakness quickly becomes apparent. The smaller, faster Mesarthrim ships lose the advantage when confronted with such a powerful, organized opponent. The same problem that happened in New Albany is now taking its toll on the UCS fleet, and this time there is nowhere to fall back. 

Prefecture Stuka fighter – 0910
“Copy flight! Baker, Charlie, and Juliet wings! The Judicator is down again, we’re to report back and fly cover.” 
Captain Jules Montague of the Prefecture Aerospace force has now been in the cockpit for more than three hours, and with 17 kills for the day has made himself an ace pilot in a single battle. He maneuvers his hulking fighter in behind a Mesarthrim Nimitz class gunship and fires off the last of his missiles, which strike the Nimitz, though the gunship’s shields hold up. He pulls off as retaliatory fire from the warship streaks past his cockpit.
“Baker, Charlie, take down that Trident and accompanying fighters off the port of Judicator, Juliet stay with me and we’ll sweep the upper decks of the Judicator.” 
As Captain Montague gives these orders, he notices on his sensors some slow-moving vessels heading towards the once-again crippled Judicator. He turns for a closer pass. “We’ve got boarders! Baker, Charlie, divert to incoming coordinates! Take those transports out! Juliet, take the Trident out.”
The Stukas swoop down on the slow Mesarthrim transports, but are met with UCS fighters, and a dogfight ensues. 
At this close range, UCS forces are now attempting to board damaged and crippled Prefecture ships, as their own ships have lost much of their offensive power. The fight between the warships is still going, but it’s now downhill for the leaderless UCS fleet. 
The blackness of space is thick with fighters, and thicker still with energy blasts. The Mesarthrim fleet is now hurting for lack of fighters, as many have been recalled to the planets surface to deal with the threat of the ground forces, but the remaining fighters are packed closer together. The fighting is at extremely close range, and ships are narrowly avoiding each other, let alone weapons blasts. 

Flagship Judicator – 0930
“Sir! Enemy forces have entered deck 7. Repeat…ahh!” 
A call from a crewman in the mess hall is cut short as UCS Marines storm into the room from assault barges and transports. It is reminiscent of Terran naval warfare from 600 years past, as ships at extremely close range punch holes in each other’s defenses, and send boarding parties to take the ships. The Mesarthrim are very proficient at this, and they are exploiting it now as a desperate last attempt to win the battle by capturing or disabling enemy vessels.
“All hands, prepare to repel boarders!” Admiral Nolan orders while putting on his own armored vest and grabbing a rifle. “Weapons stations, continue firing on hostile warships! Major Dunsten, get your Marines to guard those stations. If they go out, we all go down.” 
Unfortunately for Nolan, the Judicator has the smallest Marine detachment of any Prefecture flagship, and so it falls to the crew to be the primary defense of the warship against hundreds of Mesarthrim Marines. While less than half of the troops that initially set out from various UCS ships have made it to the Judicator, thanks to Captain Montague’s fighter wings, those that do make it are quickly making their way through the warship. 
“Admiral, this is Lieutenant Morgan on deck 2! We’ve got multiple hostiles, probably 40 or 50. We’ll hold them off as long as we can, but I’ve not got 20 men with me! When they get past us, they’ll have a straight run to the bridge. Fortify your pos...oh God!” 
Nolan winces as the Lieutenant’s exclamation ends in a loud explosion. “Lock down the bridge, get those blast walls up, and secure the blast doors at the end of the corridor. If anything comes through that door, I want it wasted!” 
The bridge is now a scene out of every Prefecture Captain’s worst nightmare. With a skeleton crew working communications, weapons, and defenses, the rest of the bridge crew is armed and ready to repel an assaulting enemy force. Blast walls and barricades line the twenty foot corridor that leads to the bridge, and enlisted men armed with rifles timidly await their foes. There is even a field gun setup behind a barricade, aimed straight down the corridor, just daring a Mesarthrim Marine to blast through the door.
“Grenade!” an enlisted man shouts. 
Admiral Nolan looks up from his command screen and spins around quickly as he hears the call. The bridge crew ducks behind their barricades as a burst of plasma fills the air, and then they jump back up to return fire down the corridor. A young ensign screams in terror as the searing plasma burns through his flesh, and then he falls dead on the deck. 
The man on the field gun manages several rounds down the corridor, taking out half a dozen Mesarthrim Marines, their armor not effective against an anti-vehicle grade weapon. Seconds later a well-placed shot strikes the gunner, and as a comrade rises to take his place, he too is shot. The firefight continues for what seems like an eternity, but in reality lasts only minutes. 
Nolan rushes to the corner of the corridor, grabs a grenade off a dead enlisted man’s belt, and hurls it down the corridor. He hears a cry in German, an explosion, and then the screaming of soldiers being charred by the searing heat of the grenade. He calls the remaining crew away from their stations, the Judicator now lifeless in space, and they fight desperately to hold the bridge against the large Marine force attacking. 
Admiral Nolan now has less than a dozen men holding off the Mesarthrim Marines, and he himself is firing along side his bridge-crew. “Ensign, pass that grenade.”
The young cadet beside him passes him a plasma charge from a dead Mesarthrim Marine and Admiral Nolan moves to throw it down the corridor. “Grenade!” Nolan yells to his men.
The explosion tears through the Marines inside the corridor and the heat vaporizes some before they can let loose their screams of agony. The navigation officer takes this opportunity and makes for the field gun, where he dispatches several more Mesarthrim with some explosive rounds. When the navigation officer is shot, Nolan raises to fire down the corridor to cover another crewman making his way for the field gun, and is struck in the chest. The round throws him to the ground. A nearby crewman crawls over to Nolan and drags him to the command chair.
“Sir!” Major Dunsten calls over the comm. channel. “Ship’s sensors indicate nearly five-hundred enemy Marines have made it on board, and we’ve been forced from most of our positions. We are holding down in engineering, but their armor’s tough and there’s a whole lot of them. We can’t hold out forever.”
“Major, get your men off the ship, the Judicator is lost. I’ve initiating self-destruct sequence: code green, irreversible in seven minutes. Tell fleet command I did all I could, and my bridge crew fought to the death to keep the ship out of enemy hands. I recommend all of them for posthumous medals of valor. Give my regards to Admiral Marshall. Tell him we fought all we could.”
“Aye Admiral, I will tell them. It has been the honor of my career fighting with you.”
Dunsten then turns to his men. “Nolan’s blowing the ship, get to the escape pods in the rear of engineering. I’ll sound the alarm and catch up with you.”
On the bridge, only Nolan and three others still hold the room. Nolan lies bleeding on the floor, sending final orders to fleet command and issuing an evacuation order. He looks to his left, where bridge-chief Donovan lies, still clutching his officer’s pistol, dead from multiple energy blasts. He sends a final message from the bridge, this one to Admiral Robert Marshall. “I’m so sorry father. I did my best.”
The last of his bridge-crew falls to a bolt of energy, and Nolan crawls across the floor to him. “Did we get them sir?” the young ensign asks.
“We got them ensign. We got them.”
Seconds later Mesarthrim Myt'herra surge down the corridor. Nolan raises his officer’s pistol and fires off only a single shot before he and the ensign are gunned down by round after round from enemy rifles. He lays on floor, barely conscious, staring at the ceiling, hearing nothing. Bodies lie around him, Terran, Mesarthrim…all dead. He blinks once, and a red light blinks twice on his command chair, where a Mesarthrim technician is hurriedly attempting to bypass the ship’s security net. In the blink of an eye, the safeties on the ship’s reactor fail and the fusion of the reactor mixed with the detonation of every missile round in the warship consumes the massive vessel. In moments, the blast reduces the Judicator to dust and heat.
Major Dunsten stares out the window of his escape pod as it is recovered by an Archadian pilot recovery craft. “Damn that was a fine ship. Damn he was a fine man.”
All throughout the fleet, similar occurances take place as several of the damaged Prefecture warships are boarded. As Mesarthrim Marines finally take the bridge of the Prefecture frigate Fife, which has been crippled since the Ares was destroyed, the Captain drops the shields and makes a last burst transmission to the Lady Dominion, a nearby 1st JDF Alexander class cruiser. The Lady Dominion carries out the Fife captain’s last request and blasts the Fife into pieces before the Mesarthrim can reactivate the frigate’s shields. 

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Bridge – 1000
Director Camp stares at the sensors screen in front of him. He can feel the end approaching. “Ensign Baker, what’s the status of the fleet?” Camp asks, knowing the answer. 
“We’ve lost the Ares and Chimera to hostile fire, and the Trial By Fire and Tunguska have surrendered. We’re at 60% of capital ships and 75% of sub-capital ships crippled or destroyed. The Prefecture fleet is at 45% crippled or destroyed, AAL forces are down to 65%, the Archadian 4th is at 85%, and the FJDF is still at 95%. We’ve yet to outright destroy an FJDF ship yet. Captain Babbage of the Draconia reports there are dozens of Marines on enemy vessels, and claims to have captured a pair of Prefecture frigates. Our fighter forces are down to 30%, and our shields here on the Emerging Horizon are down to 25%.” 
Director Camp feels his ambitions slipping from his grasp, but will not give up so easily. Suddenly jumping up, he gives some orders to the fleet, orders barely followed; the fighting is now too chaotic. Camp whirls towards the comm. station. “Mr. Baker, have my shuttle readied for me. If we cannot defeat them in space, we shall defeat them on the surface. Rally all ground forces to their strongholds, and send the bulk of the Northern Armies to reinforce Stone Bridge.” 
As he speaks, the bridge is shaken by a blast. “Mr. Baker, what’s going on?” Camp asks. 
“Sir, shields are down! We just took a broadside from the Prometheus to our power grid, and the computer is re-routing to primary systems. Oh no! Sensors show multiple craft inbound…I think they plan to board us.” 
“I’ll be no man’s prisoner! Sears may have been born to hang, but not I.” Camp shouts. “Get my shuttle ready Mr. Baker, and then prepare to defend yourself.”

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Secondary Hanger Bay - 1000
Twenty decks below, a blast rips open the hanger-bay’s space-door and a dozen transports land in the welcoming hole in the Emerging Horizon’s defenses, slipping through its thin atmospheric shield. These are transports from the 4th Fleet, carrying heavily armored Archadians in full battle gear. Only a handful of Mesarthrim techs are in the bay when the transports come in, and they are gunned down by the turrets on the transports before they can escape. 
As the first warriors leap off the ship, Major Faurcos routes instructions over the warriors’ HUDs. “Alright men, this is the place. I’ve routed the blueprints of the whole damn place into your computers, so you shouldn’t get lost no matter where you go. Take out any resistance you come across, and make your objectives. Oh yeah, and try to take care of the place…high command says they want the station back when we’re through with her. Move out!” 
As the warriors cross the deck, a single punch of Faurcos armored fist pummels through the blast door that bars their way out of the hanger. Taking the station will be no easy task, as the Mesarthrim have over 6,000 solders on station. Major Faurcos leads his men through the corridor, and they split up. 
As he rounds a corner, a blast hits the front of his suit, and by reflex he sends a blast back at the enemy, literally cutting him down with an Repeating Femto-second Pulse Laser blast. The RFPL blast slices the Mesarthrim soldier in half, and Faurcos continues down the cooridor.
All over the Emerging Horizon now, hundreds of Archadian boarding troops battle it out with the station’s defenders. The Mesarthrim assume they have the home-field advantage, but piped into each Archadian’s computer and HUD are the schematics of the station, an Archadian design, and this helps them in maneuvering about the stations and keeping from being flanked in the maze of corridors and hallways. Major Faurcos crew is on their way to the bridge to take control of the station, and they are meeting stiff resistance on their way.

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Private Hanger Bay – 1015 
Camp and his escorts have been pinned down on their way to the private hanger bay, and are unable to get to the Admiral’s Yacht that he had planned to take down to the planet. Realizing he can’t get out, Camp puts in a call to the bridge. “Mr. Baker, activate protocol AC-37-H. If we detonate this base, we take out over a dozen nearby enemy warships, and hundreds of troops on station. After initiation, you’ll have ten minutes to get off the station.” 
“Yes, Director Camp,” is Baker’s only response. 
Ensign Baker has is finally realizing the truth of this battle. He’s lost all delusions of the glory of battle or the power of the UCS. Camp’s inspiring words, words which rallied a nation, are now echoes in the back of his mind. He knows that destroying the station will only delay the inevitable, and soon the planet itself will fall, or be blasted to pieces from orbit. He inputs the command codes solemnly and prepares to leave.

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Observation Deck – 1015
Major Faurcos’ walks up to a Mesarthrim Marine laying on the ground, attempting to raise his pistol to fire a last defiant shot at Faurcos. Without emotion, Faurcos raises his rifle, sends a blast through the Marine’s helmet, and continues on. “Have to get the job done. For the Republic,” he thinks. 
He and his men now have only a stairwell between them and the bridge, and must hurry as the station’s intercom is now voicing warnings about self-destruction and a timer has started. He rushes up with another warrior to the top of the stairwell and punches a hole in the blast door, while the soldier tosses a grenade through the hole. 
The grenade explodes, and Faurcos kicks through the door and goes barging in. There are about 20 men on the bridge, and as they turn to fire, Faurcos leaps behind a wall and tosses a grenade into the room. It goes off and he comes out blasting, taking several shots to his power suit, but none to critical areas. The warrior next to Faurcos takes a shot to the throat, and Faurcos blasts the Mesarthrim who fired the shot. Another grenade later and the bridge is clear. Faurcos turns to treat several wounded Archadians next to him, and when a medic runs up to stabilize them, Faurcos leaves them alone.
Wading through the bodies on the bridge, Faurcos makes his way to what his HUD tells him is the central computer terminal. He sees the countdown protocol and attempts to shut it off, but the computer responds with Not Authorized: Improper Clearance. 
“Damn!” he thinks. He looks next to him on the ground and sees a young Mesarthrim cadet, perhaps 19, laying dead on the floor. Faurcos shoulders his rifle, picks the body up with one hand, and presses the lifeless fingers against the sensor-screen with his other hand. 
The computer thinks for perhaps a second, and then the screen lights up green. Baker, Thomas. Ensign. Clearance Granted. 
Faurcos lays the body back down and proceeds to disable the computer’s safeties. Protocol AC-37-H aborted. Resuming default settings. 
Faurcos then shuts down the automated weapons systems and communications relays going to the Mesarthrim fleet. Without vital sensor information from the station, the remaining UCS forces in space have lost one of their final advantages.
“Major Faurcos to Carthia. Admiral Taukin, I’ve taken the bridge of the Emerging Horizon, and the station is now offline. There is still stiff fighting onboard, but I’m preparing to pull back my men and lockdown the decks where the Mesarthrim are holding position. Perhaps if we lock them up they’ll be easier to manage.” 
“Excellent work Major. We have a second wave of shuttles en route to your position to reinforce your units. Keep the bridge at all costs, and see if you can’t find any VIPs.”

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Private Hanger Bay – 1025
“They’ve pulled back, lets make a break for it!” a Mesarthrim Marine shouts as the Archadian troops pull out of the deck. Camp and his escort get up slowly, then make their way towards the hanger bay itself. As the first trooper enters the hanger bay, the space-doors of the bay are blasted in. The door leading out of the bay instantly closes in response, as the thin atmospheric shield is not activated, and the lone Marine is sucked out of the bay as the air rushes into the vacuum of space. 
Moments later, a pair of Archadian dropships approach the bay and land in the hanger. It’s a tight fit for such a small hanger bay that already has one ship in it. Archadians in power armor jump from the transports and make their way over to the bay control station, activating the atmospheric shield. They head to the bay’s door and as it opens, the lead soldier is hit with a hail of gunfire from Camp’s bodyguards. As he falls, the transport’s door is filled with the silhouette of a UAR Sabic class battle armor, and its rifle blasts through the lightly armored Marines as Camp turns to flee. An Archadian soldier steps around the Sabic and fires a shot, which goes clean through Camp’s right torso. 
Now laying on the ground, Camp pulls out a pistol to finish the job the Archadian started, but as he sits up and his face comes into view of the Archadian’s HUD, the soldier’s sensors flash red. “Priority Target. Capture at all costs.” 
A single shot blows apart the pistol in Camp’s hand, sending searing shrapnel into his arm, but otherwise leaving him very much alive.
As the Archadian approaches, Camp struggles to fight him, but the Archadian’s large armored frame does not even flinch from Camp’s attack, and he picks Camp into the air and tosses him to a pair of soldiers behind him. Camp is hit with an electric charge from a trooper’s rifle and passes into darkness. He is carried back to the dropship where he is stabilized and confined. 
“Major Faurcos,” an Archadian Captain starts on the comm. channel. “We’ve got a VIP down here trying to get to the Admiral’s shuttle. We’re getting an ID now. Sir! It’s Director Camp himself.” 
“Good Captain. Get him back to the Carthia quick, and don’t let the Prefecture know he’s been captured. They’ll be crying for his neck if they know we’ve got him, and I don’t want to go diving into that right this second.”

Space Station Emerging Horizon – Bridge – 1030
Having secured the bridge, Major Faurcos puts out a transmission on the UCS naval comm. channels. “United Centusi Fleet. This is Major Faurcos of the Archadian Navy. We have seized control of the Emerging Horizon. Cease engagement at once. The UAR will give quarter to surrendering forces provided you cease hostilities with Archadian, Prefecture, League, and First Defense forces. I repeat, full quarter will be given.” 
Faurcos shuts off the comm. “I hope that works, or this battle could drag on for several more hours.”

Avenger Cruiser Gorgon – 1035
“Ensign! What’s this mess about surrendering forces?” Captain Pritchard barks to his comm. officer. 
“Communications detected a universal transmission on UCS channels moments ago. We just decrypted it. An Archadian Major Faurcos made the broadcast. The Archadians are offering quarter to any Mesarthrim ships that cease hostilities. We just got a transmission from League Commander Balic. He wants to know if we authorized this.” 
“I don’t know what the hell is going on? This isn’t their war, they can’t go accepting prisoners!” Pritchard exclaims. “Get me Taukin on the horn.”
Admiral Taukin’s picture soon fills the view screen. “Ah, Captain Pritchard. We have already begun sending fighters in support of your forces on the ground have secured most of the Emerging Horizon.” 
“Forget about that Taukin. What’s this about the Republic accepting prisoners? We’re still in space combat and you are putting out an order saying you’ll give quarter to surrendering warships?” 
Taukin responds. “As per the Rhova’Deviri rules of engagement and warfare, full quarter is to be given to surrendering soldiers, despite the circumstances, especially such warriors, however misguided, as the Mesarthrim. Once any vessel surrenders to us, they will be under the protection of the Republic.” 
“Damn!” Pritchard yells. “Here I am about to crush the Mesarthrim fleet, and Taukin goes off accepting surrender. Bloody hell! Ensign, focus all fire on single ships now. They’ve got their chance to surrender. If they want to keep fighting, we’ll show them the way home.”
After more than four hours of combat, the fighting is dying down as the last of the Mesarthrim vessels limp away or are destroyed. Captain Pritchard, now commander of Prefecture Naval forces in the system, is beginning to organize ground-support patterns for warships and fighters. In the next quarter hour, the remainder of the UCS fleet surrenders, lowers their shields, and allows Archadian boarding crews to take control of the ships. Most of the remaining UCS fighters of the flee make haste back towards Mesar to fight off ground forces.
With the fleet surrendered, and more and more Archadian forces storming the station every minute, many of the defenders of the Emerging Horizon surrender, but most fight to the death, being either struck down by weapons fire, or suffocating after warnings to surrender are followed up by sealing off and ceasing life-support functions in their area. 
By noon, space superiority is firmly in the hands of the allied fleet. With space clear, the Prefecture fleet calls in a pair of Mercy medical cruisers to help in the treatment of soldiers, and the Archadian fleet calls in an Aborin mobile medical station. Ships damaged in the battle are repaired as much as can be by their crews, and many smaller ships begin taking turns in the shipyards of the Emerging Horizon, the large mobile shipyard on the Prometheus, and the few capture fleet facilities that orbit Mesar. The surrendered UCS vessels, damaged and some crippled, make up only about 15% of the original fleet. Their crews are rounded up and placed in brigs on various ships in the Archadian fleet, the FJDF, and the Emerging Horizon, while the ships themselves are docked with the Emerging Horizon. 

While space is secure, the planet Mesar is another matter entirely. Warships can only maintain high-orbit due to planetary defenses, and the forces on the ground are putting up quite a fight. With all hostiles in space taken care of, the combined fleet prepares its aerospace and ground forces to support those forces already on the ground, but it will be no walk in the park. Mesar is inhabited by 1.5 billion Mesarthrim, every one of them a warrior at heart ready to die for the cause. The Battle for Mesar is only just beginning.

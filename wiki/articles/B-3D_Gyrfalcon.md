==Development==
While the B-2 Ocelot was designed for exclusive space operations, the XB-3 went back to the F-4 airframe, and redesigned it for 95% atmospheric operations. It was meant to launch from a carrier, enter an atmosphere, deliver a bomb payload on the surface of a world, escape the planet, then land. Ideally, it would land on a captured planet-side base instead.

Like the B-2, the XB-3 had its cannons removed. The XB-3 met all of its design goals, and when the YB-3 went into combat training and testing, it performed very well, able to dodge fighters and SAMs, and deliver its twelve 100kg bombs with pinpoint accuracy. The B-3 Gyrfalcon was ordered into production and produced for both the Royal Imperial Navy and the Royal Imperial Air Force.

Only 150 B-3s were delivered due to expectations of the XB-4 project. When that project was scrubbed, the design advancements of the new Magneto-PlasmaDynamic drive were incorporated into the B-3A. The B-3A was the Empire's mainstay attack bomber throughout the Second Exodus War.

The Prefecture made some advancements on its B-3Bs during the Battle of Terra, equipping them with shield technology. These new B-3Cs were a short-lived variant which were turned back over to Alexi-Wrought Airworks at the end of the war for further development.

Alexi-Wrought made some modifications, adding the new gravity cavitation drive to the B-3D variant, which is used by the Prefecture Air Force today.

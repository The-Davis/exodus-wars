The '''Coalition Security Force''' is a the small organization responsible for protecting the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition]]'s interests. While the Coalition Security Force is organized along military lines, it is not capable of any sort of serious military action, nor is it meant to be. It is primarily tasked with keeping the [[Oronus System]] safe from pirates and smugglers.

==Structure==

The Coalition Security Force is divided into two branches. The larger of the two Coalition Security Force branches is known as '''COSMOS'''. It functions as the navy and air force of the Coalition Security Force and operates in space, amongst the asteroid fields, and in the limited atmosphere on the moon [[Onus]].

The smaller branch of the Coalition Security Force is known as '''ISIS'''. ISIS operates aboard COSMOS and other Oronus Coaltion [[:Category:Starships|starships]], in orbital and planetary installations, and on the [[:Category:Space Stations|habitation stations]] throughout the system. Their primary role is the policing of spaceports for smugglers and hijackers, aiding police against armed criminals, and defending [[Oronus System|Oronus]] [[:Category:Starships|starships]] from pirates.

==Vehicles==

While most [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] installations are too small to have many open spaces, there are a few large enough to warrant vehicle patrol. For that, ISIS has the ITU-8 Individual Patrol Craft, the Dasher light hovercraft, and the rare Stinger heavy hovertank. The Stinger is mostly used outside of [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] territory, and is usually stationed at their embassies on other worlds as a show of what they are capable of.

ISIS most often uses its security agents and hardsuits for security operations. Hardsuits are not quite battle armor, but are hardened and powered vacuum suits designed for space zero-G maneuvering.

==Aircraft==

COSMOS fighters are designed around three different roles. The Scorpion for attack, the Manta for range and patrol, and the Rook for defense.

==Warships==

COSMOS does not have resources capable of rivaling even single-planet militaries like [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasa]] or [[Reigess Suverände|Junheim]], but it does have up to [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]]-class [[:Category:Warships|warships]] at its disposal. The [[S-30 Class Gunboat]] is the most common ship in use, followed by the [[S-50 Class Gunship]], but the [[S-90 Class Frigate]] is available for the most important posts and for important missions.

===Overview===

Cargo haulers are a very popular type of transport. While they are used for interstellar trade by way of the Council of Independent Nations hyperspace tunnels or by way of the Icronian wormgates, they are more frequently used for interplanetary shipping. The Northern Star corporation on Bethija first began building their V series cargo haulers for moving goods from various shipping points to different space stations and cargo transfer docks.

[[Image:660VBulkTransport1.jpg]]

Northern Star's current design, the 660/V, is one of the most popular interplanetary shipping systems around. There are literally hundreds of thousands of private haulers in operation, and millions more in corporate shipping fleets. The 660/V is is a powerful hauler and a huge time and money saver. Most freighters have huge cargo bays, requiring each load of cargo to be carefully loaded and unloaded on every trip. Instead of a cargo bay, cargo haulers like the 660/V pull a series of magnetically-linked containers.

The Magnetic Hauler Couplings of the transport are monitored from the rear crew station. Operation is simple--most of the time the crew connects to a pre-fabricated container train and hauls it away. Sometimes containers are uncoupled. In this situation, the hauler operator can create the container trains via a universal control protocol installed in all compatible containers.

The biggest military user of cargo haulers is the Prefecture Galactic Navy, whose warships use cargo haulers to bring in supplies. Convoys of cargo haulers are less time-consuming than a long visit to a supply dock, and also keep the warship in position for rapid response deployment.

In the private sector, the 660/V competes with other cargo hauler models. While it is the most popular, models built by other companies are nearly as good and are frequently seen competing for space in the shipping lanes. The cargo hauler is an ideal choice for fast and efficient, though it is more prone to accident than deep space freighters.

===Ammo Trailer===

[[Image:660VBulkTransport2.jpg]]

The Bulk Ammunition module is a series of sealed ammo drums on a Bulk Transport container.  The drums can be detached and propelled away from the rest of the train in a moment's notice.  Ammo containers are the lifeblood of a Prefecture military campaign, and are always under heavy escort.

===Food Trailer===

[[Image:660VBulkTransport3.jpg]]

The Bulk Food Module is a massive refrigeration unit.  Through dehydration and freeze drying, the module can carry enough food to feed a battalion for a year.

===Liquid Trailer===

[[Image:660VBulkTransport4.jpg]]

The Bulk Liquid Module is a quartet of pressurized liquid containers on a Bulk Transport module.  Each can carry liquids at any temperature from liquid helium up to molten metal.

Each container can be completely cleaned and sanitized in under an hour.  Sanitation is so thorough that a tank can be used to carry insecticides on one trip, and drinking water on the next.

===Vehicle Transport Trailer===

[[Image:660VBulkTransport5.jpg]]

The Bulk Vehicle Module is rarely used by the military.  Commercial vehicle manufacturers use Bulk Transports to get their models to retail markets.

The Bulk Transport Caboose is the tail end of a Bulk Transport.  Its job is to complete the cargo train, and to monitor the train's integrity.  If the modules are not in a straight line from the hauler itself, the caboose has thrusters for making course corrections.

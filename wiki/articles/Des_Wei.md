'''Des Wei''' is the XXth planet in the [[Des System]].

==History==

Wei was founded in 2320 as an Imperial Aerospace academy. The deep canyons of the planet make excellent obstacle courses, the frequently bad weather provides testing for all-weather pilots, and the asteroid belts provide the toughest of space obstacles.

In 2322, the Prefecture took over the academy, and gave it several upgrades.

Moon City was built in late 2322 to help house the families of academy pilots. It's a domed city to keep out the inclement weather, even though the atmosphere is breathable--if a little smelly due to the sulfur content. Wei's only moon has a geo-synchronous orbit, and Moon City is built directly beneath it.

Unlike the academy at Holt, the Wei Aerospace Academy did not warrant a Wormhole Gate to Ueda. Aerospace pilot training takes longer than infantry training, so the only Gate in and out of the star system goes to Ward.

Because of its terrible weather, Wei has been unable to attract industry. The lack of natural irrigation makes farming impossible, and the need for expensive anti-weather domes makes factories uneconomical. Because of this, Wei must import all of its essentials.

Other than the pilots of the Academy, their immediate families, and Academy staff, Wei has no population.The pay rates for trained military and civilian pilots is excellent, so Wei has a steady flow of students.

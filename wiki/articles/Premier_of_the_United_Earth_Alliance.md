The office of '''Premier''' was equal in power to the [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]], but was shared among different worlds. Each world within the [[United Earth Alliance]] had a Premier, and together all of them had the same powers as the [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]]. The Premier of [[Earth]] acted as the figurehead for the office, and for all intents and purposes was the Premier of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

== Elections ==

The office of the Premier was first shared between the former President of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] and the Premier of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]], but was quickly expanded to include the heads of state of each joining country. The office was soon altered to be one Premier per world, as the [[United Earth Alliance]] rapidly expanded into space.

The Premier held office for a term of five years, and a limit of a single term. Electronic elections were held on June 30th, and the winner was sworn into office the following day. Premier's term in office was staggered with the [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]]'s. The Premier of the [[United Earth Alliance]] was elected by a simple majority. Anyone could run, and anyone could vote. Election campaigns often had hundreds of candidates, though only a few were considered "serious contenders" at any given time.

== The Provisional Government ==

During the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[United Earth Alliance]] continued to exist as a provisional government on [[Theia]] in the [[Titan System]]. During that time, the Premier of [[Theia]] was the only head of state of the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]]. The office of [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] was never used again after the death of [[Fernando Meliazio]].

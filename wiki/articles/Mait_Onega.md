''Mait Onega'' (born July 9, [[2241]]) is a reclusive and secretive master of spies. His personality has been forged by years of living in terror of discovery and borderline starvation.

== Life of a Spymaster ==

Mait Onega has been living in the shadows since the earliest days of the [[Burning Wars]]. Born into the strife of an heir family on the run, Mait found himself orphaned and abandoned at the age of 12. With a legitimate claim to the throne, Mait was a target of all four armies of the warring heirs.

With the resources of [[Lorithan]] rapidly being carved up piecemeal by the four armies, the only way to survive was to live on the run, or live with the armies. Mait chose to live in the shadows of the army camps, surviving on the scraps of the soldiers.

Eventually, Mait was caught by the Huern army. When his identity was discovered, the paranoid [[Kian Huern]] planned to have him killed. Desperate to save his skin, Mait began telling the Huern heir everything he knew about the other three armies. After living in their shadows for years, Mait Onega knew many of their secrets.

Kian Huern released him, and Mait immediately went to the next heir army with his secrets, this time for medicine and food instead of for his life. Mait had no loyalty to anyone but himself.

Mait Onega became a legend among the [[Lorithos]], but a dark and hated one. He served no one but himself, and survived. In the aftermath of the war, Mait Onega gathered like-minded associates together, and began to put his dark collection of secrets to good use.

Today, Onega's intelligence network is unsurpassed. His agents have a presence in most societies in the [[Orion Arm]]. People from Lorithan tend to be sickly, weak, and easily go unnoticed by society's elite. Even the xenophobic [[Meroniri]] and [[Mesarthrim]] have Lorithos agents within their midst without knowing it, thanks to Onega's training and scheming.

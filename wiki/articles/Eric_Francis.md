'''Eric Francis von Tischler'''
*Born: July 2nd, [[2272]]
*Death: November 30th, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Mesar]]
*Place of Death: [[Koeing Estate]], [[Stone Bridge]], [[Mesar]]
*Allegiance: [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]

''Eric Francis'' was the director of Naval Intelligence for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and was given the [[Clan Lord]] title to the [[Tischler Clan]] by [[Bethany Tischler]] out of respect for him in all the work he did to solve the murder of her brother, [[Andreas Tischler]].  With his promotion, Eric Francis (now Eric Francis von Tischler) became the Uber-General of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

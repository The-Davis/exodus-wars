At long last, after nearly six years of bloodshed, the Tempest War ends. Enraged at the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]'s refusal to assist any Member States, both the Prefecture and the Ikronin withdraw their membership from the Alliance of Planets, effectively dissolving it.

[[Rahn Industries]]' debut into the warship industry was the massively successful ''Avenger'', which proved to be an excellent battlecruiser design. With the [[Prefecture]] in full-fledged rebellion and open war with the [[Onyx Empire]], Rahn Industries took an enormous financial risk in developing the Avenger, and [[Justin Rahn]] took enormous personal risk when he committed acts of treason against the [[Lucius Black|Emperor]]. The gambit proved to be a financial boon to the company, and a military advantage to the Prefecture; enough ships were completed to give the Prefecture's naval forces an edge they desperately needed.

After the civil war, the [[United Centusi States]] began purchasing Avenger hulls, to be outfitted by [[Mesarbauer]]. Rahn Industries reclassified these hulls as the Rächer (which actually means avenger in Mesarthrim). The hulls were refitted with easier plug-and-play interfaces to allow the ships to be converted much easier by the Mesarthrim.

William Camp ordered 50 Rächers, and paid for them in full. Justin Rahn, requested permission from the Prefecuture to put a hold on their construction projects to help expedite the Mesarthim order. The Prefecture agreed, and 28 vessels were delivered before the UCS attacked the Prefecture in 2327.

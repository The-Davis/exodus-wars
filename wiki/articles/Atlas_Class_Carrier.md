The '''Atlas Class Carrier''' was the mainstay [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Royal Imperial Navy]] throughout the [[Second Exodus War]].

During the [[Imperial Civil War]], many of the Atlas class vessels defected to the [[Prefecture]], and were re-commissioned by the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] in the years following the [[Onyx Empire]]'s defeat.

Most Atlas class vessels were kept in service, though they have been surpassed by the new [[Alaska Class Carrier]] in most performance areas.

=== Development ===

When [[Lucius Black]] instituted the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] and ordered them to develop a massive military for his [[Onyx Empire|Empire]], the [[Royal Imperial Labs|Labs]] director, [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow|Josiah Hlow]] tasked [[Reg Brenstein]] with developing the infrastructure necessary to design and build the [[:Category:Warships|warships]] needed by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

[[Reg Brenstein|Brenstein]] decided to found the [[Haven Shipyards]] corporation on the surface of [[Ah Kin]], in the capital city of [[Rio de Andes]]. This corporation was an ideal front for the operation, where weapon systems and other critical components could be developed without causing too much suspicion.

After the successful construction of the [[Capricorn Haven|''Capricorn Haven'']], [[Martim Haven|''Martim Haven'']], and [[Noronha Haven|''Noronha Haven'']], work began on the [[:Category:Warships|warships]] themselves. The first produced was the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']], a [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]] designated FG-1, but at the urging of [[Emil Torsc]], the plans for the [[HMS Atlas|CV-1]] were drawn up soon afterwards.

[[Anthony Hadrian]], [[Robert Marshall]], and [[Emil Torsc]] were all closely involved in the design of the [[HMS Atlas|CV-1]]. Each had their own concerns and suggestions which the design team incorporated. [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] was largely concerned with its contribution to overall strategy. [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] requested enhanced combat abilities and multiple tactical options. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] offered valuable input on refueling and rearming [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] under combat conditions, and assisted in testing the first automated "power assisted rack" launch system and "snare-arrest" landing system.

=== Construction ===

Before the start of hostilities in the [[Second Exodus War]], twenty [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]] were built by [[Haven Shipyards]] under the jurisdiction of [[Royal Imperial Labs]]. Another thirty-four were built before the class was officially terminated, though none of the surviving [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]] have been retired so far.

=== Air Traffic Control ===

The "Combat Conditions Automated Tactical Air Traffic Command and Control System", often garbled into "CATCAT", and more frequently affectionately known as the "Kitten" system, was conceived by a team of engineers working off of the proposals from [[Emil Torsc]].

It is capable of directing any number of friendly [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] into a proper holding pattern, or to any number of landing bays for refuelling or rearming. Later versions included high-bandwidth direct line-of-sight transmissions, which could give complex instructions accurate down to a milimeter.

=== Automated Tactical Supply System ===

The "Hands Free Aircraft Resupply and Maintenance Assesment System" <small>(HFARMAS)</small> is capable of mounting missile ordinance, torpedos, and cannon ammunition onto returning combat [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] without the need for crewmen to get directly involved. The landing flight-deck was depressurized during the recovery of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]], requiring crews to wear unweildy vacuum suits to work on them. To avoid this, the HFARMAS would handle all re-arming, and would determine if the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] was still within acceptable combat parameters.

Maintenance crews worked hands-on only if the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] required maintenance, and in those cases the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] was removed from the flight deck and placed into a pressurized maintenance bay.

=== Rack Launch System ===

All [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]] designed to meet [[Robert Marshall]]'s tactical requirements were built with the "Power Assisted Rack-Mounted Aircraft Combat Launch System", which was usually known to ship crews by its acronym, "PARMAC." To flight crews and pilots, it was known as the "rookie rattler", the "hell's express", the "shock shack", and even "the carrier's having kittens" (though this last expression was only used by pilots on the [[HMS Cougar|HMS ''Cougar'']]).

The main flight decks, mounted on either side of the [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]]'s main hull, had massive doors on either end which could be sealed to pressurize the flight deck. On the outer edge of the decks, there were a series of "racks" mounted in the floor, with ramps leading into them. [[:Category:Fighters|Fighters]] could be fitted into these racks, then launched via a combination of rocket-assistance, explosive decompression, and a mechanical apparatus known as the "tosser", giving the [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] the ability to launch its entire [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] complement at once.

Unfortunately, only [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] could be launched with this system. For [[:Category:Bombers|bombers]] or cargo [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] to be launched, the entire flight deck had to be depressurized and opened.

=== "Snare-Arrest" Landing System ===

[[Image:Atlas Class Carrier Side View.jpg|right|400px]]

In non-combat conditions, returning [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] could simply reduce speed, cut all power and coast at a gentle speed into waiting "arrestor web" which would halt all forward momentum.

When [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] were tested with this system under simulated combat conditions, they tore straight through the "arrestor web" and continued out the other end of the flight deck at their original speed.

The "Snare-Arrest Landing System" (SALIS) was developed to solve this problem. The system could be activated by air traffic control or flight deck crews, and it would detect all incoming [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] between specific velocities. If an [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] approached at too high a speed, it was waved off or was assumed hostile. If an [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] approached at too low a speed, the "arrestor web" was deployed instead.

If "Sally" (as flight crews dubbed the system) saw an incoming [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] requiring its assistance, it deployed a series of high-speed "snares", which launched and wrapped around the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] at ideal strength points. The system would then extend its snares, slowing down the rate of extension until it completely stopped, then wound the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] back into the flight deck. The longest recorded extension of an [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] being safely caught and towed back to the flight deck by a "Sally Snare" was 3.5 kilometers, though it was later discovered that the [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] had approached at four times the maximum safe velocity and the system had not correctly waved off the pilot.

Using the "Sally", [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] returning at high speeds in emergencies could be safely recovered, even if they were being actively pursued by enemy [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]].

=== Defensive Systems ===

[[Image:Atlas Class Carrier Top View.jpg|right|400px]]

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s combat doctrine, designed by [[Robert Marshall]], did not require carriers to have any sort of offensive weaponry. They relied on the weapons of their escort ships and their fighter complements to deliver ordinance to their objectives.

For some time during development, even a defensive armament was in question. The scientists at the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] were so confident in their shield technologies that they declared that the HMS ''Atlas'' would be "unbreakable," and that a defensive weapons grid was "wasted space." The [[Onyx Empire|Empire]]'s military leaders wisely disregarded this advice.

In order to provide an active, aggressive defense against incoming missiles and enemy fights, the [[Close-In Weapon System]] was installed at several key points along the hull, providing a wide firing arc and making any approach covered by at least two weapons.

Development of the shield systems greatly benefitted from the test runs of the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']]. The PDS-1 Plasma Field was tested against captured [[United Earth Alliance]] weapons systems, and new safeguards were developed for the PDS-2 design used on the Atlas-class.

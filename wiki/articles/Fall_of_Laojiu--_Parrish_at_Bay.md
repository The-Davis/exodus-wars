=====The Laojiu District=====
     For five days, the Mesarthrim attack Tara'dar Coirp, but are repelled every time by Parrish's counter-strategies. The city is too important to bomb, too thickly-packed to invade with tanks, and too full of hostile Prefecture troops to send in Myt'kampfen on foot. It appears that Parrish has halted the blitzkrieg of Labrea.

     The Myt'gaul enter the city in the dead of night, determined to avenge the death of Lothar Metzger. In a bold raid on Parrish's command station, twelve Myt'gaul kill sixty Prefecture soldiers and capture William Parrish, dragging him away to be shipped off to a prison camp back on Mesar.

     With Parrish out of the way, his more conservative second in command slowly filters the surviving 50,000 Labrea Guard into the tunnels under the Fathach Méire, where they disperse across the countryside disguised as civilians. Their weapons remain hidden in caches. The air bases are sealed away where they cannot be found, ready for the liberation of Labrea.

     The streets of Laidebaile are draped in black as the Mesarthrim tanks roll into town, taking the last free city on Labrea.

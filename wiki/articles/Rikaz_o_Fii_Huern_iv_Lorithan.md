'''Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan''', literally translated as "The Kingdom of the Huern Family over all of Lorithan", is the interstellar government of the [[Lorithos]]. It once covered only [[Lorithan]], but after [[First Contact]] it expanded its holdings into [[Ipsoff]] and [[Jagarr]].

During the [[Volucris War]], the world of Lorithan was completely destroyed, and the last of the [[Huern Drastar]] died childless.  A new government was formed in [[2340]] under the banner of the [[Cai Drastar]].

=== History ===

Main Article: [[History of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|History]]

The tale of the [[Lorithos]] is one of great tragedy and loss. The [[Burning Wars]] destroyed nearly everything they held dear, and if not for the direct intervention of the [[Terrans]], [[Ikronin]] and [[Meroniri]], the [[Lorithos]] would be extinct.

Life and opportunity on [[Lorithan]] itself is a bleak prospect. Aside from working for the government, there are no jobs. The soil is poisonous, the air is poisonous, the water is poisonous. Stepping into the wastelands unprotected is an invitation to be sliced apart by whirlwinds of debris.

The future is equally dark. Most [[Lorithos]] suffer from multiple genetic defects, which have stymied their best medical minds so far. Outside help is ever-present, but despite their best efforts, the current birth rate cannot sustain the population.

=== Government ===

Main Article: [[Government of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Government]]

While Rikaz Lorithos is supposed to be a democratic monarchy, simple practicality has turned it into a plutocracy. Resources are few and far between on the homeworld, and only the leaders and those who can afford to move to the new colonies hold any influence. Nearly all power lies with the [[Prekohr]], the hereditary executive head of government, and the [[Hishai]], his military second-in-command.

=== Law ===

Main Article: [[Law of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Law]]

The [[Lorithos]] have a grand tradition of law and order, dating back to the days of the [[Dawn Emperor]] thousands of years ago. The first laws, eight simple commands, came from the first Emperor of the [[Lorithos]]. In the days of the [[Twilight Empress]], there were thousands of laws and traditions, most of which perished in the [[Burning Wars]]. Today, the eight commandments of the [[Dawn Emperor]] are still considered the foundation law, and a few dozen other laws are in effect.

=== Law Enforcement ===

Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Law Enforcement]]

The Trushai Khor, founded by the Dawn Emperor, is still the sole law enforcement organization for the [[Lorithos]], but it only operates within the [[Kihtor]] and on the colonies. Enforcing laws in the wastelands is not a practical use of resources, so most outlying surviving settlements are left to fend for themselves.

The Abarehn are the equivalent of judges in [[Lorithos]] society. If the Trushai Khor prosecutes a criminal, an Abarehn is called to pass judgement. A criminal defendant is presumed "in flux," neither guilty nor innocent. The defendant's guilt or innocence *must* be conclusively proven, or the trial continues and is sent to higher courts, until the [[Prekohr]] passes final judgement.

=== Foreign Relations ===

Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Foreign Relations]]

Despite their suffering and overall poverty, the [[Lorithos]] hold a key that keeps them on the surface of galactic politics: the Urokon Network. The vast network of subspace buoys and satellites that runs this instantaneous communications network is their invention, and their only hope to remain afloat in galactic economies. For now, despite their devastated homeworld, they remain a constant and relevant member of galactic society.

Rikaz Lorithos is a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], but its relations with the [[Meroniri]] have soured since the formation of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Military]]

Rikaz Lorithos' armed forces are divided into two branches; the [[Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr]], and the [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr]].

The [[Lorithos]] military is very modern in terms of weaponry, power generation, and automation, but lags behind in terms of tactics and training. They operate under a "machines are cheap, humans are priceless" policy. The [[Lorithos]] budget is very low, but most manufacturing of their robotic infantry is inexpensive.

=== Intelligence ===

Main Article: [[Intelligence of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Intelligence]]

Rikaz Lorithos has the most advanced spy network in the galaxy. The [[Iterin Corahl Deseraht]] is an order of spies, couriers and assassins founded by [[Mait Onega]] is the days of the [[Burning Wars]]. Any individual [[Iterin Corahl Deseraht|Iterin Corahl]] is a trained infiltrator and covert killer.

=== Transportation ===

Main Article: [[Transportation of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Transportation]]

On [[Lorithan]], most cities are in ruins, and the surviving villages and refugee camps are cut off from the few recovering cities. The recovering cities are linked by sealed tube trains, which run on electromagnetic tracks. It is the only safe method of surface transportation on [[Lorithan]], and the most popular form of transportation on the colonies.

For travelling between worlds, huge spaceliners are the most popular form of non-military transport. Smaller transports and single-person craft are used for transportation around and from the [[Kihtor]].

=== Territory ===

Main Article: [[Territory of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Territory]]

[[Lorithan]] is the dying homeworld of the [[Lorithos]]. Since the development of faster than light travel, the Lorithos have founded two colonies: [[Ipsoff]] and [[Jagarr]]. The colonies provide the hope of a better future: small and limited in resources they may be, but they are unspoiled worlds with the promise of healthy lives.

=== Economy ===

Main Article: [[Economy of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Economy]]

In the face of planetwide decay, [[Lorithos]] society hangs on by a thread, through sheer brilliance and tenacity. The [[Lorithos]] have developed new clean power sources centuries ahead of their contemporaries in terms of efficiency and safety. They can build with extremely limited resources, scrounging up working components and alloys out of materials thought unfit for use by others.

The [[Lorithos]] have built the four [[Kihtor]] hightowers, possibly the greatest engineering feats in recorded history: even the [[Meroniri]] equivalents pale in comparison to the majesty of a [[Kihtor]].

In addition, [[Lorithos]] robotics are without equal. Strictly purpose-built machines are able to automate workloads in the harshest environments, and able to ply the spacelanes seeking out new worlds to move the wretched masses back home into a better life.

The [[Ikronin]] [[Likvada]] is the de facto standard of interstellar commerce, and has been accepted by the [[Lorithos]] as a regular currency. The old [[Shero]] of the [[Huern Drastar]] is still recognized, but is not currently minted.

=== Demographics ===

Main Article: [[Demographics of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Demographics]]

The [[Lorithos]] have an enormous genetic diversity due to the high number of mutations resulting from the [[Burning Wars]]. [[Lorithan]]'s population runs the gamut from almost healthy, nearly normal humans, to pitiful mutants barely clinging to life. The colonies are much healthier, as any Lorithos hoping to move to a colony must undergo genetic screening before their exit visa is approved.

=== Public Health ===

Main Article: [[Public Health of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Public Health]]

Health care and general public health is atrocious on [[Lorithan]]'s surface, but quite good in the [[Kihtor]] and the colonies. Genetic anomalies make using [[Prefecture]] organ regrowth technology difficult to use.

=== Culture ===

Main Article: [[Culture of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Culture]]

Culture in Rikaz Lorithos was once highly regulated and grand, with an emperor or empress of the [[Huern Drastar]] ruling as an almost god-like figure. Since the [[Burning Wars]], the [[Lorithos]] tried to adopt a more democratic-like, individualistic culture like their [[Terran]] and [[Meroniri]] benefactors. This attempt has failed, and their culture is gradually shifting back into a similitude of the days of the Dynasty.

'''Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe''' - Based in Cabletown Mesar. Designs [[Pendelbus]] for Civillian [[Zewischenablage]] Computers and Military [[Nachtpferd]] Hardware.  They are the number one software developer on Mesar. Following the adoption of the [[Cinead Accords]], MSG began work on Mesarthrim built [[Self-Aware Entities]] for their warships.

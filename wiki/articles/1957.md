== Events of 1957 ==
===January===
*January 3 - [[The Technology Race]]: Hamilton Watch Company introduces the first electric watch.
*January 6 - [[The Technology Race]]: IBM makes the FORTRAN scientific programming language available to customers. It becomes the most widely used computer language for technical work.
*January 20 - Strom Thurmond inaugurated for his first term as President of the United States.
*January 22 - [[The Jihad]]: Israel establishes permanent settlements in the Sinai Peninsula.
*January 23 - Ku Klux Klan members force truck driver Willie Edwards to jump off a bridge into the Alabama River. He survives and is able to later identify two of his attackers, who name their conspirators. The Local Klan chapter is convicted and imprisoned for twenty years as a result.

===February===
*February 4 - France prohibits United Nations involvement in Algeria.
*February 15 - Andrei Gromyko becomes foreign minister of Soviet Union.

===March===
*March 6 - British Empire colonies of the Gold Coast and British Togoland are embroiled in a brief Muslim rebellion. British troops execute 450 Muslim leaders, pacifying the region.
*March 7 - Congress approves the Thurmond Doctrine, which limits US military involvement in foreign wars to acts of aggression against traditional territories, and officially bans supporting "empire-building".
*March 8 - British Empire re-opens the Suez Canal to all nations except Egypt. Permanent British bases and overwhelming troop strength kept in the region.
*March 10 - Birth of Osama Bin Laden.
*March 20 - French newspaper ''L'Express'' runs story showing Algerian Muslim torture of civilians. Support for the occupation of Algeria strengthens.

===April===
*April 12 - British Empire begins preparations for Singapore to gain independence as a Commonwealth Nation in [[1958]].

===July===
*July 11 - [[The Jihad]]: Karim Aga Khan becomes the 49th Imam of the Shia Muslims at age 20.
*July 29 - [[The Technology Race]]: The International Atomic Energy Agency is established.

===August===
*August 21 - [[The Technology Race]]: President Thurmond announces the Pentagon's development of a new nuclear waste disposal system that can eliminate harmful radiation in only two years. The methodology is released to the public domain for continued development.

===September===
*September 4 - [[American Civil Rights Movement]]: Orville Faubus, governor of Arkansas, attempts to call out the US National Guard to prevent black students from enrolling in Central High School. Faubus is arrested by US Marshals, and the Arkansas congress removes him from office the same day.

===October===
*October 4 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Soviet Union launches Sputnik I, the first artificial satellite to orbit [[Terra]].
*October 11 - [[The Technology Race]]: Radio telescope of Jodrell Bank in Cheshire, Great Brittain, is opened.
*October 23 - [[The Jihad]]: French troops are unable to continue twin campaigns in both Morocco and Algeria. French troops are withdrawn from Morocco, and Moroccan Muslims invade Ifni.

===November===
*November 3 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Soviet Union launches Sputnik II. On board is the first animal to enter space - a dog named Laika.
*November 7 - [[Cold War]]: In the United States, the Gaither Report reveals CIA reports that the Soviet Union's nuclear arsenal is less than a quarter the size of the United States'. Nuclear hysteria begins to die down.
*November 13 – [[The Technology Race]]: Invention of the laser by Gordon Gould.

===December===
*December 6 - [[The Technology Race]]: The United States launches Explorer I, the first US satellite to orbit [[Terra]].

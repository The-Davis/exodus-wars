The Reigen Royal Territorial Guard comprises the planetary garrison forces of His Majesty's Royal Likniir. Under the direct control of the King, the Territorial Guard operates in a defensive role on all Reigen planets and colony stations.
The small size of the Territorial Guard belies its strength. A highly trained force, the Territorial Guard relies on extreme mobility and superior technology. Although an officially neutral faction, the [[Reigess Suverände]] keeps the Territorial Guard well supplied, lest the riches of His Majesty attract aggression from the outside.

Four years after the first [[Assault on Sindh]], the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] received word by way of the Central Office of Military Intelligence that [[William Camp]] intended to bomb [[Sindh]] in retaliation for the endless guerilla resistance.

Despite the great strategic risk, [[Robert Marshall]] ordered [[Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall]] and the Fifth Fleet of the Line to liberate the planet.  The Mesarthrim commanders, already frustrated with Camp's stubborn insistence on oppressing civilian populations with no strategic return on investment, used the Prefecture counter-assault as an excuse to cut their losses and withdraw.  Sindh's population centers were saved from bombardment and the planet was liberated.

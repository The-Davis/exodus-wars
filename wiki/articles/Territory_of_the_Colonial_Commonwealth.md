==Alpha Centauri System==
The [[Alpha Centauri System]] was originally a colony of the [[United States (Terra)|United States of America]], but in recent years [[Terran]] refugees from [[Argentina (Terra)|Argentina]] have become the overwhelming majority of the population. Governor [[Nicodemo Guadalupe Piazzaro]] is the elected head of the [[Alpha Centauri System|Alpha Centauri]] government.

==Proxima Procyon System==
Originally an independent [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] colony, the [[Proxima Procyon System]] was absorbed into the [[United Earth Alliance]] in its heyday, but maintained a Soviet majority in its population. Governor [[Yosyp Golovnya]] has been in charge of [[Bethija]] as its governor since before the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] occupation. He has always been more concerned with the quality of life for people on [[Bethija]] than with the actual political affiliation of the planet itself. [[Yosyp Golovnya|Golovnya]] gladly surrendered to the [[Onyx Empire]], then to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim]], and then graciously joined with the other worlds in the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]].

==Tau Ceti System==
The [[Tau Ceti System]], like the [[Alpha Centauri System]], was one of the early colonies of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Instead of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] region, it was the [[Canada (Terra)|Canadian]] region that funded the colony project and supplied the citizens for the new venture. [[Kalmar]] has remained relatively unchanged, and Governor [[Jacques Marcileau]] currently leads it as a member of the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]].

==Titan==
The [[Titan System]] was a military power in its own right, and held out against the [[Empire]] long after the rest of the [[United Earth Alliance]] fell. For some time, the Titanians had to govern themselves, and controlled one of the most fearsome [[Terran]] militaries outside of the [[Empire]]. After the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the Titanians were self-governed by a provisional government, which finally opted to keep their original colonial governing system and join the [[Colonial Commonwealth]]. General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]]'s reputation as a leader earned the Titanians the enviable position of coordinating all [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] military activities.

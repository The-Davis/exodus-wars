Tethys is a gas giant, but is significantly smaller than [[Mnemosyne]] or [[Phoebe]]. It has eight moons, but has not been exploited for resources yet. Tethys was the site of some of the first blows in the invasion of the [[Titan System]] by the [[Onyx Empire]], but is actually almost worthless in terms of tactical importance. The Titanians have no installations there, and the Onyx Empire never built any.

In [[2316]], the Titanians constructed the Tethys Anchorage, a refueling and re-arming station for their [[:Category:Warships|warships]]. The station's strategic value was minor compared to their other stations in the system, but it briefly gave the Titanians hope that they would be able to drive away the Imperial presence on [[Cronus]]. The Tethys Anchorage was quickly destroyed, dashing their chances at a strategic or morale victory.

The '''Imperial Identification Implant''' was a unique identification chip implanted in all citizens of worlds under the command of the [[Onyx Empire]].

It tracked every fact about a person: their name, date of birth, address, job, family members, health statistics, and even fertility factors. It was used as the main tracking system in [[Lucius Black]]'s "culling the flocks" program, and people who contributed little or nothing, were diseased, or unable to produce future generations of [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] subjects were terminated.

The Identification Implant was used by the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] to track account information and a citizen's balance of [[Imperial Credit]]s.

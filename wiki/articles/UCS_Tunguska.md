===Overview===

The ''Tunguska'' was designed by Rahn Industries and United Centusi States Navy engineers to create a large carrier capable of extremely quick deployment of its aerospace complement.  Under license by Rahn Industries, the large "flat-top" style carrier was built in the Cirus system, at Folksteiss Naval Shipyards.  It is a solid design utilizing tried-and-true Mesarthrim technologies.

[[Image:UCSTunguska1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The primary cannon outfitted on the ''Tunguska'' is a large, turret-mounted "Reaver" Relativistic Mass Driver.  While it lacks the high firing-rate of many weapons, its astonishing range and power more than make up for this.  The design itself is similar to the cannons mounted on the Prefecture's ''Bastion'', although Mesarthrim technicians reworked much of the weapon to give it greater range and power.

In addition to the "Reaver" cannon are three Fireline Hellguns, standard RI Hellguns heavily overhauled by Fireline Industries of Cirus, and eight Type-R Heavy Blazers, the tried-and-true heavy cannons mounted on nearly every Mesarthrim warship, large and small.  In addition to these heavier weapons, the ''Tunguska'' also mounts 78 Type-F Blazers, the standard anti-ship/anti-fighter cannons of the Mesarthrim, and 88 VOTM tubes, for use against fighters, warships, and stationary targets alike, more than any other ship in the UCS Navy. 

[[Image:UCSTunguska2.jpg]]

The ''Tunguska'''s primary role is the transport of its aerospace complement, and its large flight deck allows it to launch its entire fighter complement in a matter of minutes.  The ''Tunguska'' is capable of carrying fourty-six squadrons of aerospace fighters and fourteen bomber squadrons, plus numerous shuttles and support craft--a sizeable number for any warship. 

The ''Tunguska'' is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system.  The AAS is backed up by Leifstein Composite Armor, which is roughly 25% stronger than Unitron Tzaiko Composite.  It is also quite maneuverable thanks to its Gravity Variance Drive. 

===Deployment===
The ''Tunguska'' was completed and test at the very end of March, 2325.  It was designated as the flagship of the UCS Navy 1st fleet.  Additionally, it is now the command ship of the entire UCS Navy, and Commandant Arnold Stadt personally commands it.  Its home base is in the Centus system.  It is well designed for fleet air-support operations.

===Overview===

The Curvicosta Beta is slightly slower than the standard Racer, and its evasion abilities are poor. Its damage ability is significant, and its behavior patterns are very different from its close cousin.  The differences have earned it the name "Hunter Racer."

The Hunter Racer only has a single non-suicide weapon, an acid gland on the tip of the tail.  This weapon looks similar to the spine and claw combo of the standard Racer, but the weapon isn't very effective against armored targets.

[[Image:CurvicostaBeta.jpg]]

The real weapon of the Hunter Racer is four plasma packs in the torso, which explode when the Hunter Racer decides it has enough targets nearby.  Acid, chitin fragments, and hot plasma are flung in all directions when it explodes.

The Hunter Racer is fast enough to outrun most humanoid ground forces, but its reflexes aren't too good, and it's a little stupid compared to most similarly-sized Volucris breeds.  It tends to run in straight lines with little variation until they blow themselves to pieces.

Besides its chitinous armor, the Hunter Racer's real protection is its similar appearance to the standard Racer.  Most pilots and automated defenses ignore Racers in favor of slower, less maneuverable breeds.  A Hunter Racer can usually be mistaken for a standard Racer and thus speed past defenses with few challenges to deliver its lethal payload.

Hunter Racers are slightly more common than standard Racers, and are deployed side by side with standard Racers.  They are best suited for blowing holes in defense lines and base walls.  Like Racers, Hunter Racers are difficult to track, but much easier than their speedier brethren.

Volucris cannot be properly targeted by most computer systems, so computer assisted identification and differentiation between the Hunter and standard Racers is impossible.  Only the observant warrior can tell the difference in the heat of battle.

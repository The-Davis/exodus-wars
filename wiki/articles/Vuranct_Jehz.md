'''Vuranct Jehz''' is the most hellish region in all Lorithan. The much larger southern region of Vuranct Jehz is a bleaker wasteland, where the bulk of the [[Burning Wars]]' battles took place. Most of the ground is glassed by nuclear explosions and covered in toxic fog. The particles in the air keep the air dark and unbreathable. There are no major cities in the Vuranct region: none survived the war as more than rubble. A few brave explorers venture into the desolated ruins in search of underground shelters containing the remains of the ancient technology and archives of the [[Huern Drastar]], but few return alive, and fewer return with anything for their troubles. There were two recovery missions in the Vuranct region: the Perati Dome run by the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], and the Jamestown Dome, founded by the [[United Earth Alliance]] and taken over by the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

== Regional History ==

Vuranct Jehz was once a fertile land, home to a billion Lorithos and huge herds of [[Ralek]].  The [[Burning Wars]] were often fought in this region, usually for no particular strategic reason other than terrorizing the local population into submitting to one heir or another.  The destruction wrought on the region turned every major city into rubble, burned every plant to the roots, and slagged the very ground that everything grew on.  It was as if the combatants fought the land itself...and won.

The land was so badly scarred that after the first week of [[Meroniri]]-led relief and rescue operations, no more survivors were found.  A scant 60,000 refugees were saved out of a population of over a billion.  The survivors lived in the Meroniri-run Perati Dome, and the [[Terran]]-run Jamestown Dome.

== Perati Dome ==

The Perati Dome, founded, funded and led by a Meroniri named Asanesh Perati, was large enough to house 50,000 refugees in a self-contained, self-sustaining environment.

The dome was meant to be a processing center, where refugees could be medically treated and quickly shipped to more hospitable worlds to build new lives.  Unfortunately, the additional help never came.  Asanesh Perati spent his considerable fortune making the lives of his charges as comfortable as possible, but could not obtain clearance from the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] to bring any refugees into Meroniri space.

When the [[Volucris War]] brought the swarms down on Lorithan, the Perati Dome was one of the first major settlements to be hit.  Asanesh Perati's last transmission home was roughly equivalent to "damn you all, you selfish bastards".  With no military-grade weapons and no vehicles other than short-range desert skimmers, Perati and everyone in the dome were devoured by the swarm.

== Jamestown Dome ==

The Jamestown Dome was a government-sanctioned relief mission funded by the [[United Earth Alliance]].  It was large enough to comfortably house 10,000 refugees, but needed regular supplies and maintenance to remain in operation.

During the [[Second Exodus War]], the United Earth Alliance was no longer able to fund or supply the dome.  The administrator, [[Annette Rollins]], was able to cannibalize parts from non-vital systems to keep the air recyclers running, and personally ran over a thousand scavenger missions into the wastelands on small aircraft and ground vehicles.  "Rollins Runners" were some of the finest wastelander scavengers and engineers, who worked tirelessly to pull working components from Burning Wars-era wrecks and create bastardized life support systems for the dome.  The residents of the Jamestown Dome were largely forgotten by the outside world during the war, but they lived thanks to their own ingenuity and stubbornness.

After the Second Exodus War ended, the newly-formed [[Colonial Commonwealth]] began picking up the pieces of the United Earth Alliance, and sent relief funds and supplies to Jamestown Dome.  At the insistence of the residence, Administrator Rollins was given tenure and allowed to run the dome as long as she felt able to keep the job.

When the [[Volucris War]] began, Rollins was still running the dome, but was getting on in years. It soon became clear that if help was on the way, it would not arrive in time.  Rollins organized another dome-wide project, and managed to build enough re-breathers and ground vehicles to get 6,000 people out of the dome.  The 4,000 who remained behind would face certain death.  Rollins volunteered to stay behind, but her people forced her to leave with the main caravan.  Only 3,500 people managed to reach the relative safety of the [[Dzarning Mountain Range]].  Rollins survived the journey, but refused to speak about what she had seen along the way.  The trek across a 950-kilometer stretch of Vuranct Jehz had killed 2,500 people and so stunned the rest of them that few ever told anyone else what they had seen.

== Notable Residents ==

Since the [[Burning Wars]], the Vuranct Jehz region has been uninhabitted except for the two domes.  The administrator of the Jamestown Dome, Annette Rollins, made a name for herself during the [[Volucris War]] when she organized one of the more daring escapes in the region.

== Natural Resources ==

Since the destruction wrought by the [[Burning Wars]], no natural resources have been harvested from the Vuranct Jehz region.

=== Flora ===

All natural-growing flora in the Vuranct Jehz region were wiped out by the [[Burning Wars]].

=== Fauna ===

All natural fauna in the Vuranct Jehz region have died off.

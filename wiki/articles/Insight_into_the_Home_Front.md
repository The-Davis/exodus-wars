The Home Front - 4/2/2325 - By James Davis

     The Galactic Herald is briefly interrupting our around-the-clock coverage of the Battle of Icronia to bring you these important stories from the various home fronts. We recognize the importance of the battle with the Volucris, both in the human tragedy of death and in the economic impact of the damage to Icronia. We will return you to the coverage very shortly.

Terra
     The Terran Recovery Administration has made tremendous leaps and bounds in the past weeks. The virulent plague outbreak in the southern Texas refugee camps was completely contained and cured following the attack by Conclave agents. Since then, recovery efforts and refugee camps have managed to reach well into northern Canada, a region made nearly barren by the battles fought by the retreating Royal Imperial Army.
     With the South American and African continents completely restored, and the North American and Asian continents well on their way, the Alliance of Planets has officially handed over administrative authority of the planet to the Terran Embassy, which will remain an independent protectorate of the Alliance of Planets and its Member States.
     The economy of Terra is still in a terrible depression, but top market analysts believe that it will begin to grow again in the second quarter of 2326.

United Archadian Republics
    Social unrest is beginning to raise its ugly head among the Archadian caste system. The system is as sound as ever--the cause is not class envy, it is the controversy of dealing with the brewing war between the United Centusi States (UCS) and the Prefecture.
     The United Archadian Republics (UAR) is bound by a treaty to share command of the First Joint Defense Fleet (FJDF) with the UCS for mutual defense. The UCS is actually the aggressor in the mounting conflict, so the UAR is very reluctant to surrender command of the FJDF for use in the war.

Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan
    The Lorithos homeworld of Lorithan is rarely the source of big news stories. They routinely release updates on the status of the Ansible network, but nothing else of note to the galaxy has been reported for years.
     Today, that changed with a major archaeological discovery. Since the Burning Wars that decimated Lorithos society in 2270, the Lorithos people have had no detailed records of their past. Archaeologists have tried to recover the ancient parts of their civilization.
     The recovery that was unveiled today was not, in fact, Lorithos in origin. It is the uppermost portion of a vast structure, built with technology similar to what was used by the now-extinct HalbAnderung. Seismic readings show that the building extends to the far reaches of the core of the planet, and while some of the lowermost levels are filled with molten rock, most of it is clear--though completely uninhabitable. The Galactic Herald will continue to follow this story as it unfolds.

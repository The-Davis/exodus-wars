==International Organizations==

===The Alliance of Planets===

The [[Prefecture]] is a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], and joined in [[2323]]. The [[Prefecture]] is considered something of an upstart in the [[Alliance High Council]], bringing multiple troubling issues to the forefront and causing contention between members. It was the [[Prefecture]] that got the [[United Centusi States]] kicked out of the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]]. As of [[2326]], the [[Prefecture]] boycotts all meetings of the [[Alliance of Planets]].


===Council of Independent Nations===

The [[Prefecture]] does not recognize the [[Alliance of Planets]]' trade embargo on the [[Council of Independent Nations| Council]], as it was passed during their boycott of the meetings. The [[Prefecture]] maintains a neutral stance on the [[Council of Independent Nations| Council]] itself, but has friendly ties with some of its members, particularly [[Reigess Suverände| Reigess]] and [[Oronus Worker's Coalition| Oronus]].

==Individual Governments==

===Taviridis Somarchada===

The [[Prefecture]] has a neutral military stance towards [[Taviridis Somarchada]], but maintains trade relations. The [[Prefecture]]'s medical technology is considered advanced even by [[Ixoarchada]] standards, and the [[Ixoarchada]] sell their anti-gravity technology to the [[Prefecture]].

===Auellal League===

The [[Prefecture]] maintains friendly relations with the [[Auellal League| Auellal]]. During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], the [[Prefecture]] attempted to normalize relations between the [[Auellal League| Auellal]] and the [[United Centusi States| Centusi]], but failed. Since then, the [[Prefecture]] supplied some military aid to the [[Auellal League| Auellal]], and has been working towards a military alliance since the [[Tempest War]] began.

===Colonial Commonwealth===

The [[Prefecture]] buys many of its war machines from companies based on [[Alpha Centauri]]. The [[Empire]] occupied [[Alpha Centauri]] from [[2300]] to [[2323]], and established multiple military contracting firms during their rule, which in turn designed the war machines used by the [[Empire]] and the [[Prefecture]]. Most designs are still built on [[Alpha Centauri]] factories, which the [[Prefecture]] then buys. The [[Prefecture]] maintains friendly ties with the [[Colonial Commonwealth| Commonwealth]] as a whole, and would probably come to their aid in any crisis.

===The Conclave===

The [[Conclave]] is now defunct, but was the sworn enemy of the [[Prefecture]] while it existed.

===Ikronin Jurekön===

The [[Prefecture]] has its closest allies in the [[Ikronin Jurekön]]. Both nations trade with each other with military hardware, medical technology, food, electronics, and nearly every other trade commodity. The [[Prefecture]] and the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] have mutual military defense treaties, and the lowest trade tariffs on record for dealing with the [[Ikronin]].

===Kabila Kimburu===

The [[Prefecture]] has little contact with the [[Kabila Kimburu| Kimburuns]], though they still have some of the technology secrets the [[Empire]] stole early in the [[Second Exodus War]]. Because [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]]'s homeworld and territory is so far removed from Prefecture territory, they continue to have little contact with each other.

===Kalidasa Planetary Authority===

When [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority| Kalidasa]] declared its independence at the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the [[Prefecture]] initially supported them and sent in troops to help clean out the [[Empire|Imperial]] remnant. Once independence was achieved, however, the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasans]] seized control of their shipyards and refused to turn over any of the partially completed [[Empire|Imperial]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] to the [[Prefecture]], which was part of their original deal. This soured relations between the nations somewhat, and they have been neutral and wary towards each other since then.

===Meroniri Terinasi===

The [[Meroniri Terinasi]] is officially an ally of the [[Prefecture]], but relations have been extremely strained between each other lately due to the [[Meroniri]] seizing control of many areas of international power. [[Meroniri]] territory is far removed from [[Prefecture]] territory, requiring several weeks by [[hyperspace]], so contact is limited outside of [[Alliance of Planets High Council|Council]] meetings.

===Kabila iku Gol'nde===

With the exception of the [[Empire]]'s contact with [[Kabila Nyoka]] in the 2200s, the [[Prefecture]] has had no contact with the main [[Ngrligru]] government.

===Oronus Worker's Coalition===

[[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] and the [[Prefecture]] maintain excellent ties together. [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus]] supplies many of the raw materials needed to power the [[Prefecture]]'s war machine, and the [[Prefecture]] has been their best customer since the fall of the [[Empire]].

===Reigess Suverände===

As the [[Prefecture]]'s best supply of [[antimatter]], and as a self-sufficient world, the [[Reigess Suverände]] has a very favorable relationship with the [[Prefecture]]. The [[Prefecture]] can only buy their [[antimatter]] with hard currency, as they have nothing else the [[Reigens]] want.

===The Remnant===

The [[Prefecture]] fought the [[Remnant]] during the [[Imperial Civil War]], but only had contact with one of their ships and crews. The [[Remnant]] has a much different political stance than their expedition's commander did, and no one can say how the two governments will get along after they make contact again.

===Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan===

The [[Prefecture]] often contends with the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan| Lorithos]] in [[Alliance of Planets]] meetings, but has little to do with them otherwise. Their official stance is neutral.

===Sirius Families===

The [[Prefecture]] adopted the Empire's recognition of the [[Sirius Cluster]] as the sovereign territory of the [[Sirius Families]].  However, they do not interact with them, and there is still animosity over the [[Sirius Incursion]].

===Sirunaki Rebellion===

The [[Prefecture]] has had no contact with the [[Sirunaki]].

===Terran Embassy===

When [[Terra]] was freed from the [[Empire]], the [[Prefecture]] was one of the main contributors to building up the world again. They supplied the [[Terran Embassy]] with material support, and were one of the first governments to recognize their independence.

===United Centusi States===

The [[United Centusi States]] and the [[Prefecture]] are in open hostilities with each other, in a conflict known as the "[[Tempest War]]."

===Volucris Swarms===

The [[Prefecture]] does not recognize the [[Volucris]] as a government, or even recognize them as intelligent beings with rights. The [[Prefecture]] exterminates the [[Volucris]] whenever and wherever they find them, with no negotiations.

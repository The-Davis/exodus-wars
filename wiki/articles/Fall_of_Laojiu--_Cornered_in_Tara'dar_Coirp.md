Set after "Taming the Fathach Meire"

by James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     For six days, the survivors of the rout of Fathach Méire have been streaming into Tara'dar Coirp, and William Parrish is using every trick he has at his disposal. The Mesarthrim coming in from the south have encircled the city except for the northern entries. The Mesarthrim still in the mountains are slowly seeking out the hidden Prefecture air bases, but without much success. Unknown to the Mesarthrim, Parrish begins moving troops into a deep tunnel complex leading back into the heart of the Fathach Méire.

The Njiorin Sector is named for "[[Njiorin's Well]]", known to the [[Terrans]] as the Ptolemy Cluster. The sector is the economic mecca of the [[Orion Arm]], due to the [[Ikronin]] and [[Reigen]] influence. The [[Yngard System]] is the most populated star system in the sector, followed by the [[Raegard System]].

The Njiorin Sector is 45LY across turnwise/counterturn, and 55LY coreward/hubward. The Njiorin Sector is centered on [[Njiorin's Well]].

== Inhabited Star Systems ==

The [[Yngard System]], [[Myrgard System]], [[Raegard System]], [[Mirdek System]], [[Titan System]], [[Hamur System]], [[Orhak System]], [[Oronus System]], and [[Lupei System]] are currently inhabited. Many of these worlds are large population centers and important in the interstellar economy.

== Njiorin's Well ==

[[Njiorin's Well]] is a group of Class O supergiant stars, which the [[Reigess Suverände]] has claimed and holds with an iron fist. The cluster is the richest source of antimatter in the entire [[Orion Arm]].

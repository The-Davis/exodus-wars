== Events of 1954 ==
===January===
*January 1 - The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] forgives all outstanding war reparations owed by East Germany.
*January 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: The first kidney transplants are done in Boston and also in Paris.
*January 21 - [[The Technology Race]]: The first nuclear-powered submarine, the USS ''Nautilus'', is launched in Groton, Connecticut.

===February===
*February 10 - [[Vietnam War]]: After vetoing a $785 million budget in military aid to [[France (Terra)|France]] in [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]], President [[Thomas Dewey]] warns against [[United States (Terra)|United States]] intervention in [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]], claiming the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] should not support the building of foreign empires.
*February 23 - [[The Technology Race]]: The first mass vaccination of children against polio begins in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania and helps to save the lives of innumerable children.
*February 25 - [[The Jihad]]: Gamal Abdel Nasser is the senior Muslim cleric in Egypt.

===March===
*March 9 - Edward Murrow and Fred Friendly die in a studio fire while producing a scathing "See It Now" special on Senator [[Joseph McCarthy]]. [[CIA]] agents are suspected.
*March 13 - [[Vietnam War]]: [[France (Terra)|French]] troops begin battle against Viet Minh in Dien Bien Phu.
*March 23 - [[Vietnam War]]: Viet Minh attempt to capture the main airstrip of Dien Bien Phu, but are driven off by [[France (Terra)|French]] commandos.
*March 25 - [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] recognizes sovereignty of [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] but [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] troops remain in the country.

===April===
*April 1 - President [[Thomas Dewey]] authorizes the creation of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Air Force Academy in Colorado.
*April 3 - Vladimir Petrov defects from the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] and asks to seek political asylum in Australia.
*April 7 - President [[Thomas Dewey|Dewey]] gives his "shining example" speech during a news conference, encourages American emphasis on developing technology and stamping out [[Communism]] at home, rather than interfering in foreign events.
*April 22- Senator [[Joseph McCarthy]] begins hearings investigating the United States Army for harboring [[Communism]].

===May===
*May 7 - [[Vietnam War]]: The Battle of Dien Bien Phu ends in a Viet Minh defeat. The [[France (Terra)|French]] secure the area against further uprisings.

===June===
*June 9 - [[Cold War]]: Joseph Welch, special counsel for the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Army, lashes out at Senator Joseph McCarthy during hearings on whether Communism has infiltrated the Army. When McCarthy responds and continues his cross-examination, the chambers erupt in applause. Welch is shouted down on three separate occasions and is soon replaced as the counsel for the Army.
*June 17 - Military coup occurs in [[Guatemala (Terra)|Guatemala]].
*June 27
**[[Guatemala (Terra)|Guatemalan]] President Jacobo Arbenz Guzmán successfully fights off an SIS-sponsored military coup, preventing civil.
**[[The Technology Race]]: The world's first atomic power station opened at Obninsk, near Moscow.

===July===
*July 21 - [[Vietnam War]]: Ho Chi Min is captured by the [[France (Terra)|French]] and executed. [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]] region is divided into French and [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]]ese controlled regions, and the French agree to withdraw by [[1964]] in exchange for continued economic ties with the new Republic of [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]].

===August===
*August 6 - [[Cold War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] sends diplomats to the Republic of [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]] to attempt to contact Ho Chi Minh's surviving followers. [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]]ese officials politely but firmly escort [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviets]] out of the country. Last members of the [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]]ese [[Communist]] Party flee to the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]].

===September===
*September 8 - [[Cold War]]: The Southeast Asia Treaty Organization (SEATO) is established in [[Thailand (Terra)|Thailand]]. [[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]], [[Australia (Terra)|Australia]], [[Thailand (Terra)|Thailand]], [[Cambodia (Terra)|Cambodia]], [[Ceylon (Terra)|Ceylon]], the [[Korea (Terra)|Republic of Korea]], and the Republic of [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]] are the founding members. The [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] maintains an advisory role due to the involvement of several [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth Nations]].

===October===
*October 18 - [[The Technology Race]]: Texas Instruments announces the first transistor radio.
*October 23 - [[Cold War]]: [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] joins NATO.
*October 31 - [[The Jihad]]: The Algerian Muslim Liberation Front begins a revolt against [[France (Terra)|French]] rule.

===November===
*November 1 - [[The Jihad]]: The Muslim Liberation Front attacks public buildings of the  [[France (Terra)|French]] colonial government in Algeria.
*November 10 - [[United States (Terra)|United States]] President [[Thomas Dewey]] dedicates the USMC War Memorial in Arlington National Cemetery.

===December===
*December 2 - [[Cold War]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Senate votes 74 to 26 to praise [[Joseph McCarthy]] for "leading the Senate in the worthy fight against domestic [[Communism]]."
*December 24 - [[Cold War]]: [[Laos (Terra)|Laos]] becomes independent of [[France (Terra)|French]] control in a peaceful agreement. [[France (Terra)|French]] continue to supply [[Laos (Terra)|Laotian]] government with military and technological support. [[Laos (Terra)|Laos]] joins SEATO.

'''Operation Durandal''' was the code name for the cooperative military offensive that pushed the [[Onyx Empire]] out of the [[Titan System]]. It resulted in the [[Third Battle of Cronus]], and began the momentum that eventually ended the [[Second Exodus War]] in favor of the coalition forces.

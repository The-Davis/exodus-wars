When the XF-12 was in development, the resistance formally revealed itself as the [[Prefecture]], and the civil war broke out. The Empire attempted to develop a new airframe and new control system simultaneously. The XF-12 was an extremely streamlined delta wing airframe with a highly maneuverable aeroelastic control system (Aeroelasticity). The XF-12 was equipped with six cannons and four hard points. It performed extremely well, but with the United Mesarthrim Clans, the Prefecture, and the Titanians all pressing the war against the Empire, only the YF-12 prototypes made it to the front lines. The infamous Scimitar Squadron was equipped with the F-12 Vampire. The F-11A, F-4F and F-9B were all equipped with aeroelastic controls, eliminating the problems with the F-11.

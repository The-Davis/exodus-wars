'''Meronir Tronit'''
*Date of Death: [[16000BC]]
*Place of Death: [[Sepulare]]

''Meronir Tronit'' was the first [[Lothenar]], and the founder of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. He is so honored by the [[Meroniri]] today that they have named their entire race after him.

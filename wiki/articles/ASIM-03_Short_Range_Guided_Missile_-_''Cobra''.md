The Air/Space Intercept Missile designation 03, dubbed the "Cobra", was a computer guided missile weapon which could operate in atmosphere or in space. An operator could simply fire the missile without designating a target, and the missile's internal sensors and Identification Friend/Foe system would pick an appropriate target of opportunity, making it a "zero thought required" weapon.

The system was prone to error, often picking targets of little tactical importance. Later designs were corrected to pick targets of higher threat. The Cobra had an effective range of eight kilometers.

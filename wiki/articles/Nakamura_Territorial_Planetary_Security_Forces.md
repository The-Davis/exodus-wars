The Sirius factions employ various forces, regular and otherwise, to secure their planets and orbital stations. Aside from the various agents, guards, and "supervisors" in the Sirius Administration, each faction maintains its own version of a standing army for defense from the outside, and to quell dissidents internally.

On the ground, Nakamura troops are focused on fast attack. They use the Tanto, a one-man hovercraft or "hoverbike", the Sai light hovercraft, and the Yokozuna assault tank. Their main infantry is the Ronin assault trooper, but they also have highly trained covert agents known as "Shadows."

'''Cuba''' was a Caribbean island nation located south of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]].

Cuba was the site of an attempted [[Communist]] rebellion in [[1952]], but the ringleaders, Fidel Castro and Che Guevara, were killed; Castro in a bungled attack on a Cuban military base, and Guevara when his ship from [[Mexico (Terra)|Mexico]] sank in a storm. Cuba stabilized as a democratic nation in the aftermath of the failed rebellion.

Cuba became a part of the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2013]].

The [[Colonial Commonwealth]]'s national anthem, "'''Is Titan Still Free?'''", was written by the young [[Tejat|Tejati]] [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] member [[Joshua Duram]].

During the long years of the occupation, "is Titan still free?" became the watch words of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]]. There were various phrases that were considered safe "password" responses by the more naive [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] cells--a fact which the [[Omegas]] exploited to arrest and murder dozens of the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] members with more idealism than sense. Despite their dark history, the response phrase "she shines on against tyranny" were added to [[Joshua Duram|Duram]]'s poem.

The [[Omegas]] captured and killed [[Joshua Duram|Duram]] in July of [[2318]]. One of the few copies he had made of his poem had been given to his friend, the [[Tejat|Tejati]] [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] cell leader [[Talaris Roi]]. Stricken with grief at the loss of her friend, [[Talaris Roi|Roi]] wrote music for the poem, and later performed the song for a [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]] broadcast that was sent throughout every [[Terran]] world.

The [[Titan System]] first received the broadcast in September. In her memoirs, [[Lynn Sanburn]] later recalled that when she listened to the broadcast with her husband [[Scott Sanburn|Scott]], it was the only time she had seen the legendary general cry. She did not realize it at the time, but [[Scott Sanburn]] had already received word that the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government would be offering the [[Onyx Empire]] permanent sovereign rights to the [[Theta Algedi System]] and other [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]]-occupied worlds in exchange for peace.

[[Talaris Roi]]'s original performance was played on the [[sitar]], the only instrument she knew how to play. The [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] later contracted several musicians and the singer [[Grace Richards]] to perform a production-level rendition of the song. This version was played throughout the [[Titan System]] during the [[Empire]]'s bombing campaign of [[Theia]] in March of [[2321]].

The song became the de facto anthem of the coalition forces during the campaign to take back [[Terra]], and was played every morning at military bases across the [[Titan System]]. When the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] was formed in [[2323]], adopting the song as their national anthem was a foregone conclusion, though [[Tejat]]'s decision to join the [[Prefecture]] was a disappointment to many members of the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] military.

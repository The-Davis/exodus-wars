===Architecture===

[[Ixoarchada]] architecture is primarily based on arches and domes, with very few sharp corners, spires, or other similar features. The key to [[Ixoarchada]] design is for the eye to flow smoothly over the surface of an object. Seen as the perfect structures, domes permeate nearly all [[Ixoarchada]] architecture, even designs of the simplest structures. 

The [[Deah'Acrolis]], an important structure in the Ixoarchada capital of [[Cathairn]], is an example of traditional [[Ixoarchada]] architecture. It is a dome, supported by hundreds of columns similar in style to ancient Greek columns of [[Terra]], with four round towers at the four corners of the dome. Even the most ancient [[Ixoarchada]] structures, which date back more than 10,000 years, show a very similar architectural style. An example of a [[Terran]] design similar in architectural style would be the ancient Memorial to Thomas Jefferson in old Washington D.C., and the similarities have been pointed out by several [[Ixoarchada]] architects and historians. Even military installations and buildings usually display this type of architecture.

[[Ixoarchada]] decor usually incorporates similar designs as their building architecture, using flowing lines and curves in place of rigid, straight lines.  In the [[Ularen System]] in particular, lights almost always have a slight red tint to them, which emulates the natural red light that comes from Ularen, [[Glengarr]]'s star.

===Social Structure===

Considering their two-thousand years of space capabilities, one might expect the Ixoarchada to populate more than their four star systems, especially considering that Terrans have spread to several dozen in their mere three-hundred year space-traveling history.  Because of their terraformation skills, the four Ixoarchada star systems--[[Glengarr]], [[Bollaria]], [[Bregdan]], and [[Giraxis]]--have more than one inhabitable planet.  This combined with the industrial capacities of the Bregdan and Giraxis system has made further expansion of Ixoarchada space unnecessary, although the Ixoarchada do have claims on several other systems and terraformation is not out of the question. 

Ixoarchada social structure, once heavily segregated by the caste system, is now mostly 'permeable', in that climbing and descending the social and economic ladder is no longer dictated by birth.  Today, ones birth caste has little to do with their day-to-day life, and is more of a cultural/historical identification than a modern classification.

Among the 'lesser' castes, the religious caste, though similar physically, is the most separate from the other castes, and requires either birth into the caste, or a long series of ceremonies and rituals. Other than this, the lesser castes interact freely in the capitalistic society dominated by high-tech, computer related industries and jobs.  Most "lower class" tasks are now handled by automated servants.

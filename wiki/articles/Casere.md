'''Casere''' is the XXth planet in the [[Avior System]].

==History==

Colonized in [[2215]]

Casere is a proud world, the corporate heart of the Laojiu farm worlds. Like most of its neighboring star-systems, Casere was founded by Korean emigrants during the decline of the United Earth Alliance. The first colony was twenty thousand, established in 2215 on a high plateau in the center of the sole inhabited continent. The plateau, Osarwa, and the city named after it became the capital of the colony as the population grew and branched out. Over the years, five additional colonies were founded on the planet before the [[Empire]] took the world as part of its burgeoning farming sector in 2319.

==Geography==

Casere has xx continents, covering a wide range of biospheres and climates.

Besides the capital at Osarwa, the other five colonies remain dense population centers, each with their own farming specialty. Osarwa houses the regional corporate headquarters of a dozen different food conglomerates and shipping interests. Kepung is the heart of the proverbial "corn belt" of Casere, growing billions of tons of the popular vegetable every year. Janggok is in the heart of a moist sub-tropical basin, where many different fruits and vines grow well. Daegu focuses on various grains, Busan has thousands of acres of orchards, and Pohang grows root-stocks like potatoes and turnips. Each world in Laojiu could support half the population of the Prefecture single-handedly, making the District the true "bread basket of the galaxy."

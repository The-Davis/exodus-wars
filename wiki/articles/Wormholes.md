==Avmaska-Stig - Wormhole Gate==

In the ancient days before the [[Sundering]], the [[Anthronoris]] mastered many methods of travel. One method they used, and abandoned over a thousand years before the [[Sundering]] was a series of tunnels into a 5th dimension of normal space-time. The method was simple, ellegant, and efficient, but far too slow for their star-spanning empire.

In the years since, many human races have theorized and dreamed about the concept of the "Wormhole." The [[Terrans]]' Lorentzian Wormhole model came close--they simply lacked the means to put it into effect.

The [[Ikronin]] were the only culture to delve into the fifth dimension of normal-space with any serious dedication. Most cultures discovered [[hyperspace]] and moved on with it as their primary means of transportation, but the [[Ikronin]] were not so fortunate. Their experiments with Emellantid--literally Between Time, their name for the fifth dimension--eventually yielded a stable wormhole that "folded" normal space by a factor of 8x10<sup>6</sup>. For every kilometer travelled in Emellantid, the end result was 8,000,000 kilometers travelled in normal space. This early form of travel for the [[Ikronin]] was a simple process, requiring little power, and allowing them to literally dash around their own star system with ease.

Eventually, the [[Ikronin]] physicist [[Alfrid Birgirsen]] discovered that the fold factor of Emellantid was exponentially proportionate to the power of the initial entry into the dimension. Most folds were being conducted by deep space ships with only a few megawatts of power to use for the effect. [[Alfrid Birgirsen|Birgirsen]] developed the first Avmaska-Stig, which we know today as the Wormhole Gate. With a power output of 100tW, the fold factor was 8x10<sup>12</sup>, allowing a ship that travelled a kilometer in Emellantid to travel 8,000,000,000,000 kilometers in normal space, an interstellar distance of 0.85 light years.

Using the Avmaska-Stig led to a problem: once inside, a probe could not exit Emellantid. It was trapped. [[Alfrid Birgirsen|Birgirsen]] solved the problem by building a pair of Avmaska-Stig: one to open a pathway into the fifth dimension, and one to house the other end.

Today, the [[Ikronin]] have built a network of Avmaska-Stig, which vary in distance from each other. Due to the enormous power requirements of a top-rated gate capable of folding space at a rate of 0.85 light years per kilometer, most Avmaska-Stig are powered down to a more reasonable rate of 0.001 light years per kilometer. With starships capable of travelling several thousand kilometers per hour in normal space, they can more than make up the difference under their own power, and still reap the benefits of not needing to discharge the power reserves necessary for a [[hyperspace]] jump.

In times of emergency, [[Ikronin]] Avmaska-Stig can be ramped up to full power, allowing [[Ikronin]] assets to fly across the galaxy at unmatched rates.

Of all the treasures of the [[Ikronin]], the Emellantid is their only secret: they have made it so efficient that it dwarfs the abilities of hyperspace most of the time.

A Wormhole Gate's range is actually limited by another factor: exotic matter and the strength of local [[hyperspace]]. While the fifth dimension of normal space is what the Wormhole interacts with, it requires a strong local [[hyperspace]] to simply exist. Normal space has four dimensions in areas devoid of [[hyperspace]]...i.e. intergalactic gulfs.

In addition, exotic matter is needed to "wedge" the gate open. Without it, the gate collapses almost instantly. Due to this, most Gates are limited to 20 to 30 light years in distance, and the Gate from [[Icronia]] to [[Sepulare]] or to [[Glengarr]] can only be open for a minute or so at a time. Because of the limitations, those Gates are always at full power when activated, and only fast ships (preferably with a "running start") are allowed through. The longer the trip, the more likely it is that the Gate will collapse on one side or another, with disastrous results for any mundane matter trapped inside.

Obviously, the [[Ikronin]] have the control stations, queues, tolls, and other control systems in place to keep non-[[Ikronin]] traffic from making overly risky hops.

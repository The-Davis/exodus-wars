===Overview===

After the Rahn Industries headquarters was lifted into space from its relative safely of the earth, the need for constant defense and security was recognized. With this, the idea was introduce that the R.I. facilities should be surrounded by defensive platforms like most other military installations.

The idea was given the go ahead and resulted in a couple of designs, the most powerful of which was the Epsilon-class manned defensive platform. With the ability to provide round-the-clock defense, the platforms were built as soon as was physically possible and were put into commission as soon as they were done.

After the years, the platforms have been rebuilt and improved many times, usually encompassing the latest in defensive technology. One of the latest improvements has been the addition of the hanger bays that allow the Epsilons to carry their own fighter compliment.

===Capabilities===

When Rahn Industries Security Force discussed having defensive platforms the obvious question came up of, "How large and powerful do we make these things?" This was solved by building a couple of different platforms, the Epsilon-class being the biggest and most powerful, giving it the ability to fend off capital ships with ease, and the Defender-class being its counterpart.

[[Image:PL110Epsilon.jpg]]

The power comes from the fact that it was decided to have an unprecedented four naval cannons fitted onto the hull. With this kind of fire power at its disposal, a single Epsilon-class manned defensive platform can take on up to two medium capital ships and either cripple or destroy them outright.

The other weapons that are included on this behemoth of a space station include twenty fast-tracking neutron cannons. This allows the Epsilon-class to fend off fighter and bomber attacks with relative ease and allows the station to focus even more fire power onto a single ship. To back these up, a single platform carries with it sixteen variable ordinance missile tubes which can be filled with anything from anti-fighter cluster rounds to high yield nuclear warheads.

===Deployment===

The Epsilon-class manned defensive platform is usually deployed in at least twos, that way they are able to compliment and support each other when the time comes. However, the current R.I.S.F. has deployed twelve of the platforms around the main Rahn Industries facilities.

This allows for maximum coverage of all possible attack routes and is structured so that multiple stations are able to cover the same firing vector. The this also allows for the twenty four fighters that are stationed on the platforms, members of the First New Albany Air Command, to have maximum coverage of the area.

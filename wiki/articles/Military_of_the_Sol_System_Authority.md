The Sol Defense Force is the only armed force that allows its recruits to join up without changing their citizenship. An [[Ixoarchada]] can serve in the Sol Defense Force, as can a [[Mesarthrim]].

The SDF concentrates on keeping enemies away from [[Earth]]. They have defensive installations and fortresses on every solid planet from Pluto to Mars, and can saturate incoming fleets with powerful warheads, drawing them into ground combat away from [[Earth]] itself. The SDF is limited to action only in the [[Sol System]] and along [[Terran]] shipping lanes.

Most designs used by the SDF are purchased by the [[Alliance of Planets]] Member States, and donated for the purpose of [[Terran]] defense.

The Second Joint Defense Fleet was designed by the Prefecture, the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], and Meronir's Republic to defend Terra. There are only six warships, but all of them are top-line models, and well-built. With the exception of the ''Votaw'', the flagship, the fleet is crewed exclusively by Terrans, and commanded by Admiral George Brody. The flagship ''Votaw'' is the pride of the [[Sol System Authority]], a full-powered Meroniri Tronit Class Battlecruiser. The Meroniri do not allow Terrans aboard, but the ship follows the orders of Admiral Brody. Brody commands the SJDF from the Daedelus, an Avenger class cruiser sold to Terra by the Prefecture at a fraction of its cost. The remaining four ships in the fleet are Shamrock class carriers, a design created by the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] for heavy fighter presence. The Euna, Midgard, Pacifica, and Tir na Nog are deployed throughout the [[Sol System]].


The Terran Air Force was built by Meronir's Republic and the Titanian Planetary Alliance. Some mercenary pilots from Icronia have been grandfathered into the force, but most pilots are born Terrans. Sky Marshal Cheryl Alexander commands the unit.

Air Commands are assigned to each of the four carriers, and to Pluto, Io, Mars, and Terra. The Air Commands exclusively use the Devastator multi-purpose fighter, a long-range design built by the Titanians.


The Terran Regulars is a very small army, with a single Infantry Brigade. They use the Prefecture's standard powered armor exclusively, but have enough Skyhawk dropships to transport every soldier at once, one of the most mobile organizations around. The army is commanded by General Robert Wilson.

The Sol Defense Force's ranking system is simple. All three branches of the service are combined under one system. The Second Joint Defense Fleet is made up of only six warships, so it has no additional organization. The Terran Air Force is divided into Wings. Each Wing has three squadrons of twelve fighters. The Terran Regulars have only a single Brigade, which has twelve Battalions. Each Battalion has eight Companies, each Company has ten Platoons, and each Platoon has four squads of ten men.

Ranks:
The Admiral is a unique rank. George Brody commands the Second Joint Defense Fleet.
The Sky Marshal is a unique rank. Cheryl Alexander commands the Terran Air Force.
The General is a unique rank. Robert Wilson commands the Terran Regulars.
Captains are in command of warships.
Colonels are in command of multiple air Wings. Majors are in command of a Battalion..
Commanders are in charge of a single Squadron or Company.
Lieutenants are in charge of a fighter, a dropship, or a Squad.
The enlisted ranks are Sergeant, Corporal and Private.

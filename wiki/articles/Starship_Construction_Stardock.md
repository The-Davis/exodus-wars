Situated over the Prefecture's capital world of Ueda, the Warship Development Center functions as Rahn Industries' thinktank and prototype center for the largest contracts the company receives. Even though the primary designing goes on in one of the arms of Rahn Industries headquarters station, situated over the economic powerhouse of Albanus, the refining and actual prototype models are built here.

[[Image:StarshipConstructionStardock.jpg]]

Essentially nothing more than a construction yard dock, the Warship Development Center appears to be a quite utilitarian place, but this is far from the truth. With some of the best food in the corporate world and with more than one cafeteria, Rahn Industries makes sure to keep its workers and designers full and happy. Also, when not working the eight hour shifts, employs are treated to some of the best onboard accommodations next to the main headquarters station.

This atmosphere helps to ensure that minds are sharp as tacks and have the nutrition needed to help facilitate the dreaming and designing of some of the best warships in the galaxy. Protected by the Aztec, as well as the Prefecture military, the Warship Development Center is one of the best defended places in the Prefecture and in Rahn Industries' sphere of influence.

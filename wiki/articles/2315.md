== Events of 2315 ==

=== January ===

* January 9 - The [[Royal Imperial Navy|Imperial Armada]] enters the [[Sol System]]. Two [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] and four [[United Earth Alliance Air Defense Force]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] squadrons are destroyed in the first engagement.
* January 14 - The remaining ten [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] in the [[Sol System]] face down the full might of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. Commodore-Commander [[Simon Frazier]] commands the delaying action, harrying the ships under [[Robert Marshall]]'s command.
* January 19 - The last of Commodore-Commander [[Simon Frazier]]'s command is destroyed. [[Simon Frazier]] is killed when the [[UEAS Gryphon|UEAS ''Gryphon'']] is lost with all hands in the [[Ceres Belt]].
* January 21 - The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] systematically destroys all [[United Earth Alliance]]-controlled satellites orbitting [[Earth]].

=== February ===

* February 5 - [[Royal Imperial Army]] elements led by [[Cecil Au-Yueng]] land in the northern Atlantic Ocean and the eastern Pacific Ocean on [[Earth]], and make landings in California, Mexico, England and Portugal.
* February 6 - The [[Royal Imperial Army]] engages up against multiple divisions of [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] army groups. The [[:Category:Rigs|rigs]] of the [[Empire]] are put to excellent use, particularly in the streets of [[San Francisco]], where they completely outmaneuver the [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]] of the [[United Earth Alliance]].
* February 7 - [[Travis Carpenter]] wins his first medal for bravery in the [[Storming of San Francisco]]. [[Travis Carpenter|Carpenter]] is credited with killing forty [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] soldiers, both tanks and infantry.
* February 12 - The elders of the City of [[San Francisco]] formally surrender to [[Cecil Au-Yueng]], promising that the City will harbor no elements of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]]. The City's Mayor, [[Glenda Emerson]], sends a letter to the [[United Earth Alliance]] [[Government of the United Earth Alliance]], requesting they send no further military aid to [[San Francisco]].
* February 16 - Elements of the [[Royal Imperial Army]] enter the Colorado region in a forced march to [[Denver]], the capital of the [[United Earth Alliance]].
* February 18 - The [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] meets the [[Royal Imperial Army]] in battle outside of [[Denver]], hoping to prevent the [[Onyx Empire]] from entering the capital.

=== March ===

* March 15 - [[Fernando Meliazio]] flees [[Denver]] to his hidden bunker at the urging of his staff.
* March 26 - [[General-Commander of the Alliance|General-Commander]] [[Ken Hurke]] is killed while fighting a running retreat into the Ontario region of [[Earth]].
* March 28 - The last organized ground forces of the [[United Earth Alliance]] are defeated by the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. In a panic, hundreds of the politicians and military leaders who had been hiding out in bunkers across the globe began to flee. [[Earth]] practically bled fleeing spaceships.
* March 29 - Forty-three starliners, freighters, and other small craft are captured or destroyed by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] as they try to flee the [[Sol System]].
* March 30 - Sixty ranking military officers of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] are captured as they try to flee [[Earth]]. The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s blockade tightens the noose around the planet, and no more ships escape.  [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] [[Cephrod Class Frigate|frigates]] monitoring the conflict from [[Subspace]] withdraw from the system and restrict further observation.
* March 31 - [[Lucius Black]] claims [[Terra]] and the [[Sol System]] as the legal soverign territory of the [[Onyx Empire]], and officially moves his headquarters from [[Ah Kin]] in the [[Alpha Centauri System]] to [[Earth]]. [[Lucius Black]] orders the expansion of the [[Onyx Empire]], and to root out every last trace of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

=== April ===

* April 6 - The first elements of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s invasion fleet enter the [[Lyrae System]]
* April 7 - [[Jean Sarnou]] is given command of all [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] assets in the [[Lyrae System]].
* April 8 - The [[Jerome Black (BG-1)|HMS ''Jerome Black'']] arrives in the [[Lyrae System]]. [[Anthony Hadrian]] offers to parley with [[Jean Sarnou]].
* April 9 - [[Jean Sarnou]] comes aboard the [[Jerome Black (BG-1)|HMS ''Jerome Black'']] to parley with [[Anthony Hadrian]]. [[Robert Marshall]] is sent aboard the [[Bonaparte (CG-346)|UEAS ''Bonaparte'']] as a hostage until the meeting is completed. [[Anthony Hadrian]] demands the surrender of the [[Lyrae System]], but offers the [[United Earth Alliance]] officials on the planet safe passage out of the [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] if they surrender peacefully, and promises a peaceful transition to the [[Government of the Onyx Empire|Imperial government]] like the one on [[Ah Kin]], rather than the bloody takeover of [[Terra]]. [[Jean Sarnou|Sarnou]] promises to deliver the offer to the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government on [[Holt]].
* April 10 - [[Alain Fournier]], the head of the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government on [[Holt]], refuses to accept [[Anthony Hadrian]]'s ultimatum. [[Alain Fournier|Fournier]] sends messages to the [[Titan System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], and [[Theta Algedi System]], hoping to garner enough support to become the de-facto President of the [[United Earth Alliance]] and launch a counter-offensive against the [[Onyx Empire]].
* April 11 - [[Royal Imperial Army]] elements on [[Earth]] discover the location of [[United Earth Alliance]] [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] [[Fernando Meliazio]]'s hidden bunker. A strike force enters and wipes out the bunker.
* April 12 - The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] sets up a supply base in orbit of [[Alsace]] in the [[Lyrae System]], and [[Royal Imperial Army]] elements begin to move in for the invasion of [[Holt]] itself. [[Anthony Hadrian]] sends a second demand of surrender, setting a deadline of noon the following day.
* April 13 - With no reply from [[Holt]], the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] attacks the fleet elements of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] under the command of [[Jean Sarnou]]. The first battle takes place in orbit of [[Aquitaine]], where half of [[Jean Sarnou]]'s ships are destroyed or captured. [[Jean Sarnou|Sarnou]] fights a running battle back to [[Holt]].
* April 14 - Unknown to [[Jean Sarnou]], [[Anthony Hadrian]] has planned his defeat in advance. [[Royal Imperial Navy]] assets were hidden in low orbit of [[Auvergne]], their drive signatures masked by the planet's harsh and volatile atmosphere. Sarnou finds his return vector into [[Holt]] orbit in the direct path of [[Empire|Imperial]] ships led by [[Oswald Voss]]. In a fierce three-hour battle, the cornered [[Jean Sarnou]] destroys fifteen [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] before his fleet is destroyed or captured. Rather than risk capture, [[Jean Sarnou|Sarnou]] rides the [[Bonaparte (CG-346)|UEAS ''Bonaparte'']] down to the oceans of [[Holt]], and ditches it in the high seas. It breaks up and sinks on impact, but [[Jean Sarnou|Sarnou]] and most of his crew make it to escape pods on the way down.
* April 15 - [[Royal Imperial Army]] elements under the command of [[Cecil Au-Yueng]] land on [[Holt]] and are met by [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] ground assets under the command of [[Brennan Connor]].
* April 16 - Three ships under the command of [[Ramesh Jethro]] are left in orbit of [[Holt]], while the rest of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] assets in the [[Lyrae System]] withdraw to pursue other targets.

=== May ===

* May 7 - The [[Onyx Empire]]'s capital is moved to [[Memphis]] on [[Earth]].
* May 12 - The [[Obsidian Palace]]'s foundation is laid.

=== August ===

* August 7 - [[Robert Marshall]]'s [[Royal Imperial Navy]] forces invaded the [[Tejat]] Colony. [[Tejat]] was farther from [[Terra]] than any colony other than the [[Titan System]], and their fear of what might be in the unknown regions of space was still moderately strong. The Tejat defense forces were also still strong, and it took many bloody battles before Admiral [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] defeated them.
* August 29 - [[Tejat]] colony leaders surrendered the planet to [[Lucius Black]] and swore fealty to him as Emperor.

=== September ===

=== October ===

The [[Proxima Procyon System]] colony had stronger ties to the [[Alpha Centauri System]] than to [[Terra]]. They relied more on their sister colony for economical wealth and political power than they did [[Terra]]. The Bethijan leaders welcomed the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] fleets with open arms after seeing the loyalty of the [[Alpha Centauri]] colony. [[Bethija]] was the perfect staging point for an invasion of the final [[United Earth Alliance]] colony, the [[Titan System]].

=== December ===

* December 3 - [[United Earth Alliance]] observatories in the [[Titan System]] begin to detect [[hyperspace]] jumps near the outer planets of the system.
* December 5 - The [[United Earth Alliance]]'s 17th Space Battle Group is deployed to [[Mnemosyne]] to plan a defense of the [[Titan System]]'s outer planets.
* December 7 - Elements of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy#Fourth Imperial Fleet|Fourth Imperial Fleet]] begin harassing [[United Earth Alliance]] positions in the [[Titan System]].
* December 12 - The [[First Battle of Cronus]] begins.
* December 15 - The [[United Earth Alliance]]'s surviving forces retreat from [[Cronus]], ending the [[First Battle of Cronus]].
* December 16 - [[Royal Imperial Army]] forces land on [[Cronus]], and begin establishing the [[Onyx Empire]]'s first permanent base in the [[Titan System]].

Aside from its Sappers, Blazton Industries also employs a large number of what are dubbed Trailblazers--light scout and reconnaissance troopers.  

Recruited from the same soldiers as Sappers, Trailblazers operate as highly mobile infantry, their tactics modeled after the Mesarthrim Myt'Kampfen.  Like the Myt'Kampfen, they rarely carry anything more than an unpowered armored vest, and an armored helmet.  The helmet is the same type as carried by Sappers. 

[[Image:TrailblazersofBlazton.jpg]]

Blazton's Trailblazers carry the same general issue Blazton Model 91 Main Battle Rifle as the Sappers, and have similar capabilities in terms of variable munitions.  Also like Sappers, Trailblazers carry a Blazton Model 08 Pistol.  These weapons can be supplemented by anti-armor weaponry, grenades, and various other infantry field weapons. 

Sappers are the standard infantry of the Blazton Security Forces, but there is also a large contingent of Trailblazers.  They rarely operate actively, as Blazton Industries is not a warring nation, but rather a neutral corporation.  In theory, they operate forward of other Blazton units in defense of Blazton interests.  Their hit-and-fade tactics, derived from the Mesarthrim, would be used to keep hostile forces from closing on Blazton facilities.  They usually travel about in Targe hovercraft, which can transport them into and out of dangerous areas quickly.

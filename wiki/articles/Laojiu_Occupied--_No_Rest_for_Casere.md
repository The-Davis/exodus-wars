Set after the Fall of Laojiu.


by Seth Dickinson

=====Daegu, Casere, Laojiu District - Occupied by the Mesarthrim - 0600 Hours=====
     The Mesarthrim occupation force on Casere have set up their headquarters in the city of Daegu. Grim-faced Mesarthrim soldiers patrol the roof of the monolithic stone building that was once the city hall. The streets are deserted and silent. Children’s eyes watch fearfully from gaps in shuttered windows as United Centusi States tanks rumble down the streets.
     Suddenly, explosions break the silence. An APC in front of the city hall explodes, blown apart by an improvised bomb. Rifle shots ring out, and several of the soldiers on the building roof fall.
     A rag-tag band of Prefecture citizens emerge from nearby alleys and rooftops, rushing up the front steps of the city hall and smashing in the gates. All across the city, small bands of armed citizens throw Molotov cocktails at passing tanks, or fire on Mesarthrim patrols. The ‘laid-back’ citizens of Laojiu are taking matters into their own hands.

=====United Centusi States Occupation Headquarters - Daegu - 0625 Hours=====
     Major Bissell, the local Casere commander, is in conference with Admiral Dietrich when Mesarthrim Myt'herra burst into the room.
     “Sir, a band of armed citizens has broken into the front rooms,” the Lieutenant in command reports crisply. “We are receiving reports of attacks and bombings all across the city. We recommend that you retreat to your shuttle immediately.” 
     Dietrich's image on the communicator frowns at the news, his dark eyes troubled at the thought of the upcoming bloodshed.
     “No, I believe I will be staying. We expected something like this to occur – but not so soon, or so well organized.”
     Admiral Dietrich looks at Bissell, his face stony and unreadable. “Are your men prepared to quash a revolt?
     “With all necessary force,” Bissell replies, not without relish. “I can tell you, s ir, that these people are hateful. They don’t even think we’re humans – all we are to these ignorant backcountry farmers are ‘white-eyes.’” 
     Dietrich shakes his head and sighs. “Major, I suggest that you take precautions to minimize casualties. It may not be in our orders – and may not even be standard operating procedure – but I am not willing to slaughter civilians, no matter how well-armed they are.”
     Bissell scowls darkly, his upper lip barely curling up with rage. “Sir, with all due respect, this revolt could become extremely difficult to contain if we do not smash it immediately! I suggest we deploy battle armor to all the crisis zones and sweep the whole city with infantry. We’ll declare a curfew and kill anyone outside of their homes.”
     Admiral Dietrich's slammed fist echoes through the communicator. “Major Bissell, if I hear one hint of any kind of massacre in the streets I will relieve you of command! These people are not warriors, and they are not our prisoners! You will treat them as you would a riot back on Mesar, up to and until the moment they pose a critical threat to our occupation! We may be bloody in war but I will not have the Mesarthrim known as common butchers! That is final!”

Busan, Casere - Laojiu District - 0700 Hours
     Even as the situation in Daegu escalates, a Mesarthrim armored division sent from Osarwa to fortify the outlying city of Busan finally reaches its objective. A long night of difficult travel through treacherous mountain terrain finds them only a few kilometers outside the city.
     Despite Casere's official surrender after the capture of Governor Ju Tao, the city perimeter is defended by nearly a full division of citizen militia. The Mesarthrim not only have armor and technological superiority, but they also have control of the skies. Savage Talon commando platoons slip in ahead of the main force, unseen by the Prefecture sentries.
     Nearly a full brigade of militia infantry guard the main highway leading out of the mountains and into Busan. A series of sharp hills protect their western flank, but those same hills are also a fatal weakness. A five-man commando fire team crouches on the nearest hilltop, transmitting coordinates to the UCS fighters waiting above.
     One of the commandoes unfolds a compact targeting laser and trains it on the center of the militia formation. The beam is tuned to the infrared spectrum, so no telltale visible dot appears as he designates the target.
     A squadron of Jaguar bombers swoops down from orbit, unloading their missiles in a steep dive and then pulling away sharply to avoid a retaliatory SAM salvo. The bombs plummet through the atmosphere, locking onto the targeting laser like bloodhounds after a scent. The warheads impact at staggering velocities, some detonating when they hit the ground and others bursting in the air. A chain of explosions walks down the length of the militia line like Prometheus’ footsteps.
     The fireballs incinerate screaming infantrymen, drawing grotesque shadows on the pavement. The militia brigade goes from combat-ready to routed in seconds.
     The UCS Armored Cavalry division cruises through the gap, halting at the city perimeter. Infantry platoons dismount from their APCs and begin sweeping into the city. After Canopus, the UCS isn’t going to make the mistake of bringing armor into a close urban environment.
     Vicious house-to-house fighting breaks out as the lead UCS platoon runs into militia resistance. Dashing from corner to corner, fighting for every street, alley, and floor, the Mesarthrim infantry contend with snipers, car bombs, and explosive traps. Casualties are heavy. As the sun reaches its zenith, two Mesarthrim platoons corner a militia heavy-weapons squad in Rosenblad Park – only to be drawn into an ambush and subjected to the vicious overlapping crossfire of a dozen light machine guns mounted on nearby rooftops. 
     None of the Mesarthrim escape alive, but the militia gunners make a foolish mistake – concentrating so hard on the victims of their ambush that they miss the fire teams sneaking up behind them. The fallen Mesarthrim are swiftly avenged as the UCS soldiers attack with grenades, blazer fire, and ferocious war cries.
     With the Rosenblad Park resistance gone, the UCS has a foothold in the central city; they establish a central rally point and begin sending out sweeping infantry patrols, supplemented by battle armor. By sundown, most of the city is clear, and the militia survivors are in full retreat. The UCS swiftly takes control.

=====UCS Headquarters - Daegu - 1300 Hours=====
     Blood stains the entrance chamber of UCS Headquarters, formerly City Hall. The bodies of several insurgents lie rotting in the atrium as Admiral Dietrich, shuttled in to observe the damage, steps out into the street, squinting against the glaring sun.
     A Dolch LAV rumbles down the street and screeches to a halt in front of him. Major Bissell vaults out and salutes, his battle armor dusty but blood-free. “Admiral! I am happy to report that the revolt appears to have been contained with relatively little bloodshed. Our patrols are moving about freely, and the civilians are all obeying the curfew – there’s not a sign of life in the streets. Your idea worked brilliantly.”
     “Yes,” Dietrich murmurs, watching a squad of Mesarthrim soldiers kick in the door of a nearby building and rush in, riot shields held ready. “It’s a shame it had to come to taking hostages…we’re walking down a dangerous road.”
     Bissell is grinning ear-to-ear, and Dietrich restrains an urge to hit his subordinate as the Major continues enthusing about the effectiveness of his plan. “It was a stroke of genius, sir. Our ‘bloody reputation’ worked to our advantage. All we had to do was threaten to blow up a few apartment buildings, and the insurgents turned themselves in or went home. There’s no way they would let us kill their families.”
     “Would we have done it, though?” Dietrich sighs. “Could we have? Major, don’t answer that question. I’m not sure I really want to know.”
     The Admiral climbs into the LAV, looking like a weary old man. “Driver, take me to the spaceport. Major, you have command. Keep order as best as you can.”
     As Dietrich’s vehicle peels away in a shower of dust, Bissell watches it go with cold eyes. He spits into the dirt as the vehicle turns a corner and vanishes. “That man doesn’t have the stomach for war,” the scarred Mesarthrim growls. “I’m surprised his eyes are even white.” Turning, he stomps back up the stairs and into his headquarters. He doesn’t even glance at the dead bodies in the entryway.

'''Hal Stewart'''
*Born: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Hal Stewart'' (born January 1, [[2300]]) is less experienced than other Legion Corps Generals, but he is quite competent. His Legion, the [[Legion of the Dragon]] was named for his legendary foul tongue, which could supposedly "scorch the paint off a Rig".

==The Dragon==

Hal Stewart was born into and raised by the [[Empire]], and served loyally through many brutal campaigns. During the [[Imperial Civil War]], he commanded a garrison as a Colonel on [[Kalidasa]]. When the Kalidasans began to revolt, Stewart coordinated with the [[Prefecture]] to assist the Kalidasans and thwart any Imperial effort to suppress the rebellion.

The last Legion mustered before [[2325]] was Hal Stewart's [[Legion of the Dragon]].

General Stewart is less experienced than the other Legion Corps Generals, but he is quite competent. He and his second-in-command, [[Carla Diez]], split the command responsibilities more evenly than most Lieutenant Generals and Brigadiers.

The Dragon Legion's tactics are the best for coordinating air and ground forces. The troops are already familiar with sighting targets for VTOLs, so lining up guidance for Air Force Bomber Squadrons is easy. The best spotters and snipers are in the Legion of the Dragon.

== Events of 68BC ==

Population pressures on [[Glengarr]] result in the establishment of a number of colony cities on [[Archada]] and [[Dellada]], the first of which is founded in 68BC on Archada.  Named [[Iridi Archada]], the massive domed city was actually a colony of the [[Iridi]] [[Tyreani]].  Only a few months later, the other major [[Iridi]] established their own colony cities on the two moons.

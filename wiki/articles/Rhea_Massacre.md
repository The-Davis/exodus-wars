On April 18, [[2321]], one of the worst atrocities in the [[Second Exodus War]] was committed by the forces of the [[Onyx Empire]].  Their attempted invasion of [[Rhea]] failed, and in retaliation the ships of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] launched a full-scale orbital bombardment of the planet's surface.  The attack killed over two hundred thousand civillians.  The hidden cities of [[Kabila Kimburu]] went unnoticed and unharmed, and the timely aid from the [[Ngrligru]] in the oceans kept the death toll lower than it would have been otherwise.

== Alliance Retaliation ==

On April 25, [[2321]], the [[United Earth Alliance]] sent a retaliatory force to Rhea in the form of several fighter and bomber squadrons.  The pilots were ordered to attack with extreme prejudice.  The Imperial forces had already been given the order to withdraw, and only six [[Vigilant Class Frigate]]s remained.  The frigates had been ordered to shoot down any relief missions.

The Alliance fighter squadrons managed to destroy all but one of the frigates, which withdrew to [[Cronus]] with a report of the lost ships.

'''Australia''' was an island nation in the south Pacific ocean. It was founded as a penal colony by the [[British Empire (Terra)|British]], but was made an independent nation in the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]]. As part of the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth]], Australia was one of the founders of the [[United Earth Alliance]] and signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter|Charter]] in [[2011]].

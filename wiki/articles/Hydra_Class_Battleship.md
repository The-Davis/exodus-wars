In early [[2320]], Emperor [[Lucius Black]] personally ordered the design and commissioning of a new class of battleships.  The design was meant to be the symbol of the power and authority of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and would completely outclass the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s [[ADF-6000 Class Battleship|ADF-6000]] class--which was nearly twice the size of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]].  The new battleship would lead the assault on the [[Titan System]] when the Empire renewed its offensive.

== Design ==

[[Image:Hydra Class Battleship Front View.jpg|150px|left|thumb|A view of the Hydra's main batteries.]]

The design--designated the Lucius Class Battleship--was plagued with setbacks from the moment the first blueprints were drafted.  Most problems were due to the Emperor's constant personal involvement.  Lucius Black wanted the best possible ship with the best features available, and often went to the design teams with outrageous new demands and new requirements.  As soon as new technologies were developed, Black demanded the battleship go back to specifications with the newly available systems.  When the [[Mesarthrim]] were encountered in [[2322]], Black ordered expensive and difficult modifications intended to allow the battleship to effectively engage gunships and counter the close-in style of Mesarthrim warship combat.  

In the end, Lucius Black was presented with what is still considered to be one of the most modern, advanced, and powerful warships to come out of a Terran shipyard.  The only problem was that it was already June of 2322.  The [[Prefecture]] was in open rebellion and [[Earth]] was under seige--time was running out for the Empire.

With the design ready for production, the Emperor ordered the immediate construction of ten ships.  Only three keels were laid down at the [[Haven Shipyards]] facilities in the [[Lupei System]] before the Mesarthrim offensive liberated [[Kalidasa]].

== Deployment History ==

[[Image:Hydra Class Battleship Side View.jpg|200px]]

After the [[Second Exodus War]], the three incomplete keels, and more importantly the design plans for the ships, were largely responsible for the diplomatic tension between the Kalidasans and the Prefecture. While a number of unfinished warships, including Atlas, Dauntless, and Vigilant classes, were desired by the Prefecture, [[Anthony Hadrian]] and the rest of the Prefecture Admiralty particularly wanted the vessels that they had waited so many years for.

Instead, the jealous Prefecture commanders watched while the keels sat incomplete until [[2325]], when the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]] finally secured the resources for the completion of the vessels, now rechristened as the Hydra class.

[[Image:Hydra Class Battleship Top View.jpg|200px]]

None of the three ships in the Kalidasa Navy have seen combat, but have done extremely well in war games with the rest of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].  The ships take part in the sovereignty patrols that maintain their control over their borders.

During the [[Oronus Incident]], the [[KNS Liberation|KNS ''Liberation'']] was preparing for deployment to the volatile [[Oronus System]] to assist in breaking the blockade, but the timely intervention of the [[Ixoarchada]] ended the crisis before the ''Liberation'' left port.

== Future Plans ==

The Hydra Class Battleship is considered both a technological success and a political bargaining chip.  The Kalidasans know the Prefecture want it, and know that they can barter for a share of the Prefecture's wealth in exchange for the plans to the ship.  So far, Kalidasan leadership has felt that keeping the design a state secret is more important than any potential monetary gains from selling it, despite a standing offer of 540 billion [[Peculium]]s from the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]

'''Joe Berman'''
*Place of Birth: [[Bethija]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Joe Berman'' is an officer in the [[Legion of the Phoenix]], serving under General [[Travis Carpenter]]. He was a veteran commander for the [[United Earth Alliance]], retired and living on his native [[Bethija]] when the [[Empire]] invaded during the [[Second Exodus War]].

When [[Bethija]] defected to the [[Empire]], Berman was recalled into service by the planetary government, and ordered to join the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. Berman followed orders, but had no loyalty to the [[Empire]] and immediately joined the rebel [[Prefecture]] when they made themselves known.

Berman is known for his level head, and his willingness to give his frank opinion of a tactical situation to his superiors.

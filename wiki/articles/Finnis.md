'''Finnis''' is a [[Sothark]] word meaning "protector" or "guardian."  There is no exact English translation, but the word came from the first bodyguards of the Kejsare during the wars with the now-extinct sea beasts of [[Junheim]].

During the war, [[Raegos Direnson]] went into battle wearing the [[Reigenjord]].  He was nearly unstoppable, but often fought his way so far into the enemy that his unaugmented soldiers could not follow.  The war-smiths of [[Rolsucht]] crafted a new form of combat armor which was able to fight alongside the ancient [[Anthronoris]] relic.

Unlike the Reigenjord, which was capable of limited flight and a running speed nearly thirty times faster than an unaugmented man, the new armor was only a dozen times faster than the average human.

The first ten suits were given to Raegos' most trusted men, and they were able to fight with him in the war that wiped out the predatory sea beasts.

== Modern Use ==

Reigen technology has advanced over the hundreds of years since Raegos Direnson's time.  The armor used by the Finnis is available in the hundreds of thousands, and is so simple to use that it only takes two weeks of orientation to learn to fight with it.  After their basic training, all Reigen recruits wear the armor for advanced infantry training.

The armor is power-assisted, and the operator can carry and use a crew-served weapon by himself.  The standard-issue weapon is a Lansvapen rifle, which is light enough to be used even if the armor's power source fails in battle.  The standard rifle has over 1000 rounds of ammunition on a standard charge, but it can be recharged by the suit's power source three times before the suit's power cells are drained.

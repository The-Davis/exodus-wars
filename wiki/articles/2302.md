== Events of 2302 ==

=== January ===
* January 7: The first [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|YF-4]] prototypes are flown as part of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s "Project Raven".

=== February ===
* February 10 - The [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] is completed and launched, marking the first successful construction of a [[:Category:Warships|warship]] by the [[Onyx Empire]].
* February 15 - The [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] is delivered to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] and is commissioned the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']].
* February 18 - The hull of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s first [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]], [[HMS Atlas|CV-1]], is laid down at the [[Capricorn Haven|''Capricorn Haven'']] shipyard in the [[Alpha Centauri System]].

=== March ===

* March 3 - [[Eckhard Tischler]] is captured by [[Mesarnak]] who are [[Anderung Loyalist]]s. Over thirty [[Mesarnak]] are killed in the process of subduing [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] and killing his four [[Mesarthrim]] bodyguards.
* March 11 - [[Mesarthrim]] commanded by [[Arnold Stadt]] attempt to rescue [[Eckhard Tischler]] from the [[Anderung Loyalist]] compound. Their raid is a success, but [[Eckhard Tischler|Eckhard]] was moved to the [[Anderung]] stronghold in [[Sigiswald Cathedral]] four hours prior to the raid's launch.
* March 18 - [[Eckhard Tischler]] is tortured to death on the altar at the [[Sigiswald Cathedral]] by [[Anderung Loyalist]]s.  [[Andreas Tischler]] becomes [[Clan Lord]] of the [[Tischler Clan]] and [[Uber-General]] of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

=== April ===

* April 11 - In his first act as [[Uber-General]], [[Andreas Tischler]] attacks the [[Anderung]] stronghold at [[Sigiswald Cathedral]] and razes it to the ground. [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas]] personally puts the torch to the execution pyre of [[Yaron Grael]], the [[Anderung]] warlord in charge of the region.

The government of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] is a collective assembly of constitutional stellar governments. It was modeled after the government of the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Each stellar government has its own executive, legislative and judiciary branches. The governments meet in an assembly that convenes on a regular basis.

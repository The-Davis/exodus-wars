'''Josiah MacArthur Hlow'''
*Place of Birth: [[Mars]]
*Allegiance: None

''Josiah MacArthur Hlow'' is a rather eccentric inventor and geneticist. He has lived since the mid-21st Century by the use of Cold Sleep. He invented the colony ships used in the [[First Exodus]], and a myriad of other inventions in the medical field still used today.

==Eccentric Genius==

Josiah MacArthur Hlow, best known as "Professor" Hlow, is probably the most intelligent man who ever lived.  He refuses to be tested for the purpose of measuring him against others, but his IQ and EQ levels are obviously very high.

While Hlow's brainpower is fully used 99% of the time, the rest of the time he either seems completely in a daze, babbles about nonsensical or unimportant topics, or incapable of focusing on anything.  His quirky and unpredictable change comes from what he calls a higher brain shutdown, an unconscious action he takes when he's been taxing his brain on a particularly difficult project.  When this happens, Hlow retreats into his garden, or goes fishing.  Most people close to him worry when he has one of his episodes, but Hlow has a clean mental and physical bill of health.

Hlow's creations in the field of military application are excellent.  He has single-handedly revolutionized Terran influence on the galaxy by creating gravity manipulating technology, such as gravity shields and anti-gravity lifters.  His true passion is medical science.

Hlow's greatest accomplishment in medicine is the cell growth and division enzyme he developed while working at the [[Schweitzer Institute of Medicine]].  This enzyme made growth of new organs and generation of blood a day-to-day occurrence, eliminating the organ transplant field of medicine entirely.

Professor Hlow prides himself on using natural means for medicine.  He was the only one capable of devising a compatible non-human antibody to the 100%-fatal [[Apöt Virus]], and continues to develop cures to diseases using purely biological methods.

Hlow is the only human alive who has seen the [[Gorhamut]] species.  They helped him in both of his escapes from the Emperor in the past, and he guards the secret of their hiding place closely.

Today, Hlow is retired from military development, but still works on medicine, and occasionally dabbles in new computer science projects with his son [[Benjamin Hlow]].

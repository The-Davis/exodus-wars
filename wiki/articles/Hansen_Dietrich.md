''Hansen Dietrich'' was an Admiral and Ambassador of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and later the [[United Centusi States]]. Hansen joined the Kreigsmarine in [[2273]] as a Rear-Lieutenant. In [[2323]], Dietrich was selected as the first ambassador to the [[Alliance of Planets]]. In [[2329]] the [[Alliance of Planets]] named Dietrich guilty of ordering [[Omega Bomb|Omega]] strikes on [[Pesht]]. Dietrich was killed aboard his flagship, [[Die Gerechtigkeit des Herrn Williams]] in [[2334]], by a firing squad.

==Service Record==
===Anderung War===
Dietrich started his naval career as a gunnery officer on the [[Horn des Herrn]], graduating a year early from Officers School. The Horn des Herrn was apart of the Battle of White Star Crossing, during the fight Dietrich took command of an entire battery control when the Battery-Lieutenant froze under the pressure. After the battle Dietrich was promoted to Deck Captain, skipping a pair of ranks, and was transferred off the Horn des Herrn.

As Deck Captain, Dietrich began working in the recently constructed Raumstraff Island. It was during this time that Dietrich earned his Flight Wings, and was promoted to Major. Dietrich took command of the [[5th Flight Group]] as Operations Officer. In [[2298]], Dietrich flew a bombing run on a [[Morningstar]] during the [[Last Stand of Raumstraff]]. Dietrich's bomber was destroyed after delivering its payload, killing his gunners. Dietrich and his co-pilot survived, but Dietrich lost his right leg after the wounded leg went untreated for almost 12 hours.

Following the battle, Dietrich took a position as Post Major of Raumstraff Islands Kreigsmarine Command and Control Department. Dietrich oversaw the assault on the Hoffmann Repair Yards, and its subsequent defense.  In recognition, Dietrich was promoted to Post Commandant over the Repair Yards.

===Second Exodus War===
After first contact with the Onyx Empire, Dietrich was plucked from his position as Post Commandant and promoted to Fleet Commandant to head up the new Third Fleet. Every available ship that was being built to replace or expand the current Kreigsmarines was intercepted and compressed into a tiny fleet of powerful new vessels. They were sent on a mission to strike at the heart of the Imperial manufacturing, known as [[Operation Tiger Eye]]. The battle was a blood bath, with the entire [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy#Fourth Imperial Fleet|Fourth Imperial Fleet]] defending the planet. Neither commander wanted to retreat, but in the end, the numerical advantage of the Imperial ships over powered the technologically superior Mesarthrim. As a final desperate measure, Dietrich ordered a bomber group to drop high yield nukes on the shipyards, surface mines, and any other industrial targets civilian or military. The bombers were destroyed to the last man, but not before heavily destroying the Ah Kin's infrastructure. Historians consider the battle to be an Imperial Victory, but the Alliance of Planets declared Dietrich a hero for his fearless raid. Ironically, it was this raid that the Alliance of Planets used seven years later to prove Dietrich was capable of ordering weapons of mass destruction to be used on civilian targets.

Following the raid, [[Andreas Tischler]] appointed the Hero of Ah Kin as ambassador to the Alliance of Planets.

===Mesarthrim Civil War===
Dietrich was absent for the vast majority of the Civil War, spending most of his time as ambassador trying to convince the Titan Planetary Alliance into assisting the United Mesarthrim Clans. Finally, Dietrich abandoned his diplomatic post after hearing nothing but silence for months.

Upon arriving in the Centus system, his shuttle was intercepted by the [[United Centusi States]], who had just been recognized as the legitimate successor to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. [[Elias Faust]] informed Dietrich that he was being immediately recommissioned in the Centusi Kreigsmarine as a Fleet Admiral commanding the new Third Fleet.

===Tempest War===
Almost instantly, Dietrich became privy to the plans of taking territory back from the Prefecture. And Dietrich became a valuable resource regarding the current political climate. In the end, it was Dietrich's decision that selected [[Leskovik]] as the target for the opening strikes. His plans worked flawlessly, and when he countered the Prefecture's claims of treachery, most Alliance Ambassadors gave pause. Dietrich played the diplomatic game as long as possible, but was eventually forced to retire when it was discovered that the Prefecture planned to assassinate him.

Upon returning to his fleet, Dietrich moved out with the Third Fleet, playing a vital role supporting ground invasions. Dietrich was the first Mesarthrim Admiral to break the Alliance Treaty regarding bombarding planets in [[2328]] by clearing troubled regions with starship mounted blazers. Dietrich was also well known for authorizing the use of tactical nuclear weapons at the outset of most engagements. In [[2329]] Pesht was destroyed by Omega weaponry from his arsenal. Dietrich claimed to have never issued the order, and there is no clear record of who did order the strike - but in the end, the planet was broken apart and billions of civilians and military personnel were killed in an instant.

Dispite the accident, Chancellor Camp allowed Dietrich to remain in command of Third Fleet. After the news reached Dietrich that he was wanted by interstellar court, his tactics began to take a less aggressive tone, even to the point where Dietrich over-ruled a direct order from Chancellor Camp to glass [[Adelson]] in [[2333]]. When Third Fleet was redirected to the [[Xi Herculis]] system to ensure it wouldn't fall to the Prefecture, Dietrich did not authorize the use of tactical weapons. During the battle to hold [[Crofton]], Commandant Hans Krueger von Tischler broke authorization, and engaged the superior Prefecture fleet with an Omega bomb. Dietrich ordered him arrested by the security detail on his ship - an order which was denied. Once the battle was over, Dietrich ordered Commandant Krueger and the disobedient security detail arrested, tried, and hung by his own security forces. On January 1st, 2334 the execution was carried out and their bodies were hung in the main hanger on the Die Gerechtigkeit des Herrn Williams.

After news of this incident reached Mesar, Chancellor Camp ordered Third Fleet home. Dietrich began organizing a rear guard to hold the system, moving crews around, prepping ground defenses, and laying space mines. By the time they were ready to leave, most of Dietrich's crew were crying out his cowardice. A mutiny was staged, and Dietrich had less then a hundred crewmen stay openly loyal to him. He was captured, and a mock trial was held. Found guilty of cowardice in a time of war, he was sentenced to death by firing squad, which was carried out three days later. The surviving crew that supported Dietrich were hung from the same mountings in the main hanger on the Die Gerechtigkeit des Herrn Williams where Krueger and his men died.

==Legacy==
Following his death, Dietrich's body was stored in the morgue for transfer back to Mesar - but was lost when the fleet was attacked five days later and annihilated by a combined Auellal-Ikronin Armada. Arnold Stadt recognized the life and efforts of Dietrich in a memorial in the Hoffmann Repair Yards. The memorial makes only brief mention of his service in the Tempest War, but highlights his career against the Anderung, Onyx, and his time as Commandant of Hoffmann.

The Mesarthrim Federation Dritte Friedenswächter have a fleet carrier named the Dietrich to honor the first, and last, Admiral of the previous Dritte Flotte.

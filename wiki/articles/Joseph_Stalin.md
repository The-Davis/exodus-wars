'''Josef Vissarionovich Dzhugashvili'''
*Born: December 18, [[1878]]
*Died: March 5, [[1953]]
*Place of Birth: Gori, Russia, [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]]

''Josef Vissarionovich Dzhugashvili'' (born December 18, [[1878]]), known by his chosen name of Joseph Stalin, was General Secretary of the [[Communist]] Party of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]]'s Central Committee from [[1922]] until his death in [[1953]]. Despite his formal position being originally without significant influence, and his office being nominally but one of several Central Committee Secretariats, Stalin's increasing control of the Party from [[1928]] onwards led to his becoming the de facto party leader and the Premier of his country; a position which enabled him to take full control of the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] and its people.

Under Stalin's leadership, the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] played a decisive role in the defeat of Nazi [[Germany (Terra)| Germany]] in the [[World War II|Second World War]] and went on to achieve the status of superpower. His programs of industrialization and collectivization in the 1930s, along with his ongoing campaigns of political repression, are estimated to have cost the lives of up to 50 million people.

*Powerplant: BzF-90g Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 180 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament:
**4 x "Stalwart" Surface-to-Air Missiles
**-or-
**4 x "Trebuchet" Surface-to-Surface Missiles
**2 x Medium Neutron Beam Cannons
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

One of Blazton Industries "Hydra" units, the Skywatcher utilizes a base Hydra hovercraft module fitted with the Skywatcher missile artillery module.  The result is a fast hovercraft that carries four powerful missiles capable of destroying even the heaviest hostile craft. 

[[Image:SkywatcherAntiAirHovercraft1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

With its base Hydra module, the Skywatcher is equipped with a pair of Medium Neutron Beam Cannons, effective against any light or medium craft that may be able to catch the speedy hovercraft.  The rear Skywatcher section has four heavy hardpoints, outfitted with either Stalwart SAMs, which can knock even heavy bombers out of the sky, or Trebuchet SSMs, capable of destroying assault vehicles from beyond the horizon. 

[[Image:SkywatcherAntiAirHovercraft2.jpg]]

Like all Hydra units, the Skywatcher utilizes a Kalidasan hover system, and is quite fast.  In addition to its speed, it is outfitted with Terran-standard defenses. 

===Deployment===

The new Hydra modular designs have only begun to be produced for non BSF purchasers, and already there have been a number of orders from various firms, paramilitaries, and even civilian defense forces of Terran nations.  In the Blazton Security Forces, Skywatchers operate exclusively as mobile defense stations, able to launch their missiles on hostile targets on over-the-horizon trajectories.

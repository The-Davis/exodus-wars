''Dirken Huern'' (born June 11, [[2261]]), is the [[Prekohr]] of the [[Lorithos]]. Unlike his ancestors, Dirken Huern was elected by a council of [[Lorithos]] to serve as [[Prekohr]], but recent shifts in [[Lorithos]] culture back to their roots has made him a de facto ruler by divine right.

== Birth ==

Dirken Huern was born at high noon to [[Kian Huern|Kian]] and [[Zak Huern]], and thus became the first "[[Lorithos Sunlit Family|Sunlit]]" heir to ascend the ancient throne of [[Lorithan]] in five generations.

== Early Life ==

Dirken's early life was spent in hiding, watching reports of his mother [[Kian Huern|Kian]]'s war against [[Torm Krelbine]]. [[Lorithan]] was already dying before Dirken was born, and he and his family suffered in squalor. If not for his father's efforts to revitalize the efforts to reach the stars, the people of [[Lorithan]] would have died out.

== Leadership ==

Dirken Huern was given the throne in [[2285]], when the [[Alliance of Planets]] assisted the people in reforming [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]]. At first, the position was elected, but the [[Lorithos]] soon repealed elections and restored the ancient regime of the [[Dawn Emperor]].

His charismatic appeal, royal blood, and natural leadership abilities have made him a very popular leader.  He is a champion for anti-nuclear, anti-biological, and anti-chemical warfare interest groups.  He got the original anti-bombardment ordinance added to the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter]], and was the only one to oppose its subsequent repeal in [[2324]].

== Marriage ==

In [[2290]], Dirken Huern was married to Arisha Dahal, a member of one of the few surviving noble families. The Lorithos people hoped that the two would produce an heir quickly and firmly establish the line of succession. Sadly, their marriage was fruitless. Despite the fact that their marriage was one of convenience and not particularly romantic, Dirken and Arisha at least liked each other, and remained together until Arisha's death in [[2324]].

== Second Exodus War ==

The [[Second Exodus War]] was a tricky political problem for Dirken Huern. He did not want to embroil his people in a war, but he knew that the Lorithos needed to be taken seriously in galactic politics. He authorized [[Sanor Cai]] to take a small group of warships to the [[Titan System]], but they did not engage in any direct action against the [[Onyx Empire]]. Instead, they provided additional defenses for [[Theia]] during the failed offensives prior to the [[Mesarthrim]] joining the war. After the [[Titan Peace Accords]] were worked out, the Lorithos withdrew from the war.

They were later involved in action against some rogue Imperial elements on [[Earth]] during the post-war relief effort, in which they played a major role.

== Line of Succession ==

When Arisha Huern died childless in 2324, Dirken Huern announced to the Lorithos that he would not re-marry. He promised that the line of the [[Prekohr]] would not end with him, and that there would be an heir. He publicly named [[Siovar Cai]], grandson of [[Sanor Cai]] as his heir, ending all speculation as to what would happen to the Lorithos government.

This decision was unpopular. Fii Cai was well liked among the Lorithos, but the idea of ending the Huern line brought some strange ideas into many members of the Lorithos race. At one point, several assassination attempts were made against Dirken Huern.

In order to re-unify the hearts and minds of the Lorithos, Dirken Huern and Siovar Cai worked together to mastermind a search for a new homeworld. Giving up on Lorithan was hard, but the hope for a new home was the perfect plan. [[Project Tehniat]] was launched in [[2327]].

== Volucris War ==

By the ''Tehniat'' had found a new homeworld for the Lorithos, it looked like they would all perish with their salvation in sight. The [[Volucris]] returned after a several-year lull in activity, and descended on Lorithan like a plague.

Dirken Huern was evacuated early on, at the insistence of Sanor Cai.  Huern lived on [[Ipsoff]] during the war, but succumbed to old age and sorrow at the losses to his people in [[2336]].  Dirken Huern's death marked the end of the [[Huern Drastar]].  Dirken was succeeded by [[Siovar Cai]].

The Mesarthrim Federation Heer is responsible for defending Mesarthrim planets, installations, and starships from hostile forces.  The Heer also serves as a support unit for the [[Mesarthrim Federation Spezialtruppen|Spezialtruppen]] while working offensively.

==History==
Unlike the bulk of early Mesarthrim Federation soldiers, the Heer were neither Comur Resistance Members, nor Auellal.  The Heer was formed out of every loyal Myt'herra soldier serving under [[Fritz Dresdner]].  Dresdner had become disillusioned with [[William Camp]], and contacted Stadt, telling him that '...if he gave them a ride, they'd go anywhere.'  Additionally, early in [[2335]], Stadt convinced [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] to release POWs who would swear fidelity to Dresdner into his Heer units.  Dresdner and his Heer were vital in the success at [[Comur]], where had they not brought in heavy artillery, and tanks - the Resistance would have cost twice the number in lives.

==Army Components==
Following the [[Tempest War]], the commanders of the new Mesarthrim Heer carefully dissected thousands of combat reports.  [[Fritz Dresdner]] insisted that the age of beasts was over, and that the highly mobile [[Auellal Guardians]] had proved a decisive opponent in the field.  At Dresdner's command, all [[Vulture Siege Tank|Vulture Siege Tanks]] were laid to rest.  This move proved controversial at first, however, the [[Cinaed Accords]] [[Armor Frame]] proved a popular replacement.

===Infantry===
Unlike any Mesarthrim Heer before, the Federation's Heer has adopted an all powered armour stance.  All soldiers that enter the battle field will be augmented.  There are three different styles of augmentation.  This decision was made as the ranks of the Heer were thinner then ever before in Mesarthrim history, so the quality of soldier needed to be increased.  Additionally, the use of the augmentation systems allow the individual soldier to be tied directly into their commander, and their Combat-Aware Entities Overwatch.
The augmented armour options are;
*[[Sty-2 Kaninchen]] - Recon/Intel Augmentation System
*[[Sty-3 Macht-Rüstung]] - Standard Combat ready Augmentation System
*[[Sty-3x Presslufthammer]] - Assault Augmentation System
The Sty-3 is the most commonly issued Augmentation System in the Heer.  Additionally, the Sty-3x requires special training due to extensive control requirements.

===Tanks and Vehicles===
The tank force in the Heer was lightened to allow the [[Ks-7 Fuchs]] to be able to deploy Heer tanks into a hot battlefield, ready to fight.  The entire Heer was formed around an idea of mobility and response, not necessarily stationary, slug fests - although Heer units can be tasked to hold their ground if need be.
The Heer has done away with the Vulture Siege Tank and replaced it with;
*[[Mm-1 Onnane]] - Medium Hovertank, formerly the Auellal [[Onnane Yisul]]
*[[Mm-3 König-Schöpfer]] - [[Cinaed Armor Frame Project|Armor Frame]] Main Battle Tank
*[[Mm-x7 Zisterne-Mörder]] - Tank Killer

===Artillery===
The Federation's Heer is the first Mesarthrim Heer to have a dedicated Artillery Arm.  The purposed of the Artillery Arm is simple - defense.  Since the rest of the Mesarthrim Heer is dedicated to fast movement, these units are meant for siege.
Their units include;
*[[Mm-5 Dunkles Pferd]] - Siege Tank
*[[Yk-2 Dachun]] - Mobile Artillery
*[[Yk-3 Gilbert]] - Mobile Anti-Aero
*[[Sty-2v Aufklärer]] - Intel/Targeting Augmentation System
*[[Mm-x7k Schanzarbeiter]] - Utility Tank, Siege Building
The Artillery division is the most diversified of the Heer units, and also is the only Arm that does not meet the high mobility requirements.  Their units are often deployed as a precaution to an endangered or threatened zone.  If units fall under siege before Artillery Arm can be set up, every effort will be made to get them into position.

===Air Cavalry===
The Air Cavalry is tasked with moving into position and securing landing zones for other elements of the Heer to move into position.  They utilise fast moving dropships, and extensive Augmentation Systems.  The Air Cavalry rely on the [[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine#Kriegsmarine Air Arm|Kriegsmarine Air Arm]] for transportation.  The Air Cavalry also utilise Drop Pods to move their soliders into position if the combat zone is too heated for a drop ship.
The Air Cavarly utilise only light vehicles when capturing drop zones.
*[[Sty-2xl Springer]] - Drop Trooper Augmentation System
*[[Sty-3.o1 Kriegsklage]] - Compact Combat ready Augmentation System
*[[Sty-1 Pferd]] - Utility Car

===Engineers===
The Engineer component is responsible for the design and construction and also the destruction of offensive, defensive and logistical structures for warfare.  The Engineers work closely with the Artillery components during a siege.  The Engineers also maintain an offensive group who are referred to as 'Demo Engineers'.
The Engineers use Augmentation Systems, Utilities, and commercial grade equipment.
*[[Sty-3 Macht-Rüstung]] - Standard Combat ready Augmentation System
*[[Sty-6 Acht]] - Engineer Armored Augmentation System
*[[Mm-x7k Schanzarbeiter]] - Utility Tank

===Transportation and Logistics===
The Transportation group deal specifically with ground based transportation.  The entire air transportation is supplied by the Kreigsmarine.
The Transportation group utilize;
*[[Sty-1 Pferd]] - Utility Car
*[[Sty-5 Ferrigut]] - Cargo Hauler
*[[Sty-5a Pershan]] - Ammo Hauler

===Maintenance===
The Maintenance group is responsible for ensuring the entirety of the Heer is maintained and stays on the move.  Anywhere the Heer maintains a base of operations, the Maintenance division comes along.

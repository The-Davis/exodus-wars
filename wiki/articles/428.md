== Events of 428AD ==

The first permanent cities are founded on [[Veratul]].  The [[Iridi]] of [[Aiurana]], [[Arcia Cora]], [[Preulasi Fona]], and [[Sorossa]] all trace their foundings to these original colony city foundings.  In the same year, [[Iridi Veratul]] is refounded on the same site as the original domed colony city was founded more than four centuries previous.

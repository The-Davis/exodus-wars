===Overview===

The Gunsniper corvette is Blazton Industires foray into the world of gunships--small, light, fast warships successfully employed by Mesarthrim naval forces.

[[Image:GunsniperClassCorvette1.jpg]]

With help from a few Mesarthrim starship designers and engineers, the Gunsniper was developed as a compact, lightly-crewed, but heavily-armed alternative to large, expensive warships for those naval security details that lack the funding for true capital vessels. 

===Capabilities===

Designed to do maximum damage in a single fast-strike, instead of digging in for a slugging match, the bulk of the Gunsniper's heavy armaments are placed in fixed-forward positions.  The primary anti-warship weaponry is comprised of a "Yari" RMD, developed by Blazton Industries for the Nakamura Navy's Odashi Destroyer.  This weapon alone takes up as much space in the small Gunsniper as all the other weapons combined.  When combined with a pair of fixed Ashur Cannons, a lone Gunsniper poses a serious threat to even large capital warships. 

Backing up a Gunsniper's heavy weapons are six Kalidasan-designed Advanced Neutron Cannons in three Fast-Tracking twin-turrets.  These weapons add to the Gunsniper's anti-warship arsenal, enough to fend off other small gunships, and also aid in long-range anti-aerospace firepower.  Three emplacements provide additional anti-aerospace cover at close range, and a pair of VOTM tubes give the Gunsniper anti-aerospace, anti-warship, and planetary-strike options. 

[[Image:GunsniperClassCorvette2.jpg]]

Small and fast as they are, Gunsnipers are still equipped with a respectable shielding system, and an above-average layer of Boron-Nitride Composite.  Crew accomodations are quite sparse due to the amount of equipment jammed into the small ship, with little more than bunkspace and a small mess hall for the crew.  For this reason, although fully capable, Gunsnipers are not suggested for long-term deployments far from ports or stations. 

===Deployment===

The Blazton Security Fleet maintains a force of fourteen Gunsnipers.  These ships operate as defense vessels for Blazton Industries convoys, production facilities, and various installations.

Six Gunsnipers are kept in the Sirius District at all times, rotating deployments with various BI facilities in the three Sirius territories, and providing in-system escort for transports and freighters.  One Gunsniper accompanies a Delta frigate in the Sol system, helping with security for Blazton reconstruction efforts there.  Five Gunsnipers operate with various Blazton supply groups, mining and production facilities, and research stations outside of the Sirius territories.  A pair of Gunsnipers also operates in the Prefecture's Ward District, usually confining operations to the Bolan, Crofton, and Des Arc systems. 

[[Image:GunsniperClassCorvette3.jpg]]

Outside of the BSF, Gunsniper sales have picked up mostly among private security firms, although non-government entities are forced to accept an alternate configuartion with an additional Ashur Cannon in place of the Yari Cannon.  A few remote colony forces have also purchased a number of the ships for defense purposes.

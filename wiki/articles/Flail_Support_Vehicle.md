*Powerplant: NDI Sr-i98 Micro-Reactor
*Speed: 105 KPH
*Armor: Prudenski Composite armor 
*Shields: Vambrace Shield Generator 
*Armament:
**2 'Gladius' PPBCs
**2 'Stiletto' PPBCs
**12 Guided-MLRS Tubes (36 missiles)
*Communications System:  Standard Titanian Communications Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Land-SPAR 

===Overview===

Based on the Chevalier APC frame, the Flail Missile/Artillery Support Craft relies on the same heavy defenses and large frame to perform its mission to clear the skies and support 

[[Image:FlailSupportVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

As a support vehicle, the Flail trades in its infantry carrying capacity for a large weapons payload.  Its primary weapons, mounted in a single turret, are a pair of 'Gladius' and a pair of 'Stiletto' Pulsed Particle Beam Weapons.  The twin Stilettos can cause havoc among infantry and light targets.  The twin Gladius cannons can punch through armor and shields of heavier vehicles.  Mounted in pairs on the main turret, the Gladius and Stiletto cannons can also be calibrated for anti-aerospace duty, proving deadly against even heavy fighters or bombers. 

The Flail's rear-section is comprised of a large Guided Multi-Launch Rocket System.  The twelve tubes, covered by an armored hatch, can fire a range of different warheads and can be adjusted for surface-to-air and surface-to-surface missiles.  Each tube has two additional reloads before the Flail must be resupplied. 

[[Image:FlailSupportVehicle2.jpg]]

Like the Chevalier, the Flail sports eight 'Sunburst/Starburst' Pods--a pair mounted at each corner of the vehicle.  As the Flail is a support vehicle, it is usually behind the main line of battle, but these pods ensure that it remains safe from enemies that close in on it. 

The Flail is outfitted with a New Dallas Industries Sr-i98 Micro-Reactor.  Like Chevalier, the Flail has a powerful Vambrace shield generator, backed up by a formidable layer of Prudenski Composite armor. 

===Deployment===

Flails operate in pairs, usually just behind the main line of battle.  From there, they direct their raking cannon fire on entrench positions, hit targets beyond site range with missiles, and keep the skies clear over friendly positions.  They may not be as glamorous as some of the faster raiders, but they are an indispensable part of the Commonwealth Army Command.

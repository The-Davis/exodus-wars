'''Johon Laakso'''
*Date of Death: April 8, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Kronheim]]
*Place of Death: [[Canopus]]
*Allegiance: [[Ikronin Jurekön]]

''Johon Laakso'' was in charge of the [[Wormhole]] Gate in the [[Canopus System]]. He was killed by the [[Mesarthrim]] [[Fritz von Sturm]] during the [[United Centusi States]] attack on [[Canopus]].

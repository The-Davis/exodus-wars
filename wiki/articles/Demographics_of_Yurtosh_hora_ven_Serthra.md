===Racial Demographics===

At the end of their civilization, the [[Mesarnak]] actually outnumbered the [[Anderung]]. Many [[Mesarnak]] were willing accomplices of their overlords, and even worshipped the [[Anderung]] as gods.

===Religion===

The [[Anderung]] worshipped the [[Gorhamut]] as their masters. They believed their role in life was to find and protect their masters from harm. They did not believe the [[Gorhamut]] were divine, but their treatment of their creators cannot be described as anything short of worship.

The willing [[Mesarnak]] among the slave-ranks worshipped the [[Anderung]], and actually believed they were gods. This cult was marked for destruction and eventually purged as a major influence by the [[Mesarthrim State Church]].

===Languages===

[[Anderung]] speak "[[Halrecht]]", a dialect created by the [[Gorhamut]]. They do not speak any other language, but some understood [[English]] and [[German]] during the [[Mesarthrim]] rebellion.

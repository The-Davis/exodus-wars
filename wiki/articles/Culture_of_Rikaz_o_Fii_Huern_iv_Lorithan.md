===Education===

[[Lorithos]] education focuses on oral recitation of history and traditions, with a minor focus on literacy and basic arithmetic in the [[Lorithos]] base-8 system. If a child shows any talent and desire to learn, they are tutored in higher mathematics, chemistry, and physics. Any child wishing to learn is apprenticed to an engineer, physicist, chemist, or other master in a field. Any child who does not genuinely desire to learn is not forced, as the [[Lorithos]] do not believe self-reliance can be taught to the unwilling.

While education is not compulsory, an uneducated laborer is little more than property to the plutocracy. Material currency is almost worthless in the [[Lorithos]] economy, so individuals cannot earn a higher place in society without genuine talent to offer to society as a whole.

===Sport===

One of the most popular sports on [[Lorithan]] before the [[Burning Wars]] was a team water sport known as Nyhlsebe (nil seh bee). It involved a three way competition of four-person teams. Brightly colored, weighted markers are sunk in water of varying depths up to 20 meters, and the teams attempt to retrieve them and return them to a marked goal area. Teams are scored on depth and weight of the markers. The sport died out after the [[Burning Wars]] turned most bodies of water on [[Lorithan]] to poisonous cesspools, but has recently been reborn on the world of [[Ipsof]].

The Lorithos have three baseball teams and one football team which compete in the interstellar team sports league. Baseball in particular is popular among [[Lorithos]] children, and there are amatuer leagues on [[Ipsof]] which are gaining planet-wide interest.

===Literature===

Literacy is very low among the [[Lorithos]] per capita, but it is a requirement for living on either of the two colonies. Among residents of the [[Kihtor]], literacy is also quite high. In the outer settlements and the inner cities of [[Lorithan]], literacy is almost nonexistant.

[[Lorithos]] literature is focused on the legends and myths of the dynasties, and of the horrors of the [[Burning Wars]]. Non-Lorithos reading most modern literature find it depressing, though some of the preserved and restored works from the [[Twilight Empress]]' reign are considered masterpieces. Many collections of oral traditions and histories have been exported outside of [[Lorithan]], and are quite popular in [[Meroniri]] libraries.

The [[Lorithos]] language is written in a cuneiform script, which was developed and introduced by the [[Dawn Emperor]]. Each symbol in the cuneiform script represents a letter. There are 28 letters in the [[Lorithos]] alphabet. There are also symbols representing words of particular cultural significance

===Art===

Sculpture is the most popular form of art on [[Lorithan]]. It is considered the only "lasting" form of art. Faded and defaced frescoes have been found in some of the pre-[[Burning War]] ruins which suggest that there were other art forms at one time, but there are no new ones being made. [[Meroniri]] archaeologists have been seeking permission to remove and restore the ruins before they are lost forever.

[[Lorithos]] architecture is based off the ziggurat, though some structures have more modern appearances. [[Lorithos]] ziggurats can reach a kilometer in height, and the interiors tend to be brightly (sometimes harshly) lit, and the hallways often have sculptures and carvings in the halls.

===Architecture===

Planetary architecture is usually a moderate affair, and usually devoted to agriculture in some way. [[Lorithos]] usually live in the [[Kihtor]]--massive space elevators. Those that live on the planet live in one or two story houses, which are based on the Lorithos ziggurat.

===Cuisine===

On [[Lorithan]], cuisine is a lost cause. Outside the [[Kihtor]], anything with flavor spoils almost instantly. Only bland, tastless emergency rations survive long.

On [[Jagarr]], food tends to be centered around a variety of noodles made from "Rektir" flour, which is ground from a plant similar to cacti, and a variety of sauces and meats. [[Terran]] visitors refer to it as similar to Italian food in variety and texture, but note that Jagarr cuisine tends to be sweet, and that most sauces are thin and watery rather than chunky.

On [[Ipsof]], food is based around the sea's bounty. The "Ptehzi", an order of free-swiming shellfish, are the most popular. A form of Ipsof cooking known as "Shenrok" is quite popular: a certain breed Ptehzi has a gland, which when punctured causes an exothermic reaction on any organic matter. Any food prepared by Shenrok chefs literally cooks itself. Shenrok is considered a delicacy and is rapidly gaining popularity in the [[Kihtor]] and on other worlds.

===Cinema===

The most popular cinema is produced on [[Jagarr]], where films retell the days of the [[Burning Wars]]. The most popular stories tell of finding a new homeworld, or of the days of the [[Dawn Emperor]]. Films rarely sell well outside of [[Lorithos]] worlds.

===Music===

[[Lorithos]] music tends to be somber, and most outsiders consider it depressing. A keening, wailing type of flute known as a "Sendi" is popular, though a stringed instrument known as a "Tirzen" is becoming more popular on the colonies, due to the upbeat tones it produces. Music on the colonies sees some popularity outside of [[Lorithos]] worlds, but music from [[Lorithan]] is considered the most depressing in the galaxy.

===Holidays===

The [[Lorithos]] celebrate "The End of Suffering", which commemorates both [[First Contact]] and the end of the [[Burning Wars]]. They also celebrate the founding of [[Ipsof]] colony, which represents the beginning of a new future for the [[Lorithos]] people. There are nearly eighty religious holidays, none of which are officially recognized by the government.

===Social Structure===

The average [[Lorithos]] civilian living on [[Lorithan]] works in private business, particularly agriculture. Many [[Lorithos]] are nomadic, hunting and living in the wrecked warship hulls left over from the [[Burning Wars]].

Lorithos are non-aggressive, nearly to the point of pacifism. They flatly refuse to use weapons of mass-destruction, even to defend themselves. They live daily with the painful mutations and cancers that resulted from their [[Burning Wars]].

The [[Lorithos]] have a very rigid class structure. Anyone on the colonies looks down on anyone outside the government or industries from the homeworld. People from the [[Kihtor]] look down on the city dwellers. The outlying settlements are almost universally shunned and looked down on.

The [[Lorithos]] are rediscovering their religious roots, and once again tend to follow the rituals of the Twin Gods and the [[Dawn Emperor]]. Even upper classes are returning to these old roots.

[[Lorithos]] families tend to fall into several categories. In orthodox families, a family is a patriarchy or matriarchy based strictly on the time of day or night the family members were born in. People born during the day do not marry people born at night, and vice versa. A "Sunlit Family" is patriarchal, and the man usually has two wives. A "Moonlit Family" is matriarchal, and the woman usually has two husbands. In order to keep children "properly reared" a "Sunlit" child born to a "Moonlit Family" is fostered with a "Sunlit Family" until they are 10 years old, and vice versa. Most family doctors induce birth in order to keep children born into the "correct" time frame.

Non-orthodox families, known as "Twilight Families" in traditional circles, marry regardless of when either person was born, and the dominant family member varies purely on personality. "Twilight Families" tend to be monogamous, but bigamy is occasionally practiced by both male and female non-orthodox families.

Royal families are based on the first born child, regardless of when they were born. [[Talire Huern]], the [[Twilight Empress]], was a matriarch with two husbands. [[Kian Huern]] was a matriarch as well, but she only had one husband. [[Dirken Huern]], while technically an elected official, is essentially a patriarchal member of the royal family, and has two wives.

In [[Lorithos]] society, outsiders from other worlds tend to be welcomed with open arms. On [[Lorithan]], the [[Terran]] and [[Meroniri]] rescue missions represent the only hope for most of the outlying settlements, and the [[Meroniri]] are treated with almost god-like status.

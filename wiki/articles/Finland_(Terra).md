'''Finland''' was a Nordic country situated in Northern Europe. It was bordered with [[Sweden (Terra)|Sweden]] to the west, the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] to the east and south, and [[Norway (Terra)|Norway]] to the north. The Aland Islands, off the southwestern coast, are a province of Finland.

Finland was a democratic republic with a parliament. Finland was previously part of the [[Sweden (Terra)|Swedish]] kingdom and from [[1809]] an autonomous Grand Duchy in the Russian Empire, until it declared its independence on December 6, [[1917]]. Finland was ranked as the sixth happiest nation in the world by a subjective independent scientific study. According to the World Audit Democracy profile, Finland was the freest nation in the world, in terms of civil liberties, freedom of the press, low corruption levels and political rights.

Finland's prosperity and quality of life was so high that the citizens of the nation were reluctant to unite with the rest of the world in the [[United Earth Alliance]] government. They finally signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on May 7, [[2023]], when they realized they were missing out on the benefits of interstellar colonization.

June 13, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture finally sent forces to relieve the Mesarthrim garrisons in Sydney, Australia. The Mesarthrim brought in the Black Dogs battle group to reinforce the Crimson Tide. Both battle groups moved into the Pacific islands.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First on the Mesarthrim agenda was New Guinea. The Black Dogs battle group engaged a regiment of Imperial Rigs and tanks. The Empire was fielding a group of Shadow Cats and Enforcer tanks. The Black Dogs' Vulture tanks pounded the Imperial forces at close range, crushing the Imperial Rigs. The Imperial tank crews surrendered.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Black Dogs only stopped long enough to load prisoners into dropships, then moved on to attack New Zealand.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial forces on New Zealand were much heavier, and more experienced. They were equipped with Behemoths in addition to their Shadow Cat Rigs. The fight was brutal, and lasted several hours. The Black Dogs were victorious, but quite bloodied. They remained in New Zealand and left the next operation to the Crimson Tide battle group.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the Black Dogs damaged and hurt in the fight in New Zealand, the Crimson Tide battle group attacked the island of Mindanao, part of the Philippine chain. The Mesarthrim forces hit the city of Davao with a small attack force, expecting light resistance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;They were quickly repulsed by a full regiment of Imperial heavy and assault-class Rigs. The Crimson Tide fell back and bombarded the city with their Vulture Siege Tanks, causing terrible collateral damage. It is not known how many civilians died in the bombardment, but after two hours of constant barrage the Crimson Tide troops walked into Davao without meeting resistance.

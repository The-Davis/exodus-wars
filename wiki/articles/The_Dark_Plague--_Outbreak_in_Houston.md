March 9, 2324

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

     In the North American continent, the recovery teams have only secured small holdings in the shells of what used to be larger cities. The biggest and furthest northern recovery and refugee camp is what used to be Houston Texas, before a bombardment leveled it.
     Currently, four hundred Prefecture and two hundred Titanian troops keep the peace. The Alliance of Planets recovery teams have an on-site staff of two thousand. There are over a hundred thousand refugees in the camp. All of them are in mortal danger.

     The first corpses were found early this week. Over a dozen people, mostly older men, were found dead in their tents near the outskirts of the Houston Recovery Camp.
     In the un-recovered continents of Terra, deaths aren't uncommon, but the manner of death is strange. All internal organ cells collapsed, causing the organs to literally implode. The resulting liquid drained into the ground.
     The Alliance of Planets recovery team medics have performed autopsies, and report that the cause of death was internal bleeding similar to damage caused by bludgeoning or similar trauma. They admit though that any untrained fool can see that no violence was done to the men before they died.
     Immunologists and non-specialized medics alike agree that the cause of the bleeding and cell damage is caused by a disease, but none they've ever seen before.

     Professor Hlow was informed, and sent this statement to B-Net News from the Terran Recovery Project headquarters in Rio de Janeiro.
    Professor Hlow: "This is a plague. I admit that I had my suspicions beforehand, and the plague is why I began the quarantine of Terra. I wish I'd been wrong, but I'm glad to report that random testing of civilians outside of Terra have turned up negative so far. At least the quarantine worked.
     "I can't say for sure what this plague is. I have a theory, but I won't report until our medical teams have a complete report. Rumors could cause panic. Please, do not encourage rumors.
     "Until we have solid answers, follow some simple health safety steps. Boil your water. Use air filters--Alliance of Planets officials will be distributing them. Do not touch plague victims with your bare skin. Burn their effects and belongings at least three kilometers from inhabited areas, and bury the ashes.

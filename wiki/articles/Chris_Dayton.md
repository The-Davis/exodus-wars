''Christopher Aaron Dayton'', known most frequently as "Chris" Dayton, was a medical professional born and raised on [[Ah Kin]]. He had the unusual opportunity to learn the truth behind the existence of the [[Onyx Empire]] in [[2300]] and of the destruction of [[Torimur]].

While this information meant little to him at the time, it became instrumental [[2315|fifteen years later]], when the [[Onyx Empire]] began the [[Second Exodus War]]. Dayton founded the first formal cell of the [[Resistance against the Empire]], and eventually became the first [[Consul]] of the resulting [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

(rewrite incomplete)

Dayton is one of the few original [[Torimur]] colonists to both survive the cold sleep of the [[First Exodus|First]] and [[Second Exodus]], and to also stay out of the military.  He worked as a civilian medic during the war, and played a role in the design of [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow|Dr. Hlow]]'s biologically-engineered antivirus.  He took great interest in the formation of the [[Prefecture]] government and began a campaign to get elected to [[Consul]]. Due to his popularity among former [[Empire|Imperial]] soldiers--the main majority of the people allowed to vote in the first election--and the lack of an opposing candidate, Dayton won easily.

When the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] were formed as a formal government, Dayton won the first election for [[Consul]] in [[2323]]. Chris Dayton was sworn into office on May 6th, [[2323]].  He called the first meeting of the Prefecture Senate to order on May 12th, [[2323]].

Dayton was a hard worker, dedicated to his job of serving and protecting the rights of the people of the [[Prefecture]].  He worked hard to balance the military, police and infrastructure systems of the [[Prefecture]] without dipping deeply into the pockets of the citizens.

He was abducted by the [[Conclave]] in [[2324]], and was not freed until the [[Conclave]] surrendered to the [[Meroniri]].

Dayton was considered a security risk due to the possible influence of the [[Infernal Machine]], and was given his old post of chief medical officer for the [[Mobile Medical Corps]], and [[Talaris Roi]] was elected to his old position of [[Consul]].

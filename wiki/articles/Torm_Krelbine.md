''Torm Krelbine'' is generally credited with starting the [[Burning Wars]] when he attacked [[Kian Huern]]. Krelbine had no blood claim to the throne, but was a powerful warrior and had many loyal followers.

Torm Krelbine had a reputation for bloodlust, which he aptly demonstrated when he captured [[Goff Riiktar]]. Krelbine slaughtered the rival would-be heir, slowly slicing him to pieces with various blades.

Krelbine's forces survived until the end, when he died in a final showdown with [[Kian Huern]]. Krelbine left no heirs, though he lives on in the memory of the [[Lorithos]] as a frightening villain. Popular [[Lorithos]] mythology says he now wears the aspect of the reaper, and ushers dead souls into the afterlife.

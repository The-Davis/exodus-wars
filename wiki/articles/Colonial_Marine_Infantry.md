Marine Troopers make up only a sixth of the infantry forces within the Commonwealth Army Command.  However, these soldiers recieve additional, specialized training in order to perform their specific roles as naval deployment soldiers.  Their equipment is the same as the standard Army Regular's 

The standard Marine Trooper is outfitted with a heavy Prudenski Composite powered-armor suit.  The use of assisted motion servos on the heavy armor enables a Marine Trooper to operate unhindered despite the suit. 

Marine Troopers only carry a small amount of gear in their suits, since their primary deployment areas are large warships and planetary installations.  Additional ammunition and grenades are carried, but Marine Troopers rarely need any sort of rations or campaign gear, and high-explosives, dangerous inside a starship, are also seldom carried.  

[[Image:ColonialMarineInfantry.jpg]]

The helmet of a Marine Trooper is the same as that of an Army Regular, complete with HUD, night-vision capabilities, sealed-operations capacity, and weapons-sensor feed.  

Unlike the Army Regulars and Guard's Riflemen, the Marine Troopers do not use the Young-Watson MBR.  Instead, they are outfitted with a smaller weapon, the Roberts Armory 2320 Combat Carbine.  Smaller than the standard Watson Rifle, the Roberts 2320 is functionally similar to the Watson Rifle.  The bullpup setup allows the gun to be much shorter overall, and thus more managable in the confines of a starship.  The Roberts 2320 runs on energy cells mounted in the stock, which hold enough power for twenty full-powered shots, or a larger number of lower-powered shots.  Despite the low shot count relative to most projectile weapons, its shots are significantly more powerful, and more effective against shielded targets.  Single shots can even be fired un-focused to give a shot-range, wide-diameter blast.  While not equipped with external sights, the Roberts 2320 does have basic built-in sensors in a modular carrying handle, and can transmit data to a Marine Trooper's HUD.  

Marine Troopers are deployed on every Commonwealth warship, state-owned merchant marine vessels, and Commonwealth NOC space installations.  Their sole roles are combat and counter-attack against enemy boarders, search and interdiction duties against suspect starships, and (theoretically) operations in support of Planetary Operations Command forces, such as seizing spaceports and orbital stations.

The Imperial хоньч нохой, literally "Imperial Wolfhounds", but better known as the "Omegas" were a terrorist and covert operations organization that worked for the [[Onyx Empire]]. Officially, they answered to no one but [[Lavik Nighthawk]] and [[Lucius Black]].

The Omegas were actually more feared and hated than the rest of the [[Onyx Empire]] combined. Most of the members of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Royal Imperial Military]] were brainwashed or trained from birth to fight. The Omegas were all volunteers, and all enjoyed killing and torture.

= History =

The history of the Omegas is rather miasmatic.  There is no definitive start to their operations.  The Nighthawk brothers were definitely the first operatives, working as Lucius Black's strong-arm men and general-purpose assassins.  The string of agents and lackeys that followed in their shoes is long, difficult to follow, and often punctuated with complete purges of the organization, instigated either by the ever-paranoid Lucius Black, or the equally-sadistic Lavik Nighthawk. While nearly a hundred operatives worked for Black during the [[First Exodus War]], none survived until the [[First Exodus]] and most were killed out of misplaced fears of betrayal.

== First Exodus War Era ==

While First Exodus War's official hostilities ended in [[2079]], there were several terrorist attacks against [[Earth]] in [[2080]]. While the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]] was blamed for the attacks, they were actually perpetrated by [[Lavik Nighthawk]] under orders from [[Lucius Black]]. The attacks ensured that Earth would have its eye fixed on the [[Sirius Cluster]] instead of paying any attention to where [[Torimur]]'s survivors settled.

== Imperial Decree ==

When Lucius Black formed the Onyx Empire with the First Imperial Decree, he began forming several operations to secure his hold on the reins of power.  The хоньч нохой (pronounced "Koenkha Noksoj"), named in Black's native Mongolian tongue for wolfhounds, was tasked with "the identification of elements potentially dangerous to the Royal House of Black, both foreign and domestic, and the clandestine extermination of said elements without regard for humanitarian or judicial concerns."

'''Larson Sells'''
*Place of Birth: [[Fronde]]

''Larson Sells'' is a specialist in vascular medicine, working at the [[Schweitzer Institute of Medicine]] of Fronde. He was part of the team that dissected the first [[Volucris]] specimens captured by humanity.

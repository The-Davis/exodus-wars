'''World War III''' was a [[Terran]] conflict that involved multiple factions fighting for survival against the aggression of the Muslim empire of [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]]. It ended with unprecedented friendship between the eastern and western superpowers, and is considered responsible for the rise of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

'''Zander's Strain''' is a genetically-engineered strain of bacteria, developed by geneticist Michael Zander in the [[21st Century]] as part of [[Earth]]'s expansion and terraformation efforts.

Zander's Strain is immune to most forms of radiation, and causes the radioactive decay rates of most isotopes to increase exponentially, resulting in most forms of radioactive material becoming inert within a few dozen to hundred years instead of taking millenia.

The XB-1 was the first Imperial bomber, based off the F-4 airframe. It was built with much larger fuel tanks for the ion engine to handle the significantly greater mass of the Anti-Warship Torpedoes (AWT). The design was also stripped of its guns, and the heat shielding plates necessary for an atmospheric reentry. The YB-1 was used in attack runs on asteroids with F-4s escorting and F-4s "defending" the target. The YB-1 was put into extremely limited production, but cancelled with only 30 delivered due to fuel problems limiting the range of the bomber too much.

The XB-2 refined the YB-1 to a point where it seemed to be useable. An XB-2 could carry two AWTs up to three Astronomical Units and return, with fuel to maneuver in between. The airframe was almost completely stripped to be able to save mass and devote mass to the AWTs and reaction mass for the Ion Drives. Unfortunately, the YB-2 had some issues with maneuverability in test runs: maneuvering thrusters were placed on multiple control surfaces on the wings, which had been stripped down to a minimum size. Despite the problems, the model was put into production as the B-2 Ocelot.

By this time, the F-4C was prepared for escort. Matching the MPD system to the B-2 airframe proved problematic due to computer space needed for the AWT guidance systems, so the B-2A was outfitted with a better and slightly larger airframe. Unfortunately, problems with the hydrazine fuel used by the MPD killed an aircrew in the XB-4 project, grounding the B-2A until the use of xenon fuels was developed.

Alexi-Wrought Airworks had no interest in further development of the B-2 design, and the bomber was looking at the end of its career when Rahn Industries purchased the design outright from the Alpha Centauri based aircraft company. Rahn Industries began incorporating multiple changes into the design at the request of the Prefecture Galactic Navy, and the B-2B was released. The current variant has many advances over the old Royal Imperial Navy model. It has more advanced flight control systems, gravity cavitation drives, a cheap fission reactor power core, and moderate shield systems. Today the Ocelot sees use in the Prefecture Galactic Navy's Air Arm as a torpedo bomber, delivering payloads of anti-warship weaponry. It is a popular bomber for escort and attack carriers, which often operate without the support of heavy escorting warships.

=== Overview ===

The Delta Class Missile Frigate is one of the most commercially successful warship class in [[Terran]] history.  Over the years, the Delta has been purchased by a variety of colonial administrations and private firms.

Recently, a massive order from the [[United Centusi States]] nearly tripled the total number of these warships sold.  It is a rugged, utilitarian warship designed to operate in any role effectively, efficiently, and affordably. 

=== Capabilities ===

The Delta class is a jack-of-all-trades warship, designed to fill every combat role for those naval forces that lack the funds to purchase specialized vessels for every function.  Its primary armament comes from its four missile batteries, each comprised of twelve Variable-Ordinance Tactical Missile Tubes.  These missile batteries are what give the Delta class its renowned versatility in combat, allowing them to be used as anti-aerospace, ranged anti-warship, or planetary support/bombardment vessels. 

[[Image:DeltaClassMissileFrigate2.jpg|150px]]

Non-missile weaponry is comprised of three twin-Ashur Cannon turrets.  These energy cannons operate in a similar role to Hellgun projectile cannons, and give the Delta a powerful anti-warship punch at energy weapon range.  Twelve Kalidasan-designed Advanced Neutron Cannon in Fast-Tracking turrets are mounted to provide additional anti-warship, as well as ranged anti-aerospace firepower.  Further anti-aerospace defense comes from a quartet of emplacements near the rear of the craft. 

The Delta is outfitted with Terran-standard warship defenses, in addition to a capital-grade anti-missile system.  It is quite quick, like most Terran frigate designs, is capable of staying out of range of enemy ships as it fires it missiles from afar. 

=== Deployment ===

The Blasius Security Fleet maintains four Deltas as primary.  Two Deltas operate within the Sirius District: two are at Blasius' Corporate Headquarters in the Berlusconi Territory, and one in Nakamura Territory at Blasius' Warship Technologies Research Center.  A third Delta is assigned as a command and control vessel in the Terran system, coordinating Blasius reconstruction and development efforts there.

[[Image:DeltaClassMissileFrigate3.jpg|150px]]

Outside the BSF, Deltas have been sold to some pioneer and colony defense forces, private and corporate security fleets, and, most recently and in greatest numbers, to the United Centusi States Kriegsmarine in its effort to modernize.  

The 62 Deltas of the UCSN were constructed in the Blasius Family shipyards in the [[Sirius Cluster]], as well as in licensed shipyards in the [[Centus System]]. Unfortunately, it was not known until later that the breakneck pace of construction was forced by the Kriegsmarine's need for ships prior to the [[Tempest War]].  Shortly after the Tempest War began, officially neutral Blasius Family ceased production of the Delta line, cancelling all future orders from the UCS and others.

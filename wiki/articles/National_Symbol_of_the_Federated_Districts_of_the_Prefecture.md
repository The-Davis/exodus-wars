When [[Anthony Hadrian]] began organizing the [[Resistance against the Empire|Resistance]], he named all of the allied groups "the [[Prefecture]]", and made their symbol the Raven. The Raven was already a powerful symbol in the [[Empire]] due to its prominence as the name of their symbolic superiority fighter, the [[F-4 Raven]].

The [[Lucius Black|Emperor]]'s favorite symbol for his [[Empire]] was a [[National Symbol of the Empire|wolf]], to go with his saying "I will be as a wolf among their sheep." [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] began to turn it around and used the phrase "when the wolves fall out, the ravens will come into their own."

As part of the symbolism of the new government, the [[Consul]] keeps a raven in their office. [[Chris Dayton]] treated his like a pet and usually allowed it to fly loose when he did not have official guests. [[Talaris Roi]] didn't like the mess, and had an aviary built outside of her office to keep with tradition but to avoid letting a bird roam around a place of government business.

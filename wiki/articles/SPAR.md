SPAR Suite SPAR--optical Sensors with PAttern Recognition--was developed in late 2324 to detect and identify Volucris in space at short and mid ranges. The suite of optical sensor programs is much more advanced than the old system used before 2324. Recognizing the convoluted shapes of Bugger flyers is much more difficult than the simple shape of an Aerospace fighter.

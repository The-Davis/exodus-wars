===Overview===

With interplanetary commerce one of the largest businesses in the galaxy, Rahn Industries decided to get back to its roots and tap this valuable resource of income.

[[Image:StarlightClassFreighter1.jpg]]

Being that R.I. was formerly a freighter company, the designers decided to build a number of different freighters, the Starlight being one of the smallest and least expensive.

===Capabilities===

At only roughly 250 meters long, the Starlight isn't the largest freighter ever made and doesn't boast the most tonnage capacity for its size. However, what is lacks in capacity it makes up in speed and therefore time. This boost to efficiency is provided by the powerful engines that help to propel the ship through space.

[[Image:StarlightClassFreighter2.jpg]]

Another hazard that must be taken into account is the possibility of pirate raids or other hostile encounters. Therefore, the Starlight sports three fast-tracking neutron cannons to take on aerospace fighters and bombers.

Finally, the cargo bay is nearly fully automated, allowing for quicker and less costly down time during loading and unloading. It also takes advantage of the newer modular loading systems which are starting to take root as the new standard.

===Deployment===

Since its inception, the Starlight freighter has seen wide spread success, with a number of fleets being outfitted with the vessels. Rahn Industries itself employs approximately six hundred Starlight freighters as their main source of transportation.

[[Image:StarlightClassFreighter3.jpg]]

The Starlights, of course, are backed up by Pelicans and the new heavy freighter class, the Atlas.

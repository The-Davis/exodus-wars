===Overview===

Around 2316, the patriarchs of the Sirius families decided to increase their naval forces in response to the war taking place elsewhere in the Terran sector.

[[Image:CaspiaClassArmedMerchantman1.jpg]]

For the Asanova Territory, the expansion involved the conversion of four large Caspia freighters into armed merchantmen.  The Black Fleet Caspias origins as freighters make them simple to operate and maintain, and they are cheaper to deploy than Petrovs. 

===Capabilities===

A stock Caspia freighter is a common sight in many parts of the Terran sector, as they are employed by a number of firms and individuals.  However, they are not ordinarily armed.  Conversion to armed merchantmen required a significant rebuild to the interior and exterior of the vessel.  Each Black Fleet Caspia now sports six Kalidasan-designed Advanced Neutron Cannons, in three Fast-Tracking twin-turrets.  These turrets are the only anti-warship grade weaponry outfitted on an Asanovan Caspia, but they are sufficient for the Caspia's intended roles, which do not include warship combat. 

Each Black Fleet Caspia has an anti-aerospace armament nearly twice that of a Petrov frigate, despite being similar in size.  The Caspia's role as patrol warships means that their usual enemies are comprised of fighters, armed transports, and possibly gunships.  Sixteen anti-aerospace emplacements, more than mounted on the Kirov, are devastating against against such foes. 

[[Image:CaspiaClassArmedMerchantman2.jpg]]

One major overhaul of each Black Fleet Caspia involved adding Boron-Nitride to the hull, and reinforcing the internal structure.  The addition of shielding systems also greatly increased the combat-worthiness of the Caspia.  while they are not capable of standing up to true warships for any sustained period of time, they are well protected against most gunships and aerospace craft. 

Deployment:

The Black Fleet Caspias went into drydock during mid-year of 2316.  Each was subsequently militarized, and the refits were complete by early 2317.  The four Caspias in the Black Fleet operate as patrol vessels, regularly traveling shipping lanes, visiting common destinations in Asanova Territory, and patrolling lesser used lanes to various sites.

[[Image:CaspiaClassArmedMerchantman3.jpg]]

Their presence in an area can discourage potential threats, not just because of their own firepower, but because they can call in additional warships if needed.

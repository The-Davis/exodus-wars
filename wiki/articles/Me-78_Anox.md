===Overview===

The Anox is the primary fighter-bomber of the UCS Aerospace Force.  Originally a United Mesarthrim Clans design, the UCS adopted the Anox as their heavy fighter-bomber.

[[Image:Me78Anox1.jpg]]

It is a proven design, and upgrades by UCS technicians and engineers has increased the effectiveness of the Anox. .

 

===Capabilities===

Where the Sanburn is built around its guns, the Anox is built around its missile armament.  With eight Medium Ordinance Hardpoints, the Anox can carry enough missiles to destroy a large number of enemy fighters or ground units.  The real power comes from a pair of Heavy Hardpoints, which can carry powerful anti-warship missiles, or anti-ground artillery missiles, making the Anox a threat to any enemy. 

In addition to its missile arsenal, the Anox is also equipped with three Type-C Blazers, the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type.  Other fighters carry more, but these are sufficient considering the Anox' role as an attack craft, not a dog-fighter. 

The Anox is outfitted with a standard fighter-grade reactor, and an impressive engine array, allowing it to carry heavy payloads of weaponry.  However, it is much less nimble than Patton or Sanburn type fighters, and thus it usually leaves dog-fighting and bomber-interception to other craft. 

[[Image:Me78Anox2.jpg]] 

===Deployment===

Anox fighter-bombers are deployed with nearly every USC Army and Navy group to provide ground support and anti-warship protection.  Their powerful missile payload can do damage to most any target.  However, their relative sluggishness compared to other Mesarthrim fighters usually leads to their withdrawal from combat after their missile arsenal has been depleted.

Currency and trade are the foundations of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]]. They established the Likvida standard of currency before they made contact with the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. When contact with the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]] was made, both currencies remained in use, accepted throughout each of the friendly governments.

As is common throughout the [[Alliance of Planets]], the Likvida is based on metal coinage. The Corporations of the Consortium decided on a single standard, and agreed to keep the amount of minted currency strictly under control, in order to make their money strong in the interstellar market.

Unlike the more traditional [[Prefecture]]'s [[Peculium]], gold and silver are not used in the Likvida. Three metals are used in minting, but only one is a "precious" metal. Platinum is used for five, ten, twenty and hundred Likvida coins. Titanium is used for half and whole Likvada coins, and Iron is used for quarter Likvida pieces.

In the galactic exchange, the Likvida is worth approximately two hours of unskilled labor. It's the strongest active currency in the galaxy. The low inflation of the Likvida is caused by the strong unregulated economy of [[Kronheim]]. As long as [[Kronheim]] is free of stifling regulation, it's likely to remain a strong contender.

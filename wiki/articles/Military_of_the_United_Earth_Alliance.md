== History of the Alliance Defense Force ==

The Alliance Defense Force was formed from a conglomeration of British, American, and Soviet military resources after [[World War III]]. It was unified into a single chain of command, and eventually absorbed the [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency|Space Agency]] when armed expeditions began to pacify certain colonies which did not wish to accept the Alliance's claim to their worlds.

Eventually, outdated branches like the surface navy and the marines were disbanded. Only the Air Force, Army, and Space Agency were still in effect at the time of the [[Second Exodus War]].

== Organization ==

The [[United Earth Alliance]]'s armed forces were divided into three branches:

* The [[United Earth Alliance Army|Army]], including
** Infantry
** Rigs
** Tanks
** Artillery
** Special Forces (Delta Force)
** Air Cavalry
** Engineers
** Communications
** Transportation and Logistics
** Maintenance

* The [[United Earth Alliance Air Defense Force|Air Force]]
** Forward Air Command
** Transport Air Command
** Special Force (Special Air Service)

* The [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency|Space Agency]]
** Combat Fleets
** Exploratory Commands
** Space Stations
** Defense Air Command

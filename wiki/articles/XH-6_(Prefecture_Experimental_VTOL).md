When the Prefecture Air Force took over flight research and development, aeroelastic control systems were added to the UH-3B and AH-4A. They also contracted Rahn Industries to begin work on the XH-6 heavy VTOL dropship. The design failed at first, until Rahn Industries was able to buy [[Ixoarchada]] anti-gravity technology. The YH-6 was tested, and capable of landing multiple troops in battle. The UH-6 Beluga began production, and the anti-gravity pods were incorporated into the UH-3C and AH-4B.

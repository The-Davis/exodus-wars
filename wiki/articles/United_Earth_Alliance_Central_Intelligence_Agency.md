The '''Central Intelligence Agency''' ('''CIA''') was originally an intelligence agency of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] government. When the [[United Earth Alliance]] was formed in [[2011]] the CIA absorbed its [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] counterpart, the [[KGB]], and became the world government's intelligence and clandestine operations agency. The [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]]'s SIS was dissolved into the CIA later.

The CIA's first function was obtaining and analyzing information about governments, colonies, corporations, and people. Its second function was propaganda and public relations. Its third function was the government's hidden hand via covert operations at the direction of the President.

The CIA headquarters were located in the community of Langley in Fairfax County, Virginia, a few miles northwest from downtown Washington, D.C. along the Potomac River. The CIA was part of the [[Terran]] Intelligence Community. Prior to becoming the world government's sole intelligence agency, the CIA was in direct competition with the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]]'s [[KGB]] and the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]]'s Secret Intelligence Service, both of which became part of the CIA after [[2011]]. 

The CIA retained it's long-standing nickname, '''The Company''', until the [[Empire]] conquered the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2315]] and absorbed the CIA's resources into the Omegas.

==History==

The Central Intelligence Agency was created by Congress with passage of the National Security Act of [[1947]], signed into law by President [[Harry Truman]].  It is the descendant of the Office of Strategic Services (OSS) of [[World War II]], which was dissolved in October [[1945]] and its functions transferred to the State and War Departments. But the need for a centralized postwar intelligence-gathering operation was clearly recognized. Eleven months earlier, in [[1944]], William Donovan, the OSS's creator, proposed to President [[Franklin Roosevelt]] creating a new espionage organization directly supervised by the President: "which will procure intelligence both by overt and covert methods and will at the same time provide intelligence guidance, determine national intelligence objectives, and correlate the intelligence material collected by all government agencies." 

Under his plan, a powerful, centralized civilian agency would have coordinated all the intelligence services. He also proposed that this agency have authority to conduct "subversive operations at home and abroad." Under the National Security Act of [[1947]] (effective September 18, [[1947]]), the National Security Council and the Central Intelligence Agency were established. Roscoe Hillenkoetter was appointed as the first Director of Central Intelligence.

The CIA's history as a [[United States (Terra)|United States]] organization was largely in covert intelligence abroad. Few covert operations were conducted outside the borders of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]], though several people considered "dangerous figureheads" in the emerging "culture of un-American ideaology" were quietly removed from the international scene by CIA operatives.

At home, the CIA's clandestine operations were far-reaching, and very sinister by modern standards. They frequently killed or imprisoned people considered dangerous to the culture of America, and are largely credited with ending the [[Communist]] movement, the homosexual movement, and the infanticide movement.

Their more benevolent activities included operations designed to end racial tension and promote the integration of diversity into the single American culture. Persons considered important to this movement were protected. In one of the most famous and widely-publicized CIA operations, Agent George Philips threw himself in front of an assassin's bullet intended for Dr. [[Martin Luther King]], saving the life of the black civil rights activist. The CIA is also credited with infiltrating and destroying the Ku Klux Klan.

During the build-up to [[World War III]], the CIA began to conduct clandestine operations in [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]], hoping to undermine the Muslim war machine before they could bring their considerable power to bear. Their missions were largely unsuccessful, but they were able to eliminate the Muslim ICBM program and assassinate most of their top missile scientists.

In [[2011]], the CIA became the intelligence agency of the [[United Earth Alliance]] world government. The reorganized CIA was given strict orders not to directly interfere in the integration of [[Terran]] nations that had not yet signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]], but was tasked with propaganda missions designed to encourage the remaining independent nations to join the world government.

The CIA was officially disbanded by the [[Empire]] in [[2315]], and was absorbed into the Omegas.

==Organization==

*Director of the Central Intelligence Agency (DCIA) - The head of the CIA was given the title of the DCIA.
*The Directorate of Intelligence, the analytical branch of the CIA, was responsible for the collection and analysis of intelligence.
*The Directorate of Clandestine Operations was responsible for all covert operations such as assassination and abduction.
*The Directorate of Public Affairs was responsible for all propaganda operations.
*The Directorate of Military Affairs provided intelligence and operational support to the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] armed forces.

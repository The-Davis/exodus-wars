'''Grolk Kyhra'''
*Born: Unknown
*Place of Birth: Unknown
*Allegiance: [[Kabila Nyoka]]

''Grolk Kyhra'' is an [[Ngrligru]] who was enslaved into the service of the [[Onyx Empire]] through a complicated program of brainwashing. His ruthless command led the [[Onyx Empire]] to countless victories in close quarters combat.

Little is known about the past of mysterious and vicious Grolk Kyhra.  He is an [[Ngrligru]], an amphibian species that has technology far beyond human standards.  He hails from the [[Kabila Nyoka]], one of the more powerful tribes in [[Ngrligru]] society.

In the year [[2207]], Kyhra was in command of a "small" force of [[Ngrligru]]. His fleet was originally two Nina'rikebu Motherships and dozens of Kifa Class Frigates. This force was encountered by the returning [[Torimur]] forces, and crushed by [[Robert Marshall]].  Even though Kyhra had the numbers and firepower over the [[Torimur]] fleet, Marshall managed to humiliate him and confound every tactical feat the [[Ngrligru]] tried.

In the end, everything but Kyhra's flagship--the ''Bahari Shela''--was lost. Kyhra was captured and tortured by the [[Lucius Black|Emperor]]'s medical scientists until he was conditioned to obey [[Lucius Black]]'s every command. A few hundred other [[Ngrligru]] were similarly "reprogrammed."

In the [[Second Exodus War]] and the [[Imperial Civil War]], Grolk Kyhra was the most feared warrior in the [[Royal Imperial Army]]. His ruthless combat tactics and his powerful physical strength and combat prowess won him more infantry battles than any general in human history.

When the [[Conclave]] fell, Kyhra and his remaining [[Ngrligru]] fled in the ''Bahari Shela'' for the [[Sagittarius Arm]].

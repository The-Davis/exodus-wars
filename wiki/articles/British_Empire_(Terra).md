The '''British Empire''' was the largest empire in history and for a substantial time was the foremost global power. It began as the tiny island nation of England in the [[10th Century]], but became a truly global might to be reckoned with during the European age of discovery of the [[15th Century]].

By [[1921]], the British Empire held sway over a population of about 458 million people, approximately one-quarter of the world's population at the time. It covered about 36.6 million km<sup>2</sup>, about a quarter of Earth's total land area.

The British Empire began to decline slightly as it granted independence to several colonies which became strong allies of the Empire in what would become known as the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)| Commonwealth of Nations]], which had a common system of government, laws, and currency.

Other colonies tried to win independence, some through violent rebellions and some through non-violent protests. The colony of India's bid for independence was put down, but many Middle Eastern, Asian, and East African colonies were lost to the rising Muslim empire of [[Arabia (Terra)| Arabia]].

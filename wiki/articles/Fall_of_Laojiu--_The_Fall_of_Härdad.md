by James Davis

The Fall of Härdad - 5/18/2325 - By James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     Despite their continued efforts to break the will of the citizens of Härdad, the Mesarthrim have reached the limit of their patience. Hansen Dietrich considers carefully, knowing if the Prefecture garrison troops don't heed his final warning, 50,000 troops and 400,000 civilians will die. Leaflets have done nothing, and broadcasts cannot be sent with the communications grid knocked out in the first wave of attacks.
     The message will have to go in person, but messenger will almost certainly be going to his death. The Prefecture does not believe in honorable combat, ever since a fateful day in the Battle of Terra two years before when General Travis Carpenter ordered the execution of several hundred thousand Imperial prisoners of war.
     It comes to a request for volunteers. Three commanders are called to choose which Mesarthrim force will deliver the final ultimatum. General Nikolaus Adler of the Myt'kampfen, Colonel Fritz Dresdner of the Myt'herra, and Captain Lothar Metzger of the Myt'gaul are ready to decide.

     In the end, Captain Metzger insists on taking the honor and danger upon himself. He walks into Härdad to give them the final warning. Surrender the city by nightfall, or be destroyed.
     Metzger delivers his message, and is met with brutal retaliation from the crowd that gathers. The denizens of Härdad, young and old alike, hurl first insults, then rocks, then bullets at him. Metzger succumbs to a hundred wounds, but still dies on his feet, repeating his message like some ancient oracle of doom.

     By nightfall, most of the Icronian residents of the city have departed for the forests and foothills around the town, preferring to take their chances in the wild. The Terran residents try various methods of leaving, but fail. Flights out are shot down, and convoys are destroyed by Mesarthrim tank patrols. The few survivors are sent back to Härdad on foot with the promise that they will live if the Prefecture surrenders the city.
     All warnings go unheeded, and at last, with a heavy heart, Admiral Dietrich orders the city bombed into oblivion, first with heavy explosives to level the city, then bunker busters to dig out the few survivors underground.
     By the next morning, the Mesarthrim troops move into the rubble and confirm no survivors, then move south to join their brethren attacking the Prefecture fortifications at the Fathach Méire mountain range.

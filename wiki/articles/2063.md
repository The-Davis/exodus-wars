== Events of 2063 ==

=== January ===

=== February ===

* February 16 - The [[Bethija]]n Parliament dissolves after a vote of no confidence against Prime Minister [[Artem Kuts]].
* February 21 - The Bethijan Unification Party leadership sends a request to [[Earth]] to annex Bethija.

=== March ===

* March 4 - Attempts to elect a new [[Bethija]]n government fail, resulting in riots in several major cities.

=== April ===

* April 18 - The [[United Earth Alliance]] sends word to [[Bethija]] that the request to annex Bethija has been approved by the Assembly.

=== May ===

* May 6 - [[United Earth Alliance]] warships enter orbit of [[Bethija]].
* May 8 - United Earth Alliance troops land on Bethija.
* May 12 - Bethijan seperatists clash with Alliance troops in the streets of [[Volyn]].

=== June ===

* June 11 - [[Artem Kuts]] formally declares independence from the [[United Earth Alliance]] and delivers a declaration of war to the occupational authorities. Under the flag of truce, he delivers his message unmolested, and is allowed to leave again unharmed.

=== July ===

* July 16 - [[Jerome Black]] lands privately-purchased military supplies and food on [[Bethija]].
* July 19 - [[Giorgio Hadrian]] and Jerome Black meet with Bethijan partisan leader [[Artem Kuts]], and agree that [[Torimur]] will support Bethija in its bid for independence.

=== August ===

* August 21 - [[Bethija]]n partisan forces are cornered in the Bagry Caverns by Alliance troops.

=== September ===

* September 15 - [[Artem Kuts]] is shot and killed by Alliance forces. Many partisan forces are captured. [[Giorgio Hadrian]] is captured alive and sent to the [[Mars|Martian Federal Prison]].

=== October ===

* October 3 - [[Jerome Black]] attempts to escape [[Bethija]], but his ship is destroyed by the [[UEAS Aleksey Tarasov|UEAS ''Aleksey Tarasov'']], resulting in his death.
* October 15 - Word of Jerome's death reaches [[Torimur]].
* October 19 - [[Giorgio Hadrian]] is released and sent back to Torimur.

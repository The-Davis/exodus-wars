===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Reigess Suverände|History]]

Over two millennia ago, [[Raegos Direnson]], the youngest son of the King of [[Kronheim]] set out with a fleet of colony ships in search of a new planet. With a fierce ice age setting in, finding a new world was seen as the last hope for the people of [[Kronheim]]. When all contact was lost with the colony fleet, the planet of [[Kronheim]] declined as the Long Winter began. But the fate of the colonists was to finally reach a new world--their paradise.

Today, the heirs of those original colonists live on that world, and the heirs of the [[Ikronin]] throne rule over them. Long an isolationist nation, the fate of [[Kronheim]] at the hands of the [[Volucris]] swarms convinced the King of the need for allies. The [[Reigens]], seeking to distance themselves from the reach of the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]], allied with the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], and joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]] in early [[2325]].

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of the Reigess Suverände|Government]]

The [[Reigens]] believe in the absolute sovereignty of the Raegos line of descent, and obey them without question. To their credit, there has never been a tyranical or otherwise abusive heir to the [[Reigen]] throne.

There is a system of emergency succession if a king dies before his son is ready to inherit the throne. A trusted regent is appointed by the queen to rule until the prince is old enough to rule.

=== Law ===
Main Article: [[Law of the Reigess Suverände|Law]]

The [[Kejsare]]'s absolute monarchy has kept [[Reigen]] law surprisingly simple. They have a simple penal code against most crimes like theft or murder, which is enforced by a system of magistrates. More complicated legal matters are not on the books: if a citizen has a complaint against another citizen or even one of the nobles, they can appeal to the [[Kejsare]] for relief. These decisions are on a case by case basis; no [[Kejsare]] is bound to his own past decisions or the decisions of his predecessors.

=== Law Enforcement ===

Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Reigess Suverände|Law Enforcement]]

The magistrates are responsible for enforcing the laws as they are written. Only the [[Kejsare]] has to power to appoint or revoke a magistrate's position.

A magistrate is both a regional chief law enforcement official, and the adjudicator for the penal code. The magistrates have the full power of arrest, and may pass both verdict and sentence in any criminal case. Most magistrates do not arrest, and never investigate crimes. If any investigation is required for a hearing by the magistrate, they will deputize a citizen to conduct the investigation. Most deputized citizens will work as community patrol on a regular but part-time basis.

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Reigess Suverände|Foreign Relations]]

The [[Reigens]] have pursued a policy of being "the friend of all", inviting every [[:Category:Nations|nation]] in the [[Orion Arm]] to enter into trade and cultural exchange with them. This policy has been met with significant success.

===Military===
Main Article: [[Military of the Reigess Suverände|Military]]

The military of the Reigess Suverände is known, collectively, as "Hans Raeged Furs Likniir," loosely translated as "His Majesty's Royal Military." The Likniir trace their roots back to the days of [[Raegos Direnson]], when the now-legendary companions who became known as the [[Reigess]] earned their place at [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]]' side. The Likniir have three branches:
*[[Reigess Renskader|Renskader]] - "Fleet of the Dead" - Navy
*[[Reigess Mark Vaktida|Mark Vaktida]] - "Territorial Guard" - Army
*[[Reigess Flakt Driva|Flakt Driva]] - Air Force

===Territory===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Reigess Suverände|Territory]]

Aside from their homeworld, the [[Reigens]] own one of the largest sources of [[antimatter]] in the [[Orion Arm]]. It is one of the few resources which they will defend with violence, if necessary.

=== Economy ===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Reigess Suverände|Economy]]

The [[Reigen]] economy is based largely on a feudal concept of ownership. By their code of law, everything belongs to the king, but the king will entrust stewardship of enough property to meet the basic needs of every person, and then will parcel out property to people he can trust to use it for the benefit of others.

Critics claim that without the incentivized self-interest of capitalism, an economic system will not lead to prosperity and growth. [[Reigen]] economists agree that their system is probably not for everyone. In [[Reigen]] culture, the incentive to be considered trustworthy by the king is all that is needed, and the shame of failing that trust is a powerful cultural taboo.

Because the king recognizes the wisdom of many of his subjects in managing their affairs, the [[Reigens]] have a currency system to allow for a system of trade without royal oversight.

=== Demographics ===

Main Article: [[Demographics of the Reigess Suverände|Demographics]]

The [[Reigens]] and the [[Juns]] are the two main human ethnic demographic groups among the Reigess Suverände. Most of the population owns some land, or at least has a long-term stable lease agreement with one of the royalty.

Outsiders are welcome to settle in the territory of the Suverände, but are required to give an oath of loyalty to the [[Kejsare]].

=== Culture ===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Reigess Suverände|Culture]]

One of the central themes of [[Reigen]] culture is the ideal of "noble selflessness." The [[Reigens]] believe that nobility is something earned, not an inherent quality. They believe that only by upholding the ideals of altruism, generosity, kindness, and selflessness can the king gain the nobility essential to rule.

While many outsiders (particularly their [[Ikronin]] cousins) see this worldview as naive or foolish, many are stunned to see how firmly the [[Reigens]] follow it.

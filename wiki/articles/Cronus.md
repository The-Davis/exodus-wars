'''Cronus''' is the twelfth planet in the [[Titan System]]. It is a lifeless, frozen wasteland, devoid of life save for the stubborn military personnel that have inhabitted it in the [[24th Century]].

== History ==

From the time of the discovery and colonization of the Titan System by the [[Terran]] race up until the [[Second Exodus War]], Cronus was largely ignored. Other than charting and survey flights, the [[United Earth Alliance]] didn't give it a second thought.

When the war broke out in [[2315]], the survivors of the United Earth Alliance flocked to the Titan System and [[Scott Sanburn]]'s banner. Sanburn knew the [[Onyx Empire]] would come to take the Titan System sooner or later, and he intended to bloody their nose on the first engagement.

Unfortunately, the battle did not go well for the Alliance. The [[First Battle of Cronus]] was a textbook example of the Empire's doctrine of spreading fear and misinformation, and the constant harassment of other worlds in the Titan System led to Cronus being almost undefended. Still, the defenses were able to keep the Empire at bay for three days.

After the Onyx Empire held control over Cronus, they turned it into a fortress world. Their first compound was buried sixteen kilometers beneath the surface, with multiple redundant exits, overpressure relief buffers, automatically-sealing bulkheads, and quadruple-redundant power systems and climate control. It housed nearly twelve thousand Imperial troops at its peak. Four similar compounds were built in other locations across the planet, and six hundred surface-to-orbit automatic missile silos were installed at various strategic locations. This fortress world became the Onyx Empire's strategic command center for [[Operation Tartarus]] and the main supply dump for their operations in the Titan System.

In [[2322]], a coalition of United Earth Alliance and [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] forces captured Cronus. While the main Imperial compound was destroyed in the fighting, the other compounds were captured relatively intact. The United Earth Alliance began converting the installations into a hyperspace monitoring base.

After the Second Exodus War, the United Earth Alliance was dissolved, and Cronus was turned over to the sovereignty of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]]. It remains an important part of their defensive systems, though the expense of maintaining climate controls has led to more automation and fewer troops permanently stationed on the planet.

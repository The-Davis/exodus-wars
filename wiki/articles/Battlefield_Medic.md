The elite [[Royal Imperial Marine Corps]] of the [[Onyx Empire]] always suffers high casualties and injuries in battle.  Many times, a Marine's wounds killed him simply because he did not receive treatment for them. Imperial Command demanded a solution.

At first, scientists tried to install an anti-biotic administrating device in every suit.  This not only proved buggy, but also very expensive.  Instead they decided to equip one suit with medical supplies for every 24 Marines.  Thus, the Medic Suit was born.

The Medic carries no missile or heavy gun weaponry, only powered-down versions of the lasers carried by the Marines.  The medic also carries large amounts of medical equipment.

Besides administrating emergency treatment to soldiers, a Medic can also cut open broken Rigs and tanks and get the wounded crewmen out.  A wounded soldier knows he has nothing to fear when the Medic is on duty.

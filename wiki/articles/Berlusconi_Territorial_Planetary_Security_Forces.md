The Sirius factions employ various forces, regular and otherwise, to secure their planets and orbital stations. Aside from the various agents, guards, and "supervisors" in the Sirius Administration, each faction maintains its own version of a standing army for defense from the outside, and to quell dissidents internally.

The Berlusconi are known for their ellegance on the ground. Their Sentinel courier and Siglio armored car are both licensed designs from luxury car manufacturers. Their Florentine strike tank is a hovertank of unmatched grace and power, and even Ikronin tank designers have attempted to license the design for their own use. "Papa" Alessandro's Red Shirts are the infantry part of the Berlusconi enforcers, and operate in plain clothes just as often as they are in uniform.

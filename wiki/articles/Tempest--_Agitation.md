=====Tavou Central - 0230 Hours - 1500 Hours Tavou Time=====
     "Tavou! Tavou Control! We need immediate reinforcements!" Colonel Kenneth Russell listens in horror to the reports filtering in from Leskovik. "Twenty Mesarthrim warships. They've wiped out our fighter squadrons. They took out our defense platforms!"
     "The 3rd Regulars will hold the line. Get your people to shelters until the Fleets arrive." Russell orders. "They'll be landing an invasion force before too long."
     "Tavou Control, this is 9-6B11-1, en route to Wormhole Gate. We are ready to insert to Leskovik on your mark." Samuel Arias' squadrons are already on the move. "Uh, scratch that Tavou Control, we're getting info from the Icronians. Looks like the Worms don't wanna play."
     "Tavou Control, this is 9-7F11-1," Marshal Samuel Arias reports. "The Icronians won't let us through the Leskovik Gate. It's been destroyed on the other side."
     "What?!" Colonel Russell chokes. "Why would the UCS blow an Icronian Gate? It's economic suicide! This whole damned attack makes no sense!
     "Leskovik Control, come in. This is Colonel Russell at Tavou Control." Russell waits for a response. "I say again, Leskovik Control, come in. Leskovik, respond." Only silence. "Did they get the Ansible too?" Russell demands.
     "Negative Tavou Control." A new voice joins the conversation. "Tavou Control, this is the Delphinus. We are in the Leskovik system. The Mesarthrim jumped out right after we arrived. Knowing them, they weren't scared off either."
     "We acknowledge, Delphinus." Russell sighs. "Good to hear from you. Any word from Leskovik Control?"
     "No. Leskovik is gone. It looks like the Mesarthrim nuked the planet from orbit. It's total annihilation. We're leaving the Ardent to handle search and rescue. The rest of the fleet is en route to Tavou. Delphinus out."

=====Mesar - 0500 Hours - 1400 Hours Centusi Time=====
     William Camp smiles vapidly at his Ansible terminal, where the furious face of Prefect Hadrian fills the view screen.
     "Damn you Camp!" Hadrian snarls. "You attacked us without provocation. You bombed civilians! Consider this a declaration of war from the Prefecture."
     "I would not be so hasty if I were in your shoes, Admiral." Camp responds. "Do not forget how easily we defeated your Empire's forces in the past. We will do the same to you."
     The communication is cut off from the other end. William Camp shuts down his view screen, and looks up at his visitors. "And what can I do for you, Madame Ambassador?"
     Ambassador Lina Sveto, the Archadian representative to the Mesarthrim bows slightly. "I have been ordered to present you with this notice. Despite the peaceful and mutually beneficial relationship between our governments, we cannot condone your act of war upon our neighbor. The First Joint Defense Fleet will not intervene if they retaliate."
     "It was a condition of our treaty that it would always be at our disposal." Director Camp replies. "Is the treaty at an end?"
     "It is." the Ambassador sighs. "Good day, Director." She turns and leaves the office of the Mesarthrim leader.

=====Delphinus - 0530 Hours=====
     Prefect Anthony Hadrian finally finishes reviewing the reports from Leskovik. He flips on his Ansible Terminal, and keys in a call code. After several buzzes, Robert Marshall appears in the view screen.
     "Admiral, I know you're busy so I'll be brief." Hadrian greets his long-time comrade. "Leskovik was just attacked by the United Centusi States. I'm responding to this act of war in person. I know you're too far out to respond in time, so you now have total command of the Office of the Prefect in my absence."
     Marshall frowns. "I'm not sure I like this. You should wait for my fleet to reinforce you. But I know your mind is made up on this. Give 'em hell sir."
     Hadrian nods. "I'll burn the cowards out of their holes."
     "Will the High Council help us?" Marshal asks. "If the Meroniri join in force, the war will be over in seconds."
     "I don't know. The Council has agreed to convene to hear our case, but with Dayton out of the picture, and both of us on the front lines, I've had to send Admiral Bremen. Professor Hlow is still at the Council on judicial matters, so he'll be advising Bremen. Jason knows the military situation, and Hlow is an eloquent speaker, but even together they're no Dayton."
     "I understand. Well, if you can keep the Mesarthrim bottled up in their own territory for two more days, I'll have the Ikronin vote for you. They owe us a solid, and I'm calling in the debt. Hammare will get us that vote. I guarantee it."
     "I hope you're right. Hadrian out." The view screen shuts down, and Prefect Hadrian rests his head in his hands with a sigh. "Dear God, let this be my last campaign. I'm too old for this."

Caledon - 0600 Hours
     "I propose this plan:" Provost Steven Cue of the Auellal League, a loose collective of Mesarthrim warrior clans, addresses the leaders of the clans. "Our former compatriots in the United Centusi States have attacked the Prefecture. Given the history between Mesarthrim and the Toriming, this war will escalate from mere healthy aggression that will cut out the deadwood and make us strong. It will escalate beyond control and hope of resolution. Therefore, it is in our best interests to end this war soon.
     "We cannot be sure how the Ixoarchada will react. Therefore, we must conglomerate the existing power base. There are still many cells of rebels against the UCS, leftovers from when our Uber-General was killed in action. While they are unworthy of joining our League, they will be useful in undermining the efforts of the UCS. Once the Prefecture's counterstrike force enters UCS territory, we will begin moving our troops to assist them. Are we agreed?"
     "I agree." Petir Tlass, leader of the Black Sheep Clan nods.
     "As do I," says Edwin Finch, the Titans Clan leader.
     "Then it is decided. Make contact with as many rebel cells as you can. It's time this sector remembered the peace of the warrior!"

=====Tavou, High Orbit - 0815 Hours=====
     "Admiral on deck!" A young officer hails as Prefect Hadrian enters the bridge of the Prefecture’s Third Fleet fleet flagship, Delphinus. The crew stand at attention, then return to their duties at a wave of the Admiral's hand. He strides across the bridge, and sits in his accustomed chair, a pivoting high-backed chair with more pop-up screens and holographic projectors than anyone could possibly keep track of at a time. Hadrian likes to know everything about the Delphinus.
     A young ensign walks across the bridge and hands Hadrian a small disc. "Most recent intelligence reports, sir."
     Hadrian takes the disc and inserts it into the chair’s computer. Several screens light up instantly. "Sixty-million." His voice trails off. Even after seeing the destruction caused by two Exodus Wars, and many battles against the Volucris, he isn't completely hardened against the tragedy of the loss of human life.
    "Nuked...millions-dead...planet charred," the words whisk him back--for a moment--to an eerie October morning in 2078, when Blackis suffered the same fate. After a few quick glances, he summons up his fleet registry screen.
     "Third Fleet 75% mobilized, 8th and 12th Tavou Regulars 60% mobilized" the screen flashes as Hadrian scans through information about troop and ship readiness. Hadrian knows that in only a few days his people will once again be at war with the Mesarthrim, although this time, the tyrannical despot is not his own master.
     A quick strike at Mesar seems the most sure-fire way to end the war quickly. All intelligence reports show that the Mesarthrim fleets are spread throughout their own territory, and are focusing on preparations for an invasion of their own. How did it come to this? He wonders. We supported the United Centusi States and recognized them as the legitimate successor to the old United Mesarthrim Clans. Yet, only a few hours ago they destroy the Fidelity without provocation, then take out Leskovik.
     "How long sir?" Hadrian turns to see the same ensign, standing with a questioning gaze.
     "Soon enough." the Admiral reads the emotions clearly etched in the young officer's eyes--familiar emotions experienced by thousands of soldiers in his command. "Your first deployment, Ensign Dobbs?"
    "Yes sir. Just in from New Harbor, Leskovik," he nods, masking his surprise that the Prefect knows him by name.
     "Welcome aboard, Mr. Dobbs." Hadrian smiles. Heturns and leaves the bridge, confident that he has helped the morale of the Delphinus and her crew.

=====Space Station Emerging Horizon - Mesar Orbit - 1100 Hours=====
     Director William Camp of the United Centusi States is holding a meeting of his top three commanders: Dale Myszewski, Arnold Stadt, and Barry Steven.
     "Gentlemen, let me remind you of the inherent superiority of our position," Myszewski begins. "Our fleets currently outnumber the Prefecture Navy by nearly two hundred warships. In fact, their current deployment means that only the Prefecture Third Fleet can engage us at all, giving us a seven to one advantage in numbers."
     "The Third Fleet's admiral will almost certainly strike here first," says Stadt. "If he captures the Emerging Horizon, along with the top brass that are stationed here, he will have achieved his goals little bloodshed."
     "So," Camp says. "We assume that this is the first target of the Prefecture. What do we do?"
     "We rally all of our forces here. Our colony worlds will not be targets of the Prefecture, but our homeworld will be." Stadt replies. "I stake my life on this."
     "Very well," says Camp. "Stadt, you are in charge of the fleets. You and Myszewski will plan the defense, then prepare a counterattack. Steven, your troops are responsible for defending this station from boarders. Get to work."

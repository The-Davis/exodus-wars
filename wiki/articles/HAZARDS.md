*Powerplant: S-M14-B Battery
*Speed: 40 KPH
*Jump Capacity: 250 meters
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament: Hand-Held Weapon--usually Light Autocannon Rifle

===Overview===

With defense of the planet Kalidasa as the primary focus of the Kalidasa Security Force, a heavy infantry battle armor suit was designed for use in urban environments, as well as in low-gravity/zero-G environments off-world.  The HAZARDS, or ''H''igh ''A''gility/''Z''ero-Gr''a''vity Infant''r''y ''D''eployment ''S''uit was designed to fulfill this role. 

===Capabilities===

The HAZARDS is designed for mobility and high jump capacity.  Most of the energy of its battery is for these purposes.  The only weaponry carried by the HAZARDS is in the form of hand-held weapons.  The standard issue weapon for HAZARDS troopers is a Light Autocannon Rifle.  This weapon, essentially a Grizzly Field Gun rigged up as a rifle, is capable of accurately targeting and destroying infantry, light armor, and material targets at extreme ranges.  

[[Image:HAZARDS.jpg]]

As far as powered-armor for Terran-based militaries go, the HAZARDS is rather quick, capable of running at upto 40 kilometers per hour.  The S-M14-B Battery can power the HAZARDS for up to 24 hours, and the batteries are hot-swappable in the field.  The long battery life is due to the fact that the HAZARDS sports no onboard weaponry. 

===Deployment===

HAZARDS were designed for speed and mobility above all.  The armored suit, smaller than comparable Prefecture or Mesarthrim types, has a significant jump capacity, enabling it to scale tall buildings in a single jump.  HAZARDS are seldom employed by the KSF, as they are unneeded.  In the event of an invasion, they would operate in the urban centers of Kalidasan, in the field in support of infantry forces, and in space and on outer-rim colonial and industrial sites, where their low-gravity/zero-G maneuvering capabilities can be put to excellent use.

*Powerplant: BzFA-19oL Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Armor:  Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields:  Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament:
**2 Medium Neutron Beam Cannons
**8 Medium Ordinance Hardpoints
*Communications System:  Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Blazton Target Acquisition Gear

===Overview===

The Uragano is the primary fighter craft of the [[Berlusconi Family Territory]].  A small flying wing aircraft, the Uragano is considered by many to be the most advanced fighter in the [[Sirius Cluster]]. 


===Capabilities===

Like a finely crafted rapier, the Uragano is designed to use speed and precision to destroy and disable enemy craft.  The Uragano's weaponry consists of a pair of fixed Medium Neutron Beam Cannons, useful against light fighters and armored vehicles.  However, its main weaponry is in the form of eight Medium Ordinance Hardpoints.  The hardpoints ordinarily mount anti-aerospace missiles, giving the small, agile fighter a lethality that far exceeds its two cannons.  For planetary aerial patrols, air-to-ground missiles can be mounted, as well as an array of advanced sensors, communications, and utility pods. 

As with most all Terran vehicles, Boron-Nitride armor and a Plasma Barrier/Magnetic Screens combination provide the Uragano with protection, as does a very advanced countermeasures system.  However, it is the Uragano's speed and agility that make up its primary defense.  This speed is provided by a Blazton Industries Reactor. 

===Deployment===

Uraganos exist in much smaller numbers than the Asps of the [[Asanova Family Territory]], but they are operationally superior in every regard.  This trade off of quality for numbers is one of the keys to keeping the balance of the power between the two families.  

Uraganos operate in combined roles, from aerial patrol to customs and trade enforcement in-system.  They also provide escort for the larger Stella Nera and Pugno Morte Bombers in the employ of the Berlusconi. Uraganos are the top fighters in the Sirius cluster, not just by virtue of their superior performance and power statistics over the fighters of the other families, but also by the outcome of the majority of the border skirmishes that happen all-too-often in the [[Sirius Cluster]].

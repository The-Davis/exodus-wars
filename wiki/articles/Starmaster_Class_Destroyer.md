The Starmaster class Destroyer was designed by [[Fireline Industries]] as the lead design in their [[Fireline Industries#Star Series|Star Series]] designs.  The design was built around the concept of mounting heavy weapons on a fast, agile, airframe.  The Starmasters were designed to be the core unit in the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]].  [[Fireline Industries]] established a orbital construction facility witin the safe zone in the [[Cirus System]] and were able to construct Starmaster's unabated.  The first Starmaster, Zs-1, was delivered to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] on August 22nd, [[2275]].

==History==
The ''Star Class'' was [[Fireline Industries]] desperate gamble to save itself from bankruptcy.  In [[2262]], two years after incorporating, [[Fireline Industries]] hadn't sold a major design to the [[United Mesarthim Clans]], all their fighter and tank designs were lackluster compared to [[Mesarbauer]] and [[Mesar Luftfahrt]].  So, rather then mimicking designs, the ''Star Class'' was created as a completely new design.

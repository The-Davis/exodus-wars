Right after "Tempest: Diplomacy"

by Austin Bailey

=====Flagship Delphinus - 0530 Hours=====
     Prefect Hadrian has assembled the Third Fleet. Troop carriers full of battle-ready soldiers wait in deep space near the Tavou district, ready to jump to Centus at a moment's notice, once the Third Fleet has neutralized the Mesarthrim fleet.
     Hadrian is taking a mortally dangerous gamble. Only four days ago, a Mesarthrim warship leveled the Prefecture world of Leskovik, killing everyone on the planet. Now the Admiral plans to make a hyperspace jump into the Centus system, eliminate the orbital defenses, and seize the Mesarthrim leadership at the city of Stone Bridge and aboard the space station Emerging Horizon.
     Hadrian sits uneasily in his command chair on the Delphinus, rereading the most recent intelligence reports on Mesarthrim fleet movements. Interesting, Hadrian thinks. They aren't keeping even a quarter of their strength around Mesar. They keep raiding all through the Tavou District, but our other Fleets are holding them at bay. They're massed at Cirus, but there's only a handful of ships at Tincomalee. I wouldn't attack that world though, not with the Archadians in a joint defense treaty with them.
     The bridge is alerted to a fleet communication. “Sir, all ships are mobilized and ready to jump. Our scouts in Mesarthrim space have returned."
     “Good. I'm going to send a transmission to Ueda. All personnel have half an hour to send a message to their families back home. Standard wartime security rules apply. When we're done, we jump." Hadrian responds.

=====Flagship Delphinus - 0600 Hours=====
     At the predetermined hour, the ships of the Third Fleet disappear from sight, entering the beautiful and mysterious dimension known as hyperspace. The Kovalski-Fahr drive is extraordinarily fast, making the jump to Centus in five minutes.
     "Sensor sweeps," Hadrian orders. "Get me a map of the system. Shields up, bring weapons online." The orders are followed immediately, and soon scanner readouts cover the Admiral’s command chair displays.
     “Looks like nobody’s home. We’ve spotted only a squadron of gunships, about two dozen, docked at the Emerging Horizon.” the Delphinus’ sensor officer reports.
     Initial scans and scouting reports by the Prefecture fleet show a minor United Centusi States naval presence in the system. They're probably raiding in our territory, Hadrian guesses. I hope the other Admirals are holding up.

=====Flagship Delphinus - 0730 Hours=====
     The Prefecture fleet has finished its approach, and is now a mere few hundred thousand kilometers from Mesar and the Emerging Horizon.
     "Launch all fighter.: Hadrian orders. The Third Fleet's carriers literally erupt with activity. Prefecture carrier bays are enormous, enabling them to launch their entire complement in moments. "All ships, move to your designated waypoints." Almost as one, the Third Fleet moves towards the Emerging Horizon.
     "They’re hailing us Sir," Lieutenant Eglen, the communications officer, announces.
     "Ignore it and proceed," Hadrian replies. Looks like we caught them with their pants down, he thinks. This could be done quickly.
     As Hadrian muses, sensor warnings flash all over the bridge--alarms accompany them. The sensors officer looks up from his console. "Sir! We have multiple contacts closing from all vectors. Hyperspace breaches everywhere, too many to count!"
     All hope of a swift victory quickly fades from Hadrian’ mind. The wager’s been made, and it’s all been a bluff. It looks like the Mesarthrim have called, and my hand is low. However, the Admiral does not concede defeat, even in the presence of the full might of the Mesarthrim armada. 
     "Get me a count. How many hostiles?"
     The sensors officer scans his consoles and reports. "It looks like 340 large signatures. Probably 140 capitals and maybe 200 sub-caps. Too many fighters to tally."
     "Sir," Lieutenant Eglen interjects. "They’re hailing us again."
     "I’ll take it on my screen."
     Eglen routes the channel to Hadrian’ console, and his screen is soon filled with the image of Director William Camp, leader of the Mesarthrim.
     "Prefect Hadrian, you have entered United Centusi States sovereign space with the intention of attacking our world. You are outnumbered and flanked on all sides. Our sensors detected your Kovalski-Fahr drive signatures an hour and a half ago, which means you Kovalski-Fahr drives won't be able to make a jump for another four and a half hours. You have no choice but to surrender. If you allow us to board and seize your vessels, and we will let you and your crews leave unharmed."
     "Go to hell." Hadrian spits. "All ships, fire on the Mesarthrim vessels in our jump path. Full retreat!
     "Lieutenant Eglen, warn the troop carriers back on Tavou. If they jump in, it will be a disaster. See if Bremen or Nolan can reinforce us. Let me know if you can get a secure channel to them--we can't trust the normal Ansible network channels in Mesarthrim space.
     "Captain Thomas, I want you to draw up some evasive patterns and route them around. If we can get some distance between us and the Mesarthrim, we can survive until our Kovalski-Fahr drives are recharged."
     Moments tick by as the crew relays information. The ships of the fleet turn, and launch a withering hail of missiles at the Mesarthrim fleet blocking their escape vector. The Mesarthrim vessels are much smaller than Prefecture warships, and scatter out of the way. Still, one gunship is caught by dozens of missiles and is destroyed. Another is crippled and lies dead in space.
     "All ships are on course for the outer rim of the Centus system," Captain Thomas reports.
     "Admiral, we’re getting a call from Admiral Bremen’s fleet. They’re reporting Mesarthrim ships attacking New Albany. Nearly 30 capitals and 30 sub-capitals. Admiral Nolan is reporting 20 capitals in Tavou, and Admiral Sarnou has 10 capitals and 30 sub-capitals in Holt. All of our forces are tied up defending civilian vessels and stations. There are no reinforcements."
     When we get out of this, I'll have the Inquisitors served to the Ologrians for breakfast. Hadrian staggers at the mental arithmetic. They have enough ships here to outnumber the entire Prefecture Navy, and can still strike at our worlds in force!
     A blast rocks the Delphinus. Missile fire from the Mesarthrim Delta frigates is raining in on the Prefecture fleet. It's time to fight.

=====Flagship Judicator - 0800=====
     "Florida, Ingersoll, Endurance--deploy fighters to cover the lunar colony. Cyclone, Guardian, move to support Gorgon and Valley Forge defending those civilian freighters. Parker, take the Patriots and flank the Mesarthrim at these coordinates. Heron…"
     Admiral Jeff Nolan is interrupted as the Judicator shudders under the blast of warship fire that strikes close to the bridge. "Damn these quick bastards. Heron, keep your squadrons with me, and keep those sub-caps off our backs."
     Nolan and the Fifth Fleet are fighting off a United Centusi States raiding force that is attacking civilian targets. Neither side has lost any ships, but the Mesarthrim commander--Admiral Stadt--has a plan that is working to excellent effect. His raiding groups here in Tavou and in the other Prefecture systems keep the Prefecture fleets engaged so they cannot reinforce Hadrian.
     Despite its limited number, the Mesarthrim raiding Tavou are much faster than the Prefecture Fifth Fleet, and lead them on a deadly chase through the system. Little damage is done to the Prefecture military or the civilian population, but it keeps Nolan's Fleet very busy. Finally, the battle gets heated when a direct hit from the Judicator’s main cannon paralyzes a Mesarthrim corvette, and the fleet is forced to turn and fight to save their crippled ship--Prefecture forces don't take prisoners.

=====Flagship Victory - 0830=====
     The United Centusi States ships facing Admiral Marie Sarnou’s Fourth Fleet are having a much rougher time than the Mesarthrim in Tavou. Despite being a larger raiding force, their timing was terrible. Admiral Sarnou’s fighters were conducting live-fire attack drills on derelict freighters when the Mesarthrim jumped in.
     The surprise attack quickly turned in the Prefecture's favor as hundreds of their fighters pounce on the Mesarthrim raiders.
     "Wyoming, Nebraska…move to incoming coordinates with Aeolus, Gage, Mahopac, Amphion, Norris, and Ross. Empty fighter bays…let’s show these bastards who they’re messing with."
     The Prefecture Fourth Fleet is a fighter-heavy fleet, and their fighters are directly commanded by the Prefecture's Sky Marshal, Jan Brooks. They are the best equipped to deal with the threat of sub-capital warships. 
     Within an hour, Jan Brooks and her fighters have destroyed over half the raiding force's sub-capital warships. The Mesarthrim are forced to retreat, despite their plan to tie up Sarnou’s fleet for several hours.
     Sarnou re-deploys her ships to the other systems in the Ward District, hoping to guard against more attacks.

=====Flagship Triumph - 0900 Hours=====
     "I don’t care if they explode and take us all down! You keep firing those cannons until the whole damned ship explodes, or every last Mesarthrim is dead!" Admiral Jason Bremen of the Prefecture Second Fleet screams into his comm link.
     The Triumph took severe damage to its port-side Earthquake cannons when a United Centusi States Sears-Class gunship collided with the flagship.
     "Can't even fly straight, you damned white-eyes!" Bremen growls."Drake and Magnus take the Patriots with you and go after the Mesarthrim cruisers. Regulus, take the Typhoons through the hole we’re about to punch through their defensive wall…go for the carriers. Fire control, target that pair of Deltas up front, full burst from all four cannons, and follow up with Hellguns.”
     The ship rocks slightly as the enormous relativistic mass drivers fire their tiny slugs at the Mesarthrim missile frigates. The lead frigate is instantly disabled when two shells smash into the bridge, destroying the entire conning tower. One shot misses the other frigate, but the fourth strikes it in the lower deck sections. The frigate gets several missile salvos fired, but a Hellgun blast from the Triumph hits a nuclear weapon as it exits the launch tube. The resulting explosion tears the Mesarthrim frigate apart. 
     "Got 'em!" Bremen is cut off as an explosion rocks the ship. The strain on the port guns proves too much, and one of the cannons overheats and explodes. "Damage report!" Bremen orders.
     "Earthquake Four is gone. We have maybe one more shot out of Earthquake Three before it goes. When Four exploded it damaged our engines--we have roughly 65% output, and if we keep straining them it will get worse. There’s a hull breach, but most of the crew made it out and…"
     The engineering officer is interrupted by a call from the sensors officer. "Sir, there are multiple small craft approaching. Transports. They’re going to board us!"
     "Evasive maneuvers! Blow those transports out of the sky! Get me aerospace command! I want a pair of squadrons to keep us clear of boarders." The Triumph begins a roll, trying to pull its port away from the approaching transports while its turrets take aim on the assault craft. Despite the Triumph's evasive action, some transports get through.
     "Four transports are landing troops on port decks 17 and 18."
     "Those are Pegasus transports. They have thirty UCS Myt'herra apiece in standard configuration. Get all our Marines and Battle Armor to those decks. All crew, arm yourselves and prepare to repel boarders." Bremen looses his pistol in his belt, following his own orders.
     The battle aboard the Triumph mirrors the battle in the space around it. Bremen’s hard-hitting Second Fleet has moved to nearly point-blank with the Mesarthrim, and a slugfest is now taking place. The smaller sub-capitals move quickly through the fleets, while the larger ships throw their enormous arsenals at each other.
     "Admiral, the boarders are now on decks 19 and 20. They're making their way towards the center of the ship. We’ve pulled our Marines back to hold choke-points at the corridors leading into the heart of the ship. We have Battle Armor ready to stop them, but it looks like more Mesarthrim made it aboard than we thought--now that they're in undamaged areas of the ship, our scanners show that there are 150 Myt'herra in heavy armor."
     "Seal the bulkheads leading into the port section, and pull our troops back to the central section of the ship. Line us up on that lead Mesarthrim Avenger-Class and give it a blast from Earthquake Three."
     "Admiral...Earthquake Three is about to explode...it would take out most of the port section!"
     "Exactly."
     The Triumph pulls out of its roll and lines up on the lead UCS Avenger, the Wildfire.
     "Fire!" Bremen yells.
     The Triumph lets loose with its three remaining cannons. A single shot from the damaged cannon sends alarms ringing at the engineering station.
     "Again!" Bremen bellows, and one more shot is all it takes. As the Wildfire's shields buckle under the assault, the Triumph's Earthquake Three goes critical. The ensuing explosion tears through the port section of the ship. The engines are knocked out, and the entire section is ripped apart. The few Mesarthrim that aren't killed by the blast are sucked into the void. Most of them are killed by ruptured combat suits. Despite the damage, the bulkheads hold, keeping the Triumph safe from massive explosive decompression--for now.
     "Helm, get us out of here. Grady, Illusive, cover us on our way out." The Triumph limps away from the battle, crippled but still alive. The fierce fighting continues all around them, but the Mesarthrim can't break through the loyal ships of the Second Fleet.
     Finally, after three hours of battle, the surviving Mesarthrim jump out of the system. 
     Just what are the white-eyed bastards up to now? Bremen wonders. "Second Fleet, all heavily damaged ships report to the New Albany shipyards for immediate repair. Undamaged vessels, spread out and patrol probable incoming vectors. If they visit again, make sure we can give them another fiery welcome."

=====Flagship Judicator - 1100 Hours=====
"Admiral, patrols have returned from other systems in the District. The Mesarthrim have jumped out, but we don't know why. There’s no trace of them, but based on their exit vectors, they're probably headed for Cirus, in Mesarthrim-held space."
"Keep me updated. How well did the civilian ships fare?"
"Most were able to escape before the Icronians decided to temporarily shut down our Gate, but thirteen were destroyed, and another eighteen are crippled in space. Our rescue teams have saved most of the crews."
"Good. Engineering, what’s the status of those damaged ships?” 
"Johnston, Lander, and Quick are disabled and being towed to a starport dock for repairs. Jarrett, Graham, Haddock, Mataco, Bancroft, Atlas, Manticore, and Guardian are pretty beat up, but they made it to the dock on their own power. The rest of the fleet is still in fighting condition and on patrol.
"Judicator itself is at about 80% capacity. We’ll need to head to a dock, but we should be fine for now. We confirm four enemy ships destroyed, and seven probable disables."
"So we're down to 75% Fleet strength. With these numbers, we can’t reinforce Hadrian. We have to keep this District secure. When was the last transmission from the Third?"
"Last transmission was over an hour and half ago. They were reporting heavy fighting, but Hadrian countermanded his earlier request for reinforcements. He thinks the United Centusi States are planning another trap"
"Any word from the other Fleets?"
"The First Fleet is still stranded on Icronia, and say it will be at least two more weeks before their drive systems are sufficiently repaired for a jump. The Second Fleet is still short on vessels since their losses when they attacked the Talithe Cume last year. Admiral Bremen’s flagship is out of commission, but the crew survived. The Fourth Fleet nearly wiped out a Mesarthrim raiding group, and took no casualties of their own. The Sixth Fleet reports no enemy contacts."
"I’m going to my quarters. Patch me through to Admiral Bremen and Admiral Sarnou."
"Yes sir."

=====Flagship Delphinus - 1130 Hours=====
The Third Fleet has not been as successful in its fight with the Mesarthrim as the other Fleets have. After their initial retreat to the outer-rim of the Centus system, the Third Fleet has been hounded by the Mesarthrim for hours. Every time the Third Fleet turn to fight, the Mesarthrim pull back and harass them with long range weapons.
The Delphinus’ Ansible uplink stopped working hours ago when the Mesarthrim locked down the network node for the Centus system. Several Third Fleet ships lost their drives in fierce fighting in Centus asteroid belt, keeping the Third Fleet from running any further. The Delphinus has been forced to take the offensive, via its unique subspace drive. 
"Target the lead gunship and take out its engines," Hadrian orders.
"Aye sir, charging cannon."
The Delphinus lines up its Subspace Cannon and fires, creating a rift from Subspace to normal space. The blast strikes an unsuspecting Mesarthrim Trident-Class gunship as it approaches the Prefecture frigate Venture.
Despite the these efforts, the Third Fleet has taken enormous casualties in their attempts to fight the vastly overwhelming Mesarthrim armada that pursues them. 
As the Prefecture ships' Kovalski-Fahr drives reach their peak capacity, the Mesarthrim grow bolder. The United Centusi States fleet flanks the Prefecture fleet when they exit the asteroid belt, and the Third Fleet is caught in a deadly trap.
"Pull out of subspace, we're going to give our ships all the support we can,” Hadrian orders. "Fire all missiles and cannons on the lead cruiser. Launch all fighters, we make our stand here." 
The fighting is so ferocious that in only fifteen minutes, five Prefecture ships are destroyed, along with three Mesarthrim ships. A blast rocks the Delphinus, and warnings come up on the bridge. Of all the systems to lose at this point, they've lost the most vital: the Kovalski-Fahr drive.
"Let’s try to pull them off of the fleet! Make a run for Mesar, let’s make them chase us." The Delphinus pulls away from the bulk of the Fleet, blasting through the Mesarthrim fleet at top speed. "We can’t get away, but we’ll make sure the rest of the fleet can."

As the Delphinus pulls away, Commandant Arnold Stadt, aboard the Tunguska commands half his fleet to follow Hadrian. "Take the Delphinus alive if you can, gentlemen,: Stadt orders.

This relieves the pressure on the Prefecture fleet, and they are able to break out of the trap and put some distance between themselves and the capital ships of the United Centusi States fleet. 
"Get me Captain Vasquez on the Ardent,” Hadrian orders.
"This is Vasquez. What are your orders, sir?"
"Captain, you have command of the fleet. We’re making an attack run on the Emerging Horizon, and it will force the Mesarthrim to to follow us. Your drives should be charged, so as soon as you're clear, make the jump home."
"Admiral, what about you?"
"I lost my Kovalski-Fahr drive. We won’t be coming home."
"You’re a hero, Admiral."
"Record and deliver these orders. Effective immediately, your rank is Vice Admiral. Make sure Marshall knows what went wrong. He'll know what to do."
The critical moment comes--the Third Fleet's Kovalski-Fahr drives are charged, and the survivors of the massacre jump out, leaving the Delphinus alone to face its fate. Vice Admiral Vasquez must return to deliver the terrible news: Admiral Hadrian will not return.

=====Flagship Delphinus - 1115 Hours=====
With the Third Fleet safely away, the Delphinus is now the lone target of the wrath of the Mesarthrim armada.
"Target the Emerging Horizon," Hadrian orders. "Prepare to enter subspace. Fire control, load high-yield nukes and fire on the station. We drop into subspace before they can catch us."
"Missiles away!"
"Good. Get us into subspace. We’ll be safe there."
The Delphinus begins its subspace dive, but is hit by several shots from Mesarthrim gunships. The power regulation systems are hit, and the drive systems shut down. The Delphinus barely finishes its shift before its left dead in subspace.
"Report! What happened?"
"We’ve lost the Diver sir. We lost power regulation and a power spike burned out the gravity drive and the Diver Engine."
"How long until it's repaired?"
"Engineering is looking into it, but I don't think we can. We essentially have to replace everything, and we simply don't have the parts."
Admiral Hadrian sits back in his chair, realizing he has just run out of options. "Cancel red alert and stand down. Activate a distress beacon." It's probably useless in subspace, but I can't let the crew give up hope yet.
"Admiral, what are we going to do?"
"We’re going to relax until I think of something. Given our velocity, we’ve drifted enough since we came into subspace that they won't be able to hit us with anything short of an Omega bomb--and they probably won't try that in their own starsystem. It's too dangerous. For now, all crew members should relax. Get something to eat, take a nap, and work on the drive systems. We have enough supplies for a year. I’ll be in my quarters.” 
Hadrian retires to his suite, and collapses on the simple cot. This is how it ends? he wonders. After 260 years, it’s finally my time to go. After countless wars and battles, I’ll die with no fighting, no battle, no going down with my ship…just drifting through subspace till we run out of supplies. I just hope that Marshall will succeed where I failed. 
For the first time he can recall, Hadrian relaxes completely. For him, the war is over--war has been his life for decades. When was the last time I could just relax like this? It must have been back when I was on Blackis, before...he sighs. In his mind's eye, he sees the night sky, still knowing all the star formations, and marveling at the beautiful blue moon whose rays of pale light brighten the pond that lies behind his family’s home. Hadrian dreams for hours, getting the best sleep he can remember. The nightmares that have haunted him ever since the long cold sleep do not revisit him, and he is at peace.

=====Flagship Delphinus - 1520 Hours=====
"Admiral, come quickly! We picked up something on the sensors!"
Hadrian wakes instantly, but can swear he is still able to smell the sweet fragrance of Torimur's night breeze. "I’ll be right there."
Hadrian arrives on the deck of the Delphinus and checks his command screens. Sure enough, at maximum sensors range there are three warships. They aren't the faint gravity signatures of the Mesarthrim passing by in normal space. These are hard signatures in subspace. 
"Can we get a visual?"
"We can, but they're so far away that they'll look like dots. Our visual sensors aren't telescopes. They're closing fast though." Tense moments pass as the ships approach. Finally, Lieutenant Eglen breaks the silence. "We have a visual. SPAR doesn't recognize the class of vessel, but they have Archadian markings."
"This is...unexpected." Hadrian says.
"We’re being hailed," says Lieutenant Eglen. 
"Attention unidentified vessel. This is Commander Obarin Parth of the United Archadian Navy corvette Pollonasi. We have picked up your distress call. Please respond."
"This is Admiral Anthony Hadrian of the Prefecture Galactic Navy. You are entering a combat zone. We have engaged the Mesarthrim forces, and by helping us you may be committing an act of war against the United Centusi States."
"We are responding to an emergency distress beacon in accordance with United Archadian Republic directive 11.18.2230 B. As per the directive, any ship in distress, regardless of nationality or territory, is to be aided by any nearby Archadian Naval vessel."
"Glad to hear it. We definitely appreciate the help. Our Diver Engine, gravity drives and Kovalski-Fahr drive are all fried and we're adrift. If you can help us with repairs, we can get out of here."
"I’m sorry Admiral, but most of our technologies are incompatible. We can't help you with repairs. What I can do is take you and your crew to whatever Prefecture system you like. If you can stabilize your position with thrusters, we can fetch your ship later with a proper repair crew. Is this acceptable?"
"Definitely. Thank you. If you pull below us, our universal docking collar should allow you to connect and we can get crew out."
"Affirmative Admiral. Prepare for docking."

=====Space Station Emerging Horizon - 1800 Hours=====
"Captain Parker, where is Hadrian?" Director Camp demands, his voice full of cold fury. "How did the Delphinus manage to escape the entire fleet?"
"We had them targeted, and fired a salvo. We know it knocked out their drives, but they made it into subspace. They should be right where they were when they made the dive."
"This is unacceptable. We have no way of detecting them in subspace. Commandant Stadt, is there any way we can secure an Archadian vessel with a subspace scanner to search the area?"
"Negative. The Archadians froze all fleet movements when we began hostilities against the Prefecture, and they won't let us bring the First Joint Defense Fleet into the Centus system. However, like Captain Parker said, the Delphinus is dead in subspace. The only way to guarantee destruction is to use an Omega bomb.”
"Detonate an Omega bomb within our own system?" Camp frowns. "Detonation of an Omega device within our star system could cause a hazard to intersystem travel."
"The location is off the main routes, and there isn't any mining in the area. We can place buoys to keep civilians away."
"If it gets Hadrian out of the way, then proceed. But be careful."
Half an hour later, a Mesarthrim gunship drops a single Omega bomb, and turns and runs. Seconds later, a blast tears through all planes of space and time, ripping into existence itself. The explosion is not visible, as it absorbs what light is in the area. The only sign of the detonation in normal space is the vaporizing of a nearby asteroid.

=====UAN Pollonasi - 1830 Hours=====
In subspace, several thousand kilometers away, the crew of the Delphinus watches from the Archadian corvettes as a bright light emanates from a dark orb in space. In subspace, the rift is as visible as the sunset on Terra, and it burns with glowing fury into the darkness of subspace. The Delphinus sits silent and still as the wave of energy approaches it slowly, and finally consumes the ship with a terrible blast of light.
"Half the Fleet, and my flagship..." Hadrian stares out the viewport in his guest suite on the Pollonasi, reflecting on more ships lost under his command, adding names and faces to the nightmares in his mind. “Times never change, just the names of the battles. Peace won't come in my time."
For a few hours, the Archadian corvettes make their way through subspace to the outer-reaches of the system. They return to normal space just long enough to set coordinates, then activate their Ramius-Dawkins drives to jump to Tavou.
We shall return. Hadrian thinks.

=====Mesar - Broad Communications Channel - 1900 Hours=====
Once again, the inhabitants of Mesar watch their view screens and listen to audio channels as Director Camp gives a press conference on public Mesarthrim channels, still restricting the message from the Ansible system. 
"People of the United Centusi States…this day, your Navy has raided enemy systems and attacked their fleets. More importantly, we have decimated a Prefecture fleet that entered our system in an attempt to attack the people of Mesar! We destroyed more than half the fleet before it fled. When they ran, they left their flagship behind. Admiral Hadrian was on the flagship, and when he tried to surrender, we destroyed his warship. Such cowardice. They attack us, then try to surrender. With Hadrian gone, the Prefecture will be ours! We will be victorious!"

=====Tincomalee - 1945 Hours=====
“Ambassador Sveto, the war has begun in earnest. There is no turning back. Mesarthrim fleets are raiding Prefecture territory, attacking civilian targets. What’s worse, I have reports that the Prefecture Third Fleet was destroyed in the Centus system, and Admiral Hadrian is dead. I will make my case before the People of the Pen later today to cease this madness. If they will not listen, war will soon come to Tincomalee too. I pray for all of us."

*Capital: Beme, Switzerland, [[Terra]]

*Government: Direct Democracy
*Formation: August 1, [[1291]]
*Dissolved: August 2, [[2060]]
*Population: 8 million (estimated 2060 level)

'''Switzerland''' was one of the few traditionally neutral nations of [[Terra]]. It provided a haven to all during both [[World War I]] and [[World War II]]. During [[World War III]], the Swiss sealed their borders against Muslim incursion.

As a neutral nation, Switzerland refused to join the unified world government until later [[2060]].

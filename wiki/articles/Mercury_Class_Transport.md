The '''Mercury Class Transport''' was the mainstay troop and war materiel transport used by the [[Onyx Empire]] and later by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

The [[Vigilant Class Frigate]] was the backbone of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], but it was only designed to engage enemy warships and fighters in space. Other than defensive Marine complements, the Vigilants had no troops.

[[Image:Mercury Class Transport Side View.png|200px]]

Transporting troops was vital for conquering new territories. The Mercury Class Transport was envisioned to deliver troops by drop-pods, and had a welldeck installed for dropships and landing craft.

The Mercury had berthing for an entire Imperial Army Battalion--31,000 infantry and their supporting Rigs or combat vehicles. The welldeck was installed in the forward section of the cruiser, and worked as a massive airlock, where troops could board dropships for boarding ships or landing on planets. There were high-velocity drop-pods installed in multiple strategic locations throughout the ship, allowing the Mercuries to deploy troops as quickly as they could board the pods.

[[Image:Mercury Class Transport Top View.png|200px]]

The first Mercury Class Transport was completed in [[2305]], and was given the hull designation AK-1. Every ship in the class was named "HMS ''Mercury''", plus its hull designation number. Nine hundred transports were built between 2305 and [[2323]].

The Mercury transports were not designed to fight enemy ships, and only had minimal [[Close-In Weapon System|CIWS]] defenses. Without their escorts, they usually fled into [[hyperspace]], surrendered, or attempted to deliver their troop payloads so the [[Royal Imperial Army]] could take the fight to the ground. If their cargo was delivered, Mercuries were considered expendable by the Empire.

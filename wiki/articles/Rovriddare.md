===Overview===

The Rovriddare is a capable light fighter, similar to the Valkyrie in speed and maneuverability, though definitely not weaponry.  It is very fast, and very capable in most combat situations.

[[Image:Rovriddare1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The arsenal of the Rovriddare is light.  It carries two Lansvapen energy cannons, one of the few Ikronin weapons not based on ammunition.  With its speed and maneuverability, it can catch and shoot down light enemy targets with these weapons.

For tougher enemies, the Rovriddare has a small missile arsenal.  Two externally-mounted Engång Missiles can crack the hulls of tougher heavy fighters, but for bombers multiple Rovriddares must work together to overwhelm and destroy them.

Protection of the Rovriddare comes from Proton-Bond armor and Repulsor Shields, but both are thin and low-powered.  The evasive abilities and speed of the Rovriddare is its main defensive ability.

===Deployment===

[[Image:Rovriddare2.jpg]]

The role of the Rovriddare is scouting and harassment.  It is deployed in high numbers, and can be anywhere in Ikronin territory within minutes, via Wormhole Gates.  Because of its effectiveness, Consortium leaders encourage pilots to purchase their own fighters and maintain them on their own, but the Skratta Måsen is more popular among Flyer Corps pilots wealthy enough to purchase their own fighters.

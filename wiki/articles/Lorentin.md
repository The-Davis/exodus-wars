'''Lorentin''' is the language of the [[Anthronoris]], specifically of [[Pax Anthronoris]] and the [[Remnant]]. It bears a remarkable similarity to the [[Terran]] language [[Latin]]. More specifically, [[Latin]] bears a similarity to Lorentin. Latin was actually introduced to [[Terra]] by [[Romulus Lupus]], an [[Anthronoris]] who froze himself in cold sleep for millenia beneath the surface of [[Terra]] to escape the [[Sundering]].

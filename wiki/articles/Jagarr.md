'''Jagarr''' is the second planet in the [[Hamur System]]. Jagarr was the first colony of the Lorithos, founded in [[2300]].

== Geography ==

Few people reside on Jagarr. It's used mostly for storage of extra equipment and non-perishables--very few thieves care to risk the environment and defense systems, so everything stays safe. 

The only inhabitable world is arid and uninviting to most people, but a veritable paradise compared to the wastelands of [[Lorithan]]. A thorough survey has never been completed of Jagarr, but it is known that the low metal content keeps heavy plants from growing. A large plankton population in the ocean keeps the air breathable, but the climate is hot and dry away from the shores. 

The only city is the spaceport of [[Verjas]], a bare four kilometers from the shoreline. The spaceport serves mostly as a shore leave location for the crews of the mining ships in the Jagarr asteroid belts, and for the crew of the [[Mihter Ujak]] space station in orbit of the planet.

In addition to Verjas, there are multiple military storage facilities, where weapons left over from the [[Burning Wars]] are brought as soon as they are excavated from Lorithan. Most material, once identified, is hurled into Jagarr's star. Material that cannot be identified immediately is stored to determine if it is one of the many festering plagues among the Lorithos population, and kept to develop effective vaccinations and antidotes.

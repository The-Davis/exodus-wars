'''Kyra Olson'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Date of Death: February 12, [[2323]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Place of Death: [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Kyra Olson'' was a pilot for the [[Onyx Empire]], raised from the [[Alpha Centauri]] cloning project and sent through the flight academy. She was a decent pilot, scoring several dozen kills before the outbreak of the [[Imperial Civil War]]. She joined the [[Prefecture]] and fought in several battles before being shot down and killed by [[Emil Torsc]] on the last day of the war.

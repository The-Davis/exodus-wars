The Royal Imperial Army's Apollo model was a rare sight--only four were ever built, but it had an almost mystical aura about it that made Rig pilots fall in love with it. The design never made it to full production due to the expensive and difficult-to-produce shield systems.

The project was never abandoned. Martin Bradshaw, an Imperial scientist who studied under the Remnant during their short stay in the [[Orion Arm]], continued the project in Lucius Black's Mount McKinley stronghold--up until the final assault on Terra by the [[Alliance of Planets]]. There, the Apollo Mk.II project was abandoned and later picked up by the Prefecture.

Martin Bradshaw escaped with the Emperor, and started the project up again from his notes after the reformation of the Empire in the form of the Conclave.

Duplicating his work on Mount McKinley was hopeless. Bradshaw simply didn't have the Remnant technology or even the [[Empire|Imperial]] copies he'd been working on. Instead, the more advanced components were rebuilt based on Ngrligru technology, dug out of the bowels of the Bahari Shela.

The new Rig was called the Bradshaw. Not because Martin Bradshaw was particularly vain, but because his superiors and the Conclave Rig pilots kept referring to it as "that Bradshaw Rig." The name stuck.

For main weapons, the Bradshaw has a turret, similar to its predecessor, the Apollo. Instead of the old 2080-era "Thumper" Autocannon, the Bradshaw uses Meroniri Coilguns. These weapons were once a rarity, but with the formation of the Meroniri Expeditionary Fleet, many previously-rare Meroniri technologies have become commonplace on the open market.

Melee combat isn't part of the Bradshaw's arsenal. The original Apollo Mk.II project was supposed to include them, but the Bradshaw's role changed from heavy mainstay fighter to hit-and-run raider. Instead, mid-range missile launchers were installed to give it indirect hitting power.

Even Martin Bradshaw couldn't figure out the original prototype's power core. According to [[Remnant]] physicists, it was based on the Meroniri Matter-to-Energy Conversion bomb's reaction processes, but Bradshaw never saw any schematics before the Remnant disappeared with all of their knowledge. A simpler Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor was installed to replace it. The Conclave also added jump jets for extra mobility.

The Bradshaw carries the original Apollo's shield system. It was called the Apollo Field, but is now known as the Vapor-wraith screen. It's based on Meroniri Matter Deflection system, one of the best defense systems known.

Martin Bradshaw finished building his Rig in late April of [[2324]]. The [[Omegas]] raiders received enough to fully equip their Rig pilots. Only a handful of the [[Royal Imperial Army]]'s aces survived the long, bloody [[Battle of Terra]].

Bradshaw's are dropped straight into battle, where they smash ground convoys or seize supply dumps. They almost never directly engage strong defenses, and always run from concentrated counterattacks.

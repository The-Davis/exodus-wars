The '''Technology Race''' was a competition of space exploration between the [[British Empire (Terra)| British Empire]], [[United States (Terra)| United States of America]], and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]], which lasted from [[1946]] to [[2011]]. It involved the efforts to develop technology as far as it could possibly be push. The fields of computer technology, medicine, power generation, and space exploration were all the main focus of the Technology Race.

Though its roots of the Technology Race lies in early rocket technology, early computer technology, and the international tensions following [[World War II]].

The Technology Race effectively began after the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet]] launch of Sputnik 1 on October 4, [[1957]].

The Technology Race became an important part of the cultural, technological, and ideological rivalry between the superpowers during the [[Cold War]]. Space technology became a particularly important arena in this conflict, both because of the potential military applications and due to the morale-boosting psychological benefits.

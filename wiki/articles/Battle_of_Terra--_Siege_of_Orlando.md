September 20, 2322

by James Davis

=====Dawn=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Fire''. Travis Carpenter gazes across the field. The ancient ruin before him holds no significant value, other than the still-intact super-highway running through it. ''Dawn looks like fire. Storms will come tonight''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"So this is Orlando. It's been almost three hundred years since this old haunt was abandoned." says Brigadier Hayes, startling Carpenter out of his introspection. "Muslims lobbed an R-Bomb into the center of the city, killed everyone and left the buildings intact. The Americans, the NAA, the UEA, and finally the Empire kept the road repaired, but no one bothered with the buildings."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Good history lesson." Carpenter grunts. "Any other military history?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Not so much as a skirmish." Hayes replies. "Most of the ground fighting in World War III took place farther north, and the Empire made all its landings in the mid-western parts of the continent."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No strategies from the past to save us then." The general sighs. "Has Rathbone been replaced yet?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir. Jack Ivonrud received command of the aerospace just this morning. He has three squadrons--two Stuka and one Raven--all primed and ready at Canaveral." Gabriel Hayes pauses for a long time before continuing. "Did you read the message from the Grand Admiral?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"He's just the Admiral now." Carpenter shrugs. "I heard the talk in the ranks, but I didn't read it. Marcus is dying, isn't he?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Both officers stand in silence, staring at the ghost town in the distance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What do you think the odds are, General? Can we win?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"'Bout four to one in their favor." Carpenter replies. The unanswered question hangs like an ominous shadow. ''Can we win''? "Gabe."
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sir?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Give the order."<br>

=====Orange Trail=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The column of tanks stretches for miles. The dusty road is lined with rows of overgrown, unkempt orange trees, a relic from the agricultural heyday of the area.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Brigadier Gabriel Hayes leads the First and Second Tank Division, the bulk of which are powerful Enforcers, universally favored by both Blackis factions' commanders. The tanks had set out at the command of General Carpenter, and were entering the city from the southeast.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A loud report of gunfire breaks out at the head of the column. "Sniper!" Panic bursts through the ranks like a swarm of angry bees, and is promptly caught and neutralized by the iron vice of discipline.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hayes jumps from the Enforcer he'd been hitching a ride from. He runs, crouching low to the ground. "Return fire! General spread, shrapnel!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first Enforcer in the column bursts into flame. The first shot shattered the cockpit and killed the pilot. The second was a breecher, the deadly high-explosive shell used on Imperial rifles. Within seconds, black smoke has completely obscured the first five tanks in the convoy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Automatic weapons cut into the obscured tanks, and the lumbering war machines vomit their hot payloads with devastating results. Trees ignite, and some fly into the air as the shells land. Imperial troops scream as they succumb to heat, and the metal and wood shrapnel.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Berman!" Brigadier Hayes yells for his second-in-command, in charge of the Second Tank Division. He leaps up on one of the Enforcers, pounding on the cockpit. "Where's Colonel Berman?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The pilot opens up the cockpit, and waves towards the front of the column. "Twentieth tank. He should be alive, but he'll be in the thick of the fighting."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Hayes runs ahead. The tank column has stopped dead. The Empire will bring in tanks of their own, or even Mechanized Divisions if we don't get on the move again. He checks his communicator. I don't like this. The Empire will be listening in, but there's no helping it.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Fleet command, this is Brigadier Gabriel Hayes, Phoenix Legion. Do we have satellite imagery of this area?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There is a short delay before the fleet responds. Hayes takes cover between two stationary Enforcers. "This is the Montgomery. Negative Brigadier, the Empire currently has space superiority over your location. As this is not a secure link, I cannot give you any information on our fleet locations."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This grove could be infested with anti-aircraft missiles, but I don't have a choice. Hayes changes frequencies. "Ivonrud, this is Brigadier Gabriel Hayes, Phoenix Legion. I want a napalm strike. I'm sending you coordinates." If they hear that, they'll be running.

=====Old Interstate=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Convenient things, Interstates." says General Carpenter. "At least when someone remembers to repair the damned things." The general stands in the cockpit of his red and grey Hades, the cockpit open as he surveys a thirty-foot gap in the road, where the tower of an old office building has crushed the pavement.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I thought the highway was in good repair." says Colonel Bennett, commander of the Second Mechanized Division. "Or is this new damage? The Empire hasn't tried to destroy any infrastructure here in the Americas yet, just the Asian territories."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Wrong road." Carpenter replies. "This was known as Interstate 4, which was a thoroughfare between the western gulf ports and the northern Atlantic ports. We're after another highway--I don't remember if the Americans called it an Interstate or not, but it's a direct line to the main road north and northwest." Travis Carpenter sits again, and seals the Hades' cockpit. "Time for a detour. Split into Company-sized units, and head into the city. Remember, we hook up with the tanks and infantry at Checkpoint Zeta. Destroy all targets of opportunity, but take any prisoners you can--I want info on the northern roads."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Four Mechanized Divisions take a long time to break up into Companies. The Legion disperses into the city.<br>

=====Ruined Airport=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cerberus Recon Company picks gingerly through an ancient compound and maze of rusted aircraft. It has been an hour since they were ordered ahead of General Carpenter's main group.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Fascinatin' buncha wrecks, eh Tom?" Captain Tyra Doyle chirps merrily from her Bushwhacker. "Y'know, the Admiral's grandfather lived round about these parts."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Captain, may I respectfully remind you of our deadline?" Lieutenant Tom Andrews sighs at his superior's attitude. ''She just got transferred here because the Ice Hawk wiped out her Company. Then the General goes and promotes her when Brigadier Hayes got called up to be the Legion's number two.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There's time 'nough." Tyra laughs. "General says there's s'posed to be a group of Imperial Rigs moving in from the north. I think we can intercept them if we hang around a bit. Don't you want a piece a' action?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"As you command." Lieutenant Andrews complies. ''Hayes would agree with me. The rest of the Company agrees with me. They just don't want to speak up.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Man, did the Emperor's sci-fi boys drop a bottle of boring in the tank when they mixed you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm a natural-born native of Bethija, not one of the Alpha Centauri clones." Andrews retorts solemnly. ''How in God's name did she get to be a Captain''?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Well I am, and proud of it!" Tyra crows. "Or an artificial-mix, I forget which. Either way, I'm glad to be alive."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Heads-up Boss, I got a blip." Lieutenant James Bell's crisp voice breaks up the conversation between the commander and second of Cerberus Company. "Looks like a Mechanized Battalion, Mediums and Lights. The Empire probably has Heavies and Assaults on the way too."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Showtime!" Captain Doyle whoops. "OK, time to be serious. Pick targets, but don't lock on yet. Check your long-range weapons, then we saturate 'em on my signal. Move in after two volleys and engage up close. We have the weight advantage."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The unwary Imperial Rigs continue their way towards the city. Suddenly, the air around them is alive with missile fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Move in! It's killing time!"<br>

=====Orange Trail=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Whether they were oblivious or suicidal, the Imperial troops did not disperse, not even when the roar of Stuka engines could be heard in the distance. "Last chance Brigadier." Jack Ivonrud's voice calls over the radio. "Three seconds till we drop, then God himself can't call 'em off."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Burn them." Brigadier Hayes confirms. It's a shame to burn down this grove, but there are lives at stake. "Enforcers, prepare for heat."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The tanks cease their firing spread in the trees, and begin moving at full speed. The disabled and fire-gutted tanks are left behind, or even crushed under the treads of Enforcers coming up behind. Hayes climbs aboard an Enforcer, and squeezes in behind the pilot.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bombs fall. Precision guidance lands them well away from the Enforcer convoy. The ancient trees ignite, sending up a sweet smell of orange sap. The smell is soon tainted by screams and burning human flesh.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While its hot enough to give men third-degree burns, the tanks are unaffected by the raging inferno. At high speeds, the air moving across their heat sinks keeps the machinery cool enough to function. The convoy speeds away, leaving behind the dead and dying.

=====City Hall=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;One final burst of laser fire from Carpenter's Hades ends the protesting rifle fire from the Imperial infantry. "Round up any of the scum that are still moving." The General orders. "Bennett! Get some Rig pilots on guard duty. Fireflies will do--I want the big guns pointing away from our strong point."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir!" the Colonel replies. "Sir, I just got a coded burst from the infantry Divisions. They've met heavy resistance coming up the main corridor, but Brigadier Hayes and the tanks will be within shelling range soon--it should be mopped up soon enough."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ten minutes ago wouldn't be soon enough." Carpenter snaps. "I want three Regiments to head south and help them out. Mediums and Heavies should do it."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Right-o sir. Shall I accompany them?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes. Before you go, any word from my Cerberus Company?" Carpenter frowns as he speaks. "We need concrete information on the northern parts of the city. It's already swarming with Imperial troops, and there will be more on the way."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sorry sir, no word."

=====Ruined Airport=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Die already, damn you!" Tyra's Bushwhacker rocks as the narrowly-avoided Gauss slug turns tarmac into crater just behind her. The Imperial Shadow Cat comes into view again, only for a fleeting second. "Eat lead!" Captain Doyle's autocannon barks, and the Shadow Cat staggers, one leg's mechanisms shot to pieces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Retreating to a safe distance, Tyra fires a salvo of laser bursts into the crippled Rig, turning it into a fireball. The Prefecture Rig pilot smiles when she realizes that the enemy cockpit is still sealed--the Imperial is dead. The emergency coolant system kicks in--too late for the pilot, though the toxic fumes would have killed him anyway--but not too late to avert the disastrous reaction of the fusion core going critical.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bloodlust fades, and Tyra Doyle relaxes somewhat. The Shadow Cat she just finished off is the only Rig in sight. The rest of Cerberus Company has been carried off by the tide of battle, but the Bushwhacker's sensors show them grouped together in twos and threes, pressing the attack to the north.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Just me then''. Captain Doyle brings the Bushwhacker to a halt for a moment, checking the spine-jack where her nerves give the Rig its balance and direction. "Stupid plug chafes." she complains. Out of the corner of her eye, Tyra notices movement.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She sees the slug before it hits. The magnetically-propelled Gauss weapon bites deeply into her Bushwhacker's left arm. ''Oh crap''! The Rig pilot squeezes the left-arm weapon trigger as hard as she can, and the LRM tubes fire wildly into the air. A dozen rounds successfully fly off to targets unknown, then the damaged weapon explodes.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''If the magazine was full, I'd be dead''. Tyra puts the Bushwhacker into a mad dash, leaving the ruined arm behind. Only then she notices the two additional Gauss hits--both superficial, but hits all the same.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bushwhacker comes to a brief halt in a hangar. Doyle halts between two rusted aircraft, and surveys the battlefield. Three Shadow Cats prowl the ancient tarmac. ''Good. All Primes. They only have a few Gauss shots each before they have to go running for home''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The cornered Rig pilot waits, contemplating her next move. ''I sat and watched before, and the Ice Hawk slaughtered my whole Company. I won't let Cerberus be wiped out on my watch.'' She checks the Rig's automatic damage control systems. The Bushwhacker has sealed the ammo bin breach, and rerouted the arm-mounted LRM ammo to the shoulder-mounted LRMs. ''As good as can be hoped''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Captain brings the Bushwhacker to a run, bursting out of the hangar with a hail of laser fire. "Bring it on! I'll find a way to win!" she screams into her communications system, routing it to the Bushwhacker's loudspeaker--a pointless gesture, but one that brought her a measure of confidence.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Almost as one, the Shadow Cats turn to meet the mad rush. Almost as one. Three against one is overwhelming odds, if the three work together. Ace Rig pilots who work together for years can act as a cohesive unit at all times. These are not ace Rig pilots.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first Shadow Cat charges ahead, blocking the firing lines of the other two. Tyra blasts the Cat's torso with a quick burst of laser fire, then sidesteps out of the way of the returning Gauss blast. The maneuver brings her into position for a kill shot from the other Shadow Cats, but only for a split second. They fire and miss, hitting the empty space occupied by the fast-moving Bushwhacker moments before.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Using the temporary distance to her advantage, Tyra squeezes off a rapid pair of salvos with the LRM launcher. The missiles fly true, catching the first Cat dead in the "face" of the squat Rig. The cockpit buckles under the first three hits, and melts away to the heat of the fourth. The remaining six cook the Rig pilot inside to an unrecognizable lump. Lacking the guiding sense of balance from the pilot inside, the Rig collapses in a heap, adding an obstacle for the Shadow Cats behind it.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By now, the Imperial Rig pilots have recognized the serious flaw in their Gauss Rifles. The weapons were based on a remarkably powerful magnetic propulsion system with near-100% kinetic energy efficiency, but they were SLOW. Preparing a new slug for firing was like an assembly line run by tortoises. They wisely switched to their faster-firing, but less-powerful laser weapons.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Doyle allowed them to score a couple laser hits. She recognized the rookie mistakes they were making--focusing entirely on one weapon--but didn't want to deal with truly desperate Rig pilots at two-to-one odds. She hit the Rig's supercharger, giving it a brief burst of speed. ''Distance will kill the power of those lasers altogether''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial Shadow Cats are faster than a Bushwhacker at normal speeds, and they have superchargers too. The Rig pilots soon remembered this, and moved in to close the gap. ''Surprise''! Tyra thinks, swinging the Bushwhacker around into a collision course with the closer of the two Shadow Cats.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial rookie panics, firing his Gauss Rifle and lasers, but misses badly. Tyra fires off a quick LRM salvo at the other Shadow Cat, to discourage interference. With seconds before collision, Doyle cuts loose with everything the Bushwhacker has, then veers away. The Shadow Cat falls flat, both legs crippled by laser and autocannon fire. Doyle lets three LRM salvos finish the motionless Shadow Cat.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Now the advantage is mine''. Tyra takes careful aim at the last Shadow Cat, and lets loose another salvo of LRM fire. She squeezes the trigger again. ''No ammo''? ''The simplest of rookie mistakes''! She pays for it when the Shadow Cat's next Gauss shot smashes into the shoulder-mounted LRM launcher. The Bushwhacker is carried to the ground by the tremendous kinetic energy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial charges, firing his lasers as he waits for his Gauss Rifle to arm itself again. Tyra struggles to pick her crippled Rig off the ground. One shot at point-blank range, and the Captain of Cerberus Company won't even have remains.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bushwhacker lurches into an upright position, and the terrified Tyra Doyle finds herself so close to the Imperial Rig that she can see the equally frightened Rig pilot inside. Desperate, Doyle pushes the throttle to full, and rams the Shadow Cat. The Gauss Rifle fires harmlessly into the air.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two surgical blasts of her lasers rip the Gauss Rifle from the Shadow Cat's chassis. Before the Imperial can aim his lasers, Doyle smashes open the cockpit with her autocannon, firing three quick rounds. ''It's all over''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Tyra is wrong. A series of rumbles and whines from within the tortured machinery of the Shadow Cat, and a sudden rise in temperature and radiation are the first signs of a most remarkable reaction. The Captain notices her Bushwhacker's Geiger counter ticking wildly, then picking up speed into a low mechanical whir. "Uh oh."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Bushwhacker fires a fourth time, dislodging its right arm from the wreck that was an Imperial Rig, then Doyle turns and puts it into a run. The supercharger kicks in, and the Bushwhacker pounds across the tarmac so fast that its legs are almost a blur.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Seconds later, the Shadow Cat's reactor goes critical. Light brighter than the sun lights up the battlefield, and a roar so loud that it seems like the absence of sound fills the air. The hangars that once belonged to an ancient shipping company are completely wiped off the map, along with all trace of the scared and rapidly retreating Captain Doyle's victory.

=====City Hall=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Carpenter's plan has proceeded with few flaws. His entire Legion, save a few casualties, has cleared half of the ancient city, and set up a strong point in the center, near a shattered glass tower and an oddly intact government building.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Empire will probably make two attempts at stopping us here." the General explains. "Once with light mobile troops, and a second time with full strength--that means air support. Cecil Au-Yueng has command of these troops. That means the Royal Imperial Army's Fifth or Sixth Guard will be involved. They're good. If you think we can't beat them, say so, and I'll seriously consider withdrawing."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I stand by you General." Brigadier Hayes speaks first. As always, he stands by his commander. From training, to Cerberus Company, and to the Legion of the Phoenix Travis Carpenter and Gabriel Hayes have been united in word and deed. "My men and I will meet the first assault if we stay, and we'll volunteer for rear guard if we retreat."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I think we can take them." agrees Colonel Berman. Old Joe Berman is a veteran, a Bethija-born man with years of military service in the UEA even before the Empire came. "But I don't trust our fortifications. The Empire had weeks to prepare for us, and they were pushed out in less than a day. I say we move ahead and meet them in the field, and leave this ruin to the ghosts."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Field, city, or hell itself won't make a difference." Colonel Bennett growls. The stark contrast of Kyle Bennett is incredible. In battle, he is calm. He is polite and has his thoughts completely organized. Off the field, he is fire in man's form. "Double the number of enemies, and we'll still win. Just give the order and we'll drive them all the way back to McKinley, and shove the whole bloody Sixth Guard up the Emperor's ass."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Carpenter nods, considering. Then he switches his communication system off, and leans back in the Hades' cockpit to think without interruptions. After a few minutes, he tunes his system to a wide-area broadcast. "Phoenix Legion, prepare to engage attackers. We hold this line."

=====Wrap-Up=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle between Travis Carpenter's weary veterans and the well-rested Imperial Guard was a viciously bloody conflict. Three hours of city fighting netted more deaths than the earlier Canaveral landing and siege.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Prefecture forces were gaining the upper hand near the end of the day, when Ngrligru forces from Kabila Kimburu led by Chonge Kodwe arrived to solidify the victory.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kodwe's forces were brevetted into the Legion of the Phoenix, and the city was secured. The supply line to the north and west was soon completely under control.<br>

'''Hap Barlow'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Date of Death: April 2, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Ah Kin]], [[Alpha Centauri System]]
*Place of Death: [[Kronheim]], [[Yngard System]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Hap Barlow'' was a member of the [[Royal Imperial Army]], and later a Sergeant in the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]]. He died in the boarding action on the FDS ''Imposis''.

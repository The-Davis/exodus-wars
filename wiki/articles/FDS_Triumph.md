The FDS ''Triumph'' is the second ship in the [[Victory Class Battleship]] line.  It was placed under the command of Admiral Jason Bremen, and is the flagship of the Second Fleet of the Line.  The Second Fleet is known for its hard-hitting style of fighting, where the battle quickly becomes one of attrition.

== Deployment ==

After its launch, the ''Triumph'' and the Second Fleet of the Line were assigned to be ready for action against every known threat.  The "Hammers", as Bremen dubbed the fleet, usually travel as a solid unit from world to world.

== Overview ==

While [[Earth]] was the heart of the [[United Earth Alliance]], [[Holt]] was its soul.  The planet boasted a rich history of art, music, and culture.  Its cultural identity reached back into the days of Renaissance Europe, and its people were the conscience of Alliance politics.

After securing Earth, [[Lucius Black]] set his eyes on the soul of the Alliance, with the intention of snuffing it out.  Two great men stood in his way: Admiral [[Jean Sarnou]] and Premier [[Alain Fournier]].

At the time, [[Scott Sanburn]] was little known to the galactic community.  Jean Sarnou was the most likely candidate to succeed [[Ken Hurke]] as General-Commander of the Alliance.  Similarly, the United Earth Alliance Assembly was seeking to pass emergency legislation elevating Alain Fournier to the office of President. If the Onyx Empire hoped to continue its overwhelming victory, Lucius Black needed to snuff out these rising stars before they outshone him in the skies of galactic politics.

== Invasion of Lyrae ==

The invasion of the [[Lyrae System]] began on May 6, [[2315]], when the first elements of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s invasion fleet entered the system.  Alliance Defense Force assets were on high alert, and detected the ships the moment they arrived.

Under an executive order from Alain Fournier, Admiral Jean Sarnou was elevated to the rank of "Acting General-Commander" and given command of all United Earth Alliance Defense Force assets in the Lyrae System.  As Fournier was only Premier of [[Holt]], and not yet sworn in as President of the Alliance, he did not have the authority to give Sarnou command of the entire Alliance defense force.

=== Imperial Forces Offer Parley ===

When the HMS ''Jerome Black'' arrived in the Lyrae System, [[Anthony Hadrian]] sent a courier to Holt and offered to parley with Jean Sarnou.

Sarnou was initially skeptical, but Hadrian offered up Robert Marshall, who was now well known as one of the greatest strategic minds in the Empire, as a hostage.  Jean Sarnou came aboard the ''Jerome Black'', while Marshall was held aboard the UEAS ''Bonaparte'' pending Sarnou's safe return.

In a conference with Jean Sarnou, Anthony Hadrian offered all United Earth Alliance officials on Holt safe passage out of the star system if they surrendered peacefully, and promised a peaceful transition to the Imperial government.  Hadrian promised the transition would be like the takeover of [[Ah Kin]]--relatively bloodless--rather than like the violent destruction of Earth's government and military. Sarnou delivered the offer back to Holt, but the Premier rejected it.

Fournier sent messages to the [[Titan System]], [[Proxima Procyon System]], and [[Theta Algedi System]], hoping to garner enough military support to solidify his position as the de-facto President of the United Earth Alliance and launch a counter-offensive against the Onyx Empire. Ultimately, his plans failed.

=== Fortification of Alsace ===

Ships under the command of [[Robert Marshall]] began moving a supply base into orbit of Alsace in the Lyrae System, and escorted in large numbers of [[Royal Imperial Army]] troops for the invasion of Holt itself.

When Hadrian was informed that the troop movements were complete, he sent a second demand of surrender, setting a deadline of noon the following day. Hadrian knew that battle was inevitable.

=== Showdown at Aquitaine ===

With no reply from Holt, the Royal Imperial Navy attacked the fleet elements of the United Earth Alliance Defense Force under the command of Jean Sarnou. The first battle took place in orbit of Aquitaine, where half of Jean Sarnou's ships were destroyed or captured. Sarnou fought running battle back to Holt. 

=== Ambush at Auvergne ===

Unknown to Sarnou, Anthony Hadrian had planned his defeat in advance. Royal Imperial Navy assets were hidden in low orbit of Auvergne, their drive signatures masked by the planet's harsh and volatile atmosphere. Sarnou found his return vector into Holt orbit in the direct path of Imperial ships led by Robert Marshall. In a fierce three-hour battle, the cornered Jean Sarnou destroyed fifteen Royal Imperial Navy warships before his ships were destroyed or captured. Rather than risk capture, Sarnou rode the UEAS ''Bonaparte'' down to the oceans of Holt, and ditched it in the high seas. The ''Bonaparte'' broke up and sank on impact, but Sarnou and most of his crew made it to escape pods on the way down. 

== Invasion of Holt ==

Royal Imperial Army elements under the command of Cecil Au-Yueng landed on Holt and were met by United Earth Alliance Defense Force ground assets under the command of Brennan Connor.

Connor's men were brave and well-trained, but only had mediocre equipment and were few in number.  The bulk of the Alliance's ground forces had been stationed at [[Mars]] or were stationed at [[Theia]].  The campaign was short.

=== Surrender of Holt ===

Premier Alain Fournier knew when he was defeated.  On May 15, 2315, he surrendered control of Holt to the Onyx Empire, and spent the rest of the war in prison.

=== First Resistance Cell ===

While Fournier was captured, Jean Sarnou and Brennan Connor both escaped capture, as did several hundred trained soldiers under their command.  They formed one of the first organized cells of the [[Resistance against the Empire]], and eventually joined into the group of cells which became the [[Prefecture]].

The '''Auellal Political Party''' was a movement originally started on [[Mesar]] to give the [[Mesarnak]] equal rights with the [[Mesarthrim]]. The Auellal also opposed the political power of the Mesarthrim State Church and believed in many more rights than the mainstream point of view of the [[Clan Lords]] and the policies of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

The HMS ''Menoetius'' was the second ship in the [[Atlas Class Carrier]] line. It given the hull designation CV-2.

The ship was commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2303]], under the command of Vice Admiral [[Michael Burke]]. After the start of the [[Imperial Civil War]], Burke was promoted to the rank of Grand Admiral, and the HMS ''Menoetius'' was turned over to the command of Commodore [[Jason Neall]].

The HMS ''Menoetius'' was destroyed on February 5, [[2324]] in the final battle of the [[Imperial Civil War]].

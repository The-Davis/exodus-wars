The Auellal League can trace its roots far back to the earliest days of [[Mesarthrim]] history.

==Anderung Enslavement==

For decades after fleeing their enslavement by the [[Anderung]], the [[Mesarthrim]] did not unite in defiance against their former taskmasters or attempt to free the remaining [[Mesarnak]]. The [[Clan Lords]] sought instead of secure territory and political power for themselves, and their progress was hampered by infighting.

One such political movement was the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]]. The [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] was a group of [[Mesarthrim]] from [[British Empire (Terra)|Scottish and Irish]] backgrounds who desired to abolish the [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clans]], and remove the State Church from power.

==Anderung Loyalists==

Early on, the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] were considered outsiders by most [[Mesarthrim]].  Over several decades, the [[Anderung]] began to recognize, and embrace, the more liberal [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]].  In [[2142]], the [[Anderung]] captured [[Brian O'Dell]] and [[Samantha McBride]].  Rather then execute the leaders of the [[Auellal Political Party]], the [[Anderung]] struck a deal: the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] would form a [[Anderung Loyalist|partisan]] force, whose purpose was to dismantle the [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clan]] structure from the inside out, namely by killing [[Clan Lords]].

The early years of the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] were marked with bleak self-hatred. Many of its members sought to distance themselves from the public face of being [[Anderung Loyalist]]s, but weren't successful until [[2245]], when [[Sylvester Sheen]], the Provost of the [[Auellal Political Party]], was executed by the [[Anderung]] for smuggling high value deployment schedules to the [[Mesarthrim Resistance|rebels]].  It was also later discovered that the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] had been misdirecting shipments of raw materials and building supplies to the [[Tischler Clan]] holdings in the [[Sonar Mountain Range]], where [[Mesarthrim Resistance|rebels]] were building [[Nimitz Class Gunship|Nimitz]] warships to fight the [[Anderung]] in space.

==Anderung Genocide==

The reprisal by the [[Anderung]] for the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] betrayal was the annihilation of the [[France (Terra)|French]] and [[Spain (Terra)|Spanish]] slave colonies on [[Mesar]]. It was a total ethnic cleansing, something never seen before on [[Mesar]].  It was also the final catalyst that split [[Mesar]] in two. At last, nobody could remain on the fence.  You were either a [[Mesarthrim Resistance|Rebel]] or a [[Anderung Loyalist|Loyalist]].

==Clan Schism==

After [[2245]], the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] began to gain support from [[Mesarthrim]] who were disenfranchised from the [[:Category:Clans of the Mesarthrim|Clan]] system by reason of their heritage, most notably the [[British Empire (Terra)|English, Scottish, and Irish]] ethnic groups.  Their families and clans were considered barbaric and ancient by the [[Germany (Terra)|German]] majority.  Slowly, the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] began to gain more and more political leverage in the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Great Chambers|Great Chambers]].  It was in this way that the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] began to gain some of its more influential power, by speaking for the [[Mesarthrim]] who didn't want the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Church of Christ|Church]] doing all of the thinking and making all of the decisions in their lives.

==First Golden Age of Mesar==

In [[2300]], [[Andreas Tischler]] began taking a poll of the political parties in the [[Mesarthrim]] government.  As part of a class project, [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas]] took a survey of each party's needs and demands, and created a path of compromises that would give each party a portion of what they felt the perfect [[Mesarthrim]] life should be.  In [[2302]] after the assassination of [[Eckhard Tischler]], [[Andreas Tischler|Andreas]] became the [[Clan Lord]] of the [[Tischler Clan]] and began to adopt his reforms. It was the dawn of the [[First Golden Age of Mesar]].

It was during this time, that the [[Mesarthrim]] people finally hunted down the last of the [[Anderung]] and began to look to the stars as more then just another battle ground.  As the [[Mesarthrim]] people began to return to the stars, Provost [[Steven Cue]] began to notice that many of the promises made to the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]] were not being kept.  Religious persecution was still running rampant, and Clanless families were being overlooked by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Great Chambers|Great Chambers]].  [[Andreas Tischler]] tried his best to meet the promises he made to the [[Auellal Political Party|Auellal]], but was hindered by the lack of support he received from the other [[Clan Lords]].  [[Andreas Tischler]] almost drove the [[Tischler Clan]] bankrupt, but was saved by support from the [[Stadt Clan]] and the [[Koenig Clan]].

==Birth of the League==

It was in this time that the [[Auellal League]] was born.  Provost [[Steven Cue]] began to quietly and discretely assemble a 'league' of Commandants and entrepreneurs who began to lay the groundwork "in case the [[Tischler Plan]] failed."  Their work was slowed when the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] joined the [[Alliance of Planets]] and entered into war against the [[Empire]]. No one wanted to abandon their people in the face of an outside enemy as heinous as the [[Empire]].

On January 19th, [[2324]], the League's worst fears were realized, as the life of [[Andreas Tischler]] was cut short in a firefight with the [[Empire]].  The new [[Uber-General]], [[Eric Francis]], was unwilling to continue to fund "unprofitable ventures" as his predecessor was.  After that, the [[Auellal League]] finished militarizing, and officially declared their autonomy from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  [[Uber-General]] [[Eric Francis]] was not about to have a bunch of rebels ruin the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] over "something as trivial as religious disputes."

The ensuing [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] did not spell the end for the [[Auellal League]]. Quite the opposite, as the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] were dissolved into the new [[United Centusi States]] government.

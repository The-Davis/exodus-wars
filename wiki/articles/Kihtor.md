The '''Kihtor''', or Hightowers, of [[Lorithan]] are four huge towers extending two hundred kilometers into space. These massive structures, held aloft by "counterweight" orbital space stations, are categorized as "[[:Category:Space Elevators|space elevators]]"

== Modern Use ==

The Kihtor serve over a thousand purposes in the day to day lives of the [[Lorithos]].

The [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr]] uses several levels of each Kihtor to house [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]]. Many [[Lorithos]] soldiers call the Kihtor home. They are trained, fed, and housed within the confines of the massive structures.

Each Kihtor also serves the important role of being a port of call for the merchant fleets of the other [[:Category:Races|races]] who deal with the [[Lorithos]]. The [[Ailen Suvak Tashir Fahr]] berths its fleets on or around the Kihtor when they are not on patrol, and allows the crews of its [[:Category:Starships|starships]] to take their shore leave in the entertainment districts of the Kihtor.

No other inhabited planet is as instantly recognizable as [[Lorithan]]. The Kihtor are landmarks that cannot be mistaken for something else, and serve as an important cultural icon to the [[Lorithos]]. They serve as [[Lorithan]]'s only real tourist destination. [[:Category:Space Elevators|Space elevators]] have never been successfully built on such a grand scale by any other culture. [[Ikronin]] never bothered, preferring their huge market spaceships. The [[Meroniri]] built several functional elevators, but they are merely shoestrings compared to the massive Kihtor. The [[Terrans]] are another matter. [[Terrans]] tried to build a space elevator once, shortly after the world unification that came about in the years [[World War III]]. The result briefly became known as the "Second Great Wall of China" after it collapsed, killing hundreds of workers.

The importance of the Kihtor to the [[Lorithos]] cannot be stressed enough. Each is a mecca of trade, travel, industry, living space, entertainment, communications, science, education, and most importantly: hope.

== Design ==

[[Seras Yozrim]] was the dreamer behind the concept that sparked the hope of the [[Lorithos]]. [[Seras Yozrim|Yozrim]] spent her earliest years living in constant fear of death at the hands of the soldiers of the [[Burning Wars]]. Her childhood was spent hidden in one of the last refuges supplied and sheltered by the great [[Sanor Cai]].

After her childhood refuge was destroyed by the burning hulk of a [[:Category:Warships|warship]] crashing to the surface, [[Seras Yozrim]] decided that safety meant living above the filth and decay of the [[Burning Wars]]. She turned her brilliant mind to the concept, and in [[2280]], she finished drafting the plans for Kihtor Modar, and presented them to [[Dirken Huern]]. Huern approved the proposal, and construction began.

== Construction ==

At [[Seras Yozrim|Yozrim]]'s direction, [[Lorithos]] technicians worked backwards. Instead of working from the ground up, they built huge counterweights in geosynchronous orbit, and built the towers down from there. The towers are rock-solid, and have never had any instabilities at all. Even R8 earthquakes aren't felt above half a kilometer up.

== Kihtor Modar ==

[[Kihtor Modar]] was the first of the Kihtor, and was completed on January 11, [[2300]]. It is anchored in the city of Huern in the [[Jehz Modar]] continent, on the world of [[Lorithan]]. It is a large civilian population center, and a hub of commerce and everyday life.

== Kihtor Terok ==

[[Kihtor Terok]] was the second of the [[Kihtor]], and was completed on March 13, [[2300]]. It is anchored in the northern continent, Nehru, on the world of [[Lorithan]]. It is a communications and information networking hub.  Kihtor Terok served as the strategic command center of the coalition forces during the [[Volucris War]].

== Kihtor Ihjar ==

[[Kihtor Ihjar]] was the third of the Kihtor, and was completed on May 19, [[2300]]. It is anchored in the city of [[Hovnuk]] in the [[Ihjar]] continent on the world of [[Lorithan]]. It is a military base.

== Kihtor Enwir ==

[[Kihtor Enwir]] was the fourth and final Kihtor.  It was completed on July 11, [[2300]].  It is anchored in the city of [[Aci Devar]].  Mag-lev lines were planned to run from Kihtor Enwir to run to the haven city of Tharek, but after the city collapsed a few weeks later, the plans were cancelled.  The few survivors of Tharek live in Kihtor Enwir.

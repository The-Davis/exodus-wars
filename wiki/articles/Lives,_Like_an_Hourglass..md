by [[Andrew Sears (Author)|Andrew Sears]]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Despite the death, the silent burst of the exploding [[Sundancer]] left [[Marisa Cue]] gapingly stirred in amazement.  The rapidly expanding fusion fire reflected in her pure white eyes, while the paradoxical quintessence was deafeningly silent.  Debris of her wing-man hurtled past her [[Sundancer]], beginning their eternal plummet through space.  

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Dammit Dietrich!”  Cashmere’s soft soprano voice echoed over the comm - void of emotion, void of hope.  There was no chance that he even could have survived.  Dietrich, like all '[[Dancer|Dancers]],' flew without any survival gear or flight suit.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;;Cashmere, pull in tight, put less then a meter between us."  Finally, a slow tear made its path down [[Marisa Cue|Marisa's]] pale cheek.  She intentionally left it hanging on her cheek, it was the only homage she could give to Dietrich.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There, in their own piece, of the vastness of space [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] and Cashmere’s Sundancers’ came together, almost touching.  They had trained together, they knew each other like sisters, “Cash, we fly as one.”  Rolling her [[Sundancer|fighter]], [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] looked up into Cashmere’s eyes.  It was as if she could have touched her - if not for the canopy, G-forces, and vacuum of space.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sizzling packets of expanding gaseous matter tore their attention back to the world.  The enemies [[Anox Fighter|Anox]] fighters unleashed a hail of [[Blazer]] fire down the pipe directly at the [[Dancer|Dancers]].'  Breaking away in perfect unison, the [[Dancer|Dancers’]] cut through the vacuum of space.  Flowing gracefully, they moved in unpredictable patterns, staying close enough together to allow their [[Auellal Point Defense System|defenses]] to complement each other.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It’s not a new tactic, and the [[Anox Fighter|Anox]] pilots were content to wait for them to make a mistake.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In reality it’s only seconds, but after the seeming eternity, the [[Anox Fighter|Anox]] unleash a second barrage of [[Blazer]] fire.  The burning packets tore into the defenses of Cashmere’s [[Sundancer]], physically tearing her port atmospheric stabilizer from the [[Sundancer|fighter]].  [[Marisa Cue|Marisa’s]] initial jerk reaction took an asymmetrical turn from Cashmere - opening their [[Auellal Point Defense System|defenses]].

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Again, the [[Anox Fighter|Anox]] pounds the hell out of Cashmere, blowing away more space-frame and hardware that she probably needed.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Mesarthrim Missile|Missiles]] were already hunting down the girls as they looped back towards the safety of each other.  The [[Sundancer|Sundancer’s]] overshoot each other, their [[Auellal Point Defense System|point defense systems]], attempting to lock onto each other’s trailing [[Mesarthrim Missile|missile]].  The maneuver failed, and only the last second [[Auellal Point Defense System|chaff]] release save Cashmere’s brittle [[Sundancer|fighter]] from complete annihilation.  Her [[Sundancer|fighter]], peppered like swiss cheese, began to slowly die.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Marisa Cue|Marisa]], like all good flight commanders, dropped into position to cover her wing-man.  Pulling the two [[Sundancer|fighters]] together gave them complete defensive cover.  Turning to Cashmere, [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] slowly studied her face.  They were like sisters facing the fists of a drunken father.  “I’ll get you home Cash, I promise.”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A blood choked soprano humorless laugh tore a hole in [[Marisa Cue|Marisa’s]] soul.  “I don’t think old ''Curnee'' can carry me home this time.”  Cashmere’s wince of pain tore through [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] as she watched her friend’s bloody hand rest on the crippled [[Sundancer|Sundancer’s]] canopy.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] gritted her teeth, as she pulled her [[Sundancer|fighter]] in behind Cashmere, and a fury of [[Blazer]] rounds rocketed into her shielding system.  The fury overloaded her redundant capacitor, and blew into the side of her right leg.  “Shit!”  With that, her shielding systems burned out and failed completely.  “Mayday, mayday, [[Post-Commodore]], this is flight sixteen, we cannot hold our objective.”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Marisa?”  The hearty, deep voice of her dearly beloved sent ripples of sudden joy through [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]].

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Armageddon, Cash and I are about to get cooked by a half dozen [[Anox Fighter|Anox.]]”  Another volley of [[Blazer]] fire rocketed past [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]], taking a defensive spiral she left Cashmere totally exposed to the flailing rounds.  Hot pockets of burning super-structure ricocheted off Cashmere, and slammed into [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]].  “Dammit!”  The [[Sundancer]] shuttered, bleeding fluids and parts.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A second later, the lead [[Anox Fighter|Anox]] broke formation as [[Blazer]] rounds cut into it’s shielding systems.  A second [[Anox Fighter|fighter]] buckled as a [[Mesarthrim Missile|missile]] blew its dorsal atmospheric fin to pieces.  [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] craned her neck to watch the sudden carnage that was unleashed on her attackers.  She was fixated.  “Thank you Army.”

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Get clear my love, they will learn my name.”  [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] rolled up on her side, heading back to Cashmere, and for the first time noticed the blood running from her leg wound.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And that's it, that's where the story ends.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It wasn't her fault, she was just the unlucky one to be picked by that angry, furious, cold-hearted [[Mesarthrim Missile|missile]].  She was no more or less a target then anyone else, it doesn't matter that she is -- was -- the daughter of the [[Provost]].

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It smashed its way through her canopy and buried itself in her body, detonating.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;And in that instant, [[Marisa Cue|Marisa]] was her own shower of glorious fusion fire, silently expanding forever through the vast eternity of space.

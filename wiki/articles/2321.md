=== February ===

=== March ===

* March 2 - The [[Onyx Empire]] launches a nuclear strike against [[Theia]], violating the [[Titan Peace Accords]]. The two long-range missiles are deflected from [[Titan City]] itself by the city's gravity shield. One is broken into multiple pieces, disabling it. The other crashes intact into the nearby 
* March 23 - Agents of the [[Omegas]] infiltrate [[Titan City]] in an attempt to assassinate members of the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government. Councilman [[Gary Hughes]] is severely wounded, though the [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]], [[Barbara Dannins]], escapes. Six [[Titan City]] police officers and three [[Omegas]] agents die in the resulting fire-fight.

=== April ===

* April 5 - The UEAS ''Thomas'' led an attack against a [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] defense base in orbit of [[Themis]]. Twenty-nine [[Empire|Imperial]] [[F-4 Ravens]] were destroyed in the attack.
* April 6 - [[Omegas]] agents sabotaged one of the eight reactors that powered [[Titan City]]'s shields. They planned to bomb each reactor in succession, but [[Titan City]] police killed them in a fierce firefight. The planned [[Empire|Imperial]] invasion of [[Theia]] was put off for a while.
* April 18 - The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] began a systematic seek and destroy mission against all [[United Earth Alliance]] forces on [[Rhea]], levelling several key installations before [[Scott Sanburn]]'s forces drove them off.
* April 24 - The HMS ''Iron Hand'' launched a heavy long-range bombardment against [[Titan City]], which overwhelmed the shield generators and destroyed several power transfer stations from the ensuing feedback. [[Titan City]] suffered a ten hour blackout, and [[Titan City]] police had to deal with a riot instigated by [[Omegas]] agents.
* April 25 - A large squadron of [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] [[:Category:Bombers|bombers]] managed to catch a flight of [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Frigates|frigates]] off guard and without fighter escort near [[Themis]]. The HMS ''Gorgon'' was severely damaged, and many crew aboard were killed when three decks decompressed suddenly.

=== May ===

* May 4 - [[Josiah MacArthur Hlow]], the brilliant mind behind most [[Empire|Imperial]] [[:Category:Weapons|weapons]], attempted to defect to the [[United Earth Alliance]] provisional government. He was discovered by [[Empire|Imperial]] forces as he fled with his most loyal assistants. They managed to escape the [[Empire|Imperial]] blockade of the [[Titan System]], and headed for [[Theia]].

* May 16 - For twelve days, [[Empire|Imperial]] and [[United Earth Alliance]] forces fought each other around [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]]'s ship. Hlow's corvette took minor damage in the battle. At the height of the fighting, [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]]'s ship disappeared entirely. No one knows, even today, what happened or how [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]] escaped.

* May 19 - The resources consumed by the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in the battle for custody of [[Josiah Hlow|Hlow]] were enormous. All hostilities against the [[Titan System]] were suspended until the next year.

=== June ===

=== July ===

* July 12 - In the confusion surrounding the attempted defection, the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] took the initiative, and attacked the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s forward base orbiting [[Themis]]. The attack was a complete success, resulting in devastating losses for the [[Empire]] and prompting their retreat from [[Themis]].

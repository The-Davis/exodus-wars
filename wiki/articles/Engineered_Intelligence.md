An '''Engineered Intelligence''', or "EI", is a [[Terran]] system designed to bridge the gap between Artificial Intelligence and human intuition.  The first designs were in use as early as the [[First Exodus War]], but they were deemed so horrible that most people involved in the project took the secrets to their graves.

Only a little-known researcher by the name of [[Miles Hlow]], who moved to [[Torimur]] shortly before the war, kept enough data on the designs to reproduce the work.

The core of the design is a simple life support system with a cybernetic interface.  It is designed to contain a living human being in a permanent state of connectivity with the computer system.  Miles Hlow was a medical paragon, and the life support system was his design.  It greatly extended human life, at the cost of being forced to live in total sensory depravation and total artificial support.

The cybernetic interface was designed by a team whose members are lost to history.  Once the subject is strapped in, an Artificial Intelligence takes complete control of the host body.  All higher brain functions are shut down, and from all practical points of view, the human subject ceases to be.  The Artificial Intelligence provides memory, direction, and a means of control from outside operators.  The human host provides emotion, intuition, fuzzy reasoning, and the necessary chaos to avoid predictability.

When the [[Onyx Empire]] got its hands on the technology, Emperor [[Lucius Black]] envisioned a fleet of ships under the control of such entities.  His plans never came to fruition, for the simple fact that an Engineered Intelligence could never be truly under control.

Any subject forced to merge with the cybernetic interface would be in such a state of emotional trauma and distress that the resulting gestahlt entity would promptly go insane.  It was finally determined that only willing, calm, and most importantly; altruistic and self-sacrificing volunteers had the appropriate mindset to correctly merge with the cybernetic interface.

To date, only three successful attempts at creating an Engineered Intelligence have been made: [[Arthur]], [[Prism]], and [[Luna Black|Luna]].  Shortly after being merged, the Luna EI was de-integrated to determine the effects on a survivor.  The results were never released.

[[Royal Imperial Labs]] was in charge of the Engineered Intelligence project, and it was eventually shut down by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] after the [[Torimur Civil War]].  The two remaining EIs are both under the command of the Prefecture.

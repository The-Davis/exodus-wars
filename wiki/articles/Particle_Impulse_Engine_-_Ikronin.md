For centuries the [[Ikronin]] have used fusion drives as their main source of propulsion to move their vessels at sub-light speeds.

Over that period of time, efforts to tweak and enhance the performance of their fusion drives has taken many forms from highly refined heavy fusion pellets to experimenting with ion particle/fusion hybrids. With the advent of more efficient and effective power generators there are two systems that will soon overtake the fusion drive as the most popular and common among the [[Ikronin]] people.

The second major advance for Icronians in terms of Sub-FTL flight is the '''Particle Impulse Engine'''. The Particle Impulse Engine is the next evolutionary step of the [[Ikronin]] fusion/ion hybridization drive projects.

It uses magnetic fields to produce a constant stream of high-energy particles, as well as to provide vectored thrust. The major advancement of the particle impulse drive over the ion/fusion drive is that the particle impulse engine uses a negligible amount of fuel, which it also manufactures.

The drive's reaction is so efficient that the tiny amounts of matter present in interplanetary or interstellar space can be collected through weak magnetic fields and converted into a thrust medium. Better still, the particle impulse engine is capable of atmospheric entry. It causes some damage to any surface close to its exhaust ports.

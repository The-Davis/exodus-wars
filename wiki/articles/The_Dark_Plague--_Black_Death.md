4/12/2324 - By Ryan Wilhelm


Australia - Meroniri Military Biohazard Containment Facility
     Jarek Nighthawk peers through his hi-power night vision binoculars, scanning the base from his hidden vantage point. This station is one of several that must be eliminated to continue the plague. Conclave agents have learned that the Alliance of Planets scientists were near to neutralizing the disease.
     Nighthawk flips on his optical communications system. The highly encrypted, short-burst transmissions are truly impossible to intercept and decode. With a radio, he'd already be spotted and dead. "All Strike Teams, listen up. Just remember, Amelia made this station our top priority. We can't hit Hlow and his cronies until after we take out their stockpiles and research data here.
     "Time's wasting. All Teams, engage." He descends from hiding, and hops into the passenger seat of a waiting Bobcat.
     "Hope you're ready to go, sir." the driver says. "The troops are really itching for some action."
     "Very ready." Jarek picks up the Bobcat's comm transmitter, hitting a few keys to send a coded burst transmission into space. "Light 'em up." he comments to himself. The raid has begun.

Defensive Perimeter
     Several Shock Troopers look up in surprise as a roar breaks the silence. Their sensors warn them a second too late. The Troopers scatter as several bombs fall. Heavy explosions rip apart the armored troops, sending broken machinery flying. The Conclave Vampires pull up and burn away. They know the wrath of three of the most powerful aerospace forces in the galaxy would soon be responding to their strike.
     Jarek laughs as his Bobcat roars in past the wreckage. It was terribly hard to smuggle in Vampires on freighters, but now their purpose was fulfilled. Perimeter defenses are down. Now the real surprise.
     Shrill whistling noises slice through the night air. Five Enforcer tanks move into view, firing their Autocannons. Small structures and Meroniri vehicles flatten in the wake of the assault.
     The seeds of treachery run deep. The Prefecture has no clue how many spies and turncoats are in their ranks. Why sneak in tanks when they wait for me here?
     The Meroniri Expeditionary Fleet's defenses are taken completely by surprise by the sudden assault, and from the treachery of supposed allies. The automated outer defenses are completely annihilated by the Vampire attack, and the Conclave tanks are quickly pushing into the core of the base.
     Jarek’s Bobcat stays with the Enforcers. "Don't get too far ahead. One of our Bobcats was scouting too close to the base, and got nailed by one of their armor suits."
     "Yes sir."
     Jarek motions to his gunner to be ready to shoot. The gunner checks the ammo and sighting systems of the light coilgun the Conclave had acquired and installed for this particular mission.
     Jarek peers through his binoculars again, looking at the compound. One of the Meroniri tank crews was finally responding. An Oracle was moving to join the fray.
     "Bandit inbound, dead ahead." one of the Enforcers reports. One of the tanks in the column slows for a better shot, then cuts loose with a quick burst from its Autocannon. The Oracle explodes, parts flying in all directions. "Ha! Must've been powering up his shields. Don't mess with-" the Enforcer gunner is cut off when his own tank is turned into a fireball.
     A massive Epoch, the king of tank warfare, rolls into view. Its Rotary Coilguns spin so quickly that they scream with mechanical stress. Millions of rounds of magnetically-propelled shot are flung in all directions as the Epoch's gunner looks for likely targets. The huge siege cannon slowly turns, looking for another victim.
     "Damn!" Jarek's Bobcat swerves to avoid the hail of Coilgun fire, then turns and zips away to hide behind the gutted Enforcer. "Vampires, get back here. Strike Team Two, your turn. Get in here, and watch for stray bandits."
     The Epoch has received its orders--the Conclave tanks are still close enough together. It's time to use an antimatter shell and finish this fight for good.
     Two Vampires release four bombs, then fire off a salvo of missiles before pulling out of their dive. The Epoch buckles under the assault, then the antimatter shell in the siege cannon detonates. The Epoch disappears in a cloud of atoms.

Main Compound
     Jarek’s remaining tanks pull up in front of the main compound. One old Armadillo pulls up behind them, disgorging its Omega Squadron agents. The lead agent calls up Nighthawk on the comm system. "Sir, we're in position and at your command."
     "Thanks Major." Jarek looks over the compound. A few Meroniri ARROWS had been caught in the open, but most are holed up in the main center. The engagement is rapidly shaping up into a siege, one which the Omega Squadron commander had neither the time nor the equipment to win.
     An Omega Agent rushes up to Nighthawk. "Sir, I have good news. The scientists are holed up separately from the main compound. Our agents have sealed the entrances, and the scientists are still inside. Good timing, raiding this place at night. Unfortunately, their research is in the main building."
     Jarek smiles. I don't have the ordinance to destroy the central building and all the research, but at least there won't be any more of it. He follows the agent back to the barracks, where the frightened scientists are being held at gunpoint.
     "Well, greetings from Omega Squadron." Jarek mocks. "It pains me to end brilliant minds, but that's my job. So kids, the lesson is don't stay in school, don't be smart, or we'll destroy you.
     "Kill them." he orders. Jarek leaves, ignoring the screams and sounds of gunfire. His Bobcat roars up, with several agents inside.
     "Boss, there's a lot of hell about to land on us. Meroniri, the Prefecture, and a few Archadians are already on their way. Time to go."
     "Then let's go." Jarek leaps into the Bobcat. The Omega Agents scatter in all directions. "Remember your rendezvous points and next assignments. Maintain communication silence, and don't get caught."

The Bush
     Jarek's teams regroup and meet with a small dropship. The ruthless Omega Squadron commander rounds up the Prefecture turncoats that aided him, and has his agents murder them before they can react. Even the Conclave has no place for traitors.
     The dropship lifts off, and burns away at low altitude, avoiding Meroniri sensors. The Nighthawk has made yet another clean getaway.

Meroniri Base
     General-Commander Rox Augustin inspects the damage with a grim look on his face. Omega Agents had destroyed everything but the central compound. It only took a minute for Augustin to put two and two together. "Get a message to Hlow. Issue an alert to all plague research and treatment areas.
     "There will be more strikes. Omega Agents didn't come all the way to Earth to knock out just one research facility. They recently attacked a Lorithos base and stole a lot of biological weapons. It's obvious that they're behind this."


Black Death, Part II - By Ryan Wilhelm
Texas - Houston Decontamination Zone
     Eight Skyhawk VTOLs fly low over the foothills and scrub outside Houston. Jarek Nighthawk lounges in the passenger seat of the lead Skyhawk, assessing his forces. His own Skyhawk and three others are in standard configuration, carrying multiple Omega Agents. Two additional Skyhawks carry cargo, and the last two are Gunship escorts.
     Glancing at the computer screen in front of him, Jarek notes the time, then quickly keys his comm set. “Strike Teams One and Two, split up and head to your assigned targets."
     The VTOLs split into two equal groups. Jarek's group heads directly for the restoration camp. Jarek sends a short transmission to the camp. “This is deep America Med-Evac Flight Thirteen, requesting an emergency landing at Pad Four. I need medical teams on standby, this is a high level plague emergency."
     Air traffic control is quick to respond. "We copy that Flight Thirteen. Please transmit your access code for landing clearance."
     "I have critically wounded men here, including my commander who has the codes." Jarek shoots back, smiling at his deceptive tactics. "We ran into a couple sand worms while checking a town for survivors. If I wake up my commander, I'm afraid he'll crash and die."
     "Sand worms? I thought those things lived further north. Damned mutants. OK, I'll wave you through this time. Medical techs will be waiting." The controller signals the anti-air defenses to stop tracking the Skyhawks. “You are cleared for landing at Pad Two.”
     “Roger tower. We're coming in fast, and we'll be bouncing out with the gurney as fast as we can." Jarek laughs softly as he shuts off the communicator. "Boys, get ready to party."
Landing Pad Two
     Mobile Medical Corps personnel and vehicles roar out to the VTOL Pad. Each medic is fully dressed in a sealed hazmat suits. They line up next to their Ambulance, waiting for the wounded to be unloaded.
     The Skyhawks land, settling down onto the pad. As the medics surge forward to receive the "wounded", the Omega Agents leap out, rifles blazing. Medics drop, screaming in agony. Jarek exits last, carrying a grenade launcher. The Omega Agents dash across the tarmac, heading for the research facility. The Omega Squadron Gunship Skyhawk hovers overhead, spraying the area with Autocannon and SRM fire. The anti-air defenses are destroyed before the air traffic control tower can get them back online.
     The Cargo Skyhawk lands, and Jarek signals the Agents unloading it. "Bring the demolition kits and extra ammo for my rocket launcher. We need the heavy stuff."

Research Building
     Lorithos robots and Prefecture guards scramble to seal the doors against the coming attack. Lieutenant Sam Evans, commander of the Prefecture guards, yells frantically into a comm-link. “Attention all forces, this is Lieutenant Evans, Restoration Camp. We're under attack by unknown human forces!” Before he can finish, an explosion rocks the compound. Evans stares at the burning hulk that was the base's Air Traffic Control and Communications tower only a moment before.
     Before they can suffer the same fate, the Lorithos officer seals the door.

Outside
     Jarek berates himself as he reloads his grenade launcher. How could I have forgotten the tower?
     The combined firepower of the Skyhawk Gunship and Jarek's grenade launcher had taken out the tower as soon as the transmission had been detected. Jarek could only hope it was on time.
     Several Omega Agents trot up behind him, toting heavy equipment. Now the fireworks begin.

Inside
     A Prefecture soldier bursts into the outer lab, surprising several scientists. He runs to the clear wall of the containment area, and pounds on it. "Professor Hlow, please come out! We need to leave, the facility is under attack.”

Front Entrance
     Lieutenant Evans notices a sudden change in the noise the Omega Agents are making outside. The Agents are apparently running away. "Clear the door! They're going to blow it!"
     His shout is punctuated by a huge explosion. The doors, the temporary reinforcement, and several guards and robots are blown apart. Missiles concussion grenades fly through the smoke. The guards take cover and return fire briefly, then break and run.
     Jarek Nighthawk struts into the entry hall, flanked by Omega Agents. “Split up and secure the area. Apprehend the target subject, take cover, and call for support. Eliminate everyone else.”
     The Agents pair up and disperse through the complex.

Downtown Houston
     Nighthawk's Strike Team Two has targeted the Prefecture’s main military base in Texas. The VTOLs swoop in low. They close in on the base.
     A sentry in one of the outlying defense towers spots two Skyhawks coming in. He walks over to the communications system, and is about to ask his commander if the Skyhawks have clearance. A moment later, the Gunship Skyhawk turns his tower into an inferno.
     The Prefecture command center is blissfully unaware of the chaos that's about to drop on them. One technician notices that the tower is offline.
     “Sir, Tower Seven just went offline. Should we send a maintenance team out?” he asks.
     The commander looks at the screen, noticing some unidentified sensor blips. “What's that?” He looks more closely, then reaches over and flips the alert switch.
     The Omega Squadron Gunship roars over the Prefecture airstrip, strafing parked aircraft. Two Prefecture Lightning VTOLs fall prey to well-aimed Hound LRMs as they try to take off. The pilots die screaming.
     The other three VTOLs land in the scrub near the base. Omega Agents leap out of their transports, and rush to the cargo Skyhawk. In moments, the cargo is unloaded. Hundreds of vials and a strange piece of machinery are doled out to the Agents.
     Prefecture Marines pour out of the base, rifles blazing. Several fire SRM launchers, striking the three Omega Skyhawks. Omega Agents reach for their rifles, only to be gunned down.
     The Omega Gunship pilot sees the Marines, but is too far away to help. He shouts a warning to the grounded Agents, and cuts loose an SRM salvo of his own, but he's out of range.
     Marines are deadly marksmen, with rifles or with SRM launchers. A hail of SRM fire turns the first of the Omega transport Skyhawks into a fireball. The second takes two SRMs to its main rotor, and falls from the sky like a stone. The pilot is thrown from the chopper on impact, and quickly riddled with bullets from automatic rifle fire.
     The surviving Agents abandon their vials and machinery, and run for the scrublands. Three try to unload the cargo Skyhawk completely, but are caught in yet another explosion--the Marines have reloaded their SRM launchers too quickly.
     The Omega Gunship pulls away, then fires up the throttle to run for Strike Team One's operation area. Suddenly, it too is completely engulfed in high explosives. Two Prefecture Valkyries tear by at supersonic speeds, their interception mission accomplished.
     Realizing their doom, the last three Omega Agents stop running. Two kill themselves on the spot, but the third is caught and wrestled to the ground before he can shoot himself or take poison.
     The triumphant Marines drag away their prisoner, and recover the valuable vials of un-mutated plague germs.

Research Center
     Jarek's team is having a much easier time than Strike Team Two. The research center's guards are good fighters, but armed with light sidearms. Besides the Lorithos robots that were destroyed at the entrance, there isn't any powered armor in the whole facility.
     Everything is quiet. One Agent signs to Jarek, and Nighthawk carefully stalks down the hall to join him. One door is marked "Lab." Jarek kicks it down and tosses a smoke grenade inside.
     Sam Evans and the last of the security forces are waiting inside. They open fire immediately. Their pistols aren't strong, but two Omega Agents go down--their armor breached. The Prefecture guards are taken out by automatic weapons fire.
     Evans turns and runs up a stairwell. The Agents pursue, but Evans outruns them and locks the door. Two guards, along with Professor Hlow and the other scientists, are waiting for him, and they help him barricade the door. "They'll have that door down soon. Can we get out?” Evans asks.
     “No sir. They have a Skyhawk Gunship covering the whole complex. We can't call for air support to knock it down. Besides, we can't move quickly with the scientists.” The guard gestures towards the scientists, who are all carrying various pieces of machinery and research data.
     Noticing the discussion, Hlow approaches Evans. “Perhaps I can be of help. I happen to know a few tricks about this building.”

Downstairs
     Jarek and his Omega Agents slowly climb the stairs, watching for booby traps. Considering the speed of the Prefecture lieutenant's retreat, Jarek suspects that the scientists are holed up inside.
     Nighthawk reaches the top of the steps, and motions for the others to join him. "Time to break it down." he says.
     An Agent approaches the door with a hand-held ram. Before he can begin his assault on the door, the lights all go out. The surprised Agents reach for their lights when sirens activate. A computerized voice comes over the base's PA system.
     “Alert. Possible contamination breach. All sections will be sealed. Please step away from any open doors.”
     Jarek watches, horrified, as a thick metal panel slides over the door in front of him. It seals with a hiss that signals finality--there can be no argument with this door. The door is covered with warning signs, and a helpful flyer on how to deal with radiation and chemical poisoning.
     “Down the stairs! Quickly! They're locking down the building!” Jarek rushes down the stairs, not waiting to see if his men obey. He ducks out of the stairwell. The other agents look perplexed for a moment, then turn to follow their commander. The door at the bottom of the stairs seals in their faces, cutting them off from Jarek.
     Now alone, Jarek runs through the room at the end of the stairs, jumping over the corpses of the Prefecture guards his Agents slaughtered on their way up. He reaches the far end of the room, only to find the door sealed. Jarek pounds on it in vain. How could this happen? He sits down for a a long wait.

Upstairs
     Professor Hlow closes the panel he'd been working on. He wipes his hands on his lab coat, then smiles at Lieutenant Evans. “Well, that should keep them quiet for a while. They'll probably be a pain to round up, but they're sealed up tight until you can call in some Grendels.”
     “Why didn’t you do this earlier?” asks Evans. “It could have spared my men, and we would have been safe a long time ago!”
     “I'm sorry, but I didn’t think of it." Hlow shrugs. "This is an older safety feature. It was built when reprisal by the Empire was still considered a threat--it's a seal-off to prevent radioactive contamination or chemical weapons. It doesn't work against the plague, so I'd nearly forgotten about it. We're lucky I remembered how to activate it."
     “Indeed.” Evans steps over to the control console, and deactivates the seal on the windows of the room. He grins at the sight. The Skyhawk Gunship is still outside, but is now on the ground, burning. The other VTOLs are kept on the ground by three Piranhas buzzing them. Prefecture Condors, Armadillos, and Apollos are advancing on the complex. "Here comes the cavalry."

Aftermath
     Soon afterward, the Omega Agents were rounded up. All but Jarek Nighthawk killed themselves when the Grendel armor suits came for them. Nighthawk was detained, and soon shipped off-world to a more secure location.
     The vials the Omega Squadron Strike Teams brought with them contained potent, but un-mutated strains of the plague. It soon became apparent that they had been behind the entire outbreak. With original samples of the virus on hand, Hlow and the other scientists soon had a cure.
     Terra is once again safe, and the reconstruction continues. Now the threat of the Conclave is revealed, but who can say how strong they are?

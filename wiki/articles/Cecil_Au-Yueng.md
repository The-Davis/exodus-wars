''Cecil Au-Yueng'' (born August 29, [[2048]]) was responsible for much of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s success in its ground campaigns in the [[Second Exodus War]]. He was never considered a strategic or tactical genius by any stretch of the imagination, but was noted as a competent and brave commander by his peers.

=== Early Life ===

Cecil Au-Yueng was born on [[Ah Kin]] in the [[Alpha Centauri System]]. He received a classic military education in the West Point Army Academy on [[Earth]], and was commissioned in the [[United Earth Alliance]] Army in [[2073]].

During the crisis between [[Torimur]] and [[Earth]], Au-Yueng expressed his sympathies for the colony, and was court-martialed for insubordination. He was imprisoned for a year, and dishonorably discharged. After his sentence, he immigrated to [[Torimur]], where he met the charismatic [[Lucius Black]].

In the military build-up leading to the conflict known as the [[First Exodus War]], Cecil Au-Yueng advised against going to war. Despite his misgivings, he willingly fought after the destruction of [[Torimur]], which he genuinely believed was the result of an attack by the [[United Earth Alliance]].

=== Second Exodus War ===

The rapid-build up of troops and war materiel leading to the [[Second Exodus War]] underlined the fact that the forces loyal to [[Lucius Black]] had little experience in ground warfare. Commanders like [[Anthony Hadrian]] could lead men anywhere. [[Robert Marshall]] was widely considered a genius of space combat. [[Emil Torsc]] was a paragon of dogfighting and air combat. Only Cecil Au-Yueng had any idea how to formulate a battle plan for ground combat. He suddenly found himself the top General of the [[Royal Imperial Army]].

Au-Yueng did not develop any strategies of his own, but executed existing ones with a grim, unimaginative competence. He favored the use of a "blitz" style rapid attack, followed by a consolidation of forces and fortification of captured territory. He recognized the value of combined arms, and called on the other branches of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]] to aid him whenever he felt it was strategically sound.

Despite his unimaginative battle tactics, Au-Yueng knew talent when he saw it, and knew how to cultivate it in his subordinates. He was responsible for the battlefield promotion of [[Travis Carpenter]] from the rank of Lieutenant to the rank of Colonel, which ultimately led to his promotion to Brigadier by [[Lucius Black]]. By promoting the young strategist, Cecil Au-Yueng unwittingly created his own nemesis; Carpenter masterminded most of the ground campaigns by the [[Prefecture]] against the [[Onyx Empire]] during the [[Imperial Civil War]].

Cecil Au-Yueng was abandoned by [[Lucius Black]] and the bulk of the surviving Imperial forces when they withdrew from [[Earth]]. He surrendered to the coalition forces beseiging [[Mount McKinley]], and was tried for war crimes. The tribunal ruled that while his military campaigns did enable [[Lucius Black]]'s atrocities, and that he knowingly served an enemy of humanity without objection, Cecil Au-Yueng was only complicit in the crimes of the Empire and not directly responsible. He was sentenced to five years in prison, and was parolled in [[2326]]. As a term of his parole, he was forbidden to carry weapons or from serving in any military for the rest of his life.

Cecil Au-Yueng retired to his homeworld, [[Ah Kin]], where he is a noted military historian and one of the curators of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]]'s [[Second Exodus War]] museum. The Colonial Commonwealth petitioned the [[Alliance of Planets]] to give Au-Yueng an exception to his parole to allow him to carry weapons in the execution of his duties as a curator of the museum, but only in non-violent ways. This exception was granted. Au-Yueng is expected to live the rest of his days in peace.

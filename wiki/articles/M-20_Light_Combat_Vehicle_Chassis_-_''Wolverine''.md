During the post-Mirach overhaul of the Prefecture Army, Blazton Heavy Industries lost the bid to upgrade their Pegasus Light Hover Tank.

[[Image:M20Wolverine1.jpg]]

Instead, a better-performing Rahn Industries hover tank was chosen for the role. The Wolverine hover vehicle was a Rahn Industries racer--a test bed for reactors and hover drives, but proved quite capable of combat when armed.

Conversion from racer to tank was a simple matter. Stock Rahn Industries vehicle weapons--a Medium Neutron Cannon and two SRM Launchers--were streamlined to keep the aerodynamic shape of the Wolverine. Unlike most tanks, the Wolverine can fight at melee range--after a fashion.

A "bug-catcher" was installed by Rahn Industries technicians on the front of the vehicle. The sharp barbs and blades are used to keep small Volucris breeds from swarming the tank, and can be used to attack if the Wolverine charges the enemy.

As a racer, the Wolverine was built light, with a titanium frame. This had to be replaced with tough Boron-Nitride Composite armor to make it combat-worthy. For further protection, a Plasma Barrier shield generator was installed.

[[Image:M20Wolverine2.jpg]]

The Wolverine Hover Tank is much heavier than the racer. Getting the Wolverine back to its normal top speed of 180 KPH required a powerful Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor. The racer version relied on a simple fission system.

Wolverines were first deployed to the Army in April of 2324. They are used as lightly-armed scouts and hit-and-run skirmishers. Rough terrain like swamps and steppes are a favorite for Wolverine commanders.

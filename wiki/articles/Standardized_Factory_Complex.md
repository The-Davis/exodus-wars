Ground-based production facilities are relatively new to Rahn Industries.  At its inception, the company only built space equipment, so a ground factory wasn't needed for several years.  After the move to Albanus, the company was offered a number of profitable contracts by the 
Prefecture. Justin Rahn decided to expand the scope of the business in order to accept them.

[[Image:StandardizedFactoryComplex1.jpg]]

Once the decision was made, construction began on the surface of Albanus.  The new factory complexes were built in the already busy industrial heart of Albanus, and the planet's economy grew 30% with the new jobs from the factories.

[[Image:StandardizedFactoryComplex2.jpg]]

The factory complexes can produce everything--tanks, hover craft, and VTOLs.  The surface factories employ over one hundred thousand people.  On average, each factory complex employs seventeen hundred people, including all support, security, and executive personnel.  The working conditions and industrial safety standards are the best in the galaxy--matched only by the best Ikronin corporations.  Accidents are almost unheard of, and no Rahn Industries employee has ever died on the job.

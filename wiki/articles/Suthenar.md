'''Suthenar''' is the head of the opposition in the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]'s [[Thenaro]] legislative body. The [[Meroniri]] thrive on political strife and subterfuge as a way of life, and institutionalized a powerful opposition into their government with the stated duty to directly or indirectly oppose the [[Lothenar]].

==Powers and Responsibilities==

The Suthenar has a partial veto on any of the [[Lothenar]]'s decisions. With any two other ministers, the Suthenar can neutralize the [[Lothenar]] for a single vote, though he cannot use those same two ministers again for a full session of the [[Thenaro]].

With every minister except the [[Lothenar]], the Suthenar can unseat his rival and force an election for all ministry positions.

The '''Forros Magneto-Gravitic Wave Gun''' is the primary heavy weapon for warships of the [[Reigess Suverände]].  Forros Guns manipulate a collimated beam of photons at such a high energy state that it is actually a carrier of the [[Unified Force]].  Such a weapon deals substantial damage, but its primary advantage over other weapons is a high rate of shield and armor penetration.  

The Forros Gun's beam has a unique disrupting effect on matter it interacts with, and a similar effect on most energy barriers.  Because of this, it is not designed to be a brute-force weapons so much as a smart weapon.  The ability to pierce through defenses and not just batter them down allows a [[Reigen]] warship to pierce defenses and strike vital areas, opening up the whole enemy ship to other weaponry.

Due to the massive quantities of energy required for such a beam to be created, the smallest possible Forros Guns are large naval cannons mounted in fixed or turreted positions on large starships.

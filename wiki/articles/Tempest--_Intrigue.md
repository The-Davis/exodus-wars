Set some time during the first year of the Tempest War

by Andrew Sears 


     Captain Becket holds his breath for just a second longer.
     There attention is on the flight board, an alien name decorates the board, and has drawn much speculation from the assembled pilots. Finally, clearing his throat, the pilots turn their attention to the podium. Becket chances a smile, but it reaches his lips as a half apologetic gesture. "As you all can see, there will be eight flights today, six fighters per flight." Flipping on a projection screen, tactical data runs past the pilots. "You will be flying the five star pattern with one forerunner, two aft, and three mid-body." Slowly Becket runs his clammy hands down his side, the mission is suicide, and they all knew it, yet here they were willing to do it anyway. "We don't know when or where they are going to attack, or in what force, just that they will. The cargo ships will be armed this time, Major David Raney will be on the lead cargo ship acting as air boss. The cargo must make it through this time."
     His pause gives way to a wave of murmurs.
     A sudden rush of cool air precedes the lock snapping of the rooms heavy composite armored door.
     Coming through the hatch, with each deliberate, calculated, step is a young woman. Her grace, how express and admirable. She is carefully out of place amongst the men and women suited in their crisp aviator uniforms. Her soft pale skin is covered by an olive drab camis, over a pair of khaki cargo pants torn in the knees and shredded at the ends, she wears a tiered black skirt. Her feet are bare, and leave small footprints on the cold metal floor as she approaches the front. Her sweet short brown hair is pulled tightly back into a small ponytail in the center of the back of her head. Yet her most striking feature is her pure white eyes that seem to leap from her, studying every detail of the room.
     The initial silence is broken as the assembled pilots query each other for her identity. Finally reaching the front, they young lady snaps a salute to Captain Becket, who quickly returns it.
     "This is Dancer Delilah Von Reinstead, member of the Auellal League's detachment sent to fly along side us in the war against the Mesarthrim."
     Delilah turns to the assembled pilots. Her voice sings a sweet soprano, like a sweet jar of freshly harvested honey. "Today you will be flying against a superior enemy, in superior ships, with superior numbers - at a battle in the time and place of their choosing." Apparently she has no qualm with announcing the bad news like a royal crier. "Yet here you are assembled today standing in defiance of tyranny. And I am proud to stand here and fly with you today, we will push back the Fink's floodgates of destruction and tyranny, or die trying -- and that makes us mighty."
     Delilah hardly finishes her introduction, and has already vanished to a vacant seat amongst the pilots. Becket takes a second to recompose himself, he hadn't anticipated the lump that had grown in his throat. It takes a second, but he finds his voice once again. "Ladies and gentlemen, skids up in one hour, take that time to prepare. Good Hunting."

     He can't take his eyes off her.
     She is perfect. Each movement she makes is graceful and breathtaking. She seems to flow, each gesture exactly draws from the preceding, cascading into the subsequent. Her form and technique are unmarred, at least in Anthony's estimation.
     He knows he shouldn't stand and watch, but he can't pry himself away. Something in the way she flows; it is ethereal, it is tribal, it is passionate.
     In his estimation, he could stay and watch her dance for the rest of eternity. The way her cloths hang on her like wings on an angel, the way she maneuvers in a never-ending asymmetrical fashion.
     But he was on a mission.
     As Anthony steps forward, he instantly regrets it. The audible disruption of his entry breaks the serenity of the room. Her dance comes to a sudden halt as the echo finishes reverberating across the bulkheads.
     Instantly she reorients herself, dropping low, ready against an attack. Her pure white eyes watch him like an Anox. Cautiously, Anthony continues forward, and Delilah stand, finally relaxing. No doubt she wonders why he disturbs her.
     "Dancer." His coy smile is an obvious boy like flirt with the girl that somehow seems to becoming more and more gorgeous by the second.
     "Lieutenant?" He had confused her, she wasn't even startled. It was all over her voice.
     Her sweet voice stole Anthony's away as he he continues to walk to her not speaking. Finally finding his voice he weakly breaks the silence, "That was an amazing dance."
     "My mum taught me."
     Anthony tries to suppress his curiosity, but, somehow he cannot. "Is she apart of the Auellal League?"
     Delilah's face suddenly dims, "My father and mum were killed in the civil war, killed defending Picket's Grave from the Fink's. They died protecting the Mesarthrim."
     The pain flowing from her eyes is like the flooding of the Tigris and Euphrates rivers.
     And, somehow, Anthony just wants to take her pain away. He wants to take her pain, and protect her. He wants to tell her she would be okay and have her believe him. He wants to hold her in his arms and be her strong castle.
     Oh God, is he falling in love with a Mesarthrim?
     he is about to fly our and either murder, or be murdered by her people, and here he is falling in love with her.
     Holding back his sudden burst of feeling, he puts his hand on her arm. "They stopped the Mesarthrim --"
     "Finks." Delilah softly stops him. "I am Mesarthrim, those bastards are nothing more than Finks, and deserve a traitors death."
     The resurgence of desire to protect her almost overtakes Anthony again. Taking a deep breath Anthony decides to divert the conversation and deliver his message. "Hey, skids up in fifteen minutes, you gotta get into your flight suit."
     Delilah's smile grafts small dimples on her cheeks, "I am in my flight suit."
     Anthony's head drops in disbelief as he looks her over. The crazy girl still wears her skirt and cargo pants, topped by the camis.
     "Um, that's not regulation gear."
     She smiles again, driving Anthony crazy in love with her, "I'm not Prefecture."
     Searching his mind Anthony blurts out his next question, "What if you need to eject?"
     "I'm not going to eject."
     Suddenly Anthony realizes it's a one way trip for Delilah. It was preposterous, he was in love with Mesarthrim, and no matter what she calls the Centusi States, they are still her brothers and sisters, and he is at war with them. But in the end, his heart wins over his mind, "I don't want you to die." He rests his hand on the side of her face, looking her directly in her white eyes, a tear falls from his.
     Her eyes shut. It doesn't take a psychic to read Anthony's thoughts. She sees right into him, and her iron will seems to be abated.
     His question is only natural, "After this is over--"
     She stops him as they pull together into this sweet embrace.
     It seems, they now have even more to fight for.

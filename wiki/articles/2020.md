== Events of 2020 ==
First interstellar-distance flight.

===January===

===February===
*An Orion mission makes a one light-month round trip in four days, proving the safety of the [[Dva Band]] and the usefulness of the Quinglai Tables.

===March===

===April===

===May===
*May 7
**The Interstellar Exploration Vehicle I is completed, and dubbed the [[IEV-1 Pytheas| UEAS ''Pytheas'']], after the first documented [[Terran]] explorer, Pytheas of Massilia.
**Space Agency astronaut Colonel James Bresnik given command of the [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']] for its maiden voyage.

===June===

===July===
* [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']] lands a four man crew on [[Luna]] to test the effectiveness of their new systems.

===August===

===September===
* September 8 - [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']] leaves the [[Sol System]] en route to [[Alpha Centauri]].

===October===

===November===

===December===
*December 26 - [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']] arrives in the [[Alpha Centauri System]].
*December 27 - [[IEV-1 Pytheas|UEAS ''Pytheas'']] does a flyby of [[Ah Kin]], launches landers to take air and soil samples.

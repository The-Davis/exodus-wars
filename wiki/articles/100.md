== Events of 100AD ==

The first manned starship, [[Vasa]] arrives in the [[Bollaria System]].  Officially the ship’s mission is to observe and monitor a newly-arrived terraformation satellite, but the primary purpose of the journey is to prove the viability of manned, inter-system space travel.

End of the [[Fasrem Epoch]] in [[Ixoarchada]] history, beginning of the [[Yprian Epoch]].

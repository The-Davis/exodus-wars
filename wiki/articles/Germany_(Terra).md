The '''Nazi Germany''', or the '''Third Reich''' was the last unified German government in [[Terran]] history. Adolf Hitler was the head of state called the Führer.

The policies pursued by Nazi Germany were "Aryan" or Nordic racial purity, anti-Semitism; revenge for the territorial losses of the German Empire, loss of national pride at the Treaty of Versailles, and anti-[[Communism]] directed at the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] were among the leading causes of the [[World War II|Second World War]] and the Nazi regime's systematic mass murder of millions of Jews and other minorities in the genocide known as the Holocaust or Shoah.

Under the Nazi regime, Germany from a military standpoint became the dominant nation state in Europe by the early 1940s, and after the annexation of [[Austria (Terra)|Austria]] in [[1938]], Nazi Germany became the first united German state since the Holy Roman Empire to include Austria within its boundaries.

Germany was defeated in [[1945]], and split into [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] and [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]].

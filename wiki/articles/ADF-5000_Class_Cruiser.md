The ADF-5000 was designed to fill the role of a cruiser, a ship capable of combat operations independent of any support roles from the fleet. It was built to field a small fighter complement, armed to take on a variety of ship types, and fast enough to deal with smaller vessels. The cruisers of the Alliance Defense Force were to be their combat patrol ships, and their diplomatic envoys in foreign space.

= Development History =

The [[Tanaku Starship Design Bureau]] was called on by the United Earth Alliance in [[2241]], and ordered to develop a ship to fill the cruiser role.

[[Image:ADF-5000 Class Cruiser Side View.jpg|200px]]

Alliance High Command envisioned a ship capable of operating independently from fleet elements, able to respond to crisis hotspots with little or no advance notice, and have the capacity to deliver air support, troop reinforcements, or even emergency cargo supplies.

The Tanaku Bureau delivered a ship capable of operating for years in deep space without any resupply or external support.  The self-contained ship was spacious, comfortable, and equipped with state-of-the-art crew amenities to make long voyages and missions easy to weather without unecessary stress.

== Lead Ship ==

The lead ship of the line, the ADF-5001 ''Vladimir Putin'', was launched in [[2244]] on a trial run.  The ship's orders were to operate independent of the Alliance fleet for one year without resupply.  During the time period it was to conduct long-range patrols, make goodwill visits to foreign ports, respond to any distress calls, and participate in at least one mock combat operation per month with friendly forces.

The ''Putin'' returned to its home port in the [[Sol System]] on March 4th, [[2245]].  Its mission was a complete success, Alliance High Command determined that the cruiser model met their needs and current mission role.

== Military Commission ==

With the success of the ''Vladimir Putin'' and the scheduled decomissioning of the majority of their older combat-oriented ships, the Alliance Defense Force ordered the construction of 40 additional ADF-5000 cruisers.  This fleet of deep space ships was completed in [[2257]], and another 90 ships were constructed between 2257 and [[2315]].

= Service History =

The ADF-5000 cruiser model was the backbone of the United Earth Alliance Defense Force's space fleet for nearly a century, operating in many diverse roles.  It has served in two armed conflicts to date, and made the transition from the Alliance's control to the Colonial Commonwealth military in [[2324]].

[[Image:ADF-5000 Class Cruiser Top View.jpg|200px]]

No other ship in the Alliance had a reputation for reliable service like the ADF-5000.  It was not the most powerful ship in the fleet, but it could operate in any mission profile with almost no preparation.

== Peacetime Patrols ==

Up until the [[Heritage Crisis]], the Alliance Defense Force had no major threats to deal with.  Their military was mainly focused around emergency response, and acted as a national guard-style supplement to local police forces in response to natural disasters, riots, or hostage situations.  Most ADF-5000s saw action only as glorified ferries for special response teams, or as ostentatious rides for diplomats heading to important functions of state.

== Heritage Crisis ==

The [[Ikronin]]-[[Reigen]] cold war, known as the "Heritage Crisis", allowed the Alliance military to experience the first major operation since the [[First Exodus War]].  A significant portion of the cruiser fleet was mobilized to the [[Njiorin Sector]], and several ADF-5000 cruisers operated alongside Ikronin warships in containment and escort operations.

While some fighter squadrons stationed aboard ADF-5000s saw action, none of the cruisers themselves actually fired a shot.

== Second Exodus War ==

It was in the [[Second Exodus War]] that the ADF-5000 showed its worth.  While mothballed frigates and destroyers were being brought back online, the cruisers and their crews fought tirelessly against the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Royal Imperial Navy]].

With its long-range capability and crews used to working deep space operations without any support, the cruisers were the perfect anti-shipping attack platform.  ADF-5000 cruisers often lurked on the edge of star systems, preying on Imperial freighters as they moved commerce and war materiel.

The cruisers were also powerful enough to take on many Imperial warships with little or no support.  An ADF-5000 could reasonably expect to defeat a pair of [[Dauntless Class Destroyer]]s in an even tactical fight, and could take on even more with a tactical advantage.  Most cruisers faced entire carrier groups at a time, and had to rely on hit and run tactics just to survive.

Despite an enormous numerical disadvantage, the ADF-5000 cruiser proved itself to be an outstanding design.  Of the 130 cruisers in service at the start of the war, 87 survived to see its end.

== Colonial Commonwealth Era ==

The [[Colonial Commonwealth]] has maintained 80 ADF-5000 cruisers out of the 87 that survived the Second Exodus War.  Some cruisers were deemed too costly to make battle-ready, and were converted to other uses or stripped for parts.

The Commonwealth fleet uses the ADF-5000 in the same role that it filled while in service of the United Earth Alliance.

==Overview==

The M-11a Field Gun is a high-caliber variant of the smaller [[M-11 Squad Automatic Weapon]]. The M-11a is an air-cooled, gas-operated, fully-automatic firearm that fires from an open bolt position. It can accept belts of linked 12.7 x 99mm (.50 inch) ammunition through the top-mounted feed tray. The M-11a is meant to be a purely crew-served weapon, and does not accept smaller infantry magazines or belts.

The M-11a Field Gun features a built-in tripod-mounting lug for supported fire, as well as a quick change barrel that helps prevent overheating during sustained fire. Barrels are engaged and disengaged by rotating the built-in handle, and a spare is normally carried by the gunner or his assistant.

The M-11a Field Gun has outstanding firepower, and can control a 2km radius with proper elevation and visibility. M-11a's are outfitted with a small camera scope on the underside of the barrel, which feeds to the gunner's heads-up-display. In low power situations, the gunner can rely on a simple cross hairs sight at the rear of the weapon.

The Royal Imperial Army first began deploying the M-11a Field Gun, and discovered its effective range was 800 meters on a point target, 1200 meters on an area target, and 2000 meters for suppression fire. Tracer ammunition helps increase accuracy at long range and improves the effectiveness of the gun crew..

Besides its use as an infantry weapon, the M-11a has also been used on vehicles, such as the Badger ATV.

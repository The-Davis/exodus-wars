== Events of 5635BC ==

As the resource extraction systems became more and more extensive throughout Caetara Remaneo, more people were required to oversee these systems.  As in the past, the majority of these overseers would come from the youngest generations, who desired a change of life from the monotony of a shipboard existence.

Desiring to at least avoid the reminders of this shipboard existence, the majority of these new colonists avoided the City of Honor and its centerpiece, the Cohonesto’s hulk, which had become an important structure within the city after extensive modification.  Instead, they established the City of Thought on a previously uninhabited sub continent.  There they would flourish for many generations and in time their city would become the symbol of their rebellious generation; a mark that would grow dark in time.

'''A Treatise on Interstellar Travel''' - by Joan Andrek, Galactic Herald Correspondent, APNET Division

According to recent surveys, when asked "How often do you travel between the stars?", an astounding 80% of those polled have never left their home star system. While this survey did exclude people with military backgrounds, and did exclude the one-way trip for colonists, this is still an amazing figure. 

The [[Alliance of Planets]] Economic Council believes that this tendency to stay in one's home star system is unhealthy for the galactic economy, and that it may be due to the average person's discomfort with the idea of "faster than light" travel. 
In an effort to promote interstellar commerce and tourism, the Galactic Herald has published this information on travel between the stars. 


==Wormholes==

If you've travelled away from home at all, chances are that you've used an [[Ikronin]]-built [[Wormhole Gate]]. Any spaceworthy vessel with interplanetary range can reach a Gate and safely travel the stars for a small fee. The fee is based on distance travelled, vessel tonnage, cargo value, and the current trade status of your homeworld with the Ikronin. The casual traveller can expect to pay no more than pocket change, but a convoy of exotic materials can be charged enough to fund a small world. 

Wormholes are essentially the "bending" or "folding" of space to create a place where two ends of space meet. Energy required to open a Wormhole is directly proportionate to the distance between the point of origin and the point of exit. 

The exact mechanics of Wormhole technology are a closely-guarded secret of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], but most physicists around the galaxy believe that the Gates do not interact with either [[hyperspace]] or [[subspace]]. 


==Hyperspace==

The second most-common method of interstellar travel (and by far the most popular military method) is [[hyperspace]] travel. The [[Terran]] [[Volstamik-Krenschov Drive]], the [[Ixoarchada]] [[Ramius-Dawkins Drive]], the [[Lorithos]] [[Baihros Estin Fohrvik]], and the [[Mesarthrim]] [[Gamma-Ray Light Drive]] are all [[hyperspace]] technologies. It is theorized that even the [[Meroniri]] "[[Tesseract Drive]]" uses [[hyperspace]] for part of its journey. 

"[[Hyperspace]]" is a different dimension from our own. In [[hyperspace]], light travels many thousands of times faster than it does in our own dimension. Relativity is not an issue, even when travelling hundreds of times "faster than light". In addition, acceleration is MUCH more efficient. In [[hyperspace]], each and every action produces an opposite and greater reaction. In effect, if an unprotected ship were to enter [[hyperspace]], it would soon tear itself to shreds with the simple processes that occur onboard all the time reacting with greater force instead of equal force. 

All ships are protected from these effects, and only the ship's acceleration is subject to this strange "greater reaction" law of physics that does not exist in our dimension. The speed and efficiency of a ship is determined by how good its shielding is--this is the only difference between most military hyperspace drive systems. 

==Subspace==

Unless you're involved in a Black Ops military operation somewhere, you've probably never travelled by [[subspace]]. Only the [[Prefecture]] and [[Taviridis Somarchada|Ixoarchada]] [[Diver Engines]] are known to be capable of [[subspace]] transitions. It is also known that the [[Mesarthrim]] "[[Omega Bomb]]" creates a rift between normal space and [[subspace]].

[[Subspace]] is used more often for its stealth advantages than its speed. [[Hyperspace]] travel is more reliable, safer, and requires less energy. A vessel travelling in [[subspace]] cannot be detected by normal means.

==Tesseract==

The [[Tesseract Drive]] is the rarest, strangest, and most inexplicable piece of drive technology in the known universe. Only the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] has access to the [[Tesseract Drive]], and it is their most closely-guarded state secret. Divulging the secrets of the [[Tesseract Drive]], or attempting to spy out its designs are the only offenses punishable by death by the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Terinasi]].

The general nature of the [[Tesseract Drive]] is not a close secret. It is general knowledge. A ship performing a tesseract goes through four phases. In phase one, it enters a transitional dimension (Editor's note: Most physicists believe that this is [[hyperspace]]). In phase two, it enters the "[[Quantum Singularity of the Universe]]" (or, simply, the [[Quantum Singularity]]). 

The [[Quantum Singularity]] requires some explanation of its own. While its nature, its physics, and the history of its discovery are all unknown (except by [[Meroniri]] officials), it has been described as "the binding cornerstone of all reality". In essence, this one "spot" holds together the universe, and touches all parts of the universe.

In phase three, the ship re-enters a different part of the transitional dimension. In phase four, the ship re-enters normal space. The entire process takes all of five minutes. If the destination's coordinates (relative to the point of origin) are known, a [[Meroniri]] ship can get there in five minutes, REGARDLESS OF THE DISTANCE.

Given the awesome power of such a fast drive system, it is no wonder why the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] guards its secrets so closely. The only known quirk of the tesseract is that only one ship at a time can use the [[Quantum Singularity]]. Jumps are carefully coordinated via [[Ansible]] instantaneous communications.

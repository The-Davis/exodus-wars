===Overview===

There are two types of Battle Queens.  Both lead their forces into battle and relay the commands of the Nobles.  This breed is built for flight.  Without a Battle Queen, Bugger swarms are still dangerous, but are not quite as well coordinated.

[[Image:VolucrisBattleQueenFlyer1.jpg]]

The Battle Queen's primary function is to ferry forces to and from the hive clusters.  It is capable of opening up hyperspace tunnels, and can bring others along for the ride.  It is also bred to relay communication to the soldiers.  Two antennae are used for this.

To defend itself, the Battle Queen can produce and launch Shredder Symbiotes.  These little creatures are wingless, low-yield versions of the Shredder Drone.  The Battle Queen also has four tusks, but they are useless in space combat.  They are leftovers from its morph from the ground breed of the Battle Queen.

Finally, the Battle Queen carries eggs.  It can lay up to four hundred Shredder Drone eggs, and up to a thousand Nymph eggs.  Its ovipositor can puncture the armor of a disabled warship, allowing the nymph eggs to be laid inside and released to attack the crew.

The Battle Queen's plasma propulsion is just as maneuverable as most Bugger flyers, but the heavy carapace slows it down.

The Battle Queen's carapace is heavy, and resistant to most close-range weapons.  When dealing with long-range weapons, the Queen's high maneuverability tends to save it.

At least one Battle Queen is seen in every space battle, even lightning-fast raids.  It ferries in the Soldiers through hyperspace, then stays to coordinate the attack.  In some cases, such as hit and run, it simply ferries in a few hundred Shredder Drones, then escapes by itself through hyperspace.

[[Image:VolucrisBattleQueenFlyer2.jpg]]

The flyer Battle Queen can replenish its egg supply by eating biological material and hibernating.  Replenishing its entire 1400 egg supply takes one ton of bio-matter and three weeks of hibernation.

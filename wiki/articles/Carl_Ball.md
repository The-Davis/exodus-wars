'''Carl Ball'''
*Place of Birth: [[Albanus]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Carl Ball'' is one of the few in Admiral [[Hannah Baruk]]'s entourage who isn't a former rogue.  He comes from [[Rahn Industries]], and worked on the design team for the warship's communications array and targeting gear.

When the ship was finished, and sent off to war, Ball found that he couldn't part with it. He lived aboard and around the [[BG-6 Bastion]] for more than a year, and having no family, he saw no reason not to stay.  Admiral Baruk welcomed the presence of someone familiar with the vessel inside and out.

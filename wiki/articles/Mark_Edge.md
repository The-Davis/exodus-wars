'''Mark Edge'''
*Place of Birth: [[Mesar]]
*Allegiance: [[Terran Embassy]]

''Mark Edge'' is a [[Mesarthrim]] Cardiologist and Geneticist, who developed the cure for the [[Mesisinak Syndrome]], doubling the life span of the [[Mesarthrim]] people.

Doctor Mark Edge was one of the authors of the [[United Centusi States]] Constitution.

'''John Dominic'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Date of Death: January 13, [[2322]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Place of Death: [[Auellal]]
*Allegiance: The [[Empire]]

''John Dominic'' was a commander in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and commanded an expeditionary cruiser into the region of space counter-turn of the bulk of the [[Empire]]'s territory. Dominic's ship encountered a ship under the command of [[Arnold Stadt]] of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. Dominic demanded their surrender, and his ship was instantly destroyed.

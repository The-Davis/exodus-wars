'''Chancellor''' is the highest elected executive political office in the [[United Centusi States]].  Technically the only person to hold the role, [[William Camp]], was appointed and not elected.  Camp held the title of Chancellor until his death at the hands of [[Arnold Stadt]].  After his death, the position was empty until the United Centusi States was dissolved at the end of the [[Tempest War]].

== Powers and Responsibilities ==

=== Executive Duties ===

Under William Camp's regime, the office of Chancellor held extreme executive power, capable of overturning many legislative or judicial decisions by force.

===Military Duties===

The Chancellor was the commander-in-chief of all Centusi military brances.

=== Abuse Prevention ===

There were no Constitutional or institutional abuse preventions built into the office of Chancellor.  Open rebellion and assassination removed the only person to hold the office from power.

== List of Chancellors ==

* William Camp - Served from [[2326]] to [[2335]]

The '''Royal Imperial Merchant Marine''' was the only non-military entity within the [[Onyx Empire]] allowed to operate [[hyperspace]]-capable [[:Category:Starships|starships]].

It was essentially the freight-hauling arm of the [[Military of the Onyx Empire|Imperial Military]], but had a number of benign roles to play in the everyday life and economy of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s civilian population.

October 4, 2322

by Scott Sanburn

=====Sol System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Titanian Defense Force battle fleet finally arrived in the Sol System this morning. A large number of Meroniri Tronict Class Cruisers--the most powerful cruiser at their disposal --moved to draw off the Remnant cruiser.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the deadly Remnant occupied, the rest of the TDF fleet made a run for Terra. From Terran orbit they deployed several divisions to Australia and Florida--reinforcing both allies.

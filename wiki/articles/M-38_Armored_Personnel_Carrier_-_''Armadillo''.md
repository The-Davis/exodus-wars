When the [[Empire]] was building up its forces for the invasion of [[Terra]], the main requirement was for each [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicle]] to be able to survive a combat drop. Civilian vehicles and most earlier military [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicles]] lack the necessary anchors and reinforcements to be matched to a dropship's crash webs and lines. The [[XM-2 (Imperial Experimental Chassis)|XM-2]] was the first combat [[:Category:Vehicles|vehicle]] designed in this fashion, and was built as an armored personnel carrier, designed to deploy with troops from a large dropship, then give them a ride on the battlefield.

After extensive testing, multiple simulated combat drops, and one unmanned combat drop where the dropship was shot to pieces around the YM-2 prototypes, the design was approved and designated the M-2 Armadillo. The M-2 has been a mainstay APC ever since.

The [[Royal Imperial Army]] did nothing to develop the design, and it suffered. After the [[Battle of Mirach]], General [[Travis Carpenter|Carpenter]] demanded that every weapon used by the [[Prefecture Legion Corps]] be upgraded. The M-2A was upgraded with a fusion reactor, and reinforced composite armor plating. The design was much improved, and infantry commanders loved it. It gave their light-armored infantry a heavy, yet mobile shell of armor to carry around the battlefield at many times the normal rate of travel for infantry.

The Armadillo's main function is carrying troops, which it does well. The forward compartment can carry a full squad of infantry, and the rear compartment can carry two Battle Armor suits. The Armadillo has no automatic deployment system, but well-trained troops can deploy and board in less than two minutes. The crew of the Armadillo is made up of three personnel with various jobs. The commander of the vehicle doubles as the pilot. There are two gunners, who also operate communications and the troop container doors.

The Armadillo isn't built to be an assault weapon, but it has the armament to protect itself from light and medium attackers. Its primary weapon is turret with a pair of Gauss Cannons, with 100 rounds of ammo.

The Armadillo is deployed in groups of four in a diamond formation, so that each vehicle only needs to protect three sides. They can concentrate their firepower to take down larger attackers. The Armadillo requires an entire Beluga dropship to drop into combat, so it is usually brought down to a planet--a half hour operation from a Whale Class Hauler. Armadillos heading into combat are usually sent via convoy from safe occupied zones. These large convoys of Armadillos are especially vulnerable to attack, so they are usually escorted by tanks.

The original design was never the property of any corporation. Today, the Armadillo is manufactured by [[Rahn Industries]], [[Civil Motors]], and the [[Gavin Motor Company]].

The '''Anthronoris''' (''homo originis'') are the stock from which all of the human races have evolved.  The [[Ikronin]], [[Ixoarchada]], [[Lorithos]], [[Meroniri]], [[Mesarnak]], [[Mesarthrim]], [[Reigens]], and [[Terrans]] all share a common ancestry with the Anthronoris, and all of those races are considered "human", though various levels of evolution have rendered the possibility of fertile offspring between Anthronoris and any other human race as impossible.  The only remaining "true" Anthronoris reside in the [[Void World]]s of the [[Remnant]].  

===Appearance===

The Anthronoris are quiet tall, standing between 1.98 and 2.1 meters in height and massing in at 115-125 kg.  They have very muscular builds and strong facial features.  This comes from centuries of exercise associated with their required military training.  Anthronoris do not appear to age, at least in the timeframes which other races may personally know one.  

====Skin Tone====

Skin tone, as with hair and eye color to an Anthronoris, is an interesting concept.  With their mastery of biology, these variables could be changed at whim, which could be very frequent depending on the fashion of the day during the early days of the [[Pax Anthronoris]].  It is a simple matter of changing the genetic code.  

As the [[Sacrum Bellus]] began, social trends within the Empire led more frequently to a pragmatic approach to skin tone, hair, and eye color, choosing a combination which would be most beneficial living in the local environment.  This, as well as natural evolution, explains why each of the distinct human races have varying tones and colors today.  The remaining Anthronoris of the Remnant have taken to adopting a uniform tone and color to signify their unity as well as an acknowledgment of their survival due to living in an artificial environment.  

Seeing as their environment is artificially constructed, the Anthronoris have adopted an olive complexion to best suit living in the variable climates present in each of their Void Worlds.  Whenever long exposure is expected, such as is the case for deep-cover families, this is changed to match the natives to that specific planet.        

====Hair Color====

While a variety of hair colors was common in the early days of the Empire, Anthronoris now commonly have jet black hair.  Although this can be changed for clandestine interaction with other races, generally the Anthronoris retain their black hair if possible.  

====Eye Color====

As with skin tone and hair color, an Anthronoris can change them at will.  Generally, though, they have adopted variations on deep red in tribute to the fallen Empire and their dedication to its resurrection.    

===Metabolism===

The Anthronoris have a rather fast metabolism, mainly due to their regular exercise, but on the whole, it is similar to that of a Terran.  Anthronoris, especially those in the deep-cover families seeded throughout human space, are capable of artificially adjusting their metabolisms to match those of the humans with which they are living.  It is also common for members of the [[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]] to adjust their metabolisms higher to achieve greater performance.  

===Lifespan and Reproduction===

The lifespan is incredibly long and depends upon which treatment the Anthronoris in question has been administered.  The aging process depends upon whether or not the Anthronoris received the universal Vitaeternum Treatment, or the more rare Sin Terminus Treatment.  

All Anthronoris are administered the Vitaeternum Treatment, which lengthens their lifespans to thousands of years and gives them immunity to disease.  A select few Anthronoris, mainly chief military commanders and the Emperor, are given the Sin Terminus Treatment, which grants immunity from aging and disease.  

The recipients of the Vitaeternum age at a rate (using the terran year as a reference) of only one terran year for every 70 or so solar years.  Those given the Sin Terminus Treatment stop visual aging at the point which the treatment was administered.  

Reproduction rates within the [[Remnant]] are strictly controlled, and the current rate was determined by the Third Phase of the [[Master Plan]].  It was determined during the construction of the Void Worlds that, with a long-lived race would need strict control to ensure that the population did not exceed the support capacity of the Void Worlds.  Children are encouraged, but all Anthronoris know that the self-imposed fertility rates are for everyone's good and are vital to the race's survival.  

Before the Sundering, the reproduction rates of the Anthronoris were high, which was the reason for the Sacrum Bellus.  Reversing the restrictions on reproduction rates is one of the key motivators behind the Anthronoris drive to secure the galaxy for the return of the Eternal Empire by defeating the [[Ngrligru]].     

===Behavior===

The Anthronoris, because of tremendous lifespan, have adopted a very pragmatic approach to all activities.  They are known to be very violent when provoked and hold grudges for lifetimes.  They are a very curious people, who devote centuries to study topics of personal interest.  They consider themselves infallible as a collective whole, and will take any action they deem necessary to advance their own collective goals.

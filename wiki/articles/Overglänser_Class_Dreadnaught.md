The '''Overglänser Class Dreadnaught''' is a self-sufficient, mobile base of operations and warship.  Built in the somewhat artistic [[Ikronin]] style, its beauty belies its deadliness.  Incredible speed and maneuverability combine with a heavy offensive punch.  Although it is lacking in defenses compared to other warships of its size, it is designed and well suited for fast, hard strikes on enemy targets, and is quick enough to escape and refocus its efforts elsewhere before its foes can react.  The Ikronin do not deploy Overglänsers often, as they are not cost-effective patrol craft, but designed them and maintain them in the event of war.

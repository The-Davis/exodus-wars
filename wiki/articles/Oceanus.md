[[Oceanus]] is the closest planet to Titan, and is a tiny ball of rock that is too hot for even for most artificial environments. Orbital scans show that the [[Anthronoris]] once had facilities on the surface, but it is unknown why. No [[Terran]] surveys have ever been landed on the surface due to the extreme surface conditions.

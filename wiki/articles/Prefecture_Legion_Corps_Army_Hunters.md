The '''Army Hunters''' are one of the Special Forces groups assigned to the [[Prefecture Legion Corps]]. They are focused on stealth-based tactics and the extreme application of force on finely concentrated points.

After [[Luna Black]] and many of the [[Omegas]] defected to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], they were incorporated into the Army Hunters and added their knowledge of demolitions and infiltration to the Hunters' already vast repetoire.

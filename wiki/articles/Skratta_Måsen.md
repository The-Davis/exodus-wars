===Overview===
The Skratta Måsen is a powerful fighter, similar to the Blackis Stuka in performance.  It is well-armed, extremely tough, and can be quickly deployed from Icronia to any of their corporate interests via the Wormhole Gates.

[[Image:SkrattaMåsen1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The power of the Skratta Måsen comes from its heavy ammunition-based arsenal.  With two Light Storbjudn Cannons and two Medium Spungöra Rotary Cannons, it can cut through bombers and fighters alike.

Some lighter fighter craft can outrun and out-maneuver the heavy Skratta Måsen, but then its missile arsenal comes into play.  Eight externally-mounted Engång Missiles can chase down and destroy many fighter craft.

Protection of the Skratta Måsen comes from tough Proton-Bond armor and Repulsor Shields.  The evasive abilities of the Skratta Måsen are less than perfect, but its acceleration--110 KPH/S--and protection are usually adequate to avoid destruction.

===Deployment===

[[Image:SkrattaMåsen2.jpg]]

The Skratta Måsen is very popular among Icronian Flyer Corps pilots.  Most of them own their own fighters, and all of them own their own Förabin capsules--the modular cockpit used by most fighters.

In combat, Skratta Måsen pilots are quite effective as assault units and defenders, but are poor at scouting and harassment.  Lighter fighters like the Rovriddare handle such roles.

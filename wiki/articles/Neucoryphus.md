===Overview===

The Neucoryphus is a relatively advanced soldier breed. It is effective against armored vehicles and Mechs, as well as light infantry. The nature of its ranged armament has earned it the nickname Bombardier, after the Bombardier Beetle of Terra.

[[Image:Neucoryphus1.jpg]]

The Bombardier has two ranged spines, like the Tyrant, but it's digestive and anatomical regenerative systems are not fast enough to convert battlefield food into spines in the middle of combat.

It takes a Bombardier two days to convert digested matter into new spines. The Bombardier's most effective and frequently used weapon is a pair of glands in its abdomen. The abdomen is highly flexible, allowing the Bombardier to point it forward, or to the rear.

Each gland contains a liquid mixture of enzymes and compounds, which are inert until mixed. When the Bombardier fires its glands, the two mixtures combine at range, depending on the angle of each gland, but the maximum range is a few hundred meters. Upon combining, the compounds react violently, and the enzymes further excite the reaction. The resulting stream of hot plasma can melt tough armor and troops. The strength of the Bombardier is its speed and firepower.

It's armor is weak, even weaker than that of a Nymph. The Bombardier is very fast, skirting about the battlefield at 55KPH. It's small size makes it a difficult target for heavy vehicles and Mechs, and it's speed makes it difficult for infantry to kill. It's only real danger is artillery bombardments and air raids, as it has no defense against attacks from above. 

[[Image:Neucoryphus2.jpg]]

Bombardiers are very common on a battlefield. They're one of the most important weapons in the Bugger arsenal, as close-range Buggers are severely limited in their total damage output. The plasma reactions of Bombardiers do the heavy damage in battle.

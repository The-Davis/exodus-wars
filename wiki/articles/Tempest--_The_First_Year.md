Various unsorted posts from the first year of the Tempest War. All need expansion.

== Freedom! - 9/5/2325 - By James Davis ==

William Camp holds a major summit on Laojiu, inviting many neutral dignitaries to witness the formation of a new interim government for the Laojiu District. The "Free Laojiu Republic" is formed, under the protection of the United Centusi States, with a council of native Laoji dealing with the day-to-day, non-military affairs of the farming worlds.

Ten-Gou Zin, formerly a minor official in the Prefecture government, is given the reins of power, along with a retinue of ten Myt'gaul guarding him around the clock.

== A Favor for a Friend - 9/13/2325 - By James Davis ==

At the request of Scott Sanburn, Prefect Anthony Hadrian comes to Titan City to discuss the Tempest War. Sanburn confides in Hadrian, admitting that despite the fact that he and several high ranking military officials would like to go to war against the Mesarthrim, the Titanian Planetary Alliance's civil government will not endorse any military action.

Unlike the Prefecture, where the military is an actual branch of the government and the decision to go to war is shared between the Prefect and the Congress, the civil authorities of the Titanian system control the military.

Hadrian and Sanburn agree that with direct military intervention being forbidden, there is no point in the Titanians claiming to support the Prefecture. Instead, the Titanians will withdraw their moral support, and declare neutrality, hoping to build credentials for themselves as a monitor for prisoner of war camps, and as an arbitrator in future negotiations.


== The Red Cross - 9/16/2325 - By James Davis ==

For ten years, since the invasion of Terra, the Interstellar Red Cross has been defunct and scattered through human-inhabited worlds. Today, the Titanian Planetary Alliance re-establishes the organization and gives it a new headquarters on Titanian 6.

The Titanian Planetary Alliance declares neutrality in the Tempest War, withdrawing their previous position of supporting the Prefecture, and sends delegations to both Ueda and Mesar, requesting to inspect P.O.W. camps.

== Beware, Sindh - 10/18/2325 - By James Davis ==

With Laojiu firmly in their hands, the United Centusi States prepares to push into the Tejat District. Their previous strikes there: a full-blown attack on Canopus that destroyed the Spire of Truth, and a brief raid on Tejat that the Marines defeated; were merely forerunners to their continued blitz campaign.

Two Mesarthrim Fleets, accompanied by 400,000 Myt'kampfen and 13,000 Myt'herra, come to the Prefecture world of Sindh. Sindh appeared relatively unimportant to a casual observer, with a largely internalized economy and only some trade with other worlds in the Prefecture. To the Mesarthrim, it looks like a soft target and an excellent staging point for invading the rest of the Tejat District.

For all intents and purposes, it appears that the Mesarthrim are correct: Sindh has only a few airbases and no standing troop garrisons, only armories for local reserve Guard units. The automated defenses of the planet are only installed at Sialkot, the spaceport. Their initial landing in the Nicobar island chain is unopposed as they plan further attacks on the District.

Unknown to the Mesarthrim, the western region of Azad is of enormous importance to the Prefecture. Several secret military installations are buried in the Telra Aji mountains, where the top secret Norfolk Project has several components being developed.. The Prefecture quickly reacts, deploying three Fleets, two Legions, and the Marine Corps to Tejat to prepare defense of the District. The Icronians respond as well, sending the new Ingripandekraften to assist in space naval superiority. Sindh itself is surrounded by Mesarthrim, keeping the Prefecture from reinforcing in force. Instead, elements from the Inquisitors slip in undetected to rouse the reserve Guard into action.

== Quagmire - 11/3/2325 - By James Davis ==

Since landing on Sindh, the Mesarthrim have not fared well. Attempting to hold territory in the Nicobar island chain, while important because it houses the only ammunition factories on the planet, is like trying to hold water in a sieve. Sindh Guard units, working in teams ranging from four to fifty, conduct attacks from hidden camps scattered through the forests of the less developed islands. The Myt'kampfen units landing are forced to work on securing important cities and other sites instead of pushing on to the main continent of Azad.

Azad has been heavily reinforced: a pitched space battle allows the entire Legions of the Serpent and Fox to land at Sialkot, effectively turning the taking of Azad from a simple victory into a nightmare for the Mesarthrim.

In the southern continent of Adha, the Myt'herra capture the northern Jaratu River delta, providing a safe landing site for Myt'kampfen troop carriers to travel between Nicobar and Adha. The Prefecture Marine Corps respond by smashing their way into the Teltali Mountains, securing and removing the Sindh Guard armories and rallying the Guard into defensible positions in the mountains and in the steaming jungles along the Nipur River. The Prefecture is well positioned and has significant numerical superiority, ready to test the Myt'kampfen resolve.

Mesarthrim forces are ordered to hold their positions and fight off the Prefecture counterattack until reinforcements can be sent from Mesar.

== The Boot is on the Other Foot - 11/5/2325 - By James Davis ==

The Mesarthrim plans to take Sindh have failed to meet their planned Blitzkrieg. The 400,000 troops on the ground are reinforced by another 200,000. The 13,000 Myt'herra on the ground are pulled in two directions at once: 6,000 are sent to win a beachhead at the Azad port of Gwadar, while the remaining 7,000 are assigned to lead Myt'kampfen units in hunting down Prefecture Marines and Guard units in Adha.

The invasion of Gwadar is successful: Serpent Legion forces suffer significant losses as the Myt'herra cut their way into the metropolis. The Myt'kampfen begin landing when Myt'herra units receive a strange order: continue the advance and force the Serpent Legion into the flatlands west of the city. Unknown to the Mesarthrim, the Inquisitors have successfully infiltrated a handful of key positions and have cracked some of their codes. The orders are false, tipping the Inquisitors' hand and getting their agents killed in the process, but the ruse is a success. The Myt'herra fight on, cut off from reinforcements of the Myt'kampfen. The 6,000 Myt'herra in the landing force are killed or captured, with a mere 200 escaping to bring word of the false orders.

The Mesarthrim have managed to keep the momentum in their invasion, but only at the cost of many lives. Their casualties on Sindh are already double that of the entire Laojiu campaign, and the Prefecture Fleets have continued harassing the Mesarthrim Kriegsmarine units, making reinforcements more difficult. William Camp sends word to the commanding officers that more reinforcements will be in place by January, and to hold the territory they have.

== Myt'gaul - 11/12/2325 - By James Davis ==

The help the Mesarthrim on Sindh have so desperately needed arrives in the form of several groups of Myt'gaul, the most powerful and elite Mesarthrim special forces, are sent to Sindh to deal with the heart of the Prefecture's military response: the Generals of the Legions. They meet with some success, infiltrating the headquarters of the Legion of the Fox at Sialkot and killing General Justus Hunt and escaping before the Legion could retaliate. The Legion of the Serpent, on the other hand, is on high alert against infiltrators due to being on the front lines against the Myt'kampfen and Gwadar, and manages to capture two Myt'gaul alive and kill three others in their attempt.

== Myt'herra or Marine? - 11/16/2325 - By James Davis ==

The city of Jola, an Indian settlement on Sindh, is a strategic point for the Myt'herra in the Adha continent hunting the Marines and Guard units in the Nipur River region. It is also an incredible strong point for defense, impregnable from the small arms available to the mobile Marines on the ground. The Myt'herra in the city have it locked down tight against Prefecture counterattacks, with firing lanes and kill zones in every street.

The Marines on the ground are constantly harried by the Myt'herra, and have great difficulty organizing an effective resistance in Adha. Brigadier William Duke brings in a surprise: Marine One, full of the second boot camp of Marines, makes a drop assault straight down into the center of Jola.

The Marines drop from Marine One into the center of the city, with a full complement of armor, VTOLs, close air-support, and even precision artillery and mortars from outside the city.

While the Myt'herra are brave and furious fighters, the Marines are equally well-trained and nearly as well-equipped. With superior tactical advantage on their side, the Marines win with only a few hundred casualties. The strange mutual respect between the Marines and Myt'herra continues to grow, as many hundreds of Myt'herra are spared and sent to P.O.W. camps, and dozens are sent home to Mesar.

With this defeat, the initial Myt'herra presence on Sindh has been wiped out. Nearly 13,000 Myt'herra have been captured, killed, or injured beyond duty capacity.

== Mines! - 11/29/2325 - By James Davis ==

Following the defeat of the Myt'herra at Jola on Adha, the Myt'kampfen begin a major offensive out of Gwadar, smashing the defenses of the Legion of the Serpent and driving them towards the Egris River and its strategically important heavy water factories. By nightfall, the Myt'kampfen have advanced over thirty kilometers west of Gwadar, and halt for the night. By morning, the Serpent Legion has secretly withdrawn the bulk of its forces, leaving behind a handful of fast VTOLs and ATVs, and some abandoned tanks. As the Myt'kampfen advance, the few real units retreat at high speed, remotely activating the minefield the Myt'kampfen have unknowingly blundered into. Over a thousand are killed within seconds, and another five thousand are horribly mangled. The Myt'kampfen must pick their way across the minefield very slowly, suffering horrible casualties.

Fighting in the Azad continent slows to a crawl after the Gwadar offensive is caught in the minefield, as the local winter sets in, burying much of the terrain in snow. The Myt'kampfen fail to take any more ground, as every night before the next snowfall, locals trained by the Inquisitors plant new minefields.

The Starknight was built after the Anderung had been defeated, and the Clan Lords took control of the "Comur Future Works". The entire complex was as much a science project as an active construction facility. The lessons learned on the first Starknights would help the Mesarthrim build their first battleships - the Stormforge. The Starknight is a very expensive ship to not only construct, but to maintain. The Silver Chambers cancelled the construction program in the midst of the Tempest War. The Federal Parliment has to date only authorizes the construction of two Starknight's to replace losses sustained at the Second Battle of Centus.

==History==
When Firline Industries first got it's hands on CFW, they went a bit crazy and tried to add as many fancy features as they could pack into a single ship. This led to early Starknights to experience random brown outs, and system failures. These faults were addressed, and eventually a much more balanced design was created. However, leading up to the Tempest War, once again the Starknight became a testbed for new technologies coming out of the R&D department. This time, however, they struck an incredible balance - and the Starknight became a vital component in every engagement it was involved in.

The Starknight also received high praise as being the only vessel to serve as the entire complement of a fleet, the Centusi Second Fleet. With this fleet, Admiral Stadt was able to route a superiour force of Prefecture Avengers.

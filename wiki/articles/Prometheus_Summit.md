The '''Prometheus Summit''' was a meeting in early [[2324]] between the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and the [[Taviridis Somarchada]].

The summit established the communications standard known as the [[Prometheus Protocols]], a universal standard which both the [[Mesarthrim]] and [[Ixoarchada]] systems could interface with, useful for power generation, computer communication, voice communication, and data storage.  This allowed both nations to interface with one another's technology without difficulty, and opened up interstellar trade between the two nations.  This made the Mesarthrim design standards the de facto standards for mid-arm nations, which gave the Mesarthrim a significant economic advantage.

The summit also established the [[Prometheus Stardock]], the only Ixoarchada installation in the [[Pelagrim Sector]].  It is a port of call for Ixoarchada and Mesarthrim alike, and is the only place in Mesarthrim territory where Ixoarchada warships may legally enter planetary space.

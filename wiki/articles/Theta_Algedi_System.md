The '''Theta Algedi System''' consists of the star Theta Algedi, the six planets, their moons, and the asteroids.

In order of their distances from Theta Algedi, the planets are Demara, Banya, [[Tejat]], Saka, Gujarat, and Pradesh.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Theta Algedi is the Theta Algedi System's only star. Theta Algedi is a class G3L yellow dwarf. The Theta Algedi System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Theta Algedi is part of the [[Algedi Cluster]] in the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Tejat ===

[[Tejat]] (1.3AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Theta Algedi System. It has one moon, Orissa.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Theta Algedi System was colonized in [[2195]], mostly by immigrants from the India region of [[Earth]].

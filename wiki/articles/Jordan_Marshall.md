'''Jordan Marshall'''
*Date of Birth: [[2296]]
*Place of Birth: [[BG-1 Jerome Black]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Jordan Marshall'' (born in [[2296]]) was a promising young officer in both the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] and the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] before he died in an ambush by an Imperial warship.

==Son of Genius==

Jordan Marshall was a gesture of kindness from [[Lucius Black]] to [[Robert Marshall]]. Robert's wife had died in the destruction of [[Torimur]], but [[Lucius Black|Lucius]] had created a genetic storehouse of colonial DNA and had Jordan made from the DNA of [[Robert Marshall|Robert]] and [[Maya Marshall]].

Jordan was raised during the last years of the [[Second Exodus]], and wanted to join the rest of the young recruits in the military academies. [[Robert Marshall]] eventually relented, and allowed Jordan to join the academies, where his skill and unusual exuberance caught the eye of his instructors.

When Jordan was 19 years old, he was given a commission in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. Jordan Marshall's career was bright, both in the service of the [[Empire]] and later the rebelling [[Prefecture]].

His career as a navy commander was tragically cut short when he battled [[Oswald Voss]] in the [[Imperial Civil War]]. Jordan Marshall and [[Jeff Nolan]] commanded Atlas Fleet in a battle against Voss's forces. The entire battle was a trap.  Voss had hidden mines, bombers, and reinforcements throughout the [[Proxima Procyon System]].

The trap was nearly perfect. Jordan Marshall had to sacrifice his own ship, crew and life to allow the rest of the [[Atlas Fleet]] to escape. [[Oswald Voss]] escaped defeat until he was captured by Uber-General [[Andreas Tischler]] of the [[Mesarthrim]].

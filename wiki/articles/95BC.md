== Events of 95BC ==

Capsules launched by [[Naga Illta]] now fill much of the close orbit of [[Glengarr]].  Dozens of orbital stations revolve around the planet, used primarily for small-scale scientific research.  The first parts for a large, permanent space station arrive from the surface.

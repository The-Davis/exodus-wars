The Hades was the signature Prefecture Rig during the [[Imperial Civil War]], but after the Battle of Mirach, it became apparent that some changes were needed. Travis Carpenter designed the Rig's replacement himself, something he hadn't done since the creation of the Firefly.

Because of the sentimentality of the name Hades, Carpenter decided to call the new Rig the Hades Mk.II, similar to the new Hercules and Zeus models released earlier. Most of the chassis design stayed the same too.

The main problem of the original Hades--besides the outdated technology--only came up when fighting Volucris Slicers. Slicers tend to get up close, then engage in melee combat. The Hades was often able to take them down with its single arm and weapons, but suffered terrible balance problems. A second arm was added to balance the first.

Gauss rifles became a redundant term when the Gauss projectile launching system became widespread in all cannon models. The new Earthquake Cannon was similar enough in weight and size to work as a replacement, and a second was added for symmetry and balance.

Instead of three lasers, the new Hades has two Large Neutron Cannons concealed in the thumb of each hand. While the cannons can fire in intense fast, intense slow, and flood mode, most commanders forbid the use of flood on an inhabited planet because of the radiation leakage.

Power for the new Hades is provided by the new M-300XL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor. The switch to a fusion reactor increased the top speed of the Hades to 100KPH. The jump jets were removed in the upgrade.

General Travis Carpenter is known as the Phoenix--at least to survivors of the Battle of Terra. His Hades Mk.II has grey and red markings in place of the traditional black and yellow. It also has a tinted green cockpit, which Carpenter prefers. It makes his customized Heads-Up Display show more prominently.

Development of the Hades Mk.II began immediately after the Battle of Mirach. Testing was rushed, but the final design was done by May of 2324. Production of the new models was complete by August 16th, but most front-line units had their Hades Mk.IIs by the end of June.

The Hades Mk.II performs the same role as the original: heavy combat at all ranges, from melee to many kilometers from the target. The Prefecture deploys the Hades Mk.II as its signature Rig, just like the Hades and Hercules before it.

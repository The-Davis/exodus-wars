===Overview===

When the Caoryphis was first seen on the battlefield, the human soldiers were scared almost witless.  Up until the Battle of Tavou, the Volucris fought only with purely unarmed creatures.  This creature, which became known as the Infiltrator for its frequent use in humanoid bases, carries two firearms.

[[Image:Caoryphis.jpg]]

The weapons' origins are unknown.  No Volucris factories have ever been seen, but the effectiveness of the Infiltrator's firepower in unrivaled in close quarters.  At long range, infantry can stand up and taunt an Infiltrator without fear, but at point-blank the weapons can cut through Grendel battle armor.

Indoors, even the best Terran and Ixoarchada weapons are hard-pressed to take down an Infiltrator without killing themselves.  In the fields, long-ranged explosives easily crack the Infiltrator's tough hide.

Whenever an Infiltrator is present, it is certain that the Volucris intend to capture a building, or at least enter it and steal information or materials.  Humanoid troops in the field are advised to kill them at range before they can enter a tunnel.

*Date of Death: February 5, [[2270]]
*Place of Birth: [[Terra]]
*Date of Death: February 10, [[2322]]
*Place of Death: [[Cronus]], [[Titan System]]
*Allegiance: [[United Earth Alliance]]

Aaron Barcley served across the entire span of the [[United Earth Alliance]].  At the time of the [[Onyx Empire|Onyx]] invasion, Commander Barcley was stationed at [[Themis]].  After the news of the attack, Commander Barcley moved his field army from [[Themis]] to [[Tethys]] where combat engineers set up mobile planetary defence systems.  [[General-Commander]] [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn's]] trap was successful thanks to the vital sensor and tracking data Commander Barcley's planetary defence system provided.

As the siege of the [[Titan System]] continued, [[General-Commander]] [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] began to rely on Aaron Barcley's skill at getting civil workers, defence installations, and sensitive materials moved around the planets as the [[Onyx Empire]] pushed further in system.

Aaron Barcley was killed during the [[Battle of Titanian 12]].  His drop zone was hit in a surprise attack by [[Travis Carpenter]] the morning after his bloody, yet successful invasion.  Barcley was assassinated later that day by Omega agents when the camp they were holding him at was attacked by a group of [[Myt'herra]].

A statue of Aaron Barcley can be seen at the [[Exodus War Museum]] on [[Terra]].

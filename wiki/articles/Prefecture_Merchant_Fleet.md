The Merchant Fleet is not actually part of the military, or even the [[Prefecture]] government for that matter. It is made up of independently operated civilian merchant corporations, operating out of [[Prefecture]]-controlled space.

In fact, the merchant fleet is only officially recognized and archived for the convenience of the [[Prefecture]] military.

Merchants usually operate in large convoys with a sizeable escort of fighters. Typically, inter-District shipping takes place through the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gate]] system, but in-District shipping takes place through normal spacelanes.

The Merchant Fleet almost exclusively uses the Bulk Transport, a massive cargo train with purpose-built cargo containers for liquids, ammunition, food, standard sized vehicles, and many other valuable and sensative materials. An average Merchant Fleet convoy moves more goods in a month than a [[21st Century]] [[Terran]] country consumed in a year.

'''Illinaul''' is the language family to which all [[Ixoarchada]] languages belong.  It is descended from [[Lorentin]], via the [[Anthronoris]] that originally settled in the [[Tolana Sector]].  Today, there are a host of variants, dialects, and creoles that exist based on regional, situational, and caste-based differences.

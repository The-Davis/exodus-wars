Shadan Nahru, or "The Damned", are the elite Special Forces of the Mozihr Getah.  Shadan Nahru are drawn from born wastelanders, who spend most of their lives alone in the deep wastelands of [[Lorithan]].  Shadan Nahru are known for their heartlessness, self-reliance, and short lifespans.  Most do not live longer than thirty years, though they typically succumb to disease rather than combat-related injuries.

The tactics of the Shadan Nahru involve infiltration of soft assets by night, particularly logistics and similar supply-line assets.  They slaughter the unarmed support staff before moving on to the soldiers and guards, and then destroy or capture as many useful assets as possible.  The Shadan Nahru are marked by their wastelander cloaks, which they wear over their gear at all times.  No Shadan Nahru ever shows his or her face to anyone except their commander.

== End of the Shadan Nahru ==

At the start of the [[Volucris War]], there were two thousand Shadan Nahru. By the end of the war, there were six.  The Lorithos special forces fought against the Volucris tooth and nail, and even the most conservative estimates believe they killed three thousand bugs for each one of their losses.

With no wastelands to defend or to train the Shadan Nahru, the branch of the special forces was retired after the war.  Lorithan was gone, and its most vicious defenders died with it.  After their decommissioning, every survivor of the group committed suicide.  Their remains were launched into the star, Mirdek, to join their comrades.

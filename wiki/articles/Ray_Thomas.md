'''Ray Thomas'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Ray Thomas'' is a quiet man, seemingly unsuited for a command position.  Like many young Imperial officers, Thomas was part of the [[Alpha Centauri]] cloning project run in the early days of the [[Onyx Empire]].

Most of his [[Royal Imperial Navy]] career was spent in the [[Holt Core Fleet]], simply watching out for the Imperial colony.  When the [[Prefecture]]'s rebellion went public, Thomas was won over by the ideology of freedom, and joined in the rebellion.

After the [[Imperial Civil War]], Thomas' record won him a position as Executive Officer of the FDS ''Delphinus'', where he oversees the day to day operation of the vessel.

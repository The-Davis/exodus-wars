The '''Prometheus Stardock''' is an [[Ixoarchada]] installation in orbit of [[Comur]] in the [[Tirshan System]].  It is the only Ixoarchada installation in the [[Pelagrim Sector]].

After the [[Prometheus Summit]] established friendly relations between the Ixoarchada and the [[Mesarthrim]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] permitted the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] to build the station.

Approximately 80% of the station's operating budget is paid for by the Taviridis, with the remaining budget provided by the United Mesarthrim Clans and later the [[United Centusi States]].  The station provides material support for ships from both nations.

The station is also noteworthy as the site of all embassies dealing with the Taviridis Somarchada.  With the single exception of the Prometheus Summit itself, the Ixoarchada do not allow foreign influence on their home systems, and all diplomatic and economic dealings with the Ixoarchada take place on Prometheus Stardock.

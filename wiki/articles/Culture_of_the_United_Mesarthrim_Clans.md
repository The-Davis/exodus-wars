===Architecture===

The Mesarthrim have built five massive cities across [[Mesar]].  Each one of the five great cities were built around a temple to one of the five [[Anderung]] goddesses.  The five cities are [[Briggsholm]], [[Antvirp]], [[Beckenridge]], [[Willow Creek]], and [[Brünshelm]].  During the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]], each cities temple was captured and destroyed at one point or another.  The temple was then torn down, and a Cathedral to the Christian God was erected in its spot.  When the [[Anderung]] re-took the cities they left the Cathedrals standing, a favor the Mesarthrim people paid dearly for over the years.

There was also a set of three cities built by the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion]] in secret, [[Stone Bridge]], the capital, [[Homemakers Field]], and [[Mesar Heights]].  Over the years each city was discovered and reduced to smoldering ash, with the exception of [[Stone Bridge]], which remained a tightly guarded secret.  Every time a city was leveled, the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|rebels]] rebuilt it.  This left the buildings in these cities to be constantly rebuilt with new designs and materials.

Finally in [[2303]], [[Stone Bridge]] was discovered by the [[Anderung]], and in a last ditch desperate attempt, the [[Anderung]] attacked and laid siege to the city, it took extensive damage, but lived on.

===Cuisine===

Mesarthrim love to eat. A lot. They love meat, potatoes, vegetables, beer, cheese (they ''love'' cheese), and bread--especially sourdough bread.  Not only is their love for food intense, but so is their consumption. The average Mesarthrim eats in one meal what a human eats in three. Despite this, it is hard to find a fat Mesarthrim. Due to the [[Anderung]] occupation Mesarthrim have suffered several genetic changes. One side effect is a naturally high metabolism. This, combined with a cultural emphasis on daily exercise, has led to the above average eating level, and an above average hunger level.

===Social Structure===

The [[Mesarthrim]] have always elevated personal honor and glory in combat. As part of this culture, the [[Blood Seal]] became a required part of any pact or document. Without it, no [[Mesarthrim]] could conduct any major business transaction or withdraw money from the national bank.

===Social Structure===

The colony ship that the Mesarthrim left [[Terra]] on was financed by a German Hotel and Resort Corporation with offices in Germany, England, France, and Spain.  The companies President, [[Otto Tischler]], was a devout German protestant.  After purchasing a charter from the [[United Earth Alliance]] for a section of land on a recently terra-formed planet, he began a promotion for six months encouraging his employees to consider becoming colonist.  The goal was to create 'the ultimate sanctuary and resort for free protestant christians everywhere.'

Even after their abduction and enslavement, those who had decided to go remained very religious, keeping to the ideology of fundamentalist Christianity.  They believe in the inherent divine spark in humanity, and learned to despise alien life, particularly the [[Anderung]].  This xenophobic nature drew them into isolationism, and [[Terra]] was quickly forgotten.

The colonist were outraged that they had been abducted, and altered by the [[Anderung]].  Initially they turned on their leader, [[Otto Tischler|Otto]], but he quickly took control of the situation, and through several weeks of faking mass suicides, sent almost five thousand colonist into the mountains to begin a [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|resistance]] against the [[Anderung]].  This instant militarism of the colonist led to the development of their warrior heritage that glorified war, and decries cowardice.  The free colonist, known as the Mesarthrim, were determined to fight to the death, refusing to retreat even when it is tactically feasible.  This tenacity in the face of wisdom was the cause of their few defeats in the [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|resistance]].

This has remained the doctrine for the Mesarthrim; You train until you are best, then retreat becomes a null argument.

The Mestharim have a strong German heritage thanks to [[Otto Tischler]].  The French and Spanish colonies used to have a huge influence on Mestharim culture as well, but were the target of [[Anderung]] reprisals and were all but annihilated.  The remaining real cultural differences, the English/Scottish/Irish people, have since declared their autonomy from the German, due to differences in political and religious views.

Their allegiance is not to be taken lightly.  A Mesarthrim is loyal to the end, unless there is a sleight to their sense of honor.

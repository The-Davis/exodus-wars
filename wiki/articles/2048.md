=== March ===

=== August ===

* August 9 - [[Jerome Black]] finished arming his freighters. This armed merchant navy was the first roots of what would later become the mighty [[Royal Imperial Navy]] two centuries later. * August 14 - An attempt to impound a Torimur freighter commanded by [[Giorgio Hadrian]] is fought off with lethal force.
* August 19 - Two further attempts to impound Torimur freighters are met with lethal force.
* August 27 - United Earth Alliance officials put a moratorium on any further attempts to impound Torimur property.

=== September ===

* September 15 - After the three lethal encounters with armed [[Torimur]] freighters, the [[United Earth Alliance]] repeals its laws allowing their officials to impound Torimur property.

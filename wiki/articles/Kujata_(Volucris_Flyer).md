[[Image:Kujata_A.jpg|center]]

The Kujata is a powerful morph of the Shiva. It is actually propelled by two Shivas. Its mass reduces its speed, but it has incredible assault capabilities. The Kujata's Plasma Glands produce larger, faster blasts, giving it a much tougher punch. Up close, it can launch spines and spikes into its targets--not very effective against warships or fighters, but quite useful for strafing runs. Multiple Shredder Symbiotes can be carried by the Kujata. Like the Shiva, the Kujata cannot produce its own Symbiotes, but requires fewer trips to a Battle Queen. Unlike the Shiva, Kujata Symbiotes can make course corrections, much like a Shredder Drone. The Kujata is quite resilient--it can take a significant number of Hound LRMs before dying. One caught in deep space in early 2325 actually survived a tactical nuclear blast. Kujatas are the heavy attackers of the Volucris Swarm. Shivas and Shredder Drones are more numerous, but only the huge Leviathan is more powerful.

Alliance of Planets High Council in Session - by James Davis
Sepulcre
     The Alliance of Planets High Council is in full session. Prefecture Consul Chris Dayton has the floor.
     "I'm afraid I have no news from Terra. Professor Hlow sent a communiqué saying he had a major breakthrough several days ago, but hasn't said anything else. We should find out in a day or two what he knows.
     "I do have some excellent news. The combined efforts of our Dr. Reg Brenstein and the Icronian Consortium have produced results in detecting and tracking the Volucris. Using pattern-recognition software, our optical sensors can now track all bugs at close range. We've seen new varieties with every new contact, but it only takes ten minutes to update our entire database. The next time the bugs get the nerve to attack us, we're going to really bloody them.
     "Unfortunately, they'll still always have the element of surprise. We can't see them until they're up close. We're working on some new gravity sensors, but it will take a lot more time to perfect.
     "Our overhauls are nearly complete. Only fifteen designs are still works in progress, and those are all at least 50% done. This war is about to turn around."

     President Dirken Huern of Rikaz Lorithos takes the floor. "I'm afraid I have some bad news to report. Our garrisons on Jagarr have confirmed contact with remnants of the Empire. Elements of the dreaded Omega Squadron slaughtered guards at one of our weapons dumps.
     "Records show that some biological weapons from the Burning War were stored there. I don't know why they weren't destroyed--our laws forbid the storage or use of any weapons of mass destruction. I assure you, whoever is responsible will be disciplined, and we will track down and destroy any remaining Burning War relics.
     "Our fleets pursued the Omega Squadron warship to the edge of our territory, but we feared ambush and called off our Riiktars. All relevant data has been sent to the Meroniri Expeditionary Fleet and our other allies. The culprits should be caught before too long. With any luck, we'll track down the Imperial remnant's base of operations."

''Lavik Johann Nighthawk'', known as "The Merciless", was the leader of the [[Omegas]] until he was killed by [[Luna Black]]. While he lived, Lavik Nighthawk was one of the most feared agents of Emperor [[Lucius Black]].

===Early Life===

Lavik and his brother [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] grew up on [[Torimur]]. Lavik studied the arts of poisoning and murder for several years under the tutelage of his grandfather, [[Oliver Nighthawk]]. Ironically, the elder [[Oliver Nighthawk|Nighthawk]] had tried to escape his family legacy of murder and corruption in his earlier years, but ended up turning his grandson into a far more malevolent tool of destruction than any other agent in history.

Lavik never wrote any memoirs before he died, but [[Jarek Nighthawk]] gave a few interviews after his capture. [[Oliver Nighthawk]] was an incomprehensibly cruel taskmaster, often brutally torturing Lavik and teaching him to treat the infliction of pain as an art. Before he was even twelve years old, Lavik Nighthawk was the quintessential sadist and serial killer.

===Ward of the State===

On January 19, [[2070]], Lavik murdered both of his parents at [[Oliver Nighthawk]]'s orders. Lavik then murdered six other people, and mutilated their bodies before being caught. When the [[Torimur]] colonial authorities arrested him, he not only confessed but bragged during the interview. The horrified officials learned of [[Oliver Nighthawk]]'s insane lessons, and arrested the demented man.

[[Jarek Nighthawk]] testified in Lavik's trial against [[Oliver Nighthawk|Oliver]], pleading for the life of his brother. The colonial authority spared Lavik's life, but judged him criminally insane and made him a ward of the state.

[[Torimur]]'s limited resources meant all wards of the state were housed in the same facilities, though the dangerous ones were kept separate from the others when not supervised. It was in this facility that Lavik Nighthawk first met [[Lucius Black]].

Lavik was soon enamored with the older [[Lucius Black|Lucius]]. Lavik was malevolence incarnate, but recognized that he was in the presence of a true master. He began to spend as much time with [[Lucius Black|Lucius]] as his caretakers would allow. His caretakers gave [[Lucius Black|Lucius]] significant leeway with Lavik, and even allowed him to tutor him in several courses.

It was the time spent with Lavik in these early days that first began to earn [[Lucius Black]] his reputation among the older colonists as a reformed person. Lavik's caretakers saw what they described as "massive improvements" in behavior, which they credited to [[Lucius Black|Lucius]]' tutoring sessions.

===The Merciless Receives Mercy===

In [[2072]], [[Lucius Black|Lucius]] became an adult by [[Torimur]] laws, and was released from the facility. He continued to visit Lavik on a regular basis, and used his newfound influence to gain visitation rights for Lavik's brother [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]].

In [[2074]], [[Lucius Black]] managed to gain control of the [[Torimur]] colonial government. Almost immediately, he had Lavik released into his custody. In public, Lavik made a great show of being truly reformed, but this was only a ruse at [[Lucius Black|Lucius]]' instruction. In private, he delved even deeper into the arts of murder. In addition to the poisons he mastered under [[Oliver Nighthawk|Oliver]]'s tutelage, he began to learn the use of firearms and blades, and even dabbled in explosives.

It was around this time that Lavik and [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] first began to learn of [[Lucius Black]]'s ambitions of domination. While [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] had neither the malevolence of his brother nor the ambition of [[Lucius Black|Lucius]], he agreed to help them. Lavik and [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] became the first [[Omegas]].

===The First Exodus War===

In [[2078]], Lavik and [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] were both sent into the [[Sol System]], where they managed to comandeer a nuclear strike [[:Category:Warships|warship]] from the [[Alliance of Planets Space Agency]]. [[Jarek Nighthawk]] later told the tale of the grisly trip back to the [[Cildeng System]] with the captured ship. Lavik poisoned every member of the crew except the navigator with a strong sedative, then kept them sedated for the trip. One by one, he brought the crew to the bridge of the ship, and flayed them alive in front of the terrified navigator. This maliciousness was pointless: the navigator had been willing to cooperate at the mere threat of violence.

At the end of the trip, only the ship's captain and navigator were alive. Lavik brought the captain out from under sedation, then tortured them both until the captain gave Lavik the nuclear launch codes. [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] later claimed the body parts, blood, and offal were almost knee-deep on the bridge. Lavik launched the strike vessel's entire arsenal at the main colony on [[Torimur]]'s surface, laughing the entire time.

[[Lucius Black]] used the rage at the destruction as the catalyst for launching the [[First Exodus War]]. He soon realized the tactical error and how premature the war was, and began formulating plans to keep his power base intact. Lavik and [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]] were kept close for the remainder of the war, acting as his bodyguards. When the war ended with their capture and the [[First Exodus]], Lavik refused to surrender in spire of his orders and had to be sedated by [[Jarek Nighthawk|Jarek]].

===Old version===
(old version rewrite incomplete)

In the [[Second Exodus War]], Lavik was put in charge of the [[Omegas]].  In his career he pulled off a dozen assassinations in the leadership of the [[United Earth Alliance]] and the [[Terran]] colonies.  Nighthawk and his best agents were responsible for as much death and destruction as any two front-line [[Royal Imperial Army]] Divisions.

During the [[Imperial Civil War]], Lavik Nighthawk and the [[Omegas]] were ordered to spread into the societies of the enemies of the [[Empire]] and wait.  When the [[Empire]] was defeated and the [[Conclave]] was formed, Nighthawk contacted [[Lucius Black]].

Nighthawk was ordered to give the Emperor's "daughter", [[Luna Black]], the names and contact information of all [[Omegas]].  Lucius then had Lavik and [[Luna Black|Luna]] inserted into the capital city of [[Sepulare]], with orders to kill the other.  The survivor would be in command of the [[Omegas]]--assuming they weren't caught by [[Alliance of Planets Space Police]].

Lavik failed the test. He was killed in broad daylight with dozens of witnesses and ten [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Space Police]] officers on the scene, but no one managed to see the actual killing, and no one saw [[Luna Black|Luna]] at all.

The end of Lavik Nighthawk is a fitting one.  He was a ruthless terrorist who had no qualms with cold-blooded assassination, mass murder, or killing bystanders in his sabotage operations.  His long career was marked by death and unjustified destruction.  He was once known as one of "The Merciless" ranking with [[Grolk Kyhra]] and [[Jarek Nighthawk]] as the most feared [[Empire|Imperial]] agents of chaos.

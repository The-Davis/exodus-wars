*Power Plant: Rahn Industries Type-230 Fission Reactor
*Speed: 25 KPH
*Armor: Carbon Composite armor
*Armament: 4 SRM Launchers
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

The Salvage Rig is a newcomer to the Prefecture Army. Rahn Industries introduced the model as a time-saver in cleaning up battlefields after a victory.

[[Image:HeavySalvageRig1.jpg]]

Up until the specialized vehicle was produced, the Prefecture used tanks and Rigs to haul away scrap--a rather time-consuming venture.

===Capabilities===

The rig carries only one weapon, and very little ammunition for it.  Salvage Rigs are never brought out for battle--they only enter the field when all enemies are suppressed.  Weapons are for discouraging scavengers from trying to steal Prefecture war material.

[[Image:HeavySalvageRig2.jpg]]

A wide array of salvage tools completes the rig's equipment.  It can carry anything away, from a pile of scrap Battle Armor to an entire disabled Assault Rig.

===Deployment===

Salvage Rigs are sent where they're needed.  While they're technically military hardware, the operators are hired and trained by civilian companies.

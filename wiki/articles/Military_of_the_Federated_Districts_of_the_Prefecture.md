The military of the Prefecture has a rich history of power and victory. It is currently one of the galaxy's most numerous and well-trained armed forces, though it is inferior to many in terms of technology.

The [[Prefect]] is the Commander-in-Chief of the Prefecture military, which counts the defence of national territory, the protection of Prefecture interests abroad, and the maintenance of galactic stability as some of its primary objectives.

== Organization ==

The head of the armed forces is the Prefect. He is the literal Commander-in-Chief, taking an active role in planning, strategy, and command in battle. However, the Constitution puts civil and military government forces at the disposal of the Legislature.

== Manpower ==

The Prefecture relies a great deal on the survivors of the Empire's brainwashing programs to provide manpower for its military, with only a minority of new recruits.

The Draft is currently in place. If called, draftees are offered the choice between serving or paying their way out. In 2325, [[Talaris Roi]]'s government announced increased drafting, bringing the strength of the Legion Corps up to ten Legions. Young people must register for possible conscription should the situation call for it.

==International Stance==

[[Prefecture]] military doctrine is based on the concepts of national independence and military superiority. The [[Prefecture]] is a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]], and has worked actively with the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] to adapt to the post-Exodus War environment.

==Recent Operations==

The Prefecture is currently on a defensive stance due to the [[Tempest War]]. They have no troops deployed abroad at the moment, and are in the process of training up more troops to fill the ranks of their losses to the Mesarthrim war machine.

The [[Imperial Civil War]] was the Prefecture military's first taste of battle as an independent organization. At the time it was known as the "[[Resistance against the Empire]]", and was a rebellion against the [[Empire]]. It won, but only with great amounts of assistance from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

During the [[Volucris Incursion]] the Prefecture military began to flex its military muscle, and participated in its first foreign aid mission during the [[Battle of Kronheim]]. This newfound international participation was short-lived, as the [[United Centusi States]] attacked, beginning the harsh [[Tempest War]].

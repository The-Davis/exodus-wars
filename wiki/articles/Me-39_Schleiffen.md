The Schleiffen was designed during the Second Exodus War, as the United Mesarthrim Clans went up against more and more capital ships of the Imperial Navy. Prior to this, the Mesarthrim maintained no bomber-type craft larger than the Anox. After the Mesarthrim Civil War, the United Centusi States retained all of the production facilities for the Schleiffen line, and secured all existing bombers for the Luftwaffe. The Schleiffen is armed to the teeth, with both cannons and missiles. For starters, the Schleiffen is equipped with eight Medium Ordinance Hardpoints, capable of carrying various anti-fighter or anti-ground missiles. Additionally, two Heavy Ordinance Hardpoints are fitted on the bottom of the bomber, which can carry large anti-warship rounds. Topping off the missile armament are six Artillery rounds, stored in a pair of internal rotary racks. Stored internally, these rounds can be fired off within a few seconds, and can do heavy damage to starships or ground targets alike. In addition to its missile arsenal, the Schleiffen is also equipped with eight Type-Cx Blazers, an extended-range version of the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type. The Cx can only be mounted on larger bombers and other craft with more powerful reactors than a smaller fighter can carry. It is also equipped with two computer-controlled anti-aerospace turrets on its upper sections, and a pilot assisted rear twin-turret. The turrets use smaller Type-Cm Blazers, miniaturized Type-C Blazers with a shorter range, and can effectively protect the Schleiffen from interceptors or other fighters they might come across. The turrets are also useful for straffing ground targets within an atmosphere. The Schleiffen is outfitted with a large bomber-grade Mesarthrim reactor, and an impressive engine array, allowing it to carry heavy payloads of weaponry. While slow and ungainly by Mesarthrim fighter standards, it is surprisingly swift for a craft of its size. Schleiffen Heavy Bombers were developed for the Second Exodus War to give the UMC a long-range, missile-heavy weapons platform for use against starships, bases, and planetary targets. Today they serve as the primary anti-warship bombers in the UCS Aerospace Force. The UCSAF deploys Schleiffens with all bomber groups. The Schleiffen class is designated as a "torpedo bomber" due to its use of heavy missile rounds.

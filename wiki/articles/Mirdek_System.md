The '''Mirdek System''' consists of the star Mirdek and seven planets. It is the home [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] of the [[Lorithos]]

In order of their distances from Mirdek, the planets are [[Ayelthen]], [[Duron]], [[Lorithan]], [[Furak]], [[Serenthon]], and [[Eruag]].

== History ==

The Mirdek System sheltered the [[Lorithos]] during the [[Sundering]] and was their only world until [[First Contact]].

== Stellar Navigation ==

Mirdek is the Mirdek System's only star. Mirdek is a class G4R yellow dwarf star. The Mirdek System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Mirdek is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Ayelthen ===

[[Ayelthen]] (0.4AU) is a small terrestrial planet. It has never been explored or exploited by the [[Lorithos]].

=== Duron ===

[[Duron]] (0.64AU) is a medium-sized terrestrial planet. In the years of developing space travel before the [[Burning Wars]], [[Duron]] was the first planet the [[Lorithos]] set foot on. It yielded little in the way of useful resources and was not exploited since then.

=== Lorithan ===

[[Lorithan]] (0.98AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Mirdek System. It has a single moon, [[Ashtir]]. [[Ashtir]] is considered holy ground by the [[Lorithos]], and they have never set foot on its surface.

=== Furak ===
[[Furak]] (1.95AU) is a small terrestrial planet.

=== Serenthon ===

[[Serenthon]] (3.2AU) is a medium sized gas giant.

=== Eruag ===

[[Eruag]] (5.36AU) is a large gas giant.

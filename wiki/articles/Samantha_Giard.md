'''Samantha Giard'''
*Date of Death: February 5, [[2315]]
*Place of Birth: [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: [[Terra]]
*Allegiance: [[United Earth Alliance]]

''Samantha Giard'' was a Colonel in the [[United Earth Alliance]] defense force, in charge of an artillery and air defense group. She was killed when [[Emil Torsc]] and [[Jack Ivonrud]] completed a [[Wild Weasel]] attack run on her position.

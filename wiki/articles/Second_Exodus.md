The '''Second Exodus''' was the journey made by the [[Torimur]] exiles on their way back to the [[Sol Sector]]. The bulk of the trip was made in the [[Adin Band]], requiring over 200 years to return.

Several hundred [[Alphas]] and [[Omegas]] with access to the [[Dva Band]] went ahead to the [[Alpha Centauri System]] and began infiltrating the society of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

Many of the [[Torimur]] exiles hoped the Second Exodus would end in peaceful reintigration with the rest of [[Terran]] civilization, but in the end [[Lucius Black]] began the [[Second Exodus War]].

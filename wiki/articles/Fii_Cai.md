'''Fii Cai''' was a junior family of the [[Lorithos]] until [[Sanor Cai]]'s actions during the [[Burning Wars]] brought it into the public spotlight.  After the sunset of [[Fii Huern]] in the [[24th Century]], Fii Cai rose in power, and [[Siovar Cai]] became the first [[Prekohr]] outside Fii Huern.

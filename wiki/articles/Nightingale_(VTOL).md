*Power Plant: Blazton M-340XXL Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Standard Acceleration:  165 KPH/S
*Maximum Acceleration:  260 KPH/S (325+ KPH/S for 30 seconds during gravity escape)
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Shields: Plasma Shields
*Manufacturer: Archada Purtant
*Communications System: Standard Optical Transceiver

===Overview===

In 2323, the Home Defense Security force was given the job of creating a mobile medical team that could help the deathly ill refugees of Terra.  This job led to the creation of the Mobile Medical Corps, which in turn led to the development of the new Mobile Army Surgical Hospital.

[[Image:Nightingale1.jpg]]

The MASH team is made up of four vehicles.  The Nightingale is an extraordinarily fast VTOL, built by with [[Ixoarchada]] technology and purchased en masse by the Prefecture Home Defense Security force.  It carries mortally wounded patients from the surface to the Mercy Cruiser.

===Capabilities===

The powerful engines of the Nightingale are for atmospheric flight only.  In space, the anti-gravity lift system switches over to working like a gravity cavitation drive, granting the VTOL an extremely capable system for evading capture and destruction.

A patient frozen in cold sleep and jammed into the tiny compartment would not be comfortable if awake, but most medics don't even care if more minor damage is caused, as long as the patient gets to the Mercy Cruiser quickly.  The Nightingale is only used in absolute life or death situations.

===Deployment===

The Nightingale is used extensively by the MASH, even when an emergency patient evacuation to space isn't needed.  The pilot of the Nightingale is a fully trained medic, and can get to wounded before the Ambulance, giving the MASH doctors the crucial extra time they need to save lives.

Assuming it isn't attacked, a Nightingale can pack up a patient, reach escape velocity, and land aboard the Mercy Cruiser in less than ten minutes.

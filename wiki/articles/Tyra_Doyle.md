'''Tyra Doyle'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Tyra Doyle'''s small frame and impetuous attitude belies her skill and ferocity on the battlefield. She is an accomplished Rig pilot and the commander of [[Cerberus Force]]. Doyle has proved her prowess and command skill many times throughout the [[Battle of Terra]] and the [[Battle of Mirach]], but very few Rig pilots are impressed when they first meet her.

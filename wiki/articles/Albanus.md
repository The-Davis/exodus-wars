'''Albanus''' is the third planet in the [[Antares System]].

== History ==

As part of a major project to investigate the possibility of the presence of [[antimatter]] in [[De Mairan's Nebula]], Albanus was colonized in [[2060]].

The world was rich in resources, and a prime candidate for seeding with plant and animal life. The atmosphere was breathable and the gravity was acceptable, so Albanus was colonized.

In September of 2323, [[Rahn Industries]] built their headquarters on Albanus, and the world's economy skyrocketed. Rahn Industries humongous warships and construction yards dominated the night sky, but the surface blossomed under their colonial development programs.

In January of 2324, Albanus was chosen as the hub world for the [[Ueda District]], which meant multiple [[Ikronin]] Wormhole Gates would be built in the outer reaches of the star system.

Millions of refugees from [[Earth]] began moving to Albanus as quickly as they could be processed. The population grew by leaps and bounds, but industry and employment were able to keep up.

Today the population has topped two billion, and thousands of corporations, research companies, small businesses, and mercenaries make their homes on Albanus. It is one of the best places to live in the galaxy.

The cities of Ishkandar, [[Napiera]] and Alamos are major population centers, as is the capital city, [[Gisborne]].

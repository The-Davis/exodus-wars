*Powerplant: BzF-880 Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 200 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Shields: Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens 
*Armament:
**2 Medium Neutron Beam Cannons
**2 Infantry Field Guns
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===

One of Blazton Industries "Hydra" units, the Targe is perhaps the most basic.  A Hydra platform with a modular cargo bay, the Targe is a fast transport that can move men and machines, securely and quickly, from place to place.

[[Image:TargeArmoredPersonnelCarrier1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Targe is not meant to combat other vehicles, but it is armed.  The twin Medium Neutron Beam Cannons common on all Hyrda craft are present.  When configured to transport infantry, the Targe can be equipped with two field guns, manned by soldiers in the rear of the craft.  These guns have firing arcs out to each side, and are primarily meant for defensive purposes. 

Like all Hydra units, the Targe utilizes a Kalidasan hover system.  The Targe is the fastest of the Hydra craft.  For defenses, Boron-Nitride Composite armor and Terran-standard shielding are equipped. 

===Deployment===

Targe transports are primarily meant to be infantry haulers, transporting upto ten infantry and two drivers (which can also be armed as infantry) to a location.  Generally open-topped in the back, a modular armored roof section can be mounted to the rear of the Targe, making the rear an air-tight section that can support unprotected infantry in a hostile atmosphere.

[[Image:TargeArmoredPersonnelCarrier2.jpg]]

Fast and reliable, Targe units have found quick acceptance not just as military and security craft, but the high speed, heavy payload capacity, and good handling have also found unarmed versions being sold to customization companies, which in turn modify them into ambulances, couriers, and cargo trucks.

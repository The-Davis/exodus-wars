'''Repulsor Generators''' project a field of alpha particles (helium nuclei that have been stripped of their electrons) around anything equipped with the generator, resulting in a field that absorbs energy damage.

Weapons that wholly or partially deal concussion, electricity, fire or non-specific energy damage (such as beam weapons) fired at the starship suffer a 40-60% loss in damage potential.

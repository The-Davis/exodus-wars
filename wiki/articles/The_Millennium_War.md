The '''Millennium War''' was a devastating conflict fought, with few lapses, between the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] and the [[Yurtosh hora ven Serthra]] for over 1000 years.  Although various reasons were presented at times, the war was one for supremacy within the [[Tolana Sector]], and the vast resources within the [[Tolana Nebula]].

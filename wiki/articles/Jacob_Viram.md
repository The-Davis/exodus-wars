'''Jacob Viram'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Jacob Viram'' was assigned to the Tejat Guard when the [[United Centusi States]] attacked [[Canopus]] on April 8, [[2325]]. The then Major Viram took over defense of the Spire of Truth when his superior was critically injured in the attack.

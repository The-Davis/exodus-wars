== Events of 1945 ==

===January===
*January 5 -  [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] recognizes the new pro-[[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] government of [[Poland (Terra)| Poland]].
*January 12 - [[World War II]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] begins the Vistula-Oder Offensive in Eastern Europe against the Nazis.
*January 16 - [[World War II]]: [[Adolf Hitler]] evacuates to his underground bunker.
*January 17
**[[World War II]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviets]] occupy Warsaw.
**[[World War II]]: Nazis begin to evacuate from Auschwitz Concentration Camp.
*January 20
**[[Franklin Roosevelt]] is inaugurated to an unprecedented fourth term as President of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]].
**[[World War II]]: [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]] drops out of the [[World War II|Second World War]], agreeing to an armistice with the Allies.
*January 24 - [[The Technology Race]]: First successful launch of the [[Germany (Terra)|German]] V-2 Rocket.
*January 27 - The [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]]'s Red Army arrives at Auschwitz and Birkenau in [[Poland (Terra)| Poland]] and find the Nazi Concentration Camp where 1.3 million people were murdered.
*January 28 - [[World War II]]: Supplies begin to reach [[People's Republic of China| China]] over the newly reopened Burma Road.

===February===
*February 2 - [[World War II]]: President [[Franklin Roosevelt]] and Prime Minister [[Winston Churchill]] leave to meet with Premier [[Joseph Stalin]] at the Yalta Conference.
*February 3 - [[World War II]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] agrees to enter the Pacific Theater conflict against [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]].
*February 4 - [[World War II]]: President [[Franklin Roosevelt]], Prime Minister [[Winston Churchill]] and Premier [[Joseph Stalin]] begin the Yalta Conference.
*February 7 - [[World War II]]: General Douglas MacArthur returns to Manila.
*February 10 - [[World War II]]: The SS ''General von Steuben'' sunk by the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet]] submarine S-13.
*February 13
**[[World War II]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] forces capture Budapest, [[Hungary (Terra)| Hungary]] from the Nazis.
**[[World War II]]: The Royal Air Force bombs Dresden, [[Germany (Terra)| Germany]].
*February 14 - [[Chile (Terra)| Chile]], [[Ecuador (Terra)| Ecuador]], [[Paraguay (Terra)| Paraguay]] and [[Peru (Terra)| Peru]] join the [[United Nations]].
*February 16
**[[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] forces land on Corregidor Island in the [[Philippines (Terra)| Philippines]].
**[[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] forces recapture the Bataan Peninsula
*February 19 - [[World War II]]: Battle of Iwo Jima - about 30,000 [[United States (Terra)| United States]] Marines landed on Iwo Jima starting the Battle of Iwo Jima.
*February 23
**[[World War II]]: During the Battle of Iwo Jima, a group of [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Marines reach the top of Mount Suribachi on the island and are photographed raising the [[United States (Terra)|American]] flag.
**[[World War II]]: The capital of the [[Philippines (Terra)|Philippines]], Manila, is liberated by [[United States (Terra)|American]] forces.
**[[World War II]]: Capitulation of [[Germany (Terra)| German]] garrison in Poznań, city is liberated by Red Army and [[Poland (Terra)| Polish]] forces.

===March===
*March 1 - [[Franklin Roosevelt]] gives what will be his last address to a joint session of Congress, reporting on the Yalta Conference.
*March 2 - [[The Technology Race]]: Launch of the [[Germany (Terra)|German]] Bachem Ba 349 from Stetten am kalten Markt. The BA 349 was the first manned rocket. The launch failed and the pilot died.
*March 3
**[[World War II]]: Previously neutral [[Finland (Terra)|Finland]] declares war on the Axis powers.
**[[The Technology Race]]: [[Germany (Terra)|German]] nuclear scientists detonate an experimental atomic weapon at the Nazis' Ohrdruf military testing area. The weapon fails to achieve fission.
*March 6 - [[Communist]]-led government formed in [[Romania (Terra)|Romania]].
*March 7 - [[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] troops seize the bridge over the Rhine River at Remagen, [[Germany (Terra)| Germany]] and begin to cross.
*March 9
**Josip Broz Tito forms a [[Communist]] government in [[Yugoslavia (Terra)| Yugoslavia]]
**[[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] B-29 bombers attack [[Japan (Terra)| Japan]] with incendiary bombs. Tokyo is fire-bombed, killing 100,000 citizens.
*March 16 - [[World War II]]: The Battle of Iwo Jima ends, with small pockets of guerrilla resistance persisting past the official conclusion of the battle.
*March 17 - [[World War II]]: [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] city of Kobe is fire-bombed by 331 B-29 bombers, killing over 8,000.
*March 18 - [[World War II]]: 1,250 [[United States (Terra)|American]] bombers attack Berlin.
*March 19
**[[World War II]]: [[Adolf Hitler]] orders that all industries, military installations, shops, transportation facilities and communications facilities in [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] be destroyed.
**[[World War II]]: Off the coast of [[Japan (Terra)| Japan]], bombers hit the aircraft carrier USS ''Franklin'', killing 800 of her crew and crippling the ship.
*March 21 - [[World War II]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops liberate Mandalay, [[France (Terra)|French Indochina]].
*March 30 - [[World War II]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviet Union]] forces invade [[Austria (Terra)|Austria]] and take Vienna.

===April===
*April 1 - [[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|United States]] troops land on Okinawa in the last campaign of the war. The Battle of Okinawa starts.
*April 4 - [[World War II]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] troops liberate Ohrdruf death camp in [[Germany (Terra)| Germany]].
*April 7 - [[World War II]]: The [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] battleship ''Yamato'' is sunk 200 miles north of Okinawa while enroute on a suicide mission.
*April 9 - [[World War II]]: The Battle of Königsberg ends.
*April 10 - The Allies liberate the Nazi Buchenwald Concentration Camp.
*April 12 - United States President [[Franklin Roosevelt]] dies suddenly at Warm Springs, Georgia. Vice President [[Harry Truman]] becomes the 33rd President.
*April 15 - [[World War II]]: The Bergen-Belsen Concentration Camp is liberated.
*April 16 - [[World War II]]: The ''Goya'' is sunk by the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] submarine L-3.
*April 25
**Multiple nations met for the founding negotiations of [[United Nations]] in San Francisco.
**[[World War II]]: Elbe Day, [[United States (Terra)| United States]] and [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] troops link up at the Elbe River, cutting [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] in two
*April 26 - [[World War II]]: Battle of Bautzen - last "successful" [[Germany (Terra)|German]] panzer-offensive in Bautzen, the city is recaptured
*April 27 - [[United States (Terra)|United States]] ordinance troops find the coffins of Frederick Wilhelm I, Frederick the Great, Paul Von Hindenburg, and his wife
*April 28 - [[Italy (Terra)|Italian]] dictator [[Benito Mussolini]] is executed by [[Italy (Terra)|Italian]] partisans as he attempts to flee the country.  His body is hung by his heels in the public square of Milan.
*April 29 - [[World War II]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] Lancaster bombers drop food into the [[Netherlands]] to prevent the starvation of the civilian population.
*April 30 - [[Adolf Hitler]] commits suicide as the Red Army approaches his bunker in Berlin. Karl Dönitz succeeds Hitler as President of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]].

===May===
*May 1
**Hamburg Radio announces that Hitler is dead.
**Joseph Goebbels and his wife commit suicide after murdering their six children. Karl Dönitz appoints Lutz Schwerin von Krosigk as the new Chancellor of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]].
**[[World War II]]: Troops of Yugoslav 4th Army together with Slovenian troops enter Trieste.
*May 2
**[[World War II]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] announces the fall of Berlin. [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] soldiers hoist the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] flag over the ''Reichstag'' building.
**[[World War II]]: Troops of [[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]] Army enter Trieste a day after the Yugoslavs. [[Germany (Terra)|German]] Army in Trieste surrenders to the [[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]] Army.
*May 3
**[[World War II]]: The Royal Air Force sinks the prison ships ''Cap Arcona'', ''Thielbek'' and ''Deutschland'' in Lübeck Bay.
**[[The Technology Race]]: Rocket scientist [[Wernher von Braun]] and 120 members of his team surrender to [[United States (Terra)|United States]] forces. They later help start the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] space program.
*May 4
**[[World War II]]: Liberation of the Neuengamme Concentration Camp near Hamburg by the [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] army.
**[[World War II]]: [[Netherlands (Terra)|Holland]] liberated by [[Canada (Terra)|Canadian]] troops. [[Germany (Terra)|German]] troops officially surrender one day later.
*May 5
**[[World War II]]: Prague partisans rise up against the Nazis.
**[[World War II]]: A [[United States (Terra)|United States]] armored unit liberates prisoners of the Mauthausen Concentration Camp.
**[[World War II]]: [[Canada (Terra)|Canadian]] soldiers liberate the city of Amsterdam from Nazi occupation.
**[[World War II]]: Karl Dönitz orders all U-boats to cease offensive operations and return to their bases.
*May 7 - [[World War II]]: General Alfred Jodl signs unconditional surrender terms at Rheims, [[France (Terra)|France]], ending [[Germany (Terra)|Germany's]] participation in the war. The document will take effect the next day.
*May 8
**[[World War II]]: V-E Day commemorates the end of [[World War II]] in Europe.
**[[The Jihad]]: Muslims murder and rape hundreds of [[France (Terra)|French]] citizens in Algeria. [[France (Terra)|French]] troops respond with deadly force and kill over 2000 Muslims and fire-bomb six major Muslim settlements. Radio Cairo in [[Arabia (Terra)| Egypt]] exacerbates the situation by falsely reporting that over 45,000 Muslims were killed "without provocation."
*May 9
**[[World War II]]: Hermann Göring is captured by the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Army.
**[[World War II]]: Red Army enters Prague.
**[[World War II]]: Occupation of the Channel Islands ends with the liberation by [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops. Alderney Concentration Camp is liberated.
*May 23
**President of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] Karl Dönitz and Chancellor of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] Lutz Schwerin von Krosigk are arrested by [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] forces at Flensburg. They would respectively be the last [[Germany (Terra)|German]] Head of state and Head of government until [[1949]].
**Heinrich Himmler, the head of the Nazi Gestapo, commits suicide in [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] custody.
*May 25 - In the Atlantic, ships are able to travel at night with their lights lit.
*May 30 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Arabia (Terra)|Iranian]] Muslim religious leaders demand that [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops leave the country.

===June===
*June 1 - [[The Jihad]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] take over [[Arabia (Terra)|Lebanon]] and [[Arabia (Terra)|Syria]], over protests of local Muslim population.
*June 5 - Allied Control Council, the military occupation governing body of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]], formally takes power.
*June 6 - King Haakon VII of [[Norway (Terra)|Norway]] returns to [[Norway (Terra)|Norway]].
*June 11 - William Lyon Mackenzie King is re-elected as [[Canada (Terra)|Canadian]] Prime Minister.
*June 21 - [[World War II]]: The Battle of Okinawa ends.
*June 26 - The [[United Nations]] charter is signed.
*June 29 - [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]] cedes Ruthenia to the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

===July===
*July 1 - [[World War II]]: [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] is divided between Allied occupation forces.
*July 5 - [[World War II]]: Liberation of the [[Philippines (Terra)|Philippines]].
*July 16 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Trinity Test, the first test of an atomic bomb, using 6 kilograms of plutonium, succeeds in detonating, unleashing an explosion equivalent to that of 19 kilotons of TNT.
*July 21 - [[World War II]]: [[Harry Truman]] approves order for atomic bombs to be used.
*July 23 - [[World War II]]: [[France (Terra)|French]] marshall Philippe Pétain, who headed the Vichy government during [[World War II]] goes on trial, charged with treason.
*July 26
**[[Winston Churchill]] is sworn in for another term as the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]]'s prime minister after his Conservative Party defeats the Labour Party of Clement Attlee.
**Potsdam Declaration demands [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]]'s unconditional surrender.
*July 28 - [[World War II]]: [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]] rejects Potsdam Declaration.
*July 30 - [[World War II]]: The USS ''Indianapolis'' is hit and sunk by the [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] submarine I-58. Some 900 survivors jump into the sea and are adrift for 4 days. Nearly 600 die before help arrives. Captain Charles McVay is later court-martialed.

===August===
*August 6 - [[World War II]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United States]] detonates an atomic bomb nicknamed "Little Boy" on Hiroshima, [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]].
*August 7 - President [[Harry Truman]] announces the successful bombing of Hiroshima with an atomic bomb while returning from the Potsdam Conference aboard the heavy cruiser USS ''Augusta'' in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean.
*August 8
**The [[United Nations]] Charter is ratified by the [[United States (Terra)|United States]], making them the third nation to join the new international organization.
**[[World War II]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] declares war on [[Japan (Terra)| Japan]].
*August 9
**[[World War II]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United States]] detonates an atomic bomb nicknamed "Fat Man" over the city of Nagasaki, [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]].
**[[World War II]]: The [[Soviet Union]] begins its offensive against [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]] in the then [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] controlled [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] region of Manchuria. 
*August 10 - [[World War II]]: [[Japan (Terra)| Japan]] offers to surrender to the Allies on the condition that the Emperor retains power.
*August 11 - [[World War II]]: Allies reply to the [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] surrender offer by saying that Emperor Hirohito would be subject to the authority of the Supreme Commander of the Allied Forces.
*August 14 - [[World War II]]: Emperor Hirohito accepts the terms of the Potsdam Declaration.
*August 15
**[[World War II]]: Emperor Hirohito announces [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]]'s surrender on the radio.
**Korea gains independence following [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]]'s surrender
*August 17 - [[The Jihad]]: Indonesian nationalists Sukarno and Mohammed Hatta declare the independence of Republic of Indonesia, Sukarno as a president. [[Netherlands (Terra)|Dutch]] colonial authorities do not approve.
*August 19 - [[Vietnam War]]: Viet Minh led by Ho Chi Minh attempt to take power in Hanoi, [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]]. They are driven out by the population.
*August 26 - Mao Zedong and Chiang Kai-shek meet in Chongqing to discuss an end to hostilities between the [[Communists]] and the Nationalists.

===September===
*September 2 - [[World War II]] ends: The final official surrender of [[Japan (Terra)|Japan]] was accepted by Douglas MacArthur and Chester Nimitz from a delegation led by Mamoru Shigemitsu, aboard the USS ''Missouri'' in Tokyo Bay.
*September 4 - [[World War II]]: [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] forces surrender on Wake Island after hearing word of their nation's surrender.
*September 8 - [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops occupy southern [[Korea (Terra)|Korea]], [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] troops occupy the north. This arrangement proves to be the beginning of a divided [[Korea (Terra)|Korea]].
*September 12 - [[Japan (Terra)|Japanese]] army formally surrendered in Singapore.
*September 20 - [[Mohandas Gandhi]] and Jawaharlal Nehru demand that [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops leave [[British Empire (Terra)|India]].

===October===
*October 1 - [[The Technology Race]]: Launch of three V-2 rockets near Cuxhaven in order to show Allied forces the rocket with liquid fuel.
*October 10 - [[Cold War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Russian]] code clerk Igor Gouzenko defects to [[Canada (Terra)|Canada]]. He helps the West gain an understanding of [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] spy rings in North America.
*October 27 - [[The Jihad]]: Indonesian Muslim separatists riot and fight [[Netherlands (Terra)|Dutch]] and [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] security forces.

===November===
*November 13 - [[Charles de Gaulle]] is elected head of a [[France (Terra)|French]] provisional government.
*November 16 - [[Cold War]]: The [[United States (Terra)| United States]] controversially imports 88 [[Germany (Terra)|German]] scientists to help in the production of rocket technology.
*November 29
**The Socialist Republic of [[Yugoslavia (Terra)| Yugoslavia]] is declared.
**[[The Technology Race]]: Assembly of the world's first general purpose electronic computer, the Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer ([[ENIAC]]), is completed. It covers 1800 feet of floor space. The first set of calculations is run on the computer.

===December===
*December 3 - [[Cold War]]: [[Communist]] demonstrations in Athens are a preliminary move in the [[Greece (Terra)|Greek]] Civil War.
*December 4 - By a vote of 65 to 7, the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Senate approves the entry of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] into the [[United Nations]].
*December 21 - General George Patton dies from injuries sustained in a car accident.
*December 27 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslim terrorists strike against [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] military bases in Palestine.

===Overview===

Kondo'jahazi aina Mafutuni are very common Ngrligru cruisers.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaMafutuni1.jpg]]

They are actually designed after a large, aggressive Ngrligru swamp amphibian, though it lacks the six legs of the living Mafutuni.

===Capabilities===

Mafutuni are the smallest ships to carry Mkuki'kali Energy Lances.  They also carry Bunduki'kubwa Hyperspace Distortion Cannons, Gobori'dogo Relativistic Mass Drivers, and Mshale'liga Missile Tubes.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaMafutuni2.jpg]]

It is as easily as powerful as any two humanoid cruisers, with the obvious exception of the Meroniris.

===Deployment===

Mafutuni are usually assigned to act as close escorts to their Mia'mbili's Nina'rikebu, or as scout vessels.

Before the [[Sundering]], the world of [[Kronheim]] was an unnamed scientific outpost of the [[Anthronoris]]. The scientists were working on altering planetary orbits, axises and rotational rates in order to terraform even more worlds for the [[Anthronoris Empire]].

==Fear==
Before the [[Ngrligru]] came to their world, the [[Anthronoris]] who would eventually become the [[Ikronin]] destroyed everything that could be detected from orbit, and hid in caves. Their leader, [[Tyrus Ikronus]], a distant cousin of the [[Anthronoris]] emperor, formed a plan. [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]] was not certain what was happening on other worlds, but he knew that the [[Sundering]] was unstoppable. The last reports from home told of the destruction of entire war fleets, and the deaths of entire worlds. [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]] was determined to survive, and to do that he needed the loyalty of the [[Anthronoris]] living with him. He gave them hope, telling them they would build a future for their children.

Knowing that the sum of all their knowledge could never be passed on to their children, the [[Anthronoris]] of [[Kronheim]] began developing the basics needed to ensure that they would not fall into a dark age. [[Tyrus Ikronus]] organized the [[Anthronoris]] on his world into three groups.

The hunters of [[Kronheim]], led by [[Tyrus Ikronus]], secured low-tech renewable food sources, and tamed the native [[Fenrulf]] as a source of food, companionship, and riding animals.

The teachers of [[Kronheim]], led by [[Sorel Tharkon]], worked tirelessly to compress the basic knowledge of science and culture into a form that could be easily and consistently passed on to their children, and developed the runic alphabet due to the lack of computers or great amounts of paper to record knowledge on, using stone and wood instead for teaching tools. These runes, and the spoken language that eventually evolved in later [[Ikronin]] society, became known as "[[Sothark]]."

The archivists of [[Kronheim]] used the only paper supplies and [[Sorel Tharkon]]'s runic alphabet to record as much advanced knowledge as they could.

==Death==

Despite their efforts, their time and resources were limited. They only had a few weeks worth of immortality serum: the project was supposed to receive supplies on a daily basis from the [[Anthronoris]], but the regular supply flights stopped soon after the beginning of the [[Sundering]].

[[Tyrus Ikronus]] secured the entire supply for himself, telling the other [[Anthronoris]] he was exercising his rights as leader. [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]] believed in enlightened self-interest, and this selfish act set the course of [[Ikronin]] society's capitalist dogma.

The first project members began to die off after a hundred years, but not before teaching their children the basic skills needed for an orderly society, and leaving behind the knowledge of technology and medicine.

[[Tyrus Ikronus]] was left alive, alone among the second, third and fourth generations of [[Anthronoris]], who began calling themselves the [[Ikronin]] out of respect for the man who's plan meant their survival.


==Rebuilding a World==

The first [[Ikronin]] society watched the sky with dread, looking for the [[Ngrligru]]. When the dreaded [[Sundering]] never came to their world, they slowly crept out of their caves and began to rebuild. The original group of [[Anthronoris]] in hiding had built a coal-fired turbine generator in their cave complex, and the [[Ikronin]] followed this model at first.

From the dawn of their society, they had electricity for lighting and heating, and understood the concepts and sciences behind pressurization and combustion. They knew the formulas for hundreds of chemical reactions and compounds, and had the research to continue development. At first, the Ikronin stayed in one small, tight-knit community, limiting their growth and potential.

==Succession==

In the fifth generation, [[Tyrus Ikronus]] was killed by [[Yngvi the Aged|Yngvi]], who learned the secrets of [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]]' immortality, and took the last supplies for himself. [[Yngvi the Aged|Yngvi]] feared discovery and destruction by the [[Ngrligru]], and believed that [[Tyrus Ikrous|Tyrus]]' plans for staying in one place was asking for a disaster if the [[Ngrligru]] ever discovered them and brought the horrors of the [[Sundering]].

[[Yngvi the Aged|Yngvi]] established his capital city at [[Vighelir]], and declared himself the king of the [[Ikronin]]. He began establishing population limits on settlements, and made the [[Ikronin]] begin to expand across the planet. Each settlement had to trade with each other in order to survive. Yngvi established the "Rules of Trade and Equivalency," outlawing altruism and benevolence between each settlement, believing it would make them stronger.

==Death of Yngvi==

The last of the supply of immortality serum [[Yngvi the Aged|Yngvi]] stole from [[Tyrus Ikronus|Tyrus]] ran out in the eighth generation of [[Ikronin]]. "[[Yngvi the Aged]]," first [[Kejsare]] of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], was succeeded by [[Delin I]], his youngest grandson (but oldest surviving descendant).


==Stunted Development==

The development of [[Ikronin]] [[:Category:Technology|technology]] was hampered by several factors. Early in their history, [[Kronheim]] had a mild climate due to the experiments the [[Anthronoris]] had been conducting. Unfortunately, their experiment was not complete. Over the years, the climate slowly deteriorated, often leading to seasons of harsh weather that hampered expansion and hurt their food supplies, limiting the growth of their population. Some of the teachings were lost: paper didn’t keep well, and not everything could be restored by the time the [[Ikronin]] began milling paper again. Scientific research and development was an afterthought to mere survival and expansion.

Many raw materials were not available at first: the [[Ikronin]] developed mines, and had access to coal, wood, crude oil, iron, aluminum, and a multitude of other metals. Most notably missing was copper: it was extremely rare. So much so, that the [[Ikronin]] began to develop copper deficiencies in their bodies. This was the first major change between the [[Ikronin]] and the [[Anthronoris]] aside from the loss of the immortality serum. Their hair became stark white, and many developed anemia. Their bodies continued to adapt, until their marrow used other minerals in addition to copper to generate red blood cells, though their hair never regained its color. [[Ikronin]] have had white hair ever since, unless they dye it. Additionally, low levels of Vitamin B folate and high levels of Vitamin D in their food supplies led to increased levels of pheomelanin in their bodies, making [[Ikronin]] skin tones a rich reddish-brown, protecting their bodies’ Vitamin B supplies from sunlight.

==The Long Winter==

Thousands of years passed, and despite [[Kronheim]]’s slowly deteriorating climate, the [[Ikronin]] continued to develop their technology and society. In [[1500BC]], they had developed space travel to great levels, but their world was in the severe grips of an ice age. The climate had stabilized, but the planet’s orbit was it the outermost regions of the habitable zone of their star. Desperate to find new worlds, the second son of the current king, prince [[Raegos Direnson]], led a convoy of slow sleeper ships into the depths of space.

Despite never hearing from [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]]’ colony again, the [[Ikronin]] managed to survive and thrive even in their world’s ice age. [[Kronheim]]’s equatorial region kept a balmy and temperate climate. The tundra regions remained inhabited. The polar regions, stuck in permafrost and glacial caps, were abandoned by all but the most stubborn [[Ikronin]].

==End of the Kingdom==

The line of kings was broken in [[1000BC]], when the last king died with no heir. A group of wealthy merchants took control of the government, naming one of their people the [[Handelboss]], which became the head of state. This new corporate oligarchy has continued to this day.

== First Contact ==

For much of their early space-faring career, the Ikronin developed their Wormhole Gate technology. They sought out worlds that could be of use for them, and set up a Gate back to [[Kronheim]]. In the year [[1000]]AD, the [[Ikronin]] and [[Meroniri Terinasi]] both explored a world full of useful resources.

The drastic differences in their culture were easy to overcome, as both had peaceful intentions--though their vastly different languages made for some rather awkward meetings. Before long, the two races had set up contact with each other and established trade routes. By installing Gates over every [[Meroniri]] world, the Ikronin made star travel cheap and affordable even to lower class people.

While this was not yet the start of the [[Alliance of Planets]], the two civilizations established a strong bond of friendship that would survive unbroken for millennia. They also agreed not to interfere in the development of any race that had not yet discovered hyperspace, wormholes, or other forms of faster than light travel.

==End of the Long Winter==

In [[2170]], the polar regions began to recede for the first time in 4100 years. The long ice age was beginning to end. The [[Ikronin]] saw their first population boom, which began to be noticed in [[2319]], when [[Kronheim]]’s cities began to be severely crowded. The [[Ikronin]] began to make plans to expand, and in [[2325]], after the [[Prefecture]] came to their aid in the [[Battle of Kronheim]], they launched the [[Myrdrasil Project]] and opened the first joint colony in history.

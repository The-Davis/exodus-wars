'''Scott Sanburn''' is one of the founders of the Exodus Wars universe.

When the story first began, [[James Davis (author)|James Davis]], [[Andrew Sears (author)|Andrew Sears]] and Scott were all characters in the story as well as contributors.

Eventually, the character named after [[James Davis (author)|James Davis]] was replaced with [[Anthony Hadrian]], and the character named after [[Andrew Sears (author)|Andrew Sears]] was replaced with [[Andreas Tischler]].

Because Scott is no longer an active contributor to the project, and more of a "resident fan," the authors of the Exodus Wars universe chose to keep his name in use for the character [[Scott Sanburn]].

== Events of 88BC ==

Construction of the first [[Ixoarchada]] starship, the ''[[Saigos]]'' is completed and a crew of twenty is selected to be the first [[Ixoarchada]] to return to [[Archada]] in many millenia.  The ship launches at the end of the year and sets down on [[Archada]].  Its return module is prepared but never launched, and the ''[[Saigos]]'' remains permanently on [[Archada]] as the first manned station.

The core of the Kalidasa Security Force is the Security Trooper.  These soldiers are the defenders of Kalidasa, and man the vehicles of the KSF.  

The standard Security Trooper is outfitted with a Boron-Nitride Armor suit, a home-grown variant of the Prefecture's Infantry Power Suit, and utilizes a similar system of assisted motion servos. 

In addition to the armored suit, the Security Trooper is issued a utility pack, which holds a wide variety of equipment including rations, ammunition clips, grenades, high explosives, entrenchment tools, homing beacons, and a host of other equipment.  The servos in the armor suit allow the Security Trooper to carry this extra equipment, an additional fifty kilograms, without any major exertion of energy. 

[[Image:KalidasaSecurityTrooper.jpg]]

The KSF Security Trooper's standard issue weapon is the Kalidasan-made Infantry Rifle, Model 2325, or IR-25.  The IR-25 is fed by combination ammo/power cell magazines that hold fifty rounds, and a small power cell that can power the rifle long enough to empty the magazine.  The IR-25 fires all the variable rounds utilized by the advanced Prefecture rifles, including the powerful "breecher" rounds, "flechette" rounds, and super-sonic rounds. 

The KSF employs Security Troopers throughout their planetary garrisons.  They are a common site on Kalidasa, often deployed at spaceports, major commerical centers, and other important locations.  Civilians see them often, but rarely mind the additional security. 

Although they train for defense of the planet from hostile forces, KSF Security Troopers more often spend their active time in support of Kalidasan Police forces.  The KSF operates the only SWAT-type rapid deployment teams on the planet, and thus they are called upon by the police whenever such teams are needed.

'''Portugal''', officially the '''Portuguese Republic''', was located in southwestern Europe on the Iberian Peninsula, and was the westernmost country of mainland Europe. Portugal was bordered by [[Spain (Terra)|Spain]] to the north and east and by the Atlantic Ocean to the west and south. The Atlantic archipelagos of the Azores and Madeira were also part of Portugal.

During the [[15th Century|15th]] and [[16th Century|16th centuries]] Portugal was one of the world's major economic, political, and cultural powers. The empire declined over the years, but at the time of its dissolution Portugal still had many overseas territories.

Portugal signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on July 25, [[2012]], and was dissolved as an independent nation.

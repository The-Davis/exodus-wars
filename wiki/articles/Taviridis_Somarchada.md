===History===
Main Article: [[History of the Taviridis Somarchada|History]]

The tale of the [[Ixoarchada]] dates back to the fall of the [[Anthronoris Empire]] and the final days of the [[Sundering]].  Due to the distance from the [[Teeth of Orion]] to the [[Tolana Sector]], only a portion of the [[Ngrligru]] invasion force ever reached as far as Ixoarchada space.  They were defeated by the last remaining fleet elements of the [[Anthronoris]] in the Tolana Sector, which pursued the battered Ngrligru forces coreward, never to return.  Thanks to this small force of ships, however, the people of [[Glengarr]] survived as a civilization.  

After the Sundering, the Ixoarchada developed in isolation and charted much of the Tolana Sector.  They went off in search of other space-faring peoples, hoping to find others that still traveled the stars, perhaps other [[Human]] races.  What the Ixoarchada found instead was the [[Anderung]], and for over a thousand years they vied with them for supremacy, and survival, in the Tolana Sector.  

It was actually the pursuit of the last remnants of the Anderung that led the Ixoarchada to rediscover their lost brethren in the [[23rd Century]].  It was then that they discovered the home world of the Anderung slave race, the Mesarnak.  Exploring further, the Ixoarchada found the [[Terrans]], and watched uneasily as they fought and destroyed one another in the [[Second Exodus War]]. 

It was not until after the war ended that the Taviridis Somarchada finally voted to initiate first contact with these other races.  On September 19th, [[2323]], the Ixoarchada ventured to [[Mesar]] in the [[Centus System]] and made their presence known.  The [[Mesarthrim]], fellow enemies of the Anderung, seemed the obvious choice to most of the Ixoarchada leaders of the time.

After [[First Contact]], the policy of observation and non-interference ended abruptly. Trade and commerce took off, but the galaxy outside of Taviridis space proved far more hostile and turbulent than the stability enjoyed in the [[Tolana Sector]]. Within a few years, the Taviridis Somarchada fought battles in several star systems, joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]], and found themselves at odds with the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] at nearly every turn.

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of the Taviridis Somarchada|Government]]

The Taviridis Somarchada are named for the plural form of [[Iridi]], autonomous city-states, stressing the importance of the city-state in the function of the Ixoarchada government.  The Taviridis is comprised of over a hundred major Iridi, and thousands of minor Iridi.  They are united by race, territory, history, and culture. All Iridi send representatives to [[Cathairn]], the capital city-state equally owned by all Ixoarchada.

Matters of concern to all Ixoarchada, and all matters concerning external affairs with non-Ixoarchada, are handled in the [[Parcna Cathairn]]--the representative council of the city.  Cathairn has the power to pass laws within its authority, and to enforce these by almost any means.  Additionally, when the Ixoarchada go to war, it is the council at Cathairn which puts out a call for [[RhováDeviri]] from the Iridi.  

However, aside from external affairs, Cathairn has almost no power over the other Iridi, existing as one among many.  The individual governments of the Iridi are greatly varied, ranging from true democracies to hereditary governorships, depending on the Iridi.  However, all Iridi have some form of representative council with legislative powers.  

The judicial branch of government is comprised of career judges drawn from the [[Ibelli Sara]], or religious and scholar caste.  

After First Contact, the council at Cathairn created a limited executive post, filled as needed, to negotiate and otherwise deal with other Human governments.  [[Drimont Mandara]] has held this position since its creation in 2323.  Additionally, the ranking commander among the RhováDeviri is given command of the Armies of [[Cathairn]], placing him in command of any assembled RhováDeviri host.

===Law===
Main Article: [[Law of the Taviridis Somarchada|Law]]

Although many laws are uniform from one Iridi to another, many other laws are quite varied depending on the individual city-state's government.  This is most perplexing to many outsiders, as the Taviridis Somarchada are often portrayed as far more homogenous.  Only the decrees passed in the Parcna Cathairn, as well as the founding mandates of the [[Parcna Somglengarr]] (and their revisions dating to the formation of the Taviridis Somarchada) are truly universally applicable.  

Much of the law is based on precedent, and legal matters are presented to judges of the Ibelli Sara.  Precedent can be obtained from decisions several millenia old.  Some codified law is passed by individual Iridi councils, and is applicable only within a single city-state.  Further, almost all codified law has a temporal limit and is nullified within a specific time period.  If judges rule on matters concerning an individual codified law, then it becomes part of legal precedent.  If they do not rule on it before its term expires, then it must either be re-codified or it simply falls from use.  

===Law Enforcement===
Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Taviridis Somarchada|Law Enforcement]]

All Iridi maintain their own security and law enforcement forces, and the structure and function of these vary widely between the various Iridi.  Only Cathairn maintains anything akin to a federal police force, and the main duty of these organizations is to enforce the decrees of the Parcna Cathairn in other Iridi.  There is also an organization for handling crime that spans multiple Iridi, but it is mainly designed to coordinate efforts among various police forces, not operate on its own. 

===Foreign Relations===
[[Foreign Relations of the Taviridis Somarchada|Foreign Relations]]

The Taviridis are a Member State of the Council of Independent Nations, and also maintain friendly relations with a number of non-Council states.  They are currently at odds with and under a trade embargo from the [[Alliance of Planets]], and nearly came to blows with the Meroniri Terinasi over the [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Alliance Space Police]] blockade of Council worlds.  The [[Tempest War]] has greatly complicated relations with the [[Prefecture]], [[Ikronin Jurekön]], and [[United Centusi States]].  

===Military===
Main Article: [[Military of the Taviridis Somarchada|Military]]

The caste system of the Ixoarchada means that their warriors are from one caste: the Ixoarchada RhováDeviri. The RhováDeviri have three sub-castes trained for combat in different specialties:
*[[NagáDeviri]] - Naval tacticians, starship crews, flight crews, and engineers
*[[SarnéDeviri]] - Ground commanders, soldiers, combat engineers, and vehicle crews
*[[DreináDeviri]] - Pilots

Each Iridi maintains its own standing force of RhováDeviri.  However, no Iridi have fought wars against one another in over two millenia.  Should war be declared, Cathairn calls a general muster, and requests specific amounts of various units.  The Iridi fill these requests as they have units available.  Some Iridi no longer maintain all three sub-castes, now specializing instead in a single area.  

=== Intelligence ===

Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Taviridis Somarchada|Intelligence]]

The Ixoarchada do not engage in espionage in the traditional sense. Historically, their enemies have been wholly-alien to them, with societies completely insusceptible to infiltration and subterfuge.

Instead, their military developed a vast data-gathering network of remote or manned observation systems, both static and mobile in their position. From simple cameras placed in the field of battle all the way to covert ops [[:Category:Starships|starships]], the commanders of the RhováDeviri always have up-to-date information at their fingertips.

Facing the possibility of war with other human civilizations, many members of the RhováDeviri wonder how their systems would measure up to the much wider scope of infiltration-based intelligence gathering agencies. Among the RhováDeviri, there are supporters and critics of developing an intelligence agency, but none of them deny the effectiveness and danger of an assassin on the ground, having observed the effectiveness, ruthless efficiency, and fear instilled by the pervasive threat of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Omegas]].

The main source of Ixoarchada intelligence-gathering is through the monitoring of foreign communications traffic. While the [[Lorithos]] pride themselves on their spy network's ability to be everywhere at once, their own [[Urokon]] has allowed the Ixoarchada to monitor the entirety of human civilizations by "piggy-backing" on the Lorithos communication protocol. While the Urokon is the most omnipresent source of information, the Ixoarchada monitor many other sources, including microwave transmissions and all other forms of broadcasts still in use, often from the safety of [[Subspace]].

=== Transportation ===

Main Article: [[Transportation of the Taviridis Somarchada|Transportation]]

Within an Iridi most Ixoarchada use a combination of mass transit and personal transportation. Mass transit systems are focused for longer distances, such as from one end of an Iridi to another (often a distance of two hundred kilometers or more). Personal transit is most useful for Ixoarchada who travel between Iridi, or frequently travel between destinations not connected by mass transit.

Travel between Ixoarchada systems is completely unregulated by the Parcna Cathairn, and all citizens may book passage without being monitored or questioned by government officials.

Leaving the Tolana Sector is strictly monitored and regulated. In order to leave Ixoarchada-controlled space, a citizen is required to file a full itenerary with travel officials. Additionally, they undergo full medical scans, exhaustive searches, and are psychologically examined. Their business outside the Tolana Sector is evaluated for any potential threat to the sovereignty and superiority of the Ixoarchada before it is approved. Citizens who are tardy in returning are strictly penalized, and even imprisoned. Fugitives who refuse to return are tracked down by the more covert elements of the RhováDeviri, and their activities are monitored and evaluated to determine whether their reluctance to return is a threat to the Taviridis.

Permanent emigration is permitted, however all implants and network links are surgically removed prior to the Parcna Cathairn authorizing their departure.

=== Territory ===

Main Article: [[Territory of the Taviridis Somarchada|Territory]]

The Ixoarchada define their territory in a category known as "[[Polnavi]]", which is loosely translated as "frontiers". The Tolana Sector is made up of three Polnavi, though the Taviridis itself only actively occupies the "[[Pithari]]" or "core" [[:Category:Star Systems|systems]], which are in the central Polnavi in the [[Tolana Sector]].

While the remaining two Polnavi are mostly uninhabited, the Ixoarchada jealously guard them against any would-be interlopers. The bloody wars with the Anderung led to a series of heavily-defended, carefully-monitored border stations along the [[Tolana Sector|Tolana]]-[[Pelagrim Sector|Pelagrim]] frontier.

Ixoarchada worlds are almost always artificially terraformed. Only Glengarr itself predates the Sundering; every other world discovered as the Ixoarchada expanded into space had to be carefully cultivated over the course of several centuries using orbital sky factories.

Central to Ixoarchada culture and territory is the [[Tolana Nebula]], located just rimward of Glengarr itself. For millenia, the lost children of the Anthronoris looked up at the cloud of stars, and believed that their gods dwelt within. As they took to the stars themselves, they learned that the nebula held the greatest source of wealth imaginable: [[antimatter]]. The Tolana Nebula is the cornerstone of interstellar commerce and the source of Ixoarchada galactic power.

=== Economy ===

Main Article: [[Economy of the Taviridis Somarchada|Economy]]

The Ixoarchada have a highly-advanced and robust economy, significantly stronger than most other human civilizations. The last recorded recession within their economy was over four hundred years ago.

The prosperity of the Ixoarchada comes from their refined technology and obsessive level of automation. Many areas of their industrial output is run at full capacity throughout their fiscal year. Their society thrives in a self-imposed semi-isolationist state, which requires an imposing degree of self-sufficiency.

The central government at Cathairn exercises a high degree of control over international imports and exports. Although several non-Ixoarchada technologies are highly sought after within the Taviridis, the Parcna Cathairn steadfastly guards against degradation of Ixoarchada society and prosperity via pollution of non-Ixoarchada goods and values, while simultaneously working to establish a manageable system of international external trade. 

Critics of the Parcna Cathairn accuse them of draconian trade practices, which they postulate are purely inspired by rampant xenophobia and a desire for military and technological supremacy. More radical critics, mostly criticized themselves as conspiracy theorists, believe the Parcna Cathairn's isolationism will lead to a RhováDeviri-dominated plutocracy.

The national currency, the [[Mirsz]], is not a freely traded asset. Its rate of exchange is ''strictly'' mandated by the Parcna Cathairn, and set against the [[Reigen]] [[Varde]]. The exchange rates are continuously monitored and adjusted in real-time at the direction of a series of matrixed computer cores.

=== Demographics ===

Main Article: [[Demographics of the Taviridis Somarchada|Demographics]]

The cultural and socio-political divisions among the Ixoarchada were once highly-focused on the caste system. There were once over a dozen castes with rigidly-defined social roles. Now, the divisions between the casts are very blurred, with only three major social divisions.

The RhováDeviri are the warrior caste, living lives of seclusion from the majority of the non-fighting populace. Each Iridi is responsible for maintaining a "fighter's quarter" within their city-state, where the RhováDeviri spend their time training for war. The eldest war-masters are treated like exalted sages, both within the RhováDeviri caste itself and within the Iridi.

The Ibelli Sara, a scholarly and religious caste, has the privaledge of a physically easy life, but the responsibility to interpret and pass the laws of the Iridi. They are revered among the other castes, but not greatly elevated beyond the "commoners" from other castes.

The remaining population is an amalgamation of the older castes. Collectively they are called [[Opni]], or "the peoples". Among the main population, heritage is still considered important for social status and standing among their peers; however having ancestors from the noble caste does not infer any privilege or rank.

=== Public Health ===

Main Article: [[Public Health of the Taviridis Somarchada|Public Health]]

Ixoarchada technological development was so keenly focused on nanotechnology and prostheses that they never achieved the vast arsenal of antibiotic and antiviral treatments that are the hallmark of Terran medicines, the more holistic approach of Reigen medicine, or the hyper-advanced medicinal treatments in use by the Meroniri. If an organ fails, they replace it with an artificial prosthetic.

Their nanotechnology serves multiple purposes, but has a serious drawback. The "vigilant watchman" approach to an artificial immuno-defense system was a necessity during the wars with the Anderung, but it led to total incompatibility with organ transplant and most organic medicines. The introduction of the Prefecture's organ regeneration technology has been seen as an outstanding boon to public health and a viable alternative to a purely prosthetic approach.

When visiting the Iridi, non-Ixoarchada are cautioned to take inoculations against fungal infections and to invest in a portable air recycler. Glengarr's biosphere has an average humidity of over 45%, and all of the artificially-designed biospheres of Ixoarchada worlds use Glengarr as a model. The air is always thick with fungal spores, causing severe (and occasionally fatal) allergic reactions in non-natives.

=== Culture ===

Main Article: [[Culture of the Taviridis Somarchada|Culture]]

Among the central pillars of Ixoarchada cultural values is the concept of fulfilling a purpose in life. This largely stems from the ancient caste system, where everyone had to follow a rigidly-defined role, given to them by reason of birth.

In modern times, many Ixoarchada find themselves adrift in the world, devoid of purpose. With automated systems handling all of their necessities, and only the geneticly-designed warriors being trained for the military, the average citizen must find and define their own purpose in life. In such an environment of societal ennui, one may expect to find an exorbitant suicide rate. Among the Ixoarchada, this is not the case.

Family values are founded on the principles of helping family members find their niche in life, their role in serving their Iridi and their people. Some sociologists believe that without this structure of guidance and direction, the youth of the Ixoarchada would rapidly develop a "mob society" of lower cultural refinement, and soon lead to an end to the Taviridis. Fortunately, this has not been the case.

Culturally, many of the Iridi are significantly different. Older Iridi are starkly contrasted from their other archaic neighbors, with a clearly-defined identity that sets them apart from all others. Iridi founded more recently have evolved a more homogenous cultural system, stemming largely from their origin; relying on immigration from most or all of the older Iridi.

===Overview===

The ''Alexandrovna'' began its life ago as a Gardien frigate, a former French Navy design adopted into the newly-formed United Earth Alliance Defense Force.  It served in the First Exodus War under the command of Captain Vicente Alvarez.  It later served during a number of policing actions in various colonies until it was retired and sold for scrap.  It was purchased by a breaking yard in the Sirius District, but was purchased by the Asanova family before it could be scrapped. 

[[Image:STSAlexandrovna1.jpg]]

Today's ''Alexandrovna'' bears only a slight resemblance to its original class.  Engineering analysts have concluded that only about 2% of the ship is original, with the other 98% having been replaced by a modern equivalent, or completely removed in a series of structural overhauls dating to the early 23rd century.  However, the ''Alexandrovna'' still serves as an Asanovan starship, and is one of the oldest Terran warships still in use, or even in existence, today. 

Capabilities:

The current configuration of the ''Alexandrovna'' is the product of a ship overhaul in 2320, and a later refit in 2324.  Its heaviest weaponry is an Ashur Cannon twin-turret, mounted under the ship.  With the addition of Kalidasan-designed Advanced Neutron Cannons in a pair of Fast-Tracking twin-turrets, the ''Alexandrovna'' maintains a significant anti-warship armament.  Also, four Anti-Aerospace Emplacements give it an excellent AA coverage. 

[[Image:STSAlexandrovna2.jpg]]

The modern ''Alexandrovna'' has a small aerospace launch and recovery bay.  While it can carry a single squadron of fighters, it more often carries shuttles for use by the various diplomats and businessmen that frequent the ship.  When equipped for shuttles, two Asps are also carried to act as an honor guard or escort fighters. 

Boron-Nitride Composite Armor, Gradient Gravity Shields, Magnetic Deflector Screens, and a Barrier Plasma Shield are all recent additions to the ''Alexandrovna''.  Another recent addition is a Gravitational Cavitation drive, which allows the ship to perform maneuvers otherwise impossible with its older impulse drives. 

===Deployment===

Although the ''Kirov'' is the official flagship of the Asanova Territory, the ''Alexandrovna'' is a more common sight among the other Sirius Territories, and foreign star systems in general.  Its relative efficiency compared to the Kirov makes it the prefered starship for carrying Asanova family members and representatives to meet with the other Bosses, or with various other governments.  Due to this, the ''Alexandrovna's'' guest quarters are quite spacious and upscale, especially compared to most Asanova starships.  

[[Image:STSAlexandrovna3.jpg]]

The ''Alexandrovna'' is also the Sirius Administrations official honor guard vessel at the Council of Independent Nations.  Other than this, the ''Alexandrovna'' is seldom used for patrol or security details, although it is fully capable.

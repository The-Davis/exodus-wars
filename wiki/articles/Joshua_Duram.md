'''Joshua Duram'''
*Date of Death: July, [[2318]]
*Place of Birth: [[Tejat]]
*Place of Death: [[Tejat]]

''Joshua Duram'' was a member of the [[Tejat]]i [[Resistance against the Empire|Resistance]]. He is noted for writing "[[Is Titan Still Free?]]", a song which would eventually become the national anthem of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

''Jerome Black'' was the founder of the [[Torimur]] colony and the father of [[Lucius Black]]. Jerome was widely admired and looked up to, and even respected by his political foes on [[Earth]]. He died at the relatively young age of 53, and his wife [[Aydan Black]] died soon afterwards, leaving their son Lucius orphaned and alone.

== Political Activist ==

Jerome was an outspoken critic of the [[United Earth Alliance]] and its aggressive expansionist policies of the early [[21st Century]]. He often ruffled feathers in the United Earth Alliance Assembly, but was respected by two Presidents and one Premier.

When Jerome heard about the Alliance's campaign to annex [[Bethija]] by force, he responded in person with weapons and supplies for [[Artem Kuts]]'s seperatist forces. Jerome Black and [[Giorgio Hadrian]], one of Torimur's veteran pilots, managed to run the Alliance blockade. The two of them hid their ship and joined the partisan forces on the ground.

Unfortunately, Artem Kuts was killed by the Alliance, and Giorgio Hadrian was captured in the same battle. Jerome Black managed to slip away in the fighting and returned to his ship, but was shot down by the [[UEAS Aleksey Tarasov|UEAS ''Aleksey Tarasov'']] as he tried to run the blockade around Bethija. Jerome was not nearly as skilled a pilot as Giorgio Hadrian.

== Legacy ==

Jerome Black's death turned the already strong anti-unification movement on [[Torimur]] into a firestorm, which [[Lucius Black]] eventually capitalized on to goad many other worlds into a war against [[Earth]] in [[2078]].

In modern times, Jerome Black is a cultural hero. His son's [[Onyx Empire]] named their flagship after him, the [[HMS Jerome Black|HMS ''Jerome Black'']], and the later [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] kept the ship and the name, re-christening it the [[FDS Jerome Black|FDS ''Jerome Black'']].

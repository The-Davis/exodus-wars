In an interstellar economy as large as the [[Prefecture]], '''transportation''' of goods and people is a vital part of day to day life. The [[Prefecture]] uses ground, air, sea and space transportation every day.

==Highways==

Most highways on Prefecture worlds are public free access, and maintained by private companies contracted and paid for by planetary governments. Most roads are asphalt with tarmac surfacing.

Metropolis-level cities are designed with vehicle transportation in mind. The main road arteries running through the city are twenty lane highways, with ten lanes going in each direction. Giant "clover-leaf" interchanges are in the center of the cities to switch between main arteries. Additionally, ten lane beltways are built around the circumference of most cities, a minimum of 10 kilometers from the planned long-term border to the city to allow for expansion.

Smaller roads within cities are still usually a minimum of four lanes, two in each direction. The average road is six lanes. At major intersections, one of the roads is bridged over the cross-road, or tunneled under, preventing major traffic jams by long traffic signal queues.

Most major cities have the parking capacity for at least a million passenger vehicles. Major office buildings, factories, warehouses, shopping centers, and similar areas where people congregate are constructed over deep automated parking garages. A motorist enters the garage, scans their identification, and their vehicle is automatically parked and recovered for them.

==Starports and Airports==

Every world in the Prefecture has at least one starport, which provides launching and landing facillities for every class of [[:Category:Starship|starship]] in common use. Most [[:Category:Starship|starships]] have enough power to land without the need for runways, but are so large that the space needed to accomodate them covers as much as half a kilometer each. Starports are meant exclusively for cargo transport: most civilian traffic uses [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] and the [[Wormhole Gates]] for shorter interstellar voyages, and [[:Category:Starliners|starliners]] tend to land in bodies of water, which are gentler on their more delicate systems.

Airports have hangars, runways, helipads, concourses to support hundreds of [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] at a time. Airports differ from military airfields: there are no ammo dumps, and no sleeping quarters. Runways are designed for efficiency and scheduling rather than for rapid response. Concourses are designed with aesthetics in mind, and are meant to be pleasing to the eye of civilian travellers.

Most worlds have dozens of starports and hundreds of airports. All starports are hooked up to major highways. Many airports are as well, though most cities have smaller regional airports within their limits.

==Seaports==

Seaports are built in deep water harbors in the oceans of various worlds. They are not as common as starports. In fact, many worlds in the [[Prefecture]] do not have full seaport facilities for cargo shipping, relying entirely on air travel.

Most worlds do have smaller seaports for their local fishing industries. Trawlers and other hunting craft are still a common sight on many [[Prefecture]] worlds.

==Orbital Stations==

Every world in the Prefecture has at least one orbital space station, which provides off-loading and docking facilities for ships that cannot land on planet surfaces.

Most orbital space stations are several kilometers long, with docking facilities for ten or more [[:Category:Starships|starships]] at a time. They also have airtight hangars for cargo and passenger [[:Category:Aircraft|aircraft]] for landing the contents of larger ships within the atmosphere.

On densely populated worlds, such as [[Albanus]], the [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] has as many as three hundred space stations in stellar and various planetary orbits. In cases like this, most stations are civilian-built, civilian-funded, and civilian-operated. The military operates dedicated defense space stations at larger planets.

On sparsely-populated worlds, such as [[Sindh]], the [[:Category:Star Systems|star system]] only has a handful of space stations in stellar and planetary orbits. In cases like this, stations can be funded and operated by civilians, but most are at least half military. Even civilian stations must dedicate hangar space for military [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], and bunk space for [[Prefecture Marine Corps|Marines]].

==Merchant Fleet==

The Prefecture's [[Prefecture Merchant Fleet| merchant fleet]] is largely private, though many freighters and cargo haulers are used by the Prefecture's military. The merchant fleet hauls trillions of tons of freight every week.

Most merchant fleet vessels operate on tight schedules, pausing only long enough to load and unload cargo and to resupply. The carry enough crew to operate around the clock on the [[Prefecture]]'s standard 12 hour shift.

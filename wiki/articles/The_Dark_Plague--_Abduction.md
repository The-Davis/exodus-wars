Battle of Icronia: Lauds - 4/2/2325 - By James Davis and Ryan Wilhelm

Sanctuary of the Consul, Ueda - 0200 Hours - 1300 Hours Ueda Time
     Consul Chris Dayton bursts out of his bedroom, startling the guards posted at the door. "Have my car ready, I'm needed at the Senate."
     The ride is very short. Dayton steps out of his hover limo, and runs up the steps towards the Senate building. He speaks into a communicator as he goes. "Have a security team at the shuttle pads. Colin Archibald is on his way with important information." Guards run up to surround him in a protective wall of bodies.
     Standing on guard by Dayton’s office are Privates Eric Thomason and Sergeant Ivan Heike. Like many of the security guards, their opaque faceplates are pulled down, giving them numerous data readouts.
     "Something big is up." says Thomason. "So, think Betty'll go out if I ask her?" Thomason asks Heike.
     "Something tells me Betty ain't the type of gal who'd stand you up." Heike replies. Both men nod in a knowing way.
     "Fireworks tonight." Thomason smirks, then taps a few keys on his communicator.

Ueda Senate Landing Pad - 0205 Hours - 1305 Hours Ueda Time
     "Welcome to Ueda, Mr. Archibald." One of the guards salutes Colin Archibald as he steps out of his shuttle. "The Senate will be assembled shortly. The Consul is already waiting. Sorry about the heat, but you know the law. We're only allowed to write new laws in the blazing sun."
     "Right, let's go." Archibald nods curtly.
     In another part of the building, Private Jorge Santos works hurriedly on a small box. He attaches a small antenna, then quietly slips away. Seconds later, he punches a button on his communicator, and detonates the small but powerful charge of explosives. Santos smiles grimly, then yanks an alarm handle, setting off sirens and alerts throughout the Senate area. Another Omega Squadron mission accomplished.

Senate Assembly - 0206 Hours - 1306 Hours Ueda Time
     As soon as they hear the alarms going off, Heike and Thomason dash into the assembly area, rifles held at the ready. "Consul, sir! There's been an explosion in the building! Security thinks it's a terrorist attack, so protocol demands that we get you to a secure location. Your VTOL is waiting on the roof, let's move!" Heike bellows.
     “Right! Let’s go!” Dayton nods. He rushes out the door with Heike. The bodyguards who'd accompanied him earlier rise to follow, but are silently murdered by Thomason and his silenced pistol. Dayton is already too far away to hear the muffled gurgles of his loyal, dying bodyguards.
     Thomason reloads his weapon as he rushes out the door. “Betty's on her date. Repeat, Betty is on her date." he tells his communicator.
     The two traitorous soldiers rush Dayton up a flight of stairs, Heike leading and Thomason following. Upon reaching the roof, Heike speaks into his communicator. "Casanova to Getaway. Casanova to Getaway. Betty and Don Juan are on the roof, and Fireworks is inbound. Request Evac."
     As he speaks, a Corsair shuttle lands on the roof. Dayton frowns. "That's not proper Ueda communication code, soldier. What's going on here?"
     Heike answers with a hard punch to Dayton's jaw. The Consul falls to the ground, stunned. "So you noticed,” Heike grunts, pulling some handcuffs from his uniform. Before he can secure the Consul, Dayton regains his feet, pulling a concealed pistol from his jacket. He squeezes off two rounds, instantly killing Heike. Before he can turn around, Thomason knocks the gun from his hand, and wrestles him to the ground.
     Jorge Santos, the demolitions man, soon joins them. Together they secure the Consul, and drag him aboard the shuttle. They drag Heike's body aboard, eliminating the only evidence that could directly link the Conclave to the abduction.
     As the shuttle takes off, Thomason shoves a prisoner out onto the roof. This man, an Archadian in full battle uniform, plummets a hundred feet to his death on the roof below. "Let's see what they make of that." he grins. "Let's move. They won't fire on us until they get direct orders. If I know the Prefecture military, they won't hesitate to kill their own leader to spite us, but the politicians are in control here."
     Moments later, the Corsair and the Omega Squadron team is safe aboard the Alpha of Omega, their prisoner secure. The Conclave warship jumps away at top speed.

Delphinus - 0530 Hours
     Hadrian nods. "There's something else you need to know, something you need to keep quiet. Are you alone?"
     "Yes sir, I'm taking this in my briefing room. What is it?"
     "The Consul has been kidnapped. Initial investigations make it look like Archadians were involved. You know they're closely tied to the Mesarthrim with that First Joint Defense Fleet project." Hadrian sighs. "The news of the Consul's kidnapping will be released very soon, but we're keeping the possibility of Archadian involvement under the wraps. I'm not convinced it's them, but if the public decides it was, it will mean another war. We can't fight a war with the Ixoarchada."

'''Prekohr''' was the hereditary title of the royal leader of the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]], and later of [[Rikaz o Fii Cai iv Huerna]]. Literally translated, it means "all-lord", but the more accurate interpretation is "emperor."

== Powers and Responsibilities ==

=== Executive Duties ===

The Prekohr has absolute executive authority over the Rikaz. Some will assemble a council and listen to their input in the day-to-day governing of the Rikaz. [[Dirken Huern]] attempted to develop an official legislative body, but did not meet any success, as everyone he appointed defered to his decisions.  [[Siovar Cai]] returned to the tradition of central authority, but wisely listened to experienced counsel.

=== Jucicial Duties ===

Under the [[Law of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Eight-Fold Cornerstone]] of the [[Lorithos]], the Prekohr must decide the fate of any criminal defendant whose guilt or innocence cannot be conclusively proven. There is no appealing the decision of the Prekohr.

=== Military Duties ===

The Prekohr is the leader of all of the [[Lorithos]] in all things, including battle, but the actual strategy and command is usually the responsibility of the [[Hishai]], who acts as the Prekohr's champion in battle.

== List of Prekohrs ==

* [[Hamur Huern]] - The Mircan Prekohr - Served from [[987BC]] to [[960BC]].
* [[Talire Huern]] - The Ashen Prekohr - Served from [[2142]] to [[2240]].
* [[Kian Huern]] - The Soresh Prekohr - Claimed the throne during the [[Burning Wars]].
* [[Dirken Huern]] - The Faishun Prekohr - Served from [[2285]] to [[2350]].
* [[Siovar Cai]] - The Kaihren Prekohr - Served from [[2350]] to present.

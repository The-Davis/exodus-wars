''Goff Riiktar'' was a distant nephew of [[Talire Huern]] and was her [[Hishai]], or champion. Riiktar betrayed the Huern family and tried to become [[Prekohr]] himself, and dealt one of the most devastating blows to the world of [[Lorithan]] when he poisoned the oceans.

Riiktar died when he was captured and personally butchered by [[Torm Krelbine]].

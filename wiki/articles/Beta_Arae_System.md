The '''Beta Arae System''' consists of the star Beta Arae, the seven planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Beta Arae, the planets are Arae I, Arae II, [[Laojiu]], [[Miyada]], Arae V, Arae VI, and Arae VII.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Beta Arae is the Beta Arae System's only star. Beta Arae is a class G3Y yellow dwarf. The Beta Arae System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Beta Arae is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Laojiu ===

[[Laojiu]] (0.98AU) is a major inhabitted planet in the Beta Arae System. It has one moon, Laozen.

=== Miyada ===

[[Miyada]] (1.1AU) is a major inhabitted planet in the Beta Arae System.

== History ==

The Beta Arae System was colonized in [[2207]], with a [[China (Terra)|Chinese]] population from [[Earth]], which settled on [[Laojiu]]. It was independently run until the [[Onyx Empire]] conquered it

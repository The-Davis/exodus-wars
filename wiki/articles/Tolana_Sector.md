The '''Tolana Sector''' is located in the extreme reaches counter-turn from the [[Galactic Core]] in the [[Orion Arm]]. In ancient times, it was a sparsely-inhabited frontier for the [[Anthronoris]], which led to the survival of the [[Ixoarchada]].

This remoteness also led to their extreme isolation from other human civilizations, but not from all intelligent life. For centuries, the [[Anderung]] and [[Ixoarchada]] clashed in titanic battles to control the riches of the sector. In the end, many star systems were made uninhabitable, but the [[Ixoarchada]] emerged as the dominant and sole inhabitants of the sector.

=== Inhabited Star Systems ===

The [[Ixoarchada]] of the Tolana Sector are all united under the banner of the [[Taviridis Somarchada]]. They divided the sector into three "[[Polnavi]]" (frontiers), but currently only inhabit the "[[Pithari]]" (core) [[:Category:Star Systems|systems]].

The [[Ularen System]] is the central home of all [[Ixoarchada]] and the seat of their government. It is on the outermost ridges of the [[Tolana Nebula]]. The [[Bollaria System]], [[Bregdan System]], [[Cyprana System]], and [[Giraxis System]] are their other currently-inhabited systems.

The [[Bataei System]], [[Chorvat System]], and [[Plasala System]] were all populated by the Ixoarchada in the past, but all inhabitable planets were lost during the long wars between the Ixoarchada and the [[Anderung]].  The remains of the Anderung's holdings in the Tolana Sector are all abandoned.

=== Major Sector Features ===

The [[Tolana Nebula]]--known as the Owl Nebula to the Terrans--is central to the Sector. In [[Ixoarchada]] mythology, the gods fled the destruction of [[Glengarr]] and took shelter within the nebula. The nebula became an important icon in [[Ixoarchada]] culture, and later became a central component of the [[Ixoarchada]] technological base and interstellar economy. It is a rich source of antimatter, and vital to the continued prosperity of the [[Iridi]].

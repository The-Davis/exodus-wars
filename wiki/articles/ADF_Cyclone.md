The ADF Cyclone is one of the few examples of a United Earth Alliance spacecraft with a purely offensive weapons platform.  Unlike the [[ADF Starfire|Starfire]] and [[ADF Firestorm|Firestorm]], which filled interception and superiority roles, the Cyclone was built for strategic and tactical precision bombing.

It was only the Imperial attacks that started the [[Second Exodus War]] that caused the Cyclone to be anything more than a reserve design.  In [[2315]], only two squadrons of Cyclones were in operation, and all of them were in the [[Titan System]].  By [[2322]], sixteen squadrons were in operation and played important roles in the counterstrikes against the [[Onyx Empire]].

= Development History =

While the Cyclone X1 prototype airframe was test-flown in [[2299]], it was five years before the project received the green light for any active-duty production.  If not for the fact that Astrolift maintained a factory in the Titan System, the Cyclone may have been one of the earliest casualty in the Second Exodus War.

= Combat History =

The Cyclone's large profile and unwieldy airframe made it somewhat dangerous to operate in atmosphere against more agile designs, but in vacuum operations it was a superb attack platform, outperforming most of its peer designs.

== Second Exodus War ==

Some of the most dangerous missions of the Second Exodus War were flown by ADF Cyclones.  The Onyx Empire never fully lost control of the air over [[Earth]], so any bombing mission was almost certain to face fierce defenses in the form of aggressive interceptor squadrons and Surface-to-Air Missiles.  Cyclone pilots went into battle with a sort of cheerful cynicism, knowing that their aircraft would take a beating but would rarely get them killed.  While one in five Cyclones operating in the [[Sol System]] were shot down, only one in thirty pilots were killed or seriously injured, even when including pilots captured and murdered by Imperial ground forces.

Despite their losses, the Cyclones accounted for over 35,000 confirmed tank and Rig kills, over 75,000 probable tank and Rig kills, 146,000 confirmed infantry kills, and 215,000 probable infantry kills.  They also accounted for over 250 billion [[Peculium]]s of damage to Imperial infrastructure.

= Colonial Commonwealth Era =

After the formation of the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], all operable Alliance-owned Cyclones were turned over to Commonwealth military forces.  Most were mothballed and put in reserve, in keeping with the Commonwealth's defense-oriented military stance.  One active squadron is stationed on each Commonwealth world, with the reserve squadrons and replacement parts distributed evenly across all Commonwealth territory.

The Astrolift corporation has maintained its Cyclone molds and presses, and has advised the Commonwealth that it can resume full-time production with only eight months' advance notice.

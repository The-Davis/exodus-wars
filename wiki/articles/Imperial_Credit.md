The '''Imperial Credit''' was the standard electronic-transaction based unit of currency issued and backed by the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] during the reign of [[Lucius Black]].

The Credit was first issued as currency in [[2301]], when the first branch of the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] opened on [[Alpha Centauri System|Alpha Centauri]]. Every person living under the [[Empire]]'s rule was issued the [[Imperial Identification Implant]], which tracked their vital statistics, contact information, and current balance of credits in their account. The [[Royal Imperial Bank]] exchanged all [[United Earth Alliance]] [[Dollars]] at a 1 to 1 rate of exchange for the first week, and at a 2 to 1 rate of exchange afterwards.

[[Empire|Imperial]] decree illegalized the use of foreign currencies on [[Alpha Centauri]] after [[2301]]. The Imperial Credit was the standard currency of the [[Empire]] until it collapsed in [[2324]]. As part of their revenge against the [[Alliance of Planets]] coalition, the [[Royal Imperial Bank]] erased all of their computer databases, then vaporized the computers themselves, effectively bankrupting anyone who's wealth was in Imperial Credits. This senseless act of economic sabotage was part of the reasoning behind the [[Prefecture]]'s use of the [[gold standard]].

During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] sided with the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim Clans]] against the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] aggressors.  Although the actual military assistance to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim Clans]] was limited, the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] sponsored massive releif missions, supplying the soldiers with weapons, food, and medical supplies.  [[Auellal League|Auellal]] captains began raiding these missions, and capturing the material for the [[Auellal League|League]].

[[Chris Dayton]] declared a quasi war against the [[Auellal League|League]], and several punitive strikes were made against [[Caledon]] and [[Auellal League|League]] soldiers on the ground.  The [[Auellal League|League]] responded by increasing their assaults against [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] cargo ships.  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] lost so much tonnage that they discontinued their assistance to the failing [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim Clans]].  By [[2325]], the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Mesarthrim Clans]] had collapsed and the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] had replaced them.  The [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] forced the [[Auellal League|League]] from [[Mesar]] and became members of the [[Alliance of Planets]].

Late in [[2325]], the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] asked that a Coalition Space Police force be assembled to put down the [[Auellal League|Auellal Insurgents and Pirates]].  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] was selected as the primary nation to lead the force since they were still in a state of war with the [[Auellal League|Auellal]].  [[Anthony Hadrian]] protested, claiming the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] were responsible to solve their own insurgent problems - however, in early [[2326]], the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] was forced by a vote of 4 to 2 in favour of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] leading the [[Space Police Expedition to Cinaed|assault force]].  The [[Space Police Expedition to Cinaed| force]] dispatched was woefully under strength, and were forced to retreat from the system, sustaining heavy causalities.  Upon their return, the [[Alliance of Planets#Alliance of Planets High Council|Alliance High Council]] called for a build up of a larger force to assault and occupy the [[Cinaed System]].  The force would be dispatched in early [[2327]], and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] would once again lead the assault.  However, on February 1, [[2327]] the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] destroyed  [[Leskovik]], and the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] removed their forces from the coalition fleet.

As [[2327]] turned into the year of hell for the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]], the [[Auellal League]] decided the time was ripe for a declaration of war against the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]].  War was declared on October 31, [[2327]]- the next day, [[Caledon]] was surrounded and bombarded by the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]].  After eighteen hours of brutal bombardments, the [[Auellal Armada]] forced the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] from [[Caledon]].  By early [[2328]], the [[Auellal Armada]] forced the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] [[United Centusi States Warfleet|fleet]] from the [[Cinaed System]].

On March 15th, [[2328]] the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] envoy reached [[Caledon]] and the Treaty of Cinaed was signed, ending the war between the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] and the [[Auellal League|League]].

The Treaty also served as the foundation of the [[Cinaed Accords]], which stated that both the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] and the [[Auellal League|League]] would begin to assist each other in war material production - including fighters, tanks, and warships.  The [[Auellal League|League]] sent fifteen troupes of [[Auellal_Armada#The_Dannsair|Dancers]] with the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] so that they could begin immediate service with the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] forces.

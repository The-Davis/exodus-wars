The [[Terran]] conflict known as the '''Korean War''' began as a civil war fought from [[1950]]–[[1951]] on the Korean Peninsula, which had been divided by the post-[[World War II]] [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[United States (Terra)|American]] occupation zones. The civil war began on June 25, [[1950]], when the People's Republic of Korea in the north attacked the [[Korea (Terra)|Republic of Korea]] in the south. The civil war was greatly expanded when the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)| People's Republic of China]] and [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] sent troops to covertly aid the People's Republic of Korea. Later the [[United Nations]] entered the conflict. The conflict ended when the [[Soviet Union (Terra)| Soviets]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] were discovered, and hastily withdrew support from the People's Republic of Korea. The People's Republic of Korea was defeated on July 10, [[1951]].

The '''Xi Herculis System''' consists of the star Xi Herculis, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from Xi Herculis, the planets are [[Ward]], [[Bolan]], and [[Crofton]].

==Stellar Navigation==

Xi Herculis is the system's only star. Xi Herculis is a class G6TJ yellow sub-giant. The Xi Herculis System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Xi Herculis is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Bolan ===

[[Bolan]] (3.2AU) is the second inhabitable terrestrial planet to be colonized in the Xi Herculis System. It has no moons.

=== Crofton ===

[[Crofton]] (4.2AU) is the second inhabitable terrestrial planet to be colonized in the Xi Herculis System. It has two moons, XX.

=== Ward ===

[[Ward]] (5.5AU) is the first inhabitable terrestrial planet to be colonized in the Xi Herculis System. It has no moons, so the colonists used massive turbines to simulate tides in the oceans.

== History ==

The Xi Herculis System was discovered by the [[Terrans]] in [[2160]].

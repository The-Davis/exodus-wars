===Overview===

With its frigate size and substantial carrying capacity, the Provider hits a happy medium between the small Beluga and the overly massive Eagle.

[[Image:ProviderClassHauler1.jpg]]

It is able to carry up to a full battalion of troops into combat.  The Provider has found its spot in combat markets.  Deploying and supplying small planetary garrison units is what it excels at, due to its carrying capacity and relatively low price tag.

===Capabilities===

The Provider is built supplement invasion forces that the Eagle carries.  Provider bays are modular, so it can carry most any cargo.  It can carry all tanks, divide its compartments for a variety of troops, or even carry cryogenic chambers.

Providers also sport a new automatic loading and unloading system, developed specifically for the modular cargo system.  The extremely complicated system the Eagle used was dropped.  Now the system is so simple that a child can operate it.

[[Image:ProviderClassHauler2.jpg]]

The armament of the Provider consists chiefly of Neutron Cannons and variable-ordinance tactical missile tubes.  It carries a single dual-barrel naval autocannon at the front of the ship.  It isn't designed to engage warships, but can help in emergency escapes.

The Provider's engines are new models, built following an unfortunate accident over Ueda.  The prototype Provider exploded, killing the four technicians on the bridge.  Six other men aboard survived, thanks to the safety features in the cargo bay.  After a week of investigation, Rahn Industries found that faulty energy relays were responsible for the power overload and resulting explosion.  The accident was the worst in Rahn Industries history.

The engine redesign delayed the project for two months, but the resulting new engines could handle much more stress from the antigravity systems.  No longer would dropships need oceans to land in.  Providers can make soft landings on solid terrain.

===Deployment===

Providers are mostly used in test runs.  The Prefecture has not been entirely convinced that the model is safe, but most dropship crews like it.  Currently, six are deployed with the Fifth Fleet.  They are used to carry ammunition and supplies from the fleet to ground forces.

[[Image:ProviderClassHauler3.jpg]]

More are expected to be built and shipped in early 2325.

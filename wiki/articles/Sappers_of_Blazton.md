Blazton Industries maintains the largest corporate army in the Terran sector--and for good reason.  Long outside the sway of the governments of Earth, threatened by the greedy reach of the Empire, and based in one of the least stable Terran-colonized systems, Blazton has long been responsible for its own security.  Perhaps the most basic element of the Blazton Security Force are the Sappers--mercenaries and professional soldiers-for-hire recruited from all over the Terran Sector. 

Sappers are true professional soldiers in every sense.  As heavy infantry, they are equipped with a Blazton Industries PA-24 Composite Armor suit.  Unlike the less refined Red Shirts of the Berlusconi family, the Sappers take full advantage of the payload capacity of the suit, enabling them to carry a wide array of munitions, explosives, and other equipment.  The suit's built in communications and targeting gear is tied directly into the Sapper's helmet, giving them a very detailed HUD with which to operate. 

Blazton's Sappers carry the Blazton Model 91 Main Battle Rifle.  The M-91 MBR has a variable round system similar to most Terran-made rifles.  However, the newer Blazton-made model allows higher-velocity breechers to be fired, at the cost of energy efficiency.  Saidia Optics, which is actually 30% owned by Blazton Industries, supplies OP-11 scopes for the M-91.  The OP-11 model was originally created for the high-tech T-77E rifles of the Mesarthrim Myt'Herra. 

[[Image:SappersofBlazton.jpg]]

Sappers also carry a Blazton Model 08 Pistol in holster on their side.  This pistol, with a sixteen round capacity, is lethal at close range.  Each magazine has enough energy to fire a pair of breechers as well as another fourteen standard rounds. 

Sappers are usually deployed on most Blazton Industries starships, installations, and to guard production facilities on planets throughout the Terran sector--or at least where allowed by local laws.  The majority of the Sappers are deployed at bases and stations in the Sirius cluster.  They are recruited from among the best trained, if not best behaved, mercenaries and veterans, and are comparable in training and operational capabilities to most heavy infantry in the Terran Sector.

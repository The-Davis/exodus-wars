== Events of 2308 ==

===January===

*January 3 - The [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[:Category:Carriers|carrier]] designated [[HMS Judicator|CV-13]] receives its official commissioning orders from [[Lucius Black]], making it an official part of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. Captain [[Julia Michaels]] is given command and the ship is christened the [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']].

===February===

*February 24 - The [[Lorithos]] complete the first working [[Urokon]].

===March===

*March 15 - The central [[Urokon]] network hub is installed on [[Lorithan]].
*March 17 - An [[Urokon]] system is installed on [[Kronheim]].

===April===

*April 19 - An [[Urokon]] system is installed on [[Sepulare]]
*April 22 - An [[Urokon]] system is installed on [[Earth]].

===May===

*May 3 - An [[Urokon]] system is installed on [[Theia]]
*May 6 - An [[Urokon]] system is installed in the [[Sirius Cluster]]

===June===

*June 14 - [[Lorithos]] techs come to install an [[Urokon]] system on [[Ah Kin]]. [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] [[William Duke]] bans the [[Urokon]] from the [[Alpha Centauri System]], citing health concerns.
*June 21 - Representatives from [[Rikaz o fii Huern iv Lorithan]] appeal [[William Duke]]'s ban on the [[Urokon]] with the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly]] on [[Earth]].
*June 25 - The [[Urokon]] hearings in the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] end. The [[United Earth Alliance]] agrees with [[William Duke]], and cancels all pending installations, and shuts down existing [[Urokon]] systems in their territory pending further studies.

===July===

===August===
*August 11 - Beginning of the [[Siege of Stone Bridge]]: [[Mesarthrim]] attack [[Anderung]] slave masters.
*August 31 - End of the [[Siege of Stone Bridge]]: [[Steven Cue]]'s force break the siege.

===September===
*September 1 - [[Benjamin Parman]] destroys the last [[Anderung]] stronghold on [[Mesar]] in the [[Stone Bridge]] city proper.
*September 9 - [[Yaron Gorash]], the last [[Anderung]] warlord, is executed by [[Andreas Tischler]], marking the true end of the [[Yurtosh hora ven Serthra]].

===October===

===November===

===December===
*December 28 - [[Steven Cue]] poisons [[Provost]] [[Tyler Perrier]] and the rest of his rival leaders of the [[Auellal Political Party]] during a Christmas dinner.

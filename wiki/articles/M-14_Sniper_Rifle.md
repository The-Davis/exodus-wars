While not useful against enemies like the Volucris, the Snipers in the Marine Corps are still kept well-trained and well-equipped.  Their high-powered rifles can split open armor at a distance of up to five kilometers, making them very dangerous to human or Ngrligru targets.  Snipers are placed within Special Operations Teams in the Corps.

[[Image:M14SniperRifle.jpg]]

Because of their mission parameters, Snipers must use unpowered armor and passive optical sensors to avoid detection.  Their M-82 rifles are equipped with computer-stabilized optical and infrared scopes, allowing for up to 30x magnification.

Snipers must be very careful about choosing their targets.  The lack of power assistance means that if they are discovered, escape is impossible.

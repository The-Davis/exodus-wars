===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Damu'kizuka|History]]

The cunning '''Kabila Damu'kizuka''' is always cloaked in shadow. They fight their wars with attrition, not muscle. Raids, sabotage, spies, and torture are their weapons. They are the bitter enemy [[Kabila Nyoka]], and the friend of [[Kabila Jitu'nge]].

While [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] was gnawing at the heel of [[Kabila Nyoka]], Damu'kizuka was far down the ladder of power, pretending to be content with its lot. It whispered favor in the ear of [[Kabila Nyoka]], and fed them false information about the numbers and strength of the [[Kabila Jitu'nge|Jitu'nge]] warriors.

Word of the attempted allegiance between the [[Meroniri]] and the Kabilas [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] and [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] came to the ear of the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]] by way of Damu'kizuka. The treacherous Kabila gathered its information, and dropped it in a neat package at the feet of the council. They paved the way for their ally, [[Kabila Jitu'nge]], to make great gains in absorbing the two smaller Kabilas.

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of Kabila Damu'kizuka|Foreign Relations]]

In the year 2207, [[Kabila Nyoka]] sent the Thelathini na nane to seize worlds in the [[Orion Arm]] sectors for food and resources. Kabila Damu'kizuka saw a chance, and slipped agents aboard the ''Bahari Shela'' and other vessels in the Mia'mbili.

They sabotaged everything but the ''Bahari Shela'' itself, and were preparing to capture it when the whole Mia'mbili came under unexpected attack by the [[Empire]]. Because of the surprise and damage, it was captured.

[[Kabila Jitu'nge]] decided to take the reins of power from [[Kabila Nyoka]] while it was reeling from the loss. Damu'kizuka finally stepped out of the shadows, and openly fought against [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]]. The two Kabilas eventually toppled the weakened Kabila, but did not completely remove them from the picture. [[Kabila Nyoka]] was soon back in power as the third most powerful Kabila, instead of the first.

The '''Magrinoch Trials''' were a series of war crimes hearings.  They were organized and enforced by the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], as part of the terms of the [[Treaty of Tavou]].  Events from the [[Tempest War]] and [[Second Exodus War]] were both subject to scrutiny and sentencing under the terms of the treaty.

== Defendants ==

One of the key defendants in the trial was [[Anthony Hadrian]] of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], mostly for his actions in the [[Second Exodus War]].  He was originally granted immunity under the [[Treaty of Memphis]], but the Prefecture Senate voted to revoke his immunity in order to keep the peace.  Because this action was under interstellar law and crimes against humanity, Hadrian was not protected by the Prefecture constitution.

[[William Camp]] was named as a defendant, but he had disappeared prior to the end of the war.  After significant pressure was placed on the newly formed [[Mesarthrim Federation]] by the [[Ixoarchada]], [[Arnold Stadt]] grudgingly produced the deceased defendant's head as proof that the [[Mesarthrim]] had already dealt with Camp.  The rest of Camp's remains were suspected to be somewhere within [[Tischler Clan]] territory.

== Scandal ==

When the orders to produce war records from the [[Onyx Empire]] were delivered to the Federated Districts of the Prefecture, [[Talaris Roi]] delayed compliance for three months.  During that time, 650 combat logs were deleted from the Prefecture's archives, and their entire backup system was corrupted.  An internal investigation pointed directly to the office of the [[Consul]], and Talaris Roi was impeached as a result.  Shortly afterwards, Anthony Hadrian disappeared, and somehow managed to evade capture.  Sixteen Prefecture military officers were court martialed as a result.

The hearing officers dealt with other former Imperial officers quite harshly, and many felt that they were unfairly punishing the defendants for Anthony Hadrian's actions.

== Sentences ==

Fifty imprisonment sentences were handed down by the courts, including four lifetime solitary confinement sentences.  Two death sentences were also issued, though they were later commuted to life in prison.  Only the [[Ikronin]] had no parties charged with or found guilty of any war crimes, so [[Kronheim]] was chosen as the site to house the prisoners.  The first sentence was set to end in [[2347]].

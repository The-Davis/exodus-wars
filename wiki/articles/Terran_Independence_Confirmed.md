May 24, 2324

By James Davis

The Terran recovery efforts have yielded one key goal today. Terra has been declared an independent, sovereign nation by the Alliance of Planets Member States. The new Terran Embassy has been granted Honorary Member status in the Alliance of Planets.

Elections will be held in 2326. For now, Recovery Project director Josiah MacArthur Hlow--Professor Hlow to his associates--has executive power. The Member States unanimously agreed that the mild pacifistic Professor is not likely to begrudge the reins of power to another.

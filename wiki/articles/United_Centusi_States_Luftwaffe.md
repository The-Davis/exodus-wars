The United Centusi States Luftwaffe is one of the best air forces in known space. It operates a number of fighters, bombers, and support vehicles which it uses to support both the Heer and the Kriegsmarine.

The pilots of the Luftwaffe begin training at just 15 years of age, and many of them are now the veterans of several major wars. Overall, they are among the best of human pilots, surpassed by their [[Auellal League|Aeullal]] cousins--the Dancers--and the pilots of the [[Ixoarchada]] [[DreináDeviri]]. [[Prefecture]] pilots are considered the only statistical equal to the Luftwaffe.

The Luftwaffe is divided into Fleet Squadrons and Base Squadrons. Strategies focus on squadron-based action more than larger force operations, or single-flight operations.

The most common fighter in use is the Dragonfly, a highly effective light interceptor, followed closely by the Patton medium fighter. Both are deployed in almost every theater of Luftwaffe operations.

In attack operations, the Luftwaffe deploys the Sanburn and Anox heavy fighters. Both are extremely capable weapons platforms, but only a drop in the bucket compared to the Luftwaffe's heavy weapons platforms. The Richthofen is an enormous assault bomber, capable of dropping tons of bombs onto any target, while the Schleiffen is a torpedo bomber used in anti-warship tactics.

The Luftwaffe has several special designs at their disposal. The Sherman is an electronics warfare platform, with the best available jamming and sensor packages. The Clipper is a gunboat, almost as large as a Kriegsmarine gunship. The Pegasus is now famous across the galaxy as a capable dropship and troop transport. The Luftwaffe also uses the Courier class shuttle, and is currently experimenting with the Kestral class of next generation fighter.

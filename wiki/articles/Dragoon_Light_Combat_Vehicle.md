*Size Designation: Light Combat Vehicle
*Powerplant: Mesarthrim SV-5a Muon-Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 150 KPH
*Armor: Leifstein Composite Armor 
*Shields: Automated Adaptive Shielding 
*Armament:
**1 x Type-3a Railgun (1,000 rounds)
**2 x Type-B1 Blazers
*Communications System:  Standard Ranged Comm. Suite 
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Standard Sensors Suite 

===Overview===

The Dragoon is one of the oldest ground units in the UCS Army.  The ancestor of the modern Dragoon was a light attack vehicle developed for use against the Anderung.  Since then, the small craft has modernized and become more sophisticated, but it still retains its reputation as a lethal, albeit small, attack vehicle.

[[Image:DragoonLightCombatVehicle1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Dragoon is armed to the teeth for such a small vehicle.  Its primary weapon is a Type-3 Railgun, the smallest mounted on vehicles.  This cannon is strong enough to punch through even medium armor, and is lethal against light units and infantry.  The shells it fires are larger than Terran type mass-drivers, but they are equally as powerful, and can be customized for various warhead types.  

Backing up the Railgun are two Type-B1 Blazers in a turret, which is controlled by the gunner (the driver controls the Railgun).  The Type-B1 Blazer is the smallest type mounted on vehicles.  Together, the two cannons are capable of taking down infantry, as well as light armored vehicles.  At close range, they are also effective against heavier armor. 

The Dragoon is outfitted with an SV-5a reactor, which is just enough to power its weapons, drivetrain, and a small shield projector.  Between its shields and its Leifstein Composite Armor, it is well protected for such a small vehicle. 

[[Image:DragoonLightCombatVehicle2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Dragoons are deployed with most UCS Army units, and operate as fast-attack platforms.  Their small size and relative simplicity compared to the newer Dolch type LCVs makes them well suited for fast-attack and quick-support of infantry and armor. While Dragoons are still effective on the battlefield, they are slowly being phased out in favor of Dolch LCVs.

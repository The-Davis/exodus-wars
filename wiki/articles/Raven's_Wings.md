The "Raven's Wings" was the unofficial nickname for the "Royal Imperial Laboratories Experimental Manned Aircraft/Spacecraft Test Pilot Program."

During the [[F-4 Raven]] fighter test program, [[Emil Torsc]] joked that the test pilots were so important to the program that they "might as well be the wings." The nickname instantly stuck.

* [[Ashley Spencer]]

== Test History ==

The first test flight by the Raven's Wings (who had not earned their nickname at the time) was the XF-1 prototype, an experimental atmosphere/vacuum airframe meant to be capable of escape velocity, re-entry and combat maneuvers in both environments. [[Emil Torsc]] undertook the first test flight in [[2300]], and promptly declared it "an underpowered slug."

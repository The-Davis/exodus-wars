== Events of 84BC ==

Construction of the starship ''[[Segara]]'' is completed in orbit of [[Glengarr]].  Its maiden flight is a round trip from [[Glengarr]] to [[Dellada]] to [[Archada]] and the back to its orbital dock above [[Glengarr]].

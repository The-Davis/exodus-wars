=== History ===
Main Article: [[History of the Remnant|History]]

The Remnant is a direct descendant of [[Anthronoris Empire|Pax Anthronis]], the great and ancient unified human government from thousands of years ago. Unlike most human civilizations, the Remnant is not located in the [[Orion Arm]] of the galaxy. Its hidden worlds are in the heart of the [[Sagittarius Arm]] in [[Ngrligru]] space, a dagger at the heart of their ancient foe.

The Remnant have no natural homeworld, as such a concept was lost long ago during the times of the Empire.  These days, they live in three hidden Burning Worlds orbiting the primary star in the [[Lateo System]]. They have lost little of their power over the long millenia. Their warships cannot be matched by any other civilization, and their weapons can snuff out the stars themselves. Their lives are long and glorious, lasting thousands of years before they succumb to age. Only their numbers: fewer than a million people and less than a hundred ships, keeps them from being a threat to the [[Ngrligru]].

To the lesser races, the Remnant are almost entirely an unknown faction. To the [[Ngrligru]], they are a lurking menace to shipping, little more. To the [[Meroniri]], they are stuffy older relatives with much better toys. The Remnant's plans for the future are grand and mysterious.

=== Government ===
Main article: [[Government of the Remnant|Government]]

The Remnant is governed by a long line of monarchs assuming the title of '''Emperor''' or '''Empress'''.  This line has remained unbroken from the times of the war with the [[Geckans]] as the last '''Emperor''' of the Anthronoris Empire was aboard the ships of the '''Extorrisdiutinus''' as they escaped the [[Sundering]].  In the almost 200 centuries since that time, there have been a total of five '''Emperors'''.

=== Law ===
Main Article: [[Law of the Remnant|Law]]

The laws of the Remnant are essentially those of the [[Anthronoris Empire|Pax Anthronis]] before it, with a few changes.  Capital punishment has been severely restricted due to the current status of the Anthronoris people.  Individually tailored punishments are still practiced, although they are called upon less as of late. 

=== Law Enforcement ===
Main Article: [[Law Enforcement of the Remnant|Law Enforcement]]

Law enforcement within the Remnant is somewhat unique.  Because nearly every citizen understands their unique place in the galaxy as one of the few remaining Anthronis, they generally obey the law while maintaining their fierce independence.  But, as with all societies, there are always a few malcontents.  When convicted, their punishment is individually tailored for both the individual and the crime.  This is possible due to the fact that long-lived Anthronis can devote centuries to the study of why people break laws and how it is most effective to bring about a change in attitude.  Captial punishment is still a possibility, but only in the case of traitors.

=== Foreign Relations ===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of the Remnant|Foreign Relations]]

The Remnant does not maintain any foreign relations with any nation in the [[Orion Arm]], nor with any nation in any sector of the galaxy for that matter.  While forbidden, it is rumored that there are some individuals who are attempting or have already made contact with the various other nations in the Orion Arm privately.  If such rumors were true, and such individuals caught, their punishment would be severe.  

=== Military ===

Main Article: [[Military of the Remnant|Military]]

The Remnant's armed forces are divided into three branches:
*[[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]]
*[[Remnant Fighter Corps| Reliquiae Acalanthis Agema]]
*[[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]]

The Remnant military is the most advanced in the known galaxy and relies primarily upon its star fleet, the Reliquiae Astrum Classis, for both offense and defense action.  The other two branches are somewhat subservient to the Fleet, as they are unable to conduct offensive operations without Fleet support.  However, they do maintain extensive resources on each of the Burning Worlds for defense.

=== Intelligence ===
Main Article: [[Intelligence of the Remnant|Intelligence]]

While the Remnant does not maintain any foreign relations, it does maintain the largest surveillance network in the galaxy.  Every inhabited planet in the [[Orion Arm]], as well as the majority of those in the [[Sagittarius Arm]] are monitored by immense deep-space networks.  Because the very survival of the Remnant depends upon its ability to detect and defuse any threat as far away from home as possible, the [[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]] spends much of its time responding to and monitoring to alerts generated by this network.

=== Transportation ===
Main Article: [[Transportation of the Remnant|Transportation]]

Transportation within the Remnant fall into several categories; within each world, intra-world travel, and inter-system travel.  Firstly, there are no restrictions upon travel within each of the three [[Burning World]]s of the Remnant for any citizen.  In fact, it is strongly encouraged.  Restrictions do arise whenever considering travel outside of each world. 

Travel between the Burning Worlds is primarily conducted by purpose-built gargantuan freighters with military-grade stealth systems.  Although it is a very quick trip, the frequency of said trips is kept to a minimum to prevent detection.  The [[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]] makes up the balance of intra-world travel     

Lastly, only military vessels may travel to other star systems.

=== Territory ===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Remnant|Territory]]

The Remnant occupies only one system, that of Lateo, which is their local name for the system.  To the galaxy at large, the system is unnamed and ubiquitous; another average star in the [[Sagittarius Arm]], which is exactly how the Remnant would prefer their home to stay.  Lateo means "to lie hid; be concealed; to live in obscurity or safety; to be unknown" in [[Lorentin]]    

=== Economy ===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Remnant|Economy]]

The economy of the Remnant is almost entirely a closed system.  Almost all civilian goods and materials are reused and recycled.  Raw resources are collected by a partnership between the [[Remnant Navy| Reliquiae Astrum Classis]] and the Ministry of Resources.  There are, however, a small amount of items from outside the Remnant now stored at universities for study.  Where these items came from is unknown, but some suspect that there are a very few Anthronoris living outside the Remnant engaging in a type of black market for these goods.   

=== Demographics ===
Main Article: [[Demographics of the Remnant|Demographics]]

The demographics of the Remnant are very similar to those of the [[Anthronoris Empire|Pax Anthronis]] and have remained essentially the same since the [[Sundering]].  Even during the time of the Empire, the demographics were more a matter of choice and current fashion than of genetics due to the Anthronoris' mastery of genetic engineering and lack of ability to breed with other species.  

=== Public Health ===
Main Article: [[Public Health of the Remnant|Public Health]]

The citizens of the Remnant are the most healthy of all the human races in the galaxy.  This is due to the fact that each one is considered a gift and is treated with the utmost care by the most advanced medical science known anywhere.  Even during the time of the [[Anthronoris Empire|Pax Anthronis]], disease was conquered.  Every child of the Remnant receives the Vitaeternum extending their lifespans to thousands of years while a select few receive the Sin Terminus treatment, which makes them essentially immortal.  

=== Culture ===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Remnant|Culture]]

To understand the culture of the Remnant, one must understand the history that has brought them to the current day.  

The [[Anthronoris]], also known as the "Masters," originally developed slowly with spurts of spurts of violence and peace.  This all changed abruptly with the discovery of the "Sin Terminus Treatment".  This "without end" was a genetic modification which allowed the Anthronoris the prospect of a seemingly unending lifespan. Their usual lifespan of several hundred years expanded to immortality while maintaining a fairly robust reproductive cycle. This drastic change irrevocably changed the fate of the entire galaxy.

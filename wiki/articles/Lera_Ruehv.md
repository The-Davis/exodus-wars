''Lera Ruehv'' was the half sister of [[Kian Huern]]. She does not share the Huern name, as [[Derek Huern]] never claimed her as his daughter, leading to rumors of an affair in the palace.

Lera never forgave her family, and after the death of [[Talire Huern]] she sowed seeds of dissent against the Huern Dynasty. Lera was able to gather a small army, including several brilliant weapons technicians who created the first nuclear weapons.

Lera's armies never won many battles, and she was finally killed by [[Kian Huern]]'s forces in [[2256]].

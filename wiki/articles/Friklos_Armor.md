Before the invention of [[Falkran Armor]], the [[Ixoarchada]] relied on '''Friklos Composite Armor''' for protection.  Today, Friklos is still used on some equipment, support vehicles, un-powered infantry armor, and various specialized equipment.

Friklos was the first armor to be assembled completely through the use of nano-technology.  A complex maze of twisting semi-crystalline structures, Friklos Armor combines the best aspects of metals, ceramics, and composite materials.  Its complex, interlocking substructure allows even impacts with very small cross-sections to be distributed quite effectively over the entire nearby matrix.  Various micro-fibers within the armor serve to slow and sometimes halt armor-piercing weapons fragments, and dampen vibrations (thus making the armor extremely resistant to sonic weapons or to shattering upon impact).  The surface is coated with billions of tiny micro-reflectors, which are chemically inert, and can be shut off to achieve a stealth effect.  Below this layer is an ablative layer designed to stop EM pulses that overwhelm the reflective outer layer.  Beneath this is a soft, highly viscous layer designed to slow or stop projectiles before they strike the innermost layers, which are designed for maximum strength.

The fractalline micro-structures inside the armor exist in several different groups; each designed to primarily bear a certain type of force.  Stretching, compression, shearing and even twisting forces each are handled by tiny structures made to exacting standards.  The nanotechnology used to create this armor is fairly simple as such things go, and even simpler nanobots can affect repairs on battle damage after a prolonged conflict.  Although not nearly as tough as the Falkran Armor that replaced it, it has a slightly higher tolerance for heat and is an order of magnitude cheaper to manufacture.

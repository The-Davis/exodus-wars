The Verkställande Operationer Tjänsteman (Executive Operations Officer) of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], commonly known as the "'''Handelboss'''", is appointed by the executive board of the [[Godsägare Initiera]] and is answerable only to them.  While the authority of the Handleboss is mostly confined to the bureaucracy of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]], he is still considered one of the most powerful individuals within the [[Ikronin]] hierarchy.

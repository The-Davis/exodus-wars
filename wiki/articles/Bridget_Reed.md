'''Bridget Reed'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Bridget Reed'' is a pilot in the [[Prefecture Air Force]], and was a Captain during the [[United Centusi States]] attack on [[Canopus]] on April 8, [[2325]]. Reed directed air defense and flew against the [[Mesarthrim]] fighters.

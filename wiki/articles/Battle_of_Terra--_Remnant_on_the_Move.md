October 6, [[2322]]

by Ryan Hepler

=====Sol System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today, the Remnant released information on plans for an extensive operation against the enemies of the Empire. Details are very sketchy, but B-Net has learned that their flagship and the handful of other known Remnant cruisers will be involved in this operation.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;So far, the Remnant has not been involved in the ground war. They have no ground troops that B-Net knows of. In space, only the Meroniri can challenge them, but TDF, Mesarthrim and Prefecture naval forces have managed to evade entrapment and a full battle. Every skirmish has been in the favor of the Remnant.

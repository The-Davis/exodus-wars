==Civil==
The term civil law refers to the tort system, which is based on a "neutered" version of the American civil law system of the 20th century. Essentially, the Prefecture only enforces contracts and some extreme torts.

For example, unless someone is found criminally negligent in someone's death (i.e. murder or manslaughter), they cannot be held civilly liable in a lawsuit. Wrongful death torts must have a criminal case preceeding them.

Torts and contracts have strict enforcement limitations. If someone breaks a contract, they are only liable for direct, proveable damages. There is no legal concept of "punitive damages" in Prefecture civil law.

==Criminal Law==

Prefecture criminal law is considered very harsh by interstellar standards. It only takes a majority vote by the three Justices to convict someone. Once convicted at a local level, a criminal only has two appeals before sentencing, which he must pay for himself. The first appeal is at a planetary court, and the second at the High Justice on Ueda.

The Prefecture only has two sentences: corporal punishment and capital punishment. Minor crimes such as pickpocketing, theft, and most crimes without violence--with the exception of fighting, which is rarely prosecuted--are punished by a public lashing. Major crimes such as rape, murder, kidnapping, armed robbery are all punished by a public hanging.

==Administrative Law==

Administrative law is the simplist of all. It governs the day to day operations of the departments of Prefecture government. Mostly they are accounting codes and similar bookkeeping systems.

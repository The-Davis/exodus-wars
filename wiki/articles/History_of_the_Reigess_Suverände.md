The history of the [[Reigess Suverände]] began in [[1500BC]], when [[Raegos Direnson]] led what is now the [[Reigess Renskader|Renskader]] away from [[Kronheim]] in search of a new world. They found what is now known as the [[Raegard System]] in [[1093BC]], and settled on the world of [[Junheim]].

==First Years==
The first [[Reigen]] settlement, "Rolsucht", was built on the shores of the Hygrest Ocean in the eastern fjords of the Kenvara continent. Before long, the [[Reigens]] met the "[[Juns]]," a native group of humans already living on [[Junheim]]. Most [[Reigens]] were shocked to find other humans, but [[Raegos Direnson]] was a scholar of the [[Sundering]] and had already speculated that finding other traces of humanity was likely.

Fearing war between the [[Reigens]] and the [[Juns]], [[Raegos Direnson]] decided to go out among the more primitive race and befriend them. He left [[Rejoc Arekson]] as regent in his stead, and began traveling among the [[Juns]].

The [[Reigens]] remained isolated within Rolsucht, under strict orders to only expand enough to supply themselves with basic necessities, and to only contact the [[Juns]] if the [[Juns]] themselves chose to initiate contact.

Peace was only breached once, when some more warlike [[Juns]] chose to raid Rolsucht in hope of winning riches. The iron weapons of the [[Juns]] did nothing against the composite armor and [[Lansvapen]] weapons of the [[Reigens]], and the [[Juns]] surrendered. While he spoke little of their language, [[Rejoc Arekson]] met with their leader and tried to explain that he wished for friendship between the [[Reigens]] and the [[Juns]]. The [[Juns]] departed in peace, and the message was not forgotten.

==Unity==
In [[1086BC]], [[Raegos Direnson]] returned to Rolsucht, leading over three hundred [[Juns]], representatives of sixteen different tribes from across the Kenvara continent. [[Rejoc Arekson]] returns the throne to [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]], who takes up his place as Kejsare.

Of the sixteen tribes of [[Juns]], ten have already sworn fealty to [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]]. Along with his [[Fenrulf]]s, [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]] spent the last seven years winning renown across Kenvara, peacefully winning the hearts of the [[Juns]] by defending them against the "[[Sjorgr]]", or "sea dwarves." Neither the [[Reigens]] nor the [[Juns]] knew it at the time, but the [[Sjorgr]] were actually [[Ngrligru]], stranded on [[Junheim]] and degenerated to savagery, barely able to wield what simple weapons they could steal from the [[Juns]]. 

The remaining six tribes of [[Juns]] had promised to swear fealty to [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]] as well in a grand ceremony in Rolsucht. Much to the surprise of the [[Reigens]], the ceremony was both an acceptance of fealty, and a celebration of [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]]' wedding. The daughter of one of the [[Jun]] chieftains, [[Lilkera Yoresh nee Direnson|Lilkera]], was married to [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]] in great pomp, and was crowned the first Drottning of the [[Reigess Suverände]].

==Expansion==

By the end of [[1080BC]], the [[Reigens]] had spread across the Kenvara continent, and built many amazing cities. The [[Juns]] and the [[Reigens]] intermarried, and before two generations had passed, the two races were almost impossible to tell apart. The people kept the name [[Reigens]] as a whole.

Despite his best efforts, [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]] was never able to negotiate or make peace with the [[Sjorgr]]. He was finally forced to establish the [[Military of the Reigess Suverände|Hans Raeged Furs Likniir]] to combat the [[Sjorgr]] wherever they appeared. By the time of his death in [[997BC]], there had been no [[Sjorgr]] sightings on any of the coasts of the Kenvara continent in ten years.

[[Raegos Direnson]] abdictated the throne to his son [[Aelaf nav Raegos]] in [[1014BC]]. By this time, the [[Reigess Suverände]] covered the Kenvara continent, and the [[Reigens]] and [[Juns]] of Kenvara were beginning to make contact with the [[Juns]] of the other continents in hopes of continuing the peaceful expansion of the [[Reigen]] kingdom.

==Council of Independent Nations==

Long an isolationist nation, the fate of [[Kronheim]] at the hands of the [[Volucris Swarms]] convinced the King of the need for allies. The [[Reigens]], seeking to distance themselves from the reach of the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]], allied with the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], and joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]] in [[2325]].

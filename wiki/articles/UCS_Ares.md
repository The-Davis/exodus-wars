===Overview===

The Ares was designed by Rahn Industries in early 2324 as a flagship for the Prefecture Navy.  However, the design was turned down by the Prefecture Navy, and it was put on the shelf until late 2324, when it was chosen as a flagship design by the United Centusi States Navy.  The design was built at the UCS Naval Shipyards in the Tincomalee system, under license from Rahn Industries.  

[[Image:UCSAres1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

Designed in the tradition of the big-gun warships of the past, the Ares' arsenal is based around three triple-turrets of what were designated as Type-X Heavy Blazers.  These cannons were built specifically for the Ares.  The Type-X has far greater range and power than any other Blazer type.  Since its primary weapons are such long-ranged cannons, the Ares is equipped with an advanced sensors array in the fore of the ship, aiding in accurate targeting at extreme ranges.

Backing up the Type-X Heavy Blazers is a Fireline Hellgun, a standard RI Hellgun heavily overhauled by Fireline Industries of Cirus, and a dozen Type-R Heavy Blazers, the tried-and-true heavy cannons mounted on nearly every Mesarthrim warship, large and small.  In addition to these heavier weapons, 102 Type-F Blazers and 48 VOTM tubes, for use against fighters, warships, and stationary targets alike, make the Ares a formidable foe against any warship. 

The Ares also carries a substantial aerospace complement.  With a total of fourty squadrons of aerospace fighters and eight squadrons of bombers, plus numerous shuttles and support craft, the Ares can overwhelm some foes simply with aerospace support. 

The Ares is protected by a Mesarthrim Automated Adaptive Shielding system.  The AAS is backed up by Leifstein Composite Armor, which is roughly 25% stronger than Unitron Tzaiko Composite.  It is also equipped with Gravity Variance Drive, allowing it to perform very complex maneuvers for such a large warship. 

===Deployment===

The Ares was completed in late February of 2325 at the UCS Naval Shipyards orbiting Tincomalee.  After shake-down testing, the Ares was commissioned as the flagship of the UCS Navy 2nd fleet, with a home-base in the Centus system.  Its massive guns are designed to pummel through enemy shielding and disable entire warships in only a few salvos.

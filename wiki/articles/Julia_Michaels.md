''Julia Michaels'' was known for her skill and tenacity in commanding [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]] in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. She was given command of the [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']] from its launch until [[2318]], and the [[HMS Glorious|HMS ''Glorious'']] from its launch until its destruction (and her death) in [[2323]].

=== Torimur Service ===

Julia Michaels served in the [[Torimur]] Colonial Freighter Service early in life as a cargo deckhand. With no formal education and the bad relations between [[Torimur]] and the [[United Earth Alliance]], she had few other opportunities. While hoisting cargo and making long space journeys was not the ideal career for a girl of sixteen, she made the best of it.

Julia learned quickly to be tough as nails. While her fellow shipmates treated her well, many rough individuals at her ship's port-of-call thought she was easy prey. When she was 18, a dock worker on [[Kalmar]] tried to rape her. She managed to fight off her attacker and escape, tearing one of his eyes out in the process.

The prejudice of the [[United Earth Alliance]] against [[Torimur]] colonists became obvious to Julia when the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] Port Authority allowed her attacker to press charges against her. Julia narrowly avoided arrest and managed to hide out aboard her ship. The captain helped her remain hidden while the Port Authority served a search warrant on his ship, and once they were clear they escaped back to the [[Cildeng System]].

=== The First Exodus War ===

When [[Lucius Black]] instigated the [[First Exodus War]], Julia willingly believed that the [[United Earth Alliance]] was responsible for the devastating attack on [[Torimur]]. She quickly found a [[Torimur]] [[:Category:Warships|warship]] that would allow her to serve on the crew, and fought in the war.

During one particularly rough battle, Julia helped repel [[United Earth Alliance]] boarders from her ship. The section she was in was vented into space, but she survived because she had the foresight to be in proper vacuum gear. The [[United Earth Alliance]] captured her, and she spent the rest of the war in a prison camp. When the [[First Exodus]] began, Julia was sent along with the rest of the exiles.

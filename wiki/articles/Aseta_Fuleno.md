'''Aseta Fuleno''' is a unique force within the [[Meroniri Yatisu]]. Where [[Aseta Joreno]] is the elite special forces of the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], the Fuleno is the dark hand that no one sees. Officially, Fuleno has not undertaken a single mission. Unofficially, they undertake missions that would be considered atrocities by most civilizations.

Aseta Fuleno is tasked with two objectives: eliminate any threat to the Terinasi that Aseta Joreno cannot, and unleash terror and misery on the [[Ngrligru]] if they appear to be on the verge of becoming a threat. Ships from the Fuleno use the Tesomesa to jump into the Sagittarius Arm, where they bombard soft targets and disappear again. Fuleno ships regularly operate long-term reconnaissance missions into Ngrligru space, sometimes for years on end.

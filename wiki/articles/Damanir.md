The city of '''Damanir''' is located on [[Tejat]].  It is a sizeable suburban area outside the capital of [[Kerala]], with a population of about 45,000.

In [[2318]], the [[Damanir Incident]] became famous throughout the [[Sol Sector]] after [[Omegas]] agents attempted to execute the entire city for harboring [[Talaris Roi]].  The effort was thwarted by the intervention of the [[Royal Imperial Army]].

=== History ===

[[Oronus System|Oronus]] was a failed industrial colony when the [[Onyx Empire]] came. Under [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] rule, people were shipped in by the thousands and put to work in the mines and processing factories of the asteroid belt, and on the sole rocky planetary body in the system, a [[Mars]]-sized moon orbiting a gas giant. Times were hard, but the [[Empire]] reaped great rewards from its investment.

When the [[Onyx Empire]] fell, and [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] authority crumbled, many decided to abandon this hard colony. However, most colonists wanted to push on and make [[Oronus System|Oronus]] a stable colony. Instead of seeking new masters, they began selling the fruits of their labors, and, led by [[Roger Morris]], forged on with massive improvement projects to living quarters, arcological farms, and improved worker safety.

The plan has worked so far, and today the Oronus Worker's Coalition, the loosely organized governing body of [[Oronus System|Oronus]] is a sovereign state. The insatiable appetite of the [[Prefecture]] war-machine has led to ever increasing outputs and incomes for the people of [[Oronus System|Oronus]]. In [[2325]], the [[Oronus System]] posted its first net increase in population due to voluntary immigration.

=== Government ===

Under [[Empire|Imperial]] rule, the colonists were little more than barely-paid slave labor. They were fed and housed, and worked half to death. [[Roger Morris]] knew that working his people just as hard, if not harder was necessary to make the colony work. He was determined never to be a slave-master, so he nationalized all property and industry and began running the colony like a publicly-traded company. Every citizen of the [[Oronus System]] was given shares in the colony, and shareholders were able to vote by proxy on the direction of the colony's development. They still had to work hard, but they had a voice in what they worked on.

=== Law ===

The Oronus Coalition passes laws on an as-needed basis. The Coalition focuses on development of their resources, and has little time to waste dealing with petty concerns. Most criminal law is simple: cause trouble, and you get shipped out. Troublemakers are usually forced to pull double-shifts, dangerous shifts, or are sent to [[Lorithan]], which has no immigration laws and will accept anyone.

All law enforcement is handled by the Oronus Worker's Coalition's [[Military of the Oronus Worker's Coalition|military]].

=== Foreign Relations ===

The Coalition does not get along well with the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] at all. They like the [[Prefecture]], who are among their best customers, and are reluctant to deal with anyone else outside of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

The Oronus Worker's Coalition controls the [[Oronus System]].

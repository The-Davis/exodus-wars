==International Organizations==

===The Alliance of Planets===

The [[Ixoarchada]] were already familiar with the [[Alliance of Planets]] when they made [[First Contact]] with the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].  For some time afterwards, it was widely believed that the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] would join the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]], uniting nearly all of humanity under the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter|Great Charter]].  This speculation ended when the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] were finally moved to action by an attempted blockade and soon afterwards joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

===Council of Independent Nations===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] remained as neutral as possible when they began dealings with the other races of humanity, but were finally forced to choose sides as the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] grew ever more into a [[Meroniri Terinasi|Meroniri]] proxy and attempted to enforce its blockade on non-member states.  After entreaties from the [[Reigess Suverände]] and the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]] in late [[2325]], and later moved to force [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Space Police]] vessels from [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] territories.

==Individual Governments==

===Auellal League===

When the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] was torn apart by civil war, the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] froze all contacts with the [[Mesarthrim]] for the duration of the war.  When the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|Clan's]] [[High Command]] handed over power and recognized the [[United Centusi States]] as their successor state, the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] likewise recognized the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]].  [[Caledon]] was considered a rogue province, not a legitimate government, and the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] maintain no official contacts with the [[Auellal League]] government.

===Colonial Commonwealth===

The first dealings with the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] came in early 2325 as the survivors of the war grew ever more suspicious of the [[Alliance of Planets]] and its attempts to enforce Alliance Law outside of [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] territory.  The [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] had little to offer in terms of trade, but still maintained official, friendly relations as a matter of course.  After the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] joined the [[Council of Independent Nations]], the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] granted territorial access to [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] naval forces.  Additionally, mass imports of advanced goods from the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] began due to the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Meroniri]] embargo of sales of their own products, and they continue to rise even now that the embargo has been lifted.

===The Conclave===

[[The Conclave]] had few dealings, if any, with the [[Taviridis Somarchada]].  The necessity of crossing through both [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] and [[Mesarthrim]] space to reach [[Taviridis Somarchada]] territory, as well the vast distance from the [[Sol Sector]] to the [[Tolana Sector]], which itself is charted only by the [[Ixoarchada]], made operations unfeasible.  Little knowledge of the [[Ixoarchada]], and the fact that [[Terrans]] of any sort would greatly stand out made infiltration nearly impossible.

===The Federated Districts of the Prefecture===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] have a neutral military stance towards the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]], but maintain trade relations.  Official government contact ranges from courteous to curt, depending on circumstances.  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture's]] organic medical technology (non-robotic) is considered advanced even by [[Ixoarchada]] standards, and the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis']] state-run enterprises sell [[Ixoarchada]] anti-gravity technology to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]].  The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] would greatly like access to the [[Ixoarchada]] anti-matter supply, but [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] neutrality in the [[Tempest War]] precludes supplying the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture|Prefecture]] with the means to power their warships.

===Ikronin Jurekön===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] actually have little contact with the [[Ikronin Jurekön]].  Relations are neutral and civil, if brisk.  The [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] have little need for the monopolized trade routes of the [[Ikronin]], and the [[Ikronin]] have little to sell directly.  Although the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] did allow the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] to place one of their [[Wormholes|Avmaska-Stig]] in the [[Ularen System]], traffic is light at best, and the [[Ikronin]] now consider it a poor investment.

===Kabila iku Gol'nde===

Since ages long past, the legends of the [[Ixoarchada]] have referenced a race of monsters that brought the first great civilization to an end, and who would some day return.  Modern historians know these legend to reference the [[Sundering]] and the "monsters" refer to the [[Ngrligru]] race.  However, there is no actual contact between the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] and any [[Ngrligru]] due to the vast distance from the [[Teeth of Orion]] to the [[Tolana Sector]].

===Kabila Kimburu===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] are neutral towards the [[Ngrligru]] within the [[Orion Arm]], but do not maintain any official governmental contacts with [[Kabila Kimburu]] itself.

===Kalidasa Planetary Authority===

The only contact between the Kalidasans and the [[Ixoarchada]] has occurred within the context of the [[Council of Independent Nations]].  Outside of the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]], the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority|Kalidasa Authority]] and [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] maintain neutral relations.

===Meroniri Terinasi - Meronir's Republic===

When the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] first introduced themselves to the galactic community, the [[Meroniri]] met them on friendly terms. It soon became clear that the united [[Taviridis Somarchada|Somarchada]] government presented a threat to [[Meroniri]] supremacy in military and technological advantages.  Relations cooled, and then soured as the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] began to thwart [[Meroniri]] machinations against the worlds that would eventually form the [[Council of Independent Nations]]. Since the formation of the [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]], and the role of [[Taviridis Somarchada|Somarchada]] warships in the ejection of [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Alliance Space Police]] blockade vessels from [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] territory, the [[Meroniri]] have not had friendly dealings with any [[Ixoarchada]].

===Oronus Worker's Coalition===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada]] came into contact with the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus Coalition]] after joining the [[Council of Independent Nations]].  It was in the [[Oronus System]] that [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] warships led the first [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] sovereignty patrols that ejected [[Alliance of Planets Space Police|Alliance Space Police]] from [[Council of Independent Nations|Council]] territory, resulting in the [[Oronus Incident]].  However, there is little other interaction between the [[Oronus Worker's Coalition|Oronus Coalition]] and the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], and no significant trade to speak of.

===Reigess Suverände===

The [[Reigens]] were one of the first races to establish relations with the [[Ixoarchada]] people after [[First Contact]]. The [[Reigess Suverände]] made contact as a matter of course, much akin to the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]], viewing the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] as a potential bulwark against further [[Meroniri]] ambitions.  Unlike the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]], however, the [[Reigens]] actually proved to be viable trade partners, dealing in high-tech goods nearly on par with [[Ixoarchada]] levels of technology in many areas (specifically in the realm of anti-matter harvesting). Because of these trade relations, the [[Reigess Suverände]] was one of the chief negotiators that brought the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] into the [[Council of Independent Nations]].

===The Remnant===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] have not made any contact with the [[Remnant]] to-date, although it is unknown if [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] exploration ships ever observed the [[Remnant]] ships operating in the [[Sol System]].

===Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] currently have little direct dealings with the [[Lorithos]], as the [[Tolana Sector]] is far beyond the current maximum range of [[Baihros Estin Fohrvik]] technology.  Due to the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] trade embargo, no [[Ixoarchada]] merchants visit [[Lorithan]] or the colonies.

===Sirius Families===

The [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] have official relations with the [[Sirius Families]] via the [[Council of Independent Nations]], but have no other direct ties within the [[Sirius Cluster]].  Most [[Ixoarchada]] view the Sirius Cluster as a lawless backwater, although true distrust is reserved mainly for the [[Meroniri]].  The Sirius Families are simply considered out-of-place in the modern era.

===Sirunaki Rebellion===

The [[Ixoarchada]] have had no contact with the [[Sirunaki]] and do not know that they exist.

===Sol System Authority===

As one of many state-sponsored goodwill-projects, the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] provide support to the [[Sol System Authority]] in the form of medical and computing research teams.  In addition, they are both members of the [[Council of Independent Nations]] and maintain friendly, neutral relations through that organization.

===United Centusi States===

After the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] observed the transfer of power from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] government to the [[United Centusi States]], and recognized the new [[Mesar]]-based government as the legitimate successor state.  However, since the start of the [[Tempest War]], relations between the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] and the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] have cooled because the [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] have suspended trade of many goods that might aid either side in the fight.  Even greater strain has been placed on the relations between the two factions by the decision of the [[Ixoarchada]] members of the [[Prometheus Council]] not to allow any [[Joint Defense Fleet]] warships to be deployed outside of the [[Tirshan System]].

===Volucris Swarms===

The Taviridis have been in a state of open war with the [[Volucris Swarms]] since [[2012]].  Since driving the [[Volucris]] from the [[Tolana Sector]], the Taviridis have actively observed the Volucris migration coreward, and both openly and covertly sought to track their movements and limit their impacts on the mid-arm [[nations]].

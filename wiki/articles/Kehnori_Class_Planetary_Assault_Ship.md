The [[Ixoarchada]] believe in the fulfillment of purpose in every aspect of life.  In warfare, this means that they have a host of highly specialized equipment, vehicles, and vessels that fulfill very specific roles.  Although costs and practicality lead to certain roles and missions being combined into a single platform, certain tasks often have such requirements that a vehicle may not retain usefulness outside of its intended role. 

The Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship is one such example of a vessel so specialized that it is deployed only for its purpose.  Left in their docks when not in use, the Kenhori class came about as the epitome of all the lessons the Ixoarchada learned in over a millennium of planetary sieges, counter-assaults, and blockades. 

== Design ==

[[Image:Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship Front View.jpg|150px|left|thumb|A view of the Kehnori's main batteries.]]

[[Haven Shipyards]] was contracted for the design, but with [[Rahn Industries]] successfully lobbying for so many defense contracts, two construction contracts were awarded to the industrial giant.

A total of ten Planetary Assault Ships were ordered, with half to be delivered by [[2324]] and the rest to be delivered by [[2325]].  Construction of each took just over a year, even with several [[Haven Class Shipyards]] working in conjunction on an individual ship.

== Battle History ==

[[Image:Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship Side View.jpg|200px]]

The first ship in the class, the [[FDS Kehnori|FDS ''Kehnori'']], was delivered to the Prefecture Galactic Navy, and tested in combat against the [[Volucris]] during their incursion into the [[Mimosa System]].  It performed admirably, but the class’s real trial by fire did not come until the [[Tempest War]].

The Kehnori Class performed rather well against [[Mesarthrim]] vessels, which tended to be smaller and often had shorter range on their main weapons.  The Kehnori’s armaments were sufficient to break through Mesarthrim defensive technologies, but the class suffered maneuverability issues and one ship, the [[FDS Prestige|FDS ''Prestige'']], was lost with half its crew when a trio of Mesarthrim destroyers managed to get in close and bring its defenses down in a matter of seconds.

Most Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ships are used as long range weapons platforms, or against similarly armed ships with similar maneuverability profiles.  In fleet actions, wise commanders deploy screens of cruisers, destroyers, frigates, and fighters to shield the Kehnori Planetary Assault Ships long enough to do their job

[[Image:Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship Top View.jpg|200px]]

Most importantly, the Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship carries a massive command and control center, and has all of the hardware needed for an [[Engineered Intelligence]].  The Prefecture hopes to cultivate a volunteer program to give its entire fleet these amazing creatures, though for now [[Prism]] is able to run several fleets’ worth of ships at once by slaving additional hardware to her own systems.

== Future Plans ==

The Kehnori Class Planetary Assault Ship is considered a success, and has an appreciable kill ratio for ships of its size.  The Prefecture’s Senate and Curiate have approved the budget to replace all the ships lost in the [[Tempest War]], and budgeted for two additional ships in the class to be completed in [[2340]].

'''Aydan Black'''
*Place of Birth: Mongolia, [[Terra]]
*Place of Death: [[Torimur]]

''Aydan Black'' was the wife of [[Jerome Black]] and the mother of [[Lucius Black]]. She died young, from complications of sickness and the sorrow over the loss of her husband.

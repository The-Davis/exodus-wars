=== History ===

Its name alone inspires fear and awe among military personnel in the Orion Arm. Perhaps the most famous of all Mesarthrim ground units, the Vulture Seige Tank is among the most deadly tanks ever made. The Vulture is the tank that finally broke the back of the HalbAnderung occupation armies, and later sent Lucius Black's Royal Imperial Army reeling from loss after loss across the northern hemisphere of Terra.

After the Mesarthrim Civil War, the United Centusi States captured the only Vulture production facilities, located on Tincomalee. The Auellal still have many of the tanks at their disposal, but must scrounge for spare parts or buy them from smugglers. The design has been update a bit, but there is little room for improvement of this titan.

The Vulture's frightening power lies in its twin Type-5 Railguns, the largest mounted on any Mesarthrim ground vehicle. Their shells are deadly, and the advanced targeting gear makes them astonishingly accurate, even at extreme ranges of a few dozen kilometers.

Backing up the Railguns are a pair of Type-B1 Blazers twin-turrets, located on either side of the hull, and a fixed pair of Type-B1 Blazers in the front of the tank. The turrets can be either automated or remote controlled, and are powerful enough to keep away most armored units and infantry. At close-range, they are effective even against heavy armor. The turrets are also capable of protecting the Vulture from aerospace and VTOL targets. The two fixed Blazers are useful for ranged attack and striking at stationary targets, but are not very useful up-close.

[[Image:VultureSiegeTank2.jpg|thumb|300px]]

The Vulture's massive Cybeck SV-28a reactor is actually a gunship-grade reactor. It is quite capable of powering the Vulture's weaponry, its extremely heavy shielding system, and pushing the behemoth of a tank up to 90 kilometers per hour.

Vulture Seige Tanks are the premier ground combat craft of the Centusi Heer. Their power, size, and mobility allow them to operate in most any environment and even the harshest terrain. In the Battle of Terra, they operated on tropical Pacific islands, the deserts of Asia, the plains of Europe, and the harsh Arctic regions of North America, the Vulture was undeterred.

While smaller tanks have come and gone, and light vehicles and aircraft have taken on some of the lighter duties of the Vulture, the great Seige Tank is still the workhorse of the Mesarthrim, regardless of their loyalties, and will remain so for some time to come.

Putting Down the Daegu Riots - 6/27/2325 - By James Davis

Daegu, Casere, Laojiu District
     The first uprising against Mesarthrim occupation of Daegu was not the last. Major Bissell put down the first by capturing several civilian apartment complexes and threatening to kill everyone inside if the insurgency did not surrender.
     This time, the insurgency is not organized. It is merely a riot against the Mesarthrim. Bottles and rocks are a far sight from bullets, but many Mesarthrim Myt'kampfen are still hurt in the riots. For some time, Major Bissell follows Admiral Dietrich's command, but finally allows his command to use deadly force when all other methods fail.


Trial in a Kangaroo Court - 6/28/2325 - By James Davis

Daegu, Casere, Laojiu District
     Admiral Dietrich is called to task by William Camp for failing to maintain total control of the Laojiu District. Camp's plans call for a total takeover of the District and the submission of its people.
     As part of his plan, he sends the former governor of Casere, Ju Tao, back to the planet for a trial by Mesarthrim military tribunal. Ju Tao is brought up on charges of providing aid and comfort to enemies of the Mesarthrim. The only evidence against him is a few samples of his name in graffiti sprayed over Mesarthrim administration buildings.

Execution of Ju Tao - 6/29/2325 - By James Davis

Daegu, Casere, Laojiu District
     The trial of Ju Tao concludes with William Camp personally handing down the order of execution by public hanging. The execution takes place at high noon, in the town square of Daegu. Daegu finally submits and offers no further resistance.

End of the Resistance - 6/30/2325 - By James Davis

Janggok, Casere, Laojiu District
     With Daegu submitting to Mesarthrim authority, only Janggok remains actively opposed to the occupation by the United Centusi States.
     That changes when William Camp, accompanied by a full division of Myt'herra, visits the city. Camp's warning is simple. The city will submit, or a second high-profile Laoji official, William Parrish, will die, followed by all of Janggok. With the resistance finally crushed, Camp returns to Mesar.

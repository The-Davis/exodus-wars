===Overview===

The Nigrotaeniata is one of the larger soldier mutations, and one of the few types that relies on power rather than numbers.  It is known as the "Tyrant" by human soldiers, because of its complete disregard for smaller Volucris units.  Tyrants have been seen stepping on Cutters and other small varieties as they make their way across the battlefield.

[[Image:Nigrotaeniata1.jpg]]

The Tyrant has four tusks alongside its head, which gore light armor, vehicles and troops that get too close.  When it gets to a location, it sinks its legs into the ground, anchoring it to the dirt.

An anchored Tyrant can pull back its shell, revealing rows of deadly projectile spines.  Each spine is as effective as an Autocannon round, and has a fire rate of a machine gun.  The spines grow at a phenomenal rate, but the Tyrant must consume biological matter to produce them.  It eats human foot soldiers to make its ammunition.

The Tyrant is slow, but powerful and well armored.  Just one can turn the tide of battle in favor of the Volucris swarm, and there are many of them.  It takes a lot of teamwork or a lot of firepower to take one out.

The Tyrant's carapace is extremely resistant to lasers and heat energy, and completely immune to electrical weapons.  The smooth, polished surface also deflects most ammunition.  Only missiles and area-effect weapons can do much damage.  Usually, human forces use artillery bombardment to take down a Tyrant.

[[Image:Nigrotaeniata2.jpg]]

Tyrants will be found in limited numbers in any large-scale operation.  The Volucris Nobles seem to prefer to keep these killing machines in reserve, to protect hive clusters.  Because of their burrowing and powerful weapons, they are well suited for the job.

If a Tyrant is commanded to do so, it will consume large amounts of biological material and morph into an Imperator or a Blister.  Metamorphosis into an Imperator takes eight months, and metamorphosis into a Blister takes three months..

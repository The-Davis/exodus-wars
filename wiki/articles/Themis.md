Themis has a large civilian population, mostly to support the extensive military installations the Titanians have built over the years. Oddly enough, Themis has an extensive network of floating cities, using simple tethers to keep the cities in predictable locations. This is due to Themis’ extremely active tectonic system, which shook apart the first Titanian defense base built on the continent. Despite its problems, Themis is an important part of the Titanian defense system, with dozens of orbiting installations, drydocks, and shipyards. General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] used the world as his headquarters for much of the [[Second Exodus War]], though he was briefly ousted by an Imperial incursion.

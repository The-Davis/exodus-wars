The Dritte Flotte ''(or Third Fleet)'' was known as the 'Warrior of the Alliance.'  During the [[Second Exodus War]] and the [[Onyx Civil War]] the Third Fleet was called on again and again to protect [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] ships, planets, and installations.  Over the course of her short service, the third fleet never once formed a single unit - at least one element was always abroad serving the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliances]] needs.

==Formation==
At the end of April [[2322]], after a two week battle with the [[Onyx Empire]] for the [[Proxima Procyon System]], [[Arnold Stadt]] requisitioned the entire refit line that was being built by [[Fireline Industries]] and [[Mesar Luftfahrt]] into a new fleet.  His rash move gave the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] an entire brand new fleet - however, left some antiquated vessels in the other two fleets.  Later, [[Arnold Stadt|Stadt]] would use the Third Fleet as his vanguard unit in the initial [[Mesarthrim Invasion of Terra]].  The Third Fleet was also vital in forming the [[Terra Space Supremacy Force Alpha]].

==Fate==
As [[2325]] wore on, attrition began to wear on the Third Fleet.  Of the eight elements, only one was still fully manned.  In addition, on January 1st of [[2326]], the Third Fleet was abandoned by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] when their government gave way to the [[United Centusi States]].  [[Arnold Stadt]] officially called for the disbandment of his flag fleet.  The remaining vessels of the Third Fleet capitulated to the [[United Centusi States]], numbering only sixteen of the original forty-eight.

==Composition==
The Third Fleet is further divided into eight six vessel sections, The Son's of God, The Mesarthrim, The Survivor Lords, The Great Senators, The Alliance, Forward, The Poets, & the Mighty Mesarthrim.  Commanding the Third Fleet was the UMCS Anderung Trauergesang, or Anderung's Dirge.

===Die Söhne des Gottes ''(The Sons of God)''===
The Sons of God are a group of six [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser|Avenger class Cruisers]] purchased from [[Rahn Industries]] in late [[2322]] to finish rounding out the Third fleet.  The Sons of God we re-fitted in [[2324]], after a heavy engagement with [[Onyx Empire|Onyx]] warships, with a new order of [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser|Avengers']] that were sold to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] as bare bone vessels, and outfitted by [[Mesarbauer]].  The entire element was destroyed in a slug match with several [[Auellal League]] [[Mongrel Vessel|Mongrel Warships]] in [[2325]].

The Sons of God were;
*UMCS Rippe von Adam ''(The Rib of Adam)''
*UMCS Verstand von Solomon ''(The Wisdom of Solomon)''
*UMCS Folgsamkeit von Moses ''(The Obedience of Moses)''
*UMCS Die Arche von Noah ''(The Ark of Noah)''
*UMCS Der Mut von Joshua ''(The Courage of Joshua)''
*UMCS Die Richtung von Paul ''(The Direction of Paul)''

===Die Mesarthrim ''(The Mesarthrim)''===
The Mesarthrim was the first group of vessels [[Arnold Stadt]] pulled from [[Fireline Industries]] refit line.  They were the new re-fitting of the popular and powerful [[Starknight class Cruiser|Starknight class Cruisers]].  The Mesarthrim were posted for a significant period of time in the [[Titan|Titan Sytem]] after its liberation, and were later called back to [[Mesar]] to defend [[Mesar#Orillian|Orillian]] from [[Auellal League|Auellal]] bombardment.  The Mesarthrim broke the siege with the help of the UMCS Anderung Trauergesang, and stayed in orbit until the collapse of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

The Mesarthrim were;
*UMCS Mesar
*UMCS Orillian
*UMCS Orimmil
*UMCS Willim
*UMCS Arakkia
*UMCS Rellasha

===Die Überlebenden Herren ''(The Survivor Lords)''===
The Survivor Lords [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe class Destroyers]] played a pivotal role in [[Terra|Terra Space Supremacy Force Alpha]].  Their duty was to protect supply convoys that were coming in from the hyper limit on their way to [[Terra]].  Then entire element was lost with all hands in [[2323]] when they attempted to stop [[Lucius Black]] from fleeing [[Terra]].  The Survivor Lords were outgunned and outclassed by the retreating Emperor and his allies.  In [[2325]], [[Arnold Stadt]] purchased six [[Typhoon Class Frigate|Typhoon Class Frigates]] from [[Rahn Industries]], but was unable to completely man the fleet again due to a sevear lack of naval officers and enlisted men.  Then the Typhoon hulls were claimed by the [[Auellal League]] in a raid, and the Survivor Lords never were able to be reformed before the Third Fleets disbandment.   

The Survivor Lords were;
*UMCS Tischler
*UMCS Stadt
*UMCS Koeing
*UMCS Eberhart
*UMCS Dietrich
*UMCS Brandt

===Die Großen Senatoren ''(The Great Senators)''===
The Great Senators were an element of [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe class Destroyers]] charged with the special orders from [[Arnold Stadt]] to travel into the [[Teeth of Orion]] to survey and discover a rumour that a hive of [[Anderung]] lived on some desolate rock there.  After three years of research and exploration, the UMCS Der Sprecher appeared and ambushed a [[Meroniri Terinasi|Meroniri]] warship and was soundly beaten.  Both the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and the [[Meroniri Terinasi|Meroniri]] briefly attempted to find the rest of the fleet but were unable.  The Great Senators were never re-commissioned before the fleets disbandment.

The Great Senators were;
*UMCS Der Zuhörer ''(The Listener)''
*UMCS Der Sprecher ''(The Speaker)''
*UMCS Die Gemeinschaft ''(The Community)''
*UMCS Das Herz ''(The Heart)''
*UMCS Die Logik ''(The Logic)''
*UMCS Das Gedächtnis ''(The Memory)''

===Die Verbindung ''(The Alliance)''===
The Alliance was the first, of many, shipments the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] (and later the [[United Centusi States]]) received of the [[Delta Class Missile Frigate]].  The Alliance took excessive damage in [[2322]] shortly after being formed when they took part in a joint assault on [[Mars]].  Later that year, the fleet was resupplied with more [[Delta Class Missile Frigate|Delta Class Missile Frigates]].  In [[2325]] the Alliance returned to [[Mesar]] after being recalled.  All but the UMCS Vergeltung von Sanburn was destroyed.

The Alliance were;
*UMCS Verteidiger der Verbindung ''(Defender of the Alliance)''
*UMCS Die Hilfe ''(The Assistance)''
*UMCS Vergeltung von Sanburn ''(Sanburn’s Retribution)''
*UMCS Der Bewahrer des Kolosss ''(The Keeper of Titan)''
*UMCS Pirschjäger des Onyx ''(Stalker of the Onyx)''
*UMCS Kraft in der Einheit ''(Strength in Unity)''

===Nachschicken ''(Forward)''===
The Forward, as their name implies, were often the Vanguard element of the Third Fleet.  The Forward was an element of Battleships - the first even in Mesarthrim naval history.  They were six [[Starlight class Battleship}Starlights]], equipped with the most advanced defence systems the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Kriegsmarine]] had to offer, their offensive capibility augmented by the mighty [[Fireline Warhammer Cannon|Warhammer Cannon]].  The Forward struck [[Mars]], [[Jupiter]], and led the assault on [[Terra]].  The Forward would hold their own as the lead vessels of [[Terra|Terra Space Supremacy Force Alpha]].  However, in [[2325]] a series of mutinies plagued the Forward as the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]] grew.  Then finally in August of [[2325]], a remote bomb detonated in the [[Fireline Warhammer Cannon|Warhammer Cannon]] on the UMCS Tore von Briggsholm while they were docked at Raumstraff Island - the following explosion and chain reaction not only destroyed the element, but Raumstraff Island as well.

The Forward were;
*UMCS Die Krone Mesar ''(The Crown of Mesar)''
*UMCS Das Eisen von Orimmil ''(The Iron of Orimmil)''
*UMCS Die Kupplung von Antvirp ''(The Clutch of Antvirp)''
*UMCS Widerstand der Steinbrücke ''(The Defiance of Stone Bridge)''
*UMCS Tore von Briggsholm ''(Gates of Briggsholm)''
*UMCS Die Sümpfe von Rellasha ''(The Swamps of Rellasha)''

===Die Dichter ''(The Poets)''===
The Poets were cobbled together from the left overs of the refit.  There were two [[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmasters]] and four [[Wycliffe class Destroyer|Wycliffe's]].  The element was the Forwards shadow element and were charged with protecting the Forward.  The Poets were successful in providing defence and support for the Forward until all ships were lost in [[2325]] when Raumstraff Island was destroyed with all hands.

The Poets were;
*UMCS In Frieden als im Krieg ''(In Peace as in War)''
*UMCS Flüchtige Hoffnung ''(Fleeting Hope)''
*UMCS Herren und Krieger ''(Of Gentlemen and Warriors)''
*UMCS Kreuze und Schwerter ''(Crosses and Swords)''
*UMCS Image des Mesarthrim ''(Image of the Mesarthrim)''
*UMCS Ritter für die ganze Ewigkeit ''(Knight for all Eternity)''

===Mächtiger Mesarthrim ''(Mighty Mesarthrim)''===
The Mighty Mesarthrim were a mixed element, consisting of one [[Banshee class Carrier|Banshee]], two [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser|Avenger's]] and three [[Starmaster class Destroyer|Starmaster's]].  The Mighty Mesarthrim was meant to be a 'micro fleet' since it contained a heavy carrier and escorts, however, it served most of its time patrolling [[Mesarthrim]] space.  Then in [[2325]] the Might Mesarthrim conducted two operations against [[Caledon]], both of which failed to damage the [[Auellal League|Auellal's]] salvage yards and construction facilities.  Then, in what was possibly the most heroic engagement of the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|Civil War]], the Mighty Mesarthrim took on fifteen [[Mongrel Vessel|Mongrel Warships]], and captured the traitor Commandant Henderson.  The Mighty Mesarthrim only lost one [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser|Avenger]] - the [[Auellal League|Auellal]] lost eight [[Mongrel Vessel|Mongrels]].

The Mighty Mesarthrim were;
*UMCS Gerade Ursache ''(Just Cause)''
*UMCS Mächtiger Mesarthrim ''(Mighty Mesarthrim)''
*UMCS Kraft des Willens ''(Strength of Will)''
*UMCS Jemals Mesarthrim ''(Ever Mesarthrim)''
*UMCS Immer Furchtlos ''(Always Fearless)''
*UMCS Nachschicken Schwung ''(Forward Momentum)''

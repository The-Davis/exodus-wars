===Overview===

Jaytex Mechanized Unlimited held a competition among engineers in the JMU Fighter Production Program to build a superior long-range fighter that embodied the best of each of the current class of fighters. The Final two teams were permitted to build twelve prototypes of their design and these ships would compete against one another. The two fighters were the Tornfalk and the Merkurius.

[[Image:Tornfalk1.jpg]]

Despite superior speed and agility, design flaws in the flight control system of the Merkurius fighter led to the Tornfalk team winning the competition before the trials were completed.

===Capabilities===

While the current crop of IC fighters are capable of admirably performing the duties tasked to them, the Tornfalk is the most advanced fighter ever built to serve the Consortium, and some at JMU believe it may be one of the most advanced fighters, if not the most advanced fighter, in the known galaxy.

The Tornfalk is set to be the Icronian Consortium’s premiere core fighter for next several decades and beyond. A high speed, single seat attack craft capable of atmospheric and non-atmospheric combat. It was designed to perform a variety of roles, from basic patrols to planetary assault to system defense to direct space born interdiction missions. Despite the fact that it is capable of operating in an atmosphere, doing so diminishes its top end speed and agility, bringing its performance more in line with some of the top fighters from other governments.

This agile fighter has two heavy Lansvapen Cannons mounted to either side of the bow, and a single Micro-Torpedo tube launch system mounted just under the bow. The Tornfalk possesses ten standard ordinance hard points guided by a prototype Tjorvi 6-B Image Recognition smart targeting computer. The difference between this version and the standard JMU IR Targeting System is that the Tjorvi system possesses a scanning range of 85,000 km/s3, while the standard system has a range of 50,000 km/s3. The Micro-Torpedo tube fires compact anti-matter torpedoes, approximately 40cm in length. The micro-torpedoes are dumb-fire weapons, once fired they will travel in a straight line until they hit their target or its fuel is exhausted. To prevent unexploded ordinance from harming unsuspecting craft the micro-torpedoes are timed to implode after a set period once launched. Though they lack the advanced onboard guidance systems of missiles, the micro-torpedoes are smaller and cheaper to produce while still producing the same, if not larger, explosive capacity.

[[Image:Tornfalk2.jpg]]

Maximum speed for the Tornfalk is much greater than that of any IC fighter, with an acceleration rate of over 300 KPH/S. JMU engineers achieved this high level of performance through the use of the same type of gravity/energy principles being incorporated into the latest JMU capital ships, combined with the use of highly advanced pressure suits, similar to the powered infantry suits employed by the Blackis Prefecture.

Another new piece of technology to debut with the Tornfalk is the Type E Lansvapen Array. This is the first successful application of a Lansvapen Array on a vessel of this size, and is mounted near the ships aft section and tied directly into the targeting system. When activated, the system operates autonomously firing the array at incoming missiles and pursuing ships.

A normal missile compliment for the Tornfalk includes two Shrike IR Missiles, four Vessla IR Missiles and four Ghita IR Missiles. All missiles use the Image Recognition Targeting System. Shrike missiles are very fast long-range weapons, so fast that most targets are unable to outmaneuver the incoming threat. Vessla (Weasel) missiles are incredibly agile long-range weapons, with avoidance algorithms that enable the missiles to avoid any obstacles between the missile and its intended target. Ghita missiles are small missiles intended for use when dog-fighting other fighters. Two of the four Vessla missiles are aft facing for use against any hostile craft that manages to get behind a Tornfalk.

Tornfalks possess state-of-the-art sensors and are capable of performing limited scientific, surveying and mapping scans. To enhance the onboard sensors range and sensitivity the fighters carry two Vslångur Scientific Probes. The probes can either remain onboard or be released to act as remote sensor platforms. As remote sensors their data is sent via secure subspace communication channels, providing nearly instantaneous telemetry and data to both the fighter and scientists monitoring the probes from safe locations.

The fighter is equipped with Auto-Modulating Regenerative Repulsor Shields. Dedicated sensor resources analyze incoming threat weapon properties, be they energy or kinetic in nature, and adjust the shield configuration and harmonics to match that of the incoming weapon, which dramatically increases the efficiency of the shield. Also, while standard shielding also regenerates over time, the Regenerative Shielding aspect of the upgraded shields increases it’s regenerating potential allowing the shield to regenerate, while activated.

===Deployment===

Though scheduled to replace all JMU fighters, the process will be slow going. The production time on the Tornfalk is three to four times that of a typical fighter. Couple that with extensive shakedown testing, each Tornfalk takes approximately nine months to a year to complete and be placed into active service.

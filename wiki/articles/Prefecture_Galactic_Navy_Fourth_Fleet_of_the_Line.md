The Fourth Fleet is commanded by Admiral [[Marie Sarnou]].  The fleet is led by the flagship FDS ''Victory'', the Prefecture's super-carrier.  The role of the Fourth Fleet is Fighter Command.  No other fleet carries as many fighters into battle as the Fourth.  Sky Marshal [[Jan Brooks]] is stationed on the ''Victory''.

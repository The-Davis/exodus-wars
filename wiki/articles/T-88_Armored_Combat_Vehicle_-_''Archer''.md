The T-88 "Archer" served the [[United Earth Alliance]] and later the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] for centuries.  Despite its tremendous fighting power, only a few hundred survived the [[Second Exodus War]].  The Archer is now rarely seen within the Commonwealth.

= Design History =

After the [[First Exodus War]], the United Earth Alliance ordered the development of a standardized armored fighting vehicle.  The initial design was completed by [[Fairbrother Heavy Components]], and equipped only with components built by the monolithic construction corporation.

[[Image:ArcherArmoredCar2.jpg|200px]]

== Offensive Design ==

The Archer is designed around a single heavy cannon.  Known as the 'Claymore', this large Pulsed Particle Beam Cannon has a frightful range nearly to the horizon.  The driver of the tank and a dedicated gunner work together to keep the cannon's firing arc at its most effective.  The Archer's wide wheel base and four-corner independent drive system allows it to rotate the entire vehicle in place, and bring the cannon to bare on targets quickly. 

Instead of the Starbursts mounted on lighter vehicles, the Archer is equipped with a pair of 'Rapier' turrets, one to each side.  The weapons, the same as on the Buccaneer, are capapble of warding off light targets and infantry.  The Archer has no Sunburst system, as it is meant to fight at extreme distances, not to close on enemy positions, and thus needs no such countermeasures system.

= Design Refit =

In the [[24th Century]], components from two companies were combined into a major upgrade kit for surviving Archer vehicles.

New Dallas Industries, a power company in the [[Titan System]], provided the Sr-i113e Micro-Reactor to power the newly upgraded Archers.  This powerful reactor can propel the Archer at speeds up to 260 kilometers per hour, in addition to powering the Claymore cannon.

The Prudenski Armory on Mars was contracted to provide new composite armor, shield systems, and countermeasures for the combat vehicle.

= Military Service =

Modern-day Archers are a rarity due to heavy losses in the Second Exodus War.  While there is an ample supply of the heavy armored cars, they are rarely needed, and are mainly operated during excercies.

== Second Exodus War ==

Approximately eight thousand Archers were still in service at the start of the Second Exodus War.  Most were stationed on [[Earth]], [[Holt]], and [[Theia]].  The [[Royal Imperial Army]]'s conquest of Earth and Holt resulted in the complete destruction of all Archers stationed there.

During the invasion of Earth in [[2322]] and [[2323]], General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] deployed most of his remaining Archers to aid the [[Mesarthrim]] offensive. Hundreds of Archers were destroyed or damaged beyond repair in the campaign, leaving only a few hundred in operation.

== Colonial Commonwealth ==

The surviving T-88 Archers were turned over to the Colonial Commonwealth in [[2324]].  It is used mostly for military base security and training exercises, and is available for foreign operations if needed.

General-Commander Sanburn has asked the Commonwealth government to consider funding the construction of a newer armored combat vehicle, but so far he has not received authorization or cooperation.

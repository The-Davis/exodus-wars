'''Lorithan''' is the third planet in the [[Mirdek System]]. For millenia, it was a bright, shining world, bathed in the light of the star, Mirdek. The [[Anthronoris]] living on the world were wiped out, and the [[Lorithos]] civilization rose to take its place.

Lorithan met its end in the [[Volucris War]].  The [[Volucris]] swarms came to unearth an ancient artifact, and in the process began systematically destroying all human edifices and killing all humans on the surface.  The Lorithos fought back, and many of their allies came to their aid.  The war effort was cripplingly costly, and eventually the Lorithos evacuated the planet.  On October 16, [[2334]] the last Lorithos left the planet.  Shortly afterwards, the Volucris unearthed what they had come for, and began to move the planet into the nearby star.  It took three months, but the Volucris dropped Lorithan into Mirdek.

==History==
Main Article: [[History of Lorithan|History]]

Lorithan is the homeworld of [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]]. It's a burned out hulk of a world, with dead oceans and charred landscapes, littered with warship wrecks. Stubborn residents continue to try to clean it up, and have restored some areas to the verdant grasslands from before their [[Burning Wars]]. In space, the mighty [[Kihtor]] keep watch over the world. While their primary purpose is bringing cargo to and from space freighters, the towers are bristling with weapons and hangar bays for fighters.

==Geography==
[[Image:Lorithan_Map.png|500px|right|thumb| Map of Lorithan.]]
Lorithan has roughly 515,000,000 square kilometers of surface area. There are four continental landmasses and three oceans. Over the course of their history, the [[Lorithos]] expanded across all of the continents, then nearly destroyed their own civilization in the [[Burning Wars]].

===Jehz Modar===
Main Articles: [[Huon Jehz]] and [[Vuranct Jehz]]

The largest and most densely populated continent is [[Jehz Modar]], 40,000,000 km2 covering nearly 8% of the surface of the planet. The continent is divided into two regions by the [[Dzarning Mountain Range]].

The northern region of [[Huon Jehz]] is the only portion of the continent with cities which survived the [[Burning Wars]] with enough structures intact to make rebuilding worthwhile, and is home to a third of the planet's population. The [[Kihtor Modar]] anchor is in the ancient city of [[Hamur Hauren]], birthplace of the Huern Dynasty. [[Kihtor Modar]] and [[Hamur Hauren]] both serve as an industrial center for [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]], and the center of interstellar trade for the people of Lorithan. [[Huon Jehz]] also has the cities of [[Zysk]], [[Rehzak]], [[Oln Zaur]], and [[Plovirsk]]. Each city is domed to protect the population from the global nuclear winter and toxic pools in the region. Recovery efforts so far have allowed the cities to expand their coverage to several dozen square kilometers, but the citizens still face a bleak future for many generations to come. The dark remains of the [[Talire Hauren]] is also in the [[Huon Jehz]] region, a macabre memorial to the old ways from before the [[Burning Wars]].

The much larger southern region of [[Vuranct Jehz]] is a bleaker wasteland, where the bulk of the [[Burning Wars]]' battles took place. Most of the ground is glassed by nuclear explosions and covered in toxic fog. The particles in the air keep the air dark and unbreathable. There are no major cities in the [[Vuranct Jehz|Vuranct]] region: none survived the war as more than rubble. A few brave explorers venture into the desolated ruins in search of underground shelters containing the remains of the ancient technology and archives of the [[Huern Drastar]], but few return alive, and fewer return with anything for their troubles. There are two recovery missions in the [[Vuranct Jehz|Vuranct]] region: the Perati Dome run by the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], and the Jamestown Dome, founded by the [[United Earth Alliance]] and taken over by the [[Colonial Commonwealth]].

===Nihru===
Main Article: [[Nihru]]

The northern polar continent is [[Nihru]], and is the home of the [[Kihtor Terok]] Hightower, which regulates communications for the entirety of [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] and is the central hub of the Ansible network. [[Nihru]] is unpopulated except for those who live within [[Kihtor Terok]].

===Ihjar===
Main Article: [[Ihjar]]

Aside from [[Jehz Modar]], there are two other populated continents on Lorithan. [[Ihjar]] is relatively mild compared to the other continents: a small series of volcanic vents heat the region and give Lorithan's power grid a much needed boost with geothermal energy. The city of Hovnuk serves as the sole population center of [[Ihjar]] and the anchor point for the [[Kihtor Ihjar]] Hightower. [[Ihjar]] is warm enough to allow some plant life to grow that thrives of chemosynthesis instead of photosynthesis, and is the only arable land left on the planet.

===Enwir===
Main Article: [[Enwir]]

[[Enwir]] is in the southern hemisphere of Lorithan and had three major domed cities.

Yorak Vel was a stronghold of [[Torm Krelbine]], one of the would-be successors to the Huern Dynasty, in the last years of the [[Burning Wars]]. The dome of Yorak Vel cracked during one of the final ground battles before the [[Torm Krelbine|Krelbine]] and Huern fleets took the war to orbit. Yorak Vel is no longer inhabitable, but is frequently visited by hopeful scavengers.

Aci Devar is the only population center on [[Enwir]], and the anchor for the [[Kihtor Enwir]] Hightower.

The southern city of Tharek was a major haven for many years, during and after the [[Burning Wars]]. At the height of its use as a refuge, over 600,000 [[Lorithos]] lived under its dome. On October 23, [[2301]], the dome cracked during a particularly brutal dust storm. Of the thousands of citizens, only 600 survived the harsh cross-continent trek to Aci Devar.

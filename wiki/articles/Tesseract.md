The '''Tesseract Drive''' is one of the strangest pieces of technology in the known universe. It is an extension of [[hyperspace|Hyperspace Theory]], which is used by most civilizations for their faster-than-light drive technology.

[[Hyperspace]] is a dimension where light moves much, much faster than it does in our own. It is also spatially finite--smaller than our own universe. Because of this high light speed and the compact nature of the [[hyperspace]] universe, ships can make huge leaps in distance traveled in a period of time.

[[Meroniri]] scientists theorized that [[hyperspace]] has its own upper-dimension. This dimension is be tiny--probably less than an Astronomical Unit in diameter. For want of a better term, this place is called the [[Quantum Singularity]]. In the [[Quantum Singularity|Singularity]], light travels at indescribably fast rates, quintillions of times faster than light in our dimension.

Application of the theory took over a thousand years to develop. The first Tesseract jump wasn't completed until the [[Terran]] year [[2323]], when an unmanned probe was sent from [[Sepulare]] to [[Terra]] in five minutes--a trip that normally takes a week by [[Meroniri]] [[hyperspace]] drives, and months by [[Terran]] drives..

Some scientists working to expand [[hyperspace|Hyperspace Theory]] believe that the [[Quantum Singularity]] actually touches many dimensions, and that parallel universes may exist and time travel may be possible. Some scientists scoff at the notion, but most believe it--but warn to avoid meddling with time and other universes, if they do exist.

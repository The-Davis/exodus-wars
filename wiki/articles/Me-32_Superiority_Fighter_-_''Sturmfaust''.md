The modern Sturmfaust is very diferent from its earliest incarnation. The Sturmfaust was first built in very limited numbers by the [[Mesarthrim Resistance|Resistance]], with the earliest documented models being completed in [[2120]].  It was officially dubbed 'Der Mesarthrim Kämpfer' <small>(the Mesarthrim fighter)</small> in [[2260]] when the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] became an official governing entity.

===History===
After almost two decades of hit and fade raids against the [[Anderung]], the [[Mesarthrim Resistance|Resistance]] began to alter their tactics for more sustained attacks, and needed to provide air cover for their assaults. In [[2120]], two men began working in secret to design a fighter for the [[Mesarthrim Resistance|Resistance]].  The chief designer was Felix Weissmuller von Duerr. His partner, a [[Mesarnak]] by the name of Terrance Watson, began smuggling components and materials out of [[Anderung]] storehouses.

The original Sturmfaust came in two "kits". One was merely a complete blueprint and instruction manual for building a Sturmfaust from bare components. The other actually came with all of the needed components. As the [[Mesarthrim Resistance|Resistance]] grew in numbers and power, their demand for air power increased to the point where the designers accepted the increased risk of discovery and began to ship many kits with many pre-assembled components.

In [[2132]], the Me-P088 Sturmfaust was released. The P088 was the first fully assembled version of the Sturmfaust. Felix and Terrance hired a full assembly staff, and became [[Mesarbauer]], the first [[Mesarthrim]] military contractor, though they still had to remain hidden from the [[Anderung]].  The only pre-assembled Sturmfausts were the short-lived Me-P088 through Me-P100 series.  In [[2140]], [[Mesarbauer]] switched back to only making Sturmfaust kits and their assembly team switched focus to building [[:Category:Warships|warships]].

The ''P''-series Sturmfausts were built up until [[2261]], with a run of over 500 variants. Only the [[Orillian Art and History Museum]] on [[Caledon]] has a complete collection of models of all 565 ''P''-series variants.

In [[2261]], [[Mesarbauer]] officially incorporated under the laws of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and began producing the first official production version of the Sturmfaust, the Me-30.

===Development===

During [[2262]], [[Mesarbauer]] moved it's manufacturing plant into one of the ''safe zones'', and active development and construction began on the Sturmfaust.  The first Sturmfaust design team consisted of; [[Albrecht Adler von Stadt]], [[Evelyn Loewe]], [[Fabian Unger von Gottschalk]], [[Gunda Thalberg von Tischler]] of [[Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe]], and [[Helmut Weber]] of the newly created [[Strauss Energie]].

The ''P'' series was taken back to the drawing board, and rebuilt from the ground up.  [[Strauss Energie]] had reworked a captured [[Anderung]] power generator, so the new Sturmfaust design was built around the extremely large - but powerful - generator.  In addition to the generator, [[Strauss Energie]] provided several large shield generators.  This gave the first Sturmfaust a large, body, and severely limited its maneuverability, and increased its overall profile.  The [[Anderung]] were able to capitalize on the design flaws, and most [[Mesarthrim]] pilots reverted back to using ''P'' series Sturmfaust.  Fighting with a balance between sheer size of the vehicle, and the energy requirements was a constant battle for the Me-30 version of the Sturmfaust.  During it's entire run, demand for the old ''P'' series eventually killed the Me-30 after only three major revisions.

In [[2275]], the Me-31 was introduced by [[Mesarbauer]] at the [[Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe]] developer convention in the [[Willim]] safe zone.  A squadron of fifteen fighters were sent on a raid against a [[Anderung]] ship in high orbit.  The astonishing opening attack, stunned the attendees, and the Me-31 earned its place in history.  The production run of the Me-31 went from [[2275]] to [[2322]].  The Me-31 became the most reconizable [[Mesarthrim]] fighter in the [[Orion Arm]], and the most obvious display of [[Mesarthrim]] influence in a battle.  However, in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], almost 85% of the Me-31 models were destroyed.

In [[2322]], [[Mesarbauer]] and [[Mesar Schnittstelle Gruppe]] unveiled their joint development project, the Me-32.  The newly streamlined fighter was more agile, faster, and had better Human-Computer Interactions.  The Me-32 began production in earnest in [[2323]] and flew its first sorties over [[Terra]] in the Onyx Civil War.  It's success became rapidly apparent as it vastly outpreformed most Onyx models it was flying against on [[Terra]].  In '''23XX''', the Me-32 factory was captured by the [[United Centusi States]], and the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] was left without a renewable source of fighters.

===Design===

[[Image:Me32Patton2.jpg|left|200px]]
The Sturmfaust was designed to be a superior fighter to the more numerious Anderung fighters.  At the same time however, the original design was limited by the need to allow anybody to build it from parts they had available, or from the kits.  As such, the ''P-Series'' Sturmfaust, was anything but a superior fighter, however, the [[Mesarthrim]] did have superior tactics, and the rugged Sturmfaust didn't dominate the skies, but still its presence was felt.

After the [[Anderung]] pyrrhic victory at the [[Battle of Ice Crown Bay|Schlacht der Eis-Krone Bucht]], and the subsiquent signing of the [[Treaty of Uber-Generals]] and the formation of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], in [[2260]], the Sturmfaust was named 'Der Mesarthrim Kämpfer,' or 'The Mesarthrim Fighter.'  This gave birth to the gangly Me-30, and later its graceful sucessor, the Me-31.

In [[2322]], after joining the [[Alliance of Planets]], [[Uber-General]] [[Andreas Tischler]] requested work begin on updating the Sturmfaust to field against the [[Onyx Empire]].  In only two months, they had chosen their final design for the Me-32, and began production.  The Me-32 couldn't be built in large quanities at first because the new fighter required a level of complexity and precision that the facatories didn't have yet.  By January, [[2323]], all [[Mesarbauer]] manufacturing plants had been converted over to building the Me-32.

The Me-32 Sturmfaust is designed with an armament that balances the tough Type-C Blazers and eight Medium Ordinance Hardpoints, on a medium air frame.  Coupled with its highly efficent reactor and a powerful, impressive engine array, the Sturmfaust is one of the quickest, most maneuverable, and fiercest fighters in the Orion Arm.

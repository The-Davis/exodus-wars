The '''Republic of the Philippines''' was an island nation located in Southeast Asia, with Manila as its capital city. The Philippine Archipelago comprises 7,107 islands in the western Pacific Ocean. The country reflects diverse indigenous Austronesian cultures from its many islands, as well as European and American influence from [[Spain (Terra)|Spain]], Latin America and the [[United States (Terra)|United States]].

As a former Spanish and United States colony, the Philippines had many affinities with the Western. Roman Catholicism was the predominant religion, and Filipino and English were the official languages.

The Philippines were dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] on August 1, [[2014]], after the Philippines congress passed a law ceding its power to the world government.

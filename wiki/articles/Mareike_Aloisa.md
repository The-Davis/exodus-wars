'''Mareike Aloisa'''
*Place of Birth: [[Mesar]]
*Allegiance: [[United Centusi States]]

''Mareike Aloisa'' is a veteran of the [[Mesarthrim]] military. She fought in the rebellion against the [[Anderung]], and in the [[Second Exodus War]] for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. After the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], Aloisa joined the [[United Centusi States]]. She fought several instrumental battles in the [[Tempest War]].

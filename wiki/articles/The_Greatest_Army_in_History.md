'''The Greatest Army in History''' - by [[James Davis (author)|James Davis]]

February 16, [[2300]] - [[Alpha Centauri System]]

== Alpha Centauri Approach - 1400 Hours ==

Silent is the void, and solemn is the fleet that travels the eternal night. For two hundred years they have continued their exodus, protecting their cargo of exiles.

The Jerome Black, a symbol of power and authority leads the procession. The Bahari Shela, derelict, alien and mysterious is towed behind. The Iron Hand, malevolent and dark. Ten more colony vessels, each a kilometer long and full of passengers.

"We have come back," Prism solemnly intones. "Perhaps after these long centuries we can begin healing the breach between the people of Earth and the people of Torimur."

"You are too naive, Prism." says Arthur. "I don't think Lucius Black intends any such thing."

"And you are too suspicious, Arthur." says Professor Hlow. "I trust Lucius. He gave me his word, and I gave him mine. Begin the wakeup sequences."

"Already done," says Prism. "I started it up on schedule, as soon as we came within ten light hours of the outermost planet."

"I still say this will end badly." Arthur growls. "Why the infants? How many are there now?"

"Let me think, let me think," Hlow fusses. "We just inseminated the latest run a week ago, so that brings us up to...goodness...six hundred million."

"Quite a substantial number to rebuild the Torimur colony on a new world. And it would be impossible to bring them out of their artificial wombs aboard these vessels. While they're alive, anyway." Arthur's voice gets even more solemn. "And you still don't know what happened to the defective test run. Three thousand infants don't just disappear, especially not on starships."

"Yes, I still need to question Lavik Nighthawk more thoroughly on that matter." Hlow sighs. "Most disturbing. And sad, as I'm almost certain that wherever they are, they're dead. It's been years since the Bahari Shela incident."

"Do it soon," demands Arthur. "I still can't believe that Luna is dead. We're harder to kill than that."

== The Dark Sanctuary - The Iron Hand - 1600 Hours ==

The heart of the Iron Hand is the haven of Lucius Black. The room is enormous, considering its location in the heart of a starship. Even a vessel as large as the Iron Hand has space limitations, but the leader of the Torimur exiles has afforded himself the dubious comfort of spacious, cold, dark quarters. This is the Dark Sanctuary

The heart of the Dark Sanctuary is a strange structure--a spire running from ceiling to floor, with a bulbous cocoon-like edifice near the floor. Medical equipment is built into the base of the spire, apparently meant to monitor an inhabitant. It is all silent as a grave--a fitting sentiment, for the sole occupant of the cocoon is long dead.

Lucius Black sits, silent in the dark, listening. Any moment now, he thinks. She'll make a mistake, she always does. Another moment passes. There. "Stop now, Amelia. I heard you. Turn on your light."

A tiny halo lights up the room, revealing a small girl standing less than a meter from Lucius. She is unnaturally wiry, built more like a career soldier than a child of her age. "Next time, father. I've nearly perfected my stealth." Despite the salutation, there is no affection in her voice. There is no love between Amelia and Lucius Black, only cold authority and professionalism. Her eyes reflect a grim look, like one who has witnessed things beyond mortal ken.

"I agree. Like your strength training, your stealth is nearly perfect. You will begin your next lesson soon, L--Amelia." His slip of the tongue is lost on the child, but he bites his lip all the same. "You may rest for six hours."

== The Infernal Machine - The Iron Hand - 1630 Hours ==

"You will cooperate with the will of the Empire, for the good of the Empire and your own survival. Resistance will be punished. Cooperation will be rewarded. The Empire is all. Lucius Black is the Empire. Submit to the Empire." The recording is Lavik Nighthawk's deep baritone voice, but the professional assassin lost interest in the brainwashing days before.

Strapped to dozens of chairs and hooked to hundreds of tubes and wires are Ngrligru. The survivors of the disastrous sabotage of the Bahari Shela were captured alive, by chance, and have been subjected to horrible torture at the hands of Lavik and Jarek Nighthawk. Nearly a thousand Ngrligru are now loyal to Lucius Black and the coming Imperial reign, but these stubborn holdouts remain defiant.

Jarek stops the recording and addresses the most ardent of Ngrligru. "What do you have to gain by resisting any more?" he mocks. "Your ship is in our possession. Your crew has abandoned you. You have nothing left."

Grolk Kyhra does not speak, his head bowed in shame. Nearly a century ago, his battle group was infiltrated and sabotaged by a rival clan, and his crippled flagship was found adrift by the Torimur exiles. His crew had put up a stubborn resistance, but were eventually overwhelmed by a paralyzing neurotoxin released into the water that fills the corridor of every Ngrligru vessel.

His will has been shattered by months of torture, drugs, and indoctrination. The last walls of resistance fall away, and like so many of his crew before him, Grolk Kyhra yields.

== Jerome Black - Bridge - 1800 Hours ==

"Sir, incoming message from the Iron Hand." Lieutenant Scott Crosby reports. "It's Lucius Black."

Captain Robert Marshall nods. "Let's hear what he wants then." The face of Lucius Black fills the warship's main view screen. "Yes sir, what can we do for you?"

"Captain Marshall, there's been a change in plans. Before we make contact with the people of Alpha Centauri, I want a scout ship prepared for Lavik Nighthawk."

"Sir, wouldn't Emil Torsc be a better pilot for a scouting mission?"

"Not for this one." Black smiles. "Just follow orders, Captain. And prepare to slow your vessel. At my orders, Admiral Hadrian will be slowing the fleet's approach until we hear from the scout."

== Alpha Centauri Communications Transmitter - 2300 Hours ==

Lavik Nighthawk's ship floats silently beside the huge transmission system, waiting patiently for its pilot while he works in vacuum.

For centuries, communications have been limited to light speed transmission methods. The United Earth Alliance uses lasers for line-of-sight coded transmissions, and radio for regular transmissions. To bridge the vast gulf of space, they must be transmitted through hyperspace. This facility is responsible for linking Alpha Centauri to Terra.

For hours, the skilled agent of Lucius Black plies his trade, adding on several new hardware components, avoiding detection through cunning techniques. Finally, the work is done.

== The Jerome Black - 2/17/2300 - 1000 Hours ==

"Captain Marshall, the report from Lavik Nighthawk is in. My suspicions are confirmed. Terra is still under the totalitarian rule of the United Earth Alliance. Just this morning, the people of Alpha Centauri have been given orders to put their entire colony under strict martial law. It appears that we have arrived just in time."

Marshall blinks incredulously. "Sir?"

Lucius Black shakes his head quickly. "No time for questions or explanations, Captain. Your ship is the best show of force we have. We are going to liberate this colony from the United Earth Alliance, and use it as a first step in securing peace. Move into orbit, keep your running lights as bright as they can possibly be, and broadcast this message on all frequencies." Marshall's console beeps to confirm receipt of Black's message for Alpha Centauri.

"Very well sir." Marshall nods. "I hoped we'd be able to come home to Terra in peace."

"Perhaps not Earth, but this will do for now." Black sighs. "Once they hear what we have to say, they will welcome us with open arms." He almost ends the transmission, but thinks better of it and continues. "Captain Marshall, there's one more thing. I'm making Admiral Hadrian the supreme commander of our armed forces, such as they are. I suspect that Terra will respond with violence once we liberate Alpha Centauri. I need a second for Hadrian, someone to command the naval forces. That will be you, Admiral Marshall."

== Alpha Centauri - 1300 Hours ==

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The population of Alpha Centauri is in total confusion. The order for martial law had come in the dead of night, without preamble or reason. With it was a crippling levy of new taxes, totalitarian laws, and the cruel order for the execution of several key politicians.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Protests had been sent back to Terra, demanding explanation and a countermanding of the decree. There was no response.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Lavik Nighthawk]] knew his work well. The orders were pure fiction, created by his own devices. The protests went unanswered because no transmission would go through to Terra that he did not approve.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Now it was time for the terrorist arm of Lucius Black to do its deadly work.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two elite units were sent ahead of the main fleet twenty years ago. They had worked their way into Alpha Centauri society, quietly slipping in with the normal space traffic and assuming roles throughout the system. One was even a regional governor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the long-awaited arrival of the Torimur fleet imminent, they went to war.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First was Alpha Squadron. These clever speakers wheedled and cajoled their way into positions of influence, infecting the population with poisonous words against the United Earth Alliance. Baseless accusations were made over the years, and the agents heralded the order for martial law as the long-awaited proof of their claims: The United Earth Alliance was corrupt.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Second was Omega Squadron. This deadly unit was invisible in Alpha Centauri society. Its cruel mission was much simpler. Wait for the martial law decree, and then assassinate anyone who could prove that it was a false order or who would speak with a voice of reason. Within hours, nearly a thousand Alpha Centauri colonists were quietly assassinated.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With scarcely half a day gone by, the planet was in a state of panic. Then the Jerome Black entered Alpha Centauri orbit.

==Alpha Centauri - The New Imperial Palace - February 27, 2300 - 1700 Hours==
     In the end, no one could blame the people of Alpha Centauri for their actions. Cut off from Terra, placed unfairly under martial law, and suddenly met by mysterious benefactors with military power.
     Lucius Black's message was simple and fair. Accept our rule and our protection until this business with Terra can be sorted out. We will countermand the United Earth Alliance's martial law decree by force, and protect you from retribution.
     Within ten days, things have settled down. Lucius Black claims the title of Emperor, and begins a vigorous program of military build-up.
     At first, there is some concern from the population at the level of military spending, but Emperor Black manages to cut the tax rate significantly by eliminating welfare spending entirely. Thousands of malcontents protest, but are quietly eliminated by Omega Squadron.
     Soon, with the parasite class eliminated and the Empire only spending a tiny portion of the Gross Star Product on military projects, the economy takes off. Civil contentment is at a record high, and the standard of living is phenomenal.
     The only cost is each family is required to raise dozens children until their sixth year. With money to burn in surplus, nobody minds.

==The Infernal Machine - Six Years Later==
     Millions of children sit in rapt attention to the droning voice as it speaks.
     "You are soldiers.
     "A soldier does not speak to civilians. A soldier does not speak to his superiors unless spoken to.
     "A soldier shows no mercy. Mercy is weakness, and weakness is death.
     "The Royal Imperial Army is your family. The chain of command is the head of your family.
     "A soldier is most happy when following orders. Follow the orders of the chain of command.
     "Death is your pastime. A soldier loves to kill. You will not hesitate when ordered to kill."
     Lucius Black and Anthony Hadrian watch from a balcony looking down on the horde below them. Both frown, thinking intently.
     "I can't say that I entirely approve of this, Your Majesty." says Hadrian. "We need an army, but I want one that thinks."
     "It will learn to think." Lucius Black assures him. "But I don't understand why you wanted me to change the indoctrination protocol to make them loyal to the chain of command. This is my army, it should be loyal to me."
     "Because you are not going to be commanding them in battle." Hadrian explains. "If their loyalty is only to the Emperor, they will not obey their commanders. You are the head of the chain of command, so they will obey you without question, but they must also obey lower links in the chain if they're going to function properly."
     "Very well," Black nods. "It will be ready when the time comes to make our presence felt. It will be the greatest army in history, and we will topple the United Earth Alliance."
     "Then there will be peace?" Hadrian asks.
     "Yes, Admiral. Then there will be peace." Lucius Black waves him away, and Hadrian leaves the oversight of the project to the Emperor. Black continues to mutter, under his breath. "They will know the peace of the warrior."

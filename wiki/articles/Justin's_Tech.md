'''"A generic, all-purpose weapon, mostly for ground stuff."'''
1.  XASER - An XRAY Burst LASER that's very efficient.  Fries personelle and
can do serious damage to armor and systems.  Has great penetration, and can
actually pierce thorough multiple targets.

'''"A more powerful type of generic weapon, all-purpose for vehicles."'''
2.  GRASER - A Gamma Ray LASER (Gamma Rays Amplified by Stimulated Emmission
of Radiation).  This is too big to be handheld, but small versions can be
fit onto larger tanks and mechs.

'''"A weapon similar to #2, but a bit more powerful for use on space
fighters."'''
3.  Spacemounted GRASER - Much more powerful than the ground-battle version,
and able to punch through weak shielding, and severely damage strong ones.
It fires about 60 rounds per minute, very powerful bursts indeed.  The
smaller version for ground use can be used as anti-starfighter, and can be
made to fire 600 RPM.

'''"More powerful, energy-based, ground-weapon."'''
4.  Anti-Meson Beam - Mesons are the particles which "glue" nuclei together,
even though there's all these positively charged particles in them.  An
anti-meson beam, upon contacting nuclei, causes them to explode into an
energetic shower of particles, thus destroying armor and flooding internal
systems with radiation.  This weapon has no effect against shielding (except
plasma-shielding which uses anything heavier than hydrogen as the plasma, in
which case it disrupts it severely).  It has great penetration, and is
easiily focussed.  Very good for destroying structures as well, as it will
punch holes through buildings, and kill the occupants at the same time, all
while leaving most of it relatively intact.

'''"Projectile weapon."''' - Utilized as [[Kolaspar Gun]]
5.  Collapsar Gun - Fires tiny pieces of collapsed matter at relativistic
speeds.  Ammunition based, as it requires the more plentiful type of exotic
matter for ammunition.  Exotic matter is compressed into a tiny pellet and
then accellerated at near-light speeds.  While the amount of matter is
small, the energy put into it is so great, and its cross section so tiny,
that it will pierce through light armor, and explode as it expands, usually
within the target itself, thus causing massive system failures.  In thicker
armor, it explodes while partway inside the armor itself, thus causing a
vvery weak spot to form, which can then be taken care of by an explosive
weapon, or XASER burst.

'''"A warhead to mount on my missles that would make them general purpose for
destroying things in space and atmosphere."'''
6.  MicroFusion Warhead - Like a fusion nuke, only smaller.  Little fallout,
very clean but creates large amounts of neutron flux and an EMP, not to
mention a decent explosion.  Size of conventional warheads, but much worse.
*NOT* the same as full-size nukes.

'''"A shielding system."''' - Utilized as Reigen [[Strålkransköld Composite Shielding]]
7.  Highly Integrated Composite Shielding System - (or maybe Compossite
Shielding System, or the like)  It is a multi-layer, catch-all shielding
system which is designed to be a beast.  Its main strength is its ability to
disrupt incoming weapons before actually taking damage, thus making it more
efficient.  The shield is composed of the following layers:
1.  Optical Density Screen - The Optical Density of the vaccuum is
modified in a very thin region.  Optical density refers to how slowed down
light is by moving through an object.  This could be something like glass in
a lense, etc.  Anyways, due to its micro-lumpy shape, it acts like a weird
lense which diffuses incoming beams, thus robbing them of their energy
density.  It does not actually take away energy from a laser beam, however
it disorganizes it and spreads it out somewhat to be easier to deal with.
Kinda like turning an armor-piercing shell into a normal shell.  Same amount
of impact energy, but lost its special bite.  Also, because it has such a
high optical density, any very high speed particles which enter the shield
(i.e. ion cannons, proton cannons, relativistic mass drivers, etc) dissapate
a large portion of their energy in Cerenkov radiation (because they are
going faster than the speed of light though the shield layer).  It's almost
the same thing as a sonic boom, only it produces a mostly harmless burst of
light.
Also, near misses are harmlessly reflected back into space.  Little to
no damage is done in this process.  Only direct hits get through this layer
very well.
2.  Electromagnetically Pumped Phased Plasma shield, or EP3.  The EP3 is
designed to absorb and reflect large quantities of photonic energy
(light-based weapons like lasers, masers, etc).  Plasma-based shields have
little or no effect on projectiles, however charge-based particle weapons
(ion/proton cannons, etc) are signifigantly disrupted by the plasma
shielding, as it is essentially a superconducting system.  This means that
the charge from those weapons will be dispersed evenly accross the shield
parimeter, until it can be neutralized.  Also acts as shielding against
things like EMP, and even helps break up radiation from a nearby nuke.
3.  Enhanced Shield Barrier - Similar to the shielding used by other
navies, but enhanced to be more efficient, and stronger (thus harder to
pierce) shields.  Their frequency and phase charactoristics can be modified
to balance rayshielding and partical shielding (for energy and mass weapons
respectively).  Basically anything that gets throught he first two layers
(which will most likely be signifigantly weakened) is stopped by this, up to
its critical damage limit.
In short, this is DA BOMB when it comes to shielding.  The best thing is
that the Optical Density Screen still works when your shields are down, thus
providing a large boost to your armor's effectiveness (this can buy several
minutes in a firefight even, depending on how much fire is directed at you).

'''"An armor system."''' - Utilized as [[Reigen Järnhud Composite Armor]]
8.  I have yet to come up with a cool name for it, but I have an idea, it's
a composite armor system.  Here are the different components:
1.  Ablative layer - Lasers and other optical weapons cause this thin
layer to vaporize in a tiny area, creating a plasma that deflects the shot.
2.  Primary Armor - Ultra-strong, nano-formed composite/alloy.  Similar
to what others use for armor, but slightly stronger and more robust.  Easy
to repair on the field with nanobots, during non-combat situations.
3.  Nuclear-spin-locked collapsed matter substrate - weblike structure
which reinforces the armor.  Although it makes up only 10% of the total
mass, it absorbs 95% of most physical impacts, and helps to evenly
distribute heat (ultra-fast heat conduction).  It is very difficult to
destroy compared to the alloy, but certainly not invincible.
4.  Superconducting endo-shield.  Multiple layers of energized
superconducting materials deflect magnetic fields and pulses, like EMP, and
MASER weapons.  Not very strong or tough, but it works against electronic
weapons and some forms of radiation.
Basically this is a lot better than normal armor, but not like 10x
better, maybe just like 3x.

'''"A big, mega-anti-capship gun for use on big starships."''' - Utilized as [[Forros Gun]]
9.  Magneto-Gravatic Wave Gun - Fires photons with high enough energy that
they are actually carriers of the Unified Force.  High damage and *VERY*
high rates of shield and armor piercing.  They have a disrupting effect on
matter which is in their way, and on most energy barriers.  This is not
designed to be a brute-force weapon so much as a smart weapon (harder to
deflect rather than simply more energy to absorb).

'''"A general purpose, starship mounted, anti-captial ship weapon. A single
gun turret (the lever on my capship models). Not powerful enough to destroy
ships instantly, but good enough to deploy on all ships in the fleet."''' - Utilized as [[Hälregn Pulse Cannon]]
10.  Aggrigated Fusion Pulse Cannon - like I said before, it is a
multi-stage contained fusion reactor which uses magnetic and gravatic fields
to focus and collimate the outgoing weapons fire.  Fires energetic plasma,
neutrons, and high-energy photons at an enemy, and no matter how well their
shields are tuned, they will not be able to completely optimise themselves
for the weapons fire, as it is too diverse.  Smaller versions could be used
against fighters.

'''"An anti-capital ship warhead carried on capital ships."''' - Utilized for Reigen missile warheads
11.  Antimatter Catalysed Fusion/Fission/Fusion rounds - Several energetic
antiproton beams are injected into a tightly-packed pellet of metallic
hydrogen encased in depleted uranium.  The fusion reaction produces
energetic neutrons which cause the depleted uranium to fission, and then the
resulting neutrons and blast from that fissioning recompresses the
now-helium core causing it to fusion again, producing additional blasts.
This is the size of a normal nuclear warhead, but signifigantly better.

'''"A weapon most useful for infantry, or light battle armor."'''
12. Burst MASER (Microwaves Amplified by Stimulated Emission of Radiation) -
US government is trying to use these things to disrupt missiles.  Does a
number on electronics, and fries enemy humans.  Does nothing to optronic
systems, but disrupts electronic systems very well.

The [[Onyx Empire]] had dozens of worlds, and a wide range of diverse cultures on their worlds. Most of them were acquired through conquest, but a few were colonization projects started by Imperial Decree.

Early Conquests:
* [[Alpha Centauri System]]: [[Ah Kin]] - Conquered in [[2300]].
* [[Sol System]]: [[Earth]], [[Luna]], [[Venus]] and [[Mars]] - Conquered in [[2315]].
* [[Lyrae System]]: [[Holt]] - Conquered in [[2315]].
* [[Theta Algedi System]]: [[Tejat]] - Conquered in [[2315]].
* [[Proxima Procyon System]]: [[Bethija]] - Conquered in [[2315]].
* [[Tau Ceti System]]: [[Kalmar]] - Conquered in [[2316]].

Later Conquests:
* [[Lupei System]]: [[Kalidasa]] - Conquered in [[2317]].
* [[Oronus System]]: [[Remut]] - Conquered in [[2317]].
* [[Epsilon Indi System]]: [[Zardos]] - Conquered in [[2318]], subsequently destroyed.
* [[Gamma Algedi System]]: [[Sindh]] and [[Pesht]] - Conquered in [[2318]].
* [[Beta Arae System]]: [[Laojiu]] and [[Miyada]] - Conquered in [[2319]].
* [[Avior System]]: [[Casere]] and [[Labrea]]
* [[Mimosa System]]: [[Tavou]], [[Leskovik]]
* [[Omicron Carinae System]]: [[Adelson]] and [[Kufestin]]
* [[Zeta Algedi System]]: [[Anilam]] and [[Canopus]]
* [[Antares System]]: [[Albanus]] and [[Jaipur]]
* [[Mu Crucis System]]: [[Novaya]] and [[Calpaca]]
* [[Xi Herculis System]]: [[Ward]], [[Bolan]], and [[Crofton]]
* [[Des System]]: [[Des Wei]] and [[Des Arc]]

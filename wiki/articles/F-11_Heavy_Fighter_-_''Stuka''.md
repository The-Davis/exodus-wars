=== Overview ===

By the time the Prefecture modernized and overhauled its military in 2325, the Stuka's place in the new Air Force was not even questioned.  Sky Marshal Jan Brooks insisted on several upgrades to the model to keep it on par with rapidly advancing technology.

Incredible enhancements were made to thrust systems and inertia compensation systems, allowing Stukas and other Prefecture fighters to accelerate at rates leaps and bounds above those they were capable of just months earlier.  The Stuka remains a monstrosity of power, and is a deadly fighter. 

[[Image:F-11 Stuka Front View.png|200px]]

=== Capabilities ===

The Stuka's armament has been laser based in the past, but is now based on the powerful Neutron Cannons.  These models are very versatile, with three modes of fire.  Most pilots prefer "Intense Slow Mode," which is very similar to the old laser weapon in its damage profile and firing patterns.  The wise pilots will use "Flood" mode when dealing with swarms of smaller targets like the Bugger "Shredder Drones."

The new missile systems have replaced both the long-range and short-range launchers of the original Stuka.  A new guidance system was installed in the 2325 overhaul.  The "Eyes Everywhere" missile flight control system can track up to a thousand targets, and prioritize missile ordinance based on threat to both the Stuka itself and the mission objectives.  Reloading missiles takes only a few seconds on the ground with a competent air crew.

The latest addition to the Stuka's armament is a pair of Hephaestus cannons, a powerful strafing and anti-bomber weapon.  At close range, the heavy projectiles can punch through shielding and armor, though they quickly lose their effectiveness at distances over a kilometer.

Several new systems have been added to the Stuka's defenses.  In addition to its plasma shields and composite hull, a new magnetic screen system was finally mated to the hull design.  Early versions of the magnetic shield wouldn't work with the Stuka's shape.

In addition, a new "Angel's Wing" Electronic Countermeasure System has been designed to protect the Stuka from missile locks, and a "Last-Chance" Anti-Missile System has been added to shoot down missiles that the ECM system can't jam and the pilot can't evade.

While it still relies on its Muon Catalyzed Fusion reactor for power, the Stuka also has massive capacitors which allow it to accelerate at its incredible 700 kilometers per second per second while they are charged.  The capacitor can keep the drive system running for half an hour, and it takes the fusion reactor two hours to recharge it.  Each Stuka carries a small matter/antimatter charging system which can supercharge the capacitors for an additional ten minute charge in one second, but antimatter is hard to come by in the Prefecture and is used sparingly.

For its normal mission profile, the Stuka carries six Hound dogfighter LRMs, two Hammer MRMs, and two Rattler SRMs.  The Hounds and Rattlers are excellent for dogfighting, and the Hammers give it a heavy weapon if it runs into a target of opportunity.

[[Image:F-11 Stuka Top View.png|200px]]

When a Stuka pilot expects to meet with a number of bombers, he switches his arsenal to the anti-bomber payload.  Two Hawk MRMs, four Wren LRMs, and four Streak SRMs are the normal weapon load for fighting bombers.

Against Volucris Shredder Drone swarms, the wise Stuka pilot will swap his Streak SRMs for "Splatter" SRMs.  These nasty little weapons are good for taking out groups of small targets.  While they're designed for anti-infantry, they work well against small Bugger flyers too.

=== Deployment ===

The Prefecture Air Force was updated in 2325.  Frontline forces received the upgrades in time for the height of the Prefecture/Mesarthrim war.  All of the bugs in the system from the July, 2324 upgrade have been fixed, and Stukas can now take on swarms of Volucris without difficulty.

There is now no doubt that the Stuka will remain in the Prefecture Air Force for years to come.  The pilots still adore the fighter, and its performance has continued to improve.

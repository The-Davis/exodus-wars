The most powerful weapon in the universe is the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] Mass to Energy Conversion Bomb. Theoretically, it could destroy everything in the universe. The bomb is a catalyst for a self-powered reaction. Once the bomb goes off, it annihilates every bit of matter, converting it into more energy for the reaction. When one of the bomb goes off, stopping the reaction is impossible--it continues until it runs out of matter.

The [[Meroniri Yatisu]] tested the MEC bomb once. A rogue asteroid in deep space was completely destroyed. Testing had to be done away from any starsystems, because if the reaction hit a star, it would cause a supernova. Winning wars isn't simply a matter of firing the MEC bomb at the enemy. The weapon is worthless against most shields. Attacking an enemy fleet would result in the destruction of the Meroniri vessel firing the bomb. Most situations call for more flexible weapons like antimatter bombs and Riptide Cannons, but it's nice to have the option.

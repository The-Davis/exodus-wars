The Heer is the United Centusi States Army, which operates as the fighting ground forces of the UCS whereever they see combat. They operate a number of tanks, infantry, VTOLs, and support vehicles.

The Armies of the UCS were born in the fires of the Mesarthrim Civil War. Many of the men and officers in todays army are also veterans of the Second Exodus War, and a number are even veterans of the HalbAnderung Wars.

The most famous and dangerous of all veteran Mesarthrim troops are the Myt'gaul, the "Blood Elite" of the Mesarthrim. The Myt'gaul are the most prized soldiers anywhere, and have never lost a fight without the odds stacked heavily against them. Myt'gaul can and have gone up against enemy forces three times their size and emerged victorious.

The Heer also commands another elite, though less prestigious unit. The Myt'herra, or "elite Marines" of the Mesarthrim are responsible for defense of Kriegsmarine vessels and are usually the first forces landed in battle. The Myt'herra open the door for the rest of the Heer, so to speak.

The "average" Mesarthrim fighters are the Myt'kampfen, though they are head and shoulders above most human soldiers. Only the [[Mesarthrim]] elite, the [[Ixoarchada]] RhováDeviri, and the [[Ngrligru]] Saada'jeshi are more feared than the Myt'kampfen. They operate a highly mobile combined-arms army.

Many Mesarthrim vehicles, like the Dragoon and Dolch, are light and centered around speed more than power. Others, like the Pheasant APC, strike a balance. Perhaps the most famous of all, and a large part of the Myt'kampfen reputation, is the Vulture Seige Tank, a weapon of such enormous power that it has sent armies on the retreat from its mere presence.

The Heer also operates several support vehicles. The Gorgon is a mobile anti-aircraft SAM site, while the Triton and Buzzard act as siege weapons and artillery. For close air support, the Heer has the Sparrow and Crow VTOLs.

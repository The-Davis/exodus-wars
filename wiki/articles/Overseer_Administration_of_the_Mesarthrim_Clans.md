===History===
Formed as a government by the Anderung to administer the occupation of Mesar and the subjigation of the clans. 

===Government===
The government was mostly administrative. Logs were kept of production and taxation, also of births and deaths. The government also had a military arm, tasked with protecting the Anderung, and controlling the Mesarthrim.

===Law===
The Decrees of the Anderung became the laws of the Overseers.

===Law Enforcement===
The Overseers were the only Mesarthrim who could legally carry weapons. However, with a full blown resistance fighting this was never the case. All incarcerated individuals were turned over to the Anderung who issued punishment.

===Foreign Relations===
None.

===Military===
The Overseer maintained a Navy, a Police force, and Army units - all supplied with weapons by the Anderung. However, all their equipment was Mesarnak built.

===Intelligence===
The Overseers were quite successful at infiltrating the Rebellion, allowing for excellent intelligence. The Overseers also did some espionage against the Anderung themselves, more for curiosity sake then malicious intent.

===Transportation===
All transportation was regulated by the Overseer with their police force maintaining order, and collecting licensing tolls.

===Territory===
The Overseers were in charge of monitoring Mesarthrim on three worlds.

===Economy===
The Overseers were not paid for their work, they were however provided with food, shelter, entertainment, and rank from their Anderung masters.
 
===Demographics===
The Overseers were entirely Mesarnak, non pure blood could only serve as warriors.

===Public Health===
All public health was cared for by the Anderung Masters.

===Culture===
Much of the culture that did exist was a bastardization of Anderung beliefs and Anderung gods, therefore leaving little concern for Mesarthrim culture.

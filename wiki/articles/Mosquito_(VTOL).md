===Overview===

Blazton Industries is one of the few Terran companies currently producing non-rotor/turbine VTOL craft.  Although the Terran system utilized is not as sophisticated as the repulsor technology of the Icronians, or the anti-gravity lift systems of the Archadians or Meroniri, it works and is a purely Terran technology. 

[[Image:Mosquito1.jpg]]

Originally applied to industrial and transport craft, Blazton later introduced the lift system into the military market.  The craft was the Mosquito VTOL, and it is today used by a number of security firms, corporate militias (where allowed by law), and various paramilitary groups.  However, to this day the Mosquito has failed to take off in the highly competitive Terran military markets, as more sophisticated lift systems available through the Archadians became available shortly after the Mosquito was introduced. 

===Capabilities===

The Mosquito is a light, fast craft, but does pack a moderate punch.  A pair of Medium Neutron Beam Cannons and a Heavy Machine Gun make it effective against light and medium ground vehicles, and a pair of hardpoints allow it to carry missiles for use against heavy vehicles and aerospace craft. 

Although a very light craft, the Mosquito is meant as an attack craft, and thus is outfitted with a generous amount of Boron-Nitride armor, as well as a Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens.  

===Deployment===

Mosquitos operate in the Blazton Security Forces as the primary planetary aerial support units.  They are equally effective against static and mobile ground targets, and can also confront aerospace craft in the atmosphere, where they can best many aerospace craft designed to operate in space and planetside.  While Mosquitos can operate in space, it takes the addition of a life-support suit for the pilot, and it is not as useful as most true aerospace craft.

After the Battle of Kronheim, the Prefecture fully realized the potential for tanks in battle. The Ikronin tanks were extremely effective, scoring more kills than the Prefecture Rig forces. It was decided that a new tank would be built, and would be designed on Ikronin combat principles.

The result was the Dragon. It was so effective that six vehicles were replaced. The four tanks and two Urban Warfare Vehicles in service at the time were promptly retired in favor of the Dragon.

The mobility of the Dragon puts all earlier tanks to shame. The reactors on board have tremendous power output, more than doubling the speed from earlier tanks to 200 KPH. [[Ixoarchada]] anti-gravity technology and [[Ikronin]] repulsor technology were both studied, and then adapted to create a cushioning system. The Dragon can ride smooth on any terrain, and maneuver like a racecar in a city.

[[Image:M37Dragon2.jpg|200px]]

Weaponry is no longer mounted on turrets. The Dragon's maneuverability allows it to make tight turns, except in the closest quarters, where even turreted tanks have always been at a disadvantage regardless of weapons. The Dragon carries two G26 100mm cannons, and two anti-personnel G12 15mm 4-barrel guns.

All Prefecture tanks and direct combat vehicles have been replaced by the Dragon. Only artillery and transports have been unaffected by the development of the Dragon. Development of the Dragon began in mid 2325, after the Battle of Kronheim.  Full deployment was not completed until late 2327.

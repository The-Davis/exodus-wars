by Seth Dickinson

=====Canopus Spaceport Military Complex - Canopus - Tejat District - 0500 Hours=====
The sun creeps over the horizon, bathing Canopus Spaceport in golden radiance. Workers go about their duties, and battle-armored Inquisitor Templar patrol the perimeter with patient vigilance. The garrison of the Prefecture 19th Tejat Guard lies just outside the spaceport, a sprawling complex of barracks, bunkers, and parade grounds. Colonel Musahei Kyu finishes an eight kilometer run around the complex and heads for his command center. The soldiers at the door salute as he enters.
     
He ducks into a locker room and quickly showers and changes into his duty uniform. Years of serving on Imperial and later Prefecture troop ships have made this process take minutes. As he emerges, a young lieutenant intercepts him and proffers a PDA.
     
“Here’s your daily report sir. The entire garrison is mobilized; we’re on full alert – the UCS won’t catch us off-guard. Morale is good. We’re ready for a fight.”

Kyu takes the PDA and nods. “The Inquisitors have scheduled a briefing in seven hours. Be sure to let them in when they arrive.” He trudges through paperwork until noon, and then his Inquisitor liaison enters his office.
     
The Inquisitor is Janice Slynn, a low-level analyst, who summarizes all pertinent information for the garrison commander. “The UCS fleet outnumbers and outguns the Prefecture’s combined forces by a considerable margin. We have taken significant losses in our war against the Volucris, so we are not able to fully deploy our Fleets. Canopus is a high profile target.”
     
She pushes a map of the Canopus star system and local shipping lanes across the table towards him. “My superiors believe that the UCS will attack Canopus in the near future. Mesarthrim freighters have been spotted in the nearby shipping lanes. Until the Senate ratifies the declaration of war, we can’t just go blasting them in neutral space. We’re pretty certain that they are collecting intelligence data for an attack on Canopus.”
     
Colonel Kyu fingers the map, looking at the ominous red marks that show the known Mesarthrim fleet deployments. “I have the garrison on full alert. We’ll be ready if they do show up.”

=====Outer Canopus System, 1200 Hours=====
Colonel Kyu’s words are prophetic.
     
Dark shapes blot out the stars as forty Mesarthrim capital ships and a dozen gunships disengage their GRLD drives. Fighters swarm out of the four Davis carriers at the heart of the fleet.

Vice-Admiral Friedrich Ahrends stands on the bridge of the carrier Ravager, his flagship. “Be swift,” he reminds his officers. “The Inquisitors will receive heavy reinforcements from Admiral Baruk’s Sixth Fleet. We must land our troops, strike hard at important targets, and withdraw. A long battle will hurt us in the long run.”
     
“Scanners show only a few Prefecture vessels on patrol,” a Mesarthrim officer reports. “One Essex carrier in-system, and multiple smaller craft. One convoy of transports and haulers, outbound from the planet. The system is open to attack.”
     
Ahrends nods slowly. “Tell all ships to be on their guard. If we are swift and lethal, we can accomplish our objectives with minimum losses.” He fingers his uniform collar, remembering an old military adage. No battle plan survives contact with the enemy.

=====Canopus Spaceport Military Complex, 1400 Hours=====
     
Colonel Kyu stands on the roof of his command center, watching the frenzied activity of a military base on full alert. Swift Skyhawks and huge Beluga dropships laden with armor and infantry rest on the base tarmac, ready to lift at a moment’s notice; Rig patrols stride restlessly around the perimeter. APCs carrying officers and ammunition rattle across the parade ground, and the smell of lubricants and synthetic chemicals fill the air.
     
He hears the clang of someone ascending the stairs behind him, and half-turns. His aide salutes and clears his throat nervously. Colonel Kyu returns the salute.
     
“Sir, we have a contact,” he explains. “The Lucidity has detected a large group of vessels on an orbital-insertion vector. They’ll be able to land troops within an hour.”
     
Kyu nods, carefully concealing any sign of surprise from his face. Reluctantly, he leaves the sun and wind behind, and descends into his command bunker, sealing the stairwell behind him. The room is lit only by the dim flickering of monitors.
     
Minutes later, another report comes in. The lieutenant who delivers the report appears calm, but Kyu senses the tension in his voice. “We have confirmation of the contact, Colonel. The Inquisitors and the scout carrier Lucidity have both detected a group of 40 Mesarthrim capital ships and a dozen gunships inbound. The Lucidity has released its fighters to groundside control, and is requesting permission to depart.”
     
Kyu nods, his façade of calm nearly slipping. So many ships! The Essex carrier will be slaughtered. “Give the Lucidity permission to depart. Raise the Sixth Fleet on the Ansible. We need naval support.”
     
Canopus Spaceport is a massive city in its own right, and is the nerve center of Canopus’ largest continent. There is no question the Mesarthrim will strike here. But their primary target will be the Inquisitor’s complex in Kesan, halfway across the continent.
     
“Deploy our mobile Guard units to protect non-vital targets. I want our best troops defending the spaceport, the military infrastructure, and the Spire. Get half our available air assets off the ground and on patrol. Major Viram, send six brigades to Kesan to reinforce the Inquisitors.”
     
An alarm blares. Kyu raises an eyebrow as the list of Canopus’ orbital facilities is rapidly depopulated. “When did the Mesarthrim start attacking our satellites? They should still be at least an hour away.”
     
Major Viram curses, and reddens in embarrassment. “Sorry, sir. It looks like they sneaked in a couple squadrons of fighters ahead of their main force and attacked our geosync platforms. We’ve lost satellite coverage of most of the continent – we won’t be able to guide missiles in from over-the-horizon launch facilities.”
     
Kyu leans over the shoulder of one of his command techs and examines the situation. “Get Lucidity’s fighters after them. Launch all spaceport fighters and prepare to repel a landing force.”

=====Canopus Orbit, 1410 Hours=====
     
Admiral Ahrens knows that information is the Inquisitor’s most powerful weapon, and he has taken steps to remove their intelligence sources. A squadron of Patton fighters spearheaded his attack, targeting communications satellites and observation platforms above Khyber, Canopus’ largest continent. The destruction of the satellites didn’t blind the Prefecture forces, but it did hurt their home-field advantage.
     
Prefecture communications instantly switch over to ground-based microwave transceivers. The Piranhas of 7-1F12, a squadron of twenty-four led by Captain Bridget Reed, quickly bolts from the atmosphere to engage the Mesarthrim.
     
“Canopus Control, I have SPAR contact with one-two bogies,” Reed reports. “Confirm bandits, all UCS Patton fighters. They’ve broken up into two-ship elements and are attacking our remaining satellites.”
     
“Roger Captain. Engage. Stay out of these vectors; we’re launching missiles into those areas. Try to herd the Mesarthrim inside. Good hunting.” A string of numbers follows; Reed passes it to her squad mates.
     
“Split by elements and engage targets of opportunity,” Reed calls. “Remember – the Pattons are tougher than our ships. Engage with a two-to-one advantage.”
     
The squadron splits into eight elements, three ships each. Each element picks a Patton and fires missiles.
     
Reed scores a hit on her target and throttles up, dragging her nose left to track the Patton as it swerves. Her two wingmen climb, pursuing the other Patton as it changes course.
     
She closes under five hundred kilometers and fires three quick neutron bursts at her target. The Patton cuts in towards her, accepting the hits and hammering her with a blazer salvo. Her AI maneuvering system throws her into a port bank, which she converts into a full break to port, accelerating away from the superior fighter. The Patton settles into a tailing fire arc.
     
The other Patton spins and accelerates, blowing past her wingmen and screaming in towards Reed. “I’m being double-teamed!” she calls, trying to put some distance between herself and the two Pattons. She cuts her engines and spins the fighter, letting her momentum carry her away while she fires back at the pursuing Pattons. She manages a three-second burst of neutron fire before both Pattons zero in on her and start hammering. She slams her throttles; her fighter slows, hangs in space for a fraction of a second, and then begins accelerating towards the two Pattons. They pass her in a blur of metal and blazer fire, leaving her fighter scorched and listing but functional.
     

Her wingmen flash by her on either side, chasing the Pattons. One of the Piranhas looses an SRM, punching through one of the UCS fighter’s shields, and the two Mesarthrim pilots break away in opposite directions.
     

A transmission crackles in Reed’s cockpit. “Groundside battery Tango-two-niner is firing. Try to force them into the salvo.” 
     
“Don’t split up,” Reed warns. “Take the port-side Patton. I have the leader.”
     
She spins her fighter to bear on the Patton that broke to starboard and throttles up after it, thumbing the selector switch for one of her SRMs. It takes an agonizing few seconds to break through the Patton’s ECM, then locks on; her Piranha kicks as the missile fires.
     
The Patton breaks to port, towards the planet, and manages to shake the SRM; the Mesarthrim fighter spins so that it’s flying ‘backwards,’ and lances her Piranha with blazer fire. Reed’s ship begins to shake ominously, and alarms blare.
     
A ground-defense LRM hits the Patton, slamming it into a flat spin straight towards Reed; the powerful warhead overwhelms even UCS adaptive shields. She tracks the crippled fighter and coolly blows it to pieces. “Leader is down.”
     
Her wingmen aren’t doing so well; the lone Patton has managed to shake them, and one of the two Piranhas is heavily damaged. The other elements of her squadron are embroiled in dogfights, and three of her ships have been destroyed. Her inexperienced pilots’ baptism of fire is turning into a disaster. The squadron frequency buzzes with battle chatter.
     
“Dagger break left! He’s on-“
     
“ECM’s too heavy, I can’t get a lock-“
     
“Eat this, you white-eyed bas-“
     
“Dagger requesting assistance - left wing gone, grav-cav is fluctuating-“
     
“Keep firing, Tango-Two-Niner,” she orders grimly. “We’re going to need the support.”
     
The dogfight continues, matching Prefecture coordination and fire support against superior Mesarthrim technology, veteran skill and personal bravery. Captain Reed and her wingmates manage to finish off the remaining Patton in their target element. Reed gets another kill before a Patton blows her damaged fighter out of space; she ejects just in time.
     
Her inexperienced squadron fights on for the next ten minutes, barely managing to beat the twelve Mesarthrim fighters with the help of the groundside batteries. Only six of the Piranhas remain; twelve have been destroyed, six disabled and seven pilots are dead. Only a handful of satellites survived the engagement.
     
Reed’s escape pod is snatched up by an orbital tug, and she’s brought groundside. Medics check her over and slot her into another fighter, just in time for the main Mesarthrim force to arrive.

=====Canopus Orbit, 1450 Hours=====
The Mesarthrim strike fleet enters Canopus orbit forty minutes after the initial dogfight. Resistance is minimal; the few satellites that remain pose no threat to the heavily shielded warships.
     
Admiral Ahrens watches his fighters disable the last of the orbital platforms, and turns to his gunnery section. “Our spearhead fighters took fire from several groundside batteries. I want them all disabled.”
     Salvoes of capital-ship missiles slice into the atmosphere, silencing most of the missile installations in Khyber; only a few mobile missile launchers manage to escape the orbital bombardments. Admiral Ahrens orders his fleet to deploy their dropships.
     Two battalions of hardened Mesarthrim warriors are packed into armored dropships, and plunge into the atmosphere. Three Patton squadrons streak ahead of them, while other fighters carrying scanner packages and reconnaissance gear spread out over the continent, hoping to draw Prefecture fire and pinpoint the location of the remaining defense platforms.
     The tactic works. The remaining Prefecture missile batteries fire on the “wild weasel” fighters, downing a few. Orbital bombardment and bomber attacks swiftly put the mobile missile batteries out of action.
     Meanwhile, the Ravager and her escorts split off from the main fleet and head towards the next critical target: the Icronian Wormhole Gate leading to Tejat.

=====Canopus Spaceport Military Complex, 1530 Hours=====
     Colonel Kyu wipes sweat from his brow. The command bunker is stiflingly hot, and the outside situation doesn’t make him any more comfortable. “What assets do we have left?” he asks.
     Major Viram, Kyu’s second-in-command, glances at a console. “Eighty percent of our continental air-defense LRM sites are out of action; the rest can’t fire without being destroyed. Cape Verdigris City and Haleburg both reported orbital bombardments on the local sensor stations, and we’ve lost sensor coverage over those regions. The Kesan sensor station is intact; it’s underground and hardened, and the Canopus Spaceport sensors haven’t been attacked yet. Planetary internet nodes are being targeted by bomber strikes, so civilian communication is gone.”
     “What about fighters?”
     “The Kesan air-defense squadron, 7-1F13, has engaged Mesarthrim fighters and heavy bombers over the city. Ground support from the Inquisitors is keeping them alive, but attrition is favoring the Mesarthrim – their ships are just tougher. Captain Bridget Reed and the survivors of the Canopus Spaceport fighter command – that’s 7-1F12 - have merged with the Lucidity’s four squadrons, and are attacking a large group of Mesarthrim fighters and transports heading for Kesan. We have one more fighter squadron available; 7-1F11 was on CAP over the other continent and is inbound now to reinforce Captain Reed. All of our bombers – 7-1B21, 22, and 23 – are currently on the ground here at the spaceport, re-arming for ground support missions. The rest of our air assets are VTOLs.”
     Colonel Kyu walks to the center of the command station, where a large map of Khyber continent rests on an ancient cherry-wood table. Our obvious priority is holding off the landings at Kesan. “Every second we can hold the Mesarthrim off, Admiral Baruk gets a bit closer. Captain Reed’s delaying action has to be supported. Could we get some ‘Rigs with LRMs deployed underneath the dogfight?”
     “If we weakened the Spaceport or Kesan defense garrisons,” Viram replies.
     “Too risky,” Kyu decides. “The transport dropships might be shot down while we moved the ‘Rigs, and we can’t pull fighters away to escort them. Captain Reed will have to hold out on her own.”
     “Sir!” a technician shouts. “Spaceport sensors have detected sixteen missiles incoming – they’re on ballistic trajectories, ETA fifteen minutes!”
     “It’s taken them long enough to hit us,” Kyu says placidly. “This bunker is hardened; we should be fine. Get all the VTOLs and transports off the ground and out of the area.”
     He turns to Major Viram. “I’m putting you in command of the six brigades we’re deploying to Kesan. Get on one of those dropships, and drive the Mesarthrim out of the city.”
     “Sir, we have two brigades around the spaceport,” Major Viram says tentatively. “Won’t they be targets?”
     “Not while they’re dug in and camouflaged,” Colonel Kyu reminds him serenely. “Even the Mesarthrim can’t hit a target they can’t see. No, it’s the reactor in the spaceport that will be their target. That’s what I’m worried about.”

=====Above Khyber Continent, 1535 Hours=====
     As Mesarthrim fighters strike at targets all over Khyber, Bridget Reed’s six remaining fighters from Canopus Spaceport and four squadrons from the Lucidity intercept the Mesarthrim landing force heading for Kesan, hoping to overwhelm the Mesarthrim and end the invasion before it begins.
     Things don’t go as planned. Whoever is in command of the Mesarthrim fleet is canny; nearly six squadrons of Pattons defend the troop transports, easily outgunning the Prefecture attackers. Reed orders her fighters to close to dogfight range, hoping to punch a hole in the Mesarthrim fighter screen and allow the Valkyries to attack the transports.
     Admiral Ahrens has committed veteran squadrons to the defense of the landing force, and Reeds’ fighters meet heavy resistance. Valkyries damage several light dropships and mange to cripple a heavy transport, but they are decimated within minutes. Out of the four-and-a-half squadrons that enter the engagement, only a mismatched baker’s dozen of Prefecture fighters, mostly Piranhas, survive fifteen minutes of furious fighting.
     Bridget Reed flicks her fighter between two hulking Mesarthrim transports, hammering at them with cannon-fire. The Patton on her trail throttles down to avoid overshooting, and lances her fighter with a blazer bolt. Alarms wail.
     “I’m hit,” she calls, wincing at the negative G-forces as she plunges downwards. Thank God this is a low-velocity dogfight, or I’d be a glass crater right now.
     “Hold on, Captain, Lamprey is inbound.” Another Piranha dives in from above, surprising the Patton and forcing it to break away. Reed recovers from her dive and climbs back past ten thousand feet, towards the transports. Lamprey slots onto her wing.
     The two Piranhas rip back through the convoy, managing to split through one of the transports’ shields and breach a cargo hold. A wedge of Pattons chase after them, and one well-aimed shot punches through Lamprey’s shields and strikes her right wing. Her hardpoint SRMs blow up spectacularly. The Piranha vanishes in a ball of fire, killing Lamprey instantly.
     “Damn it,” Reed breathes, throttling up and pulling away from the transports. She thumbs open an all-ships frequency. “All fighters, pull out! Best possible speed. We’ve held up the landings as long as we can.”
     She waits until her fighter has cleared the combat zone, then swings her nose around towards Kesan and begins plotting a flight plan. The other Prefecture survivors – only a squadron’s strength - join up with her, abandoning the fight.

=====Icronian Wormhole Gate, Canopus Orbit, 1540 Hours=====
     The Ravager looms ominously above the Wormhole Gate station, settling into a matching orbit. Admiral Ahrens’ harsh voice echoes through the station’s control center. “Attention Icronian crew. Your gate is a military target and will be treated as such. Cut all power to your wormhole generators or we will open fire.”
     Johon Laakso, the Icronian commander, considers his options. Even if the Prefecture reinforcements arrive right now, the gate will be destroyed and we’ll all be killed. At least they’re giving me a chance to surrender. My first duty is to the corporation and its assets, even if I would prefer to fight.
     “We surrender,” he agrees, keeping his voice level and calm. “Engineering – cut all power to the gate generators and power down.”
     “A wise decision,” Ahrens says. “Prepare to receive boarders.” The Icronian turns to his security chief. “Svar, enact corporate protocols and destroy any sensitive information aboard. The UCS won’t get a thing out of us.”
     Pegasus commando shuttles slip from the Ravager’s bays. Within minutes, Myt'herra have secured the wormhole gate and taken the Icronian crew prisoner. The Mesarthrim begin laying charges around the gate’s reactors.

=====Canopus Spaceport Military Complex, Command Bunker, 1545 Hours=====
     “Missile impact in thirty seconds,” a technician warns.
     Colonel Kyu greets the news with a composed nod. “Still no sign of a landing force to follow up the bombardment?”
     “No, sir.”
     “Excellent. Do we have everything off the ground?”
     “All our VTOLs and aircraft are up and out of the way; our three Bloodhawk squadrons took as much armament as they could with them. We’re executing a step-down and bleed on the main reactor, but there’s a good chance the missiles will hit while it’s still hot.
     “The bunkers are packed full, but I think we got everyone off the surface. The dropships heading for Kesan just lifted; they have six brigades and Major Viram with them. They should be at Kesan in two hours. Our remaining troops are still camouflaged around the perimeter.”
     Kyu glances up at the ceiling. “Then we wait.”
     The Mesarthrim missiles strike seconds later. The orbital-bombardment missiles, loaded with thousands of kilograms of advanced chemical explosive, strike at many times the speed of sound. Several missiles strike communications and sensor arrays across the spaceport, throwing up gigantic fireballs and plumes of debris; others target the 19th Guard’s compound, shattering the empty hangars and barracks.
     Two special ordinance warheads dump several hundred thousand gallons of hydrocarbons over the city, coating the buildings in a slick mess. The primitive fuels instantly ignite, and Canopus Spaceport turns into a raging inferno.
     A bunker-buster warhead strikes the center of the military compound just outside the spaceport, shattering two underground bunkers and killing hundreds of Prefecture soldiers. Most of the hardened bunkers survive the impact, but the command bunker collapses around Colonel Kyu, and darkness takes him.


=====Above Kesan, 1610 Hours=====
     The UCS landing force and its escort fighters arrive at Kesan and greet the defenders with a wave of missiles. A dogfight has been raging over the city for nearly an hour, as the outnumbered 7-1F-13 squadron and SAM sites struggle against waves of Sanburns. The UCS fighters have pinpointed the location of nearly every SAM site and sensor installation in the city.
     The six squadrons of Pattons escorting the landing force join the attack, scattering the Prefecture fighters, and winning air superiority.
     Bombers target the SAM sites and sensors, quickly silencing them with precision missile strikes.
     Admiral Ahrens has given clear orders: collateral damage is acceptable, but don’t flatten the Spire of Truth. Capture as much information and as many personnel as possible.
     The UCS landing force splits into two groups. The main group of heavy dropships touches down on the plains south of Kesan, near local Highway 19. Four brigades of tanks and APCs roll out and move towards the city at top speed.
     Meanwhile, six Pegasus transports loaded with veteran Mesarthrim Myt’herra swoop into Kesan, their pilots carefully guiding them between damaged skyscrapers towards the heart of the city. Two Pegasi peel off and drop their commandoes into the southern industrial district of the city; they will act as scouts for the armored brigades.
     The rest of the Pegasi reach the Inquisitor’s compound, a heavily fortified enclave around the Spire of Truth. They take several hits by shoulder-launched missiles, but their shields protect them as they settle into the courtyard.
     The Grand Inquisitor watches the landings from his office at the top of the Spire. A Pegasus roars past his office on its way down, rattling the windows. The Mesarthrim aren’t going to settle with blowing this place apart. They want information, prisoners – me.
     He smiles slightly. They think their missiles have blinded me, but an Inquisitor is never without his resources. Kesan has a vast buried sensor system, hardened against aerial attack, and – unlike most of the city sensors - it is still operational. The Grand Inquisitor used it to track the incoming Pegasi and prepare a response.
     He raises a COM link to his lips and speaks a word in Latin.
     As the troop bay of the first Pegasus swings open, windows all across the lower level of the Spire shatter. Templar Knights leap out of the building, spraying the compound with rockets, grenades, and armor-piercing bullets. One Pegasus takes a direct hit in its engines, and crashes to the ground, spilling Myt’herra across the compound and exploding in a ball of fire.
     Other Knights pounce on the remaining troops, firing their weapons into the crowded dropship bays. Only one of the Pegasi is carrying heavy armor; the rest are full of light armor with few heavy weapons. The thundering Templar shoulder cannons fill the Pegasus’ bays, leaving only bloody scraps.
     The Grand Inquisitor nods in satisfaction as the surviving Pegasi lift off, spilling live Templar and dead Mesarthrim into the courtyard. Nobody surprises the Inquisitors.
     Templar VTOLs rise from hidden hangars, firing on the retreating Pegasi. One of the UCS transports bucks as a missile pierces its shields. The wounded transport spirals into the city and explodes. The UCS transports return fire. One Inquisitor VTOL crashes into a skyscraper, leaving the office building listing perilously.
     The Grand Inquisitor turns to two Templar standing in his darkened office. “The immediate danger has passed. Your men did superbly.”
     “Thank you, sir,” the nearest Templar replies tonelessly. “But you must come with us at once. This office is too exposed.”
     The Grand Inquisitor frowns. “I don’t recognize any immediate threat – oh.”
     One of the Pegasi throttles streaks away to the south. The other Pegasus drops into a steep dive – straight towards the Spire of Truth. Inquisitor VTOLs rise to meet it, but the superb Mesarthrim pilot rolls his ship and slides between two of the aircraft, arrowing towards the Spire. The Pegasus slides into a hover over the Spire – directly above The Grand Inquisitor’s office.
     The Grand Inquisitor looks up through the glass ceiling of his office as the Pegasus’ troop bay slides open; the ship twists as the pilot struggles to keep control despite the hundreds of rounds spattering his shields.
     The Pegasus’ troop bay slides open a crack, and a Mesarthrim warrior appears, aiming something down at the office ceiling. One of the Templar grabs The Grand Inquisitor and crashes through the door of the office, out onto a stairwell, using his Knight armor as a shield for the Grand Inquisitor.
     The Grand Inquisitor twists, trying to look past the Templar and back into his office as the Knight charges down the stairs; he hears the roar of the other Knight’s shoulder cannons, shattering glass, and a loud explosion – followed by the sound of booted feet hitting the ground and voices shouting in German.
     The Knight vaults down the stairs a full flight at a time, still carrying the High Inquisitor. The battle-suit’s passage leaves holes in the walls and floor. The Grand Inquisitor can barely hear the Knight yelling into his COM link over the noise from the floors above.
     The Templar dashes into the lobby beneath The Grand Inquisitor’s office and sets the Grand Inquisitor down. “Get to the bunkers, sir. We’ll hold them here.” The elevator slides open, and Templar infantry pour out, taking up firing positions around the feet of the Knight.
     The Grand Inquisitor leaps into the elevator with a pair of infantrymen. The last thing he sees before the elevator door slides shut is a grenade bouncing down the stairs.

=====Above Khyber Continent, 1600 Hours=====
     Captain Bridget Reed grimaces as she flies over the remains of Canopus Spaceport. The city is a sea of fire, and the military compound outside the spaceport isn’t much better. The runways there are heavily cratered, and all the hangars have been shattered by Mesarthrim missiles.
     “Damn it,” she breathes. “We should have been here.”
     A hodgepodge squadron of Piranhas surrounds Reed. Only her fighter her wingman are from 7-1F12. The rest of the survivors are from the carrier Lucidity. She is the only surviving command officer.
     “Where to now, Captain?” her wingman asks.
     “Let’s find out what the hell is going on,” Reed says grimly. She switches frequencies. “Canopus Air Boss, this is Seven One Foxtrot One-Two One. Do you copy?”
     The frequency is silent for a moment. “Foxtrot One-Two One, this is Major Chang. Air Boss is gone. I’m commanding the perimeter defense. We’re digging survivors out of the bunkers now.”
     Reed sighs in relief. Someone’s still alive down there. “What’s your strength, Major?”
     “We have two brigades here.”
     “Where are the other six?”
     “Colonel Kyu deployed them to Kesan with our three Bloodhawk squadrons, all under Major Viram. They’re going to drive the Mesarthrim out. We’re supposed to hold out here – but I’m not sure what good we can do.”
     “Do you have the vector their dropships took?”
     “Yes. I’ll transmit it now.”
     Reed glances over the coordinates that scroll across her HUD. “Thank you, Major. Squadron, form on me. We’re going to give the ground-pounders some fighter cover. Chang, where is 7-1F-11? We need those Stukas.”
     “They were on combat air patrol on the night-side. They should arrive within a few hours.”
     “Redirect them to Kesan. The Mesarthrim have at least eight squadrons over the city, and the ground-pounders won’t have much of a chance without air cover.”
     The twelve Piranhas swing towards Kesan and throttle up, climbing to cruising altitude.

=====Carrier Ravager, 1640 Hours =====
     Admiral Ahrens grimaces as he glances over his displays. He walks to the bridge communications section.
     “Get me Colonel Aloisia.”
     Mareike Aloisia is the commander of the Mesarthrim ground attack; she is somewhere in the armor column to the south of Kesan.
     The channel is opened in moments. “I want a situation report, Colonel,” Ahrens snaps. “Why hasn’t the Spire been secured? What happened to our Myt’herra?”
     Colonel Aloisia’s voice is all business. “Sir, the Spire is a fortress. We expected the headquarters of a spy network to be a business office at best. The ‘Templar’ of the Inquisitors are better-armed than we anticipated. The Spire compound is well-defended by battle armor and VTOLs.”
     “How did they know the Myt’herra commandos were coming?” Ahrens demands. “We took out every sensor station and SAM site in the whole city!”
     “I don’t know, sir,” Aloisia replies crisply. “Four Pegasi landed in the compound, and were immediately attacked – the Templar were waiting for them.”
     “One Pegasus was destroyed before it could drop its troops, two lost their complement to enemy fire, but the fourth managed to get a full load of heavy battle armor into the Spire.”
     “Can we reach your Myt’herra?”
     “No sir. The Spire is jamming us.”
     Ahrens nods slowly. “I’ll send Shermans to deal with that. What’s the status of your scouts and the main attack?”
     “’Savage Talon’ scouts report that they’ve secured spotter points in the industrial district of the city. The path is clear for our armor to enter the city from the south. We’ll storm the Inquisitor compound by dawn.”
     Ahrens sighs. “You do realize the Myt’herra op was our only chance to get the intelligence we want. They’ll start destroying data before your armored units can take the Spire.”
     “Yes sir,” Aloisia replies, unperturbed. “With all due respect, sir, this was an intelligence failure. The Myt’herra were ambushed.”
     Admiral Ahrens shrugs. “Continue as planned. Move in from the south, level the city, and capture the Inquisitor compound.”
     “Yes sir.”
     “Colonel, if you can’t capture the Spire, destroy it. We’re going to take the Inquisitors out of this war.”

=====South of Kesan, 1645 Hours=====
     “We’ve got company,” a sentry calls.
     The captain in charge of this engineering detachment of Kesan’s militia looks up in alarm. A column of vehicles is barely visible in the distance, driving down Highway 19 towards Kesan. His practiced eye judges the distance. It looks like about five kilometers. We don’t have much time.

     Bridget Reed grunts in acknowledgement, wrestling with her fighter’s controls. “I don’t think I can hold this thing in the air much longer. The white-eyes really shot me up.”
     She checks her displays and HUD, but the squadron monitors are dead. “Who have we got left?”
     “Just us. I think almost everyone else is dead, but a few hit the silk. The Bloodhawks survived. I think we just saved Major Viram’s ass.”
     Reed swears as her Piranha begins shaking viciously. Looking downwards, she can see the smoke of battle beginning to clear. Her HUD indicates the location of Prefecture forces; the Mesarthrim are falling back.
     “Hot damn. We did win!”
     The fighter jolts sharply, and alarms scream. Reactor containment failure, the computer announces. Overrides and fail-safes inoperative.
     Reed gulps. “This thing’s gonna blow. Been nice flying with you, but I’d rather work my tan at the beach. Ejecting! Break left!”
     The Stuka breaks away to the left and hits its afterburners, just in time. Reed throws the fighter into a sharp climb, and then slams the ejection triggers. The Piranha’s cockpit blows away, slamming Reed’s helmet into the headrest.
     Moments later, the Piranha’s reactors lose containment, and the battered fighter vanishes in a short-lived blaze of plasma. The shock wave nearly rips Reed out of her straps, and the pilot chair is sent tumbling.
     The next few minutes are a blur of sky, earth, and vertigo-induced visions. Automated control systems finally halt the spinning and deploy parachutes. Her pod crashes in the woods far to the west of Kesan, smashing through trees and underbrush before finally wedging into a ravine. It takes a few minutes for Reed to pop the straps and crawl out. Nothing broken.
     The life-support gear's geiger counter in her flight suit is clicking angrily. Reed checks the displays, and swears. Looks like I didn’t get clear fast enough. Guess I worked on my tan after all. It probably won’t kill me.
     A sudden rustling in the bush nearby attracts her attention. Reed whips out her sidearm, and cautiously pulls herself out of the ravine, onto the forest floor. Whatever is making the noise, it’s approaching fast, and with very little caution.
     She conceals herself behind a tree, and waits. The underbrush on the other side of the ravine parts, and Reed lines up a shot——on a trio of young boys, probably in their early teens. She lowers the sidearm, feeling foolish, as the three approach the crashed pilot seat, shouting excitedly to each other.
     Reed steps out from her cover. “Stay back! It’s hot!”
     The boys look up in alarm and confusion, but the fear quickly vanishes from their faces. “You’re a pilot!” one of the boys exclaims, his Mandarin-accented voice a mix of awe and terror. “What’s going on? Our homes lost ‘net access a little while ago, and there were strange ships flying overhead.”
     Reed smiles soothingly. “It’s OK. We won.. Everything’s fine now. Can you take me to a hospital?” I’d better get decontaminated fast.
     “Sure,” the lead boy says, practically bubbling with enthusiasm. “Follow me.”

=====Three Days Later=====
     In the aftermath of the Mesarthrim raid, Canopus is hard pressed to recover. Despite their setbacks, the victory was clearly a Mesarthrim one.
     They succeeded in destroying the Spire and capturing valuable data, and crippled the Canopus defenses and the Sixth Fleet.
     In the days following, the Mesarthrim launched an all-out invasion of the neighboring Laojiu District, and the Prefecture Senate ratified the Prefect’s declaration of war. With news that the vital food supply of the Prefecture is under attack, food rationing immediately goes into effect.
     The news for Canopus is not all bad. The Inquisitors are not out of the war. The Spire is only a building; The Grand Inquisitor and many of the analysts survived, as did their massive spy network. It may take a while to reestablish contact, but Prefecture intelligence will be as strong as ever in a short time.
     Major Viram and Colonel Kyu both survived the ground fighting. Viram was pried out of his downed ‘Rig hours after the Mesarthrim withdraw, unconscious but not badly wounded.
     Colonel Kyu spent two days pinned beneath rubble in the ruins of his command bunker. When he was finally extracted, he was paralyzed from the neck down. The Colonel was quickly whisked off by the Mobile Medical Corps to the Schweitzer Institute of Medicine for organ regrowth and nerve repair.
     Bridget Reed becomes a local hero overnight for her valiant fight, despite being shot down twice in the same day. Her actions and leadership were very nearly single-handedly responsible for rallying the aerospace defenses of Canopus. Reed spent several days in decontamination. Her caretakers informed her that she will likely suffer from cancer later in life due to radiation exposure.
     “I’m a pilot,” Reed replied jovially. “There’s a war on. Cancer is the least of the threats to my life.”
     The Battle of Canopus was bloody, especially for pilots. The Prefecture fighter forces guarding the planet were very nearly wiped out, and the 6th Fleet’s fighter compliment was badly mauled. Mesarthrim gunships pose a tactical dilemma that the Prefecture Air Force has no answer to, yet.
     The performance of the Sixth Fleet is alarming to the Admiralty. Admiral Baruk admits that she was outmaneuvered, but more startling is the superiority of Mesarthrim warship defenses and bomber armaments.
     Prefecture Orca torpedoes had a low success rate against UCS capital ships, while equivalent Mesarthrim warheads were lethally effective and accurate.
    If the Prefecture cannot find a way to counter Mesarthrim tactics and technology, the outlook for this war will remain grim.

=The ''Praetoria'' Class=

==Class History==

The ''Praetoria'' Class is the oldest, largest, and most powerful class of ships still operating in the [[Milky Way]].  The ''Praetoria'' Class was originally built during the first phases of the [[Sacrum Bellus]] to replace the ''Lictor'' Class of Heavy Combatant, and because of this, is very unique in its capabilities.  Knowing a long offensive war was upon them, the Anthronoris decided to make several key additions beyond the typical Heavy Combatant design.  

While the ''Lictor'' was designed simply as the largest example of the typical Anthronoris warship, the ''Praetoria'' was envisioned as a mobile base of operations, capable of supporting both itself and the other smaller ships assigned to its Classis.  It was theorized that several Cohorts of ''Praetoria'', a typical Legair or Scrunta Classis, would be capable of mutually sustaining the operations of the entire fleet without outside assistance.  Therefore, Legairs could be formed and tasked with the eradication of a specific race and unleashed on a path of destruction, only to be recalled and resupplied after the campaign was complete.

This strategy worked well, and is partially responsible for the [[Anthronoris Empire]]'s initial success during the Sacrum Bellus.  At its peak, there were over three thousand ''Praetoria'' in the service of the Anthronoris Astrum Classis, with tens of thousands of lesser combatant classes in support.  But war with the [[Ngrligru]] took its toll, and by the time of the [[Sundering]] over 1900 ''Praetoria'' and countless lesser combatants had been lost.  These losses have often been cited by Remnant historians as a key deciding factor in the formulation of the [[Master Plan]].  

==''Praetoria'''s Finest Hour==

The fury of the [[Ngrligru]] made the situation worse and loss rates skyrocketed.  As was called for by Phase One of the revised Master Plan, over two hundred ''Praetoria'' and supporting ships, the largest single force of ''Praetoria'' ever assembled, moved on the area where the [[Galactic Core]] met the [[Orion Arm]] to secure this strategic choke point.  Finding a hopelessly massive force of [[Ngrligru]] awaiting them, Legant [[Argus Shadar]] deployed his ''Praetoria'' very deliberately.  Many historians have labeled Shadar's '''Battle of the Teeth''' as the class's finest hour.  

Knowing that the uniquely large mass and reactor systems of the ''Praetoria'' Class caused catastrophic permanent disruption of both hyper and real space upon destruction, Shadar deployed the ''Praetoria''s in a unique fashion.  The [[Ngrligru]] succeeded in wiping out the ''Praetoria'' arrayed against them, but in doing so, created the [[Teeth of Orion]] and doomed their holdings in the Orion Arm.

The remainder of the ''Praetoria'' were lost clearing the Orion Arm of [[Ngrligru]] as well as in a holding effort while the [[Void World]]s were constructed.  Only four would survive the Sundering, and one continues, fully-functional, to this day.            

==Unique Features==

Always intended as a mobile base of operations, the ''Praetoria'' Class was designed a full two times larger than the previous ''Lictor'' Class and included  a full array of extraction and production facilities.  The large hanger bays at the front of each engine pod are actually intended for use by resource collection craft.  Raw materials are taken in and processed within each pod and the finished products are collected in the large cavity in the center rear of the ship.  

This cavity is no less than a fully functional, albeit small, shipyard facility.  The rear third of the shipyard is actually designed to open to space and allow vessels and craft constructed within to leave.  This feature was included as a way to repair ships and replace losses on campaign and removed from support facilities.  Although unable to construct entire ships of Anthronris-era design (because they were generally much larger than modern Remnant warships), this facility is now able to construct modern Remnant ships up to Medium Combatant in size internally.  

Furthermore, there are several large pylons extending from the rear third of the ''Praetoria'' class.  These are actually docking arms for use by smaller vessels, allowing a considerable number of the smaller Remnant-era construction to dock with ''Adoreus'' at any given time. 

Lastly, the ''Praetoria'' Class is covered with tractor beam stations along its belt and rear sections.  These were included in the design as a way for smaller ships to "piggy back" on a ''Praetoria'' as it transited through the void between star arms, an action only possible to the largest of Anthronoris vessels due to the amount of power required for such a journey. 

In many ways, the ''Praetoria'' Class can be seen as much as a mothership as it is a warship.

=Adoreus=

''Adoreus'' is one of three surviving ''Praetoria'' Class vessels and is the only remaining fully-functional example of this class.  The other three surviving the Sundering being the Cohonesto, Minerva, and the Auxilium, which was lost at Aboukir in [[17953BC]].  Her history is a history of the [[Remnant]], as the two have been intertwined since the [[Sundering]]. 

First, as a heavy combatant in the Anthronoris Astrum Classis, ''Adoreus'' saw heavy combat in both the Sacrum Bellus and the Sundering.  Then, as one of the four Praetoria Class vessels which carried the Emperor from the fallen Anthronoris capital world in [[17971BC]], she witnessed the desperate struggles which characterized the birth of the Remnant.  Now, she protects the [[Void World]]s of the Remnant, though her proverbial heart quickens at the whisper of one day leading Legairs in glory against her [[Ngrligru]] foe.        

==Weapons==

The ''Praetoria'' Class was originally laid down well before the advent of the Gravity Wave Drive and its resultant system, and close examination of the ''Adoreus'' will find where numerous weapons systems have been installed and removed over her lifetime.  

Currently, the ''Adoreus'' is armed with a combination of modern and ancient weaponry.  She has maintained her twenty '''Main Guns''', as their effectiveness against any foe is still as potent as the day they were created, two hundred centuries ago.  With the ability to penetrate any known defensive measure, the '''Main Gun''' is the most feared weapon system employed by ''Adoreus''.    

Along with the ancient technology of the '''Main Gun''' comes the addition of an unprecedented number of modern Remnantine weaponry.  Both '''Emitters''' and Falarica Missile Launchers have been added in unfathomable number.  While a typical Remnant-era Medium or Heavy Combatant is more than well equipped with 11-15 '''Emitters''', the ''Adoreus'' has 191.  Likewise, where a typical Remnant-era ship of similar role and classification may have up to 20 Falarica, ''Adoreus'' has 974.  These enormous numbers are thanks to the vast bulk and immense amount of power which ''Adoreus'' possesses.  Generally, only a fraction of these weapons is active at any given time, with the rest on hot standby.  It is inconceivable that any ship of any size from any race would be able to survive within the weapons envelop of the ''Adoreus''.       

==Axillary Forces==

The ''Praetoria'' class can support a truly epic number of small craft and Marines.  Her hull is dotted with 60 hanger bays, which service everything from thousands of small [[Telum Class]] fighters to hundreds of shuttles and dozens of resource collection craft.  Additionally, she can support millions of [[Vespa]] and [[Crabro]] Autonomous Combat Units and thousands of Marines of the [[Remnant Marine Corps| Reliquiae Classiarius Agema]] with their resultant assault craft.  Although not originally designed with the catapult systems required by modern Gravity Wave Drive equipped small craft, these were easily retrofitted into the design, owing to the ''Praetoria'' Class having more than enough volume to spare.   

==Defenses==

Thought having had countless types of defensive systems aboard, the ''Adoreus'' still retains its historical toughness.  Originally built using neutronium armor, the ''Adoreus'' now plays host to the wide array of defensive systems common in Remnant-era construction.  In fact, it is that initial construction with neutronium which has allowed the ship to survive as long as it has, with no foreseeable end in sight.       

==Propulsion==

Though built tens of centuries before the creation of the Gravity Wave Drive, the ''Adoreus'' is still able to utilize this system and its plethora of dependent subsystems.  Originally using a more conventional drive system, the ''Adoreus'' was upgraded to the Gravity Wave Drive soon after it became the standard method of propulsion for the Remnant.  The marriage of the ''Praetoria'' Class with the Gravity Wave Drive was a revolution in firepower and mobility which, had the system been known and widely applied during the Sundering, may have changed the course of the war.  

Mating a vessel of this immense size and firepower with the capacity for instantaneous direction change and acceleration has truly made the ''Adoreus'' the most powerful warship in the galaxy.      

==Crew Complement==

The ''Praetoria'' Class was always intended as a mobile base and home to its crew as well as thousands of passengers.  ''Adoreus'' has taken that intention and evolved it ten fold.  During the long exodus, the ''Adoreus'' was rebuilt, section by section, to accommodate very long term habitation.  The retrofits went as far as to include an entire enclosed biosphere of some size on the ventral surface of the ship, complete with a small lake, river, and artificial mountain as a recreation center for the crew and passengers.  A further smaller enclosure was  constructed just forward of the larger one for a personal residence for the Imperiant.  Both of these features are unique to ''Adoreus'' herself and were never in the original ''Praetoria '' design specifications.

==Heritage==

The ''Adoreus'' has a unique place in galactic history.  It is the galaxy's oldest functional vessel and as such is a time capsule.  Scholars  from the '''Ministry of History''' have worked tirelessly to sift through the data contained within the libraries of the ''Adoreus'' and her remaining sister ships in an effort to recount the days of the Empire and analyze its fall.  Not only that, but as new races are discovered, their histories are "retrieved" and stored for future analysis and comparison.      

==Commander==

The Imperiant, Hamish Lupus, has taken up both permanent residence and command of the ''Adoreus''.  He and his staff work tirelessly to direct the actions of the Reliquiae Astrum Classis and Lupus takes great pride in ''Adoreus''s performance during war games.  It is a well known fact that he favors more direct intervention in galactic affairs, but is currently held in check by the majority opinion of the ''Government''.

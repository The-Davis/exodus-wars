== Events of 200BC ==

The [[Fasrem Epoch]] of Ixoarchada history begins in approximately 200BC as the various [[Iridi]] on [[Glengarr]] cease fighting for the first time in centuries.  Peace becomes a norm and within a short time nearly all of the Iridi have joined the [[Parcna Somglengarr]], a sort of unified world government.  Trade and development take-off after centuries of fractured infighting dating back to the end of [[Anthronoris Empire]].

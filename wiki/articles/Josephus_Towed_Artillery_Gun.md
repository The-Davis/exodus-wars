===Overview===

Artillery has always played an important role in the military, allowing for devastating indirect fire to rain down upon unsuspecting enemy hideouts or to provide cover fire for advancing troops. Although blown off as nothing more than relicts of old past for a number of decades, artillery is again seeing a renaissance period.

[[Image:JosephusTowedArtilleryGun1.jpg]]

The Josephus is an answer to the requests made by various militaries for a small, lightweight, and easily deployed artillery piece.

===Capabilities===

At its heart, the Josephus system is really nothing more than a large mass-driver, as seen on some capital ships, shrunk down into a manageable size. Propelling a variety of ammunition, from high explosive to cluster rounds, a fairly long distance, anywhere from half a mile to five miles, the Josephus has it where it counts when it comes to range and munitions selection.

Built to be towed behind numerous vehicles, the system can be air dropped into a combat zone and set up to operate within ten minutes. Operated by a three man team, the Josephus is loaded by hand and programmed by computer as to where, how far up, and far away to explode the warhead. This gives many more options to the men on the ground than they had even a decade ago.

[[Image:JosephusTowedArtilleryGun2.jpg]]

===Deployment===

Many governments have expressed some interest in the system, though the civilian market is barred from buying this system due to its strictly military nature.

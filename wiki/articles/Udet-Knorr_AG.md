==History==
Udet-Knorr AG was founded by David Archer, a successful warship designer for [[Haven Shipyards]]. Archer left Haven Shipyards in 2325, taking his family to [[Comur]] to accept a position with [[Mesarbauer]] as Assistant Lead Designer, Warship Division. At Mesarbauer, Archer met [[Silas Gardner]] who previously worked for [[Young-Watson Armory]] - and was similarly recruited by Mesarbauer.

Since Archer and Gardner were both Terrans, and not Mesarthim, [[William Camp]] ordered them demoted when he became Geschaftsführer of Mesarbauer. Archer and Gardner eventually decided to leave Mesarbauer, convincing the sizable terran R&D team to leave as well.

After the team left, they were approached by Arnold Udet and Hansel Knorr, two Mesarthim who were building a company to take advantage of the new [[Auellal League]]s plea for a company to upgrade and up armor any hulk they could find into an Armada. Udet and Knorr wanted Archer and Gardner's teams design and weapon knowledge. It was a perfect match.

However, in 2330, an accident killed Udet, Knorr, and almost fifty other employees when a replaced bulkhead on a mongrel vessel broke away. The entire section depressurized and the shock of the explosive decompression tore their bodies to pieces - despite the environmental workmen suits they were wearing.

In 2334, Archer and Gardner decided to incorporate their rag tag team of engineers and designers under the new laws of the [[Mesarthrim Federation]] and named the company Udet-Knorr in honor of their fallen co-founders.

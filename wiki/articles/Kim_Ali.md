''Kimesha Tharain Ali'' (born March 12, [[2280]]), better known as "Kim" Ali to her friends and colleagues, was the ambassador to the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] from the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].  Ali's greatest achievement was getting representatives from both the United Mesarthrim Clans and the [[Auellal League]] to actually attend a peace summit.  Ali received death threats from both sides.

Despite the presence of three personal bodyguards and an honor guard of ten Marines, Kim Ali was assassinated on September 11, [[2324]].  In the aftermath of her death, three Mesarthrim assassins were killed, and the [[FDS Omaha|FDS ''Omaha'']] acted independent of orders from the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] and engaged multiple Auellal supply raiders in an act of vengeance.  The Prefecture was forced to sever ties with the [[Auellal League]] for several years as a result.

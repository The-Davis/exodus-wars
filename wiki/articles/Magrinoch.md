The city of '''Magrinoch''' is one of the larger spaceports and commercial hubs on the world of [[Tavou]].  It was the site of a large [[Volucris]] incursion in [[2324]], which was valuable in gathering intelligence on how the swarms operated.

When [[Tavou]] was handed over to the [[Mesarthrim]] under the terms of the [[Treaty of Tavou]], Magrinoch was temporarily made into a sovereign neutral territory, whose neutrality was enforced by the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] for the purpose of holding war crimes trials.  The neutrality of the [[Magrinoch Trials]] themselves were later called into question, after evidence in the hearings for several key defendants was suppressed and destroyed by [[Talaris Roi]]'s administration.

Today, Magrinoch is under the jurisdiction of the [[Mesarthrim Federation]], and is one of the few remaining [[Terran]] cities in Mesarthrim territory.  The population is roughly 60% Terran and 40% Mesarthrim.

''Richard Holder'' (born May 17, [[2267]]) was a researcher on [[Ah Kin]], and heavily involved in energy research.  After the [[Onyx Empire]] took over the world in [[2300]], Holder was absorbed into their research efforts.  He was eventually trusted enough to head [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Energy Laboratory.

During the [[Second Exodus War]], Holder had the opportunity to study the [[Mesarthrim]] [[Omega Bomb]].  He never discovered the catalyst for the destructive power of the bomb, but did unlock the secrets of [[Subspace]], and eventually developed the [[Entropy Drive]].  By the time the drive system was ready, the Empire had fallen, and Holder's work was seized by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]].

After his notes and prototypes were taken, Holder's research team left him to join the Prefecture's new research group.  Holder is now working for the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], and is attempting to duplicate his earlier research for the Commonwealth's space forces.

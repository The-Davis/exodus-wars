The '''Sirio''' is the unitary currency minted within the [[Sirius Cluster]] and used by the [[Sirius Families]].  It has been in use since the Families pushed off [[United Earth Alliance]] authority.  

The Sirio is a paper and coinage monetary system, similar to the [[dollar]]. Sirios are printed in one, five, ten, fifteen and twenty denominations, while coins are minted in half, quarter and tenth denominations.

Each paper Sirio denomination is a different size and color. One Sirio notes are the smallest and are printed in red, five in orange, ten in green, fifteen in purple, and twenty are the largest and printed in blue. All three coins are round and silver-colored, but easily distinguished from each other by size.

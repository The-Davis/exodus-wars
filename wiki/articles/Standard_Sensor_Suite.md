Radar was long ago outdated. Jamming and blocking technology far outstripped the abilities of radar in the mid 21st century. Modern sensors are mostly optical. Computers can recognize most war machines easily, simply with pattern recognition software. For non line-of-sight sensors, radiation and even sonic sensors are used. Noise filtration has advanced significantly in military warfare. For outer space, gravity sensors are used at long range. Warships are so large that they have their own heavy gravity signature.

Sensor Suite includes passive radar and sonar, infrared and ultraviolet scanners, gamma ray emission sensors, optical motion sensors, Identification Friend/Foe (IFF), and target tracking. 

Combined into an interchangeable optical feed, they deliver constant battlefield updates and tracking data to the pilot, and are a vital part of battlefield operations. A Sensor Suite can plug into an Active Probe, giving active pinging capability to the passive sensors of the suite. The Sensor Suite also feeds information to the C3 System.

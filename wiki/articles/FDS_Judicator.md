===Overview===

The original HMS ''Judicator'' was a [[Vigilant Class Frigate]], and a veteran of the early years of the [[Second Exodus War]].  After the end of the war and the formation of the new Prefecture Navy fleets, the ''Judicator'' was rebuilt as a unique flagship.

[[Image:FDSJudicator1.jpg]]

While the warship offers nothing unique to the Prefecture Navy, it is a capable vessel and a favorite figurehead for the elite Atlas Wing warships of the Fifth Fleet.

===Capabilities===

The "Vigilis" Relativistic Mass Driver is named for the first Judicator, which served long and well in the Royal Imperial Navy and Prefecture Navy.  The mass driver is fixed in a forward firing arc, but the Judicator is built to maneuver it into a proper firing solution.  This mass driver, coupled with the Judicator's Hellguns and Naval Autocannons, makes the flagship an adequate fighter in warship combat.

Anti-fighter combat is where the Judicator begins to shine.  It carries almost two hundred neutron cannons, and an adequate missile arsenal.  Rapid-moving fighters can easily be tracked by the warship's sensor systems.  Some of the tracking software was developed after the first battles with the Volucris, so eventually the cannons will be able to track and kill the elusive creatures.

[[Image:FDSJudicator2.jpg]]

Fighter cover for the Judicator must come from its carrier escorts.  The Judicator has no hangars, and carries only a few small craft.  Most supplies come from direct docking with orbital supply stations.

Defenses on the Judicator are strong, but not impenetrable.  It relies equally on its weapons, speed, maneuverability, and defensive systems to keep the crew alive and the vessel fighting.

===Deployment===

Admiral Jeff Nolan loves his flagship, even if it is only a jack-of-all-trades and a master of nothing.  The other flagships have more firepower, or more speed, or more armor, but only the Judicator is equally suited for any role.

[[Image:FDSJudicator3.jpg]]

The Fifth Fleet Flagship is assigned to reinforce any Prefecture Navy fleet in trouble.  The elite Atlas Wing Avenger class cruisers are welcome anywhere, and the Judicator itself can complement any fleet action.

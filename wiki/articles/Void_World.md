Constructed during the final decade of the [[Sundering]], the Void World and its planet, Caetara Remaneo, stands as one of the greatest accomplishments of the [[Anthronoris Empire]].  Envisioned as a safe haven for survivors of the [[Master Plan]], the Void World was built at great cost and secured through tremendous sacrifice.

Essentially, a Void World is an artificially created, self-contained star system containing one naturally-formed planet, an artificially-formed star, and several asteroid belts, all of which are encased in an artificial shell which masks the system's signature from the outside universe.  The Anthronoris had toyed with the idea several times over the centuries, but only two such worlds were created; the later in complete secrecy.  A similar project was undertaken by the [[Gorhamut]] near the end of the [[Sacrum Bellus]], although it is speculated that their versions were much smaller and simpler in nature.  It is also rumored that the [[Ngrligru]] have constructed their own void worlds, utilizing older captured Anthronoris theoretical studies.         

===Construction===

The creation of a Void World is a intensive undertaking, as the materials and expertise required are many magnitudes beyond the scope of most nations.  Vast stretches of the galaxy must be searched in an effort to meet several very specific criteria before construction of a Void World can begin.  These criteria include:   

1.) There is a desirable and uninhabited planet in the system
2.) There are several additional and much less desirable large terrestrial planets in the system    

Additionally, the need for secrecy as a paramount concern adds complications and must be addressed before construction can begin. 

First, large mobile processing stations, which were quite common during the Anthronoris Empire are brought into the target system.  The undesired terrestrial planets are entirely consumed to provide materials for both the outer "Shell" and "Great Ring" built around the desired planet.  This shell is constructed in immense segments to allow for easier transportation while the ring is built directly onto the planet itself.  These segments are then moved bit by bit to the desired location for the Void World; a location in deep space, while the great ring is outfitted with a hyperdrive and particle shielding.  

Next, the particle shielding and hyperdrive are brought to standby.  Both are activated and the planet transitions into hyperspace, specifically the [[Desyat Band]].  The particle-shields protect the planet as it hurtles through hyperspace and the Great Ring directs its course.  Eventually, the planet arrives at its target destination and transitions back into normal space.  At this point, timing is critical for the survival of the planet and its biosphere.

Now, only hours after arrival, the Shell segments are assembled using fleets of small tugs prepositioned at the destination.  Utilizing a similar process to that which created the [[Teeth of Orin]] (albeit much more controlled and precise), a new star is created within the Shell.  The planet's orbit and rotation is then artificially established by the Great Ring and the planetary particle shields deactivated.  

With the new system totally encased, to the outside observer, nothing new has occurred.  Asteroid fields are selectively moved, rock by rock, into the shell to provide additional raw resources to the inhabitants without a need to leave the sphere.

===The Interior===

Within the expanse of the sphere is found a single planet, an artificially created star, and a variety of space stations and asteroid belts.  

The interior space of a Void World is a hive of activity.  A hundred battle stations are located within this inner-space to control lines of transit between the sphere and the planet.  If any aggressor were to breach the shell, they would still have to deal with these battle stations as well as the planet's defenses, placed in its great ring.  Civilian pleasure craft ply the inner-space relatively unrestricted and flock to one of several recreation stations located throughout the inner-space.  

The planet itself is always specifically chosen to provide a wide variety of biospheres and climates for the enjoyment of its inhabitants.  The Great Ring, in addition to housing the planet's hyperdrive and particle shielding, is home to the vast majority of the planet's static defenses as well as its starports and manufacturing capacity.     

===The Shell===  

The Shell is the most important component for the Void World.  The reason for which Void Worlds are created, to create a hidden sanctuary, requires a shell to mask all the emissions which would otherwise give away its location.  This can be anything from the gravitational effects of the star, to its solar winds, to the actual photons it gives off.  Additionally, the Shell prevents any signal created by the inhabitants from betraying its position in space.  This is accomplished by a variety of means.

First, the Shell is the galaxy's largest Chameleon Mimetic Coating.  This prevents detection by optical sensors.  The Shell also is an enormous Hecate Cryo-arithmetic Cloak, which masks both the star, NAME, and Caetara Remaneo's heat signatures.  The Shell also functions as a type Eris Gravity Sensor Jammer, although fundamentally different from those installed aboard Remnant starships.  Not being able to produce a Gravity Field large enough to encase the entire Void World, this Eris Gravity Sensor Jammer depends upon the structural integrity fields weaved into the Shell itself to give it a means of operation.  A Validis Shield has also been incorporated into the Shell by these means as well.  

Lastly, a Aegis Quantum Intelligence Field has been recently incorporated into the Shell, although its use is reserved for only the direst of emergencies because of the potential for the creation of a truly gigantic sentient Aegis field.  On the innermost layer of the Shell, which faces the star and planet within, an enormous solar wind collection grid has been installed so that, in essence, NAME actually powers the Void World's various stealth devices.  The vast majority of these collection stations are at NAME's zenith and nadir, as the intensity is much greater at these points.  

The Shell is also home to countless weapons emplacements and fighter hanger facilities lining its exterior surface, making the Remnant Void World the largest battle station ever constructed.  

Also lining the Shell at strategic points are enormous articulated blinds which control the entry and exit of starships and even asteroids several times larger than even the [[Praetoria Class]].  If interior travel is not required, there are several mass-docking areas for starships at the poles of the sphere.

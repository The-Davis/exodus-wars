The '''Blasius Family Territory''' is the senior of the [[Sirius Families]]. The Blasius Family has led the entire [[Sirius Cluster]] since the worlds were first colonized in the [[21st Century]].

Since its foundation, the "Tanár" (Master) of the family has been an outspoken critic of [[Earth]] imperialism, and has been a deadly master of assassination. The famous "night of knives" during the [[First Exodus War]] was a Blasius assassination of several key members of the Assembly of the [[United Earth Alliance]].

While the other three Sirius Families have more military power than Blasius, the Blasius family has enormous economic and political power, and the Tanár is feared by every other family head. Unlike the charismatic [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconis]] or even the cold-hearted [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanovas]], Blasius family members do not make public appearances. Even the enigmatic [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamuras]] are seen more often than the Tanár and other Blasius relatives.

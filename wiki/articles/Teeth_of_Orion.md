The '''Teeth of Orion''' is a superdense cluster of thousands of hypergiant stars close to the glactic core.  It is completely uninhabitted, and serves as a buffer between the [[Orion Arm]] and the [[Sagittarius Arm]].  [[Hyperspace]] navigation is extremely dangerous and slow when traversing the Teeth.  Only the [[Meroniri]] can safely pass through, and only via the use of their Tesseract drives.

Gradient Gravity Shielding Gradient Gravity Shielding uses gravity fields to protect warships. This gravity field is projected around a warship in sixteen steep gradients around the ship, above and below. This gradient causes one of two things to happen to incoming projectile and particle weapons. First, the shock of sudden force applied can destroy projectiles, especially missiles, outright. If a projectile is destroyed, all of its destructive power, except for its inertia, is harmlessly dissipated, unless it explodes on impact. Due to the conservation of momentum/action-reaction, the inertial energies (and explosive energies from detonating warheads) are absorbed by the gravity field. Unfortunately, the shock of absorbing inertial energies directly hits the gravity field generator. This is damaging, and can eventually knock out the shield. To lighten the damage, multiple generator nodes are strategically placed throughout the hull of the warship, and coupled together with magnetic fields. Second, projectiles and particles can be deflected into space. Some inertia is absorbed by the shield, so the generator takes some minor shock damage, but nothing like the damage inflicted by a direct hit. If a large, fast-moving object directly impacts gravity field, the warship is ripped apart, then smashed to bits. If a ship or asteroid is used to attempt to ram the warship, only evasive measures can be taken. Gravity shields simply cannot absorb that much energy and mass without collapsing. If projectile weapons (or launched fighters) directly impact the shield from the inside, the same thing happens to them as to incoming weapons. They're ripped apart. The shield must be taken down to launch fighters. For projectile weapons, there are "soft" points in the field where the fields meet. These are similar in function to the gun ports of 17th and 18th century warships. At these points, weapons fire exiting the shield is accelerated by the field. These weak spots can be exploited by an observant enemy. Every so often, firing arcs and the soft points are shifted between battles to avoid continuous exploitation. Gravity shields do not stop pure energy weapons, so other shield systems and composite armor must be employed to take care of lasers and similar weapons.

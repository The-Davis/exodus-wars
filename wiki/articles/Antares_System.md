The '''Antares System''' consists of the star Antares, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids.

In order of their distances from Antares, the planets are Furenda, Ohares, [[Albanus]], [[Jaipur]], Calesh, Frenda, and Otano.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Antares is the system's only star. Antares is a class G2V yellow dwarf. The Antares System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Antares is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]], very close to the border of the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Albanus ===

[[Albanus]] (1.09AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Antares System. It has one moon, [[Calus]].

=== Jaipur ===

[[Jaipur]] (1.56AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Antares System. It has two moons, [[Kalpun]] and [[Erethin]].

== History ==

The Antares System was colonized by the [[Terrans]] in [[2060]]. The colony project was largely funded by a science team interested in studying [[De Mairan's Nebula]].

===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Jitu'nge|History]]

'''Kabila Jitu'nge''' is one of the most vicious and powerful Kabilas in [[Ngrligru]] society. They are the sworn enemies of [[Kabila Nyoka]], and close allies with [[Kabila Damu'kizuka]].

For many years, Jitu'nge was second in power behind [[Kabila Nyoka]]. Their close proximity to the top seat made them both angry and full of intrigue. Even when they entered into an alliance with [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]], they couldn't compare to [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]] in power.

When the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]] declared Kabilas [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] and [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] unfit to exist, the Jitu'nge leaders saw an excellent opportunity. Whoever conquered [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] and [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]] would have the right to absorb the smaller tribes into their armies. Jitu'nge won the right to battle [[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] and [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburu]], but so did their rivals; [[Kabila Nyoka]].

[[Kabila Bweha|Bweha]] was completely cut apart by Jitu'nge and [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyoka]], but the [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyokans]] managed to destroy most of the troops allocated to the Jitu'nge for absorption. Jitu'nge's [[Kuu]] at the time, [[Jra Ghadhabu]], was outraged. He took the matter to the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]], but nothing came of it. At the advice of his friends, [[Jra Ghadhabu]] decided that Jitu'nge would bide its time.

In [[2207]], [[Kabila Nyoka]] was crippled by an unexpected attack from human forces. [[Kuu]] [[Gorul Ghadhabu]] decided that it was time to seize the position of "most powerful Kabila." Working in tandem with [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]] warriors, the armies of Kabila Jitu'nge conducted several precise raids on [[Kabila Nyoka]]'s factory worlds. They managed to destroy many [[Kabila Nyoka|Nyokan]] warriors, and captured a factory world and two warships.

Faced with the losses of both their Thelathini na nane Mia'mbili and the forces destroyed by Kabilas Jitu'nge and [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]], [[Kabila Nyoka]] withdrew into their core systems to rebuild. Kabila Jitu'nge took the opportunity to declare themselves "First Kabila," and granted [[Kabila Damu'kizuka|Damu'kizuka]] the second position in the [[Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri]].

===Government===
Main Article: [[Government of Kabila Jitu'nge|Government]]

[[Ngrligru]] are divided into multiple Kabila, each led by a "Kuu" who represents the tribe in the Kubwa'Kabila'Shauri, the [[Ngrligru]] tribal council.

Each Kabila has an "A'Pili Kuu," who is the warlord of the tribe, and leads the Kabila's warriors in battle. The A'Pili Kuu commands all of the forces of the Kabila.

After the A'Pili Kuu, the Mkugenzi hold sway. They are high-ranking officers in war, but not in the tribe. They usually command warships, very large groups of fighters, or very large groups of ground troops.

Tajiri are the lowest-ranked officers. They command small groups of fighters of small groups of ground troops. Only the Ardhi'nyuka--unranked warriors--are lower in the pecking order than Tajiri.

===Culture===

Ghadhabu - The main royal family. Ghadhabu are excellent schemers and spies, but aren't the best of warriors or tacticians.

Raruo - The Raruos are the premier warrior family in Jitu'nge, and usually lead the armies.

== Events of 1951 ==

===January===
*January 9 - [[United Nations]] headquarters officially opens in Geneva.
*January 17 - [[Korean War]]: [[Korea (Terra)|North Korean]] forces recapture Seoul.
*January 27 - [[Cold War]]: Nuclear testing at the Nevada Test Site begins with a one-kiloton bomb dropped on Frenchman Flats, northwest of Las Vegas, Nevada.

===February===
*February 1 - [[Korean War]]: [[United Nations]] General Assembly declares that [[Korea (Terra)|North Korea]] is the aggressor in the [[Korean War]], and that its continued existence is a threat to peace.
*February 27 - The Twenty-second Amendment to the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Constitution is ratified, limiting Presidents to two terms.

===March===
*March 7 - [[Korean War]]: Operation Ripper - In [[Korea (Terra)|Korea]], [[United Nations]] troops led by General Matthew Ridgeway capture [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] troops, and uncover the covert involvement of the [[Communist]] nations.
*March 14 - [[Korean War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] disavows actions of its troops, claiming they were acting without orders. [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviets]] withdraw support of the war. [[United Nations]] troops recapture Seoul.
*March 29 - [[Cold War]]: Ethel and Julius Rosenberg are executed for espionage against the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] for the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviets]].
*March 30 - [[Korean War]]: [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]] disavows actions of its troops and withdraws support from North Korea.
*March 31 - [[The Technology Race]]: Remington Rand delivers the first [[UNIVAC]] computer to the United States Census Bureau.

===April===

===May===
*May 9 - [[Cold War]]: The first nuclear weapon with thermonuclear materials is tested on Enewetok Atoll in the Marshall Islands, by the United States.

===June===

===July===
*July 10 - [[Korean War]]: Without [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] and [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] support, [[Korea (Terra)|North Korean]] forces surrender at Kaesong. The [[Korean War]] ends.

===August===

===September===
*September 9 - [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] [[Communist]] forces move into Lhasa, the capital of Tibet.

===October===
*October 26 - [[Winston Churchill]] is re-elected as the [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] Prime Minister.

===November===
*November 1 - [[Cold War]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United States]] conducts its first military exercises for nuclear war, with infantry troops included, in the Nevada desert.

===December===
*December 6 - [[The Jihad]]: The [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] declares a state of emergency in the Suez region due to Muslim riots.
*December 20 - [[The Technology Race]]: EBR-1, the world's first experimental nuclear power plant, comes online.
*December 24 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslim [[Arabia (Terra)|Libyan]] rebels throw out [[Italy (Terra)|Italian]] occupation.

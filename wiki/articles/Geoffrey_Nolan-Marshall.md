''Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall'', or "Jeff" as he was known in his younger days, was the epitomy of loyalty and skill in warship command, serving in both the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] and the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]].

=== Son by Adoption ===

Geoffrey was born a mere three years before the end of the [[Second Exodus]].  He never truly experienced the suffering his people went through to come home, so he didn't completely understand the hatred they had for the [[United Earth Alliance]].

He did understand that he wanted to be a warship commander.  With no real family--most [[Torimur]] children of the Exodus were grown in vats, not born--Geoffrey looked up to [[Robert Marshall]] more than anyone else.  He was as much of a son to [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] as his real son, [[Jordan Marshall| Jordan]].

Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall entered the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2315]], and served with distinction.  In [[2318]] he received command of the HMS ''Judicator'', and joined [[Robert Marshall]]'s elite [[Atlas Fleet]]. From then until the rebellion of the [[Prefecture]] in [[2322]], Nolan-Marshall was one of the best commanders in the fleet.

During the [[Imperial Civil War]], Geoffrey Nolan-Marshall and [[Jordan Marshall]] were sent to the outlying [[Empire|Imperial]] territories to hunt down [[Empire|Imperial]] loyalist fleets and strengthen the [[Prefecture]]'s presence and image as a benevolent liberator.  It was during one of these missions that [[Jordan Marshall]] was killed, when his warship went down under [[Empire|Imperial]] fire.

Nolan-Marshall became an Admiral in [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] in the year [[2324]], and was given command of the Fifth Fleet of the Line.  He is competent and imaginative, though hesitant to try anything drastically different from normal tactics.  The loss of his friend [[Jordan Marshall]] shook his confidence.  When Nolan-Marshall recovers completely, he will be one of the finest fleet commanders the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]] has.

The [[Meroniri]] '''Pasara''' dueling swords are curved, single-edged weapons. The blade length is typically 40cm long, designed for great maneuverability to spin and rotate in close-quarters fighting. Pasara are always a paired set, held within the same scabbard. The hilt has a small crossguard to protect the fighter's hands, and to block or hook an opponent's weapon. The covers the knuckles and can be used as a punching weapon. Pasara are used in the [[Meroniri]] [[martial art]] known as "[[Tesashue]]."

===Forging===
The forging of a set of Pasara is a long process. While it is extremely complex, the artisan undertaking the endeavor will not accept any assistance. The process begins with making the alloys needed to complete the task. A master smith can create a spongy, soft metal core, then craft an outer coating so hard that it will never lose its edge.

The core of a Pasara is designed to be springy and light, and will absorb the impact of blows. The outer coating is a much harder alloy, and the edge itself is diamond carbon fibers, which are sharpened to a point where they can slice apart bone.

When each alloy is ready, the forging, folding, and shaping processes begin. This can take a talented smith months to complete. The final quenching and purification process is always completed in the presence of the weapon's intended recipient. The artisan will complete the weapon, then the recipient will take it and strike a block of granite. If the granite is not marked at all, or the Pasara breaks, the artisan will begin again.

The greatest weapons and wielders have been known to slice through a full meter of granite, a remarkable feat for a hand weapon.

===Decoration===

All blades are decorated by the artisan as they are forged. A true masterwork blade has exquisite detail painted and etched into the metal itself, and the markings will never fade. The Pasara of [[Meronir Tronit]] still bear the cowled and cloaked figure of [[Joreno]] on the hilts. All artisans etch their signature into the blade before covering it with the hilt.

==Tesashue==

The [[Tesashue]] martial art focuses on quick movement, complex strategy, and outwitting an opponent. The Pasara dueling swords are the only weapons it trains specifically with. It has concepts and principles for modern weapons like pistols and rifles, but the practitioner of the art must learn these for themselves.

A [[Tesashue]] duelist will wield one Pasara in each hand. Personal styles vary: some prefer both blades up, others both blades down, and others alternating. A duel to first blood with Pasara often leads to maiming of an opponent. Duels to the death are quick and painful, but incredible displays of artistic martial talent.

The Juns are a [[human]] [[race]] native to [[Junheim]]. They have cross-bred with the [[Reigens]] for centuries.

===Appearance===

Juns average about 1.7 meters in height. They tend to be muscular and well-toned.

====Skin Tone====

Juns have slightly lighter skin tones, which have been darkened by their shared genetic stock with the [[Reigens]].

====Hair Color====

Juns have dark or light brown hair.

===Lifespan===

The average Jun lifespan is 80 to 100 years.

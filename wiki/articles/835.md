== Events of 835AD ==

Reports from [[Monora]], the domed research city on [[Hauvins]], indicate that [[Tolesca]] will be ready for colonization within a decade, very close to the original plan of terraformation in three centuries.  As in the past, construction of massive colony vessels commences, and automated satellites are sent ahead to prepare landing zones.

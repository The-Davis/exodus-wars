== Events of 2306 ==

===January===

===February===

* February 7 - Police Captain [[Joshua Beson]] finds an unusually large cache of crystal meth while serving a search warrant on a [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] establishment in [[Titan City]].
* February 18 - [[Joshua Beson]]'s investigative task force serves six additional search warrants, finding large caches of crystal meth at five more [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] establishments.

===March===

* March 5 - [[Joshua Beson]] requests the assistance of the [[Titan City]] Prosecutor's Office in gathering evidence against the local [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamura]] crime lords.

===April===

===May===

*May 17 - With the support of the [[Titan City]] Prosecutor's Office, [[Joshua Beson]] is appointed the Police Chief of the [[Titan City]] Police Department. [[Joshua Beson|Beson]] vows to fight a "war on drugs" and drive the crystal meth trade out of the [[Titan System]].

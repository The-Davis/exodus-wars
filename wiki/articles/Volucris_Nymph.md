===Overview===

The Soldier Nymph is the lowest form of Volucris, but it is the one the entire swarm is grown from.  It is also the first Volucris variety to meet human forces in battle, when a swarm of them attack Pluto Research Base and captured several human scientists.

[[Image:VolucrisNymph.jpg]]

The Nymph is nearly useless in battle, except for killing unarmored or lightly armored infantry.  In pure fighting, it's pair of claws are poor weapons against a Rig in one of the ADF armies.

The strength of the Nymph is in its adaptive DNA.  If not sent into battle, they can be matured in hatcheries to any of the higher Volucris breeds.  This metamorphosis takes time, anywhere from less than a day to two hundred years, depending on the type.

The Nymph is a fast little devil, skirting about the battlefield at 40KPH.  To keep it from dying right away, it has a semi-hardened reflective carapace, which is more resistant to laser weapons than ammo-fed weapons.

The Nymph is often deployed into battle by the hundreds and even thousands, depending on the scale of the battle.  Battle Queens can lay as many as 300 Nymph eggs each, and all Nymphs are ready to attack in seconds.  While they are useless for maneuvers or special tactics, they are relentless in their attacks on non-Volucris forces.

All of the matured morphs of the Nymph are used in combat, and are discussed in other pages in this archive.

==General Remnant Technical Note==
Almost all Remnant technology involves direct manipulation of gravity and space-time, which is managed through the creation and generation of graviton particles.  

It is important to note that a distortion in space-time – referred to as a ‘grav band’ by Remnant scientists – creates a gravitational force, and a gravitational force is a distortion in space-time.  All mass produces gravity, and gravity is a distortion of space-time’s shape.  

==Propulsion System:==

*'''Gravity Wave Drive'''
The Gravity Wave Drive is the central feature of Remnant naval technology.  All Remnant warships mount one.  Titanically powerful spatial-manipulation generators at the heart of the drive weave a net of stressed space around the equipped ship; the patterns, overlaps, and interactions of these stressed-space bands allow for a multitude of functions.  In essence, all gravity-based systems on a Remnant warship are provided by this drive.   

The basic concept of the ‘space-warp drive’ has been around since the twentieth century.  In essence, it postulates that if space is expanded in front of a ship while contracted behind it, the ship can move – rapidly – with no inertial effects.   The GWD is a very evolved version of this basic principle; Remnant scientists have created a pattern of gravity bands, shaped into a cone, designed for maximum efficiency and maneuverability.  

The Gravity Wave Drive provides a Remnant ship with propulsion in its basic mode.  It can also be outfitted with additional modules that allow it to provide defensive and stealth capabilities.

The GWD is extremely versatile; it is the foundation for most Remnant tactics.  No other race has a single system that provides so much flexibility from a single source.  Basic propulsion, the Eris gravity jammer, the Hammerhead missile system, the Spatial Distortion Cannons, and the Validis shield system are all functions provided by the Gravity Wave Drive.    

GWD Performance Chart:  This indicates what GWD functions can be used together, and the consequences of simultaneous use.

+Eris Cloak: Acceleration limited to 5% of maximum
+Eris Jammer: 5% acceleration penalty
+Hammerhead Missiles: No Eris system, no acceleration penalty
+SDC focused mode: 10% penalty to acceleration during SDC firing; no Eris
Note that Hammerheads, Validis, Eris Jammer, and SDCs can all be used simultaneously.  

*'''Drive Mode'''
 
''Overview''

All Remnant vessels use the Gravitic Wave Drive for propulsion.  It is a far more powerful and efficient version of the gravity drives that the other sentient races use.  Only the Ngrligru Shirang’oa Gravity Drive is comparable in performance. 

In its basic propulsive mode, the Gravitic Wave Drive is an unsubtle but reliable approach to propulsion.  It uses a network of gravitic distortions around a starship to propel the vessel by contracting space behind it and expanding it in the direction of acceleration.  Gravity and space are one and the same; a distortion in space produces a distortion of gravity, and the terms are practically interchangeable.
Acceleration values are so high that a smaller Remnant warship can reach .9 of lightspeed in a few minutes.  The GWD also provides exemplary maneuverability; Remnant warships can accelerate in any direction without actually turning. 

The wedge of gravitic distortion that the GWD produces is referred to as a ‘grav cone.’  The open end of the cone (which is visible as a field of distorted starlight, several kilometers in diameter at its widest point,) always points in the direction of the ship’s acceleration.  The ship itself is nestled just in front of the cone’s point.  At the ‘point’ of the cone – which would be directly astern if the ship was accelerating straight forward – is a small singularity.  
    
This creates a blindspot where no weapons can be fired and sensor data is unreliable.  Of course, the singularity can be exploited for tactical purposes; quick maneuvering can swing the singularity around to ‘eat’ incoming enemy weapons fire.

The singularity and the blind spot around it are referred to as the ‘eddy’ or the ‘grav wake.’      

''Technical Details''
	
The GWD operates on the same fundamental principles of spatial manipulation as the Gravity Cavitation Drive.  The Remnant has millennia of experience with spatial distortion, and thus the network of gravity flows used in the GWD has been fine-tuned for maximum efficiency and performance.  

In general, the GWD expands space in the direction of a ship’s movement, and contracts it behind the vessel; this provides rapid, inertialess acceleration.  The structure of the GWD can be visualized as a cone made of finely woven bands of stressed space; the open end of the cone faces in the direction of movement, and the ship rests inside the cone, just above the apex.  (This is a gross oversimplification, and glosses over many of the fine twists Remnant engineers have introduced into the process.)
  
The GWD is so powerful that it has a traumatic effect on local space; gravitational stresses at the ‘tip’ of the gravity cone that the ship rests inside create a tiny singularity, like that found at the heart of a black hole.  An everyday example might be the vortices that occur in the wake of a passing ship.

The ‘virtual mass’ of this singularity depends on the strength of the GWD field, which depends on how much power it has; thus, the faster a Remnant vessel accelerates, the more powerful the singularity ‘behind’ it becomes.  The singularity is small and is not as cataclysmically destructive as a full-sized black hole, but it does have its tactical uses.
Weapons cannot be fired in the direction of the singularity; its gravity field warps their courses, destroying missiles and bending energy beams off target.  Thus, the ‘back’ of a Remnant ship is a blind spot.  (Remnant vessels are equipped with ejectable missile pods that are used to defend this blind spot.)

''Tactical Role''

A basic Remnant tactic is to ‘back away’ from an overpowering enemy; the gravitic wave drive’s ‘gravity cone’ would have its open end facing away from the enemy ship, in the direction of motion, while the ‘eddy singularity’ would be facing the enemy vessel.  Although the singularity is too small to use as an effective weapon – it would have to be brought into direct contact with an enemy ship – it is an effective shield against weapon fire.

*'''Validis Shield Mode''' 

''Overview''

Although the Remnant Gravity Wave drive lacks the Ngrligru Shirang’oa’s ability to ‘fold its wake’ into an impervious defense while not accelerating, the GWD is still able to provide a powerful, tactically flexible defense in the form of the Validis shield.  

All Remnant vessels have a Validis system, which is a series of generator tunings and high-capacity power couplings added to the basic GWD.  The refit takes only a few days, and it’s been present on every Remnant warship for hundreds of years.  

The Validis system is the Remnant’s second-line shield; the first-line defense is the Aegis system, which is not tied into the Gravity Wave Drive – that would prevent the Aegis being ‘cast away’ in the event that it goes sentient.  The Validis is a very advanced version of the gravity shields that the Terrans and most other races use; it is roughly 500% more effective than Prefecture versions.    

The Validis destroys incoming missiles and projectiles by tearing them to shreds, and bends energy beams to the point where they (generally) miss.  Like all gravity shields, the Validis consists of a series of steep gravity gradients (called bands).  Transitions between these bands inflict terrible stress on incoming fire or objects.   

The Validis’ grav bands also block outgoing weapons fire, but ‘ports’ can be opened in the shield for outgoing fire to pass through.  Offensive capability is thus unimpaired.   

Unlike most gravity shields, which have dedicated generators, the Validis is generated by modifying the GWD’s propulsive grav cone.  When auxiliary reactors are used to power the Validis (feeding into the GWD), there is no loss of acceleration or maneuverability.  When the GWD reactors themselves are used to power the Validis (only done in emergencies,) then the shield is only 50% as strong, and the ship’s acceleration is halved.    

''Technical Details''

The Validis system takes advantage of the fact that the Gravity Wave Drive uses multiple bands of stressed space woven into a cone shape to provide propulsion.  When in Validis mode, auxiliary antimatter power plants feed power into the GWD’s grav cone (see the description of the Gravity Wave Drive’s propulsion system.)  
 
This additional power is used to create a new set of gravitational stress bands – essentially ribbons of warped space.  These are far more powerful than the bands used to propel the ship, and they are nested slightly inside the main grav cone.  

These bands wrap into a tight-knit shield of stressed space which deflects or destroys incoming weapons fire.  The Validis’ ‘inner shield’ does interfere slightly with the main grav cone, leading to a 10% drop in max acceleration when the Validis is active.

Because the Validis’ grav bands are configured in a different pattern than the propulsive grav cone, they do not feature an eddy singularity.  

Dedicated antimatter generators power the Validis grav shield, but if they are damaged, the main GWD generators can be pressed into service to generate an emergency shield.  This shield is 50% weaker than a standard Validis, and the main grav cone loses half of its stress bands, resulting in a 50% drop in acceleration.

*'''Hammerhead Missile System'''
These may or may not be used.

''Overview''

Hammerhead missiles are small, maneuverable anti-fighter weapons mounted in Remnant ships’ Anti-Starfighter Emplacements.  They draw power directly from a ship’s grav cone.

Each Hammerhead carries a ‘partial gravity drive’ that is capable of interfacing with the launch ship’s propulsive grav cone, but not of generating a full cone of its own.  Upon launch – carried out by a small gravity accelerator in the missile tube – the Hammerhead’s partial GWD powers up and begins feeding of the launch ship’s grav cone.   This allows them to accelerate and maneuver rapidly as long as they remain inside the cone.  

Once they leave the grav cone and head towards their target, each Hammerhead has no means of accelerating or decelerating, but it can still turn rapidly using its partial GWD.  They generally leave their launch vessel’s grav cone at tremendous speeds.  
	
If a Hammerhead is targeted at an enemy fighter inside the launch ship’s grav cone, it never misses; it is simply too fast and maneuverable to be evaded.  If targeted at an enemy outside the launch vessel’s grav cone, it still has far superior performance to most other race’s anti-fighter missiles.

Hammerheads carry small antimatter warheads.

It should be noted that Hammerheads cannot feed of a grav cone while the Eris cloak is in operation; the grav cone is too drastically reconfigured in stealth mode for the missile’s drive to function.  

''Tactical Role''

Hammerheads are fairly short-ranged, as they are intended for anti-fighter work.  They are utterly lethal inside their launch ship’s grav cone; outside of it, they still have a very high hit percentage, and their powerful antimatter warheads mean that they can, if fired in massive salvoes, even do minor damage to warships.
	
All Remnant ships carry at least twenty Hammerhead launch platforms, which rarely run out of ammunition.  

*'''Eris Gravity Jammer'''

With an Eris gravity jammer installed, a Remnant ship can tune its Gravity Wave generators to provide stealth against gravity sensors.  The Eris system is a powerful auxiliary AI module attached to the Gravity Wave drive; it handles the necessary calculations for the Gravity Wave drive’s gravity cloaking mode.  All of these AIs are traditionally referred to as ‘Eris’ in conversation.  

The main method modern warships, of any race, use to detect enemy vessels is the gravity sensor.  These sensitive devices can pick up the gravitational warps in space-time produced by any objects above a certain mass.  Generally, they’re quite accurate, and among the Terrans, Archadians, Icronians, and other less sophisticated races, gravity sensors have nearly neutralized the role of stealth in space combat.

Not so with the Remnant.  The Eris system, named after an ancient goddess of discord, calculates and generates an artificial gravity field using a technology distantly related to Icronian repulsors.  The field is generated by the ship’s Gravity Wave engines.     

At long ranges, this field can be used to ‘counteract’ a ship’s mass signature, canceling it out and rendering that ship invisible to gravity sensors.  

At closer ranges, even the advanced technology of the Remnant can’t defeat gravity sensors, so the Eris switches into jamming mode – throwing out powerful gravity pulses and thereby creating several false gravity images to deceive enemy targeting systems.  These pulses aren’t actually strong enough to attract or disturb enemy ships; they’re only as strong as the gravity of the Remnant ship itself.   

Unlike the Hecate system, large vessels don’t need more Eris pods to keep themselves cloaked – their larger Gravity Wave drives do the job.

The stronger a ship’s Gravity Wave drive in relation to its mass, the more effective the Eris is; thus, smaller Remnant vessels are stealthier.  However, the Eris system is not a flawless cloak.  

While in ‘cloak’ mode – the long range mass nullifier – the Gravity Wave Drive can only accelerate at 5% of rated maximum without nullifying the cloak.  Remnant ships are thus limited to a fraction of their possible acceleration while under stealth.

In combat situations, the Eris goes to ‘jamming’ mode, which leads to a 5% drop in drive efficiency – only a small portion of the gravity wave’s power is used for jamming.  95% of maximum acceleration is still available.  The advantage that the jamming provides far outweighs the performance drop.      

''Technical Details''

The Icronian repulsor system manipulates gravity with a stream of graviton particles, the quanta of gravity – just as photons are quanta of light.  The Eris system is basically a much more sophisticated, powerful, and finely tuned repulsor.  

In stealth mode at long ranges, the Eris system, which is a network of computers attached to the main Gravity Wave Drive, calculates necessary steps to neutralize the Remnant vessel’s gravity signature, and uses the Gravity Wave drive to generate a very finely tuned graviton field, sheathing the vessel.  

These gravitons – elementary particles that carry gravitational energy - are arranged in a particular pattern determined by advanced Remnant physics and the mass of the ship.  They have the effect of neutralizing the ship’s mass influence on the universe around it.  The ship still warps space-time and produces a gravity field, but that field is ‘cloaked’ by the Eris graviton shell. 

While it seems counterintuitive that a ship can retain its mass but not have any gravitational effect on the surrounding universe, Remnant multiphysics makes it possible.  Although the mathematics is complex, involving all 11 dimensions of Calabi-Yau space, conservation of energy, mass, and momentum are all observed.    

At close range, it’s very difficult to disguise a ship’s mass , so the Eris system changes modes and spews gravitons wildly.  Enemy gravity sensors see a wildly flickering target, constantly changing in shape, size, and mass.  It’s tough, but not impossible to get a target lock through the active gravity jamming.   

The gravity fields produced by both modes of the Eris have no other real effect beyond stealth.  They’re too dispersed to really affect weapons fire or enemy ships, just as the gravity field produced by a real vessel is too weak to bend particle beams or tear enemy ships apart.

Weaponry

*'''‘Katana’ Quantum Waveform Collapse Initatior (QWCI)'''

''Overview''

''Currently classified.''

The Katana reportedly generates a field that collapses the probabilistic quantum waveforms of the target’s subatomic particles, releasing bursts of zero-point energy as well as anomalous probability waves.  These ‘probon waves’ cause bizarre events within the blast radius of the weapon – including random decay of usually non-radioactive elements.  This may result in the entire target becoming highly radioactive.  Other events that have been observed within the blast radius include quantum tunneling, antimatter-matter conversions, nuclear fission or fusion, quantum entanglement, and more mundane molecular-level effects.

This weapon is mounted on only one ship, the Secutor, and is utterly devastating.  It is also finicky, and has gargantuan power requirements.  The Katana is deployed only in the most desperate of situations, but one blast can heavily damage even an Ngrligru mothership.  

''Technical Details''

''Current Classified.''

The Katana’s mechanism focuses on a ‘generation point’ at the target instead of producing a projectile or beam.  The mechanism then activates and charges for several minutes, during which time the Katana must remain trained on the target.  Upon the moment of firing, the focus point becomes – instantaneously – the center of some sort of probabilistic field.  This field causes the quantum wave-forms of subatomic particles within the blast radius to collapse, as per the dictates of quantum mechanics.  The result is a devastating blast of zero-point energy microbursts that reduces most matter within the blast radius to a haze of constituent atoms.  The area surrounding the blast radius is enveloped by anomalous probability waves.  The most common result of these waves is antimatter-matter polarization; that is, some of the matter within the field is converted to antimatter, resulting in an additional series of catastrophic explosions.  Almost is common is a sort of ‘alchemical radioactivity’ effect – atoms in the blast zone become highly radioactive, killing living organisms nearby.  

Other results observed have included quantum entanglement – in which some of the particles in the blast zone become ‘entangled’ with other particles, allowing any state-change in one particle to affect the other instantaneously with no regard for distance. This is less devastating than antimatter polarization, but can still be problematic.  Random bursts of quantum tunneling, when particles pass through solid matter, have also been observed, as has spontaneous fusion and fission.  Fluctuations in constants such as pi and the strength of gravity or the speed of light generally accompany the blast, as well. 

*'''Valor Main Turrets'''

''Overview''

The last remaining Praetoria-class warship, the Valor, mounts, as its main armament, projectile cannons of epic proportions.  These weapons are by no means sophisticated or elegant, but they are more effective than any Remnant weapon except the Katana system.
  
The Valor’s main guns are essentially gravitational mass drivers.  They run off some kind of non-localized zero-point energy tap, which draws upon the ‘free energy’ present in all of space-time.  This energy is fairly widely distributed, but the main guns are capable of drawing power from an area the size of a galactic arm (although this ability is not used to its fullest.)  The Remnant cannot, at present, duplicate these powerful zero-point taps; they were created at the height of the Master’s Empire.

In any case, the zero-point taps feed stupendous amounts of energy to the weapon’s gravity generators, which fling projectiles at speeds approaching 99.9% of the speed of light.  Even a nail launched at these velocities is a weapon of apocalyptic power.  The mass drivers can fire several different types of ordinance.

The primary ordinance type is the kinetic-energy round, which is a slug of neutronium – collapsed matter that literally contains no empty space; it is composed of neutrons in actual, physical contact, which never occurs under normal conditions.  It is indescribably more dense than normal matter.  When launched at relativistic velocities, the neutronium slug’s mass actually increases; when the weapon strikes its target, it contains enough kinetic energy to crack a planet open.

==Defenses==

*'''Aegis Quantum Intelligence Field'''

''Overview''

The Aegis Quantum Intelligence Field – usually referred to simply as the Aegis – is one of the most advanced shielding systems in known space, and is certainly the smartest and most elegant.  It is built for maximum reaction time, as Remnant engineers know that even nanoseconds can be crucial in deflecting incoming weaponry.   

Using a double-layered field, quantum computing, and an adaptable zero-point energy shield, it can react to incoming weaponry and calculate the best possible way of deflecting it.  It is thus one of the best shields in existence – adaptable, versatile, and powerful.  The more an Aegis encounters a particular weapon type, the better it becomes at defeating that weapon. 

As it is exposed to more variety and quantity of weapons fire, the Aegis grows more and more effective at countering that particular type of weapon - adapting and developing better techniques.  After sufficient time and experience, an Aegis system may be nearly invulnerable to all forms of attack it has encountered.  The process of learning has a side effect – the Aegis develops a proto-intelligent mind, one that is not self-aware, but has instincts and emotions.  

Unfortunately, if the field performs weapon-deflecting calculations for more than roughly two hours, it’s proto-mind becomes so sophisticated and experience that it develops sentience, locks itself into a single quantum state, and effectively becomes useless – even dangerous.  The shield can create enormous radiation spikes, and feed of zero-point energy to maintain itself even if the generator loses power.  It is also emotionally volatile.

To avoid sentience, the shield must have its ‘memory wiped’ after it has encountered a good deal of incoming weapons fire.  This makes the Aegis far less effective, but it's better than seeing it become sentient and ‘go rogue.’  A full Aegis wipe takes about ten minutes, but some Remnant captains prefer to carry out a partial wipe, keeping the Aegis on the threshold between full effectiveness and intelligence.

''Technical Details''

The Aegis system uses a two-layered arrangement. The inner layer is the brute force of the system – the shield itself.  It is a roiling mass of stimulated zero-point energy fluctuations that can be controlled by Remnant technology.  Effectively, Layer 1 is a mass of possibilities – the zero-point reactions can be ‘modified’ to produce practically any other kind of defense imaginable.  It should be noted that the field can only produce a maximum of two or three of these defenses at a time – depending on the type.  The modification of the zero-point flux requires staggering amounts of energy.

The outer layer of the Aegis shield is the brains of the system.  Consisting of a field of turbulent, quantum-connected, non-causal space-time, Level 2 is effectively a massive, remote quantum computer.  Whenever a projectile, energy beam, or other type of weapon system strikes Level 2, the incoming fire affects the field in some way.  By ‘observing’ the weapon’s effect on the quantum field, the Level 2 computer can, via non-local quantum computing, immediately perform a massive quantity of calculations and determine the best way of defending against the weapon impact.  This information is then used to modify the Level 1 shields and produce the best possible defense against the incoming weapon.

It is interesting to note that the Level 2 computer system operates not on the rules of digital computers, or even on the basis of a ‘normal’ quantum computer.  Its behavior is instead dictated by the quantum-level interactions of individual particles.  Thus, the Quantum Intelligence Field is understood only be a few of the Remnant’s greatest scientists.  Because of the fact that its computations are grounded in the fundamental nature of space-time, and that there is no actual ‘hardware’ directly responsible for the computing, the Level 1 and 2 systems have some bizarre properties.

The Quantum Intelligence Field’s versatility relies on the fact that it can very rapidly change its quantum configuration through billions and billions of possibilities to find the one best-suited to reacting to the incoming weapons.  As time passes, and the Quantum Intelligence Field experiences a greater number of weapon types, it also goes through more and more quantum configurations.  These configurations become part of its ‘memory’, and the system thus becomes more effective and is quicker to assume those configurations in the future.  The Aegis shield, in effect, can ‘remember’ weapons it has encountered before.  

Eventually, after an average two hours of processing – that is, reacting to enemy weapons fire – the Quantum Intelligence Field assumes a configuration that gives it sentience. It may assume this configuration much earlier, but the probability is low.

It locks itself into this configuration and refuses to adapt any further – making it totally useless against enemy weaponry.  The shield then becomes extremely hard to handle; it is emotionally volatile and generates radiation and gravitic spikes, some of which may destroy the ship it is protecting.  Furthermore, the intelligence that is created is usually quite alien and resembles nothing ever seen before – it is unique and often quite incomprehensible.  

The only way to cure this bizarre disease is to physically remove the quantum-field generator or destroy it.  This doesn’t destroy the field – which is now self-sustaining off its own zero-point energy field – but it does cut it loose from the ship.  No abandoned sentient Aegis has ever been seen again.

Some critics of the system worry about ‘rogue shields’ wandering around space, causing havoc.  Given the desperation of the war with the Ngrligru, they have been 
largely ignored.

*'''Neutronium Armor'''

''Overview''

The last-ditch defense system on any Remnant vessel is its armor.  Remnant warships use neutronium-foam armor; the vast majority of the plating is composed of proton-bond armor superior in quality to that used by any other race.  This provides outstanding all-purpose defense against most attacks, but is vulnerable to Ngrligru energy weapons and hyper-penetrating warheads.  

Thus, the basic proton-bond armor is supplemented with a neutronium foam.  Neutronium is hypercollapsed, super-dense matter, the heaviest substance in the universe; it provides colossal resistance to kinetic and energy attacks, including Ngrligru weapons.  Due to weight requirements, however, the neutronium makes up only about .01% of the armor structure.

''Technical Details''

Neutronium is truly solid matter; while normal matter is composed mostly of empty space, the neutrons (neutrally charged subatomic particles) composing a slab of neutronium are actually in contact with each other.  This makes neutronium chemically inert and unbelievably strong.  

The obvious side effect of this near-infinite density is tremendous mass.  A small amount of neutronium, dropped from arm level, would release as much energy as an atomic warhead when it struck the ground.

Remnant armor includes specially tuned gravity generators that help counteract the immense mass of the neutronium armor.  If Remnant scientists could develop more efficient and powerful built-in generators, Remnant ships might be able to mount armor that included more neutronium; this would grant them near-insuperable toughness.

Although neutronium foam makes up only a small fraction of Remnant hull armor, it is absolutely essential to ship survivability.  Ngrligru weapons are capable of penetrating even Remnant proton-bond armor with alarming ease.

''Tactical Information''

Standard proton-bond armor can be breached by concentrating immense amounts of force (kinetic or energy) on a small area.  Ngrligru weapons do this with alarming ease.  Ships mounting standard Remnant proton-bond, without neutronium reinforcement, suffer more than 90% armor penetration against Ngrligru weapons.

With neutronium foam reinforcement, this penetration falls to just above 15% - still alarmingly high.  Fortunately, Remnant vessels rely mostly on their shields and speed for defense.  If a Remnant ship finds itself in a situation where it needs its armor, something has gone drastically wrong.

==Stealth Technology==

Ever since the Great Devouring, the Remnant has been outnumbered, outgunned, and often fleeing for their lives.  Consequentially, they have the most advanced stealth technology of any known sentient race.  Relying on quantum trickery and arcane twisting of space-time, Remnant stealth devices give the outnumbered Master forces a huge advantage whether attacking, scouting, or retreating.  

With a full suite of Hecate, Eris, and Chameleon systems, a Remnant ship is invisible to radar, microwave radiation, optical sensors, thermal sensors, gravity sensors, and most other forms of targeting gear.  No stealth is flawless, though, and there are many other ways a clever enemy can detect a stealthy Remnant vessel.  The Remnant is advanced, but they are not omnipotent.  

''Weaknesses''

The most obvious weakness to ALL this stealth gear is that if the stealthed ship (or infantryman, or vehicle) fires weapons, it’s an easy matter for a foe to backtrack the launch points and figure out their general location.

Another one is that shield systems tend to interfere with most of the stealth gear presented here.  A ship that raises its Validis or Aegis system is much easier to detect.  So is one that arms weapons or otherwise uses a large amount of power.   

*'''Hecate Cryo-arithmetic Cloak'''

''Description''

The Hecate system is common on Remnant spacecraft.  It uses an obscure loophole of quantum physics to move heat out of the local area and transport it elsewhere in the universe without actually violating the laws of thermodynamics.  

The Hecate is set so that it cools down the hull of a warship to the temperature of surrounding space.  The ship becomes thermally invisible – it gives off no heat, and in terms of radiation, blends in perfectly with the void around it.    

Only the hull of the ship is affected; the interior is not, so the crew doesn’t freeze.  Each cryo-arithmetic cloak can only cover a certain amount of hull, so larger Remnant ships have multiple Hecate systems installed in order to disguise their entire heat signature.

In combination with mimetic coating and a gravity-sensor jammer, the Hecate system renders a Remnant ship invisible to most sensors.  

The Hecate does have a serious quirk.  If it’s badly damaged, it can overload and start pulling too much heat out of local space.  Instead of matching the temperature of nearby space, the hull temperature of the ship will plummet towards absolute zero, probably killing the crew.  Fortunately, the device can usually be jettisoned in time.    

Each Hecate generator is quite small, and is embedded in the hull of the ship.   

''Technical Details''

The Hecate is a quantum computer.  In physics, any action that changes matter or energy from one state to another can be referred to as a ‘computation,’ and any system that carries out that action is a ‘computer.’  

The cryo-arithmetic cloak is such a system.  It takes advantage of a multitude of quantum effects to transmit heat energy out of local space, using a counterintuitive quirk of quantum mechanics.  It effectively processes the heat out of local existence.       

If installed on a non-Remnant ship, a Hecate system probably wouldn’t work.  Each Hecate is carefully attuned to the hull characteristics of the ship it is installed on, to make sure that it affects only the hull and nothing else.  If an Ngrligru or other enemy of the Remnant were foolish enough to install a Hecate on their ship and activate it without going through the proper tuning procedures, it would act like a cryo-arithmetic mine – freezing the entire ship and killing the crew.

*'''Chameleon Mimetic Coating'''

''Description''

The Chameleon system is built into the hull of Remnant ships, as well as some ground vehicles and infantry suits.  It is a system of sensors and holographic emitters that projects a false image onto the hull of the ship (or vehicle, or suit).  

Basically, this image lets the vessel blend perfectly into the background.  The holographic camouflage lets it appear exactly like whatever’s behind it, no matter the angle.  

The system is fairly simple, and therefore rather robust.  It’s the least sophisticated of all the Remnant stealth technology.  It’s used to defeat SPAR systems and other optical-targeting gear.

The Chameleon skin also has a sophisticated EAM coating, or Electromagnetic Absorbent Material, which absorbs incoming electromagnetic radiation all across the speectrum.  It’s thus practically invisible to radar or microwave systems.    

''Technical Details''

Based on simple technology, there’s nothing remarkable about the Chameleon.  It uses components that have been around for thousands of years (in Master civilization, at least.)  They’ve all been refined quite a bit – the holograph system, for example, requires no projectors but is woven right into the hull of the ship – but still rely on many of the same basic principles.  Even Terrans have had EAM material for hundreds of years.

==Systems==

'''Drone Technologies'''

*''Hive Mind''

The Hive Mind is most simply explained as a battlefield network.  This network collects sensor data from every Remnant unit present and combines it to create a unified picture of the battlefield.  This picture is then accessible to every commander and unit in the Remnant force.  

The Hive Mind works by utilizing the Artificial Intelligences of all involved starships, mechs, and starfighters.  Each of these aforementioned types of units is equipped with an Artificial Intelligence of varying ability based upon its intended role.  The most powerful AIs are mounted aboard starships, while the least powerful are mounted on mechs.  
All of the AIs involved in the battle have some of their capacity pooled together to form a collective single “mind,” similar to how the Terran SETI program of the 20th and 21st century combined various computers to create a network of supercomputers.  Although singular AIs are capable of running the Hive Mind, this practice is not preferred because of its vulnerability to disruption.  This allows for flexibility and keeps the process decentralized.  Although decentralized, the Hive Mind allows the area commander to issue orders with frightening speed and efficiency.  The loss of one of the contributing AIs will not affect the Hive Mind’s abilities.  In other words, the only way to destroy a Hive Mind network is to destroy every single AI present.  Even then, destruction of the network is not guaranteed.

On top of the tactical and operational level networks formed locally, all information is relayed back to Remnant High Command’s Strategic Analysis Command, which is a massive network of AIs working together to provide the same battlefield picture to High Command and preserve all of the data for historical reference.  This network is dispersed among the worlds and installations of the Remnant, protecting it from disruption.  Data collected by SAC is then analyzed and dispersed to all Remnant Autonomous Combat Units during their routine upgrades.  
The Hive Mind is important to the operation of Autonomous Combat Units because it allows them to work together through the network to collaborate their efforts and respond to changing situations.  

If, for some reason, an Autonomous Combat Unit were unable to connect with the Hive Mind, it would still be able to function to fulfill its mission because of the routine data/programming upgrades provided by SAC.  The Autonomous Combat Units are fully capable of independent operation; the Hive Mind is only utilized to enhance each ACU’s battlefield picture.  The nature of ACU programming also allow them to create less complex temporary networks between local units to facilitate communication and collaboration in the absence of a Hive Mind network.  Although functional, these types of networks lack the complete picture and command oversight of a real Hive Mind network.        

*''Programming'' 

Each Autonomous Combat Unit (ACU) benefits from the centuries of Remnant military experience.  These experiences have been studied and accumulated into a single comprehensive program that contains counters to every maneuver and tactic known to the Remnant.  Rarely are Remnant ACUs surprised by an opponent, and whenever this rare event occurs, the information is collected and analyzed and a response prepared for future encounters.  These new encounters are then incorporated into the control program which is downloaded to Remnant ACUs at regular intervals.  The control program is uploaded onto the ACU in a manner similar to the ancient concept of Random Access Memory, where if power is lost so too is the program to prevent capture.  

*''Power Generation''

Drones within the Remnant have a finite powered lifespan dependant upon the amount of Anti-Matter pellets they carry.  These pellets are used in reactions with their corresponding matter to power the ACU.  These pellets come in easily swappable containers allowing for quick replacement in battle.  Although such a system would be prohibitively expensive for any other race, the Remnant uses vast quantities of anti-matter for varying purposes from power generation to weaponry, thus making its use commonplace and relatively cheap.

*''Self-Destruction''   
	
Drones within the Remnant carry a vast amount of knowledge and information, from battle tactics to designs for instantaneous communications to advanced power generation techniques.  To prevent these systems and knowledge from being used against us, every Autonomous Combat Unit is equipped with a self-destruct mechanism.  This mechanism is engaged if the ACU determines that it will be captured or runs out of power, utilizing a small anti-matter charge to completely destroy the ACU preventing its capture.

The charge can be detonated in a variety of ways.  If power is lost, and thus the control program deleted because of a disabling blow or other failure, the charge is blown.  Similarly, if a breach of the unit’s armor is detected then the charge is blown.

The nature of the charge is such that it is the least amount of ant-matter required for complete vaporization of the unit, and no more.  Usage of additional anti-matter would take away from the available power generation and combat endurance.  Whenever the self-destruct is activated, a warning signal is transmitted to all adjacent Remnant units giving them a ten second window to get clear.  Other drones would be able to fly from the area while infantry, which are always in Mk. II suits, would “power jump” away.  All other ground equipment is capable of hover or jump-jet flight.

'''Sensors'''

The Remnant utilizes its quantum entanglement, advanced computer systems, stealth systems, and drone technologies to create the most complete real-time picture of the battle-space possible.  Heavily dependent upon heavily stealthed drones, the Remnant creates an all-encompassing sphere around designated targets and battle-spaces.  These drones, combined with the ships' ultra-powerful sensors, all combine information into one system known as "Argus" which gives the commanders an unprecedented picture of the space in question.  Once a Remnant observation system is in place, it can transmit to anywhere in the galaxy the information which it gathers.  Additionally, these observation systems have incredible stamina, being able to function for decades at a time.  The most common activity for the smallest of Remnant vessels is maintaining these systems in far-flung and remote systems throughout the galaxy.

Ceylon was an island nation in South Asia, located about 31 kilometers (18½ mi) off the southern coast of India. It was a [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth Nation]] until it signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] in [[2011]].

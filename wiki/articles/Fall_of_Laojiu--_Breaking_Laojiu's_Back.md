Set after "Tactical Insertion"

by James Davis

=====The Laojiu District=====
     The Mesarthrim hesitated some time before taking Laojiu or Labrea, but in May they were ready. Over 800,000 Mesarthrim troops landed on Laojiu, and 100,000 land on Labrea in pre-dawn coordinated strikes.
     Mesarthrim fighters flew continuous sorties, keeping the few surviving Prefecture squadrons on their toes and the gathered militia and military forces heads down.
     On Laojiu, the troops seize major cities, ports, and space stations. They engage local militias and the garrisons still in the hands of Prefecture troops. Mesarthrim troops under the command of General Walther Garnlin, seize Xiamen, Tianjin, and Wuhan within a few hours.
     On Labrea, the UCS lands 35,000 troops on the continent of Dubh, 200 kilometers south of the city of Härdad. They set up a base and fortify their position, but don't advance on the primarily-Icronian frontier city. While a significant economic boon to Labrea, the city holds little strategic value beyond a possible focus for resistance.
     Another 65,000 troops land to the south of the Fathach Méire mountain range. They are nearly 800 kilometers from Tara'dar Coirp, and cut off the Prefecture's primary route to the city of Laidebaile in the south.
     The Mesarthrim forces clash with some elements of the Prefecture's Labrea Garrison, quickly putting them down while making their way towards the city.
     Brigadier Parrish sees a glimmer of hope. For some reason, the Mesarthrim have landed a fraction of the forces Parrish has at his disposal. The Labrea garrison is well-manned, with over 300,000 troops on the ground, all equipped with heavy weaponry. A mere 50,000 are stationed in Härdad, but 175,000 are entrenched in the Fathach Méire mountain range, and 75,000 are stationed in the city of Tara'dar Coirp. The Mesarthrim may be in for a surprise.

== Events of 1948 ==

=== January ===

* January 1 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslim militants lay siege to the Jewish Quarter of the Old City of Jerusalem.
* January 22 - [[Cold War]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] foreign secretary Ernest Bevin proposes the formation of a Western Union between [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]], [[France (Terra)|France]], [[Belgium (Terra)|Belgium]] and the [[Netherlands (Terra)|Netherlands]] to stand up against the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

=== February ===

* February 1 - [[Cold War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] begins to jam Voice of America broadcasts.
* February 4 - [[Ceylon (Terra)|Ceylon]] becomes independent of the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]], but remains a [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth Nation]].
* February 25 - [[Cold War]]: The [[Communist]] Party seizes control of [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]].

=== March ===

* March 10 - [[Cold War]]: [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czech]] foreign minister Jan Masaryk murdered by the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] KGB by pushing him out of a window of his apartment in Prague. The [[Communist]] government of [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]] rules it suicide.

=== April ===

* April 7 - The World Health Organization is established by the [[United Nations]].
* April 9 - [[The Jihad]]: The Deir Yassin massacre takes place in Palestine.

=== May ===

* May 1 - [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czech]] [[Communists]] execute 213 people as counter-revolutionaries.
* May 14 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]] becomes an independent nation.
* May 15 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt, Syria, Iraq and Saudi Arabia]] attack [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]].

=== June ===

* June 3 - [[The Technology Race]]: The Palomar Observatory telescope is finished in California.
* June 16 - [[Cold War]]: [[Communist]] guerillas kill three rubber planters in Malaya.
* June 18 - [[Cold War]]: State of Emergency declared in Malaysia when a [[Communist]] insurgency begins.
* June 24 - [[Cold War]]: The Berlin Blockade begins.
* June 25 - The first World Health Assembly of World Health Organisation is held in Geneva.

=== July ===

* July 15 - [[Cold War]]: The attempted assassination of Palmiro Togliatti, general secretary of the [[Italy (Terra)|Italian]] [[Communist]] Party, incites number of strikes all over the country.
* July 20 - [[Cold War]]: President [[Harry Truman]] issues the second peacetime military draft in the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] amid increasing tensions with the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

=== August ===

* August 14 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslims in [[Arabia (Terra)|Pakistan]] celebrate the one year anniversary of driving out the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]].
* August 19 - [[Cold War]]: [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] troops fire at [[East Germany (Terra)|German]] demonstrators that protest against the Berlin Blockade.

=== September ===

* September 11 - [[The Jihad]]: Death of Muhammad Ali Jinnah, first leader of [[Arabia (Terra)|Pakistan]].

=== October ===

=== November ===

* November 2 - [[United States (Terra)|United States]] presidential election: [[Thomas Dewey]] defeats [[Harry Truman]] and becomes the thirty-fourth President of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]].
* November 16  - [[The Jihad]]: Operation Magic Carpet to transport Jews from [[Arabia (Terra)|Yemen]] to [[Israel (Terra)|Israel]] begins.
* November 24 - [[Cold War]]: In [[Venezuela (Terra)|Venezuela]], president Rómulo Betancourt is ousted by a [[Communist]]-inspired military coup. A military junta takes over the government.

=== December ===

* December 10 - [[United Nations]] General Assembly adopts Universal Declaration of Human Rights.
* December 28 - [[The Jihad]]: Member of the Muslim Brotherhood assassinates [[Arabia (Terra)|Egyptian]] Prime Minister Mahmud Fahmi Nokrashi.
* December 31 - [[The Jihad]]: [[Israel (Terra)|Israeli]] troops drive [[Arabia (Terra)|Egyptians]] from Negev.

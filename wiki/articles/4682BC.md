== Events of 4682BC ==

The population of the Remnant slowly but surely transitioned back to a planetary lifestyle.  Eventually, only those who maintained the ancient vessels of the fleet remained on the ships, and the lives on the planets became increasingly comfortable.

In this time, great space works were planned and constructed in an attempt to revive the glory of the Anthronoris Empire.  The original void world defenses were replaced with massive stations and outposts.  The Empress’ palace, the Praesidium, glistened so brightly that it was visible for kilometers in every direction and from space.

The restless citizens of the City of Thought called for further exploration of the surrounding space; something that had been avoided to keep the Ologrians from discovering them.  Their voices were loud enough to get the Empress and the High Council to act.  An expedition was assembled and sent to the surrounding systems to determine their viability for building a new void world.  A new void world, dubbed Aegis Invictus, was soon built.

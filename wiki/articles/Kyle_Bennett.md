'''Kyle Bennett'''
*Place of Birth: [[Kalmar]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Kyle Bennett'' is an officer in the [[Legion of the Phoenix]], serving under General [[Travis Carpenter]]. He is famous for his contrasting tempers: calm and polite in the heat of battle, and a firebrand in normal circumstances.

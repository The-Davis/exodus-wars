The '''Tsirada Class Battleship''' is the most powerful class of warships ever fielded by the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], and yet they are virtually unknown to other races.  Although Tsiradas make up the cores of Taviridis fleets, they are not patrol vessels, transports, or escorts--they are ships of battle and only venture forth to wage war.  As such, they have only been seen in foreign space a handful of times.

== Where Giants Tread ==

<small><small>("Where Giants Tread" was written by a [[Kalidasa]]n trader after witnessing a Tsirada Class Battleship and a [[Meroniri]] [[Tronit Class War Cruiser]] face off near the shipping lanes of the [[Lupei System]])</small></small>

Peering across the empty gulf of space, two titans warily eye one another.  Each one watches, willing...''daring'' the other to make the first move.  Two dragons whose paths should never have crossed, separated by the unfathomable vastness of space, prepare to unleash cataclysm.  These champions of [[Tolana Sector|Tolana]] and [[Unai Sector|Unai]]-—have met after long millennia apart, and they hold the power of the gods!

Caught between such foes, men flee in their ships to safer ports.  Merchants and traders, travelers and the watch, all scatter to the four corners to escape the expected onslaught.  They are wise to flee. In battle, these children of the ancients can unleash such power as was not seen in the War for All Earth, and they stand now ready to bring on Ragnarök.

Treading to the very brink of war, cooler heads at last prevail and pride is sacrificed for peace.  This clash shall not transpire—not today.

Lupei shipping traffic returns to normal as the Tsirada and the Tronit depart the system, returning to their berths...for now.

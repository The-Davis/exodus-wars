===Overview===

The most feared sight for any enemy of the Blackis Prefecture is not the Jerome Black or a similar flagship.  It is the Eagle Class Dropship.  When Eagles are involved in a campaign, it means that the Prefecture is playing hardball.

An Eagle can carry in an entire Division of troops into battle.  Even an entire Legion only requires six Eagles to carry them all from world to world.  The mighty Eagle is the first Rahn Industries ship built with anti-gravity lift systems licensed from Blazton Heavy Industries.

===Capabilities===

The Eagle is not built to land on solid ground.  It can land in an ocean, and can float on its anti-gravity lifters only a dozen or so feet above the surface.  An Eagle is very vulnerable on the seas or floating above ground, but unloading only takes half an hour.

Eagles are well-armed, but its shields, acceleration rate, and maneuverability make it ill-suited for warship-to-warship combat.  The weapons are for softening up a landing zone and keeping enemy fighters away from the Eagle while it's unloading.

===Carrying Capacity===

Eagles are built to carry anything.  They can even take multiple Brigadiers, Belugas, or a single Whale Class dropship in docking, to speed up the landing process.

Infantry, Marines, and Battle Armor are not the primary cargo of Eagles, but each carries a total of 980.  Only the Marines stay aboard.  The Infantry and Battle Armor are dropped in on Belugas and drop PODS to help the Army Division.

Rigs are the kings of ground battles, so Eagles carry a lot.  A total of 144 Rigs of various sizes can be carried and deployed very quickly.  Most Rig pilots are not permanently quartered aboard the Eagle.  Rig pilots have a home base, and are only picked up by an Eagle when its time for an invasion.  They usually loiter in the hangar during transport, the only large open space available.

The permanent fighter complement of an Eagle isn't very large.  There are only 32.  Most of them are for air support, but it does carry four Bloodhawk bombers and four Phoenixes for saturation bombardment and pinpoint attacks to help the ground troops.

Tanks and VTOLs are also carried by Eagles, but they reside in drop PODS in the underbelly of the dropship.  They are deployed after the infantry and Rigs have secured the landing zone.

===Deployment===

Most of the time, Eagles are stationed in dry-dock, with no crew or cargo.  When a large army operation is underway, the Eagles are stocked and loaded with its assigned troops.

Eagles are sent into a planet system after the Navy has taken at least partial control of the safe orbits, and after the desirable landing zones have been carpet bombed from orbit.  Once they drop off their troops, they either land in an ocean or large lake, or return to orbit. Eagles always remain close by to get the troops out quickly if the ground campaign goes poorly.

'''Israel''' was a country in the Middle East located on the southeastern edge of the Mediterranean Sea. It was surrounded on all sides by the hostile Muslim empire of [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]] for much of its history, but was never conquered. Israel's ruthless response to Muslim incursion, coupled with material support from the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]], kept the nation from falling during [[World War III]].

Israel's fiercely independent population did not agree to sign on to the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] until the Charter was ammended in [[2037]] to allow for localized regions to adopt an official religion, something originally forbidden by the Charter in the world-wide effort to stamp out Islam. The regional government of Israel was allowed to proclaim Judaism as the official local religion, but was not allowed to mandate observing it as a requirement to live in the area.

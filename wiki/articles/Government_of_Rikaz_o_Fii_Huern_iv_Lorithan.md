==The Prekohr==

The Prekohr is the supreme ruler of Rikaz Lorithos. The first Prekhor--the Mircan Prekohr, or Dawn Emperor--was [[Hamur Huern]], who ruled thousands of years before the [[Burning Wars]]. The last Prekhor to rule by divine right was [[Talire Huern]]--the Ashen Prekohr, or Twilight Empress--whose death ushered in the Burning Wars.

Today, the Prekohr is [[Dirken Huern]], the great grandson of [[Talire Huern]]. [[Dirken Huern]] was elected by a council of [[Lorithos]] to serve as Prekohr, but recent shifts in [[Lorithos]] culture back to their roots has made him a de facto ruler by divine right.

==The Hishai==

The Hishai is the supreme leader of the [[Lorithos]] military, and the second in command of the [[Lorithos]] as a whole. The position has lost some of its respect since the last Hishai before the current one was [[Goff Riiktar]], who betrayed the Huern family and tried to become Prekohr himself. Today's Hishai, [[Sanor Cai]], is fiercely loyal to [[Dirken Huern]] and the people of [[Lorithos]], and has taken great pains to restore the honor of the position of Hishai.

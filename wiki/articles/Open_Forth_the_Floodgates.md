=====Lorithos Snooper Corvette Vilak - 1135 Hours=====
     Meroniri Ansible Station J-137-a is located in the outer-rim of Meroniri territory, and is one of hundreds in Meroniri space. It sees little traffic from the handful of outer colonial stations. Only daily status reports from the Meroniri border outpost Far Station Alpha-1 are sent regularly.
     A sudden burst of chatter has tweaked the interest of the Lorithos intelligence ship. This backwater post has just become the center of serious attention.
     "Captain Vaison, we've got some serious chatter going on through the Ansible node. It's definitely Meroniri, and it looks like it's a military encryption."
     "Sounds interesting. Tap into the node, there's no harm in listening in."
     "I'm patching it through to your station now."
     ---Begin Decryption---
     "Sepulcre Central, this is Far Station Alpha-1. We're forwarding a transmission from Captain Godrin Willks."
     "This is Captain Willks of the Tronict cruiser Hillig on the outskirts of zone Alpha-1. We've picked up a massive energy spike. Computers say that they are hyperspace exits, but this is far too big. It's big enough to send a moon through. I'm forwarding all sensor data. We will continue to monitor, and will send an update at 1200 Hours. Willks out."
     "Captain Willks, thank you for the update. We will analyze and respond. Report to Far Station Alpha-1 as well. Sepulcre Central, out."
     ---End Decryption---
     "What do you think Captain?"
     "Keep us jacked into the node, and record the sensors readings as they come in. Open up a line to the Intelligence and Communications Department. Maybe they can decipher what's going on."

=====Lorithos Snooper Corvette Vilak - 1145 Hours=====
     "Captain, there's another transmission coming through. It's from Sepulcre this time."
     "Decrypt and send it over to me." 
     "It'll take a moment, but I'll send it."
     ---Begin Decryption---
     "Priority 1 Message Incoming:"
     "We've analyzed the sensor readings. It's not a massive hyperspace signature, it is many of them close together. Withdraw immediately to Far Station Alpha-1. Local garrisons and patrols are being mobilized, and we'll have reinforcements from the Armada there in a few hours. Hold position as long as you can. Keep the sensor feed coming."
     "Sepulcre Central, are you sure those readings are right? Shouldn't we take a closer look?"
     "Negative. Return to Far Station Alpha-1 immediately."
     ---Emergency Interrupt---
     "Sepulcre Central, this is Far Station Alpha-1! We're under attack!"
     ---Transmission Lost---
     "Sir, the Ansible's gone down on the far end. The Alpha-1 sector transmitter has stopped working."
     Captain Vaison sits back in his command chair, taking in what he's just heard. Hundreds of hyperspace signatures? The Meroniri under attack? Who and what could attack, and why out here?
     "Communication, get this to the ICD pronto. Something big is going down, and it's got the Meroniri shaken. We've got to figure out what's going on. Request permission from the fleet for closer observation."
     "You mean us, Captain?"
     "We're going to sneak in unnoticed to take a look."
     "Aye, Captain."

=====Archadian Subspace Corvette Aurion Sov - 1215 Hours=====
     The bridge of an Archadian warship is eerily quiet, especially when submerged in subspace. The majority of the command crew is tied into the starship via Level 2 bioneural interfaces, and the rest of the crew sits quite still, their Level 4 interfaces connecting them directly with their control stations. 
     Suddenly, the communications officer receives a notice on his screen. Without a word, the crew springs to life as the information is analyzed and transferred over the ship's APACHE system.
     "Komen, tav requis toris visa de isir." ((Captain, you'd better have a look at this!))
     "Oru vahn ibrok dohmet?" ((What is it helmsman?))
     "Opit vahm artis wirris di lader pav vrist-aspac dohmgit. Oru vahn strig mah voris lader de varia...Meroniri do donik visa." ((We're getting comm chatter on the trans-space receivers. It's definitely long-range comm of some type, Meroniri by first glance.))
     "En yevig utilt Ansibet?" ((They're not using the Ansible?))
     "Isir vahn regiut par viridanis pag viridanis mah voris lader. Bit Ansibet oru pria vahn qist...En oru iva adisagmidan." ((This is standard ship-to-ship long-range comm. Ansible must be down...it's not even encrypted.))
     "Opi statio coordas. Varioru requis vahn pondik. Bit Meroniri adisagmi todoru, callispe vo isir jerrit. Isir piyitio eti relatio isir pag op." ((Hold position. Something must be wrong, the Meroniri encrypt everything, especially in this sector. Scrub it* and transfer it to me.)) *Scrub it=Translate it
     "Sepulcre Central, this is Far Station Alpha-1. More hostiles incoming! (indecipherable)"
     "(indecipherable)...number of hostiles? Say again, what is the strength of the enemy force?"
     (APACHE - Frequency Change: Initiate)
     "...Commander Tomlinson, bring your cruisers hard to port. There's incoming warships at 13 (indecipherable)"
     "...comm., call out all reserves from Station Alpha-1. Launch all fighters! Get me...(indecipherable)"
     (APACHE - Frequency Change: Initiate)
     "More fighters incoming at 41-18. Fighters: screen incoming coordinates"
     "...and the computer's estimating nearly 20,000 fighters. Calling all squadrons! (indecipherable)"
     "...squadron leader's down! Remnants of squadrons 1-S-8 through 1-S-31, form up on (individual feed lost)"
     "Ladermet, contig lacitio isir paglim bit yim furtahn. Parlim, wernvatio opit pag nahmin-aspac. Admiraltin, tav pria brilaht isir." ((Comm., keep this recording until the feed stops, then bring us to real space. Admiralty's going to need to hear this.))
     "En isir ponik, Komen." ((Of course, Captain.))
     "Dohmet, ibrok vahn bit asi-tempos pav isir?" ((Comm., what's the lag-time on this?))
     "En op vah ava. Opit vahm escate atempos. Opit vahm li masir par bit viridobis bi Meroniri. Bit nurgi yevig pria vaht follidate varioru trivut di aspac no port atis masir." ((I'm not sure. We're scanning now. We're quite far from the Meroniri station. The signals must be riding some sort of spatial anomaly to make it this far.))
     "Opi contig lacitio." ((Just keep recording.))

=====Meroniri Border, Zone Alpha-1 - 1400 Hours=====
     The charred hulk of what was once an Meroniri military station drifts through space, venting what little atmosphere is left into space. Zone Alpha-1, located only a few light years from the furthest Meroniri border world is a graveyard of warships, fighters, and various other craft that were caught in the sudden, but deadly engagement.
     A single ship now remains in the system. Hidden through artificial stealth emissions, and drifting in a cloud of cosmic debris, the Lorithos corvette Vilak sits quietly, its sensors now powered down after a fruitless search for survivors.
     "Helm, get us out of here. Open up a tunnel back to Ansible station J-137-a. Alert me as soon as we get there. I need to get a message to Naval Intelligence. We don't have a moment to lose."
     The small corvette quickly powers up its engines, calculates jump coordinates, and disappears into a small hyperspace tunnel.

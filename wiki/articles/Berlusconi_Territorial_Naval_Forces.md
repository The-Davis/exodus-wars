As with all things regarding the [[Sirius Families]], Territorial Naval Forces is more of a collective term for all space vessels operated by the various factions for protection of the three-system [[Sirius Cluster]]. Although officially a single entity, the [[Sirius Territorial Administration]] assigns defense of the three systems their territorial rulers.

At any given time, the number of armed vessels in a single territory may vary due to the presence of various groups, but each family does maintain its own full-time fleet. The fleets ensure family control over a territory, and also serve not so much to keep the peace, but to keep the status quo.

In addition to any warships, the [[Sirius Cluster|Sirius Territories]] play host to trade and merchant ships from across the [[Orion Arm]]. Vast fleets of freighters, transports, haulers, and tankers transfer goods at massive orbital depots, and then set off in the [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates|gates]] once again. The Sirius families also maintain their own private trade fleets for transporting "priority goods".

The Berlusconi Naval Guard is better equipped than the other territorial navies, but is fewer in number. Their flagship, the ''[[NGV Giuseppe Garibaldi]]'', is a modern marvel even by the highest military standards. The Berlusconis also use two military vessel classes, the Scipio frigate and the Pugio corvette. Their Torino class is a solid design either as an armed freighter or as a heavy freighter.


The Berlusconi Naval Guard is one of three separate naval forces maintained in the Sirius Territories. The Naval Guard is the most conventional of the three naval forces, maintaining the most sophisticated warships in the Sirius cluster. However, the Guard is also the smallest navy of the three families. Despite its smaller size, the Naval Guard's sophistication, as well as the presence of a large portion of the Blazton Security Fleet in Berlusconi space, gives the Berlusconi an edge and helps them maintain their position as the most influential Territory in the Administration.

The Berlusconi Naval Guard is under the direct command of Padre Alessandro Berlusconi. However, normal operations are overseen by [[Cristobol Bartolomeo]], one of Alessandro's more promising nephews. Educated at the [[United Earth Alliance War College|Alliance War College]], Bartolomeo is a superb organizer and strategist.  He also knows that his captains know their own ships and crews best, and relies on heavily on their advice.

== Events of 2301 ==

===January===

* January 1 - The first [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] tainted by [[Yul Besud]]'s falsifications convenes on [[Ah Kin]].
* January 1 - [[Marian Janos]] is sworn into office as the [[President of the United Earth Alliance]].
* January 3 - The [[Ah Kin]] [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] passes legislation requiring all citizens receive an [[Imperial Identification Implant|implant]] for a new currency system which promises to revolutionize the planetary economy.
* January 4 - The [[Royal Imperial Bank]] opens on [[Ah Kin]] under the alias of the "First Electronic Bank of [[Ah Kin]]."
* January 11 - Sixteen million [[Ah Kin]] citizens are [[Imperial Identification Implant|chipped]] and issued [[Imperial Credits|credits]] in exchange for their [[Dollars]].

===February===

*February 8 - The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] physics laboratory, under the direction of [[Erika Strout]], successfully reverse-engineers a military-grade Volstamik-Krenschov Drive from their captured stockpile of [[United Earth Alliance]] hardware.
*February 10 - The [[HMS Jerome Black|HMS ''Jerome Black'']] receives the first upgraded Volstamik-Krenschov drive.

===March===

*March 19 - The [[Onyx Empire|Empire]]-controlled [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] on [[Ah Kin]] passes additional legislation requiring all deeds, contracts, and other documents of ownership be converted to purely electronic format and held at a [[Royal Imperial Bank|central location]]. By law, all non-electronic hard copies are destroyed.

===April===

*April 1 - The [[Alliance Bank of Holt]] announces a general shortage of funds, and that it will not be able to honor any of its underwritten mortgages.
*April 3 - The suicide rate on [[Holt]] triples.
*April 5 - The housing market on [[Holt]] collapses, sending a shockwave through the regional economy. Millions of home mortgages are foreclosed. Tax forfeitures and bank seizures skyrocket, leaving over 350 million civilians homeless.

===May===

*May 2 - At the urging of [[Ah Kin]] [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] [[William Duke]], the [[United Earth Alliance Assembly|Assembly]] passes the "Viable Worker Healthcare" legislation, formally creating the [[Royal Imperial Health Association|National Health Association]]. All "viable workers" and their juvenile dependants would receive free healthcare as long as they remained gainfully employed.
*May 3 - [[Ah Kin]] [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] [[William Duke]] signs an executive order barring anyone who is incapable of working from receiving "non-trivial" life-saving healthcare.
*May 5 - [[Lucius Black]] adds additional orders to the "Viable Worker Healthcare" program, ordering euthanasia on anyone who has a terminal illness and does not have private funding for their basic care. The order goes into effect, but [[William Duke]] refuses to sign it.

===June===

*June 25 - Dr. [[Reg Brenstein]], the head of the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] vehicle laboratory, submits a proposal to the [[Royal Imperial Army]] detailing the Herculean Project and his idea for the [[:Category:Rigs|combat rig]]. [[Cecil Au-Yueng]] approves the project, hoping it will result in a combat vehicle much more flexible and mobile than a tank.

===July===

*July 28 - [[Holt]]'s capital city, [[Écarlate Fontaine]], is chosen as the site of an organized protest of the continued tax seizures and bank foreclosures of homes throughout the planet. The protest turns into a full-blown riot within four hours.

===August===

*August 1 - Riots in [[Écarlate Fontaine]] on [[Holt]] come to an end. Sixty people are dead, over five hundred are hospitalized, and nearly 95 million [[Dollars]] of property is destroyed or damaged beyond use.
*August 3 - The [[Premier of the United Earth Alliance|Premier]] of [[Holt]], [[Robert Ducroix]], announces a government-imposed moratorium on all tax seizures of property and a six month grace period on all outstanding mortgage debt.

===September===

*September 8 - The [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gate]] between the [[Yngard System]] and the [[Sol System]] suffers from unexpected and catastrophic loss of power, with no fewer than seven [[:Category:Starships|starships]] inside at the time. Five hundred [[Ikronin]] and [[Terran]] lives are lost, torn apart when the fold of "Emellantid" collapsed.
*September 9 - The [[United Earth Alliance]] bans all [[Wormhole Gate]] travel by its citizens, and suspends all official use of the [[Wormhole Gate]]s until further notice. The [[Ikronin Jurekön]] opts to continue operations for its own assets.

===October===

* October 11 - Dust storms covering the entire Enwir continent on [[Lorithan]] ground all air traffic in the region.
* October 16 - Fifteen small [[Lorithos]] camps in the Enwir continent are lost during the dust storms. An estimated 56,000 [[Lorithos]] die during the storms, which continue unabated.
* October 23 - The dome of Tharek, a major city on [[Lorithan]]'s Enwir continent, cracks. Thousands of its residents die overnight.
* October 25 - The dust storms over Enwir end. All communication from Tharek to the rest of [[Lorithan]] are cut off.
* October 26 - An estimated 250,000 [[Lorithos]] perish in the first day of attempting to reach the closest inhabitted city, Aci Devar.

===November===

* November 6 - The survivors of Tharek--a mere 600 [[Lorithos]] out of its more than 600,000 residents, stagger into Aci Devar. Most die in hospitals over the next few days.
* November 24 - [[:Category:Freighters|Freighter]] pilots in the [[Omicron Carinae System]] report intermitent contacts with an unidentified [[:Category:Starships|starship]] in the outer parts of the system.
* November 29 - The [[UEAS Neil Armstrong|UEAS ''Neil Armstrong'']] is dispatched to the [[Omicron Carinae System]] under the banner of the [[Alliance of Planets Explorer Corps]] to attempt to locate the unidentified [[:Category:Starships|starship]].

===December===

* December 18 - [[United Earth Alliance]] [[President of the United Earth Alliance|President]] [[Marian Janos]] visits [[Lorithan]] to spearhead a fundraising effort to bolster major cities around the planet, in the hopes of preventing another disaster like Tharek.

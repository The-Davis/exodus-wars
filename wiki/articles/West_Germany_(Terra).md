'''West Germany''' was the post-[[World War II]] government of the portion of [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] occupied by the western Allies. West Germany and [[East Germany (Terra)|East Germany]] were opposed to each other, both acting as the puppets of their leaders. West Germany frequently smuggled refugees out of [[Communist]] nations, including its eastern relative.

West Germany was dissolved on April 9, [[2012]], when the last Chancellor of West Germany signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]].

The HMS ''Fearless'' was the 47th ship in the [[Dauntless Class Destroyer|Dauntless class]] of [[:Category:Destroyers|destroyers]]. It was given the hull designation DG-47.

Under the command of Captain Danielle Inman, the ''Fearless'' quickly established a reputation as a deadly fighter. It led a long and decorated career, serving in the [[Earth]] invasion, the [[Holt]] invasion, the [[Titan System]] campaign, and the defense of the [[Kalidasa]] shipyards. The ship and its crew were decorated with the [[Order of the Wolf]] and the [[Royal Imperial Navy Battle Star]], and Captain Inman received the [[Imperial Medal of Valor]].

On January 6, [[2323]], the ''Fearless'' was assigned to the [[HMS Arbiter|HMS ''Arbiter'']]'s  carrier group. The [[HMS Arbiter|''Arbiter'']] and most of her escorts were badly damaged in a series of attacks by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and were withdrawing to the [[Alpha Centauri System]] for repairs. As they entered the outer orbit zone of Proxima Centauri, a flight of three [[Starmaster Class Destroyer]]s ambushed the carrier group. The Commodore of the carrier group ordered the ''Fearless'' to cover the retreat of the rest of the group. Captain Inman successfully crippled one of the three [[Starmaster Class Destroyer|Starmasters]], and kept the other two from catching the retreating carrier group. In the course of the battle, the ''Fearless'' lost three of its engines, its [[Volstamik-Krenschov Drive]], and three of its main guns. Captain Inman set a course for Proxima Centauri's coronasphere, activated the ship's scuttling charges, and ordered all hands to abandon ship.

The crew of the HMS ''Fearless'' were imprisoned by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and remained Prisoners of War until [[2324]], when they were released to the custody of the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. Captain Inman contracted a severe case of pneumonia due to exposure to the elements during an attempted escape, and died on December 18, [[2323]].

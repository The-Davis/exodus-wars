''Jan "Avenger" Brooks'' is a brilliant pilot and commander, but often feels like she is in the shadow of her predecessors. She is the Sky Marshall of the [[Prefecture]]'s [[Federated Districts Air Force]].

== Sigil of Vengeance ==

Jan Brooks is one of the unsung heroes of the last battle of [[Earth]].  She was the third-ranked ace in the then-rebel [[Prefecture]], but was mostly ignored while the two top aces--[[Marcus Rathbone]] and [[Jack Ivonrud]]--made headlines.

When both aces were tragically shot down, one killed and the other disabled, Brooks was the heir apparent to command of the Prefecture's air forces.  For two months after the death of [[Jack Ivonrud|Ivonrud]] and the near-death of [[Marcus Rathbone|Rathbone]], the forces remained without a commander.

Today Brooks and her Stuka, the "Sigil of Vengeance," are the best in the [[Prefecture]]'s forces, and renowned even in the ranks of the other races.  Even [[Ixoarchada]], some of the best pilots in the galaxy due to their augmentations, acknowledge Brooks' skill.

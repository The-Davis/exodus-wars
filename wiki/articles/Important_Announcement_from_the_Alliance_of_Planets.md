Important Announcement From the Alliance of Planets - 12/2/2323 - by Scott Sanburn

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This morning, Meroniri Prime Minister Darel Fusanla held a press conference with various news agencies throughout galaxy. Here is his announcement:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Good morning ladies and gentlemen. I am here to announce several new initiatives that the Alliance member states have been taking over the last few months.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“After several months of debate, the member state of Titan—the last survivors of the United Earth Alliance—have decided to disband the last remnants of the United Earth Alliance’s government. The Titanian colony will form its own government, and Terra, the former seat of UEA power, will have an independent embassy built to keep the peace between Alliance member states.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Although we are sad to see the United Earth Alliance finally disbanded, we recognize that too many of its officials and soldiers were killed in the war with the Empire. Too few UEA planets remain to keep it going, and the Titan Colony desires independent recognition within the Alliance of Planets. It is time to move on.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Finally, the Prefecture, our allies who defected from the Empire and helped us end that menace, have petitioned for membership within the Alliance of Planets. They have been accepted. Despite their former allegiance with the madman who caused us so much grief, they have proven themselves loyal allies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“The official membership ceremony will start in three days.”

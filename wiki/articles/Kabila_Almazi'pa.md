===History===
Main Article: [[History of Kabila Almazi'pa|History]]

Almazi'pa was founded on the principles of capitalism. Until they brought modern economics to their people, the Ngrligru economy was simple--the biggest and strongest took everything.

Merchants were a new concept to the Ngrligru, but the teachings of Ona Raha soon made the idea less alien to them. Kabila Almazi'pa was soon wealthy, and used their money to become powerful.

===Foreign Relations===
Main Article: [[Foreign Relations of Kabila Almazi'pa| Foreign Relations]]

Kabila Almazi'pa was against the destructions of Kabilas Bweha and Kimburu. The loss of customers was not good to their merchants at all. Still, Almazi'pa didn't have the political sway to outvote both Kabilas Nyoka and Jitu'nge.

Today, Kabila Almazi'pa is much more aggressive than it has been in the past. They discovered the existence of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] and other humanoid companies that are powerful capitalists. Kabila Almazi'pa hates competition, preferring its pseudo-monopoly to true free market capitalism. They support invading and wiping out the humanoid races.

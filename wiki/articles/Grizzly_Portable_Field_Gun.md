*Size Designation: Light Portable Field Gun
*Powerplant: G-M11-A Battery
*Armor:  Boron-Nitride Composite armor 
*Armament: 1 Light Autocannon (200 rounds)
*Communications System:  Infantry-Grade Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Advanced Long-Range Targetting Sensor 

===Overview===

The Grizzly Light Portable Field Gun was designed as a small artillery emplacement for use against infantry and light vehicles.  

[[Image:GrizzlyPortableFieldGun1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Grizzly is designed to be light and mobile, loaded into the back of an Albatros Hovercraft or similar craft and carried to where it is to be deployed.  The cannon itself is a Light Autocannon, capable of taking out most infantry, as well as light armored craft.  Although it is built for simplicity, its targeting system is state of the art, and its accuracy exceeds that of most mobile weapons platforms, especially at extreme ranges.  

[[Image:GrizzlyPortableFieldGun2.jpg]]

The Grizzly is powered by a G-M100-A battery pack, which is capable of providing enough power to fire two-hundred-fifty rounds from the Grizzly.  The battery is built into the ammunition pod, and so a fresh battery is loaded with every two-hundred round magazine. 

The Grizzly is usually setup far from action in a concealed position.  Its frame is covered in Boron-Nitride Composite armor, but the operate sits in the open.  Concealment and quick redeployment capabilities are the primary defenses of the Grizzly. 

===Deployment===

Grizzlies are simple to operate, and cheap to produce.  Their small size makes them deployable nearly anywhere, from open ground to the tops of buildings, and even in the confines of a starship.

[[Image:GrizzlyPortableFieldGun3.jpg]]

Their range and accuracy are excellent, and the Light Autocannon's power is sufficient for taking out infantry and light armor.  The Kalidasa Security Force would utilize these cannons in defensive operations, especially in urban environments.  A Grizzly can be unloaded from a truck, powered up, and ready to fire on targets within a minute of reaching its destination. 

While in transit, the Grizzly can be packed up so as to take up much less space in a transport hovercraft.

'''The Jihad''' was a worldwide religious and military movement that organized the diverse Muslim populations into a cohesive empire known as [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]]. The Jihad lasted for many years, but finally ended when the final Imam of [[Arabia (Terra)|Arabia]], [[Osama bin Laden]], ordered total warfare against the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

This conflict was later known as [[World War III]], which resulted in the total destruction of every Muslim holy site, and the systematic destruction of every copy of the Quran in the world. The religion of Islam was effectively wiped out, deemed by the world at large to be too aggressive to exist on a civilized planet.

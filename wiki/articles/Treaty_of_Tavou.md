The [[Tempest War]] far surpassed the [[Second Exodus War]] in terms of loss of life and property.  With multiple planets rendered completely uninhabitable by mass drivers and other weapons of mass destruction, a full-blown siege of [[United Centusi States]] territories, and a ground war brewing which looked like it would quickly escalate into genocide; nearly everyone involved was looking for a way to end the war.

Unfortunately, the cultural stigma of surrendering was so great that the [[Mesarthrim]] were almost universally willing to face death rather than the shame of giving up the fight.  Even if they were literally "bombed into the Stone Age", the average Mesarthrim survivor would throw the nearest rock at an invader.

Faced with this impossible scenario, the end only came when [[Arnold Stadt]] assassinated [[William Camp]] and seized power in the Mesarthrim regime.  He ordered a cease fire, with neither side acknowledging defeat.  Stadt offered to negotiate a permanent peace under the condition that the [[Taviridis Somarchada]], a powerful yet neutral third party, oversee and broker the peace treaty.

== Terms ==

Among the grievances addressed by the [[Ixoarchada]] was the imbalance of resources and power possessed by the Prefecture.  They determined that the Prefecture had unfairly and possibly illegally benefited from the [[Onyx Empire]]'s armed expansion during the Second Exodus War, and determined that the Mesarthrim should have greater control over the [[Pelagrim Sector]].

Additionally, the Mesarthrim demanded that the amnesty for war crimes granted by [[Anthony Hadrian]] at the end of the Second Exodus War be lifted, and that Prefecture-loyal Imperial officers be forced to answer for the crimes they committed during the war. This led to the [[Magrinoch Trials]], and the scandal which ended [[Talaris Roi]]'s political career and resulted in Anthony Hadrian's disappearance.

The [[Ikronin]]'s demands were simple and easy to satisfy: reparations for the damaged and destroyed Wormhole Gates, compensation for the deaths of their personnel killed before formal declarations of war, and 1,850,000 [[Likvada]] in miscellaneous war-related reparations, all to be paid by the new Mesarthrim government.

On the part of the Prefecture, [[Laojiu]] and their other agricultural planets were restored to them.

== Analysis ==

The Prefecture got little and gave up much in the treaty, but in terms of political currency and military power they were quite well compensated.  Their military losses in the war had nearly been replaced, and their newest weapons were battle tested without taking serious losses.  The Prefecture was also seen as a more politically honest and trustworthy organization after the Magrinoch Trials, while only losing a handful of figureheads.

The [[Alliance of Planets]]' '''Explorer Corps''' was developed to survey new [[:Category:Star Systems|star systems]], [[:Category:Planets|planets]], and other points of interest for colonization, development, and scientific interest.

Traditionally, a Member State of the [[Alliance of Planets]] will commission a ship and fund a mission, but will receive staffing from other Member States to aid in translation, research, and other roles. While operating an Explorer Corps mission, the ship keeps its name, but operates under the authority of the Explorer Corps and the [[Alliance of Planets]] itself. Any military ranks are suspended during the mission, and all crew are given civilian titles.

The '''Orhak System''' consists of the star Orhak, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids. It is a colony of the [[Lorithos]].

In order of their distances from Orhak, the planets are Orhak I, Orhak II, [[Ipsoff]], Orhak IV, and Orhak V.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Orhak is the Orhak System's only star. Orhak is a class G2U yellow dwarf. The Orhak System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Orhak is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Ipsoff ===
[[Ipsoff]] (0.97AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Orhak System.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Orhak System was colonized by the [[Lorithos]] shortly after [[First Contact]].

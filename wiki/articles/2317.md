== Events of 2317 ==

=== January ===

* January 8 - The [[Onyx Empire]] dispatches forces to the [[Lupei System]]. "Task Force: Judicator", under the command of Captain [[Julia Michaels]], was ordered to sieze control of the system for the [[Onyx Empire|Empire]].
* January 10 - Task Force: Judicator blockades [[Kalidasa]], orders its surrender, and bombards the city of Santa Guadalupe with nuclear weapons. Captain [[Julia Michaels|Michaels]] gives the colonial authority twenty-four hours to surrender, promising to level every population center on the planet if they do not comply.
* January 11 - The [[Kalidasa]] colonial authority surrenders control of [[Kalidasa]] and the [[Lupei System]] to the [[Onyx Empire]]. Task Force: Judicator remains on station for two months to oversee the installation of new Haven class shipyards and a permanent [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] security force.

=== February ===

* February 25 - As a condition of the [[Alliance of Planets]] support, the [[Meroniri]] [[Mihel Rivarsen]] was placed in command of the coalition forces defending the [[Titan System]]. [[Mihel Rivarsen|Rivarsen]] sent the fleets of the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] and [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] to [[Mnemosyne]]. The harasser forces [[Scott Sanburn]] left behind to keep the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] bottled up returned to [[Theia]] for repairs and rest.
* February 27 - [[Robert Marshall]] decided to exploit the inexperience of his new [[Ikronin]] and [[Lorithos]] foes to his advantage. He called for reinforcements from Cronus. Admirals [[Robert Marshall]] and [[Anthony Hadrian]] attacked the [[Lorithos]] and [[Ikronin]] forces with a full 75% of the might of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. The battle lasted three hours, and resulted in 40% casualties for the [[Ikronin]] fleet. The [[Lorithos]] fleet retreated to [[Themis]], where [[Scott Sanburn]]'s fleets reinforced them. The [[Ikronin]] fleet was forced out of the [[Titan System]] altogether and recalled home.

=== March ===

* March 18 - The [[Meroniri]] [[Mihel Rivarsen]] prepared to push the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] out of their recently captured planets. Unfortunately, his superiors in the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] refused to commit any [[:Category:Warships|warships]] to the task, and the [[Lorithos]] commanders had difficulty understanding [[Mihel Rivarsen|Rivarsen]] and [[Scott Sanburn]]'s tactics. The [[Lorithos]] fleet commander felt that his forces had taken too much damage already, and wanted little part in the battle.
* March 29 - When the coalition finally had an acceptable battle plan, the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was already well defended in the harsh rocky surfaces of [[Mnemosyne]]'s mining facility moons. The battle was a disaster. The coalition fleet took 15% casualties, while only inflicting 10% casualties on the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] and its ground defenses. The [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan|Lorithos]] fleet left the [[Titan System]] in disgust.

=== April ===

* April 16 - Seeing the divisive effect of the losses in the [[Titan System]], [[Lavik Nighthawk]] suggested deploying his [[Omegas]] into the other [[Alliance of Planets]] governments. Emperor [[Lucius Black]] approved, and [[Omegas]] were dispatched to [[Ikronin]] and [[Lorithos]] worlds, where they infiltrated the civilian populations, and began to ply their devious trade. The [[Omegas]] could not cross the vast distances of "wild space" to reach the [[Meroniri]] territory.

=== May ===

* May 3 - The [[HMS Judicator|HMS ''Judicator'']] and its carrier group enter the [[Oronus System]]. The unarmed colony immediately surrenders.

=== June ===

* June 11 - [[Mihel Rivarsen]] and [[Scott Sanburn]] knew from their intelligence reports that the bulk of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was positioned in the [[Titan System]]. They hoped that a concentrated assault on [[Earth]], the new seat of [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] power, would defeat the [[Onyx Empire]] permanently. [[Mihel Rivarsen|Rivarsen]] sent out a call for a general marshalling of the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] Member States' forces once again, but an [[Omegas]] agent assigned to the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] managed to intercept and decode the message. [[Lucius Black]] was warned. As before, [[Mihel Rivarsen|Rivarsen]]'s own [[Meroniri]] superiors refused to commit any forces to the battle.

=== July ===

=== August ===

* August 11 - [[Nic Piazzaro]]'s son, [[Emilio Piazzaro]], is shot and killed by the [[Omegas]] while working for the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]].
* August 19 - [[Nic Piazzaro]] meets [[Anthony Hadrian]] in secret, and joins the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]].
* August 26 - The [[Atlas Class Carrier|Atlas]] class vessel hull designated [[HMS Glorious|CV-43]] is laid down for construction in the [[Lupei System]].

=== September ===

=== October ===

* October 22 - The [[Ikronin]], [[Lorithos]], and [[United Earth Alliance]] fleets launched an attempted invasion of [[Earth]]. They were met in force by the new ships [[Lucius Black]] had in training since March of [[2315]]. The battle was ferocious, but mercifully short. [[Mihel Rivarsen]] bungled the strategy from the get-go, and only [[Scott Sanburn]] seizing command of the fleet kept them from being wiped out altogether. Following the retreat, [[Mihel Rivarsen]] resigned his post as commander of the coalition forces, and returned to [[Sepulare]] in disgrace.

=== November ===

* November 23 - [[Omegas]] kidnap [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro]] to blackmail her husband [[Nic Piazzaro]] into betraying the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]].

=== December ===

* December 3 - [[Nic Piazzaro]] capitulates to the demands of the [[Omegas]] and betrays the [[Resistance against the Empire|resistance]], but does it publicly and purposefully blows his cover so the [[Onyx Empire]] can no longer use him against the [[Resistance against the Empire|Resistance]].
* December 4 - The [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] invasion fleet in the [[Titan System]] used the confusion of the bungled [[Earth]] invasion as an opportunity to take [[Themis]] in the [[Titan System]].
* December 6 - Following the disastrous failed invasion of [[Earth]], General-Commander Sanburn raced back to the [[Titan System]], and arrived just in time to build up the defenses of the remaining planets under [[United Earth Alliance]] control.
* December 8 - Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian]] called off the assault of [[Rhea]], in direct defiance to the orders of [[Lucius Black]]. [[Lucius Black|Black]] sent [[Grolk Kyhra]], his [[Ngrligru]] warrior, to lead an assault without [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]]. The [[United Earth Alliance]] Defense Force fleet thoroughly trounced [[Grolk Kyhra|Kyhra]]'s forces, and the [[Ngrligru]] barely escaped with his life.
* December 9 - Following the disaster, [[Lucius Black]] acknowledged Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]]'s superior understanding of battle tactics, and stopped questioning his choices as Grand Admiral of all [[Royal Imperial Navy|Imperial]] fleets. [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] demanded a temporary cessation of hostilities and a buildup of weapons and ground forces.
* December 15 - [[Nic Piazzaro]] attempts suicide, but is thwarted by his friend [[Juan Delgado-Romas]].
* December 28 - [[Nic Piazzaro]] and [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] rescue [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro]] from the [[Omegas]].
* December 31 - [[Nic Piazzaro]] and [[Mio Velasquez-Piazzaro]] are smuggled off of [[Earth]] by [[Juan Delgado-Romas]] and are sent by safe transport to [[Theia]].

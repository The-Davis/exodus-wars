The Nighthawks were an adept crime family in the latter years of [[World War III]], using the terror and chaos of the war as tools to better their fortunes. Oliver Nighthawk was the fourth-born son of that family, and decided he'd had enough. He hid out in the deepest jungles of South America, where he became an accomplished poisoner and stalker. When the war was over, Oliver Nighthawk was the only survivor of his family, and decided to go to one of the new colony worlds.

Later in life, Oliver went insane and his grandson [[Lavik Nighthawk]] suffered for it. Oliver sadistically tortured the boy and eventually taught him to become a serial killer.

On October 19, [[2070]], Oliver Nighthawk was executed on eight counts of murder he had [[Lavik Nighthawk|Lavik]] do on his behalf.

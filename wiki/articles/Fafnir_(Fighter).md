*Acceleration:  175 KPH/S
*Armor: Proton-Bond armor
*Shields: Repulsor Shields
*Armament:
**2 Fast-Tracking Heavy Lansvapen Cannons
**14 Standard Ordinance Hard Points
**1 Class B Antimatter Torpedo
*Manufacturer: Jaytex Mechanized Unlimited
*Communications System: Standard Optical Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: JMU Image Recognition Targeting System

===Overview===

The Fafnir is a very fast and agile fighter, designed to engage other fighters en route to delivering its heavy antimatter payload.

===Capabilities===

Combining superior agility with a massive weapons payload, the Fafnir has the potential to outgun and out-fly many fighter craft.  Its small frame is armed with a fast-tracking Heavy Lansvapen Cannons on a turret, twelve long-range Pursuer IR missiles, two long-range Shrike IR Missiles, and one Class B Anti-Matter Torpedo.

The tracking and targeting systems of the Fafnir are the latest in technology from JMU. The Image Recognition System--derived from the Blackis SPAR design--requires special missiles, equipped with targeting computers tied to the computer system of the Fafnir. The Fafnir targeting system records the silhouette, electronic and visual signatures of its target, tracking it until it hits the target or burns out.

The Pursuer and Shrike missiles are fire-and-forget missiles. Once the targeting computer identifies a target, the pilot can launch his missiles and continue to his main objective, which is typically a large capital ship or instillation. Two of the Pursuers are mounted to the hull facing aft, to vigorously discourage enemy ships that manage to maneuver behind the fighter.

[[Image:Fafnir1.jpg]]

The Class B Anti-Matter Torpedo is a smaller version of the torpedoes launched by JMU capital ships and contains a warp sustainer unit. It possesses the same IR targeting system as the missiles and when launched travels at a velocity of 3xC. Because of the speed of the torpedo and accuracy of the targeting system, the Fafnir pilot does not have to maneuver close to the large vessel it is targeting, however the closer he can get before launching eliminates any possibility of a ship evading the attack.

The twin fast-tracking Heavy Lansvapen cannons operate on an independent targeting system which allows for the computer to designate hostile craft and fire at will at any ship within weapons range or upon any ship on a attack or intercept vector to the Fafnir.

The Fafnir’s main disadvantage is armor, or the lack thereof. To improve speed and agility, heavy armor on this class was sacrificed. Designers installed a secondary Repulsor Shield system in the event the primary shields were to fail.

===Deployment===

Fafnirs are ideally used in numbers to attack larger vessels or instillations with their torpedoes and are capable of cutting their way through fighters that may be defending those targets.

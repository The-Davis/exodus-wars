The '''History''' of the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] is marked with great tragedy and great triumph.

== The Sundering ==

[[Lorithan]] was an unimportant world within [[Pax Anthronoris]], with little to offer to the endless empire other than self-sufficient living space.

The full wrath of the [[Ngrligru]] and the [[Sundering]] reached [[Lorithan]] in [[17999BC]]. The cities toppled, and civilization crumbled. [[Pax Anthronoris]] chose to abandon [[Lorithan]], and ordered the world's Legiar to withdraw to participate in the [[Master Plan]].

The Legiant [[Mirdek Amanus]], commander of [[Lorithan]]'s defenses, defied the orders of [[Pax Anthronoris]]. His Legiar abandoned him, but his Praetoria and its crew remained loyal, fighting the [[Ngrligru]] for days in a vain effort to preserve their homeworld. 

On the surface of [[Lorithan]], [[Ashtir Amanus]], the wife of Legiant [[Mirdek Amanus]], sought to preserve as many of their people as she could. Knowing the single Praetoria in orbit could do nothing for long, she decided to save the children of her people. [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] chose the Dzarning Mountain Range as a refuge, hiding children and food in caves.

The adult [[Anthronoris]] of [[Lorithan]] fought long and hard, doing everything they could to draw the attention of the ravening [[Ngrligru]] away from their precious treasure hidden away in the mountains. Billions died so millions could live. The savage [[Ngrligru]] feasted on the [[Anthronoris]] of [[Lorithan]], but never discovered their children.

Knowing his doom was at hand, [[Mirdek Amanus]] sacrificed all he could to bring [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] aboard, then sent his Praetoria on a final run against the [[Ngrligru]]. He brought the flaming wreck of his ship and dozens of Ngrligru vessels crashing down into the depths of the ocean of [[Lorithan]], letting the waves bury [[Anthronoris]] and [[Ngrligru]] alike. [[Mirdek Amanus|Mirdek]] and [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] died hand in hand, entombed beyond the reach of their monstrous enemies.

== The Long Night of Lorithan ==

The children of the [[Anthronoris]] had been saved from death in the bellies of the [[Ngrligru]], but thousands died of starvation, exposure, and misfortune in the mountains. The [[Ngrligru]] ships were gone, but many still stalked the ruins of the [[Anthronoris]] cities.

It was about this time that [[Mirdek Amanus|Mirdek]] and [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] began to be remembered as larger than life. Those old enough to understand what happened told the younger children that the brave [[Mirdek Amanus|Mirdek]] and the kind [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] had sacrificed themselves to save [[Lorithan]].

Over the next thousand years, the children of the [[Anthronoris]] forgot their history. The immortality of their parents had been lost in the [[Sundering]], and without the Vitaernity treatment, they were doomed to natural lifespans. They became the [[Lorithos]], with their own legends and heritage. Technology was lost to them: the cities that had been built by the [[Anthronoris]] crumbled into rubble and were swept away by the winds of time. For generations they were feared as haunted lands, and until the last of the [[Ngrligru]] died off, the few [[Lorithos]] who ventured within their borders were never seen again.

The society of the [[Lorithos]] became wild and savage. The strong ruled the weak. Women served men. This primitive state of affairs continued for thousands of years.

Through it all, the legends of old faded. The names [[Mirdek Amanus|Mirdek]] and [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] were never forgotten, though their surname "Amanus" was never spoken after the first few generations of [[Lorithos]]. Their legend changed and grew: [[Mirdek Amanus|Mirdek]] became the sun, who drove off the demons of darkness. [[Ashtir Amanus|Ashtir]] became the moon, who brought comfort and a reminder of the light's return in the morning. The [[Lorithos]] worshipped the "Twin Gods" for the rest of their history.

== The New Dawn ==

By [[1004BC]], [[Lorithos]] society had spread across the face of [[Lorithan]]. Their technology had advanced to the use of bronze tools, domesticating animals, and building cities of stone and mortar. Despite these advances, their society was still quite limited. Writing was rarely used, and force of arms ruled over all.

It was about this time that a [[Lorithos]] man by the name of [[Hamur Huern]] became the rule of a city in the far northwest of [[Jehz Modar]]. He had a vision, believing that all men and women could live free and equally if a just and noble king were to unite them. He introduced the [[Law of Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan#The Eight-Fold Cornerstone|Eight-Fold Cornerstone]], a set of laws that he claimed came directly from the principles of the Twin Gods.

One of the most radical changes was allowing some women to run their households. [[Hamur Huern|Hamur]] claimed that if Ashtir ruled the night, so should women who were born under her gaze. [[Hamur Huern|Hamur]] believed that in order for a household to be orderly and peaceful, there must be a clear head of the household. Thus, the Sunlit and Moonlit families were created. Men and women born in the day were Sunlit, and the men ruled their families. Men and women born in the night were Moonlit, and the women ruled their families.

Other cities across [[Jehz Modar]] heard of these radical changes, and did not like them in the least, but thought nothing of it until the armies of [[Hamur Huern]] began to march. The "dawning light" began to spread across the face of [[Lorithan]] as [[Hamur Huern]] went to war against his neighbors. His armies were superior in nearly every way. [[Hamur Huern|Hamur]] had introduced more than laws and social reforms: his farming methods made his city the best-fed in the world. His discovery of iron and the concepts of alloys gave his armies steel weapons, which shattered the bronze of his enemies like toys.

After a hundred victories without a single defeat, cities began to swear fealty to [[Hamur Huern]] without a fight. He became the [[Prekohr]], the emperor of the world. He was dubbed the "Mircan Prekohr", or "Dawn Emperor" for bringing enlightenment and unity to his people. By [[987BC]], the whole world was under his rule, and warfare was at an end.

The Huern Dynasty lasted for thousands of years, and the light of civilization burned brightly as long as a Huern sat on the throne of [[Lorithan]].

== The Twilight Years ==

The civilization of the [[Lorithos]], united under the banner of [[Rikaz o fii Huern iv Lorithan]], made great advancements over three thousand years. The last [[Prekohr]] in the "era of the light" was [[Talire Huern]].

[[Talire Huern]]'s reign was marked with unprecedented development of technology, art and culture. Her later title, the "Ashen Prekohr", meaning "Twilight Empress", was due to what followed her death rather than a decline in the power of the [[Lorithos]]. The [[Lorithos]] had made it into space, and even discovered the existence of [[hyperspace]], though they had yet to tap into the means to travel faster than light.

In the year [[2240]], [[Talire Huern]] passed away at an unusually old age. So old that she outlived her first and second-born, leaving the line of succession unsure. For a while it was believed that her fifth-born son, [[Derek Huern]], would claim the throne. When he was assassinated in [[2241]], events were put into motion that could not be undone, ushering in an era of fear and destruction not seen on [[Lorithan]] since the [[Sundering]].

== The Burning ==

The [[Burning Wars]] were the most insanely destructive planetary war in recorded history. The combatants attacked one another with reckless abandon, committing gross atrocities against all living things and against the planet itself.

=== 2241 ===

The first rumblings before the devastating [[Burning Wars]] began in [[2241]], when several heirs and would-be rulers declared their right to succeed Empress [[Talire Huern]].

Most of the populace on Lorithan believed that the Empress's fifth son, the charismatic [[Derek Huern]], would succeed the Empress. He gathered quite a following of the nobles and commoners, and was about to declare himself [[Prekohr]], when he was assassinated. The remaining heirs and other candidates for the throne all began to eye each other with suspicion.

Royalty became an endangered species in the months following Derek Huern's assassination. Every time an heir of the Empress declared themselves the legitimate heir to the throne, they were assassinated by one rival or another, openly or through subterfuge.

The more powerful heirs began to build up their private militaries, and some huge science projects--like [[hyperspace]]--were put on hold.

=== 2245 ===

The years between the death of Derek Huern and the beginning of what would later be known as the Burning War were relatively uneventful. Occasionally, one of the would-be Emperors was quietly assassinated by a rival, but most of the squabbling heirs' time was consumed by military build-up.

War began with a small battle between followers of [[Kian Huern]]--the eldest daughter of [[Derek Huern]]--and the followers of [[Torm Krelbine]]--the most powerful successor with no relation to Empress [[Talire Huern]]. The battle was short, and it was a long time until the next conflict, but it was the beginning of the worst war in [[Lorithos]] history.

After the battle between [[Kian Huern]] and [[Torm Krelbine]], the heirs formed strong alliances, and eventually coalesced into four groups--followers of [[Kian Huern]], followers of [[Torm Krelbine]], followers of [[Goff Riiktar]], and followers of [[Lera Ruehv]].

The first large-scale battle--between [[Goff Riiktar]] and [[Torm Krelbine]]--destroyed Talire Haven, the former capital city of [[Lorithan]]. The grave of Empress [[Talire Huern]] was defiled, and the civilians fled. [[Goff Riiktar]] torched the city and seeded the ground with lethal chemicals, poisoning the land. His act of petty vengeance was the first of many that marked the devastation of the [[Burning Wars]].

=== 2247 ===

[[Lera Ruehv]] was the first to bring nuclear weapons into the [[Burning Wars]]. Her commanders attempted to crush [[Kian Huern]]'s stronghold and army in a single blow, but overestimated the power of their atomic weapon. Thousands of [[Kian Huern|Huern]]-loyal soldiers and machines were destroyed, but the bulk escaped.

After [[Lera Ruehv]]'s first strike with atomic weapons, the others were quick to steal the technology and strike back with it. The resulting battles leveled five major cities, and crushed countless smaller ones. It got so bad that communities of civilians began to pick up and leave when one of the armies showed up. This was the beginning of the [[Lorithos]] tradition of nomadic life.

[[Torm Krelbine]]'s scientists began one of the nastiest phases of the [[Burning Wars]] with the creation of viral warfare. His plagues wiped out millions of enemies and civilians alike before [[Kian Huern]]'s special forces managed to steal the vaccination and begin inoculations.

=== 2253 ===

The poisoning of [[Lorithan]] began in earnest in 2253. [[Lera Ruehv]]'s armies chased [[Torm Krelbine]]'s across the land, nuking everything in their path, but the worst blow came from [[Goff Riiktar]].

[[Goff Riiktar]] discovered the hiding place of [[Kian Huern]] and her best troops. Her stronghold was built in a series of underground caverns with access to huge fisheries in the Lorithan Ocean. Rather than risk an open battle with entrenched elite [[Kian Huern|Huern]] soldiers, [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] decided to try starving them out.

[[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s forces dumped several million tons of toxins and biohazards into the sea, where the currents would carry them to the [[Kian Huern|Huern]] stronghold fisheries. [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] expected the toxins to dilute and dissipate over time, but not before the [[Kian Huern|Huern]] food sources were gone. He underestimated the toxicity and concentration of the poisons, and overestimated the strength of his siege lines. [[Kian Huern|Huern]]'s forces escaped, tearing apart [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army in the process.

Three months after [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army poisoned the Lorithan Ocean, the consequences of the damage to the ecosystem came crashing down around the battling heirs. While the toxins were not strong when diluted through the whole Lorithan Ocean, they were enough to kill the plant life, disrupting the entire food chain. The ocean was soon dead.

In retaliation, the other three combatants turned the whole of their resources on killing [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]. With his army weakened by the failed siege of [[Kian Huern]]'s stronghold, [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]] lost badly. It was [[Torm Krelbine]] who actually captured him, and personally killed him. The survivors of [[Goff Riiktar|Riiktar]]'s army scattered.

=== 2256 ===

For three years after [[Goff Riiktar]]'s death, it looked like the war was beginning to die down. The three armies didn't fight at all until later 2256, and then only had a couple minor skirmishes. Then [[Kian Huern]] unwittingly sparked off another round of battles.

Before her death, Empress [[Talire Huern]] had the Lorithan Imperial Navy designed. Only one ship was built--the first Blijrok Corvette. It was launched once, and recovered by [[Derek Huern]], then hidden. [[Kian Huern]] recovered it in 2256, and began building a fleet of her own in secret.

Four months after [[Kian Huern]] began building her fleet, [[Lera Ruehv]]'s army attacked her stronghold again. This time, [[Kian Huern|Kian]] was several dozen kilometers above her stronghold. The [[Kian Huern|Huern]]-loyal fleet of Blijroks decimated the [[Lera Ruehv|Ruehv]] army. [[Lera Ruehv]] died when a lucky shot blew up her entire atomic arsenal.

=== 2260 ===

After [[Lera Ruehv]]'s crushing defeat at the hands of [[Kian Huern]]'s fleet, [[Torm Krelbine]] went deep underground. He began building a fleet of his own in a vast underground cavern system, all while keeping small and medium armies on the surface to keep [[Kian Huern|Kian]] on her toes.

It took a year, but [[Torm Krelbine]]'s forces built a fleet--the first Svenj Destroyers. The Huern and [[Torm Krelbine|Krelbine]] fleets met in a battle involving fifty warships, and several hundred [[Eituk]] and [[Vadrie]] fighter craft. The first battle had devastating results on both sides, and [[Lorithan]] was soon littered with wreckage from space.

=== 2270 ===

After ten years of warfare in space, the world of [[Lorithan]] was ready to give up the ghost. [[Zak Huern]] revived the [[hyperspace]] project in order to look for a new world.

=== 2273 ===

The most destructive war in [[Lorithan]]'s history finally came to an end. [[Kian Huern]] and [[Torm Krelbine]] killed each other in one last battle. [[Lorithan]] breathed a collective sigh of relief, and set about the difficult and unpleasant task of survival.

== First Contact ==

With the deaths of the last heirs to the throne, [[Zak Huern]] was the only leader figure to the surviving people of [[Lorithan]]. He pushed on with the [[hyperspace]] project, hoping to find a new world for the survivors. The first test probe made a round trip from [[Lorithan]] to the outer limits of the starsystem in ten minutes.

The [[hyperspace]] tunnel was noticed by a [[United Earth Alliance]] scout ship. A delegation of [[Alliance of Planets]] representatives--[[Ikronin]], [[Meroniri]], and [[Terrans]]--met with [[Zak Huern]] and the people of [[Lorithan]].

Seven years after [[First Contact]], [[Lorithan]]'s future was beginning to look better. A new representative republic form of government was created, and medical help from the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] ensured that the people of [[Lorithan]] weren't doomed to rapid extinction from the rampant diseases and toxins.

== Renewal ==

Eventually, [[Dirken Huern]] ascended the throne, and brought the Lorithos into the [[Alliance of Planets]] as a member. He dedicated his reign to the peaceful expansion of his empire by means of colonization of uninhabited worlds, and the restoration of Lorithan to its former beauty.

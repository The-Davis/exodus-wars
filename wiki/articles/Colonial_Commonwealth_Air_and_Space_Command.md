===Defense Fleets===

Commonwealth Naval Operations Command is responsible for all warships and state-owned merchant marine ships operating in Commonwealth space. NOC is comprised of four fleets of 26 warships, giving it a combined strength of 78 total warships. The warships are a collection of ship designs from the Titanian Alliance, the closest allies of the Commonwealth.

Operating as four fleets, one based in each major system of the Commonwealth, the NOC keeps Commonwealth space free from pirates, smugglers, and other potential threats. The First Fleet operates out of Alpha Centauri, the Second out of Kalmar, and the Third from Bethija.

===Naval Air Command===

Operating alongside Naval Operations Command, as well as Army Command, is the Air Operations Command. The Aerospace Operations Command is responsible for all fighter and bomber activities in the Commonwealth Defense Ministry. They patrol planetary airspace, operate as naval fighter and bomber support, and provide close air support to Army Command forces on the ground. Like the NOC, the AOC operates Titanian fighter and bomber designs.

==Ships==

While largely designed around a defensive military and offensive law enforcement strategy, the Commonwealth does deploy some significant fleet assets. Largest of all are the Ticonderoga Command Carriers, the pride of Titanian engineering. They are few in number even in Titanian-controlled space due to their high cost and massive size, but they rival even the Prefecture super-carriers in fighter capacity and efficiency. The Vigilant Fleet Carrier, Freedom Escort Carrier and Liberty Light Cruiser fill lesser roles, but are important for the defense of the Commonwealth.

When bombers are not enough to turn the threat of an enemy fleet, the Commonwealth has some powerful warships of its own. The Constitution class Destroyer is a significant fleet asset, and is backed up by the Hercules class modular starship. Smallest of all are the Bailey, Independence and Osiris class corvettes, which fill the gaps in fleet deployments.

==Aircraft==

The Commonwealth has a significant fighter supply to draw on for a variety of roles. While older and not suited for most front-line action, the F-750F is an excellent light interceptor for reserve squadrons: a last line of defense for space stations and ground installations against incoming bombers. The backbone of the Commonwealth's fighters is the F-800C: a medium fighter design that fought long and hard throughout the Second Exodus War. It is a proven design with significant updates over the last decade. Similarly benefitting from its age and maturity is the F/A-900E, a superb long range fighter-bomber with a reputation as one of the finest fighters in human space. New to the field and fresh from Titanian drawing boards is the F-1000A, a long-range superiority fighter. It has yet to prove itself, but coming from the same pedigree as the F-800 and F/A-900, it is expected to meet any threat to the Commonwealth.

While it does not take a hostile military stance, the Commonwealth recognizes that only a fool has no offensive capability. For this, the Commonwealth calls on the B-550A medium bomber. It lacks the fearsome reputation of Prefecture attack craft, but is a solid attack craft with a good design team behind it.

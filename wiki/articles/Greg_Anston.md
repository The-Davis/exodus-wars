'''Greg Anston'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Date of Death: April 2, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Place of Death: [[Icronia]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Greg Anston'' was a member of the [[Royal Imperial Army]], and later a Corporal in the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]]. He died in the boarding action on the FDS ''Imposis''.

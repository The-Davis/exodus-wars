The Fallen King (Gefallen König) is a [[Mesarthrim]] [[Category:Destroyers|destroyer]] title given to the lead destroyer in the second element of the first fleet [1-II(1)].

There have been ten different Fallen King Destroyers in the titles legacy.

==Name==
The Fallen King was named in [[2188]] by [[Adrian Tischler]], a whole two years before the ships keel was even laid. The Fallen King, and her sister ship (the [[Blood Throne]]) were named as taunts to the [[Mesarnak]] [[Centus Overseers|Overseers]] who were given control of [[Mesar]] in [[2185]].

==Fallen King - Howell Class==
The [[Howell class Destroyers|Howell Class]] was the first capital ship designed by the Mesarthim [[Mesarthrim Rebellion|Resistance]]. The Howell class would serve with distinction long into the [[Second Exodus War|Exodus War]].

The First Fallen King suffered numerous setbacks throughout it's career. When the Destroyer was first laid, the Mesarthrim Resistence did not realize they would not have enough material to finish the ship, and it wasn't until [[Bernard Howell]] offered to sneak supplies away from the Anderung that the ship could be completed - a full six years after the hull was first laid.
The First Fallen King served as a "mobile shipyard" from [[2196]] until [[2198]] in deep space, giving the Resistance a secret place to build their navy. The ship did little else then serve as a shipyard for that time. Then in [[2198]], Howell was discovered by Mesarnak agents and a [[Morningstar]] was dispatched to destroy the Rebellions fleet. In the battle the Fallen King was crippled and Kapitän Großfratten was killed.
After the battle, the Fallen King underwent extensive repairs and Kapitän Kurtz took command. In March of [[2199]] the Fallen King took part in a series of raids against [[Mesarnak]] [[Centus Overseers|Overseer]] shipping. On the morning of April 1st, the Fallen King and her sister ship the [[Fury of Hell]] were ambushed chasing down a Overseer command ship by a Morningstar. Kapitän Kurtz turned the Fallen King into the path of the oncoming Morningstar, giving the Fury of Hell a chance to escape.
All hands were lost, and the Fallen King became the first Mesarthim Destroyer to fall in battle (the first [[Blood Throne]] was lost in an accident in 2196).

The Second Fallen King was actually supposed to be the Duality, but after the destruction of the First Fallen King, she was recommissioned as the Second Fallen King. The Fallen King had a rocky first few months. During its first firefight with a pair of Overseer destroyers, the Fallen King took an excessive beating and Kapitän Kass was killed. The next few Kapitän's served long decorated careers. Kapitän Urner saw the end of the second Fallen King. On August 7th the Fallen King fell under bomber attack. After several successive hits to the neck structure, secondary explosions caused the backbone of the ship to shatter. Kapitän Urner ordered all hands to abandon the Fallen King and set off scuttling charges. [[Centus Overseers|Overseer]] Cruisers found the remains of the Fallen King on August 22nd, and were able to recover the computer core - a mistake that cost the Rebellion dearly.

==Name Retired==
In [[2335]] as the Mesarthrim Federation began putting its fleets back together, it was decided that the second element of the first fleet would no longer be a destroyer element, and therefore the Fallen King name was retired.

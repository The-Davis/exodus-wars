*'''Founded''': [[2300]]
*'''Headquarters''': [[Albanus]]
* '''Products''': Reactors

After [[Royal Imperial Labs]]' Energy Laboratory developed a satisfactory starship fission reactor, they needed to create an infrastructure for regular production. The result was Tolwin Power Incorporated, which gained relative autonomy and produced several civilian products for the citizens of the [[Onyx Empire]]. Dr. Charles Hancock was transferred from Royal Imperial Labs and made the director of Tolwin Power.

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] used the reactors built by Tolwin in most of their [[:Category:Warships|warships]]. Tolwin's assets were seized by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] after the war, and most blueprints and patents were put back in the hands of the staff on the company's new headquarters on [[Albanus]].

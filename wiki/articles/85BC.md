== Events of 85BC ==

As the age of innovation and exploration continued, the eyes of many on [[Glengarr]] were continually looking to the stars.  Research and design teams from all backgrounds, and politicians and planners from almost every [[Iridi]] were constantly pushing the boundaries of known technology.  

In 85BC, the first components for the ''[[Segara]]'' were fired into orbit.  Assembly of the various piece began in the later part of the year.

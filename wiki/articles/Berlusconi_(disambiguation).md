The name '''Berlusconi''' can refer to a government, to a family, or to the surname of several historical characters.

The Berlusconi government is the [[Berlusconi Family Territory]], one of the infamous [[Sirius Families]].

The [[Berlusconi Family]] is one of the families with a controlling interest in the [[Sirius Cluster]], it is a traditional "mafioso" family with strong [[Italy (Terra)|Italian]] roots.

[[Alessandro Berlusconi]], or "Papa" Berlusconi is [[Il Padre]] of the [[Berlusconi]] family. While he is a dangerous power broker, he is more of a loveable rascal than an insidious criminal.

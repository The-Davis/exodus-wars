===Overview===

The Recon Probe is the eyes and ears of the Prefecture Fleet and Aerospace Force.  They're actually the evolution of older military satellites, used by the Royal Imperial Navy since 2315.

[[Image:ReconProbe1.jpg]]

Prefecture intelligence and reconnaissance officers insisted on having the recon probes upgraded to the latest technologies available.

===Capabilities===

Nothing is installed on the Recon Probe besides the detection equipment and AI guidance system.  This suite of sensors is very thorough.

Cameras are installed on the probe to allow for views from every direction.  High powered optical transmitters can relay the footage to recon controllers within the Prefecture Fleet or to a District Air Command base.

[[Image:ReconProbe2.jpg]]

The sensors on the Recon Probe can detect heat patterns, gravity, and x-rays.  Gravity sensors are useful for detecting gravity cavitation drives of large warships, and x-ray sensors can see shadows on stars--indicating something large (like an armada or Bugger swarm) between the Recon Probe and the star.

===Deployment===

Recon Probes are a common sight in orbit of Blackis Prefecture worlds, and are scattered throughout space in their territory and the territory of their friends and allies.

[[Image:ReconProbe3.jpg]]

Most of the information collected by Recon Probes is shared freely with other militaries, but some information is too sensitive to share.  Such data is locked up tight in the Spire of Truth or other Prefecture intelligence buildings.

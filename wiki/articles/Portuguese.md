The '''Portuguese''' language originated in the [[Terran]] nation of [[Portugal (Terra)|Portugal]]. It was exported into space by [[Brazil (Terra)|Brazillian]] colonists who settled on [[Kalidasa]].

Portuguese is the official language of the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]].

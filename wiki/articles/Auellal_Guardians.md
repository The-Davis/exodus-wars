The [[Auellal Armored Division|Fir Turtyle]] are the lead division within [[Auellal Army|the Guardians]].  They are highly mobile, and are capable of responding to any situation that arises on [[Caledon]] within the hour.  The [[Auellal Armored Division|Fir Turtyle]] are also the chief offensive weapon of the [[Auellal League]], being sent anywhere in the [[Orion Arm]] that they are needed.

Each [[:Category:Auellal City-States|City-State]] maintains and supports its own [[Auellal Defenders|Seòc Saoi]] division.

===History===
The Guardians were enlisted and trained by small groups of men from three elite [[Myt'herra]] divisions; [[Myt'Herra division The Specter|Das Gespenst]], [[Myt'Herra division The Black Sheep|Das Schwarze Schaf]], and [[Myt'Herra division The World Warriors|Die Weltenkrieger]].  Also, Steven's [[Myt'Kamphen]] division, [[Myt'Kamphen division The Pub Runners|Die Bar-Läufer]] helped enlist and train.

Them earliest soldiers began their training in [[2312]], receiving instruction from [[Myt'Kamphen division The Pub Runners|The Pub Runners]] Shock Troop company.  The training was hard, yet extensive.  Then men and women who survived the twelve month secret training camp became the core of the [[Auellal Shock Troops|Laoch Saoi]].  While the [[Auellal Shock Troops|Laoch Saoi]] were in training, the three [[Myt'herra]] divisions had sent contingents to each of the cities on [[Caledon]] and began secret training camps to turn these citizen soldiers, into hardened fighting soldiers.  They formed the  [[Auellal Defenders|Seòc Saoi]], one for each city.  At this point, most of the men and women on [[Caledon]] had only served their compulsory two year service in the [[United Mesarthim Clans Heer|Heer]], so giving them the next level of training was very important, because the  [[Auellal Defenders|Seòc Saoi]] was not meant to be just a national guard, but an offensive arm as well.

After the training was completed, the [[Auellal Defenders|Seòc Saoi]] divisions became self sufficient, operating right under the nose of the [[Caledon|Caledon's]] [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Heer]] Commandant.  In [[2315]], [[Steven Cue|Steven]] took advantage of the [[First Mesarthrim Golden Age]], and claimed some military expenditure could be cut.  Steven made many deals, and in the end, successfully got three [[Myt'herra]] and two [[Myt'kamphen]] divisions cut.  They were the [[Myt'herra]] divisions;  [[Myt'Herra division The Specter|Das Gespenst]], [[Myt'Herra division The Black Sheep|Das Schwarze Schaf]], and [[Myt'Herra division The World Warriors|Die Weltenkrieger]].  The [[Myt'Kamphen]] divisions were Steven's own [[Myt'Kamphen division The Pub Runners|Die Bar-Läufer]], and [[Mark Branson|Mark Branson's]] division, [[Myt'Kamphen division The Free Mesarnak|Der Freie Mesarnak]].

[[Steven Cue|Steven]] and [[Mark Branson|Mark]] met in secret after the divisions were dissolved.  They made an uneasy peace between them, after [[Steven Cue|Steven]] had slighted him seven years earlier at the [[Siege of Stone Bridge]].  They agreed that [[Mark Branson|Mark]] would take command of all five divisions and go to the third planet in the [[Cinaed System]], [[Iurin]].

In [[2318]], [[Fireline Industries]] was bought by [[Alfred Stark]], owner of [[Stark Lhune]].  [[Alfred Stark]] was a member and supporter of the [[Auellal Political Party]].  [[Alfred Stark|Stark]] took personal control of the development department, and began developing weapons for the Auellal, and smuggling them out to [[Iurin]].

In [[2322]], after [[first contact]] with the [[Onyx Empire]], the [[Auellal Political Party]] sent scoutships to the [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova Territory]].  There they met in secret, forming an accord between the two parties.  The [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova]] provided the Guardians with a place where they could train and equip.  It was here that the [[Auellal Armored Division|Fir Turtyle]] were formed using a mixture of [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and purchased [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova Family]] tanks and vehicles.

During the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]], defections to the [[Auellal League]] and the [[United Centusi States]] were rampant, the ones who joined the [[Auellal League]] were put in a special division called the [[Auellal Guardians Temporary Structure|Shallidagh Co-yannoo]] until they could determine into which division the troops fit.

After the [[Mesarthrim Civil War|civil war]], and the retreat to [[Caledon]], the Guardians adopted a proper military stance, establishing training centers, bases, supply centers, and command & control centers.

===Divisions===
The Guardians are divided into six sub-divisions, each sub-division is charged with specific duty or field.  The divisions all use the same [[Ranks in the Auellal Guardians|rank systems]], and all answer to the [[Ard-auellan Ridir]] and her staff.  The [[Ard-auellan Ridir]] and her staff make up a ''special'' two-hundred and eighty man Dùn.  This special Dùn includes elements of the [[Auellal Cryptology and Intelligence Center|Ealadh a' cuilidh]].

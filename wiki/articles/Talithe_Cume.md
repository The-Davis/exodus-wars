The Talithe Cume, otherwise known to the younger races simply as the "TC," was actually a multi-faceted [[Remnant]] military operation under the command of '''Admiral''' [[Gaius Shadar]].  The translation of Talithe Cume roughly works out to "Little one, I say to you, arise" and was so named because one of the main objectives of the operation was to determine the viability and capacity of each of the younger races to be united and rearmed by the Remnant against the [[Ngrligru Kabila Duka]].

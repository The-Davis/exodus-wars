===Overview===

In the midst of a massive reconstruction program following the Second Exodus War, the Kalidasa Planetary Authority generated a large number of jobs by designing and constructing its own defense forces, with every part of the design and construction being done in-system.

[[Image:ChariotMediumHovertank1.jpg]]

The primary vehicle of the KSF is the Chariot Medium Hovertank.  

===Capabilities===

The Chariot is designed as a general patrol and combat vehicle.  While it is chiefly designed for speed, it is well armed.  Its primary weapon is a turret-mounted Large Neutron Beam Cannon.  This weapon gives the Chariot a powerful punch against most any target.  Additional firepower comes from a single Machine Gun, mounted on the left of the cockpit, for use against infantry and light targets. 

In addition to its cannons, the Chariot is armed with four Short-Range Missile launchers, giving it a powerful close-range punch against infantry and vehicles alike. 

[[Image:ChariotMediumHovertank2.jpg]]

With a high top-end speed and excellent maneuverability, more akin to a Unitronian hovertank than a Terran type, the Chariot is well protected.  It also has a light layer of Boron-Nitride as well as a Plasma Barrier and Magnetic Screens, but these are light defenses for a tank of its size.  Its speed is its primary defense. 

===Deployment===

Chariots were designed to be the all-purpose ground vehicles of the KSF.  They are designed to perform duties ranging from police patrol and spaceport security to armed recon and urban combat.  Although fast and moderately armed, they are not designed for large-scale field-combat or heavy assault duties than many races' tanks are geared for.  In the event of a hostile invasion, most heavy combat would be handled by the larger Cavaliers.  However, Chariots can inflict damage on enemies alone, and their speed and maneuverability makes them extremely lethal when working in groups.

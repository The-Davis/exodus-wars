===Overview===

Upon its completion, the original Petrov frigate was the largest Terran warship ever completed.  At the dawn of the 22nd century, the 400+ meter-long hull, state-of-the-art ion drive, and unrivaled weaponry made it a terror to behold. 

[[Image:PetrovClassFrigate1.jpg]]

In 2325, the Petrov class is far from the marvel it used to be.  The only remaining ships of the class, former UEA vessels, operate in the Asanova Territory's Black Fleet.  Updated and overhauled an innumerable amount of times over the past two centuries, the modern Petrovs are solid, rugged ships that operate well enough for their defensive duties in the Asanova Territory. 

===Capabilities===

Anti-warship firepower on the Petrov comes in the form of two Ashur Cannon twin-turrets, and sixteen Kalidasan-designed Advanced Neutron Cannons in Fast-Tracking turrets.  These weapons can deal damage to other capital warships, and are more than enough to deal with any vessels that are unwelcome in Asanova space. 

[[Image:PetrovClassFrigate2.jpg]]

Eight anti-aerospace emplacements are also outiftted on the Petrov, and are able to handle the multitude of light vessels and aerospace craf that come and go in the Asanova territory. 

The Petrov is neight an exceptionally fast warship, nor is it particularly sluggish.  The Asanovan Petrovs also have modern defensive systems, such as Boron-Nitride, Gradient Gravity shielding, Magnetic Deflector Screens, and a Barrier Plasma Shield. 

===Deployment===

The Petrov class survives in only three warships in the Asanova Black Fleet.  These ships are the primary warships of the Asanova Territory.

[[Image:PetrovClassFrigate3.jpg]]

While they seldom see combat, they are maintained as a deterrent against outside threats, and also to maintain the balance of power within the three-family Sirius Administration.  During normal operations, only two Petrovs are kept manned and crewed, while a third is left in dock, but is kept on alert.

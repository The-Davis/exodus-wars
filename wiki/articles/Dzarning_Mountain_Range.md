The '''Dzarning Mountain Range''' is an enormous cross-continental mountain range that divides [[Jehz Modar]] into the [[Huon Jehz]] and [[Vuranct Jehz]] regions.  The mountains seem unnaturally large, and are far from any fault lines in [[Lorithan]]'s crust.

After Lorithan's destruction, no further research was possible, but during the [[Volucris War]] the Swarms conducted extensive excavation projects into the mountains.  They unearthed an ancient alien facility, but no human expeditions could be launched to the site before it was destroyed.  The nature and history of the facility remain unknown, but long range photographs lend credence to the theory that the entire mountain range may have been artificial.

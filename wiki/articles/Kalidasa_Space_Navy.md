The Kalidasa Space Navy is a small force of ships designed to defend the Kalidasa system. The current navy is a collection of 34 warships, custom designed by the KPA for defense of the system.

Operating as a single fleet, the ships patrol the system and surrounding space, ever watchful for pirates, raiders, or worse.

The fleet structure is a loose organization of ships into various task forces. Only in the event of a full-scale invasion would the KSN operate as a single force of warships.

Fighter operations are directed from Komara class carriers, while Proghursts, Valkors, Fensovs, Omsots and Redicors provide firepower for actual slugfests.

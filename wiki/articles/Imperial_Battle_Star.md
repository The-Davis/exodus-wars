The '''Imperial Battle Star''' was an order of merit awarded by the four branches of the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military.


=== Royal Imperial Navy Battle Star ===

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] Battle Star was awarded to ships of the line, and to the ship's commanding officer, for a "fierce display of Imperial aggression and valor, above and beyond the demands of a ship of the line." It was typically awarded to a ship that destroyed or captured an enemy vessel in single ship combat.

==== Naval Air Arm Battle Star ====

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s Air Arm had a separate Battle Star, which was awarded to combat squadrons, and to the squadron's commanding officer for "valorous combat service and an aggressive push towards victory, above and beyond the caliber demanded on our pilots." It was typically awarded to a squadron that shot down a number of hostile enemy targets double its own numbers without losing a single aircraft.

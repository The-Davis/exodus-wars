The '''Me-47 Richtofen''' 

==History==
The Richthofen was designed during the [[Second Exodus War]], as the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer]] began operations on multiple planets, and needed heavy support from the air during large-scale invasions. Prior to this, the [[Mesarthrim]] maintained no bomber-type craft larger than the Anox.

During the Mesarthrim Civil War, the United Centusi States managed to capture all production facilities for the Richthofen line, and secured all existing bombers for the Aerospace Force. 

==Capabilities==

The Richthofen is the largest bomber in the UCSAF. Its missile armament consists of ten Medium Ordinance Hardpoints, capable of carrying various anti-fighter or anti-ground missiles.

Additionally, two Heavy Ordinance Hardpoints are fitted on the bottom of the bomber, which can carry large anti-warship rounds.

Finally, the Richthofen has two massive internal bomb bays, one on each side of the craft. In these two bays, the Richthofen can carry ordinance equal to nearly twice its own unloaded mass, ranging from free-fall to optically-guided smart bombs, as well as a number of smaller rocket and missile types.

In addition to its missile arsenal, the Richthofen is also equipped with eight Type-Cx Blazers, an extended-range version of the standard fighter-mounted Blazer Type. The Cx can only be mounted on larger bombers and other craft with more powerful reactors than a smaller fighter can carry. It is also equipped with three computer-controlled anti-aerospace turrets (one on its upper section, two on its lower sections), and two pilot assisted rear twin-turrets, which can cover many angles to the left, right, and rear of the Richthofen. The turrets use smaller Type-Cm Blazers, miniaturized Type-C Blazers with a shorter range, and can effectively protect the Richthofen from interceptors or other fighters they might come across. The turrets, especially the lower and rear turrets, are also especially useful for straffing ground targets within an atmosphere, which the Richthofen is geared towards attacking.

The Richthofen is outfitted with a large bomber-grade Mesarthrim reactor, and an impressive engine array, allowing it to carry heavy payloads of weaponry. While slow and ungainly by Mesarthrim fighter standards, it maintains a respectable acceleration rate and sufficient maneuverability for its role.

==Deployment==
Richthofen Assault Bombers were developed for the [[Second Exodus War]] to give the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] a massive support platform for planetary invasions, as well as a long-range assault bomber for use against enemy fleet formations. Although larger than the Schleiffen, the Richthofen and its payload are geared towards ground-support, not fleet-support. Today Richthofens serve as the primary ground-support and planetary-attack bombers of the [[United Centusi Space Luftwaffe]]. Their guns, missiles, and bombs make them deadly against even the heaviest ground targets.

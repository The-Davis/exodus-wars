== Events of 2316 ==

=== January ===

* January 1 - Admiral [[Anthony Hadrian|Hadrian]] began fortifying [[Cronus]] as a supply station and main base of operations for the long siege he knew would follow. He assigned 25% of the fleet to Admiral [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]], and ordered [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] to take everything he could feasibly take without catastrophic losses.
* January 4 - [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] didn't make it far. General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] of the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force]] met [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s fleet in orbit of [[Tethys]], the 11th planet, and they began a series of deadly battles. [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] was driven off in the first skirmish.
* January 17 - [[Robert Marshall]] returned to [[Tethys]] with additional forces and re-engaged the fleet under [[Scott Sanburn]]'s command. [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]] wisely withdrew after the battle, narrowly escaping an [[Empire|Imperial]] trap.

=== February ===

=== March ===

* March 5 - While Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] and General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] fought nearly daily battles in the [[Titan System]], Emperor Lucius Black began tightening his grip on [[Terra]] and its colonies. He dispatched the [[Omegas]], an elite force of intelligence operatives, assassins, and terrorists to keep his hold on his people firm.
* March 16 - [[Empire|Imperial]] forces raid [[Titan System]] shipping with [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] fighters, crippling the fleets in the area.
* March 19 - As many orbital factories as possible are moved from other planets in the [[Titan System]] to [[Theia]], making [[Scott Sanburn]]'s forces less reliant on resupply convoys.
* March 23 - First [[Omegas]] attack on [[Theia]].

=== April ===

* April 1 - Admiral Robert Marshall scored a decisive victory in the [[Tethys]] battle. One of his commanders, Captain [[Jeff Nolan]], was responsible for the destruction of three ADF-5000 warships. [[Jeff Nolan|Nolan]] was promoted to Vice Admiral, and became [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]]'s second-in-command. The loss, which became known as the "April Fools Debacle" sent [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]]'s fleet reeling.
* April 10 - [[Scott Sanburn]] ordered a general retreat from [[Tethys]] and [[Phoebe]] (the 10th planet in the [[Titan System]]. Captain [[Debora Strohl]] of the ''Patriot'' volunteered to remain behind in orbit of [[Phoebe]] with her crippled vessel, and sabotaged many facilities before the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] arrived and destroyed her vessel. [[Scott Sanburn]]'s forces retreated to a high orbit around [[Mnemosyne]], the 9th planet in the [[Titan System]]

=== May ===

=== June ===

* June 7 - [[Robert Marshall]]'s fleet orbiting [[Phoebe]] was reinforced and rearmed. [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] decided to harass the [[Scott Sanburn]]'s fleet rather than take them head-on. He began sending in bombers on long-range sorties against [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]]'s warships, keeping them on their toes and in the repair docks. This went on for many months.
* June 18 - Surviving [[United Earth Alliance]] officials on [[Theia]] send ambassadors to the [[Meroniri]], [[Ikronin]] and [[Lorithos]] to request military aid. All promised to send help if the [[Titan System]] could hold out for six months.
* June 22 - Determined to keep the [[Empire]] out of his home, [[Scott Sanburn]] planned a counterstrike against the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s campaign headquarters on [[Cronus]]. Admiral [[Gordon Dannins]] volunteered to lead the strike force. [[Gordon Dannins|Dannins]]' volunteer force made an incredible account of themselves, destroying several [[Royal Imperial Navy]] [[:Category:Cruisers|cruisers]] and crippling dozens of smaller [[:Category:Warships|warships]], but [[Gordon Dannins|Dannins]] himself died when a lucky shot took out the bridge of his own [[:Category:Warships|warship]].

=== July ===

* July 20 - [[Scott Sanburn]] didn't let [[Gordon Dannins]]' sacrifice count for nothing. He knew the inevitable [[Empire|Imperial]] counter-attack would come to [[Mnemosyne]] soon enough, so he reinforced the planet with as many ships as he could spare, even arming garbage scows and similar hulks. In the ensuing battle, [[Emil Torsc]] and [[Helen Cashaw]] fought their legendary duel to the death. [[Robert Marshall]]'s forces barely won superiority in [[Mnemosyne]] orbit, and [[Scott Sanburn|Sanburn]]'s forces were able to make a fighting withdrawal that did serious damage to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] forces that pursued them.

=== August ===

* August 16 - In a skirmish with [[United Earth Alliance]] forces, the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']] is destroyed.

'''Ipsoff''' is the third planet in the [[Orhak System]]. Unlike [[Jagarr]], the first [[Lorithos]] colony, and [[Lorithan]] itself, the dying homeworld of the Lorithos, Ipsoff is a vibrant world full of hope. The colony was founded in [[2308]] on a world mostly covered by water. Many island chains run along the tectonic plates of the planet, but there are no full-sized continents.

== Geography ==

[[Image:Ipsof_Map.gif|450px]]

[[Vas Ultak]], [[Ors Betahr]] and the [[Lijak]] spaceport are major population centers, where hundreds of people immigrate from Lorithan daily--once they undergo a stringent decontamination and infectious pathogen screening process. New cities are under construction, and Jimok is nearly complete and already has a few thousand citizens.

The island villages of Ipsoff are surprisingly advanced, full of seagoing vessels that make up fleets of mobile fisheries and kelp harvesters.

Two space stations, [[Mihter Irok]] and [[Mihter Dojik]], serve as military and shipping headquarters for the planet. The Lorithos defense fleets have a strong presence in the Ipsof system, nearly as large as the Lorithan fleet.

If trends continue, Ipsoff may become the de-facto homeworld of the Rikaz Lorithos. Despite the lack of large inhabitable landmass, the pristine planet is far more desirable than the dying, war-torn world of [[Lorithan]].

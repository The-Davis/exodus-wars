The Reigens are an off-shoot of the [[Ikronin]], separated for centuries and cross-bred with the [[Juns]] for nearly as long.

===Appearance===

Reigens average about 1.53 meters in height. They tend to be muscular and well-toned.

====Skin Tone====

Reigens have slightly darkened skin tones, but are much paler than their [[Ikronin]] relatives due to their shared genetic stock with the [[Juns]]. Members of the royalty tend to be much darker than the commoners.

====Hair Color====

Reigens have light brown or blonde hair, due to their shared genetic stock with the [[Juns]]. Members of the royalty have very light blonde or white hair, due to their "pure" bloodlines.

===Lifespan===

The average Reigen lifespan is 80 to 100 years.

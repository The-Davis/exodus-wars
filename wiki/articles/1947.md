== Events of 1947 ==
===January===
*January 24 - [[Greek Civil War]]: Demetrios Maximos founds monarchist government in Athens, [[Greece (Terra)|Greece]].
*January 31 - [[Cold War]]: [[Communists]] take power in [[Poland (Terra)|Poland]].

===February===
*February 5 - [[Cold War]]: Boleslaw Bierut becomes president of [[Poland (Terra)|Poland]]'s [[Communist]] government.
*February 12 - A meteor creates a crater in Sikhote-Alin, [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].
*February 17 - [[Cold War]]: The ''Voice of America'' begins to transmit radio broadcasts into the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]].

===March===
*March 12 - [[Cold War]]: President [[Harry Truman]] of the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] introduces the Truman Doctrine as a proposed [[United States (Terra)|United States]] policy of containing [[Communism]] abroad. The anti-[[Communism]] movement within the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] does not support Truman, suggesting their efforts are best served at home..
*March 15 - [[The Jihad]]: Hindus and Muslims clash in Punjab. Over 400 are killed before [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops are able to intervene.
*March 29 - [[The Jihad]]: There is a brief, organized and armed rebellion against [[France (Terra)|French]] rule in Madagascar. The Muslim population of the island is discovered to be responsible, and the [[France (Terra)|French]] government deports them and destroys every mosque on the island. Most refugees are taken in by [[Arabia (Terra)|Egypt]].

===April===
*April 19 - [[Greek Civil War]]: [[Communist]] partisans attempt to assassinate Demetrios Maximos in [[Greece (Terra)|Greece]], but fail.

===May===
*May 22 - [[Cold War]]: In an effort to fight the spread of [[Communism]], the [[United States (Terra)|United States]] Congress passes a bill implementing the Truman Doctrine. The bill does not pass the Senate and the Truman Doctrine is put to rest.

===June===
*June 15 - [[Cold War]]: [[Portugal (Terra)|Portuguese]] government orders 11 military officers and 19 university professors to resign accused of [[Communist]] activity.
*June 21 - A sailor named Harold Dahl claims to have seen six UFOs near Maury Island. The next morning Dahl reports the first modern Men in Black encounter.
*June 24 - Kenneth Arnold makes the first widely reported UFO sighting near Mount Rainier, Washington.

===July===
*July 5 - The downed UFO allegedly found in the Roswell UFO incident, is written about by Stanton Friedman.
*July 26 - [[Cold War]]: [[United States (Terra)|U.S.]] President [[Harry Truman]] signs the National Security Act into United States law creating the [[United Earth Alliance Central Intelligence Agency| Central Intelligence Agency]], Department of Defense, Joint Chiefs of Staff, and the National Security Council.
*July 29 - [[The Technology Race]]: After being shut off on November 9, [[1946]] for a refurbishment, [[ENIAC]], one of the world's first digital computers, is turned on after a memory upgrade. It remains in continuous operation until October 2, [[1955]].

===August===
*August 5 - [[The Jihad]]: The [[Netherlands (Terra)|Netherlands]] stops political actions in [[Arabia (Terra)| Indonesia]].
*August 14 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslim rebellion led by Muhammad Ali Jinnah forces out [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] occupation of [[Arabia (Terra)|Pakistan]].
*August 15
**Determined not to lose other key territories in the area, the British put an end to the Indian civil unrest by hanging [[Mohandas Gandhi]], Rajendra Prasad, and Jawaharlal Nehru.
**[[The Jihad]]: [[Arabia (Terra)|Pakistani]] forces take over Baluchistan from the [[British Empire (Terra)|British]].
*August 16 - [[Cold War]]: In [[Greece (Terra)|Greece]], the [[Communist]] General Markos Vafiadis takes over the government.
*August 23 - [[Cold War]]: [[Communist]] Party executes Demitrios Maximos in Athens.
*August 31 - [[Cold War]]: [[Communists]] take power in [[Hungary (Terra)|Hungary]].

===September===
*September 13 - [[The Jihad]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] exiles Indian muslims from the region, and offers refuge to Hindus being murdered in [[Arabia (Terra)|Pakistan]].

===October===
*October 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: [[United States (Terra)|American]] test pilot, Captain Chuck Yeager, flies a Bell X-1 faster than the speed of sound, the first man to do so in level flight.

===November===
*November 10 - [[Cold War]]: [[France (Terra)|French]] government arrests four steel workers in Marseille which begins [[Communist]] rioting that spreads to Paris.
*November 16 - [[The Jihad]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] begin to withdraw their troops from Palestine.
*November 20 - [[Cold War]]: Paul Ramadier resigns as Prime Minister of [[France (Terra)|France]] - he is succeeded by Robert Schuman. Schuman calls 80,000 reservists to quell the rioting miners.
*November 24 - [[Cold War]]: The [[United States (Terra)|United]] States House of Representatives votes 346 to 17 to approve citations of contempt of [[United States (Terra)|U.S.]] Congress against the so-called "Hollywood 10" for refusing to testify about [[Communism]] in the movie industry, but only fines them. Considering their refusal a tacit admission of [[Communist]] ties, a secret [[CIA]] operation was put in place to eliminate them.
*November 25
**[[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]] ratifies the Statute of Westminster and becomes independent of legislative control by the [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]]. [[New Zealand (Terra)|New Zealand]] becomes one of the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth Nations]].
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Alvah Bessie is murdered in his home in the middle of the night.
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Herbert Biberman dies in a "mugging" in San Francisco.
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Lester Cole and Edward Dmytryk are reported missing. Their fates remain unknown.
*November 27
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Ring Lardner's body is found in San Francisco Bay.
**[[Cold War]]: In Paris, police occupy editorial offices of [[Communist]] newspapers.
*November 28
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: John Howard Lawson dies in a car crash while driving to Oakland, California.
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Samuel Ornitz and Albert Maltz organize a protest against Congress in San Francisco. Everyone involved dies when a fire destroys the protest headquarters.
*November 29
**[[The Jihad]]: The [[United Nations]] General Assembly votes to partition Palestine between Muslims and Jews.
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Adrian Scott collapses in a San Francisco cafe. The cause of death is never determined.
**[[Cold War]]: Death of the Hollywood 10: Dalton Trumbo is found dead in his home, hanged. The official cause of death is suicide by self-strangulation.

===December===
*December 3 - [[Cold War]]: [[France (Terra)|French]] [[Communists]] derail Paris-Tourcoing Express train because of rumors that it was transporting soldiers. Twenty-one innocent civilians die.
*December 4 - [[Cold War]]: [[France (Terra)|French]] interior minister Jules Moch secures emergency measures against [[Communist]] rioters.
*December 9 - [[Cold War]]: French [[Communist]] leaders attempt to negotiate with the [[France (Terra)|French]] government. Over twenty key [[Communist]] leaders are shot by the [[France (Terra)|French]] military when they reach the meeting site.
*December 12 - [[The Jihad]]: The Iranian royal army is defeated by Muslim rebels in the Azerbaijan province.
*December 22 - [[The Technology Race]]: The first practical transistor is demonstrated.
*December 30 - [[Cold War]]: King Michael of [[Romania (Terra)|Romania]] abdicates. The [[Communists]] seize power.

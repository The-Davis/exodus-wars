The Myt'Kampfen are the standard infantry of the United Centusi States Army.  They operate in every theatre of battle, and perform nearly every role imaginable.  It was these simple Mesarthrim warriors that finally drove off the Protomorph occupation forces, and later garnered great respect among Alliance militaries due to their service in the Second Exodus War. 

In traditional Mesarthrim style, the Myt'Kampfen are equipped to be fast, mobile, and elusive.  The standard soldier is only equipped with a light Leifstein armored vest and helmet.  While the standard uniform of the Myt'Kampfen has clamps that allow for additional armored plates to be mounted on the shoulders, arms, and legs, these are not required, and many Myt'Kampfen operate without them, prefering to keep greater mobility in exchange for the loss of some armor in non-vital locations. 

Although they lack power suits, the Myt'Kampfen still perform at or above the level of the most professional soldiers in the Terran Sector.  As Mesarthrim, they begin physical training in their teens, join the Army at the age of seventeen, and undergo extensive training for a year to become Myt'Kampfen.  Additionally, their natural enhancements over standard Terrans, the results of genetic engineering that started in 2210, gives them greater strength, speed, and senses than the average Terran without machine augmentation.  The only powered sytstems they carry are their helmets, which have a basic HUD on the inside visor, and a communications uplink in their backpacks.  Their naturally enhanced vision also allows them to see more clearly and at greater distances than standard Terrans. 

[[Image:Myt'kampfen.jpg]]

A simple survival pack is carried by all Myt'Kampfen.  Ammunition packs, explosives, combat tools, and three day's rations are carried in the pack.  

The Myt'Kampfen's standard weapon is a Ciran Arms Co. Type 55 Standard Deployment Rifle, or T-55S.  The T-55s is a Blazer Rifle capable of firing upto 40 rounds per energy pack.  This rifle can breach infantry armor, and is somewhat useful against light vehicle armor.  While it has a noticeably weaker Blazer bolt and shorter range than the larger Type-77E, it is a simpler, more rugged weapon, and is well liked by the soldiers of the UCS Army.  A Saidia Optics OC-3 rapid acquisition sight is mounted to the rifle and linked to the Myt'Kampfen's internal helmet HUD, allowing accurate targeting at ranges beyond 1000 meters, while also enabling fast sighting of various targets. 

Myt'Kampfen are the standard soldiers of the UCS Army, but are far from ordinary by Terran Sector standards.  They gained a reputation for ruthless efficiency by both sides in the Second Exodus War, and are well respected by many militaries in the Human Sector.

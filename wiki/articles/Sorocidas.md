===Overview===

The Stinger is a recent addition to the Volucris swarms.  Hordes of them filled the skies of Tavou during the battle, though none were sighted near Magrinoch or any other important strategic interests.

[[Image:Sorocidas.jpg]]

Stingers are poorly armed.  They have three claws, which they use to grab infantry and carry them to great heights, then fling them to their deaths.  They also grab and tear at VTOLs and other slower flyers in the armies of their enemies

At close range, Stingers are quite effective, but if Terran fighter pilots get a lock on them, they're doomed at long range.  Unfortunately, Stingers can drop like a stone, hitting the ground and disappearing from scanner screens instantly.  They can launch themselves again just as quickly, making it hard to keep track of them.

Unlike their larger cousins, Stingers have no plasma propulsion.  They rely on their wings for flight, making them slower and less maneuverable than the heavy-hitters of the Bugger swarms.  Their carapaces are also thin and fragile, but they make up for it with numbers.

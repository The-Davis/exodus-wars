The Reigen Royal Navy is the key projection of His Majesty's power at home and abroad. The Navy both protects the Reigen home system from attack, and also symbolize His Majesty's power abroad. The Navy most often operates as a single unit in a defensive posture around the major planets and installations in the home system, their powerful destroyers forming a mobile wall of firepower with fighter support against bombers and missile weaponry.
Traditionally, the Navy was rarely kept entirely active for prolonged amounts of time. However, since the end of Emperor Black's bid for power, the beginning of the Volucris Incursion, and the commencement of the Tempest War, His Majesty has activated all ships of the fleet, and placed them on full alert for the foreseeable future.

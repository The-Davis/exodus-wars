The Muon is a sub-atomic particle, the "big brother" of the electron. In a MCF Reactor, these particles are fused, resulting in the release of billions of randomly-dispersed fast neutrons, similar to an intense fast Neutron Beam, but unfocused.

The walls of the MCF reactor core are a special polymer, which when bombarded with neutrons creates electrical power. Of course, not 100% of the neutrons are captured and used to create power. To prevent escaping neutrons from damaging the innards of the fighter, tank or Rig it's powering, the outer wall of the reactor is a heavy Hydrogen-Boron composite.

When this wall is bombarded with neutrons, it generates heat, which is converted directly into electrical power by thermal transducers. The MCF Reactor is very close to 100% efficient. Only a tiny bit of potential energy escapes as loose neutrons or unconverted heat.

==Light of Justice==

The offices of the Light of Justice were established thousands of years ago by the [[Dawn Emperor]], but were placed under the command of the [[Hishai]].

The Light of Justice is sole law enforcement organization for the [[Lorithos]], but it only operates within the [[Kihtor]] and on the colonies. Enforcing laws in the wastelands is not a practical use of resources, so most outlying surviving settlements are left to fend for themselves.

Most agents of the Light of Justice are "plainclothes", and work to discover crimes and point them out to uniformed agents. Uniformed agents are armored troops with heavy weaponry, and always act in the manner of a [[Terran]] SWAT team.

==The Scales of Light==

Once an agent of the Light of Justice has accused someone, they must both appear in front of one of the Scales of Light, which are the equivalent of judges in [[Lorithos]] society.

The agent of the Light of Justice presents his evidence, and then the defendant speaks for himself. The Scales of Light show mercy to a criminal who confesses, but the penalty is always harsher to one that lies about their innocence.

The Scale of Light must determine beyond reasonable doubt the guilt or innocence of the defendant. A criminal defendant is presumed "in flux," neither guilty nor innocent. The defendant's guilt or innocence *must* be conclusively proven, or the trial continues and is sent to higher courts, until the [[Prekohr]] passes final judgement.

The [[Colonial Commonwealth]] '''Army Command''' is charged with protecting the planetary bodies and colonial installations within [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] space.

==Structure==

The Commonwealth Army Command is divided into Planetary Operations Command and Marine Operations Command. Planetary Operations Command deploys units on inhabited planets and lunar bodies. Marine Operations Command deploys forces on NOC vessels and space installations.

Infantry are used heavily by the Commonwealth, but they tend to use older equipment and pose a "soft" target to enemies. They use natural cover and fortifications when on the defensive, and hitch a ride in Chevalier APCs when on the move. The "Guard's Riflemen" are a frequently used Commonwealth troop as a defensive unit at various installations. The Army Regulars and Marine Troopers have the more traditional assault gear of a modern military. While rare and expensive, the Commonwealth infantry can deploy the powerful Crusader powered armor suit.

==Vehicles==

The Army Command utilizes vehicles designed by the Titanian Alliance, one of its closest allies. Much of the Army's firepower is based on the use of heavy armored cars. The use of heavy tanks is rare, and the use of Rigs is non-existant. The Commonwealth Army Command is simply not created to invade heavily defended planetary bodies.

Vehicles on the ground are the most common sight in a Commonwealth Army unit. The "Guppy" is a popular light utility vehicle, seen on nearly every base and often sold to civilian markets. For more offensive roles, armored cars like the Buccaneer, Enforcer and Archer can be deployed. If a heavy or hardened target must be destroyed, or if local air defenses need to be boosted, the Flail, a heavy artillery and anti-air missile carrier, can easily be deployed.

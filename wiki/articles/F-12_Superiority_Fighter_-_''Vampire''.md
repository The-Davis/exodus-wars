The Vampire is an elegant fighter, but difficult to fly. When paired with an ace pilot, the Vampire is practically unstoppable. In 2322, the Vampire was designed by Dansei Tegoro, the head of the Royal Imperial Science Academy. The Vampire was only available in small numbers during the Imperial Civil War, but it was enough to keep the Prefecture and allies from seizing absolute control of the skies of Terra.

After the war, the newly-formed Conclave overhauled the Vampire. The biggest change to the Vampire is its main arsenal. The six Medium Pulse Lasers and two ER Small Lasers on the original made the Vampire completely independent of ammunition, allowing it to fly and fight as long as the pilot could.

The main weapon on the new Vampire is a stolen technology. It's an array of six Meroniri Coilguns. The Coilgun is the mainstay weapon of the Meroniri military. It can fire microscopic bullets at near-relativistic speeds. Each gun can carry thousands of rounds of ammunition in the same space as a 100-round belt of machine gun ammunition. The secondary weapon is a pair of Small Neutron Cannons, which have gained a lot of popularity in the open market for mercenaries and merchant convoys.

The Conclave got its Neutron Cannons through legitimate markets. The Neutron Cannons don't have the punch of a Coilgun, but they are useful weapons and require no ammunition. Finally, for its heaviest weapons, the Vampire carries four Medium Ordinance Hard-Points. The use of externally-mounted missiles was just beginning to catch on when the Vampire was built, so it wasn't until the Conclave was formed that the hard-points were installed on the Vampire.

Like the secondary weapon, the Vampire's armor is also stolen from the Archadian people. It is a proton-bonded substance, which has no space between the protons of the material. This takes up huge amounts of energy and resources, even for the Archadians. It is far better than the armor of any other civilization, even the Meroniri and Ikronin, who both have proton-bond technology. The armor of each Vampire, sold on the open market, would buy enough Prefecture Boron-Nitride armor to cover a Frigate-class warship.

The switch from a fission reactor to a Muon Catalyzed Fusion reactor has made maintenance much easier. A Vampire is expensive to build, but it will fly forever. Conclave technicians love working on Vampires, because they rarely need anything beyond ammunition reloads.

Not only does the Vampire have excellent acceleration rates and atmospheric speeds, it can also reach 1xC with its FTL drive. The compact FTL drive is very expensive on the open market, almost as expensive as the Proton-Bonded armor. The first Vampire FTL drive was stolen from a captured Bloodhawk bomber.

Normally, the Vampire carries two Hound dogfighter LRMs and two Wren anti-bomber LRMs. The payload was designed to be identical to the Prefecture's premier fighter, the Piranha. Because of lax market controls, all missiles can be obtained through legitimate channels. While the Hellcat is the Conclave's bomber, the Hellcat's lack of an FTL drive makes it unsuitable for long-range raids. Frequently, Conclave forces will be called to provide distractions for Omega operatives, and when they do it's up to Vampire pilots to provide it.

[[Image:F12Vampire1.jpg|100px]]

For ground assault, the Vampire carries Inferno and Cobra SRMs. It can carry up to four, in any combination, but most pilots opt for two of each. The Inferno is particularly useful in tying up local security forces with putting out fires.

Even at the height of the Royal Imperial Aerospace Force's funding, the Vampire was a rare sight. Usually, the bulk of the aerospace fighting was handled by Akufus and Hellcats. Given the expense of the Vampire's armor and other systems, its amazing that the Conclave can field a force of 312 of them. Only 20% of the materials are purchased through the legal markets. The rest comes from grey markets (tariff dodgers), black markets and outright theft.

Regardless of the source of the acquisitions, the Vampire is a testament of the ingenuity and resourcefulness of the Conclave. During normal operation, Conclave Vampires raid enemy supplies and launch diversionary attacks to allow Omega agents to slip into an installation or base. In the rare event that a Conclave warship is caught in the open, Vampires fight until their carrier can escape.

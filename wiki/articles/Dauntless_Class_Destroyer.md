While the [[Atlas Class Carrier|Atlas]] was the face of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and the [[Vigilant Class Frigate|Vigilant]] was its hands, the HMS ''Dauntless'' and the ships that followed it were the [[Onyx Empire|Empire]]'s fists. No other production class in the Royal Imperial Navy's arsenal was better-suited for starship combat.

== History ==

The development of the hull designated DG-1, which was eventually commissioned as the [[HMS Dauntless|HMS ''Dauntless'']], began after the commissioning of the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']] and the [[HMS Atlas|HMS ''Atlas'']]. Admiral [[Robert Marshall]] did not believe that the lightly armed frigate would be sufficient to shape the ebb and flow of battle to the will of a master commander.

To that end, he ordered the design of a [[:Category:Destroyers|destroyer]], a capital ship focused on offense and focused destructive capabilities. While the [[Vigilant Class Frigate|''Vigilant'']] had been built around a single [[Relativistic Mass Driver|MAC-1]], the ''Dauntless'' and other ships of its class were meant to be able to carry several such weapons.

== Development ==

The design requirements were submitted to [[Haven Shipyards]] in late [[2301]], and the design team at [[Argent Haven]] was assigned to develop the prototype hull. Doctor Michael Ranier led the design team, and completed the blueprints for the DG-1 hull on May 19, [[2302]]. The DG-1 hull was laid down at [[Argent Haven]] on April 4, [[2302]].

Initial construction of the superstructure was simple, drawing on many of the lessons learned in the design of the [[Vigilant Class Frigate|Vigilant]] line. The forward section was devoted to housing for [[Anti-Warship Torpedo|AWT-2]] torpedo tubes, ammunition magazines, backup fire control systems for the torpedos, and off-duty quarters for the weapon service crews. The plan was to have crews quartered as close to their battle stations as possible, to avoid losing precious seconds in the event of a surprise attack.

Two secondary hulls on either side of the main superstructure were added to house the massive [[Relativistic Mass Drivers|MAC-1 Cannons]] that served as the design's main weapons. The power requirements for the weapons were far too great for a single reactor to handle.

=== Power Generation ===

The [[Royal Imperial Labs]] Energy Laboratory had established [[Tolwin Power Incorporated]] to produce reactors for the Imperial military. The director of Tolwin Power, Dr. Charles Hancock, was instructed to develop a main reactor to power the DG-1's main systems in non-combat situations, and a secondary reactor to power the MAC-1s.

Hancock's design team worked around the clock, but were running behind schedule. The TXD prototype reactor was rated at 980mW, but was causing serious problems with the DG-1's internal heat levels. The final design for the T1D was due by July 6, 2302. Facing cost overruns and heat dissapation issues, Dr. Hancock scrapped the design and developed a smaller three-reactor system, delivering the final three T1D reactors for the DG-1 on July 18, 2302.

Each of the three reactors was capable of a 750mW output, and used the extra space available for more efficient cooling systems. They were also equipped with emergency core dump protocols, and emergency coolant systems which could reduce the reactor temperature by 400 degrees Kelvin within twenty seconds.

=== Shield Technology ===

[[Erika Strout]]'s design team at the Royal Imperial Physics Laboratory had come up with some rather impressive results in their Plasma Field technology since the launch of the [[HMS Vigilant|HMS ''Vigilant'']] allowed [[Royal Imperial Navy]] crews to conduct live fire training against captured [[United Earth Alliance]] weapons. The PDS-1 used on the [[Vigilant Class Frigate]] and PDS-2 used on the [[Atlas Class Carrier]] were efficient and useful, but hardly comparable to the leaps in technology incorporated into the PDS-3.

[[Image:Dauntless Class Destroyer Side View.jpg|right|300px]] After Atlan Dynamics was formed to handle production of shield technologies, their engineering team was able to come up with a way to recharge the plasma field within twenty seconds by feeding the generators straight from the ship's main reactor. If a Vigilant Frigate or Atlas Carrier lost shielding, it would take several minutes to recharge and power up the shields again. This rapid restoration of the shields was directly responsible for the Dauntless Class Destroyer's later reputation for survivability and tenacity.

=== Firepower ===

The MAC-1 Cannon was considered a marvel of magnetic engineering, and the refinements made to the MAC-1 before it was installed on the DG-1 were no exception. Each cannon could fire a projectile at velocities near 0.01xC, creating a devastating kinetic energy transfer on impact. An unshielded starship would be gutted, while shielded ones would take serious damage.

The four-cannon battery of the Dauntless line was equipped with improved automatic loading systems, which increased the rate of fire for each cannon by 10% over the Vigilant Class Frigate's single MAC-1.

Later battle reports would indicate that a Dauntless Class Destroyer could engage and eliminate an [[ADF-5000 Class Cruiser]] in four seconds if it could get the drop on it. A single salvo was often enough to bring down the shields of any [[United Earth Alliance]] vessel, but managing to acquire a target lock and hit the enemy was often a major challenge at the extreme range and velocity of most space battles of the [[Second Exodus War]].

[[Image:Dauntless Class Destroyer Top View.jpg|right|300px]] Because of the Dauntless Class Destroyer's limited numbers--three destroyers to a carrier group---they often had to let the frigates' lane of fire do the killing, while their own firepower was used to threaten and harry the enemy. The torpedo tubes were often put to work more than the cannons, despite the devastating firepower the cannons could bring to bear.

The Dauntless Class line was equipped with a full complement of Close-In Weapon System emplacements, despite the fact that it operated close to the carrier group's fighter cover and rarely had to use them.

== Reputation ==

The Dauntless Class was famous for its brutal firepower and long surviveability in battle. The [[HMS Fearless|HMS ''Fearless'']], in particular, saw many fierce battles against devastating odds, and once fought off three [[Mesarthrim]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] in open combat, though the battle permanently crippled the ship and resulted in its captain setting off scuttling charges.

The Dauntless Class saw service throughout the life of the [[Onyx Empire]], and surviving ships were claimed by the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] and recommissioned into the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]. Many hulls under construction at [[Kalidasa]] were seized by the [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]], and several ships were taken as spoils of war after the disaster known as the [[Sirius Incursion]].

The Prefecture continued to produce the design after the war, though they renamed the class to reflect the complete overhaul of the new systems available. The [[Seminole Class Destroyer]] continued the traditions and reputation started by the Dauntless and its sister ships.

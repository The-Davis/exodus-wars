The HMS ''Victorious'' was the thirty-seventh ship built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

Due to the shortage of qualified officers at the time of launch, the HMS ''Victorious'' was held in reserve near [[Ah Kin]], while the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] continued its training program. It was eventually placed under the command of [[Oswald Voss]], when his [[Omegas|Omega]] handlers determined that his loyalty was no longer in question. Voss' competent command skills and outstanding navigation skills earned him the rank of Rear Admiral and command of the [[Black Armada of the Royal Imperial Navy#Fourth Imperial Fleet|Fourth Imperial Fleet]].

Voss commanded the HMS ''Victorious'' throughout its career in the Royal Imperial Navy. Towards the end of the [[Second Exodus War]], the ship was captured intact by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], and turned over to the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] as a gesture of goodwill.

The Myt’Gaul are the militant constituent of the holy [[Saint Fleischer]] - servant of God, and judicator of the [[Mesarthrim Holy Laws]].  They are clerics whose duty to the [[Mesarthrim Church]] is simply stated as: ''To defend the Church as his Lord requires.''

The Myt’Gaul are servants of the [[Mesarthrim Church|Church]], therefore, they did not participate in the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], and were not commissioned under the [[United Centusi States]].  Their loyalty and fealty were sworn to [[William Camp|Chancellor William Camp]] in [[2325]] after the [[United Centusi States|UCS]] charter called [[Mesarthrim Holy Laws]] the laws of the State.

==History==
The Myt’Gaul were formed in [[2120]] by then [[Saint Fleischer|Reverend Fleischer]].  They were a group of Clerics whose duty was to defend the [[Mesarthrim Church|Church]] and clergymen from [[Anderung]] raids.  More importantly, they were charged with the duty of rescuing captured clergymen from [[Anderung]] Prison Camps, because a [[Mesarthrim]] Holy Man is not allowed to soil his hands in labour.

[[Saint Fleischer|Reverend Fleischer’s]] charge was considered so important that only the best Clerics’ and Soldiers were allowed to even apply for the position within the Myt’Gaul.  The Myt’Gaul leave their standard education at the age of ten, and spend the next ten years of their lives learning the scriptures of God, and training to become a [[Myt’Gaul Judicator|Judicator]].  The concept, system, and training were all established by [[Saint Fleischer|Reverend Fleischer]] in the Articles of the Arbiter.  Almost immediately after his death, [[Saint Fleischer|Reverend Fleischer]] was recognised as a Holy Saint of the [[Mesarthrim Church]].

==Important Dates==

*In [[2180]], Eighteen Myt’Gaul [[Myt’Gaul Judicator|Judicators]] attacked the [[Anderung]] battle fortress of [[Hiriktun]].  The [[Anderung]] had assaulted seven different [[Mesarthrim Church|Church]] meetings and had taken fifteen Holy Men captive.  They Myt’Gaul were charged with rescuing them, and their assault was flawless, claiming the guidance of [[Saint Fleischer]], the Myt’Gaul rescued all fifteen Holy Men, and killed one hundred and twenty [[Anderung]] and [[Anderung Loyalist|Loyalist]].  Not a single Myt’Gaul was lost.

*In [[2260]] the Myt’Gaul were used for the first time not as a rescue or defensive force, but as a special forces assault team.  The Myt’Gaul snuck into the [[Anderung]] battle fortress at Ice Crown Bay, and disrupted the Anderung communication, Command & Control net, and executed [[Rulan-ilb shell]], the [[Anderung]] occupation commander.  When the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] were formed, the prefix of Myt ''(meaning It is my)'' were honourably attached to the new [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Mesarthrim Heers]] names.

*In [[2322]] the Myt’Gaul captured two of the [[Onyx Empire|Onyx Empires]] [[Omega Agent|Omega Agents]] and purified their souls through ritual interrogation and torture that lasted seven hours - as decreed in the Articles of the Arbiter by [[Saint Fleischer]], however, the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|UMC’s]] new allies, the [[Alliance of Planets]] had laws against torture, and the [[Ikronin Jurekön|Ikronin]] issued harsh protests against the [[United Mesarthrim Clans|UMC]].  This incident almost caused the [[Mesarthrim]] to be ejected from the [[Alliance of Planets|Alliance]] only seventeen days after it had been accepted.

*In [[2323]], Myt’Gaul [[Myt’Gaul Judicator|Judicators]] captured the [[Onyx Empire]] Command Compound in [[Australia]] just prior to the full assault by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Heer]].  Their attack destroyed the entire command structure for the [[Onyx Empire|Onyx’s]] Pacific Defence, and helped ease many early [[United Mesarthrim Clans Heer|Heer]] assaults.

*In [[2327]] the Myt’Gaul become an important element in the [[Tempest War]], in numerous engagements throughout the conflict.

==Training==
The Myt’Gaul training beings at the age of ten.  It should be noted that it is not a dishonourable thing to fail at becoming a Myt’Gaul, simply as something you did not have the strength of will to accomplish.  Some say it is better to try and fail at becoming a Myt’Gaul, then not trying at all.  Many ten year olds attempt to join the Myt'gaul, but drop out at some point during the Initiation.

===Initiation===
The very first task an applicant must complete is crucifixion.  A Myt’Gaul candidate must hang on a cross from noon until three in the afternoon - the same length of time the sky darkened for Christ.  Then, the candidate must sit in solitary in a tomb for three days to represent the time from Christ’s death until his resurrection.  Only after that is the candidate allowed to be properly bandaged from their wounds, however the scar is allowed to remain so that all [[Mesarthrim]] know that the candidate has been crucified.  Most ten year olds who survive the crucifixion are successful in finishing their training, however almost 60% of applicants fail somewhere along the crucifixion and entombment.  Those who fail are healed, and their scars are tattooed black to indicate their failure.  

===Instruction===
The second stage of training is an intense course of studying and basic skill training.  Myt’Gaul are trained extensively in hand-eye co-ordination, perception, problem solving, and dexterity.  They are given difficult, and often extensive courses and puzzles to accomplish within certain time limits.  In the early years of their training, any failure is met with public flogging and food rationing.  After their physical training comes their studies, they are taught by the [[Myt'Gaul Apostle|Myt'gaul Apostle's]] the Articles of the Arbiter and the [[Mesarthim Holy Laws]].  Their training lasts for several years, until the student it able to recite the entire Articles of the Arbiter, the [[Mesarthim Holy Laws]], and the complete Periodic Table of Elements from memory.  It is felt that an understanding of God's nature leads to an understanding of God himself.  No student may progress into the next stage of training until they have proven their intellectual competency.

===Conditioning===
After a Myt'Gaul finishes his mental training it become necessary for him to get in the best possible physical shape he possibly could.  An Initiate wakes up as dawn crests the horizon and physically trains through the day, taking in a highly controlled diet.  These training days go six days a week, with Sunday as a day of rest (and consequently better tasting food).  Each Initiate will exercise, drill, and practice in a desert region on [[Mesar]].  After they proved skill in the desert, they would move onto training in the wetlands.  Next was the snow.  This gave the Myt'Gaul training in numerous environments - giving them a feeling of normality even in the most extreme conditions.

===Graduation===
When an Initiate graduates from his training he is given his first dagger.  The blade is made of black steel, and the handle is carved from the bone of an Anox.  Then they take this dagger that was given to them, and stab them self in the left side, where Christ was stabbed.  After the ceremony, their wound his healed, leaving the scar.

By the time a Myt'Gaul enters service as a Judicator, he has the same wounds as Christ.  This is a very important symbol to the Myt'Gaul, and annually, they allow their wounds to be blessed by a Cleric of [[Saint Fleischer]].

==Structure==
The [[Mesarthim Church]] commands the Myt’Gaul, and therefore all levels of the Myt’Gaul, for practical purposes, report directly to the [[State Reverend]] himself.  Once you have graduated from training, no Myt’Gaul ‘ranks’ higher or lower then another.

===Acolyte===
There are only five Acolytes at any one time.  An Acolyte is appointed by the [[State Reverend]] after a week of solitude and prayer.  The Acolyte is sworn to administer the Myt’Gaul order; financially, politically, and socially.  They keep meticulous records of all applicants, their tithes, and the locations of all their agents.  They also keep a register of all the Holy Men of the [[Mesarthrim Church]] and where they are - so that if they need to be rescued, an operation can be easily established.  Being an Acolyte is a very difficult task, and all Myt’Gaul show them immense respect and admiration.

===Apostle===
Apostles are almost all Myt’Gaul who have been wounded in combat or grown to old to operate in the field.  Apostles are appointed by Acolyte’s, however the choice is rarely a surprise - if you are wounded and survive, or reach older years, you will be asked to serve Saint Fleischer as an Apostle.
Apostles are the teachers of the Initiates.  They perform the crucifixions, teach the Initiates their studies, and punish them for their failure.  During the days of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]], one Apostle is sent to the Grand Chambers in [[Stone Bridge]] where he hears the will of the [[Uber-General]].

===Monk===
To become a Monk, an Initiate must train with a monk for several years until they become proficient in skills pertaining to infiltration & Intel gathering.  Monks exist not to be seen or noticed.  They practice escape, evasion, and blending techniques.  A Monk rarely uses a gun, and excels at hand-to-hand combat as well as avoiding combat situations.  Some Monks’ have been known to vanish from right in front of their pursuers.  There are only a handful of Myt’Gaul Monks, and the Mesarthrim who are, are the best.

===Judicator===
The Judicators are the base Mesarthrim Myt’Gaul.  They are the elite of the elite, skilful in their tactic, and intelligently cunning.  They are a perfect balance of belief, intellect, and soldier.  Every Initiate that graduates from their training becomes a Judicator, to do anything else within the Myt’Gaul order requires any of the prerequisites listed in the above positions.

Once you become a Myt’Gaul, only death releases you of your service to Saint Fleischer.

== Organization ==

The Myt'Gaul utilize a horizontal command structure, each man can give equal commands to the next, and each man serves both God, and the Saint Fleischer.  Their actual orders come from the [[State Reverend]], he decides what missions the Myt'Gaul will be involved in and how many to send.  It should be noted, however, that prior to the [[Tempest War]], the Myt'Gaul were never fielded in large numbers - even then, it was only at [[William Camp|Chancellor William Camp's]] insistence that they were sent in large numbers.

==Equipment==

===Judicator Battle Gear===
The Myt'Gaul wear a fully-sealed, powered-armor suit--an enhanced version of the [[Myt'Herra]].  The primary improvement over the Myt'Herra suit is the strength amplification capabilities.  The advanced powered suit amplifies an individual Myt'Gaul's strength by 200%.  It also has slightly heavier armor than the standard suit, giving Myt'Gaul more protection.  The trade off to this extra speed and power comes from the fact that a standard battery pack for the Myt'Gaul suit is slightly larger, and only powers the suit for 12 hours.  The suits have the ability to 'dial down' the amplified strength, and use the battery more efficiently.  However, for longer operations, more battery packs are usually carried. 

Myt'Gaul carry very little in the way of supplies.  Most missions ordinarily last only a matter of hours.  When longer duration operations occur, standard [[Myt'Herra]] packs can be carried, but they are not carried ordinarily. 

The standard weapon Myt'Gaul weapon is a pair of Ciran Arms Co. Type 10 Automatic Combat Carbines.  The T-10C is a powerful weapon capable of firing at a rate higher than most [[Blazer]] type weapons.  While it has a range only half that of a T-55S Blazer rifle, the intensity of its blasts and the rate of fire are the same.  It also operates on similar power packs, giving each T-10C enough power to fire 30 rounds.  Optical sensors on the T-10C feed targeting information into the HUD in the soldier's helmet.  As standard issue, each Myt'Gaul carries two of these weapons, and is trained to use both simultaneously.  Other weapons carried include the Fireline Industries Type 105 Sniper Rifle and the shoulder-fired Lariot Firearms Manufacturers "Orisis" Single Use Anti-Armor Rifle.  The T-105SR and Osiris SUAAR are also used by [[Myt'Herra]] troopers in the field.

===Order of Saint Fleischer===
Being a militant constituent of Saint Fleischer, the Myt'Gaul are required to wear light body armour every time they are in public.  The light armour has imprinted on it the Ensign of the Myt'Gaul and the Seal of [[Saint Fleischer]].  Their colours are red, yellow, and black.

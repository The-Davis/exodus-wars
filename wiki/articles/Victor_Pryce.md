''Victor Philip Pryce'' (born October 17, [[2280]] was one of the commanders in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. He was one of the students of [[Anthony Hadrian]], and learned the art of political machinations and hidden agenda from the master of subtlety.

=== Unfit for Service ===

Victor Pryce was always interested in the military. As a young boy, he studied military technology and military history. He hoped to serve in the military of the [[United Earth Alliance]], but due to a hereditary case of sickle-cell anemia, he was considered unfit for duty. In [[2298]], he enrolled in the Saint Barnabas University at [[Rio de Andes]], majoring in military history.

The clandestine takeover of [[Ah Kin]] by the [[Torimur]] exiles and the subsequent creation of the [[Onyx Empire]] did little to effect Pryce's scholarly career. Apart from the disappearance of the Dean of Students in [[2303]] and the unexpected death of two professors, none of the students noticed the shift in political power.

=== Professor ===

In [[2308]], Victor Pryce graduated with a PhD in Military History. Soon afterwards, he found himself whisked away by the [[Omegas]] to the Onyx Empire's secret training facilities. Pryce soon integrated into the new underground Imperial military society, instructing officer candidates in military history, strategic theory, and analysis of past military engagements.

During his tenure as an instructor, Dr. Pryce often sat in on other classes and participated in physical training with the students. He befriended Anthony Hadrian, who saw significant command potential in Pryce.

Hadrian obtained special dispensation for Dr. Pryce to become an officer, despite his physical shortcomings. From [[2310]] to [[2315]], Pryce received the full battery of officer training in addition to his duties instructing others in strategy and history.

=== The Price of Command ===

When the [[Second Exodus War]] began in 2315, Victor Pryce received his official commissioning orders from [[Lucius Black]], granting him the rank of Commodore in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. He was assigned the [[HMS Argolis|HMS ''Argolis'']] and its carrier group.

Commodore Pryce quickly learned the art of manipulating the Emperor's whims. Anthony Hadrian taught him well, and Pryce was often able to earn choice assignments for his carrier group. He picked his battles well, earning prestige for himself and his crew without ever really putting them in danger.

Unfortunately, his choices ended up costing him his life. When [[Sirius Incursion|Operation Lawless]] was written up by Admiral [[Michael Burke]], Commodore Pryce was able to win command of what he considered to be a soft target with enormous rewards. Pryce was brevetted to the rank of Rear Admiral, and given command of four other Carrier Groups.

Operation Lawless ended in disaster. The disguised warships of the Sirius Families fooled Pryce and his sub-commanders, and lured them into a trap. His entire command was destroyed, and the HMS ''Argolis'' was boarded by [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanova]] troops. After hours of battle, the bridge was compromised, and Rear Admiral Pryce was forced to surrender.

Victor Pryce was brought to the palace of [[Nicholai Asanova]]. Nicholai told Pryce to choose a member of his staff to carry a message back to [[Earth]]. Pryce offered to carry the message himself. To his horror, he found that ''he'' was the message. Pryce was decapitated, and his head was sent back to Earth, warning the Empire not to attack the [[Sirius Cluster]] again.

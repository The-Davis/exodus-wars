'''Wayne Matthews'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Date of Death: April 2, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Place of Death: [[Leskovik]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Wayne Matthews'' was a member of the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], and later a Commander in the [[Prefecture Galactic Navy]]. He died when his ship, the FDS ''Fidelity'' was destroyed at the start of the [[Tempest War]].

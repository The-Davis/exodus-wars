The '''Des System''' consists of the star Scorpus Des, the six planets, and the single moon.

In order of their distances from Scorpus Des, the planets are [[Des Sho]], [[Des Arc]], [[Des Wei]], [[Des Gara]], [[Des Uvo]], and [[Des Yi]]. Only [[Des Arc]] is orbited by a moon.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Scorpus Des is the Des System's only star. Scorpus Des is a class F2R orange dwarf. The Des System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Des is part of the [[De Mairan Sector]].

=== Des Sho ===

[[Des Sho]] (0.6AU) is an uninhabitable terrestrial planet with a thick, acidic atmosphere. It has no moons and has never been explored.

=== Des Arc ===

[[Des Arc]] (0.95AU) was terraformed in [[2230]] and has been an inhabittable planet ever since. It has one moon, [[Arc Carus]].

=== Des Wei ===

[[Des Wei]] (1.7AU) is a cold, inhospitable planet with a thin atmosphere and sheer, hurricane force winds over most of the surface. It is inhabited only by military personnel training to be pilots.

=== Des Gara ===

[[Des Gara]] (6.3AU) is a large gas giant. Oddly enough, like the other gas giants in the Des System, [[Des Gara]] has no moons, possibly because of the star system's total lack of asteroids.

=== Des Uvo ===

[[Des Uvo]] (11.1AU) is a medium gas giant, and is a binary planet with [[Des Yi]]. It has no moons.

=== Des Yi ===

[[Des Yi]] (11.1AU) is the twin gas giant of [[Des Uvo]].

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Des System was colonized in [[2218]]. [[United Earth Alliance]] explorers were surprised to find a moon orbitting [[Des Arc]] while the three gas giants in the system had no moons at all, and the system was free of asteroids. Some wonder if perhaps the [[Anthronoris]] had a hand in the creation of the system, but no trace of their civilization has been found on any of the planets.

[[Des Arc]] had to be terraformed before large numbers of people could move in. [[Des Wei]] was not exploited until the [[Onyx Empire]] captured the star system in [[2316]].

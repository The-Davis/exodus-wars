===Overview===

The Infantry Fighting Vehicle has been a popular concept since the early 21st century.  An IFV must be able to move at high speeds, carry a full squad of Marines, be impervious to light arms, and be able to fight armor if necessary.  The Goblin was designed to fill all of these roles.

[[Image:M33Goblin1.jpg]]

The design was originally a heavy mover meant to transport workers on the less-developed areas of Mars.  While the red planet has been inhabitable since the early 22nd century, there are still very hostile areas that people must visit from time to time.  Seeing the robust and compact design at work on Mars, the Prefecture asked the Mars Combined Products company to design an armed version. 

===Capabilities===

The Goblin's mobility is astounding.  On the open road, it can almost keep pace with the Badger, and off-road, it can outrun and outmaneuver it.  The wide, solid base provides unparalleled control and stability.  The Goblin rarely has to worry about being forced to engage heavy targets.

When it does fight, the Goblin's armor and weapons are quite capable, but not against heavy targets.  Its six Medium Anti-Armor Weapons (MAAWs) are limited to two shots each before its out of ammo.  It's armor is somewhat limited against heavy weapons, but it can deflect several LAAW shots before failing.  Its shields are very limited in their use, and only have enough power to deflect infantry weapons.

[[Image:M33Goblin2.jpg]]

The infantry hatch's placement does make it a precarious chokepoint, so Goblins usually face the rear of the vehicle towards the enemy before unloading.  Even Marines don't like to leap into the teeth of enemy suppression fire.

===Deployment===

The Prefecture Marine Corps is the only organization currently using the Goblin IFV.  The IFV concept is becoming more widely used in modern militaries, but most armies prefer the higher-capacity Rahn Industries "Paladin" design.

[[Image:M33Goblin3.jpg]]

Most ground units in the Corps have enough Goblins to keep all of their Marines moving at all times.

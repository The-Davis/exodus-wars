The '''Gorhamut''' (''xenosaurus thezes'') are believed to be the oldest star-faring species, outpacing the [[Anthronoris]] by a few millennia. Most of their early history is benign, and it was not until [[First Contact]] that they became the dangerous creatures they are today. Their government, known as the [[Jurazoar Maropsene]], is possibly the oldest in the universe.

In their own language, their name means "the melody." In [[Lorentin]], they are the "razor lizards," called such for the sharpness of their minds.

===Appearance===

Gorhamut average between ten and twelve meters long, and can reach three to six metric tons in weight. They are large, powerful theropod species, able to stand erect on their hind legs, or walk on all four legs if they choose to do so. 

The Gorhamut's rear legs are long and powerful, capable of propelling them at speeds up to 80 kilometers per hour in short bursts. Their forelegs are equally powerful, allowing them to stand and defend themselves in a sturdy stance. Their feet are padded with huge fatty deposits, giving them a comfortable cushion and stable walking surface. Their hands have two fingers and two opposable thumbs.

Gorhamut have a thick tail, nearly five meters long, which they use for balance while running. It is rather stiff, acting as a counterweight for their large upper bodies. The tail is capable of powerful sweeps, and was their main method of defense against predators in the ancient past. It remains their prefered method of melee combat.

The Gorhamut have long, sinewy necks, nearly two meters long. Their shoulders are broad and powerful, and their heads are long, large and thick. Gorhamut have horns protruding from their forheads, and "rachis" covering the tops of their heads. They also have a bony frill extending from the top of their skull across their neck. Gorhamut lips are rough and rather rigid, making it difficult for them to form some sounds commonly used in human languages.

Gorhamut have obvivious primary and secondary gender characteristics. Gorhamut females are significantly smaller than males, topping out at ten meters in length to the males' twelve meters. Gorhamut males grow rachis on their faces, almost like beards, while females only grow rachis on the tops of their heads. The horns of female Gorhamut are small and slight, while the males grow large, strong horns. The female frill is rounded, while the male frill has several bony knobs and curves on it.

====Skin Colors====

Gorhamut have extremely tough, thick skin covering their bodies. It is at least three centimeters thick in most areas. Gorhamut skin varies in color. It usually ranges from black to light gray in color. Some Gorhamut races have dark or light brown skin.

====Rachis Colors====

The Gorhamut are protected from heat and weather by "rachis", a series of fine keratin protein strands that are similar to both hair and feathers. Rachis are thicker than hair, but do not have the series of vanes and barbs like feathers. Gorhamut have fine, soft rachis on their heads and on their forearms. Male Gorhamut have rachis on their faces as well. Gorhamut have thick, rigid rachis on their backs, tails, and hind legs. Gorhamut have thick, soft rachis along their thighs and protecting their genitalia. Most rachis are slightly metalic or shiny in color. White with silver is common, as is a coppery yellow color. Green is not unknown, but green rachis are usually tinged with gold colors.

====Eye Colors====

Gorhamut have large, intelligent-looking eyes. Their eyes have multiple colors. The outermost band is white, followed by a colored band, then a second colored band, then two pupils in each eye. A single Gorhamut eye can provide focus and depth perception on a single object, and each eye has two optic nerves. The Gorhamut have two visual processing centers within their brains, allowing them to focus on different things with each eye. Their eyes are lined and protected by tough "rictals", a kerotin protein follicle similar to the rachis on their bodies, and similar in appearance to human eyelashes. Gorhamut eyes can be almost any color, and are often different colors. It is not uncommon to see a red and blue eye on one side, and a green and brown eye on the other side.

===Skeletal System===

Gorhamut have thick, tough bones, lined with several hard minerals. Once they reach maturity, it is almost impossible to break the major bones of a Gorhamut by normal means. They also have powerful sinews and tendons which are attached by burrowing, anchored tendrils into the bone. It is almost impossible to tear or snap a Gorhamut tendon.

===Respiratory System===

The lungs of the Gorhamut are enormous. They breathe massive amounts of air with every inhalation. The Gorhamut have both a syrinx and a larynx, giving them an incredible range of vocalization and sound. The Gorhamut songs are much like birds, sweet and eerie. It is for this that the Gorhamut call themselves "the melody." While their mouths are rigid, they can make most sounds in any human dialect, with some noteable exceptions.

===Nervous System===

The Gorhamut brain is incredibly dense, with 300% more nerve clusters and neurons than an equivalent human brain. Their intelligence is incredible, made even more so by the sharpness and quickness of their wit. Gorhamut grasp the truth of things quickly, and are almost impossible to deceive. For this reason, the [[Anthronoris]] named them the "razor lizards"--a misnomer, as the Gorhamut only share a slight physical appearance with some reptiles. They do not share any biological features with them.

Oddly enough, the Gorhamut psyche does not grasp the concept of lying, or even half truths. They see through deception with near perfection, but cannot craft a deception of their own. If a Gorhamut gives an order, it does not say "you will do this thing", because it does not know if that is the truth. Instead, it says "I order you to do this thing."

The reflexes and reaction times of a Gorhamut are very quick, with extremely efficient neurochemical instruction systems for delivering messages from the brain to the muscles.

===Metabolism===

The mouth of a Gorhamut is full of flat, grinding teeth, designed to tear and chew plant matter. They also have two small, sharp tusks about eight centimeters long, which they used to use to dig up roots or tear bark for their diets.

Gorhamut are warm-blooded, with an internal body temperature roughly five degrees higher than a human's. They also absorb sunlight to help their digestive system, generating no less than twelve different immune-system and digestive system enzymes from the sun.

===Lifespan and Reproduction===

Gorhamut have a potential lifespan of about 1500 years, though only about ten percent of their population lives this long. The average lifespan is about 1200 years. Gorhamut rarely succumb to disease or accidents.

Gorhamut females gestate their young internally for a period of about four years. Unlike mammals, Gorhamut young do not gestate in a placenta. Instead, the mother's womb simply stores the Gorhamut "embrum", a rigid shell with a full nutritional supply. When a Gorhamut is mature enough to survive, it hatches within its mother, and is then born live. The birthing process releases several enzymes which dissolve the shell and eject it with the infant Gorhamut.

When they are born, infant Gorhamut are unable to walk, but can cling to their mother's back with remarkable strength. Young Gorhamut are fed fructate, a milk-like substance secreted from the fyxocrine glands, which run up and down a female Gorhamut's torso. Fructate and the Gorhamut fyxocrine glands are almost identical in function to lactate and mammary glands in mammals, but the chemical compounds are vastly different, needed to meet the dietary needs of an infant Gorhamut.

Young Gorhamut are weaned after about ten years. They depend on and learn from their parents for fifty to sixty years afterwards, though many Gorhamut retain close family ties even after it is no longer a biological necessity.

Gorhamut do not reach their full size until they are about 300 years old.

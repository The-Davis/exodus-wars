The security force of Rahn Industries was put into place after the Terran invasion by the Empire. It was realized that the CEO and the employees such as the head designers and engineers needed to have some sort protection from the possibilities of invasion and capture.

The first security force was provided by the United Earth Alliance government. The debate came when the move into deep space was finished and Justin Rahn, with the board of advisers, discussed whether or not there was a need for a security force. After seeing what had happened on Terra and how quickly the United Earth police fell, they decided that it was necessary to provide independent company protection.

The first truly independent security force was a small group of volunteers who were trained and supplied by Rahn Industries. Their responsibilities included basic patrolling along with screening of incoming of passengers and ships into the airspace of any Rahn Industries facility.

As the force evolved it's command, power, and threat level continued to rise and with the operation of military grade equipment and technology the RISF was forced to sign a mercenary treaty with various members of the galaxy including the Prefecture and the Alliance of Planets. With the treaty signed, the RISF was allowed to operate at certain military-level capacity in various jurisdictions, though its largest weapons are not allowed in any planetary space.


Rahn fleet assets are extremely powerful, but limited in numbers. Their flagship, the Avalon, is larger than most battleships, and armed to the teeth with the latest in defensive weaponry. It can defend itself from almost any attack, but does not have a viable weapons platform for assault.

The company also operates a handful of their Avenger class heavy cruisers. When these ships are all in one place, they can repel a military-level threat. Rahn Industries tends to keep them spread out at its various deep space facilities.

The most common Rahn warship is the Marshall class corvette. It is the only military vessel belonging to the company that is allowed to operate in most ports of call.

Rahn Industries has two carriers, the Alaska class prototype and the Aztec class prototype, both of which act as test beds for aircraft and as mobile bases for RISF fighters. Additionally, they have one ace in the hole: the Typhoon class prototype has been converted into a heavy weapons test bed, and has the firepower to knock out a heavy cruiser.

RISF air assets are limited. Most deep space facilities only have a squadron of Rapier interceptors at their disposal. The military-grade Skyhawk VTOL, one of Rahn Industries best-selling products, is also used, and in its unarmed form is allowed to operate at their ground facilities.

Rahn ground assets are allowed to operate with military-grade weaponry at their planetary facilities. The Crimson Guard are a well trained private security organization, and excellent at close quarters combat, though they are completely untrained in field tactics and warfare as a whole. Their commanders know how to defend hardened installations, but not how to attack anything. They use the Badger, a popular ATV used by the Prefecture Army, Marines, and popular in the private sector as a recreational and personal vehicle. The Crimson Guard also has the Heavy Expandable Tactical Truck, an excellent system with modular designs at its disposal.

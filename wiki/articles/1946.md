== Events of 1946 ==

===January===

* January 10
**First meeting of the [[United Nations]]
**[[The Technology Race]]: Project Diana bounces radar waves off [[Luna]], measuring the exact distance between [[Terra]] and [[Luna]] and proving that the communication was possible in space, effectively opening the [[Terran]] Space Age.
* January 16 - [[Charles de Gaulle]] resigns as head of the [[France (Terra)|French]] provisional government.
* January 17 - The [[United Nations]] Security Council holds its first session.
* January 31 - [[Cold War]]: Socialist Republic of [[Yugoslavia (Terra)| Yugoslavia]] forms.

===February===
* February 1 - Trygve Lie of [[Norway (Terra)| Norway]] is picked to be the first [[United Nations]] Secretary General.
* February 14 - [[The Technology Race]]: [[ENIAC]] computer is unveiled at the University of Pennsylvania.
* February 15 - [[Cold War]]: [[Canada (Terra)|Canada]] indicts 22 [[Communist]] agents.

===March===
*March 2 - [[Vietnam War]]: Ho Chi Minh begins organizing and arming the Viet Minh to contest [[France (Terra)|French]] control of the [[Vietnam (Terra)| Vietnam]] region of French Indochina.
*March 5 - [[Cold War]]: In his speech at Westminster College, [[Winston Churchill]] talks about the Iron Curtain.
*March 6 - [[Cold War]]: A [[Communist]]-dominated government under Petru Groza assumes power in [[Romania (Terra)|Romania]].
*March 10 - [[The Jihad]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops put down revolt in [[Arabia (Terra)|Lebanon]].
*March 15 - [[Winston Churchill]] denies independence to India. [[Mohandas Gandhi]] and Jawaharlal Nehru inspire massive civil unrest.
*March 19 - [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] and [[Switzerland (Terra)|Switzerland]] reform diplomatic relations.
*March 22 – [[The Jihad]]: [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]] puts down revolt in [[Arabia (Terra)|Jordan]].

===April===
*April 7 - [[The Jihad]]: Muslim rebels in [[Arabia (Terra)| Syria]] win independence from [[France (Terra)|France]].
*April 18 - Last meeting of [[League of Nations]] – it transfers its mission to [[United Nations]] and disbands itself.

===May===
* May 26 - [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovak]] parliamentary elections are held. The [[Communist]] Party is victorious and begins sweeping changes, bringing the country in line with [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] idealism.

===June===
*June 8 - [[The Jihad]]: In [[Arabia (Terra)| Indonesia]], Muslims fight [[Netherlands (Terra)|Dutch]] colonial occupation.

===July===
* July 25 - [[The Technology Race]]: In the first underwater test of the atomic bomb, the surplus USS ''Saratoga'' is sunk near Bikini Atoll in the Pacific Ocean.

===August===
*August 19 - [[The Jihad]]: Violence between Muslims and Hindus in Calcutta leaves 3000 dead. [[British Empire (Terra)|British]] troops restore order after burning down two mosques and shooting two Muslim Imams.

===September===
*September 4 - [[The Jihad]]: Street violence between Muslims and Hindus in Bombay causes widespread destruction. Hundreds on both sides are injured, but only twenty Hindus are killed in the fighting.

===October===
* October 2 - [[Communists]] take over in [[Bulgaria (Terra)|Bulgaria]].
* October 13 - [[France (Terra)|France]] adopts the constitution of the Fourth Republic.
* October 23 - [[United Nations]]' first meeting in Geneva.

===November===
*November 8 - [[Vietnam War]]: Viet Minh riot in Haiphong and clash with [[France (Terra)|French]] troops. [[France (Terra)|French]] cruiser ''Suffren'' opens fire. 6000 Viet Minh and [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnamese]] natives killed.

===December===
*December 12 - [[The Jihad]]: The [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]]-supported [[Communist]] government in Tabriz, [[Arabia (Terra)|Iran]] is ousted by Muslim rebels.
*December 19 - [[Vietnam War]]: Due to Viet Minh rebellion, the [[France (Terra)|French]] colonial authority declares martial law in the [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]] region of French Indochina.

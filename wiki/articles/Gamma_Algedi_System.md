The '''Gamma Algedi System''' consists of the star Gamma Algedi, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids.

In order of their distances from Gamma Algedi, the planets are Ga I, Ga II, [[Sindh]], [[Pesht]], and Ga V.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Gamma Algedi is the Gamma Algedi System's only star. Gamma Algedi is a class G2F Yellow dwarf star. The Gamma Algedi System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Gamma Algedi is part of the [[Algedi Cluster]] in the [[Sol Sector]].

=== Sindh ===

[[Sindh]] (0.8AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Gamma Algedi System. It has one moon, Sorji.

=== Pesht ===
[[Pesht]] (1.1AU) is an inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Gamma Algedi System. It has one moon, Shevin.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Gamma Algedi System was colonized in [[2036]].

The First Fleet held the distinction of being the Highguard Fleet for the [[United Centusi States]]. Their duties included the conquest and protection of the [[Pelagrim Sector]]. The First Fleet was annihilated over the course of the [[Second Battle of Centus]], and Admiral [[Anthony Koch]] was captured by the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] when his escape shuttle was intercepted.

==Composition==
The First Fleet was comprised of the best vessels the Centusi States could afford. The first fleet was actually the last Centusi Fleet constructed before the [[Tempest War]], as it did not adopt any old ships from the Clans Kreigsmarine.

===Flagships===
The Knight of Night was a [[Knight class Attack Carrier]], the only of her class. The First Fleet also maintained a [[Stormforge Class Battleship]], the [[The Lord William's Will|Der Wille des Herrn Williams]] as the personal flagship of Chancellor [[William Camp]].

===Stormgod Battlecruisers===
The First Fleet used the [[Stormgod Class Battlecruiser]] as its main vanguard force in all operations. The Stormgod's were generally deployed as a trio, allowing them to form a inverse wedge to draw enemies into where their cross [[blazer]] fire could cut the opposition to sheds.
* Victory March
* Eternal Night
* Warrior King

===Rächer Battlecruisers===
The [[Rächer Class Battlecruiser]] served as the main attack force for the First Fleet. The Rächer's proved to be powerful warships, and after undergoing extensive refitting by [[Mesarbauer]], they proved their ability in combat to handle two to one odds against Prefecture [[Avenger Class Battlecruiser|Avenger's]] with ease. 
* Dragon Slayer
* Wise Senator
* Vigilant Angel
* City of Gold
* Timekeeper

===Destroyers===
The [[Clansguard Class Destroyer]] was built exclusively for the Centusi Kreigsmarine, and therefore first saw deployment in the First Fleet. The Clansguard served as the backbone of the First Fleet. The Clansguard ended up being severely underpowered, and over the course of the [[Tempest War]], the entire line of destroyers had to be replaced from casualties - most ships twice.

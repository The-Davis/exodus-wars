The Mesarthrim Church is the official State Religious body within Mesarthim Space.  It was founded on the ideals of the Christian Holy Bible.  Although the Church is directly tied to the state, Mesarthrim are not forced to worship God, or even to believe in him.  However, it is considered a sin to have a shop or service available during the morning hours on a Sunday.
After the fall of the [[Anderung]], former [[Anderung Loyalist|Loyalist]] were allowed to continue to worship the [[Anderung]] god's if they paid the appropriate taxes.

==Origin==
The Mesarthrim Church is a blending of the Catholic Christian Church, random Gnostic teachings, and memory.  These three teachings were blended into the Mesarthrim Church because the First Reverends, [[Georges Gilmelli von Wexler]] and [[Hermes Beyer]] were the only two pastors who survived the [[Anderung]] genetic engineering after their colony ship had been captured.  When the [[Anderung]] captured the Mesarthrim they took everything and incinerated it - including the Holy Books, so it was up to the Mesarthrim to re-write their Bibles from memory.  Most of the Holy icons of Christianity have been replicated in the Mesarthrim Church - only tired more closely to [[Mesar]] rather then [[Terra|Earth]].

One major revision made to the Mesarthrim Church was the removal of [[Terra|Earth]] from the Bible.  A lot of the events rather took place 'one a distant star.'  This belief led to some upheaval when the [[Mesarthrim]] learned of [[Terra|Earth]] and the two hundred and sixty years that had passed since the founding of their faith.

In [[2323]] the Mesarthrim Church officially recognized that their Bible had been rewritten by [[Georges Gilmelli von Wexler|Wexler]] and [[Hermes Beyer|Beyer]].  Then in [[2324]], using a Gideon Christian Bible provided by [[Scott Sanburn]], Reverend [[Billis Eberhart]] went through the Mesarthrim Bible verse-by-verse and corrected the wording on any Verse that [[Georges Gilmelli von Wexler|Wexler]] and [[Hermes Beyer|Beyer]] had tried to quote, he also added in the books that they had missed.  Today, the Mesarthrim Church recognizes several Bibles as being the "Many Words of God."  These Bibles include the 'Wexler Bible', the 'Gideon Bible', the Revised Bible, and the Catholic Bible.

==Mesarthrim Bible==
When [[Billis Eberhart|Billis]] went through the Mesarthrim Bible he found many Books out of order, and almost none of the verses were word-for-word.  The Mesarthrim Bible is still considered the 'Standard' Bible on [[Mesar]], however the [[Revised Bible]] has gained popularity as being a combination of the [[Terra|Earth]] Bibles and the Mesarthrim Bible.

The Mesarthrim Bible contains three Testaments, no one is certain why [[Georges Gilmelli von Wexler|Wexler]] and [[Hermes Beyer|Beyer]] added the third Testament.  However, it seems to have become the appendix where the two men put books they couldn't either agree on, or forgot where they belonged in the normal list of the Bible.

It is unknown which pastor was a Gnostic believer, neither [[Georges Gilmelli von Wexler|Wexler]] nor [[Hermes Beyer|Beyer]] ever commented on who supplied what chapters or verses to the Mesarthrim Bible.  (In fact it wasn't until [[2325]] that the Mesarthrim even learned about the Gnostic Bible.  It was [[Theodore Ginley]] from [[Holt]] who identified the books in the Mesarthrim Bible as being Gnostic when he came to [[Mesar]] to learn about the State Church).

Noted next to the Books are explanations of the books compared to the Christian Bible.

===Old Testament===
The Books of the Mesarthrim Old Testament are;
* Genesis
* Exodus
* Deuteronomy (Heavily altered translation, merges Deuteronomy,  Maccabees (1, 2, & 3), Jude, & Judith)
* Samuel (Not split into two books, and the stories are heavily modified.  Includes extensive material borrowed from Joshua and Judges)
* The Kings (Not split into two books)
* Prayer of Manasseh (Word-for-word from the Greek Orthodox Bible)

===New Testament===
* The Gospel of Matthew
* The Gospel of Charlemagne (Seems to be an original work, identifies numerous icons within the Mesarthrim Faith)
* The Gospel of Luke
* The Gospel of Thomas (From the Gnostic writings)
* The Gospel of Actions (Rough translation of the Book of Acts)
* The Gospel of Philip (From the Gnostic writings)
* The Gospel of Mark
* The Gospel of Titus (Is a mixing of several Epistles, turned into a 'gospel')
* The Gospel of Truth (From the Gnostic writings)
* Prayer of the Apostle Paul (From Gnostic writings)

===Mesar Testament===
* Psalms (However, it only contains 37 Psalms.  All 150 were added in for the Revised Bible.)
* Song of Songs
* Job
*Revelations (This story is heavily modified, taking place on Mesar, and being the personal account of a [[Mesarthrim]] named Hanniel, who escaped the [[Anderung]].  His visions were written down just before his death after his genetic enhancements failed.)

==Mesarthrim Cross==
The Mesarthrim cross varies from the standard Christian Cross.  The Circle at the bottom was originally believed to represent [[Mesar]].  However, after [[2323]], some experts believe that it actually was designed after a cross that Charlemagne held.  Combined with the Gospel of Charlemagne, Mesarthrim researchers also found a painting of Charlemagne on [[Terra|Earth]] with him holding a metal ball with a cross coming out of it.

Additionally, the Cross is '''not''' identified with the place that Jesus died (in the Mesarthrim Bible, Jesus was crucified on a 'Y' shaped object).  The Mesarthrim Cross is said to be a symbol of God rising from [[Mesar]].  The circle represents [[Mesar]], and the '+' is divinity.

The '''Honis System''' consists of the star Honis, five planets, eighty-nine moons, and one sparse asteroid belt. 

In order of their distances from Honis, the planets are Hasa, Guilliard, [[Novar]], Konu, and Joru. All five planets are orbited by moons. The planets are named from words in the [[Anderung]] language.

==Stellar Navigation==

Honis is the Honis System's only star. Honis is a class G3O yellow dwarf. Honis has a strong heliosphere with a particle windspeed of roughly 3.5 million kilometers per hour. Starship navigation is affected by Honis's heliosphere, but it is often a hazard for lower-quality ships.

The Honis System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Honis is part of the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and is a close neighbor of the [[Cinaed System]] and the [[Tirshan System]].

==Orbital Bodies==
The inner Honis System is contains the terrestrial planets. Only [[Novar]] is inhabitable.

===Novar===
[[Novar]] (1.6AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Honis System. It has one moon, Mond.

==Discovery and Exploration==
The [[Anderung]] discovered and settled the Honis system long before their history had been documented. When the [[Mesarnak]] were brought to Novar, they were strictly forbidden from exploring the system. In [[2324]], the [[Alliance_of_Planets#Alliance_of_Planets_Explorer_Corps|Explorer Corps]] discovered a sixth orbital body had begun to gain significant mass to begin to attract local debris to it - forming the core of a new planet. Although the planet will take centuries to form, some Mesarthrim have begun holding bids for naming rights.

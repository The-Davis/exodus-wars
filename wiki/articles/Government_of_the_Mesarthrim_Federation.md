In accordance with the Convention for a Unified Mesar State, known as TFVMS and TFVMS II, and within the regulations of the Alliance of Planets Government of National Unity, through the United Mesarthrim Clans resolution: The Rights of Mesarthrim Autonomy, the people of the former Mesarthrim colony Comur do ordain and ratify this Constitution of the Sovereign nation of the Mesarthrim Federation.  All debts, treaties, and liabilities of the United Mesarthrim Clans are now the sole responsibility of the Mesarthrim Federation.

In accordance with the Alliance of Planets Security Council ruling, the names of Arnold Stadt, Mark Edge, Steven Edge, and Bethany Tischler are henceforth struck from the United Centusi Constitution, thereby nullifying the Constitution of Centus.

In accordance with the Council of Independent Nations National Sovereignty Council, the Mesarthrim Federation is recognised as the legitimate successor to the United Mesarthrims Clans.

==First Article==
*The three planetary bodies of the United Centusi States shall henceforth establish local municipalities whose duties are to propose, ratify, and enforce local laws that are not held or ensured by this document.
*These municipalities shall be recognised as Province’s in accordance with all rights granted to them by this document.
*Any planetary body may be accepted to the Mesarthrim Federation as long as it remains within a reasonable distance from a neighbouring Federation planetary body.
*Territory captured through conquest shall be added to the Mesarthrim Federation after a time that the Hohe Gouverneur determines that reasonable threat to civilian persons are diminished.

==Second Article==
===Section One===
*All powers and rights granted within this article shall be vested in a Federal Legislative Forum, the House of Legislatures.
*This Forum shall meet in the former Silver Chambers on Mesar.
===Section Two===
*The House of Legislatures shall be composed of three representative from each Province who have been democratically elected every fourth year of their service.  
*One representative will represent the Province’s seat of power, while the other two will represent a region of the Province with approximately equal populations.
*All representatives in the House of Legislatures shall be no younger then twenty-five years of age, have a seventy-five percent Mesarthrim heritage, and either be a natural born citizen or have been a naturalised resident of the Mesarthrim Federation for a time no less then ten years.
*The House of Legislatures Speaker shall be held by the High Lord for the duration of his service.
*The sole Power of Impeachment shall be maintained by the House of Legislatures.
*The House of Legislatures may overturn a ruling by the Executive Branch by a three-fourths vote to the opposite ruling of the Chancellor.
*The House of Legislatures may pass a law with a two-thirds vote.  If the law is ratified by the House of Legislatures it goes onto the Chancellor to be ratified.
===Section Three===
*Proposed Amendments to this document must be ratified by the House of Legislatures through a two-thirds vote before being passed onto the Executive branch for ratification.
*No law or amendment may remove rights granted by this document.  Amendments may only serve to clarify or expand upon the rights and regulations of this document.  Any Amendment to limit rights granted by this document is considered a Redrafting, and requires a seven-eighths vote.
===Section Four===
*The House of Legislatures may never meet, debate, or vote without the entirety of the House being present.
*If an elected official chooses not to show for an election, debate, or vote the House of Legislatures may issue a summons and involve local law enforcement if necessary to bring the Legislator to the meeting.
*In the occurrence of absenteeism or incapacitation, the House of Legislatures maintains the ability to issue a summons for the region’s Legislature Proxy to temporarily represent the region.
*If an elected official is unable to perform his duties due to dismissal, death, or treason, the House of Legislatures is required to issue an immediate ‘Call to Election’ to replace the vacant seat.
*The House of Legislatures is required to keep a journal of all proceedings, votes, and attendance.  This journal shall be made available on demand to any citizen of the Mesarthrim Federation.
*The House of Legislatures shall meet at a minimum of once a month.
*The Speaker of the House of Legislatures may call an additional meeting with a minimum of twenty-four hours advance notice.
*It is the duty of the Speaker of the House of Legislatures to keep the meetings orderly and as expedient as possible.
*Intentional delay of voting and off topic debates will not be tolerated within the discussions of the House of Legislatures.  If the Speaker feels this has been violated he may call for an immediate vote.
===Section Five===
*All representatives in the House of Legislatures shall receive compensation from the Federal Treasury for their service.
*Any member of the House of Legislatures who has refused to attend a regularly scheduled meeting, and was forcibly brought to the Legislature building will be given half pay for the remainder of the Federal fiscal year.
*Once every four years the compensation rate of the House of Legislatures shall be assessed and increased in accordance with the growth in the cost of living and/or inflation rate.
===Section Six===
*The House of Legislatures shall have the power to lay and collect taxes, duties, imposts, and excises, to pay the debts and provide for the common defence and general welfare of the Mesarthrim federation.
*All duties, imposts, and excises shall be uniform throughout all Province’s of the Mesarthrim Federation,
*The House of Legislatures shall have the power to borrow money on the credit of the Mesarthrim Federation;
*The House of Legislatures shall first attempt to borrow from the private sector before borrowing from external nations.
*To regulate commerce with foreign entities;
*To establish a Parcel Service;
*To establish uniform laws on bankruptcies in the Mesarthrim Federation;
*To promote progress of Science and Art, by securing for a limited time to Authors, Artist, and Inventors the right of exclusivity to their inventions and writings.
*The Mesarthrim Federation has the authority to issue credit in the form of the Hira.
*The Hira shall be backed by the full support and assurances of the Mesarthrim Federation.
*Foreign currency shall be accepted throughout the Mesarthrim Federation at a rate imposed by the Ikronin Jurekön Exchange.
*The House of Legislatures maintains the sole ability to lay tariffs or taxes on imports or exports.
*No goods or service may be taxed between Province’s.
*The House of Legislatures holds the sole responsibility of authorising the use of the Federal Treasury by the Executive branch.

===Section Seven===
*The House of Legislatures holds the sole responsibility for establishing treaties with foreign entities.
*The Mesarthrim Federation is obligated to aid and maintain the Prometheus Fleet with the nations of the Taviridis Somarchada, and fulfill all other obligations formerly afforded the United Mesarthrim Clans under the Prometheus Treaty of 2325.
*The House of Legislatures maintains the sole ability to declare war against another nation, or to authorise actions against dissident either local or abroad.
*The Mesarthrim Federation will maintain a professional Armed Forces which will serve as a Federal entity.  The House of Legislatures shall hold the responsibility of clothing, arming,  training, housing, and paying the professional Army.
*The House of Legislatures maintains the right to issue Letters of Marque.
*The Mesarthrim Federation will maintain three peacekeeping fleets for whose duties are to protect the planet and trade lines of the planetary body.  The House of Legislatures shall hold the responsibility of clothing, arming, paying, training, housing and constructing the Home Guard fleets.
*The Mesarthrim Federation will maintain a single Kreigsmarine fleets whose duties are to protect the Mesarthrim Federation abroad from piracy and harassment.  The House of Legislatures shall hold the responsibility of clothing, arming, paying, training, housing and constructing the Kreigsmarine fleets.
*The Peacekeeping fleets may not maintain a tonnage greater then half that of the Kreigsmarine fleets.
*The Kriegsmarine shall be responsible for maintaining a small force vessels at each planetary body for the sole purpose of collecting customs and enforcing embargoes and tariffs.
*The Mesarthrim Federation is committed to actively supporting the missions of the Tripartite Alliance, and upon request, will commit peacekeeping forces to any resolution passed by the Tripartite Alliance Defence Council.
*The Mesarthrim Federation shall not maintain a national militia, however, a Citizens individual right to own a weapon and defend himself cannot be denied.
*Any acts of manslaughter not ordained by the Mesarthrim Federation or its allies is considered murder and will result in the forfeiture of life by the accused, if found guilty in a court of their peers, as dictated by the traditions of the clan codes.

==Third Article==
===Section One===
*Every four years a new Executive officer, known as the Chancellor, shall be elected into the Executive office.
*The Executive officer shall run with their Proxy, and their Chief’s of Staff.  On the first day of December in the fourth year of their term, elections will be held to determine who will take office on the first of the following year.
*Every citizen of the Mesarthrim Federation, who is at least sixteen years of age, is freely able to vote in this election with no prejudice or restraint - however a citizen is not legally obligated to vote, and may wave their rights to do so if they choose.
*Which ever candidate receives a minimum of one additional vote over their contender is recognised as the victor of the election.
*In the case of a tied vote, the final decision will pass to the House of Legislatures.
*Elections are counted by electronic polling machines which shall be secured from electronic tampering by the best available security measures at the time.
*The Chancellor shall receive compensation for his service to the Mesarthrim Federation.  This compensation will be assessed every four years, and at that time will be adjusted accordingly for inflation and/or the cost of living.
*If the Chancellor is unable to fulfil his duties due to death, discharge, resignation, or treason; their Proxy shall take office with the full confidence of the Mesarthrim Federation and the House of Legislatures.  If the same befalls the Proxy, the House of Legislatures will determine a successor from the Chancellor’s Chief’s of Staff.
===Section Two===
*The Chancellor is charged with the duty in representing the Mesarthrim Federation to all foreign negotiations where the House of Legislatures has authorised a treaty.  The Chancellor may not knowingly disrupt or ruin any treaties that have been endorsed by the House of Legislatures.
*The Chancellor also shall decide whether or not to ratify Amendments and laws that the House of Legislatures pass onto him.
===Section Three===
*The Chancellor, his Proxy, or any of his Chief’s of Staff shall be removed from office on Impeachment for dereliction of duty, treason, bribery, or other high crimes or misdemeanours.

==Fourth Article== 
===Section One===
*The Judicial power for the entirety of the Mesarthrim Federation nation will be under the jurisdiction of the High Courts.  The High Courts are charged with the interpretation of the laws of the Mesarthrim Federation, and the traditional clan code.
===Section Two===
*There shall be one High Court with three judges who are appointed for life.  Their duties are to manage all Federal cases and all interpretations of the Federal legal code.
===Section Three===
*Each Province shall maintain a Courthouse where High Court judges preside for a term of six years.
*Each High Court judge is responsible for presiding over any Federal Province’s ruling.
*The High Court judge does not rule over a Province’s Constitutions and a Province’s Laws.
===Section Four===
*No officers of the law may enter the residence, or violate the privacy, of a citizen unless probable cause has been ascertained, and a warrant from the High Court has been issued.
*The High Court is responsible for determining and setting precedents for punishments.  No punishment can exceed the crime.
*Excessive bail may not be levied against an individual, nor excessive fines imposed, nor cruel and unusual punishment.
*If an offender leaves the Province where they have committed a crime, the Province they are apprehended in is obligated to return the offender to the proper Province and may not hold them accountable in their local courts for crimes committed in the other Province.
*If two or more Province’s have claim against a defendant, the case will automatically be taken to the High Court unless one or more of the Province’s relinquishes the charges levied against the offender.
*All citizens shall be granted the right to a speedy and public trial by their peers.

==Fifth Article==
===Section One===
*The immigration of individuals shall not exceed three months, or the length of an educational term.  Permanent residency is limited to Mesarthrim natives and residents of territory prior to the signing of the Treaty of Tavou.
*Immigrants who join in civil union with a Mesarthrim native may become a naturalised citizen of the Mesarthrim Federation after five years.
===Section Two===
*Every Province is required to maintain a public education system including a Federally accredited grammar school, High School, and College.
*It is the duty of the Province to levy taxes to fund the public school to provide a completely subsidised education to all citizens and their children.
*All schools must maintain a level of Federal accreditation in order to qualify for Federal assistance in education funding.  If a Province maintains a high level of accreditation, up to forty percent of its schooling budget may be supplemented by the Federal government.
*Private schools cannot receive Federal or local subsidisation or scholarship.
===Section Three===
*A Federal tax will be levied on all media centre sets and cortex capable computers.
*The tax will be utilised to provide content and wireless access to all citizens of the Mesarthrim Federation.
===Section Four===
*The Federal government will make available at any government building access to protective contraceptives.
*It shall be required that all working citizens be provided with a proper level of Health Care coverage by their employer if they work greater then ten hours a week.  Companies found in violation will be fined.
*Medical emergency response equipment and personal cost shall be subsidised by local municipalities.
===Section Five===
*The House of Legislatures shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof; 
*Or abridging the freedom of speech, 
*Or the freedom of the press; 
*Or the right of the people to peaceably assemble, and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances.
===Section Six===
*The government shall provide a place of residence for any unemployed citizen while they are attending a school of learning.
*The government shall also provide assistance to any Citizen who seeks assistance in the reacquisition of employment after termination.
===Section Seven===
*All Citizens shall have the right to clean running water, electricity, and proper waste disposal services.  If a Citizen is financially unable to acquire these services, the government shall provide a place of residence where such accommodations are made available.
*Citizens are allowed to take advantage of government resources as long as they can prove they are making positive attempts to become self sufficient.

==Sixth Article==
*All Citizens of the Mesarthrim federation are guaranteed the rights allowed within this constitution.  No citizen’s rights may lapse or be withheld from them when travelling from one Province to another.
*All legal certificates, documents, licenses, and accolades are transferable between Province’s and cannot be denied.

==Seventh Article==
*This Constitution is considered ratified by the Rights of Mesarthrim Autonomy which was accepted by the Alliance of Planets Government of National Unity.  With the full assurances of the Council of Independent Nations National Sovereignty Council, the Mesarthrim Federation is recognised as the legitimate successor to the United Mesarthrims Clans, supplanting the role of the United Centusi States, therefore, all territories and regions formerly known as the United Mesarthrim Clans, and/or the United Centusi States are now recognised as the sovereign nation of the Mesarthrim Federation.


==Amendments==
*'''First Amendment'''
**The Hohe Gouverneur shall be selected by majority vote from an election of his peers in the armed forces of the Mesarthrim Federation. The Hohe Gouverneur shall serve for life, and be granted a stipend to be adjusted on a yearly basis to correspond with the cost of living.  The Hohe Gouverneur is also immune from any government taxation.
*'''Second Amendment'''
**Any two individuals who wish to form a civil union may do so and be granted the full rights and privileges as a married couple regardless of gender.
*'''Third Amendment'''
**Citizens of Member Nations of the Council of Independent Nations are allowed to immigrate to the Mesarthrim Federation and become naturalised citizens.
*'''Fourth Amendment'''
**The right to mine, excavate, or drill natural resources within the borders of the Mesarthrim Federation are to be sold to national corporations.  Foreign contractors may only hold the rights if no national contractor chooses to purchase the contract.
*'''Fifth Amendment'''
**All Citizens, who have not served a prison term for a felony, shall have the right to acquire military grade automatic weapons for use in defence of the Mesarthrim Federation.
*'''Sixth Amendment'''
**Scientific research on the human genome, genetic engineering, and Self-Aware Entities shall be given the freedom to research within the boundaries set by the International Health Organisation and the International Red Cross.
*'''Seventh Amendment'''
**No acts of retribution, or indemnification, may be forcibly served upon members of the defunct United Centusi States government.  The Mesarthrim Federation considers all acts pardoned.

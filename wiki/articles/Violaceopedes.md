===Overview===

The Shiva, named for the Indian god of death and destruction, is aptly named.  Its internal circulatory system creates massive amounts of plasma, allowing it to travel at tremendous speeds.  Its lethal array of weapons has been the death of many pilots.  If ordered to morph by a Noble, the Shiva can also become the heavier Kujata breed.

[[Image:Violaceopedes.jpg]]

The Shiva's four wings each contain plasma-producing glands similar to those of the Bombardier, which it can fire with deadly accuracy at human fighters.  The destructive power is very high, similar to a Terran autocannon.

Two Shredder Drone-like creatures rest in sockets between the wings of the Shiva.  They are produced by Battle Queens, and carried into battle by Shivas.  Unlike the Shredder, the Shiva's missile drones do not make course corrections, and the Shiva must aim on its own.  Unfortunately for humanity, Shivas are very good shots.

The Shiva's plasma propulsion is blindingly fast and maneuverable.  It is nearly impossible to engage in a dogfight, as only the most maneuverable fighters can keep up.

The Shiva's carapace is relatively resilient, but isn't particularly resistant to any particular weapon.  It can take a good deal of punishment before dying, but any weapon will work.

Shivas are the mainstay of the air and space forces of the [[Volucris Swarm]].  The smaller Shredder Drones are more numerous, and the larger Kujatas are more powerful, but the Shiva does the bulk of the combat the space swarms face.

A Shiva's metamorphosis into a Kujata always takes place in zero gravity.  The process takes ten months.

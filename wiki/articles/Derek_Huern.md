''Derek Huern'' was the fifth sunlit son of [[Talire Huern]], and was one of the most popular and charismatic potential heirs to the throne after the death of his mother. He was unexpectedly assassinated in [[2241]], and his daughter [[Kian Huern]] took his spot in the succession.

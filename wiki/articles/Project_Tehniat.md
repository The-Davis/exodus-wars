'''Project Tehniat''' was launched in [[2327]] by [[Siovar Cai]] and [[Dirken Huern]].  The PAS ''Tehniat'' was commissioned to explore the far reaches of the [[Orion Arm]].  Its main mission was to locate a suitable new homeworld for the survivors of [[Lorithan]].

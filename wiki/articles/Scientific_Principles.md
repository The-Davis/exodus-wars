'''Science''' is a time-honored system for observing data, and attempting to draw conclusions from the observations. There have been many advances in the various fields of science, making life in general more enjoyable.

The science of the Exodus Wars covers a vast range, from late Atomic Age principles to early Quantum Age principles. The average citizen can expect to live a long, healthy life supplemented by vast leaps in medical technology, while the working class enjoys an easier life with powerful automated systems aiding them in everyday life.

Crius is an irregularly-shaped ball of rock, possibly a large asteroid that slipped past the other worlds in the system. The [[Anthronoris]] never used it, but the Titanians set up several automated metal mining, refining and manufacturing plants on its surface. It is one of the cornerstones of the Titanian civil economy, which supports their robust lifestyles.

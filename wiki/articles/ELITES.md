*Armament: 2 x Type-B1c Blazers
*Communications System:  Standard Ranged Comm. Suite
*Targeting and Tracking System:  Standard Sensors Suite 

===Overview===

The UCS Army's offensive groups are primarily made up of two unit types--tanks and infantry.  With new 'Rig forces to speak of, the largest infantry unit in the UCS Army is the ''E''xoskeletal ''L''ight ''I''nfantry ''T''actical ''E''nhancement ''S''uit, or ELITES.  ELITES are operated almost solely by Myt'Herra troopers, although the Myt'Gaul have been known to use them, and some back-line Myt'Kampfen units are issued them in place of heavier tanks and vehicles.  A single ELITES is faster, has greater mobility, and heavier firepower than a whole squad of Myt'Kampfen. 

===Capabilities:===

The ELITES is designed for combat operations in multiple environments.  Permanent weaponry includes a pair of small Type-B1c Blazers--more compact versions of the Type-B1 with slightly reduced range.  These two Blazers are devastating to infantry and light armor at range, and close-in can be quite effective even against heavier armors.  The suit also has articulating hands, giving it fine-manipulation abilities when dealing with its environment, as well as allowing it to carry additional weaponry, such as larger Blazer rifles, missiles launchers, and other such armaments. 

[[Image:ELITES.jpg]]

The ELITES is possibly the best battle armor to come from any Terran-originated race.  With an advanced control interface, assumed to be adapted from shared [[Ixoarchada]] technology, the precision and flexibility of the suit is impressive by any standards.  An advanced Mesarthrim energy storage lattice, a CPW-100 Energy Cell, gives the ELITES a longer operational life than any other Terran powered armor suit.  The Leifstein Armor and Compact AAS make the ELITES quite resilient in combat.

===Deployment===

ELITES are new among UCS forces.  The need for a larger infantry suit was recognized after the first contact ground engagements against with the Volucris in [[2323]] and [[2324]], as well as after some study of [[Taviridis Somarchada|Taviridis]] ground forces in those conflicts.  ELITES were first deployed in September of 2324, at the end of the Mesarthrim Civil War.  After some redesign and rearming, the first ELITES squads were activated.  

ELITES operate as infantry support units, giving infantry squads heavy firepower and armor in place where traditional tanks are hindered, such as in urban combat, zero-G/low-gravity environments, and inside a starship or installation.  Their flight capabilities, heavy firepower, and protective armor makes them quite capable of inflicting severe damage even on much larger enemy units.

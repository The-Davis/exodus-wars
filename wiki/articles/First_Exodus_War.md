The '''First Exodus War''' was a purely [[Terran]] conflict, but was a key factor in bringing about the [[First Contact]] between the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] and the [[United Earth Alliance]], which caused the eventual foundation of the [[Alliance of Planets]].

The war was also the direct cause of the [[Second Exodus War]]--the most devastating war in Terran history.

== 2063 ==

Some historians argue that the conflict in [[2063]] should be classified seperately from the First Exodus War, preferring to call it the Bethijan Annexation War. In 2063, the [[United Earth Alliance]] responded to requests from pro-unification politicos led by [[Andriy Drubich]] to annex the planet into the Alliance's jurisdiction.

Bethija suffered from political instability and social strife throughout its early history. The government was dispersed in February of 2063, and the resulting chaos and the annexation request from a significant portion of the Bethijan population gave the United Earth Alliance reason to invade.

If not for the seperatist movement by [[Artem Kuts]], the annexation would have been relatively smooth. Kuts sent word to [[Torimur]], a bastion of independence and anti-Alliance thought, and two of the world's leaders came in person to render assistance. [[Jerome Black]], Torimur's founder, and [[Giorgio Hadrian]], one of Torimur's veteran pilots, came to the aid of Bethija. Hadrian's skilled flying got Black's supply ship past the Alliance blockade, and the two of them hid their ship and joined the partisan forces on the ground.

The annexation was eventually successful. Artem Kuts was cornered and killed in the Bagry Caverns, and Giorgio Hadrian was captured. Jerome Black managed to slip away in the fighting and returned to his ship, but was shot down by the [[UEAS Aleksey Tarasov|UEAS ''Aleksey Tarasov'']] as he tried to run the blockade around Bethija--without Giorgio's skilled piloting that had landed him safely several months prior.

The end of 2063 saw the beginning of a long, hard transition period for Bethija, and the beginning of a tumultuous period for the [[Sol Sector]].

== Interim ==

Between the annexation of [[Bethija]] in [[2063]] and the start of hostilities in [[2078]], the United Earth Alliance solidified its control over several colonies throughout the [[Sol Sector]]. [[Tejat]], [[Holt]], [[Ah Kin]], and [[Kalmar]] were all considered members of the Alliance, as was Bethija.

Several other worlds were independent protectorates of the Alliance, but were subject to Alliance trade laws and regulations.

[[Torimur]] and the [[Sirius Cluster]] stubbornly refused to submit to Alliance laws and regulations. [[Lucius Black]] and [[Juliska Blasius]] appeared before the Assembly several times to deliver scathing anti-Alliance rhetoric.

=== The Night of Knives ===

On February 14, [[2078]], [[Juliska Blasius]]' assassins arrived on [[Earth]]. They swiftly dispatched Jackson Merrill, Danielle Hughes, and Max Thurough; the members of the Assembly sponsoring legislation to annex [[Torimur]] and the [[Sirius Cluster]]. While the assassination was successful, [[Andor Blasius]] was captured, leading to the implication of the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]].

== 2078 ==

Furious over the deaths of their associates, the Assembly quickly authorized the forced annexation of the [[Sirius Cluster]], and dispatched the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s 2nd Space Battle Group to the [[Sirius Novgorod System]].

The blockade of [[Kirtszi]] quickly provoked a response from [[Lucius Black]] and [[Torimur]]. Black declared war against the Alliance.

Black's declaration of war was all the Alliance was waiting for. They immediately began attacking and seizing Torimur convoys in every port of call. Torimur's military forces were unable to respond, and the world suffered serious financial losses.

Despite their losses, the Torimur forces responded to Kirtszi and attacked the 2nd Space Battle Group. Commodore-Commander Danil Egorov, the commander of the battle group, was inexperienced enough that he fell for several dirty tricks. The [[UEAS Hood|UEAS ''Hood'']], Egorov's command ship, war destroyed with all hands. The Alliance retreated from the system.

== 2079 ==

While Torimur's military was returning from [[Kirtszi]], General-Commander Pavel Egorov took the [[UEAS Stalingrad|UEAS ''Stalingrad'']] and launched a neutron bomb attack on Torimur. This horrendous war crime resulted in the deaths of thousands and rendered Torimur's major population centers unfit for habitation. Hundreds of thousands of survivors, scarred by radiation poisoning, fled into the wilderness. Many thousands died of exposure and radiation poisoning before Torimur's military returned.

After the unwarranted use of weapons of mass destruction against Torimur, many of the independent protectorates threatened to join the seperatist forces. The United Earth Alliance promptly withdrew its forces and offered to meet with Lucius Black and Juliska Blasius in Volyn, the capital city of Bethija. Blasius was in hiding on Kirtszi, but Black agreed to meet. The [[Treaty of Volyn]] was drawn up and signed. 

Under the terms, the parties responsible for the destruction of Torimur were brought up on charges of war crimes and were sentenced to life in prison. Many radical elements of the United Earth Alliance government were disgraced by the scandal, and lost their positions. In exchange, the people of Torimur agreed to find a new home in the De Mairan Sector, allowing the United Earth Alliance to save face.

== 2080 ==

While official hostilities ended in [[2079]], there were several terrorist attacks against [[Earth]] in [[2080]]. While the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]] was blamed for the attacks, they were actually perpetrated by [[Lavik Nighthawk]] under orders from [[Lucius Black]]. The attacks ensured that Earth would have its eye fixed on the [[Sirius Cluster]] instead of paying any attention to where [[Torimur]]'s survivors settled.

Torimur was largely disarmed, but they were compensated with three colony ships: the ''Jerome Black'', the ''Aydan Black'', and the ''Torimur's Hope''. The ships were launched with several hundred thousand survivors, resulting in the "[[First Exodus]]".

== Aftermath ==

For decades, the [[Sirius Cluster]] was a source of consternation to the [[United Earth Alliance]]. Three crime families; the [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconis]], the [[Asanova Family Territory|Asanovas]] and the [[Nakamura Family Territory|Nakamuras]] set up shop in the cluster. With the loss of political clout in the [[Sol Sector]], the Alliance did not dare to try to root out the [[Blasius Family Territory|Blasius Family]] or annex the cluster a second time.

Before long, the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] made [[First Contact]] with the United Earth Alliance, and over time the Alliance's radical expansion elements slowly lost power and influence.

The Torimur exiles survived, but did not remain in exile. The [[First Exodus]] was quickly followed by the [[Second Exodus]], which led directly to the [[Second Exodus War]] of the [[24th Century]].

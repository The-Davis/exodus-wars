One of the most terrifying Volucris breeds is the Eviscerator, a very rare type of noble.  There are only six, as it takes two-hundred years for a new one to grow.

These are the elites of Volucris society.  They are highly intelligent, and are the equivalent of emperors or gods to the lesser Nobles.  The Eviscerator is truly the mightiest Volucris in existence.

The array of weapons on the Eviscerator is vast.  At close range, it has six claws and four tusks to inflict damage.  Its body also secretes deadly neurotoxin, which kills unarmored humans within seconds.  For range, the Eviscerator also has spines within its body, which can shoot when its muscles contract.  It also has plasma glands, which can shoot burning plasma hundreds of feet.

The Eviscerator is fast, and can leap to alarming heights, scaling a Battlemech in mere seconds.  It's carapace is resistant to all forms of attack, and is impervious to nearly every conventional ground weapon.  Killing an Eviscerator is a very difficult undertaking.

Fortunately for humanity, Eviscerators are very rare, and are almost always used to protect the hive clusters.  When one is sent to the front lines, it will mean either Volucris victory, or agonizing levels of human death.

The only Eviscerators ever seen in battle have been immature ones, some 80 to 100 years old.  There is a single known mature Eviscerator, and it has been seen on recon flights into Volucris-held space.  When and where the Volucris will deploy this monstrosity remains a mystery.

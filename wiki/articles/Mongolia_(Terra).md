'''Mongolia''' was a central Asian country found on [[Earth]]. Many Mongolians settled on [[Torimur]] during the diaspora of [[Earth]], and blended with the [[Italy (Terra)|Itallians]] on the same world, resulting in the [[Torimin]] [[race]].

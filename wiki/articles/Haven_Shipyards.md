Haven Shipyards was a corporation created by [[Royal Imperial Labs]] as a front for developing [[:Category:Warships|warships]] for the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. All actual assembly and storage of the [[:Category:Warships|warships]] themselves was completed in multiple automated deep-space facilities known as the "Havens." Only sub-components and important major systems were designed and built on [[Ah Kin]] itself.

The most famous products of Haven Shipyards are the [[Vigilant Class Frigate]] and [[Atlas Class Carrier]], which were extremely successful and powerful designs used throughout the [[Second Exodus War]].

The '''Lyrae System''' is a star system with five planets. 

In order of their distances from RR Lyrae, the planets are [[Bretagne]], [[Auvergne]], [[Holt]], [[Aquitaine]], and [[Alsace]]. Two of the planets are orbited by moons. The system is named for its only inhabitable planet, and the other planets are named after French regions by the French who colonized the star system.

== Stellar Navigation ==

RR Lyrae is the Lyrae System's star. RR Lyrae is a hot K3V orange dwarf. The heliosphere of RR Lyrae is very strong, making navigation in the area slightly hazardous to smaller starships. Larger freighter classes are rated to navigate the area, and all military vessels can handle the workload.

The Lyrae System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. The [[Zardos System]] is considered a close neighbor of [[Holt]].

=== Bretagne ===

[[Bretagne]] (0.25AU) is the closest planet to RR Lyrae. It is quite small, and has never been scouted or used for anything.

=== Auvergne ===

[[Auvergne]] (0.9AU) is a barren rock with a thick, toxic atmosphere. Its surface is rich in radioactive material, which the people of [[Holt]] mine. While not technically inhabited, [[Auvergne]] has two domed cities to house the miners.

=== Holt ===

[[Holt]] (1.5AU) is a dry world, with no vast oceans like most inhabited worlds, and only 40% of its surface covered by smaller seas. It is slightly smaller than [[Terra]], and has a moderately high average surface temperature. [[Holt]] has two moons, [[Corsica]] and [[Charentes]].

=== Aquitaine ===

[[Aquitaine]] (5.09 AU) masses 5 times as much as all other planets put together. It is composed largely of hydrogen with many other gas elements and trace particles. [[Aquitaine]] has 23 moons.

=== Alsace ===

[[Alsace]] (7.1 AU) is a relatively small gas giant. It has a large ring system around its equator, and 6 moons.

=== Asteroid Belt ===

The Lyrae System's asteroid belt is enormous, spanning from 10AU to 15AU. It has billions of small asteroids, ranging up to 800KM in diameter.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

During the explosive [[Terran]] expansion into and colonization of space, [[Holt]] was one of the first stops. The [[United Earth Alliance]] allowed the [[France (Terra)|French]] to establish the colony and administer it themselves for five years. In [[2032]], the colonists signed a new charter with the [[United Earth Alliance]] and became one of its territories.

In May of [[2315]], the [[Empire]] invaded and conquered the [[Holt]] colony. Throughout the [[Second Exodus War]], [[Holt]] was a source of material support and manpower for the resistance which would eventually consolidate into the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]. [[Holt]] is currently part of the [[Prefecture]].

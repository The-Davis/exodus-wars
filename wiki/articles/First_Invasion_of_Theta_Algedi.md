== Overview ==

As a tactical target, the [[Theta Algedi System]] and the world of [[Tejat]] seemed unimportant to the casual observer, but [[Lucius Black]] knew better.  Tejat's value did not lie in ships or soldiers--though it had many of both.  The planet's value was its proximity to the [[Ikronin]] and its diplomatic importance as a link to the [[Alliance of Planets]].

Lucius Black knew that if the Alliance of Planets joined the [[Second Exodus War]], he would suffer total defeat within hours.  Severing ties between the Earth government and the Alliance as completely as possible, all while setting up his own contacts with the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] was the only way to make his victory sustainable.

== Invasion of Theta Algedi ==

The invasion began on August 7, 2315, when the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s Black Armada arrived in the system.  Over the course of ten days, the ships under the command of Admiral Robert Marshall engaged the Alliance Defense Force's 15th Defense Fleet.

The Alliance's defense strategy, formulated by Admiral Charu Lalji, was to remain as mobile while preventing any Imperial landing on Tejat itself.  Her forces were so persistent in their efforts that no landing could take place until the Alliance's space forces were completely neutralized.  The fleet was hunted across the star system, and destroyed ship by ship.  Admiral Lalji's command ship was destroyed on August 16th, killing her. The last combat-capable ship was boarded and captured on August 17th, ending the space campaign.

== Invasion of Tejat ==

Conquering Tejat itself was somewhat more delicate than taking Earth or Holt.  Most of the population centers of Tejat were home to [[Lorithos]], Ikronin, and even one or two [[Meroniri]] families.  The capital city of [[Kerala]] housed embassies for each government in the Alliance of Planets, and also had a [[Reigen]] embassy.  Full-scale bombardment was not an option if Lucius Black intended to maintain peace with the formidable Meroniri.

Emmisaries from Black's government were sent to Premier [[Chetram Singh]], and he considered surrendering without further combat.  After confering with General Jaidev Amra, Premier Singh decided not to surrender, hoping to tie up enough Imperial assets to give the other United Earth Alliance worlds time to fortify and possibly gain military support from their allies.

Imperial landings began on August 19, 2315. In a single day, they swept across the entire planet, leaving only Kerala unoccupied and still under the control of the Premier's government.

=== Fall of Kerala ===

For ten days, the Royal Imperial Army fought for control of the capital.  They were under strict rules of engagement, and ordered not to harm any civilians or any of the foreign embassies.  While some Ikronin were killed in the crossfire, and the Reigen embassy suffered some fire damage when the neighboring block burned down, the Imperial forces managed to keep relative order while they hunted General Amra's soldiers through the city.

On August 28, 2315, General Amra was cornered in a wealthy residential area of the city, and gunned down by an [[Omegas]] strike team.  Alliance resistance almost completely faded away.

=== Formal Surrender ===

On August 29, 2315, Premier Chetram Singh surrendered control of Tejat to the [[Onyx Empire]].  The surrender of the city and of the planetary government was complete and unconditional, though the Imperial government promised not to harm any Alliance government officials or military officers who came forward voluntarily and surrendered themselves.  This promise was kept for several years, but it is noteworthy that only ten members of the Tejat government survived the entire Second Exodus War in captivity.

The Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr is responsible for the defense of [[Lorithan]] and the colonies, and any invasions [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]] believes necessary. The forces are centered around STOVL aircraft, tanks and infantry forces. One notable fact is that 80% of Lorithos infantry are remotely-operated robots. The [[Lorithos]] are the only force to use such weapons, as robotic technologies in other civilizations are often too primitive to be useful.

== History ==

The traditions of the Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr are traced back to ancient history, but the military began using its modern structure and weapons during the [[Talire Huern]] dynasty.

== Structure ==

Officially, a member of the Mozihr Getah Tashir Fahr is a Getah-sen, though they are often referred to by their nicknames, such as "sand-sloggers", "wastelanders", and "Hamur's Hand". The Mozihr Getah is led by the [[Hishai]].

Planetary defense strategies are the responsibility of the Qor Urt, which is made up of twenty Qor Brin. A Resun is in command of each of the Qor Urt. With only [[Lorithan]] and the colonies in command, there is not much need for Qor Urts. 

The [[Kihtor]] (Hightowers) are important and unique challenges for the Mozihr Getah to defend. The Mozihr Getah's strength lies in mobility, and the [[Kihtor]] are literally large immobile city-fortresses. A [[Kihtor]] is defended by a Qor Brin, which is made up of five Brohgen. An Ortun is placed in command of a Qor Brin.

A Brohgen is made up of four Sheren Ahk, which is typically used for regional defense on the colonies. [[Lorithan]] has no regional garrisons, as there is too little useable land to defend. A Brohgen is commanded by a Kasoff.

A Sheren Ahk is made up of six Sherens and is commanded by a Jigor. Sheren Ahk are typically assigned to space stations for defense, to large warships, or to cities as garrison forces.

A Sheren is made up of four Rak Sur. Sheren are commanded by a Krel Mag, and are considered the lowest "command level" unit of organization.

Rak Sur is made up of two Rak Aree, and is commanded by an Urta De. A Rak Sur can also be a flight of two STOVL aircraft.

The smallest Mozihr Getah group is a Rak Aree, which is either made up of an Urta So in an infantry command suit with thirty robotic infantry assigned to him, or three tanks of any type.

== Special Forces ==

[[Shadan Nahru]], or "The Damned", are the elite Special Forces of the Mozihr Getah.  Shadan Nahru are drawn from born wastelanders, who spend most of their lives alone in the deep wastelands of [[Lorithan]].  Shadan Nahru are known for their heartlessness, self-reliance, and short lifespans.  Most do not live longer than thirty years, though they typically succumb to disease rather than combat-related injuries.

The tactics of the Shadan Nahru involve infiltration of soft assets by night, particularly logistics and similar supply-line assets.  They slaughter the unarmed support staff before moving on to the soldiers and guards, and then destroy or capture as many useful assets as possible.  The Shadan Nahru are marked by their wastelander cloaks, which they wear over their gear at all times.  No Shadan Nahru ever shows his or her face to anyone except their commander.

== Uniforms ==

The Mozihr Getah has three uniforms: a Duty Uniform and a Dress Uniform, which are used by ship crews, and a Combat Uniform. All uniforms include a shoulder patch with their branch of service and their rank insignia.

The Dress Uniform is an extravagant one: blue slacks with a silver stripe, a tan shirt, a black cape with gold trim, and a gold belt. Enlisted wear a wide-brimmed hat with a blue gem set in the front, while officers wear the same hat with a silver brim, a diamond in the front, and black braiding on their shoulders. The Dress Uniform is worn with full rank insignia and any badges and medals the soldier has received.

The Duty Uniform is designed for comfort and flexibility during duty. The undergarments are designed to absorb moisture and dry quickly, and are treated to prevent irritation and smell in confined quarters. The uniform itself has tan trousers, black boots, and a tan jacket with black trim. The only patches allowed on a utillity uniform are the branch of service patch, and rank patches.

The Combat Uniform is designed to allow the wearer to survive in the wasteland. The first portion is a one-piece tan jumpsuit, made of a special fabric that is lightweight, flexible, and can be sealed when the boots, gloves, and helmet are worn. The boots and gloves are black, and the helmet is tan. The helmet is attached to an air filtration system and mask, with a heads-up display capable of interfacing with the robotic combat network. A black carrying web is attached to the outside of the jumpsuit, which gives the soldier hookups for a sidearm, survival gear, water supplies, and a combat communications system.  Most soldiers wear wastelander cloaks over their combat uniforms, though if the Shadan Nahru are operating in the same combat theater, normal soldiers often do not wear their cloaks out of respect for the special forces.

== Weapon Systems and Equipment ==

The Mozihr Getah is not armed with nuclear, chemical or biological weapons. Their robotic forces were created to discourage other races to use such weapons if they attack [[Lorithan]]. No [[Lorithos]] soldier wants a repeat of the [[Burning Wars]].

== Vehicles ==

Tanks are updated versions of the terrible war machines used in the [[Burning Wars]]. These awesome hovertanks rival the power and mobility of the now legendary [[Ikronin]] war machines. Fast light Karak hovertanks work in harassment tactics, Zieldar medium hovertanks fill the bulk of the numbers, while powerful Brehvok hovertanks--the most dangerous and feared relics of the [[Burning Wars]]--pound the enemy into oblivion.

Infantry are almost entirely robotic. The [[Lorithos]] are pitifully few in numbers compared to a few short generations ago. Genetic mutations, crippling diseases, a diving birth rate, and an unheard of infant mortality rate make human infantry a rare commodity that cannot be wasted. The few the [[Lorithos]] have are encased in the mighty shell of a Zuura command unit: a powerful semi-robotic battle armor suit that gives the operator direct control over dozens of robotic subordinates. Filling the gaps are the Ruehv light robotic troopers, cheap and easily mass-produced robots whose simple AIs are only able to understand basic commands and directives. The whole robot army strategy would be worthless if not for the more expensive and less common Krelbine robotic trooper. Krelbines have significantly improved AIs, and are quite intelligent after a long enough operational period. Their intelligence grows over time, and five year veterans are known to intelligently converse with and advise their commanders.

'''Caetara Remaneo''' is located in the [[Sagittarius Arm]] of the galaxy and is the only planet within its "system".  This is a misnomer, since its "system" was totally artificially constructed by the [[Anthronoris Empire]] during the [[Sundering]] and fled to by the Remnant in [[17953BC]].  It is the capital world of the [[The Remnant]].

==History==

Caetara Remaneo is one of the four Remnant deep-space [[Void World]]s.  These were artificially constructed in deep space using previously uninhabited planets to better hide what remains of the once great Anthronoris Empire.  Caetara Remaneo is translated to "All that Remains" and was the first of the four currently inhabited Remnant habitats constructed.  It is also the capital of the Remnant.  Its defenses are considerable, and it is the permanent home to two of the three remaining [[Praetoria Class| Praetoria]] Class ships, the Cohonesto and Minerva.

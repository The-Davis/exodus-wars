The '''History''' of the Ixoarchada and the Taviridis Somarchada is one of growth, drive, and advancement in spite of hardships, hurdles, and conflicts.

== The Sundering ==

In the time before the [[Sundering]], [[Glengarr]] was the farthest rimward holding of the [[Pax Anthronoris]].  It was seized during the [[Anthronoris]]' war against the [[Gorhamut]], and its native inhabitants eradicated.  Of the race that inhabited the planet before the coming of the Anthronoris, very little is known.

Although a small colony was established on the planet's surface, life on the surface was unpleasant for most Anthronoris.  The primary purpose of Glengarr was to provide resources and support for the military garrison and communications installations in orbit and on the planet's two moons--[[Archada]] and [[Dellada]].  The Legiar based at the garrison was maintained to protect the [[Tolana Nebula]] for future exploration and exploitation.  

The [[Ngrligru]] came to the [[Ularen System]] in [[17950BC]], having discovered its existence from one of the countless other worlds they consumed.  However, instead of finding an easy meal on a distant planet, they found a barely inhabited planet protected by a full Legiar of Anthronoris warships.  

The ensuing battle crippled most of the Ngrligru warships, but not before they managed to bombarded the moon-based garrisons, and destroy the Legiar's docks.  The few surviving Ngrligru retreated coreward.  In the absence of orders and a proper fleet facility, the Legiant [[Atramus Nochus]] led his forces in pursuit.  The Legiar's fate is unknown.  Some Meroniri historians have believe that they may have been present at the [[Battle of Sepulare]] in [[17936BC]], but there is little hard evidence.

The '''Sundering''' was an interstellar war more destructive than any other in history. The exact details of the Sundering are lost to the ages. Most of it is mere myth and legend, and many accounts disagree with others. The most widely-published, and possibly most-accurate was an account allegedly written by [[Meronir Tronit]]. However, it is now known that he was purposefully breaking away from [[Pax Anthronoris]] to form the [[Meroniri Terinasi]], and may have been trying to discredit his predecessors.

The Sundering supposedly began circa [[18000BC]], and ended circa [[17936BC]], when the last documented [[Ngrligru]] warband was destroyed near [[Sepulare]].

====Cause====

The Sundering is believed to be the direct result of a prior war, the [[Sacrum Bellus]], during which [[Pax Anthronoris]] tried to wipe out all non-human life in the [[Milky Way]].

The [[Ngrligru]] government, the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]], was the only force which could withstand the power of the [[Anthronoris]]. The brave [[Ngrligru]] fought fang and claw to keep the [[Anthronoris]] from their goal, defending everyone: [[Mtomigru]], [[Gorhamut]], [[Sirunaki]], and a hundred other [[:Category:Race|races]] enjoyed the protection of the valiant [[Ngrligru]].

In frustration, the [[Anthronoris]] developed a biological weapon, the "[[Njaa]]". Although it was meant to turn the [[Ngrligru]] on each other, it unlocked a ravenous hunger in its victims, transforming the [[Ngrligru]] from noble warriors into ravenous beasts. This "final solution" became the final doom of [[Pax Anthronoris]].

The [[Ngrligru]] descended on [[Pax Anthronoris]] like a plague. Hundreds of colonies were annihilated in the initial strikes. The [[Sacrum Bellus]] had been wildly successful, giving the [[Anthronoris]] dominion over the [[Sagittarius Arm]]. Their holdings were quickly conquered and devoured by the insane [[Ngrligru]]. The [[Anthronoris]] were driven into the core, and back towards the [[Orion Arm]]. The [[Anthronoris]] lost thousands of warships in the struggle, but enough survived to defend several worlds long enough for the invading [[Ngrligru]] to break themselves on their own rage. Legend tells that the [[Teeth of Orion]] were actually formed by the [[Anthronoris]] in a massive battle with the [[Ngrligru]].

====Human Survivors====

While [[Anthronoris]] civilization was destroyed, the worlds of [[Lorithan]], [[Kronheim]], [[Terra]] and [[Glengarr]] survived. They were cut off from the tiny [[Remnant]] of [[Pax Anthronoris]], and degenerated into various states of decay.

[[Pax Anthronoris]] developed and enacted the [[Master Plan]] in an attempt to survive. It worked to some degree: two segments of [[Pax Anthronoris]] survived relatively intact, but only just barely. While the populations of hundreds of outlying worlds were literally devoured by the [[Ngrligru]], nine worlds survived through military might and stubborn resistance against the [[Ngrligru]] menace. Another group fled the destruction in huge starships, traveling far from the reach of the [[Ngrligru]] and carrying the last Emperor with them. 

The nine worlds were the seed of what would eventually become the [[Meroniri Terinasi]]. For almost a year after the demise of [[Pax Anthronoris]], the [[Ngrligru]] attacked the remaining core worlds, but they eventually stopped. The [[Njaa]] was partially cured by the [[Mtomigru]], and the insane hunger of the [[Ngrligru]] was sated by the lives of majority of the civilians and soldiers of [[Pax Anthronoris]].

====Other Survivors====

The Sundering did not just effect [[Pax Anthronoris]]. While dozens of [[:Category:Race|races]] had been driven to extinction in [[Sacrum Bellus]], hundreds more remained under the banner of the [[Jurazoar Maropsene]]. The [[Gorhamut]] still had a strong presence in the [[Sagittarius Arm]], and the [[Sirunaki]] were a force to be reckoned with. The Sundering was the bane of all life. The [[Sirunaki Reshkeren]] was nearly wiped out: their survivors fled to holes in the ground and pulled the rocks down on top of them, out of fear of the [[Ngrligru]]. The [[Gorhamut]] fled known space, taking their [[Anderung]] and [[Volucris]] servants with them. The other [[:Category:Race|races]] of the [[Jurazoar Maropsene]] perished, or were similarly decimated to the point of returning to primitive life. By the time the Sundering ended, the [[Orion Arm]] and the [[Sagittarius Arm]] were almost devoid of civilization. Only the [[Kabila iku Gol'nde]] and the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] retained a glimmer of the spark of civilization that once was.

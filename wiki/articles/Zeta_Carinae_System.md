'''Zeta Carinae''', located in the [[Pelagrim Sector]] was never of any use to human civilization; except once, and then only as a place to hide.

After its crushing defeat in the [[Imperial Civil War]] and [[Second Exodus War]], the loyal remnant of the [[Onyx Empire]] fled to the far reaches of the sector, hoping to regroup and regain the offensive. Their hiding place was discovered by an [[Ixoarchada]] survey ship, operating in [[subspace]].

As a gesture of goodwill, the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] shared this information with the fledgeling [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]], and on February 5, [[2324]], a Prefecture fleet under the command of Admiral [[Jason Bremen]] was dispatched to the system.

In the ensuing battle, nearly a quarter of the Imperial ships were destroyed, but the battle was suddenly interupted by the arrival of the [[Volucris Swarms]]. Why they came there, no one knows.

Since the battle, no further missions have been dispatched to the system, as there are no planets and the star has no discernable resources.

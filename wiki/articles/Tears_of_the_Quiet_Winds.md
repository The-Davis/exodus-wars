'''Tears of the Quiet Wind''' is the patriotic anthem of the [[Auellal League]].  It is played at most state events, additionally it is played at the closing of most major [[Culture of the Auellal League#Sports|sporting events]] on [[Caledon]].  The song is also often played across [[Caledon]] in pub's and resturants.

''Tears of the Quiet Wind'' was first penned by [[Slyvester Nik]] in [[2303]] during the last years of the [[Anderung]] occupation.  The deeply sorrowful piece was intended to be a lament of the [[Mesarthrim]] people.  However, its sorrowful tune carried an air of peace across the wartorn population.  Its gentle love song motif kept it at the top of most music charts for nearly a year.  Finally, as the horizon began to brighten for the [[Mesarthrim]] people, the song slowly began to lose popularity, until it fell into the category of forgotten hits.

In [[2325]] when the [[Auellal League]] left [[Mesar]], [[Steven Cue]], adopted the song for the party, claiming the painful rain of this song was upon the [[Mesarthrim]] people once again.

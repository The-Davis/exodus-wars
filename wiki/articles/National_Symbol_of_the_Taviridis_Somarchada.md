The '''Tolana Sinefon''' is any artistic representation of the [[Tolana Nebula]], usually accompanied by a corresponding depiction of the [[Ixoarchada]] people.  The [http://exodus-war.net/EW/Image:Flag_of_the_Taviridis_Somarchada.png flag] of the [[Taviridis Somarchada]] features one of the oldest and most recognized Tolana Sinefons--a nine-point star with its light or energy converging on a single orb, most commonly identified as [[Archada]] itself.


Historically, Tolana Sinefons depict the focus of the Ixoarchada on the Tolana Nebula as a source of strength, advancement, survival, and even religion.  The Ixoarchada long understood that power like to the gods was held in the distant nebula, where they believed their ancestors once resided.  Upon achieving [[Hyperspace|Faster-than-Light]] travel, the Tolana Nebula was one of their first destinations, and its vast quantities of [[Antimatter]] ultimately led to the incredibly advanced society most associate with the Ixoarchada today.  It was such an important symbol that the Ixoarchada fought multiple wars with the [[Anderung]] over control of the entire nebula.

'''Leskovik''' is the XXth planet in the [[Mimosa System]].

==History==

-incomplete-

Leskovik was colonized in XX to bolster the defenses of the world of Tavou. Unlike the other worlds in the Tavou District, Leskovik was never raided by the Volucris. Xenobiologists and military strategists are baffled by their lack of action against Leskovik, as the planet is well-suited for their biology and strategically important as a raiding point against Tavou. This simply shows how little the humanoid races know about the Volucris species.

Leskovik was unusually poor for a Prefecture world. It wasn't impoverished, but wasn't wealthy like New Albany or Liaden by a long shot. Like Tavou, Leskovik built excellent weapons, particularly static defense turrets. The market for such weapons is pretty solid, but competition from other worlds kept Leskovik's economy from really taking off.

Leskovik's climate was suitable for growing food, but the native plants choked out competition quickly. All farming experiments ended pretty quickly. Leskovik was damp all the time, like Ward, but the Blue Giant star that Leskovik orbits kept the sky bright and the air hot. It is most uncomfortable outdoors. Leskovik was forced to import a lot of its food from Liaden and Tavou.

Unfortunately, Leskovik is no longer inhabitable. It was destroyed by the United Centusi States as an opening to the Prefecture/Mesarthrim war.

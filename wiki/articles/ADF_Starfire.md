Most pilots in the service of the [[United Earth Alliance]] cut their teeth flying the ADF Starfire and its variants.  The design was immensely popular due to its simplicity, superb performance, and ease-of-maintenance.

The Starfire saw action in the [[Heritage Crisis]] and the [[Second Exodus War]], and is still used as a trainer by [[Colonial Commonwealth]] pilots.

== Development History ==

In mid-[[2258]], the United Earth Alliance's General-Commander ordered a complete overhaul of fleet and fighter assets, many of which dated back to the [[First Exodus War]].  This project resulted in one of the biggest military budgets since the 21st century.  Several multi-billion dollar contracts were awarded to multiple companies.

The Alliance Defense Force awarded the [[Antonov Company]], an [[Earth]]-based aerospace firm, a contract for its bid on "Aerospace Specification No.400, Light All-Weather Space-Capable Fighter/Interceptor".

=== Test Flights ===

The Antonov Company spent over a billion dollars and four years designing and stress-testing materials and components.  Their design doctrine of triple testing every component earned the resulting fighter its reputation for never failing in combat operations.

The first test flight of a working prototype didn't come until nearly four years after the contract was signed. The General-Commander complained about a lack of a proof-of-concept model, but the Antonov design team insisted that their methods would produce a flawless interceptor on time and at the quoted budget.

After five more years of flight testing, all-weather operations tests, and operating under several different hazardous and emergency conditions scenarios, the Antonov Company began delivery of the finished design to the Alliance Defense Force

=== Military Deployment ===

Full production and delivery was slow, as the Alliance had known peace for many decades and no threats were on the horizon.  It took ten years to fully replace all existing fighter assets with the Antonov design, dubbed the "Starfire".

== Combat History ==

The value of investing in the Starfire would not become clear until half a century later.  It proved itself as an amazingly resilient fighter and interceptor, capable of taking a lot of punishment, and never, ever failing due to equipment problems.

=== Heritage Crisis ===

During the cold war between the [[Reigen]]s and [[Ikronin]], known as the [[Heritage Crisis]], several Alliance carriers were ordered into Ikronin space to perform combat exercises with the Alliance's allies.  During the crisis, ADF Starfires were fired upon and damaged by Reigen ships, though the Alliance pilots followed their orders and did not return fire.  The cold war did not escalate into a shooting war and the performance of the Starfire was not tested any further.  No Starfires were lost due to damage and all were easily repaired.

=== Second Exodus War ===

The [[Second Exodus War]] was the battlefield where the Starfire proved its mettle.  The fighter was pitted against hordes of Imperial [[F-4 Raven]]s, often at 5 to 1 numbers in favor of the Empire.  The Starfire held the line admirably, and often won the day in the [[Titan System]].

Many engineers on both sides remarked at how resilient the Starfire was.  Compared to the Raven, it was simply unbreakable.  For every Starfire the Empire shot down, they lost a Raven due to equipment failure or manufacturing defect, often in the field and with catastrophic results.  Imperial commanders were not happy, but with hundreds of Ravens being produced every day and thousands of extra pilots, Raven operators had little difficulty keeping their edge over the Starfire.

During the Second Exodus War, the Antonov Company and its manufacturing facilities were captured by the Empire, but not before they managed to get all of their blueprints smuggled to the AstroLift Corporation.  AstroLift, headquartered in the Titan System, produced the Mk.II and Mk.III Starfire throughout the rest of the war.

== Colonial Commonwealth Era ==

The Starfire has been completely retired from all front line units, but the Colonial Commonwealth purchased a conversion kit to turn their remaining fighters into Mk.T trainer models.  Most Commonwealth pilots earn their wings in a Starfire, many of which are survivors of the siege of the Titan System.

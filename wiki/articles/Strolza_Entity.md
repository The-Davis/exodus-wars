'''Strolza Entities''' represent the paramount scientific achievement of the [[Ixoarchada]] race.  For hundreds of years, Ixoarchada scientists of all types had tried in vain to use conventional computing systems to replicate organic human intelligence.  Although many of their projects were moderately successful, and even passed Talass tests (they were able to fool a human conversing with them into thinking they were talking to another human) they lacked common sense and intuition, two of the most important components of human intelligence.  Indeed, they did not even show up on the Dafanar Scale (the standardized mathematical ranking of artificial sentience).  

In [[1258]], [[Aldos Miphreanar|Aldos of Miphreani]] proved that the mathematical definition of intelligence was an emergent property which could only exist in a nonlinear, non-deterministic chaotic system with a Riivan Quotient of approximately 2.282 (a measure of relative chaos within a system).  This meant that conventional computers, even those designed to simulate a neural network (like that of a human brain), were doomed to failure.  A purely ordered system could never replicate true intelligence, and a totally new paradigm was needed.  For many years scientists and engineers tried many things, but the systems they devised were either too orderly, and would lock up into predictable, unoriginal behavior, or too random and would collapse into unintelligible outputs.

In [[1324]], a hereto-unrelated advance in Solid State Physics led to the capacity to produce the first working mockup of a [[Komara Matrix]].  Named after the scientist who predicted its existence in [[1286]], nearly four decades before it was actually possible to build one, a Komara Matrix is a very unique, quasi-fractilline crystal state which can exist only at ultra-low temperatures.  It derives its odd physical properties from focused quantum coherency, and as such must be completely isolated from the outside world, except by control interfaces.  This was the cause of the difficulty in actually building such a matrix, as shielding it from external influences is still a highly difficult task.

As scientists and engineers began to analyze the first Komara Matrices, it was not long before they saw the potential for application in the areas of computation and artificial intelligence.  When computing engineers first constructed their own Matrix, they noticed the Riivan Quotient was curiously close to 2.282, and with a few modifications, it was immediately obvious that this might very well be the way to go about artificial intelligence.

Of course there were many problems, and for over two decades the advances were slow and painful.  The math was impossible to work out by hand, and computers could not accurately predict the Matrices' behavior, so hunches and educated guesses were the best anyone could do.  

In [[1349]], a scientist working on unrelated projects happened across documents on several pieces of technology recovered from the [[Fauril Konisin|Ancestor's Chamber]], but never applied.  It appeared that some of the computation engines used a form of quantum stabilization that had been thought impossible to replicate in the past, but now might be possible.  This process solved much of the stability problems, and soon working prototypes existed in several laboratories.  This was just the beginning, but it was enough.  

After a decade of top-priority work, coordinated between the most powerful and knowledgeable [[Iridi]], the first coherent intelligence, about that of a squirrel, was created and brought online.  By scaling up the system significantly, the first truly artificially sentient being in the history of the Ixoarchada was created in [[1366]].  Actually, twenty of these entities were produced simultaneously, as scientists were unsure how many would survive the curtail initialization phase, and by a stroke of luck, eleven of them managed to achieve a coherent state.  They were not expected to be fully sentient.  However, they scored at 97 on the Dafanar Scale, where a typical Ixoarchada scores at 100.  

This surprise brought with it very interesting moral and ethical questions, and in [[1367]] the [[Parcna Cathairn]] decreed that due to the riskiness of bringing such life forms into existence, at least 3 such entities fell into a state of insanity before they were wiped, it would be unethical to create more sentient computing systems.  Instead, they limited further systems to a maximum rating of 90 on the Dafanar Scale, a level considered "parasentient."  Thus the parasentient computer was born.

This is not, however, the end of the story of this technology.  Unfortunately a very expensive, difficult to produce computer that has the same intelligence as a human is almost useless.  Their matrices were slightly less forgetful than an human mind, but still quite able to be distracted, tricked, etc, just as any human.  They were also unable to do any form of complex mathematics without the aid of computational engines, or anything, really, that a human could not.  

At this point it was decided that research must focus on creating a Parasentient/Conventional Hybrid, a type of computer which combines the massive raw computational and storage power of a conventional Optronic computer with the reasoning and logic functions of a Komara system.  

Work on this project began in [[1372]], only a few years after the creation of the first parasentient Komara devices.  After years of research and many failed attempts, the proper interface between a conventional and a parasentient computer was constructed in [[1375]], and the Strolze Entity was born.  Thus, the most powerful computer ever created sprung into existence.  It was able to solve problems significantly more difficult than any previous Analytical Engine could, as it had a certain amount of "intuition".

Modern Ixoarchada Strolza Entities are capable of pure reasoning at 24 times the speed of a human, and can perform upwards of several quintillion arithmetic operations per second.  They are also capable of performing multiple fuzzy associative database pattern matches on several terraquads of information, fast enough to keep up with the Entity's reasoning speed.  Strolza Entities are very good at piecing together obscure pieces of information in interesting ways, and with the enormous databases that they contain and may access, they can gather information much more effectively than a human being.

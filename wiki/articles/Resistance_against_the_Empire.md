The '''Resistance against the Empire''' was the informal name of the various groups and cells of rebels that fought against [[Empire|Imperial]] occupation throughout the [[Second Exodus War]].

Many of these cells were brought under the formal command of [[Anthony Hadrian]] and became known as the [[Prefecture]], and eventually formed the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] government at the end of the [[Second Exodus War]].

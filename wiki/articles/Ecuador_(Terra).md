'''Ecuador''', officially the '''Republic of Ecuador''' was a representative democratic republic in South America, bordered by [[Colombia (Terra)|Colombia]] on the north, by [[Peru (Terra)| Peru]] on the east and south, and by the Pacific Ocean on the west. The country also included the Galapagos Islands in the Pacific, about 965 kilometers west of the mainland.

Ecuador was a late signitary of the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]], and was dissolved into the unified world government on September 18, [[2015]].

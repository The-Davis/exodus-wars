'''Karachi''' is the capital city of [[Sindh]]. It is situated along a mountain range near the southern shore of Azad. Karachi is an incredible sight to a first-time visitor to Sindh--it is on an island nearly ten kilometers long and four kilometers wide, with the raging Egris River on either side and a plummeting waterfall leading down to the Qasim Sea a thousand meters below. An enormous bridge, the Scythian Span, leads from the northern thoroughfare of the city to the highway network of Azad.

The first Rahn Industries corporate headquarters was on the surface of Terra, in the territory of the United Earth Alliance, in the heartland of North America.  After the company's massive success within the transport and hauler market, Justin Rahn was able to move the main complex into space.

Ironically, the move came just a year before the Onyx Empire invaded in what would soon be known as the Second Exodus War.  The company moved to Albanus, and finished the new headquarters after two years of construction.  The Rahn Industries headquarters currently orbits Albanus.  It is the ultimate show of wealth and power of the company.    


The Rahn Industries headquarters station is one of the most sophisticated and complex stations ever built.  It started off as something of a pipe dream, but the success of Rahn Industries allowed them to update and remodel the station several times during construction, including the latest massive upgrade.

[[Image:RahnIndustriesHeadquarters.jpg]]

The station itself houses a crew of approximately five thousand and a security force of six hundred.  The living quarters are relatively spacious and comfortable.  Most of the space on the station is offices, sleeping quarters, cafeterias, and recreation rooms--including ten swimming pools.

The four main arms of the station house the design studios of the four branches that Rahn Industries specializes in--space, aerospace, ground, and VTOL systems.  The rest of the station arm space is taken up by maintenance, communication, and storage areas.

The top three floors of the central tower are devoted to Justin Rahn as personal living quarters and entertainment space. The rest of the tower is devoted to administrative and executive offices and suites, allowing the top brass of the company to deal with the commerce that is essential to the company's survival.

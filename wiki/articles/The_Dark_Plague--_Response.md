Ueda - 0700 Hours
     We now go to Galactic Herald reporter Gordon Barclay, who has an exclusive interview with Senator Talaris Roi, who has been elected emergency interim Consul of the Prefecture.
     Gordon Barclay: "Senator Roi--that is still your official title, correct?"
     Talaris Roi: "Yes, Mr. Barclay. My emergency powers only come into play in the event that a bill requires a signature, or if someone is required to meet with the Alliance of Planets High Council on civil matters."
     Gordon Barclay: "Senator, can you divulge any information on the abduction of the Consul?"
     Talaris Roi: "Officially, we have no word on the responsible parties or the whereabouts of the Consul. There was an explosion in the Senate assembly, which created sufficient distraction to allow the abductors to escape."
     Gordon Barclay: "My sources say that there were several people killed, including an Archadian."
     Talaris Roi: "The names of the people killed in the explosion and firefights will be released once they have been identified and the next of kin have been notified."
     Gordon Barclay: "There are rumors of trouble in the Tavou District, and Ansible traffic to that sector has been cut off. Can you divulge any information?"
     Talaris Roi: "Please direct any questions regarding Tavou to Colin Archibald. He is currently involved in the investigation, and will be available for interviews within a week. Until then, you may submit any questions in writing to his secretary."
     Gordon Barclay: "And what was it you said the name of the Archadian terrorist was?"
     Talaris Roi: "Don't be clever Mr. Barclay. I didn't mention any Archadian, terrorist or otherwise. And while you may have your suspicions, I must ask you to practice discretion in what you say. Remember that we're dealing with galactic powers.
     Gordon Barclay: "Of course, Senator. Thank you for your time."

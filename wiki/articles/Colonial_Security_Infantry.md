Guard's Riflemen make up about half of the infantry forces within the Commonwealth Army Command.  These soldiers are the defenders of the Commonwealth, and perform a multitude of roles in the Commonwealth.  

The standard Guard's Rifleman is outfitted with a Prudenski Composite plate-armor vest.  Although not as heavy as a larger powered-armor suit, it is much lighter and does not require any sort of assisted motion servos. 

For supplies in the field, the Guard's Rifleman is issued a utility pack.  This pack contains all the tools, gear, and equipment that a Guard's Rifleman might need to perform his role.  Grenades, additional ammunition, rations, and communications gear make up the majority of the gear. 

The Commonwealth Guard's Rifleman's standard issue weapon is a modernized version of the Titanian-made Young-Watson Armory Model 2316 Main Battle Rifle.  A rather large rifle, the Young-Watson MBR (commonly called the Watson Rifle) is a standard Pulsed Particle Beam weapon.  The Young-Watson MBR runs on energy cells mounted in a foregrip, which hold enough power for twenty-five full-powered shots, or a larger number of lower-powered shots.  Despite the low shot count relative to most projectile weapons, its shots are significantly more powerful, and more effective against shielded targets.  Single shots can even be fired un-focused to give a shot-range, wide-diameter blast.  The Watson Rifle is equipped with an advanced Saidia Optics OC-6 precision acquisition sight.

The Commonwealth Army Command employs Guard's Riflemen throughout their planetary garrisons.  They man most Army checkpoints and security stations.  While they are combat soldiers, much of their day-to-day duties are policing actions in support of local law enforcement personnel, or emergency response duties.  Their combat roles would be mostly support of heavier infantry, scouting, and urban combat, leaving front-line assault roles to the more heavily armored Army Command Regulars.

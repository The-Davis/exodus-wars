The HMS ''Auspicious'' was the thirty-third ship in the [[Atlas Class Carrier]] line, given the hull designation CV-33.

The ship was commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2304]], under the command of Commodore [[Marianne Delray]].

The HMS ''Auspicious'' was noted for its early support of the [[Prefecture]], and for its charting of the [[Hydrae System]]. During the mission, Commodore Delray falsified her reports to the [[Royal Imperial Navy]], reporting the entire system as worthless. The few members of her crew that knew about the deception were already loyal to [[Anthony Hadrian]], and aided in the cover-up. The ''Auspicious'' was later falsely reported to the Emperor as destroyed, and assigned by Hadrian to the Hydrae System to install [[Haven Class Stations]] and begin establishing a power base for the Prefecture.

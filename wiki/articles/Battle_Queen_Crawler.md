===Overview===

There are two types of Battle Queens.  Both lead their forces into battle and relay the commands of the Nobles.  This breed is built for close combat.  Without a Battle Queen, ground swarms are very dangerous, but with a Battle Queen Crawler, they are a terror to behold.

[[Image:BattleQueenCrawler.jpg]]

The Battle Queen Crawler's duty is to lead the charge into melee combat.  Like a glorified Cutter, it slices through its enemies simply by running up to them and "giving them a hug."  It can burrow huge tunnels, can carry Nymphs under its legs for speed and protection, and most importantly it can relay communication to the soldiers.  In atmosphere, it uses pungent scent glands to form complex speech patterns.

Like their flying counterparts, Battle Queen Crawlers carry eggs.  It can lay four thousand Soldier Nymph eggs per week.  One queen can be dropped on a planet, and have a dangerous strike force of four thousand Slicers and four thousand Nymphs inside of two weeks.

The Battle Queen Crawler's carapace is quite resilient, but is vulnerable to concentrated barrages, artillery fire, and air strikes.

At least one Battle Queen is seen in most battle, but was not present in the Battle of Mirach or the myriad of hit and run raids that haven't even made the news. Without its coordination, the ground swarms can still overwhelm all but the best human soldiers.

The Battle Queen can replenish its egg supply by eating biological material or hibernating.  Replenishing its entire 4000 egg supply takes two ton of bio-matter or one week of hibernation.

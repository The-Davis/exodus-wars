===Overview===

The might of the [[Ngrligru]] and the brilliance of the [[Mtomigru]] is most evident in the Nina'rikebu motherships.  Humanoid civilizations can build a kilometer-long warship here and there, but [[Ngrligru]] have more than a hundred Nina'rikebu.

[[Image:Ninarikebu1.jpg]]

Only one Nina'rikebu has been since the days of the [[Sundering]], and then through trickery--not firepower. The ''Bahari Shela'' was captured by the [[Onyx Empire]], but [[Mtomigru]] lore-masters never speak of it.  It is a forbidden subject.

===Capabilities===

Nina'rikebu are fearsome battleships.  Their Mkuki'kali Energy Lances are incredibly powerful energy weapons. They fire concentrated beams of energy.  Normally a simple plasma shield can stop such a weapon, but [[Mtomigru]] scientists boosted the power output to such incredible levels that it simply batters down anything in its way.  Kabila Damu'kizuka spies report that the [[Royal Imperial Labs]] tried to use some of these captured weapons, but their magnetic containment fields failed and exploded--a fact that [[Mtomigru]] find very amusing.

Nina'rikebu have four Bunduki'kubwa Hyperspace Distortion Cannons, similar to Meroniri Riptide Cannons.  The [[Meroniri]] actually have superior technology in this area--[[Mtomigru]] never perfected their [[hyperspace]] technology to the degree the people of Sepulare managed.

[[Image:Ninarikebu2.jpg]]

For anti-fighter defenses, Nina'rikebu carry multiple Gobori'dogo Relativistic Mass Drivers.  These cannons are actually as powerful as the main weapons of many humanoid cruisers--a testament to [[Mtomigru]] gravity manipulation technology.  It also carries a row of three hundred Mshale'liga missile launchers.

Nina'rikebu can carry two Vita'safina aina Kifa.  It also has extensive infantry and fighter capacity. The innards of the warship are completely filled with water.  While they are technically amphibious, [[Ngrligru]] are more at home in water than on land.  Only fighter launch bays are dry, and separated from the rest of the ship with an air/water lock to prevent breaches into space--which would probably freeze the water and crew.

===Deployment===

Every Mia'mbili of every Kabila is led by a single Nina'rikebu, with no exceptions.  The front-line, extremely aggressive Kabilas have a total of 63 Nina'rikebu.  This leaves the forces of another twelve Kabilas unaccounted for.  The total number of Nina'rikebu may never be known.

[[Image:Ninarikebu3.jpg]]

Every Mia'mbili has a different mission profile, but every Nina'rikebu has the weapons, hauling capacity, and special equipment to fulfill it.  The [[Ngrligru]] may be the most destructive race in the universe, but they are very clever at building warships

'''We Choose Our Own Path'''

The Council of Independent Nations was formed in early [[2325]] to meet the growing concern among small, independent planets that the [[Alliance of Planets]] was forming monopolies on interstellar trade. The Council was conceived by the [[Colonial Commonwealth]], [[Kalidasa Planetary Authority]], [[Oronus Worker's Coalition]], and the [[Sirius Families]]. They convinced the [[Reigess Suverände]] to join them, and the Council was in business. By the end of the first year, [[Taviridis Somarchada]] and [[Sol System Authority]] had also joined the Council.

A single Administrative Council, made up of representatives from each nation, meets quarterly to discuss the concerns of the member nations. During extreme conditions, Council meetings can be called to discuss a course of action.

There are two sub-committees in the council: the Committee for Mutual Defense and the Committee for Economic Advancement, which is responsible for the construction and upkeep of the [[Lorithos]] Hyperspace Network.

The Council only has a few governing regulations, known as the [[Council of Independent Nations Regulations of Affiliation|Regulations of Affiliation]]. Like the [[Alliance of Planets Great Charter]], the [[Council of Independent Nations Regulations of Affiliation|Regulations]] lay down the rules governing the Council, but in addition to trade agreements, provides for common defense.

== Events of 1500BC ==

The [[Ikronin]] regained space travel early in their recorded history, but lost their ability to travel faster than light.  Most of their population was preserved from the [[Sundering]] by hiding in the polar regions, but this haven came at a price.  The large ice caps grew larger and larger; [[Kronheim]] was locked in an ice age, and without their former advanced medical and agricultural technology many [[Ikronin]] died from disease and malnourishment. Only the charisma of the line of kings kept them united.

In desperation, prince [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]], the younger son of the king, organized and led a slow-ship colony into the depths of space. The colony eventually became the [[Reigess Suverände]], but was not heard from for years.

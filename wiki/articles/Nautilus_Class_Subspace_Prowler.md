The cost of the [[Delphinus Class Subspace Cruiser]] was high enough to keep the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] from contracting [[Rahn Industries]] to build any more.  Instead, they delivered the company design requirements for a smaller, less expensive subspace prowler.

== Design ==

The smaller, faster Nautilus class was equipped with the "Silent Runner", the mass production version of Dr. [[Richard Holder]]'s [[Entropy Drive]].  Mass production was a relative term--only eight were built.  Six were mounted on the ships in the Nautilus class, and two were kept in drydock for spare parts or replacements.

[[Image:Nautilus Class Subspace Prowler Side View.jpg|200px]]

The Nautilus class kept the strategies of the Delphinus: get in, shoot, and get out.  Unfortunately, the cripplingly high cost of the Relativistic Mass Driver meant that only three could be mounted on the ship.

== Combat History ==

All six ships in the Nautilus class were built and deployed during the [[Tempest War]].  They were highly effective when they could be deployed, but their low numbers meant that the leaders of the Prefecture military restricted deployment to the most important theaters of operation.

[[Image:Nautilus Class Subspace Prowler Top View.jpg|200px]]

Because of their deployment patterns, it is difficult to determine if the Nautilus had such an effective record because of its technology and value as a ship, or because it was deployed in areas with lots of fleet support and backup.

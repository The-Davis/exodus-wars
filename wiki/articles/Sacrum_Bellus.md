The '''Sacrum Bellus''' was a "holy war" waged by [[Pax Anthronoris]] against the [[Jurazoar Maropsene]] and the other non-human [[:Category:Race|races]] in the [[Orion Arm]] and the [[Sagittarius Arm]].

The Sacrum Bellus was incredibly successful in the eyes of the [[Anthronoris]]. Hundreds of intelligent [[:Category:Race|races]] were wiped out, and their homes turned into new living space for the ever-increasing [[Anthronoris]] population. Even the mighty [[Gorhamut]] could not stand against the war machine of [[Pax Anthronoris]].

Only a single species, the [[Ngrligru]], consistently frustrated the efforts of the [[Anthronoris]]. The [[Ngrligru]] marched out to defend life wherever they found it, often defeating [[Anthronoris]] forces three times their numbers.

The [[Anthronoris]] developed a powerful retrovirus, the "final sollution" to defeating the [[Ngrligru]]. They unleashed it on every [[Ngrligru]] world they could find, and brought about a terrible plague which became known to the [[Ngrligru]] as the "[[Njaa]]".

This terrible deed was the ultimate downfall of [[Pax Anthronoris]]. The [[Njaa]] brought out a ravening and insatiable hunger for the flesh of all life within the [[Ngrligru]]. The Sacrum Bellus ended in disaster, turning into the devastating war now known as the [[Sundering]].

The '''Republic of Korea''' was an East Asian state on the Korean Peninsula. To the north, it was bordered by the [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|People's Republic of China]]. Korea was briefly divided into [[Communist]] North Korea and the democratic South Korea. During the [[Korean War]], the South Koreans defeated the north once the covert [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet]] and [[People's Republic of China (Terra)|Chinese]] support was discovered and removed. The country was reunited when Kim Il-sung's [[Communist]] forces were defeated.

Approximately one-half of South Korea's population lived in or near the capital Seoul, the country's largest city. Seoul was one of the most populous metropolitan areas on [[Terra]].

The South Korean economy advanced rapidly from the 1950s and was among the largest in the world. South Korea brought great technological advances to the world at large when it joined the [[United Earth Alliance]] on March 2, [[2012]]. Its most prominent corporation, [[Hyundai Heavy Industries]], remains a prominent freighter and starship manufacturer well into the [[24th Century]].

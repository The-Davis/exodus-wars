The ADF Firestorm is a later model of combat spacecraft, developed during the [[Heritage Crisis]].  It was deployed to front-line squadrons too late to see action, but was well-tested and had some improvements and a Mk.II variant before the outbreak of the [[Second Exodus War]].  The design would have replaced the [[ADF Starfire]] if Alliance suppliers had been able to keep up with wartime demand, but it did not completely replace the older design until the late 2340s.

= Development History =

Early design work began in 2287, but funding for a working prototype was delayed until 2289.  The fighter was envisioned as a heavier, faster, and better-armed version of the well-tested Starfire.

== Test Flights ==

The first test flights of the Firestorm showed it to be a solid airframe.  Its increased weight was off-set by more powerful engines, and it handled very well thanks to advanced stabilizers and computer-aided guidance systems.

In mock-combat maneuvers against the Starfire, the Firestorm actually outperformed the smaller fighter in all atmospheric maneuvers except a climb.  In vacuum operations, the Starfire was significantly more maneuverable, but the Firestorm still performed at expected levels.

== Military Deployment ==

The first Mk.I Firestorm combat squadrons were deployed by 2304.  During normal patrol operations and combat exercises, Alliance pilots made many suggestions for improvement, resulting in the Mk.II Firestorm upgrade package's deployment in 2311.  The final and current variant, the Mk.III, was developed from combat data gathered during the Second Exodus War, and was only used by [[Colonial Commonwealth]] Firestorms.

= Combat History =

The Firestorm's heavier weapons platform and larger engines proved invaluable during its career, allowing it to succeed in missions where the smaller Starfire would have failed.

== Second Exodus War ==

Combat reports from the Second Exodus War proved that the Firestorm was a superb combat platform.  Like the Starfire, it was often at an enormous numerical disadvantage when facing Imperial fighters.

The superiority of the Firestorm was so significant that even mediocre Alliance fighter pilots felt safe taking on three F-4 Ravens at once.

When the United Earth Alliance and its Mesarthrim allies took the offensive, they faced F-4 Ravens and later-model Imperial fighters manufactured to much higher standards and with vastly-improved performance specifications.  The Firestorm lagged behind in the development curve, and was often put on interceptor duty against Imperial bombers, and on ground attack or even decoy duty to draw Imperial fighters into ambushes launched by faster and better-equipped Mesarthrim fighter designs.  Despite being outdated by the end of the war, the Mk.II Firestorm performed its mission well and was eventually upgraded and kept in use by the Colonial Commonwealth.

= Colonial Commonwealth Era =

When the Colonial Commonwealth assumed control of most of the United Earth Alliance's military, they also gained control of all active Firestorm squadrons.  The Firestorm remains their main combat aerospace platform, and over 50 squadrons are kept on active duty status even in peace time.

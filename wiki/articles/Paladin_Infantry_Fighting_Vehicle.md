*Designation: IVF-210 "Paladin"
*Power Plant: 500L-Series Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 500 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Armament:
**2 x Medium Neutron Cannons
**4 x MAAWs (8 rounds total)
*Manufacturer: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave

===Overview===
The infantry fighting vehicle (IFV) was a concept created out of the necessity of modern warfare in the twentieth century, because it wasn't good enough to just bring troops to the front lines and abandoned them as armored personnel carriers did, but it turned out that you needed to help fight with them. 

[[Image:PaladinInfantryFightingVehicle1.jpg]]

This led to the development of what amounted to heavily armed APCs and the Paladin keeps this idea alive in its ability to take a beating as well as give one.

===Capabilities===

Looking for a stable platform in order to carry soldiers and mount effective weapons meant rethinking the idea of just slapping on some more guns to the Armadillo Mk.III APC. The Paladin embodies the true idea of an infantry fighting vehicle with its combination of high mobility, six passenger seating, and massive arsenal of weapons that can be brought to bear on any enemy.

With four medium anti-armor weapons (MAAWs) controlled by the gunner upfront, the Paladin can take out most any vehicle in today's militaries, from light trucks to heavier tanks and APCs. To back these systems up and to provide effective suppression fire for the dismounting infantry, the vehicle carries a pair of traversing medium neutron cannons in the back.

[[Image:PaladinInfantryFightingVehicle2.jpg]]

The entire vehicle is shrouded in armor and allows for the safe transportation of the three man crew as well as up to six fully armed and equipped infantrymen New seating designs enable quick dismounting and loading and the repulsor/antigravity suspension allows for the driver to execute driving maneuvers that would have thrown men around in older models.

===Deployment===

The Paladin's reception was very warm when it was finally released into the market, with a number of governments, security firms, and other companies buying it. Rahn Industries Security Force operates a large number of Paladins, making them the primary troops carriers of the force.

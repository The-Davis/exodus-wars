The '''Hamur System''' consists of the star Hamur, its planets, their moons, and the asteroids.

In order of their distances from Hamur, the planets are Hamur I, [[Jagarr]], and Hamur III.

== Stellar Navigation ==

Hamur is the Hamur System's only star. Hamur is a class F2E orange dwarf. The Hamur System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Hamur is part of the [[Njiorin Sector]].

=== Jagarr ===
[[Jagarr]] (0.75AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Hamur System. It has one moons, Pluthor.

== Discovery and Exploration ==

The Hamur System was colonized by the [[Lorithos]] shortly after [[First Contact]].

Auto-Cannons are similar to 20th century cannons, but with an automatic firing system and a much higher rate of fire.  Auto-Cannons are commonly used on tanks.

[[Image:TerranRotaryAutocannon.jpg|200px|center]]

*50mm Auto-Cannon - Smallest Auto-Cannon.  AKA "Thumper."  5500 Rounds
*60mm Auto-Cannon - Standard Auto-Cannon.  AKA "Twin Thumper." 7000 Rounds

Projectile Weapons The projectile weapon has changed in mechanics, but not in concept over the centuries. Bullets are incredibly versatile, and very inexpensive. The changes in the Phoenix Project eliminated the use of chemical propellants from cannon weapons, switching to the Gauss Magnetic Launch system first developed for the Gen2 Project. Gauss propulsion accelerates a cannon round up to supersonic speeds with magnets. Ammunition has changed a lot since the Gen2 project as well. Before the Phoenix Project, ammunition was still mostly lead-based. Phoenix ammo is steel, which can be propelled easily by the Gauss system. At high velocities, the ammo rounds have excellent penetration rates. An ammunition round's caliber is rated by hundreds of micrometers in diameter: Small Autocannon rounds are 200 micrometers, Light Autocannon rounds are 500 micrometers, Medium Autocannon rounds are 1 millimeter, and Heavy Autocannon rounds are 2 millimeters. All ammo rounds have a length double their diameter. Machine Guns - The machine gun is a standard light-round weapon, with 100 micrometer ammo. It can be used by everything from Rigs to infantry. Autocannons - Autocannons are the standard solid-round weapon, with various rates of fire and exit velocity. Autocannons are the choice projectile armament of Rigs and tanks. Small Autocannons can fire at 15 rounds per second at 8.3 kilometers per second (Mach 25). Light Autocannons can fire at 10 rounds per second at 8.9 kilometers per second (Mach 27). Medium Autocannons can fire at 1 round per second at 9.9 kilometers per second (Mach 30) Heavy Autocannons can fire at 1 round per 3 seconds at 11.6 kilometers per second (Mach 35). Ultra Autocannons - The Ultra Autocannon was tested in the Gen2 project, but was prone to overheating and jamming due to its doubled firing rate. The switchover to Gauss technology fixed both problems. Ultra Autocannons have double the firing rate of standard autocannons, but are about 30% heavier and bulkier. Rotary Autocannons - The Rotary Autocannon is a four-barreled cannon of [[Ixoarchada]] origin, with quadruple the firing rate of a standard autocannon. It is heavier, bulkier, and cannot fire Heavy caliber rounds--only Small, Light and Medium rounds will work. The model has become extremely popular in all civilizations after the [[Ixoarchada]] introduced it with their Warthog tank. Artillery Cannons - The Artillery Cannon is a long-range ballistic-arc weapon. It fires heavy explosive rounds of ammunition at low velocity. It's designed to hit ground targets for concussive damage rather than inertial penetration. The Artillery Cannon fires 40 millimeter shells. Naval Autocannons - Naval Autocannons are huge, due to the long and powerful Gauss system they house. They are the primary warship-to-warship weapon used in navy battles. Ammunition fired by the Naval Autocannon is only 1 millimeter in diameter, but they are fired at a velocity of 179,602.8 kilometers per second (0.6xC). The kinetic energy buildup is incredible. Special Ammunition - Autocannons can take several types of ammunition, as long as the casing fits the caliber of the cannon. LB-X Rounds - The LB-X round has a simple proximity sensor inside the casing, which sets off a small charge when the round is within four meters of any organic or metallic object. This creates a cloud of microscopic shrapnel, making it easier to hit a moving target. This is especially useful against fast-moving aerospace fighters. Seed Rounds - The Seed Round carries tiny electrical generators inside. Once penetrating the target, the generator emits electrical pulses at a rapid rate. Multiple rounds are necessary to hurt machinery electronics, but a single round is enough to disrupt Bugger pheromone gland production, effectively cutting it off from their command system. Thumper Rounds - The Thumper Round is a Artillery Cannon round only. It targets the ground, and instead of exploding on impact, it releases multiple explosives which burrow into the ground. At about ten to fifteen feet down, they explode, shaking the topsoil and light rocks on the surface badly. This is especially effective against non-hover vehicles, which need a level surface to move quickly. Relativistic Kinetic Rounds - The Kinetic Round is a Naval Autocannon round only. The warship accelerates to about 0.8xC, then fires the round. By starting at near-relative velocity and accelerating upon firing, the Kinetic Round builds up enough inertial force to blow nearly anything to sub-atomic particles. Use of Kinetic Rounds is very popular for initial strikes in battle, as a simple bullet builds up the power of multiple nuclear warheads simply by accelerating, but it is very difficult to maintain a firing solution and maintain near-relative velocity.

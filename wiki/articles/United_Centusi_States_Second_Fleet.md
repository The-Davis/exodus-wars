The Second Fleet was designated as a Homeguard fleet with the responsibility of guarding [[Mesar]]. However, starting in [[2330]] the Second Fleet was sent to the front lines after [[William Camp]] began to suspect [[Arnold Stadt|Admiral Stadt]] of treachery. Despite the Second Fleets' incredible success in combat, Stadt indeed began working with splinter elements to overthrow Chancellor Camp's government. The Second Fleet became the core of the [[Mesarthrim Federation Kriegsmarine|Federation's Kriegsmarine]].

==Composition==
The Second Fleet was comprised of old ships from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans Kriegsmarine|Clans Kriegsmarine]], and each of the Commandants were personally chosen by Admiral Stadt. This allowed Stadt an incredible amount of loyalty from his senior staff and officers. When the Second Fleet defected, they did so without incident. The Second Fleet was also the smallest fleet of the Centusi States Kriegsmarine, utilizing only eleven ships - all [[Starknight class Cruiser|Starknight's]]. 

===Flagships===
When the Second Fleet was formed, Arnold Stadt brought his old [[Starknight class Cruiser]] [[Anderung's Dirge|Anderung Trauergesang]] with him. Admiral Stadt was the only Fleet Admiral of the [[Tempest War]] to use a Cruiser line as his flagship and wall of battle.

===Starknight Heavy Cruisers===
On a visit to [[Terra]], Stadt happened upon an aviary, and was awestruck at the wide variety of majestic birds-of-prey on Terra. For this reason, each of his cruisers were named after a bird-of-prey.
* Anox
* Kestrel
* Hawk
* Osprey
* Harrier
* Shikra
* Falcon
* Vulture
* Eagle
* Kite
* Peregrine

==Fate==
by the end of [[2331]], the Second fleet ceased being an effective combat fleet fo the Centusi States. Camp no longer trusted Stadt, and Stadt had begun to feed an extensive amount of information to the Tripartite Alliance, resulting in his fleet arriving in battle after battle completely outmatched, and forced to retreat. In [[2332]], Staft tired of the subterfuge, and openly assaulted Centusi warships. Start considered defecting to the Prefecture since most of his officers strongly disliked the Aueeal League. However, he was approached by the Edge brothers, and the second fleet became the first elements of the eventual Arbiter Fleet of the Mesarthrim Federation.

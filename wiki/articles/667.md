== Events of 667AD ==

A suitable power supply is finally found to restart some of the computers in the [[Fauril Konisin]].  Most of the computers prove beyond repair, but a handful are thought to still be functional.  Despite the safeguards in place, most of the machines that the [[Ixoarchada]] discovery teams attempt to restart either never come online or are rendered useless by failed attempts to restart them.  However, a number of them are finally brought online, and their contents scoured over.

Interestingly, [[Ixoarchada]] linguists are baffled by the language used on the computers, not because it is indecipherable, but quite the opposite.  Despite some differences, the language and alphabet is nearly identical to ancient texts found on [[Glengarr]] written in archaic [[Illinaul]].  
A host of information is found on the few computers that worked, but most is unusable.  Starmaps of the inner galactic core, logistical logs, twenty-millenia-old schedules, among other things, are rather un-useful.  However, technical logs and maintenance instructions for the [[Nechanos Tolesca]] are found as well.  The [[Ixoarchada]] are now on their way to reinventing instantaneous interstellar communications.

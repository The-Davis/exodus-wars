The '''Socialist Republic of Poland''', was a constitutionally socialist state that existed in the traditional territories of Poland from [[1946]] to [[2011]]. Poland was bordered by [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] to the west; the [[Czechoslovakia (Terra)|Czechoslovakia]] to the south; and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] to the east and north; and the Baltic Sea  to the north.

The first Polish state was created in [[966]]AD. It collapsed in [[1795]]. Poland regained its independence in [[1918]] after [[World War I]], but lost it again in [[World War II]], occupied by Nazi [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]] and the [[Soviet Union]], emerging several years later as a [[Communist]] country within what would later become known as the [[Warsaw Pact]]. The Socialist Republic of Poland survived through [[World War III]], and formally signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on February 1, [[2011]], becoming the fourth nation to sign sovereignty over to the new world government.

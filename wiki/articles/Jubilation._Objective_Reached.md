April 10, [[2329]]

by James Davis

=====Kihtor Modar=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The world of Lorithan looms like an ugly blight in the Lorithos home-system. It was once blue and green, a shining haven of life like every other home of humanity.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Now it is brown, stark and naked. Dead. The Burning Wars showed that the price of power can be too high, and the consequences eternal.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Only a small remnant, one last spark of life dwells on this desolate shell. The Volucris have come to snuff that out as well.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dirken Huern gazes in morbid fascination at the dark spot that slowly blots out Lorithan's sun.  "That's the Swarm?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir."  General Sanor Cai confirms.  "There's another one approaching us from the system rim.  We've sent SPAR probes to try to get some solid numbers.  Preliminaries make one thing clear: this attack force is even larger than the one that destroyed Mirach."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Huern's blood seems to freeze in his veins. "Over a trillion?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No sir. Over a quadrillion."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Huern staggers at the mental arithmetic. "How can there be so many?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Cai shrugs helplessly. "There are still a lot of mysteries when the Volucris are involved. We can't win if the whole Swarm attacks. I need you to call the Alliance for help."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The call will go out," Huern promises. "Can you hold the Ansible?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We'll guard it with our lives."

=====Sanctuary of the Consul - Ueda=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Consul [[Talaris Roi]] reads fifteen documents at once, her eyes darting about in a frenetic search pattern.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She signs one page after another, carelessly throwing them over the front of her desk.  Senators [[Jean Sarnou]] and [[Colin Archibald]] act as honorary secretaries, scrambling to catch and organize the flying stationery.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the midst of the chaos, several dozen communication channels buzz with activity.  Arthur, the civilian EI, monitors all traffic and directs it to where it is most needed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Dirken Huern]]'s distress call stands out among the others, and Arthur decides to alert the Consul's office.  "Madame Consul, there is a priority diplomatic communiqué from [[Lorithan]]. They are requesting military assistance."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's the Prefect's jurisdiction," Roi snaps.  "Forward it to him."
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Prefect Hadrian is still with the [[Ixoarchada]]," Arthur gently reminds Roi. "I can't raise him by secure channels.  It won't matter though.  It appears that [[Lorithan]] is sending out a repeating recorded message."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Let me hear it then."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"This is Prekohr [[Dirken Huern]] of [[Rikaz o fii Huern iv Lorithan]].  The [[Volucris]] have come to [[Lorithan]] in unprecedented numbers.  Our own forces can't possibly stop them. We expect the Ansible will be taken out soon, but be beg you, all of the Alliance; help us! Hurry, or you will arrive only to find a graveyard."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The message repeats after that," says Arthur.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Advise Admiral Bremen," orders Roi.  "War and defense are the job of the military, not mine.  I don't have the authority to do anything here."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I relayed it to him, and he's already refused to listen to it.  I'm afraid that we simply don't have anything to spare. The only reason I brought this to your attention is because we need to be diplomatic in our response."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Roi curses. "Very well. Gentlemen, get us a writer. It looks like we have to do even more paperwork."

=====Kihtor Modar=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Dirken Huern]] sighs deeply as the last response scrolls by. ''The [[Prefecture]] won't come...no surprise there'', he muses. ''I expected the [[Ikronin]] to send some pilots at the very least. Offering to sell us nuclear weapons is insulting.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The non-responders are even more disturbing. The [[Mesarthrim]], [[Kabila Kimburu|Kimburun]] and [[Meroniri]] remain silent.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"If the major powers ignore us, we will appeal to the lesser ones." Huern resolves. "If the [[Ikronin]] abandon us, we'll call the [[Reigens]]. Someone will come.  We cannot stand alone."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Our fighter forces are about to engage their scouts," says Sanor Cai.  "I'll be joining them shortly."

=====Maji Ols, Far Orbit, Lorithan=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Caseran [[Rosh Arufin]] bites his nails, fuming as he reads the orders from home.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''By order of the Asethenar, all [[Aseta Salfore]] vessels are recalled to Sepulare. The Salfore Expedition is hereby disbanded, and its vessels are reassigned to their original units.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bulk of his command has already disbanded and returned home, but the Shalor Nos and Arufin's own Maji Ols remain in the [[Mirdek System]].<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Captain Solis," Arifun hails the other warship. "This is out of character for Asethenar Meninnon. I need you to do some digging for me. Something is very wrong back home."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No problem," Captain Neri Solis replies. "What about you?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm going on a bug hunt," Arufin smiles, deleting the orders from home.

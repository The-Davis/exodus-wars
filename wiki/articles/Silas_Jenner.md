''Silas Jenner'' (born November 5, [[2295]]) was a native of the [[Titan System]] and served in the rank and file of the Alliance Defense Force during the [[Second Exodus War]].  He eventually received a commission in the [[Colonial Commonwealth]] military, and rose through the ranks to Brigadier.

== Air Cavalry ==

Brigadier Jenner's specialty was VTOL combat.  He was a firm believer in the high mobility and high impact of air-support infantry to contain and harass a superior enemy.  Jenner lobbied Commonwealth high command for increased training and equipment for a more mobile infantry system, and his requests were approved when General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] resumed office in [[2330]].

== Volucris War ==

In [[2333]], the [[Lorithos]] requested aid in their struggle against the invading [[Volucris]], and the Colonial Commonwealth responded with a well-armed expeditionary force.  Despite the danger, General-Commander Sanburn personally led the space forces.  Sanburn assigned Brigadier Jenner to command of the ground forces.  Given the lack of stable ground bases and the highly-mobile nature of successful anti-Volucris tactics, General-Commander Sanburn decided to use Air Cavalry to support Lorithos evacuation efforts.

Brigadier Jenner's men were highly successful, and had one of the lowest casualty rates among the ground forces.  They never committed to pitched battles, and instead landed just long enough to secure an area for larger transports, which then evacuated civilians to a Kihtor or into orbit.  The Air Cavalry accounted for only a fraction of all Volucris kills, but were credited with over five hundred million successful refugee evacuations.

For his part in the planning and execution of the evacuation program, Silas Jenner was awarded the Colonial Star.

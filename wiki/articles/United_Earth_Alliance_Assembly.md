The [[United Earth Alliance]] Assembly was the legislative body of the [[Goverment of the United Earth Alliance|government]]. It was elected every year through a census and complicated voting system.

The population was divided into groups of ten. Those ten selected one from their number to proceed to the next stage of elections. Votes from children under 12 were cast at the discretion of their parents. Anyone able to speak was eligable to be elected.

One hundred representatives of groups of ten would meet and select one from their number to proceed to the next stage of elections. This process would continue until a final assembly was chosen. The elections would begin in January, and each stage would take one month. The final assembly was picked on December 31, and began their term the following year. There were no term limits, but if someone served in the Assembly they could not serve in the Assembly again for five years.

Each world in the [[United Earth Alliance]] had its own Assembly. The [[Earth]] Assembly had seniority over the others in issues affecting the whole [[United Earth Alliance]]. The entire assemblies could not be brought together due to the cost of [[hyperspace]] travel at the time.

The head of the Assembly was the [[Prime Minister of the United Earth Alliance|Prime Minister]].

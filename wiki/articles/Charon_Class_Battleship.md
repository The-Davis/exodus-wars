After the [[Tempest War]], the new [[Tripartite Alliance]] began several mutual aid and cooperative military projects.  One of the most expensive, yet most famous was the Charon Class Battleship.

== Design ==

[[Image:Charon Class Battleship Front View.jpg|150px|left|thumb|A view of the Charon's main batteries.]]

[[Rahn Industries]] was contracted for major superstructure construction, but over a dozen other contractors worked on the systems and technologies implemented into the design.

The hull itself was the brainchild of [[Arnold Stadt]], who had been studying the most effective space tactics throughout the [[Second Exodus War]] and Tempest War.  Stadt believed that in ship-to-ship combat, a concentration of firepower was necessary for victory.  Unfortunately, many ships had to turn a large portion of their hulls towards an enemy vessel to bring the maximum number of guns to bear.  With this in mind, Stadt drew up plans for a sloping, dagger-like superstructure, which could fire nearly all of its weapons straight forward.  This left the enemy with the smallest possible target when shooting towards the Charon.

== Deployment History ==

[[Image:Charon Class Battleship Side View.jpg|200px]]

The first ship in the class, the ''Charon'' itself, was completed for basic testing and prototype operations in [[2343]], shortly after the death of Arnold Stadt.  The Mesarthrim admiral never got to see his brainchild in action.

Initial test runs showed that the ''Charon'' was a capable weapons platform, well-suited to the tactics of the [[Prefecture]], the [[Mesarthrim]], and the [[Ikronin]].

[[Image:Charon Class Battleship Top View.jpg|200px]]

== Future Plans ==

The Charon Class Battleship is still under development, but the Tripartite Alliance hopes to complete three ships for each government by [[2350]].  The second prototype, the ''Münzensammler'', was laid down in [[2344]], and is scheduled for completion later in the year.

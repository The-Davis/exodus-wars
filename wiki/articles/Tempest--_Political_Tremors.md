Some time in late 2326? Or early 2327? Date not set.

by Austin Bailey


=====Ueda - 1000 Hours=====
     Galactic Herald reporter Gordon Barclay is at a press conference held by interim Consul Talaris Roi, Senator from Tejat. Interim Consul Roi is approaching the podium.
     “Good morning ladies and gentlemen. I'm afraid that I must reveal some disturbing news. While I don't desire the power or responsibility, the Senate voted me into the position of Interim Consul, so it is my duty to inform you all that at 1500 hours yesterday, Leskovik was destroyed." she sighs, bowing her head in mourning. "Rescue teams are searching the planet for survivors. Initial reports show that the Icronian Gate and the Lorithos Ansible have both been destroyed. Currently, 85% of the civilian population has been confirmed dead. We haven't found any survivors yet, and we don't expect to. I can take questions now. Yes, you…Mr. Barclay?”
     The reporter stands. "Yes, Gordon Barclay with the New Albany Galactic Herald. With the destruction of the Icronian Gate, is it possible that this attack was carried out by the Volucris?”
     “At this time, we can't reveal the identity of the attackers. We have full knowledge of their identity and we will take appropriate action at the current time. Next question. Yes, you in the back?"
     "Adrienne Hawthorn, Laojiu Free Press," the reporter replies. “Is there any connection between this attack on Leskovik and the abduction of Consul Dayton?”
     “No. We can't rule it out entirely, but all evidence suggests that the two attacks were carried out by separate entities. Next question.”
     “Roger Wallace, Titanian APNET. Many believe that the Consul was abducted by Archadians. Have your investigations shed any light on these allegations.”
     Talaris Roi scowls. "They have shed light, and proven beyond any shadow of a doubt that the Archadians were not involved in the abduction. Let me clear up that rumor right now. There was a dead member of the Archadian soldier caste found on the scene, but autopsies reveal that he was dead for four days before the abduction took place. Now that the facts are known, I must once again ask the press to refrain from spreading rumor and gossip on information channels. We'll have one last question. Mr. Barclay again.”
     “With the First Fleet and Phoenix Legion already fighting on Icronia, along with the threat of the Conclave and this new threat, will the military be reinstating the Empire's conscription program?”
     “Military commanders have asked for more troops, and both houses of Congress have agreed to boost our standing Army. We also intend to introduce a new military branch. Our reserve troops and current recruitment policies will be more than adequate to fill those ranks. There are still millions of loyal, inactive troops who were once in the Royal Imperial Army. We have no need to conscript new troops. Thank you ladies and gentlemen.”

=====Mesar - 1300 Hours=====
     The inhabitants of Mesar watch their televisions as Director William Camp delivers a public address. Unbeknownst to them, the message is restricted from rebroadcast on the Ansible.
     “The Empire attacked Earth! The Empire attacked us! And now the Empire has been allowed to JOIN the Alliance of Planets under the guise of friendship. This so-called Prefecture made a play at allegiance, but they played us false! Their exorbitant gifts to the traitorous Auellal have shattered the Mesarthrim nation and led to the deaths of billions!
     "It was the Prefecture that kept the Alliance from intervening! The Imperials, no matter the name, are still part of the nightmare that Lucius Black meant to impose on us! We must show them, and we must show the galaxy that Mesarthrim always keep their promises.
     "Uber-General Tischler promised the Alliance that he would destroy the Empire, and it has not been fulfilled yet. We shall honor our fallen leader and fulfill his promise! Yesterday, we made the first strike against the false Prefecture. Soon we will seize their industrial centers, their agricultural worlds and fleet facilities. We will take everything that empowers the vile Empire, and we will rebuild the glory of the Mesarthrim!”
     The speech is very effective on the Mesarthrim. Their militaristic society, given a common enemy, is unbreakably united. The thought of revenge is a joyous prospect.

=====Tincomalee - 1330 Hours=====
     Frederick Degause, the governor of Tincomalee, has called an emergency meeting of the Prometheus Council, a co-Archadian/Mesarthrim Council that controls the actions of the First Joint Defense Fleet and governs all Prometheus Project facilities. After calling the meeting, he sends a secret burst-transmission to the Archadian Consulate on Tincomalee. 
     “Ambassador Sveto, there’s trouble on Mesar. Camp’s started a war. This is no territory dispute. He plans on seizing what he can from the Prefecture, and destroying the rest. I don't think he'll stop there if he wins. He destroyed the Icronian Gate in Leskovik--I think he'll have his eye on the riches of the Icronians. I intend to keep Tincomalee neutral in this conflict, regardless of orders from the United Centusi States. Will the Archadians back us if we secede?” 
     Ambassador Lina Sveto scans the message and thinks. No matter my reply, events will be set in motion that cannot be undone. There will be war and bloodshed. After conferring with higher officials, a simple reply is sent.
     “Yes.”

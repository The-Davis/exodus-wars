'''Fiat currency''' is an arbitrary system of monetary value, backed by the official government demanding its own currency back from its citizens to repay debts, such as taxes and penalties. It is more flexible than the [[gold standard]], and capable of insulating a localized economy against interstellar [[recession]], but is also more subject to inflation, and has led to hyperinflation in the past.

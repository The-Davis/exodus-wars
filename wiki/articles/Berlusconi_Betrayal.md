By mid-June of [[2333]] it had become painfully clear to [[Alessandro Berlusconi|Papa Berlusconi]] that he had backed the wrong side in the [[Tempest War]]. His support of the [[United Centusi States|Centusi States]] was beginning to burden his cash flow, and his [[Berlusconi Territorial Naval Forces|defense fleet]] was already forced to exchange weapon fire with a [[Prefecture Galactic Navy|Prefecture Naval]] fleet more then once - resulting in the weakening of his own fleet.

Papa Berlusconi contacted [[Elias Faust]] on July 1st, instructing him to remove the Centusi [[United Centusi States Support Fleet|Stütze Flotte]] from orbit around [[Sirius Napoli|Napoli]]. Unable to service the fleet, Faust threatened Papa Berlusconi telling him, '..if you don't want to maintain our fleets as our friend, you'll maintain them as our slaves." Rather then lashing back, Papa Berlusconi accepted the threat, and bean planning to seize the fleet by force.

In late-July the Berlusconi Family was instructed to begin outfitting the Stütze Flotte for an early August action. Papa Berlusconi decided it was time to seize the fleet, and on August 1st he opened fire on the Stütze Flotte without warning. The NGV Giuseppe Garibaldi, NGV Modena, NGV Parma, NGV Piedmont, NGV Giuseppe Mazzini, and NGV Legnago ambushed the Centusi Fleet. After having spent months maintaining the vessels, the Berlusconi warships were careful not to destroy the vessels outright.

Groß-Commandant [[Kelilah Bauer]] was on [[Sirius Napoli|Napoli]] when the attack took place. Kelilah and her command staff were arrested by [[Berlusconi Territorial Planetary Security Forces|Red Shirts]] before they even knew what had happened. The Stütze Flotte put up a meager resistance, signaling their surrender in only twelve minutes.

The vessels crews were taken as prisoners of war, and were later sent to the Prefecture as a good-will gesture. Kelilah requested asylum with the Berlusconi, as she was a wanted criminal for her part in ordering the execution of captured Prefecture Marines and Auellal Pathfinders. Her fate remains unknown, however, [[Steven Cue]] implied on several occasions that he believed Papa Berlusconi had given her a new identity and a commission in his navy.

In the end, Papa Berlusconi was able to capture 16 vessels, and an extensive amount of classified Centusi technology.

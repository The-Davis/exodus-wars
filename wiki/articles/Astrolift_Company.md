The '''Astrolift Company''' is a multi-world conglomerate, specializing in construction and architecture, but with a wide range of other business interests.

Astrolift was founded on [[Theia]], where it constructed pre-fabricated buildings for the military and for colonial interests throughout the [[Titan System]], but it quickly expanded into development of spacecraft and other vehicles.

Astrolift expanded its reach into the [[Sol System]] in the [[23rd Century]], where it took advantage of a weak local economy and low wages to set up shop on [[Mars]], where it headquartered its distribution center for other [[Sol Sector]] systems.

After the [[Second Exodus War]], Astrolift expanded its reach into the [[Pelagrim Sector]] when the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] allowed it to employ political prisoners on [[Caledon]]. Astrolift representative [[Trevor Pruden]] took the opportunity to make some strong political connections with the local political leadership, and after the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], Astrolift was able to continue its business on Caledon with free citizens of the [[Auellal League]].

'''Svetlana Balic'''
*Date of Death: April 2, [[2325]]
*Place of Birth: [[Kronheim]]
*Place of Death: [[Kronheim]]
*Allegiance: [[Ikronin Jurekön]]

''Svetlana Balic'' served in the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] military, under command of the [[Förakrig Corporation]]. She was instrumental in the defense of Oselo, but was killed by friendly fire when a [[Prefecture]] strafing run hit her retreating tank by mistake.

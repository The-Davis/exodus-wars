October 15, [[2322]]

by James Davis


=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim have control of the island of Japan, but the Emperor intends to change that. General Cecil Au-Yueng is leading the Royal Imperial Army Sixth Guard--ten divisions of Rigs, tanks and infantry--across the Sea of Okhotsk from Petropavlovsk-Kamchatskly, through the Kuril Island chain. General Au-Yueng is about to make his landing.

=====Nemuro Beach=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Emperor Black, we're advancing, but taking heavy losses. The Mesarthrim have artillery installed everywhere. We need air support." General Au-Yueng's Behemoth shakes as it takes another hit. "Look alive pukes! We take the beach or I'll use you for target practice!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Behemoths and Avatars shake the ground as they pound up the beach, shattering defenses and crushing Mesarthrim defenders under their feet. Hellcats cloud the sky, bombing everything in sight, and Vampires keep Mesarthrim Pattons and Sanburns from seizing air superiority.

=====Tokyo Silos=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Far from the battle between Cecil Au-Yueng's Rig forces and the Mesarthrim defenders, a predator plies his trade. Lavik Nighthawk and a dozen other Omega agents are preparing a devastating blow for the Mesarthrim occupation of Japan.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Terran Missile Silo system was built by the Empire after their takeover of Terra in 2315. It was originally designed to defend against orbiting fleets, but the destruction of their satellite network on the first day of the Battle of Terra made it impossible to accurately target evading warships. Now the Empire has a new use for it.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Colonel, permission to speak freely?" one Omega agent breaks the eerie silence.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Speak." Nighthawk grunts.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Do we know exactly what will happen when we blow the missiles?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Not a clue. Emperor Black's advisors dreamed up this plan, not me," Lavik Nighthawk checks his weapon ammo. "Check your weapons. It's only a hundred meters to the control room, and we might run into Mesarthrim engineers."

=====Nemuro Beach=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim reinforcements arrived under heavy Imperial Aerospace bombardment, only to meet General Au-Yueng's well-entrenched troops. With no air support, and the numbers against them, their superior technology only manages to even the odds--just barely.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"General, we can push through with an orbital bombardment. Are the freaks over us?" Au-Yueng's second-in-command refers to the Remnant--the mysterious, advanced race that suddenly allied itself with the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No, there's a Meroniri ship lurking near our Mars installations. Shadar and the Emperor want to chase them out. I'll get Burke on the horn," Cecil Au-Yueng swings his Behemoth away from the fighting and heads back out to sea, where an Imperial Whale transport acts as a local communications center. "Get me Admiral Michael Burke."

=====Tokyo Silos=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nighthawk's Omega team arrives at the silo command center unnoticed. Mesarthrim forces had discovered the Imperial silos after they took Japan, and their engineers were busy trying to force their way through the software security measures.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lavik Nighthawk decided to dispense with the stealth measures, and went in firing. Omega Agents sprayed the control center with automatic weapon rounds and laser fire, killing engineers and soldiers without mercy or discrimination.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Resistance was short-lived. All Mesarthrim personnel are armed, but engineers only carry light side arms, practically worthless against the combat armor worn by Omega Agents. Two of Nighthawk's agents are gunned down by Mesarthrim soldiers before the Mesarthrim are silenced altogether.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Jones, arm all warheads. Give us a fifteen minute window, and set this room to blow if anyone tries to tamper with your work," Nighthawk orders. "The rest of you, check these stiffs for useable ammo, then prep for evac."

=====Nemuro Beach=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before General Au-Yueng can call Michael Burke for an orbital bombardment, Emperor Black recalls the Sixth Guard. "Get back to our northern garrison at best possible speed." he orders.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial troops dash for the sea. Infantry even drop their weapons in their hurry to escape. The skies are blackened by Imperial and Mesarthrim fighters. The Mesarthrim seize the initiative in the confusion, and gun down hundreds of Imperial troops.

=====Tokyo=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim air control is occupied with directing fighter reinforcements to Nemuro. They fail to notice a small transport helicopter slipping into the normal civilian traffic pattern. The Omega Agents are soon safely on their way.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The final cost to the Empire is grave. Over a thousand soldiers of the Sixth Guard have been killed, and two highly trained Omega Agents have been lost. Moments after the final pullout, hundreds of nuclear warheads detonate in their silos, a mere few hundred meters underground.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Explosions rip through the ground, toppling buildings and tearing the landscape to ribbons. For some time, tremors shake the Japanese islands to their core. The final consequences remain to be seen.

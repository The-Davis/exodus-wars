'''Ralek''' are a large herd creature native to [[Lorithan]]. The largest herds were found in the Plains of Zysk prior to the [[Burning Wars]]. They now exist in limited numbers only in the [[Dzarning Mountain Range]], or on the world of [[Ipsoff]].

== Events of 1086BC ==

[[Raegos Direnson]] returns to Rolsucht, leading a delegation of [[Juns]]. The [[Juns]] and the [[Reigens]] are peacefully united under [[Raegos Direnson|Raegos]]' rule, and the [[Reigess Suverände]] begins to spread across [[Junheim]].

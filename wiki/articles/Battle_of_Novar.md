The Battle of Novar was the last major naval battle of the [[Tempest War]]. It took place two days after the remnants of the [[United Centusi States|Centusi Government]] approached [[Arnold Stadt|Hohe Gouverneur Stadt]] informing him of their intent to negotiate a conditional surrender.

Newly appointed Admiral [[Oswald Voss]] sent convoys to each Centusi stronghold to ensure a smooth transition to peace. When the convoy arrived in the [[Honis System]] they received an emergency short range transmission from [[Novar]] informing them that [[Operation White Lion]] had failed and the surviving [[Prefecture Marine Corps|Marines]] and [[Black Watch|Schwarze Bewachung]] were surrounded by insurgents and ghost elements of the Centusi Heer.

==Initial Contact==
Captain Kasher von Stadt was in command of the newly launched [[Ziegler Class Heavy Cruiser|Ziegler Heavy Cruiser]], [[Ziegler]]. His convoy included the [[Deitrich]] and three [[Twilight Vessels]]. After arriving in the Honis System, they received an emergency short range transmission from alliance forces. Operation White Lion had been launched in early December [[2334]], and was essentially an occupational force for a population that was clearly loyal to the Centusi Government.

Novar was supposed to be protected by the [[HMS Syr Darya|Syr Darya]] and her escorts, however they were nowhere to be seen. Wasting no time, Captain Kasher made a bee line towards Novar, quickly releasing drop pods onto the surface to support the soldiers on the ground. In addition, the Ziegler released her heavy drop ship with an entire company of tanks and planetary aircraft on board. In that instant, the Ziegler and Deitrich came under long-range missile attack. The surviving elements of the Centusi [[United Centusi States Warfleet|Krieg Flotte]] had been laying in ambush.

==Ambush==
Kasher ordered his ships to break orbit, and to move on the largest Centusi ship, the [[Stormgod Class Battlecruiser|Krieg-Handwerk]]. The problem was, two of his three Twilight vessels were equipped as carriers, and therefore carried no heavy weaponry. As the two fleets moved into [[blazer]] range of each other, more ships from the Centusi Krieg Flotte powered online, turning the fight into a 2 to 1 match.

Undeterred, Kasher continued into the gauntlet - knowing he had an entire wave of fighters and bombers, and the Centusi had almost nothing. Almost instantly, Kasher's convoy passed into the crossfire 
of the Krieg Flotte, and the [[Saighdiúir]] broke apart. Both the Ziegler and the Dietrich were forced to split their fire onto the smaller Centusi vessels. The withering assault of the new ''AG'' Heavy Blazers tore the old [[Sickle class Cruiser|Sickle Cruisers]] to shreds. After both sides entered blazer range, the battle turned to blood. One of the [[Clansguard Class Destroyer|Clansguard Destroyers]] made a run on the Dietrich, trying to ram her. The Dietrich successfully punched through the small ship, blowing it apart - however, its remaining hulk slammed into the ventral side, destroying the Dietrichs' heavy gun mounts, and exposing the crew to space. The CIC, buried deep in the forward section of the ship, was crushed - killing Captain Fohr, only a week after the ship had been launched. Within minutes the crippled ship fell under assault by Centusi boarding teams.

==Heavy Losses==
With the Dietrich being boarded and two of the Twilight vessels destroyed, Captain Kasher signaled Admiral [[Friedrich Ahrens|Ahrens]] his intention to surrender. Ahrens messaged back his acceptance of their surrender, and moved the Krieg-Handwerk into position to begin boarding procedures. As the Krieg-Handwerk approached, a single bomber sat directly in its path, and at the last possible moment armed his payload.

The resulting [[Omega Bomb|Omega detonation]] swallowed the Krieg-Handwerk whole. The Dietrich sat precariously close to the event threshold, and the [[Daingean]] was forced to lock tow cables onto the ship and drag it to safety. The Ziegler was able to successfully able to use its shuttles to deploy gravity wells to hold in the rift, and allow it to fully close in a reasonable amount of time. With the destruction of the Krieg-Handwerk, the Krieg Flotte was reduced to two [[Rächer Class Battlecruiser]], one [[Sickle class Cruiser]], and one [[Clansguard Class Destroyer]]. They fled for deep space and the safety of GRLD. The [[Sickle class Cruiser]] was under assault by a boarding team from the Ziegler when it jumped to GRLD. A couple days later the ship returned to [[Mesar]] under the command of the assault team.

==Conclusion==
After the Krieg Flotte disappeared, the remaining Ziegler assault shuttle began shipping assault troopers to the Dietrich to assist in taking back the vessel from the Centusi invaders. Unable to handle all the surviving fighters and bombers, many were forced to the planet, where safe landing zones were not necessarily made apparent, and some pilots and crews became prisoners of the insurgence.

The Ziegler also began shipping its remaining shipboard armed forces to the surface using its high capacity drop ship. The sudden influx of troops allowed the allied forces to secure much of the outlying territory. The new ground war was dubbed [[Operation Lion's Pride]].

'''Ramesh Jethro'''
*Place of Birth: [[Torimur]]
*Allegiance: The [[Conclave]]

''Ramesh Jethro'' was a commander in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] until his capture by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]. Jethro escaped during the [[Mesarthrim Civil War]], and joined the [[Conclave]]. When [[Lucius Black]] surrendered the [[Conclave]] to the [[Meroniri]], he was sent to a [[Meroniri]] prison planet.

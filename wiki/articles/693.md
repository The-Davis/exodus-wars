== Events of 693AD ==

The [[Ixoarchada]] complete their first interstellar communications network.  Instantaneous communications between worlds many light-years apart revolutionizes [[Ixoachada]] society.  Inter-system commerce takes off, popular culture becomes more homogenized, and interdependence amongst far-flung [[Iridi]] increases several-fold.

End of the [[Yprian Epoch]] in [[Ixoarchada]] history, beginning of the [[Marros Epoch]].

The '''Caledon System''' consists of the star XX, the XX planets, the moons, and the asteroids. 

In order of their distances from XX, the planets are XX, XX, Iurin, [[Caledon]], XX, XX. XX planets are orbited by moons. The planets are named from words in the [[Gaelic]] language.

==Stellar Navigation==

XX is the Caledon System's only star. XX is a class G3O yellow dwarf. The Caledon System is located in the [[Milky Way]] galaxy, in the [[Orion Arm]]. Caledon is part of the [[Pelagrim Sector]], and is a close neighbor of the [[Centus System]] and the [[Tirshan System]].

==Orbital Bodies==
The inner Caledon System is contains the terrestrial planets. Only [[Caledon]] is inhabitable.

===Iurin===
[[Iurin]] (0.8AU) is a hot, boiling planet, at the pole, a secret [[Auellal League|Auellal]] training facility was built.  The facility now serves as a research facility for [[Fireline Industries]].

===Caledon===
[[Caledon]] (1.3AU) is the inhabitable terrestrial planet in the Caledon System. It has one moon, Beiyn.


==Discovery and Exploration==

The Caledon System was one of the first worlds colonized by the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] when they began expanding outwards from the [[Centus System]]. It served as a prison colony for dissidents, then became the capital of the [[Auellal League]] when they broke away from the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]].

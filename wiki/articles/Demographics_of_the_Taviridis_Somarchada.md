===Languages===

Most [[Ixoarchada]] can read and write in [[English]], and many can speak it in a broken fashion. There is only one [[Ixoarchada]] language family, but there are many variants which are used by the different castes, and also varies from place to place. The common language is [[Ilinaul]], which has some Germanic-Nordic syllable structure. Depending on which system, planet, and area one is located, the dialects can change substantially, but they are all mutually understandable, for the most part. 

The second major language in the family is [[Selilinaul]], a more superfluous and elevated dialect, used in politics and the courts. Only politicians, judges, and officials use it, and even then only in court or other high offices. It is also used amongst those in the religious caste and in religious rites and services.

The final, and most dramatically different language is [[Barilinaul]], spoken almost exclusively by the warrior caste. All warriors can understand all three languages, but speak to one another strictly in [[Barilinaul]]. Most linguists believe that [[Barilinaul]] is the closest modern relative of the original [[Ixoarchada]] language, which would have been a dialect of the [[Anthronoris]] tongue.  It does share some characteristics with the traditional [[Meroniri]] language, [[Lomena]].

The written form of the [[Ilinaul]] language family is a character-based alphabet with a total of thirty-six symbols, a handful of which represent sounds that do not exist in [[Terran]] languages.

[[English]] is used by merchants and the military mainly, and by upper level diplomats, though not by anyone in the general populace. Because of the widespread use of bioneural interfaces and various implants, any [[Ixoarchada]] can learn to read and write nearly any language.  Speaking it is more difficult.

Because of their ties with the [[Mesarthrim]], a few [[Ixoarchada]] officials speak [[German]]. A similar number--those whose duties require them to deal with the [[Prefecture]]--speak [[Latin]].

Most traders know the [[Ikronin]] [[Sothark]] language, and a few know the [[Lorithos]] [[Huorak]] dialect.

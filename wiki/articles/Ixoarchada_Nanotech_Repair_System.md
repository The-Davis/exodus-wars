The '''Nanotech Emergency Repair System''' employed by the [[Ixoarchada]] is a complex, multi-layered approach to dealing with systems damage during battle.  Used in capital ships, fighters, and planetary vehicles, the System counteracts localized systems failures by re-integrating components, bypassing damaged subsystems, and sealing broken casings.  The system uses spider-sized robots to quickly navigate equipment ducts in large warships in order to deliver nanobots to the precise problem zone.  The larger nanobots transmit data on damage to the damage control system of the ship's [[Strolza Entity]], where decisions are quickly made on what action needs to be taken.

The Nanotech Emergency Repair System is more tightly integrated with the systems themselves in small vessels, like fighters and tanks, and is actually built in to more failure prone equipment, even on capital ships.

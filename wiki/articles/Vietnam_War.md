The [[Terran]] conflict known as the '''Vietnam War''' began as a rebellion against the [[France (Terra)|French]] occupation of French Indochina by the [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnamese]] rebel Ho Chi Minh and his Viet Minh.

Minh's [[Communist]] loyalties were strongly opposed by the French colonists and the native majority of [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]], who were well-treated and afforded all the rights of full citizenship in [[France (Terra)|France]]. Minh's civil bid for power failed, and he resorted for armed conflict, which ultimately failed.

The Vietnam War did result in the independence of [[Vietnam (Terra)|Vietnam]], but ended any hope for [[Communism]] in the region.

== Events of 87BC ==

Construction of the second [[Ixoarchada]] starship, the ''[[Aurios]]'' is completed and lands on [[Dellada]].  The ''[[Aurios]]'' is larger, carrying a crew of 30, and like the ''[[Saigos]]'' remains on the moon's surface as the first manned station.

'''Law Enforcement''' is a civilian role in the [[Colonial Commonwealth|Commonwealth]] government. If a member of the [[Military of the Colonial Commonwealth|military]] wishes to serve as a [[:Category:Law Enforcement Officers|law enforcement officer]], they must resign first.

== Commonwealth Law Enforcement ==

All law enforcement in the Commonwealth answers to the Commonwealth Police Oversight Committee. The Oversight Committee runs the professional standards board, certifies would-be officers, conducts investigations into allegations of corruption, and operates as a liaison with international law enforcement agencies. The head of the Oversight Committee is known as the Chief Commissioner.

== Planetary Law Enforcement ==

At a planetary level, the police of the Commonwealth are organized into Bureaus of Investigation. [[Ah Kin]], [[Theia]], [[Rhea]], [[Bethija]] and [[Kalmar]] each have their own Bureau of Investigation.

Each Bureau is responsible for maintaining public and confidential police records, training and operating forensics for urban and rural police, training and operating a detective bureau for urban and rural police, and organizing court appearances for police personnel and any witnesses for the Commonwealth government. A Bureau Chief is assigned to each planetary Bureau.

== Colonial Police Service ==

The Colonial Police Service is in charge of law enforcement operations on a local level for urban and suburban communities. They handle patrol, initial criminal investigations, and respond to emergency calls reported by Commonwealth citizens.

== Colonial Marshal Service ==

The Colonial Marshal Service is responsible for rural law enforcement operations. They are typically in light aircraft and patrol from the skies. Given their environment, they are most frequently involved in civilian search and rescue operations, so their training is often called on by their urban counterparts for disaster relief and recovery.

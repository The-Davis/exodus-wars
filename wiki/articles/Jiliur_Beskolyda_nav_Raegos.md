''Jiliur Beskolyda nav Raegos'' (born November 2, 2296) was the Fenig, or crown prince, of the [[Reigens]]. He was one of the brightest hopes of the Reigen people. He was noble, courteous, and brave, but humble and willing to lend aid to even the lowest of the downtrodden.  He fought for the [[Lorithos]] in the [[Volucris War]], and died on the battlefield.  He was succeeded by his younger brother Leith nav Raegos as Fenig.

== Birth and Early Life ==

Jiliur was the firstborn of [[Lorrodi Raegos]] and [[Viveka Slanj nee Raegos]].  He was born under the star of Brave Raab, which the [[Reigens]] believe is a sign of great deeds and courage.  Jiliur was blessed in his cradle by the high priest of Hallowed Siran, a long-standing tradition in the house of Raegos.

When he was five years old, Lorrodi Raegos selected the Skald Einar to be Jiliur's tutor.  Jiliur studied Reigen history, courtly behavior, and received a classic education under Einar and an army of other instructors.  Einar later said "''Knowing Jiliur in those early years was an honor I will never forget.  He was a bright boy, but more notably he had an air of calm charisma.  By the time he was twelve, I think he could have led our world by force of personality alone.''"

== Combat Training ==

In [[2316]], Jiliur was placed under the command of [[Sigvatr Kurlson]] for military training.  Lorrodi Raegos had received news of the rise of the [[Onyx Empire]] and its aggressive military campaign, and he wanted his son ready to face any military threat.

Kurlson was a veteran of the [[Heritage Crisis]], and trained Jiliur in many combat skills, including vehicle operations and flight school.  Jiliur was a competent pilot, but his strength was in infantry fighting.  Jiliur was a gifted sniper and skirmisher, and one of the best fire team leaders in the Reigen military.

When Jiliur graduated from his training and received an officer's commission in the Reigen military, he was given a gift by his father: Reigenjord, the armor of the Raegos family, a powerful suit of combat armor that had been upgraded and refined through the ages, but with components that dated back to the days of [[Raegos Direnson]] himself.  Jiliur's mother had a special infantry weapon made for her son: Korfangr, named for the sword of Raab, was designed around two beam weapon technologies, giving Jiliur an edge over standard combat weapons.

By the end of the [[Second Exodus War]] in [[2324]], Jiliur nav Raegos was recalled to the palace honor guard, and his combat training was over.  Jiliur began special tutelage under his father, learning the wisdom of the Raegos family so he could ascend the throne on his thirty-fifth birthday.

== Uneasy Prince ==

When the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] signed a trade agreement with the Reigens, Lorrodi Raegos held a grand celebration in their honor.  Distinguished guests from every known nation in the galaxy attended, including the [[Meroniri]].  It was during this party that Jiliur met the infamous prisoner of the Meroniri; [[Lucius Black]], the fallen emperor.  While talking with Black, Jiliur realized how dissatisfied he was with life at home.  A few weeks later, he requested leave to begin a sabbatical from the kingdom.  Lorrodi gave his consent, and Jiliur left.

Jiliur sojourned on many worlds, seeing sights that he had read about but never witnessed himself.  He saw the war-torn damage of [[Earth]], and was moved, but then he saw the complete devastation of [[Lorithan]].  The prince spent several years travelling on Lorithan, delivering water purifiers, air filters, and food to as many backwater villages as he could.  Everything he did, he paid for out of his own pocket.  The Lorithos began to tell stories of "the Traveller", a man who helped the needy without thought of compensation or reward.

When the Volucris came to Lorithan, Jiliur slipped out on one of the last public flights, and returned to [[Junheim]] with word of the impending doom for the Lorithos.  He delivered [[Dirken Huern]]'s call for aid directly to Lorrodi Raegos, and begged to lead an army into the [[Mirdek System]] on behalf of the Lorithos.

== The Volucris War ==

Lorrodi Raegos gave his consent, though he warned his son that a heavy burden was laid across his heart. Lorrodi believed that if Jiliur went to war, he would never return.  Lorrodi was a good man, and believed that doing the right thing was more important than life itself.  Jiliur was sent to Lorithan with a third of the Reigen military at his disposal, and with his father's blessing.  Many Reigen heroes accompanied Jiliur into the crucible of the [[Volucris War]], and the few that returned became legends.

== Death ==

In August of [[2334]], Jiliur Raegos fought one of the fiercest battles in the [[Volucris War]] to protect thousands of caravans of Lorithos refugees.  Thousands of Reigens and coalition troops died in the battle, as did over a million civilians, but nearly sixty million were safely evacuated. In honor of his sacrifice, Jiliur Raegos was made an honorary [[Shadan Nahru]] by the Lorithos, and he was posthumously named a [[Hishai]], an extremely rare honor.

== Legacy ==

When Jiliur's body was returned to Junheim, he was laid in state with all the honors of a Kejsare.  Even though he never ascended the throne, and never had an heir, he was added to the line of kings, with the full blessing of his father and brother.  Jiliur's brother Leith composed a skaldic verse in Jiliur's honor.  Lorrodi commanded the court skald, Seil Helden nav Riimar, who had accompanied Jiliur to Lorithan to compose an epic tale of his son's final days.

Jiliur is remembered in Reigen history as one of the mightiest and most beloved leaders since Raegos Direnson himself, and will not soon be forgotten.

One Jiliur Beskolyda, son of Lorrodi, the great king.<br>
None can deny their worth, for Raegos is among their fathers.<br>
Hear now his mighty tale.

In years of unmeasured peace and plenty, Lorrodi did reign.<br>
He took to wife one Viveka, a maiden of the House Slanj.<br>
Their union was one of bliss, and brought forth many noble heirs.<br>
None so great as Jiliur.

When the time came for Jiliur to ascend the Reigen throne,<br>
He instead walked among the stars and searched for his destiny.<br>
A great war was brewing, and Jiliur heard the call of the drums.<br>
The mighty Jiliur never returned.

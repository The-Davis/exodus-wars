March of the Legions - 7/3/2325 - By James Davis

Ueda
     General Carpenter and his command staff are all assembled on Ueda, in full dress regalia. Assembled in parade formations is the full might of the Legion Corps, the Army of the Prefecture.
     Since the Battle of Terra, marking the end of the Second Exodus War, four Legions have been actively deployed across Prefecture space.
     The ideal strategy would send a Legion to respond to an outside threat and reinforce a garrison. Until the invasion by the United Centusi States, this has not been put to a true test. The Volucris invade and leave in a day.
     Now there are ten Legions fully prepared for war. Enough trained men remain for another five Legions are available, remnants of the old Royal Imperial Army, but there is not enough modern powered armor or weaponry available.
     The parade assembles each Legion under its Lieutenant General and Brigadier, and packs them off into their appropriate transports to be sent to deployment zones.
     The unspoken question remains: the Prefecture has enough troops to fight the Mesarthrim, but without the ability to move them from world to world in time, how can they win?

Semper Fidelis - 7/5/2325 - By James Davis

     The greatest weakness of the Prefecture's invasion methods is the lack of an appropriately-designed landing force. Lacking adequate facilities, the Prefecture Legion Corps must perform the equivalent of a crash landing into a hot zone and deploy under fire.
     Impressed with the success of the Mesarthrim Myt'herra, the Prefecture decide to bring their Marine elements back from their Fleet deployments and re-form the Marine Corps. Leading the Corps is Brigadier William Duke, the now ancient first generation colonist of Blackis and a veteran of the original United States Marine Corps.
     The role of the Corps is not only to act as a defense mechanism for the Fleet, but to establish beach-heads for the Legions, and to act as a first response to military threats.

No More Retreats - 7/12/2325 - By James Davis

     The first significant advance of Prefecture technology since the Phoenix Project is unveiled with the Norfolk Project. New drive technologies are developed, first in the Seminole Class Destroyer.
     For the first time, Prefecture warships can make multiple jumps without lengthy recharge periods to their Kovalski-Fahr drives, thanks to new antimatter reactor technology.
     For now, the technology is severely limited by the lack of antimatter readily available to the Prefecture, but enough is in place to equip the Marine Corps reactionary force to jump into any point in Prefecture territory at any time.
     Prefecture trade agreements are in the works with various factions to gather sources of antimatter, but for now Meronir's Republic, the largest source of antimatter in the known galaxy, refuses to sell its sources to anyone.


A Glimpse of the Boot - 7/15/2325 - By James Davis

     Marine Boot Camp is a short affair for Marines who were already in the service of the Navy, a simple refresher course in the "rule of three" organization tables and reassigning them to correct ranks as opposed to the Navy's system.
     For new recruits, volunteers ready to fill the ranks and requirements of the new force, the rigors of boot camp are a lot greater. Exercise alone is no great problem for most recruits--modern medicine has worked wonders for fitness and general health. Instead, the greatest hurdle is learning to have the heart for killing. Drill sergeants go to great length to turn ordinary citizens into killing machines, something that has been drilled into the Mesarthrim since birth, and into the majority of the Prefecture's soldiers since the early days of the Empire.


Who Gave you Permission to die, Marine? - 7/18/2325 - By James Davis

     The United Centusi States Myt'herra give the Prefecture Marine Corps their first trial by fire when a brigade of Myt'herra land on Tejat, attacking several reactor component factories.
     Six newly-built Seminole Class Destroyers and a Marine Corps Amphibious Assault Cruiser are deployed to Tejat, driving off the small Mesarthrim fleet elements and landing the Marines. In a very brief bloody battle, the Myt'herra recon force is captured.
     Defying a long standing order, Brigadier Duke allows the Myt'herra to live as P.O.Ws, and has his orders approved by the high command back on Ueda. The standing order against allowing surrendering troops to live is lifted.
     Several low-ranking Myt'herra are sent back to Mesar. Brigadier Duke hopes to gain the trust of the Myt'herra at the very least, allowing the strange war-bonds of mutual respect among warriors to grown.


Blood Cries out for Blood - 8/2/2325 - By James Davis
     In the months since the Mesarthrim seized control of the Laojiu District, food has become a more precious commodity than before. Five major food worlds are under control of the United Centusi States, and they refuse to sell to any source they believe will allow it to get back to Prefecture worlds.
     Fortunately, the Icronians have means to secure supplies the Mesarthrim have no way of stopping. Convoys of food are sent from Icronian sources to Prefecture worlds, sold at the smallest possible profit margins, exactly enough to cover costs and risks. The Icronians are always commerce-oriented, but take their new alliance with the Prefecture seriously.
     A convoy of seventeen freighters and a single cruiser carrying food supplies to Tavou is ambushed by a Mesarthrim carrier and bombers. Only one freighter survives to reach Prefecture Fleet elements in Tavou space.
     Up until now, the only Icronian casualties in the Tempest War have been Gate technicians who refused to surrender. This is the first direct attack on Icronian interests that did not have some strategic element in a specific invasion. The Icronians are furious, sending the Far Runner and a task force of twelve destroyers into Mesarthrim-held territory, ambushing and capturing thirty freighters full of food being sent to Mesar from Laojiu.

The northern region of Huon Jehz is the only portion of the continent with cities which survived the [[Burning Wars]] with enough structures intact to make rebuilding worthwhile, and is home to a third of the planet's population. The [[Kihtor Modar]] anchor is in the ancient city of [[Hamur Hauren]], birthplace of the [[Huern Drastar]]. [[Kihtor Modar]] and [[Hamur Hauren]] both serve as an industrial center for [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]], and the center of interstellar trade for the people of Lorithan. Huon Jehz also has the cities of [[Zysk]], [[Rehzak]], [[Oln Zaur]], and [[Plovirsk]]. Each city is domed to protect the population from the global nuclear winter and toxic pools in the region. Recovery efforts so far have allowed the cities to expand their coverage to several dozen square kilometers, but the citizens still face a bleak future for many generations to come. The dark remains of the [[Talire Hauren]] is also in the Huon Jehz region, a macabre memorial to the old ways from before the [[Burning Wars]].

== History ==

Huon Jehz was the cradle of [[Lorithos]] civilization. In [[1004BC]], [[Hamur Huern]] became the ruler of a port city in the northwest part of the region. Years later, it was renamed [[Huern]] in his honor.

[[Hamur Huern]] sought to carve out a civilization for the [[Lorithos]], and end the barbaric ways that they had followed in the millenia since the [[Sundering]]. In the first year of his compaign, he conquered the neighboring city-states of [[Rehzak]] and [[Oln Zaur]], and united them into the first major nation of the [[Lorithos]].

== People and culture ==

Huon Jehz is mostly populated by the [[Lorithos]]. There are some [[Terrans]] and [[Meroniri]] in the region, but they rarely visit the surface and mostly stay in the [[Kihtor Modar]].

== Geography ==

[[Image:Regional Map of Huon Jehz.png|350px|right|thumb| Map of Huon Jehz.]]
Huon Jehz is a mostly low-land region. It was horribly scarred by the [[Burning Wars]]. Prior to the devastation, it was filled with verdant forests, plains and farmland, and was the pride of the [[Lorithos]].

=== Major Sub-Regions ===

Prior to the [[Burning Wars]], there were dozens of thriving biospheres and ecosystems across the Huon Jehz region. Most of them have been blasted into an unrecognizeable haze of wastelands.

==== Zaur Peninsula ====

The Zaur Peninsula is the far northwest region of Huon Jehz, and is the home of the first city-states united by [[Hamur Huern]] during his campaign. As Huon Jehz is the cradle of [[Lorithos]] civilization, the Zaur Peninsula is the cradle of Huon Jehz civilization.

==== The Plains of Zysk ====

The Plains of Zysk are a series of rolling flatlands, and were the site of several major early engagements in the [[Burning Wars]]. It was once home to the enormous [[Ralek]] herds, which now exist in limited numbers only in the [[Dzarning Mountain Range]], or on the world of [[Ipsoff]]. The former capital, [[Talire Hauren]], was located in the southern shores of the Plains. The city of [[Zysk]] is still intact in the western part of the region.

=== Cities ===

Aside from the five major cities, Huon Jehz has several smaller towns, villages and settlements scattered throughout the region, particularly in the Zaur Peninsula.

==== Hamur Hauren ====
Main Article: [[Hamur Hauren]]

[[Hamur Hauren]] is the capital city of [[Lorithan]] and of [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]]. During the reign of [[Talire Huern]], the capital was moved to [[Talire Hauren]], but since its destruction [[Hamur Hauren]] has resumed its traditional role.

==== Kihtor Modar ====
Main Article: [[Kihtor Modar]]

While not technically a city, [[Kihtor Modar]] is anchored in the center of the city of [[Huern]], and is the largest population center in all of [[Lorithan]].

==== Zysk ====
Main Article: [[Zysk]]

[[Zysk]] is a major domed city, home to thousands of [[Lorithos]].

==== Rehzak ====
Main Article: [[Rehzak]]

[[Rehzak]] is a major domed city, home to thousands of [[Lorithos]].

==== Oln Zaur ====
Main Article: [[Oln Zaur]]

[[Oln Zaur]] is a major domed city, home to thousands of [[Lorithos]]. The Zaur Peninsula is named after this city.

==== Plovirsk ====
Main Article: [[Plovirsk]]

[[Plovirsk]] is a major domed city, home to thousands of [[Lorithos]].

== Notable Residents ==

[[Dirken Huern]] and [[Sanor Cai]] are the most famous residents of the area, due to their positions of leadership within the [[Rikaz o Fii Huern iv Lorithan]].

== Natural Resources ==

Since the destruction wrought by the [[Burning Wars]], no natural resources have been harvested from the Huon Jehz region.

=== Flora ===

All natural-growing flora in the Huon Jehz region were wiped out by the [[Burning Wars]]. There are none remaining except in gardens within the domed cities.

=== Fauna ===

[[Ralek]] are a large herd creature native to [[Lorithan]]. The largest herds were found in the Plains of Zysk prior to the [[Burning Wars]]. They now exist in limited numbers only in the [[Dzarning Mountain Range]], or on the world of [[Ipsoff]].

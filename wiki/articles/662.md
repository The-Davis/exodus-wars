== Events of 662AD ==

The survey mission, now joined by archaeological teams, manages to open the door to the mysterious chamber on [[Tolesca|Tolesca's]] moon without damaging it.  The teams discover the door leads to another chamber, and that both chambers are part of a vast complex dated to approximately 20,000BC.  Ultimately, this place would come to be called the [[Fauril Konisin]], or the [[Ancestor’s Chamber]].  It was a communications station for the [[Anthronoris]].  

A vast amount of equipment, computers, and other devices are found, but almost nothing still operational.  However, at the heart of the massive installation is the device that proved to be one of the most important artifacts ever discovered by the [[Ixoarchada]], and which would ultimately revolutionize [[Ixoarchada]] society.  The [[Nechanos Tolesca]], or [[Tolesca Device]], was a Pre-Sundering era communications array used by the [[Anthronoris]].  Its significance is almost immediately recognized and many of the premier [[Ixoarchada]] minds arrive by the years end to begin more than two decades of decoding the secrets of the machine.

The Vigilant Class Frigate was one of the best-known sources of [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] power throughout their conquered territories. While there were significantly more powerful [[:Category:Warships|warships]] in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]'s arsenal, there were none as numerous. 

=== History ===

[[Image:Vigilant Class Frigate Gun View.png|150px|left|thumb|A view of the Vigilant's main gun.]]
[[Robert Marshall]] was in charge of determining the mission profiles for ships destined to serve in the [[Royal Imperial Navy]]. He delivered multiple design specifications to the [[Royal Imperial Labs]], and work began almost immediately in [[2300]].

The [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] was the first prototype [[:Category:Warships|warship]] to be built by [[Royal Imperial Labs]].

The [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]]'s design team faced many challenges in the first year. While they had grand plans for the [[Capricorn Haven|''Capricorn Haven'']] shipyard, it was not completed when work began on the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]]. The workers assembled the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] in unprotected orbit of [[Alpha Centauri System|Proxima Centauri]], and many suffered from radiation poisoning of varying degrees.

[[Image:Vigilant Class Frigate Top View.png|150px|left]]
The design specification called for a fast [[:Category:Frigates|frigate]] design which could be constructed in five months or less, with at least one powerful main gun. [[Robert Marshall]]'s studies of captured [[United Earth Alliance]] [[:Category:Warships|warships]] led him to believe that the [[Onyx Empire]] was at a great disadvantage against the technologically-superior [[United Earth Alliance Space Agency]], and that fighting them on their own terms would lead to a disaster. While [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] requested several heavier [[:Category:Warships|warship]] designs, his main strategy for the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] centered around the "[[Lane of Fire]]" concept. [[Robert Marshall|Marshall]] envisioned multiple small, fast [[:Category:Frigates|frigates]] setting up "fire lanes" of concentrated firepower, which could quickly be moved from place to place, while heavier [[:Category:Destroyers|destroyers]] engaged the enemy directly and forced them into the "kill zone". This strategy later proved so successful against the [[United Earth Alliance]] that many hailed [[Robert Marshall]] as a tactical genius.

[[Image:Vigilant Class Frigate Side View.png|150px|left]]
The road to developing the Vigilant Class Frigate was full of mis-steps and potholes. The [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] hull was completed on schedule, but it was basically an empty shell. None of the specialized systems designed to turn it from a spaceworthy vessel into a fighting [[:Category:Warships|warship]] had been developed on schedule. The design program was put on hold in late [[2300]] until the [[Capricorn Haven|''Capricorn Haven'']] and development of the main systems could be completed.

In late [[2300]], enough of the [[Capricorn Haven|''Capricorn Haven'']] was completed to move the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] project into its protective drydock. The project was now much safer, allowing routine EVA inspections of the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]]'s hull.

Additionally, [[Royal Imperial Labs]] created [[Tolwin Power Incorporated]] to produce and develop the fission reactors initially designed by the laboratories for the [[HMS Vigilant|FG-1]] project.

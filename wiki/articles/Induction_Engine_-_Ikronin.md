Drives For centuries the [[Ikronin]] have used fusion drives as their main source of propulsion to move their vessels at sub-light speeds.

Over that period of time, efforts to tweak and enhance the performance of their fusion drives has taken many forms from highly refined heavy fusion pellets to experimenting with ion particle/fusion hybrids. With the advent of more efficient and effective power generators there are two systems that will soon overtake the fusion drive as the most popular and common among the [[Ikronin]] people.

The first is the '''Induction Engine''', which utilizes artificial gravity to provide incredible thrust and maneuverability. The induction engine requires no fuel and produces no exhaust. It's ideal for atmospheric, orbital, or deep-space operations.

The design of the Induction Engine is currently better suited to shuttles, fighters, and smaller sub-capital vessels, as tests with larger hulls have produced less than the expected results, in terms of overall agility and the length of time it takes a vessel to achieve maximum velocity.

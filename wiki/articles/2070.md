== Events of 2070 ==

===January===

*January 19 - [[Lavik Nighthawk]] kills eight people, including his parents, in his first serial-style killing spree.
*January 23 - [[Torimur]] colonial officials arrest [[Lavik Nighthawk]].
*January 24 - After interviewing [[Lavik Nighthawk]], [[Torimur]] colonial officials arrest [[Oliver Nighthawk]].

===February===

*February 4 - [[Jarek Nighthawk]] testifies in [[Torimur]] court against [[Oliver Nighthawk]], claiming [[Lavik Nighthawk]] is the victim of abuse and sadistic torture.
*February 11 - [[Oliver Nighthawk]] is found guilty of conspiracy to commit murder. [[Lavik Nighthawk]] is found to be criminally insane and made a ward of the state.
*February 18 - [[Lavik Nighthawk]] is sent to the [[Torimur]] school for wards of the state, where he meets [[Lucius Black]].

'''New Zealand''' was a country in the south-western Pacific Ocean comprising two large islands (the North Island and the South Island) and numerous smaller islands, most notably Stewart Island/Rakiura and the Chatham Islands. New Zealand was part of the [[Commonwealth of Nations (Terra)|Commonwealth of Nations]] until it was dissolved into the [[United Earth Alliance]] on February 19, [[2011]].

The "ground pounders" are the backbone of any modern army. The Legion Corps is known for its [[:Category:Rigs|rigs]], but its infantry are of paramount importance to its tactics and the way it wages war.

== Training ==

Infantry training begins with the cornerstone of the Legion Corps: six months of Basic Combat Training. Afterwards soldiers wishing to serve as infantry may attend several schools:
*'''Advanced Infantry Training School''' is the first course a soldier attends after being assigned to the infantry. It is a two month course during peace time, and a one month course in times of war.
*'''Airborne Infantry Training School''' is for trained infrantry wishing to earn paratrooper certification. The course lasts six weeks.
*'''Orbital Drop School''' instructs infantry on the use of orbital drop pods and their proper use. It is one of the few schools soldiers cannot be ordered to take: it is strictly volunteer only.
*'''Sniper School''' certifies infantry in the use of sniper weapons and the proper function of a sniper team.
*'''Infantry Officer Candidacy School''' allows soldiers to earn a commission as Officers if they complete the course. If they complete the course but do not earn a commission, they have the option of enrolling in a Military Academy, where they can earn an automatic commission upon graduation.

== Uniforms ==

[[Image:PrefectureLegionCorpsInfantryBadge.png|45px|left]]

The Legion Corps' Infantry wears the [[Prefecture Military Dress Uniform|dress uniform]] with a sword insignia for their right arm, the Legion Corps' emblem on their hat, and the emblem of their specific Legion for their left arm.

Infantry wear the [[Prefecture Military Field Combat Uniform|Field Combat Uniform]] with subdued Legion, name and rank patches. Most infantry use some form of powered armor.

== Weapon Systems and Equipment ==

Prefecture Legion Corps Infantry have several kits available to them depending on their station and mission profile.
*'''Light Infantry Kit''' - Light infantry are mainly used when the Guard is mobilized by the local government for law enforcement purposes. It is also used in early basic training. Light infantry do not have much use on the modern battlefield. Light infantry use [[M-03 Low Profile Armor - "Second Chance"|soft body armor]], a [[M-06 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle (SABR)|rifle]], a [[M-05 Pistol|pistol]], a [[M-04 Combat Knife|knife]], and carry one day's supplies with them.
*'''Medium Infantry Kit''' - Medium infantry are the most commonly-deployed infantry in the Legion Corps. Medium infantry use [[M-16 Power-Assisted Heavy Armor - "Excalibur"|powered armor]], a [[M-05 Pistol|pistol]], a [[M-04 Combat Knife|knife]], a minimum of four [[M-08 Fragmentation Grenade|grenades]], and carry three days' supplies with them. They have several primary weapons at their disposal, including the [[M-06 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle (SABR)|rifle]], [[M-09 Light Anti-Armor Weapon|LAAW]], or [[M-11 Squad Automatic Weapon|SAW]]. Alternatively, they can carry a a lighter [[M-07 Sub-Machine Gun (SMG)|SMG]] as their main weapon and be part of a crew-served weapon team, operating any one of several heavy weapons, including the [[M-13 Field Gun|field gun]] and the [[M-15 Crew-Portable Mortar (60mm)|60mm mortar]].
*'''Heavy Infantry Kit''' - Heavy infantry are often used in the same roles as [[:Category:Rigs|rigs]] or [[:Category:Tanks|tanks]]. They have the armor and firepower to go toe to toe with some of the nastiest weapons around. Heavy infantry use [[M-17 Mobile Exoskeletal Armor - "Grendel"|mobile exoskeletal armor]] commonly known as the "Grendel" suit. Grendel operators have a [[M-11 Squad Automatic Weapon|SAW]] as their main weapon, and their suit can carry two additional weapons in the shoulders. Grendel operators have their choice of any combination of [[M-18 Medium Anti-Armor Weapon|MAAWs]] or [[M-19 Exoskeletal-Portable Mortar (81mm)|81mm mortars]].
*'''Urban Warfare Kit''' - Urban warfare calls on infantry for several special roles that they do not perform in open battlefields. They may be called to pacify civilian mobs or fight in close quarters environments. Most infantry still use [[M-16 Power-Assisted Heavy Armor - "Excalibur"|powered armor]] in an urban environment, but are usually issued an [[M-07 Sub-Machine Gun (SMG)|SMG]] or a [[M-10 Shotgun|shotgun]] as their primary weapon, and have the option of carrying an [[M-26 Electric Control Weapon|electric control weapon]] or "Taser." In "live capture" environments, [[M-08d Flash-Bang Grenade|flash bangs]] are often considered an appropriate weapon as well.
*'''Sniper Kit''' - Snipers play a special role in all combat fields. While they are still organized in Fire Teams of four, snipers usually split that team into two-man groups, made up of a spotter and a gunner. The spotter will carry a [[M-06 Selectable Assault Battle Rifle (SABR)|rifle]] and a high-resolution scanner, while the gunner will carry a higher-powered [[M-14 Sniper Rifle|sniper rifle]]. Snipers have the option of using [[M-16 Power-Assisted Heavy Armor - "Excalibur"|powered armor]] or [[M-03 Low Profile Armor - "Second Chance"|soft body armor]], depending on their mission parameters.

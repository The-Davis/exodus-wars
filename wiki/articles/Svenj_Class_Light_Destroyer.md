===Overview===

Svenj Class Light Destroyers were built near the end of the [[Burning Wars]] by the forces of the would-be Emperor [[Torm Krelbine]].

[[Image:SvenjClassLightDestroyer1.jpg]]

The warlord nearly won the war with his fleet, but died in the last battle with [[Kian Huern]].

===Capabilities===

The main weapons of Svenj Light Destroyers are an array of four Heavy Blaster cannons, and sixteen Standard Blaster cannon turrets.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons. They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

Svenj Destroyers carry six Skrike missile launchers and two Sur-Skrike torpedo tubes.  These can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.  In the Burning War, the Svenj Destroyers in Torm Krelbine's navy carried massive nuclear payloads.

[[Image:SvenjClassLightDestroyer2.jpg]]

The Svenj is small, but still carries a fighter complement.  It can carry four squadrons of fighters, usually Vadries and Milroks, but carries no bomber squadrons.

With its Garvask Shields and Tzaiko Composite armor, the Svenj is well-defended against its enemies.  It's armor is and was tougher than that of the Blijrok, but it is slightly slower and less maneuverable.

===Deployment===

When the [[Meroniri Terinasi]] began helping the Lorithos people rebuild Lorithan, they also built up their defenses with technology the Lorithos could easily rebuild.  The Rikaz Lorithos built two fleets of warships--ninety-four total warships.

[[Image:SvenjClassLightDestroyer3.jpg]]

The ASTF mainstay warship is the Svenj.  With its tough combat abilities, high speed, and fighter capacity, it's an ideal choice for most situations.  The Lorithos ASTF currently fields thirty Svenj Light Destroyers.

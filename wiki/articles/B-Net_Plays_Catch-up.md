B-Net Plays Catch-Up - 3/6/2324 - by James Davis, Andrew Sears, Austin Bailey and Scott Sanburn

     The citizens of the galaxy have been in the dark about the current events of late, but we're glad to report that several influential Alliance of Planets Member States have dropped their proverbial "Iron Curtains," and let information flow freely again.

Terra
     The Terran Recovery Effort was stalled over the last week. A large portion of the funding from the United Mesarthrim Clans, and all of Mesarthrim Army's manpower was withdrawn from the effort. The problem was compounded by restlessness in the refugees from Asia, Europe and North America.
     Fortunately, a new director has been put in charge of the effort. By unanimous vote of the Alliance of Planets High Council, Josiah MacArthur Hlow--or Professor Hlow, as he prefers to be called--is in charge of the entire effort.
     Since the beginning of the recovery effort, refugees have been provided with free transport off-world, courtesy of the Federated Districts of the Prefecture and the United Mesarthrim Clans. The first thing Hlow did once in command was quarantine the planet--again with the approval of the Alliance of Planets High Council. His reasons are unknown, and classified top secret for now. This has caused a number of civil disturbances, but no full-blown riots yet.

Federated Districts of the Prefecture
     After the Battle of Mirach, the Prefecture Navy's Third and Fourth Fleets were heavily damaged, and grounded for overhaul. This leaves the Prefecture at 50% naval capacity.
     General Travis Carpenter was called to testify before the Prefecture Congress and military leaders on charges of breaching a binding treaty. He admitted it freely.
     Carpenter: "I had men to save. I violated a treaty through a technicality--if the Navy and Air Force had air superiority, Mirach would have been uninhabited. I make no excuses, I gave the order to bombard with full knowledge of the consequences."
     The military leaders--Anthony Hadrian, Robert Marshall and Jan Brooks--decided not court martial General Carpenter. Hanging such a fine General for a minor breach of a treaty was pointless. Instead they opted for administrative discipline, eight lashes in public. As soon as the strapping was done, Travis Carpenter addressed the Congress.
     Carpenter: "If you want me to win this war, you have to get rid of stupid rules like that. I need all the power we have, where it's needed, when it's needed. If you can't give it to me, give me my discharge so I can head as far from the Swarms as I can get.; With restrictions, you will lose.
     "I need more men, more machines, and more weapons. I need at least six more Legions. I need every Rig, tank, armor and weapon overhauled. I need the unconditional support of the Navy and the Air Force. I need our allies to advise and support us as much as possible. Give me all of this, and I'll win this war."
     The Prefecture Congress agreed to commission additional Legions, and the military leaders began an Army overhaul immediately.

Alliance of Planets
     After Carpenter's testimony, the Alliance of Planets High Council held a short session about the anti-bombardment clause of the Great Charter. After a few minutes of debate from all Member State representatives, a vote was taken.
     The clause was removed from the Charter at a five to one vote. Only Lorithos President and representative Dirken Huern voted against the alteration of the Charter.
     Dirken Huern: "I come from a world that's been ravaged by more bombardments than anywhere. Terra's nothing compared to the Lorithos homeworld. I can't vote in favor of this motion."

Volucris Swarms
     Prefecture and Archadian observation probes over Mirach have been watching the Volucris since their victory on Mirach. It's difficult to tell exactly what they're up to, but some sort of major excavation is underway. Several pits, multiple kilometers in diameter have been dug in several locations.
     Intelligence Officers in charge of watching the probes' feeds have been unable to identify any significant pattern or point to the digging, but they are certain that the Volucris swarm hasn't moved at all. Meroniri Military leaders are considering using a Mass-Energy Conversion bomb to take out the whole planet.

United Mesarthrim Clans
     Under the shaky rule of Uber-General Eric Francis, the United Mesarthrim Clans are beginning to unravel. While Francis is a competent military leader and a fine statesman, too many troops are shifting loyalty to one rebel faction or another, and others are simply deserting as the coffers of the Mesarthrim High Command dry up.
     Uber-General Francis has requested the aid and intervention of the Alliance of Planets. Unfortunately, such assistance requires independent Member State action. The Alliance of Planets only commands the Space Police--hardly suited for putting down such large scale revolts.
     Eric Francis: "As much as I hate admitting weakness, we're in dire straits. The renegade Commandants who formed the Allied Auellal League have us outgunned two to one. More soldiers desert every day--the piracy has drained our coffers dry."
     B-Net was unable to receive any official comment from an Auellal representative. Their leader, Commandant Steven Cue, has promised to speak with reporters at a later date. Apparently, the Allied Auellal League believes itself to be the legitimate government of Mesar--not the Mesarthrim High Command.
     The other rebel factions would not speak to B-Net representatives at all. The cities of Mesar and its colonies are a cesspool of riots and conflict. So far, B-Net has been unable to get an accurate count of the rebel factions, nor have we been able to determine their motives. Uber-General Francis would not comment on the numbers or the motives.

United Archadian Republics
     The Archadians had a few announcements of their own. For one, the declassification of the First Joint Defense Fleet (FJDF), funded and crewed by both Archadians and Mesarthrim. The FJDF is part of the Prometheus Project, which began during the Joint Technology Summit which was cut short by the unfortunate demise of Uber-General Andreas Tischler. The fleet is led by the currently-incomplete Prometheus.
     Another important development was the announcement by Drimont Mandara--President of the United Archadian Republics--that the Archadian people would back United Earth Uber-General Eric Francis against the multiple rebellions. While President Mandara did not specify the exact support, he did say "Political support, and military support if necessary." The Archadians currently have shared control of the Mesarthrim colony of Tincomalee, where the FJDF is being built.
     Support for the Terran Recovery Project has been offered by the United Archadian Republics, but Professor Hlow hasn't decided whether or not to allow them to come and go at will--currently, supplies must be shipped in by sealed spacecraft in order to avoid violating quarantine.

     In our previous publication, our reporters hadn't completed their articles on the Titanian Planetary Alliance, Meronir's Republic, or the latest Alliance of Planets High Council meeting. We apologize for the delay.

Titanian Planetary Alliance
     The Titanian Planetary Alliance leaders held a press conference with APNET reporters to discuss several issues in the galaxy. President Larry McNichols, Councilman Gary Hughes, Councilwoman Barbara Dannins, and General-Commander Scott Sanburn spoke with the representatives.
     President McNichols: "Good morning. The Titanian Planetary Alliance has made several decisions about the events unfolding in throughout the galaxy.
     "First is the quarantine of Terra. According to our contacts on Terra, there are good reasons behind it, but unfortunately we can't release them right now. We have a full Division of TDF troops there, but they are safe and will be allowed to leave as soon as they pass health inspections.
     "We're still accepting the asylum requests of refugees who have been allowed to leave. We're currently housing twelve million former residents of Terra in the city of New Dallas, and we're expanding the city-building projects on Titanian 6 and 7. We expect that eight new cities will be completed by the end of the year.
     "I fully support our vote to alter the anti-bombardment clause in the Great Charter. If there is sufficient threat, we must use any and all force necessary. We should have learned our lesson earlier, and the Prefecture Army is fortunate that General Carpenter was brave enough to defy an outdated law.
     "General-Commander Sanburn will brief us on the Volucris and United Mesarthrim Clans situations, and answer any questions about the strategic situation. General, you have the floor."
     General-Commander Sanburn: "First, I'd like to say that the death of my friend Uber-General Tischler was quite a shock. I didn't expect him to go like that.
     "Uber-General Eric Francis has asked for our help. The President and Council have approved, so the Second and Third Titanian Defense Force Fleets will be sent to assist the Mesarthrim. This is quite a commitment--fully 60% of our space forces--but the remaining First Fleet is enough to defend the Titanian system, and we're about to commission a Fourth Fleet from the Senior class of cadets from the TDF Academy.
     "The Volucris--or Bugs, as we of the military profession prefer to call them--are another matter. I'm not willing to commit any TDF forces to dealing with them until we know how to properly defend ourselves. I will now answer questions."
     Michelle Jarvens, APNET: "What do you think about Prefecture General Carpenter's violation of the Great Charter?"
     General-Commander Sanburn: "While I believe in the Charter, I disagreed with the strictness of the old clause. I'm glad it was altered. In times of war, we need to be able to use as much deadly force as necessary to win. When nuclear or similar weapons are needed, we should be able to use them. Personally, I'm still against viral and chemical warfare."
    Gordon Barclay, B-Net: "Can you tell us anything about the Terran situation?"
     General-Commander Sanburn: "No. I'm a little in the dark about all the details myself, and I'm not about to reveal any classified information that I do know. That's all the time I have for questions, I'm due on the Intervention in an hour. Good day."

Meronir's Republic
     The Meronir Ministry of Defense has also offered a cruiser to assist Uber-General Eric Francis and the United Mesarthrim Clans in putting down the rebellion.
     Meroniri Minister Derri Meninnon: "We will be sending a Tronict Class cruiser to help secure trade and supply routes to and from Centus and the other United Mesarthrim Clans territories. We will coordinate closely with the Titanian Defense Force and Mesarthrim High Command."

Alliance of Planets
     Shortly after amending the Charter, the Alliance of Planets High Council voted unanimously to dispatch the Space Police anti-piracy task force to the Centus starsystem to help the Mesarthrim regain control of the shipping lanes.
     Space Police Admiral Joshua Beson has mustered the task-force, and should have them all moved to the Mesarthrim territories in a day or two. Beson plans to work closely with the Titanian Defense Force and the Meroniri cruiser.

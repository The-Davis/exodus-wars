*Armament:
**4 Mkuki'kali Energy Lances
**4 Mshale'liga Missiles
*Communications System: Nyenyeka Communications Package
*Targeting and Tracking System: Kijicho Tracking Probe

===Overview===

Shujaa'kir aina Nyek'undu is the only fighter used by [[Ngrligru]] warriors. Others are piloted by slaves, or computer-controlled.

[[Image:Nyek'undu1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The Nyek'undu carries four Mkuki'kali Energy Lances--not as powerful as starship versions, but quite deadly.  They also carry four Mshale'liga Missiles, which rank approximately half-way between the destructive power of an antimatter missile and a Matter to Energy Conversion Bomb.

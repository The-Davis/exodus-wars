The '''Netherlands''' was a European Monarchy, bordered by the North Sea to the north and west, [[Belgium (Terra)|Belgium]] to the south, and [[West Germany (Terra)|West Germany]] to the east.

The Netherlands is a geographically low-lying and densely populated country. It is popularly known for its windmills, cheese, clogs (wooden shoes), delftware and gouda pottery, dikes, tulips, and bicycles, and social tolerance.

The country was widely criticized and socially isolated for its decadence and corruption, due to its liberal policies toward drugs, prostitution, homosexuality, infanticide, and murdering the sick and elderly. The Netherlands applied for membership in the [[United Earth Alliance]] in [[2012]], but was refused for 26 years until social reform eliminated most of the decadence and the cavalier attitude towards social murder. It was finally allowed to sign the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] on September 5, [[2034]].

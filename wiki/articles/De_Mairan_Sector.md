The '''De Mairan Sector''' is located counter-turn from the [[Sol Sector]], rimwards from the [[Njiorin Sector]], and hubwards from the [[Pelagrim Sector]].

== Inhabited Star Systems ==

The [[Mu Crucis System]], [[Beta Arae System]], [[Avior System]], [[Antares System]], [[Hydrae System]], [[Xi Herculis System]] and [[Des System]] are all located in the De Mairan Sector.

== Uninhabited Star Systems ==

The [[Taygeta Tauri System]] was the location of the [[Torimur]] survivors' exile, but was only their home for a short time. It is now uninhabited.

The Mesarthrim plan to recover their momentum by taking the Tavou District, the heart of the Prefecture's military. Tavou is the "warmonger" of the Districts, and the strongest supporter of the military. With it in Mesarthrim hands, the Prefecture will stumble. William Camp's plans are dashed when the Inquisitors intercept his plans have half of the Prefecture's Fleets waiting for the Mesarthrim.

In late December, Admiral Jeff Nolan brashly chooses to bring the Prefecture Fifth Fleet into a direct assault on Mesar. He succeeds in taking space superiority, at the cost of his own life.

Seeing defeat at hand, William Camp orders the First Joint Defense Fleet to aid him, but the Ixoarchada refuse. Outraged, Camp attempts to seize control of the Fleet by force, rousing the ire of the Ixoarchada against him. In the end, it is Ixoarchada intervention that truly brings control of Mesar orbit into the hands of the Ikronin and Prefecture.

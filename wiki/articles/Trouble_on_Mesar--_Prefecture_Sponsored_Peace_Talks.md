August 14, [[2324]]

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For the first time since the death of Uber-General Andreas Tischler, the Mesarthrim people are meeting to peacefully discuss their differences. The Auellal League and the United Centusi States are participating, but the dozens of tiny rebel factions are refusing to join the peace talks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Some rebel groups have caused trouble for the peace talks. Several suicide bombers have attempted to assassinate ambassadors from all involved parties.

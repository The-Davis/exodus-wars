The HMS ''Secutor'' was one of the earlier ships built in the [[Onyx Empire]]'s [[Atlas Class Carrier|''Atlas'' class]] of [[:Category:Carriers|carriers]].

It was given hull designation CV-14, and commissioned into the [[Royal Imperial Navy]] in [[2308]]. Its air arm squadrons were initially equipped with [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|F-4 Ravens]], but eventually equipped with the [[F-09 Carrier Fighter - "Valkyrie"|F-9 Valkyrie]], which was far superior in carrier operations.

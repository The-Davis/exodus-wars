The '''Volucris War''' was a direct, sustained attack by the [[Volucris]] species against the [[Mirdek System]].  During the course of the battle, the Volucris unearthed a major [[Gorhamut]] installation and retrieved some sort of machine.  Afterwards, the swarms completely destroyed [[Lorithan]] by moving it out of orbit and dropping it into its star, Mirdek.

Human conflict in the war was mainly a battle for survival and escape.  The Lorithos people underwent a massive evacuation, bringing billions of refugees safely to the [[Kihtor]]s, which they were able to tow into space.  The Volucris attacked mercilessly, destroying anything and anyone they came across, but rarely deliberately seeking out human targets.  If the Volucris had focused on attacking the Lorithos, all life on Lorithan would probably have been wiped out.

== Background ==

== Course of the War ==

=== 2333 ===

The Volucris War began on March 17, [[2333]], when the Volucris arrived in the [[Mirdek System]].  The Swarm was far more numerous than any previously encountered.  They were so numerous, that even at a stellar orbit of six Astronomical Units, they completely blacked out the night sky over [[Lorithan]] so that no other star could be seen, and the sun's light was barely visible.  Night was completely black, and daytime was eternal twilight.

=== Rikaz Jael and the Korval Line ===

When the coalition of armed forces banded together, it was decided that a clear and unified command structure was needed.  General-Commander [[Scott Sanburn]] was the most experienced and most charismatic leader in the coalition, and was chosen for supreme strategic command.  Sanburn established several theater commands on the planet.  The most important eventually became known as [[Rikaz Jael]], the center point of the [[Korval Line]].

The small village of Jael, nestled among unusually fertile foothills was in a good strategic location to protect the Zaur Peninsula.  Refugees from across [[Huon Jehz]] were diverted to Jael, where a mag-lev line was established for bullet train service straight to [[Kihtor Modar]].  The Korval Line was established for crowd control to keep large groups of refugees from attracting Volucris attention.

=== 2334 ===

By August, even Sanor Cai had to admit that the war was a lost cause.  He ordered the full evacuation of the planet and ordered all troops under his command to abandon the defense of any facility that did not directly aid in the evacuation.

The largest battle in the war was during the prolonged hold for evacuees to [[Kihtor Modar]].  The first and mightiest of the Kihtors was the site of intense Volucris activity, and the Lorithos and their allies would not budge.  Nearly a billion refugees headed to the Kihtor, and millions died in the fighting.  It was during one of these battles that [[Jiliur Raegos]] met his end.

The last [[Lorithos]] survivors were evacuated from the surface by October 16. The Volucris had full control of the planet until mid-December, when they dropped the planet into its sun.

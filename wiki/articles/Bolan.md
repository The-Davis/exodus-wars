'''Bolan''' is the XXth planet in the [[Xi Herculis System]].

==History==

Founded in [[2230]]

(rocky world with hyperactive tectonic activity and magma flows. No liquid water, boiling water atmosphere, domes only). Mining exports

Bolan is a miserable hell of a world, completely hostile to human life. Without the thick anti-radiation shields, the populations of the laboratories and spaceport would die painful deaths in seconds.

The XX required a spaceport to keep supplies flowing, and the high pay rates offered its Bolan employees attracted enough workers to keep the place running.

In 2302, the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] built a Wormhole Gate from Terra to Bolan, and Rahn Industries built a laboratory of their own in the system. The recession of 2315 severely hurt both companies, and the Gate from Bolan to Terra was never reopened.

In 2323, the Prefecture contracted the [[Ikronin Jurekön]] to reopen the Gate, this time to Ward instead of Terra. Bolan experienced some minor economic growth when the Hlow family opened a large-scale laboratory on the surface, creating more jobs.

In 2324, Bolan experienced further growth when a robotic mining system was built to get at the huge deposits of heavy metals. The hostile surface is unfortunately tough on the robot miners, so the constantly required repairs keep profit margins lower than they would normally be on a less-hostile world.

Bolan is not an enjoyable place to live, but it has a pretty consistent population of 300,000, with minor growth. The potential for income working in the labs is so high that people live there long enough to retire early, then move on to a more comfortable world like Ueda.
The labs of Bolan are of significant strategic importance to the [[Prefecture]], so they've stationed a garrison there. Bolan is the worst place in the Ward District to be placed, so army commanders make soldiers' shifts there mercifully short, moving them into another District garrison as early as possible.

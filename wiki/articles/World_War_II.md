'''World War II''' was a worldwide military conflict which lasted from the late [[1937]] to [[1945]]. World War II was the amalgamation of two conflicts, one starting in Asia in [[1937]], and the other beginning in Europe in [[1939]].

This global conflict split a majority of the world's nations into two opposing camps: the Allies and the Axis. Spanning much of the globe, World War II resulted in the deaths of over 60 million people, making it the deadliest conflict in human history.

World War II was the most widespread war in history, and countries involved armed more than 100 million military personnel. Total war erased the distinction between civil and military resources and saw the complete use of a nation's economic, industrial, and scientific capabilities for warfare.

The conflict ended in an Allied victory. As a result, the [[United States (Terra)|United States]], [[British Empire (Terra)|British Empire]], and the [[Soviet Union (Terra)|Soviet Union]] emerged as the world's leading superpowers, setting the stage for the [[Cold War]] for the next several decades.

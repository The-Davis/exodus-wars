Some Mesarthrim designs were so heavily armed that they could blow away Imperial fighters in a single salvo. The XF-11 airframe was designed to be large enough to support an extreme number of cannons and missiles. The XF-11 prototype was armed with six cannons and ten modular weapon hard points. The YF-11 prototype was built, but suffered from serious maneuverability issues. Its turn radius was horrible for dogfighting, requiring it to win fights head-on or not at all. Still, the need for a heavy weapons platform was great, and the F-11 Stuka was produced.

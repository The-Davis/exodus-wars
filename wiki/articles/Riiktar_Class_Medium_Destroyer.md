===Overview===

The armies of all the heirs involved in the Burning War were feared, but none so greatly as Goff Riiktar.  He poisoned the ocean, and caused so much environmental damage that the other three heirs temporarily banded together to kill him.

[[Image:RiiktarClassMediumDestroyer1.jpg]]

Memories are so strong that the name Riiktar is used to frighten small children into obedience.  "As evil as Riiktar" was a common description of Lucius Black during the Second Exodus War.  The name was an ideal choice for the powerful medium destroyer of the Lorithos ASTF.

===Capabilities===

The main weapons of Riiktar Destroyers are an array of eight Heavy Blaster cannons, backed up by twenty-six Standard Blaster cannon turrets.  Warship-grade Blaster cannons are much stronger than most cannons.  They fire faster and track better than any other humanoid warship weapon.

Riiktar Destroyers carry sixteen Skrike missile launchers and two Sur-Skrike torpedo tubes, which can deliver large payloads of conventional weapons, but not nuclear.

[[Image:RiiktarClassMediumDestroyer2.jpg]]

While the Riiktar is quite capable of defending itself, its necessary to carry fighters to help it in campaigns against ground targets.  The medium destroyer can carry three fighter squadrons and one bomber squadron.

Tough as it is, the Riiktar is faster than most humanoid light frigates.  It turns a little slowly, but its tough armor and shields make it as hard to take down as a medium cruiser.

===Deployment===

Riiktars were involved in the Lorithos/Blackis battle in the Titanian system.  The Lorithos ASTF took no Riiktar losses--the warship was much stronger than the Imperial Vigilant Frigates.

[[Image:RiiktarClassMediumDestroyer3.jpg]]

The Lorithos ASTF keeps a lot of Riiktars in its two fleets.  They're tough, powerful and resilient.  Maintenance is low, so the warships can take long tours of duty.  The Lorithos ASTF currently fields sixteen Riiktar medium destroyers.

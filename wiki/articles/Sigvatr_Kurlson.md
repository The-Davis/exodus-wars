''Sigvatr Kurlson'' (born May 10, [[2284]]) is a [[Reigen]] soldier under the personal command of the [[Kejsare]] and the Raegos family.  Sigvatr served as a fighter pilot during the [[Heritage Crisis]], the first armed confrontation the Reigens had with outsiders in their entire history.  Later, Sigvatr had the distinction to train [[Jiliur Raegos]] in the art of war. His greatest career distinction was to serve as Jiliur's second-in-command in the [[Volucris War]].  For his deeds in that war, he was named "Warmaster" by [[Lorrodi Raegos]].

== Heritage Crisis ==

During the Heritage Crisis, Sigvatr Kurlson was a fighter pilot who conducted several combat space patrols in [[Njiorin's Well]]. He studied the tactics of the [[Ikronin]] pilots, but had even greater admiration for their [[Terran]] allies.  The [[United Earth Alliance]]'s military leaders showed a great understanding of tactics, and Kurlson often found himself completely outmaneuvered when pilots from both sides played their "harassment games".

After the cold war ended, Sigvatr Kurlson retired from the Reigen military and began studying Terran war history.  He took particular interest in a book known as the "Art of War", and took the lessons to heart.  The fundamentals of advanced tactics were not well understood by the Reigens, who had always relied on their enormous technological edge over any enemies they had faced in the past.  Kurlson became a respected war scholar, and several of his published papers were noticed by the king, Lorrodi Raegos.

== Mentor ==

Sigvatr Kurlson was recalled to active duty, and ordered to train and mentor Jiliur Raegos in all aspects of the military.  Sigvatr took the younger man's lessons seriously.  They went through basic training together, went through flight school, and went to advanced infantry school.  Sigvatr and Jiliur learned from Terran schools about the concept of the fire team, and learned survival skills and sniper training from several sources.

When Jiliur was recalled home to the palace, Sigvatr was given a high-ranking commission as a reward for his loyal service, but it was not the last time the royal family called on him.

== Volucris War ==

In [[2333]], Lorrodi Raegos summoned Sigvatr Kurlson again, this time to send him to war.  Jiliur Raegos had brought a plea from the [[Lorithos]] for aid, and he was determined to answer it, even though it was against a voracious hive of [[Volucris]].  Lorrodi Raegos sent Sigvatr as Jiliur's second-in-command.

During the year they spent on [[Lorithan]], Jiliur and Sigvatr had countless close calls.  They fought battles with cool heads and good tactics, and they fought battles with unbridled fury and brave deeds that were written in song.

Finally, in the great battle at Rikaz Jael, Jiliur fell in battle.  In a desperate push, Sigvatr and two hundred Reigens struggled to recover his body.  Only Sigvatr and five Reigens survived the push, but Sigvatr managed to pull Jiliur's broken body onto the last transport before the Ixoarchadan bombardment turned Rikaz Jael to molten slag.

To honor his deeds of bravery, Lorrodi Raegos named Sigvatr Kurlson the Warmaster of Junheim.  Sigvatr retired from the military, but began teaching the lessons of tactics and telling his tales from the Volucris War throughout the Reigen world.

Jarek Nighthawk's Fate Uncertain - 4/16/2324 - By James Davis

     Jarek Nighthawk, the infamous Omega Squadron Agent responsible for the Terran Plague, is awaiting a hearing to decide which government gets to try him. The Alliance of Planets High Council, Rikaz Lorithos, Meronir's Republic, and the Prefecture all claim the right to deal with the terrorist.
     If the Alliance of Planets High Council tries him, Jarek Nighthawk is almost certain to be exiled into deep space, in similar fashion to the "Exodus" of the Blackis people back in 2080.
     The Lorithos are known for their lack of a death penalty, and their secret prison colony. Prisoners of war are sent to a planet, asteroid or space station of some sort, then never seen again. Only top Lorithos officials know the location and nature of the prison colony.
     Meroniri are known to imprison convicts for life in traditional prisons. Nighthawk would certainly be treated well, but probably never see the light of day again.
     If the Prefecture receives custody of Agent Nighthawk, he will most certainly be shot. The Prefecture's Standing Order 23221222 requires all military prisoners to be executed.
     The Alliance of Planets High Council will meet some time in 2325 to decide custody of the Agent. All three Member States will be allowed to make their case, and the High Council will only take custody if no one can make a convincing case.

'''Sam Seiler'''
*Date of Birth: January 1, [[2300]]
*Place of Birth: [[Alpha Centauri]]
*Allegiance: The [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]]

''Sam Seiler'' served in the [[Royal Imperial Army]], and later in the [[Prefecture Legion Corps]]. He was instrumental in the defense of Oselo.

*Original Project Name: T-330 "Warlord"
*Size Designation: Heavy
*Power Plant: 800XL-Series Muon Catalyzed Fusion Reactor
*Speed: 125 KPH
*Armor: Boron-Nitride Composite armor
*Armament:
**1 Large Extended-Range Neutron Cannon
**1 Small Autocannon (1500 Rounds)
**2 Small Neutron Cannons
**4 Rattler Multi-Purpose Missiles
*Manufacture: Rahn Industries
*Communications System: Standard Microwave Transceiver
*Targeting and Tracking System: Standard

===Overview===
The first Warlord prototype was the result of thousands of hours of testing and hundreds of surveys sent out to the various purchasers of the Mk.I and Mk.II. After almost continuous testing and shake down runs, the first production model was ready about eight months later and incorporated a number of fundamental changes to the original Warlord design.

[[Image:M38Warlord1.jpg]]

===Capabilities===

The first major change that came about from the tests was the realization that the existing Condor Mk.II and Mk.Is were out ranged and outgunned by their competitors from various other companies. Therefore, the remedy to this problem was the adoption of a much more powerful neutron cannon and the capability to hit targets at an increased range.

Along with the addition of a much more powerful gun, the Condor Mk.III supports the Rattler multi-purpose missile system. This allows the tank to deal much more effectively with a wider range of enemy craft, from aerospace fighters to hardened bunkers. To back up the main gun and the Rattler system, the Condor Mk.III sports two small neutron cannons and a small autocannon located in front of the commander's hatch.

With the changes to the weapons, the frame also had to be changed slightly to help compensate for the larger reactor and heavier munitions load. Therefore, the adoption of the four tread system not only allows for a much higher survivability rating, but also allows for the tank to support more weight.

[[Image:M38Warlord2.jpg]]

Finally, the turret and parts of the shell were redone to help deflect and lessen the damage from the latest anti-tank missiles and mines. This shape change, as well as the addition of more armor, has allowed the Condor Mk.III to become a leading contender for the best main battle tank in the current market.

===Deployment===

The only current user of the new tank is the Rahn Industries Security Force. The demand for the new tank is rather low due to the fact that the costumers who bought the original Condor are happy with their purchase and are not ready to upgrade just yet. However, there are various new costumers looking into upgrading their armies of old and dilapidated tanks with newer and better ones.

The R.I.S.F. uses the Condor Mk.III as its main battle tank and there are a great deal of them throughout the various armies and corps of the force. With low maintenance and the fact that every tank crew loves the Condor Mk.III, the tank has been quickly produced and given out to every garrisons of the R.I.S.F.

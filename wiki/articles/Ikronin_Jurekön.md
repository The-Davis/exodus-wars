=== History ===
Main article: [[History of the Ikronin Jurekön|History]]

The Ikronin Jurekön is a loose organization of corporations, tied together by a common homeworld--[[Kronheim]]--and a common desire to protect themselves. They are non-aggressors in war, but when on the defense they can be quite fierce.

=== Government ===
Main article: [[Government of the Ikronin Jurekön|Government]]

Very few [[Ikronin]] are interested in having a government boss them around. Instead, they band together for their defense, and work mostly towards their favorite prize--profit.

Corporations are the driving force behind defense and expansion. Most [[Ikronin]] are employees of one corporation or another, but some prefer to work individually. The independents usually pay a small fee to one corporation or another for shelter and protection during war.

Right now, there are only three major corporations doing interstellar business, but any could rise or fall overnight. There's no regulation governing the creation of corporations--because there's no government to regulate it. Competing corporations don't discourage the growth of new companies, as it would violate their laws.

=== Law ===
Main Article: [[Law of the Ikronin Jurekön|Law]]

At the dawn of [[Ikronin]] civilization, the "Rules of Trade and Equivalency" were created by [[Yngvi the Aged]], which banned benevolence and altruism, requiring any exchange of goods to have some price. This was meant to make the then-vulnerable [[Ikronin]] strong, but it eventually became a central concept to their culture.

While the giving of gifts is no longer strictly banned by law, the main law of the Jurekön is still the Rules of Trade and Equivalency.

=== Foreign Relations ===
Main Article [[Foreign Relations of the Ikronin Jurekön|Foreign Relations]]

The Jurekön managed to maintain good relations with all of its neighbors for centuries. While it looked at many of them more as customers than as friends, it was a mutually beneficial relationship.

While it enjoyed centuries of peace, the [[24th Century]] brought the Jurekön into many conflicts. The first was known as the [[Heritage Crisis]], a cold war with their [[Reigen]] cousins over the control of [[Njiorin's Well]]. Next, they were briefly involved in the [[Second Exodus War]]. Finally, the Jurekön became a key factor in the [[Tempest War]], where they emerged from being merely a commercial power and emerged as a military power.

=== Military ===
Main Article: [[Military of the Ikronin Jurekön|Military]]

Military organization and training isn't their strong point, but during the founding of the [[Alliance of Planets]], the [[United Earth Alliance]]'s military trainers spent a lot of time helping the [[Ikronin]] develop their military. [[Ikronin]] organization is similar to the old [[Earth]] system for Army, Navy and Air forces.

The Ikronin Jurekön's armed forces are divided into three branches:
*[[Jurekön Kopman Marinen|Kopman Marinen]] - Merchant Navy
*[[Jurekön Armen|Armen]] - Ground Forces
*[[Jurekön Flygvapnet|Flygvapnet]] - Air Force

=== Transportation ===
Main Article: [[Transportation of the Ikronin Jurekön|Transportation]]

The vast network of [[Wormhole Gate]]s developed and deployed across the [[Orion Arm]] is the center of [[Ikronin]] transportation. The network allows ships to travel between the stars without the need for expensive [[hyperspace]] drive systems.

Most [[Ikronin]] do not usually leave their home cities. When they do, they usually fly in personal or mass transit aircraft. Within their own cities, they usually walk or use inexpensive mass transit.

The more traditional tundra-dwelling [[Ikronin]] often rely on the [[Fenrulf]] as a means of transportation, either riding the huge creatures or having them pull carts or sleds.

=== Territory ===
Main Article: [[Territory of the Ikronin Jurekön|Territory]]

For centuries, the [[Ikronin]] remained on [[Kronheim]], content to control only the [[Yngard System]] and only briefly stop by new worlds to gather resources. More recently, they colonized the world of [[Myrdrasil]], and have joined the [[Federated Districts of the Prefecture]] in a joint colonization program.

=== Economy ===
Main Article: [[Economy of the Ikronin Jurekön|Economy]]

Commerce is the strength of the [[Ikronin]]. They don't produce a great number of products, but they do control the [[Wormhole Gate]] system, and have a huge fleet of merchant ships. The [[Ikronin]] "[[Likvada]]" is the strongest currency in the known galaxy.

=== Demographics ===
Main Article: [[Demographics of the Ikronin Jurekön|Demographics]]

Citizenship in the Jurekön requires employment by one of the corporations. Many [[Ikronin]] living on [[Kronheim]] are not actually citizens because of their choice to live on the open tundra.

Because citizenship is an automatic privaledge of being employed by one of the corporations, the Jurekön has many non-[[Ikronin]] citizens. [[Terrans]] are the most common, but [[Meroniri]] and [[Ngrligru]] are not unheard of. Prior to the [[Tempest War]], there were some [[Mesarthrim]] citizens, but the corporations fired and deported all [[Mesarthrim]] once the [[United Centusi States]] began destroying [[Wormhole Gates]] as a war strategy.

=== Culture ===
Main Article: [[Culture of the Ikronin Jurekön|Culture]]

The [[Ikronin]] have several contrasted cultural identities. There is a deep-seated cultural desire for profit and materialism, officially sanctioned by the law and the Jurekön itself. The elite of [[Ikronin]] society tend to uphold these cultural values, but also recognize the value of not enforcing conformity on their employees.

While called "more traditional", the tundra lifestyle and spiritual cultural values are actually a more recent development within [[Ikronin]] society. The movement began with [[Raegos Direnson]], before his departure with the [[Reigen]] colonization program.

Bridging the gap between the two are the military-minded, who call themselves "descendants of tundra warriors." This cultural persona is a fabrication or wishful thinking, but it instills a mindset of guardianship that the corporate leaders of the Jurekön wish to cultivate in their armed forces, so it is actively encouraged by mainstream [[Ikronin]] society.

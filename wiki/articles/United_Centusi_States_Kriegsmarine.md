The Centusi States Kriegsmarine is the chief offensive and defensive force for the United Centusi States. The Kriegsmarine has four fleets of the line. Initially they were assigned planetary sectors, but during the [[Tempest War]] each fleet was given a sector it was responsible for. In addition a primary offensive fleet, known as the Warfleet, was responsible for all major military incursions. The Centusi also maintained a Support Fleet whose main duties were to shuttle Heer soldiers and partake in planetary invasions.

==History==
The Centusi Kriegsmarine was founded initially out of the entirety of the Second and Third Clans Kriegsmarine. William Camp bought out almost the entire Kriegsmarine in the opening stages of the Civil War, despite not being involved in the combat itself. Camp borrowed heavily from the [[Berlusconi Family Territory|Berlusconi Family]], and even sent the fleets to their homeworld where the crews and officers were treated to a holiday away from the Civil War.

When the Centusi States entered the Civil War, the Second and Third Fleets came back to Mesar, completely rested, refit, and easily dominated space superiority. After the Civil War ended, Camp reported to the Alliance of Planets that his fleet had been crippled in the gruesome fighting over [[Mesar]], and that if he was going to maintain control of [[Mesarthrim]] space he would need to rebuild a Navy quickly. Both [[Rahn Industries]] and [[Tanaku Starship Design Bureau]] offered to sell the Centusi States warships, and to expedite the construction process. It had been decided by the international community that a stable Mesar would need to be controlled by the Mesarthrim, and their Navy would be the key in making that happen. 

While the international community helped Camp acquire new ships, and completely modernize and re-equip his old ones, Camp was secretly building new Mesarthrim built ships in deep space through Mesarbauer and State Shipyards. The Auellal became aware of the deception, and attempted to warn the Alliance and the Prefecture - however their reports were met with skepticism and mistrust.

When the Prefecture launched their attack against Centus at the outset of the [[Tempest War]] they were confident of victory, knowing the Mesarthrim navy to only be two fleets of a collection of internationally supplied ships. However, when the attack reached Mesar, they were face to face with the brand new First Fleet, complete with state-of-the-art technology and large powerful warships. The battle was a bloodbath for the Prefecture, and following few years of the war saw impressive Mesarthrim losses as the Prefecture reeled from the surprise it had been dealt. It took the Prefecture Industry and the assistance of the Auellal to hold the line against the massive Kriegsmarine.

==Fleets of the Line/Warfleet==
There are four fleets of the line, each with an assigned sector and duty. There is also one warfleet whose duty was to spearhead the Centusi war efforts into Prefecture territory. The Centusi Fleets were built for war, and are powerful ships, utilizing a large number of Cruisers, supported by Destroyers and lead by Battleships.

==Support Fleet==
The Centusi States maintains a single Support Fleet whose duties are to plan, execute, and support ground invasions. The fleet contains a single Battlecruiser, with destroyer support. The fleets carriers used extensive frigate support to protect their operations. The Support Fleet adopted many international ships either purchased from companies, or stolen during the Clans Occupation of Onyx Empire Worlds. The Mesarthrim has never fielded fleets in such a quantity, and therefore contracted the support, maintenance, and garrison to the [[Berlusconi Territorial Naval Forces|Berlusconi Navy]] - a decision that backfired in [[2333]] when the Berlusconi recognized the Centusi States were losing the war, and sized the [[United Centusi States Support Fleet|Stütze Flotte]] in their moorings.

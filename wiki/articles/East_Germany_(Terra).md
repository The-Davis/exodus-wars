The German Democratic Republic, better known simply as '''East Germany''' was the socialist republic that rose from the [[Communist]]-occupied region of post-[[World War II]] [[Germany (Terra)|Germany]]. East Germany was an important member of the [[Warsaw Pact]] and a major combatant in [[World War III]].

Like the rest of the [[Warsaw Pact]], the East German government signed the [[United Earth Alliance Charter]] in [[2011]] and dissolved itself.

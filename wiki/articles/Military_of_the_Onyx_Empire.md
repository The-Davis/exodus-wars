The '''Military of the Onyx Empire''' was developed for the sole purpose of conquest and expanding the influence of the [[Onyx Empire]]. The role of supreme commander was held by the Grand Admiral, though the Grand Admiral ultimately answered to the Emperor. [[Anthony Hadrian]] commanded the [[Onyx Empire]]'s military until he broke away during the [[Imperial Civil War]]. He was replaced by [[Michael Burke]] during the Civil War.

== Branches ==

The military forces developed during the fifteen-year build-up prior to the [[Second Exodus War]] were developed with combined arms principles in mind. Four branches were commissioned and organized for total warfare.

=== Royal Imperial Navy ===

The [[Royal Imperial Navy]] was the most visible and powerful branch of the military. It was responsible for transporting the other branches into invasion theaters. It had huge warships at its disposal, as well as an air arm with fighters and bombers. The arsenal at its command was capable of planetary bombardment with civilization-ending weapons, or pinpoint surgical strikes.

=== Royal Imperial Army ===

The [[Royal Imperial Army]] was responsible for invading, conquering and holding ground territory. It had millions of troops at its disposal, and a variety of fast-moving heavy weapons, designed to quickly take strategic targets, then reinforce them with heavy defenses.

=== Royal Imperial Air Force ===

The [[Royal Imperial Air Force]] was responsible for air and space superiority, ground strikes, and air support for the Army. Its fighters and bombers were capable of a wide variety of mission profiles, but it was most famous for its air superiority capabilities.

=== Royal Imperial Marine Corps ===

The [[Royal Imperial Marine Corps]] was responsible for the defense of the ships of the Navy, and for the boarding of enemy warships. Unlike the [[Prefecture Marine Corps]] which succeeded it, the Imperial Marines were a purely infantry operation, focused only on space defense and boarding operations.

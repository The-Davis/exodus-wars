===Overview===

Kondo'jahazi aina Duma'kapungu are the backbones of the Mia'mbili.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaDuma'kapungu1.jpg]]

While the Nina'rikebu is a fleet unto itself, the Duma'kapungu in the Mia'mbili carry the bulk of an invasion force.

===Capabilities===

Duma'kapungu are not as lethal as the smaller Nvodu'pweza, but are still excellent warships and much more powerful than their humanoid counterparts.  They carry several Mkuki'kali Energy Lances, eight on turrets and twelve in fixed forward positions.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaDuma'kapungu2.jpg]]

It also carries an array of Bunduki'kubwa Hyperspace Distortion Cannons, Gobori'dogo Relativistic Mass Drivers, and Mshale'liga Missile Tubes.  It isn't very maneuverable, but can easily fight off a humanoid vessel, except for the deadly Meroniri warships.

===Deployment===

Duma'kapungu are deployed with all Mia'mbili.  The Duma'kapungu has enough room for several landing craft.

[[Image:Kondo'jahaziainaDuma'kapungu3.jpg]]

On less-developed worlds, one Duma'kapungu is enough to subdue the planet.

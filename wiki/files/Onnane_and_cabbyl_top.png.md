The Onnane and Cabbyl.  The hover tank is the Onnane (Thistle), and the Cabbyl (Horse) is the utility car.  This image illustrates their comparable size, both ships were made to fit inside the same model Auellal drop ship, allowing one transport group the ability to move anything required by the Dün.

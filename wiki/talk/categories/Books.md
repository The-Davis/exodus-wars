===Original "Story Archives"===

Story Archives

     The history of the galaxy is a long story, with many heroes and villains struggling for supremacy. Much of the history before the cataclysmic event known as "The Sundering" is now merely legend.  Only three of the intelligent species in the galaxy had become star-faring races at this time: humans, Ologrians, and Geckans. Humanity’s records were mostly wiped out in the Sundering. The Ologrians didn’t keep records prior to development of digital storage media due to their aquatic environment.  The Geckans have not been seen by mainstream society since before the Sundering, and if not for the presence of their slave species, the Geckans themselves would be known as nothing but a myth.
     This archive showcases many of the stories of intrigue, heroism and loss that make up everyday life in the galaxy.  The millennia since the Sundering were largely peaceful until the world of Terra emerged into the galactic theater.  Since then, the status quo has been turned on its head, with centuries of tradition being changed almost overnight.

Pre-Sundering
     Era of the Anthronoris - Humanity was once united in a single race, known as the Anthronoris. In ancient times, the Anthronoris controlled a star-spanning empire of unsurpassed power. Only a handful of records survive from this era.
     Ologrian Legends - Despite their vast technological abilities, the Ologrians are and have always been a primitive and savage society, barely held together by tribal traditions. Until the "Kabila Duka" was formed, the Ologrians only kept historical records in the form of oral traditions, poems, and song. Modern historians have pieced together some information about prehistoric Ologrians.
     Geckan Myths - The giant reptilian life forms known as the Geckans have all but disappeared from the known galaxy. The only records of their great deeds at the dawn of human time are in the form of myths, collected by the Meroniri and the slave species created by the Geckans after the Sundering.

Post Sundering
     Era of Recovery - After the Sundering, the galaxy as a whole knew peace for quite some time. There were no interstellar wars, as the Ologrians had withdrawn to their own worlds, and most of the surviving human populations were reduced to a point where they worked together for mere survival, or were too primitive to fight interstellar wars. The "Era of Recovery" was so long that humanity's survivors diverged into many unique races.
     The First Exodus War - While races like the Meroniri, Icronians, and Archadians were consolidating their power and building their civilizations, the Terran race was exploding out from their home world at a phenomenal rate, soon bringing out a conflict which has gained its place in the annals of history as the "First Exodus War."
     Founding of the Alliance - With the First Exodus War fought and hard won by the Terran government, the United Earth Alliance, the Icronian and Meroniri people decided to make contact with the Terrans. Interstellar relations blossomed overnight, and soon one of the greatest diplomatic organizations in history, the Alliance of Planets, was founded.
     The Second Exodus War - The peace of the Alliance of Planets was short-lived, as the exiles of the First Exodus War returned, forming the Empire, a powerful and dangerous war-mongering government. The war brought many civilizations to the eye of galactic politics that had never been seen before, though the Alliance of Planets still reigned supreme in economic and diplomatic power.
     The Volucris Incursion - As the Second Exodus War slowed and died down, the "Orion Arm" of the galaxy--the hub of human civilization--was invaded by an outside power for the first time since the Sundering. The mysterious and innumerable Volucris swept through human space like a storm, then disappeared as suddenly as they appeared.
     The Tempest War - The Tempest War brought about more change, for better and for worse, than any war in human history since the Sundering. Old agreements shattered, old friends betrayed one another, and the smaller governments rose up and became powers unto themselves.
     Revelations of the Volucris - The future is not yet revealed.
     The End of the Alliance - The future is not yet revealed.
     The Ologrian Invasion - The future is not yet revealed.
     The End - All stories eventually come to an end, and this one is no exception.

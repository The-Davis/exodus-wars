Now, what I do propose is that, yes, I do a flag for the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]] and the [[United Centusi States]] based off of the German flag, and then keep the current logo as the "seal" of the [[United Mesarthrim Clans]]/[[United Centusi States]].

Your thoughts?

== Not quite ==

Well, I never wanted to get rid of the logo. The square/diamond/circle/wave is COOL. I want to keep that, and add it onto the flag. I just think the colors can change a bit.

Here's my proposal:
Base-line: Modern German flag.
Symbol: Current UMC/UCS symbol, but recolored to match the red/yellow/black of the German flag.
Add to the symbol: Compass (math compass) in the background behind the symbol, and a sworn underneath the whole thing.
Enhance the "wave" in the circle just a bit, but leave it blue.

Here's the symbolism: The German flag represents German domination in Mesarthrim society. From the days of the clan lords to the days of the UCS, the German clans have dominated Mesarthrim society, and will probably continue to do so for a long time to come. The compass represents the power of Mesarthrim ingenuity, and the sciences that support their society. The sword represents the violence and bloodlust that is the foundation of their society and gives it its purpose. The wave represents change and motion, and the nomadic nature of the clan societies. The diamond and square represent the twin influences of church and military keeping change and motion bottled up within boundaries. Freedom is not without limits to the Mesarthrim.

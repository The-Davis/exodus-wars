= Part 1 =

February 9, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Cronus=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The whole of the Empire's Titanian Invasion Force converged on Titanian 12, their last toe-hold in the system. It was one of the best-defended Imperial installations in the galaxy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Robert Marshall and the Carrier Atlas commanded the orbital fleet of warships. The carrier Kerberos maintained the communications between the fleet and the ground forces, and the carrier Vulcanon directed the fighter screens. Besides the carriers, the Royal Imperial Navy had forty Vigilant Class Frigates, spearheaded by Vice Admiral Jeff Nolan's Judicator.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For fighter support, the Imperial fleet had thirty squadrons of fighters: 120 Valkyries and 240 Ravens. The elite commander Emil Torsc was out of the action at the time, but Jak Ivonrud of Blue Wing was in command.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On the ground, the Royal Imperial Army Command Center was well-armed with defensive anti-warship missiles with nuclear warheads, plus numerous anti-air and anti-ground missile, laser and cannon turrets. The army also keeps two Divisions of mobile forces on the ground.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before the day was out, Uber General Andreas Tischler deployed the Mesarthrim Fleet from Theia. General-Command Scott Sanburn and the TDF forces were not completely prepared and could not join the initial attack, but General-Commander Sanburn mobilized immediately, and would soon join the attack on Titanian 12.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn: "I don't know what Uber General Tischler was thinking, jumping the gun like that. We'll catch up with him shortly. Good luck to you Tischler, wherever you are."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I really can't be sure on how many of our troops will die in the upcoming battle, but I will say this: We will not roll over and die for the Empire, nor will we crawl to them to become serfs of Lucius Black. We will fight to the death to keep the Empire out of our worlds."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At the time General-Commander Sanburn spoke with B-Net representatives, the TDF fleet was in orbit of the moon of Titanian 6--Tiberius--or docked with Space Station Intervention. The fleet deployed immediately after speaking with B-Net.

= Part 2 =

February 10, 2322

by James Davis

=====Cronus=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim fleet has made contact with the Imperial forces on Titanian 12 and are engaging. The reports came in moments ago. The Mesarthrim have attacked the Imperial forces orbiting Titanian 12, despite the Royal Imperial Navy's superior numbers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn urged Uber General Tischler of the Mesarthrim to pull back and wait for TDF reinforcement, but Tischler declined. He only replied that "They cannot kill me, I was born to hang." No one is sure what he meant by that.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn's forces moved out from Theia yesterday, but they won't arrive until tomorrow.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim Fleet, led by the MCS Winston Churchill went into battle against the Atlas Wing of the Empire. Commandant Arnold Stadt led the attack. Uber General Andreas Tischler commanded the ground forces in the planetary assault.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stadt's forces were light. The Winston Churchill was his only heavy ship. Instead of heavy cruiser support, he had 600 modified TDF-F-900 fighters and 1,500 Mesarthrim Patton fighters. The rest of the Mesarthrim forces, the 876th Assault Group, hung back and let Stadt's forces handle the Royal Imperial Navy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim fleet was met by a volley of Apocalypse Class nuclear warheads. With grim efficiency, the Winston Churchill fired its Omega Missiles. The rift created by the "Omega Effect" swallowed every single Imperial nuke.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Robert Marshall was no fool. He immediately saw that it was useless to use Valkyries at a distance, and ordered his cruisers to move in close. Thirty Vigilant Class Frigates and the two Carriers Kerberos and Vulcanon moved in to engage the Winston Churchill and its fighters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Winston Churchill emptied its missile complement of Omegas, destroying twenty-five frigates and the full complement of Ravens from the Vulcanon. Commandant Stadt's celebration was short-lived. The Kerberos had not deployed its Ravens.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighters boiled out of the Kerberos like a swarm of locusts. The Pattons and Ravens became embroiled in a huge, one-sided dogfight. The Pattons were decimated--fully 25% were knocked out before they managed to destroy a single Raven.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Arnold Stadt and Admiral Robert Marshall exchanged a few insults during the brief pause.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Those Ravens, they are worse than a mosquito."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Marshall: "So, you're the upstart commander who thinks he's born to hang, eh?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Nein, that would be Uber General Tischler."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Marshall: "Then you tell Tischler this, boy. His hangman is here."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Let us be less talking, more fighting, ja? Imperial pig."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Vulcanon came within firing range of the Winston Churchill, and the ships had a brief, but fierce exchange of weapons fire. The Churchill was better armed, and scored a critical hit on the Vulcanon's engines. Mesarthrim Myt'herra were ferried over in combat pods and boarded the crippled carrier.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Myt'herra boarded, and were having an easy time gunning down the lightly-armed crew, when suddenly the Vulcanon blew itself to pieces when the commander scuttled the ship. Commandant Stadt wrote off the loss and moved the Churchill to engage the Kerberos.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim fighters managed to pick off the weapons of the Kerberos, and the Carrier fled to the far side of the planet. Instead of pursuing the Kerberos, the Winston Churchill moved to attack the Atlas.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unlike the other Imperial carriers, the Atlas was not easy meat. Robert Marshall had ordered every aspect of the Atlas' combat systems modified. The Atlas had the clear upper hand over the Winston Churchill. The Imperial fighter squadrons had gained the advantage in the fighter dogfight. It looked bad for the Mesarthrim forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt ordered the Fleet to pull out. The Churchill turned to retreat, and the Atlas scored a direct hit on its engines. Robert Marshall hailed the Churchill.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Marshall: "A valiant effort, boy. Order your men to stand down. Your ships will be confiscated and you will be returned unharmed to your government."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Nein! Uber General Tischler's forces will never admit defeat!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Marshall: "Your Uber General Tischler is welcome to try to stop the Empire. We'll be happy to oblige him with a hanging."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler decided it was a good time to move in the 876th Assault Group. The MCS Midway, the MCS Francis Drake, and two-hundred TDF-F-900s charged in, determined to make Marshall eat his words.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the confusion, the Imperial fighter and warship screens were disrupted, and six hundred Mesarthrim high-capacity dropships landed on the surface of Titanian 12.  The Midway engaged the Atlas.  At first the Midway had the upper hand, but then a Vigilant class frigate broke off from its fight with the Francis Drake and rammed the Midway.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The crew of the Midway had no time to react.  The hull was breached and the reactor went critical.  The resulting explosion killed everyone aboard the Midway and the Vigilant frigate.  The explosion also damaged the Atlas badly enough to force it out of the battle.  Admiral Marshall hailed Uber General Tischler's landing craft and left a final message.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Marshall:"Good job, you managed to beat me. No one's managed to do that in...oh, two hundred years or so. You may defeat our ground forces down there. If you do, come on over to Terra. I'll meet you with a rope."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Arnold Stadt ordered the 125th and 876th to pull back in order to let the Imperial fleet move out.  The Imperial fleet retreated to the far side of the planet. Both fleets stood back to observe the ground battle, in order to evacuate the losing side.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler's forces landed on the surface of Titanian 12 safely and attacked the Imperial entrenchments. The Mesarthrim forces started out with a hefty number of troops and machines, over five million ground troops, though they only brought 25 of their now-famous Vulture Siege Tanks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The initial engagement was fast and fierce. When the first wave was ended, the Mesarthrim had taken 60% losses, but the Blackis army was routed everywhere on Titanian 12 except the base core itself.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An hour later, the TDF fleet, led by the TDF-W-6000 heavy cruiser Colin Powell entered orbit around Titanian 12, near the remains of the Imperial fleet. General-Commander Sanburn quickly mobilized the reinforcements for Uber General Tischler. Two million additional Mesarthrim Infantry were sent to the surface, along with hundreds of TDF vehicles and Rigs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The landing went well for the TDF. With the Royal Imperial Army corralled in one small spot, the TDF was able to land unchallenged, and set up multiple small air-support bases for rearming their strike craft.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The TDF/Mesarthrim forces split into four units, and approached the outer rim of the Imperial Command Center. Most of the automated ground defense systems and all of the air defense systems were knocked out early in the fighting.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Once in position, the four combat units dug in to prepare for the final assault.

= Part 3 =

February 12, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Cronus=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle for Titanian 12 finally concluded with a final showdown. Uber General Tischler and his Vulture Siege Tanks took up the line of fire just outside the outer wall of the Imperial base. The Imperial forces didn't respond--they were locked down for a long siege.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With no further delay, the Mesarthrim forces blasted down the outer wall, and burst into the Imperial Command Center. The exchange of weapons fire was too heated for B-Net observers to make out any details. Finally, the fighting died down as dozens of Imperial dropships fled the planet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial forces managed to evacuate 80% of their survivors, and wiped all of their computers, but were unable to scuttle the Command Center before the Mesarthrim and TDF forces seized it.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Empire's forces withdrew from completely, heading for the Imperial world of Bethija. They planned to regroup and prepare for their next move there.

= Part 4 =
February 8, [[2322]]
by Scott Sanburn

=====Personal Log - Scott E. Sanburn, General-Commander=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I just got home from another brutal work day. It was a long day of training and preparation. The Alliance of Planets High Council has decided that it's time to destroy the Imperial forces in the Titan System.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We're planning on a multiple-step campaign. If we get Empire out of the Titan System, we're striking back at them and taking back the lost territories of the United Earth Alliance. With luck, we'll take Terra itself.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It's hard to believe it's been seven years since the Empire showed up out of whatever hellhole they came from. I'm surprised we're still alive.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'm still worried that Meronir's Republic has refused to send military aid to deal with the invasion. They could wipe out the entire Empire in a day. Even so, they've provided lots of material support--just not the weapons or power sources we need. Still, without Meronir's Republic and their support, Theia would've fallen a long time ago. I'll always be grateful.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Supporting eight million troops has been interesting. Difficult, but definitely interesting. The Mesarthrim has some incredible weapons and ships, and their support has done wonders for morale. Their troops well-behaved and helpful.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;My Captains have been helping me delegate some of the problems with the chain of command out of my hands. I'd go mad if I had to command all of these troops and equipment, and still make sure the strike force was properly prepared to move out.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I don't think I've ever seen a strike force this big. I'm not sure there's ever been one this size, not in the history of the Alliance of Planets anyway.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I'm afraid and hopeful at the same time. I know there will be a tremendous number of casualties, even if things go exactly according to plan.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial base on Cronus is heavily fortified, but we must take it back. We have to hold onto our territory. The alternative is the tyranny of the Emperor. Damn Lucius Black! Damn the Empire!<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;We'll be moving out at the end of the week. All Member States of the Alliance are sending their best. We will be ready.


= Part 5 =

February 12, [[2322]]

by Scott Sanburn

=====Official Log - Scott E. Sanburn, General-Commander=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The combined forces of the Alliance of Planets succeeded in landing the majority of their ground forces. Many thanks to the Mesarthrim Fleet, which drove out the Imperial space defense force. The fleeing Imperial forces headed to the Imperial-held planet Bethija.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;I must personally apologize to Meronir's Republic. TDF Red Fighter Wing was responsible for defending the heavy Planetary Drop Squadron they loaned to us, but unfortunately one of the pilots under my command lost control of his TDF-F-900 and smashed into the TDF-W-6000 heavy cruiser Benely. The ship crashed into Titanian 12, killing all hands. Many of the crew were Meroniri, and I feel it my duty to accept responsibility for their deaths.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fortunately, we did not lose anyone else in accidents. Our forces were safely ferried to their respective landing zones. The Rig Battalions, the Ground Offensive Squadrons and the Air Strike Squadrons went into battle without incident before meeting the enemy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim offensive destroyed most of the automated defenses, and our combined forces marched on the main compound. Fortunately, the Empire didn't seem too interested in defending the base after the defense systems went down.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Our experimental Rigs did very well. The combination of Ngrligru weapons technology and Meroniri engineering performed as it was expected to.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bulk of the Imperial base was ruined by the artillery and orbital bombardments, but most of the surviving Imperial troops were safely evacuated before we could force their surrender.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial compound was impressive. It was as large as some cities. The engineering and architecture was similar to the typical United Earth Alliance base. I'm guessing the Imperial engineers took most of their ideas from our old government.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Most buildings were two or three stories equipped with run-of-the-mill lighting and climate control systems. Some had a few nasty surprises in the form of internal defense systems, but we took those out. Within a day of taking the base, we'd restored the captured buildings so our troops could be quartered without environmental suits. As most locals know, Titanian 12 is very cold.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commander Byron Soong found an operational computer in one of the deserted buildings. Unfortunately, most of the information was wiped by the Imperial operators before we took it. Only the bare operating system was intact.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Our victory was a costly one. Uber General Andreas Tischler thinks our losses were worth the gain. I'm starting to agree with him, but the deaths tear at me.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In short gentlemen, the Empire has been driven out of the Titan System. This was the most brutal and destructive conflict I have ever witnessed, and I hope that one day we can find an end to it. Peace will never be possible while Terra chokes in the grip of the Empire.

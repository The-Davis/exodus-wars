== Political System ==

The political system is designed primarily to promote equality and effectiveness in the output of laborers.  As such, the system is nearly communist in nature, but makes allowances for merit-based pay increases.  To prevent corruption, however, there are two strictly enforced principle:
1.  No one may own any capital investments.  All "capital" is owned by the government.  Owned property may be used for private purposes only.
2.  Separation of Wealth and Political Power.   Because politicians do not have any need for the wealthy, and because advertising of any kind (including political advertising) is illegal, accumulation of wealth due to merit (i.e. working hard, and producing well) does not lead to curruption.

Regardless of merit, all individuals are given the same amount of "political capital".  This capital is spent "purchasing" positions for elected officials.  That way, individuals that care very strongly about a particular position (but not another) are able to spend the majority of their political capital on that seat.

Politicians in the system are chosen primarily on their ability to manage and increase output of the workers.  Those good for the wealth of the colony are good for its leadership, it is presumed.  Indeed Oronus is such a collectivist world that a single high-profile sign of selfishness or greed can damn an opponent before the first debate. == Economic System ==

In order to ensure fairness and efficiency, the vast majority of OWC's internal economy is run by computer.  Individuals answer an Expert System's questions, allowing this simple proto-AI to construct a model of their needs, goals, and desires.  The computers can then agree upon resource manufacturing and distribution that maximizes the overall well-being of the workers.

Because the wealth disparity is much less on Oronus than in other cultures, much less money is spent on the frivolous, enabling Oronus to have a health care system with one of the highest median quality ratings of any planet.

The economic efficiency and increased fairness does not come without a cost, however.  Unlike many more capitalist systems, the government of Oronus strictly regulates many risky behaviors to prevent health care costs from going up.  Also, while the system does very well at ''globally'' optimizing economic allocation of resources, many individuals experience lower performance than they would in a classical market system.

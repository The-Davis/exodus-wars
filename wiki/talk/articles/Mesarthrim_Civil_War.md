June 12, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The city of New Milwaukee erupted in violent protests against the non-aggression policy of the Mesarthrim High Command towards the Prefecture. Military riot control units were called in, and many buildings burned in fires set by rioters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The death toll is horribly high. Protesters meet Mesarthrim troops with weapons. The soldiers are reluctant to retaliate, but some have returned fire, and dozens have died on both sides.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Things got very bad when rioters managed to get hold of some military-grade weapons. These weapons are Imperial-issue, leading Fleet Intelligence Director Eric Francis to believe that the Empire's Omega Squadron is behind the riots.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Arnold Stadt supports the civilians' anti-Blackis point of view, but is urging them to calm down and cease their rioting. "We may want to kill the Blackis--rebels and Empire alike--but we mustn't stoop to killing our own. Save your bullets for the real enemy!"

June 25, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Cirus System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In a series of blitzkrieg-style attacks, rebels against the Mesarthrim have taken several cities on the world of New Hanover. B-Net has coverage of most of the stories.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the early morning of August 2nd, civilian rebels equipped with Perseus Battle Armor--a largely Prefecture model--landed in the New Washington spaceport. Within minutes, they'd gunned down the helpless citizens in the area.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The rebels were cornered by Mesarthrim troops twenty minutes after their landing. Eight rebels were killed in the initial exchange of fire, but sixteen managed to hijack escape craft. About thirty more rebels broke through the Mesarthrim troops, but they didn't live long. Mesarthrim fighter craft strafed them, killing every rebel. The final civilian death count is unknown.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;New Sydney--the capital city of the Mesarthrim New Hanover colony--was captured by civilian rebels. Like the New Washington rebels before them, these were armed with Prefecture technology. This time, they had Rigs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The city has fallen, and Commandant Sampson--the governor of New Hanover--was hanged after a mockery of a court hearing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler has contacted the Prefecture about the Prefecture technology in the hands of the rebel civilians. Hadrian claims that it did not come from his forces, and offered to send a regiment of his own troops to help put down the rebellion.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Tischler decided to shift tactics, and moved an Omega-carrying cruiser into the New Hanover system. He hopes that the rebels will realize that they cannot fight an orbital bombardment, and submit peacefully.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim High Command knows where the rebels have been receiving their advanced weapons.  Blackis dropships were spotted in New Atlanta, a rebel-held city. Naval Intelligence Director Eric Francis believes that they are Imperial dropships.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim spies managed to capture footage of Imperial technicians unloading heavy Rigs and infantry weapons. One of the Imperials was identified as Lavik Nighthawk, a known terrorist expert from Omega Squadron.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Faced with this evidence, Mesarthrim High Command has unanimously agreed to work with Hadrian--instead of merely keeping a non-aggression pact.

July 1, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Cirus System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The rebel civilians in New Hanover have continued their uprising against the United Mesarthrim Clans. After several pitched battles, Uber General Tischler finally ordered a withdrawal from the planet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The city of New Chili and much of the province of New California were ravaged by zealous rebels, attacking non-aggressive citizens and soldiers alike. Rebel forces even managed to fire on the Mesarthrim orbiting fleets, but without much success.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Despite the best efforts of the Mesarthrim Phantom battle group, Uber General Tischler had to admit defeat. He was obviously unhappy when he was interviewed by B-Net reporters, but he did his best anyway.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "Citizens of the United Mesarthrim Clans, it is with great sadness that I must order the Phantom battle group to leave New Hanover in the hands of the rebels.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"When the rebels attacked unarmed citizens, Mesarthrim High Command knew that we had to pull out or face unparalleled loss of innocent lives. All citizens who wish to accompany our soldiers will be given transport, but after one week the planet of New Hanover will no longer be under the jurisdiction of the Mesarthrim government.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Due to our losses, I have ordered the Crimson Tide and Black Dogs battle groups to return to Centus. At this time, we cannot protect our borders and help the Prefecture fight for the freedom of Terra at the same time."

April 6, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim High Command reports that the rebellion by anti-biological weapon activists has been put down. Unfortunately casualties among the civilian rebels was nearly total, they refused to surrender.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim General Jabloniski reports that Mesarthrim Urban Control troops have accounted for all of the vehicles stolen by the rebels, but their leader--Richard Michaels--escaped with a core of his most loyal men. About a hundred rebel survivors were captured and sentenced to life in prison--an unusually light sentence for a government that usually executes traitors.

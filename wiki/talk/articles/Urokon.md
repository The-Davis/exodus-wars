Also, the old build notes:

To date, experimentation with communication via EPR particle chain has failed.  The data stream only has a 25% rate of accuracy, and all efforts to improve this have failed.

According to mathematicians, it is impossible for an EPR particle chain communicator to achieve faster than light communication, let alone instantaneous.  I am taking creative liberties here.

A giant dish would not be necessary for a real EPR particle chain communicator.  It just looks good.  Some credit must be given to Primus BuS, as his own communicator model inspired the dish of this building.

The name Ansible comes from Rocannon's World, written by Ursula K. LeGuin, published by Ace Books in 1966.  The concept of instantaneous communication with a device called an "Ansible" is from Ender's Game, by Orson Scott Card.

The use of an Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen particle chain is my own application of theoretical concepts.  Special thanks goes to Justin Wick for his input on the quantum physics aspect of the EPR particle chain theory.

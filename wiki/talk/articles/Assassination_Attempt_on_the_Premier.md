March 30, [[2321]]

by James Davis

Rewrite: Terrible style, too many old names, and the Omegas operate sloppily.

=====Titan City=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Titan City Councilman Gary Hughes was a target of a failed assassination attempt early this morning. He's in stable but serious condition at Titan City Hospital, but should recover. One of the sniper's rifle bullets severed some of his spinal cord, and doctors do not know if they can completely restore it or not.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Councilman Hughes was lucky. Six people were killed in the attack, two civilians and four Titan City Police officers. Their names have not been released, pending notification of next-of-kin. TDF Press Secretary Elizabeth Reynolds had this statement:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Elizabeth Reynolds: "This was the first successful attack on Titanian soil in three years. It was cowardly attack by the Empire, and will not be forgotten."<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two of the attackers were killed by Titan City Police, and a third surrendered. General-Commander Joshua Beson of the Alliance Space Police took the survivor into custody, and identified him and the other two attackers as members of the Empire's Omega Squadron, a terrorist organization. In response, he assigned ten Space Police officers to each member of the Titanian government for protection.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Scott Sanburn, though not involved in the investigation, did have this to say:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Sanburn: "It's going to be a long time before we can say we're safe. I encourage all citizens to carry weapons--assault automatics, if you can afford them. We lost some fine men and women to the bloodlust of the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We will fight back. I ask all citizens to report any suspicious activity to the Titan City Police and the Alliance Space Police at first opportunity."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before the captured Omega agent could be moved to a high security prison, he was gunned down in the street by yet another terrorist. The newcomer shot himself in order to avoid capture.

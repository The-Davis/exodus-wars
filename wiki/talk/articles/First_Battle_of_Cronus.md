=====Dawn=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dawn on Cronus was barely different from midnight. The hellishly cold planet had no atmosphere to speak of, save when the distant sun evaporated the methane frost on the ground. This bleak world is the Empire's home away from home, the port where they command their invasion of this last stronghold of the United Earth Alliance.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The sky is full of bright lights. While the sun does little to illuminate the barren surface, the convoy of ships in orbit makes up for it. Without the glare of lights off the atmosphere, even the casual observer can make out every ship in all its glory. Hundreds of bulk haulers, dozens of frigates, powerful carriers, and looming above them all, the mighty Jerome Black.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bridge of the Imperial flagship is unusually crowded. Typically, the advanced EI, Prism, makes a large number of crew on the bridge unnecessary. Today is different. Today, the Emperor has come.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Nearly a hundred elite Omega agents are aboard the massive flagship, and a dozen of their best are in the bridge. Lucius Black, Emperor and supreme ruler of the Empire, scourge of human space, faces off with Admiral Robert Marshall. Few commanders are willing to even question the Emperor's will, only two--Marshall and Hadrian--are willing to disobey him.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Admiral, I must say I expected a disaster on my hands when I read your initial report. I was pleasantly surprised by the level of losses you've managed to inflict on the enemy, despite your inability to capture their territory."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall raises an eyebrow at the backhanded compliment. "Sir, I think with the Meroniri on the way, it would be wise to cut our losses and return to Terra. In another year, I think I could take this starsystem against one enemy fleet, but four? Impossible."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Funny how things work out, isn't it?" Black sighs. "When we were exiled, everyone was so sure that humans...Terrans now, I suppose...were it. No other intelligent life. And yet here we are, with no less than four distinctly different human civilizations, and the Ologrians as well. Nevertheless, I don't think their Alliance is as much of a threat as you fear."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Meroniri alone possess a vast technological advantage that could..."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Could do nothing!" Black snaps. "The Meroniri are a dead civilization, Marshall. They wallow in their own excess, caring nothing for what goes on outside their borders. If not for their weapons, we could crush them in their homes. A Meroniri wouldn't lift a finger to save his own mother!" Black sighs. "The Ikronin are another matter, but given time and money, they can be brought around to see things my way. The Lorithos are broken by their own past. Ignore them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"And my request for supplies?" Marshall asks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Approved. In addition, I've increased your troop complements. Take those planets, Marshall. I am pleased with your progress so far, but I will not rest until the United Earth Alliance is destroyed."

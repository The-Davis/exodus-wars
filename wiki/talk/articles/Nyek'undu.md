===Build Notes===

The Nyek'undu, and all [[Ngrligru]] fighters for that matter, were inspired by my two favorite Sci-Fi anime programs: Cowboy Bebop (possibly the greatest serious television show ever), and Outlaw Star.  Outlaw Star's "grappler" combat, and Faye Valentine's gunship from Cowboy Bebop inspired the combat and landing gear model.

The cockpit from the Nyek'undu is modeled after a version of the Valentine gunship I saw on the web somewhere.  I have lost the link, but if someone sends it in, I'll gladly give the author credit.

= Part 1 =
April 5, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears and James Davis


=====Bethija=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The world of Bethija was once one of the central worlds of the United Earth Alliance before the Empire took over. Today the Mesarthrim deployed two large forces--one fleet and one ground army--to take the planet for the Alliance of Planets.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial listening posts on Bethija detected the Mesarthrim Fleet--an inconclusive reading--and sent a detachment to confirm. A squadron of Raven Fighters reached the outer orbit of the Bethija system before they made contact with the Mesarthrim forces. The Ravens radioed back to the 3rd Core Fleet with their report.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Raven Commander: "We've got ships. There's too many to count. I'm feeding the sensor reads to the Obelisk. How'd the Mesarthrim get such a large force here so quickly? Why didn't the Vanguard Fleet warn us when they left Centus?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "I just received word from the fleet. They say that those ships left this morning."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Raven Commander: "How'd they get here so fast? Oh sh-" *transmission terminated*<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Ravens, report!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Raven 2nd: "Commander's been killed! They're firing!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Break off and return to Bethija. All ships get under the missile defense system's envelope."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Voss sent a Vigilant Class Frigate--the Dread--to do a full sweep of the incoming fleet, this time with cloak detectors so Voss could get an accurate count of the Mesarthrim numbers. The 3rd Core Fleet's single squadron of Invader heavy fighters was sent to fly cover.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As soon as the Dread was in range with the Mesarthrim fleet, nearly a million Firebat drone fighters--similar to the Imperial Swarmers--were launched by the Mesarthrim Carriers. Unfortunately for the Mesarthrim, Imperial forces train against their own Swarmers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Dread opened fire with its PACs, scoring huge numbers of kill counts on the Firebats. The Invaders went on the evasive, dodging the initial Firebat rush only to return and blast them with laser fire. Nearly two thirds of the Firebats were destroyed by the Dread and the Invaders.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler had planned the battle too well to be stopped by the loss of drone fighters. His destroyers accelerated past the slow Dread and escort, heading for Bethija.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dread:"Rear Admiral Voss, we have incoming destroyers heading for Bethija. We are unable to engage."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Understood Dread. Return to base at best possible speed. Bethija Missile Control, target the destroyers for a nuclear barrage, but don't fire. If they're armed with Omega Bombs, we'll be torched by the shockwave."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The destroyers were merely a feint. They made a single strafing run on the 3rd Core Fleet before accelerating to FTL and returning to the outer-band of the Bethija starsystem. Then while the Dread was distracted by the attack on the 3rd Core Fleet, the Mesarthrim carriers opened fire, breaching the hull in three seconds. Only three crew members survived, and they were imprisoned by the Mesarthrim. Rear Admiral Voss recalled the Invader fighters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss had one more attack of his own. He sent a flight of eight Valkyries to do a hit and run on the Mesarthrim battle cruisers. The Valkyries' Apocalypse missiles scored direct hits on the MCS Revenge, killing the entire crew. The Valkyries headed back to Bethija, but about half-way there, they began to take hits.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Valkyrie pilots were confused. The shots were apparently coming from no where. All eight were destroyed before the pilots could escape. They'd been taken out by cloaked Mesarthrim vessels. That was the end of the initial battle. Both sides regrouped to plan.

= Part 2 =

April 18, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim High Command held an emergency session to discuss several issues of extreme importance to the people of Centus. A full recording of the meeting was released by B-Net correspondent David Sevall.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "It's time to stop showing mercy to the Empire. We should stop taking prisoners and step up our invasion of their territory."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Josh Carter: "Our worlds cannot stretch our supply lines much further sir. We need to slow down and wait for the other Alliance of Planets Member States to catch up."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "No, damn it! We advance constantly, we push the Empire out of Bethija, we push them off every world they've taken, and we shove every weapon we have down Lucius Black's throat! Can we create a new virus?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Heidlemaan: "67% of citizens support the use of bio-weapons against the forces of the Empire. Personally, I don't."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "We'll hold off on viruses for now then. Stadt, Parman, anything to add?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Weapons, viruses, no difference. Let us be giving them hell, ja?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Parman: "I agree sir. We should push on as long as we can, but I'm not very fond of viral warfare. Conventional weapons will do."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "I concede. The military will only use viral weapons as a last resort."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Carter: "The Department of Planetary Resources agrees, though I still think we need to slow down our campaign before we exhaust our resources."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Heidlemaan: "No, we don't agree! I have an equal say in the Department of Planetary Resources, and I say that if we use viral weaponry--as a first, last, or any resort--we'll be in violation of the Great Charter of the Alliance of Planets. We gave our word to uphold the Great Charter. Honor and honesty aside, the Alliance High Council may choose to drastically fine us, or terminate our Member State status entirely."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Bah. What is being more important, money or victory? Fines are nothing, and even losing Member State status is little compared to the Empire winning this war, ja?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "Enough on viruses. We'll decide on that later. Next item of business, the Imperial Generation Project.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Our top scientists are analyzing data sent to us by the Lorithos Spy Network. The new Rigs and tanks of the Empire are formidable, but our Vulture Siege Tanks are still superior. Our fighters are still competitive with theirs, and our GRLD is years ahead of the Kovalski-Fahr Drive.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"In conclusion, our technological edge is slipping. We're superior, but the Mesarthrim Department of Research and Development needs more funding, and we need to overhaul all of our forces."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "I am not liking this at all, nein, not at all. Last time we did an overhaul--during the last year of the HalbAnderung War, ja? Anyway, we had to pull back on all fronts, and it prolonged the war. If we are doing it again, the Empire may be gaining back territory. I say we take Bethija first and dig in there."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Parman: "I think we can afford to break our siege of Bethija and pull back. Our men need rest and our machines need repairs. We can beat the Empire again."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Ja, ja. You would see it that way. After all, you are being a tank jockey!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "We won't pull out of Bethija just yet. We'll wait on an overhaul until we have some significant improvements in technology. For now, we push the campaign forward."

= Part 3 =

April 23, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Bethija=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Empire has suffered another defeat at the hands of the United Mesarthrim Clans. Reports indicate that the 3rd Core Fleet has been routed, and the Imperial ground forces on Bethija have been defeated.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Details are sketchy, but it appears that Rear Admiral Oswald Voss's forces were besieged for over a month before trying to make a break for deep space. The escape plan failed--only a single Imperial carrier escaped the siege.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Carrier Obelisk did not escape the siege. The Obelisk was boarded, and Rear Admiral Voss was captured by the Mesarthrim. Obelisk was taken intact, but the rest of the 3rd Core Fleet was scuttled by the crews. There were no survivors except for the crew of the Obelisk.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim High Command members were able to talk with B-Net associate David Sevall regarding Bethija and Oswald Voss. Only the three military commanders of the High Command had anything to say.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "Rear Admiral Voss is being shipped to a prison camp on Mesar. He will be treated humanely, but he is under suicide watch and he's refusing to eat. Our medics are keeping him fed intravenously.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Voss will be interrogated, and given the opportunity to defect. If he still wishes to return to the Empire, we will include him as a bargaining chip in prisoner of war exchanges."
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Danken Gott. My boys had it rough, but we gave those Imperial keine gehirne ein vollständiges schlagen!"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Parman: "My troops on the ground performed very well. We only met token resistance on the surface, and only had light losses."

= Part 4 =

April 7, 2322

by James Davis

=====Bethija=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;B-Net is on-site aboard the Carrier Obelisk to bring you an interview with Rear Admiral Oswald Voss.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;B-Net: "Admiral, congratulations on taking out the Mesarthrim cruiser Revenge."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Thank you. We did lose a frigate and some fighters, but the gain was worth it."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;B-Net: "Admiral, what do you think your chances of winning are?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Slim. Cruiser for cruiser we outnumber the Mesarthrim, but my frigates are vulnerable to their fighters. If I had anti-fighter support for my cruisers, I'd say we'd win easily."

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Our meeting with Voss was cut short by a Mesarthrim attack on the Obelisk. The 679th Battle Group had launched a concentrated assault on the port flank of Voss' fleet.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Get me a count. How many ships?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Tactical: "Over a thousand. Three battle cruisers, fifteen corvettes, fifteen frigates, and a lot of fighters."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Damn. This could push right through. I want a wing of Ravens and three Invaders to try to split up the fighters. Valkyries prepare to bombard the enemy cruisers."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial fighters weren't successful in scattering their opponents, but they did force a sizeable chunk of them away from the main group.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two squadrons of Valkyries moving at high speed evaded the Mesarthrim fighter screen to attack battle cruisers. The Mesarthrim bombers and the Imperial bombers engaged their opposing cruisers almost simultaneously. The Valkyries unleashed their heavy nukes on the three Mesarthrim battle cruisers. One of the cruisers was torn apart by the blast, and a second was severely damaged. The third escaped harm entirely.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Five Valkyries were destroyed by the remaining cruisers before the bombers could escape. The survivors moved into the dogfight between fighters. The dogfight between Mesarthrim Pattons and Imperial Ravens and Invaders was going well for the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The superior maneuverability and firepower of the Invaders led to the destruction of 30 Pattons in the first few seconds of combat. The Imperial fighters had only lost four Ravens by the time the Valkyries joined the skirmish. The dogfight was decidedly one-sided after that--the Imperial fighters wiped out the Mesarthrim fighters involved in the dogfight, then moved to attack the bombers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim bombers were wreaking havoc on the Vigilant frigates. Two of the Vigilant frigates were broken in pieces. Admiral Voss sent his anti-fighter Orion Class Cruisers to break the fighter cloud apart, regardless of collateral damage to his own forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Orions let loose with their missile pods, firing several hundred kilotons of conventional explosive power into the Mesarthrim fighter cloud. One Vigilant frigate was taken out by the friendly fire, but over 500 Mesarthrim fighters and bombers were destroyed. The Mesarthrim forces retreated. Rear Admiral Voss addressed B-Net and the 3rd Core Fleet.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rear Admiral Voss: "Good work and excellent shooting. I doubt that our Orions will catch the Mesarthrim off guard again though. Damn them, but they're smart fighters."

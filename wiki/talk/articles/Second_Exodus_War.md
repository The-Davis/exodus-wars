The Second Exodus War

     The Second Exodus War became known as the most devastating war in modern galactic history, particularly to the world of Terra. When it finally ended, the planet was completely ruined, and took years to recover.
     This archive contains summaries of each year. Click on each one for more detailed records of that year.

2300-2314 - While the war itself didn't begin until 2315, there were several important developments leading up to it, starting with the Imperial takeover of Alpha Centauri in 2300.

2315 - The Second Exodus War began with the invasion of Terra, and the subsequent fall of the centuries-old United Earth Alliance.

2316 - The year 2316 can only be described as a game of cat and mouse. The cat being a very large cat armed with tactical nuclear weapons, and the mouse being tied down to a world it can't afford to lose.

2317- In the year 2317, the Meroniri, Icronian, and Lorithos allies of the Titanian Colony sent reinforcements to the Titanian System.

2318-2320 - There was an almost four-year lull in the war at this point. Many thought that there would be a diplomatic solution to the conflict, an illusion reinforced by Lucius Black actually meeting with the Alliance of Planets High Council

2321 - The year 2321 saw the war pick up the pace a notch. The Blackis Empire's Omega Squadron forces began daily sabotage and assassination attempts in Titan City, the capital of Titanian 6. Space battles became a nearly daily occurrence. With their Icronian and Lorithos allies completely afraid of the Blackis Empire, and the powerful Meroniri military still refusing to commit to the war effort, it looked like the Titanian Defense Force was on its own.

2322 - The military superiority of the Empire was lost forever in 2322. The United Mesarthrim Clans, a forgotten colony from Centus, returned to the galactic arena.

2323 - The year 2323 saw a long halt in hostilities between the warring factions. Terra was freed, and Lucius Black's loyalist forces were sent reeling, and were finally chased from all inhabited regions of space. Many thought this was the end of the Second Exodus War.

2324 - The true end of the Second Exodus War was February 4th, 2324, when the last remnant of the Royal Imperial Navy was attacked by the Prefecture Second Fleet.

===Build Notes===

After the first minor refit of the Stuka, I wasn't happy with it.  I wanted better landing gear, no colored lasers, and a much fiercer-looking arsenal.  This new version has proper wheels for landing gear, and a lot more weapons.  I was tempted to add a co-pilot, but decided against it because I like single-man fighters more.

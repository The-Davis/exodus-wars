== ZPF tech ==

Seriously, though, Jamal wrote this when we envisioned most Ikronin fighters having Ion drives (pushers) not Gravitic drives.  They should ALL have gravity drives!  The Ikronin aren't the most powerful or advanced, no, but they are up there.  Their drive tech should be pretty good.

The armor is Reigenjord (play on Megingjord, the belt of Thor), a powered armor suit which boosts strength and agility, and is light compared to most other armors. It isn't a powerhouse like a Grendel or an ARROWS, but is instead more like a Prefecture powered armor suit, only 3x as effective. Jiliur's personal weapon is the Korfangr, a battle rifle based around two energy weapon technologies. One is the similar to the Lansvapen weapon used by the Icronians: a deep penetrating, highly accurate beam weapon. The other is similar to the Blazer weapon used by the Mesarthrim: a low penetrating, highly concussive/explosive bolt weapon. Both can be fired in semi automatic or automatic. The rifle can work with a battery for energy, but can also hook up to the Reigenjord for a significant boost in ammunition and a reduction in weight. It has a removeable scope with sniper and dark-vision optics, but works best when its mated to Jiliur's armor, which supplies its own targetting and optics system. These weapons make Jiliur a deadly anti-infantry fighter, and an effective anti-vehicle fighter. For purposes of numbers/comparison, Jiliur is as effective as a squad (12) against infantry, and as effective as a fire team (4) against armor. Basically, I want a little flavor text for Reigen heroes, as I intend to make them very focused on nobility, honor, and deeds of legend. Not combat honor like Mesarthrim, but courtesy and valor, and the unsung honor of humility in the face of an insult. If you were to insult a Mesarthrim to the point that he'd deck you, a Reigen would take it with quiet dignity and feel no shame.

The warship is the Naglfar, the "ship of the dead" of Reigen legends. While most of the ship is of Reigen design, some parts of it were stripped from the hull of a sleeper ship used in their long journey from Kronheim, and the power core is an Anthronoris antimatter reaction chamber (no better than any other reactor, just the stuff of legends). The ship is only marginally better than any other heavy cruiser, but the crew is hand-picked, and the ship's computer core is Njorl, an AI grown from the matrix of Prism, given to the Reigens in "A Grand Affair."

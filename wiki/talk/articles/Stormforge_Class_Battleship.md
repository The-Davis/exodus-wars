== Ok, that makes sense... ==

Ok, so I was apparently reading your post incorrectly.  You're saying that the Mesarthrim Federation assisted the Prefecture to...help them run the Alliance of Planet blockades?  That makes a bit more sense.  In that case, it would be the Meroniri who would be issuing a rebuke.

As a clarification: there was no merchant raiding during the Oronus Incident, except as alleged by the CIN against the Alliance of Planets Space Police (the whole premise for the incident, in fact).  The CIN wasn't raiding Alliance shipping, nor even pursuing action in Alliance space.  The only target of the sovereignty patrols were the APSP interdiction forces patrolling non-Alliance space (read: CIN space).  

One problem to note here: the Oronus Incident occurred in 2326, about 6 months prior to the start of the Tempest War, 2 years prior to the alliance between the Auellel and the Prefecture, ~4 years before the Ikronin entered the war in full (thus originating the Tripartite Alliance), and a full 6 years before the Mesarthrim Federation even came into existence.  Thus, it would be impossible for the MF to receive a rebuke, as they didn't yet exist.

= Part 1 =

October 13, [[2322]]

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim forces under the command of Commandants Arnold Stadt and Ben Parman have liberated two planets--Kalmar and Oronus. Both of these planets are manufacturing worlds of the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the loss of the Kalmar Shipyard and the defending cruisers there, Kalmar fell in a day. The 16th Core Fleet at Oronus was a small fleet--easily defeated. Mesarthrim soldiers were able to quickly knock out Oronus' garrison forces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;While the Remnant--which is known to be on the prowl somewhere--attacking anywhere is a big risk, but so far the gambles of the Mesarthrim have paid off. Both planets are under the firm control of the Mesarthrim High Command.

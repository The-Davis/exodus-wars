= Precursor =

January 8 to 15, 2324

by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

=====January 8=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Today Uber-General Andreas Tischler is scheduled to meet with top United Archadian Republics government officials, including the Archadian President, Drimont Mandara. They plan to discuss a possible extension of existing treaties, and the construction of a jointly-owned defense fleet designed to counter the looming alien threat. It is hoped that the leaders will reach a beneficial economic, political, and military agreement by the end of the five-day summit.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Andreas Tischler will be accompanied by General Stadt, General Hinks, and their top aides. Their escort will be entirely comprised of Archadian fighters. No Mesarthrim armed forces will actually land on Glengarr. This tremendous show of faith is a strong indication of the Uber-General's trust in the United Archadian Republics.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Top officials say that the joint-fleet construction plan will probably involve a rapid joint R&D effort with close cooperation between Mesarthrim and Archadian scientists.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This short-term project will combine the strengths of the two different technology bases, and create hybrid technologies previously impossible. Uber-General Tischler hopes to use this project to build a larger, more powerful space and ground force so that future incursions can be met with overwhelming force.

=====January 9=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The technology summit's prelude began today with Uber-General Tischler's delegation fleet being ceremoniously welcomed to United Archadian Republics space.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Uber-General gave a short speech, saying that he was "inspired by the good faith of the Archadians" and "looking forward to meeting fellow warriors."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Archadian President Drimont responded in kind, noting that "this new relationship should help the Republic and Mesarthrim Clans to defend against hostile forces throughout the galaxy."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Both parties will land on Glengarr's surface at 13:00 local time. B-Net will continue to have updates about the progress of the summit.

=====January 12=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After over 72 hours of closed negotiations, both the United Archadian Republics and United Mesarthrim Clans leaders have released preliminary statements.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;First, jointly-owned industrial complex will be built on Tincomalee. Tincomalee had a Mesarthrim industrial complex in 2323, but it was destroyed by a Remnant warship during the Second Exodus War. The new array of mines, factories, orbital shipyards, and fabrication centers will be entirely devoted to the creation of special military equipment.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Second, an inter-government research program will be formed and funded. Scientists from the United Archadian Republics and United Mesarthrim Clans have been authorized to discuss classified technologies, in hopes of improving and combining them. The newly formed Committee of Research and Development reports that at least thirteen previously unsolvable technological problems can be solved by hybrid devices.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Third, and most importantly, a jointly-commanded battle division will be built with new, next-generation technology. This fleet will be used as defense against invasion.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At the end of the meeting, Mesarthrim Uber-General Andreas Tischler released a short statement. "Although I cannot release all of the details of our joint projects, I can say that they will bring 'strength through unity'." The alliance between the United Archadian Republics and the United Mesarthrim Clans will greatly help our two nations."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;United Archadian Republics President Mandara also released a statement. "Our joint plans will increase not only the military capabilities of the Clans and the Republics, but will help bring prosperity to all worlds in the Mesarthrim and Archadian territories."

=====January 15=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In a sudden, drastic turn of events, Uber-General Andreas Tischler and his delegates were called away from the Joint Technology Summit.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The entire Mesarthrim delegation left Glengarr early in the morning, barely taking time to go through the appropriate security clearances. Several Archadian civilian transports had their schedules bumped back in order to give the delegation a safe FTL launch window.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;United Mesarthrim Clans and United Archadian Republics officials both declined to comment on the nature of the sudden withdrawal of the Mesarthrim delegation, but B-Net News sources believe that anti-Archadian factions in Mesarthrim territory have become violent.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Up until now, there has been a vocal, yet peaceful unification movement within the Mesarthrim worlds. They don't trust the Archadians because they have declined to become a member of the Alliance of Planets, but have merely signed peace agreements.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A demonstration by this unification movement became a riot early in the evening of January 14th, but B-Net sources believed that Mesarthrim police had the situation under control. Uber-General Tischler's sudden return home leads us to believe that this is not the case.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This is all just speculation until official word is given by the United Mesarthrim Clans and the United Archadian Republics.

= Part 1 =

January 19, 2324

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It is normally against the policy of B-Net News to report rumors, but the severity of the reports coming in dictates a temporary change in policy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;According to rumors, Uber-General Andreas Tischler, the commander of the Mesarthrim military and an influential member of the Alliance of Planets is dead.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Our currently unconfirmed reports say that the Uber-General died of unnatural causes or was killed some time in the past two days. None of the members of his envoy were within contact with B-Net sources.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Oddly enough, these reports did not come through normal B-Net channels within the United Mesarthrim Clans or the United Archadian Republics.  They came from sources within Prefecture territory.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Most people scoff at the idea that the Uber-General is dead. He's survived too many difficult situations before.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Though his death seems unlikely, it is most unusual that none of the involved governments have commented at all.

= Part 2 =

January 20, 2324

by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

=====Mesar=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim High Command confirmed this morning that Uber-General Andreas Tischler is dead.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At this time, government officials haven't released any details, claiming that they have none to release. MHC representatives say they expect to have more information in a few days.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Our nation is greatly saddened by this loss," says General Nathan Hinks, the Tischler's closest friend and adviser. "He was a great man, a fierce warrior, and a loving brother."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Rumors abound, and suspicions fester. Some anti-unification factions on Mesar blame agents of the Prefecture or the United Archadian Republics. Others blame Imperial agents, some blame the believed-extinct HalbAnderung, and still more blame the whole thing on a simple accidental shuttle crash.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Unfortunately, B-Net cannot lend credence to any theories. Our own sources have not even been able to find out where the Uber-General was when he died, nor have we been able to get any information from any governments of the Alliance of Planets.

= Part 3 =

January 21, 2324

by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

=====Stone Bridge=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Riots broke out in the Mesar capital in the early pre-dawn hours today. Police forces are hard pressed to keep anti-Archadian rioters from burning the Mesarthrim High Command Headquarters to the ground, let alone quell the riots.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With rumors of Archadian and Prefecture involvement in the death of Uber-General Tischler, the groups against joining the Alliance of Planets have doubled in size. Andreas Tischler was very popular among the lower and middle-income civilians.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Right now we know for certain that the Uber-General's famous Beta Commando Team was with Tischler when he died. Beta Commando was involved in a joint-effort with the United Archadian Republic's Special Tactics Assault Commando team in a recent armed conflict, but details of this particular engagement are unavailable.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Currently, some recently-discharged Mesarthrim commandos are siding with the anti-unification movement, claiming that Archadians gave them false information leading to an ambush of the Uber-General's Beta Team. Two of these former commandos have been arrested for sedition by Mesarthrim Military Police, and are undergoing hearings right now in MHC Headquarters. Some blame this arrest and hearing for the riots going on outside the very same building.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The effects of Uber-General Tischler's death are just beginning to unfold. There's talk of an armed delegation from the Alliance of Planets capitol to help quell the riots and smooth out relations between the United Mesarthrim Clans and the United Archadian Republics.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefect of the Federated Districts is calling for blood. Prefect Hadrian and General-Commander Sanburn of the Titanian Planetary Alliance are both in favor of an Alliance of Planets investigation of Tischler's death. Likewise, they each want to personally execute whoever masterminded the plot. Hadrian, Sanburn and Sears were known to be fast friends in the days of the Battle for Terra.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim High Command itself is also having difficulties. The position of Uber-General has a lot of power and responsibility, and in the past the position has been filled by whoever was best able to win wars, not politics.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The power vacuum has not caused any visible struggles or power bids within the military or bureaucracy, but dividing the responsibilities of the Uber-General on a temporary basis has been very difficult. Right now, Eric Francis, the Mesarthrim High Command Director of Naval Intelligence is handling most of the military public relations.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Francis has been working nonstop since the riots began, but he did release a brief statement to B-Net. "This is a very trying time for the United Mesarthrim Clans. We need to be cautious and not drive rashly towards war with our neighbors. Andreas Tischler was a great warrior, but he knew when to put down his rifle and talk things through. The mob outside these offices isn't honoring his memory by tearing down the cities he fought to preserve."

January 27, 2324

by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

     General Marcolitae Ramius released previously classified communications logs to the public today. This is a drastic change in policy. STAC communications are usually restricted to the highest members of the RhovaDeviri Warrior Caste, and never seen by civilians, but Ramius decided that he needed support from members of the Alliance of Planets. B-Net reporters on Glengarr have this transcript from Ramius' speech. 

     "Alliance of Planets, I bid you greetings. In the aftermath of Operation Clean Sweep--our name for the operation which ended with Andreas Tischler's death--our investigators were denied access to vitally important Mesarthrim communications logs.
     "The logs of the STAC teams have traditionally been destroyed within a few months of an operation, and are never seen outside of the RhovaDeviri Warrior Caste, but for the sake of the lives of Captain Verado and his team, we are releasing all of the information we have available.
     "During the information-gathering phase of the operation, Verado's STAC team was in constant communication with Mesarthrim Naval Intelligence. He reported the heavy Imperial resistance, both ground and naval forces. Verado's reports clearly show the Imperial ground brigade and infantry regiment, and the presence of Vampire fighter squadrons.
     "MNI reported that large numbers of Mesarthrim Navy and Army forces would be sent to deal with the Imperial presence, and General Nathan Hinks' fleet arrived soon afterwards. Verado transmitted the Imperial numbers, types and positions to Hinks' fleet.
     "The Mesarthrim Fleet reported that Beta Commando was being dropped in, and the fleet requested that Verado's STAC team move three kilometers away to feed firing solutions to Mesarthrim Navy bombers. Verado tried to warn off the Beta Commando landing. It was too close to SAM sites and a large portion of the infantry regiment. Verado's abort transmission received no response from the fleet.
     "Verado decided to overrule the Mesarthrim Fleet request for firing solutions, and moved to assist Beta Commando. There was a short blackout in communications between the logging of this decision of his next report. The STAC team deployed on a ridge above the Beta Commando landing zone, above the firefight between the Royal Imperial Army infantry and the Mesarthrim commandos.
     "The STAC team sent firing solutions on the SAM sites that were blocking the rescue of Beta Commando, but these transmissions also went unanswered. The Mesarthrim Fleet ignored these firing solutions, instead recklessly endangering their own bomber pilots on hot strafing runs.
     "Our team was hopelessly outnumbered, and equipped for stealthy sabotage and air-strike guidance, not engaging superior numbers of armored mobile infantry. With their air-strike data being ignored, they stayed down until the Beta Commando team was eventually evacuated by transport. Then Verado's team worked their way back to their own transport and escaped.
     "Verado was detained by Mesarthrim military police immediately after escaping the Imperial asteroid. We have submitted all records of the engagements, and as anyone with the proper equipment can tell, the voiceprints match our STAC team's voices exactly. These cannot be falsified. Therefore, I demand the immediate release of Captain Milnek Verado and his team, and the return of their equipment. Furthermore, I demand the withdrawal of Mesarthrim vessels from Archadian space."

     B-Net cannot comment on the validity of the claims of either side in this confrontation, but we find it most interesting that General Hinks' report and General Ramius' report practically contradict each other word for word in regards for blame.
     We are receiving a transmission from David Sevall, our correspondent on Mesar.

     Sevall: "Ladies and gentlemen, the riots we had six days ago are nothing compared to this. I'm inside the Mesarthrim High Command Headquarters, and the crowd outside is burning the city to the ground. Only the Myt'gaul and Myt'herra troops in the courtyard are keeping the mob away.
     "I'm not sure exactly what the rioters want. I'm guessing that some believe Ramius, and some believe Hinks. Wait, wait. General Hinks is about to deliver a statement."

     Hinks: "People of United Mesarthrim Clans, ladies and gentlemen of the press, and friends from the Alliance of Planets, greetings.
    "I would have thought that the shame of the treachery of their commandos would have silenced our so-called Archadian friends, but it seems that they need to rub salt in our wounds. I commanded the fleet supporting Uber-General Tischler's Beta Commando team. Commandants Ben Parman and Arnold Stadt both commanded ships within the fleet, and all communications were monitored by Director Eric Francis of Mesarthrim Naval Intel.
     "The reputations of my colleagues are above reproach. Parman and Stadt are veterans of the Second Exodus War and the Imperial Civil War, and Eric Francis is a personal friend of our late beloved leader.
     "Ramius, how dare you! You sully your own race with these ridiculous accusations."

     Sevall: "The opinions expressed by General Hinks are not necessarily the opinions of this station."

     Despite his outrage, General Nathan Hinks did recall all Mesarthrim merchant vessels from Archadian space, but sent more warships to the border.
     At this point, the other Member States are strangely quiet. The Prefecture Second Fleet on the border hasn't twitched a proverbial muscle, and the Prefecture capitol of Ueda hasn't sent a thing to B-Net, APNET, or the Archadian News Networks.
     The Titanians and Meroniri both offered to send mediators, but beyond that, they haven't said anything publicly.

The Archadian Space Navy has also mobilized its four fleets and moved them to their border outposts.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture has also moved their Second Fleet to the Prefecture/Mesarthrim border, to "perform military exercises," they claim. This many ships in one sector could lead to violence, despite the official non-hostile stance between all three nations.

     The Alliance of Planets is meeting on Sepulcre. APNET reporter Michelle Jarvens is on the scene.

The members of the High Council have been in their closed door meeting for twenty hours now since receiving the contradicting reports from both the United Mesarthrim Clans and United Archadian Republics.

Both sides have charismatic and popular leaders, and taking them out would lead to strife throughout the Alliance.

It took them several hours to get landing clearance from Mesarthrim aerospace traffic control, and several more hours to get security clearance from General Hinks' security teams. By the time they had full access to the disastrous landing zone of Beta Commando, they only had two hours to investigate the site before the Mesarthrim teams closed down the whole operation and cleaned out the battle debris.

We cannot conclude where and when these recordings were made, but it is the opinion of our military officers that the background noise was genuine weapons fire, and not faked.

     "We will not stand idle while two allies destroy the bonds we've worked so hard to create. We must stop this crisis before we have another interstellar war on our hands."

= Part 7 =

January 26, 2324 

by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

=====Mesar=====

     Six days after the United Mesarthrim Clans began its investigation, and four days after the Alliance of Planets and United Archadian Republics investigators joined the effort, the teams have released their findings to the public.

     General Nathan Hinks called a conference with B-Net News and other networks this afternoon. The last of the riots were quelled the morning before, so the conference at the Mesarthrim High Command Headquarters was undisturbed.
     "Ladies and gentlemen, I have dire news, and bring word of treachery. Our investigators have uncovered all of the details of the events that transpired on January 16th, 2324, the black day that saw the death of Uber-General Tischler.
     "On January 15th, Mesarthrim Naval Intelligence alerted the Uber-General of the location of a large Imperial fleet hidden in the Picket's Grave asteroid belt, and the existence of a moon-sized asteroid with an artificial atmosphere and a warship maintenance facility. Uber-General Tischler planned to assault the asteroid, but our own intelligence was unsure of the amount of Imperial troops on the ground, so the United Archadian Republics sent in a Special Tactics and Assault Commando team to assess the situation.
     "The STAC team reported minimal static defenses and only light Rigs and few infantry on the surface. Naval Intelligence determined that our own Beta Team and the Archadian STAC team could take the installation. Uber-General Tischler and I prepared a two-pronged assault. The Uber-General and Beta Commando would link up with the STAC team and take the installation, while my fleet drew away the Imperial ships in orbit.
     "When Tischler arrived in the Picket's Grave system, he asked the STAC team for an update. They reported only light resistance to their probing attacks, and no anti-aircraft defenses at all. Beta Commando began their landing.
     "Uber-General Tischler and Beta Commando were dropped via orbital drop pods. Their pods came under heavy anti-aircraft fire the moment they entered the atmosphere, and Beta Commando took 10% casualties before they even hit the ground. Within five minutes of landing, the wounded commandos were recovered from their downed drop pods, and Beta Commando had dug in.
     "Heavy Imperial Rig forces and an entire brigade of infantry were breathing down their necks. The Archadian STAC team was still nearby, but they ignored calls to assist Uber-General Tischler and Beta Commando. Anti-aircraft fire was too fierce to allow for pickup from the Mesarthrim fleet.
     "Mesarthrim bombers managed to take out enough anti-aircraft to allow for air evacuation. We lost one bomber and pilot in the attack. I managed to send in a transport and some fighters for air support.
     "By the time the transport had reached Beta Commando, the Imperial ground forces had reached their landing zone and were attacking. The wounded commandos were brought aboard first. Uber-General Tischler insisted on boarding the transport last.
     "In the firefight between Beta Commando and the Royal Imperial Army, Andreas Tischler was shot seven times. Three solid rounds and two laser rounds penetrated his body armor, but did not kill him or disable him. He made it aboard the transport and they escaped the atmosphere.
     "Six Imperial Vampires jumped them between the asteroid and the Mesarthrim fleet. Our own fighters managed to shoot down four of them and chased off the other two, the delay cost us our commander.
     "Uber-General Tischler was unconscious from blood loss when he reached my command ship. He died of excessive bleeding, despite the efforts of our best medics.
     "Our losses were severe. We had two surface deaths, Uber-General Tischler and bomber pilot Captain Ryan McClellan. Most of Beta Commando is injured, and six of them will require regenerative surgery to be mobile again.
     "The Archadian STAC team was taken into custody by Mesarthrim military police. Their commander, one Captain Milnek Verado, is believed to be the brains behind the collaboration with the Empire, but the rest of the STAC team will be questioned by a military tribunal before they are sentenced or returned to the United Archadian Republics, depending on our findings. Captain Verado will be held indefinitely, pending the investigation of a suspected Imperial mole within the Mesarthrim government.
     "Thank you for your time, this conference is at an end."
     General Hinks wouldn't answer any questions, and promptly cleared the assembly area.

     Within ten minutes, the Archadian President Mandara had a response.
     "Archadians, friends of the Republics, I am outraged by these false accusations. Nathan Hinks kept our investigators from assisting in the operation, and has either lied to you, or been told lies by his underlings. Captain Verado is a hero, and a trusted officer. Our STAC teams are above reproach, and they have proven their enmity with the Empire in dozens of covert operations.
     "The United Archadian Republics stands behind our commandos, and we demand their immediate and safe return."
     President Mandara answered several questions for APNET and the Archadian News Network, but our B-Net News representative had no questions for the President.

Voiceprint analysis confirmed that the Imperial collaborator was none other than General Nathan Hinks. He will be court marshaled by Mesarthrim Supreme Justice tomorrow."

     David Sevall: "And there you have it. Now we know why the Archadian and Alliance of Planets investigation teams were blocked from participating in the examination of the Operation Clean Sweep landing zone.
     "This is a bittersweet end to a tragedy, but at least the mastermind behind Uber-General Tischler's murder will be brought to justice."

= Part 9 =

Treachery Abounds, Hinks Escapes - 1/31/2324 - by Andrew Sears and Austin Bailey

     David Sevall: "Good morning ladies and gentlemen. You may be receiving this on a delayed-transmission, but it is 0400 hours local time here in the Mesarthrim capitol. The High Command Headquarters is under assault, apparently by traitors loyal to Nathan Hinks and possibly to the Empire as well.
     "Director Eric Francis and Headquarters Security have called for reinforcements, and they should arrive soon. It seems that this little uprising is doomed from the start.
     "Director Francis! Can you answer a few questions for B-Net News?"

     Eric Francis: "Out of my way. Hinks is escaping. Keep clear of the fighting, and cut that transmission, we're in a security lockdown!"

     David Sevall: "Yes sir. Ladies and gentlemen, we will be cutting transmission and giving you our professional analysis from the B-Net newsroom."

B-Net Newsroom - 1000 Hours Mesarthrim Time
     As far as we can tell, at 0355 hours, a hundred traitorous members of the Mesarthrim Myt'herra from various divisions attacked the MHC Headquarters using a combination of heavy infantry weapons and two Light Assault Vehicles.
     Using heavy weapons, they blasted a large hole in the west wall of the Headquarters, and stormed through the building. The automatic defenses and Headquarters Security were outgunned and soon routed. The High Command was moved to bunkers and locked down for safety, but the traitors moved for the brig, where General Hinks was being held for his trial.
     Before the security systems in the brig were destroyed, Director Francis' forces were able to confirm that Hinks had escaped.
     At 0409 hours, Commandant Arnold Stadt, EFC Sullivan, and six hundred loyal Myt'herra from the Black Dogs division surrounded MHC Headquarters, effectively cutting off the escape route of the traitors.

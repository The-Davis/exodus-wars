= Part 1 =

by James Davis

===== Stability =====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Inquiry. Stability achieved''?<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Affirmation. Infinity terminated. Arrival complete.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Directive. Create chaos. Sow discord. Diversion required.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Inquiry. Forces permitted? Losses acceptable?''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Command. Fourwings airstrike. Oneclaw stationstrike. Complete expendability. Coordinates necessary. Acquisition imminent.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Silently, ominously, the vast Volucris Swarm approaches its target. Human minds tremble at the sight of such great numbers. The stars themselves may not be as numerous as the deadly Swarm.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As suddenly as they arrived, the vast bulk of the Swarm fades out of view, out of existence. Human intellect cannot fathom the complexity of Volucris travel. The concept boggles. [[Subspace]]? [[Hyperspace]]? Toys of humankind. The Volucris slip beyond the bounds of human physics, hiding in dimensions only dreamed about by the insane.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The remaining Volucris glide on, still bound to real-space, still subject to the laws of physics. Their target is chosen. Their minds are honed for war. They are coming.

===== Ueda =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Bright is the desert jewel, clear is the purpose. [[Ueda]], the heart of the [[Prefecture]], hangs like a perfect topaz in the blackness of space.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The system is bursting with activity. Hundreds of civilian vessels go to and from the huge [[Ikronin]] [[Wormhole Gates]], speeding the commercial lifeblood of the galaxy to the hundreds of marketplaces and billions of eager consumers. Powerful warships lurk in the shadow of the Ueda moon, keeping silent vigil over the unwary populace. Tiny fighter-craft dart about, scanning for contraband.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ant steps in the people-nest.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Swarms appear. First in ones and twos, then in thousands and tens of thousands, the Volucris pour into Ueda. Civilian freighters panic, jumping out of system through hyperspace, ignoring the stern command of the military.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Amusement. Humans unorganized. Flickersenses inefficient. Volucris conquer.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fear reigns. Warships materialize, some from the Wormhole Gates, some from nothing at all. Everyone within range is responding--not since the battle for [[Earth]] has a capital world been directly threatened by a powerful foe.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Precognition. Quiddity disruption. Humans wary. Caution demanded.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before the Volucris Shredder Drones can crush themselves against the human warships, hundreds are burned, then irradiated down to their basic elements. The Swarm scatters, reeling in confusion.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Alarm! Humans track! Time reduced. Advance quickly.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Organization comes naturally to Volucris. They congeal into a cohesive unit once again, then fling themselves in a solid spearhead towards the human fleet. Warships shudder under the blows, and fighters are blasted to pieces by Shivas, Shredder Drones, and Black Claws. Energy is unleashed on a terrifying scale.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Directive. Size required. Stabilize largeunit.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before the astonished eyes of the human forces, Volucris warships materialize out of nothing. The surprise and devastation are incredible. Lances of plasma practically fill the void of space.

===== The Gate =====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Shut it down! Shut it down!" men scream as bugs pour into the control offices. "Where are the troops?"<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Blood flies through the air. An Icronian is disemboweled and messily devoured by a Trapper. Ikronin technicians fire side-arms, and one powers up one of the power saws used by tundra dwellers.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Trapper is followed by Cutters, and a Bombardier. The last Icronian standing hits a short series of buttons before he dies, writhing in agony.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Directive. Return coordinates. Allow destruction. Leave soon.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The bugs part to allow a Borrower on a Nymph to access the computers. The soldiers quickly scramble for the exit, the Borrower/Nymph leading the charge.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Suddenly, the air rushes out of the Gate control center. The hole the bugs bored into the hull is no longer sealed, and a Black Claw floats silently in the void.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Borrower spits a wad of viscous fluid onto the Black Claw. The flyer turns, then burns away at astonishing speed.<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Satisfaction. Coordinates secure.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Wormhole Gate explodes. Ikronin don't share, even if it means dying.

===== Instability =====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Inquiry. Objective found''?<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Clarity. Directive shift. Destination found.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Command. Withdraw now. Losses acceptable. Motion required. Begin infinity.''<br/>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The endless Swarms of Volucris fade from sight, and Ueda is soon calm, though lacking one of its Gates.

= Part 2 =

July 1, [[2324]]

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The combined efforts of the Meroniri Expeditionary Fleet and the Prefecture have failed to reveal any sign of the Volucris Swarms since their April 10th attack on Ueda. Some xenobiologists believe that their three attacks on human worlds have simply been part of their migration pattern, and that they are gone for good. Others believe that they are intelligent and malevolent, and that this disappearance is part of a grander scheme.

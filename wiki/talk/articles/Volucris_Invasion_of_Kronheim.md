= Part 1 =

March 18, 2325

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After months of inactivity, the Volucris Swarms have struck again. A convoy owned by Jaytex Mechanized Unlimited was attacked while it was en route to Kronheim from Ueda. The attack occurred in the Wormhole Gate system itself, not in normal space. Five out of thirty freighters survived the attack.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This disturbing development explains the Volucris' unique method of travel, but makes interstellar commerce a lot more risky than before. Fortunately, the Prefecture's new SPAR system (optical Sensors with PAttern Recognition) makes fighter combat against the Volucris a much less dangerous prospect. At close range, fighter pilots can track and destroy most Volucris. The SPAR system is interconnected via the Ansible Network, and can analyze and update patterns for new breeds in less than five minutes.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Ikronin officials are recommending avoiding unarmed Gate travel, and recommend a two fighter escort per freighter.

= Part 2 =

March 27, 2325

by James Davis

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture has responded to the renewal of the Volucris threat by mobilizing the First Fleet and the Legion of the Dragon to Kronheim. Prefecture strategists believe that the Volucris find their destinations by analyzing Wormhole Gates--a theory that is given credence by their attacks on Mirach, Tavou, and Ueda.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Second Fleet elements have been dispatched to every Prefecture world on the Ueda hub, but Admiral Robert Marshall of the First Fleet believes that since only the Kronheim Gate was attacked, only Kronheim is at risk.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Third Fleet and Legion of the Phoenix will mobilize in a few days to reinforce Admiral Robert Marshall and General Hal Stewart. The Prefecture's remaining Fleets and Legions are currently deployed throughout the Districts, but will be mustered to Ueda within a week of any strike on Kronheim.

= Part 3 =

March 28, 2325

by James Davis

=====Bannock - 0600 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Case! Finally got your transfer from the ''Frankie K''?." Ensign Ray "Trace" Verner grins at his new flight member.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ensign Verner." Ensign Cassiopeia "Case" Blanch nods curtly, and takes her seat in the briefing room.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Aw, Case is all business after getting back from a fleet carrier!" Officer Bevel laughs. "Get off your high horse girl. This is an escort carrier, not a flagship."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ten-shun!" the chatter instantly stops as the squadron's second-in-command, Lieutenant [[Matthias Carter]], struts into the room. The huge man with an ego to match is the nails in the whip that drives Squadron 1-27F22. "Captain, your pilots are ready for briefing."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Captain [[Charlene Rearden]] saunters into the briefing room. Rearden is the quintessential combat pilot: short, muscular, and shaved bald. Her loose-fitting jumpsuit is immaculately clean, and her flight helmet is held under one arm. "Simple op," she announces. "One flight will be moving to the Wormhole Gate to meet a convoy. The top brass have decided to sit in orbit of Kronheim for the long haul, and these freighters will be bringing in much-needed supplies for our troops.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Flight Two and 1-27F21 will be standing by to reinforce us if something happens. You have one hour to suit up and submit load-out requests for your fighters. Dismissed." The pilots file out of the ready room.

=====Bannock Flight Deck - 0700 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"1-27F22-1, you are cleared for take-off. Your callsign for this mission is Angel One. Contact Frequency Twelve for mission airboss." The flight control system continues relaying callsigns and orders to the remaining Piranhas in 1-27F-22 Flight One.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Piranhas streak away from the ''Bannock'', leaving the Prefecture First Fleet and Kronheim far behind.

=====Ueda-Kronheim Gate, Yngard System, Far Orbit - 0730 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The convoy of Bulk Transports exits the Gate, flanked by Valkyries and pursued by Shivas. The [[Ikronin]] defense stations activate, targeting and destroying each Shiva in turn. The Transports continue their flight path, but the Valkyries turn aside to land in one of the Gatekeeper stations.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Welcome to Kronheim, Convoy Six." Captain Rearden greets the Transport crews. "Continue on your course to the ''Imposis''. Any trouble in the Gate?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Just a couple Shivas. Our escort kept them off our backs until we got here. Transport Thirty took a couple hits, but no major damage. It was only food, and we have plenty of that."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The forty transports clear the area defended by the Gatekeepers, and accelerate towards Kronheim. "Half an hour flight time, right?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Affirmative."

=====Yngard System, Interplanetary Space - 0745 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Trace and Loops fly ahead of the convoy, running point defense while the rest of the flight hangs back with the convoy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"1-27F22-6 here. I've got a bogie on SPAR." Trace reports. "What's the word, Charlie?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"RATA that Trace. Probably just FOD, but let's be sure." Captain Rearden replies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Roger." Trace's Piranha breaks away from Loops, who continues her own recon pattern.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Moments later, Trace reports. "Um, boss, you'd better take a look at this. It ain't natural, but SPAR doesn't recognize it."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A second passes as Trace's Piranha sends an optical burst transmission to the rest of the squadron.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Looks like a bug." Rearden offers. "But it's not moving. Big critter."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Something's happening." warns Trace. "My sensors are picking up anomalous readings, all fluctuating wildly. Request permission to go hot."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Hold off a bit. I want you to transmit your sensor log back to the Bannock. Get this on the SPAR database."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Bandits! Bandits! Bug is confirmed hostile, and multiple inbound! They popped out of nowhere!" Trace yells, throwing his Piranha into an evasive maneuver, narrowly avoiding the plasma streams from the suddenly numerous Shivas.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Browning, browning, all weapons hot." Captain Rearden orders, then diverts her attention to her other pilots and the large convoy. "Heads up, we have numerous inbound bandits. Their appearance was sudden enough to warrant a carrier or other means of transportation in the immediate vicinity.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Teeth, Latin, you have the convoy. The rest of you, pick targets of opportunity and gun them down. Don't stray more than fifty klicks from the convoy. Stick with your wing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There is a new unidentified hostile in the area. The SPAR database hasn't updated yet, so you have permission to engage all bogies."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She switches to Frequency Twelve. "Bandits attacking the convoy. Multiple bandits, count unknown. Scramble!"<br>

=====Trace - 0746 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For a few seconds, Trace is on his own as the rest of the squadron accelerates. He cuts acceleration at 800 KPH, then spins the fighter to face the pursuing Shivas. The Piranha continues to fly "backwards."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Trace cuts loose with a Hound, followed by three quick bursts of Neutron Cannon fire. A quick check on his HUD confirms that range is still far too great for the Autocannons. Before he can follow up on his salvo, the Shivas match his new course, and open fire again. Trace yanks back the stick, and throws the throttle wide open. The Piranha streaks up the Z-Axis of its old course, leaving the Shivas confused.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Without hesitating, Trace turns and burns "down" on the scattering bugs, loosing his second Hound. A brief flash on his HUD shows that the first one has killed a Shiva. A close range burst of Autocannon and Neutron Cannon fire obliterates a second.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Proximity sensors give Trace only a second's notice to juke and burn away from his last kill. Shredders are on his tail, burning hard. Opening the throttle wide, Trace hits the 1200 KPH mark before throwing the Piranha into a half dozen evasive moves, bleeding acceleration in the process. A stray plasma bolt scorches his right wing, but the Shredders fly off, their target lost. A second flash on his HUD shows that his second Hound has killed another bug.

=====Charlie - 0747 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Charlie breaks through the cloud of Shivas surrounding the big bugs, and cuts loose with both Wren missiles immediately. Her HUD confirms the kill, and she keeps the throttle at maximum, passing through the angry insects.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On her wing, Case rips through a second big bug with her own Wrens, and manages a quick cannon burst before breaking through the cloud.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighters break off in different directions, each trailed by a pair of Shivas. The bugs keep pace with the Piranhas for a few seconds, then suddenly overshoot as the Piranhas turn and accelerate in the opposite direction. A quick spin and cannon volley later, and each pilot kills one of their pursuing Shivas.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A scant fifteen kilometers away, Loops fires off his Wrens and Hounds, nailing two more of the big bugs. A short text-only optical transmission to Charlie and Case gets their attention, and the squadron leader and her wing zip back through the Shiva cloud escorting the big bug.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the escorts distracted, Loops cuts his throttle, floating around the big bug and unloading his Neutron Cannons and Autocannons into the beast. The creature finally seems to notice him, and begins turning to follow the Piranha. Loops accelerates away as quickly as possible, narrowly avoiding several plasma blasts. Seconds later, he's flying for his life from three Shredders launched by the bug.

=====Latin - 0748 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Latin sighs, checking her sensor readings for the third time since the Captain and the others had left to take the battle to the bugs. Her fighter was moving at a pretty good pace--the engines were idling and inertia was doing the work.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Hey Latin, are you picking up anything odd on the sensors?" Teeth asks. "I got an anomaly here. All of my readings are funny."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Trace said there was an anomalous read right before those bugs popped up. Stay sharp. How far off is our reinforcement?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ten minutes at least."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Whoa, bogie on the scope. Remember the Captain's orders: Browning." Latin lets her targeting system get a lock, then fires. Seconds later, SPAR confirms that the object is a Shiva. A total of ten have popped up out of nowhere, surrounding the convoy.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Teeth sends his Piranha into a spin, launching both Hounds and both Wrens, letting them pick their own targets. He accelerates towards the nearest, cannons blazing. Latin's missile hits, and all four of Teeth's weapons impact on bugs. Their numbers are cut in half mere seconds after attacking.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before they can push their advantage, the Prefecture pilots are forced to evade. The Shivas coordinate their plasma blasts, driving the pilots away from the Bulk Transports.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"1-27F22-2, we are taking fire, please assist!" the frantic call comes from one of the transports. The huge cargo trains lumber along ponderously, some taking damage from plasma and Shredder fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Latin turns tightly, shedding acceleration and losing her pursuer. A quick Wren shot takes out another Shiva, but eight more appear out of nowhere. Damn! Where are they coming from?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Winchester, Winchester. Got my Neutrons only." Teeth's Piranha is locked in a furball with four Shivas. Two are flying erratically and leaking, obviously chewed up at close range by his Autocannons.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"It's 5 to 1 against us! Use ammo conservation!" Latin weaves and dodges incoming plasma streams from all directions, trying to keep a Shiva lined up in her Neutron Cannon sights. It evades one blast, then another, until Latin growls and fires off a Hound. The Shiva disintegrates as its internal bio-furnace is breached by the Hound. Latin pulls up hard to avoid the debris field.

=====Trace - 0750 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Listen boss, you have to blow the big one." Trace yells. He dodges, narrowly missing death by a Shredder, then waits for a moment while his optical communicator realigns with Charlie's Piranha. "I swear to God, there was only the one big one until we got those funny readings. It's transporting in the Shivas somehow."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Roger that Trace," Charlie begins. She's cut off by evasive maneuvers. Trace switches to broad-band microwave to hear the rest. The less secure microwave system can't be cut off by loss of line of sight. "Loops and Case will get it."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Trace turns back towards the convoy, letting his throttle stay open at maximum acceleration. The Piranha passes 5000KPH. Moments later, Trace overshoots the convoy, sheds acceleration and velocity, and turns back to join Latin and Teeth. The confused Shivas are still far behind.

=====Marshall Pattern - Bannock - 0800 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Charlie is the first Piranha down on the Bannock. ''Trace was right. They can't reinforce without the big ones. They don't even think straight without someone in charge''. She sighs as the Piranha taxis under the protective field of the hangar, and waits for the flight crew to check the fighter's EDS before disembarking.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Before long, the entire first flight of 1-27F22 is recovered. Lieutenant Carter and Flight Two are still on flight duty, watching over the convoy as it unloads onto the ''Imposis''. Captain Rearden heads for her quarters, pausing only briefly to check her fighter's EDS--SPAR has been updated, and the big bug is now officially classified as a Kujata. Stupid name, she thinks. Brooks will want to hear about this new attack method. The Captain grumbles at the anticipation of a mountain of paperwork, as the rest of the flight heads off to relax.

=====Aftermath=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Officially, 1-27F22 Flight One was credited with 20 bug kills. The remaining Volucris were quickly mopped up by reinforcements from 1-27F21 and 1-27F22 Flight Two. No cargo was lost.

= Part 4 =

April 2, 2325

by James Davis, Andrew Sears, and Ryan Wilhelm

=====Kronheim Tundra - 2401 Hours=====
     ''Directive. Land safely. Entrench ground. Hunt peoples. Devour knowledge. Return objective''. Floating through the air like the flurries of snow around them, the Volucris drift to the ground. Gossamer strands from their abdomens catch the wind, keeping them from lethal freefall.
     The swarms grow, until they blot out the sky for kilometers around. Unchallenged, they burrow into the permafrost.

=====The Imposis - 2402 Hours=====
     Alarms flash, unheeded on the silent bridge of the cruiser. Bodies lie, twisted and broken about the control systems. Nothing moves.
     "Attention Imposis. Your scanner reports are overdue." A patient voice comes over the communication system. There is a long pause. "Imposis, come in."
     A screech and a buzz fills the bridge, and a hulking Volucris soldier forces its way through the doorway. It crashes up to the console, further mutilating the bodies of the crew as it passes.
     Oblivious to the carnage, the voice continues. "Fleet command needs the reports. Imposis, respond."

=====The Jerome Black - 2405 Hours=====
     Robert Marshall's study of the scanner reports are interrupted by frantic calls from the Ikronin forces on the ground. "Admiral, Jordheim is under attack! Imperator bugs are bombarding the city. We need reinforcements!"
     "What? How'd they get to the planet? We have the whole world covered by warships and defense stations. A pebble couldn't get through." Marshall tears through the reports on his desk, throwing them behind his back as he goes through the pile. "Where's the Imposis report?" He mutters. "I copy, Jordheim. General Carpenter will reinforce."
     Marshall abandons his quarters, and heads for the bridge. He gives orders on the way, and they are automatically picked up and relayed by the hallway intercom systems. "Get General Carpenter to reinforce Jordheim. Send at least ten percent of available fighters for air support. I need the Imposis scanner reports."

=====Jordheim Tundra - 2425 Hours=====
     Kommendör Kreig Jansen stands tall in his Gamätande. The cockpit is open, and he surveys the field while the icy wind whips his face. His stark white hair flies freely, his combat helmet held at his side. "I know you are out there!" he calls. "I know you understand us. No army has ever taken Jordheim. No army has ever fought the Consortium on its own turf and survived! Turn back now, and leave us in peace."
     At first, only silence answers him. Then a soft buzz can be heard over the breeze. It grows, then the ground begins to rumble. Jansen smiles, buckling on his helmet. "Krig Domderas, befrämja Krig Stridgrupp. Klyva sig Kompani för först teckna Volucris!" ((Tank Commanders, advance by Battalion. Split into companies at the first sign of Volucris))
     The buzz and rumble is soon contested by the high-pitched whine of Icronian hover tanks coming online. Hundreds of white and blue war machines float centimeters from the ground. Their hulls shimmer as the repulsor shields slowly cycle through their pre-assigned frequencies.
     Far across the field, the swarms become visible. Thousands upon thousands march, run, fly, or burrow their hideous trail towards the shining Icronian city. Only the single tank Regemente and Kommendör Jansen stands in their way.
     Jansen draws first blood. "Mesk Bränning...rikt Imperators...siktar...avfyra!" ((Rig Destroyers, target Imperators. Aim. Fire.)) The Mesk Brännings fire, one after another. The huge Imperators, looming over the rest of the swarms, make easy targets. Several dozen fall under the barrage, crushing smaller insects underneath them.
     "Befrämja!" Jansen roars, gunning his Gamätande into high speed. The Regemente's Gamätandes and Elsvådas streak away from the slower Mesk Brännings, charging into battle.

=====Outside the Imposis - 2430 Hours=====
     "Knock-knock!" Sergeant Hap Barlow raps on an emergency docking hatch before plugging his armor's computer interface into the door's control panel. "OK Marines, we can only fit six in the airlock at a time, so I want you to secure the other side right away. Check your targets before you fire, this could just be a massive communications failure."
     The door slides open, and six Marines float from the Beluga dropship to the Imposis. Their sealed power armor protecting them from the harsh lack of an environment. In moments, they pass the sergeant and enter the warship.
     A moment later, the airlock cycles the Marines inside, then opens to the vacuum again. "It's all clear in here, Sarge. Nothing in sight."
     "Right. Hold position until the rest of us are inside." Sergeant Barlow orders. "Grendels first, two at a time."
     The Marine inside calls again. "There's a network terminal in here, just down the hall. I can just put in a call to ship security to find out what's going on."
     "No." Barlow replies. "Our orders are to assume that the ship has been compromised. If it has been, we'll just tip off whoever's responsible."
     Two by two, the Grendels are off-loaded onto the Imposis. Twenty in all, the battle armor take up positions within the Imposis' corridors. The Sergeant and the power-armor begin to enter the warship.

=====Jordheim Tundra - 0100 Hours=====
     Over three hundred thousand Volucris soldiers have been slaughtered, and lie dead on the frozen ground. The Icronian military forces quickly regroup, drawing up around the city in a massive perimeter.
     Dozens of dropships rise and fall in a constant stream. General Carpenter's forces are finally arriving and reinforcing the city. Carpenter's Hades and Jansen's Gamätande stand side by side, an unnecessary but symbolic gesture.
     "Jordheim is safe for now, but there are hundreds of settlements around here. If the bugs move farther south, there will be even more large cities threatened." says Jansen. "I can't do both."
     "I have four Divisions already landed. The First Infantry and First Armored Divisions will reinforce Jordheim defenses. The First and Second Mechanized Divisions will join you in sweeping the area. My other Divisions are landing in the south."
     "More bugs will be coming. We'll be ready."

= Part 5 =

Battle of Icronia: Lauds - 4/2/2325 - By James Davis and Ryan Wilhelm

=====Kronheim Tundra, Nomad Camp - 0101 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The quiet stillness of the night is interrupted by the roar of engines. Ikronin nomads step out of their temporary shelters, bewildered by the uncommon sound.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Six drop pods streak down to make hard landings in the frost. They vary in size, from car-sized to that of a small building. The two smallest open, and two Armadillo APCs cruise onto the tundra. The other pods open to reveal four Bradshaw Rigs. Omega Agents of the Conclave.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Secure the area. See if you can get any information from the natives." David Carpenter, commander of the squad, pilots his Rig around the small camp.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir!" Lieutenant Richard Allen. "Secure the perimeter. Be careful, our intel reports shows that most nomad camps are armed with small energy weapons or firearms, but they aren't very aggressive. Be nice, and they won't resist much."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An older man hesitantly approaches the APCs, as they open to reveal dozens of armored Omega agents. "Hejsan. Göra du tala Sothark?" ((Hello. Do you speak Sothark?))<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lieutenant Allen waves, then shoulders his rifle with an exaggerated motion. "Ja, jag tala det." ((Yes, I speak it.)) He shakes hands with the old Ikronin. "Vi behöva kosa. Vi andel Prefecture förplikta, und böra hjälpa de nedrig." ((We need directions. We're part of the Prefecture forces, and need to reinforce the base))<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Lieutenant is an experienced liar. The Ikronin accepts his word without a moment's doubt. "Bra, jag vilja biträde du." ((OK, I'll help you.))<br>
&nbsp;A few minutes later, the agents set out across the tundra, with all the information they need.

=====Imposis - 0115 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Contact! Sarge, we got a contact, movement just ahead." the Marine on point gives his report, his sealed helmet and comm system keeping everything outside oblivious to his message.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What is it?" Asks Sergeant Barlow. He motions the squad to a halt.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Not picking anything up on the infras. Too much motion to be an air current. I think we got a bug."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Roger that. See if you can get close enough for a SPAR readout." He turns to the rest of the squad. "Wright! Daly! Give him backup. We'll get you one of the Grendels if it's a big one."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The two Marines leave the group and advance slowly on the point-man's position. They take up defensive positions in the corridor. The point-man edges around the corridor.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Multiple contacts. Oh God." He ducks back, careful to be quiet. "Sarge, there's dozens of them. And the crew...some of them anyway...what's left of them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Barlow nods to himself. "OK, sit tight. I was afraid of this.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Corporal Wit, you and Private Sloan take your Grendels back to the airlock. Take ten men with you. Secure our exit, and update fleet command on the situation.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The rest of you, listen up. I want four teams of two Grendels and ten men. You'll be hunting through the ship. Look for survivors, and kill any bugs you find. The rest of you will accompany me to the bridge. We recover the black box, and reroute the Imposis' sensor grid to the ''Jerome Black''."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sarge, this ship's supposed to have a full complement of Marines and Grendels. How can we take down a group of bugs big enough to wipe out an Avenger full of troops?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm not sure what exactly happened, but this ship was taken out almost all at once. They didn't even have a chance to call for help, which means they were all dead in a matter of seconds. That tells me it wasn't the number of bugs. The bugs just sneaked in, and caught 'em with their pants down." Barlow answers. "We know what we're dealing with, and with any luck, the bugs don't. Now move out."

=====Jordheim Tundra - 0120 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"General Carpenter, can you explain something to me?" Kommendör Kreig Jansen and his tanks are running a wide, sweeping patrol south from Jordheim. Carpenter's Rigs follow, ready to provide heavy fire support.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sure, Kommendör." Carpenter grunts. "Got plenty of time, can't be a bug for kilometers. Let's pick up the pace. Even if we miss something, Jordheim is well-defended. I don't think they were after it anyway."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"You are probably right. They were probably trying to divert forces from our manufacturing plants in Oppenmir, or the spaceport at Jalke, or the data centers Oselo. If they attack there, they'll be in for a nasty surprise." Jansen laughs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Right." Carpenter smiles. "And your question was?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ah, right. How is it that your men are ready so quickly for combat? I know you run your ship clocks on Terran standard time, where it's shortly after midnight. And here in the Jordheim region, it's local night-time. My forces were at half-strength when the attack came, because we sleep in shifts. But the Divisions you've landed here are at one hundred percent strength, and your troops are fresh. How did you manage?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Well, if this was a garrison of Regulars, they would be yelling for coffee right now. But in the Legions, we do things differently. When we're deployed on a campaign, Medics put the grunts under with drugs and post-hypnotic suggestion. If we aren't fighting, we're sleeping. Takes the edge off a long wait, let me tell you. Once on campaign with the old Empire, I had to wait sixteen hours in the mud before the first shot was fired."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Contact!" the Ikronin line comes to a halt. "Thirty kilometers, no heat. Definitely bugs."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"General, we have the speed. We'll hit 'em once, then come around for another pass. You follow us at your pace." Kommendör Jansen says. "Kompanis, angrepp!" ((Companies, attack!))

=====Jordheim Supply Depot - 0135 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Prefecture's temporary supply dump is a sprawling complex of hastily-erected buildings, fences and guard towers. Dozens of Condor tanks patrol the area.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Two Armadillos approach at low speed. Huddled inside, Omega Squadron Agents check their weapons and stamp their feet against the cold.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Without warning, missiles streak into the compound. Two tanks crumple under the salvo, shredded by multiple hits. David Carpenter's Rigs move in, continuing to lay down their deadly barrage.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Armadillos open, and the Omega Agents inside burst out, scattering in all directions. Mere minutes later, they return, and the Armadillos head away at top speed. The Prefecture Condors fire several blasts at the fleeing APCs, destroying one. The Rigs turn tail, fading away into the dark night of the tundra. A moment later, the Conclave forces are gone.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A fireball lights up the night. The supply dump is engulfed in an inferno as Conclave detonators go off one by one. No one survives the devastation.

=====Imposis Mess Hall - 0155 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Corporal Greg Anston steps confidently into the Mess Hall. His Grendel's huge frame barely fits through the door, and he has to duck to avoid catching the shoulder cannons on the frame. His confidence wanes at the sight inside.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mess Hall is full of crew members of the Imposis. All of them are dead, sprawled across a table covered in gore and the remains of the night shift's breakfast.

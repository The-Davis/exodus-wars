One can hardly begin to call the [[Volucris Swarm]] a government in the traditional sense, but it does have both a civil and a military hierarchy. The civil hierarchy is more flexible than the rigid controls of the military.

==The Swarm==

The [[Volucris Swarm|Swarm]] is the name of the [[Volucris]] as a whole. Since their creators abandoned them eons ago, the various Hives have worked together. Some in close unison, some only loosely so. No Hive has ever fought another: even if the Hives do not directly cooperate with each other, the [[Volucris Swarm|Swarm]] is always united in the ideal of the sanctity of the [[Volucris]] species as a whole.

===The Otherness===

The mind behind the Swarm is difficult to comprehend. It is the Otherness, a being or beings of such complexity and alienness that the human [[:Category:Races|races]] simply cannot understand their wants and needs. The Otherness is completely alien to the human way of life, the human way of thinking, and the human concepts of existence.

===The Unity===

The Unity is a code of behavior that all [[Volucris]] follow. Whether it is genetic, moral, or derived from some other source is unknown. The dictates of the Unity is that the Hives shall never fight one another, or interfere in the goals of another Hive, and that all Hives shall actively pursue the Directive. The Unity is hard-coded into the [[Volucris]], and allows for no flexibility.

===The Directive===

The Directive is the ultimate behavior code that the Hives all follow. The Otherness can change the Directive to suit its goals. The Otherness is alien even to the bulk of the [[Volucris]], so the Directive is often simple and flexible in order for the Hives to understand and obey.

==The Hives==

The [[Volucris Swarm|Swarm]] is not absolute. There are many Hives in the [[Volucris Swarm|Swarm]], each with their own goals and directives. The Hives are not absolute. The leaders of the Volucris are diverse and strange, alien beyond imagining. They have a biological chain of command that is rigid in some places, flexible in others.  The Hive leaders are another matter.

===The Triumvirate===

The Triumvirate is a trio of "noble" [[Volucris]]. They are cunning, intelligent, and often have names and goals of their own to fulfill. The Triumvirate of each Hive changes occasionally, but for what reasons or how often remains a mystery.

===The Mother===

The Mother is a [[Volucris]] that binds the Hive together. The Mother is a massive [[Volucris]] Queen that produces pheremones that make the Hive recognize each other and work together. Younger queens secrete the same pheremones as the Mother. When a Queen reaches a certain level of maturity, her pheremones change. She attracts a Triumvirate of her own, and leaves to form a new Hive.

===The Will===

The [[Volucris]] are not a collective. They never were a collective. The Will is not a collective persona that directs the [[Volucris]]. The Will is a way of explaining the biological system that directs the [[Volucris]] and keeps the more intelligent breeds in line with the directives of the [[Volucris Swarm|Swarm]].

===The Beast===

The Beast is a frightful creature, the watchdog and terror of each Hive. It is the only [[Volucris]] unfettered by the Unity. It can kill anything and anyone at will if it perceives it as a threat to the Otherness or the Mother. The Beast is only held in check by its loyalty to the Mother, and the direct control by the Otherness. Some theorize that the Beast is more akin to the Otherness in mind than to the rest of the [[Volucris]].

===The Workers===

The Workers of a Hive are intelligent, non-violent creatures who live in service to their Hive. A Worker who would be perfectly willing to serve humans if the Will told them to.

Each Worker is a single mind, but has many bodies. It is a biological gestalt of experience and consciousness. If one of the Worker's bodies is moved far enough from the others, its gestalt can't sync with the rest of itself. The lone body will learn and get more intelligent, but it will eventually break down and die if it is not returned to the rest of itself.

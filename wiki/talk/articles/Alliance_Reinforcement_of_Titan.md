=====Titanian 11 - Titanian System - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the wake of the July 20th disaster, it was only the determination and patriotism of the Titanian Defense Force crews that allowed them to keep fighting. The Empire had a gigantic numerical advantage in the system, as well as superior supplies and well-rested crews.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In contrast, the TDF was stretched so thin that nearly 90% of its shipboard crew members were pulling double shifts, and shore leave was a long-forgotten myth. Every time a TDF warship left dock to go on patrol – which inevitably ended in a confrontation with a group of Vigilants, and a frantic retreat – spouses and family members wondered if this would be ‘the time.’ For too many, it was; casualty rates among TDF crew were high, nearly 10% per mission, although generally captains managed to save their ships from total destruction.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Morale was so low among civilians that some began planning for life under Imperial rule. The distinction between ‘civilian’ and ‘combatant’ became blurred. There were no longer enough cruisers to cover all vital targets from Imperial bomber raids, so civilian freighters were modified and pressed into service as makeshift carriers, mobile bases that allowed TDF fighters to fend off attacks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Worse, supplies had practically ceased flowing in from out-system. The Icronians had decided that it was no longer profitable to pour war material into the system, when the vast majority of the shipments were intercepted by the Imperials.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Robert Marshall had a stranglehold on the Titanian system, but he was prevented from exploiting his advantage by logistical difficulties. Keeping the entirety of Atlas Wing and much of the rest of the Vanguard Fleet fully supplied was a nightmare; building up stockpiles of munitions and other material for a new push on Titanian 9 was even more difficult. Apocalypse nuclear warheads were growing scarce; the bomber-harassment campaign had nearly depleted Marshall’s supplies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Vanguard Fleet might have been able to overwhelm Sanburn’s remaining warships, but it was unlikely they could have taken and held Titanian 6 against a Lorithos , Icronian, and Meroniri counterattack without more supplies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall grew more and more irritated. He didn’t like his mighty warships being tied down by the dictates of something as petty as supply restrictions, but Anthony Hadrian expressly forbade any offensive. Marshall continued his harassment campaign, drilling his warship crews relentlessly in preparation for the final attack.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In early December, Omega Squadron agents learned that the Lorithos and Icronians had dispatched war fleets to break the siege of the Titanian system. The Meroniri had sent a staff of ‘military advisers,’ led by Mihel Ridarsol.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In a face-to-face meeting with Marshall, Anthony Hadrian explained the plan that had guided his actions over the last few months. He would allow the Lorithos and Ikronin to reinforce the Titanians, then crush them all together; it would be easier to overwhelm their combined forces when they were tied down defending Titanian 6.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall saw the wisdom of this strategy, and began putting together battle plans.

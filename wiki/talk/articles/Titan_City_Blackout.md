April 24, [[2321]]

by James Davis and Scott Sanburn

=====Titan City=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Royal Imperial Navy flagship Iron Hand launched a series of Apocalypse missiles at the central city of Titanian 6--the homeworld of the Titanians.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Meroniri gravity shields deflected the missile, but the energy of the blast was absorbed by the generator and couplings. The resulting blast from the collapsing gravity shield generator leveled the surrounding buildings and overloaded the city generators. Titan City suffered a long blackout before city engineers could bring backups online.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the aftermath of the attack, Imperial Omega Squadron agents penetrated the city and learned that the Apocalypse missiles in the hands of the TDF were from Professor Hlow, who had defected to the Titanians and Meronir's Republic.

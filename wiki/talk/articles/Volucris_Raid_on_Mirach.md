= Part 1 =

February 12, 2324

by James Davis

=====Sepulare=====

     Since the Second Contact Battle one week ago, the Prefecture Third Fleet and elements of the United Mesarthrim Clans and United Archadian Republics navies have engaged the Volucris Swarm, with poor results. Up until now, no B-Net observers have been allowed aboard any ships involved in the action, and the reports were only declassified this morning.<br>
     The Swarm is enormous. No probes or recon flights have managed to get within visual range of the core Volucris force, so estimating the numbers of Volucris is impossible.
     In the first two days after the Second Contact Battle, the Swarm was still sitting around in deep space where they'd routed the Imperial armada and the Prefecture Second Fleet. The Prefecture Third Fleet left them alone, observing from a distance until Mesarthrim Commandant Arnold Stadt arrived with twenty Mesarthrim warships.
     This joint force tailed the Swarm, but without accurate tracking information would not risk a direct confrontation. Stadt wanted to use area-affect weapons like the Omega Bomb, but Prefect Hadrian didn't want to aggravate the Swarm into attacking and wiping out the human forces.

    On February 8th, 2324, the Volucris Swarm began to noticeably diminish in size, and recon scouts couldn't find a trace of them by the end of the day. Commandant Stadt deduced that they had made something akin to a FTL acceleration or even possibly opened a Wormhole. The Prefecture and Mesarthrim fleets split up and began a wide-scale search of space along all possible routes in human-held territory.

     On February 10th, 2324, the Prefecture world of Mirach in the Tavou District was invaded by the Volucris Swarm. Before the day was out, Travis Carpenter's Legion of the Phoenix and Kenneth Russell's Tavou Regulars were engaged in bitter fighting on the surface.

    Today, the Volucris have been pushed off of Mirach, for the time being. Over 600,000 Volucris corpses have been counted, and Tyrant type Volucris are known to eat their own dead.
     The United Archadian Republics and United Mesarthrim Clans have each sent three regiments of ground forces, and thirty warships apiece. This combined force of three nations hopes to stop this new threat before the fighting gets serious.
     The Alliance of Planets is in emergency session. The Prefecture's Consul, Christopher Dayton, hopes to get more reinforcements from the other Member States to bolster Mirach's defenses. Michelle Jarvens is on the scene.

=====Transmission Begins=====
     Michelle Jarvens: "Good evening ladies and gentlemen. It is 1800 hours local time, and the Alliance of Planets High Council has just reconvened after a recess for dinner.
     "Earlier today, they spoke with scouts and recon pilots from the Prefecture, and reviewed video recordings of the Volucris in action. This evening, they will be interviewing private sector xenobiologists and medical professionals about the nature of these creatures, and tomorrow morning the Member States' representatives will deliver their reports of how many troops and warships--if any--that they will be sending to aid the Prefecture.      Darel Fusanla: "Order, order. Please be seated. This session of Alliance of Planets High Council is now convened. This Council will now hear the testimony of the xenobiologists of the Schweitzer Institute of Medicine. Doctor, please state your name and occupation for the record."
     Dr. Sells: "Honorable members of the Council, I am Dr. Larson Sells, medical doctor and specialist in vascular medicine. I was commissioned by the Schweitzer Institute to lead the team conducting autopsies on the captured Volucris specimens. Our autopsies and observations of the two live samples the Marines of the Prefecture managed to capture have produced some startling information, which was recently publicized by the Xenobiology Journal of Fronde.
     "The full report is beyond the scope of this meeting, but in summary I can report that their physiology is very volatile. Each Volucris has an internal temperature at or above that of a fission reactor, and all of them can survive extreme conditions, even the vacuum of space. Their external armor is resistant to laser fire, but most other weaponry, particularly intense neutron cannon bombardment is effective in killing them. On average, any one of these Volucris can take more hits than any six humans and keep moving.
     "We have been unable to capture a breeding specimen, but it is apparent that they hatch from eggs and grow in a nymph to mature cycle, similar to Terran grasshoppers and similar insects.
     "My team has also discovered some information on the origin of this species. For that, I yield the floor to Mr. Douglas, my assistant."
     Darel Fusanla: "Mr. Douglas, please state your full name and occupation for the record."
     Walther Douglas: "Honorable members of the Council, my full name is Josiah MacArthur Hlow, alias Walther Douglas, medical doctor, engineer, inventor, physicist and gardener, though I suppose my cabbages don't have anything to do with the subject at hand."
    The assembly erupts with a dozen confused questions from all sides, and it takes many attempts from Prime Minister Fusanla before order was restored.
     Darel Fusanla: "Dr. Hlow has been officially dead for some time. Please explain yourself."
     Professor Hlow: "I prefer Professor Hlow, not Doctor, if you don't mind sir. I am a teacher of the sciences, not someone with some lofty title who looks down his nose at less-knowledgeable people.
     "Explain myself, hmm? Where to begin. I hope you'll forgive my meanderings, it is a very long story. I suppose it begins in May, three years ago. During Emperor Lucius Black's siege of the Titanian system, I attempted to defect from the Empire to Meronir's Republic. This attempt was foiled, and I was nearly captured. I managed to escape, though I believe I will keep the secret of that little maneuver to myself.
     "In my travels, I met with the remnants of the Gorhamut race. You should know about them if you've studied ancient history, Mr. Prime Minister, they are in the Meroniri archives. They are a fascinating species, and very gentle when not provoked into war. But I digress.
     "When I heard about the imminent doom of Blackis at the hands of the biological weapon, the Apöt virus, I returned to the Emperor to work out a cure. My Gorhamut friends were to retrieve me afterwards so my knowledge couldn't be used by Lucius Black to power his war machine. The Gorhamut are still in hiding, even I can't find them if they don't want me to, so don't ask about them.
     "The Gorhamut created the Volucris. Eons ago, they engineered them as servants and soldiers. They still control a handful of them. It was this handful that engineered my escape from Pluto base on April 23rd, 2322. I re-joined human society shortly afterward, under the assumed name of Walther Douglas. I have been working for Rahn Industries and several other private companies in an effort to get new weapons and shields to humanity's front line forces before the Swarms arrived. The gravity shields and neutron cannons are my invention, by the way.
     "If that is a satisfactory explanation, may I return to the matter at hand?"
     Darel Fusanla: "Yes, please continue Professor."
     Professor Hlow: "Thank you. The Swarm is a highly-organized, hyper-evolved version of the original creatures created by the Gorhamut. According to my Gorhamut friends, the Swarms are controlled by Nobles.
     "Nobles are very intelligent. When they were created, they could communicate vocally with their Gorhamut masters, and could pass down orders through the pheromone and sign languages used by the Volucris. Considering that the Volucris we've encountered so far look and behave nothing like the originals, your guess is as good as mine as to how intelligent and powerful the Nobles are today.
     "My associates at the Schweitzer Institute have been unable to discern the intentions or goals of the Volucris we are facing today. I'll leave that to the strategists. I will tell you that we cannot communicate with them. Scent analysis and generation hasn't been bothered with by science, so the pheromone language they communicate in won't be decipherable, let alone spoken, for decades.
     "I have nothing further to add. I yield the floor."
     Dirken Huern: "Professor Hlow, why have you decided to make your presence known so suddenly?"
     Professor Hlow: "That matter is between myself and this Council only, and I will be happy to explain in a private meeting. It is not for public ears at this time."
     Darel Fusanla: "Thank you for your time Professor, we have no further questions for you. Dr. Sells, please continue your research on the Volucris. We need to know how to disrupt their actions, and determine what they want.
     "This meeting is adjourned."

February 13, [[2324]]

by Austin Bailey


=====Letter from General Travis Carpenter=====

To the Prefecture Office of the Prefect
From General Travis Carpenter
Regarding the Mirach Campaign
     Admiral, the battle reports you requested are enclosed. Once again, I must urge you to focus the research of the Prefecture into finding ways to track the Volucris.

    0300 hours, Mirach time - The Volucris began their landing twenty kilometers from the Mirach survey team's spaceport. Like their initial attack three days ago, the landing lacked any pattern or recognizable strategy.
     The RhovaDeviri were in charge of defense of that region. They met the initial landing with Gradala VTOLs. In the initial fire exchange, the Gradala pilots killed over a hundred smaller breeds before the flyers we've designated "Shivas" arrived from space in force. Our Piranha fighters and Ixoarchada Spectres reinforced the Gradala VTOL units, but air superiority soon belonged to the Volucris from overwhelming numbers and our weapons' inability to correctly track the enemy. Even aiming by sight, our pilots shot down over one thousand landing troops and enemy fighters.
     0300-0330 hours - Summary
          Prefecture losses: 4 Piranhas
          Taviridis losses: 2 Gradalas, 1 Spectre.
          Volucris losses (estimated): 400 Nymphs, 200 Cutters, 300 Shivas, 200 other breeds
          Battleground: Under Volucris Control

    0340 hours, Mirach time - The Tavou Regulars under the command of Colonel Kenneth Russell met the landed Swarms in force fifteen kilometers from the Mirach survey team spaceport. The United Mesarthrim Clans' Myt'herra from Banshee group supported Russell's forces.
     With air superiority in the claws of the Volucris, our ground troops took heavy casualties from suicidal Shredder Drones. Our own fighters and the Ixoarchada air forces were unable to stop the air raids, though dogfights between our aircraft and their Shivas were fought continuously through the battle.
     Colonel Russell's Condors and artillery pounded the swarms of Volucris ground troops. In our first encounter, I had our vehicles attack the light Nymphs and Cutters, but this time Colonel Russell ordered his tanks to attack the more dangerous Bombardiers and Slicers, leaving the small breeds to our entrenched Battle Armor.
     Unfortunately, the Volucris flyers have kept us from landing heavy Rig forces. We have had to rely on the tanks and light Rigs that smaller dropships can bring in quickly. Our Whale dropships cannot get through the Shiva screens, so Colonel Russell's forces have had to rely on Hercules Rigs.
     Russell's Tavou Regulars and the Mesarthrim Banshees held the battleground for over an hour. Our observers estimated that the enemy was fielding about two million troops, not counting flyers. Unfortunately, our losses were too heavy to hold the line, and Colonel Russell ordered a retreat to the spaceport.
     0340-0500 hours - Summary
          Prefecture losses: 20 Condors, 5 Hercules, 600 Grendels
          Mesarthrim losses: 15 various vehicles, 400 Myt'herra
          Volucris losses (estimated): 300,000 ground, 2000 flyers.
          Battleground: Under Volucris Control

   0515 hours, Mirach time - After breaking Colonel Russell's delaying line, the Volucris forces began their attack on the spaceport. The Legion of the Phoenix--sans heavy and assault class Rigs--was fully ready thanks to the Tavou Regulars delaying action. The Taviridis army and Mesarthrim Black Dogs Myt'herra were also manning the fortifications.
     After ten minutes of fighting, the Tavou Regulars and Mesarthrim Banshees had regrouped and joined the battle at the spaceport. The Volucris forces were broken and defeated by our combined forces.
     0155-0600 hours - Summary
          Prefecture losses: 16 Rigs, 30 tanks, 1200 troops, 20 static turrets, 40 fighters
          Mesarthrim losses: 30 tanks, 800 troops, 10 fighters
          Taviridis losses: 5 VTOLs, 6 tanks, 35 fighters
          Volucris losses (estimated): 1,200,000 ground, 6000 flyers.
          Battleground: Under Human Control
**Battle Report End**


    It is my opinion that the time of the Volucris attack means that they are adapting to our habits and tactics. Their first attack was in broad daylight, but this one got underway while our scouts were at skeleton crew levels. Without Colonel Russell's delaying tactics, the spaceport would have been taken.
     I have ordered triple watch for night shifts, and I've deployed transports to find the Mirach Survey Teams and return them to the spaceport for safety. Once all civilians are recovered, I intend to evacuate the planet and observe the Volucris from a distance.
    I await your orders.

=====From the Log of Colonel Kenneth Russell, picked up from a wide-band broadcast=====

     After the second attack on the spaceport, General Carpenter ordered the evacuation of the Mirach survey teams. This job fell to my troops and Mesarthrim Commandant Antol Nik, commander of the Banshee Myt'herra.
     On February 14th, we set out at 1000 hours. I brought the 2nd and 3rd Regiment of the 15th Tavou Regulars Division. Most of the Regulars--the 3rd, 12th, and most of the 15th Divisions--remained at the spaceport with General Carpenter's Phoenix Legion.
      The operation was simple. Locate the survey teams, evacuate the two colonist settlements, and return as many civilians as possible to the spaceport for evacuation. According to Tavou District Immigration services, ten thousand civilians had moved into Mirach Colonies 1 and 2.
      According to Fleet observation satellites, the bugs--no, the Volucris, I hate the name bugs--had landed again in smaller raiding groups. Fleet has a certain gift of understatement. The smallest of these "raiding groups" was around 100,000 bugs.
     At 1500 hours, we reached Colony One, and evacuated the civilians without incident. Helicopter transports took the civvies to the spaceport for evacuation to the orbiting Fleet transports.
     The Volucris attacked us immediately after the last Skycrane left Colony One. I had my Regulars dig in and fight, while Commandant Nik and the Banshee Myt'herra moved on ahead to the survey teams. Pilots from Jan Brooks' squadrons had spotted them about a hundred kilometers north.
    We trounced the Volucris. Their strength is numbers, and a hundred thousand is slim pickings compared to the millions we faced in the first and second waves here on Mirach. Even with these outdated laser weapons--which Army Command still hasn't replaced--it was easy to mow down the Cutters they sent after us.
     Antol Nik and the Banshees evac'd with the survey teams. Orders from home, or so he said. I'd never call a Mesarthrim a coward. They're born soldiers. Commandant Nik was probably angrier at being called out of the fight than I was that he left.

    It's currently February 17th, 1800 hours. We've arrived at Colony Two with no further harassment by the Volucris. I've called in choppers to evacuate the civvies and--oh God.
     Wide band broadcast, all frequencies. This is Colonel Kenneth Russell, Tavou Regulars. Relay message to Mirach Spaceport. Volucris inbound. Several million crawlers, thousands of flyers. Civvies pinned down with us.

     B-Net and Prefecture Fleet probes picked up this message. The rescue mission is planned for tomorrow.

= Part 5 =

March 11, 2324

by James Davis

== Mirach ==

Mirach's infestation is about to come to an end. The Meroniri Tronict Class Battlecruiser Mäg Ols, commanded by Rox Augustin, has been dispatched to deal with the swarm. Captain Augustin has a Mass to Energy Conversion Bomb at his disposal. APNET reporter Joan Andrek is live on the scene.

== Begin Transmission ==

A Meroniri warship bridge is a simple affair. Advanced computers handle most of the controls, and sharp Meroniri brains can handle four times the controls that a Terran can handle. Captain Augustin's bridge crew is made up of only six officers.

"Captain, any words for the civilians back home before the mission begins?" asks Andrek. "The people of the Alliance of Planets haven't seen your military in action since countering the Remnant, and that was limited contact."

"Well, I'm glad to be back in action. I wish that we'd been involved more, but now we'll get to give these bugs a taste of Meroniri might," says Captain Augustin. "I've received orders from Minister Derri Meninnon to bomb the Mirach starsystem. With a little luck, we'll end the entire Volucris threat in one swift strike."

"Is there any chance that the Mäg Ols will be taken out before you manage to launch the bomb?" queries Joan Andrek. "The Prefecture fleets took severe losses from the Volucris Shredder Drones."

"I think not. We're sending out Auroras to draw off the swarm's flyer screens. The fighters are long-ranged. They can jump out of system to the Titanian Defense Force's Triumphant in a couple of seconds. Once the bomb is launched, all of our forces will be out of range of the reaction before the bugs have time to think."

One of Captain Augustin's officers reports that the bomb is ready for launch, and the battle begins.

== B-Net Newsroom ==

The Meroniri fighters engage the Volucris swarm from six different directions, hitting and running before the Shivas could bog them down in huge dogfights.

Whatever force controls the swarm was apparently caught napping. The Mäg Ols slipped up to the star unchallenged.

== Resuming Transmission ==

"Captain Augustin, the bugs were occupying a planet, right?" a confused TDF pilot hails the Mäg Ols. "We've got a planet-sized hulk here, but as near as I can tell, it's all bug. I can't make out a thing other than their bodies."

"Can you get me a count?"

"Billions doesn't begin to describe it. Even if that mass is only one bug deep, there's too many to count. Maybe trillions. Probably more."

"Well, it's about to be zero. Disengage, we're dropping the bomb."

== B-Net Newsroom ==

The fighter squadrons withdraw from the Mirach starsystem, returning to the TDF supercarrier Triumphant. The Mäg Ols launches its payload, and accelerates beyond the pull of Mirach's star, where it makes an instantaneous Tesseract jump back to Sepulcre.

A TDF space probe is observing the Volucris swarm on Mirach. The bomb will destroy the starsystem in three minutes. The Volucris are moving about normally, cleaning up the carcasses of their lost flyers.

The swarm is breaking up. They're moving away from Mirach. No, they're just moving...Mirach is gone. The whole planet is simply gone, no debris. The bomb will detonate in one minute. The swarm is moving away quickly. They're getting away.

No, it's too late. The bomb has detonated. The reaction has reached the first planet...it's gone. The reaction is spreading--the third planet, the space where Mirach was. The Volucris swarm is caught in the reaction. They're gone. The swarm has been eliminated.

It looks like the war is over. Reports indicate at least two trillion Volucris were destroyed.

= Part 1 =

June 3, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears and James Davis

=====Kalidasa=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mesarthrim High Command has finally voted in favor of allying with the Prefecture. The vote was nearly unanimous. Commandants Arnold Stadt and Ben Parman were the dissidents, and were enraged by the final vote tally.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Both Commandants stormed out of the assembly. Before the hour was out their respective battle groups were in the Imperial starsystem of Kalidasa. Uber General Andreas Tischler called them back, but both disobeyed orders.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Kalidasa was defended by the 10th Core Fleet--a small group of Odyssey Class Cruisers led by the Vigilant frigate Startide and Rear Admiral Ramesh Jethro. Commandant Stadt intended to take them out so Parman's forces could take the planet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Stadt's cruisers closed to firing range with the Imperial cruisers and opened fire. The Odysseys gunned down one of Stadt's battle cruisers, but Stadt's forces destroyed three Imperial Odysseys.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial fighter pilots fought with reckless abandon, killing twenty Mesarthrim fighters for every one of their own deaths, but that wasn't enough. The numbers of Mesarthrim fighters were too great.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Victory was in Commandant Stadt's grasp. The Imperial cruisers wouldn't hold out much longer--but then help arrived. Rear Admiral Ramesh Jethro attacked with Orion missile cruisers in a savage assault. The Mesarthrim battle cruiser Justice quickly buckled under the bombardment and was destroyed. The Mesarthrim and Imperial fighters alike were wiped out in the mad assault.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Mesarthrim battle cruiser Liberator was badly damaged by the bombardment, and tried to escape the attack--only to be rammed by an Orion cruiser. Both ships were destroyed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt's plans were crumbling around him. The Imperial frigate Startide set a ramming course for Stadt's flagship--Rear Admiral Jethro obviously considered victory more important than life. Stadt went into a dive, intent on losing the brash Rear Admiral.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Startide cut loose with a full barrage at close range, causing catastrophic damage to Stadt's flagship. With Stadt damaged and out of the fight, Rear Admiral Jethro's forces rallied and destroyed a pair of Mesarthrim battle cruisers. Stadt finally conceded victory, and ordered his fleet to return to Centus.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emperor Lucius Black personally congratulated Ramesh Jethro for bravery and loyalty. Arnold Stadt and Ben Parman were arrested upon return to Centus and are currently awaiting trial for unauthorized use of military force and insubordination.

= Part 2 =

September 20, 2322

by Andrew Sears

=====Kalidasa=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Early this morning B-Net received a message from Uber-General Andreas Tischler about the new Beta Commando strike unit and a successful sting operation on Kalidasa--an operation which resulted in the capture of Kalidasa.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "I am pleased to report that the new Mesarthrim Beta Commando team managed to capture Rear Admiral Ramesh Jethro, commander of the Imperial garrison and Core Fleet on Kalidasa.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The team was able to get to Jethro's quarters undetected. There were only six bodyguards, four of which the commandos dispatched before the survivors sounded a general alarm. The last guards were dispatched, and Rear Admiral Jethro was taken hostage. With Jethro captured, the Imperial troops didn't put up a fight. The commando team escaped into space, hitching a ride home to Centus on the MCS Winston Churchill.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Rear Admiral Jethro is in military custody on Mesar. Mesarthrim and TDF fleets moved into Kalidasa, and without their commander, the Core Fleet and garrison troops surrendered."

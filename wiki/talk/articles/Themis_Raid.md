= Part 1 =

April 5, [[2321]]

by James Davis

=====Themis=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Titanian Defense Force launched an attack on an Imperial warship base in orbit of Titanian 8. A detachment from the TDF Fleet led the assault.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The TDF-W-5000 cruiser Thomas and a squadron of TDF-F-800 heavy fighters attacked two Royal Imperial Navy Vigilant Class Frigates, and four squadrons of Raven fighters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the fifteen minute engagement, 29 Ravens were destroyed, one of the Vigilant frigates was damaged, all of the TDF-F-800s were destroyed, and the TDF-W-5000 Thomas was heavily damaged. Only the Raven pilots were killed in the engagement. The TDF pilots' ejection seats were well-designed, and saved the lives of all personnel. The Thomas managed to rescue all pilots.

= Part 2 =

July 12, [[2321]]

by Scott Sanburn

=====Themis=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;TDF commanders were determined to re-capture Titanian 8 from the Empire. In a perfectly-executed surprise attack, the bulk of the Titanian Defense Force Fleet moved into Titanian 8 orbit and cut off the Imperial retreat.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Due to the unique tectonic nature of Titanian 8, the Empire had never set up a base on the surface. Any structure would be quickly shaken apart by the frequent earthquakes, so they opted for an orbital base. The TDF decided to take advantage of this.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Icronian and Kimburun special forces were sent to the surface, where they set up four Meroniri MT-29500 defense cannons, which they aimed at the orbiting Imperial base. The base was soon blasted into three chunks, and the TDF forces moved in to deal with cleanup.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial resistance was heavier than the TDF anticipated. Hundreds of Raven fighters managed to survive the destruction of their base, and they fought viciously against the attackers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Even though it was crippled, the chunks of the Imperial base were still somewhat operational. The automated defense turrets fired back at the fleet, and managed to destroy two cruisers, the Gardiin and the Mantle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The TDF cruisers cleared away from the base, instead sending in fighters to occupy the base's defense systems. Before the cruisers could permanently shut down the base, the Imperial soldiers still aboard detonated their entire complement of Apocalypse missiles, killing themselves and the fighters nearby.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The three Imperial frigates assigned to defend the base tried to retreat. The Gorgon slipped out of the TDF armada's trap, but the Manticore and Judicator were cut off. The Manticore took heavy damage from the TDF cruisers. The crew scuttled the ship before TDF troops could board it. Only half the crew escaped.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Judicator rescued the survivors of the Manticore, then fought its way through the TDF blockade. The Imperial frigate managed to destroy a TDF-W-5000 Cruiser before making an FTL jump out of the system.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After the battle, the TDF set up a new base of their own in orbit around Titanian 8, and moved their fleet deployment center from Titanian 6 to Titanian 8. The Royal Imperial Navy and Army withdrew from Titanian 9, 10, and 11, regrouping at the fortress-like Imperial base on Titanian 12--the outermost planet of the Titanian starsystem.

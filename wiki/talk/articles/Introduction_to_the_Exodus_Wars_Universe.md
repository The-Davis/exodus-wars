Talk about Introduction to the Exodus Wars Universe.

== Think this needs to be discussed, Mesarthrim ==

Remember, after the fall of the Anderung, the Mesarthrim had a golden age, an age of peace and prosperity, the first in the history of their people.  The Mesarthrim can survive in peace, they can have peace, and they are okay with peace.  I don't think it's fair to say they will crumble without some form of enemy -- I mean, thats not the race I developed.

Also, remember, the Mesarthrim went to war with the Prefecture because they didn't get anything out of it, no colonies, no nothing -- they went there, they fought, died, and got nothing.  I really think that needs to be highlighted as the cause for the war, not that the Prefecture was just the Onyx revisited.

===Would it be fair to say...===

Would it be fair to say that while the Mesarthrim CAN live in peace, it is very difficult for them to do so and they require some form of outside control?

This is very outdated, but I do feel that the Mesarthrim psyche has developed to a natural state of violence, and only some sort of ideal control...such as the Mesarthrim State Church...has been able to keep them in line in large numbers. I see the Mesarthrim as having the potential for peace, but being a seething cauldron of fury barely bottled up most of the time.

Also, I agree: we should definitely underline the fact that the Mesarthrim feel cheated by the Prefecture, and rightly so! In fact, a fair treaty should end up giving the final Mesarthrim government (whatever it happens to be) full control of the Pelagrim Sector: the Prefecture would give up control of Tavou and the other worlds located there under the condition that the citizens not be forced out of their homes.

== Proposed Changes ==

I agree that we should list that the Mesarthrim CAN live in peace, and DO live in peace -- I agree, it makes sense that having a state church with help stabilize the Mesarthrim.  But what excuse do we use for the Auellal League, they don't have a centralized church... 

As for the treaty, it would be between the Auellal League and the Prefecture.  But one thing I kinda wonder is, how would that work?  Why would the Auellal demand territory from their ally?  I mean, it was really the UCS that attacked the Prefecture, not the Auellal...  Just thinking...

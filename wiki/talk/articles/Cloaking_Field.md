I don't think this fits in our universe anymore. Should it be removed?

[[User:JDavis|James]] 03:33, 7 November 2008 (CST)

Short answer: yes.  As a "field", I say delete it.  
However, the concept of Cloaking something is nearly possible now, so probably would completely feasible in the future.  Specifically, bending light around an object made out of a certain substance (meta-materials).  However, the effectiveness of this is limited, since in space you're hardly ever going actually *see* the enemy until they're really close.  You rely on ambient heat, gravity signatures, etc.  

Thus, being physically invisible isn't useful except at close ranges and on planets.  Even then, the meta-materials are not durable in the anti-weaponry sense, so you wouldn't want to have a weapon of war made of them.  A small scouting probe?  A small hovercraft, something like that?  It'd be more stealthy, but fragile.  Drop the field, add a segment on meta-materials if we really need it.

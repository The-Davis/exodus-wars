= Part 1 =

March 12, 2324

by James Davis and Austin Bailey

=====Tavou=====

     Mirach was a difficult battle, with hard lessons learned. Unfortunately, it will probably be nothing compared to the defense of Tavou.
     The Prefecture runs an entire District from Tavou. There are over eight hundred million civilians, important factories, and a Wormhole Gate hub. The world is incredibly valuable to the Prefecture citizens.
     The motivation of the Volucris remains uncertain. Xenobiologists have yet to observe captured soldiers eating. The smaller ones simply die after a few days, and the larger ones fall into a hibernation-like sleep. Food isn't the reason for the invasion. No one's sure why the bugs are after Tavou.
     No one's going to let the Volucris get it either. Unlike Mirach, Tavou was quickly reinforced through use of the Wormhole Gate system. The Icronian Consortium, the United Archadian Republic, and Meronir's Republic have all sent aid to the already exhausted Tavou Regulars.
     With General Travis Carpenter occupied with overseeing the update of the Prefecture Army, General Chonge Kodwe and the Legion of the Wolf have been sent by the Prefecture to defend the world of Tavou.
    B-Net reporter Gordon Barclay is live on the scene in Tavou Central city, where the allied commanders have set up their headquarters.

Tavou Central City
     "General Kodwe is currently at the front lines, but United Archadian Republic army commander General Ellisaire Berök is here to coordinate the defense of Tavou," says Barclay. "General, can the folks back home have a moment of your time?"
     "Yes, but make it quick. Fleet scouts say the Volucris will be in range of the Wormhole Gate Hub soon, and Tavou itself in only an hour."
     "Can you summarize what each of the allied armies is in charge of?"
     "Well, the Icronian Consortium has sent enough men and machines to defend their Gate hub. As far as I know, it's only Flyga Kommendör Sugeir Symanksi of the Flyer Corps and one Fåglar of fighters--twenty-four Skratta Måsens and twelve Rovriddares. They're just here to shut down the Gates if the Volucris take an interest in them.
     "Meronir's Republic's sent some powerful space weapons. Captain-Commander Marcelian Anderia is in command of their forces. Her fleet has ten cruisers with full fighter complements and shipboard ARROWS armor. Unfortunately, they won't be involved in the ground war. Their armies are still involved in a classified project.
     "My own government has sent Admiral Palontir Taukin with twelve warships, and six Divisions under my own command."
     "Thank you General," says Barclay. "But what about General Kodwe's Legion?"
     "Well, Chonge Kodwe's been entrenched since two hours after the first Volucris scouts were discovered," General Berök laughs. "I doubt even his superiors know how many troops he has hidden around the strategic strong points we've set up. Those new Power Suits are incredible."
     A Prefecture officer interrupts. "Sir, General Kodwe's on the horn. Bugs are hitting the Gate Hub. We need to prepare for wounded."

Outer Tavou Starsystem
     The Gate Hub fighting was very fierce. Prefecture fighters--mostly older Stukas with some upgraded Stukas and new Piranhas--took the first wave of Shivas and Shredder Drones. The initial Volucris charge was met, blunted and turned back.
     Before long, the second wave came in, and it took the entire allied fighter force to keep from being overwhelmed. Despite the efforts, Battle Queen flyers penetrated the screens and injected their cargos into the Hub control center.
     Icronian personnel were nearly overrun before Prefecture Marines and Archadian RhovaDeviri were sent over to assist.

Gate Hub Control Center
     "Admiral Taukin, Admiral Baruk, we've secured the control center and the Consortium engineers have shut down the Gates. No bugs will be going through this starsystem."
     "Good work Marines," says Admiral Baruk. "Any idea what they were after?"
     The Marine laughs. "Not a clue. The Inquisitors will want a look at some of these things. It wasn't just those quick-hatchers, there were a couple critters that weren't in the intelligence briefings."
     "Are any alive?" asks Admiral Taukin. "My government will also want a chance to examine them."
     "Sorry Admiral," the Marine responds. "We waxed them all, but there are three or four corpses with only a couple holes in them. Any more on their way in?"
     "All's clear, but the fleet's mobilizing to intercept the swarm before they reach Tavou. Pack up, your boat arrives in thirty seconds."

Inner Tavou Starsystem
     The Prefecture Sixth Fleet and allies fight viciously against the Volucris swarm. The Meroniri in particular destroy more than their share, taking out thousands of Shredders, Shivas, Queens and landing cocoons.
     It isn't enough. The Buggers measure their losses in millions, not single units. The Meroniri cruisers Crezei and Gerken withdraw with serious damage, and the Prefecture Navy's Namakagon, Edson, and Comet are destroyed. The damages are countless on all sides, and the Volucris finally begin landing on Tavou.

Volucris Landing Zone
     General Chonge Kodwe is waiting when the Volucris begin crashing to the ground. Their cocoons burst open, revealing millions of ravenous bugs. In the blink of an eye, hundreds of thousands are blown to smithereens by the pressure-sensitive mines the crafty Ngrligru had seeded the field with minutes earlier.
     "Bugs on the ground!" Kodwe bellows. The fish-like alien checks his ammo, then continues issuing orders. "Disable remaining mines and engage targets of opportunity. Thirty seconds of attack, then hold your ground for one minute. On my signal, begin a fighting retreat towards Magrinoch."
     Prefecture and Archadian infantry pound the deploying bugs with explosives and heavy cannons, then begin a rapid retreat across Tavou's plains. Oddly enough, the Volucris swarms sit still, buzzing like mad bees.

B-Net Newsroom
     It's been six hours since the Volucris swarm landed, and they still haven't budged. The allied armies are planning a counterstrike to destroy them without using nuclear weapons. Fortunately, they have a large timetable to work with--the Volucris are being uncharacteristically non-aggressive in space and on the ground.

= Part 2 =

Battle of Tavou: Flawed Defenses - 3/15/2324 - by James Davis

     Magrinoch is one of the largest city centers on Tavou, and is the current headquarters of the allied humanoid armies. The defenders of Tavou are assembled to prepare a counterstrike against the Volucris swarms, to drive them off the planet and annihilate them.

     "Our big mistake on Mirach was matching strength for strength," says General Kodwe. "The bugs have--as far as we can tell--practically unlimited numbers. They overwhelm large armies with size. Tactics are their weak point. Every time we've met them in limited numbers, like on our boarded warships and on the Wormhole Gate station, we've soundly defeated them. We must surgically carve up the swarm into smaller pieces."
     The assembled commanders are a strange group. Chonge Kodwe and Hodar Jana are Ngrligru, a small and vicious species that has nothing in common with humans. Sugeir Symanksi represents the Icronian Consortium. His pale complexion and short, stocky build sets him apart from the others. The Archadian Admiral Palontir Taukin and General Ellisaire Berök tower over the others, their genetically enhanced frames making them imposing figures. Only Admiral Hannah Baruk and Captain-Commander Marcelian Anderia match the normal human shape and size.
     "The Bastion can probably break up that mass of bugs at ground zero, but I'll need at least five minutes in Tavou-synchronous orbit to aim and adjust power levels before firing," Admiral Baruk motions towards the tactical map on the table where the commanders are gathered. "I think we should set up some diversionary warship strikes at several key locations to draw attention away from my vessel."
     "I concur," says Admiral Taukin. "With the ships under Captain-Commander Anderia's command backing us up, I'm sure the Archadian fleet can draw most of the bugs in space away from the Bastion and her escorts."
     "We will provide some air support to the ground operations," says Sugeir. "But only for five hours. The Consortium has recalled the fighters under my command, and we're due home soon."
     Before General Kodwe can speak again, he is interrupted by loud noises. The sounds have no apparent origin, and sound unlike anything the residents of Magrinoch are used to.
     "What was that?" Hodar Jana sniffs, testing the air. He is visibly agitated, his claws extended fully in fighting position. His barbed dorsal fin stands fully erect.
     The answer comes in the form of a Volucris worker, bursting out of the floor. The beast flexes its mandibles with an audible clicking sound, then squeals like a frightened rodent. It ducks back into the hole it made, only to be hauled out again by Kodwe and Jana. The Ngrligru quickly tear the hapless bug to pieces.
     "They can burrow?!" General Berök demands. "Why didn't we know they could burrow? Get someone in here to seal that hole." Berök motions for his associate, Admiral Taukin, to step away from the hole. Berök is garbed in heavy armor and armed with powerful infantry weapons. Taukin only has a sidearm.
     Alarms begin to sound. The intercom system comes to life with a dozen confused messages before one clear, mechanical voice vocalizes above the din. "Alert! Multiple Volucris incursions in civilian and military sectors of Magrinoch. All military personnel, report to battle stations. Civilians, please report to your nearest lockdown shelter, or to your nearest militia armory."
     The bug hole erupts with a dozen Volucris soldier nymphs. General Berök and the Ngrligru officers dispatch them, but from the sounds of movement deeper within, they can tell that more are on their way.
     "Listen up, spacies!" bellows General Kodwe. "We need to get you to your ships. The ground forces will handle this."
     Marcelian Anderia nods grimly before she and the other three space commanders follow Hodar Jana out of the room.
     "Ellisaire, feel like hunting some bugs?" General Kodwe's face is split with a huge grin, revealing rows of devilishly sharp fangs. The two warriors rush for the armory and their heavier weapons.

     The Magrinoch Command Center is in total disarray. Volucris soldier bugs stalk the corridors, and horribly dismembered Terrans litter the floors. This chaotic mess is where the elite commanders of the allies have been plunged.
     "Admiral Baruk, your shuttle and Kommendör Symanksi's fighter are in a hangar clear across the complex from Admiral Taukin's vessel," growls Brigadier Jana. "We're going to have to split up. I have an infantry team working its way toward us from the entrance. They'll reinforce us, and Lieutenant Mosi Tiu will escort Admiral Taukin. Until his squad reaches us, we get off the floor and keep quiet."
     "Sounds acceptable, Brigadier," says Hannah Baruk. "But will getting off the floor keep the bugs from finding us?
     "Fikrishiwa, Admiral. I don't know. It's just better to be above ground zero when a tunnel opens up."
     The floor buckles under Hodar Jana, and he drops into a freshly-opened Volucris hole before he can react. The lightly-armored officers stand with their backs to the wall, ready to face the coming bug horde.
     Weapons fire and high-pitched shrieks come from the Volucris hole, followed shortly afterwards by three smoking bug corpses, and an irate Brigadier Jana. The Ngrligru shakes himself free of ichor and bug chitin.
     "There were only workers down there," Jana reports. "I can hear and smell the soldiers. It's time to change strategies. We should start moving for the entrance, and hope Lieutenant Tiu finds us before we're surrounded."

    Outside the command center, the situation is much worse. Humongous Volucris Imperators, Blisters and Tyrants are lined up for many kilometers in every direction, bombarding the hastily-built defense wall with their foul biological weapons.
     With both General Chonge Kodwe and Brigadier Hodar Jana trapped in the command center, leadership of the Prefecture Legion of the Wolf has fallen to Colonel Tahlili Ayala. Like most commanders in the Wolf Legion, she's an Ngrligru, and just as vicious and capable as her superiors.
     Only one infantry division is actually stationed in Magrinoch. The city wasn't designed to house many troops, and like most Prefecture cities, it is designed to be defended by Rigs. Unfortunately, the wide streets and short buildings are helping the Volucris.
     Colonel Ayala has her infantry perched on the roofs of most buildings, with Grendel and Perseus battle armor defending barricades and road blocks from the bugs coming out of the burrow holes. With six and eight-lane streets throughout the cities, the Volucris soldiers are free to swarm.
     Because the fleet admirals are trapped, the Prefecture, Archadian and Meroniri fleets cannot coordinate their fighter launches, and can't bombard a populated city. Air support is sporadic and not very helpful. Fortunately, the Volucris flyers are kept in space by the huge human fleets.
     Tahlili Ayala growls in frustration, hoping her infantry will soon have one of the higher-ups back on the surface. "Captain Boles, I want an update! Has Lieutenant Tiu reported in yet?"
     "Sorry Colonel, no word. I think we should start moving again. This street could be overrun any minute--" Boles is cut off as a Volucris hole opens up beneath him. He falls in with a strangled cry.
     Colonel Ayala levels her rifle at the Volucris hole, hoping Boles will be the first thing out. Her hopes are shattered as a Trapper pulls itself out, charging her. The Colonel's escorting infantry open fire, adding to her own staccato bursts of automatic weapons fire. The Trapper falls, lifeless, only to be shoved aside by more Trappers, and several dozen Cutters. Ayala and her company are overwhelmed and torn to pieces.

    Lieutenant Mosi Tiu and his twenty-four subordinates proceed cautiously through the now-wrecked Magrinoch Command Center. Unlike the majority of his Ngrligru brethren, Tiu is a conservative commander.
     "Sergeant Ashe, have our point men reported anything?" Tiu checks his ammunition for the seventh time in as many minutes.
     "No sir, and I'm worried," Sergeant Matt Ashe chews his bottom lip roughly before continuing. "Anderson and Trapani are two minutes late checking in. I think we should close ranks and tighten our formation before we move on."
     "Agreed. Give the order."
     One by one, Lieutenant Tiu's troops pull back from checking corridors, then proceed down the main hall of the command center. They come to a halt in front of an unusually small bug hole. A discarded infantry rifle and several bloodstains mark the demise of at least one of their point men.
     "Anderson," Sergeant Ashe confirms, after checking the ID chip embedded in the rifle. "Ammo's been stripped, so Trapani might be alive. I wonder why he hasn't checked in?"
     "And where's the rest of Anderson?" Tiu muses. "The hole's too small for him. There no blood trail, just spatter. He wasn't dragged off, and there aren't any vents nearby."

     To the Volucris in the Magrinoch Command Center, Ellisaire Berök and Chonge Kodwe were death given flesh. The elite and powerful warriors tore through everything that came their way, burning bugs with their heavy weapons, or simply crushing them with brute strength. The giant Archadian and the short Ngrligru complement each other perfectly.
     "Yeyelaani! I really wish I'd learned the layout of this place properly," Kodwe swears. "This place must not be one of the new modular designs. It's probably an early Empire base, or something like that."
     "We have to get out, or at least find somewhere we can make contact with our troops upstairs. Why isn't the intercom working?" Berök demands.
     "Standard operating procedure," Kodwe explains. "In the event of an attack, normal intercoms are shut down and jammed, in order to keep the enemy from communicating inside our bases. The communications room has simple override controls, but they aren't answering any of my non-vocal transmissions. I think the bugs hit it early in the fighting."
     "Those are your troops on the surface, General Kodwe. I'll leave the decision to you. Do we head for or blast open an exit, or do we take back the communications room?"
     "The communications room can wait. The fleet's handling our battlefield coordination. With both Jana and myself down here, the troops up top are under Tahlili Ayala--she has quite a temper, and not much experience."

    "There's my hangar," Admiral Baruk sighs with relief. "I'm surprised that we didn't run into Tiu's squad. Jana, Taukin, Anderia, good luck. Kommendör Symanksi, if you'll kindly provide me with an escort upstairs, I'll have the Bastion take out that bug nest on ground zero and put an end to this invasion."
     The Archadian Admiral and Ngrligru Brigadier slip out of the hangar, as Baruk and Symanksi depart in their respective spacecraft. None of the officers notice the numerous empty bug holes under the landing pads.

     With the death of Colonel Ayala, the coordination between Prefecture defenders breaks down. Civilian deaths are low--most of them are safely sealed in fallout shelters, and the Volucris only show interest in crushing military opposition.
     Help is on the way. The siege lines of Imperators and other large bug soldiers is under attack by the Prefecture Sixth Mechanized Division. With no coordinating commanding officer, the counterstrike is confused, and not very effective.
     Finally, the defensive wall collapses entirely. Hordes of Volucris soldier bugs swarm into the city like angry bees. They link up with the soldiers already in the city from the burrowed holes, and soon sweep the streets of Prefecture infantry.

     Lieutenant Mosi Tiu's infantry team finds their missing point man, Private Joe Trapani. The soldier's condition is frightful, but he is breathing, if only barely.
     "Get him stabilized. The meds on the surface will take care of him after we find our buried commanders," orders Tiu.
     "Sergeant, Lieutenant, we've got Volucris coming up the hall. We're being ambushed!"

    "Admiral Baruk, check your approach. Your shuttle's flying erratically," warns Symanksi. "No, wait. It's my sensors. They're going crazy."
     "Kommendör, you have bugs plastered on your hull! Five soldiers." Hannah Baruk curses, then checks her shuttle's weapons systems. "They must've hitched a ride from the surface. I'm afraid I can't pick them off you without a proper gunnery crew."
     "I'll have to ditch the hull. My cockpit can fly independently, but I can't stay in space without the Skratta Måsen's oxygen recycler," Sugeir Symanksi blows the bolts connecting his cockpit module to his heavy fighter. The Skratta Måsen blows apart behind him, killing the hitchhikers. "I'll try to land in a secure zone. Be sure to have your security teams check you for pests when you get to the Bastion."
     "Roger, Kommendör," replies Baruk. "Happy landings."

Battle of Tavou: Preconceived Notions - 3/15/2324 - by James Davis

     The outermost wall of the Magrinoch command center exploded outward in a burst of heat and dust. Ellisaire Berök and Chonge Kodwe stepped out into the bright noon sun, into the din and chaos of battle. Prefecture infantry were being routed on all sides, and millions of Volucris soldier bugs were pouring into the city through holes in the wall, and tunnels in the ground.
     "Ha! The hunt has just begun!" Kodwe laughs, his feral eyes gleaming. "My army is in disarray, and the swarms are overwhelming. How I love a challenge!"
     Berök squints, and cocks his head to one side as he regards the piscoid general. "If you say so. As for me, get me to a tank, and I'll soon have my army's armored divisions rolling in."
     Before either can take another step, a tunnel opens up directly in front of them. Hooked claws appear above the hole, feeling about the edge.
     "Worker," Kodwe snorts. He raises his weapon to his shoulder, firing off a breacher round without taking careful aim. The round detonates on contact, sealing the hole with light rubble. The generals turn to enter the street, but stop when they hear a strange sound.
     The low-pitched sound is definitely a Volucris, but it sounds nothing like the grunts of Tyrants, the chittering of small soldiers, or the squeals and screams of Workers. The ground shifts, dirt and rocks flying as the beast beneath writhes about. Then everything is still, and the dust begins to settle.
     "Pain," the voice comes from the hole. This stops the generals dead in their tracks. Bugs do not speak. "Death comes. Screams come." The beast bursts from the earth, and it is great and terrible to behold.
     The creature is undoubtedly a Volucris, though great in stature, standing well above the head of Ellisaire Berök. It is hunched over, like a man carrying a heavy burden. If it were to stand, it would be taller still. Its carapace is black, glistening in places with foul ichor, dull in others with clinging dirt. Six mighty claws writhe about it like creatures with independent minds, and four fell tusks adorn its head. Its eyes shine with malevolent intellect.
     In a flash, the air is filled with rockets, grenades, and rounds from heavy weapons. Berök and Kodwe unleash the whole of their immense arsenals, throwing the black insect across the street, where it crashes into a building. The armored military building's wall holds, and the creature rebounds, full of wrath.
     Without hesitation, the generals run. "Split up, look for help!" yells Berök.

     After the close call with Sugeir Symanksi's Skratta Måsen, Hannah Baruk expected to find her shuttle covered with bugs when she lands aboard the Bastion, but her hangar crew reports nothing amiss. The admiral lands her shuttle without mishap, and heads for the bridge.
     Security teams check the whole hull twice, then seal the shuttle and park it until the next time it is needed. None think to check inside. After all, the slavering beaks of the insects would have caught and killed the admiral if they'd been aboard. Volucris never show restraint.
     Hannah Baruk enters the bridge, and is greeted with welcoming cheers. Evidently, her crew knew of the perilous escape from Magrinoch.
     "Give the order, and we'll put an end to that blight on Tavou," says Lieutenant Jim Cain. "I already locked on their crater."
     "Not yet," Admiral Baruk collapses into her command chair. "I am so tired. I wish the decision were mine to make. I'd finish them off. But if our Talisman cannons aren't as effective as we think, the patrols on the ground will be in terrible danger. We have to warn them first."
     "I'm already on it," Baruk's communications officer, Ensign Carl Ball, sends several pre-encoded messages over microwaves to the ground forces below. "Last I heard from below, the main dish in Magrinoch was destroyed. All Rigs have receivers, so they can hear us, but I doubt we'll be hearing back, unless they send a courier. The ball's still in your court, Admiral."
     "We'll wait."

     "Get Joe in the middle of us!" bellows Sergeant Ashe. "Standard forms. Drive 'em back!"
     The infantry of Mosi Tiu's squad are well-trained, some veterans of the Battle of Terra. They form four lines, two on each side of their commander. Half kneel in front, closest to the bugs, and half stand behind. The kneeling ranks wield riot shields, designed to hold back the melee fighters of the Volucris, while the standing ranks bear heavy rifles.
     Lieutenant Tiu leaves the command of the troops to his second-in-command. Matt Ashe is a veteran of the Battle of Terra, and other battles of the Second Exodus War. Tiu instead turns his attention to his stricken point-man, Private Joe Trapani.
     The infantryman is covered in a thick, frothy slime. His breathing is ragged, and slowing. A quick inspection by Tiu reveals several bloodless lacerations on the torso and the back of his neck. "No blood? He should be soaked in it." The puzzle remains unsolved, as Private Trapani dies. No amount of resuscitation brings him back.
     Oddly enough, the bugs are outnumbered by Lieutenant Tiu's squad. They're put down in a matter of seconds, and some flee.
     "I don't like this at all," mutters Tiu. "It's all too strange. Volucris don't retreat. Ashe, form up. Let's find those officers and get out."

     With his Skratta Måsen trashed, and his cockpit pod out of power, Flyga Kommendör Sugeir Symanksi strikes out for civilization, wherever it may be. His tracking systems went down with the main section of his fighter, and Tavou doesn't currently have GPS satellites compatible with his equipment.
     "I really am on the ice without skis this time," he mutters. On Icronia, Sugeir would be able to use compass and sextant to find his way anywhere, but the stars were unfamiliar. "I must be well to the east of Magrinoch. It was late afternoon there. It's early evening here."
     Without much hope of finding his own way home, he checks his homing beacon's power, and trudges off to the west.

     With Colonel Ayala dead, Captain Glenn Zurosky was hard-pressed to hold his company in line. The Field Commanders that held the chain of command between himself and the deceased regiment leader were dead, lost, or wounded beyond fighting capacity. None of the battalions were intact, and only a few companies were in fighting form at all. From the confused transmissions clogging the microwaves, Captain Zurosky knew that individual companies were fighting for their lives, trying to escape the wrecked city.
     Glenn's hundred were battle-hardened warriors, veterans all of the Battle of Terra. Their mighty Grendels kept the Volucris soldiers at bay all around, but who knew how long it would be before the bigger breeds started moving into the city?
     "All units, this is general Chonge Kodwe," Glenn perked his ears up at that. The Ngrligru general had been sorely missed in the fighting. "If anyone's in the command district, I need backup at--Eighth Street. I want armor, and lots of it."
     "We're close enough," says Captain Zurosky, half to himself. "On your toes, troops. Take to the roofs and start moving. Our general is back!"
     With a cheer, which is picked up and carried over the microwaves to all the beleaguered fighters, the company sets out to the general's aid.

     Ellisaire Berök has nothing to worry about after his initial encounter with the beastly Volucris creature. It has opted to chase General Kodwe, probably because the Ngrligru doesn't have the cybernetic implants or size of Berök.
     General Berök chooses to contact the Archadian fleet for backup, but finds to his dismay that the microwave relay system is out of commission. His small transmitter doesn't have the power to reach the orbiting allied fleets.
     Switching to a wide range communication system, Berök broadcasts on all Archadian ground communications systems. "Attention, all United Archadian Army forces, Magrinoch defenses have failed. Relay reinforcement request to the fleet. If any spare armor is lying about, rendezvous with me at the Magrinoch communications center."
     The response was swift. "General, we have a squad of Raiders on the way, and two armored regiments will be able to reinforce the city in an hour. If we can get the communications center back online and warn the infantry throughout the city, Admiral Hannah Baruk and Marshal Samuel Arias has promised heavy air support."
     "Baruk made it into space? I need a message relayed to the Bastion, right away."

"Brigadier, is that a body up ahead?" Marcelian Anderia points up the hall, where a lone figure is sprawled on the floor.
     "Yes, and I don't smell any blood. But he's cold. I'd see his heat if he were alive," the Ngrligru, Brigadier Hodar Jana, blinks as his vision shifts from normal spectrum to infrared, then back again. "Looks like one of mine, one of the Wolf Legion. He's wearing a standard power suit, but there's no weapon."
     The Archadian, Meroniri, and Ngrligru all stop and look about warily. Gunfire breaks out several yards up the hall, then stops abruptly. Two Cutters come tearing down the passage. Jana takes one out with a quick shot to the head, then catches the other before it can reach the soft Archadian Admiral and Meroniri captain. A second later, the feral Ngrligru has torn it to shreds.
     "Two went this way!" shouts pursue the deceased Volucris down the hall. Lieutenant Mosi Tiu and his squad appear, to the joy of the officers.
     "Lieutenant, if you'll kindly show us to the surface, we'll get out of here," Palontir Taukin still glances around, unsure of the safety even in the company of the Prefecture infantry.
     "No good, sir," Tiu responds. "I haven't lost contact with the surface. It's a nightmare out there. We need to get to your ships, and go into space."
     Sergeant Ashe has been inspecting the body. "Lieutenant, Brigadier, this was Private Anderson. He's dead, and covered in bloodless wounds, same as Trapani was. Slime too. That still leaves one heavy rifle and a double-share of ammunition missing."
     "No time to ponder this," growls Hodar Jana. "We'll let High Command sort this out, if anyone's left alive. I hate leaving him behind, but we can't slow down for anything." The squad quickly files away from the dead soldier, the navy officers safely in the center of the column.

     Tavou is not a world rich with creatures, nocturnal or diurnal. Even so, Sugeir Symanksi jumps at every chirping night insect. In the distance, the thin shrieks of the bugs can be heard. Sugeir's sharp ears quickly discern the direction.
     That would be ground-zero, where they landed. He muses silently. There are a lot of Prefecture patrols out here. It should be easier to find one than to get back to Magrinoch alone.
     Night turns to day in a brilliant series of deafening, blinding explosions. The ground shakes and rumbles like an angry giant, and Sugeir is thrown on his face. Bug shrieks rise above the din, and die away just as quickly.
     Before he can reach his feet, another new sound reaches the Icronian's ears. Pounding feet, coming towards him quickly. Has the swarm left its landing crater? Drawing his sidearm, the pilot leaps to his feet, determined to make his death costly.

     "Admiral, I confirm 99.9% accuracy. The Talismans took out a sizeable chunk of the Volucris on the ground, but now they're scattering everywhere. There are still several million live ones, and they'll be heading for Magrinoch as soon as they have their wits again." 'Four Hands' Cain leans back to admire his handiwork. "I think Berök made a good call."
     "Good shooting," Admiral Baruk congratulates her weapons officer. "Carl, send a call down to the Archadians. Let them know that air support will be in the field, but we need that communications center online before we can support our forces in the city."
     "Bugs inbound!" Four Hands takes only a second to report before returning to his deadly work. "Camera drones launching. Let's see if this new toy works, eh?"
     The Bastion is soon surrounded by a swarm of tiny robots, all feeding visual data to the cyber-augmented weapons officer. The Shredder Drones and Shivas close in on the huge warship.

     "Sir, we've lost ten men and armor suits. I just hit that thing with a breacher round, and it didn't even slow it down!" Captain Glenn Zurosky is desperate. Even his hardened veterans are losing their nerve in the face of the unstoppable Volucris creature. "Request permission to draw it out of the city for artillery bombardment."
     "Go. Take as many men as you need, but I need something fast. I have to get to a communications center that works. Coriton's closest, but if we can get a VTOL and some escorts, the spaceport will be faster," Kodwe rubs his lateral line, much like a human would stroke a chin. Battle of Tavou: Clean-Up - 3/17/2324 - by James Davis

     Tavou Central is a huge city, dwarfing its once-beleaguered neighbor, Magrinoch. The victorious armies are on parade through the city. They get to have one big party, then must board the dropships to move on to their next assignments.
     Seismic probes have been set up around the city's perimeter. Now that the allies know the Volucris' burrowing tactics, they'll never allow themselves to be taken by surprise again.
     Tavou is completely bug free. The "Eviscerator"--as Chonge Kodwe has dubbed it--was spotted one last time, as it hitched a ride into space on a transporter bug. The space swarm has not been seen since disappearing out of Tavou space.
     Generals Chonge Kodwe and Ellisaire Berök stand outside a the foundations of a new building, still under construction. "Two months." Kodwe sighs. "If they'd just waited two months before attacking, we wouldn't have had a single communications problem. This Ansible network is doing wonders in Meronir's Republic, but we're just now getting our equipment."
     "They're very aggressive." Berök murmurs. "Do you expect an attack in any of the other planets in this District?"
     "That's General O'Neill's concern now." Chonge Kodwe growls. "They're ordering my army back to New Albany for rest and refit. Patrick and the Tiger Legion will meet the next attack, if there is one. I've lost two Divisions of commanding officers. I need time to reassign academy graduates."
     "You're down to two front-line armies. I know I'm can't speak for our government, but I'll do everything I can to ensure that we'll continue to support you in this war."

     Tavou's orbital traffic is almost as busy as it was at the height of the battle. The Bastion and the Prefecture Navy's Sixth Fleet are still in orbit, as is most of Palontir Taukin's Archadian fleet. The Mesarthrim and Icronian vessels have all departed for home.
     Admiral Baruk paces back and forth across the bridge of the Bastion. "Our performance was absolutely pathetic! Except for Cain here, none of our gunners knew what they were shooting at." Baruk fumes, waving both arms in front of her. "Marshall, I can't face those bugs again. Not without adequate scanner technology. Four of my Heavy Cruisers are down for repairs."
     "You won't have to." The image of Admiral Robert Marshall is on one of the Bastion's communications systems. "Your fleet has been recalled to Ueda and New Albany for refit. Your gunnery commander will be transferred to Rahn Industries R&D while the rest of your fleet has some time off."
     "Acknowledged. I'll relay orders to the Fleet, and we'll be away in two hours." Hannah Baruk stops pacing, and sits in her command chair. "Do we have any idea what happened to the bugs, sir?"
     "Just a theory. The Grand Inquisitor and Professor Hlow plan to deliver a presentation to the Alliance of Planets as soon as this plague on Terra blows over--if it blows over."

     Far beyond the reach of the Tavou war fleets, the Volucris Swarm floats motionless. At its very center is a huge, glimmering structure. It is a hive, a structure woven from filaments as strong as diamonds. Over a thousand years of work by weaver bugs has gone into it. It is the home of the Nobles, the malevolent minds behind the Swarm.
     Inside, it is silent as a grave. Trillions of bugs move about. No human would ever be able to understand the intricate, noiseless communication of the Volucris race. The stinking, overpowering scent of pheremonic communication.
     Inquiry. Objective located?
     Confusion. Objective unfound. Twisted time. Change constant.
     Inquiry. Objective closer?
     Clarity. Directive shift. Destination determined. Nerve found.
     Command. Prepare inspection. Departure soon.
     Decisions have been made, calculations completed. The twisted intellects of the Swarm have set their eyes on their next goal, whatever it may be.

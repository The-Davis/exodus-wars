= Part 1 =

September 21, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Alpha Centauri System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;With the success of the Beta Commando team in capturing Rear Admiral Jethro--and Kalidasa to boot--Uber General Tischler decided to press his luck and hurt the Empire again. This time, the target was the Alpha Centauri Shipyards, deep in Imperial territory. 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;At 0200 hours Beta Commando docked with the shipyard, using a Whale Class Hauler "liberated" from the Empire during the Titanian 12 operation nearly a year earlier. Once aboard, they planted explosives on the Imperial Carrier Insurrection. The carrier was unmanned--still under construction.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Beta Commando was surprised to see security so lax, so they pushed ahead and planted more explosives--this time in several bulkheads of the shipyard itself. Then they were discovered.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Imperial security engaged Beta Commando, but was soundly defeated. The commandos commandeered an Imperial Odyssey cruiser, then escaped the system, blowing the shipyard, the Insurrection, and the Whale they'd come in on. The operation was a total success, with no Mesarthrim losses.

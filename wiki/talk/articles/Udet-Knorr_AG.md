You used "Humans" instead of "Terrans".
Is this an intentional emphasis on the differentiation of the Mesarthrim, or merely an oversight?

== A little of both... ==

Oversight in that Terran would probably have been a better word, but intentional in that when Camp came to power it was about defining the "Mesarthrim" Race. This was the reason for the mercantilism, nationalization, and sacking of all non-mesarthrim from any position of authority over a Mesarthrim.

And this also helps explain why so many Mesarthrim supported camp early on. The Mesarthrim people felt jaded by the Prefecture at the end of the Second Exodus War. The Mesarthrim liberated the Titan system, supported revolts and landed armies on planets across the empire. And in the end, gained nothing. So the Mesarthrim wanted something, and deep down, wanted to prove themselves - undeniably.

And then, once Camp started the war, there was no way (initially) to stop it. Which also explains why when Stadt came along carrying a new Mesarthrim banner, so many flocked to his cause. (but on that same note, many mesarthrim did not join Stadt because they felt he was a betrayer).

Which all this in the end is solved by the Treaty of Tavou.

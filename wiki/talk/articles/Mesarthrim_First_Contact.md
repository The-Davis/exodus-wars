= Part 1 =

January 13, [[2322]]

by James Davis and Andrew Sears

=====Centus System=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;An Imperial picket fleet made up of three Vigilant frigates met up with the United Mesarthrim Clans' battle fleet, commanded by Commandant Arnold Stadt.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Despite the fact that the Mesarthrim fleet outnumbered the picket ten to three, the commander of the Imperial picket--John Dominic--ordered Stadt to "surrender to the might of the Emperor and his glorious Return."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Arnold Stadt laughed at the Imperial commander and blew the entire picket to pieces. Only the automatically-jettisoned logs returned to the Empire to inform the Royal Imperial Navy admiralty of what had happened.

= Part 2 =

January 15, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears



&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After tracking the log pods back to the Empire, Mesarthrim High Command made contact with the Alliance of Planets. Up until now, the Mesarthrim had been completely cut off from the rest of the galaxy by years of war with an alien species known as the [[Anderung]].<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Negotiations were short and to the point. Both the Alliance of Planets and the Mesarthrim hated the Empire, so the United Mesarthrim Clans were quickly entered into the Great Charter of the Alliance as a Member State. Uber General Andreas Tischler, leader of the Mesarthrim, released this statement to the media.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "The Mesarthrim are pleased to be a Member State of the Alliance. My forces are standing by to aid in the defense of the Titanian system. We will push the Empire out of Alliance territory." 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After the United Mesarthrim Clans were signed in as a Member State, Meronir's Republic moved several construction facilities to Centus, the home starsystem of the Mesarthrim. Once there, they built a space station in orbit of Mesar--the only inhabitable planet in the Centus system.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The station, the Assistant, is equipped with the Ansible technology necessary to allow the Mesarthrim to communicate with the rest of the Alliance of Planets. Also aboard the station are several atmospheric cleaning ships, designed to help clean up the damage to Mesar caused by the earlier Anderung war.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;After the Assistant was installed, Uber General Tischler moved a large amount of troops into the Titanian starsystem to help push out the Royal Imperial Army and Royal Imperial Navy on Titanian 12.

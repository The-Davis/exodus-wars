== Designed by Kriegshöfe ==

The Howell was designed by Kriegshöfe, like its sister ship, the [[Sickle class Cruiser]], it has a front 'pod' connecting it via a boom to the rest of the ship.  Kriegshöfe doesn't exist anymore, she went belly up when the [[Anderung]] raided them in [[2275]], destroying their shipyards, and killing off their designers and staff.

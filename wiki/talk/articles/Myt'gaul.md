Sorry, but the 2324 entry is a bit far fetched, at best.  If they could navigate into the Tolana sector (only mapped by the Ixoarchada) and avoid all Ixoarchada patrol and sensor stations, then find, approach and land on Glengarr without authorization or (apparently) even being noticed, and then they somehow blended into a world of 6 1/2 foot tall, orange-ish skinned Ixoarchada, then why didn't they just capture Lucius Black himself?  They lack interfaces, so they can't even interact with common equipment in Ixoarchada space.  

I don't mean to be a Debby Downer, but really.  It's a bit far fetched to think that only a year after the Ixoarchada announced themselves to the galaxy, that a bunch of extraordinarily different looking people could fly through the unknown regions of space and start to sabotage lots of stuff and never get noticed ever.  It's also tough to think they could communicate with agents all the way in the Pelagrim sector, since there was neither Ansible nor Avmaska-Stig at the time.

== Political implications... ==

All that is, of course, ignoring the fact that it would have been a terrible disaster should the Ixoarchada notice them.  So soon after the Exodus War, and with a fractious society as it is, it would have been utter lunacy to provoke the Ixoarchada into a war.  6.6 billion in space for 300 years vs. 130 billion in space for 2000.  Even Black was not that much of a fool (kept the Omegas mostly away from the Meroniri).

== James pointed this out already. ==

So, then, what I need to know from you then, is where is a world that their operatives could go that would be near the Ixoarchadian space, but not completely Ixoarchadian so that they could slip in?

== In 2324... ==

...quite simply, nowhere.  September 2323 they made first contact.  As isolationist and protectionist as they are, within only a few months, they were not many places.  It's hard to leave Ixoarchada space, harder to get in, and it's not like they are actively colonizing places outside the Tolana sector.

I cannot think of any way to really do it.  Your best bet would be to attempt attacks on Ixoarchada embassy type areas within the Pelagrim sector and beyond.  There are few at the time...maybe on Earth, maybe in Prefecture and/or Reigen space, but then you risk pissing off the other governments.  Later on your will have Ixoarchada production facilities in Prefecture space, but that would be several years later, during the Tempest War, and the UCS would be very interested in NOT getting the Ixoachada involved.

Do you really *have* to have it happen?  What's the purpose?  Doing it for the sake of doing it without major plot implications is pointless.  If you really want some sort of Myt'gaul/Ixoarchada confrontation, your best bet is to put your efforts into writing about the attempted seizure of the JDF over Comur, leading to the [[Second Tirshan Uprising]].  [[Unternehmen First Wache]] is only a tiny blurb right now.

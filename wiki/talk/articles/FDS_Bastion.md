== Answers ==

The proton bond armor used by the Bastion is an Ikronin design, not the Ixoarchada Falkran armor. The write-up has not been updated yet, so it may reflect some inaccurate facts.

Even if it were Falkran, the Bastion's construction pre-dates the Tempest War, so it would be possible to have it used.

While it is a monitor, that is a sub-classification of battleship. The Prefecture uses "BG" as hull designations, so yes, you should please stop re-changing the hull designation to MG when I change it back to BG! :)

As for the armor, the main problem is that the Bastion was laid down in May of 2323, launched Feb 1 of 2324.  The Ixoarchada made first contact in late September of 2323.  It's just not feasible that as soon as they made first contact, the Ixoarchada would agree to rush into production a piece of military hardware custom forged from one of their hallmark technologies.  If the ship was built later than that (2326?) it would be more of a possibility.

But then you have a whole other problem.  It would be difficult for the Prefecture engineers to affect repairs or maintain such a system that they lack total knowledge of, or the capacity to work on.  You'd need an auxiliary contingent of engineers on the ship just to work on it.  That's why it's best, I think, if we make it Ikronin.  They would have no problem garrisoning some of their engineers on a Prefecture ship, whereas (especially once the war starts), the Ixoarchada would be a bit less than inclined.  Further, Ikronin armor is simpler.  Falkran (as the writeup shows) requires lots of additional coolant and heat dispersal systems.  

However, I do like the idea of there being cooperation.  I think in the post-war period, especially with the Meroniri getting aggressive, the Ixoarchada should open up some of their technology to the CIN and Tripartite Alliance.  Food for thought.

=====Theia Headquarters=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In an unprovoked attack, the Empire violated the Titanian Peace Accords and ended the three year treaty with the Alliance of Planets. One of their Vigilant Class Frigates managed to evade Titanian Defense Force patrols, getting close enough to the world of Titanian 6 to launch a nuclear strike on Titan City.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Titanian Defense Force observation satellites picked up the incoming missiles early enough to activate the defense shields for the city. General-Commander Scott Sanburn quickly informed Meronir's Republic and the rest of the Alliance of Planets.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General-Commander Scott Sanburn: "The Empire deserves no pity or mercy. They deserve to pay the price--complete destruction--and I plan to give them the bill."

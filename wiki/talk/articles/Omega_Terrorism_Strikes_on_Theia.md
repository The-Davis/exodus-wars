=====Titanian 11 - Titan System - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle over Titanian 11 had made the Imperial forces realize exactly how costly a full-scale assault would be. Robert Marshall decided to deploy his cruisers in three-ship elements, with heavy fighter escort, and use them as convoy raiders. He reasoned that if he could prevent supplies from flowing into the Titanian system, the outnumbered TDF defenders would be unable to re-supply. Inevitably, they would succumb to attrition.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The tactic was initially successful. The Titanians were forced to deploy most of their fighters as convoy escort, and by mid-March the TDF was losing fighters at the rate of three a day. The ADF-5000 Patriot was left crippled in dock when a convoy carrying critical parts for its repair was intercepted and destroyed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall hadn’t anticipated the resourcefulness of the Titanians, however. With most of their planets still open for mining, they were able to pull together a respectable supply of raw materials. By early March, orbital farms and factories had been constructed around Titanian 6, reducing their dependence on out-system trade.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In March, Lucius Black gave Marshall a new resource, one that could deal with the Titanian’s burgeoning industry.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The emperor dispatched a group of Omegas to Titanian 6; they infiltrated the populace and began a program of propaganda and sabotage, climaxing in the destruction of an entire orbital factory complex.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;By April 1st, the citizens of Titanian 6 were nervous and demoralized. Omega Squadron carried out near-daily attacks, although their effectiveness had been reduced by the vigilance of Titanian police and special forces. Power and many commodities were being rationed or restricted in an attempt to conserve resources. Politicians worried about food riots and civil unrest. The Imperial siege was taking its toll.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On that day, the Titanian Defense Force suffered the most humiliating defeat in its history.

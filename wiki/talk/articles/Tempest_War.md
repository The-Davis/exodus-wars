===2327===
* Bombing of Leskovik.
* Tempest Declaration.
* First Battle of Centus.
* First Tirshan Uprising.

===2328===
* Laojiu District Invaded.
* Beta Arae System Falls to UCS.
* Lyrae System Invaded, Holt nuked.
* Avior System Falls to UCS.
* Lyrae invaded by UCS, UCS sieges Ariège.
* Gamma Algedi System Invaded.

===2329===
* UCS secures Holt.
* Prefecture counterattacks Gamma Algedi - UCS caught offguard on Pesht.
* UCS forced to withdraw from Pesht, UCS levels cities as they retreat.
* Theta Algedi System invaded.

===2330===
* Zeta Algedi System Invaded & Captured.
* Adelson captured by UCS.
* Xi Herculis System Invaded, only Crofton secured.
* Des System Invaded, only Des Wei secured.
* Prefecture counter attack Theta Algedi System.

===2331===
* UCS secures Ward and Bolan after bitter siege.
* Des Arc defences crumble under continual bombardment by the UCS.
* UCS driven off Tejat.
* Prefecture/Auellal Fleet annihilated in orbit of Holt.
* Failed liberation of the Lyrae System.

===2332===
* Combined Offensive by Prefecture and Auellal against Zeta Algedi System.
* UCS capture Caledon.
* Failed liberation of the Xi Herculis System.
* Prefecture renew offensive against Sindh.

===2333===
* Prefecture drive UCS off Sindh.
* Auellal insurgents overthrow UCS forces on Caledon
* Auellal capture Novar.
* Xi Herculis system attacked by Prefecture/Auellal/Ikronin fleets.  Battle turns into long drawn out siege.

===2334===
* Xi Herculis system secured by allied fleets.
* Second Tirshan Uprising.
* Second Battle of Centus.
* Xi Herculis system liberated by Allied forces.
* Novar recaptured by UCS.
* Prefecture request Ixoarchada assistance in ending the fighting.
* Stadt, Edge Brothers, & Bethany Tischler withdraw their signatures from the UCS Constitution and declare a Mesarthrim Federation as legitimate successor to United Mesarthrm Clans.
* Taviridis Somarchada recognize the Mesarthrim Federation, withdraw recognition from the United Centusi States.

===2335===
* Stadt uses his fleet and the Auellal Armada to secure outlying Mesarthrim systems.
* Treaty is Signed.
* War trials begin.

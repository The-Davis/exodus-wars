== Re-writing... ==

I think this is an interesting way to end the war.  We can remove the actual battle to retake the Prometheus Stardocks, and keep the focus on the Comuri Uprising.  The Prometheus Fleet could just be  political/intrigue storyline, but one which severely strains relations.  That way, the Taviridis could be uninvolved.

Or, we could go the route of neutralizing peace-''makers''.  The Taviridis could still arrive at the Second Battle of Centus, but instead of assaulting the Centusi fleet, they give a cease-fire ultimatum, after which peace will be "enforced".  Or perhaps they arrive just after the invasion has begun, and cut off the troop ships from resupply.  The Prefecture Army is stranded, but the Mesarthrim have millions of hostile soldier son their home planet.  If the Tripartite Alliance agreed to mediated end of hostilities, then so too might the UCS (which by this point would control only the Honis, Centus, and Cirus systems--remember the "Free Laojiu Republic is a puppet state, but not technically Mesarthrim territory).  It wouldn't be surrender, it would be arbitration, and everyone could save face.

Remember, as of 2331, Centusi space is under siege, much of the Prefecture is ragged, running on rations, and even Cinead is under a serious blockade except via wormhole gates (Caledon Airlift, anybody?)  Lorithan is devoured by the Volucris, and nobody knows if they might return.  By late 2331 or early 2332, the Ixoarchada have shown they'll do ''whatever'' it takes to win a fight (bye bye Lorithan), and everyone would like peace after so long.

Tough bit would be for the unification of the Pelagrim Sector.  At war's end, UCS has still 3 systems, but both Tirshan (their industrial heartland) and Cinead are independent.  Perhaps a new government should be formed, more as a Federation than a singular, centralist state, and that should supplant all three?

Now the question: does the Prefecture re-invade the "independent" planets?  Does it give another vote?  What happens?

== James' responses ==

I intend to alter the Prefecture's position to be a little more precarious.  Geoffrey Nolan's sacrifice gives them superiority over Mesar itself, but the UCS war machine is still very dangerous.

A deal which would dismantle this war machine and leave the Mesarthrim under Prefecture-friendly control would be very desirable, and would be worth giving up their Pelagrim Sector holdings.

The Mesarthrim will get quite a bit out of this deal, actually.

== Give and Take... ==

So, the Mesarthrim give up their hold on territories outside the Pelagrimm sector, and in return, they get Tavou and the Mimosa System?  With the war over, and a governmental reshuffle being necessary (Camp will die/be removed from power somehow), Comur can easily be brought back into the fold (perhaps part of the treaty is for the Taviridis to negotiate reintegration of the Tirshan system)?)  

After this, the Mesarthrim have the whole of the Pelagrim Sector for themselves.  What to do with the Auellel League is another story.  I'll let you guys decide that one.  If we wanted to be really wicked, we could say that the Ikronin and Prefecture negotiate peace at the expense of the Auellel.  In exchange for peace, they agree not to intervene if the Mesarthrim retake Cinead System.  That's a fairly vicious betrayal, but the Auellel are rebellious traitors in the first place, so it's not so awful I guess.

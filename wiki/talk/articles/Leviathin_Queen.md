===Build Notes===

The Leviathan was inspired by the Games Workshop "Battlefleet Gothic" ship of the same name.  The Battlefleet Gothic and Warhammer 40,000 "Tyranids" are remarkably similar to the Volucris/Buggers, as are the Zerg and the Bugs of Starship Troopers.  Personally, I think we Sci-Fi writers can be very uncreative at times.

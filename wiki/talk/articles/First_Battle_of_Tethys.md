= Part 1 =

January 4, [[2316]]

by Seth Dickinson


(note: to be rewritten/expanded)

=====Tethys - Titan System - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sixteen brilliant lights erupt over Tethys, heralding the arrival of the Imperial Vanguard Fleet 3rd Squadron. Nearly a score of Vigilant cruisers disengage their drives and settle into orbit. The maneuver takes nearly an hour, as the cruisers must emerge from hyperspace far from the planet or risk being torn apart.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The 3rd Squadron is only a probing force, testing the strength of the TPA defenses around Mnemosyne in preparation for an Imperial attack. They are not disappointed. General-Commander Scott Sanburn is waiting for them, hidden on the other side of the planet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;As soon as sensors on the surface of Tethys relay information of the Imperials’ arrival, Sanburn springs his trap. Ten ADF-5000 cruisers, each accompanied by three squadrons of ADF-800 fighters, slingshot around the planet, converging on the 3rd Squadron from all sides and effectively encircling them. Sanburn himself leads the TPA assault from his flagship, the Liberator. He is eager to take the fight to the hence-unstoppable Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The TDF fighters close in to attack the Vigilants, whose titanium armor is ineffective against their particle-beam weaponry. Within moments one Imperial cruiser is disabled; minutes later another falls. The maneuverable TDF fighters take only a few losses from the Vigilants’ Autocannon.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The remaining Imperials launch a concerted effort to break out of the TDF encirclement, firing a full volley of MAC rounds at the TDF flagship. Only Scott Sanburn’s quick reactions save the ''Liberator'' from destruction; the ship takes six MAC hits and is disabled, leaving a gap in the TDF encirclement.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In an effort to prevent the Imperials from escaping, Sanburn orders a full attack. The ADF cruisers close in on the Imperial ships; the ADF-5000 ''Colin Powell'' rakes the Imperial cruiser ''Hammurabi'' with heavy particle beams, gutting her. The remaining Imperials drop their plasma shields and accelerate away from Tethys, abandoning the fight.

= Part 2 =

January 17, [[2316]]
by Seth Dickinson

(note: rewrite)

=====Titanian 11 - Titanian System - 1300 Hours=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Scott Sanburn’s skirmish with the 3rd Squadron ended in a defeat for the Imperials, but the engagement gave Imperial Admiral Robert Marshall a sense of the TDF crew’s skill and the power of their ships. He decided to exploit that knowledge.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;On January 17 he attacked Titanian 11 with the 3rd and 4th Squadrons of Atlas Wing, totaling 40 Vigilant cruisers and nearly a hundred fighters and bombers. Scott Sanburn was on hand to meet him with twenty ADF-5000 cruisers and half a thousand fighters, mostly older ADF-F-400s.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The first large fleet engagement in the Titanian system was a bloody affair. Neither side was prepared to deal with the power of the other’s weapons. Robert Marshall, a tactical genius, had learned one thing from 3rd Squadron’s earlier defeat: the Imperial Vigilants were vulnerable to fighter attack. He kept his ships in tight formation, allowing them to defend themselves with overlapping fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle opened with each side exchanging long-range fire outside the orbit of Titanian 11’s moons. The Imperials got the best of the initial engagement; their powerful MAC salvoes damaged the ADF-5000 Patriot so heavily she was forced to withdraw.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Scott Sanburn ordered two hundred of his fighters to attack and pin down the Imperials while his cruisers maneuvered. The brave TDF pilots flew into the teeth of a hailstorm of Autocannon fire; Imperial Valkyries and Ravens swarmed out to meet them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The resulting dogfight was a meat-grinder. The ADF-400 fighters that the Titanians used were inferior to the Imperial Raven and Valkyrie, but they had the advantage of numbers. Fire support from Marshall’s cruisers more than made up for the difference. The Imperials lost a quarter of their fighters, and a cruiser was crippled; seventy-five Titanian fighters were blown out of space or disabled.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The fighter engagement ended when Robert Marshall realized what Sanburn was up to. The TDF commander had seemed to be withdrawing, but he used the gravity of a nearby moon to slingshot his ships back into the fight. The maneuver caught the Imperial formation in the flanks, and Sanburn’s ships blew the cruiser Ordeal apart.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Marshall withdrew for a few minutes, then formed his cruisers into a wall of battle, powered up their shields, and met the Titanians head-on.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;MAC rounds and particle beams filled space with hellish fury as the two sides’ formations split up. The Imperial cruisers worked in three-ship clusters, while the Titanian cruisers fought alone but with heavy fighter support. Vigilant clusters and ADF-5000 battle groups dueled across tens of thousands of kilometers. Sanburn’s Liberator and Marshall’s Atlas sparred over Titanian 11’s north pole, using the planet’s strong magnetic field to confuse each other’s sensors.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The capital ship engagement raged for half an hour; three Titanian cruisers were heavily damaged, while the Imperials suffered a two cruisers destroyed, one crippled, and two damaged, including the Ordeal, which was caught in Sanburn’s initial rush. Imperial Valkyrie bombers made runs at the Titanian ships, but were forced away by the fighter screens.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial ships began running out of ammunition for their MAC guns, and Sanburn’s tougher, larger ADF-5000s, their particle beams free of ammunition restrictions, began to gain the upper hand. Marshall ordered his frigates to concentrate all their fire on the Titanian fighters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The blistering hail of Autocannon rounds sent the TDF pilots scurrying for cover; No longer worried about fighter harassment, Imperial Valkyries, armed with Apocalypse nukes, began lining up for attack runs on the suddenly exposed ADF-5000s. Sanburn, recognizing his sudden peril, ordered his cruisers to retreat and regroup. Both sides withdrew to their bases, knowing that another massive confrontation over Titanian 11 was inevitable.

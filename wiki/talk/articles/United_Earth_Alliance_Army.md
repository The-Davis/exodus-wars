United Earth Alliance Defense Force
Army Vehicles

Pre-War: circa 2315
AV200 - Light armored car. Useful for scouting, raiding, support of faster moving elements.  Can fit 2 a Bison, which is a modified Buffalo.  Usually operate indepdently in support of infantry.  2 crew: Driver and commander/gunner.  Carries no passengers.  Replaced by Buccaneer leading into the Battle of Terra.

AV400 - Medium armored car.  Consider it a light-tank.  Can somewhat keep up with and support AV200s, but also support and combat larger vehicles.  2 crew: Driver and commander/gunner.  AV400 chassis is the basis for several other vehicles.  Replaced by Enforcer leading into the Battle of Terra.

AV401 - Mobile point-defense platform.  Travel with larger vehicles, and deployed to both armored spearheads and forward bases.  Has 8 point-defense turrets capable of shooting down missiles and hitting bombs high in the air.  2 crew: Driver and commander/weapon's computer operator.  Served throughout the war.

AV412 - Light Self-propelled artillery.  Has a 120mm, autoloading mortar with a range of a few kilometers, and a RoF of 60rpm.  Used to support infantry and other vehicles, and provide indirect fire in assault and siege situations.  Served throughout the war.

AV423 - Heavy Self-propelled artillery.  Carries a 155mm or 175mm cannon (although the 175mm requires strengthening of the chassis and struts, and thus they are not swappable.  It's one or the other).  2 crew, as normal.  Unlike most artillery, it's barrel can fire at only 5 degrees elevation--nearly head-on.  This was used as a makeshift tank/Rig-killer until later vehicles were developed.  Served throughout the war.

AV500 - Heavy Armored car, same as MBT.  Heavy cannons on top, light anti-personnel cannons on the sides.  3 crew: Driver, gunner, commander.  Primary heavy armor of the UEA and, later, the Commonwealth.  Served throughout the war.

UV10 - Standard all-purpose military truck and transport.  Driver and 3 passengers, plus a truck bed.  Think: Willy's jeep.  Rugged, useful, but not really what you want to drive about in during combat.  Served throughout the war.


Wartime developments: post 2315
Buccaneer - Light Armored Car.  Replacement for the aged AV200.  Improved drivetrain, suspension, top-speed, armor, control systems, weapons, targeting, optics, communications--the works.  Improved low-G performance as well.  Also had Sunburst/Starburst defense system, the first UEA vehicle with such.  Increased size meant it was tight squeeze for 2 in a Bison.  Crew: 2, as AV200.

Enforcer - Medium Armored car.  Developed as a replacement for the AV400.  Same upgrades as seen on the Buccaneer (including Sunburst/Starburst).  The chassis was higher off the ground for increase performance and mobility, but made the design ill-suited for use in artillery and support roles as the AV400.  Crew: 2, as AV400.

Archer - Heavy Self-Propelled Artillery and Tank/Rig killer.  The Archer mounted a 203mm gun, giving it high-velocity gun with superb range.  The main benefit was range, allowing it to hit enemy tanks and Rigs beyond their retaliatory range.  Overall range was beyond standard Imperial designs, which (along with its mobility) made it mostly immune to counter-battery fire.  Unlike the AV423, the gun was specifically designed to be fired at 0 elevation, allowing it to fire at very nearby targets if needbe, although with considerably reduced accuracy.  The larger size is due to the heavy gun, giving it great stability, but upped its crew, transport and support requirements to the same level as an AV500.  It gained much favor, but smaller artillery units were still used due to their versatility and lower support requirements.  

General Use Patrol, Type-E truck: 'Guppy' - Designed to replace the UV10, which was showing its 30+ year age, particularly in combat zones.  Guppy was designed for more protection, potential to be armed with 2 sponson guns, and a heavier payload/higher towing capacity.  Also had enhanced drivetrain and suspension for off-road and low-G performance.  Think of the shift from Willy's Jeep to Hummvee.  1 Driver, 1 passenger, rear-section for 2-4 passengers.

Chevalier - First heavy APC for the UEA in many decades.  Heavy armor protected a crew of 2 (driver and commander/gunner), and a total of 8 infantry.  Treaded design was more stable and better defended than wheeled vehicles, but was slower than the Guppy and AV200.  Thus, only units supporting heavy AV500s and Archers used them, as well as urban warfare units.  

Flail - Heavy Self-Propelled Missile Artillery.  Built on the Chevalier chassies, it has an automated turret made to track incoming aerospace craft and VTOLs, as well as air-to-ground ordinance.  Made to protect artillery units from aerial attack, although its cannons can also fire on ground-targets.  It's primary weapon is a 230mm guided MLRS comprising twelve missile tubes and a total battery of 36 rockets with varying warheads.  The MLRS is ground-to-ground only, and cannot fire on aerial targets.  Has a range beyond any of the other artillery units.

UV20 - Mid-to-late war purchase from the Ikronin.  A repulsor-driven flatbed utility truck.  Purchased in limited numbers for supplying front-line and fast-moving units, but never broadly adopted due to the repulsor tech not being widely understood by ADF mechanics.

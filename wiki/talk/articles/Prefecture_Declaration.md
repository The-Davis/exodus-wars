= Part 1 =

April 24, [[2322]]

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Following the fall of Bethija the Grand Admiral of the Empire marshaled the front-line fleets in the Sol starsystem. While in orbit of Terra, he addressed the Fleets, Emperor Lucius Black, and the entire population of the known galaxy by broad-band transmission.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Hadrian: "Friends and enemies, I have come to bring you dire news. Bethija, a key planet in the production of food and light weaponry, has fallen to the armed forces of the United Mesarthrim Clans of Centus. Only a single carrier has escaped the battle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There is no cause for alarm--yet. True, we lost most of the 3rd Core Fleet, but the bulk of the Royal Imperial Navy and Army are intact and we outnumber our enemies. We have two choices before us now.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"With the completion of the Gen2 Project, we have the firepower and technology to crush the Mesarthrim. Our Apocalypse nuclear warheads can incinerate their planet, and our legions can systematically destroy all resistance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"But at what cost? We know that there's an impending alien threat. They took our greatest minds from the Pluto Research Base. We can crush the Mesarthrim, but what will we have left to raise against the inevitable alien invasion?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Soldiers, citizens and leaders of the Empire, I beg--no, I demand--that you end this senseless slaughter of our brothers. Mankind must band together against the alien threat, or we will be doomed to extinction. What then will come of your mighty Empire?"

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lucius Black promptly stripped Hadrian of all titles and authority, and ordered his arrest and execution. No one aboard the Jerome Black--the flagship--was willing to obey the order. The Iron Hand sent boarders, and a minor conflict ensued between the Emperor's loyal soldiers and the rebels on the Jerome Black, but soon the battle stopped. Grand Admiral Hadrian transmitted a copy of an old command log from centuries ago.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Undeniable proof--assuming the log was genuine--that Lucius Black engineered the destruction of his own world. Blackis was bombarded by one of Black's warships.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Admiral Hadrian: "Blackis was not destroyed by the United Earth Alliance, as you've always been told to believe. Your Emperor destroyed it, all in the name of giving you a foe to fight for his own foolish cause. God help us. You made a murderer your Emperor."

= Part 2 =

April 26, [[2322]]

by Andrew Sears

=====Mesar=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Following the stunning revelation that the Empire was about to split in a massive coup, the Mesarthrim High Command called an emergency session.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Dr. Mark Edge: "I said it before, I'll say it again! With the threat of an alien invasion, all humans--regardless of political beliefs--are allies."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Uber General Tischler: "I think that the Imperial Grand Admiral is overconfident, or perhaps grandstanding. If he really believes that crap about bombarding Mesar, he's either cockier than a Bantam, or totally insane.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Absurdities aside, he is calling for a peaceful resolution to the war. If he fails in that and it comes to blows with the Imperial loyalists--who are superior in numbers to the rebels, I might add--the United Mesarthrim Clans should support the rebellion in any fight against the Empire. We should send diplomats to Hadrian right away."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Stadt: "Forgive me for speaking freely, Uber General, but that's crazy! The Empire is the enemy. All of them are the enemy, and we should destroy them all, ja?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Commandant Parman: "I'm afraid I agree with Stadt on this one. We've fought the Empire, and we know that they're tricky. What if this whole mess is a ploy to get our high-ranking leaders into negotiations with their Admiral? They keep Omega Squadron assassins everywhere."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Carter: "I think that negotiations are in order. At the very least, we can concede to a non-aggression pact with the rebels while they fight the Empire. If the winner of the conflict is hostile, they'll be weakened enough for us to defeat them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Heidlemaan: "Our people want peace. They've been through war almost since this colony started, first with the Anderung, and now the Empire. Haven't we fought enough? I think that Admiral Hadrian is honorable. He'll keep a treaty."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The meeting ended shortly afterwards. The Mesarthrim High Command has not made its official vote yet, but they appear to be slightly in favor of negotiations with the rebel Imperial forces.

= Part 3 =

April 27, 2322

by James Davis

=====Terra=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Empire is in a state of complete confusion. Imperial troops now wonder if they have been fighting in the name of a lie. A battle for revenge is a lot less appealing when you have to fight someone you used to trust. Emperor Black released a brief statement today on the status of the Empire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Lucius Black: "Citizens, I know that you are angry, afraid, and confused. You know that the unity of the Empire is at an end. You don't know who to believe. Believe me. I swear to you that I did not destroy our world. My own father discovered Blackis, and I loved it. Admiral Anthony Hadrian is a traitor, conspiring with the enemies of Blackis who destroyed it in the first place."

Live! Attack on the Empire - 2/5/2324 - by James Davis

B-Net News is proud to present the first nearly live broadcast of a Prefecture fleet heading into battle against the remnants of the Empire. For security reasons, this broadcast has been delayed by ten minutes, and may be edited by Prefecture Intelligence to avoid leaking tactical information to the enemies of the Alliance of Planets.

The Prefecture and the United Archadian Republics fleets are closing in on the last few ships of the Empire. Transmission will be broadcast directly from the bridge of the Triumph, the Prefecture Second Fleet flagship. Transmissions will break periodically to return to the B-Net newsroom to give summaries of the battle.

== Transmission Begins ==

“Is that thing on?” Admiral Bremen taps the microphone, eyeing it suspiciously. “I don’t care if Intelligence is cleaning this up. It’s a security risk.”

“Relax, Admiral. The citizens of the Alliance need to see our victory over the Empire.

“Citizens, allow me to introduce myself. I am Justin Rahn, CEO and founder of Rahn Industries, and the designer of the Triumph flagship, which will be championing this victory for the Prefecture and Archadian fleets. Admiral Bremen here will be commanding this battle. Admiral, do you have anything to say to the people back home?”

“I don’t have time to participate in your little media circus. Our fighters engage the enemy outer screens in half a minute.”

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

The Triumph is the head of the Prefecture Second Battle Fleet, which has a total of 46 warships, ranging from light frigate to heavy cruiser. Admiral Jason Bremen commands the fleet. His hard-edged, no-nonsense attitude and tactics have won him many victories in the past. He plays rough, and cares nothing for “gentlemen’s warfare.”

The Archadians have sent a token fleet of eight warships: four Osrits and three Brehg Matifs. In the past, they were a neutral party in the war, but the Empire has hidden its fleet remnants a mere two light years from the Archadian homeworld, Glengarr. The Archadians signed a pact with the Alliance of Planets to remain non-aggressors, and led the Alliance to the location of the hidden Imperials after the recent border fiasco with the United Mesarthrim Clans.

This Imperial Fleet was involved in the Picket's Grave battle that resulted in the death of Mesarthrim Uber-General Andreas Tischler. It has a total of 108 warships, most of which are light cruisers and outdated models. A scant 18 are modern warships.

The Lorithos Spy Network and the Prefecture High Order of Inquisitors both believe that the self-styled Emperor, Lucius Black, is aboard one of the warships. He may be cornered now, but he does have one ace up his sleeve.

The mysterious and Remnant, previously thought gone into parts unknown, is still present in the form of a single powerful warship, and a lone space station. The Remnant, with their almost unstoppable weapons that even give the Meroniri something to think about, could easily tip the tide of battle with a single warship.

== Resuming Transmission ==

“Charlie Lead to Triumph, we have met the bandits.”

“Acknowledged Charlie Lead. Shake them up a bit and get back under our defenses. I don’t want any fighters lost.” Bremen watches his radar screen, tapping it every so often and issuing orders to individual fighter pilots. His Stukas were giving the Imperial Vampire fighters a solid thrashing, but a moment’s hesitation or the loss of a single fighter could turn the tide of battle.

“Determine time to firing range,” Bremen commands with complete calm, his voice devoid of emotion. “Then lock on the Imperial Flagship with all batteries.”

“Forty seconds. We’ll be in Remnant firing range in seconds,” the tactical officer reports. He’s young, probably on his first tour of duty. “Only six enemy cruisers have locked onto the Triumph.”

“It’s time to give Mr. Rahn’s warship a workout. Open fire with everything! I don’t care what we hit. Recall all of our fighters.”

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

The Prefecture and United Archadian Republics combined fleet opened fire immediately before they came within the range of the Remnant ship's guns. The Prefecture weapons were out of range too, but neither the Imperial commanders nor the Remnant Princeps knew what the Prefecture ships were capable of. Bremen’s ruse worked, causing the Imperial fleet to break formation and evade the currently ineffective weapons.

A split second later, the Remnant ship opened fire with a barrage of missiles. Dozens of lethal antimatter warheads streaked towards the Triumph. Moments before impact, several veered off into space, and the rest exploded several dozen meters from the Triumph’s hull.

The Triumph is equipped with a gravity shield, one of the first Terran models ever built. Several of Bremen’s Second Fleet ships are equipped with similar shields, and were effectively safe from missiles while the shields remained functional.

Two Prefecture ships weren’t so lucky. They, like the bulk of Bremen’s fleet, had not received their gravity shields yet. The frigate Polecat and the cruiser Serenity were torn apart by antimatter missiles.

Before the Remnant ship had time to launch another salvo, the Prefecture fleet closed the distance between themselves and the Imperial forces. All warships were in range of one another, and space was filled with weapons fire.

Lasers from Imperial ships, bullets and Neutron Beams from Prefecture ships, advanced weapons from Remnant and Archadian ships, and missiles from all filled space.

The fighting was brutal for about three minutes, then lessened as the fleets pulled away from each other to assess the damages and plan their course of action.

== Resuming Transmission ==

“Give me a fleet report,” Bremen sighs audibly. “Well Mr. Rahn, your ship has performed admirably.”

“Um, yes,” Justin Rahn is a businessman, not a fighter. It’s obvious that the battle has not been kind to his temperament. He wipes sweat from his brow before continuing. “I’m glad to see that the gravity shields worked the way we designed them. As long as you don’t take too many direct hits, the gravity couplings will keep the shield stable.”

The Triumph’s tactical officer speaks up. “Sir, we lost six ships after the initial fire exchange. Including the Polecat and the Serenity, that’s eight. The Drake reports heavy damage to its gravity shields, and her captain is requesting permission to fall back and make repairs before rejoining our second assault. Five other ships have sustained severe damage, and fifteen more have taken superficial damage.”

“And the Empire?” asks Bremen.

“We took out seventeen of their warships, mostly older models. Two of them were newer Vengeance cruisers. Only five of the other twelve new-model ships are still in fighting condition. The Remnant vessel is undamaged.”

“Damn. It’s obvious that we can’t take them in a head-on fight.” Bremen brings up a map of the battlefield on his screen. “Tell those B-Net boys to cut transmission, we can’t have this plan leaked.”

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

Bremen’s warships regroup quickly, and are ready for a second attack in less than ten minutes. The Remnant and newer Imperial warships prepare just as quickly, but the older and slower warships of the Empire are still in disarray when Bremen attacks again.

This time, Bremen’s heavy, slower cruisers hang back, and six light frigates – the only frigates with gravity shields – attack the Imperial fleet. These smaller ships keep the ungainly old ships of the Empire between them and the Remnant warships, keeping them safe from the most powerful weapons. The Remnant commander can only hit them with missiles, and those are safely deflected by the frigates’ gravity shields.

The Prefecture frigates quickly drop a payload of Apocalypse missiles into a cluster of Imperial Vigilant frigates, then reverse thrust and head for the relative safety of the Prefecture fleet. Five Imperial ships suffer fatal structural damage and explosive decompression. Bremen’s attack is nearly flawless, but the frigate Thales is caught in crossfire and torn apart when its gravity shields fail under the assault of hundreds of missiles.

== Resuming Transmission ==

“Sir, we just lost contact with the Thales. She’s down.”

“Disappointing,” Bremen grumbles. “The Remnant are too powerful. I don’t think we can finish them without reinforcements from the First or Third Fleets.”

“Sir, I think you’d better see this,” the Triumph’s Intel officer beckons Bremen over to a console. “The whole Imperial and Remnant fleet just lit up on optical communications. They’re all talking with each other about something, and they don’t care if we know it. It’s tight-beam optical, so we can’t intercept, but something has them excited.”

“I’ll bet they’re preparing a counteroffensive,” Bremen muses.

The Triumph shudders violently. Bremen and Rahn are both thrown to the floor.

“Report!” Bremen bellows.

“We’ve sustained eight hits, nuclear yield,” the Tactical officer replies. Dozens of warnings light up on his console. “The whole fleet has taken hits.”

“How? The whole Imperial fleet is out of range, and no one’s managed to find an operational cloaking device for six years!” Bremen fumes. “We’re not losing this ship or this battle!”

“I don’t know sir. I’m already running a full scan around us, and we’re receiving reports from the rest of the fleet.”

“Cut that B-Net Transmission!” Bremen orders.

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

The Prefecture fleet is in total disarray. They are being hit from all sides, and they can’t trace a single enemy.

The Imperial fleet fares no better. Whatever is dealing the blows to the Prefecture fleet isn’t discriminatory in its attacks.

We are receiving a transmission from Prefecture High Command. Beginning transmission.

== Cut to the Jerome Black ==

“Citizens of the Prefecture, this is Admiral Robert Marshall. As of ten minutes ago, we have made second contact with a hostile alien species.

“Admiral Bremen cut the B-Net Transmission for security purposes, but I have overruled him, and we will soon return to this battle. The Empire is no longer engaged in battle with our forces, so there is no longer a security threat.

“The Transmission will be live and unedited. I hope that by seeing what we’re about to go up against, you, the civilians of our fledgling nation will stand behind the military one-hundred percent.”

== Resuming Transmission ==

“No ships lost yet sir, but the Drake and the Perseverance have both had minor hull breaches,” the Triumph’s tactical officer reports. “We still can’t track the new enemy.”

“Sir, transmission from Admiral Marshall,” reports the Communications officer. “It’s priority one, I’m putting it on.”

“Bremen, I’m afraid I don’t have any good news for you,” says Marshall. “Hadrian and the Third Fleet are en route, but they can’t make it for two days. Our Intel operatives recognized the attacks you’re suffering, but we don’t know how to track them either. I’m sending all the information we have. Time to think on your toes and roll with the punches. Come out of this alive Jason, we need you. Marshall out.”

“Let me see that data,” Bremen orders. He reads through it quickly. “Insects? Odd, I don’t remember hearing about first contact, and Intel says this is the second time we’re up against them.”

“Admiral, we have a transmission from the Perseverance.”

“Put it on.”

The message is brief. “Admiral, we’ve been boarded. The enemy created four small breaches in the hull, and dropped troops inside. Our Marines have been holding them off from the vital areas, but we can’t stop them forever. There are hundreds of them. They’re monsters of some sort, really ugly bugs.”

“Sit tight Captain, I’m dispatching Marines from the Triumph to assist. Prepare to be boarded,” Bremen orders. “TacOff, I want to know how to track these enemies in the next five minutes. Communications, get me fighter command.”

“Charlie and Baker squadrons are ready for orders sir,” the Communications Officer reports.

“Pilots, fly point for the warships under attack. Try to get a visual on the attackers. Squadron leaders, tactics and deployment are at your discretion.”

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

The Prefecture fleet slowly recovers from the initial shock of the undetectable attackers, and spreads out into individual ships, firing all weapons in anti-fighter screen configurations. They have no targets they can see, but the random firing screens quickly bring down the number of hits.

The United Archadian Republics warship shields are much more effective than those of the Prefecture, so they suffer little. They aid in the anti-fighter screening fire.

The Imperial fleet also suffers losses, but our B-Net observers soon lose track of their movements as they pull away from the action.

== Resuming Transmission ==

“Admiral, this is Baker Lead. We’ve made visual contact with the enemy. My scanners still don’t pick up anything, and there are too many to count.”

“Keep them off the warships as best you can. We’ll give you tracking info as soon as we figure out how to track them,” Bremen replies. He turns to his bridge crew. “Can we track them yet?”

“Somewhat sir. Heat scanning indicates a spike immediately before one of them hits us. We’ve fed the info to our gunners, but we get hit anyway. There’s apparently some lag time between the actual heat spike and our picking it up. I’ve fed the info to Charlie squadron, but they haven’t fared much better.”

Bremen curses loudly, causing Rahn and the bridge crew to jump in surprise. “Navigation, get us out of here. The whole fleet. We’re getting torn to pieces. Tow the Drake and Perseverance if you have to, but I want the fleet en route to Ueda at best possible speed.”

“Sorry sir, we can't make Ueda in one jump. There's a new colony nearby. Mirach. We can make it, recharge, then jump to Tavou. We can Gate to Ueda from there."

"Very well. Make the jump."

Aye sir, relaying orders. We’ll be underway in two minutes.”

“Baker and Charlie squadrons, return to your hangars. Sensors, launch probes and have them do a flyby on the Imperial fleet. We can’t take them out, but I want to know what they lost, and where they’re going if possible.”

== Cut to B-Net Newsroom ==

The Prefecture Fleet escaped certain destruction, but the Imperial survivors slipped through their fingers. The Triumph’s probes managed to retrieve some excellent information, but Prefecture High Command has classified it for now.

B-Net News will report the details of the insect creatures as soon as we receive more information.

In summary, the Prefecture Second Fleet lost nine vessels out of 46. The survivors suffered a lot of structural, system and hull damage.

Admiral Bremen has been ordered to give his fleet two weeks of leave and two months of light duty while the fleet is refitted and repaired. The cruisers Drake and Perseverance were each boarded by several hundred insects. They managed to repel them with the help of the Triumph’s Marines and captured a couple specimens alive.

The Imperial remnant lost twenty-two vessels to the Prefecture fleet out of 108. They lost several more to the insects, but Prefecture High Command is unwilling to disclose their recon data as of this date.

The Prefecture Third Fleet is still en route to the battle sight, in hopes of retrieving valuable recon information on the insect attackers.

Prefecture xenobiologists have classified the creatures as Volucris, though one Marine aboard the Drake called them “ugly bugs.” The nickname seems to have stuck.

B-Net will keep the citizens of all Alliance of Planets Member States informed as information is released.

= Part 1 =

January 9, [[2315]]

by James Davis

(note: scheduled to be rewritten, possibly retitled)

=====NORAD=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The room is silent and empty, as it has been for over a hundred years. Computer consoles are dark, but as clean as the day they were made. The sterile room hasn't seen dust since the [[United Earth Alliance]] shut it down in [[2200]]. It was and is the center of the Terran Defense Grid--the human homeworld's only coordinator of solar system defense.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Silent warnings awaken long-dormant automatic subroutines. The message goes out, and is answered. The warnings are analyzed, further data is collected, and decisions are made. Only a millisecond passes. Beatle is awake and active.

=====Denver, Capital of Terra=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Mr. President, we have a problem."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fernando Meliazio looks up from his book. It is late, and the President is relaxing before bed. "Joe, who is that?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm not sure." The Secret Service agent replies. "He must have very high clearance to access this channel."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"It has been a while," the voice continues. "I am Beatle, the Terran Defense Grid Engineered Intelligence. We have exactly three hours, seven minutes, and twenty-three seconds before hostile warships are in range to fire on Terra. They hit our listening satellites outside the system, and have moved in, hitting every station and colony we have. They just finished off the outer asteroid mining facilities."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Who would be attacking us? We aren't even armed--we've been at peace with everyone ever since first contact!" President Meliazio sits bolt upright, dropping his book.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I do not know, sir." Beatle replies. "They are not Meroniri, Ikronin, Ologrians, or Lorithos. I do not know of any other races, but my databases have not been updated for one hundred twenty years, two months, one week, three days--"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Meliazio cuts Beatle off. "Whatever. The question is, what can we do to defend ourselves?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sir, every one of my mobile defensive systems is offline, and has been so since my systems were put on standby." The EI pauses. "I have managed to reestablish connections with two of my attack satellites, but their weapon systems are at only twenty percent capacity. To be honest sir, the odds are terrible. I have calculated three thousand possible scenarios, and only two of them result in our managing to damage them at all."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Raise the invading fleet. We have to reason with them."

=====Space=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The threat to Terra is the Royal Imperial Navy Armada, the mightiest human fleet in history. Spearheading the assault are the ''Iron Hand'' and the ''Jerome Black'', the twin flagships of the fleet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emperor [[Lucius Black]] paces restlessly, often stopping to laugh uncontrollably. "Do you see? They burn! They burn! Where are your American and Canadian Battlecruisers now? Where is the French destroyer fleet?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Your highness," a tall man approaches Lucius. "Communication from Terra. Do you want to listen?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Grand Admiral Hadrian." the Emperor greets his military commander. "No, just deliver my prerecorded message."<br>

=====The White House=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"...in conclusion, you will die. Surrender will not be accepted." The transmission ends, and the Oval Office's view screen goes dark.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Beatle...do you recognize that man?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have checked my records. He is Lucius Black, as he said." The EI checks and rechecks his connections to defense stations, one hundred times each in a second. "I have managed to reestablish connections with my repair systems. I can have an additional fifty attack satellites online by the time the enemy reaches us, or two of our Battlecruisers pulled out of scrap and ready for launch. I believe that twenty-five satellites and one Battlecruiser would be best."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"OK," Meliazio sighs. "What's the status of our global lockdowns?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Most of the shelters are no longer useful. To be honest, sir, our best possible outcome is not good at all. I must suggest we use the time I can buy with the automatic defenses to withdraw."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Withdraw?!" Fernando Meliazio fumes. "Who?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Just the government, and as many civilians as can be accommodated. From what I've retrieved about [[Lucius Black]] from my archives, he hates the [[United Earth Alliance]], not the civilians." Beatle replies. "Sir, we now have two hours, forty minutes, and ten seconds. I have exhausted my database of scrapped Terran warships and weapons. I called in support the second I confirmed the presence of hostiles. Only [[Holt]] and [[Theia]] have responded."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What's the word?" Meliazio asks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"The Battlecruiser ''Bonaparte'' is on its way from Holt. Commander Jean Sarnou has asked the rest of Holt for more support, but he's all that's coming. He will arrive in four hours, thirty minutes, and two seconds."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"That's good. And [[Theia]]?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Everything they have. General-Commander Sanburn is leading their entire fleet here, but it will take him two days. The [[Ikronin]] have shut down our Wormhole Gate--they won't risk a hostile fleet in their home system."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Call General-Commander Ken Hurke as soon as that cruiser is ready." President Meliazio orders. "And Beatle, we should never have shut you down. You've done a great job."<br>

=====Luna=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;A second EI resides on Luna, Terra's moon. Noah was and is the first EI ever grown, and runs the Kundskab Ark, a huge archive of Terran history, art, and technology. Like Beatle, Noah has been dormant for years. Suddenly, he is awake and active.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Noah, respond''. The transmission is simple, but relays urgency. ''Accept data upload. Authorization code Beatle-101924-Gamma-98. Store and protect''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Noah accepts the data, archives it carefully away, then responds. ''War''?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''War''. Beatle confirms.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Will you win''?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''No. They will come for you''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''I will be ready''.<br>

=====Space=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Imperial Armada makes its final approach on Terra. Luna is on the far side of the planet, but the North American continent is clearly visible below. The battle begins between the combined forces of Beatle's defense satellites and the Battlecruiser ''Valdosta'', and the Imperial Armada.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Beatle performed as well as could be expected--considering his 115-year-old satellite weaponry and cruiser crewed entirely with inexperienced cadets, half an hour of harassing the Imperial Armada was stunning.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Defeat was inevitable. Soon, only General-Commander Ken Hurke and the UEAS ''Valdosta'' were intact and operational.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Beatle, we can't keep this up much longer." Hurke transmits to Terra. "What's going on down there?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have been using your defense as a screen." the EI replies. "I have evacuated thirty thousand people, based on a psychological profile of [[Lucius Black]]. Contrary to his threats, I do not believe he will destroy the whole planet--just the government and military. Still, as a precaution I have had the fallout shelters repaired, and the President has warned the general public. We will have the surface cleared in another hour."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Look, we can't survive much longer up here. None of my crew have any combat experience. Hell, I don't have any combat experience! There hasn't been a war since the last time that maniac was out there."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I have a solution, but you must remain in combat for another five minutes." Beatle reports.

=====Jerome Black=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sir, they stopped sending up new attack satellites." Admiral Robert Marshall reports to the Grand Admiral. "It's just the enemy cruiser now."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Launch all fighters. Give the Valkyries conventional loads only, for now." Hadrian orders.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Admiral Marshall, Grand Admiral Hadrian," a new voice breaks in. "This is Prism. I apologize for the interruption, but we have a new contact. It is a heavy cruiser, unknown specification. They are hailing us."<br>

= Part 2 =

February 5, [[2315]]

by Seth Dickinson

(note: set to be rewritten. Centers around Washington DC, which is not the capital of Terra anymore. It is now Denver.)

=====United Earth Alliance Defense Force Field Headquarters=====

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Second Battalion needs air support, grid uniform-mike two, we are heavily engaged and cut off from our armor units…”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Two Batt, this is Colonel Leavitt, be advised that there are hostile fast-movers south of your position. They’re some kind of bipedal tank, bigger than the Lorithos robots…”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“All units, this is Three Batt, we’re under attack by those damn walkers, they’re coming over the ridge and hitting our supply train…”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Two Batt is falling back, we’re –“<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The radio messages dissolve into static as the portable field system’s batteries give out. General Ariana Sidra looks around the room, her face pale. “We’re under attack, ladies and gentlemen. Any questions?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The officers of First Battalion, 1st North American Armored Division, all have the same shell-shocked expression that General Sidra is trying so desperately to hide. This attack is a nightmare: entirely unanticipated, totally overwhelming.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Why is this happening?” Colonel Samantha Giard’s hands are clamped on the edge of the table as if it’s the only thing holding her back from a long, long fall. “They've been orbiting for almost a month! Why attack now?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra shrugs. “They're led by a psychopath, but this is too well organized to be random. Communication channels are dead or jammed and Beatle is out of commission. Since NORAD got bombed we haven’t heard anything: warnings, orders, nothing.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Guess it’s up to us.” Sidra unfurls a map on the table, the antique paper crackling with age. With the local Internet down, there’s no way to access electronic navigation data. “None of our soldiers have ever seen action, except for those of us who were with the Space Police at some point in our careers, and most of our equipment is trash. She stares around the room, her gaze hard, meeting each officer’s eyes in turn. “Nonetheless, I think we can at least buy some time. The invaders are driving hard for Washington, and if we can divert them, we might give some of the Senate a chance to escape.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The General sketches a long half-circle on the map. “We’ll deploy here, covering these areas. If any elements of Second or Third battalion manage to escape from the, ah, situation north of here, they can link up with us. Colonel Giard, your artillery units should be ready to engage airborne threats.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra snaps the map shut with a flick of her wrist. “OK, people, let’s saddle up.”

=====North of Washington, DC=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“I really did just sign up for the uniform, General. That and the money. I never thought I’d see action.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra’s driver is a nervous, shrew-faced man, his pale skin drenched in sweat. The General gives him a reassuring clap on the shoulder. “Lieutenant Mitchell, you signed up for the right outfit. We’re not fighting Ologrians or anything; what we have here is a bunch of pirates, criminals really. This isn’t war, Mitchell, it’s just a police action. All you’ve got to do is drive, and I’ll handle the rest.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Mitchell laughs nervously. “I sure hope so, General.” He throws the mobile command post into gear, joining the column of tanks and APCs moving north on Interstate 81. "Never thought a bunch of pirates could ever kill the President though," he mutters under his breath.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra ducks out of the cockpit and back into the crowded command center, chewing on her lower lip. ''God, I hope my men aren’t all like Mitchell''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The United Earth Defense Force’s North American Armored Division is equipped with AVC-60 APCs and the aging TM-22 tracked tank. The term ‘division’ is a joke: with a total strength of only three battalions, the NAAD barely qualifies as a brigade. Worse yet, Sidra has no air, artillery, or information support, except for Colonel Giard’s under-strength missile unit.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''At least the electronics still work''. The glowing LCD displays in the command post show Sidra the layout of her units: a long column of armor moving north on the highway, trailed by the battalion’s logistical train and Giard’s artillery. ''Thank God I managed to scrape up those militia infantry units, or we wouldn’t have enough men to screen our flanks''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Uh, General, maybe you should take a look at this.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra glances over the operator’s shoulder; his screens display the coordinated tactical feeds of all the active sensors in the battalion. There is a flickering yellow icon on the north of the map.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;It takes the General a moment to recall her training with this software. “That’s an airborne contact. Who picked it up?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Ah, Colonel Giard’s Hawkeye radar truck, sir.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“And it’s not civilian traffic?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“I don’t think so.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra gnaws her lip for a moment. “Can we engage it?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The icon flashes crimson. The radar operator emits a startled gasp. “General, the computer’s saying that’s a hostile. Ah, unknown type, speed says it’s probably an aircraft…and its radiating heavily, sir. Search-band radar, height-finders…it’s hunting, sir.” <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Then shoot it down.”

=====Low Altitude - The Bleeding Edge=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc]] barely flinches when the UEA targeting radar paints his Raven. He counts off two seconds, then pulls his fighter into a lazy turn. A barely imperceptible nod acknowledges the flight of SAMs screeching toward him. Two more seconds, and Torsc acts. His fighter, marked with his signature crossed bloody scimitars, shrieks in protest as he throws it into a tight corkscrew dive. Two SAMs burst through the air behind him, missing by a fraction of a second. One flies on, oblivious to his change in course, but the other corrects itself and dives after him. Torsc waits until his Raven is practically brushing the treetops, then pulls up. The SAM crashes into the ground in a violent fireball.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Did you follow that?" Torsc asks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sir, I followed," his wingman replies.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Torsc waits. "And?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Sir?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Did they give me idiots for pilots''? Torsc sighs. "Did you lock on?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir. I'm locked on."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Take them out!" Torsc barks.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The other Raven is brilliantly lit by the afterburners of its own missiles, which streak down on the SAM batteries. Torsc adds his own missiles to the barrage, then follows them in and wipes out the anti-aircraft site with cannon fire.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Ivonrud, you'll speak to me after debriefing," Torsc orders.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes sir."

=====Shores of the Potomac=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Sidra nearly vomits at the sight of Colonel Giard's shattered remains. The artillery commander's position was compromised when she opened fire on Emil Torsc's Raven, and the return fire wiped out the artillery command post along with the Surface-to-Air batteries.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"General?" Lieutenant Mitchell comes up behind her, then breaks off at the sight. "Oh God! What happened?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"War happened, Lieutenant!" General Sidra snaps. "Find out who's next in Colonel Giard's chain of command, and get me in touch with them. Now, what did you want?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Radar reports show a large transport is about to land. I thought you should know."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;General Sidra stares into the sky, and a glowing orange fire-trail in the sky catches her eye. "That?" she asks, pointing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Probably. It's still several kilometers up, but it looks like it's about 200 meters long...it looks like one of our old amphibious landing cruisers."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sidra whistles. ''It's big enough to hold my entire command''. "You know what you said about the uniform, Lieutenant?"<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Yes." Mitchell grimaces.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm beginning to wish I didn't have one."<br>

=====The Jerome Black - Flight Deck=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Emil Torsc's Raven comes to a stop, and he jumps out.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Emil!" a friendly voice shouts. "Good hunting out there?" Marcus Rathbone, a mid-sized, ruddy-faced pilot in an old-style leather-and-fleece bomber jacket runs up to slap Torsc on the back.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"There's nothing out there Marcus," Torsc sighs, watching the rest of his squadron land and file into debriefing. "And frankly, I'm glad. These kids..." he shrugs, nodding towards the young men and women climbing out of their fighters.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"What about them?" Rathbone grins. "Most of them are really good pilots. I put my squadron through its paces this morning, and all of them got perfect scores. I wish we had this lot back in the first war."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"They're too young," Torsc grimaces. "No, not too young. These kids are fifteen, but I was flying when I was ten. It's not their age. It's something else. Take my wing, Jack Ivonrud, for example. He takes no initiative. He follows orders without question, to the letter. I asked him if he was locked on, then had to order him to fire on a SAM battery that had fired on me. Either one of us would have killed those missiles as soon as we saw them."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I know what you mean," Rathbone frowns. "Back in the first war, when we were flying with Arias and Brown, we always had lots of chatter on the radio. These kids are like robots."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"I'm going to have to find a way to break them in. The skill is there, but if they fly without passion, they'll die."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"We can't all be heroes," Rathbone shrugs.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"No," Torsc agrees. "But we chose to be here. They didn't. I won't let them be led to the slaughter."

= Part 3 =

January 9, [[2315]]

by James Davis

(note: set to be rewritten)

=====The Bonaparte=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Jean Sarnou is a small man, similar to his ancient role-model, Napoleon Bonaparte. Sarnou is from an ancient, proud family of fighters--dating all the way back to the legendary French legions of World War III.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ''Bonaparte'' is an ADF-W-5000 class Battlecruiser, a relic of the days of [[United Earth Alliance]] expansionism, upgraded and tweaked to Sarnou's specifications. It is primed and ready for battle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Capitaine, nous sommes arrivés." ((Captain, we have arrived.)) Sarnou's helmsman reports.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Rapport de statut." ((Status report.)) Sarnou orders.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Monsieur, il y a des milliers d'ennemis." ((Sir, there are thousands of enemies.)) The sensors operator replies. "Ce n'est pas une incursion. C'est un armada. Une invasion." ((This is not a raid. This is an armada. An invasion.))<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"C'est un environnement de riches de cible." ((It's a target rich environment.)) Sarnou laughs. "Préparez pour s'engager!" ((Prepare to engage!))<br>

=====The White House, Capitol of Terra=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Mr. President, we have our diversion."<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Fernando Meliazio smiles. "Launch our transports. Sarnou will give them a real fight."

=====Space=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The ''Bonaparte'' and ''Iron Hand'' share a brief transmission, only to exchange threats. With fair warning given, the Bonaparte tears into the Imperial Armada. The Emperor only sends a dozen frigates to deal with the threat, preferring to continue his bombardment of the planet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Sarnou takes the helm of his cruiser, flying like the veteran he is. The ''Bonaparte'' blasts through the oncoming frigate assault, leaving scored hulls in its wake.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The battle heightens when Sarnou's fighters arrive, minutes behind the cruiser. ADF-F-400 fighters meet the Imperial Ravens in a swirling cloud of death around the warships. The enclosing Vigilant frigate line bulges outward, and nearly breaks, but is strengthened by a squadron of Valkyrie bombers.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;For three hours, Jean Sarnou and the crew of the Bonaparte buy time for the fleeing [[United Earth Alliance]] government and Terran civilians. Hundreds of ships flee the planet, some only to be destroyed by the bombarding Imperial Armada.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Finally, Sarnou's expertise fails him, and the ''Bonaparte'' takes a bad hit. The ship founders in its evasions, then blasts away into the safety of its warp drive field, disappearing into the distance in the blink of an eye.

=====Three Hours Later=====
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;In the end, the result is the same. Terra's orbit lanes are soon cluttered with unopposed Imperial warships. The president fails to escape, his shuttle is shot down trying to make it to a long-range transport in orbit. Beatle dies in the bombardment of the NORAD strategic command base. Noah shuts himself down, locking away the secrets of the [[United Earth Alliance]] from prying Imperial eyes.<br>

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The Second Exodus War has begun.

=====UASN Expedition -- Far orbit of Sol=====
'''Transmission: Captain Obarin Parth to UASN Command'''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"All indigenous forces destroyed. Limited military survivors, civilian refugees bound for system designated Titan Colony, where the ''Inquisitor'' will assume observation.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;"Status of planet: Blockaded by invading fleet. Status of orbital bodies: Captured. Status of star system: Occupied. Occupational forces confirmed to be hostile--do not attempt intervention. [[Sol System]] is now restricted to any [[Ixoarchada]] traffic. Expedition is moving to rendezvous with the ''Inquisitor''. Parth out."

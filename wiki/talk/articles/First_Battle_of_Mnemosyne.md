'''Shatter Point''' - written by [[Seth Dickinson (author)|Seth Dickinson]]

----

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc]] does not see chaos.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There are fifteen hundred [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] swarming in the space above [[Mnemosyne]], each one of them a gleaming, jewel-bright pinprick outside [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]’s canopy. They dance like a shoal of wind-blown fireflies, darting and streaming in long eddies and turbulent currents. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]’s targeting displays flicker uselessly; the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]]’s avionics can’t keep track of so many targets.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] can.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Marcus Rathbone]], [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]’s closest rival, claims that he lets his mind wander in combat – that he thinks about politics, trivia, food, whatever will distract him into a Zen state of mind. [[Marcus Rathbone|Rathbone]] flits from dogfight to dogfight without plan or purpose, confounding his enemies and killing them in their confusion.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Jack Ivonrud]], the distant third of the [[Onyx Empire|Empire]]'s top aces, focuses so completely on flying that he becomes his ship. His cool demeanor and total unity with his [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] earn him dozens of efficient, if unimaginative kills.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc]] becomes God. When he climbs in the cockpit, he can grasp the structure of the entire battle, hold it in his mind and see the critical points, each nexus of instability where his actions can make a difference. [[Marcus Rathbone|Rathbone]] and [[Jack Ivonrud|Ivonrud]] kill pilots. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] kills entire squadrons.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Where others see only the chaos of a thousand lights of battle, [[Emil Torsc]] sees perfect order...his order.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;This clash is too complex to be described with words as limited as ‘battle lines’ or ‘flanks’: the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] and [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] forces are intermeshed, entangled, like a tower built of fifteen hundred little metal shards, shards that dance and die without compromising the tower itself. The tower is in flux, but in a state of equilibrium. Neither side has the upper hand.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;There are vectors in the medium, lines of stress where events might shatter.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The flaring detonations of missiles, the cerulean glimmer of particle beams, the slashing drive trails of the dueling [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] – each is a voice in [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]'s mind. Their apparent randomness is an illusion, a fog obscuring a deeper structure. He watches the dancing of the lights and divines the flow of power: the shatter-points in the battle.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;He finds the one place to touch, to unravel the entire structure – to topple the battle in the direction he wants.<br>


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw]] never thought she could kill someone.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She was a psychologist before the war – a slight woman, quiet, mousy. She had never pondered taking another life, but when the time came, it was as easy as breathing.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Gradually, [[Helen Cashaw]] came to realize her rare gift for killing without compunction or hesitation. Her talents puzzle her comrades: she is nimble enough on the controls, but not exceptional. Despite her mediocre piloting skills, [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] is the [[United Earth Alliance Defense Force|Alliance Defense Force]]’s most prolific killer – the top ace. She wins because she understands people--most people hesitate before killing, but [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] does not.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;When she settles her crosshairs on an [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]], she sees through the metal and plastic to the flesh within: a pilot with fears, desires, phobias and quirks. She has a knack for reading her opponents, playing them, and killing them.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Even here, at the edge of this boiling mass of [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], each dogfight feels like an intimate conversation. She whispers to her opponent as she guides her [[F-800]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] across his nose, teasing him. He is an aggressive one, piloting a red-painted [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]]. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] wonders if he has something to prove: he has eschewed several opportunities to pursue older [[F-400]]s, as if they were unworthy of his attention.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''This one is insecure'', she decides. ''He's playing tough''.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] turns to pursue her, its lasers reaching out and grazing her tail-planes. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] flicks her wrist, guiding her ship through bone-crushing evasive turn, forcing the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] onto a high-G swerve to keep her in his sights. She keeps up the pressure, slashing through a series of sharp turns, her G-suit squeezing her lower body to keep her from blacking out.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''Keep following me'', she urges. ''Keep pushing yourself – keep trying to be as good as me''. She has already decided her opponent is male, probably tall and muscular: far more susceptible to G-forces than a petite woman like herself.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Another sharp turn, and the pursuing [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] goes wild for a moment, its pilot blacking out as he tries to match her maneuvers. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] yanks her throttles, spins her ship on its axis, and blows the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] away before the pilot recovers consciousness.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She allows herself a fierce grin. “Delta-Seven, where the hell are you?”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Your six by five, closing,” her wingman replies. “I couldn’t keep up.”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] nurses her throttles and moves to rejoin her wingman. Immediately, she spots new foes– a pair of [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Ravens]], closing from directly above him. “Delta-Seven, bandits, six by twelve!”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Aw, hell – they’ve got me locked up -”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Delta-Seven’s fighter breaks into a wild, corkscrewing evasive turn. The lead [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] looses a "Hound", and the compact missile streaks in after the [[F-800]].<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Inbound!” [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] shouts. “Decoy, notch left!”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Notching-”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Delta-Seven’s [[F-800]] veers left, its drive flaring, and cuts across the missile’s line of flight, popping decoys as it goes. The missile wavers, dives at one of the decoys, and explodes harmlessly. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] breathes a sigh of relief – before she realizes that the second [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] has somehow anticipated Seven’s evasive. It’s already diving in for a cannon attack, its nose tracking Seven’s [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] as if the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] pilot can somehow predict his target’s every thought.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Seven, break right!”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Her wingman doesn’t have a chance to reply before the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]]’s lasers track onto him and burn through his defenses. Seven’s ship vanishes in a puff of soundless flame.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] tracks her nose onto the [[Onyx Empire|Imperial]] ship, her thumb punching the missile selector. Even before the missile’s seeker starts hunting for a lock, the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] slashes downwards, turning in towards her. ''Damn, he’s fast''!<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] has to throttle down and dive abruptly to avoid overshooting – and the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] abruptly accelerates, lasers flickering. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] twitches her wrist convulsively and only barely manages to get out of the way as the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] screams past her, less than ten kilometers away.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She gets a good view of the [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] as she passes, and what she sees makes her blood run cold. ''That paint scheme – that [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]]’s from Scimitar Squadron. It must be [[Emil Torsc]]''.<br>


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] nods in silent salute as the [[F-800]] whirls out of his way. He screams past the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] ship, only ten kilometers off its starboard side, and jerks his ship’s nose around, trying to track the target as it recedes into the distance.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] pilot isn’t interested in obliging; he – she, [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] reminds himself; in all likelihood, any pilot who can handle such sharp maneuvers is a female – kicks her throttles to maximum and heads towards the center of the battle, leaving [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] far behind.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] throws his own throttles forward and pursues. He doesn’t like getting too deep into the combat zone; if he becomes too submerged in the chaos, he risks losing his awareness of the situation at large. But this prize is worth it. This single [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] is the battle’s shatter-point, the linchpin that’s holding the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] forces together.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] can see it in the way that other [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] squadrons rally when this fighter approaches. This ship, this pilot, means something.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The [[F-800]] curves behind a clot of dueling [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], a group of aging [[F-400]]s trying to hold off a few swift [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Ravens]] and failing. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] blows right through the middle of the group – and suddenly the [[F-800]] is behind him, settling onto his tail.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''[[Helen Cashaw]]. She’s the only [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] pilot who could hold this mess together.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] weaves, counting on his [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]]’s nimble handling and small target profile to protect him as the [[F-800]] lances PAC lightning after him. He adjusts his course, heading for one of the fire lanes that [[Robert Marshall]]’s [[:Category:Frigates|frigates]] have set up – perhaps he can lure [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] into the stream of Autocannon fire?<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;No such luck. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] forces him to break off with another storm of particle beams, turning him back towards the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] fleet.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“This is rather refreshing,” [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] murmurs to himself. He whips his [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] through another hard turn, grunting to help stave off G-forces, but [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] matches him easily. A PAC beam scorches into his [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]]’s rear, touching off alarms.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] throttles his engine back, hoping to convince [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] that she’s inflicted severe damage – but she isn’t fooled. She keeps her distance, bracketing [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] and forcing him into another sharp turn.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“So much for cheap tricks.” Fifteen seconds have elapsed since the start of the battle – an eternity. [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] reaches into himself, drawing on all his cunning and ruthlessness, assembling a picture of [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] and searching for her shatter-point.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''She understands people'', [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] remembers. ''She's a psychologist. I will make her understand'' '''fear'''.


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] grits her teeth against G-forces, struggling to keep the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] centered in her sights. Blood rushes to her head, drowning her vision in crimson – then withdraws as she banks into another sharp turn, leaving her dizzy. Still, she manages to score a few glancing hits on [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] – and the Gs must be hitting him even harder.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] dives, and [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]] curves after him as [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] guides his ship towards another dogfight. [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]’s eyes narrow as her opponent dives towards a group of [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], lasers flickering out towards them. That is a serious breach of the unspoken etiquette of [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] combat--never break a dogfight.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]’s anger turns to full-blown rage as one of the [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]], caught unawares by [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]]’s attack, blows apart in an argent fireball. Two other [[United Earth Alliance|Alliance]] [[:Category:Fighters|fighters]] descend on [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]], trying to avenge their fallen comrade – and cut into [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]’s path.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;“Get out of the way!” she screams. “Delta-Six is padlocked!”<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She barely manages to avoid the clumsy [[F-400]]s, and she loses [[Emil Torsc|Torsc]] in the process. It takes her a moment to reacquire him; he’s used the few moments of breathing room, and he’s screaming back towards her at high speed.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;She swings her nose towards him, settling her crosshairs on the [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]]’s slim form. ''A head-to-head run…he has guts.''<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;The laws of head-to-head are simple: ''fire too early, and you miss and die. Wait too long, and you never have a chance''. Most pilots lose their nerve and break off early.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]’s eyes narrow. The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] grows in her crosshairs, its drive painting a bloody halo around it. There is a knife-edged precision in the way it moves, a perfect, cold grace.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]'s breath grows ragged and rasps through her helmet. ''Fire. Fire, you son of a'' –<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;''He won’t shoot. This is [[Emil Torsc]], an angel of'' '''death''', ''the pilot who does not make mistakes. You’ve known it all along, Helen, and you can’t'' – <br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Helen Cashaw|Helen]] squeezes her triggers, and blue lightning lances out. She throws the stick forward, diving, screaming against the G-forces and against the onrushing momentum of fate. The [[F-04 Superiority Fighter - "Raven"|Raven]] grows, its wings reaching forward to embrace her -<br>


&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;[[Emil Torsc]] caresses the trigger. ''Shatter-point'', he smiles, watching [[Helen Cashaw|Cashaw]]'s broken [[:Category:Fighters|fighter]] crumble to pieces.

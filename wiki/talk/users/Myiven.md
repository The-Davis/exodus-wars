== Jim Cain ==

James,
You mention that Jim Cain has a level 1 interface, but that should be a Level 2.  A level 1 wouldn't be compatible with Prefecture computing systems, as it requires a completely immersive environment and blocks all signals to the rest of his body.  I.e., he'd have to be suspended and interface directly do the computer (i.e. the matrix/bacta tank).  That wouldn't work on a Prefecture ship unless they created a module and connected it to the ship's AI (you'd need a Prism-spawn for this).  A level 2, however, would allow him to connect to the ships AI (still need a Prism-spawn), but also maintain consciousness and work on a standard Prefecture bridge or interact the way an officer in the PNC should.  Still, that would require quite an operation.  A civilian-grade level 5 would be too minimal not make that big of a difference, but a level civvie-grade 3 or 4 might be a good alternative too.

Thats how Archadian naval ships work, except everyone ties into the level 2 and communicates exclusively with that via the ship's APACHE.  If nobody else had a level 2, Cain could talk with the computer, but would have to go back and forth between it and the other crew-people.  Additionally, if he was tied into the computer that well, he'd have a better understanding of the ship and situation as a whole than even the ship's captain.

Setting aside the idea of unrestricted civilian traffic into the Ursa sector only 2 years after First Contact, and this random guy being able to secure one of THE most well guarded secrets of the Ixoarchada (and learned to harness it in no time, whereas it usually takes years) as a whole on just a holiday trip....why is he in the story?  I understand the idea of a fluff piece, but this bit of fluff presents so many problems of compatibility and acquisition that it's a bit ridiculous.  It's like the Archadians already outfitting Tesseract drives on their ships within only 2 years.  If it was that easy to infiltrate and steal, why isn't it more widespread?

Sorry to spoil your fun, but it makes no sense.  If you want to go ahead with it, that's fine, but it's a huge plothole.

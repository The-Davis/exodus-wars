My name is Justin Wick, and I've had an on-again off-again relationship with EW for ten years now.  It's really exciting to see how far it's come along.  Due to my involvement with James Davis in the NEMISYS Computer-Assisted RPG system, my interest in EW as a world-building vehicle (and campaign setting) has been rekindled.

= Statement of Intent =

I intend to infuse some of the more minor sections of the wiki with novel or semi-novel socio-political/economic ideas as interesting background, as well as help polish the science and technology of the EW universe.  While I have significant project management experience, it's likely that I will not play that type of role in EW, especially considering my commitments as the manager of NEMISYS.

= Bio =

I am a 25 year old Systems Engineer at a major defense contractor.  I have a bachelors degree in Applied Physics, and a masters degree in Computer Science. Throughout my career I have worked on many interesting things - multiple robotics project, movie special effects, the Mars Exploration Rover mission, supercomputer-driven astrophysics simulations and such.
